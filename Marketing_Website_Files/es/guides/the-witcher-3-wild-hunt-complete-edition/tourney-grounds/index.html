Campo de torneos

Guía de The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition · Llegada a Toussaint para participar en el torneo de caballeros
← Todas las zonas de esta guía

El Campo de torneos sirve como la gran arena central del Ducado de Toussaint, donde los caballeros se reúnen para poner a prueba sus habilidades en combate, equitación y arquería. Geralt llega aquí por primera vez persiguiendo a una bestia durante la historia principal antes de ocupar el lugar de Guillaume en las competencias para desentrañar una oscura maldición durante la misión "El gorjeo de un caballero enamorado". Los visitantes pueden participar en los eventos del festival, practicar en los campos de tiro y equitación, jugar al Gwynt o comprar con varios artesanos del torneo.

Región
Duchy of Toussaint
Niveles
Level 35+
Cómo llegar
Arrive automatically during the main quest "The Beast of Toussaint" after defeating the giant Golyat at the Dulcinea Windmill; Geralt rides toward the arena chasing the Beast.
Conecta con
Corvo Bianco Vineyard, Castel Ravello Vineyard, Albertus Grotto, City of Beauclair
Misiones relacionadas
The Warble of a Smitten Knight · The Beast of Toussaint · Gwent: Never Fear, Skellige's Here · There Can Be Only One · Capture the Castle
Servicios y personajes
Journeyman Blacksmith · Armorer · Tourney Grounds Innkeeper

Versión corta

Lo importante

  • •Sparing the Shaelmaar in the arena to gain credit toward the Compassion virtue for "There Can Be Only One."
  • •Registering under your chosen alias at the inscription tent to secure custom tournament weapons, armor, and saddles.
  • •Sweeping every single tournament event to earn unique rewards, admirer fan mail, and an adoring follower.
  • •Resolving Vivienne's curse alongside Guillaume to ensure the couple stays together happily in Beauclair.

Errores comunes

  • •Executing the Shaelmaar after combat, which deprives you of demonstrating the chivalric virtue of Compassion.
  • •Progressing the main storyline into "Capture the Castle," which immediately fails "The Warble of a Smitten Knight" and locks out all tournament gear.
  • •Registering as Geralt of Rivia if you need to duel Prince Anséis on horseback to prove the virtue of Honor.

Lo que puedes saltarte

  • •Striking the front shell of the Shaelmaar, as its thick armor deflects regular strikes.
  • •Attempting to parry the colossal overhead two-handed broadsword strikes of Gregoire de Gorgon.

Guía paso a paso

La zona en el orden en que juegas. Marca lo que completes: también se marcará en las listas de abajo.

  1. 1

    Entering the Arena and Facing the Shaelmaar

    When you gallop northwest of Beauclair from the Dulcinea Windmill during "The Beast of Toussaint," you arrive at the Tourney Grounds signpost and immediately head inside The Grand Arena. You must confront the armored Shaelmaar in the central sand colosseum alongside Guillaume de Launfal and Palmerin de Launfal. Once the beast falls, Anna Henrietta watches as you make a critical choice to either execute the monster for the spectators or command the crowd to stand down and spare it.

    Combate clave: Arena Shaelmaar

    Consejo: Lure the rolling Shaelmaar into stone walls to flip it onto its back, then blast its unprotected underbelly with Aard or heavy strikes coated in Relict Oil.

    Cuidado: Sparing the Shaelmaar is required if you want to prove the virtue of Compassion for the quest "There Can Be Only One."

  2. 2

    Scouting the Encampment and Trading Rows

    After visiting the Medical Pavilion in the east area where Guillaume de Launfal asks you to look into Vivienne de Tabris, take time to explore the artisan rows and participant camps. You can buy supplies from the Tourney Grounds Innkeeper, pick up diagrams, components, or repair weapons at the Journeyman Blacksmith, and browse ducal armor at the Armorer. Be sure to check the tables and round tents across the grounds to collect regional notes, files from Damien de la Tour's tent, and the Caed Myrkvid Saddle from the horse enclosure chest.

    Consejo: Challenge the Journeyman Blacksmith to Gwent to win a card for your Skellige deck.

  3. 3

    Inscription Tent and Choosing an Identity

    Head to the Registration / Inscription Tent to speak with the Tournament Scribe and Le Papillon to register for the tournament events. You must decide whether to enter under the Red Wolf crest as Geralt of Rivia or adopt the heraldry of a woman riding a bear as Sir Ravix of Fourhorn. Your choice immediately grants your starting equipment and locks in a specific rival confrontation later in the grounds.

    Cuidado: Picking Geralt of Rivia lets you fight Prince Anséis for the virtue of Honor, whereas selecting Sir Ravix of Fourhorn pits you against Tailles of Dorndal and locks you out of Sir Geralt of Rivia's Shield.

  4. 4

    Target Practice and Mounted Racing

    Move out to the perimeter Practice & Shooting Range to test your aim in the tourney archery contest against Horm. Afterwards, steer Roach through the perimeter track along the Horse Race Course to complete the mounted competition against the clock. Emerging triumphant in both trials ensures you collect unique tourney gear before returning to the main camp.

    Consejo: Keep Roach at a steady canter and hold attack during the race to trigger slow-motion strikes against practice dummies.

    Cuidado: Missing first place in the race, losing the shooting match, or initiating the quest "Capture the Castle" will permanently lock you out of the competition crossbow and saddle.

  5. 5

    Confronting Your Tourney Rival

    Outside the arenas between match stages, your registered identity catches up to you near the tents. If you signed up as Geralt of Rivia, Prince Anséis challenges your loyalty to Queen Meve, forcing you into a mounted horseback joust. If you registered as Sir Ravix of Fourhorn, Tailles of Dorndal picks a quarrel over your presence, forcing a three-on-one fistfight against him and two hired thugs.

    Combate clave: The Mounted Duel vs. Prince Anséis Combate clave: Fistfight vs. Tailles & Thugs

    Consejo: Hold your attack button on horseback to trigger slow motion before slicing Anséis, or counter Tailles' initial swing and dodge into space during the fistfight.

  6. 6

    The Team Melee and the Arena Champion

    Re-enter The Grand Arena to participate in the five-on-five team melee, backing up Palmerin de Launfal against a squad of heavily armored combatants. Advancing past the brawl pits you directly against the reigning colosseum champion, Gregoire de Gorgon. Claiming victory brings the tournament to a close with a hefty purse of crowns, a trophy, and a master-crafted blade.

    Combate clave: The 5v5 Arena Team Melee Combate clave: Champion of the Arena: Gregoire de Gorgon

    Consejo: Never try to parry Gregoire de Gorgon's massive two-handed broadsword; roll behind his vertical swings to punish his back, keeping Quen raised for safety.

    Cuidado: Starting the late-game main quest "Capture the Castle" before finishing these rounds permanently fails "The Warble of a Smitten Knight" and loses the victor's sword.

  7. 7

    Lifting Vivienne's Avian Curse

    Once the martial trials conclude, address the affliction tormenting Vivienne de Tabris using either the Oriole Egg Ritual or the Water Reflection Ritual. You can either transfer the curse onto an unhatched bird egg without Guillaume to give Vivienne seven free years of life to travel, or bring Guillaume into the secret with her blessing so he willingly shoulders the burden. Finishing all tourney trials perfectly allows you to return to your tent to admire love letters and your new entourage.

    Cuidado: Keeping Guillaume out of the ritual leaves him an inconsolable drunk, while involving him allows the couple to remain together happily in Beauclair with minor side effects.

Fate of the Shaelmaar

After defeating the monster in the arena during "The Beast of Toussaint"

  • Spare the Beast→ Geralt leaves the creature alive, displaying the chivalric virtue of Compassion needed for "There Can Be Only One"
  • Execute the Beast→ Geralt kills the beast to satisfy the spectators, gaining no chivalric virtue

La mayoría elige: Spare the Beast

Tournament Persona

Enrolling at the Inscription Tent in "The Warble of a Smitten Knight"

  • Geralt of Rivia→ Receive Geralt's Red Wolf shield, and Prince Anséis challenges you to a mounted duel whose victory can grant the virtue of Honor
  • Sir Ravix of Fourhorn→ Receive Ravix's bear-riding woman shield, leading to a fistfight against Tailles of Dorndal and two thugs

Must be done before starting the late-game main quest "Capture the Castle"

Lifting Vivienne's Curse

Confronting Vivienne's condition in "The Warble of a Smitten Knight"

  • Oriole Egg Ritual→ Vivienne is cured without Guillaume, but is left with approximately 7 years to live before departing Toussaint alone, while a heartbroken Guillaume becomes a heavy drinker
  • Transfer Curse to Guillaume→ Guillaume shares the burden through the water reflection ritual, allowing Vivienne a normal lifespan while Guillaume gains minor feathers, keeping the pair happily in love together in Beauclair

La mayoría elige: Transfer Curse to Guillaume

Locks out permanently if "Capture the Castle" is initiated beforehand

Combates clave
Arena Shaelmaar

Enemigos: Shaelmaar

Amenazas: Thick armored shell immune to regular strikes; high-speed rolling attacks and seismic shockwave slams.

Debilidades y resistencias: Relict Oil, Aard, Samum bombs.

Tácticas: Avoid attacking its armored front. Stand near the arena boundaries; when the Shaelmaar rolls toward Geralt, dodge aside so it collides with the stone wall. The impact flips it onto its back, exposing its soft underbelly to heavy strikes and Aard blasts.

The Mounted Duel vs. Prince Anséis

Enemigos: Prince Anséis

Amenazas: Jousting on horseback; an enemy lance charge can quickly dismount or stagger Geralt.

Tácticas: Keep Roach at a steady canter and hold the attack button to trigger Witcher horseback slow-motion; strike Anséis with your sword as you pass.

Fistfight vs. Tailles & Thugs

Enemigos: Tailles and two hired thugs

Amenazas: 1v3 bare-knuckle brawl; getting cornered leads to stun-locks.

Tácticas: Counter/parry Tailles' first swing, punish with a 3-hit combo, and dodge back into open space to keep all three brawlers in front of you.

The 5v5 Arena Team Melee

Enemigos: Multiple heavily-armored knights

Amenazas: Multiple heavily-armored knights attacking at once.

Tácticas: Let Palmerin and allied knights draw initial aggro; circle the flank using Igni to strip armor/cause burning, then parry and backstab enemy combatants.

Champion of the Arena: Gregoire de Gorgon

Enemigos: Gregoire de Gorgon

Amenazas: Colossal two-handed broadsword strikes with wide reach that break normal parries.

Tácticas: Do not attempt to parry his heavy swings. Dodge-roll behind him when he winds up a vertical overhead chop, deliver fast strikes to his back, and use Quen to safeguard against glancing blows.

Tiendas y personajes
  • Herrero: Fabricación de armas de nivel oficial, reparación de armas, desmantelamiento, diagramas de piedras rúnicas, materiales de fabricación y partidas de Gwynt para obtener una carta de la baraja de Skellige

Consejos
  • Completa todos los eventos y objetivos secundarios que desees en el Campo de torneos antes de avanzar a la misión principal avanzada "La toma del castillo", ya que al hacerlo fallarás permanentemente "El gorjeo de un caballero enamorado" y se bloqueará el torneo.
