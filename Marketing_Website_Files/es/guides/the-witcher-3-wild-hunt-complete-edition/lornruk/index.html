Lornruk

Guía de The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition · Investiga la fortaleza abandonada y su guiverno
← Todas las zonas de esta guía

Lornruk es una fortaleza costera abandonada con un puente levadizo alzado, situada en el extremo noroccidental de Velen. Para acceder al interior de la fortaleza, debes rodear el puente levadizo nadando a través de una caverna submarina sumergida bajo los acantilados. En el interior, bajas el puente con un mecanismo de rueda y encuentras diagramas de fabricación de la Escuela del Grifo mientras lidias con un guiverno que sobrevuela y anida en lo alto.

Región
Velen (No Man's Land)
Niveles
7–14
Cómo llegar
North-northwest of Heatherton and northwest of the Harpy Feeding Ground, at the far northwestern tip of the peninsula.
Conecta con
Harpy Feeding Ground, Heatherton
Misiones relacionadas
Scavenger Hunt: Griffin School Gear

Versión corta

Lo importante

  • •Drawing from the Quen Place of Power at the base of the eastern cliff to earn an Ability Point and a temporary Quen buff.
  • •Looting the chest on the exterior elevated platform behind the tower to secure the Diagram: Griffin silver sword.
  • •Lowering the drawbridge via the courtyard wheel winch to unlock permanent foot access from the mainland.

Errores comunes

  • •Engaging the wyvern atop the cramped ramparts; shoot it from the mainland with a crossbow beforehand so you do not roll off the cliff edge to your death.
  • •Attempting to swing your sword at Drowners underwater; use your crossbow instead to kill them in one or two hits.

Lo que puedes saltarte

  • •Bypassing the underwater cavern using the terrain jump trick along the right rampart gap.

Guía paso a paso

La zona en el orden en que juegas. Marca lo que completes: también se marcará en las listas de abajo.

  1. 1

    Approaching Lornruk and Sniping the Wyvern

    When you arrive at the Lornruk signpost via the mainland promontory, look across the raised wooden drawbridge to spot a Level 14 wyvern circling the ruined fortress. Draw your crossbow and fire at the flying beast from across the chasm to bait it over to the wide mainland terrain. Bring it down using Aard or crossbow bolts, coat your blade with Draconid Oil, and use Grapeshot bombs or Golden Oriole to negate its poison attacks.

    Combate clave: The Wyvern

    Consejo: You can pull the wyvern onto the mainland across the bridge with your crossbow to battle it in safety instead of risking a lethal fall on the tight ramparts above.

    Cuidado: Do not fight the wyvern on the fortress ramparts if you can avoid it, as dodging in cramped spaces carries a severe risk of tumbling off the cliff to your death.

  2. 2

    Drawing from the Place of Power

    Head down along the waterline at the base of the cliffs on the right (eastern) side of the bridge and fortress. Approach the glowing Quen Place of Power resting by the water to draw from it. This interaction grants you a permanent Ability Point alongside a temporary boost to your Quen intensity.

  3. 3

    Swimming the Submerged Tunnel

    Dive into the water just past the Place of Power near a rocky outcrop directly beneath the fortress to uncover an underwater cavern entrance. Swim through the submerged tunnel while keeping an eye out for several Drowners lurking in the depths. Eliminate the swimming monsters quickly with your crossbow, then loot the underwater smuggler caches along the seabed for leveled gear, alchemical items, and crafting supplies before surfacing.

    Combate clave: Drowners

    Consejo: Your crossbow can dispatch underwater Drowners in merely one to two shots while swimming.

  4. 4

    Ascending the Interior Cave

    Pull yourself out of the water onto the lower cave ledges inside the cavern underneath the fortress. Search the cavern floor for additional cache chests filled with crafting materials and gear. Afterwards, climb the series of wooden ladders and scale the ruined masonry to emerge into the fortress courtyard above.

  5. 5

    Lowering the Drawbridge

    Once you reach the fortress courtyard, turn immediately toward the main gate to find an interactive wheel winch mechanism. Interact with the wheel to drop the raised wooden drawbridge, opening a permanent walking path back to the mainland stone bridge.

  6. 6

    Claiming the Griffin Silver Sword Diagram

    Cross the courtyard toward a doorway and climb the wall ladder attached to the side of the building. Walk onto the exterior elevated wooden and stone platform behind the tower structure where the beast kept its nest. Loot the chest resting on this platform to retrieve the Diagram: Griffin silver sword.

    Consejo: Players looking to bypass the underwater swim can use a terrain parkour jump along a gap in the right rampart wall to enter the fortress directly.

Combates clave
The Wyvern

Enemigos: Wyvern (Level 14 Draconid)

Amenazas: High aerial swoop damage, tail swipes, forward bites, poison infliction, and fighting in cramped ramparts where dodging risks a fatal fall off the cliffs.

Debilidades y resistencias: Draconid Oil, Aard, Grapeshot bombs, Golden Oriole potion

Tácticas: Shoot with a crossbow from the mainland across the bridge before swimming underneath to lure it into a wide open space; use Crossbow or Aard Sign while airborne to knock it to the ground.

Recompensas: Wyvern Mutagen, Wyvern Egg, Wyvern Hide, monster claws, raw meat, and crafting materials

Drowners

Enemigos: Several Drowners

Debilidades y resistencias: Necrophage Oil, Igni, Crossbow

Tácticas: Dispatch them in 1–2 hits underwater using the Crossbow; use Necrophage Oil and Igni on land.

Enemigos
Guiverno
Débil contra Oropéndola, Metralla, Aceite para dracónidos, AardRobo / botín Huevo de guiverno, Mutágeno de guiverno, Piel de guiverno

Patrulla el cielo alrededor de la torre en ruinas y se le puede hacer caer a tierra usando ataques a distancia o señales.

Consejos
  • En lugar de enfrentarte al guiverno en las estrechas pasarelas superiores, dispárale con tu ballesta desde tierra firme al otro lado del puente para atraerlo a un espacio de combate más amplio.
