Castillo de Mont Crane

Guía de The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition · Despejar la fortaleza de bandidos durante la misión principal ✓ Verificado con fuentes
← Todas las zonas de esta guía

El Castillo de Mont Crane es una base de la hansa situada en el noreste de Toussaint que ha sido tomada por una gran banda de bandidos. Geralt puede despejar la fortaleza abriéndose paso metódicamente a través de oleadas de bandidos fuertemente armados y derrotando a su líder en las almenas superiores. Liberar la fortaleza elimina la presencia de la banda hostil y permite que los camerlengos ducales y los trabajadores locales reclamen el lugar.

Región
Duchy of Toussaint
Niveles
40–48
Cómo llegar
Northeast of the Dulcinea Windmill and northwest of Fort Ussar Ruins via the road leading up into the hills to the Mont Crane Castle signpost outside the gate.
Conecta con
Fort Ussar Ruins, Dulcinea Windmill, Cave systems underneath the castle
Misiones relacionadas
Knight for Hire · Scavenger Hunt: Grandmaster Griffin Gear
Servicios y personajes
Armorer (Master/Journeyman)

Versión corta

Lo importante

  • •Collecting Grandmaster Griffin gear diagrams from Jerome's prison vault beyond the portal.
  • •Slaying Filibert Fourfingers to liberate the fortress, which completes an objective for Knight for Hire and installs an Armorer merchant in the courtyard.
  • •Searching the topmost terrace chest and Moreau's hidden study for rare armor dye formulas and mutagen transmutator recipes.

Errores comunes

  • •Knocking Filibert Fourfingers off the ramparts with Aard, which permanently drops his corpse and loot into an inaccessible void.
  • •Letting the courtyard torchrunner ignite the signal brazier without adequate crowd-control bombs or oils ready to survive the combined garrison.

Lo que puedes saltarte

  • •Slaying Filibert immediately if you prefer to leave him alive to repeatedly farm the courtyard bandits for crowns, weapons, and experience.

Guía paso a paso

La zona en el orden en que juegas. Marca lo que completes: también se marcará en las listas de abajo.

  1. 1

    Outer Approach & Signpost

    Follow the hill path northeast of the Dulcinea Windmill and northwest of the Fort Ussar Ruins up to the castle walls. Discover the Mont Crane Castle signpost situated just outside the entrance before preparing your gear for a massive stronghold assault.

    Consejo: Check your equipment and oils before heading through the gate, as you will immediately face a dense concentration of level 40–48 foes.

  2. 2

    Main Courtyard Cleansing

    Pass through the front gates into the open courtyard where you are immediately set upon by Hanse bandits, archers, polearm wielders, shield-bearers, and war dogs. Detonate the red barrels using Igni, use Northern Wind bombs or Whirl, and funnel the horde into the entrance arch to avoid being overwhelmed. Decide whether to intercept the bandit sprinting toward the signal pyre with a bomb or crossbow to keep the fight small, or let the brazier burn to draw upper-level garrisons down into one massive brawl.

    Combate clave: Bandit Swarm & War Dogs

    Consejo: Funneling bandits down the entry corridor or stairwells prevents their archers and polearms from surrounding you.

    Cuidado: Letting the torchbearer light the pyre pulls dozens of reinforcements at once, which can rapidly overwhelm you if you are unprepared.

  3. 3

    Uncovering Moreau's Study

    Head toward the blind archway and dead-end stone wall at the far end of the courtyard. Use the Eye of Nehaleni to dispel the illusion masking the passage and descend the revealed staircase into Professor Moreau's subterranean laboratory. Gather the power crystal from the table along with Moreau's journal notes, formulas for pink armor dye, lesser mutagen transmutators, and the greater mutagen transmutator.

    Consejo: Be sure to collect the power crystal sitting directly on the table so you can power the apparatus in the room.

  4. 4

    The Portal and Jerome's Vault

    Insert the power crystal into the socket and hit it with the Aard sign to fire up the portal. Step through the gate into Jerome's prison vault and prepare for combat against a golem and cavern vampires within the deep subterranean tunnels. Search the container inside the prison vault to acquire the diagrams for the Grandmaster Griffin armor, trousers, and silver sword, then dispel the next illusionary wall to reveal the cave path leading back out the hillside.

    Combate clave: Golem Combate clave: Cavern Vampires

    Consejo: Use Elementa Oil and Dimeritium Bombs against the golem, and swap to Vampire Oil, Black Blood, and Moon Dust to counter the Fleder and Garkain.

  5. 5

    The Ramparts and Filibert Fourfingers

    Return up through the castle and scale the stone and wooden staircases across the multi-tiered floors to reach the topmost open roof where Filibert Fourfingers waits with his elite bodyguards. Check the second floor for a gap in the castle wall leading to isolated chests on the northwestern upper balcony before engaging the boss. Subdue Filibert on the terrace using Axii and Aard knockdowns, parry his two-handed weapon swings, and loot the small chest on the roof for a worn leather-bound journal, Toussaint knight's gear diagrams, white armor dye formula, and a mutagen transmutator formula.

    Combate clave: Filibert Fourfingers

    Consejo: Keep Filibert centered on the platform and rely on Axii stuns and parries to handle his heavy attacks safely.

    Cuidado: Do not blast Filibert over the edge of the roof with Aard, or his corpse and unique inventory drops will fall into an unreachable void.

  6. 6

    Stronghold Liberation

    Loot Filibert Fourfingers' body on the terrace to claim his unique items and trigger the liberation cutscene where Ducal Guards reclaim Mont Crane Castle. Meet the newly arrived Master/Journeyman Armorer who sets up shop in the courtyard, and turn in your progress toward the Knight for Hire quest for your reward.

    Consejo: If you want to endlessly farm crowns, equipment, and experience, fast travel away before killing Filibert so the courtyard bandits respawn continuously.

    Cuidado: Slaying Filibert triggers the Ducal Guard cutscene and permanently stops the Hanse bandits from respawning.

Decisiones aquí

Lo que decides aquí y a qué te lleva. Los resultados están ocultos: toca uno para revelarlo.

Dealing with the signal pyre

Upon initiating battle in the main courtyard

  • Intercept the runner→ Prevents the pyre from being lit, keeping the battle confined to the courtyard bandits
  • Allow the runner to ignite the pyre→ Calls down reinforcements from the upper parapets and nearby outposts to flood the courtyard at once

La mayoría elige: Intercept the runner

Must be stopped quickly with a sprint, bomb, or crossbow before he reaches the brazier

Slaying Filibert Fourfingers vs. keeping him alive

After fighting through the castle up to the keep roof

  • Slay Filibert→ Clears the Hanse base, triggers Ducal Guards to reclaim the castle, adds a friendly Armorer merchant, progresses Knight for Hire, awards 100 Crowns via Camerlengo, and permanently halts bandit respawns
  • Spare Filibert→ Leaves the leader alive and the base uncleared, allowing courtyard bandits to endlessly respawn upon fast traveling away and back for farming gear, gold, and experience

La mayoría elige: Slay Filibert

Slaying Filibert permanently prevents the courtyard bandits from respawning

Combates clave
Bandit Swarm & War Dogs

Enemigos: Up to 30–40 Hanse bandits, archers, polearm wielders, heavy shield-bearers, and war dogs

Amenazas: Sheer numbers, archers firing from upper wooden scaffolding, polearms, and heavy shield-bearers

Debilidades y resistencias: Exploding red barrels in the courtyard

Tácticas: Funnel enemies into the entrance archway or down the stairs to Moreau's vault to avoid being surrounded; use Northern Wind or Dancing Star bombs, Aard Sweep, Whirl, and detonate red barrels with Igni

Recompensas: Valuable swords and armor looted from bandits

Filibert Fourfingers

Enemigos: Filibert Fourfingers and elite bodyguards

Amenazas: Heavy-hitting two-handed weapon attacks on an elevated platform

Debilidades y resistencias: Axii, Aard, and standard parry/counter windows

Tácticas: Use Axii for crowd-control, Aard for knockdown/stagger, and standard parry/counters against attacks; avoid knocking him off the edge with Aard

Recompensas: Boss inventory drop, completion towards Knight for Hire, 100 Crowns via Camerlengo, and liberation of the castle

Golem

Enemigos: Golem

Amenazas: Ground slams, charge attacks, high physical damage resistance

Debilidades y resistencias: Elementa Oil, Quen shield, and Dimeritium Bombs

Tácticas: Stay behind it, evade wide overhead smashes, and use Quen and Dimeritium Bombs

Cavern Vampires

Enemigos: Fleder & Garkain

Amenazas: Leaping claw strikes, screech stuns, and high-speed lunges

Debilidades y resistencias: Vampire Oil, Black Blood, Moon Dust bombs, and Igni

Tácticas: Keep distance using dodges rather than blocks; utilize Vampire Oil, Black Blood, Moon Dust, and Igni
