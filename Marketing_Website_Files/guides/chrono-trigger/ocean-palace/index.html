Ocean Palace

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The final confrontation with Lavos

The party infiltrates this underwater sanctuary in 12,000 BC to halt Queen Zeal from using the Mammon Machine to siphon power from Lavos. Players must activate switches across multiple side chambers, descend a central elevator, and battle through monster gauntlets to reach the core. Reaching the inner sanctum triggers a climactic encounter against Lavos where Crono perishes to save his friends, causing the Ocean Palace to collapse.

Items

  • Rune Blade MISSABLE: Located beside a pillar in the large main hall
  • Aeonian Suit MISSABLE: Found near the southern portion of the large main hall
  • Aeonian Helm MISSABLE: In the chamber situated to the right of the main switch room
  • Sonic Bow MISSABLE: Inside the southwest room adjacent to the main hall
  • Kaiser Arm MISSABLE: In the southeastern room connected to the lower hall
  • Shockwave MISSABLE: Located in the room exiting out to the far lower-left
  • Empyrean Blade MISSABLE: Inside the western chamber off the main hall
  • Demonslayer MISSABLE: Concealed in a closed enclosure in the southernmost room
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Contained in an elevator-level treasure chest
  • Magic Capsule MISSABLE: Found glittering on the platform after taking the elevator down and then riding it back up

Secrets

  • You can reach the secluded Demonslayer chest by following the outer left perimeter of the southernmost room and slipping through a false wall hidden in the architecture.
  • Riding the central elevator to the bottom and immediately returning it to the top reveals a previously hidden Magic Capsule on the lift's left side.
