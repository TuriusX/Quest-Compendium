Ocean Palace

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The final confrontation with Lavos

The Ocean Palace is Queen Zeal's underwater fortress constructed to directly harvest energy from the dormant Lavos. The party descends through security systems, elevators, and hordes of monsters to reach the Mammon Machine. Confronting the beast initiates a scripted battle where Lavos awakes, culminating in the death of Crono and the cataclysmic destruction of the Kingdom of Zeal.

Items

  • Rune Blade MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest on the left side of the grand entrance room
  • Aeonian Helm MISSABLE: Inside a chest found on the right side of the main palace hub
  • Aeonian Suit MISSABLE: In a treasure chest located along the western staircase corridors
  • Shockwave MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the side rooms of the central hall
  • Magic Tabs MISSABLE: Found in chests along the elevator shafts and offshoot pathways

Secrets

  • Secret switch on a statue | Pressing a concealed switch on a gargoyle statue in the eastern corridors lowers a barrier that blocks the central elevator.
  • Left and right wing switches | Both switches hidden in the far eastern and western chambers must be triggered before the central room's elevator platform becomes operational.

Tips

  • Equip armor that mitigates fire, water, or shadow damage before descending the palace elevators to survive multi-element monster swarms.
  • Bring Ayla into the Ocean Palace so you can use her Charm tech on Mages to easily stock up on permanent Magic Tabs.
  • Heal immediately after Queen Zeal uses Hallation, as any subsequent hit will wipe out a character left at 1 HP.
