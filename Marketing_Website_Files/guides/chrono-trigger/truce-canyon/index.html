Truce Canyon

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Traveling to 600 A.D.

Crono falls through the time distortion to arrive alone in a rocky gorge in 600 A.D., where he must fend off wandering monsters. Players navigate the paths and ladders down the cliffs to exit onto the Middle Ages world map and continue toward Guardia Castle. Later, party members return to this canyon to use Lucca's Gate Key and reopen the distortion leading back to 1000 A.D.

Items

  • Tonic: inside a treasure chest on the upper cliffside path after crossing the bridge
  • PowerGlove: inside a treasure chest on the lower ledges before descending to the exit

Tips

  • You can entirely bypass the bottom monster pack near the exit by keeping close to the lower edge of the path to save time and health.
