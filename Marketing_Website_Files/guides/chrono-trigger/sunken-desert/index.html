In 600 A.D., a whirlpool vortex appears south of the Denadoro Mountains near Fiona's Villa if a woman in Antiquity was encouraged to plant a sapling. The party must descend into this shifting cavern to defeat the sand monster Retinite, who is turning the land into a wasteland and preventing the forest from growing. Defeating the boss enables Robo to stay behind for several centuries to cultivate the earth into a lush forest.

Retinite
Weak to Water/Ice (lowers physical defense of upper and lower parts)Steal / drop Speed Tab can be charmed from the core

Boss composed of an Upper Body, Lower Body, and Core that drain energy from one another

Shops and people
  • Nun at Fiona's Shrine: Sells Vigil Hat, Sight Cap, and Time Hat in 1000 A.D. once the forest is revived

Tips
  • Cast Water or Ice magic on Retinite to dramatically slash its high physical defense before hitting it with your strongest physical attacks.
  • Collect every treasure chest before leaving the area with Robo, as the Sunken Desert collapses and permanently disappears once the quest is completed.
