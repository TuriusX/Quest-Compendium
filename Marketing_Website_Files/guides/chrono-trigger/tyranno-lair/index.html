Tyranno Lair

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The final battle with Azala

The party flies Dactyls into this prehistoric fortress in 65,000,000 BC to rescue Kino and captured Laruba villagers. Inside, players navigate switches, traps, and teleporter puzzles before defeating Nizbel II and confronting Azala alongside the Black Tyranno. Once Azala is defeated, the Lavos impact completely destroys the stronghold, permanently locking the area.

Items

  • Mid Ether MISSABLE: Inside Kino's jail cell in the dungeon basement
  • Full Tonic MISSABLE: Left egg-shaped chest inside the trap switch room
  • CeraTopper MISSABLE: Southwest corner chest reached via invisible teleporters
  • Meso Mail MISSABLE: Northeast corner chest reached via invisible teleporters
  • CeraTopper MISSABLE: Chest on the right side of the throne room
  • Mid Ether MISSABLE: Chest on the left side of the throne room

Secrets

  • Stepping on the invisible floor tiles in the warp room transports the party across barriers to access hidden chests containing Meso Mail and a CeraTopper.
  • Opening the right egg-shaped container in the switch chamber drops the party straight into the lower prison cells below.

Tips

  • Keep Ayla in your group to steal the Magic Tab from Azala and the Power Tab from Black Tyranno using Charm.
  • Focus all damage on Azala first during the final fight, then stockpile full-party techs until Black Tyranno begins its countdown and drops its guard.
