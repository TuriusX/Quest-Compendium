Tyranno Lair

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The final battle with Azala

Tyranno Lair is the stronghold of the Reptites in 65,000,000 BC, accessed after recovering the stolen Gate Key. The party infiltrates the fortress to free captured villagers, conquer prehistoric beasts, and reach the summit for the final confrontation against Azala and the Black Tyranno. Defeating them triggers the impact of Lavos, which completely obliterates the lair and alters the course of history.

Items

  • Mid Ether MISSABLE: Inside the jail cell where Kino is held captive
  • CeraTopper MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest on the route leading up the fortress

Secrets

  • You can free Kino by talking to him through the jail cell door, prompting him to break it open and uncover a hidden path to help you proceed.

Tips

  • Ayla is mandatory in your party for this dungeon, so make sure to equip her with the best prehistoric gear available.
  • Equip Ruby Vests or other fire-resistant armor on your party before the summit battle to survive the Black Tyranno's countdown blast.
  • Save all your most powerful single-target tech attacks for the Black Tyranno during the phases when it removes its defensive barrier to count down.
