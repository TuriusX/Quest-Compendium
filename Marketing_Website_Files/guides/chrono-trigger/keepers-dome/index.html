Keeper's Dome

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Learning about the Epoch and Lavos

In the post-apocalyptic era of 2300 A.D., you unlock a sealed door using the energized Pendant to explore Belthasar's hidden research sanctuary. Reading shining record points details Lavos's parasitic lifecycle across time, its nest at Death Peak, and the creator's descent into madness. Past these notes, you claim Belthasar's crowning invention, the time machine known as the Wings of Time or the Epoch.

Items

  • Magic Tab: On the floor in the hallway of glowing messages, appearing after boarding the Epoch
  • Epoch: In the innermost hangar chamber past the sealed doorway

Secrets

  • Re-entering the message hallway immediately after triggering the Epoch cutscene spawns a sparkling Magic Tab on the walkway.

Tips

  • Interact with the console adjacent to the Epoch's dock before departing if you want to rename the vessel.
