Guardia Castle (600 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The trial of the Queen

After arriving in the Middle Ages, Crono is granted entry by a woman thought to be Queen Leene, who is actually Marle mistaken for royalty. When Marle vanishes from the royal chambers due to a time paradox, Crono teams up with Lucca and later rescues the true Queen from Manolia Cathedral. Returning to the castle reveals that the real Queen Leene is safe, allowing Crono and Lucca to reunite with Marle and return to their own era.

Items

  • 100 G: Inside a chest in the King's bedroom in the western tower
  • BronzeMail: Inside a chest in the King's bedroom in the western tower
  • Tonic: Inside a chest located along the western tower stairs
  • Ether: Inside a chest in the eastern tower chambers
  • Ether: Inside an additional chest on the eastern tower walkway

Tips

  • You can rest at no cost by speaking with the maid in the Knights' Quarters downstairs to restore HP and MP.
