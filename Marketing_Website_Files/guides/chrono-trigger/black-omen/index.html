Black Omen

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Infiltrating the flying fortress

The party flies the Epoch into the floating sanctuary to confront Queen Zeal and bring an end to her reign. Players must fight through linear corridors filled with powerful monsters and bosses before facing the Mammon Machine and Zeal herself. Completing this fortress leads directly into the confrontation with Lavos.

Items

  • White Rock MISSABLE: In a chest within the save point room inside the fortress
  • Haste Helm MISSABLE: In an upper treasure chest near the two Nu
  • MegaElixir MISSABLE: In a treasure chest situated on the right side of the large white energy chamber

Secrets

  • You can complete the Black Omen three times in a single playthrough by entering it chronologically in reverse (first 1000 A.D., then 600 A.D., and finally 12,000 B.C.) to fight Queen Zeal repeatedly.

Tips

  • Keep Ayla in your party throughout the fortress because nearly every enemy and boss yields exceptional gear or stat tabs through her Charm tech.
