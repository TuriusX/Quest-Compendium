Black Omen

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Infiltrating the flying fortress

The Black Omen is Queen Azala's transformed floating palace, serving as a massive multi-screen end-game dungeon accessible in multiple eras. The party must fight through hordes of high-level Zeal enemies, mutants, and defense systems to reach Queen Zeal and Lavos. Defeating the bosses at the heart of the fortress leads directly into the confrontation with Lavos.

Items

  • MegaElixir: In a chest guarded by enemies along the main exterior walkways
  • Magic Tabs: Found inside various treasure chests scattered throughout the corridors
  • Speed Tabs: Found inside various treasure chests scattered throughout the corridors
  • Power Tabs: Found inside various treasure chests scattered throughout the corridors
  • White Rock: In a chest inside the inner chambers

Secrets

  • You can clear the Black Omen up to three times in a single playthrough by completing it first in 12,000 BC, then in 600 AD, and finally in 1000 AD.
  • If you destroy the Black Omen in a later era, it remains intact in earlier eras, but clearing it in an earlier era erases it from future eras.

Tips

  • Bring Ayla in your active party throughout the entire fortress to Charm rare items and top-tier equipment from bosses and standard enemies.
  • Equip armor that protects against status effects, such as the Helmets or Vests obtained earlier, to counter the frequent debuffs used by Mutants and Queen Zeal.
  • Charge up your best Triple Techs or dual tech combinations before entering the final sequence of battles, as you face three major boss encounters in direct succession.
