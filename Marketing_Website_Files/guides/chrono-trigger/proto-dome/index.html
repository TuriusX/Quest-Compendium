Proto Dome

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Finding Robo

Crono and his companions arrive at Proto Dome after traveling across the ruined future of 2300 A.D. Inside, they find a deactivated robot whom Lucca repairs, who then takes the name Robo and joins the party. The team must head through the dome's northern exit into the Derelict Factory to restore power to the sealed door blocking their path.

Items

  • Tonic: chest located inside the dome

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Bugmechanical pests encountered in the entryway before reaching the main chamber

Shops and people

  • Enertron: restores the party's HP and MP to full, though leaves them feeling hungry

Tips

  • Rest in the Enertron to completely heal your party before heading into the Factory Ruins to restore power.
  • Since Robo is mandatory for opening the door in the Factory Ruins, you must choose whether Marle or Lucca accompanies Crono and Robo.
