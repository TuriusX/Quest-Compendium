Death Peak

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Climbing to revive Chrono

The party scales the howling, blizzard-swept summit in the future using guidance from Belthasar's Poyozo dolls to overcome intense winds, icy precipices, and monster nests. At the mountain's peak, they activate the Chrono Trigger alongside the Crono clone to freeze time at the Ocean Palace and rescue Crono from the moment of his demise.

Items

  • Power Tab: Shining on the ground to the right of the first tree on the windy opening slope
  • Magic Ring: In a treasure chest located along the western cliff edge of the lower area
  • Wall Ring: In a treasure chest found on the snowy field
  • Giga Arm: In a treasure chest located inside the mountain cave network
  • BraveSword: In a treasure chest found deep within the cavern passages
  • StarScythe: In a treasure chest inside the cave area
  • VedicBlade: In a treasure chest perched on an icy ledge accessed by dropping down from above
  • Dark Helm: In a treasure chest reached by climbing down cliffs past the windy ridge
  • Memory Cap: In a treasure chest situated on the lower cliff section near the descent

Secrets

  • Hidden Save Point and cavern passage revealed by triggering an unobtrusive wall switch inside the cavern | missable: no

Tips

  • When scaling the first screen, run upward only when the wind calms down and brace directly behind the trees facing north so the gale does not blow the party off the peak.
  • In the third Lavos Spawn encounter, push the leftover empty shell beneath the broken ladder to climb atop it and reach the final ascent.
