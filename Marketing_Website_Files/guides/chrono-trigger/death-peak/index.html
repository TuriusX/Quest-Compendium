Death Peak

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Climbing to revive Chrono

The party climbs Death Peak in 2300 AD using the Time Egg and a clone to revive their fallen leader, Crono. You must navigate harsh winds, scale cliffs, and fight through several Lavos Spawns to reach the summit. At the top, the Time Egg activates during an eclipse, freezing time at the moment of Crono's death so you can swap him with the clone.

Items

  • Magic Ring: Chest on the first screen after the wind section
  • Dark Helm: Chest on the snowy exterior ledges
  • Memory Cap: Chest tucked on a snowy path before the cave summits
  • Star Scythe: Chest inside the upper cave paths
  • Yakra Blade: Chest located on the higher snowy paths

Tips

  • In the first windy screen, hide behind the dead trees when the blizzard blows hard to keep from being blown off the mountain.
  • For the slippery ice bridge, push the directional pad gently and avoid running to keep from sliding off the edge.
  • When fighting the third Lavos Spawn, examine its empty shell after defeat to use it as a stepladder to reach the high climbing ledge.
  • Do not use multi-target or full-screen tech attacks against the Lavos Spawns, as hitting the shell triggers a devastating group counterattack.
