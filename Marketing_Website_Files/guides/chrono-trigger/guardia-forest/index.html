Guardia Forest

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Traveling to 600 A.D.

Guardia Forest serves as the wilderness path that connects the village of Truce to Guardia Castle in the Middle Ages. When Crono first arrives in 600 A.D. through the time portal, he must navigate through this forest while battling roaming creatures to investigate rumors surrounding Queen Leene at the castle. Later in the quest, you revisit the woods when following up on events involving the royal family and travels between eras.

Items

  • Power Tab: On the ground in the southeastern clearing of the forest
  • Shelter: Dropped by an enemy after interacting with a rustling bush in the upper-central area
  • Speed Tab: Inside a sealed chest located in the upper-right section near a dead-end sign (requires the charged pendant)

Secrets

  • A monster hides inside a rustling bush near the middle of the woods, and examining it causes the creature to flee and drop a Shelter each time you re-enter the area.

Tips

  • In the southeastern clearing, only examine the upper sparkling glint for the Power Tab; touching the lower shimmer will trigger an ambush instead of yielding an item.
