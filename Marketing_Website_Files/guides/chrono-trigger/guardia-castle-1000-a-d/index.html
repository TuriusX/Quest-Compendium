Guardia Castle (1000 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The trial of the Queen

Upon arriving at Guardia Castle in 1000 A.D. after securing the Rainbow Shell in the past, King Guardia is put on trial by the Chancellor for allegedly selling off royal heirlooms. The party must fight down into the castle basement to locate the Rainbow Shell, grab a prism shard as physical evidence, and break into the courtroom to expose the Chancellor's plot. Once exposed, the Chancellor transforms into Yakra XIII, forcing a boss battle to rescue the King and clear his name.

Items

  • HyperEther: Castle basement, inside an ordinary chest along the path
  • Elixir: Castle basement, inside an ordinary chest along the path
  • Lapis: Castle basement, inside an ordinary chest along the path
  • HyperEther: Castle basement, in an ordinary chest immediately before the Rainbow Shell
  • Elixir: Castle basement, in an ordinary chest immediately before the Rainbow Shell
  • Lapis: Castle basement, in an ordinary chest immediately before the Rainbow Shell
  • Prism Shard: Guardia Castle basement, broken off directly from the Rainbow Shell
  • Yakra Key: Courtroom floor, as a shining sparkle left behind after defeating Yakra XIII
  • Prism Dress MISSABLE: Guardia Castle basement, crafted by Melchior from the Rainbow Shell if chosen
  • Prism Helm MISSABLE: Guardia Castle basement, set of three crafted by Melchior from the Rainbow Shell if chosen

Secrets

  • Locked chest containing the real Chancellor, opened by finding the sparkling Yakra Key on the courtroom floor after defeating the boss and using it on the locked chest in the castle stairwell.

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes

Shops and people

Tips

  • Keep party member hit points well above 300 to 400 when finishing off Yakra XIII, as his dying counterattack hits the entire active party for severe damage.
