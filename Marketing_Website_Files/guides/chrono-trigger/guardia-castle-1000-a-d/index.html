Guardia Castle (1000 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The trial of the Queen

Crono and Marle return to the castle after the events in the Middle Ages, only for the Chancellor to arrest Crono for kidnapping the princess. After being framed and sentenced in a rigged courtroom trial, Crono is locked in the castle dungeon awaiting execution. You must break out of prison—either by escaping yourself or waiting for Lucca to stage a rescue—and flee through the castle grounds to safety.

Items

  • Ether: Chest inside the prison cells area
  • Mid Tonic: Chest found along the prison catwalks and stairwells
  • Shelter: Chest inside the prison guard quarters
  • Silver Sword: Chest inside the castle armory/storeroom during the escape
  • Lapis: In the cell with the guillotine if you rescue the prisoner Fritz

Secrets

  • Approach the guillotine in the prison block before escaping and activate the switch to free Fritz, a shopkeeper's son; visiting his shop in Truce later rewards you with free Mid Ethers.
  • Knocking out the prison guards from behind allows you to search their bodies for Mid Tonics without entering combat.

Tips

  • Destroy the Dragon Tank's Head first because it constantly repairs the rest of the machine and counters attacks with lightning.
  • Taking the non-violent dialogue choices during the court trial does not prevent Crono's imprisonment, but a "Not Guilty" verdict places a pink bag with Ethers in your prison cell.
