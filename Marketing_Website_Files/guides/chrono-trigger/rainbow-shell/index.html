Rainbow Shell

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Rainbow Shell

This quest begins by retrieving the giant Rainbow Shell from the Giant's Claw cavern in the Middle Ages and leaving it in the care of the Royal Guard at Guardia Castle. In the Present (1000 AD), you must enter the castle to clear King Guardia XXXIII's name in a trial where the Chancellor falsely accuses him of selling off the heirloom. After exposing the Chancellor as the monster Yakra XIII and defeating him, Melchior uses shards of the shell to craft supreme equipment for your party.

Items

  • Rainbow Shell: Deepest chamber of the Giant's Claw
  • Prismatic Shard: Picked up off the Rainbow Shell in the Guardia Castle basement during 1000 AD
  • Yakra Key: Dropped by Yakra XIII upon his defeat in the courtroom
  • Prism Dress MISSABLE: Reward from Melchior if choosing the defensive option (receives three)
  • Prism Helm MISSABLE: Reward from Melchior if choosing the helmet option (receives one)
  • Prism Specs: Reward crafted by Melchior after giving him the Sun Stone along with the Rainbow Shell
  • Rainbow: Crono's ultimate katana, crafted by Melchior after bringing him the Sun Stone

Secrets

  • The locked chest in the royal quarters that previously could not be opened can be unlocked using the Yakra Key to rescue the real Chancellor.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Melchior: Crafts powerful end-game equipment in the treasury room using pieces of the Rainbow Shell.

Tips

  • Marle must be in your active party to gain entry into the courtroom to present the Prismatic Shard and halt the trial.
  • When Melchior asks you to choose between the Prism Dress or Prism Helm, picking the Prism Dress grants three copies, making it generally the more advantageous choice.
