Rainbow Shell

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Rainbow Shell

After obtaining Toma's Pop in 600 A.D. and pouring it on Toma's grave in 1000 A.D., the party locates the Giant's Claw cavern northwest of Choras. Deep within these ruins, the party defeats Rust Tyrano, stores the heavy Rainbow Shell in Guardia Castle in 600 A.D., and later travels to 1000 A.D. to expose Yakra XIII during King Guardia's trial. Once the shell and the king are saved, Melchior uses shards of the shell (and the Sun Stone) to craft some of the strongest endgame gear.

Items

  • Toma's Pop: Given by Toma at the Choras Cafe in 600 A.D.
  • Blue Rock: Inside a partially hidden chest along the upper pathway inside Giant's Claw
  • Frenzy Band: Found inside an auxiliary chamber off the switch-and-trap room in Giant's Claw
  • Prism Shard: Retrieved from the Rainbow Shell in the Guardia Castle basement storage room in 1000 A.D.
  • Yakra's Key: Found on the courtroom floor as a sparkling object after defeating Yakra XIII

Secrets

  • Speed Tab | Hidden behind Toma's gravestone at West Cape in 1000 A.D.
  • Freeing the Real Chancellor | Use Yakra's Key on the locked treasure chest located down the staircase from the Guardia Castle courtroom

Shops and people

  • Melchior: Offers a choice between 1 Prism Dress or 3 Prism Helms using the Rainbow Shell; also crafts the Rainbow katana and Prism Specs if you bring the Sun Stone

Tips

  • Equip fire-absorbing or fire-reducing armor such as Red Mail, Red Vest, or Ruby Armor before challenging Rust Tyrano to negate its countdown flame attack.
  • Ensure Marle is in the active party when approaching Guardia Castle in 1000 A.D. so she can enter the courtroom and advance the trial storyline.
