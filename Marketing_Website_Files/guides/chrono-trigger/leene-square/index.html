Leene Square

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The opening fair

Leene Square hosts the Millennial Fair in Guardia in the year 1000 A.D., where Crono arrives to see Lucca's new invention. While exploring the attractions, Crono accidentally bumps into Marle, and they spend time at the fair together before heading to the telepod show. An unexpected malfunction during the demonstration pulls Marle into a time gate, launching the adventure.

Items

  • 1 Silver Point: Awarded by the dancing robot Gato upon defeating him in battle
  • 5 Silver Points: Awarded for finishing first in the footrace betting tent
  • Poyozo Doll: Won at Norstein Bekkler's tent by scoring 10 Silver Points in the mimic game
  • Crono Clone: Won at Norstein Bekkler's tent by winning the 40 Silver Point mimic challenge
  • Lode Sword: Sold by Melchior at his weapon stall on the fairgrounds

Secrets

  • Waiting patiently by Marle's side while she buys candy will prevent a juror from voting guilty during the later trial.
  • Returning the lost cat near the entrance to the little girl at the fair will count as evidence of good character at your trial.
  • Eating the old man's lunch off the table at the fair will cause him to testify against you in the Guardia courtroom.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
GatoA friendly singing training robot who gives 15 experience points and Silver Points when defeated.

Shops and people

  • Melchior: Sells early weapons and armor including the Iron Blade and Bronze Helm
  • Tent of Horrors (Norstein Bekkler): Mini-game carnival tent offering games of skill and reflex for Silver Points
  • Soda Chugging Contest: Lets Crono drink eight sodas rapidly to earn Silver Points

Tips

  • Speak to Marle before picking up her fallen pendant when you first bump into each other to ensure a not guilty vote in your upcoming trial.
  • Do not attempt to drag Marle away from the fairgrounds while she is shopping, as doing so adds to your charges at the trial.
