CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for Robo's origin

Robo must serve as the front runner of the team to bypass the entrance barrier and investigate the origin of the machines in 2300 A.D. After surviving a conveyor belt gauntlet, the party reroutes power pods, rescues captives, and collects two miniature dolls to bypass the inner sanctum doors. Inside, Robo confronts his former companion Atropos XR before the party destroys Mother Brain, permanently powering down the facility.

Secrets

  • A companion robot can be lured from the northern charging pod to mimic your movements and stand directly in front of another robot blocking the upper route, causing them to short each other out and opening access to a Poyozo Doll.

Tips

  • When fighting Mother Brain, destroy only two of the three Display monitors; eliminating all three causes Mother Brain to enter a high-damage berserk mode that bombards the entire party.
  • The entrance conveyor belt features consecutive waves of robotic foes that grant substantial experience and tech points, and jumping into the nearby chute allows you to loop and repeat the battles before finishing the area.
