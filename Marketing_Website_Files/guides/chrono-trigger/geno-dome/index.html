Geno Dome

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for Robo's origin

Robo must be placed at the head of the party to gain access to the Geno Dome in 2300 A.D. Inside, you must navigate conveyor belts, solve power pod puzzles, and defeat the facility's rogue artificial intelligence. Shutting down the factory grants Robo powerful personal weapons.

Items

  • Terabeam Blade: Chest inside the factory area
  • MegaElixir: Found in a treasure chest within the dome
  • Magic Tab: Found on the floor in the conveyor belt puzzle room
  • Speed Tab: Found lying on the ground in the side corridor
  • Crisis Arm: Received as a reward after defeating Mother Brain
  • Terra Arm: Received as a reward after defeating Mother Brain

Secrets

  • Energizing the pod puzzle requires charging Robo with electricity from an active energy pod and quickly running to the unpowered door pod before the charge dissipates.
  • Finding a companion doll requires retrieving a miniature green robot unit by pressing hidden switches to stop the conveyor belt from pushing you backward.

Tips

  • Do not destroy all three Displays during the Mother Brain fight, as eliminating the last one causes Mother Brain to enter a berserk state with devastating party-wide attacks.
  • Equipping Robo with the Uzzi Punch tech makes the solo duel against Atropos XR significantly easier.
  • The Crisis Arm's damage scales with Robo's current HP; it deals monumental damage when Robo's HP ends in 9.
