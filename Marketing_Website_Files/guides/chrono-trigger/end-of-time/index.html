End of Time

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Meeting Gaspar

Arriving via the Gate from the Proto Dome with four members causes a spacetime anomaly that drops the party at the End of Time, a realm where excess travelers must wait. Players meet an old man standing beneath a streetlamp who explains the hub's Gate pillars and instructs them to visit the room behind him. Inside, the entity Spekkio unlocks magic for compatible characters, after which the party must reduce to three active members before using the Gate network to journey onward.

Items

  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Defeating Spekkio in his Amphibite/Frog form (party lead level 1–9)
  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Defeating Spekkio in his Kilwala form (party lead level 10–19) along with 5 Ethers

Secrets

  • Inside Spekkio's room, running or walking clockwise along the inner walls starting from the doorway three complete times prompts Spekkio to awaken the team's magic.
  • Entering the glowing bucket found northeast of the streetlamp transports the party directly to the Day of Lavos (1999 A.D.) to battle the final boss.

Tips

  • Touch the sparkling green light directly above the save point to fully replenish the entire party's HP and MP free of charge.
  • When circling Spekkio's chamber, avoid diagonal movement and make firm contact with all four corners to ensure each lap registers properly.
