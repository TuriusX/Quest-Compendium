Giant's Claw

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Ruby Knife

Located on an isolated island in 600 A.D., Giant's Claw comprises the underground remains of the prehistoric Tyranno Lair. You explore its cavernous ruins, navigating trap switches and familiar prehistoric structures to reach the legendary Rainbow Shell. At the deepest chamber, you confront the Rust Tyrano before securing the artifact for Guardia Castle.

Items

  • Sight Cap: Found in a treasure chest on the left side of the second large area
  • Power Tab: Lying directly on the floor inside the skull room past the switch pit
  • Full Ether: Inside a treasure chest on the left after climbing down the first ladder
  • Power Tab: On the floor to the right of the ladder base in the lower cavern room
  • Blue Rock: Located in an open chest on the upper ledge walkway before leaving the cavern
  • Zodiac Cape: In a partially concealed chest on the far left of the lower walkway
  • Lapis: In a treasure chest down the ramp just before the room's left exit
  • FrenzyBand: Inside a chest found through the skull passageway's lower-left path
  • Power Tab: On the floor up the stairs to the left after dropping through the trap chest floor

Secrets

  • Examine Azala's former throne at the top of the ruins with any character leading the party to see a unique sitting animation and reaction.
