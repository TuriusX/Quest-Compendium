Located on an isolated island in 600 A.D., Giant's Claw comprises the underground remains of the prehistoric Tyranno Lair. You explore its cavernous ruins, navigating trap switches and familiar prehistoric structures to reach the legendary Rainbow Shell. At the deepest chamber, you confront the Rust Tyrano before securing the artifact for Guardia Castle.

Tips
  • Equip fire-absorbing or fire-resistant gear like the Red Mail and Red Vest before engaging Rust Tyrano to trivialize his Tyrano Flame attacks.
