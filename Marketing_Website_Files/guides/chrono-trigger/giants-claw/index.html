Giant's Claw

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Ruby Knife

The Giant's Claw is an optional dungeon inside the remains of the Black Tyrano, accessible in the Middle Ages (600 A.D.) after speaking with Toma at Choras Tavern. Players navigate its cavernous pathways and pitfalls to find the Rainbow Shell guarded by the Rust Tyrano. Note that the Ruby Knife is unrelated to this area, as it is obtained in 12,000 B.C. to awaken the Masamune and strike the Mammon Machine.

Items

  • Sight Cap: Chest found in the early cave passages of the dungeon
  • Frenzy Band: Chest located along the paths leading through the Tyrano remains
  • Blue Rock: Chest placed within the cavern paths, teaches the Triple Tech Omega Flare
  • Zodiac Cape: Chest found deeper within the cavern network
  • Rainbow Shell: Found in the deepest chamber of the dungeon after defeating the boss

Tips

  • Equip fire-absorbing or fire-reducing armor such as the Red Plate or Red Mail before facing the Rust Tyrano to completely negate its countdown attack.
  • Step into specific floor switches or pitfalls to drop through the floor and reach otherwise unreachable chests in the lower caverns.
