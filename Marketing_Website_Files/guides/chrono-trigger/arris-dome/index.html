Arris Dome

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Exploring the future ruins

The party arrives in the bleak future year of 2300 A.D. to discover a group of starving refugees dwelling in the upper chambers. To help them, the heroes delve into the lower ruins past a robotic guardian to retrieve preserved food storage, where they discover a seed and access the mainframe computers. Viewing the computer records reveals visual playback of "The Day of Lavos," inspiring the team to alter history and avert the apocalypse.

Items

  • Mid Ether: Inside a chest in the refrigerated food storage chamber behind the Guardian boss
  • Seed: Found in the hands of the deceased explorer inside the food storage room
  • Mid Ether: Inside a treasure chest on the catwalks leading toward the central information center
  • Bike Key: Given by Doan in the upper sanctuary after returning from the lower facility
  • Power Tab: Located in the secret repository behind the sealed door accessible later in the game
  • Lumin Robe: Found in a treasure chest behind the sealed door
  • Elixir: Found in a treasure chest behind the sealed door
  • Hit Ring: Found in a treasure chest behind the sealed door
  • Gold Erng: Found in a treasure chest behind the sealed door

Secrets

  • Catching the scurrying rat along the upper catwalks reveals the command to activate the consoles: hold the L and R buttons simultaneously and press A while interacting with the terminal to form a bridge across the gap.

Tips

  • Target the two Bit support pods first during the boss fight, because striking the Guardian while they are active triggers a punishing counterattack.
