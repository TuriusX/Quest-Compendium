Arris Dome

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Exploring the future ruins

The party enters this underground shelter in the ruined future of 2300 A.D. to find a desperate group of starving survivors. After descending into the basement storage facility to secure food, you defeat the Guardian defense system and discover a computer terminal revealing the catastrophe of the Day of Lavos. To continue your journey east toward the Proto Dome, you obtain the Bike Key from the wreckage and learn of a nearby time gate.

Items

  • Mid Ether: Chest in the northeastern corner of the lower basement
  • Bike Key: Found on the console or corpse in the basement food refrigeration room after defeating the Guardian
  • Seed: Handed over to Doan from the basement food store
  • Taban Helm: Awarded by Lucca's father, not in this area

Secrets

  • Sealed Black Door | Located in the basement passage, it can only be opened later in the game once your pendant is energized by the Mammon Machine.
  • Rat Catching Minigame | Catching the running yellow rat on the rafters of the upper basement yields the password (L+R+A simultaneously, or L1+R1+Cross depending on the platform) needed to activate the console on the lower level.

Tips

  • Resting in the Enertron fully restores the party's HP and MP, but it still leaves the party feeling hungry.
  • Avoid using multi-target attacks that hit the Guardian while both Bits are alive, as doing so provokes a harsh counterattack.
  • When chasing the rat on the rafters, hold the dash button to match its speed and rapidly press the confirm button to catch it.
