Reptite Lair

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Fighting Azala's forces

In 65,000,000 B.C., Crono and Ayla track down Azala's forces through this underground maze to recover the stolen Gate Key. You must navigate through levels by jumping down holes dug into the rock by burrowing beetles. At the bottom chamber, you confront Azala and must defeat the guardian Nizbel to reclaim the key.

Items

  • Mid Ether: Inside the cave accessed by dropping through the southwestern beetle hole
  • Hi-Potion: Found in the lower chamber reached along the southwestern burrow route
  • Ruby Vest: Inside the cavern reached by taking the left burrow on the intermediate level
  • Hi-Ether: Inside a chest found along the eastern drop route
  • Elixir: In a secluded lower cave reached by dropping into the hole dug by a beetle on the far east side of the second tier
  • Gate Key: Awarded after defeating the boss Nizbel at the lowest level

Secrets

  • Waiting patiently in the entry and secondary rooms without attacking lets the Evilweevils drill brand-new holes into the floor, opening hidden paths to otherwise inaccessible chests containing high-tier gear and an Elixir.
