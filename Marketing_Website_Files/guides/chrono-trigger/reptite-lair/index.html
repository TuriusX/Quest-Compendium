Reptite Lair

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Fighting Azala's forces

The party pursues the Reptites who stole the Gate Key from Ioka Village during the night. Ayla guides Crono and his ally through the cavern to reclaim the stolen artifact from Azala. After dropping through sandy tunnels and fighting through the lair, the party confronts Nizbel and Azala to recover the Key.

Items

  • Mid Tonic: In a chest inside the tunnel system
  • Full Tonic: In a chest accessed by falling through the sand pits
  • Rock Helmet: In a chest reached by dropping through the sand holes in the first large room

Secrets

  • The Weevil enemies dig holes in the sand that let the party drop down into lower, treasure-filled rooms.

Tips

  • Bring Crono to fight Nizbel so you can repeatedly shock the boss with Lightning tech to keep its defense low.
  • Watch where the Weevils dig in the entry chambers to access different isolated subterranean ledges containing chests.
