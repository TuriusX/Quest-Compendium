Enhasa

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The dream city

Enhasa is a floating city in the Kingdom of Zeal reached during the party's first visit to 12,000 B.C. The residents spend their days asleep and dreaming, while the party learns lore about Queen Zeal, the Ocean Palace, and the mysterious boy Janus and his cat Alfador. Solving a hidden elemental book puzzle here triggers an optional boss fight for a valuable reward.

Items

  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: In a secret room unlocked by opening the elemental books in order
  • Speed Tab MISSABLE: Received from the Nu in the secret room after choosing to scratch its back

Secrets

  • Open the three elemental spellbooks around the city in the order of Water, Wind, and Fire to reveal a hidden door leading to a room with a Nu.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Nu (Six Nu battle)Defeating the six Nu summoned after answering the Nu's challenge unlocks access to a Magic Tab.

Shops and people

  • Nu Shopkeeper: Sells various consumable items including Tonics, Mid Tonics, Ethers, and Heals

Tips

  • Do the elemental book puzzle immediately, as Enhasa and its tabs become permanently inaccessible later in the story when the floating continent falls.
