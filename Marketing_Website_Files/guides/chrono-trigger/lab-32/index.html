Lab 32

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The motorcycle chase

Lab 32 is a post-apocalyptic ruined highway connecting the Arris Dome area to the eastern portion of the 2300 A.D. map. Players can either cross it by navigating on foot while fighting enemies or by challenging the cyborg Johnny to a high-speed jet bike race using the Jet-Bike key. Winning the race bypasses the combat encounters and provides a direct shortcut between the two ends of the lab.

Items

  • Mid Tonic: in a chest on the highway when traversed on foot

Tips

  • You can use your limited turbo boosts during the race by pressing the B button to pull ahead of Johnny right before the finish line.
  • Walking through Lab 32 on foot allows you to collect the treasure chests along the road, which cannot be picked up while racing on the jet bike.
  • You can race against Johnny as many times as you like by returning to the jet bike at either entrance of the highway.
