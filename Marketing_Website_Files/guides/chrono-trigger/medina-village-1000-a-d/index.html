Medina Village (1000 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Visiting the Elder's house

Medina Village in 1000 A.D. is an isolated settlement populated by the descendants of the Mystics who still hold a grudge against humans. Visiting the Elder's house allows the party to observe their lingering hostility and gather information about their reverence for Magus. Inside the residence, you can also uncover a couple of hidden items left on the upper floor.

Items

  • Speed Tab: on the floor in the upper right room of the Elder's house
  • Magic Tab: on the counter in the kitchen of the Elder's house

Secrets

  • Check the sparkling spot on the second floor of the Elder's house to pick up a Speed Tab without alerting the residents.

Tips

  • The Mystics here will speak condescendingly to your party, but they will not attack you inside the Elder's residence.
