Magus's Castle

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The confrontation with Magus

The party infiltrates this stronghold in 600 A.D. using the repaired Masamune to challenge Magus and prevent the summoning of Lavos. To proceed, you must explore both outer wings, defeat Slash and Flea, and navigate Ozzie's booby-trapped corridors. After sending Ozzie through a trapdoor, you descend into the castle's depths for the climactic battle against Magus.

  • Slasher MISSABLE: Dropped on the floor where Slash is defeated
  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Found glistening on the floor in the room where Flea is defeated
  • Dark Mail MISSABLE: Chest located in the conveyor belt / guillotine hallway in the inner castle
  • Mist Robe MISSABLE: Chest in the conveyor belt / guillotine corridor
  • DoomFinger MISSABLE: Chest found in the guillotine corridor of the inner castle
  • Speed Belt MISSABLE: Chest sitting at the end of the monster winch corridor
  • Barrier MISSABLE: Chest surrounded by deceptive children in the east hallway
  • Magic Scarf MISSABLE: Chest on the side of Ozzie's inner sanctum room after dropping him into the pit
  • Mist Robe MISSABLE: Chest on the opposite side of Ozzie's inner sanctum room

Secrets

  • Falling into any of the trapdoors triggered by Ozzie's switches drops you into a dungeon room surrounded by four treasure chests and multiple decoy save point monsters.

Tips

  • In the diagonal staircases where rolling balls barrel downward, you can duck onto the chain ladders to let them roll harmlessly past without initiating battles.
