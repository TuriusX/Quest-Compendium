Magus's Castle

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The confrontation with Magus

The party infiltrates Fiendlord Magus's fortress in the Middle Ages (600 AD) to stop him from summoning the apocalyptic beast Lavos. After confronting Magus's three top generals—Flea, Slash, and Ozzie—the party descends deep underground to battle Magus in his inner sanctum. Defeating Magus interrupts his ritual, triggering a massive dimensional gate that pulls everyone into a time warp.

Items

  • Magic Scarf MISSABLE: Dropped by Flea after defeating them in the castle's eastern wing
  • Slasher MISSABLE: Dropped by Slash after defeating his second form in the western wing
  • Mist Cloak MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest in the castle corridor
  • Dark Mail MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest in the pit traps / dungeon area
  • Doom Finger MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest along the ascent before Ozzie's room
  • Barrier MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest inside the castle
  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Located on the floor in the room where you battle Flea
  • Speed Belt MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest guarded by monsters in the castle

Enemies

SlashFights barehanded at first, then retrieves his sword for a faster and stronger second phase
OzzieCannot be harmed directly; hit the levers behind him to drop him through a trap door
MagusChanges dynamically based on his barrierShifts his elemental barrier after taking magic damage, signaling the only element currently able to harm him until he begins casting Dark Matter

Tips

  • Keep Frog in your active party during the fight with Magus so the Masamune can lower Magus's magic defense.
  • In the battle with Magus, attack him using the specific elemental magic he casts, as his barrier only takes damage from the element it currently absorbs or reflects.
  • When Magus stops changing his barrier and starts casting his ultimate spell Dark Matter, immediately switch everyone to physical attacks and heal up to withstand the massive group damage.
  • You must recruit Frog to your party and speak with all the fake castle inhabitants in the side wings before the central save point and true enemies appear.
