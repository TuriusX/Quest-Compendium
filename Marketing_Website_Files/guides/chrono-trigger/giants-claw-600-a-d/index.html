Giant's Claw (600 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Rainbow Shell

The Giant's Claw is a cavernous dungeon revealed to be the remains of the Black Tyrano's lair from prehistoric times. Navigating through the winding paths and switches, the party faces prehistoric enemies to locate the legendary Rainbow Shell. After defeating the boss guarding the prize, the party finds the shell is too heavy to move alone, prompting a request for King Guardia XXI to store it in the castle.

Items

  • Sight Cap: Chest found in the early cave passages
  • Frenzy Band: Inside a chest along the main path
  • Power Glove: Inside a chest found deeper in the caverns
  • Zodiac Cape: Chest in the inner ruins
  • Blue Rock: Chest near the throne room area
  • Rainbow Shell: Guarded by Rust Tyrano in the depths of the claw

Tips

  • Equip Red Mails or Red Vests before the Rust Tyrano fight to absorb its massive fire breath attacks.
  • You must read Toma's note at his grave in 600 A.D. to get the clue that unlocks entry to the Giant's Claw.
