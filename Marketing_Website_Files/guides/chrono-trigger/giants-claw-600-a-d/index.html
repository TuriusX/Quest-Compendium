Giant's Claw (600 A.D.)

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for the Rainbow Shell

The party explores this sunken prehistoric cavern—the submerged ruins of the Tyrano Lair—following directions left behind by the explorer Toma. After navigating pitfall traps and prehistoric monsters, the group confronts Rust Tyrano guarding the legendary Rainbow Shell deep within the ruins. Because the treasure is far too heavy to lift, the party enlists Guardia Castle's knights to store it safely for centuries.

Items

  • Sight Cap: In a treasure chest located in the upper room just past the entrance corridor
  • Power Tab: Shining on the floor inside the skull room reached after dropping into the pitfall trap
  • Full Ether: In a treasure chest near the base of the ladder in the pitfall cavern
  • Power Tab: Sparkling on the ground to the right of the ladder in the lower cavern area
  • Blue Rock: In a chest reached along the upper catwalk path around the cavern chamber
  • Zodiac Cape: In a partially concealed chest found along the lower walkway to the far left
  • Lapis: In a treasure chest positioned down the ramp near the exit of the lower walkway
  • Frenzy Band: In a chest accessed by passing through the left skull opening and heading through the lower doorway
  • Power Tab: Lying on the floor upstairs above the dungeon jail cells
  • Rainbow Shell: Guarded at the innermost chamber beyond the boss Rust Tyrano

Secrets

  • Reading Toma's discarded note at the entrance hints to deliberately drop into pitfall switches rather than avoiding them to advance through the dungeon.
  • Triggering a seemingly trapped treasure chest in the later corridors intentionally plunges the party through the floor down into the old prehistoric jail cells.
