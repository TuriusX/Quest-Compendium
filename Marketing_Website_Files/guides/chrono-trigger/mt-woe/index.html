Mt. Woe

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Rescuing the Gurus

Mt. Woe is a suspended mountain prison in 12,000 BC where the Queen of Zeal has imprisoned the Guru of Life, Melchior. The party must climb the freezing exterior chains and paths while battling gargoyles and rubble monsters to reach the summit. Defeating the boss Giga Gaia at the peak frees Melchior, which immediately triggers the collapse and destruction of the entire mountain.

Items

  • Lapis MISSABLE: In a treasure chest along the ascending pathways
  • Barrier MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the mountain paths
  • Shield MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the mountain paths
  • Shelter MISSABLE: In a treasure chest along the mountain route
  • Time Hat MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the upper mountain paths
  • Full Ether MISSABLE: In a treasure chest along the pathways

Secrets

  • Rubble encounters | Bipedal rock monsters standing stationary throughout the mountain freeze your party's tech points and lock items, but defeating them grants a massive 100 Tech Points each.

Tips

  • Focus all high-damage multi-target or targeted techs on Giga Gaia's left and right arms first to disable its most dangerous combined attacks before damaging the main head.
  • Mt. Woe permanently disappears after defeating Giga Gaia, so make sure to collect all treasure chests and defeat every Rubble before initiating the summit boss fight.
