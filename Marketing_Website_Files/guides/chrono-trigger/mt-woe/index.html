Mt. Woe

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Rescuing the Gurus

Mt. Woe is a floating mountain anchored above the prehistoric icebound continent of 12,000 B.C., reached by ascending the chain in the Beast's Nest. Crono and his companions must ascend the rocky paths and chains to reach the summit, where Melchior the Guru of Life is imprisoned within a crystal. After defeating the guardian boss Giga Gaia, the mountain collapses and disappears from the game world for the remainder of the adventure.

Items

  • Lode Helm MISSABLE: Treasure chest in the lower-left area reached by looping around cliffs in the first screen
  • Lode Vest MISSABLE: Southern treasure chest across the northeast chain bridge on the second screen
  • Shield MISSABLE: Northern treasure chest on the isolated northeast ledge on the second screen
  • Barrier MISSABLE: Treasure chest sitting at the far top-left edge of the second screen
  • Lapis MISSABLE: Treasure chest on an upper ledge path accessed by climbing a slope in the second screen
  • Time Hat MISSABLE: Treasure chest found along the trail on the screen leading up to the summit
  • Full Ether MISSABLE: Treasure chest located along the path near the mountain peak
  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Sparkle sitting in the southeast corner of the screen just before the boss summit chain

Secrets

  • A solitary Rubble monster situated next to two treasure chests across the northeastern chain on the second area will continually respawn if you exit and re-enter the screen, unlike the rest of the Rubbles which permanently disappear after combat.

Tips

  • Equip armor that reduces or absorbs Fire damage (such as the Ruby Vest, Red Vest, or Red Mail) before approaching the summit to shrug off Giga Gaia's deadliest offensive assaults.
