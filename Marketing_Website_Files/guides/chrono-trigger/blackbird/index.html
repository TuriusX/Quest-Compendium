Blackbird

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Infiltrating the flying fortress

Following their capture by Dalton, the party awakens trapped aboard the Blackbird stripped of all weapons, armor, items, and gold. To break out, players navigate ventilation ducts and corridors to recover their confiscated gear from guarded chests. After reclaiming their belongings, they fight across the airship's wing to defeat the Golem Boss (Golem Overlord) and Dalton Plus, ultimately reclaiming the Epoch.

Items

  • 1st Char. Equipment MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the northwestern room guarded by a sleeping sentry, reached via the air ducts
  • 2nd Char. Equipment MISSABLE: Inside a chest in a room off the right side of the main conveyor corridor
  • 3rd Char. Equipment MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the southern room accessible from the conveyor belt pathway
  • Items MISSABLE: Inside a chest located in the upper-left room of the lower deck corridor
  • Money MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the room just north of the third character's equipment chamber
  • Magic Tab MISSABLE: Found in the northeastern corner of the air ducts after reclaiming all stolen possessions

Secrets

  • Escaping the starting cell by pretending to be sick at the door instead of taking the overhead duct; knocking on the door lures in a guard who can be knocked unconscious to open the cell early.

Tips

  • Bringing Ayla in the party allows immediate combat without weapons since she fights barehanded, letting you defeat guards before recovering your gear.
