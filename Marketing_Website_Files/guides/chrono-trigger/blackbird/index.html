Blackbird

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Infiltrating the flying fortress

After being captured by Dalton in 12,000 BC, the party awakens stripped of all their equipment, items, and money aboard the flying fortress Blackbird. Players must crawl through ventilation ducts, reclaim their stolen gear from various treasure chests, and reach the exterior deck to defeat Dalton and gain control of the Epoch. If Ayla is in the party, she can fight immediately without equipment to retrieve the gear, making the infiltration much easier.

Items

  • Party's Equipment MISSABLE: In a chest inside a room on the main deck guarded by sleeping guards
  • Party's Items MISSABLE: In a chest guarded by a wandering guard
  • Party's Money MISSABLE: In a chest found in one of the lower storage rooms
  • SightScope MISSABLE: In a chest located in an upper-deck cabin

Secrets

  • Vent system peeking | Pressing the interact button while looking down through floor grates in the ducts allows you to scout enemy positions and spot where your equipment chests are located.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
BashMechanical sentry that primarily relies on physical strikes.
ByteFlying robotic sentry that attacks with lasers and physical strikes.
Master GolemHesitant boss that becomes intimidated by the player party and eventually flees the battle after counting down.

Tips

  • Bring Ayla as a party member prior to being captured, as her unarmed fist attacks allow her to fight normally even while all party equipment is confiscated.
  • Avoid touching the exhaust pipes or falling off the outer hull while climbing on the exterior of the Blackbird.
