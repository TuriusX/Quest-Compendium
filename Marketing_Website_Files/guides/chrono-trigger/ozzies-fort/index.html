Ozzie's Fort

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest to defeat Ozzie

Located in the Middle Ages (600 A.D.), this stronghold serves as the final hideout for Ozzie, Slash, and Flea after Magus's defeat. The party navigates through a series of trap-laden rooms, confronting the trio in comedic battles and enduring their failed ambushes. Completing the fort defeats Ozzie for good, freeing the town of Medina in the Present from the Fiends' lingering control.

  • Gloom Helm: In the hidden room beneath the guillotine chamber
  • Gloom Cape: In the hidden room beneath the guillotine chamber
  • Doom Sickle: In the hidden room beneath the guillotine chamber
  • Magic Tab: In the hidden room beneath the guillotine chamber
  • Sight Cap: In a chest inside the treasure room near the end of the fort
  • Dash Ring: In a chest inside the treasure room near the end of the fort

Secrets

  • In the room where an enemy is executed by a guillotine, head directly down through a hidden passage in the south wall to access a hidden chamber holding Magus's ultimate equipment.

Tips

  • Bring Magus in your active party for special dialogue interactions throughout the fort.
  • In the first trap room, do not step forward when Ozzie taunts you with a chest; simply wait for the Imp to activate the trap instead.
