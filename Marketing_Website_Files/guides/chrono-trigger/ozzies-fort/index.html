Ozzie's Fort

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest to defeat Ozzie

This optional fortress in 600 A.D. serves as the final hideout for Ozzie, Flea, and Slash after the fall of Magus' Lair. The party navigates a linear set of rooms filled with failed traps, skirmishes, and a climatic showdown against all three Mystic generals together. Defeating Ozzie permanently liberates the area and alters Medina Village in 1000 A.D. into an ally-friendly settlement.

Items

  • Full Ether: Inside a treasure chest beneath the guillotine trap room, retrieved safely after Ozzie's Imp trap backfires
  • DoomSickle: Inside a treasure chest within the hidden southern room off the guillotine hallway
  • Gloom Helm: Inside a treasure chest within the hidden southern room off the guillotine hallway
  • Gloom Cape: Inside a treasure chest within the hidden southern room off the guillotine hallway
  • Magic Tab: Sparkle on the floor on the right side of the secret treasure room
  • Dash Ring: Treasure chest located in the room immediately following the trio boss fight
  • Sight Cap: Treasure chest located in the room immediately following the trio boss fight

Secrets

  • In the room with the guillotine blade trap, walk all the way to the southern wall near the stairs and run downward through a disguised false passageway to uncover a concealed chamber holding Magus' ultimate gear.
