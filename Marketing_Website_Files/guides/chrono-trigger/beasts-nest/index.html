Beast's Nest

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Exploring the wilderness

The Beast's Nest is a snowy mountain cavern located on the continent of Medina in the Dark Ages (12,000 BC). The party journeys into this lair beneath Mt. Woe to confront the Mud Beast and its handlers, the Beasts. Defeating these creatures allows the party to ascend the chain leading directly onto Mt. Woe.

Tips

  • Focus down the Red Beast or Blue Beast first using their elemental weaknesses, as the Mudbeast runs away once both companions are defeated.
  • Equip armor that reduces or absorbs Fire or Water damage to easily survive the Beasts' coordinated attacks.
