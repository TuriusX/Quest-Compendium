Located at the base of Terra Cave in 12,000 BC, this cavernous route must be traversed to reach the chain ascending to the Mountain of Woe. After pushing through packs of hostile creatures, the party must defeat a trio of bosses before proceeding up the chain.

Don't miss 0/1

Enemies
Beast
Steal / drop R'bow Helm stolen via Charm

Normal mob encountered in pairs along the path

Mud Imp
Steal / drop Speed Tab stolen via Charm

Boss that fights alongside two companion monsters, healing them and orchestrating combo assaults

Blue Beast
Weak to FireSteal / drop MermaidCap stolen via Charm

Mud Imp minion immune to Water-elemental magic

Red Beast
Weak to WaterSteal / drop Elixir stolen via Charm

Mud Imp minion that should be struck with opposing elemental techniques

Tips
  • Include Ayla in your battle lineup to use Charm, as several enemies here carry valuable unique or rare headgear.
