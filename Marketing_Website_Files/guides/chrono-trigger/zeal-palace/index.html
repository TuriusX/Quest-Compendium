Zeal Palace

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Arriving in the floating kingdom

The party journeys to the Kingdom of Zeal's seat of power high in the clouds during 12,000 B.C., exploring its halls to witness the Mammon Machine. After observing Schala using her pendant to unlock doors, the party charges Crono's pendant with the Mammon Machine to breach the throne room. There, Dalton confronts the heroes and summons the Golem to battle them.

Secrets

  • Nu's Back Scratch Point | Talk to the Nu on the upper level and agree to scratch its back. It reveals its favorite itching spot, which allows you to scratch the Nu in Kajar to obtain a hidden Power Tab.

Tips

  • Speak to the woman tending a sapling on the terrace and instruct her to secretly plant the tree instead of burning it, preserving a vital prerequisite for a future side quest.
