Zeal Palace

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Arriving in the floating kingdom

The party arrives in the luxurious Zeal Palace, the center of the magical Kingdom of Zeal, to learn about Queen Zeal's obsession with the Mammon Machine. After watching Schala open a sealed door using an enchanted pendant, the party must charge their own pendant in the Mammon Machine to pursue her into the throne room. This area sets up the major confrontation with Queen Zeal and the awakening of Lavos, leading directly into the Ocean Palace arc.

Items

  • Magic Tab: In the bottom-right corner of the room with the Nu who scratches backs

Secrets

  • Opening the elemental books in the right sequence (Water, Wind, Fire) in the library unlocks a hidden door leading to a room with a Nu and a Magic Capsule.
  • Talking to the woman tending a sapling and advising her to secretly plant it leads to the side quest that revives Fiona's forest in 600 AD.
  • Scratching the Nu's back in the palace reveals the Nu's scratching spot, which you can use on the Nu in Kajar to obtain a Magic Tab.

Enemies

Enemy
Zeal Palace (First Visit) features no standard random battles; combat only occurs during scripted boss events.

Tips

  • Do not forget to charge the pendant at the Mammon Machine, as you cannot advance past the sealed door to the throne room without it.
  • Make sure you talk to the Earthbound one who asks about the plant before finishing the Ocean Palace events, or you risk missing out on Fiona's forest side quest later.
