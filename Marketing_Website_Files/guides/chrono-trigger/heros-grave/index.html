Hero's Grave

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for Cyrus's ghost

The Hero's Grave, also known as the Northern Ruins, is a ruined castle located on an island in the Middle Ages and the Present where the spirit of Sir Cyrus wanders. You must restore the crumbling ruins with the carpenter's help, defeat the undead haunting the halls, and bring Frog to confront Cyrus's ghost. Putting Cyrus's soul to rest transforms the ruins into a proper memorial and awakens the true power of the legendary sword Masamune.

  • Shiva Edge MISSABLE: Inside a sealed chest in the Present after examining it unsealed in the Middle Ages
  • Valkyrie Bow MISSABLE: Inside a sealed chest in the Present after examining it unsealed in the Middle Ages
  • Nova Armor: Found in an unsealed chest in the ruins
  • Siren Flute MISSABLE: Inside a sealed chest in the Present after examining it unsealed in the Middle Ages

Secrets

  • By examining the sealed black chests in the Middle Ages without opening them, their contents will be upgraded and can be collected in the Present; afterward, return to the Middle Ages to collect the lower-tier versions from the same chests.
  • Cyrus's ghost cannot be harmed or put to rest until Frog is placed in your active battle party and brought directly to his grave.

Tips

  • You must repeatedly visit the Carpenter in Choras Village in the Middle Ages to repair broken stairways and floors in sections, clearing out monsters each time to allow his workers to continue.
  • You must travel to 1000 AD to retrieve the Carpenter's tools from his descendant's wife before the carpenter in 600 AD can fix the ruins.
  • Clearing this quest permanently renames the location from the Northern Ruins to the Hero's Grave in the Present.
