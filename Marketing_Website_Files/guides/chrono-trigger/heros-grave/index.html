Hero's Grave

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Completing the quest for Cyrus's ghost

Located on the island of Choras, this ruined complex houses the tomb of the fallen knight Cyrus and is plagued by monsters and broken floors. To restore passage and lay Cyrus to rest, the party must retrieve tools from a descendant in 1000 A.D., recruit a carpenter in 600 A.D. to complete repairs, and clear the halls of ghosts. Bringing Frog to the headstone triggers a reunion with his friend's spirit that permanently unlocks the full power of the Masamune.

Items

  • Masamune (Upgraded): Obtained at Cyrus's tombstone after putting Frog in the party and laying Cyrus to rest
  • Moonbeam Armor MISSABLE: Found inside a sealed chest in the 1000 A.D. version of the ruins after inspecting the chest in 600 A.D. without looting it
  • Valkyrie Bow MISSABLE: Found inside an upgraded sealed chest in 1000 A.D. after interacting with the matching chest in 600 A.D. and leaving it shut
  • Suzaku MISSABLE: Located in the sealed chest in 1000 A.D. after choosing not to open its corresponding chest in 600 A.D.
  • Nova Armor: Found in the sealed chest in 600 A.D.
  • Siren's Kiss: Inside the sealed chest on the upper level in 600 A.D.
  • Onimaru: Located inside the sealed chest on the upper level in 600 A.D.
  • HyperEther: Found inside a standard treasure chest downstairs on the lower floor in 600 A.D.
  • Elixir: Inside a standard chest on the second floor
  • Turbo Ether: Inside a standard chest found downstairs in the ruins

Secrets

  • Upgraded sealed chests: interact with the black sealed chests in 600 A.D., decline to open them when prompted, travel to the same site in 1000 A.D. to collect the evolved equipment, and then return to 600 A.D. to take the earlier items as well.

Tips

  • Do not take the contents of the black sealed chests on your first visit in 600 A.D.; inspect them, decline to open them, and collect the upgraded versions in 1000 A.D. first so you can walk away with both tiers of equipment.
