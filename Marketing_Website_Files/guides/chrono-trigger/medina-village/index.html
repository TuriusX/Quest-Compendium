Medina Village

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Arriving in 1000 A.D.

After arriving through a cupboard portal from the End of Time, the party discovers an isolated settlement populated entirely by Mystics who harbor deep resentment toward humans. To proceed with the story, the heroes must gather information from the hostile locals and then travel beyond the town toward Melchior's Cabin and Heckran Cave.

Items

  • Speed Tab: Medina Village Elder's House, sitting on the kitchen countertop
  • Magic Tab: Medina Village Elder's House, resting in the corner of the second-floor desk

Secrets

  • Eating the slice of cake found inside the initial residence replenishes the entire party's HP and MP for free.

Enemies

EnemyNotes

Shops and people

  • Medina Village Market: Iron Blade, SteelSaber, Demon Edge, Lumin Robe, Flash Mail, Glow Helm, Tonic, Mid Tonic, Full Tonic, Heal, Revive, Shelter, Ether
  • Medina Inn: Rest for the party

Tips

  • Avoid buying from the market during your first visit, as the hostile Mystics charge absurdly inflated rates of up to 65,000 G per piece of equipment.
