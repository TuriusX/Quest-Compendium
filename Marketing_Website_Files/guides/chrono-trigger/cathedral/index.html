Cathedral

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Fighting Magus's minions

Located in 600 A.D., this sanctuary operates as a secret front where Magus's minions hold Queen Leene and the real Chancellor captive. After uncovering the demonic nuns with Frog's assistance, the party explores hidden passages unlocked via the chapel organ. The mission culminates in defeating the fiend Yakra to rescue Queen Leene and restore the timeline.

Items

  • Naga-ette Bromide MISSABLE: Inside the dresser drawer in the northwestern room on the upper-left floor
  • SteelSaber MISSABLE: Treasure chest inside the hidden statue chamber dedicated to Magus
  • MaidenSuit MISSABLE: Treasure chest located in the western living quarters bedroom
  • Speed Belt MISSABLE: Treasure chest found inside the secret Magus room
  • Power Tab MISSABLE: Located in the room behind the spiked passageway, lying on the floor in front of the bucket
  • Defender MISSABLE: Treasure chest in the eastern room guarded by monsters disguised as royalty
  • Iron Sword MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest on the catwalk directly above the central entryway

Secrets

  • A hidden chamber worshipping Magus can be reached by walking behind the wall in the right-hand corridor where fiends are gathered around an altar.
  • Interacting with the pipe organ in the main chapel plays a chord that unlocks the concealed doorway on the opposite wall.

Tips

  • Ensure Crono and Lucca train together beforehand to execute Fire Whirl, which clears multiple clustered enemies quickly.
  • Taking the Naga-ette Bromide to an elderly collector in Dorino Village rewards you with a Magic Tab later on.
