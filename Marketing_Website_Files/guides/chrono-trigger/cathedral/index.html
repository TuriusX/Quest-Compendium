Cathedral

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Fighting Magus's minions

Crono and Lucca enter Manolia Cathedral to rescue Queen Leene from disguised fiends and recruit Frog to their party. The party fights their way through the monastery's hidden corridors and traps to locate the kidnapped queen. The area concludes with a boss battle against Yakra, freeing the real Queen Leene and the Chancellor.

Items

  • MaidenSuit MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest on the left wing of the cathedral
  • Iron Sword MISSABLE: inside a chest in the secret room behind the Magus statue
  • SteelSaber MISSABLE: inside a chest found along the inner cathedral pathways
  • Speed Belt MISSABLE: inside a chest in the room behind the organ
  • Defender MISSABLE: inside a chest in the lower rooms of the cathedral

Secrets

  • Play the organ in the cathedral's inner hall to reveal a hidden door leading deeper into the monastery.

Tips

  • Use Frog and Crono's dual tech X-Strike to deal heavy damage to Yakra.
  • Back away from Yakra when he prepares his counterattack needles to minimize party damage.
  • Inspect the shining hairpin dropped in the cathedral entrance hall to trigger the initial fiend ambush and Frog's arrival.
