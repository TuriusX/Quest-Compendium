Kajar

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · The secret city

Kajar is an underground city in the magical Kingdom of Zeal dedicated to scientific research and elemental magic. Players visit during the middle of the Antiquity era to gather information about the Mammon Machine and the Ocean Palace. Solving the mystery of the city's elemental spell books opens a hidden chamber containing valuable equipment.

Items

  • Speed Tab MISSABLE: Search the upper right corner inside the research room

Secrets

  • Open the three elemental spell books in order—Water, Wind, then Fire—to unlock a hidden room containing the Black Rock and a Poyozo Doll.

Shops and people

  • Nu Shopkeeper: Sells mid-tier restorative items, tonics, and status-healing consumables.

Tips

  • Scratching the Nu on the back in the Kajar library unlocks an itch-scratching technique needed for a Nu in the Zeal Palace to give you a Magic Tab.
  • Choose whether or not to tell the woman near the center of town to secretly plant the mysterious plant sprout, as this initiates the Sunken Desert sidequest later in the game.
