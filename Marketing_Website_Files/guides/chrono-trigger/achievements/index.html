CHRONO TRIGGER® achievement guide

How to unlock every achievement, which ones you can miss, and the best order to get them all. Tick them off as you go.

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Ocean Palace (confronting the Mammon Machine): Crono is lost, the Kingdom of Zeal is permanently destroyed, and all previous Zeal floating continent hubs become permanently inaccessible.
  • Defeating Lavos: Saves made during the final boss battles or entering the core cannot be reversed without loading an earlier external save or completing the game to generate New Game +.

Recommended order
  1. Start in Leene Square, meet Marle, rescue her through Truce Canyon, Guardia Forest, Guardia Castle, and Cathedral, then escape to Arris Dome, Lab 32, Proto Dome, and Factory Ruins.
  2. Travel to End of Time, Medina Village (1000 A.D.), Denadoro Mountains, Magic Cave, and Magus's Castle to defeat Magus.
  3. Venture into Prehistoric Village, Reptite Lair, and Tyranno Lair to secure the Gate Key and Gate to Zeal.
  4. Explore Zeal Palace, Enhasa, Kajar, Beast's Nest, Mt. Woe, and Blackbird before confronting the Ocean Palace disaster.
  5. Obtain the Time Egg from Keeper's Dome and revive Crono at Death Peak.
  6. Complete side quests across Sunken Desert, Geno Dome, Sun Keep, Ozzie's Fort, Hero's Grave, Giant's Claw (600 A.D.), Guardia Castle (600 A.D.), and Guardia Castle (1000 A.D.) to craft the Rainbow Shell equipment.
  7. Clear Black Omen and defeat Lavos via the End of Time or Epoch to complete Playthrough 1 and earn the "Beyond Time" ending.
  8. Clear the three post-game Dimensional Vortices and defeat the Dream Devourer at Time's Eclipse to earn "Dream's Epilogue".
  9. Begin New Game + and defeat Lavos at the Leene Square right telepod immediately to unlock "The Dream Project".
  10. Replay specific narrative milestones in New Game + to defeat Lavos and unlock the remaining 10 ending achievements: "The Successor of Guardia", "Good Night", "The Legendary Hero", "The Unknown Past", "People of the Times", "The Oath", "Dino Age", "What the Prophet Seeks", "Memory Lane", and "Reunion".
