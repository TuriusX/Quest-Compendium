Guides / CHRONO TRIGGER®

Factory Ruins

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Reactivating Robo

The party explores this decommissioned facility to restore power and open the blast doors blocking the path onward. Marle or Lucca stays behind at the entrance terminal to monitor system data while Crono, the other companion, and the newly awakened Robo infiltrate the factory floors. After shutting down the automated security system in the inner core, the party must fight off Robo's former model companions to escape.

Items

  • Shelter: Chest inside the upper lab area on the right conveyor belt path
  • Titan Vest: Chest in the crane control observation room
  • Hammer Arm: Chest located in the lower testing room on the eastern side
  • Mid Ether: Chest found in the western monitor storage room
  • Robin Bow: Chest in the inner factory catwalk area
  • Ether: Chest located on the factory conveyor platform

Secrets

  • Viewing the crane code sequence "X, A, B, Y" on the wall monitor allows you to operate the crane control terminal and clear the shipping barrels blocking the lower doorway.
  • Entering the password "Z, A, B, Y" (which corresponds to B, A, X, Y on the SNES controller) into the central computer core overrides the security lockdown.

Tips

  • Bring Lucca along to exploit the robotic enemies' universal vulnerability to Lightning and Fire magic.
  • The conveyor belt puzzle on the eastern wing continuously forces you into three consecutive automated battle waves if you step onto the active belt.
  • In the final boss encounter against the R-Series, focus on defeating them one at a time using Crono's Cyclone or area-of-effect techs to rapidly cut down their incoming damage.
