Factory Ruins

CHRONO TRIGGER® guide · Reactivating Robo

After Lucca restores Robo at Proto Dome, the party visits this abandoned facility in 2300 A.D. to reactivate power to the dome's time gate. Leaving either Marle or Lucca behind to monitor systems, Crono, Robo, and the remaining companion navigate the conveyor belts, crane controls, and security terminals of the factory. Once the generator is engaged and the facility goes into lockdown, the team must defeat Robo's aggressive counterparts, the R-Series, to escape.

Items

  • Robin Bow: Catwalk chest in the factory sector reached via the middle ladder
  • Mid Ether: Dark storage room located down the western ladder on the factory side
  • Ether: Chest located along the conveyor belt catwalk path

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
AcidLightningExtremely resilient to standard physical strikes until attacked with magic or techniques.

R-SeriesA boss encounter consisting of six hostile robots that ambush the party as they flee the facility.

Tips

  • Falling or stepping onto the moving conveyor belts forces you into three unskippable consecutive waves of automated combat.
  • In the battle against the six R-Series robots, utilize multi-target physical techniques like Crono's Cyclone to eliminate multiple units quickly and reduce incoming damage.
