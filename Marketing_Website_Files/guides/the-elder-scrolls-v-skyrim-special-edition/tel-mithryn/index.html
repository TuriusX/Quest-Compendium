Tel Mithryn

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Visiting the wizard tower on Solstheim

Tel Mithryn is a settlement on the southeastern coast of Solstheim centered around a massive giant mushroom tower grown by the Telvanni wizard Master Neloth. Players visit this area to consult Neloth during the Dragonborn main questline, train in magic schools, and complete eccentric tasks for the wizard and his staff. The location also grants access to unique facilities such as an enchanting workbench and Neloth's personal Black Book.

Items

  • Black Book: The Hidden Twilight: Inside Neloth's private study atop the main tower, unlocked after completing the quest "Reluctant Steward"
  • Staff Enchanter: Inside the locked enclosure within the main tower of Tel Mithryn

Secrets

  • The Heart Stone deposit located in the graveyard behind the settlement can be mined to harvest Heart Stones, which are necessary for crafting staves and completing Neloth's research quests.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Ash SpawnFrostOre, Heart StonesFrequently ambushes the outskirts and coast surrounding the mushroom settlement.

Shops and people

  • Neloth: Sells high-level Destruction, Alteration, and Conjuration spells, staves, and soul gems, as well as Master-level Enchanting training
  • Talvas Fathryon: Sells Conjuration spell tomes and ingredients, and provides Master-level Conjuration training
  • Elynea Mothren: Sells alchemy ingredients, potions, and food out of the apothecary mushroom
  • Ulves Romoran: Sells general goods and drinks inside the Tel Mithryn kitchen

Tips

  • Step directly into the floating pillar of light in the center of the main tower to be levitated up into Neloth's living quarters.
  • Completing Neloth's side quests eventually unlocks full access to the Staff Enchanter, allowing you to create custom staves without leveling up the Enchanting skill.
