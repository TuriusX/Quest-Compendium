Raldbthar

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Searching for the Dwemer mechanism for the College of Winterhold

Raldbthar is a sprawling Dwemer ruin located in the mountains west of Windhelm, initially inhabited by bandits before descending into Dwemer Automatons and Falmer. During the College of Winterhold questline, the Dragonborn explores the deep chambers to find the ruins of Mzulft or related Dwemer mechanisms, though Raldbthar itself famously houses a Great Lift that connects to Blackreach. Progressing through the lower level requires unclogging gears in a bridge mechanism jammed with human bones and scraps.

Items

  • Aetherium Shard: At the end of Raldbthar Deep Market near the exit lift
  • Heavy Armor Skill Book: Orsinium and the Orcs: Raldbthar Deep Market, on a table near a pen or pen-side sleeping area

Tips

  • If the bridge lowering mechanism in Raldbthar Deep Market fails to lower after pressing the button, inspect the four gear assemblies around the room and remove the bones or scraps jammed in their teeth.
  • The button behind the final Centurion in the Deep Market requires the Attunement Sphere to open the stairway leading down into Blackreach.
