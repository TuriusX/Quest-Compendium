Markarth

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Arriving at the city of stone

Markarth is an ancient Dwemer city carved entirely into the side of a mountain, serving as the capital of the Reach. Upon first entering the gates, you immediately witness a public murder committed by a Forsworn agent, which kicks off an extensive conspiracy investigation. Beyond this central conflict, the city houses the Palace of the Kings, Understone Keep, access to ancient Dwemer ruins, and multiple Daedric prince shrines.

Items

  • Aretino Family Heirloom: Given by Aventus Aretino during "Innocence Lost" (not native to Markarth)
  • Mace of Molag Bal MISSABLE: Rewarded by Molag Bal inside the Abandoned House upon completing the quest "The House of Horrors"
  • Ring of Namira MISSABLE: Rewarded by Eola in the Reachcliff Cave after completing the quest "The Taste of Death" starting in the Hall of the Dead
  • Spell Tome: Bound Bow: Located inside the treasury room of Fort Amol (outside the city)
  • Markarth Guard's Shield: Carried by any standard Markarth city guard patrolling the streets

Secrets

  • The Abandoned House locks you in as part of a Daedric encounter with Molag Bal, triggered simply by walking past it with the Vigilant of Stendarr named Tyranus.

Shops and people

  • Arnleif and Sons Trading Company: General goods, weapons, armor, and crafting components.
  • The Hag's Cure: Alchemy ingredients, potions, and apothecary recipes run by Bothela.
  • Silver-Blood Inn: Food, drinks, rented beds, and rumors run by Kleppr and Frabbi.
  • Cindha Mine Smelter / Blacksmith: Smithing services, ingots, ore, and smithing materials run by Moth gro-Bagol inside Understone Keep.

Tips

  • You can sprint forward immediately upon entering Markarth and kill Weylin before he executes Margaret in the market, saving her life and granting a small reward.
  • Do not commit serious crimes in the city until you resolve "The Forsworn Conspiracy," as your bounties and jail logic interact heavily with Cidhna Mine.
