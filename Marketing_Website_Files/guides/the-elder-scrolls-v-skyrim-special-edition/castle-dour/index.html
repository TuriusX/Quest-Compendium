Castle Dour

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Meeting the Jarl of Solitude

Castle Dour is the military and administrative fortress in Solitude, serving as the headquarters of General Tullius and the Imperial Legion in Skyrim. Although Jarl Elisif the Fair resides nearby at the Blue Palace, visiting Castle Dour is essential if you want to join the Imperial Legion or progress in the Civil War storyline. Inside, you can speak directly with General Tullius and Legate Rikke to receive your initial military assignments.

Items

  • The Gold Ribbon of Merit: Sitting on a table in the archery range in the courtyard
  • The Talos Mistake: Located on a table in General Tullius's planning room

Shops and people

  • Beirand: Blacksmith services, weapons, armor, and crafting supplies found at the forge in the courtyard

Tips

  • Speak to Legate Rikke inside the headquarters room to start the quest "Joining the Legion."
  • If you decide to join the Stormcloaks later, you cannot complete Imperial Legion quests initiated here.
