Dawnstar

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Investigating the nightmares for the main quest

Dawnstar is a northern port town plagued by pervasive nightmares afflicting its residents. Speaking with Erandur at the Windpeak Inn begins the Daedric quest "Waking Nightmare" to investigate the source of the affliction at Nightcaller Temple. Resolving the nightmare crisis also helps earn the favor of the local Jarl, which is required to become Thane of the Pale.

Items

  • Potion of Mind Exploration MISSABLE: Carried inside Nightcaller Temple during "Waking Nightmare"
  • Skull of Corruption MISSABLE: At the altar of Nightcaller Temple, obtained only if you kill Erandur
  • The Art of War Magic: On a table inside the White Hall
  • 2920, Sun's Dawn, v2: Inside the Quicksilver Mine on a crate near the entrance

Secrets

  • The Dawnstar hidden vendor chest is buried underground near the entrance of Iron-Breaker Mine, nestled between three rocks.

  • Leigelf: Buys quicksilver ore from the player at the Quicksilver Mine
  • The Mortar and Pestle: Alchemy shop offering potions, ingredients, and an alchemy lab
  • Windpeak Inn: Food, drinks, room rentals, and local rumors

Tips

  • Sparing Erandur at the conclusion of "Waking Nightmare" makes him available as an active follower, but sacrifices the Skull of Corruption.
