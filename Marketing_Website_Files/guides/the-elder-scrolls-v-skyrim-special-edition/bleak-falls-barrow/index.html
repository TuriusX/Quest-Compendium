Bleak Falls Barrow

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Retrieving the Dragonstone for Farengar

Bleak Falls Barrow is an ancient Nordic tomb located in the mountains southwest of Riverwood, explored during the early main quest "Bleak Falls Barrow" and the side quest "The Golden Claw". Players must fight through bandits, frostbite spiders, and leveled draugr to navigate puzzles and reach the final sanctuary chamber. At the end of the dungeon, you defeat a Draugr boss to retrieve the Dragonstone for Farengar Secret-Fire and learn your first dragon shout from a Word Wall.

Items

  • Golden Claw: Looted from the corpse of Arvel the Swift in the webbed chamber
  • Dragonstone: Looted from the boss Draugr Overlord in the final chamber
  • Thief (Skill Book): On a table in the bandit camp area near the entrance of the barrow
  • Scroll of Fireball: In a chest behind the word wall in the final sanctum chamber

Secrets

  • Behind the main Word Wall in Bleak Falls Sanctum, walk along the path behind the curved wall to find an unlocked boss-level chest containing high-tier early-game loot and a scroll.
  • Look closely at the Golden Claw in your inventory screen and rotate it to inspect the palm, which clearly reveals the animal sequence needed to unlock the final puzzle door.

Tips

  • For the first pillar puzzle before the trap gate, rotate the three stones to match Snake, Snake, Whale from left to right to avoid triggering the poison dart wall trap.
  • For the circular door in the Sanctum, use the Golden Claw to turn the outer rings to Bear, Butterfly, Owl from top to bottom before activating the keyhole.
  • Shoot or cast fire spells at the hanging oil lanterns suspended above oil slicks to instantly burn groups of sleeping draugr.
