The College of Winterhold

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Consulting Urag gro-Shub about the Elder Scroll
← All areas in this guide

The Dragonborn travels to the College of Winterhold during the main quest "Elder Knowledge" to find the location of an Elder Scroll. In the Arcanaeum library, the Orc librarian Urag gro-Shub directs the player to Septimus Signus's Outpost after providing relevant books. Gaining initial entry to the College requires demonstrating magic to Faralda at the gate, unless persuaded or bypassing it via high speechcraft.

Enemies
Faralda
Weak to Shock magic

She is an essential NPC who will only attack if the player assaults her at the bridge.

Shops and people
  • Urag gro-Shub: Spell tomes, soul gems, scrolls, and scholarly books
  • Faralda: Destruction magic training, Destruction spell tomes, and robes
  • Phinis Gestor: Conjuration magic training, Conjuration spell tomes, and soul gems
  • Drevis Neloren: Illusion magic training, Illusion spell tomes, and soul gems
  • Tolfdir: Alteration magic training, Alteration spell tomes, and soul gems
  • Colette Marence: Restoration magic training, Restoration spell tomes, and soul gems

Tips
  • If you lack the magicka to cast Faralda's test spell at the gate, she will sell the required spell tome to you for a mere 30 gold.
  • You can bypass Faralda's entry test entirely if you mention being the Dragonborn and demonstrate a dragon shout for her.
