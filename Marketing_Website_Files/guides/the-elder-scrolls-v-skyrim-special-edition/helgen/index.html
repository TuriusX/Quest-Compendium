Helgen

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · The opening escape from the dragon attack

Helgen is the starting town where the player character is brought as an Imperial prisoner to be executed before a sudden dragon attack disrupts the beheading. You must flee the town through collapsing ruins alongside either Ralof, a Stormcloak rebel, or Hadvar, an Imperial soldier. Escaping through Helgen Keep serves as the tutorial for basic combat, stealth, lockpicking, and resource management.

Items

  • Iron War Axe: Inside Helgen Keep, taken off of dead Imperial soldiers or Stormcloaks depending on your guide
  • Mage Hood MISSABLE: On the dead novice mage inside the cages in the torture room of Helgen Keep
  • Novice Robes of Destruction MISSABLE: On the dead novice mage inside the cages in the torture room of Helgen Keep
  • Spell Tome: Sparks MISSABLE: On the floor inside the locked cage containing the dead novice mage in the torture room of Helgen Keep
  • Longbow: On an equipment rack near the target dummies inside the keep's subterranean barracks area
  • Iron Arrow: Sitting in quivers and lying on barrels and tables near the archery targets inside the keep
  • Lockpick: In the knapsack and on the wooden table inside the torture room, and on the torturer's assistant
  • Minor Healing Potion: In various wooden barrels and on shelves throughout Helgen Keep's corridors
  • Minor Magicka Potion: On the table near the cages inside the torture room
  • Minor Stamina Potion: Found on shelves and inside chests in the keep's storage rooms

Tips

  • Choose Hadvar if you want early access to free ingots and gear from the Riverwood blacksmith later, or choose Ralof for early access to free supplies from a lumber mill owner.
  • You can use your companion inside Helgen Keep to safely level up Sneak, One-Handed, Two-Handed, or Archery by repeatedly striking them from behind, as they cannot die or turn permanently hostile here.
  • Crouch and walk slowly behind the sleeping bear in the cave to avoid combat entirely and train your early Sneak skill.
  • Loot all the bodies of the first dead soldiers you encounter so you have a full set of light or heavy armor to equip before moving forward.
