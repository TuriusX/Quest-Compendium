Solitude is the capital of Skyrim and the headquarters of both the Imperial Legion and High King Torygg's court in the Blue Palace. Upon first entering through the main gates, players witness the public execution of Roggvir for his role in Ulfric Stormcloak's escape. Visitors can join the Imperial Legion, enroll in the Bards College, trade with a wide variety of merchants, or investigate local troubles for Jarl Elisif the Fair.

Enemies
Solitude Guard
Steal / drop Guard armor, Solitude Shield

Will arrest, fine, or attack the player if crimes are committed inside Haafingar hold.

Shops and people
  • Radiant Raiment: Fine clothes, enchanted rings, necklaces, circlets, and standard robes
  • Angeline's Aromatics: Alchemy ingredients, potions, and alchemy recipes
  • Bits and Pieces: General goods, clutter, crafting materials, and lockpicks
  • The Winking Skeever: Food, drinks, local rumors, and rentable beds
  • Solitude Blacksmith: Weapons, armor, ingots, and blacksmithing supplies
  • Sybille Stentor: Spell tomes, soul gems, scrolls, and Master-level Alteration training inside the Blue Palace

Tips
  • Investigate the Bards College early to gain passive bonuses, as finishing their introduction quest grants the Gift of the Gab power to level Speech faster.
  • You must assist citizens of Haafingar and complete quests for the Jarl before you are granted the right to purchase Proudspire Manor.
