Whiterun Stables

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Arriving at the city gates for the first time

Whiterun Stables is the primary transportation and equestrian hub situated just down the hill from the main gates of Whiterun. Travelers can purchase a horse, hire a carriage to ferry them to any of Skyrim's major hold capitals, or rent a bed inside the stable house. It serves as an essential staging area before ascending the road to the city gates, especially during your first arrival.

Items

  • Tattered Journal: Inside the stable house on a table

Shops and people

  • Skulvar Sable-Horn: Horses and stabling services
  • Bjorlam: Carriage transport to all nine major hold capitals

Tips

  • If you need to quickly unlock map markers for other major cities early in the game, hire Bjorlam's carriage right outside the stables for a modest fee.
  • A horse purchased here will cost 1,000 gold and will help you traverse mountainous terrain much faster and carry you even while overencumbered.
