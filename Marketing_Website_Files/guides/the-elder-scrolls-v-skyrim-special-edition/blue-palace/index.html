Blue Palace

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Attending the court of Solitude

The Blue Palace serves as the seat of government for Haafingar and the residence of Jarl Elisif the Fair in Solitude. Players visit this location to advance Imperial Legion questlines, report on local disturbances such as Potema's awakening, and petition the Jarl's court to earn the title of Thane. It also provides access to the Pelagius Wing, where the Daedric quest for the Wabbajack begins.

Items

  • Unusual Gem: On the bedside table inside Jarl Elisif's personal quarters
  • Pelagius' Hip Bone: Received from Falk Firebeard inside the main court area to unlock the Pelagius Wing
  • Wabbajack: Rewarded at the conclusion of the quest "The Mind of Madness" within the Pelagius Wing
  • The Wolf Queen, v8: On a shelf in the court wizard Sybille Stentor's personal quarters
  • Biography of the Wolf Queen: In the bedroom of Jarl Elisif

Secrets

  • The Pelagius Wing is completely sealed and locked off until you acquire the key from Falk Firebeard or the maids during the quest "The Mind of Madness."

Shops and people

  • Sybille Stentor: Spells, soul gems, scrolls, and expert-level training in the school of Destruction

Tips

  • Speak to Falk Firebeard to start "The Man Who Cried Wolf," which is a mandatory prerequisite for earning Elisif's trust and becoming the Thane of Haafingar.
  • You can sleep in the guest beds in the basement servants' quarters without trespassing if you need to rest or gain the Well Rested bonus.
