Points of no return

  • Killing Astrid in the Abandoned Shack: Permanently locks out the Dark Brotherhood questline and the achievements "With Friends Like These...", "Bound Until Death", and "Hail Sithis!".
  • Negotiating away The Reach or The Rift during "Season Unending" before completing the Civil War: Permanently locks out the fort capture battle required for the "War Hero" achievement.
  • Siding against Daedric Princes during their respective quests (e.g. sparing Erandur in Dawnstar, taking the Rueful Axe in Falkreath, sparing Sinding without obtaining the ring/hide exploit): Permanently locks out the required count of 15 Daedric Artifacts for the "Oblivion Walker" achievement.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Start unmodded in Helgen to ensure achievements remain active, then travel to Riverwood and clear Embershard Mine and Bleak Falls Barrow.

    Also in this step: Bleak Falls Barrow

  2. Step 2

    Proceed to Whiterun, Dragonsreach, and the Western Watchtower to trigger dragon spawns and initiate core shout progressions.

  3. Step 3

    Travel across Falkreath, Morthal, The Reach, and Karthwasten, clearing Morvath's Lair, Fort Greymoor, Halted Stream Camp, Silent Moons Camp, and Valtheim Towers while gathering crafting supplies and skill books.

  4. Step 4

    Journey to Ivarstead, climb to High Hrothgar, scale the Throat of the World, and recover the horn at Ustengrav.

  5. Step 5

    Visit Solitude, Castle Dour, the Blue Palace, the Pelagius Wing, and the Bard's College, tackling Broken Oar Grotto and side quests.

  6. Step 6

    Explore Markarth and Understone Keep, carefully earning Daedric Artifacts from local quests without failing any artifact conditions.

  7. Step 7

    Head to The Rift and Riften, infiltrating the Honningbrew Meadery to progress the Thieves Guild questline to full restoration.

  8. Step 8

    Travel through Windhelm, Winterhold, and The College of Winterhold, clearing Raldbthar and Mzulft for the mage questline.

  9. Step 9

    Visit Dawnstar, Septimus Signus's Outpost, and Nightcaller Temple, then venture into Blackreach and the Tower of Mzark.

  10. Step 10

    Advance the Civil War campaign through Korvanjund before the main story peace council to guarantee the capture of Fort Sungard or Fort Greenwall.

  11. Step 11

    Progress the main storyline through the Thalmor Embassy, Reeking Cave, Sky Haven Temple, Labyrinthian, Skuldafn Temple, and Sovngarde.

  12. Step 12

    Enter the Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary by obeying Astrid rather than attacking her, then finish the assassination contracts.

  13. Step 13

    Begin the Dawnguard campaign by visiting Fort Dawnguard, Dimhollow Crypt, Castle Volkihar, the Volkihar Ruins, Ancestor Glade, Forgotten Vale, and the Inner Sanctum, unlocking all vampire and werewolf perks.

  14. Step 14

    Travel to Solstheim to explore Raven Rock, Apocrypha, and Tel Mithryn, taming dragons and learning Solstheim shouts.

  15. Step 15

    Build three complete estates via Hearthfire, grind a crafting skill to reset character levels until level 78, and slay a Legendary Dragon.

    Also in this step: Legend
