Pelagius Wing

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Entering the mind of Pelagius

The Mind of a Madman is accessed through the Pelagius Wing of the Blue Palace in Solitude as part of the Daedric quest "The Mind of Madness". Inside Pelagius's psyche, you meet Sheogorath, receive the Wabbajack, and must resolve three symbolic mental struggles representing Pelagius's paranoia, night terrors, and self-doubt. Once all three trials are cleared, speaking with Sheogorath ends the sequence and transports you back to the real world.

Items

  • Wabbajack: Given directly by Sheogorath to complete the trials, and remains in your inventory permanently upon leaving

Tips

  • Your normal equipment is completely removed while inside Pelagius's mind, making the Wabbajack your only tool for every encounter.
  • For the paranoia trial, do not zap the gladiators in the arena; instead, shoot the two bodyguards sitting directly across from you in the viewing stands.
  • For the sleeping Pelagius trial, use the Wabbajack on the sleeping Pelagius to summon each nightmare form, then zap that nightmare immediately to transform it into something benign.
  • In the Confidence trial, alternate firing the Wabbajack between Confidence and Anger so that Confidence grows larger while Anger steadily shrinks.
