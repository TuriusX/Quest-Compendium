Falkreath

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Visiting the hold for the first time

Falkreath is a heavily forested hold capital known for its massive cemetery and gloomy atmosphere. Upon arrival, you are frequently approached by a guard asking about a stray dog, which leads to a Daedric quest. The town also serves as the starting point for acquiring the Lakeview Manor homestead if you satisfy the local Jarl's requests.

Items

  • A Dance in Fire, v6: On a shelf inside Dead Man's Drink
  • De Rerum Dirennis: On a counter inside Grave Concoctions
  • The Black Arts On Trial: In Falkreath Jail, inside the locked personal belongings chest

Secrets

  • The cursed Ring of Hircine can be obtained by visiting the Falkreath Jail underneath the barracks and speaking with Sinding behind the iron bars.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Sinding (Werewolf form)Found transformed inside Falkreath Jail during "Ill Met by Moonlight"; he escapes through the roof rather than engaging the player here.

Shops and people

  • Dead Man's Drink: Inn run by Valga Vinicia offering food, drink, a room to rent, and local rumors.
  • Grave Concoctions: Alchemy shop operated by Zaria selling potions, poisons, ingredients, and alchemy recipes.
  • Gray Pine Goods: General store owned by Solaf stocking assorted weapons, armor, tools, and household goods.
  • Lod: Local blacksmith offering smithing services, raw materials, weapon/armor sales, and the initial prompt to search for Barbas the dog.

Tips

  • Avoid attacking the stray dog Barbas outside of town, as he is an essential, immortal companion tied to the Daedric quest "A Daedric Bestfriend."
  • If you own the Hearthfire content (included in Special Edition), speaking to Jarl Siddgeir and completing his favors will allow you to purchase the land plot for Lakeview Manor from his steward.
