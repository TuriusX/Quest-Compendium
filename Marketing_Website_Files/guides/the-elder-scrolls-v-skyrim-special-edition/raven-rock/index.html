Raven Rock

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Arriving at the settlement

Raven Rock is the primary Dunmer settlement on the island of Solstheim, serving as the initial port of arrival from Skyrim via the Northern Maiden. Upon landing, the player is questioned by Second Councilor Adril Arano and can begin exploring the town to uncover the mysterious trance affecting its inhabitants. The town offers multiple services, local quests, and access to the nearby Earth Stone and Raven Rock Mine.

Items

  • The Lusty Argonian Maid, Folio Edition: Underwater in the flooded section behind Glover Mallory's house
  • Shadowmark Cache: Inside the barrel marked with the Thieves Guild shadowmark near Glover Mallory's house

Secrets

  • Glover Mallory's basement contains unique Thieves Guild armor and supplies, accessible only after earning his key by retrieving the Ancient Nordic Pickaxe.

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
Ash SpawnAsh Salts, various ores, and weaponsEncountered just outside the town borders and near the town's farm fields during attacks.

Shops and people

  • The Retching Netch: Food, drink, alchemy ingredients, and inn lodging offered by Geldis Sadri.
  • Glover Mallory: Blacksmithing gear, weapons, armor, and smithing materials.
  • Fethis Alor: General goods, soul gems, and miscellaneous supplies.
  • Milore Ienth: Potions, poisons, and alchemy ingredients sold at her outdoor stall.

Tips

  • Assist Captain Veleth just outside the southern entrance of town to immediately start the "March of the Dead" questline.
  • Do not cleanse the Earth Stone just north of the docks until you learn the "Bend Will" shout from the main Dragonborn questline.
