Riften

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Seeking information on Esbern

You arrive in Riften tracking down the Blades scholar Esbern during the main quest "A Cornered Rat." You must question locals or Thieves Guild members to uncover his hiding spot beneath the city. The search leads you through the canal level into the Ratway and deeper into the Ratway Warrens.

Items

  • Beggar (Pickpocket Skill Book): On a table inside The Ratway Warrens

Secrets

  • Unlocking the Ratway Vault shortcut is done by lowering a raised wooden bridge near Gian the Fist's den using a lever on the upper platform.

Shops and people

  • Keerava: Rents rooms, sells food and basic tavern drinks, and provides rumors about Esbern
  • Brynjolf: Sells miscellaneous stolen goods after progression, or points you toward the Ratway for a price or task
  • Tonilia: Thieves Guild fence who buys stolen goods, offers lockpicks, and upgrades your Thieves Guild Armor

Tips

  • If you do not want to help Brynjolf with his market distraction to gain information, you can persuade or brawl with Dirge or Keerava to learn where Esbern is hiding.
  • Listen carefully through the reinforced door in the Ratway Warrens; Esbern will not open it until you mention Delphine or the Dragonborn prophecy.
