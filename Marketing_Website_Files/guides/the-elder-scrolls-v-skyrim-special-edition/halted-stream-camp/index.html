Halted Stream Camp

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Clearing the bandit camp for iron ore

Halted Stream Camp is an outlaw mine and camp located north of Whiterun, overrun by poachers hunting mammoths. The primary goal is to fight through the fortified surface defenses, enter the subterranean mine to defeat the bandit chief, and harvest the rich vein network inside. It is widely considered the best early-game location to mine massive quantities of iron ore.

Items

  • Spell Tome: Transmute Mineral Ore: On the bandit chief's workbench next to the boss chest inside the mine
  • Halted Stream Mine Key: Carried by the bandit chief or sitting on the table near him
  • Poacher's Axe: Mounted on a wooden chopping block inside the mine cavern
  • Heavy Armor Skill Book: Hallgerd's Tale: Sitting next to a chest inside the main underground cavern area

Secrets

  • Hidden spike pit trap: Stepping on the concealed tripwire along the mine's main ramp will release a swinging spiked wall or drop the player into spiked floor stakes.

Tips

  • Bring a pickaxe or find one near the mine entrance to harvest the 16 iron ore veins scattered throughout the tunnels.
  • Use the Transmute Mineral Ore spell obtained here to turn all mined iron ore into silver, and then gold, to easily level Smithing and Alteration early.
