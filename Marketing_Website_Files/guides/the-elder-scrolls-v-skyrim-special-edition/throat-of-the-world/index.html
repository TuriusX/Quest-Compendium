Throat of the World

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Meeting Paarthurnax to learn Dragonrend

You travel to the highest peak in Skyrim by using the Clear Skies shout to bypass the lethal blizzard blocking the path from High Hrothgar. At the summit, you meet the ancient dragon Paarthurnax, who reveals the history of Alduin and teaches you words of power. Your primary objective here is to converse with him to uncover how to learn the Dragonrend shout.

Items

  • Notched Pickaxe: Embedded near the very top of the peak above the word wall
  • Malachite Ore Vein: Located around the rocky summit near the top of the mountain
  • Ebony Ore Vein: Located around the rocky summit near the top of the mountain

Secrets

  • Climb to the absolute highest rock formation above the word wall to find the unique enchanted Notched Pickaxe along with high-tier ore deposits.

Tips

  • Use all three words of the Clear Skies shout on the magical mist barriers whenever the wind starts damaging your health.
  • Make sure you talk to Paarthurnax after learning Fire Breath so he can offer you a meditation bonus to enhance one of your shouts.
