Skuldafn Temple

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Traveling to the portal to Sovngarde

The Dragonborn arrives at Skuldafn on the back of Odahviing during the main quest "The World-Eater's Eyrie." You must fight through the ancient temple ruins and exterior courtyards filled with high-level Draugr and dragons. At the summit, you defeat the Dragon Priest Nahkriin to open and enter the portal leading to Sovngarde.

Items

  • Nahkriin MISSABLE: Carried by the Dragon Priest Nahkriin near the portal
  • Dragon Priest Staff MISSABLE: Held by Nahkriin, who uses it to seal the portal to Sovngarde
  • Diamond Claw MISSABLE: Carried by a high-level Draugr inside the interior temple section
  • Storm Call Word Wall MISSABLE: Located in the exterior courtyard before entering the second interior section

Tips

  • Skuldafn cannot be returned to once you jump through the portal into Sovngarde, making all loot in the area permanently missable.
  • If you sprint directly at Nahkriin as soon as you reach the summit, you can kill him before he reaches his staff, avoiding the lightning storm he casts.
  • The puzzle door in the interior uses the Diamond Claw, and the combination from top to bottom is Fox, Moth, Dragon.
