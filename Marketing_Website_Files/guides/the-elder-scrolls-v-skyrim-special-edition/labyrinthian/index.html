Labyrinthian

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Retrieving the Staff of Magnus

The Dragonborn travels to the ancient ruins of Labyrinthian during the College of Winterhold questline to obtain the Staff of Magnus. The expedition requires using the Torc of Labyrinthian to unlock the dungeon, navigating through spectral projections of Savos Aren's past expedition, and battling waves of undead. At the deepest sanctum, the player confronts the Dragon Priest Morokei to claim both his mask and the staff.

Items

  • Staff of Magnus: Carried by Morokei in the final chamber of Labyrinthian
  • Morokei: Worn by the Dragon Priest Morokei in the final chamber
  • Spell Tome: Equilibrium: Found on a pedestal next to an Arcane Enchanter within Labyrinthian
  • Word Wall (Slow Time): Found in the chamber shortly before descending into the final depths
  • Ancient Helmet of the Unburned: Inside an unlocked chest behind a locked gate in the Tribune area

Secrets

  • The spectral voice that drains Magicka throughout the dungeon is Morokei speaking through the ruins in the Dragon language, switching to the player's tongue as they get closer.

Tips

  • Bring plenty of Magicka potions or enchanted gear with Magicka cost reduction, as Morokei periodically drains your entire Magicka bar as you traverse the ruins.
  • Do not attack Morokei directly while the blue barrier is active; eliminate the two Enthralled Wizards on the platforms above to drop his shield.
  • Lightning resistance gear or potions are very helpful in the final chamber, as Morokei primarily attacks using potent shock spells from the Staff of Magnus.
