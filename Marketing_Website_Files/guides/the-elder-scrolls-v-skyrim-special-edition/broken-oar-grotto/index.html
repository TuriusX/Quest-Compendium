Broken Oar Grotto

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Clearing the bandit hideout near Solitude

Broken Oar Grotto is a vast, flooded sea cave located north of Solitude that serves as a stronghold for the Blackblood Marauders. You explore the elevated wooden walkways, scuttled ships, and cavern networks to eliminate the bandit gang and their leader. It is frequently visited during the quest "Lights Out!" or for radiant bandit-clearing bounties.

Items

  • Skill Book: Cherim's Heart: In the master bedroom area inside the shipwreck cabin, resting on a small table near a bed
  • Broken Oar Grotto Key: Carried by the bandit chief Captain Hargar
  • Scimitar: Carried by several Blackblood Marauders and found placed on various tables around the camp

Secrets

  • An underwater chest lies submerged beneath the raised wooden platforms near the center pool of the cavern.
  • An unlocked boss chest is located on an elevated ledge behind Captain Hargar's quarters, accessible by climbing up the wooden scaffolding.

Tips

  • Watch out for tripwires connecting rockfall traps along the narrow wooden ramps leading up the scaffolding.
  • Sneak archers can pick off most bandits from the entrance platform high above the water before descending.
