Dragonsreach

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Meeting the Jarl and the court wizard

Dragonsreach is the palace of Jarl Balgruuf the Greater, located at the highest point of Whiterun. The Dragonborn arrives here to report the dragon attack at Helgen to the Jarl and is subsequently introduced to the court wizard, Farengar Secret-Fire. Farengar then tasks the player with retrieving the Dragonstone from Bleak Falls Barrow to assist in his research.

Items

  • Unusual Gem: Jarl Balgruuf's bedchamber on the end table next to the bed
  • Elven Bow: In the display case inside the Jarl's Quarters (Atronach's Basin area)
  • The Whispering Door Key: Carried by Jarl Balgruuf or Farengar Secret-Fire

Secrets

  • The Whispering Door is located down the stairs in the basement; listening to the door begins the Daedric quest for the Ebony Blade once the required level is reached.

Shops and people

  • Farengar Secret-Fire: Spell tomes, soul gems, scrolls, robes, and enchanting supplies

Tips

  • You can use the Alchemy Lab and Arcane Enchanter located inside Farengar's study for free at any time.
  • Approaching the Jarl for the first time prompts his housecarl, Irileth, to draw her weapon; stand your ground and speak to her to proceed without initiating combat.
