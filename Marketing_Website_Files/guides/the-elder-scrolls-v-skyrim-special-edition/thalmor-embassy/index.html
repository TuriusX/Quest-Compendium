Thalmor Embassy

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Infiltrating the party to find information on dragons

During the quest "Diplomatic Immunity", the Dragonborn infiltrates an elite reception hosted by Ambassador Elenwen to investigate the Thalmor's connection to the returning dragons. With the help of Malborn inside, you sneak away from the party to recover your smuggled equipment, eliminate Thalmor guards, and search the embassy's private chambers and dungeon. After obtaining documents implicating Esbern, you escape the compound through the Reeking Cave below the dungeon.

Items

  • Unusual Gem: On a table in Elenwen's Salon (moved to Reeking Cave in later patches if missed)
  • Dossier: Delphine MISSABLE: Inside an interrogation chest in the dungeon beneath Elenwen's Salon
  • Dossier: Esbern MISSABLE: Inside an interrogation chest in the dungeon beneath Elenwen's Salon
  • Dossier: Ulfric Stormcloak MISSABLE: Inside the interrogation chest in the dungeon beneath Elenwen's Salon
  • Trapdoor Key MISSABLE: Carried by one of the Thalmor guards who enters the dungeon

Tips

  • Give Malborn all necessary combat gear, lockpicks, and potions in Solitude before the party, as you will have no access to the rest of your inventory until the quest ends.
  • If playing as an Altmer (High Elf), wearing a set of Hooded Thalmor Robes found inside the facility allows you to walk past several Thalmor guards without initiating combat from a distance.
  • You can cause a distraction at the party by giving a drink to Razelan or talking to Erikur, allowing Malborn to lead you to the kitchen unnoticed.
