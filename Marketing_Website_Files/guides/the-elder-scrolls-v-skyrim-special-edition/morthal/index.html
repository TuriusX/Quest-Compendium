Morthal

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Investigating the local mystery

Morthal is a foggy, isolated marsh town troubled by a suspicious house fire that killed a woman and child. Speaking with Jarl Idgrod Ravencrone initiates an investigation that reveals a local vampire conspiracy threatening the entire settlement. You must uncover the conspirators, clear the town's graveyard, and lead a militia to eliminate the master vampire lurking in a nearby lair.

Items

  • 2920, Sun's Dusk, v11: Inside Highmoon Hall, on a shelf behind the throne
  • Response to Bero's Speech: Inside Thaumaturgist's Hut, on a shelf behind the counter
  • The Horror of Castle Xyr: Inside Falion's House, sitting on a bedside table
  • Hroggar's Journal: Found in a basement container inside the burned house

Shops and people

  • Thaumaturgist's Hut (Lami): Alchemy ingredients, potions, recipes, and training in Alchemy
  • Falion: Soul gems, spell tomes, enchanting supplies, and the ritual service to cure Vampirism
  • Moorside Inn (Jonna): Food, drinks, rentable bed, and local rumors

Tips

  • Waiting until nightfall at the burned-down house reveals the ghost of Helgi, which progresses the investigation.
  • You can pick the lock on Alva's cellar while she is out wandering the town during the day to obtain evidence without immediate bloodshed.
  • Bring a filled Black Soul Gem to Falion if you contract full Vampirism and wish to be cured through his dawn ritual.
