Honningbrew Meadery

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Investigating the sabotage during the Thieves Guild questline

As part of the Thieves Guild quest "Dampened Spirits," you travel to the Honningbrew Meadery to poison the mead supply and frame owner Sabjorn. You must clear out a skeever infestation in the basement and tunnels, poison the brewing vat in the boilery, and retrieve incriminating documents from the private office. Once Sabjorn is arrested by Commander Caius during the tasting, the meadery is transferred to Maven Black-Briar and becomes the Black-Briar Meadery West.

Items

  • Honningbrew Decanter: On a top shelf behind an Expert-locked door in Sabjorn's upstairs office
  • Hamelyn's Journal: Next to the alchemy lab in Hamelyn's chamber inside the meadery basement tunnels
  • Three Thieves: On top of a dresser in the upstairs bedroom of the meadery

Secrets

  • The Honningbrew Decanter is a unique collectible item that can be turned in to Delvin Mallory in the Ragged Flagon for gold and a permanent display in the Cistern.

Tips

  • Crouch and maintain stealth when entering the boilery through the tunnel hatch to avoid alerting the guard stationed right by the vat.
  • You can convince Sabjorn with a Persuasion check to pay you half of your fee upfront before you head down to handle the pest problem.
  • Bring Cure Disease potions or be prepared to pray at a shrine, as the high density of skeevers in the basement tunnels makes catching Ataxia very likely.
