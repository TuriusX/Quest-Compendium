Apocrypha

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Entering Hermaeus Mora's realm during the Dragonborn questline

Apocrypha is the Daedric realm of Hermaeus Mora, entered by reading Black Books found across Solstheim. Navigating its shifting corridors of books and acidic seas is required to learn shouts, defeat Miraak, and unlock powerful passive abilities. You progress through chapters by activating Scryers and reading subsequent books until you reach the final reward.

Items

  • Black Book: Waking Dreams: Found in the Temple of Miraak, transporting you to the final chapter of Apocrypha

Tips

  • Falling or stepping into the dark, churning waters of Apocrypha causes continuous damage over time, so keep your distance from the edges.
  • You can return to previously completed Black Book realms at any time while in Solstheim by reading the book again in your inventory.
  • At the end of Waking Dreams, you can spend Dragon Souls to reset and reclaim perk points invested in any skill tree.
