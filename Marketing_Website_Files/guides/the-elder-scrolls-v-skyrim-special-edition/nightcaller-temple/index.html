Nightcaller Temple

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Investigating the nightmares in Dawnstar

Nightcaller Temple is an ancient ruin overlooking Dawnstar that houses the source of the town's recurring nightmares. Players enter the temple alongside Erandur during the Daedric quest "Waking Nightmare" to destroy the Skull of Corruption. Progressing through the temple requires drinking Vaermina's Torpor to experience the Dreamstride and bypass magical barriers.

Items

  • The Dreamstride: On a pedestal in the temple's laboratory
  • Vaermina's Torpor: Located on a counter in the temple's laboratory area
  • Skull of Corruption MISSABLE: Resting on the central altar in the inner sanctum
  • Alchemy skill book: Mannimarco, King of Worms: On an alchemy lab table in the laboratory
  • Speech skill book: 2920, Second Seed, v5: On a small shelf near a bed in the living quarters

Tips

  • Let Erandur take the initial aggression when entering rooms, as multiple enemies often awaken at once.
  • You must kill Erandur when commanded by Vaermina if you want to claim the Skull of Corruption for the Oblivion Walker achievement; sparing him makes him available as a follower instead.
