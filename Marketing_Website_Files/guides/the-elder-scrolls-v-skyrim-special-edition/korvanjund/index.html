Korvanjund

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Retrieving the Jagged Crown for the Civil War

Korvanjund is an ancient Nordic ruin where players travel alongside either Imperial soldiers or Stormcloak rebels during the Civil War questline. Your objective is to battle through waves of draugr to retrieve the Jagged Crown for your chosen faction's leader. Along the way, you must navigate traps, solve two ancient puzzle mechanisms, and defeat a powerful draugr boss sitting upon a throne.

Items

  • Ebony Claw: On the floor next to the dead draugr near the puzzle door in Korvanjund Halls
  • The Jagged Crown: Looted from the head of the Draugr Deathlord (or boss draugr) sitting on the throne in the final chamber, Korvanjund Crypt
  • Word Wall (Slow Time): In the final chamber behind the throne in Korvanjund Crypt

Secrets

  • Skill Book (Jorunn the Skald-King) | Located on a chest hidden in a small alcove above the main hall in Korvanjund Temple, reached by walking along the upper stone ledge | missable: no

Tips

  • The combination for the puzzle door in the Halls is Fox, Moth, Dragon from top to bottom, which can be seen by inspecting the palm of the Ebony Claw in your inventory.
  • In the second puzzle room with the raised portcullis, head up the stone stairs to the upper balcony to find the lever that opens the gate, but be prepared for draugr to ambush you when pulled.
  • Let your faction allies take the frontline aggro against the tough Draugr Deathlords in the final room to avoid taking heavy shout damage.
