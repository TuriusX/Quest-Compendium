Fort Dawnguard

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Joining the Dawnguard

Fort Dawnguard is the secluded headquarters of the resurrected vampire-hunting faction located within Dayspring Canyon. You come here to meet the faction leader, Isran, to enlist in their ranks and receive your first assignment. Exploring the fort grants early access to specialized anti-vampire weapons, armor, and training.

Items

  • Crossbow: Given to you by Durak outside the fort, or found lying on crates in the courtyard and forge area
  • Steel Bolt: Placed on crates near the archery targets in the courtyard, or looted from training caches
  • Dawnguard Armor: Set out on tables and weapon racks throughout the armory and forge sections inside the keep
  • Dawnguard War Axe: Resting on weapon racks and tables inside the main hall and forge
  • Dawnguard Warhammer: Leaning against weapon racks in the armory area

Shops and people

  • Isran: Offers Master-level training in Heavy Armor
  • Agmaer: Offers novice-level training once recruited fully
  • Celann: Offers Master-level training in One-Handed

Tips

  • Grab the free Crossbow and bolts from the shooting targets outside before speaking to Isran to practice your ranged combat immediately.
  • You must discover Dayspring Canyon via a hidden cave entrance located southeast of Riften, near the Morrowind border pass.
  • The fort starts off derelict and largely empty, but it gradually cleans up and gains more vendors and craftspeople as you progress the Dawnguard questline.
