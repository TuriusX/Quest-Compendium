Blackreach

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Searching for the Elder Scroll

Blackreach is a vast, bioluminescent underground cavern connecting the Dwemer ruins of Alftand, Mzinchaleft, and Raldbthar. During the quest "Discerning the Transmundane" and the main quest "Elder Knowledge," you must navigate this subterranean realm to reach the Tower of Mzark. Inside the Tower of Mzark, you solve an ocular Dwemer puzzle to transcribe the Lexicon and retrieve the Elder Scroll (Dragon).

Items

  • Elder Scroll (Dragon): Tower of Mzark, retrieved from the central mechanism after solving the Lexicon puzzle
  • Runed Lexicon: Tower of Mzark, retrieved from the receptacle pedestal after aligning the lenses with the puzzle buttons
  • Crimson Nirnroot: Growing throughout Blackreach near water and glowing mushrooms, with dozens of harvestable nodes

Secrets

  • Summoning the hidden dragon Vulthuryol by aiming the Unrelenting Force shout directly at the massive artificial glowing orange orb hanging above the Debate Hall

Tips

  • Harvesting your first Crimson Nirnroot automatically starts the quest "A Return to Your Roots," which requires collecting 30 samples scattered across the cavern.
  • You can unlock three great lifts—Alftand, Mzinchaleft, and Raldbthar—from inside Blackreach to provide permanent, direct shortcuts to the surface.
  • Crimson Nirnroots emit a loud, distinct chimes sound when you are close to them, making them easier to locate in the dark.
