Morvath's Lair

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Investigating the vampire threat in Morthal

Morvath's Lair is a cavern located north of Morthal serving as the hideout for the master vampire Movarth Piquine and his brood. During the quest "Laid to Rest," the Dragonborn explores this cave to eliminate Movarth and eradicate the vampiric threat facing Morthal.

Items

  • 2920, Sun's Dawn, v2: on a wooden shelf in Movarth's personal quarters
  • Immortal Blood: on a table next to a bed in the sleeping area
  • Movarth's Boots: on the floor near the bed in Movarth's quarters

Enemies

Tips

  • Bringing fire damage weapons or casting fire spells will exploit the natural weakness shared by Movarth and his vampire followers.
  • Sneaking through the initial tunnel allows you to bypass or eliminate the first thrall before she alerts the rest of the cavern.
