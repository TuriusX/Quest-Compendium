Mzulft

The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition guide · Searching for the Oculory for the College of Winterhold

Mzulft is a vast Dwemer ruin investigated during the College of Winterhold quest "Revealing the Unseen". Players must navigate through automations and Falmer to locate the Synod expedition and the lost Oculory. Once at the device, you must focus light beams using heat and cold spells to reveal the location of the Staff of Magnus.

Items

  • Focusing Crystal: Looted from the Falmer Warmonger/Gloomlurker in the final chamber before the Oculory
  • Tome: Flames: On a table in the Oculory control room
  • Tome: Frostbite: On a table in the Oculory control room
  • Mzulft Key: On the body of the dying Synod researcher Gavros Plinius near the entrance

Tips

  • Bring Frost and Fire spells to calibrate the Oculory, though spell tomes for Flames and Frostbite are provided on a console table in the room if you lack them.
  • Striking the Oculory's lens apparatus with Frost spells contracts the rings, while Fire spells expand them.
  • Dwarven scrap metal found here can be smelted into Dwarven Metal Ingots, making Mzulft one of the best locations for gathering Smithing materials.
