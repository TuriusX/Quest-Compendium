Sector 5 Slums

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 8

Cloud arrives in the Sector 5 Slums with Aerith after falling from Sector 1, visiting her home and the local town. Cloud takes on various odd jobs for the local Leaf House community and townspeople to pass the time. The chapter culminates in an encounter with Rude of the Turks before Cloud prepares to return to Sector 7.

Items

  • Silver Staff: Purchased from the Moogle Emporium in the Children's Secret Hideout
  • Prayer Materia: Aerith's starting equipment when she joins your party
  • MP Up Materia: Near the gate leading to Aerith's house along the central path

Secrets

  • Yellow Materia behind the locked metal gate with a dragon emblem cannot be reached until Chapter 14 when you acquire the key from the Corneo's Secret Stash quest.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Hedgehog PieIceCasts Fire spells and uses Bounce; hitting them with Ice magic interrupts their attacks.
SmoggerLightningExplodes upon defeat with Self-Destruct, releasing a haze that can inflict poison and silence.
RudeWindHand-to-hand combat specialist who blocks physical attacks easily; use Aerith's magic to pressure him and avoid his shockwaves.
ToxiratIceCapable of inflicting poison with its bites; weak to basic Ice spells.

Shops and people

  • The Moogle Emporium (Moggie): Special goods such as the Silver Staff, music discs, and key items in exchange for Moogle Medals
  • Weapons Shop: Sells Buster Sword, Iron Blade, and Guard Stick along with basic defensive bangles

Tips

  • Complete all six odd jobs available in the Center District to unlock a special scene with Aerith and gain access to the "Whack-a-Box" minigame.
  • Equipping the Elemental Materia paired with an Ice Materia on Cloud's weapon makes clearing beasts around the outskirts much faster.
