Sector 7 Slums

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 3

Cloud arrives at the Sector 7 Slums with Tifa after the bombing mission, rents an apartment from Marle, and spends time completing odd jobs to earn gil. The player is introduced to the neighborhood watch, Chadley's battle intel system, and side quests throughout the settlement. The chapter concludes after dealing with troublemakers at Seventh Heaven and resting up for the next operation.

Items

  • Ice Materia: Purchased from the Item Shop in Sector 7 Slums after completing the quest "Chadley's Report"
  • First Aid Materia: Rewarded by Chadley after completing Battle Intel Report 1
  • Cleansing Materia: Sold by the Sector 7 Slums vending machine next to the Beginner's Hall

Secrets

  • The music disc "Tifa's Theme" can be collected by interacting with the jukebox inside Seventh Heaven.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Shops and people

  • Chadley: Battle Intel reports, Materia development, and VR battle simulations
  • Sector 7 Weapons Shop: Iron Blade for Cloud and basic gear upgrades
  • Sector 7 Item Shop: Potions, Phoenix Downs, Antidotes, and basic offensive magic materia

Tips

  • Complete all available side quests in this chapter to unlock an extra discovery event and a special scene with Tifa in her apartment.
  • Equip the Assess Materia immediately upon receiving it from Chadley, as scanning enemies is required to progress his Battle Intel challenges.
