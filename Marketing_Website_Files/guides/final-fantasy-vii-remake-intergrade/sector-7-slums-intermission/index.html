Sector 7 Slums (INTERmission)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · INTERmission

Yuffie arrives in the Sector 7 Slums to rendezvous with Avalanche HQ members Zhijie and Nayo before launching their infiltration mission. While waiting for Zhijie to secure counterfeit ID cards, Yuffie can explore the residential district, challenge residents to Fort Condor, and retrieve lost flyers for the Happy Turtle campaign. The visit ends when Yuffie heads to the train station after meeting her partner, Sonon.

Items

  • Boomerang: Reward for defeating Turtle Kid in Fort Condor
  • Wind Materia: Inside a chest found along the eastern pathway near the pillar support
  • First Aid Materia: Reward for defeating Chadley in Fort Condor

Secrets

  • Happy Turtle Flyer 1 | Found on the wall of the neighborhood watch building on the southeastern edge of town | missable: no
  • Happy Turtle Flyer 2 | Located on the side of a crate behind the wooden barricade near the scrap boulevard | missable: no
  • Happy Turtle Flyer 3 | Posted on the wall inside the apartment building near the Stargazer Heights inn | missable: no
  • Happy Turtle Flyer 4 | Floating attached to a stray balloon near the north edge of town; popped by throwing Yuffie's shuriken | missable: no
  • Happy Turtle Flyer 5 | Trapped under a pile of boxes on a balcony overlooking the central square | missable: no
  • Happy Turtle Flyer 6 | Guarded by a sleeping dog in the scrapyard; sneaked past by only moving while the dog sleeps | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Old Snapper: Exchanging discovered Happy Turtle Flyers for the Magnify Materia
  • Sector 7 Weapons Store: Broadsword, Iron Blade, Steel Pinions, 4-Point Shuriken, and accessory upgrades
  • Item Store: Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-cleansing items

Tips

  • Take time to play Fort Condor against all available players in the slums to earn exclusive boards, units, and AP-boosting rewards before leaving for the train station.
  • Yuffie's ranged shuriken throw can be used in the field to smash crates and retrieve out-of-reach Happy Turtle flyers.
