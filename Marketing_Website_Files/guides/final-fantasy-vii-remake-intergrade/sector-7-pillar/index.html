Sector 7 Pillar

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 12

Cloud, Tifa, and Aerith arrive at the Sector 7 Pillar to find Shinra attempting to collapse the plate and wipe out Sector 7. Aerith is sent to rescue Marlene from Seventh Heaven while Cloud and Tifa fight their way up the spiral stairs of the pillar to reinforce Avalanche. The ascent culminates in a multi-stage battle on the top deck against Reno and Rude to stop the plate release sequence.

Items

  • Ether MISSABLE: In a chest on the Pillar 1F near the base access stairs
  • Hi-Potion MISSABLE: Inside a Shinra supply box on the 2F stairs platform
  • Elixir MISSABLE: In a treasure chest tucked behind debris on the Pillar 6F landing

Tips

  • Equip the Steadfast Block and Elemental-Lightning support materia to reduce Reno's fast electro-shock counter damage.
  • Cast Sleep on Rude during the duo boss fight on the roof so you can focus entirely on defeating Reno first.
  • Use Wind spells or Aerith's Tempest to ground Helitroopers before they can pin your party down on narrow stairwells.
