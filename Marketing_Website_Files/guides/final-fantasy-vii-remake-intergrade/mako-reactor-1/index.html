Mako Reactor 1

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 1

Cloud Strife joins the eco-terrorist cell Avalanche on a mission to infiltrate and destroy Mako Reactor 1 in Midgar. You fight your way through Shinra security forces and descend into the reactor's core to plant a bomb. After defeating the Scorpion Sentinel, you must escape the facility before the detonation timer reaches zero.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: In a treasure chest just past the first platform after getting off the train
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: In a treasure chest located along the catwalks before descending the reactor core elevator
  • Ether MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the lowest level of the reactor core near the bomb placement point
  • Healing Materia: Automatically equipped on Cloud from the start of the chapter

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Scorpion SentinelLightningBoss of the chapter; raises a barrier around its tail periodically and counters attacks from the front with a field.

Tips

  • Switch Cloud to Punisher Mode and hold the guard button against melee-attacking human enemies to automatically counter their strikes.
  • When fighting the Scorpion Sentinel, hide behind the debris piles to avoid taking massive damage from its Tail Laser attack.
  • Choosing the 20-minute timer for the bomb detonation instead of the 30-minute option awards an extra gift from Barret in Chapter 2.
