Sector 7 Sewers

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 10

Dropped through a trapdoor beneath Don Corneo's mansion, Cloud, Tifa, and Aerith must navigate the monster-infested sewer system beneath Midgar. The party solves water drainage puzzles and operates sluice gates to lower water levels and traverse the aqueducts. The chapter culminates in an escape toward the Sector 7 train graveyard after defeating Don Corneo's pet beast.

Items

  • Poison Materia: On the ground along the main walkway shortly after regaining control of Cloud at the start of the chapter
  • Feathered Gloves: Inside a purple chest behind a sluice gate in Aqueduct 1, reachable after turning the valve wheel to drain the water
  • Warding Materia: In the southern area of Aqueduct 1, found in a tunnel behind a gate after draining the aqueduct
  • Cloak of Deception: Found in a treasure chest in the Interlinking Aqueduct section
  • Key to the Sewer system (Corneo Vault Key): Not obtainable in this chapter, but the graffiti-marked locked door here can be unlocked later in Chapter 14

Enemies

Tips

  • Equip the Star Pendant accessory or prepare Cleansing/Healing materia to quickly cure the Poison and Toad status ailments inflicted by native sewer creatures.
  • Target Abzu's horns with Fire spells to pressure him quickly and break them to limit his offensive capabilities.
  • Use Aerith's ranged magic to easily stagger Scissorclaws, as their front guard deflects Cloud's physical sword swings.
