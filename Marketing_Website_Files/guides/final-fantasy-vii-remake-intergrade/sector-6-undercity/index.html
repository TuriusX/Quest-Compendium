Sector 6 Undercity

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 9

Cloud and Aerith arrive in the Sector 6 Undercity, primarily exploring the bustling entertainment district of Wall Market. The main objective is to gain access to Don Corneo's mansion to rescue Tifa by earning auditions through influential figures like Chocobo Sam, Madam M, or Andrea Rhodea. Along the way, players can participate in the Colosseum tournaments, dance at the Honeybee Inn, and complete branching side quests based on their dialogue choices.

Items

  • Music Disc 22: Cosmo Canyon: Purchased from a jukebox near the Food Stall area at the far south of Wall Market
  • Music Disc 10: Honeybee Inn: Purchased from the item shop inside Wall Market
  • Music Disc 17: Farm Boy: Obtained by speaking to a cowgirl NPC on the upper walkway north of Chadley
  • Music Disc 8: Under the Rotting Pizza: Interacted with at a jukebox on the street south of the Wall Market entrance
  • Music Disc 29: The Midgar Blues: Received from the karaoke singer inside the bar across from the gym
  • Aerith's Arcane Scepter: Rewarded upon completing the side quest "A Dynamite Body" or "Shears' Counterattack"
  • Cloud's Buster Sword Hardedge: Purchased from the Wall Market Weapon Shop

Secrets

  • The side quests you unlock in Chapter 9 depend on dialogue choices made with Chocobo Sam and Madam M, splitting into either Sam's questline (featuring "The Party Never Stops") or Madam M's questline (featuring "The Price of Thievery").

Shops and people

  • Wall Market Weapon Shop: Sells the Hardedge greatsword for Cloud alongside various basic accessories and consumables
  • Wall Market Item Shop: Sells healing items, status curatives, and Music Disc 10

Tips

  • Complete the optional Colosseum challenge battles as soon as Aerith is available in your party, as you can unlock their Level 2 Limit Breaks (Ascension and Planet's Protection) before heading into Don Corneo's mansion.
  • Equip elemental materia corresponding to all four main elements (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind) between Cloud and Aerith before the Corneo Colosseum matches to exploit Hell House's shifting elemental shields.
