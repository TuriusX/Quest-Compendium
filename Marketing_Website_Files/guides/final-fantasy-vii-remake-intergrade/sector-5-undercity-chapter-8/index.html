Sector 5 Undercity (Chapter 8)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 8

Cloud arrives in the Sector 5 Slums with Aerith after traversing the Church and the outskirts. While waiting out the heat, Cloud completes odd jobs for the local community, plays minigames, and assists the Neighborhood Watch. After finishing their tasks and returning to Aerith's house, the duo prepares to head toward Sector 7 before encountering Reno and Rude.

Items

  • MP Up Materia: Near the entrance path to Aerith's house on the ground behind some bushes
  • Magic Up Materia: Reward for scoring 30,000 points in Whack-a-Box
  • Silver Staff: Purchased from the Moogle Emporium for 2 Moogle Medals
  • Whistlewind Scarf: Purchased from the Moogle Emporium for 3 Moogle Medals
  • Prayer Materia: Equipped on Aerith upon joining the party permanently

Secrets

  • The Moogle Emporium vendor Moggie unlocks inside the Children's Secret Hideout after obtaining the Moogle Membership Card during the side quest "A Verified Hero."

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Hedgehog PieIceOften casts defensive magic; pressure it by hitting it with Ice spells.
SmoggerLightningExplodes upon defeat with Self-Destruct, emitting a harmful smog cloud you should dodge away from.

Shops and people

  • Moogle Emporium: Sells the Silver Staff, Whistlewind Scarf, the Graveyard Key, music discs, and various consumable items in exchange for Moogle Medals.
  • Sector 5 Weapons Shop: Sells equipment including Buster Sword, Iron Blade, Nail Bat, and Guard Stick.
  • Sector 5 Item Shop: Sells basic restorative items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Antidotes.
  • Sector 5 Materia Shop: Sells magic and support Materia including Healing, Cleansing, Fire, Ice, and Lightning.

Tips

  • Complete all six available side quests in this chapter to unlock an extra discovery event with Aerith outside her house before going to sleep.
  • Use Whack-a-Box with Cloud's Infinity's End or Operator mode attacks against the large 1,500-point boxes to break them quickly before time expires.
