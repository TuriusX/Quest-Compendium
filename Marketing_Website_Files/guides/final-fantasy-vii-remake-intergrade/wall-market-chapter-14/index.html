Wall Market (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

Returning to Wall Market in Chapter 14, Cloud, Tifa, and Barret search for a way to ascend the plate to reach Shinra Headquarters while helping locals with various odd jobs. The party visits Don Corneo's mansion to acquire a grapple gun and key information regarding the route up. Once preparations are complete and all desired side quests are wrapped up, the party departs through the ruins to scale the wall.

Items

  • Music Disc 12. Fight On!: Purchased from the Souvenir Shop inside the Colosseum lobby
  • Music Disc 24. Wutai: Purchased from the Wall Market urban development vendor near the south entrance
  • Music Disc 30. Stand Up: Received from an NPC in the narrow alley to the left of the Honeybee Inn
  • Subversive Karate Skills Vol. III MISSABLE: Rewarded by completing the "Secret Medicine" side quest
  • Sharpshooter's Companion Vol. III MISSABLE: Rewarded by completing the "Tomboy Bandit" side quest
  • The Art of Swordplay Vol. III MISSABLE: Obtained by completing the "Malicious Goons" side quest

Secrets

  • Corneo's Vault Key can be obtained from Kyrie at the Sector 5 church via the "Tomboy Bandit" questline, unlocking the treasure vault located inside Corneo's mansion as well as two other vaults across the slums.

Shops and people

  • Corneo Colosseum: Offers new solo and team arena challenges for Cloud, Tifa, and Barret that grant powerful accessories and manuals
  • Chadley: Resets Materia development, sells completed battle intel Materia, and offers the Bahamut VR combat simulation once all other battle intel reports are cleared
  • Weapon Store: Sells late-game weapons for Cloud, Barret, and Tifa if they were not acquired earlier
  • Item Store: Sells general restoration items, status remedies, and Phoenix Downs

Tips

  • Clear the new Colosseum combat trials immediately to obtain character-specific skill manuals before venturing above the plate.
  • Complete the "Pull-ups" minigame against Andrea Rhodea at the gym to earn Tifa's strength-boosting manuscripts and Champion Belt accessory.
  • Finishing all Chapter 14 side quests triggers an extra scene near the gate leading to the wall before you leave the sector.
