Sector 5 Slums (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

Cloud, Barret, and Tifa prepare to infiltrate Shinra Headquarters and can explore the Sector 5 Slums to complete several major side quests. Key locations including the Center District, Aerith's House, and the Children's Secret Hideout serve as quest hubs before heading over the wall. You can also acquire special key items such as Corneo's Vault Key and open locked vaults located around the outskirts.

Items

  • Music Disc 23 (Descendant of Shinra): Sector 5 Undercity Station, purchased from the Item Shop owner
  • Music Disc 24 (Wutai): Sector 5 Slums Center District, purchased from an NPC in the area southwest of the Center District
  • Corneo's Vault Key: Dropped at the hideout after defeating the Beastmaster and Hellhound in the side quest "Tomboy Bandit"
  • Prayer Materia: Inside the Steel Mountain Corneo Vault, unlocked with Corneo's Vault Key

Secrets

  • Inside the Sector 5 Steel Mountain Corneo Vault, opening the locked gate reveals ruby and gold tiara treasures along with a Prayer Materia and numerous Moogle Medals.

Shops and people

  • Sector 5 Slums Weapons Shop: Sells weapons, armor, and accessories including character-specific gear available in Chapter 14.

Tips

  • Equip Warding Materia paired with Subversion on your melee fighters to guard against instant-death attacks when facing the Tonberry.
  • Complete all Sector 5 Slums side quests before interacting with the grappling hook point in the Wall Market, as scaling the wall advances the story past the point of no return for this chapter.
