Wall Market

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 9

Cloud and Aerith arrive in the entertainment hub of the Sector 5 and Sector 6 slums to rescue Tifa from Don Corneo's mansion. To earn an audience with the Don, Cloud must gain the approval of the trio who run the district by winning in the underground Colosseum and auditioning for Corneo. The player explores the vibrant streets, participates in mini-games, and completes odd jobs to secure proper outfits for the audition.

Items

  • Music Disc 22: Cosmo Canyon: Purchased from a jukebox at the far end of the alleyway behind the Colosseum
  • Music Disc 10: Honey Bee Inn: Purchased from the Honey Bee Inn Item Shop Girl standing outside the venue
  • Music Disc 8: The Case for Chocobo: Purchased from the vending machine inside the Corneo Colosseum lobby
  • Music Disc 29: The Midgar Blues: Received by speaking to the karaoke singer performing at the bar in the lower avenue

Secrets

  • The dress that Cloud, Aerith, and Tifa wear during Don Corneo's audition depends on your choices; Cloud's dress is dictated by which specific side quests you complete for Chocobo Sam or Madame M, while Tifa's dress is chosen by your dialogue answer to her in Chapter 3, and Aerith's dress depends on how many Chapter 8 side quests you finished.

Shops and people

  • Chadley: Resets weapon upgrades and sells developed Materia based on completed Battle Intel
  • Corneo Colosseum Souvenir Shop: Sells restorative items, throwing items, and basic combat consumables
  • Wall Market Item Shop: Sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-curing items
  • Wall Market Materia Shop: Sells basic magic Materia such as Fire, Ice, Lightning, and Healing
  • Wall Market Weapon Shop: Sells character weapons including the Hardedge for Cloud

Tips

  • Equip Bio or Poison Materia before entering the Colosseum fight against Hell House, as the boss is vulnerable to Poison and takes sustained damage regardless of its shifting elemental barriers.
  • You cannot complete all side quests in Wall Market in a single playthrough, as the game branches into either Chocobo Sam's questline or Madame M's questline depending on your choices upon entering the district.
  • Win the squat mini-game at the training gym to earn accessory rewards and trophy progress.
