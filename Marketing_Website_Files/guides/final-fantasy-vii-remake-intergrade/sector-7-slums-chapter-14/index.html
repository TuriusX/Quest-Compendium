Sector 7 Slums (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

Cloud, Tifa, and Barret return to the devastated ruins of the Sector 7 Slums to search for a way to reach the upper plate. While exploring the debris, the party can undertake side quests, confront remnants of Shinra forces, and discover access points to the underground testing facility.

Items

  • Music Disc 24: Wutai: Purchased from the Gramophone / Item Shop near the entrance of the Sector 7 Slums debris

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
RingmawIceFound around the Sector 7 Undercity Station and ruins; attacks primarily in packs.

Shops and people

  • Wandering Merchant: Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and assorted basic restorative items

Tips

  • Use the Chocobo Carriage fast travel system unlocked in Chapter 14 to move quickly between the Sector 7 Slums and other districts across the lower plate.
  • Check the collapsed pillar zone near the former Seventh Heaven location to advance side quest objectives tied to the Sector 7 collapse.
