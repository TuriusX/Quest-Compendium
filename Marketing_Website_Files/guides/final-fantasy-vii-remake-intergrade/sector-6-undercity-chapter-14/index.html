Sector 6 Undercity (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

In Chapter 14, Cloud, Tifa, and Barret return to the Sector 6 Undercity as a central hub before attempting to climb to the upper plate. Wall Market and the surrounding ruins offer numerous new side quests, Corneo Vault locations, and minigames before progressing to the Sector 7 gate. Completing key preparations here allows you to earn important late-game materia, equipment, and access to Don Corneo's stashes.

Items

  • Music Disc 12. Fight On!: Purchased from the Souvenir Pharmacist at the Colosseum in Wall Market
  • Music Disc 24. Wutai: Purchased from the NPC near the Urban Advancement District fence in Wall Market
  • Music Disc 30. Stand Up: Received from the NPC in the alleyway to the left of the Honeybee Inn
  • Ruby Ring: Found in the chest inside Corneo's secret vault in the Collapsed Expressway
  • Diamond Pin: Found inside the chest within the Corneo Vault located in the Collapsed Expressway
  • Circlet: Rewarded for completing the "Wavering Heart" pull-up mini-game in the Wall Market gym

Secrets

  • Complete all Chapter 14 side quests before entering the gate at the Sector 7 ruin wall to spawn a letter from the Guardian Angel on the path, awarding an Elemental Materia.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

TonberryFought during the "Malicious Goons" side quest; evade Chef's Knife instantly as it causes instant death.

Shops and people

  • Chadley: Sells reset skill services, VR Mission battles including Bahamut, and advanced materia development
  • Wall Market Weapon Shop: Sells mythril weapons and high-tier basic gear for Cloud, Tifa, and Barret

Tips

  • Equip Warding Materia paired with Subversion on your melee fighters when fighting Tonberry to significantly mitigate its instant-death attacks.
  • Don Corneo's Vault Key must be obtained from Kyrie in the Church before you can open the vault in the Collapsed Expressway.
