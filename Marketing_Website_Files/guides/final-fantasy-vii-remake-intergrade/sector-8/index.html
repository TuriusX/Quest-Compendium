Sector 8

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 2

Cloud escapes the aftermath of the Sector 1 reactor bombing by navigating through the residential and commercial areas of Sector 8 while avoiding or fighting Shinra security forces. Along the way, he encounters Aerith selling flowers and experiences hallucinatory visions of Sephiroth. The chapter concludes with a running battle through the streets that ends when Cloud leaps onto a departing train to escape.

Items

  • Healing Materia: Given automatically by Aerith during your conversation with her
  • HP Up Materia: Found behind a fence in an alleyway near the Loveless Street area before heading up to the station
  • Deadly Dodge Materia: Found lying on the ground along the main escape route shortly after battling Shinra troopers on the city streets
  • Iron Blade: Awarded automatically at the end of the chapter upon boarding the train

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Security OfficerFireStandard Shinra infantry who attack with rifles and bayonets; easy to pressure with magic or Punisher mode.
Guard DogIceFast-moving canine units that can leap and interrupt attacks; susceptible to Ice magic and counterattacks.
GrenadierFireTroopers that throw high-damage explosives from a distance; best taken out first before they disrupt melee combos.
Riot TrooperFireShield-bearing units that block direct physical attacks; stagger them with magic or strike from behind to bypass the shield.
The HuntsmanFireA reinforced Riot Trooper mini-boss who commands squad members and uses a riot shield with high physical defense; stagger him with Fire spells or attacks from behind.

Tips

  • Use Cloud's Punisher Mode guard against Security Officers and Guard Dogs, as it automatically parries their melee attacks and delivers a high-damage counter.
  • When fighting Riot Troopers, do not strike their shields frontally with basic Operator attacks, as Cloud will bounce off; use Fire magic to instantly pressure them or dodge behind them.
