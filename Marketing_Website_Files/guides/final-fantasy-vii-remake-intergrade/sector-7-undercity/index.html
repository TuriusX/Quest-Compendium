Sector 7 Undercity

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 3

Following the Sector 1 bombing mission, Cloud arrives in the Sector 7 Undercity to rest at the Stargazer Heights apartments and reunite with Tifa. Cloud spends time earning money and building his reputation in the neighborhood by completing local odd jobs. The chapter concludes after resolving matters with the neighborhood watch, dealing with mysterious hooded figures, and preparing for the next assignment.

Items

  • Music Disc 3: Tifa's Theme: Purchased from the jukebox inside Seventh Heaven
  • Music Disc 1: The Prelude: Purchased from the Item Shop in the Sector 7 Undercity
  • Music Disc 27: Hip Hop de Chocobo: Acquired from an NPC standing next to a boombox near the Sector 7 residential entrance
  • Music Disc 4: Barret's Theme: Purchased from the Item Shop near the train station
  • Ice Materia: Purchased from the Item Shop owner after completing his introductory dialogue
  • First Strike Materia: Purchased from Chadley after completing Battle Intel Report 1
  • Auto-Cure Materia: Purchased from Chadley after completing Battle Intel Report 2

Secrets

  • The Seventh Heaven dartboard minigame allows Cloud to climb to first place on the leaderboard, which rewards a Luck Up Materia given by Wedge near the end of Chapter 4.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
WereratFireCommon pests found around the scraps and storage areas of the slums.
Gorging WereratFireA larger, more aggressive variant fought during an odd job in the scrap yard.
DoomratsFireFast-moving rodents encountered in side quests that attack in packs.
WrathhoundIceA feral beast fought during a neighborhood watch contract that enters an enraged state when low on health.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Restorative items, status cures, and the Ice Materia
  • Weapon Shop: Buster Sword upgrades, Iron Blade, and defensive armor bangles
  • Chadley: Sells unique Materia developed through completing Battle Intel Reports

Tips

  • Play the darts minigame inside Seventh Heaven early to clear the top score before leaving the bar to progress the main story.
  • Use the Assess Materia immediately in every encounter to quickly fulfill Chadley's initial Battle Intel requirements.
