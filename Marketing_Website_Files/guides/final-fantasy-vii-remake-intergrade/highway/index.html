Highway

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 18

Cloud and the party escape the Shinra Building and engage in a high-speed vehicle battle across the unfinished Midgar highway. After clearing the road pursuit, the group dismounts at the edge of the highway to make their final stand against the Whispers and Sephiroth. You must configure your party's equipment and materia before stepping through the portal at the end of the road.

Items

  • Ether: In a chest by the rest bench just before the final portal at the end of the highway
  • Mega-Potion: In a chest near the roadside railing before stepping through the portal

Shops and people

  • Vending Machine: Restorative items, music discs, and combat consumables right next to the final rest bench before the singularity

Tips

  • You can completely skip the opening highway motorcycle sequence on subsequent playthroughs via the pause menu if you have already cleared it once.
  • Equip Elemental Materia paired with an elemental spell on your armor for the Sephiroth encounter, as his later phases rely heavily on elemental magic that can be resisted, negated, or absorbed.
