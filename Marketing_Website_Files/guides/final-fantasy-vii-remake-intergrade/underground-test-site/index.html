Underground Test Site

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 13

The Underground Test Site is a covert Shinra research facility situated beneath Sector 7 where Barret and Tifa search for Wedge. Players navigate multi-tiered experimental labs, operate mechanical consoles to clear blocked paths, and battle mutated Shinra specimens. The section culminates in a multi-phase boss fight against the prototype bioweapon Failed Experiment.

Items

  • Mythril Claws: in a purple chest on the B3 floor of the research facility, accessible after using Barret to shoot down a hanging cage obstacle
  • Mega-Potion: in a chest on B4 near the elevator hub area

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
BugabooIceSmall flying mutated specimens that attack in swarms and fall quickly to Blizzard spells.
Unknown EntityFireSlime-like bioweapons resistant to physical damage that split upon defeat.
Armored VariantLightningA heavily armored experiment that charges players; attacking its back quickly drives up its stagger gauge.
Failed ExperimentIceBoss encounter that commands swarms of Unknown Entities while periodically releasing devastating wide-area electrical outbursts.

Tips

  • Equip Elemental Materia paired with Ice on Barret or Tifa's armor or weapon to deal massive damage and mitigate elemental attacks throughout the test site.
  • During the Failed Experiment encounter, focus on defeating the smaller Unknown Entities first to leave the boss vulnerable to stagger and interrupt its heavy charges.
