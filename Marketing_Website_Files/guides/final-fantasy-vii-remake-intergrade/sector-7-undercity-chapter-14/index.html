Sector 7 Undercity (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

In Chapter 14, Cloud, Tifa, and Barret return to the Sector 7 Undercity to search for a way to climb above the plate to Shinra Headquarters. The settlement is in ruins following the plate drop, but key locations like the train station and the underground test site can be accessed. Exploring the area allows the party to uncover remaining stashes, complete the Subterranean Menace odd job, and locate gear left behind.

Items

  • Gil Toss Materia: Found deep inside the Shinra Underground Test Site reached via Sector 7
  • Chocobo Sam's Delivery Service (Music Disc 24): Purchased from the item shop or vendor in the Sector 7 Undercity area

Secrets

  • Johnny's Lost Wallet can be retrieved in the train station ruins by defeating the Kyuvild (Tonberry) encountered during the related Chapter 14 questline.

Tips

  • Equip Ice materia and Steadfast Block when heading into the Underground Test Site to handle the Type 0-Behemoth efficiently.
  • Visit Marle near the wreckage of the Stargazer Heights apartments to check in on the refugees and trigger quest progress.
