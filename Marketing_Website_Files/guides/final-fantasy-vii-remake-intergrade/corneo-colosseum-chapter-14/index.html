Corneo Colosseum (Chapter 14)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 14

Cloud returns to Wall Market's underground arena in Chapter 14 to enter new combat challenges, including a storyline-relevant side quest match. Players speak with Gatekeeper Johnny or the arena attendant to access unlocked solo, duo, and trio battles. Victory in the special exhibition challenge is required to progress the "Tomboy Bandit" side quest and retrieve Johnny's stolen wallet.

Items

  • Johnny's Purse: Received from Kyrie after defeating the Beastmaster and Hellhound in the Colosseum challenge
  • Corneo Vault Key: Received from Kyrie alongside Johnny's purse after winning the special match

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
BeastmasterFireAccompanies the Hellhound during the special exhibition challenge and uses melee whip attacks.
HellhoundBlizzardA two-headed beast that ignites with red and purple flames; extinguishing its flames with Ice magic pressures it.

Shops and people

  • Colosseum Gatekeeper: Unlocks new Chapter 14 battle challenges for Cloud, Barret, and Tifa, offering combat rewards including character manuals

Tips

  • Equip Blizzard-linked Elemental Materia in your weapon or ensure multiple party members have Ice Materia equipped before starting Kyrie's exhibition match against the Hellhound.
  • Take the elevator down into the waiting room to use the bench and vending machine to fully restore HP and MP between tournament matches without spending items.
