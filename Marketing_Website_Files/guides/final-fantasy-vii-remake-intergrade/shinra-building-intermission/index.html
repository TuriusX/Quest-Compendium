Shinra Building (INTERmission)

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · INTERmission

Yuffie and Sonon infiltrate the Advanced Weaponry division and the underground research lab beneath the Shinra Building in search of the "ultimate materia." The pair fights through experimental security forces, faces off against Scarlet piloting the Crimson Mare, and confronts Nero in the Deepground depths. The mission ends with a sequence of boss encounters, narrative reveals, and Yuffie's escape from the facility.

Items

  • Steal Materia: Found on a desk inside the weapon testing observation booth on B15
  • AP Up Materia MISSABLE: In the Central Core room after completing the holographic pipe-crossing puzzle
  • Skill Master Materia MISSABLE: Awarded for completing the advanced combat simulation challenge on the training floor
  • Cacstar: Automatically retrieved in the B14 inventory storage room if downloaded via DLC

Secrets

  • Complete the Shinra Box Buster challenge on the Advanced Weaponry training floor with a high score to unlock unique accessories including the Soldier's Boots.

  • Shinra Vending Machine: Standard restorative items, Hi-Potions, Ethers, Phoenix Downs, and the music disc for "The Happy Turtle"

Tips

  • Infuse Yuffie's shuriken with Lightning Ninjutsu before engaging mechanical units and Scarlet's Crimson Mare to rapidly fill their stagger gauges without spending MP.
  • During the battle against Nero, do not step into the pools of abyssal darkness spread across the floor, as they inflict heavy continuous damage and slow your movement.
  • Use Sonon's Synergized Art of War frequently on staggered bosses to output massive burst damage and significantly boost the stagger damage multiplier.
