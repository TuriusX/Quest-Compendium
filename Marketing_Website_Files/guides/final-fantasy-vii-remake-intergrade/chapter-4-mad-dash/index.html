Chapter 4: Mad Dash

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Cloud, Tifa, and Jessie infiltrate the Shinra plate for a raid.
← All areas in this guide

Cloud joins Jessie, Biggs, and Wedge on an undercover motorcycle trip to the upper plate's Sector 7 residential area to acquire blasting agents from Jessie's family home. After evading Roche and Shinra security forces on the highway, Cloud secures the supplies and helps fend off a sudden monster attack back in the Sector 7 Slums. The chapter concludes late at night after Cloud visits Jessie's house and investigates suspicious intruders near the outskirts of town.

Enemies
Roche
Weak to Fire

Agile Soldier 3rd Class fought during the highway bike minigame and later in a 1-on-1 duel on foot; punishing his lunges with Punisher Mode counterattacks fills his stagger gauge quickly.

Sweeper Prototype
Weak to Lightning

Mechanical security boss equipped with arm cannons; striking its arms cripples its offensive power and pressures it faster.

Guard Dog
Weak to Ice

Aggressive beasts accompanying security personnel; susceptible to ice attacks and standard interruptions.

Elite Security Officer
Weak to Fire

Upgraded trooper carrying a riot shield; magic attacks bypass the shield completely.

Tips
  • During the motorcycle section, conserve Cloud's Spinning Slash special ability for large clustered mobs or during Roche's attack phases when he pulls alongside you.
  • Switching to Cloud's Punisher Mode and guarding right as Roche charges during the duel automatically counters his physical blade attacks for easy stagger buildup.

Not listed for this area: secrets, shops and people.
