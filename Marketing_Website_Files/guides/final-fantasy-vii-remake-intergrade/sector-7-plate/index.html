Sector 7 Plate

FINAL FANTASY VII REMAKE INTERGRADE guide · Chapter 4

Cloud accompanies Jessie, Biggs, and Wedge to the upper plate residential district of Sector 7 on a side mission before the next reactor bombing. Cloud must break into Jessie's parents' house to retrieve a blasting agent keycard while the others distract her mother, and then fight off Shinra security forces dispatched to the warehouse district. Afterward, Cloud returns to the Sector 7 Slums with Avalanche after a duel with Roche.

Tips

  • Switch to Punisher Mode and guard just before Roche attacks to parry his melee strikes and build his pressure gauge rapidly.
  • In Jessie's house, enter the room on the left quietly and interact with the suit hanging in the back to retrieve the maintenance keycard.
