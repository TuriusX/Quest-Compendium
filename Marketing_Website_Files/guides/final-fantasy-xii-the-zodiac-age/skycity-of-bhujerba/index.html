Skycity of Bhujerba

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party arrives via airship

The party arrives in the floating metropolis of Bhujerba via airship to seek an audience with Marquis Ondore. To attract the attention of Ondore's informants, Vaan must spread rumors around town about being Captain Basch fon Ronsenburg while avoiding the city guards. Once the notoriety gauge is filled, the story advances toward the Lhusu Mines and the Dreadnought Leviathan.

Shops and people

  • Bashketi's Armaments: Sells weapons, shields, and various armor pieces for the party
  • Rithil's Protectives: Sells helms, light armor, and mystic armor
  • Balthier's Techniques: Sells technicks such as First Aid, Poach, and Charge
  • Mait's Magicks: Sells spells including Cure, Blind, Protect, Shell, and elemental black magic
  • The Cloudborne: Sells basic recovery items and potions in the tavern

Tips

  • During the rumor-spreading sequence, shout near Bhujerban Guide NPCs and book-reading civilians to raise the notoriety gauge significantly faster.
  • Avoid shouting near Imperial guards or Parijan city watchmen during the rumor minigame, as they will cause the notoriety percentage to drop.
