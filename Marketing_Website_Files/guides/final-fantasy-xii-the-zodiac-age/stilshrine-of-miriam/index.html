Stilshrine of Miriam

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party searches for the Sword of Kings

The party explores this ancient, icy temple in the Paramina Rift to obtain the sacred Relic of Raithwall known as the Sword of Kings. Navigating the interior requires solving statue-alignment puzzles, interacting with pedestals, and defeating ancient guardians to unlock lower chambers. The trek culminates in a trial against the Esper Mateus before the sword can be claimed from the Relic Chamber.

Items

  • Sword of Kings: Relic Chamber, received as a story reward after defeating Mateus

Secrets

  • Turning all three massive statues in the shrine (Cold-Distance, Walk of Reason, and Steel Council) toward the central colossus causes the colossal statue to raise its sword, opening the way into the Ward of the Sword-King.
  • Touching the Pedestal of Dawn in the Ward of Measure while equipping the Dawn Shard activates the Way Stone and teleports the party to the Ward of Velitation; interacting without the shard spawns hostile Marionettes.

Tips

  • Cast Thunder spells or use Lightning elemental weapons against Mateus and its Ice Azer minions to defeat them quickly.
  • Equip light or mystic gear rather than heavy armor when fighting Vinuskar to avoid being crippled by the chamber's magnetic field.
  • Equipping Ice Shields or gear that reduces Ice-elemental damage will significantly mitigate the spells used by Mateus and the native monsters.
