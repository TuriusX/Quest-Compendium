The Dalmasca Westersand is a vast desert region stretching west of Rabanastre, connecting the city to the Ogir-Yensa Sandsea and the Zertinan Caverns. While traversing it, the party encounters roving desert predators and hazardous sandstorms that alter which foes spawn. Players must navigate its dunes carefully to complete early-game hunts and progress westward toward the Tomb of Raithwall.

Enemies
Dive Talon
Weak to weakness: EarthSteal / drop what can be stolen: Feather of the Flock

High-level flying bird enemy perched atop rocks that easily wipes out an unprepared early-game party.

Gnoma Entite
Weak to weakness: WaterSteal / drop what can be stolen: Earth Crystal

A powerful elemental that only appears during a sandstorm and turns hostile if magic is cast nearby.

Dustia
Weak to weakness: HolySteal / drop what can be stolen: Book of Orgain

A teleporting undead spirit that appears in the Corridor of Sand when a party member's HP drops below ten percent.

Wolf
Weak to weakness: EarthSteal / drop what can be stolen: Wolf Pelt

Common low-level predator roaming the Galtea Downs near the Rabanastre exit.

Sleipnir
Weak to weakness: IceSteal / drop what can be stolen: Pointed Horn

A flaming equine fiend encountered roaming the western dunes.

Tips
  • Do not use magic spells when near the Gnoma Entite, as elemental spirits automatically aggro against magical attacks and will quickly destroy an under-leveled party.
  • Keep away from perched Dive Talons until your party has gained ranged capabilities and significantly higher levels, as their physical strikes hit extremely hard early on.
  • You can exit and immediately re-enter an area before Dustia's death animation finishes to respawn it infinitely without healing yourself.
