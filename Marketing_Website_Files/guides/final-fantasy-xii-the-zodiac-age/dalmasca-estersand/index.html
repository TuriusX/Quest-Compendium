Dalmasca Estersand

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · Vaan hunts rats in the desert

The Dalmasca Estersand is an expansive desert region situated just outside the east gate of Rabanastre, split into northern and southern sections by the River Nebra. It serves as Vaan's first field destination where he learns basic combat, takes on the Red&Maw hunt, and later embarks on the Barheim Passage return route.

Tips

  • Avoid attacking the giant Wild Saurian strolling through the early zones until your party has leveled up significantly and acquired better gear.
  • Crossing from the South Bank Village to the North Bank requires progressing the Desert Patient sidequest later in the game to restore the ferry service.
