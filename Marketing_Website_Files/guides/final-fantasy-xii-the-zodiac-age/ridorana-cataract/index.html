Ridorana Cataract

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels to the Pharos

The party arrives at the Ridorana Cataract via the airship Strahl after the events in Balfonheim to reach the giant tower known as the Pharos. You must navigate across the ruins and cliffs surrounding the massive oceanic whirlpool while fighting off dangerous high-level monsters. Reaching the western edge leads directly to the entrance steps of the Pharos at Ridorana.

Items

  • Nihopalaoa: Footfalls of the Past, in a treasure chest on the southern pathway

Tips

  • Equip Reflect Rings or cast Dispel if fighting Mirrorknights with magic, as their permanent Reflect status will bounce offensive spells back onto your party.
  • Watch out for hidden traps scattered densely throughout the narrow paths of the Colosseum and Echoes from Time's Garden by keeping Float active on your party.
