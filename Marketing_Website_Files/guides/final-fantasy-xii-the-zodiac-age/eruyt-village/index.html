Eruyt Village

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party meets Fran's people

The party arrives in Eruyt Village within the Golmore Jungle searching for Mjrn, Fran's sister, to learn how to dispel the magick barriers blocking the path. After being rejected by the reclusive Viera matriarch Jote, the party must journey to the Ozmone Plain and the Henne Mines to locate Mjrn. Returning here with Mjrn dispels the village's cold reception and allows the story to advance deeper into the jungle.

Items

  • Haste: Found in a treasure chest in the Fane of the Path area

Secrets

  • The Dewdrop Pebble sidequest becomes available after events in the Henne Mines, requiring you to collect pebbles around the village to trade with a Viera for items.

Shops and people

  • Tetran: Sells weapons, armor, accessories, and restorative items at the village entrance
  • Moogle Cartographer: Sells the Golmore Jungle map and Ozmone Plain map

Tips

  • Speak to the Viera named Mjrn in the Spiritwood after clearing the Henne Mines to receive the Lente's Tear, which is required to pass the barriers in Golmore Jungle.
  • A save crystal is located near the village entrance, providing a safe zone to heal and save without the threat of hostile enemies.
