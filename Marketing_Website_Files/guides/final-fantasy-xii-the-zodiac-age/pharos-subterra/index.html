Pharos - Subterra

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party explores the depths of the tower

Pharos - Subterra is an optional, high-level dungeon located beneath the Pharos at Ridorana, accessible via the central elevator after the main story events here are complete. Players must navigate three dark tiers, defeat powerful fiends, and place collected Black Orbs into pedestals to restore light and unlock deeper areas. Delving to the very bottom triggers an encounter with the powerful Esper Shadow Fiend, Hashmal's counterpart Famfrit, and ultimately leads to the elite Mark, Ixion, and the superboss Shadowseer.

Items

  • Sage's Ring: In a treasure chest located in the Penumbra North-Exterior section
  • Megalixir: Found in an elusive treasure chest within the Abyssal tier

Secrets

  • Massive Black Orbs called "Huge Black Orbs" periodically spawn in the outer rings of each tier, which grant a large quantity of regular Black Orbs when absorbed to speed up lighting the area pedestals.
  • Defeating all the enemies in the lower tier chambers and inserting the exact required count of Black Orbs opens the path to the Unknown tier, where the Esper Phoenix and the Shadowseer await.

Tips

  • Equipping Ribbon accessories or casting Cleanse and Esuna is vital, as the regular enemies inflict lethal status ailments like Stop, Confuse, and Doom.
  • Bring weapons or ammunition enchanted with the Holy element to exploit the elemental vulnerability shared by many of the undead fiends dwelling in these tiers.
