Barheim Passage

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · Vaan and Balthier escape the prison

After escaping the Nalbina Dungeons, Vaan, Balthier, and Fran must navigate the subterranean Barheim Passage to return to the surface. You must maintain power levels by defeating Battery Mimics so that the lights stay on while fighting through dangerous undead and subterranean creatures. At the deepest point of the path, you face the boss Mimic Queen before finally emerging into the Dalmasca Estersand.

Items

  • Escutcheon: Treasure pot in the Garamsythe Connecting Passage section

  • Burrogh: Sells basic items such as Potions, Antidotes, and basic ammunition, and gives you the Tube Fuse to start the power generator

Tips

  • Keep the charge gauge above 50% to prevent hordes of aggressive undead monsters like Skeletons and Specters from spawning in the dark.
  • Focus Balthier on long-range attacks with his gun to pick off Battery Mimics safely while Vaan and Fran handle crowd control.
  • The Mimic Queen is weak to Ice magic, making Fran or any character equipped with the Blizzard spell particularly effective.
