Rabanastre

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The game begins here with Vaan

Rabanastre is the bustling capital city of Dalmasca and serves as the central hub throughout the majority of your journey. Players begin here controlling Vaan, taking on early marks, visiting local markets, and preparing for expeditions into the surrounding deserts and waterways. It connects directly to the Dalmasca Estersand, Dalmasca Westersand, Giza Plains, and Lowtown.

Shops and people

  • Migelo's Sundries: Potions, Phoenix Downs, and basic curative items
  • Amal's Weapon Store: Early-game daggers, swords, bows, and other introductory armaments
  • Bashketi's Armors: Light, heavy, and mystic armor for early character builds
  • Yamoora's Magicks: Introductory spells including Cure, Fire, Thunder, and Blizzard
  • Burrogh's Technicks: Basic technicks such as First Aid and Steal
  • The Clan Provisioner: Special items and equipment unlocked as your Clan Centurio rank increases

Tips

  • Visit the Sandsea tavern early to check the Notice Board for new Hunt marks posted by locals.
  • Enter the Clan Centurio hall in the North End to speak with Montblanc for bounty rewards and guild perks.
  • A fast travel system called Moogle Teleportation lets you warp instantly between major gates and plazas within the city for free.
  • Head down into Lowtown through the stairwells to access the Garamsythe Waterway and Old Dalan's place.
