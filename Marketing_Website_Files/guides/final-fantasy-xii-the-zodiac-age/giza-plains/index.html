Giza Plains

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · Vaan travels to the nomad village

Vaan travels south from Rabanastre to Giza Plains during the Dry season to obtain a Sunstone for Old Dalan. In the Nomad Village, he meets Masyua and Penelo, then tracks down the young boy Jinn to learn how to create the stone. Once the Shadestone is fully charged at the dark crystals across the plains, Vaan returns with the Sunstone to proceed with the main quest.

Items

  • Shadestone: Given by Jinn at the Crystal Glade
  • Sunstone: Created by charging the Shadestone at giant Dark Crystals across the plains
  • Amethyst Ring: Reward from Masyua after delivering the completed Sunstone

Tips

  • Completely steer clear of the two Werewolves in the Starfall Field during your initial visit, as their physical attacks will kill Vaan in a single hit.
  • Charge the Shadestone by interacting with the glowing Dark Crystals marked on your map until the energy gauge reaches 100%.
