Paramina Rift

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Mt Bur-Omisace

The party traverses this vast, snow-covered mountain pass to reach the holy grounds of Mt Bur-Omisace. Navigating the freezing terrain involves fighting native wildlife, undead, and ice elementals while dealing with shifting blizzard weather. The area also houses optional side paths that connect to the Stilshrine of Miriam later in the story.

Secrets

  • Defeating the rare game Anchag requires entering the Karydine Glacier zone and clearing out all other enemies first to make it spawn.

Tips

  • Cast Fire spells or equip fire-elemental weapons, as the vast majority of native beasts in the rift are weak to Fire.
  • Avoid casting spells in zones where an Ice Elemental floats nearby, as it will immediately aggro and devastate underleveled parties.
  • Equip Rose Corsage or other silence-nullifying gear on mages, as several monsters cast Silence.
