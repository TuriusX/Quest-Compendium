Draklor Laboratory

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party infiltrates the facility

The party infiltrates the imperial research facility in the heart of Archades to track down Doctor Cid and the nethicite. Progression involves using card keys and bulkheads across the 66th, 67th, and 68th floors to reach the laboratory's highest level. The infiltration culminates in a multi-phase boss fight against Doctor Cid and his autonomous rooks.

Items

  • Lab Access Card MISSABLE: Obtained automatically from the dead imperial researcher on the 67th floor
  • Systems Access Card MISSABLE: Obtained inside room 6811 West on the 68th floor
  • Hastega Technick MISSABLE: Chest in room 7002 East on the 70th floor

Secrets

  • Draklor Laboratory cannot be re-entered once completed, so all chests, items, and rare steal opportunities are permanently missable.

Tips

  • Cast Dispel immediately on Doctor Cid and the Rooks to remove their protective buffs like Reflect, Shell, and Protect.
  • Focus all physical attacks on the Rooks first during the boss fight to prevent them from continuously curing Doctor Cid.
  • Toggle the color-coded bulkhead controls (Red and Blue) on each floor to manipulate the security doors and reveal paths to the elevators.
  • Equip armor with high defense and use the Decoy technick to redirect Doctor Cid's high-damage firearm attacks toward your tank.
