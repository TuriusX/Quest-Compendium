Lhusu Mines

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party searches for Lente's Tear

The party enters the Lhusu Mines beneath Bhujerba to rescue Penelo and confront the bounty hunter Ba'Gamnan. After finding Penelo and Lamont, the team is ambushed by Ba'Gamnan's gang and must flee the mines back to the entrance. Surviving this sequence allows the party to secure the Magicite shard and proceed to the Dreadnought Leviathan.

Items

  • Site 11 Key: Found on the ground at the Phon Coast Hunter's Camp near the Bhujerban man who fell from the sky

Tips

  • Hold the flee button immediately after the cutscene with Ba'Gamnan begins, as fighting his entire crew at this early point in the game will easily wipe the party.
  • The bridges in the Shunia Twinspan area spawn massive hordes of Skeletons and Skullshades, making it one of the best early-game locations to build a high battle chain for Gil and loot.
