Salikawood

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Archades

The Salikawood is a dense, lush forest the party crosses while traveling northeast toward the Phon Coast and Archades. Progress toward the coast is blocked by a massive broken gate, requiring the party to locate nine sleeping moogles scattered across the forest to repair it. An optional northern route leads to the high-level Necrohol of Nabudis, which is guarded by the optional boss King Bomb.

Secrets

  • Defeating the optional boss King Bomb in the Corridor of Ages opens the western path leading directly into the Necrohol of Nabudis.

Tips

  • Equipping Bows or Guns can help flying and distant targets, but using Rose Corsages to grant Silence protects party members from the King Bomb's devastating magic spells.
  • Look at the local area map to pinpoint the locations of all nine Master-Craft moogles needed to fix the gate to the Phon Coast.
