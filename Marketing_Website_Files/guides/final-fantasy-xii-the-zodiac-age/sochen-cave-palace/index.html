Sochen Cave Palace

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Archades

The party traverses the Sochen Cave Palace on their way toward the Imperial City of Archades after clearing the Tchita Uplands. Within the palace, you must navigate multi-path corridors, defeat the Mandragora royals, and unlock sealed doors to proceed. The path concludes with a battle against Ahriman before providing access to Old Archades.

Secrets

  • Pilgrim's Door | In the Destiny's March section, open the doors following an unbroken clockwise circular route from the outer eastern path inward to unlock the Pilgrim's Door in the south.
  • Door of the Moonlit Path | In the Falls of Time and Mirror of the Soul area, open the Doors of Hours in an unbroken circular clockwise path to unlock the central door leading to the optional hunt boss Vyraal's shrine.

Tips

  • Equip Bowline Sashes or cast Cleanse/Esuna during the Ahriman fight to protect against confusion caused by Beware.
  • Focus down the five Mandragora bosses one at a time, as their damage output drops dramatically once their numbers thin.
