Archades

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party gains access to the upper city

After arriving in the Imperial City of Archades, the party must gather information and pair off citizens to collect Chops in the lower sections to gain cab fare to the upper district. Once in Tsenoble, the party proceeds toward the central government sector, only to find the way barred, leading to an encounter with Balthier's father, Cid, at the Laboratory Draklor.

Items

  • Chop: Exchanged from Chopmasters across the Grand Arcade and Nilbasse after matching paired citizens' rumors
  • Sandalwood Chop: Traded at any Chopmaster for 28 Pinewood Chops, granting permanent access to the high-class areas of Tsenoble

Secrets

  • Matching all 28 citizen pairs across Archades awards 28 Pinewood Chops, which can be exchanged for the Sandalwood Chop, opening the central terrace in Tsenoble containing a treasure chest with a Megalixir.

Shops and people

  • Archades Technicks Shop: Sells advanced technicks including Souleater and Infuse
  • Archades Magicks Shop: Sells high-tier spells including Curaja, Firaga, Blizzaga, and Thundaga
  • Archades Gambit Shop: Sells the complete inventory of target and condition Gambits once accessed

Tips

  • Take careful notes of who speaks which rumor when matching citizens, as each pair consists of one person telling a story and one person looking for that exact information in the same district.
  • You only need 9 Pinewood Chops to hire a cab and progress the main storyline, but collecting all 28 before moving on saves time if you want the Sandalwood Chop for endgame rewards.
