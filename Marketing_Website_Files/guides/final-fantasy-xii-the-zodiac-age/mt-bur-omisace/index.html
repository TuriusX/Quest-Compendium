Mt Bur-Omisace

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party meets Gran Kiltias Anastasis

The party arrives at this holy refugee settlement to seek counsel from Gran Kiltias Anastasis regarding Lady Ashe's royal lineage and the Dawn Shard. While here, you meet with Al-Cid Margrace and learn that the party must journey to the Stilshrine of Miriam to obtain the Sword of Kings. After returning later in the story, the mountain comes under attack by Archadian forces, triggering a major boss battle.

Secrets

  • There is a hidden Chocobo path leading to the Stilshrine of Miriam south through the Freezing Gorge in the neighboring Paramina Rift if you ride a Chocobo out from Mt Bur-Omisace.

Shops and people

  • Gurdy: Chocobo rental services available just outside the settlement gates.
  • Merchant Luccio: Sells various magicks, technicks, equipment, and gambits in the Sand-strewn Approach.

Tips

  • Be sure to talk to the Clan Provisioner in Rabanastre or check local merchant stocks after reaching Mt Bur-Omisace, as new tier magicks like Curaja and high-tier equipment unlock around this story milestone.
  • Equip armor that raises defense against physical strikes and use Dispel immediately to strip Judge Bergan of his positive buffs when fighting him later in this location.
