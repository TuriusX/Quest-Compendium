Tchita Uplands

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Archades

The party traverses the rolling hills of the Tchita Uplands on their journey to the Imperial city of Archades. While passing through, they encounter an Archadian Wayfarer at the hunter's camp who offers a Hunt petition and a Soul Token required to access the Sochen Cave Palace.

Items

  • Soul Token: Received automatically from the Archadian Wayfarer in The Whispering Way

Tips

  • Speak to the Imperial traveler at the campsite in The Whispering Way to receive the Soul Token, as it is needed to unlock the entrance to the Sochen Cave Palace.
  • Watch out for sudden weather shifts to rain, as this causes elemental and status-inducing enemies like Earth Elementals to spawn.
