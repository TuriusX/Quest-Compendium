Golmore Jungle

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Eruyt Village

The party enters Golmore Jungle after traversing the Ozmone Plain while searching for a way into the Viera settlement. Progression deeper into the jungle is initially blocked by a mystical barrier, requiring the party to locate and enter Eruyt Village to advance the story. Navigating the dense paths involves managing aggressive natural predators and undead fiends while finding the hidden side path to the village.

Tips

  • Equip Rose Corsages or prepare Echo Herbs because Malboros and other local monsters frequently inflict Silence.
  • If a Treant or Malboro readies a dangerous area-of-effect status move, move your controlled character away or defeat it quickly with ranged Fire magick.
  • Look for an unmapped or branching path along the eastern side of the initial zones to find the entrance to Eruyt Village.
