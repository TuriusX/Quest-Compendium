Henne Mines

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party searches for the Dawn Shard

The party enters the Henne Mines via the Golmore Jungle to find Mjrn, the missing younger sister of Fran. Navigating the facility requires operating gate switches to open red or blue fences while fighting off swarms of bats and jellies. At the deepest point of the mines, the party defeats Tiamat to rescue Mjrn and continue their journey to Mt Bur-Omisace.

Items

  • Henne Mines Map: North Entrance, inside an urn near the entrance from Golmore Jungle

Secrets

  • Phase 2 Dig access: Returning later in the game after collecting at least 10 Espers allows you to speak to the Geomancer Yugelu in Jahara to unlock the deeper Henne Mines containing high-level foes and the Esper Zodiark.
