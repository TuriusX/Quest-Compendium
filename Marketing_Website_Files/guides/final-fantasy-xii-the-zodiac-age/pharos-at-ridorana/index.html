Pharos at Ridorana

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party ascends the tower to confront Gabranth and Cid

The Pharos at Ridorana is a massive, multi-tiered lighthouse the party scales in three major ascents to reach the Sun-cryst at the summit. Along the way, you must solve navigational puzzles involving Black Orbs, seal certain party capabilities to progress, and navigate warp waystones. The climb culminates in back-to-back boss battles against Judge Magister Gabranth, Doctor Cid, and the Esper Famfrit.

Items

  • Sage's Ring: In a treasure chest on the Spire Ravel - 2nd Flight (Third Ascent)
  • Circlet: In a treasure chest located in the Subterra (Penumbra - South Interior)

Secrets

  • The Pharos Subterra unlocks after the main storyline events at the tower are finished, accessible by taking the central lift down from the ground floor.

Tips

  • In the First Ascent, defeat enemies to collect Black Orbs and insert them into the altars to forge the bridges of light needed to proceed.
  • In the Second Ascent, you must seal one of four party abilities (Attack, Magick, Items, or the Mini-Map) at an altar; choosing the Mini-Map altar is generally the least disruptive handicap.
  • Equip Float before stepping onto the glowing trap tiles scattered across the Third Ascent to prevent harmful status effects.
  • Doctor Cid cannot be damaged while Famfrit is active on the field, so focus all attacks on the Esper until it is defeated.
