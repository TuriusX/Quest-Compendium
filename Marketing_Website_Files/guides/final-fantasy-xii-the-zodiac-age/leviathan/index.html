Leviathan

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party rescues Princess Ashe

The Dreadnought Leviathan is an Imperial airship where Vaan, Balthier, and Basch infiltrate to rescue Princess Ashe and later Penelo. Guided by the guest character Vossler, the party must navigate guarded corridors, disable alarm systems, and fight through Imperial soldiers to escape. The area culminates in back-to-back boss battles before the party flees back to Bhujerba.

Items

  • Systems Access Key: Dropped by an Imperial officer after triggering the alarm in the central area
  • Brig Key: Obtained automatically after defeating the two Imperial Judges and their squad in the Sub-duct

Tips

  • You can deactivate the blaring alarms using the security consoles for a 60-second respite from continuous guard reinforcements.
  • Vossler acts as an aggressive guest character who deals high physical damage and automatically revives fallen allies with Phoenix Downs.
  • Cast Reflect on your party or Shell to mitigate the impact of Judge Ghis's damaging Aero spell.
  • The Leviathan cannot be revisited after escaping, so explore its side rooms thoroughly during the rescue mission.
