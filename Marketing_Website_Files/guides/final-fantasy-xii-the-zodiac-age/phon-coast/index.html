Phon Coast

FINAL FANTASY XII THE ZODIAC AGE guide · The party travels toward Archades

The Phon Coast is a sprawling coastal region the party enters after exiting the Salikawood on their way to the imperial capital of Archades. Players make their way through several beach and cliff zones to reach the Hunters' Camp, an important settlement and trading hub. From here, the party continues southeast toward the Tchita Uplands to resume the main story journey.

Items

  • Site 11 Key: Hunters' Camp, lying on the ground near the fallen Bhujerban man who sits beside a hut

Secrets

  • The Hunt Club sidequest unlocks at the Hunters' Camp after visiting Archades later in the story; speaking to the Huntmaster here lets you hunt 30 Rare Game monsters across Ivalice and trade their trophies to three Bangaa brothers for unique equipment.

Shops and people

  • Hunters' Camp Merchant: Sells high-tier restorative items, advanced magicks, technicks, and mid-game weapons and armor appropriate for the party's level.

Tips

  • Look out for the Cactoid and Mandragora-family enemies along the sandy shorelines, as they yield valuable loot and moderate experience for mid-game leveling.
  • The Gate Crystal located safely within the Hunters' Camp provides a crucial teleport hub for returning later to manage Hunt Club trophies.
