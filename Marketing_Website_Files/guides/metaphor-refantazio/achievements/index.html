Points of no return

  • Finishing July–August campaign events: Julian's debate book "Bygone Days" and specific Port Brilehaven debate events become unobtainable.
  • Departing the Ancient Eldan Sanctum on 9/24: The Golden Beetle in the Plaza of Light becomes permanently unobtainable.
  • October 25 deadline: All uncompleted follower bonds, open side quests, debates, recipes, and virtue-building activities lock out permanently.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete July through August in Port Brilehaven by accepting all local requests, participating in town debates on podiums, and obtaining the missable book from Julian before finishing the infiltration of the Skyrunner Charadrius dungeon.

  2. Step 2

    Progress through August and September on Virga Island by finishing the Dragon Temple operation, retrieving the required regional items before their deadlines, and using gauntlet runner journeys to read books and raise Royal Virtues.

  3. Step 3

    Advance through September by clearing the Montario Opera House storyline and returning to the Royal Capital, ensuring you keep up with all expired bounty targets and active bond events.

  4. Step 4

    Spend late September through late October completing all outstanding side quests, preparing top-tier equipment, tackling the Skybound Avatar, and entering the final battle.

  5. Step 5

    Manage Followers and Bonds across your run by prioritizing bond dialogues with party members and town allies whenever available to reach rank eight with every follower before the calendar runs out.

  6. Step 6

    Raise Royal Virtues to rank five early by reading all available travel books, participating in city debates, and choosing dedicated virtue tasks so bond rank gates are cleared immediately.

  7. Step 7

    Visit Akademeia regularly to study and master every Archetype across all lineages, forge summoner vessels, and craft high-level masks.

  8. Step 8

    Begin New Game Plus, carry over your end-game Archetype inventory, and defeat the exclusive bonus superboss to earn the remaining achievement.
