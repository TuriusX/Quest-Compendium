Recommended order
  1. Complete the main story missions up through the carnival visit in Coney Island to earn early progression achievements and unlock side activities.
  2. Fully clear Coney Island, Queens, Astoria, Williamsburg, Upper West Side, Midtown, and Financial District of all district activities, Prowler Stashes, Marko's Memories, Spider-Bots, Photo Ops, and Mysteriums.
  3. Advance the campaign until the world shifts into the Symbiote Invasion phase, clearing Symbiote Nests in Financial District (Symbiote Invasion) and Astoria (Symbiote Invasion) as they appear.
  4. Finish the remaining main story missions to unlock all campaign-related trophies.
  5. Complete any lingering district activities, reach maximum character level, craft all suits, and clean up combat and traversal misc trophies in post-game free roam.
