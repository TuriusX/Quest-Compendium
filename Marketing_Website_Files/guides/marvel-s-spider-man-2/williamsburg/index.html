Williamsburg

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 guide · Miles's neighborhood
← All areas in this guide

Williamsburg is a vibrant Brooklyn district in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 that features residential blocks, industrial zones, and seaside views. Players visit the area to stop random street crimes, clear faction bases, and complete various open-world side activities like Prowler Stashes and Brooklyn Visions requests. Achieving full district completion here rewards hero tokens, tech parts, and progress toward bonus suits.

Achievements here
