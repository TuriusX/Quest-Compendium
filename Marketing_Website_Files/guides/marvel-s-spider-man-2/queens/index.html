Queens

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 guide · Peter's home neighborhood
← All areas in this guide

Queens is a low-rise residential and commercial borough featured in Marvel's Spider-Man 2, containing Peter Parker's childhood home in Astoria. Players visit this area for major narrative missions, web-wings traversal across open suburban streets, and various district activities like Photo Ops and Spider-Bots. It offers a contrasting gameplay rhythm compared to Manhattan due to its wider roads and lower building heights.

Tips
  • Traversal in Queens relies heavily on the Web-Wings and wind tunnels due to the scarcity of high-rise swing points across suburban residential blocks.
  • You can interact with the backyard and interior of Aunt May's house in Astoria during open-world roaming for character dialogue and ambient details.
