Upper West Side

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 guide · Hunting the Lizard
← All areas in this guide

During the hunt for Dr. Curt Connors, Kraven's Hunters track the Lizard through the Upper West Side. Players must navigate the district's rooftops and alleys to track the scent and clear out hunter ambush sites. The area serves as a primary pursuit route before following Connors' trail down toward the sewer and river systems.

Tips
  • Use the Super Slingshots located on designated rooftops to quickly cross the Upper West Side and keep up with tracking markers.
  • Equipping Symbiote combat abilities helps rapidly break through shielded Hunter squads guarding alleyways in this sector.
