Coney Island

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 guide · Amusement park mission
← All areas in this guide

Peter Parker, Harry Osborn, and Mary Jane visit the Coney Island amusement park to celebrate Harry's recovery before the area comes under attack by the Hunters. You explore the carnival attractions with Peter and Harry, participate in several midway minigames, and then fight off waves of Kraven's Hunters across the boardwalk and rides.

Achievements here
Home Run!Head to the baseball field at the Big Apple Ballers Stadium in Coney Island and run across all four bases in order.

Achievement guide and roadmap →

Enemies
Kraven's Hunters

Hostile mercenaries wielding crossbows, bladed weapons, and shields who invade the park.

Hunter Brutes

Larger enemies armed with heavy clubs and shields that cannot be directly attacked from the front without parrying or using gadgets.

Hunter Hounds

Mechanical quadruped drones that charge Spidey and attempt to disrupt attacks.

Tips
  • You must play the midway carnival minigames to progress the story, but achieving high scores is optional.
  • When the Hunters attack the roller coaster, use Web-Shooters to clear debris and pull track mechanisms back into place to save the trapped civilian carts.
  • Environmental objects like carnival stall signs, prize racks, and ride equipment can be grabbed with L1+R1 and hurled into groups of Hunters.
