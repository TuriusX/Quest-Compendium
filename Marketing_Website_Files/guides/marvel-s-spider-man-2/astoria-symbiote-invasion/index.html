Astoria (Symbiote Invasion)

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 guide · Peter's confrontation with Harry
← All areas in this guide

Peter heads to Harry Osborn's home in Astoria to confront him after Venom completely takes over. The area becomes ground zero for a high-intensity battle against Harry/Venom and waves of symbiote reinforcements across multiple intense phases. You must utilize anti-symbiote abilities and sonic-based environment interactions to survive the encounter and free Harry from the alien entity.

Enemies
Venom (Harry Osborn)
Weak to Sonic attacks and Anti-Venom abilities

Aggressive boss that uses devastating melee strings, unblockable crush attacks, and airborne lunges.

Symbiote Brute
Weak to Sonic bursts, Anti-Venom abilities

Heavy symbiote enemy that charges aggressively and requires parrying or sonic disruptions to break its guard.

Symbiote Tendril Drone
Weak to Anti-Venom abilities

Fast, agile symbiotes that swarm in packs and attempt to pin Peter to the ground.

Tips
  • Save Peter's Anti-Venom bomb and surge mode for the later phases of the fight when standard symbiote swarms overwhelm the arena.
  • Use the L1 + R1 environmental throws to detonate sonic canisters or ring bells nearby to temporarily stagger Venom and clear surrounding minions.
  • Memorize the visual cues for Venom's multi-hit combos; his sweep attacks require jumping, while red flashes demand an immediate dodge rather than a parry.

Not listed for this area: items, secrets, shops and people.
