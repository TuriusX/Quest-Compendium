Trial of the Sword (Final Trials)

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Completing the trials

Link enters the deepest tier of the Trial of the Sword, spanning 23 floors featuring extreme weather conditions such as thunderstorms, scorching heat, and bitter cold. To successfully complete the challenge, you must manage temperature resistance, conserve powerful armaments, and clear all enemy encounters. Finishing the final floor permanently unlocks the Master Sword's true awakened power, boosting its base attack power to 60.

Tips

  • Save your Ancient Arrows exclusively for the deadliest threats, such as Lynels and Guardian Skywatchers.
  • Cook wood gathered from cutting down trees in single portions using a lit cooking pot to yield Dubious Food for extra healing.
  • Unequip all metal weapons, bows, and shields during the lightning-filled early floors to avoid being struck by lightning.
  • Crouch and sneak slowly without dashing when approaching the rest room ponds so you do not scare away the Fairies.
  • Carry a flame weapon on cold floors or an ice weapon on hot floors to provide passive temperature protection without consuming elixir buffs.
