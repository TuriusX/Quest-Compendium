Hyrule Castle (Post-Game)

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Post-game exploration

Hyrule Castle in post-game refers to reloading a save file marked with a star after defeating Calamity Ganon, as the game reverts to the state just prior to the final battle. The castle remains in its corrupted state covered in Malice and occupied by high-tier monsters and Guardians. Players use this state to collect missed Royal and Royal Guard's equipment, find Korok Seeds, and freely fight or avoid Calamity Ganon in the Sanctum.

Items

  • Hylian Shield: Inside a treasure chest in the Lockup after defeating the Stalnox
  • Royal Guard's Bow: Mounted above the fireplace in the Sanctum
  • Royal Guard's Claymore: On the floor inside the Guards' Chamber behind Malice and a Lizalfos
  • Royal Guard's Shield: Inside a cell in the Lockup
  • Royal Guard's Sword: Near the head of a broken statue inside the Lockup

Secrets

  • The hidden Saas Ko'sah Shrine can be revealed by lighting the large brazier at the Docks beneath the castle.

Tips

  • Entering the Sanctum automatically begins the Calamity Ganon battle, so avoid crossing its threshold if you are only exploring to loot items.
  • Your Champion Abilities will recharge significantly faster while inside Hyrule Castle compared to the rest of the map.
