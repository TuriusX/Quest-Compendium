Rito Village

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting Kaneli

Link visits Rito Village in the Tabantha region to seek help dealing with Divine Beast Vah Medoh, which circles the sky above. Climbing to the top of the spiraling village leads to the village elder, Kaneli, who explains the village's plight and requests aid. Kaneli directs Link to the Flight Range to meet Teba, initiating the Divine Beast Vah Medoh questline.

Items

  • Revali's Gale: Awarded atop the village after calming Divine Beast Vah Medoh
  • Great Eagle Bow: Given by Kaneli inside a chest after freeing Vah Medoh

Secrets

  • Akh Va'quot Shrine sits on a high terrace along the central walkway winding up the village spire, serving as the local fast-travel point.

Shops and people

  • Brazen Beak: Sells the Snowquill Headdress, Snowquill Tunic, and Snowquill Trousers for cold resistance.
  • Slippery Falcon: Sells ingredients such as Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, and various arrows.
  • Swallow's Roost: Offers regular beds and Rito-feather beds that grant extra temporary hearts and stamina.

Tips

  • Purchase the Snowquill armor set from the Brazen Beak shop before heading up to the Flight Range, as the surrounding heights require cold resistance.
  • A cooking pot is situated on one of the wooden patio landings along the central path, which is useful for preparing cold-resistant meals and warming foods.
