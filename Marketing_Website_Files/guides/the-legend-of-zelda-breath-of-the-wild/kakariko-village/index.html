Kakariko Village

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting Impa

Kakariko Village is the first major settlement Link visits to advance the main quest "Seek Out Impa" following the Great Plateau. Here, you meet the Sheikah elder Impa, who provides crucial exposition on the Calamity and directs you toward the Divine Beasts and your lost memories. The village also acts as a peaceful hub where you can obtain new armor, unlock side quests, and access a nearby fairy fountain.

Items

  • Champion's Tunic: Given by Impa inside her house after Link recovers at least one recovered memory
  • Stealth Chest Guard: Enchanted armor shop
  • Stealth Tights: Enchanted armor shop
  • Stealth Mask: Enchanted armor shop
  • Hylian Tunic: Enchanted armor shop
  • Hylian Trousers: Enchanted armor shop
  • Hylian Hood: Enchanted armor shop

Secrets

  • Great Fairy Cotera can be unlocked by following the forest path past the village shrine and paying her a fee in Rupees.
  • Cooking Swift Carrots into dishes yields speed-boosting meals, and they grow in abundance in the village gardens.

Shops and people

  • Enchanted: Stealth Armor set and Hylian Armor set
  • High Spirits Produce: Swift Carrots, Fortified Pumpkins, and Bird Eggs
  • The Curious Quiver: Regular Arrows and Fire Arrows
  • Olkin: Fortified Pumpkins sold outside his field during the day
  • Steen: Swift Carrots sold outside his field during the day

Tips

  • Sneaking through the village at night makes it easy to catch Sunset Fireflies for upgrading gear or completing side quests.
  • Talk to the painter Pikango near the goddess statue to begin the "Find the Fairy Fountain" quest, which teaches you how to locate your missing memories.
  • The Ta'loh Naeg Shrine located on the hill above the village serves as a tutorial for advanced combat moves like dodging, parrying, and flurry rushes.
