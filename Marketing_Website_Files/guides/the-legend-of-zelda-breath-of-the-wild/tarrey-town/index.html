Tarrey Town

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Building the town

Tarrey Town is an island settlement in the Akkala region built from scratch during the "From the Ground Up" side quest given by Hudson. Link assists in its construction by providing massive quantities of wood and recruiting artisans with names ending in "-son" from across Hyrule. Once fully developed, it becomes a thriving commercial hub featuring unique vendors, armor sets, and general supplies.

Items

  • Hylian Shield: Sold by Granté on the balcony of the northeastern house after finding the original
  • Climber's Bandanna: Sold by Granté on the balcony of the northeastern house
  • Rubber Armor: Sold by Granté on the balcony of the northeastern house
  • Barbarian Armor: Sold by Granté on the balcony of the northeastern house
  • Diamond: Reward from Hudson after completing the town's wedding

Secrets

  • Granté appears on the roof balcony of the northeastern house only after the town is completely finished, selling rare and secret armor pieces you have already discovered elsewhere.

Shops and people

  • Granté: Sells replacement rare armor pieces and the Hylian Shield if the original breaks or is lost
  • Rhondson (Slick & Victorious): Sells the Desert Voe armor set
  • Fyson (The Slippery Falcon Tarrey Town Branch): Sells various arrows, including normal, fire, ice, and bomb arrows
  • Pelison (Ore and More): Sells various precious gems, including diamonds and sapphires
  • Kapson: Provides free resting services in his inn

Tips

  • Gather a total of 110 bundles of wood beforehand, as Hudson will demand them in increments (10, 20, 30, and 50) throughout the town's construction.
  • You must start and advance the "Hylian Homeowner" side quest in Hateno Village before Hudson moves to Akkala to begin Tarrey Town.
  • Every recruit required for the town must have a name ending in "-son" to satisfy the Bolson Construction hiring policy.
