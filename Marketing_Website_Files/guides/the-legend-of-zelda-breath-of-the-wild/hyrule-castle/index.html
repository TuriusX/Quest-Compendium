Hyrule Castle

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Confronting Calamity Ganon

Hyrule Castle serves as the final dungeon of the game, shrouded in Malice and heavily fortified by Guardian stalkers and turrets. Link can explore its multi-layered interior passages and outdoor parapets to gather powerful late-game gear or bypass them directly to reach the Sanctum. Entering the Sanctum immediately initiates the final encounters against Calamity Ganon and Dark Beast Ganon.

Items

  • Hylian Shield: Found in a treasure chest inside the Lockup after defeating the Stalnox
  • Royal Guard's Bow: Resting atop the rubble above the Sanctum ceiling

Secrets

  • Saas Ko'sah Shrine can be revealed in the Docks by lighting the giant stone brazier at the top of the stairs.
  • Princess Zelda's Research Journal is located in Zelda's Study, accessible via an exterior broken bridge connected to the library tower.
  • A hidden cooking pot and a Royal Guard's Claymore can be found inside the Guards' Chamber behind a destructible bomb wall.

Tips

  • Equipping the full set of Ancient Armor upgraded twice significantly reduces the devastating damage dealt by Guardian laser blasts.
  • Perfect Guards (shield parries) reflect Guardian lasers directly back into their eyes for massive damage without degrading shield durability.
  • You can scale the exterior cliffs and waterfalls using Revali's Gale and the Zora Armor to bypass most interior combat encounters entirely.
