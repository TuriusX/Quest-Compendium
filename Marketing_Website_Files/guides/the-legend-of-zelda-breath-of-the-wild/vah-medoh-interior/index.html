Vah Medoh Interior

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Clearing the dungeon

After boarding Divine Beast Vah Medoh, Link must eliminate the Malice corruption inside and activate the main guidance stone to download the interior map. Controlling the beast's tilting mechanism via the map allows Link to solve environmental puzzles using physics, wind, and his runes. The final goal is to activate all five terminals scattered across the interior and wings, then defeat Windblight Ganon at the main control unit.

Items

  • Ancient Core MISSABLE: Inside a chest hanging from the ceiling inside the main body, reachable by shooting its rope or using Magnesis
  • Knight's Bow MISSABLE: In a treasure chest located in the interior room on the left wing

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Malice EyeballArrows directly to the eyeDestroying these removes attached Malice pools and floating skull spawners.

Tips

  • You can tilt Vah Medoh to the left or right at any time from your map screen to slide heavy blocks, roll metallic spheres, or reach distant platforms with your glider.
  • Use Magnesis to catch and manipulate the sliding metal carts and switches found inside the beast's wings.
  • The updrafts inside and outside the Divine Beast allow you to glide long distances between the main body and the wing chambers.
  • Bring plenty of arrows to shoot the Malice eyes blocking pathways and to easily hit Windblight Ganon's eye during the boss battle.
