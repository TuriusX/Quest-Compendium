Tabantha Tower Region

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Traveling to Rito Village
← All areas in this guide

The Tabantha Tower region lies west of the Tabantha Great Bridge and acts as the primary approach to Rito Village. Players scale Tabantha Tower amid Malice-covered cliffs, traverse cold canyons, and encounter traveling Rito before crossing the bridges into the village. The area serves as the staging ground for Divine Beast Vah Medoh and introduces significantly colder climates.

Shops and people
  • Beedle at Tabantha Bridge Stable: Sells arrows, butterflies, and various insects

Tips
  • Keep a supply of Fire Arrows or a flame weapon handy to melt ice blocks and keep Link warm before acquiring Rito cold-resistant armor.
  • Shoot bomb arrows into the flocks of Tabantha birds for an easy source of raw bird drumsticks and thighs.
