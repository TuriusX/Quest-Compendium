Great Plateau

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Beginning of the game

Link awakens in the Shrine of Resurrection after a century-long sleep and must explore the isolated plateau under the direction of a mysterious Old Man. To receive the Paraglider needed to leave the area, Link must activate the Great Plateau Tower and clear four introductory ancient shrines to learn basic Sheikah Slate runes. The journey concludes with a revelation at the Temple of Time about Hyrule's past and Link's overarching quest to defeat Calamity Ganon.

Items

  • Sheikah Slate: On a pedestal inside the Shrine of Resurrection at the start of the game
  • Old Shirt: Inside a treasure chest in the second room of the Shrine of Resurrection
  • Well-Worn Trousers: Inside a treasure chest in the second room of the Shrine of Resurrection
  • Warm Doublet: Given by the Old Man after cooking Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry, retrieved atop Mount Hylia, or found in his cabin later
  • Paraglider: Given by the Old Man atop the Temple of Time after completing all four plateau shrines
  • Traveler's Bow: In a chest inside the Oman Au Shrine or found in camps around the plateau
  • Soldier's Bow: In a treasure chest hidden in the water near the Temple of Time ruins

Secrets

  • Climb to the very highest roof spire of the Temple of Time to find a Korok waiting under a sparkling leaf spot.
  • Dive into a circle of water lilies located in the pond just outside the Temple of Time to reveal a Korok.
  • Pick up the solitary rock sitting inside the hollow of a giant fallen tree trunk near the Hopper Pond to discover a Korok.
  • Use Magnesis to lift the metal doors submerged in the mud at the bottom of the plateau's swamps to access underwater chests and safe footholds.
  • Read the Old Man's diary inside his cabin to learn the missing ingredient for his Spicy Meat and Seafood Fry recipe.

Shops and people

  • Old Man: Gives advice, torches, baked apples, and the Paraglider in exchange for clearing shrines

Tips

  • Collect and cook Spicy Peppers found near the snowy path to Mount Hylia into meals that grant temporary cold resistance before getting warm clothing.
  • Sneak up on resting wild horses or perched birds to collect resources without scaring them away.
  • Cut down trees using an axe or Remote Bombs to bridge large chasms or create firewood bundles.
  • Use Magnesis to pull metal treasure chests out of deep bodies of water and murky bogs where Link cannot swim.
  • Hitting the glowing blue eye of a Decayed Guardian with an arrow will instantly stun it and cancel its charged laser attack.
