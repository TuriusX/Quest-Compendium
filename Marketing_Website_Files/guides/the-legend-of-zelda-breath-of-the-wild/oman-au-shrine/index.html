Oman Au Shrine

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · First trial on the Great Plateau

This shrine introduces the Magnesis Rune, which allows you to lift and manipulate metallic objects. You must use this power to clear a path across floor plates, cross a gap with an iron plank, open a set of heavy doors, and overcome a Guardian Scout. Reaching the monk at the end completes the trial and rewards you with a Spirit Orb.

Items

  • Magnesis Rune: obtained from the Guidance Stone at the start of the shrine
  • Traveler's Bow MISSABLE: inside a submerged metal chest on the left side of the room with the Guardian Scout
  • Spirit Orb: given by Monk Oman Au at the end of the shrine

Secrets

  • A metal treasure chest is submerged in the water on the platform before the heavy doors, which you can retrieve using Magnesis.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Guardian Scout Ieye (critical hit), Magnesis dropswalks onto the path near the end and can be easily crushed by dropping the nearby metal plate on it

Tips

  • Use Magnesis to swing or drop the metal block on the final obstacle or Guardian Scout to clear them out safely without using weapon durability.
  • Activating Magnesis highlights all interactable metallic objects in a bright magenta color, making them easy to spot.
