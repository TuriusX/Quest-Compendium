Death Mountain

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Traveling to Goron City

This volcanic trail leads from the Foothill Stable up the rugged slopes of Eldin to reach Goron City. The extreme heat requires flame-resistant protection once you pass the Maw of Death Mountain. Along the way, you must navigate cliffs, magma, and mining camps while dealing with Fire-breath Lizalfos and falling boulders.

Items

  • Flamebreaker Armor: Southern Mine from the NPC Kima in exchange for 10 Fireproof Lizards
  • Fireproof Lizard: scurrying on the ground around the Southern Mine and along the mountain trail

Secrets

  • Shae Mo'sh Shrine | hidden behind a breakable cracked rock wall on the path right above Goron City | missable: no

Tips

  • Do not equip wooden bows, shields, or weapons while climbing, as the intense heat will cause them to catch fire and burn up.
  • Crouch and move quietly or drink a stealth potion to easily catch Fireproof Lizards around the Southern Mine before they scurry away.
  • Wooden bomb arrows will explode instantly in Link's face if drawn in this scorching region.
