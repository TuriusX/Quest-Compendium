Korok Forest

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting the Great Deku Tree

Korok Forest is the peaceful sanctuary hidden in the heart of the Lost Woods, serving as the home of the Great Deku Tree and the Koroks. Here, Link can pull the legendary Master Sword from its pedestal, trade Korok Seeds with Hestu, and activate side quests and Shrine Quests. It is a central hub free of hostile monsters where you can prepare, rest, and expand your inventory.

Items

  • Master Sword: Resting in the stone pedestal directly in front of the Great Deku Tree
  • Korok Seed: Dropped by a Korok found by interacting with the fairy lights atop the Great Deku Tree
  • Korok Leaf: Scattered on the ground near various trees throughout the clearing

Secrets

  • The interior of the Great Deku Tree holds a hollowed-out tree trunk containing an inn and a general store that Link can freely use.
  • Climbing the inner beanstalk inside the Great Deku Tree's navel leads to the tree canopy, where the Korok Chio assigns "The Korok Trials" questline.

Shops and people

  • Pepp: Sells apples, fortified pumpkins, cane sugar, and goat butter inside the Great Deku Tree
  • Natroo: Sells shock arrows, ice arrows, fire arrows, and regular arrows inside the Great Deku Tree
  • Peeks: Offers the "Legendary Rabbit" side quest inside the Great Deku Tree to take a picture of a Blupee
  • Hestu: Expands weapon, bow, and shield inventory stashes in exchange for Korok Seeds

Tips

  • Pulling the Master Sword permanently drains Link's health, requiring a minimum of 13 full red Heart Containers; temporary yellow hearts will not count.
  • You can sleep for free at the Spore Store inn inside the Great Deku Tree simply by talking to the attendant Korok.
  • Activate the Keo Ruug Shrine right behind the Master Sword's pedestal to create a permanent fast-travel point, avoiding having to re-navigate the Lost Woods.
