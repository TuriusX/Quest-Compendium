Faron Tower Region

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Exploring the south

The Faron Tower Region is a lush, subtropical jungle area located in the southern part of Hyrule filled with dense rainforests, waterfalls, and ancient Zonai ruins. It is the primary habitat for electric dragon Farosh and boasts an abundance of high-value resources like Hearty Durians, Mighty Bananas, and Voltfin Trout. Exploration here largely revolves around navigating frequent thunderstorms, scaling massive cliffs, and solving ruin-based shrine quests.

Items

  • Hearty Durian: Found in large clusters on the plateau directly north and northeast of Faron Tower
  • Mighty Bananas: Growing abundantly on palm trees throughout the Ubota Point and Lakeside Stable areas
  • Rubber Helm: Rewarded by Cima at Lakeside Stable after completing the side quest "Thunder Magnet"
  • Thunder Helm: Lakeside Stable exterior (on the roof during the side quest "Thunder Magnet")

Secrets

  • Farming Farosh at Riola Spring allows for guaranteed daily dragon horn, scale, claw, and fang drops by resting at a campfire until morning.
  • A treasure chest containing a Star Fragment is buried in the sand under water near the shoreline of Aris Beach.
  • Bombable stone walls behind the waterfalls of Floria Falls conceal hidden caves containing gem deposits and treasure chests.

Shops and people

  • Beedle at Lakeside Stable: Sells arrows, Octo Balloons, Restless Crickets, and Warm Darners
  • Kampo at Lakeside Stable: Offers regular bed rest for 20 rupees or a soft bed for 40 rupees

Tips

  • Swap out all metal weapons, shields, and bows for wooden or ancient gear because lightning strikes frequently in this region.
  • Cook five Hearty Durians together in a cooking pot to create a meal that fully restores health and grants twenty temporary bonus hearts.
  • Faron Tower is surrounded by Octoroks and high-climbing cliffs; use the large updrafts or Cryonis on nearby water pools to gain height easily.
