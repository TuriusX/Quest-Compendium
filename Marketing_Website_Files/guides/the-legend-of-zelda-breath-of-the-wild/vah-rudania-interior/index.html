Vah Rudania Interior

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Clearing the dungeon

Link navigates the pitch-black interior of the Divine Beast Vah Rudania to restore power to its Guidance Stone and five control terminals. By using the Sheikah Slate, you can tilt the entire beast 90 degrees back and forth to reach new pathways and slide massive obstacles. Clearing the corruption leads to a final confrontation against Fireblight Ganon.

Items

  • Torch: Inside an early treasure chest in the completely dark entrance chamber
  • Ancient Core MISSABLE: In a chest accessed by tilting the beast and traversing the high interior ledges
  • Knight's Bow MISSABLE: In an early chest found in the dark entryway area

Secrets

  • Unlit wall braziers can be struck with a blue flame-tipped arrow through small portholes to unlock sealed heavy doors without needing to open them manually from the inside.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Malice EyeballArrows or physical attacksDestroys nearby Malice puddles and stops them from spawning cursed flying skulls.
Fireblight GanonRemote Bombs and Ice ArrowsBoss of the dungeon that relies on a fiery protective sphere during its second phase, which can be bypassed by feeding it a bomb.

Tips

  • Bring wooden weapons or keep a torch handy, as they can carry blue fire between braziers even if you do not want to expend ancient arrows.
  • Equipping fireproof armor with two upgrades prevents you from catching fire when wandering onto external catwalks or around open flames.
  • You must tilt Vah Rudania using the map screen to turn flat walls into walkable ramps and release rolling metal balls to clear terminals.
