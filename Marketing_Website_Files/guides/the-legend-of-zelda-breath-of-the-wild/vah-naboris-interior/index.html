Vah Naboris Interior

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Clearing the dungeon

Link navigates the interior of Divine Beast Vah Naboris to activate five terminals by manipulating three rotating cylindrical sections of its main body. The puzzles primarily involve aligning electrical circuits along the ceiling and walls to power elevators and access hidden compartments. After activating all terminals and initiating the main control unit, Link must defeat Thunderblight Ganon to free the spirit of the Gerudo Champion, Urbosa.

Items

  • Map of Divine Beast Vah Naboris: Guidance Stone room on the rear upper platform
  • Ancient Shaft MISSABLE: Chest suspended by a rope in the central cylindrical room
  • Ancient Spring MISSABLE: Chest located inside one of the rotating rings in the main hall
  • Ancient Core MISSABLE: Chest accessed near the front hump by connecting circuits to open a gate
  • Ancient Gear MISSABLE: Chest in the small room beneath the tail section
  • Topaz MISSABLE: Chest secured behind an electrified gate inside the middle cylinder
  • Knight's Bow MISSABLE: Chest resting on an exterior balcony on the beast's left side
  • Scimitar of the Seven: Awarded by Riju in Gerudo Town after clearing the dungeon
  • Daybreaker: Awarded by Riju in Gerudo Town after clearing the dungeon
  • Urbosa's Fury: Received automatically after defeating the boss and interacting with the main control unit
  • Heart Container: Spawns by the main control unit after defeating Thunderblight Ganon

Secrets

  • Shoot the rope holding the chest inside the central rotating drum with an arrow, then use Stasis or catch it with Magnesis before it falls into the abyss below.
  • Manipulate the rotation of the tail cylinder to line up an electric current along the outer walkway, raising the tail section to expose an entrance to the neck area.

Tips

  • Equip the Rubber Armor set or consume an electro-resistance meal before the boss fight to prevent dropping your weapons when shocked.
  • Use Magnesis to lift the metal rods Thunderblight Ganon drops into the floor and hold them directly next to him to channel his own lightning strike into him.
  • Align the green electrical circuit along the ceiling of all three body segments at once to power the forward elevator and hump mechanisms.
