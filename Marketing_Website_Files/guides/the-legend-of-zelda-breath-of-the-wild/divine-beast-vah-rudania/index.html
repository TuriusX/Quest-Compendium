Divine Beast Vah Rudania

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Boarding the beast

Link and Yunobo scale Death Mountain to force Divine Beast Vah Rudania inside the volcano's crater. Link must escort Yunobo past several Guardian Sentries using whistling signals, stealth, and nearby tools to destroy or bypass the sentries. Once an area is clear, Link loads Yunobo into an Eldin Cannon and fires him directly at Rudania to drive it backward.

Tips

  • Use Magnesis to pick up metal blocks or iron plates along the path and smash them into Guardian Sentries to destroy them instantly without alerting them.
  • Whistling with the down button on the D-pad allows you to command Yunobo to either stay put or follow behind you.
  • Level 2 Flame Guard protection from the Flamebreaker armor set or fireproof elixirs is required to survive the extreme heat on the volcano's slopes.
  • You must strike each Eldin Cannon's lever with a melee weapon to adjust its trajectory before firing Yunobo at Rudania.
