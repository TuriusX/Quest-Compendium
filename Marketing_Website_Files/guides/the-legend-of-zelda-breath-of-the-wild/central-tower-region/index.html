Central Tower Region

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Exploring the center of Hyrule

The Central Tower Region encompasses Hyrule Field and the surrounding plains directly south of Hyrule Castle. The area is heavily patrolled by dangerous Guardian variants and dotted with ancient ruins, remnants of the Great Calamity, and scattered shrines. Traversing this region requires caution, as the open terrain leaves players exposed to long-range Guardian laser attacks while climbing Central Tower or exploring ruined settlements.

Items

  • Phantom Armor: In the Sacred Ground Ruins, submerged in the water and requiring Magnesis to pull out the chest
  • Midna's Helmet: In the Sage Temple Ruins, buried in the ground in a metal chest beneath the rubble
  • Ancient Core: Inside the treasure chest atop Central Tower's immediate scaffolding
  • Soldier's Claymore: Leaning against the wall inside the armory ruins at the Garrison Ruins

Secrets

  • Beneath Central Tower, several broken Guardian husks can be searched using the interact prompt to gain free Ancient Screws, Ancient Springs, or Ancient Gears.
  • Shooting the floating acorn inside the hollow log near the Exchange Ruins reveals a hidden Korok.

  • Beedle: Sells Arrows, Octo Balloons, Restless Crickets, and Hot-Footed Frogs

Tips

  • Use Cryonis or the surrounding stone blocks to break line of sight when the Decayed Guardians target you as you ascend Central Tower.
  • Parrying a Guardian Stalker's laser beam with a perfectly timed shield parry will deflect the blast directly back into its eye, defeating it in three hits.
  • Equipping the Ancient Armor set or taking high-level Defense-boosting elixirs drastically mitigates the lethal one-shot potential of Guardian laser blasts throughout this zone.
