Gerudo Town

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting Riju

Link must sneak into Gerudo Town disguised in women's clothing to seek an audience with the young Gerudo chief, Makeela Riju. Inside the main palace, Riju asks Link to retrieve the stolen Thunder Helm from the Yiga Clan before she will help him calm Divine Beast Vah Naboris. Navigating the town also opens up numerous side quests and access to local amenities like shops and an inn.

Items

  • Golden Bow: In a small training courtyard behind the soldier barracks in the palace
  • Thunder Helm: Displayed next to Riju's throne in the palace (unobtainable as equipment during this initial meeting)

Secrets

  • The Gerudo Secret Club (GSC) is located down an alley in town and requires a specific password overheard from a nearby house to enter, selling unique armor sets.

Shops and people

  • Fashion Passion: Sells the Desert Voe armor set once you gain access to the Gerudo Secret Club
  • Starlight Memories: Sells various jewelry and accessories crafted with gemstones that provide elemental resistances
  • Hotel Oasis: Offers a regular bed or a special soft spa bed that temporarily increases maximum hearts and stamina

Tips

  • You must remain equipped with the full Gerudo vai disguise at all times inside the town walls, or the guards will immediately spot you and throw you out.
