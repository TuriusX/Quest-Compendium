Great Hyrule Forest

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Seeking the Master Sword

The Great Hyrule Forest is shrouded by the Lost Woods, which requires following windblown ember trails to reach the tranquil Korok Forest at the center. Once inside the Korok Forest, Link meets the Great Deku Tree and finds the legendary Master Sword resting in a stone pedestal. To claim the Master Sword, you must pull it free by enduring a trial of vitality that demands a minimum of thirteen red heart containers.

Items

  • Master Sword: Resting in the stone pedestal in front of the Great Deku Tree in Korok Forest

Secrets

  • Resting inside the hollowed belly of the Great Deku Tree is a free bed made of soft leaves where Link can sleep to fully recover health.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Stalker KeeseArrows or any physical strikeThese glowing-eyed bats flutter around the Lost Woods entrance path to ambush travelers.

Shops and people

  • Hestu: Expands inventory slots for weapons, bows, and shields in exchange for Korok Seeds
  • Spore Store: Fresh mushrooms including Ironshrooms, Razorshrooms, and Stamella Shrooms
  • General Store (The Great Deku Tree's Navel): Cane Sugar, Swift Violets, and various arrow types

Tips

  • Carry a lit torch through the foggy Lost Woods and watch the direction the blowing sparks drift to safely navigate without getting enveloped by the mist.
  • Temporary bonus yellow hearts from food do not count toward pulling the Master Sword; you must possess thirteen genuine red heart containers.
