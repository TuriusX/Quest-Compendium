Ja Baij Shrine

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Second trial on the Great Plateau

This shrine teaches the player how to use the Remote Bomb rune to destroy cracked stone blocks. Link must navigate several rooms using both spherical and cubic bombs to clear paths and utilize mechanical stone launchers. Reaching the monk at the end awards a Spirit Orb.

Items

  • Remote Bomb Rune: Guidance Stone at the entrance
  • Traveler's Claymore: Inside a treasure chest behind a cracked wall in the first main corridor
  • Amber: Inside a treasure chest on a platform launched across the pit in the launcher room
  • Spirit Orb: Received from Monk Ja Baij at the end of the shrine

Secrets

  • A treasure chest containing Amber is situated on a distant platform in the launcher room; reach it by standing in the far-left catapult to be flung directly across to it.

Tips

  • Spherical bombs roll down slopes, whereas cubic bombs stay in place without rolling.
  • You can drop a spherical bomb into the funnel of the mechanical launcher to shoot it across the gap to blow up the cracked blocks blocking your way.
