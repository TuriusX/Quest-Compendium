Owa Daim Shrine

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Third trial on the Great Plateau

This shrine teaches Link how to use the Stasis Rune to freeze objects in time. Players must halt moving machinery and obstacles to safely cross gaps and clear pathways. The trial concludes by using a two-handed weapon to store kinetic energy into an immovable boulder, launching it out of the way.

Items

  • Traveler's Shield: inside a treasure chest located along a narrow ramp just past the rolling boulder path
  • Iron Sledgehammer: resting against the wall immediately past the frozen boulder puzzle
  • Spirit Orb: awarded by the monk Owa Daim at the end of the shrine

Secrets

  • After acquiring the Iron Sledgehammer, turn around and follow the narrow platform leading behind the rolling boulder chute to discover a hidden chest containing a Traveler's Shield.

Tips

  • Striking a frozen object multiple times with the Iron Sledgehammer stores kinetic energy, which releases all at once when Stasis expires to send the object flying in the direction of the arrow indicator.
  • You do not need to wait for the full Stasis timer to expire; you can manually cancel the effect early by aiming at the frozen object and pressing the rune activation button again.
