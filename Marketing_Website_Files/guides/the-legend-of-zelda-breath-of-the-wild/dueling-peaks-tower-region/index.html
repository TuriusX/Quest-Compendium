Dueling Peaks Tower Region

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Traveling to Kakariko Village

This region serves as the primary route leading east from the Great Plateau toward Kakariko Village. Players pass through the iconic canyon split by Dueling Peaks, encounter their first stables, and unlock key early-game mechanics like horse registration. Along the road, travelers also meet Hestu to begin expanding inventory capacity and discover several shrines that teach basic puzzle concepts.

Items

  • Climber's Bandanna: Inside the treasure chest at Ree Dahee Shrine located within the Dueling Peaks pass
  • Wooden Bow: Resting inside the hollow trunk of a fallen tree just south of Dueling Peaks Stable

Secrets

  • Complete the twin shrine puzzles Shee Vaneer and Shee Venath atop Dueling Peaks by recording the ball layout of one shrine to solve the floor pattern of the other.
  • Strike the ore deposits tucked away on the ledges flanking the Dueling Peaks canyon to gather early-game amber, opal, and occasional luminous stones.

Shops and people

  • Beedle: Basic arrows, frogs, and cooking beetles
  • Tasseren: Horse registration and boarding services

Tips

  • Talk to Hestu along the road to Kakariko Village to trade Korok Seeds for weapon, bow, and shield inventory stash upgrades.
  • Tame wild horses spotted in the meadows near Dueling Peaks Stable by crouching to sneak up behind them and pressing soothe immediately upon mounting.
