Hebra Tower Region

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Exploring the northwest

The Hebra Tower Region covers the frigid, snow-covered mountains in the far northwestern corner of Hyrule. Players traverse sub-zero terrain, deep snowdrifts, and blizzard winds to reach high-altitude shrines and explore icy peaks. Essential activities include hunting large wildlife, shield-surfing mountain slopes, and using warm gear or fire to melt pervasive ice blocks.

Items

  • Snowquill Tunic: Rito Village armor shop
  • Flameblade: embedded in rocks on the cliffs south of Hebra Peak
  • Falcon Bow: inside a treasure chest on the flight range grounds

Secrets

  • The Hebra Great Skeleton and To Quo Me Shrine can be reached by rolling large snowballs downhill from a high cliff to smash open a massive pair of stone doors at the bottom of the slope.
  • Goma Asaagh Shrine is trapped inside a massive block of ice along the mountain path, requiring several campfires or fire-based attacks to melt the casing.

Shops and people

  • Brazen Beak: Sells the Snowquill armor set providing cold resistance
  • Pondo: Runs the Snowling minigame at Pondo's Lodge, offering rupees for bowling over pins with a snowball

Tips

  • Keep a flame weapon equipped on your back while exploring to passively gain one level of cold resistance and slowly melt nearby ice without using weapon durability.
  • Use a wooden shield rather than a metal shield on Hebra's slopes to maximize glide speed while shield surfing on the snow.
