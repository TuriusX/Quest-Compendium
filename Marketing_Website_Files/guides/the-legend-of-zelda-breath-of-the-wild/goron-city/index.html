Goron City

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting Bludo

Link arrives in Goron City while seeking to pacify Divine Beast Vah Rudania and must speak with the Goron Boss, Bludo. Bludo explains his recurring back pain and sends Link to the Abandoned North Mine to rescue a young Goron named Yunobo. Meeting Bludo is essential to advancing the Main Quest "Divine Beast Vah Rudania" and unlocking the path up Death Mountain.

Items

  • Flamebreaker Armor: Goron City Armor Shop (Ripped and Tattered)
  • Flamebreaker Boots: Goron City Armor Shop (Ripped and Tattered)
  • Flamebreaker Helm: Goron City Armor Shop (Ripped and Tattered)
  • Fireproof Elixir: Sold by Offrak in Goron City

Shops and people

  • Ripped and Tattered: Flamebreaker Armor, Flamebreaker Boots, and Flamebreaker Helm
  • Goron Gusto Shop: Goron Spice, Rock Salt, and Cane Sugar
  • Protein Palace: Raw Seared Steak, Seared Prime Steak, and Seared Gourmet Steak

Tips

  • You must have at least Level 1 Flame Guard active before entering Goron City to avoid catching fire from the extreme environmental heat.
  • Avoid equipping wooden bows, shields, or weapons inside Goron City, as the ambient heat will cause them to burn up and break.
  • Bomb Arrows cannot be used in Goron City because they will instantly detonate the moment they are drawn in the heat.
