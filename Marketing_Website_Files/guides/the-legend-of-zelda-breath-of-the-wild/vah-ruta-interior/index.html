Vah Ruta Interior

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Clearing the dungeon

Link explores the water-filled interior of Divine Beast Vah Ruta to reclaim it from the control of Calamity Ganon. By obtaining the dungeon map, Link gains the ability to adjust the angle of Ruta's trunk to manipulate the flow of water and solve puzzles. The main objective is to activate five terminal nodes throughout the beast to unlock the main control unit and trigger the boss encounter.

Items

  • Ancient Spring MISSABLE: inside an underwater chest on the lowest floor near the entrance ramp
  • Silver Longsword MISSABLE: inside a chest locked behind a gate on the lowest floor, lifted using Cryonis
  • Ancient Shaft MISSABLE: inside a chest embedded in the hub of the small rotating waterwheel
  • Knight's Bow MISSABLE: inside a chest resting on a ledge accessed via the large rotating waterwheel
  • Giant Ancient Core MISSABLE: inside a chest on the upper platform of the snout of the trunk
  • 10 Ice Arrows MISSABLE: inside a chest in an alcove near the top exterior walkway
  • Heart Container: appears on the platform after defeating Waterblight Ganon

Secrets

  • Freeze the moving ice blocks created by the boss with Cryonis to break them instantly into pieces.
  • Shoot an arrow into the orange crystal embedded inside the hub of the small waterwheel just as it passes under the water stream to open the gate hiding the terminal.

Tips

  • Position the trunk at a high angle to send a stream of water through the ceiling hole to douse the flames guarding the central terminal.
  • You cannot re-enter the interior of Vah Ruta after activating the main control unit and defeating the boss, so collect all desired treasure chests beforehand.
  • Equip the Zora Armor inside the beast to quickly swim up waterfalls cascading from the raised trunk.
