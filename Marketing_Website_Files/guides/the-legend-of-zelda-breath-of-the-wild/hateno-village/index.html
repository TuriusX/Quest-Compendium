Hateno Village

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild guide · Meeting Purah

Link visits Hateno Village to meet the director of the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab, Purah, as directed by Impa. At the lab, Link must retrieve a blue flame from an ancient furnace in the village to power the lab's Guidance Stone and restore the missing Runes on his Sheikah Slate. After restoring the Sheikah Slate, Link can return to Purah to begin upgrading runes using ancient materials.

Items

  • Torch: Resting against the wall inside the Hateno Ancient Tech Lab near the entrance
  • Pitchfork: Leaning against the exterior wall of the general store
  • Apple: Hanging on various apple trees scattered throughout the village orchards

Secrets

  • You can purchase a dilapidated house on the outskirts of the village by talking to Bolson behind the house, which starts the "Hylian Homeowner" side quest.
  • A horned statue located near Firly Pond allows Link to trade Heart Containers for Stamina Vessels or vice versa for a small fee of rupees.

Shops and people

  • The Great Ton Pu Inn: Offers a standard bed for 20 rupees or an extra-soft bed for 40 rupees to restore Link's health.
  • Ventest Clothing Boutique: Sells the Hylian Tunic, Hylian Trousers, Hylian Hood, Soldier's Armor, Soldier's Greaves, and Soldier's Helm.
  • East Wind General Store: Sells swift carrots, fortified pumpkins, Hylian rice, bird eggs, fresh milk, and rock salt.
  • Kochi Dye Shop: Dyes Link's equipped armor sets into different colors for 20 rupees and five color-matching materials.

Tips

  • Light the stone lanterns along the road while carrying the blue flame up to the lab so you do not have to walk all the way back if your torch goes out.
  • You can use a wooden weapon like a branch or boko club to transport the blue flame if your torch breaks.
