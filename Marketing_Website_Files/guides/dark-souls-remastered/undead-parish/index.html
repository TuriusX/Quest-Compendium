Undead Parish

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The area beyond the Burg and the church

This hub area connects the Undead Burg, Firelink Shrine, Darkroot Garden, and Sen's Fortress around a large cathedral. You must navigate past a Fang Boar, fight through Balder Knights and an upgraded Channeler inside the church, and reach the roof. Defeating the Bell Gargoyles atop the cathedral allows you to ring the first Bell of Awakening.

Items

  • Mystery Key: Found on a corpse in the lower area behind the portcullis gate near the Armored Boar
  • Basement Key: Found on a corpse by the closed portcullis steps behind the main cathedral entrance
  • Fire Keeper Soul: Found on an altar guarded by a Berenike Knight inside the church ground floor
  • Knight Shield: Found on a corpse guarded by Balder Knights in the lower church hall

Secrets

  • The church elevator acts as a major shortcut back to Firelink Shrine; step into the shaft shafts near the altar to unlock it.
  • Knight Lautrec of Carim is imprisoned in a hidden cell on the upper floor behind a breakable wooden wall, requiring the Mystery Key or Master Key.
  • A giant crow nest is reachable by jumping off the ascending church elevator midway to return to the Northern Undead Asylum.

Shops and people

  • Blacksmith Andre: Sells Titanite Shards, repair items, and provides weapon ascension up to +5
  • Oswald of Carim: Sells Purging Stones, Indictments, and offers absolution for sins

Tips

  • Cut the first Bell Gargoyle's tail before the second one spawns to easily acquire the Gargoyle's Tail Axe.
  • Kill the Channeler on the upper floor of the church quickly, as he does not respawn once defeated.
  • Summon Solaire of Astora or Knight Lautrec for the Bell Gargoyles by finding their summon signs outside the boss fog gate.
