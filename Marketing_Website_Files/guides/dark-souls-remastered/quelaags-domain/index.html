Quelaag's Domain

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The spider-infested lair within Blighttown

Quelaag's Domain is a cavernous web-coated sub-area accessed from the base of the Blighttown swamp. Players must defeat the area boss, Chaos Witch Quelaag, in order to ring the second Bell of Awakening located in the bell tower beyond her arena. Ringing the bell opens the gates to Sen's Fortress and unlocks paths downward into the Demon Ruins and a hidden covenant chamber.

Items

  • Soul of Quelaag: Awarded automatically upon defeating Chaos Witch Quelaag
  • Twin Humanities: Awarded automatically upon defeating Chaos Witch Quelaag

Secrets

  • Illusory Wall leading to the Daughter of Chaos bonfire and covenant area | Found on the lower floor below the second Bell of Awakening chamber; hit or roll against the wall directly across from the archway leading down to the Demon Ruins

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Egg CarrierFireNon-hostile unless attacked, but will latch onto the player and infect them with an egg vermin parasite if provoked
Chaos Witch QuelaagLightningArea boss that attacks with a fiery curved sword and spews lingering lava puddles across the arena

Shops and people

  • Eingyi: Sells Pyromancy Flame upgrades, servant pyromancies, and the Egg Vermin cure (Egg Vermifuge)
  • The Fair Lady (Daughter of Chaos): Leader of the Chaos Servant covenant; allows players to join, rank up by offering Humanity, and receive Chaos pyromancies

Tips

  • Summon the NPC phantom Maneater Mildred right outside Quelaag's fog gate (requires defeating her swamp invasion and being in human form) to effectively distract the boss during the fight.
  • Watch for Quelaag's human torso to slump forward over the spider's head, as this signals an imminent, high-damage area-of-effect magical explosion.
  • Do not kill Eingyi, as he is the primary repeatable source of Egg Vermifuges in this part of the game and an essential pyromancy trainer.
