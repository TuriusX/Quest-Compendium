Demon Ruins

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The area accessed after ringing the second bell

The Demon Ruins is a volcanic subterranean zone accessed right after defeating Chaos Witch Quelaag and ringing the second Bell of Awakening. To explore the lower sections safely, you must first defeat the Ceaseless Discharge to cause the massive lava lakes to solidify into hardened crust. Navigating down through minor demon encounters leads directly into boss fights and the transition to the Lost Izalith ruins.

Items

  • Gold-Hemmed Black Set: Looted from an altar surrounded by rock spires at the far end of the upper path
  • Chaos Flame Ember: Found in the hardened lava lake surrounded by several Minor Taurus Demons
  • Large Flame Ember: In a chest guarded by a Burrowing Rockworm and Demonic Statues near the Demon Firesage fog gate

Secrets

  • Illusory wall found down the staircase just before the Centipede Demon boss fight, hiding a bonfire guarded by a Burrowing Rockworm.

Shops and people

  • Solaire of Astora: Summons for the Centipede Demon fight

Tips

  • Equipping the Rusted Iron Ring does not prevent lava damage, so wait until you acquire the Orange Charred Ring from the Centipede Demon before exploring the deep magma pits.
  • Wearing high fire resistance armor like the Gold-Hemmed Black Set drastically reduces damage taken from environmental fire hazards and stray lava splash.
