Depths

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The sewer area beneath the Undead Burg

The Depths is a dark, flooded sewer network located beneath the Lower Undead Burg that players access after defeating the Capra Demon. To progress, you must navigate labyrinthine tunnels, avoid deadly basilisks and falling slimes, and find the shortcut back to the bonfire before descending to fight the Gaping Dragon. Defeating the Gaping Dragon awards the Blighttown Key, opening the massive doors to the next area.

Items

  • Large Ember MISSABLE: In a chest right next to the first Butcher's table
  • Sewer Chamber Key: Inside the room guarded by a giant rat, retrieved from the corpse hanging on the back of the well
  • Ring of the Evil Eye MISSABLE: At the end of a basilisk-infested dead-end corridor in the lower flooded area
  • Blighttown Key: Dropped automatically by the Gaping Dragon upon defeat

Secrets

  • Falling down the chute behind the first Butcher's meat counter lands you safely on a high ledge directly above the Giant Rat's room, granting early high-ground advantage and the Spider Shield.

Shops and people

  • Laurentius of the Great Swamp: Pyromancy Flame and basic pyromancy spells after being freed from the barrels where he is trapped
  • Domhnall of Zena: Sells Gold Pine Resin, Crystal weapons, Boss Armor, and lore advice

Tips

  • Kill the Channeler located on the upper floor before fighting the Gaping Dragon, or he will continuously shoot magic and buff the boss from above during the fight.
  • Bring Purging Stones into this area to quickly cure Curse if you are exposed to Basilisk gas.
