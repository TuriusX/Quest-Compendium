New Londo Ruins

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Accessed via the elevator from Firelink Shrine

New Londo Ruins is a flooded city beneath Firelink Shrine overrun by ghosts and, in its lower levels, Darkwraiths. You must find Ingward on a high roof to acquire the Key to the Seal, open the floodgates to drain the water, and obtain the Covenant of Artorias from Darkroot Garden to traverse the Abyss. Draining the area grants access to the Four Kings, one of the primary Lord Soul bosses required to complete the game.

Items

  • Transient Curse: Found inside a pot right before the first wooden bridge leading into the ruins
  • Fire Keeper Soul: Located at the end of a narrow, submerged walkway on the right side of the upper ruins, guarded by ghosts
  • Estoc: Located at the base of the stairs near the start of the area, by a passive hollow looking over the water
  • Parrying Dagger: Found in a small room near the start of the ruins before crossing to the ghost-infested structures
  • Key to the Seal: Given by Ingward atop the building, or dropped by him if he is killed
  • Very Large Ember: Located on the third floor of a ruined building in the lower section after draining the water, in a chest
  • Titanite Slab: Guaranteed drop from the Stray Demon in the Undead Asylum, but in New Londo it has a rare chance to drop from Darkwraiths
  • Beaten Up Ring of Fog / Cursebite Ring: Found on a ledge of the outer wall near the seal mechanism

Secrets

  • Illusory Wall | In the lower section near the first drained building, strike a ruined wall to reveal a hidden room housing a Darkwraith and a chest |

Shops and people

  • Ingward: Removes curse, sells Sorceries (Resist Curse), and sells Transient Curses
  • Rickert of Vinheim: Blacksmith locked in a cell near the elevator to Firelink; upgrades weapons down Magic and Enchanted paths and sells basic sorceries

Tips

  • Always consume a Transient Curse before crossing the wooden bridge into the ruins if you are not using a cursed weapon, or ghosts will be completely invulnerable to your attacks.
  • Equipping the Covenant of Artorias ring is mandatory before dropping down the staircase into the boss fog, or the Abyss will immediately kill you.
  • Heavy armor with high poise (like Havel's Set) allows you to stand right next to the Four Kings and attack continuously to defeat each king before the next one spawns.
