Catacombs

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Accessed via the graveyard near Firelink Shrine

The Catacombs is an underground labyrinth filled with reanimating skeletons and Necromancers who keep them alive. You must navigate dark corridors, rotating spike bridges, and treacherous vertical drops to reach the Tomb of the Giants. Defeating the boss Pinwheel at the bottom awards the Rite of Kindling, which allows bonfires to be kindled up to twenty flasks.

Items

  • Skull Lantern: Dropped guaranteed by the last Necromancer killed in the area
  • Darkmoon Seance Ring: Found in a sarcophagus at the end of a hallway guarded by a giant skeleton
  • Great Scythe: Found on a small platform reached by jumping off the first rotating bridge after turning it
  • Lucerne: Found on a ledge in the chamber with the breakable wooden door and statues
  • Ring of the Evil Eye: Found at the end of a long tunnel guarded by basilisks
  • Rite of Kindling: Obtained directly from defeating the boss, Pinwheel
  • Mask of the Father, Mask of the Mother, or Mask of the Child: Dropped randomly upon defeating Pinwheel (one of the three)

Secrets

  • Illusory wall next to the ladder near the second rotating bridge mechanism covers a hidden bonfire.
  • Illusory wall behind a skeleton archer breaks to reveal a path to the Darkmoon Seance Ring and a Giant Skeleton trap.
  • Entering the open coffin in the titanite demon crypt allows you to be transported to Gravelord Nito's chamber to join the Gravelord Servant covenant.
  • Stepping off specific ledges during the vertical drops allows you to land safely on platforms below, skipping large sections of the area.

Shops and people

  • Patches: Apologizes after flipping a bridge lever and later appears as an NPC merchant in Firelink Shrine if spared
  • Gravelord Nito: Offers the Gravelord Servant covenant, Gravelord Sword, and Gravelord Sword Dance miracles via the coffin
  • Vamos: Blacksmith who ascends weapons down the Fire and Chaos upgrade paths

Tips

  • Use a Divine weapon to permanently kill standard skeletons even while the Necromancer in the area is still alive.
  • A 100% physical block shield with high stability is essential for surviving Wheel Skeleton ambushes at the bottom canyon.
  • Do not rest at the lower bonfires early in the game without the Lordvessel, as climbing back out of the Catacombs is extremely difficult.
