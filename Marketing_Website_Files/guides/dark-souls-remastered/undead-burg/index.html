Undead Burg

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The first major region after Firelink

The Undead Burg is a sprawling, fortified settlement filled with hollow soldiers, archers, and traps that sits directly above Firelink Shrine. Players must navigate its ramparts, narrow alleyways, and towers to find shortcuts, secure bonfires, and confront the Taurus Demon on the high bridge. Defeating the boss grants access to the bridge leading toward the Undead Parish and the first meeting with Solaire of Astora.

Items

  • Wooden Shield: Near the two spear Hollows guarding the stairs by the merchant
  • Light Crossbow: On a corpse hanging over the ledge in the small courtyard outside the bonfire room
  • Reinforced Club: Purchased from the Undead Merchant (Male)
  • Residence Key: Purchased from the Undead Merchant (Male) or dropped upon his death
  • Bottomless Box: Purchased from the Undead Merchant (Male)
  • Repair Box: Purchased from the Undead Merchant (Male)
  • Orange Guidance Soapstone: Purchased from the Undead Merchant (Male)
  • Gold Pine Resin: Inside a chest inside the locked house accessible with the Residence Key
  • Rubbish: Found on a corpse inside the lower house with the two-handed sword Hollow
  • Black Firebomb: Found on a corpse on the high balcony above the flaming-urn tossers
  • Blue Tearstone Ring: Found on a corpse guarded by the Black Knight at the bottom of the watchtower ramparts
  • Havel's Ring: Dropped by Havel the Rock inside the lowest chamber of the watchtower
  • White Sign Soapstone MISSABLE: Given by Solaire of Astora after talking to him on the balcony past the Taurus Demon
  • Claymore: Found on a corpse on the bridge guarded by the Hellkite Wyvern

Secrets

  • Two shield Hollows stand on a platform hidden beneath breakable crates, concealing a staircase down to the Undead Merchant.
  • Smashing crates in the tower right before the Taurus Demon fog gate reveals a ladder leading to two crossbow hollows who can be eliminated before the boss fight begins.
  • Shooting the tail of the Hellkite Wyvern with a bow from under the bridge cuts it off and yields the Drake Sword.

Shops and people

  • Undead Merchant (Male): Sells basic weapons, chain armor pieces, standard shields, firebombs, throwing knives, repair tools, and keys.
  • Knight Solaire of Astora: Offers co-op allegiance and gives the White Sign Soapstone if spoken to multiple times.

Tips

  • Use a kick (forward + light attack) against shielded spear soldiers to instantly break their guard and open them for a riposte.
  • Clear the two crossbow hollows from the tower above the Taurus Demon before triggering the boss so you can use plunging attacks safely.
  • Kicking the ladder down under the bonfire room opens a permanent shortcut straight back up to the bonfire from the lower square.
