DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · After obtaining the Lordvessel and placing it

After placing the Lordvessel, the golden fog barrier in Anor Londo vanishes, granting access to the vast library fortress of the mad dragon Seath the Scaleless. You must navigate through armed hollows, Channelers, and revolving staircases before facing Seath in a scripted unwinnable battle that sends you to a prison cell. From the prison tower, you must stage a breakout, re-enter the main archives, and solve the path leading out to the Crystal Cave.

Items

  • Archive Tower Giant Cell Key: Looted from the giant Pisaca resting near the bottom of the prison tower
  • Archive Tower Giant Door Key: Found in a chest behind the revolving stairs in the upper main archives
  • Moonlight Greatsword MISSABLE: Cut off Seath the Scaleless's middle rear tail during his true boss fight in the Crystal Cave
  • Avelyn MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest sitting on top of a bookshelf in the archives, reached by dropping from a moving staircase
  • Strong Magic Shield: Inside a treasure chest on the top balcony of the prison tower
  • Crystal Ember: Inside a chest in the final archive room before the courtyard, guarded by a bookshelf

Secrets

  • Re-entering the initial crystal balcony where you first fought Seath after he moves reveals a chest containing the Large Magic Ember, though the room is now infested with Crystal Lizards.
  • Attack the false wall on the lower floor behind the moving staircase near the outdoor balcony to reveal a secret room with treasure chests and the Channeler armor set.

Tips

  • Wear the Rare Ring of Sacrifice during your first encounter with Seath on the crystal elevator platform to prevent losing your souls and humanity upon your scripted death.
  • Equipping the Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring turns off the alert state for the Pisacas, preventing them from swarming you when the alarm is activated.
  • Use a bow to pick off the Channeler on the upper balconies before engaging the Crystal Soldiers below to avoid fighting buffed enemies under sniper fire.
