Darkroot Basin

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Accessed via the valley below the Undead Parish

Darkroot Basin is a deep, shadowy ravine located beneath Darkroot Garden and connected to the Undead Burg watchtower. Players navigate narrow mountain ledges to reach a large lake guarded by a massive Hydra, which must be defeated to access further secrets. The area also houses a hidden bonfire, a shortcut path toward Havel the Rock, and the starting point for the Artorias of the Abyss DLC content.

Items

  • Grass Crest Shield: On a corpse resting along the winding path guarded by a Black Knight
  • Leather Armor Set: On a corpse near the lower level by the water
  • Hunter Bow and Feather Arrows: On the same corpse that holds the Leather Armor Set
  • Knight Armor Set: On a corpse submerged in the shallows of the lake near the Hydra
  • Antiquated Set MISSABLE: On a corpse at the furthest back corner of the lake cave after rescuing Dusk of Oolacile
  • Oolacile Ivory Catalyst MISSABLE: Dropped by Dusk of Oolacile or found with the Antiquated Set

Secrets

  • A bonfire is hidden inside a small cave along the winding cliff path guarded by the Black Knight, providing a safe checkpoint between the Basin and the Valley of Drakes.
  • Defeating the Hydra and reloading the game causes a Golden Crystal Golem to appear in a hidden alcove across the lake; destroying it rescues Dusk of Oolacile.
  • A giant ladder rests to the left of the waterfall near the Hydra, providing a climbable shortcut back up to Darkroot Garden near the river.

Shops and people

  • Dusk of Oolacile: Sells Oolacile sorceries (Cast Light, Chameleon, Hidden Body, Hidden Weapon, Repair) and the Oolacile Ivory Catalyst after being summoned via her sign by the lake

Tips

  • Equip the Rusted Iron Ring before fighting in the lake to retain full movement speed through the deep water.
  • Watch your footing in the lake water, as there is an abrupt underwater drop-off that will cause instant death if you step over the ledge.
  • Raise a high-stability shield right at the edge of the water to safely block the Hydra's physical lunges, then quickly strike the heads while they are stuck in the dirt.
