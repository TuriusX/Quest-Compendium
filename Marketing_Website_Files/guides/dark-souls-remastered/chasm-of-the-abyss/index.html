Chasm of the Abyss

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Beneath the Township

This dark, cavernous area lies beneath Oolacile Township and represents the source of the spreading Abyss. Players must descend winding stone paths and rocky ledges while fending off spectral entities to reach Manus, Father of the Abyss. Along the way, players can rescue the young Great Grey Wolf Sif and locate the captive Princess Dusk.

Items

  • Dark Orb: Found on a corpse on a stone ledge early in the upper section
  • Cleansing Greatshield MISSABLE: Left behind in the spectral projection barrier after rescuing young Sif
  • Black Flame: Found on a corpse near a drop-off guarded by Humanity Phantoms
  • Twin Humanities: Found on a corpse near the bottom floor before the boss fog
  • Soul of Manus: Acquired automatically after defeating Manus, Father of the Abyss

Secrets

  • Follow the illusory path revealed by the glowing spectral Alvina to find an illusory floor, leading down to a magical barrier where young Sif is trapped surrounded by Humanity Phantoms.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Humanity PhantomFireFloats toward the player to deal contact damage; they come in small, medium, and giant sizes.
Oolacile SorceressPhysicalFires dark magic projectile attacks from high vantage points across the chasms.
BloatheadFireAggressive melee enemies that rush down the player on narrow paths.
Manus, Father of the AbyssMagicThe final boss of the DLC who uses heavy physical sweeps, slam combos, and powerful dark magic spells.

Shops and people

  • Sif (Summon Sign): Available as a phantom summon inside the boss arena for Manus if rescued earlier in the Chasm

Tips

  • Equip and use the Silver Pendant tool to create a temporary protective barrier that deflects Manus's dark magic projectile attacks.
  • Equipping a Light Cast spell, the Sunlight Maggot, or a Skull Lantern greatly helps in spotting safe footing on the dark downward slopes.
