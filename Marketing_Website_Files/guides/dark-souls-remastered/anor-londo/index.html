Anor Londo

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The city of the gods reached after Sen's Fortress

Arriving via the bat-wing demons after Sen's Fortress, the player must navigate the sprawling city of the gods, bypassing defensive sentinels, painted guardians, and rooftop archers. The main objective is to infiltrate the cathedral to defeat Dragon Slayer Ornstein and Executioner Smough. Overcoming them grants access to Gwynevere, who gives the Lordvessel required to unlock the final areas of the game.

Items

  • Ring of the Sun's Firstborn: On a corpse at the base of the central rotating elevator platform
  • Black Iron Set: On a corpse in the left corner behind the giant painting inside the cathedral's ground floor
  • Havel's Set: Found inside a hidden basement room behind an illusory wall in the main interior manor
  • Occult Club: Dropped by a Mimic chest inside the hidden basement alongside Havel's Set
  • Dragonslayer Greatbow: Found outside the broken stained glass window on the upper walkway of the main cathedral hall
  • Lordvessel: Given by Gwynevere in the Chamber of the Princess after defeating Ornstein and Smough

Secrets

  • The Darkmoon Tomb bonfire and covenant area can be accessed by rotating the central tower mechanism to its lowest position.
  • A fireplace inside the room containing Silver Knights acts as an illusory wall leading down to Havel's equipment and a Mimic.
  • Interacting with the giant canvas painting in the cathedral while holding the Peculiar Doll transports the player to the Painted World of Ariamis.

Shops and people

  • Giant Blacksmith: Ascends Boss Soul weapons, modifies +10 weapons to Lightning, and sells Twinkling Titanite alongside Giant equipment
  • Darkmoon Knightess: Upgrades Estus Flasks and serves as the caretaker for the initial Anor Londo bonfire

Tips

  • When running up the narrow buttress toward the Silver Knight archers, sprint continuously without stopping to dodge the greatarrows until reaching melee range.
  • In the boss fight against Ornstein and Smough, choose who to kill first based on the desired reward, as the remaining boss absorbs the other's power, refills their health bar, and drops their respective soul and armor set.
  • Attacking Gwynevere will dispel the illusion of Anor Londo, turning the sky dark and making the Darkmoon Knightess hostile.
