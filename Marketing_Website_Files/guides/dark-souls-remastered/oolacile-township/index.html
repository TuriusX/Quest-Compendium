Oolacile Township

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Beyond the Royal Wood

Oolacile Township is a corrupted, cascading city that players enter after defeating Artorias the Abysswalker in the Royal Wood. To progress, you must navigate treacherous staircases, rooftops, and dark alleys filled with mutated citizens and sorcerers to reach the Chasm of the Abyss. Along the way, players can unlock shortcuts back to the entrance bonfire and uncover deep lore regarding the Abyss corruption.

Items

  • Silver Pendant MISSABLE: behind an illusory wall that opens with a light source on an upper balcony
  • Dark Orb: dropped on a corpse inside an Oolacile residence guarded by several bloatheads
  • Crest Key MISSABLE: dropped by a giant Chained Prisoner on the lower level near the elevator shortcut
  • I引导/Dark Fog: on a corpse hanging over a ledge in a lower alleyway filled with bloathead sorcerers

Secrets

  • Illusory wall requiring a light source | cast Cast Light, equip the Sunlight Maggot, or use the Skull Lantern in front of the wall reading "Let there be light" on the upper terrace to reveal a room holding the Silver Pendant
  • Shortcut elevator | near the mid-point of the township, step on the stone plate inside a tower lift to create a quick path connecting back to the initial Oolacile Township bonfire

Tips

  • Equip the Silver Pendant in an active item slot once found, as activating it generates a barrier that deflects dark magic attacks from sorceresses and future abyssal bosses.
  • The dark sorceries used by enemies in this area deal both physical and magical damage, making pure magic-defense shields less effective than high-stability physical shields.
  • Take the Crest Key found from the Chained Prisoner up the arena tower in the Royal Wood to unlock Hawkeye Gough's workshop.
