Royal Wood

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Beyond the Sanctuary

This expansive forest area connects the Sanctuary Garden to Oolacile Township and the Chasm of the Abyss. Players must navigate past patrolling stone guardians and wild scarecrows to unlock shortcuts and reach the colosseum boss arena. Progressing through the woods also allows the player to find keys and pathways leading to Kalameet's valley and Hawkeye Gough.

Items

  • Guardian Helm: On a corpse guarded by Stone Guardians in a small pond area
  • Guardian Gauntlets: On a corpse guarded by Stone Guardians in the Royal Wood clearing
  • Guardian Leggings: On a corpse near the treasure guardians in the woods
  • Guardian Armor: On a corpse located in a clearing within the Royal Wood
  • Gough's Great Arrow: On a corpse near the edge of the ravine overlooking Kalameet
  • Blue Titanite Slab: Inside a chest submerged in the pool of water where two pond guardians patrol
  • Crest Key: Dropped by a Mimic chest hidden inside a ruined building on the approach to Artorias

Secrets

  • The elevator shortcut near Artorias's arena can be unlocked to provide a quick run back to the Sanctuary Garden bonfire.
  • Kalameet can be grounded for a manageable fight by unlocking Hawkeye Gough's tower with the Crest Key and requesting his archery aid.

Tips

  • Stone Guardians take significant damage from Strike weapons and can be safely lured into single combat.
  • Do not attempt to fight Black Dragon Kalameet in the valley until Hawkeye Gough shoots him down, as he will otherwise kill you with flying fire breath.
  • The elevator shortcut next to the bridge drastically cuts down runback times if you die to Knight Artorias.
