Firelink Shrine acts as the central hub of Lordran where you arrive after escaping the Undead Asylum. It connects to several key regions, including the Undead Burg, the Catacombs, New Londo Ruins, and the Undead Parish via shortcut elevators. Players frequently return here to level up, meet assembling NPCs, rekindle their Estus Flask, and branch out toward the two Bells of Awakening.

Enemies
Skeleton
Weak to Strike damage, Divine weapons

Lurks in the graveyard; will continually reassemble unless defeated with a divine weapon in the Catacombs, though these surface ones die normally.

Giant Skeleton
Weak to Strike damage

High health and hits hard; guards the Zweihander in the graveyard.

Shops and people
  • Crestfallen Warrior: Offers hints, advice, and sarcastic dialogue regarding the two bells
  • Anastacia of Astora: Fire Keeper who reinforces Estus Flasks using Fire Keeper Souls
  • Petrus of Thorolund: Sells basic Miracles and the Way of White covenant membership

Tips
  • Avoid heading into the graveyard or down into the flooded ruins of New Londo at the very start; the intended early path is up the cliffside stairs into the Undead Burg.
  • Resting at the Firelink Shrine bonfire restores 10 Estus Flask charges by default instead of the usual 5.
  • Do not attack the Crestfallen Warrior sitting near the bonfire early on, as he will persistently attack you upon every respawn.
