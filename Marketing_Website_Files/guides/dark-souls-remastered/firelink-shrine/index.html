Firelink Shrine

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The hub area after the tutorial

Firelink Shrine acts as the central hub of Lordran where you arrive after escaping the Undead Asylum. It connects to several key regions, including the Undead Burg, the Catacombs, New Londo Ruins, and the Undead Parish via shortcut elevators. Players frequently return here to level up, meet assembling NPCs, rekindle their Estus Flask, and branch out toward the two Bells of Awakening.

Items

  • Estus Flask Shard: This item does not exist in Dark Souls 1; leave out false memories
  • Homeward Bone: Found in a chest under the elevator shaft near the flooded ruin area
  • Lloyd's Talisman: Inside one of the chests down the pit shaft accessible once the elevators are activated
  • Morning Star: Found in a chest behind the breakable pots near the elevator drop shaft
  • Cracked Red Eye Orb: Found in a hidden chest down the shaft below the elevator route to Undead Parish
  • Talisman: Found in a chest down the drop-shaft area leading to the graveyard cliffs
  • Zweihander: Located in the graveyard area, guarded by giant skeletons near a large tombstone
  • Winged Spear: Found on a corpse in the graveyard behind several resting skeletons
  • Binoculars: Found on a ledge at the far end of the graveyard overlooking the cliff
  • Caduceus Round Shield: Found in the graveyard section resting near a gravestone
  • Ring of Sacrifice: Found on a corpse underneath the bridge arch leading toward the Undead Burg aqueduct
  • Fire Keeper Soul: Located down the stairs past Anastacia behind bars, or looted if Anastacia is killed

Secrets

  • The giant crow's nest can be reached by riding the Undead Parish elevator up a short distance, stepping off onto the ruined roof, and rolling to the buttress to return to the Undead Asylum.
  • Dropping down the empty elevator shafts after unlocking the Undead Parish shortcut reveals four hidden treasure chests and an alternate exit path.

Shops and people

  • Crestfallen Warrior: Offers hints, advice, and sarcastic dialogue regarding the two bells
  • Anastacia of Astora: Fire Keeper who reinforces Estus Flasks using Fire Keeper Souls
  • Petrus of Thorolund: Sells basic Miracles and the Way of White covenant membership

Tips

  • Avoid heading into the graveyard or down into the flooded ruins of New Londo at the very start; the intended early path is up the cliffside stairs into the Undead Burg.
  • Resting at the Firelink Shrine bonfire restores 10 Estus Flask charges by default instead of the usual 5.
  • Do not attack the Crestfallen Warrior sitting near the bonfire early on, as he will persistently attack you upon every respawn.
