Kiln of the First Flame

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Accessed after placing all Lord Souls
← All areas in this guide

This is the final area of the game, reached by offering all four Lord Souls to the Lordvessel beneath Firelink Shrine. You traverse a winding pathway through ash and ruined architecture guarded by respawning Black Knights to reach the final arena. At the bottom lies Gwyn, Lord of Cinder, whom you must defeat to trigger the ending of the game.

Don't miss 0/1

Achievements here
To Link the Fire MissableDefeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder, and interact with the central bonfire without leaving the arena to trigger the linking ending.
Dark Lord MissableDefeat Gwyn, Lord of Cinder, and exit the arena through the doorway without lighting the final bonfire.

Gwyn, Lord of Cinder
Weak to Parry, Fire, Occult

Highly aggressive final boss who can be repeatedly parried and riposted

Tips
  • The Black Knights here respawn indefinitely, making this the best location in the game to farm Titanite Chunks, colored Chunks, and Titanite Slabs.
  • Solaire of Astora can be summoned just before the fog gate if you saved him from the Chaos Bug in Lost Izalith.
  • Equipping the Hornet Ring makes Gwyn significantly easier by vastly increasing the damage of your parry ripostes.
  • Defeating Gwyn immediately ends your current playthrough; if you walk out of the arena or touch the bonfire, you will be thrust directly into New Game Plus.
