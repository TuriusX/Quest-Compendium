Painted World of Ariamis

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The optional area accessed via the painting in Anor Londo

Accessible by examining the giant painting in Anor Londo while carrying the Peculiar Doll, this snowy, secluded area acts as a separate sub-world. Players must navigate crumbling ruins, an overrun courtyard, and an underground sewer system to open the main gate leading to the final courtyard. You cannot warp out using the Lordvessel and must reach the boss area at the far end to return to Anor Londo.

Items

  • Peculiar Doll MISSABLE: In the player's starting cell in the Undead Asylum upon revisiting
  • Red Sign Soapstone: Located at the very top of the central spiral staircase tower on a wooden plank
  • Dark Ember: Found on a petrified blacksmith in the courtyard annex building behind a locked door
  • Annex Key: Found in the underground catacombs where the Wheel Skeletons patrol
  • Bloodshield: Found on the long bridge guarded by the Undead Dragon, just past its front half
  • Velka's Rapier: Found on a corpse behind a wooden breakable barricade in the annex building
  • Black Set: Found on a corpse in the annex building near Velka's Rapier
  • Xanthous Set MISSABLE: Found on a corpse behind Crossbreed Priscilla after defeating the invader Xanthous King Jeremiah
  • Soul of Priscilla MISSABLE: Acquired automatically upon killing Crossbreed Priscilla
  • Priscilla's Dagger MISSABLE: Cut off Crossbreed Priscilla's tail before killing her

Secrets

  • You can bypass turning the wheel in the catacombs by performing a jumping attack on the severed hindquarters of the Undead Dragon on the high bridge, causing it to stand up and allow passage beneath it.
  • Xanthous King Jeremiah invades as a red phantom in the right-side graveyard area of the open courtyard only if the player is in human form and Priscilla is alive.
  • There are multiple illusory walls inside the subterranean sewer area hiding Wheel Skeletons and the mechanism wheel that opens the main shortcut gate.

Shops and people

  • Crossbreed Priscilla: Dialogue to leave peacefully without fighting

Tips

  • Bring Blooming Purple Moss Clumps to cure the heavy toxic status inflicted by the Engorged Zombies and Snow Rats.
  • Equip a weapon that deals fire damage or use Pyromancy to prevent Engorged Zombies from releasing their toxic cloud upon death.
  • Striking Crossbreed Priscilla starts the boss fight permanently for that playthrough, preventing you from using her peaceful exit until she is defeated.
  • Once you enter the painting, you cannot leave until you reach the exit plank behind Priscilla's arena, so ensure your gear is upgraded beforehand.
