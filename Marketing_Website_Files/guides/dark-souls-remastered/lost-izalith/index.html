Lost Izalith

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The lava-filled city beyond the Demon Ruins

Lost Izalith is a subterranean domain submerged in lava, featuring ancient stone ruins and twisted, corrupted architecture. Players navigate across the vast magma lake using stone structures to reach the inner ruins and the heart of the Bed of Chaos. Progressing through this zone requires eliminating the primary boss to claim a Lord Soul to feed the Lordvessel.

Items

  • Orange Charred Ring: Defeat the Centipede Demon just before entering the lava basin
  • Sunlight Maggot MISSABLE: Dropped by a specific red-eyed Chaos Bug in the shortcut corridor
  • Soul of a Great Hero: Found inside a hollow tree structure in the lava plains
  • Red Titanite Slab MISSABLE: Located at the bottom of the poisoned pit area near the Chaos Eaters
  • Catalyst of Izalith: Dropped by the hostile Daughter of Chaos guarding the fog gate to the boss

Secrets

  • Inside the square stone pagoda surrounded by lava, attack the walls to reveal an illusory wall hiding a bonfire.
  • A massive stone door serves as a shortcut linking the Demon Ruins and Lost Izalith, openable by achieving Rank +2 in the Chaos Servant covenant or from the Izalith side.

Tips

  • Equip the Orange Charred Ring at all times while walking through the open lava to drastically reduce continuous fire damage.
  • The limbs of the Bed of Chaos do not reset their destruction status if you die, allowing you to tackle the boss in stages.
