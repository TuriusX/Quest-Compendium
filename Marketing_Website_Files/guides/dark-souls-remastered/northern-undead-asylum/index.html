Northern Undead Asylum

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · The tutorial and starting area

The Northern Undead Asylum serves as the starting tutorial area where the player escapes their cell, gathers their starting gear, and learns the core gameplay mechanics. Players encounter the Asylum Demon boss early, bypass it via a side exit, and return later from a high ledge to defeat it. Once the boss is defeated and the exit key is obtained, the player ascends the mountain path to be carried to Lordran by a giant crow.

Items

  • Dungeon Cell Key: Dropped by a corpse through the cell ceiling at the start
  • Starting Weapon: On a corpse in the hallway after fleeing the Asylum Demon through the iron gate
  • Starting Shield: On a corpse in a side room guarded by an archer, up the stairs past the first hallway
  • Estus Flask: Given by Oscar, Knight of Astora, in the broken wall room up the boulder stairs
  • Undead Asylum F2 East Key: Given by Oscar, Knight of Astora, alongside the Estus Flask
  • Big Pilgrim's Key: Dropped by the Asylum Demon upon its defeat

Secrets

  • Revisit the Asylum later by curling into a ball in the giant crow's nest at Firelink Shrine.
  • Snuggly the Crow will trade rare items in exchange for specific items left in its nest on the ledge outside.

Shops and people

  • Oscar, Knight of Astora: Offers the Estus Flask and the Undead Asylum F2 East Key when spoken to in his dying state

Tips

  • Run straight past the Asylum Demon during your first encounter through the open iron gate on the left side of the room.
  • A large iron boulder rolls down the stairs after you step onto the staircase; roll aside quickly to avoid heavy damage and open the path to Oscar.
  • Walk slowly toward the fog gate ledge before fighting the Asylum Demon a second time to execute a plunging attack for massive damage.
