Northern Undead Asylum (Revisited Asylum)

DARK SOULS™: REMASTERED guide · Returning to retrieve the Peculiar Doll and fight the Stray Demon

The player returns to the Northern Undead Asylum via the giant crow's nest in Firelink Shrine to explore newly unlocked and altered areas. This revisit features tough new enemies, Oscar of Astora turned hostile, the Stray Demon boss lurking beneath the broken floor, and the item needed to enter the Painted World of Ariamis.

Items

  • Peculiar Doll: In the starting cell where the player first woke up, guarded by a Black Knight
  • Crest Shield: Dropped by the hollowed Oscar of Astora in the side room where he originally gave you the Estus Flask
  • Titanite Slab: Dropped upon defeating the Stray Demon beneath the central courtyard
  • Rusted Iron Ring: Behind a locked gate on the upper third-floor walkway, requiring the Undead Asylum F2 West Key
  • Undead Asylum F2 West Key: Found earlier on a rooftop in Firelink Shrine, used to open the gate to the Rusted Iron Ring

Secrets

  • Falling through the center of the main boss hall floor triggers the Stray Demon fight and inflicts fall damage; tread carefully or avoid the center entirely until prepared.
  • Snuggly the Crow will trade rare upgrade materials and rings if you drop specific items into her nest on the cliff edge, quit out, and reload.

Shops and people

  • Snuggly the Crow: Trades items dropped into her nest for valuable goods like Twinkling Titanite and the Ring of Fog upon reloading the game.

Tips

  • Equip the Fall Control spell or strip off heavy armor to minimize falling damage when dropping down to fight the Stray Demon.
  • Stray Demon is extremely susceptible to bleed-inflicting weapons such as the Bandit's Knife, Morning Star, or Uchigatana.
  • Retrieve the Rusted Iron Ring while here, as it restores normal movement speed in deep water and poison swamps for later areas like Blighttown.
