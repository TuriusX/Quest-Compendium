Overgrown Ruins

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The crypt area near the crash site

The Overgrown Ruins is an ancient dungeon located east of the crashed Nautiloid containing the Refectory, the Dank Crypt, and the Tomb of Jergal. Adventurers must deal with a band of opportunistic scavengers, overcome deadly fire traps, and defeat revived undead scribes. Exploring deep within unlocks the mysterious undead companion Withers, who serves as a vital camp ally for resurrection and respeccing.

Items

  • Soul Coin: Inside a small sarcophagus in the southern side room of the Dank Crypt
  • Soul Coin: Looted from the ornate sarcophagus that Withers steps out of
  • Soul Coin: In a chest inside the hidden coastal passage alcove leading to the crypt
  • The Watcher's Guide: Inside the central, heavily trapped sarcophagus in the southern Dank Crypt chamber
  • Amulet of Lost Voices: Inside a heavy chest located inside the secret tomb room where Withers rests
  • Engraved Key: Looted from the bandit leader Taman or findable inside the Refectory

Secrets

  • Shooting the coiled rope holding a hanging block above the ruins' exterior courtyard smashes the stone floor open, creating an immediate shortcut down into the Refectory.
  • Pressing an inconspicuous skull-shaped stone button on the wall behind the giant Jergal statue in the Dank Crypt opens the hidden entryway to Withers' resting chamber.
  • Triggering the main sarcophagus in the Dank Crypt causes floor vents to flood the room with grease and wall-mounted gargoyle heads to shoot fire darts.
  • Placing mundane heavy objects such as pots, vases, or crates on top of the floor vents in the sarcophagus room prevents the explosive grease from spewing out entirely.

Shops and people

  • Withers: Offers character class re-specialization, hireling recruitment, and companion resurrection for set gold fees once he relocates to camp

Tips

  • Loot the weapons off the skeletons in the inner sanctum before opening the secret tomb button so they fight unarmed when reanimated.
  • A lever located behind a statue in the room right after the exterior wooden door unlocks the reinforced door leading deeper inside without needing lockpicks.
  • You can enter the ruins through four separate routes: picking the coastal locked door, jumping down through the smashed floor hole, picking the wooden door near the beach, or lockpicking the wooden hatch further up the cliff.
