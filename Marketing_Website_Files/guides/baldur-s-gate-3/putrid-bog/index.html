Putrid Bog

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The corrupted section of the wetlands

The Putrid Bog is the true, unmasked state of the Sunlit Wetlands, revealed after uncovering Auntie Ethel's illusion or interacting with specific creatures. Players explore this decaying swamp to confront redcaps, avoid poisonous environmental traps, and locate Ethel's teahouse. Navigating carefully allows the party to uncover hidden caches, speak to tormented animals, and prepare for the deeper descent into the hag's lair.

Items

  • The Sparkle Hands: Inside a wooden chest tucked away on an island in the south-central waters
  • Wood Woad Shield MISSABLE: Dropped by the Wood Woad enemies on the secluded mud islet
  • Letter to Kagha MISSABLE: Inside the hollow of the Twisted Tree on the southeastern mud bank

Secrets

  • Touching an addlepated frog near the northern cliffs triggers a DC 13 Animal Handling check; succeeding calms it, and defeating Ethel later makes it lead you to a hidden treasure pouch.
  • Passing an Investigation check on the illusory sheep or environment immediately dispels the picturesque Sunlit Wetlands glamour, revealing the Putrid Bog without needing to interact with Ethel.

Tips

  • Drink an Elixir of Poison Resistance before wading through the water and spiked traps to significantly reduce hazard damage.
  • Use Jump or Misty Step to navigate the deep mire, avoiding the difficult terrain and hidden spikes scattered across the swamp floor.
