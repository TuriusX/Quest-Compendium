Shadowfell

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The plane of existence accessed via the Gauntlet

The Shadowfell is a shadowy pocket dimension entered through the pool in the Verge of Shadows at the bottom of the Gauntlet of Shar. Entering this realm is the major point of no return for Act 2, locking out various unfinished regional quests. Here, you confront Balthazar and determine the fate of the Nightsong, which fundamentally shapes the story's trajectory.

Items

  • Moonlight Glaive MISSABLE: Given as a reward by the Nightsong if she is spared
  • Selûne's Spear of Night MISSABLE: Given by the Nightsong at your camp later if she is spared and Shadowheart chose to spare her
  • Shar's Spear of Evening MISSABLE: Awarded by Shar if Shadowheart kills the Nightsong
  • Dark Justiciar Half-Plate (Very Rare) MISSABLE: Awarded by Shar if Shadowheart kills the Nightsong

Tips

  • Entering the Shadowfell automatically resolves many Act 2 quests, so rescue the prisoners at Moonrise Towers and complete the Halsin portal defense before interacting with the pool.
  • The environment has permanent low-gravity jumping, allowing characters to leap massive distances between the floating rock platforms.
  • Be extremely cautious with positioning on the central platform, as Balthazar's summoned skeletons frequently use shoving abilities to push party members into the void for instant death.
  • If you plan to spare the Nightsong, allowing Shadowheart to make her own choice without forcing her hand often leads to her casting the Spear of Night away naturally.
