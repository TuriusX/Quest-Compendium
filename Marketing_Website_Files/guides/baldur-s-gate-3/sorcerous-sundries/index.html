Sorcerous Sundries

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The magic shop and library in the Lower City

Sorcerous Sundries is the premier arcane shop in the Lower City run by the wizard Lorroakan via his projection. Players can browse high-level magical wares, resolve the Nightsong's personal questline on the upper floors, and break into the underground vaults for legendary spellbooks and artifacts. Navigating the vaults requires bypassing arcane locks, solving puzzle doors named after famous wizards, and disarming deadly magical traps.

Items

  • The Red Knight's Final Move MISSABLE: Inside the Vault of Sorcerous Sundries behind the Karsus door
  • The Tharciate Codex MISSABLE: In the Elminster section of the Sorcerous Sundries Vault
  • Markoheshkir MISSABLE: Inside Ramazith's Tower lower level beneath a globe of invulnerability requiring an Arcana check or See Invisibility
  • Robe of the Weave MISSABLE: Inside Ramazith's Tower lower level beneath an arcane barrier

Secrets

  • Stepping through the portal on the top floor requires choosing the plaque that reads "The Nightsong is an immortal being, the child of a deity" to reach Lorroakan safely.
  • Accessing the Sorcerous Sundries Vault can be done via a hidden portal button in Ramazith's Tower or by lockpicking the clasp in the upstairs office.
  • In the Sorcerous Sundries Vault, navigating the doors named Silverhand, Evocation, and Wish in sequence unlocks the pathway to the Elminster vault.
  • In the Sorcerous Sundries Vault, navigating the doors named Silverhand, Abjuration, and Silver in sequence unlocks the pathway to the Karsus vault.

Shops and people

  • Rolan (or Lorroakan's Projection): Sells top-tier spell scrolls up to Level 6, unique magical gear, and rare wizard supplies.

Tips

  • Reading The Tharciate Codex removes the curse of the Necromancy of Thay and grants a permanent bonus to maximum hit points after enduring a temporary curse.
  • Bring Gale into the vaults, as reading the rare tomes provides significant inspiration and advances his personal companion quest regarding the Crown of Karsus.
  • Keep a character with high Sleight of Hand and Trap Disarm Toolkits ready, as the vault floors are lined with pressurized fire and lightning glyphs.
