Wyrm's Rock Fortress

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The fortress guarding the city entrance

Wyrm's Rock Fortress bridges Rivington and the Lower City, serving as the Flaming Fist's primary stronghold and the site of Enver Gortash's archduke coronation. You must find a way past the raised drawbridge to attend the ceremony, explore the prison depths, or eliminate Gortash in his private chambers. Beneath the fortress lies an ancient trial complex and the lair of the legendary bronze dragon Ansur.

Items

  • Netherstone: Carried by Enver Gortash on the upper levels
  • Helldusk Boots MISSABLE: Inside an opulent chest behind Enver Gortash's throne in the audience hall
  • Balduran's Giantslayer MISSABLE: Looted from the dragon Ansur in the Dragon's Sanctum beneath the prison
  • Helm of Balduran MISSABLE: Resting on an altar in the Dragon's Sanctum behind Ansur's corpse

Secrets

  • An entrance to the Wyrmway trial complex can be revealed in the fortress prison by extinguishing two dragon head wall torches and hitting them with lightning damage.
  • A wooden scaffold on the eastern external wall lets you bypass the guarded main entrance entirely via climbing and jumping.
  • Breaking Counsellor Florrick out of the prison cells safely requires walking her past guards using Invisibility or the secret passage leading to the outer cliffs.

Shops and people

  • Quartermaster Arklemy: Sells high-tier potions, camp supplies, and basic armor in the central barracks

Tips

  • Attend Gortash's coronation peaceably on your first visit to avoid triggering hostile encounters with dozens of Steel Watchers simultaneously.
  • You can leap down from the bridge to the outer lower rocks using Feather Fall to reach the prison break-in pipe without interacting with the guards.
  • Disable the Steel Watch Foundry in the Lower City before fighting Gortash to remove all Steel Watchers from the fortress.
