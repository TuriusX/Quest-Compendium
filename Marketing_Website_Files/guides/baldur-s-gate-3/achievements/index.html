Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Entering the pool to the Shadowfell in the Gauntlet of Shar: Resolves all ongoing Act 1 and Act 2 wilderness quests, locks access to Act 1 waypoints, permanently relocates Moonrise Towers NPCs, and causes any unrescued prisoners in Moonrise Towers Prison to perish.
  • Taking the road to the Lower City past the Wyrm's Rock Fortress checkpoint: Permanently locks access to all Act 1 and Act 2 regions and camps.
  • Taking the skiff from the Morphic Pool in the Undercity Ruins: Locks the player into the endgame sequence; all Lower City side quests, companions' personal arcs, and open-world exploration become permanently inaccessible.

Recommended order
  1. Nautiloid: Defeat Commander Zhalk before activating the transponder ("Devil's in the Details").
  2. Ravaged Beach & Overgrown Ruins: Recruit companions, loot thieves, and obtain the first shovel to start unearthing buried dirt mounds ("Dig for Victory").
  3. Emerald Grove & Hollow: Save Arabella, protect Arka, rescue the goblin Sazza from her makeshift cage ("She Cannot be Caged!" step 1), and invite Scratch the dog to camp.
  4. Druid Grove Underground Passage: Neutralize the goblin scouts and safely escort Sazza outside the grove.
  5. Sunlit Wetlands & Putrid Bog: Confront Auntie Ethel to rescue Mayrina, acquire Bitter Divorce, and summon Ethel's thralls.
  6. Ancient Abode: Defeat Auntie Ethel in her lair, spare Mayrina, and use the wand to resurrect Connor outside.
  7. Blighted Village & Whispering Depths: Enter the apothecary cellar to locate the summoning scroll for the quasit Shovel ("Rude, Crude, and Full of Attitude"), then enter the Whispering Depths and eliminate the Phase Spider Matriarch before any spider eggs hatch ("Pest Control").
  8. Goblin Camp: Rescue the captured Owlbear cub, speak with Volo, and guarantee Sazza is not fed to the spiders.
  9. Shattered Sanctum: Rescue Halsin, speak to Minthara with Sazza present to secure her freedom once more ("She Cannot be Caged!" step 2), and eliminate the three goblin leaders while keeping the tieflings safe for "Leave No One Behind".
  10. Defiled Temple: Solve the lunar puzzle to unlock passage downwards into the Underdark.
  11. Underdark & Selûnite Outpost: Disarm the defenses, collect Sussur Bark, and pet both Scratch and the Owlbear cub simultaneously at camp once both settle in ("You Have Two Hands for a Reason").
  12. Decrepit Village & Arcane Tower: Defeat the Duergar raiders, repair Bernard's elevator in the Arcane Tower, and craft a Masterwork Weapon.
  13. Grymforge: Craft an item at the Adamantine Forge ("Forged in Blood and Fire") and defeat Grym without activating the forge hammer ("A Grym Fate").
  14. Risen Road & Tollhouse: Recruit Karlach, confront the paladins of Tyr at the Tollhouse, and save the trapped caravan guards.
  15. Waukeen's Rest: Rescue Florrick from the burning inn and save the trapped groom to preserve civilian lives.
