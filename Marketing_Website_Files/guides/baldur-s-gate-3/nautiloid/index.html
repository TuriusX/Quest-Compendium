The Nautiloid serves as the tutorial area where your infected protagonist awakens aboard a crashing illithid flying ship traversing the planes. You must recruit allies like Lae'zel, potentially free companions like Shadowheart, and fight through imps and hellish thralls to reach the helm. Once in the control room, your primary objective is to connect the transponder before the ship crashes.

Enemies
Commander Zhalk
Steal / drop Everburn Blade

A tough cambion fighting a mind flayer at the helm who ignores the party unless provoked or attacked.

Lesser Imp

Flying devil minions encountered on the ship deck and throughout the helm corridor.

Tips
  • Prepare the Command spell on Shadowheart to cast Command: Drop on Commander Zhalk, allowing you to easily pick up the Everburn Blade without having to kill him.
  • Stack the heavy chests found on the ship in the entryway to the helm to create a barricade that stops enemy Cambion reinforcements from joining the fight.
