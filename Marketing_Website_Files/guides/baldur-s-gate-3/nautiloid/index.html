Nautiloid

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The prologue sequence on the mind flayer ship

You awaken trapped aboard an alien mind flayer vessel under attack by githyanki red dragon riders. To escape, you must navigate through burning corridors, recruit available survivors, and reach the bridge to interact with the ship's transponder. Reaching and activating the transponder before the ship crashes or before powerful enemies overwhelm you will complete the prologue.

Items

  • Eldritch Rune MISSABLE: Looted from the body of a dead thrall in the side room to the east of the console
  • Everburn Blade MISSABLE: Looted from Commander Zhalk on the ship's bridge
  • Gold Key MISSABLE: Carried by the dead Mind Flayer in the pod room next to the trapped woman
  • Onyx MISSABLE: Found inside a cartilage chest on the flesh ramp in the second room

Secrets

  • You can free an intellect devourer named "Us" from a corpse's skull on the upper platform of the starting room; a successful Investigation or Medicine check lets you extract it safely so it temporarily joins your party.
  • A captive named Shadowheart is trapped inside a mind flayer pod; by placing the Eldritch Rune into the nearby console, you can release her to immediately recruit her.

Shops and people

  • Restoration Pod: Free full heal and restores all spell slots and short rests

Tips

  • Clerics like Shadowheart can prepare the level 1 spell "Command" before the bridge fight to force Commander Zhalk to "Drop" his two-handed Everburn Blade, allowing you to easily pick it up without needing to kill him.
  • You can stack purple Nautiloid Tanks around Commander Zhalk and ignite them with fire damage to deal massive explosive burst damage.
  • Additional cambion reinforcements arrive on the bridge when the turn counter reaches around 9 or 10, so focus on reaching the transponder rather than lingering.
