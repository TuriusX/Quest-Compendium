Lower City

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The main urban district of Baldur's Gate

The Lower City serves as the primary urban hub of Act 3, filled with interconnected faction quests, high-stakes boss confrontations, and extensive companion storylines. Players must navigate political intrigue, track down a serial killer targeting the city's residents, and dismantle the plans of both Lord Enver Gortash and Orin the Red. The area connects to critical sub-locations including the Lower City Sewers, Cazador's Palace, the House of Grief, and the Undercity Ruins.

Items

  • Helldusk Helmet MISSABLE: Inside the Treasury room of the House of Hope, accessible via the Devil's Fee in the northern section
  • Markoheshkir MISSABLE: Inside Ramazith's Tower, protected by an arcane barrier on the level below Lorroakan's study
  • Robe of the Weave MISSABLE: Inside Ramazith's Tower, inside an arcane barrier next to Markoheshkir
  • Gontr Mael MISSABLE: Looted from the Steel Watch Titan inside the Steel Watch Foundry
  • Bhaalist Armor MISSABLE: Sold by Echo of Abdirak inside the Murder Tribunal under Candulhallow's Tombstones after becoming an Unholy Assassin

Secrets

  • The Murder Tribunal entrance is hidden behind a painting of a skull inside Candulhallow's Tombstones, requiring a password found on the serial killer's targets list.
  • A portal to the House of Hope can be opened on the top floor of the Devil's Fee by arranging ritual items on a bloody pentagram given by Helsik.

Shops and people

  • Helsik (Devil's Fee): Sells powerful rare artifacts, grimoires, and grants passage to the House of Hope.
  • Sorcerous Sundries: Sells high-level spell scrolls, magical robes, and wizard gear through an illusory projection of Lorroakan.
  • Bonecloak's Apothecary: Sells alchemy ingredients, elixirs, and potions managed by Derryth Bonecloak.
  • Stormshore Armoury: Sells high-tier heavy armor, weapons, and shields through two rival smiths.

Tips

  • Casting Daylight directly onto the center of Cazador's arena severely weakens him by halting his regeneration and preventing him from turning into mist.
  • You can use the spell Disintegrate or Lightning damage to instantly bypass or deal heavy damage to the Steel Watchers patrolling the streets.
  • Look for the hidden basement button behind a bookshelf inside the Elfsong Tavern kitchen to access the Knights of the Shield hideout.
