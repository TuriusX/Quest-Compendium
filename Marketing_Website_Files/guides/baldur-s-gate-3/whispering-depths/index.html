Whispering Depths

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The spider-infested caves beneath the village

The Whispering Depths is an underground cavern network located beneath the Blighted Village, overrun by hostile Phase Spiders and Ettercaps. Players venture here to clear the arachnid infestation, locate High Elf wizard Ilyn Toth's lost research materials, and recover a dark gem. A massive chasm in the deepest chamber can also be used as a direct shortcut to the Underdark.

Items

  • Dark Amethyst MISSABLE: Sitting on the ground near the bones in the lowest nesting area beneath the Phase Spider Matriarch's web bridges
  • Poisoner's Robe MISSABLE: Dropped as loot by the Phase Spider Matriarch upon defeating her
  • Spiderstep Boots MISSABLE: Found inside a heavy chest in the laboratory/workshop nook in the upper caverns
  • Apprentice's Backpack: Resting near a bedroll and stone pillar in the upper cavern camp area

Secrets

  • The bottomless green-glowing chasm in the center of the lair drops straight into the Underdark; jumping into it with the Feather Fall spell allows safe traversal to the subterranean region below.

Tips

  • Burning the web bridges while the Phase Spider Matriarch is standing on them causes her to fall, dealing massive bludgeoning damage.
  • Equipping the Spiderstep Boots prevents your character from being enwebbed or slowed down while walking on spider webs.
  • Use sneak and ranged fire attacks to destroy Phase Spider egg clutches from a distance before engaging the Matriarch to prevent her from hatching them.
  • If the Phase Spider Matriarch falls into the central chasm, you will lose the chance to loot the Poisoner's Robe immediately (though her body can be found later in the Underdark).
