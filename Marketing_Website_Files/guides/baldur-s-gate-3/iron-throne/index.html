Iron Throne

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The underwater prison for the Gondians

The Iron Throne is an underwater, timed rescue facility where Lord Gortash holds Duke Ravengard, the families of the Gondian factory workers, and Omeluum hostage. Upon docking, Gortash initiates a self-destruct sequence, giving you a strict turn limit to free as many prisoners as possible from their cells and sprint back to the submersible ladder before the station implodes.

Items

  • Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen MISSABLE: On a table in the laboratory section inside the complex

Secrets

  • You can rescue the mind flayer Omeluum in the southwest flooded wing; he has a unique teleport spell that immediately transports himself and one companion directly into the submersible.

Enemies

Tips

  • Pre-cast Longstrider on the entire party and bring multiple scrolls or potions of Haste, Dimension Door, and Misty Step to cross long hallways quickly.
  • Bring summon spells like Conjure Minor Elementals or Scratch to press door-release levers, saving your party members' crucial actions and movement.
  • Station a character with crowd control or high damage near the central corridor, as Sahuagin will continually spawn and target the slow, unarmed Gondian prisoners as they run toward the ladder.
  • Do not end your turn on the ladder when climbing back up; click all the way up to enter the submersible hatch, or you risk blocking other escaping NPCs from boarding.
