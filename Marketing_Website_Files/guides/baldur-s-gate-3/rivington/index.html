Rivington

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The initial settlement outside the city gates

Rivington serves as the outskirts settlement of Baldur's Gate at the beginning of Act 3, filled with refugees, Flaming Fist guards, and circus performers. Players must find a way past the South Span Checkpoint to enter Wyrm's Crossing and the Lower City while resolving local conflicts like the murders at the open-hand temple and the strange happenings at the circus.

Items

  • Nyrulna MISSABLE: Won from Akabi's wheel of wonders at the Circus of the Last Days by pickpocketing his Djinni Ring beforehand and being teleported to the jungle
  • Clown's Severed Hand MISSABLE: Hidden on a plate covered by a pile of items in Pooldripp the Zealous's camp inside the caves beneath the Open Hand Temple
  • Bonespike Boots: Kept in a locked DC 20 wooden chest inside the Passageway beneath the Open Hand Temple

Secrets

  • The underground tunnels beneath the Open Hand Temple contain a concealed heraldic button behind two hanging signs, which opens a passage leading to Bhaalist murderers and the ritual murder weapon.
  • Manipulating the strange ox in the barn allows you to discover its true oozing nature, rewarding the Shapeshifter's Boon Ring if dealt with.

Shops and people

  • Popper: Sells various mundane clutter, parts of Dribbles the Clown, and the Dark Amethyst at the Circus of the Last Days.
  • Exxvikyap: Sells heavy armor, weapons, and martial gear at the forge on behalf of Gyldro Angleiron.
  • Mattis: Sells various trinkets, arrows, and accessories outside the Requisitioned Barn if he survived the earlier acts.
  • Ferg Drogher: Sells rare gear and accessories, but only to characters if Shadowheart is not present or depending on her choices.

Tips

  • You can bypass the South Span Checkpoint guards and Steel Watcher entirely by jumping or using Fly along the cliffs and scaffolding to the east of the bridge.
  • Cast Speak with Animals to talk to the pigeons on top of the Sword Coast Couriers building to quickly resolve the missing letters quest.
