Rivington serves as the outer suburb where players arrive upon reaching the outskirts of Baldur's Gate in Act Three, crowded with displaced refugees and guarded checkpoints. While here, travelers can explore local hotspots like the Circus of the Last Days, investigate a grim murder at the Open Hand Temple, or assist the Ironhand Gnomes hiding beneath the settlement. Progression requires finding a way through or around the heavy security preventing refugees from crossing into Wyrm's Crossing.
