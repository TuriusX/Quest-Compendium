High Hall

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The courtyard and interior during the final assault

High Hall serves as the war-torn battleground immediately preceding the confrontation atop the Netherbrain. Players must rally their gathered allies in the watchtower, fight through heavy waves of Absolute forces in the courtyard, and scale the tower to reach the brain's stem. Survival requires managing resources carefully or using stealth to reach the upper gate without being overwhelmed by endless enemy reinforcements.

Tips

  • Use the "Call Allies" action to summon companions gathered throughout the game to absorb damage and distract courtyard snipers.
  • You do not need to kill every enemy in the courtyard to advance; reaching the central double doors and interacting with them progresses the encounter.
  • Invisibility or high-mobility spells like Dimension Door and Fly allow you to bypass the bulk of the courtyard combat entirely.
  • Avoid ending your turn inside the red circular targeting zones marked on the ground by the hovering Nautiloid.
  • Take advantage of the Restoration Pods near the entrance or inside the upper tower to fully heal spell slots and health before ascending the stem.
