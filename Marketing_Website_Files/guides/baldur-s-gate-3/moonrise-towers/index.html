Moonrise Towers

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The headquarters of the Absolute cultists

Moonrise Towers serves as the primary base of operations for the Cult of the Absolute in Act 2, overseen by the seemingly immortal General Ketheric Thorm. Players initially infiltrate the fortress peacefully under the guise of True Souls to gather intelligence and rescue imprisoned allies from the dungeon level. In the finale of the act, the tower becomes an active war zone where the player assaults the cult alongside the Harpers before confronting Ketheric on the rooftop.

Items

  • Ring of Spiteful Amity MISSABLE: Carried by Roah Moonglow on the main floor
  • Brawler's Cameo MISSABLE: Found in an unlocked chest inside the dormitory on the upper floor
  • Spurred Band MISSABLE: Looted from a locked chest in the Balthazar's secret room
  • Boots of Arcane Bolstering MISSABLE: Sold or dropped by Lann Tarv in the main audience hall
  • Risky Ring MISSABLE: Sold by Araj Oblodra in her ground-floor laboratory
  • Potion of Everlasting Vigour MISSABLE: Given by Araj Oblodra if Astarion bites her

Secrets

  • Interacting with the protruding books in Balthazar's personal quarters opens a secret study containing Moonlantern crafting materials and a ritual circle.
  • Breaking the cracked wall inside the prison using force or thunder damage leads to a hidden rear cavern network directly behind the cell blocks.
  • Interacting with the fleshy wall crevice in the kitchen rafters lets you investigate the Absolute's neural tendrils with skill checks.

Shops and people

  • Roah Moonglow: Armor, camp supplies, hand crossbows, and specialty arrows
  • Lann Tarv: Heavy armor, greatswords, and unique accessories unlocked with True Soul approval
  • Araj Oblodra: Elixirs, specialized potions, and bloodcraft crafting components

Tips

  • Complete all rescue tasks in the prison and make vendor purchases before resolving the Nightsong dilemma inside the Gauntlet of Shar.
  • You can destroy the eye-shaped scrying orbs instantly with thunder damage to prevent them from summoning reinforcements.
  • Giving the gnome prisoners their tools from the Warden's office allows them to break out their Minotaur-boat without triggering the cell doors.
