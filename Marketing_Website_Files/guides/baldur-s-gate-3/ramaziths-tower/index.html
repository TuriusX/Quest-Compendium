Ramazith's Tower

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The wizard's tower accessed via Sorcerous Sundries

Ramazith's Tower is the personal residence and laboratory of the wizard Lorroakan, accessed through a portal in Sorcerous Sundries. Here, players confront Lorroakan over the fate of the Nightsong, Dame Aylin, and explore the tower's lower levels using descending weave buttons and floating platforms. The lower floors contain powerful magical artifacts shielded by elemental traps and arcane globes.

Items

  • Markoheshkir MISSABLE: Inside an arcane barrier on the lower levels of the tower, unlocked using a nearby lever after revealing it with See Invisibility
  • Robe of the Weave MISSABLE: Inside an arcane barrier on the lower levels of the tower, unlocked using a nearby lever after revealing it with See Invisibility

Secrets

  • The lower magical vault floor containing legendary loot can be reached by jumping down floating furniture platforms from the main chamber, but the levers that lower the magical barriers are invisible and require See Invisibility to spot.

Tips

  • Focus on eliminating Lorroakan's elemental myrmidons first, as his Elemental Retort will deal massive counter-damage to your party members as long as they live.
  • Use the spell Feather Fall before descending the outer floating furniture to prevent accidental fatal falling damage on your way to the lower vaults.
  • Bring Dame Aylin to confront Lorroakan to advance her personal questline and receive her aid in the battle.
