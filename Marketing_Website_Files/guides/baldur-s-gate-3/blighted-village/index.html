Blighted Village

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The ruined town infested with goblins

The Blighted Village is an abandoned settlement taken over by a goblin raiding party ambushing travelers along the road. Players can explore ruined houses to discover hidden underground cellars, interact with goblins mistreating a deep gnome, or find paths leading toward the goblin camp and the Underdark. Navigating the town safely requires either passing conversational dialogue checks to deceive the guards or engaging them in tactical combat.

Items

  • Necromancy of Thay MISSABLE: Behind an ornate locked iron gate inside the secret laboratory beneath the apothecary's cellar
  • Dark Amethyst MISSABLE: Found in the Whispering Depths cave complex entered via the village well
  • The Very Very Faded Copy of 'A Hunter's Guide to Father Lobe' MISSABLE: Carried by the goblin boss Fezzerk near the windmill
  • Bracers of Defence MISSABLE: Found inside a gilded chest in the secret necromancy laboratory beneath the apothecary

Secrets

  • The village well can be investigated to unlock an entrance leading straight down into the Whispering Depths.
  • Moving a wooden crate inside the apothecary shop cellar reveals a hidden lever that moves a bookcase, exposing an entrance to a hidden necromancy lab.
  • A magic mirror in the apothecary's hidden lab requires correct answers regarding medicinal herbs and necromancy to gain safe passage into the inner study.
  • The blacksmith's forge cellar contains high elf blueprints and an active furnace used for forging Masterwork weapons using Sussur Bark.
  • Cracking open the fragile wooden boards covering a rooftop gap gives an ambush advantage against the slumbering bugbear and goblins below.

Tips

  • Approaching the windmill lets you resolve the encounter with Fezzerk through Intimidation, Illithid persuasion, or bribes without fighting the whole mob.
  • Pull the windmill brake lever inside the base of the mill to safely stop the sails; pulling the release lever instead will launch the gnome into the distance.
  • Disguising yourself as a drow or using a Drow character lets you walk past most goblin sentries at the entrance without triggering combat checks.
