Shadow-Cursed Lands

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The region encompassing the cursed wilderness ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

The party navigates a decaying wilderness overtaken by a lethal necromantic miasma while seeking a route to Moonrise Towers. Survival requires securing escalating sources of light and magical wards to traverse both the outer reaches and the deeper, darker zones. Along the way, players can ally with Harper scouts, confront cultist convoys, and work toward lifting the land's ancient blight.

Getting there
From Rosymorn Monastery, follow the Rosymorn Monastery Trail west and pass through the zone transition archway into the Mountain Pass path to the Shadow-Cursed Lands (alternatively, take the elevator up from Grymforge in the Underdark) (X: -175 Y: -175)

The short version

What matters

  • •Ambushing Kar'niss and releasing Dolly Dolly Dolly to grant the entire party permanent immunity to the deep shadow curse.
  • •Saving Rolan from the shadows in the southern wilderness path before he dies and transforms into an undead.
  • •Playing hide-and-seek with Oliver at the House in Deep Shadows for the Ring of Shadows and progress toward lifting the curse.

Common mistakes

  • •Entering the deeper darkness beyond the river without obtaining the Pixie's Blessing or carrying an active Moonlantern.
  • •Forgiving Madeline during the ritual with He Who Was, which triggers combat but still allows you to loot the Raven Gloves.
  • •Long resting near the southern bridges after hearing about Rolan's departure, causing his off-screen death.

What you can skip

  • •Siding with Kar'niss' cultist convoy, which needlessly forfeits the Moonlantern and forces a tougher fight at Moonrise Towers later.

    Watch out: Stepping into the shadows without active light inflicts the Shadow Curse, dealing necrotic damage every turn until you perish.

  2. 2

    The Drider Convoy and the Moonlantern

    When traveling the central road or meeting the Absolutists near the Mountain Pass trail, summon the caravan using the Spider's Lyre and link up with Kar'niss and his retinue. Join the hidden Harper ambush squad along the main path to slay the drider and cultists, loot his functional lantern, and interact with it to inspect the creature trapped inside. Free the pixie Dolly Dolly Dolly to obtain the Filigreed Feywild Bell, granting your entire party permanent immunity to the deep curse without holding a light source.

    Tip: Passing a DC 10 Deception check convinces Kar'niss to hand over the lantern and walk his followers into the lethal shadows without a fight.

    Watch out: Escorting Kar'niss safely to Moonrise Towers causes him to scale the walls with the lantern, locking you out of early deep curse immunity.

  3. 3

    The House in Deep Shadows and Oliver

    When you reach the dilapidated house east of the waypoint (`X: 65, Y: 25`), speak with Oliver, the cursed boy lingering in the ruins. Agree to play hide-and-seek, slipping into turn-based stealth to find him behind the cart outside during round one, and then avoid his summoned shadowy "family" in round two without engaging in direct combat. Claim the Ring of Shadows as your reward, taking note of his location for when your quest to lift the shadow curse requires reuniting him with Thaniel.

    Tip: Ungrouping your party and sending a single invisible character makes winning both rounds of hide-and-seek effortless.

    Watch out: Attacking Oliver immediately instead of playing his game causes him to retreat into a portal and permanently forfeits the Ring of Shadows.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Confronting Kar'niss and the Convoy

encountering the drider Kar'niss in the Ruined Battlefield (via Harper ambush or escort)

  • Side with the Harpers or deceive Kar'niss into surrendering the lamp→ fight and kill Kar'niss (or trick him into the dark where he and the cultists succumb to the curse), securing an intact Moonlantern early and gaining Harper trust
  • Side with the cultists against the Harpers→ kill the Harpers, alienating Last Light Inn; Kar'niss escorts you to Moonrise Towers, taking the Moonlantern with him to the roof until late Act 2

Most players pick: Side with the Harpers or deceive Kar'niss into surrendering the lamp

Kar'niss appears as an enemy boss later at Moonrise Towers if spared here.

Fate of Dolly Dolly Dolly

inspecting the looted Moonlantern

  • Release the pixie→ she grants Pixie Blessing (party-wide passive immunity to both levels of the Shadow Curse without holding a lantern) and the Filigreed Feywild Bell to reapply it
  • Keep the pixie trapped→ keep using the Moonlantern as an equipped held item with a limited light radius; Dolly Dolly Dolly remains hostile

Most players pick: Release the pixie

Dolly Dolly Dolly remembers your kindness and helps prevent hostile press coverage in Act 3.

Punishing Madeline (He Who Was)

completing the trial ritual at He Who Was' camp after retrieving Madeline's ledger

  • Punish Madeline (make her stab herself once or call her a coward)→ He Who Was is satisfied and rewards you with the Raven Gloves (summons Quothe the Raven)
  • Forgive Madeline (or make her stab herself repeatedly)→ He Who Was turns hostile and attacks; you must kill him but can still loot the Raven Gloves

Most players pick: Punish Madeline

Forcing her to stab herself or shaming her breaks Paladin Oaths of Devotion and Ancients, while forgiving her breaks Oath of Vengeance.

Rescuing Rolan from the Shadows

approaching the bridge/crane in the southern wilderness path near Reithwin Town

  • Defeat the attacking shadows immediately→ Rolan survives, thanks you begrudgingly, returns to Last Light Inn, and later becomes a powerful ally in Act 3
  • Delay, ignore, or long rest after triggering the area→ Rolan dies and rises as a Shadow-Cursed Undead, cutting off his Act 3 questline at Ramazith's Tower

Most players pick: Defeat the attacking shadows immediately

Proximity timer: entering the vicinity triggers the combat; moving away or resting guarantees his death.

Oliver's Games

finding Oliver at the House in Deep Shadows

  • Play hide and seek and defeat or bypass his shadow family→ receive the Ring of Shadows (grants *Pass Without Trace*) and pacify him for now
  • Attack Oliver directly→ he retreats through a shadow portal into a boss confrontation early without granting the ring

Most players pick: Play hide and seek and defeat or bypass his shadow family

Oliver is required later for Halsin's quest *Lift the Shadow Curse*.

Key fights
Shadow-Cursed Shambling Mound

Enemies: Shadow-Cursed Shambling Mound, 2 Vine Blights, 6 Needle Blights, and 2 Shadow-Cursed Harpers

Threats: Multiattacks, Adhesive Whip (pulls targets), Entangling Roots, Devour (instantly executes restrained targets), and exploding Needle Blights (Needle Volley / Death Burst piercing damage)

Weaknesses and resistances: Resistant to cold damage and non-magical physical damage, immune to lightning (heals from it); vulnerable to fire damage and susceptible to Radiant damage

Tactics: Hold high ground on the ruined tower ledges, ignite the surrounding vegetation, take out fragile Needle Blights at range before they close distance to explode, and burst down the mound with fire and radiant attacks

Rewards: Ring of Shadows, assorted scrolls/camp supplies, and heavy XP

Defend Halsin's Portal (Lift the Shadow Curse)

Enemies: Waves of Shadows, Wraiths, Shadow-Cursed Harpers, Shadow-Cursed Flaming Fists, Shadow Mastiffs, and Shadow-Cursed Githyanki Warriors targeting Halsin's Shadowfell portal

Threats: Sheer horde numbers, ranged archers firing Volley at the fragile portal, Strength Drain from Shadows, and Life Drain from Wraiths

Weaknesses and resistances: Most undead enemies are vulnerable to radiant damage and sunlight effects, but resistant to necrotic and non-magical physical damage

Tactics: Cast large area-denial spells (Wall of Fire, Hunger of Hadar, Sleet Storm) across the beach approach chokepoint, project Spirit Guardians near the rock platform, and shoot Darkness or cast Globe of Invulnerability on the portal to block ranged attacks until the timer expires

Rewards: Thaniel rescued from the Shadowfell, Halsin recruited to the active party, and Outlander inspiration "Grove Keeper's Keeper"

Ch'r'ai Tska'an Ambush

Enemies: Ch'r'ai Tska'an (Githyanki Warlock leader), Veteran Raider Zunii, and Veteran Warriors (Ma'athal, Bradi'ith)

Threats: Repelling Agonizing Blast, Form of Dread (Frightened condition), Githyanki War Magic, Soul Sacrifice (Honour mode legendary action), and heavy Githyanki melee bursts with Misty Step

Weaknesses and resistances: Resistant to psychic damage via Psionic Ward; vulnerable to crowd-control spells (Hold Person, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, Banter/Stun) and counterspelling

Tactics: Avoid triggering the choke at the western gate unprepared, detect the ambush with the Qua'nith Psionic Detector or stealth, take the stone parapets for high-ground advantage, and silence or crowd-control Tska'an immediately to stop eldritch volleys

Rewards: Psionic Ward Armour, rare Githyanki greatswords/crossbows, and Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen
