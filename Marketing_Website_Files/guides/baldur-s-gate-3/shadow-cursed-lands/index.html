Shadow-Cursed Lands

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The region encompassing the cursed wilderness

The Shadow-Cursed Lands make up the bulk of Act 2, blanketed in a deadly, magical darkness that drains life from unprotected wanderers. Players must secure reliable sources of light and magical wards to traverse the region, investigate the plight of Moonrise Towers, and seek out the Last Light Inn for refuge. Resolving the curse involves navigating faction conflicts between the Harpers and the Cult of the Absolute, as well as freeing the land's bound spirit.

Items

  • Moonlantern MISSABLE: Taken from Kar'niss during the convoy ambush or obtained from Balthazar's quarters in Moonrise Towers
  • Blood of Lathander MISSABLE: Carried into the region if solved in the Rosymorn Monastery crèche, acts as a permanent light aura here
  • Coruscating Ring: Found inside a locked chest in the cellar of the Last Light Inn

Secrets

  • Releasing the pixie Dolly Dolly Dolly trapped inside Kar'niss's Moonlantern grants the entire party the Pixie Blessing, permanently warding against deep shadow without needing to hold a light source.
  • Interacting with the three plaques beneath the central Thorm statue in the town square unlocks a hidden entrance leading to the Sharran Sanctuary.

Shops and people

  • Quartermaster Talli: Sells armor, camp supplies, the Mighty Cloth, and various rare accessories
  • Mattis: Sells trinkets, the Cloak of Cunning Brume, and miscellaneous goods
  • Dammon: Sells heavy armor, the Sword of Life Stealing, and crafts Flawed Helldusk equipment from Infernal Iron

Tips

  • Regular torches and the Light cantrip will protect you in the outskirts, but deeper shadows require a working Moonlantern or the Pixie Blessing.
  • Turn on turn-based mode immediately if caught in deep shadow without protection to prevent rapid necrotic tick damage.
  • Do not let Isobel fall unconscious or get captured during the assault on the Last Light Inn, or the inn's protective ward drops and converts nearly every resident into hostile undead.
