Cazador's Palace

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The vampire lord's estate in the Lower City

Cazador's Palace is the opulent estate of the vampire lord Cazador Szarr, located atop the Central Watch Tower in the Lower City. Players infiltrate the mansion and descend into the ritual ballroom beneath it to stop the Rite of Profane Ascension. This area serves as the climax of Astarion's companion questline, deciding his ultimate fate and the fate of thousands of vampire spawn.

Items

  • Woe MISSABLE: Dropped by Cazador Szarr upon his defeat
  • Rhapsody MISSABLE: Dropped by Cazador Szarr upon his defeat
  • Szarr Family Ring MISSABLE: Carried by Godey the skeleton in the palace lower level
  • Kozakuran Dictionary MISSABLE: Found in the guest room on the main floor
  • Pelorsun Blade MISSABLE: Located inside a hidden trapdoor chest in the lower ballroom

Secrets

  • You can speak with the corpse of the cursed girl in the guest room by using Remove Curse on her first.
  • Finding the Kozakuran Dictionary and the Szarr Family Ring allows you to bypass the magical door seal without lockpicking.
  • A hidden attic above the upper ballroom contains a chest that can be accessed by climbing high scaffolding or using Misty Step.

Tips

  • Casting Daylight directly onto the battlefield negates Cazador's mist form and inflicts passive radiant damage each turn.
  • If Astarion is trapped in the ritual circle, send a companion to use the Help action on him immediately to prevent Cazador from ascending.
  • You can use Counterspell or silence to prevent Cazador from casting high-level spells like Blight or Chain Lightning.
  • Knocking enemies off the narrow ritual arena platforms into the chasm will instantly kill them, though their loot will be lost.
