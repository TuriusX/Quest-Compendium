Last Light Inn

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The sanctuary protected by Isobel's barrier

The Last Light Inn serves as the main safe haven for refugees and Harpers within the Shadow-Cursed Lands, shielded from the deadly darkness by the Selûnite cleric Isobel. Players can rest, trade, pick up crucial side quests to rescue tieflings and find missing grand dukes, and decide the fate of the inn during an ambush. Protecting Isobel during this assault is critical to preventing the protective ward from collapsing and turning everyone inside into undead shadows.

Items

  • Coruscation Ring: Inside a locked heavy chest in the cellar beneath the inn
  • Cloak of Protection MISSABLE: Sold or pickpocketed from Quartermaster Talli near the inn entrance
  • Hat of Uninhibited Smite: Looted from a locked wooden chest on the second floor of the inn
  • Snowburst Ring: Inside a loose plank in the upstairs room occupied by Art Cullagh

Secrets

  • Beneath the inn lies a cellar with a cracked wall leading to a submerged area infested with Meenlocks and an altar to Selûne.
  • Approaching the strange ox in the stables with Speak with Animals reveals it is not an ordinary beast, but a shapeshifter with sinister thoughts.

Shops and people

  • Quartermaster Talli: Sells rare armors, shields, the Cloak of Protection, camp supplies, and arrows
  • Dammon: Sells weapons, heavy armor, crafting materials, and crafts Flawed Helldusk armor pieces from Infernal Iron
  • Mattis: Sells various trinkets, dyes, and magical cloaks if he survived Act 1
  • Barcus Wroot: Sells bombs, gloves, and miscellaneous adventuring gear if he was rescued at the windmill and Grymforge

Tips

  • Cast Sanctuary or Invisibility on Isobel immediately when the combat encounter upstairs begins to keep enemies from bursting her down in a single round.
  • Do not kill the Strange Ox in the stables during Act 2 if you want to recruit it as an ally for the final battle in Act 3.
  • Play the Battered Lute found on Malus Thorm near the comatose Art Cullagh to awaken him and advance Halsin's quest to cure the shadow curse.
