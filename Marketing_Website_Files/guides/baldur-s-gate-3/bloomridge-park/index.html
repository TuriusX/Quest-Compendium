Bloomridge Park

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The park area in the Lower City

Bloomridge Park is a public garden located in the northern section of the Lower City of Baldur's Gate. It serves as an active ambush site where Bhaalist assassins attack, and it provides access routes to nearby locations like the Undercity Ruins and Lady Jannath's Estate. Players primarily come here to resolve a combat encounter with Bhaal cultists and explore minor environmental interactions.

Items

  • Cloak of the Weave: Sold by Helsik at the Devil's Fee just north of the park, though not inside the park itself

Tips

  • Cast See Invisibility, Fairy Fire, or use Volo's Ersatz Eye before walking through the central pathways of the park to avoid being surprised by cloaked Bhaal cultists.
  • Use high-ground gazebos and stone steps scattered through the garden to establish ranged superiority against the melee-heavy ambushers.
