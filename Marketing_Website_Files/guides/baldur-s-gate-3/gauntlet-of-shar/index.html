Gauntlet of Shar

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The trial grounds for Shadowheart's quest

The Gauntlet of Shar is an ancient subterranean temple located beneath the Grand Mausoleum in the Shadow-Cursed Lands. Players must complete three trials, defeat the devil Yurgir, and acquire Umbral Gems to descend into the Shadowfell to resolve Shadowheart's questline. Completing the inner sanctum permanently changes the Shadow-Cursed Lands and locks out unfinished regional quests.

Items

  • Spear of Night MISSABLE: In the treasure room at the back of the Silent Library, accessed by solving the riddle on the pedestal
  • Dark Justiciar Helmet MISSABLE: Inside the locked gilded chest inside the Silent Library treasure room
  • Dark Justiciar Half-Plate MISSABLE: On the altar in the Silent Library next to the Spear of Night
  • Boots of Brilliance MISSABLE: Inside a locked heavy chest near the Displacer Beast's prowling ground by Yurgir's lair
  • Hellfire Hand Crossbow MISSABLE: Dropped by Yurgir upon his defeat

Secrets

  • You can find a hidden group of rats throughout the Gauntlet that share a hive mind; killing them across the temple will eventually trigger a confrontation at the base of the giant Shar statue with the last Dark Justiciar, who drops the Justiciar's Scimitar and Justiciar's Greatshield.
  • The leap of faith trial floor pattern is faintly visible on the platform tiles at the trial's entrance, providing a direct map of the safe route.

Tips

  • Bring Shadowheart in your party to handle the trials, as she gains unique dialogue, inspiration points, and story development from making blood sacrifices at the altars.
  • In the Faith-Leap trial, you can bypass the maze mechanics entirely using the Fly spell, Enhanced Leap, or Misty Step onto the final platform.
  • For the Self-Same Trial, unequip your party's armor and weapons before initiating the trial, then re-equip them immediately after the blood sacrifice to face defenseless mirror clones.
  • In the Silent Library, you cannot cast verbal spells while the central orb is active; destroy the central orb first using ranged weapon attacks to restore your spellcasting.
  • Do not enter the Shadowfell pool past the lower altar until you have completed all quests at Moonrise Towers and the Shadow-Cursed Lands, as crossing this threshold is a point of no return.
