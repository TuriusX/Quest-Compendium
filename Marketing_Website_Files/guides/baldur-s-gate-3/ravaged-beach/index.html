Ravaged Beach

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The crash site where the game begins

You awaken on the Ravaged Beach after the prologue nautiloid crashes, serving as the starting point for Chapter 1. Here, you must gather your dispersed companions, fight off small hostiles, and search for clues to remove the mind flayer parasite. Heading north and inland will lead you toward the Overgrown Ruins and the Emerald Grove.

Items

  • Thieves' Tools: Inside a cart near the Overgrown Ruins entrance
  • Guiding Light MISSABLE: Worn by the unconscious Shadowheart where she lies on the beach
  • The Sparky Points: Obtained from a chest near the water on the southwest shore

Secrets

  • Scuffed Rock | Located southwest along the beach path; requires high Strength to move, revealing an Ornate Chest underneath with gold and a Harper map.
  • Hidden Path to Overgrown Ruins | Climbable cragged rocks just north of where you find Astarion offer a route straight to the chapel's upper entrance.

Shops and people

  • None: There are no friendly merchants or trading posts in this immediate crash zone.

Tips

  • Recruit Shadowheart immediately where she lies unconscious on the beach; if left behind, she relocates further north toward the ruins.
  • Use sneak mode to surprise the Intellect Devourers inside the ship wreckage before they notice you.
  • Jump across broken gaps in the wreckage to locate hidden dead bodies holding gold and basic camping supplies.
