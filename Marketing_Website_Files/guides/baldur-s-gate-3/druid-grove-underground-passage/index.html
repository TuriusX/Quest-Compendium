Druid Grove Underground Passage

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The secret tunnel beneath the grove

This secret cavern connects the Emerald Grove to the wilderness outside, serving as a covert escape route for the tieflings or goblins. Players can navigate past defensive Guardian Statues, rescue an injured druid, or use the passage to quietly escort the captive goblin Sazza out of the grove.

Items

  • Nature's Snare MISSABLE: Found inside a locked Heavy Chest on an elevated stone ledge guarded by Guardian Statues
  • Key of the Falcon MISSABLE: Carried by the unconscious halfling Findal lying in the center of the cavern
  • Charred Key MISSABLE: Found on the charred corpse lying near the northern fire trap area

Secrets

  • A heavy stone door entrance inside the grove can be accessed either from a jump down behind the makeshift prison or through a hidden door near the enclave library.

Tips

  • Use Mage Hand or long-range stealth shots to activate the glowing Rune of the Falcon and Rune of the Bear to disable the firing Guardian Statues safely.
  • If you plan to save Findal, heal him immediately upon engaging the goblins, as the hostile scouts will execute him if left unattended.
  • Jumping across the cavern's rock pillars requires decent Strength or the Jump spell, but it allows you to completely bypass the explosive trap clusters on the ground.
