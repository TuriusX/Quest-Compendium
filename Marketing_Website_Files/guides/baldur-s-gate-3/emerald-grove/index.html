Emerald Grove

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The druid settlement and refugee camp

The Emerald Grove serves as the first major friendly hub where Tiefling refugees have taken temporary shelter alongside a secluded circle of druids. Players navigate a tense political standoff triggered by Kagha's ritual to seal the grove while searching for a cure for their mind flayer parasites. Resolving the faction tensions, dealing with goblin threats, and recruiting new companions are the primary goals in this settlement.

Items

  • Hellrider's Pride MISSABLE: Carried by Zevlor, rewarded for stopping the ritual or protecting refugees
  • Pale Oak MISSABLE: Rewarded by Kagha for resolving the Shadow Druid plot peacefully as a Druid
  • Sorrow MISSABLE: Inside the hidden vault beneath the Enclave Library, accessed with the Rune of the Wolf
  • Robe of Summer MISSABLE: Inside the hidden vault beneath the Enclave Library, accessed with the Rune of the Wolf
  • Nature's Snare: Found inside a locked chest within the Underground Passage beneath the grove
  • Ring of Flinging MISSABLE: Sold by Aaron, the halfling druid vendor near the grove's main entrance hollow
  • Key of the Ancients: Looted from Nettie or taken from the knocked-out goblin inside the makeshift prison

Secrets

  • The Underground Passage connecting the makeshift prison to the forest can be accessed by leaping across a chasm behind Sazza's cage or through an entrance along the exterior cliffs.
  • Kagha's hidden chest is tucked behind a carved stone screen in the Servants' Quarters, containing documents that expose her alliance with the Shadow Druids.
  • Placing the Rune of the Wolf into the empty pedestal in the Enclave Library unlocks the hidden stone staircase leading down to the hidden vault.
  • Talking to the strange ox in the cattle hollow with Speak with Animals reveals sinister thoughts that hint at its true non-bovine nature.
  • Doni the mute tiefling child can reveal a concealed crevice in the rocks that leads directly into the tiefling children's hideout if you earn his trust.

Shops and people

  • Arron: Sells mundane weapons, camping supplies, healing potions, arrows, and minor magical accessories near the central Hollow entrance.
  • Dammon: Tiefling blacksmith selling metal armor, weapons, and handles custom crafting requests involving Infernal Iron.
  • Mattis: Tiefling child running a deceptive trinket shop; sells low-tier utility items and the Ring of Being Really Invisible.
  • Auntie Ethel: Poses as an herbalist near the hollow entrance, selling various alchemical ingredients, potions, and three Hill Giant Strength elixirs per long rest.

Tips

  • Cast Speak with Animals before exploring to unlock unique interactions with the local wildlife, including a squirrel, the strange ox, and Timber.
  • Do not venture past the Mountain Pass or the Grymforge elevator before resolving the Grove's conflicts, or the druids will automatically finish the Rite of Thorns and seal the area permanently.
  • Turn off the Guardian Statues in the Underground Passage by interacting with the glowing wall runes or shooting them with ranged attacks from a safe distance.
  • Non-lethal attacks can be toggled to knock out Alfira or investigate Kagha without immediately turning the entire grove hostile.
