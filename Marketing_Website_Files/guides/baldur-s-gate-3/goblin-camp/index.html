Goblin Camp

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The exterior encampment of the Absolute cultists

The exterior Goblin Camp is an occupied ruin serving as the front courtyard and stronghold for the Absolute's cultists. Players can negotiate or fight their way inside, rescue captive performers and animals, and participate in cultist revelries. It serves as the primary gateway to the interior Shattered Sanctum.

Items

  • Boots of Aid and Comfort MISSABLE: Sold by Grat the Trader near the front entrance
  • Returning Pike MISSABLE: Sold by Grat the Trader near the front entrance
  • Swiresy Shoes MISSABLE: Sold by Grat the Trader near the front entrance
  • Glowing Shield: Inside a locked wooden chest on the raised terrace behind the windmill

Secrets

  • Crumbled wall entrance | Accessible by jumping along the rocks to the high platforms above the camp, bypassing the front gate entirely
  • Booze tub poisoning | Interacting with the communal alcohol tub allows you to spike it with poison or basic toxin to assassinate a large portion of the outdoor goblins

Shops and people

  • Grat the Trader: Sells standard weapons, arrows, and unique gear like the Returning Pike and Boots of Aid and Comfort

Tips

  • Rubbing dung on your face during the entrance dialogue permits free passage into the camp without requiring skill checks.
  • You can convince the goblins to let you play the chicken-chasing game to earn the Owlbear cub's freedom, or bet gold to make a profit.
  • Volo can be found performing on a podium in the main courtyard, and talking to him here sets up his subsequent rescue inside the sanctum.
  • Passing near the waterfall at the back leads to a breakable cracked wall leading into the Underdark or inner sanctum paths.
