Wyrm's Crossing

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The bridge leading into the city

Wyrm's Crossing serves as the heavily fortified bridge checkpoint connecting Rivington to the Lower City of Baldur's Gate. Players must navigate bureaucratic and physical blockades enforced by the Flaming Fist and Steel Watchers to access Wyrm's Rock Fortress. The bridge itself features lively shops, a brothel, and multiple questlines involving missing people, murder investigations, and political intrigue.

Items

  • Hellfire Engine Crossbow MISSABLE: Crafted at the Wyrm's Rock Fortress basement forge using acquired blueprints and parts
  • Duke Ravengard's Longsword MISSABLE: Carried by Duke Ulder Ravengard during the coronation or subsequent encounters
  • Bonespike Boots MISSABLE: Pass through the passage under the bridge to Rivington/Lower City border, inside a locked chest guarded by ambushers

Secrets

  • You can bypass the heavily guarded bridge checkpoint entirely by jumping or using Feather Fall down to the scaffolding beneath the wooden span to find paths leading directly inside.

Shops and people

  • Sharess' Caress: Sells unique magical rest services, drinks, and hosts meetings with key narrative figures like Raphael.
  • Danthelon's Dancing Axe: Sells various martial weapons, heavy armor, and quest-related supplies for the Harpers.

Tips

  • Cast Disguise Self or Invisible to easily sneak past the bridge checkpoint scanners if you lack the required paperwork.
  • Visit Sharess' Caress on the crossing before heading to the coronation to trigger important narrative paths regarding the Emperor and the Crown of Karsus.
