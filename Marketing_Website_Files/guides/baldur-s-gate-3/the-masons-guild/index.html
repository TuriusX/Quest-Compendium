The Mason's Guild

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The guildhall containing secrets of the town

The Mason's Guild is located in Reithwin Town within the Shadow-Cursed Lands, functioning as the historic center of the local stonemasons who secretly resisted Ketheric Thorm's Selûnite-turned-Sharite rule. Exploring the building reveals journals and a hidden trapdoor that leads into a covert underground workshop detailing their alliance with the Harpers and the infernal architecture of Moonrise Towers. Players come here to gather lore, secure crafting materials, and discover tactical secrets regarding Ketheric's stronghold.

Items

  • Infernal Iron: Found sitting on a workbench in the main workshop on the ground floor
  • Mason's Log: Located on a wooden desk near the front entrance of the main floor
  • Helmet of Arcane Acuity MISSABLE: Found inside a locked Gilded Chest behind the hidden wall in the guild's basement

Secrets

  • A concealed trapdoor is hidden behind the elevator in the main building, granting direct access to the guild's basement.
  • A movable wall in the guild's basement can be opened by passing a Perception check to reveal a tucked-away Heraldic Sign keyhole and inserting a key or lockpicking it.

Tips

  • Bring light sources or apply radiant damage buffs to your weapons before exploring the basement, as the lurking Shadows and Wraiths suffer greatly against Radiant effects.
  • Thoroughly check the walls for button and lock mechanisms if you fail a Perception check in the basement, as the hidden room contains the best loot in the building.
