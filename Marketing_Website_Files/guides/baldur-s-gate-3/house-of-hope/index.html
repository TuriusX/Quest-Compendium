House of Hope

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The infernal plane accessed via the Devil's Fee

The House of Hope is Raphael's private infernal domain in Avernus, accessed through a portal in the Devil's Fee. You infiltrate the estate to steal your contract or the Orphic Hammer, loot Raphael's personal vault, and rescue Hope before escaping. Removing the primary treasure triggers an alarm, forcing a climactic boss encounter against Raphael at the exit portal.

Items

  • Orphic Hammer MISSABLE: In the Archive resting on a pedestal under an arcane shield, unless already acquired via a deal
  • Gauntlets of Hill Giant Strength MISSABLE: In the Archive on a display pedestal across from the central artifact
  • Amulet of Greater Health MISSABLE: In the Archive on a display pedestal across from the central artifact
  • Helldusk Gloves MISSABLE: Dropped by Haarlep in Raphael's Boudoir after defeating them
  • Helldusk Helmet MISSABLE: Inside Raphael's secret vault hidden behind a painting in the Boudoir
  • Helldusk Armour MISSABLE: Looted from Raphael's corpse after defeating him in the entrance hall

Secrets

  • Raphael's hidden vault | Found in Raphael's Boudoir behind a large portrait of him, accessed by passing a perception check and pressing an interactive button | missable: no

Tips

  • Disguise your party in the Debtors' Garb provided by Hope upon entering to freely explore without immediately aggroing guards.
  • You can use the restoration faucet in Raphael's Boudoir bath infinitely to replenish spell slots and health as if taking a full Long Rest before triggering the alarm.
  • Stealing any artifact from the Archive immediately triggers the estate-wide alarm, causes the Archivist to turn hostile, and alerts Raphael.
  • Destroying the four Soul Pillars in the corners of the entrance hall prevents Raphael from casting high-tier fire spells and severely limits his combat power.
  • You must use the Orphic Hammer to smash the two glowing red crystals chaining Hope in her subterranean prison to free her.
  • A high DC Persuasion check can convince Yurgir the Orthon to betray Raphael and fight on your side during the final battle.
