Moonrise Towers Prison

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The dungeon beneath the main fortress

The Moonrise Towers Prison holds captured deep gnomes and tieflings from the Shadow-Cursed Lands awaiting execution or worse. Players must navigate watchful guards and scrying eyes to break the prisoners out before confronting Ketheric Thorm later in the act. The area connects directly to an oubliette cliff-side escape path that can be used for a stealthy evasion by boat.

Items

  • Wulbren's Hammer MISSABLE: Inside a chest on the upper level of the central warden's tower
  • Spellmight Gloves MISSABLE: Rewarded by Lucretious later if you rescue the tieflings, but obtained indirectly through completing this prison break arc

Secrets

  • You can jump into the red flesh-lined chasm at the bottom of the prison pit to enter the Mind Flayer Colony area early, though falling in without proper story progression will kill the party instantly.
  • Breaking through the cracked back walls of the tiefling and gnome cells from the hidden rear tunnels allows them to escape without opening the cell doors directly.

Tips

  • Destroy all Scrying Eyes floating around the prison perimeter quietly before engaging the guards to prevent alarms from ringing.
  • Giving Wulbren his hammer back allows the gnomes to break the rear tunnel walls themselves, but you can also smash the walls from behind using Bludgeoning or Force damage.
  • Clear out the dock guards and warden before triggering the prison escape to give the prisoners an entirely clear run to the hidden getaway boat.
