Moonrise Towers (Act 2 Assault)

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The assault on the fortress

Following the events at the Shadowfell, the Harpers and allied forces launch a direct assault on Moonrise Towers to eliminate the Cult of the Absolute's leadership. You must fight your way through the ground floor entrance hall and climb up to the rooftop to confront General Ketheric Thorm. After forcing Thorm to retreat into the depths of the Mind Flayer Colony, you can finish exploring the tower or follow him down the tower's hollowed tower center.

Items

  • Belm MISSABLE: In a locked chest inside Balthazar's secret room/quarters on the upper floor
  • Spiteful Boots MISSABLE: Dropped by Adept Ghis in the ground floor entrance assault

Secrets

  • Mimic on the upper floor: A chest located in the upper bedchambers turns out to be a hostile Mimic when interacted with.

Tips

  • Cast large area-of-effect crowd control spells like Hunger of Hadar, Sleet Storm, or Wall of Fire at the main entrance doorway to bottleneck and decimate the ground-floor cultists.
  • You can convince Jaheira to join your active party as a controllable follower before the ground floor fight, giving you direct control over her survival and spellcasting.
  • If you cleared out the prison guards during an earlier visit, the ground level assault encounters will have significantly fewer reinforcements.
  • Use Counterspell aggressively against Disciple Z'rell on turn one to prevent her from locking down the Harpers with devastating crowd-control magic.
