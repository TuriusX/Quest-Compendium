Selûnite Outpost

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The fortified structure in the Underdark

The Selûnite Outpost serves as a fortified Underdark entrance located directly beneath the Defiled Temple in the Goblin Camp. The main gates are guarded by dual magical eye statues powered by a central moonstone that incinerate approaching intruders. Players must navigate past the gate defenses, defeat encroaching Underdark creatures, and unlock the waypoint to explore the rest of the subterranean map safely.

Items

  • Luminous Armour: Inside an Opulent Chest behind a locked gate in the outpost's hidden side alcove
  • Helmet of Smiting: Inside a locked gilded chest inside the outpost's main interior room
  • Phalar Aluve: Embedded in a stone altar on the path directly northwest just outside the outpost's broken perimeter

Secrets

  • The glowing moonstone atop the central pillar provides power to the deadly eye lasers guarding the gate; shooting the crystal from afar shatters it and disables the defense system safely.
  • A wall section on the west side of the outpost's exterior upper level is cracked and can be destroyed using bludgeoning or force damage to create an alternate entrance.

Tips

  • Do not run through the main front gates while the moonstone crystal atop the central statue is glowing, as the automated defense turrets will rapidly kill your entire party.
  • Use a character with high Religion or Performance to successfully pull the sword Phalar Aluve from the stone outside without needing to pass a high Strength check.
