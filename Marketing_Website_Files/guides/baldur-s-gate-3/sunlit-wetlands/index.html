Sunlit Wetlands

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The swampy region south of the grove

The Sunlit Wetlands is a treacherous swamp hiding behind an illusion of a peaceful forest, serving as the domain of the hag Auntie Ethel. You can confront Ethel about Mayrina, meet the monster hunter Gandrel, and investigate the true, rotten nature of the bog. The area contains hazards, redcaps disguised as sheep, and a secret entrance into the hag's subterranean lair behind her teahouse.

Items

  • The Sparkle Hands MISSABLE: Inside a wooden chest on a small island surrounded by mud mephits in the south of the swamp
  • Aruglum's Journal MISSABLE: Found in the wooden sanctuary near the water mephits in the south of the wetlands
  • Letter to M MISSABLE: On a wooden table inside the teahouse
  • Well-Behaved Toad MISSABLE: Trapped inside a cage hanging from the ceiling

Secrets

  • Touching or investigating the strange objects in the swamp, like the sheep or the blooming fruit trees, triggers an Arcana check that permanently strips away the illusion covering the rot and corpses.
  • A hollow wooden tree stump located in the southern part of the swamp hides a secret letter from Kagha addressed to Olodan, exposing her plot with the Shadow Druids.
  • The roaring fireplace at the back of the teahouse is an illusion concealing wooden stairs leading down into Ethel's lair; interact with the fireplace to extinguish the flames and walk straight through it.

Tips

  • Drink water from the pristine well outside the teahouse for a temporary boost to maximum hit points, though it will turn into a debuff once the swamp's illusion drops.
  • Do not allow the Redcaps to surprise you; attack them from a distance before talking to Ethel if you plan on eliminating them without fighting them alongside her.
  • Keeping Astarion away from Gandrel prevents an immediate fight or Gandrel figuring out Astarion's identity.
  • You can extinguish the fireplace using water or ice magic before speaking to Ethel to reveal the secret pathway ahead of time.
  • Do not consume the food Ethel offers you in dialogue, as it inflicts a debuff on your party.
