Ancient Abode

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The basement/lair beneath the teahouse

The Ancient Abode is the sprawling, trap-filled lair hidden behind the fireplace of Auntie Ethel's Riverside Teahouse. Players must navigate past Ethel's tormented victims, traverse the poisonous Overgrown Tunnel, and confront the hag in her workshop to rescue Mayrina. The area offers opportunities to acquire unique permanent buffs, powerful magical items, and uncover the tragic fates of Ethel's past bargainers.

Items

  • Whispering Mask: Found resting on a stone table near the Gnarled Door
  • Staff of the Crones: Looted from the wooden chest in the Acrid Workshop behind the final boss arena
  • Bitter Divorce: Found on the table inside the Acrid Workshop
  • Auntie Ethel's Hair MISSABLE: Obtained directly through dialogue with Auntie Ethel if spared at low health

Secrets

  • The roaring fireplace in the teahouse is an illusion and can be extinguished and walked through to access the underground lair.
  • The Gnarled Door can be bypassed without wearing a mask by adjusting the camera behind it and clicking to walk through its illusion.

Tips

  • Equipping a Whispering Mask allows safe passage through the Gnarled Door, but requires casting Protection from Evil and Good to avoid losing mind control.
  • Throw an item, rock, or body onto the noxious vents in the Overgrown Tunnel to permanently suppress the poison gas clouds.
  • Dousing Mayrina's burning wooden cage with a bottle of water or casting Create Water prevents her from falling to her death during the boss fight.
  • Knocking Auntie Ethel out or killing her too quickly will prevent her from triggering the dialogue to offer Auntie Ethel's Hair.
  • Use Magic Missile to instantly clear all of Ethel's illusory clones in a single turn.
