Defiled Temple

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The puzzle area beneath the Shattered Sanctum

The Defiled Temple is an ancient Selûnite sanctuary located beneath the Goblin Camp's Shattered Sanctum, initially guarded by Priestess Gut's bodyguard. Players must bypass an ogre guard and solve a lunar stone puzzle or lockpick a hidden lever to unlock the subterranean passage. Navigating this area opens up a direct entrance into the Underdark and reveals a heavily trapped Selûnite outpost.

Items

  • Selûnite Journal: found in a stone chest inside Polma's bedchamber

Secrets

  • The Moon Puzzle can be bypassed entirely by spotting a hidden lever on the wall with a Perception check and picking its DC 15 lock.
  • A gilded chest near the passage to the Underdark is protected by an explosive rune trap that must be disarmed before opening.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Polmasusceptible to blinding and push effectsvarious camp supplies, heavy armorShe is an ogre bodyguard stationed in the first bedchamber who attacks if you enter without Gut or fail dialogue checks.

Tips

  • To solve the floor puzzle manually, align the rotating stone disks so that all four dark circles are grouped together within the circle illuminated by the beam of light.
  • Sneaking past Polma into the main puzzle room is easily done by jumping across broken columns or using invisibility.
