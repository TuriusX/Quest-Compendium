Mountain Pass

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The transition zone leading toward the Shadow-Cursed Lands

The Mountain Pass serves as a transition zone between Act 1 and Act 2, housing the exterior Rosymorn Monastery Trail and the sprawling Rosymorn Monastery grounds. You uncover Githyanki lore, delve into the Creche Y'llek beneath the monastery, and follow Lae'zel's personal quest to seek a cure. Navigating this area also unlocks an alternate overland route into the Shadow-Cursed Lands.

Items

  • Blood of Lathander MISSABLE: Found inside the Inquisitor's chamber secret vault within Creche Y'llek
  • The Whispering Promise MISSABLE: Purchased from Lady Esther on the Rosymorn Monastery Trail
  • Periapt of Wound Closure MISSABLE: Purchased from Lady Esther on the Rosymorn Monastery Trail
  • Knife of the Undermountain King MISSABLE: Purchased from A'jak'nir Jeera inside Creche Y'llek
  • Gloves of Dexterity MISSABLE: Purchased from A'jak'nir Jeera inside Creche Y'llek

Secrets

  • The hidden armory holding the Blood of Lathander can be accessed by rotating two statues in the Inquisitor's chamber so one faces north and the other faces south.
  • Solving the stained-glass window puzzle on the upper floor of Rosymorn Monastery by placing the correct ceremonial weapons grants the Morninglord's Radiance buff and the Dawnmaster's Crest.

Shops and people

  • Lady Esther: Sells various magical gear, consumables, and offers a quest to acquire a Githyanki egg
  • A'jak'nir Jeera: Sells specialized Githyanki weaponry, arrows, and dexterity-boosting gear

Tips

  • Entering the Mountain Pass will auto-resolve the Druid Grove conflict if you haven't finished it yet, so complete the Emerald Grove and Goblin Camp quests before traveling here.
  • Equipping the Dawnmaster's Crest into the pedestal prevents the monastery from triggering its self-destruct mechanism when taking the Blood of Lathander.
  • Letting your main character use the Zaith'isk machine inside the infirmary and succeeding on three difficult saving throws grants the Awakened permanent buff, turning all Illithid Powers into bonus actions.
