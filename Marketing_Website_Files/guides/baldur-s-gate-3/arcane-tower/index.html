Arcane Tower

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The wizard's tower in the southern Underdark

The Arcane Tower is an abandoned laboratory in the Underdark formerly belonging to the cleric of Mystra, Lenore De Hurst. Players must bypass or disable automated turrets to reach the lower levels and insert a Sussur Bloom into the generator to restore power to the elevator. Exploring the powered floors yields rare alchemy ingredients, magical gear, and lore regarding the missing wizard and her automaton companion.

Items

  • Guiding Light MISSABLE: Looted from Bernard on the rooftop or obtained by reciting the correct poetry line
  • Boots of Speed: Offered by Thulla as a reward, but can also be found in Lenore's bedroom chest
  • Uncovered Mysteries: Inside a gilded chest on the bedroom floor
  • Mage's Friend: Found inside an opulent chest on the laboratory floor
  • Staff of Arcane Blessing MISSABLE: Leaning against a table in the secret basement laboratory
  • The Sparkswall: Found inside a gilded chest on the basement level near the generator

Secrets

  • Equipping the Guiding Light ring reveals a hidden elevator button that provides access to the tower's secret basement.
  • Reading the plays and poetry books scattered across the floors unlocks peaceful dialogue options when confronting Bernard on the roof.
  • Pulling the suspicious lever on the bottom-level garden wall reveals a hidden entrance directly from the Underdark chasm.

Tips

  • Carry a Sussur Bloom from the garden directly to the basement generator in turn-based mode to avoid taking anti-magic field damage in combat.
  • Use Feather Fall to jump safely down the giant blue mushrooms on the tower's exterior to bypass the interior turrets entirely.
  • Dropping a Sussur Bloom next to Bernard will suppress his magical abilities and deactivate the nearby Animated Armor.
