Tollhouse

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The ruined building on the Risen Road

The Tollhouse on the Risen Road serves as a temporary outpost for a squad of self-proclaimed paladins hunting the tiefling Karlach. Players can choose to aid the leader Anders in killing Karlach or uncover their true allegiance to Archdevil Zariel and eliminate the paladins alongside her. The building also contains a locked basement vault with traps and loot.

Items

  • Sword of Justice MISSABLE: Held by Anders; obtained as a quest reward for killing Karlach or looted from his corpse
  • Soul Coin MISSABLE: On a shelf in the tollhouse basement vault
  • Tollhouse Basement Key MISSABLE: Carried by dead toll collector outside to the south near the ledge

Secrets

  • The hidden vault in the basement requires sitting two party members on two stone thrones simultaneously to lower the secret stone bookcase wall.
  • A drainpipe outside the tollhouse basement wall can be used by wildshaped or small characters to slip inside without picking the front cellar doors.

Tips

  • Speak to the dead tollhouse clerk inside the basement using Speak with Dead to learn more about the building's hidden mechanisms and history.
  • Separate your party members before activating the stone thrones in the basement so they do not step off the weight plates and trigger the traps or re-close the hidden wall.
