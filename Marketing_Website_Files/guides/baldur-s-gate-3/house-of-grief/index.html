House of Grief

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The Sharran cloister in the Lower City

The House of Grief is the covert stronghold of the Sharran enclave located in northwestern Lower City. Infiltrating the cloister leads you deep underground to resolve Shadowheart's companion quest by confronting Mother Superior Viconia DeVir. Exploring the lower sanctum grants access to the Mirror of Loss, ancient Sharran lore, and imprisoned figures from Shadowheart's past.

Items

  • Viconia's Walking Fortress MISSABLE: Looted from Viconia DeVir's corpse after defeating her
  • Handmaiden's Mace MISSABLE: Carried by Viconia DeVir, lootable upon her defeat
  • Viconia's Priestess Robe MISSABLE: Worn by Viconia DeVir, lootable upon her defeat
  • Mirror of Loss: Located in the deepest chamber of the cloister (Threshold of Loss)

Secrets

  • Behind the central statue of Shar in the lower cloister lies a hidden chamber accessible by passing a passive Perception check to spot a hidden button or by placing an offering of significant value on the altar.

Tips

  • Avoid dealing Radiant damage to enemies in this cloister who possess the Radiant Retort passive, as the damage will reflect onto your characters as Force damage.
  • Cast daylight spells or bring daylight-producing gear to counter the heavy concentration of magical Darkness spells cast by the Sharran cultists.
  • Funnel the large pack of cultists up the entrance stairs using lingering area-of-effect spells like Wall of Fire, Cloudkill, or Hunger of Hadar to control the battlefield.
  • Praying at the Mirror of Loss requires passing high Religion and Arcana checks to permanently boost a chosen ability score by +2 at the cost of a temporary curse to another stat.
