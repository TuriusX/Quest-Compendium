Rosymorn Monastery

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The Githyanki creche location

The Rosymorn Monastery is an ancient sanctuary of Lathander that has been infiltrated and repurposed into Crèche Y'llek by the Githyanki. Players explore the ruined upper levels to solve a puzzle involving ceremonial weapons before descending into the basement to advance Lae'zel's questline. Within the crèche, you navigate tense interactions with Githyanki officials, encounter the Inquisitor, and choose how to approach an audience with Queen Vlaakith.

Items

  • Ceremonial Battleaxe MISSABLE: Upper monastery, inside a room guarded by an enchanted guardian behind a glowing barrier
  • Ceremonial Warhammer MISSABLE: The highest exterior nest atop the monastery, defended by giant eagles
  • The Blood of Lathander MISSABLE: Inside the secret vault accessed behind the Inquisitor's chamber in the monastery basement
  • The Whispering Mask: In the monastery basement laboratory where the Githyanki doctor works

Secrets

  • Rotating the two statues in the chamber behind the Inquisitor—one facing east and one facing west—unseals the hidden passage to the Blood of Lathander.
  • Solving the stained-glass window puzzle on the upper level by placing four ceremonial weapons on their corresponding altars grants the Morninglord's Radiance buff and reveals the Dawnmaster's Crest.

Shops and people

  • A'jak'nir Jeera: Sells specialized Githyanki equipment, weapons, and rare consumables inside the Crèche Y'llek living quarters

Tips

  • Entering the Zaith'isk device in the infirmary can grant permanent buffs or debuffs depending on saving throw outcomes.
  • Grabbing the Blood of Lathander without inserting the Dawnmaster's Crest starts a four-turn self-destruct sequence for the entire monastery.
  • Stealing the Githyanki egg from the hatchery can be resolved peacefully by talking to the caretaker or through stealth.
