Hollow

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The inner sanctum of the Emerald Grove

The Hollow serves as the makeshift settlement inside the Emerald Grove where the Tiefling refugees have set up camp under Zevlor's leadership. Players can trade with merchants, recruit the companion Wyll while he trains children, and resolve tensions between the Tieflings and Druids. It also contains the entrance to the Secluded Cove, the Tiefling hideout, and an improvised prison holding a captured goblin.

Items

  • Soul Coin MISSABLE: In the possession of Nadira on the lookout hill above the Hollow
  • Cap of Curing: Found inside a locked gilded chest behind the bard Alfira near the eastern cliffs
  • Ring of Flinging: Sold by the merchant Arron near the Hollow entrance path

Secrets

  • A secret underground passage connects the Hollow prison cliff to the outside wilderness through a cave filled with guardian statues and sleeping goblins.
  • A concealed Tiefling hideout for children can be entered through a tiny hole if shapeshifted/small, or by learning the password from Doni.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Arron: Sells mundane equipment, arrows, potions, camp supplies, and the Ring of Flinging
  • Dammon: Sells weapons, medium and heavy armor, and offers to forge infernal iron upgrades for Karlach
  • Mattis: Child merchant who attempts to trick the player with a coin scam and sells mundane trinkets and lockpicks

Tips

  • You must intervene immediately when approaching Nadira on the cliff, or the bugbear assassin will kill her and destroy your chance to speak peacefully with her.
  • Sassa the captured goblin in the makeshift prison will offer to lead you safely to the Goblin Camp if you break her out.
  • Interacting with Alfira on the cliffs gives you the opportunity to help her finish her song, which can grant Musical Instrument proficiency.
