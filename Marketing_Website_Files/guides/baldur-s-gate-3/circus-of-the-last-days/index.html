Circus of the Last Days

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The carnival grounds in Rivington

The Circus of the Last Days is a lively carnival in Rivington filled with eccentric performers, strange stalls, and hidden dangers. Players can watch a performance by Dribbles the Clown, unravel a sinister murderous plot beneath the festivities, and participate in various carnival games. Exploring the grounds kicks off the expansive quest to recover the real clown's scattered body parts across Act 3.

Items

  • Nyrulna MISSABLE: Carried by Akabi; spun into a jungle dimension via his wheel after pickpocketing his ring, found inside a locked chest at the exit portal
  • Band of the Mystic Scoundrel MISSABLE: Inside a skeleton's pouch in the jungle demiplane accessed via Akabi's rigged wheel
  • Clown's Severed Hand MISSABLE: On a plate covered by a cloth at Popper the Kobold's treat stall

Secrets

  • Pickpocket Akabi's Djinni Ring before spinning his wheel of fortune to win the grand prize and trigger him banishing you to a hidden dinosaur-filled jungle.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes

Shops and people

  • Popper: Sells potions, alchemical ingredients, and miscellaneous junk, but watches the Clown's Severed Hand closely.
  • Boney and Stoney: Sells a permanent custom statue of any selected party member for 5,000 gold that provides a daily Bless buff.
  • Zethino: Offers a companion romance compatibility quiz for a fee to gain approval from your current romantic partner.

Tips

  • Cast Invisibility or use distraction spells like Minor Illusion to steal the severed hand from Popper's stall without angering the entire circus.
  • Talk to Crimson the Displacer Beast using Speak with Animals to offer it food and convince it not to attack when the stage fight begins.
