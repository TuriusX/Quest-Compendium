Shattered Sanctum

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The interior of the goblin fortress

The Shattered Sanctum is the central stronghold of the goblin camp, serving as the headquarters for the Cult of the Absolute's local leadership. Players venture inside to eliminate or ally with the three goblin leaders, rescue several captured prisoners, and locate a hidden route to the Underdark. Navigating the interior requires careful diplomacy or strategic stealth to avoid turning the entire fortress hostile at once.

Secrets

  • Rotating Stone Floor Puzzle | In the Defiled Temple past Gut's chambers, align the four stone discs so that all completely darkened circles are lit by the light source to unlock the secret door to the Underdark
  • Secret Back Exit | Behind Dror Ragzlin's throne room, a cracked wall can be broken with bludgeoning or force damage to access an alternate path out of the fortress
  • Spider Pit Access | Two giant spiders are held in a pit below the main walkways; the iron gate can be picked, or enemies can be shoved directly into the pit to be eaten

Shops and people

  • Roah Moonglow: Zhentarim trader selling various magic items, thievery tools, arrows, and camping supplies
  • Abdirak: Offers a blessing ritual of Loviatar granting a permanent buff if the character withstands three rounds of torture without failing checks

Tips

  • Accept Priestess Gut's invitation into her private room to isolate and kill her without alerting the rest of the goblin camp.
  • Break the wooden war drums scattered around the sanctum before initiating combat to prevent goblins from summoning reinforcements.
  • Use Speak with Animals on the giant spiders in the pit to convince them to attack the goblins rather than your party.
  • You can shoot down the hanging stone braziers from the rafters to drop heavy fire damage onto large clusters of goblins below.
  • Pushing Minthara into the chasm destroys her body and makes her unique equipment permanently unlootable.
