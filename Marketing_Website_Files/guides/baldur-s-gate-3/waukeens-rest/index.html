Waukeen's Rest

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The burning inn near the Risen Road

Waukeen's Rest is a burning estate and inn located in the northwest section of the Risen Road, recently attacked by drow and goblins. You must act quickly upon arriving to rescue people trapped inside the burning buildings before they perish. Underneath the courtyard buildings lies the hidden entrance to the Zhentarim Hideout.

Items

  • Hamarhraft MISSABLE: inside a gilded chest in the burning second-floor room of the main inn where Counsellor Florrick is trapped
  • The Sparky Points, The Joltshooter, or The Spellsparkler MISSABLE: given by Counsellor Florrick outside the inn as a quest reward for rescuing her
  • Smuggler's Ring: on a skeleton hidden in a bush down by the riverbank south of Waukeen's Rest

Secrets

  • In the small barn behind the main building, moving a crate reveals a hatch leading to the Zhentarim Hideout, accessible by giving the password obtained from Rugan or via dialogue checks with Salazon.
  • A secret door disguised as a bookshelf in the back of the Zhentarim Hideout can be detected via a passive Perception check to access the rest of the underground network.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Brem: special weapons, lockpicks, thieving tools, and the Titanstring Bow (requires unlocking his expanded stock via "Find the Missing Shipment")

Tips

  • Long resting or leaving the region after approaching the burning inn causes the fire to fully spread, killing Counsellor Florrick and Benryn.
  • Turn-based mode should be activated immediately when entering the burning rooms to easily navigate the flames and avoid taking environmental damage.
  • Casting "Create Water" or throwing water carafes will extinguish floor fires and clear a safe path to save the trapped victims.
  • Benryn requires immediate healing or aid after the wooden beam is lifted from him, or he will quickly succumb to fire damage while fleeing.
