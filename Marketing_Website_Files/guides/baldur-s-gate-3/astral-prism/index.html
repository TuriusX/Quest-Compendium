Astral Prism

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The pocket dimension inside the artifact

The Astral Prism is a mysterious pocket dimension within the mysterious artifact where the player encounters their Dream Guardian and confronts the truth about their protection from the Absolute. The party visits this realm during key story transitions, notably at the end of Act 2 and the transition into Act 3, to fight off incursions and make critical narrative choices regarding illithid powers. In late-game visits, you navigate floating platforms and psychic terrain to either ally with the Emperor or free Prince Orpheus.

Items

  • Astral-Touched Tadpole MISSABLE: Offered directly by the Emperor after defeating the Githyanki honor guard
  • Boots of Uninhibited Kushigo MISSABLE: Looted from the corpse of Prelate Lir'u'c during the honor guard battle

Tips

  • High-mobility spells like Misty Step or Dimension Door are extremely helpful for navigating the floating platforms during the combat encounters here.
  • Deciding whether to consume, use, or reject the Astral-Touched Tadpole permanently determines whether your character undergoes partial illithid transformation.
  • Falling off the edges of the floating rock formations results in instant death, so be mindful of enemy shoving actions.
