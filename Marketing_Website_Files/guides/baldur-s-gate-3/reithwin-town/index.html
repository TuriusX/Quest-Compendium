Reithwin Town

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The central ruined town of the Shadow-Cursed Lands

Reithwin Town forms the central, heavily cursed settlement of the Shadow-Cursed Lands, divided by a river and dominated by Thorm family members. Players must navigate deep shadow curses using a Moonlantern or the Pixie's Blessing to reach key locations like the Tollhouse, the House of Healing, the Waning Moon, and the Grand Mausoleum entrance. Resolving encounters with Ketheric's corrupted kin through combat or dialogue provides vast amounts of experience and quest items before heading to Moonrise Towers.

Items

  • Ironvine Shield: Inside a locked wooden chest on the roof of the Mason's Guild
  • Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet MISSABLE: Dropped by Malus Thorm inside the House of Healing
  • Helmet of Arcane Acuity: Inside a locked heavy chest in the basement of the Mason's Guild behind a hidden shelf
  • Rat Bat MISSABLE: Carried by Thisobald Thorm in The Waning Moon
  • Ichorous Gloves: Found inside a locked chest in the storeroom of the House of Healing

Secrets

  • Sharran Sanctuary under the town square statue | Press the plaques in order: North, West, East, to open the secret door underneath the large Ketheric monument.
  • Mason's Guild secret basement | Pull the ornate shield switch in the back room to reveal an underground passage leading to a Sharran workshop.

Tips

  • High Charisma and Deception or Persuasion skills allow you to defeat Malus, Gerringothe, and Thisobald without ever rolling initiative.
  • The ambient curse in Reithwin Town requires an active Moonlantern or the Pixie's Blessing, as ordinary torches will not protect party members here.
  • Sending Gerringothe's Visages far away from her or breaking them first strips her of her massive base armor rating.
