Temple of Bhaal

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The inner sanctum of the Bhaalist cult

The Temple of Bhaal is the hidden underground sanctum where players confront Orin the Red to claim her Netherstone. Reaching the inner altar requires offering an Amulet of Bhaal at the massive solid stone gate and surviving a deadly ambush path guarded by snipers. Defeating Orin here directly determines the fate of the companion or child she kidnapped earlier in Act 3.

Items

  • Bloodthirst MISSABLE: Carried by Orin the Red; looted from her remains after combat
  • Crimson Mischief MISSABLE: Carried by Orin the Red; looted from her remains after combat
  • Orin's Netherstone: Dropped by Orin the Red upon her death

Secrets

  • Orin's private chambers lie just behind the central altar platform, where speaking with the preserved corpse of Helena (Orin's mother) via Speak with Dead reveals dark truths about Sarevok that can rattle Orin before battle.

Tips

  • Magic Missile, Scorching Ray, or Arrow of Many Targets are the most efficient tools to strip away Orin's Unstoppable stacks in a single turn.
  • If playing as the Dark Urge, accepting the one-on-one duel prevents cultist interference, but companions cannot assist without breaking the sacred rite.
  • Ensure you lockpick or immediately unlock the sacrificial altar during combat to save Orin's captive before wide-range attacks kill them collateral damage.
