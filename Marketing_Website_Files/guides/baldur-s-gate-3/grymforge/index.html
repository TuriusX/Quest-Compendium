Grymforge

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The ancient Sharran fortress accessed by boat

Grymforge is an ancient, ruined Sharran fortress and foundry located deep in the Underdark, currently occupied by Duergar slavers and True Soul Nere. Players navigate political betrayal among the dwarves, rescue or execute Nere from a cave-in, and delve deeper to uncover the legendary Adamantine Forge. Progressing through the forge allows the party to craft powerful adamantine equipment after overcoming its massive guardian.

Items

  • Disintegrating Night Walkers MISSABLE: Looted from True Soul Nere's corpse in the collapsed excavation chamber
  • Sword of Screams MISSABLE: Looted from True Soul Nere's corpse
  • Deep Delver MISSABLE: Looted from Corsair Greymon's corpse at the docks
  • Real Sparky Sparkswall: Found inside a locked gilded chest behind a hidden door along the upper Sharran walkways
  • Dark Justiciar Mail: Found on a skeleton near the cave-in where the cave rothe are working
  • Splint Mould: Found lying on the high metal walkways near the central skeleton
  • Scale Mail Mould: Found on the ground to the right near the start of the walkway leading down to the forge
  • Shield Mould: Found on a skeleton at the top of the stairs behind the dormitories
  • Scimitar Mould: Found next to a skeleton near the levers that operate the moving platforms
  • Longsword Mould: Found on the metal walkway just above the entrance to the Adamantine Forge
  • Mace Mould: Found on the ground near the Underdark - Ancient Forge waypoint
  • Mithral Ore: Mined from a blue glowing ore vein guarded by magma mephits near the forge stairs
  • Mithral Ore: Mined from a blue glowing ore vein guarded by lava near the path down to the forge
  • Grymskull Helm MISSABLE: Looted from Grym's corpse at the Adamantine Forge

Secrets

  • A Harper cache with a trapped chest and mimic ambush is hidden across a cragged rock path near the docks waterfall.
  • A hidden Sharran altar room containing the Idol of Shar can be accessed by pressing a hidden stone button on the wall in the upper corridors.
  • Philomeen hides in a secluded storage room across the water to the northeast with a barrel of Runepowder, accessible by passing through a locked double door and fighting ochre jellies.

  • Corsair Greymon: Sells generic weapons, crossbows, and basic armor at the docks before hostility triggers

Tips

  • Taking a long rest after clearing the rubble or triggering the cave-in event will cause Nere and the trapped gnomes to suffocate and die.
  • You can convince Elder Brithvar near the entrance to turn against Nere, allowing you to recruit half the Duergar to fight alongside you.
  • Use minor illusion or misty step to position party members on the high platforms before starting the fight against Grym.
  • Only two pieces of adamantine gear can be crafted in total because there are only two Mithral Ore veins in the entire zone.
  • Bludgeoning damage from the giant forge hammer can kill Grym in two strikes if you lure him onto the central anvil while he is superheated.
  • Smokepowder satchels or Philomeen's runepowder vial are required to safely clear the cave-in without killing the gnomes trapped behind the rocks.
