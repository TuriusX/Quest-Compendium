Risen Road

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The mountain pass region north of the village

The Risen Road is a rugged region north of the Blighted Village plagued by gnoll attacks and burning wreckage. Players must navigate ambushes, investigate a devastated caravan, and decide the fates of Karlach, the "Paladins of Tyr", and the survivors trapped at Waukeen's Rest.

Items

  • Sword of Justice MISSABLE: Held by Anders in the tollhouse; obtained by killing him or completing his quest
  • Smuggler's Ring: Looted from a skeleton hidden in the bushes along the riverbed south of the tollhouse
  • Hamarhraft MISSABLE: Inside a gilded chest in a burning room on the upper floor of Waukeen's Rest
  • The Sparky Points, The Joltshooter, or The Spellsparkler MISSABLE: Reward choices from Counsellor Florrick after rescuing her from the burning inn at Waukeen's Rest

Secrets

  • A heavy wooden trapdoor inside the tollhouse cellar leads to a vault protected by an iron door, which requires sitting two party members simultaneously on hidden stone chair pressure plates to open.
  • Moving a wardrobe inside the back room of the wine cellar behind the burning building at Waukeen's Rest reveals the hidden entrance to the Zhentarim Hideout.

Shops and people

  • Brem: Basic adventuring gear, thieves' tools, and expanded stock (such as Titanstring Bow) if you bring the sealed caravan chest unopened

Tips

  • When approaching the burning inn at Waukeen's Rest, enter turn-based mode immediately to stop the fire from advancing while you rescue the trapped NPCs.
  • Do not open the locked Caravan Strongbox found in the gnoll cave if you want to gain peaceful access and full vendor inventory in the Zhentarim Hideout.
  • You can use the high ground above the cave entrance to rain ranged attacks and area-of-effect spells down on the gnoll pack safely.
