Decrepit Village

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The Myconid colony and surrounding area

The Decrepit Village is an abandoned duergar outpost in the Underdark located south of the Myconid Colony and right by the beach. It serves as an ambush site set by duergar slavers hunting for a runaway gnome, as well as the dock to take a boat to Grymforge. Players can either fight the duergar to fulfill Sovereign Spaw's quest or talk their way into allied passage across the lake.

Items

  • Exterminator's Axe MISSABLE: looted from Gekh Coal in the ruined central hut
  • Dark Amethyst MISSABLE: inside a nest in the phase spider area just prior to descending to the village

Secrets

  • A chest of mundanity sits atop a balcony in the nearby Arcane Tower perimeter, transforming items into heavy junk until moved to player inventory.

Tips

  • Approach the village from stealth or use dialogue, as Gekh Coal will immediately raise four animated spore servants if combat initiates from afar.
  • You can use the bodies of dead duergar raised by Sovereign Glut as powerful combat minions if you bring him along for his quest.
