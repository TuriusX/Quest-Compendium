Sewers

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The extensive tunnels beneath the Lower City

The Sewers lie directly beneath the Lower City, serving as a sprawling hub that links diverse underground factions, criminal hideouts, and forgotten ruins. Navigating this area involves confronting the Bhaalist cult, investigating the Guild's conflict with the Stone Lord, and locating companions kidnapped by Orin the Red. Players must also solve environmental puzzles to access the Temple of Bhaal and ancient transit passages.

Items

  • Amulet of the Unworthy MISSABLE: Looted from a chest in the grease-filled pipes near the central sewers
  • Hood of the Unfeeling: Inside a locked gilded chest in the Undercity Ruins near the Ancient Door

Secrets

  • The Cistern Valve puzzle requires balancing water and heat indicators simultaneously to open the floodgate leading directly beneath the Counting House vaults.
  • Shooting the hanging bodies with a ranged attack near the Temple of Bhaal entrance reveals the path forward across the chasm.

  • Danthelon: Sells weapons, specialized arrows, and armor

Tips

  • Equipping items or casting spells that grant immunity to slipping makes navigating the vast Grease-covered western tunnels significantly easier.
  • Use non-lethal attacks when subduing Minsc in the abandoned cistern to successfully recruit him alongside Jaheira.
