Underdark

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The vast subterranean cavern system

The Underdark is a massive subterranean biome accessible through multiple entrances in Act 1, including the Defiled Temple, the Whispering Depths, the Zhentarim Hideout, and the Riverside Teahouse. It features various warring and struggling factions such as the Myconids and Duergar, and serves as one of the two main progression routes toward the Moonrise Towers. Players must navigate environmental hazards like explosive bibberbang spores and tackle regional quests to secure safe passage to the Grymforge.

Items

  • Phalar Aluve MISSABLE: Stuck inside a stone altar southwest of the Selûnite Outpost
  • Mourning Frost parts (Icy Helve) MISSABLE: Carried by Dhourn, the petrified drow near the Spectator encounter west of the Selûnite Outpost
  • Mourning Frost parts (Icy Metal) MISSABLE: Looted from the dead drow inside the locked treasure room at the Myconid Colony
  • Mourning Frost parts (Icy Crystal) MISSABLE: Carried by Filro the Forgotten near the Sussur Tree
  • Sussur Tree Bark MISSABLE: Gathered from the trunk of the massive Sussur Tree guarded by Hook Horrors
  • Boots of Speed MISSABLE: Rewarded by Thulla in the Myconid Colony after curing her poisoning

Secrets

  • The Festering Cove, a hidden shrine dedicated to the fish-like Kuo-Toa worshiping BOAL, can be found by climbing down cragged rocks near the entrance to the Arcane Tower area.
  • The Arcane Tower's power can be fully restored by descending to the lowest garden level, harvesting a Sussur Flower, and placing it directly into the basement generator.

  • Blurg: Sells various magical rings, scrolls, and alchemical ingredients in the Myconid Colony
  • Derryth Bonecloak: Sells potions, alchemical extracts, and rare ingredients in the Myconid Colony
  • Omeluum: Sells psionic-themed magical equipment and rings after completing its investigation quest

Tips

  • Sussur Flowers emit an anti-magic aura that completely disables nearby spells and constructs, but they will wither and become useless if brought up to the surface.
  • The defense turrets guarding the exterior and entrance of the Arcane Tower are weak to lightning damage and can be permanently disabled by restoring the tower's generator with a Sussur Flower.
  • Soothing Spores and other friendly buffs from Glut or Sovereign Spaw can grant party-wide bonuses during the tough subterranean fights if you enlist them as temporary allies.
