Steel Watch Foundry

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The factory producing the Steel Watchers

The Steel Watch Foundry is the industrial facility where Lord Gortash constructs and commands his mechanical Steel Watchers. To weaken Gortash's grip on the city, the party must infiltrate the facility, decide the fate of the enslaved Gondian workforce, and destroy the Neurocitor control mechanism. This can be accomplished by detonating the factory with a Runepowder Bomb or by enlisting the help of the blinded Gondian leader, Zanner Toobin.

Items

  • Gontr Mael MISSABLE: Looted from the defeated Steel Watcher Titan in the Control Centre
  • Watcher Helmet: Found on a workbench in the Foundry's laboratory level
  • Watcher Crossbow: Looted from broken Steel Watcher parts on workbenches in the lab

Secrets

  • The Security Office sub-level contains an elevator directly to the control center, accessible by lockpicking the terminal door or pickpocketing the key from Black Gauntlet Tam.

Tips

  • Use Lightning damage and chain-lightning spells heavily throughout the lower levels, as mechanical enemies take double damage from it.
  • If you wish to save the Gondians, rescue their families from the Iron Throne underwater prison before assaulting the Foundry.
  • When a Steel Watcher begins its self-detonation sequence, immediately shove, teleport, or move your party outside the highlighted red blast radius.
