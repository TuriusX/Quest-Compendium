House of Healing

Baldur's Gate 3 guide · The hospital run by Malus Thorm

The House of Healing is a grim hospital in the Shadow-Cursed Lands run by Malus Thorm, who torments patients alongside undead nurse assistants. Players come here to confront Malus and retrieve an instrument needed to awaken Art Cullagh at Last Light Inn. You can either eliminate the staff in combat or use persuasion and deception to make Malus and his nurses destroy themselves.

Items

  • Battered Lute MISSABLE: Carried by Malus Thorm in the operating theater
  • Surgeon's Subjugation Amulet MISSABLE: Carried by Malus Thorm in the operating theater
  • Poisoner's Gloves: Found inside a chest in the House of Healing dispensary

Tips

  • High Charisma characters can completely bypass the boss fight by convincing Malus to let the nurses practice on him.
  • If entering combat, disarm or kill the nurses quickly because they continuously pass specialized surgical instruments to Malus to empower his attacks.
  • Take the Battered Lute immediately back to Art Cullagh at the Last Light Inn to progress the quest to lift the Shadow Curse.
