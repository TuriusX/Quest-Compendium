Chozo Ruins

Metroid Prime guide · First exploration of the ruins

Samus explores the crumbling, ancient architecture left behind by the Chozo civilization after arriving from the Tallon Overworld. You solve environmental puzzles, activate dormant machinery, and reclaim essential lost upgrades to push deeper into the ruins. The exploration culminates in purifying the local water supply by defeating Flaahgra in the Sun Chamber.

Secrets

  • Missile Expansion in Ruined Shrine | Detonate a Morph Ball Bomb against the cracked wall on the lower level of the room
  • Energy Tank in Main Plaza | Jump into the tree hollow high up on the wall using the upper ledges

Tips

  • Always scan the environment carefully; the Scan Visor reveals cracked walls, lore entries, and switches needed to advance.
  • Avoid falling into the brown, toxic water early on, as it damages Samus until Flaahgra is defeated.
