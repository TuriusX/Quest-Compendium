Chozo Ruins

Metroid Prime guide · First exploration of the ruins

Samus explores the crumbling, ancient architecture left behind by the Chozo civilization after arriving from the Tallon Overworld. You solve environmental puzzles, activate dormant machinery, and reclaim essential lost upgrades to push deeper into the ruins. The exploration culminates in purifying the local water supply by defeating Flaahgra in the Sun Chamber.

Enemies
Hive Mecha
Weak to Vulnerable center exposed when firing

Stationary defense unit that spawns Ram Warps and opens its weak spot after each swarm

Plated Parasite

Small armored creatures that march in lines and cannot be harmed by conventional weaponry

Ram Warsp

Flying insect that charges at Samus after circling around her

Shriekbat
Weak to Single Power Beam shot

Roosts on ceilings and dives toward Samus in a suicidal flaming dive

Chozo Ghost
Weak to Super Missiles or concentrated Power Beam fire

Ethereal specters that phased in and out of visibility and disrupt visor tracking

Flaahgra
Weak to Solar reflectors stunned by Power Beam or Missiles, then Morph Ball Bombs in root tunnels

Giant corrupted plant boss keeping the water acidic; stun its solar panels to expose base root paths

Warsp Hive
Weak to Missiles

Hanging nests that continuously generate Warps until destroyed

Tips
  • Always scan the environment carefully; the Scan Visor reveals cracked walls, lore entries, and switches needed to advance.
  • Avoid falling into the brown, toxic water early on, as it damages Samus until Flaahgra is defeated.
