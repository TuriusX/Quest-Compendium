Frigate Orpheon (Crashed Frigate)

Metroid Prime guide · Returning to investigate the crash site

Samus explores the submerged wreckage of the Space Pirate research vessel after it crashes into the waters of the Tallon Overworld. Navigating the interior requires using the Gravity Suit to move unimpeded through flooded corridors and the Ice Beam to power down energy conduits. Clearing the wreck opens up a direct access route to the Phazon Mines.

Items

  • Energy Tank: Cargo Freight Lift to Deck Gamma, inside the broken elevator shaft behind a blast shield opened with a Missile
  • Missile Expansion: Main Ventilation Shaft Section C, inside a Morph Ball pipe near the ceiling reached by navigating a small maze
  • Missile Expansion: Hydro Access Tunnel, at the very top of a tall underwater vertical shaft reached by bomb jumping in Morph Ball form with the Gravity Suit

Secrets

  • In Reactor Core, utilize the Thermal Visor to locate hidden power conduits that activate the doors when shot with the Wave Beam.

Enemies

Tips

  • Equipping the Gravity Suit is practically mandatory before exploring this area, as normal jumping physics are heavily restricted underwater without it.
  • Use the Thermal Visor to identify unpowered door conduits obscured by murky water and failing ship systems.
