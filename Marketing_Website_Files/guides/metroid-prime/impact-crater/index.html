Impact Crater

Metroid Prime guide · Final descent into the planet's core

The Impact Crater is the final area of the game, accessed from the Artifact Temple after collecting all twelve Chozo Artifacts. Samus descends through red Phazon-corrupted chasms filled with dangerous fission-splitting enemies to reach the core. At the bottom, she engages in the final battle against the source of the planetary infection, Metroid Prime.

Items

  • Missile Expansion: Crater Tunnel B, hidden behind a red Phazon growth that requires walking into it to collect

Tips

  • Equip the Phazon Suit to prevent damage from the blue Phazon lining the crater, but remember that the dense orange/red Phazon will still damage Samus rapidly.
  • During the second phase of Metroid Prime, switch between the Combat, X-Ray, and Thermal Visors depending on which spectrum the boss becomes translucent in.
  • Power Bombs can instantly eliminate groups of Fission Metroids if you are overwhelmed during the descent or the final boss fight.
