Phazon Mines

Metroid Prime guide · After obtaining the Gravity Suit and exploring Phendrana Drifts

The Phazon Mines is the heavily fortified research and excavation facility run by the Space Pirates to harvest Phazon. Entering after clearing the Gravity Suit sequence in Phendrana Drifts, Samus must battle through elite security forces to locate key power-ups like the Power Bomb and the Grapple Beam. The descent takes you through deep processing laboratories down toward the Phazon-infested quarry and fungal caverns.

Items

  • Power Bomb: Central Dynamo
  • Grapple Beam: Storage Depot B
  • Phazon Suit: Elite Quarters
  • Flamethrower: Storage Depot A
  • Missile Expansion: Main Quarry
  • Missile Expansion: Security Access A
  • Energy Tank: Ventilation Shaft
  • Energy Tank: Processing Center Access

Secrets

  • In the Ventilation Shaft, laying a Power Bomb clears the exhaust grate, allowing you to access a console that lowers the fans to reach an Energy Tank.
  • In the Main Quarry, use the crane controls to move the demolition crane into a weakened rock wall, exposing a Missile Expansion.

Tips

  • Watch out for the long stretch between save points in the upper Phazon Mines; prioritize caution and utilize the Thermal Visor to spot hidden Shadow Pirates early.
  • Step into the Phazon pools only after acquiring the Phazon Suit, as touching Phazon beforehand will rapidly drain Samus's energy tanks.
  • Use Power Bombs to instantly eliminate cloaked Shadow Pirates or groups of standard Space Pirates when overwhelmed in tight hallways.
