Phendrana Drifts

Metroid Prime guide · First exploration of the icy region

Samus arrives in the snow-covered Phendrana Drifts after passing through Magmoor Caverns to find crucial mobility and elemental upgrades. The primary goal during this initial run is to navigate across ruins, ice caverns, and research areas to obtain the Boost Ball and the Wave Beam. Acquiring these items allows Samus to access high ledges, power up dormant machinery, and open purple conduits to continue her mission.

Enemies
Baby Sheegoth
Weak to Vulnerable back area after baiting an attack

Fires ice blasts and deflects frontal beam shots off its hardened shell.

Sheegoth
Weak to Mouth when open after firing, or underneath using Morph Ball Bombs

Large adult predator immune to most attacks until it pauses to vent breath.

Flickerbat
Weak to Any standard beam weapon

Flying creatures with natural optical camouflage that can be tracked by listening or using the Thermal Visor later.

Ice Burrower
Weak to Power Beam or Missiles

Tunneling insect that surfaces temporarily to ambush Samus from snow banks.

Tips
  • Use Morph Ball Bombs underneath an adult Sheegoth to inflict heavy damage if you struggle to shoot its open mouth in time.
  • Keep your distance from Baby Sheegoths to bait their projectile breath, then quickly dash around them to shoot their soft backs.
