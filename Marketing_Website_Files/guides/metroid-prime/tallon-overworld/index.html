Tallon Overworld

Metroid Prime guide · Landing on the planet

Tallon Overworld is the lush, rain-slicked surface region where Samus's Gunship touches down on Tallon IV after escaping the Frigate Orpheon. It functions as the central hub of the planet, connecting directly to the Chozo Ruins, Magmoor Caverns, and the Phazon Mines. As you acquire upgrades across the world, you will continually return here to access new pathways, salvage items from the crashed frigate, and unlock the final route into the Impact Crater.

Items

  • Missile Expansion: Landing Site, inside a narrow Morph Ball alcove behind Samus's Gunship
  • Missile Expansion: Transport to Chozo Ruins West, behind a blast shield on a side door
  • Energy Tank: Cargo Freight Lift to Deck Gamma, inside the wreckage of the crashed Frigate Orpheon
  • X-Ray Visor: Great Tree Hall, through a Morph Ball puzzle in the upper branches requiring the Ice Beam and Power Bombs
  • Artifact of Truth: Artifact Temple, located on the central pedestal in the middle of the totem ring
  • Artifact of Chozo: Life Grove, submerged at the bottom of the central pool of water

Secrets

  • Space Jump Boots early access: In the Alcove room above the Landing Site, you can reach the boots early without the Grapple Beam by performing a precise scan dash or advanced jump off the Gunship.
  • Hidden Energy Tank: In the Hydro Access Tunnel inside the submerged frigate, execute a high Morph Ball bomb jump straight up into the ceiling pipe while using the Gravity Suit to reveal a concealed container.

Tips

  • Save at Samus's Gunship on the Landing Site at any time to fully restore your ammunition, missiles, and energy for free.
  • Scan the twelve totem pillars in the Artifact Temple to record hints on the locations of every Chozo Artifact required to open the endgame seal.
  • Do not attempt to explore the flooded corridors of the crashed frigate until you have acquired the Gravity Suit from the Phendrana Drifts.
