Checkpoint Rest Town

Dragon's Dogma 2 guide · The border crossing between Vermund and Battahl
← All areas in this guide

The Checkpoint Rest Town sits on the border dividing the nations of Vermund and Battahl, centered around a heavily guarded gate. Players visit this settlement to find a way past the border crossing into Battahl while engaging with distinct local merchants and questlines. The town also serves as a key travel terminal, hosting an Oxcart station that connects back to Vernworth.

Achievements here
I'm In MissableSneak past the border gate without a border permit by hiding inside the back of an enclosed noble oxcart as it passes through the gate.
Across the BorderPass through the large sealed gate leading into Battahl, either using a permit, sneaking in an oxcart, or taking the river route.

Shops and people
  • Ibrahim's Scrap Store: Sells rare miscellaneous goods, forges item duplicates, and restores lost quest items
  • Celeste's Smithy: Sells weapons and armor and enhances gear using the Vermundian smithing style
  • Morris's Pharmacy: Sells curative items, potions, and crafting materials
  • The Sword & Staff: Inn offering rest, save point creation, and vocation/skill management

Tips
  • If you hold a Beastren Border Entry Permit as a human Arisen, equipping a Beastren Mask fools the gate guards into letting you pass.
  • You can bypass the closed border gate entirely by leaping onto the roof of an official carriage while it passes through the gate.
  • Ibrahim can create a counterfeit of the Jadeite Orb, allowing you to hand in duplicates to both NPCs pursuing the quest for maximum rewards.
  • Forging a counterfeit takes at least one full in-game day; pass the time on a nearby bench to collect the finished piece.
