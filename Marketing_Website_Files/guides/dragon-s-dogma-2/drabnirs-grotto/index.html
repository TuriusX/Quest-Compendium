Drabnir's Grotto

Dragon's Dogma 2 guide · The path leading to the Volcanic Island
← All areas in this guide

Drabnir's Grotto is a multi-level cavern that serves as an alternate pedestrian route connecting south Battahl to Agamen Volcanic Island without passing through the Spellseal Door. It is filled with narrow corridors, flooded subterranean areas, and vertical drops that house numerous dangerous monsters. Traversing the grotto requires clearing out ambushes while descending toward the southern exit leading to the volcanic region.

Enemies
Knacker
Weak to Fire

Leaps from ledges and attempts to drag pawns into water or off cliffs.

Magma Scale
Weak to Ice

Tougher lizard variants with burning rocky hides that deflect attacks unless struck on their weak underbellies.

Rattler
Weak to Strike attacks

Rocky saurians that roll into you; blunt weapons crack their shells easily.

Cyclops
Weak to Lightning, eyeSteal / drop Misshapen Eye

Fought in a cavernous chamber along the main path.

Ogre
Weak to Fire, head

Enrages quickly in close quarters, drop-kicking off cave walls.

Tips
  • Pack a Ferrystone and ensure your Lantern has plenty of oil, as the cave system is pitch black and very lengthy.
  • Watch your step near flooded drops; falling into deep underground water will summon the Brine and immediately kill you or your pawns.
  • The path is primarily a one-way slide down at certain vertical junctions, making returning to Battahl through the grotto much more difficult than progressing forward to the Volcanic Island.
