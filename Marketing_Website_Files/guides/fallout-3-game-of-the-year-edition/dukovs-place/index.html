Dukov's Place

Fallout 3: Game of the Year Edition guide · Meeting Dukov
← All areas in this guide

Dukov's Place is a small, fortified house near Tepid Sewers inhabited by Dukov, an eccentric party-lover, and his companions Cherry and Fantasia. Players typically visit to obtain Dukov's special key for the quest "You Gotta Shoot 'Em in the Head" and can also offer to escort Cherry to Rivet City. You can resolve Dukov's key peacefully through speech checks, payment, or by resorting to pickpocketing or killing him.

Shops and people
  • Fantasia: Offers sleeping arrangements in her bed to the player character for a fee of caps

Tips
  • If you speak to Cherry privately, she will ask you to escort her away to Rivet City; agree to start an escort encounter where keeping her alive yields a cap reward.
  • You can obtain Dukov's key without violence by using the Black Widow perk if playing a female character, or passing a Speech check if playing a male character.
