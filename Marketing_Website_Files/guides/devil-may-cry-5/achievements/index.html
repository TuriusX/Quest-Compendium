Recommended order
  1. Play through Human or Devil Hunter difficulty to learn mechanics, defeat bosses, grab initial Blue/Purple Orbs, and complete Secret Missions.
  2. Clear Son of Spadarda difficulty to unlock Dante Must Die and purchase remaining upgrades and skills.
  3. Clear Dante Must Die difficulty to unlock Super Costumes and Heaven or Hell difficulty.
  4. Clear Heaven or Hell difficulty quickly using one-hit-kill mechanics to unlock Hell and Hell difficulty.
  5. Clear Hell and Hell difficulty while learning enemy patterns and spawn points.
  6. Replay any required missions across all difficulties to earn an S rank on every difficulty mode for "Worthy of Legend".
  7. Clear all 101 floors of the Bloody Palace with Nero, Dante, and V to finish off remaining combat trophies.
  8. Farm remaining Red Orbs using Dante's Dr. Faust in Mission 13 to acquire all character skills.
