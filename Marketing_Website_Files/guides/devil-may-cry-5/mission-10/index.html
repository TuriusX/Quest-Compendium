Mission 10

Devil May Cry 5 guide · Dante infiltrates the inner sanctum
← All areas in this guide

Playing as Dante, you advance through fleshy interior pathways of the Qliphoth while dealing with timed closing door gates. The mission requires defeating waves of enemies quickly to pass through organic teeth-like valves before they seal shut. At the end of the chapter, Dante engages in a boss confrontation against Urizen.

Fights from his throne behind a protective red Yamato crystal that must be shattered to expose him.

Shops and people

Tips
  • Use Dante's Balrog in Kick Mode or Gunslinger attacks to quickly break enemy armor and clear rooms before the organic doors close.
  • You do not need to fully defeat Urizen in this mission; the encounter progresses to a cutscene once you deal sufficient damage or shatter the red crystal.
