Mission 08: Demon King

Devil May Cry 5 guide · Nero ascends the Qliphoth to confront Urizen again
← All areas in this guide

Nero continues his climb up the interior of the Qliphoth tree using rising lava platforms and cable-like veins. He fights through hordes of demons to reach the throne room at the top. The mission culminates in another boss encounter against Urizen.

Secrets

0/1

Enemies
Baphomet

Ranged demon that casts ice and dark spells from a distance.

Proto Angelo

Heavily armored knight with a massive sword; can block frontal attacks and enter a powered-up state.

Scudo Angelo

Shield-bearing infantry demons that often fight in formations alongside Proto Angelos.

Urizen

Defended by the Yamato crystal; attack the red crystal to shatter his defensive shield before dealing damage directly to him.

Tips
  • Use Gerbera's air mobility or the Flap Top ability to dodge Urizen's ground-sweeping laser beams.
  • You do not need to defeat Urizen to complete the mission; surviving long enough or shattering his shield triggers a cutscene that progresses the story.
