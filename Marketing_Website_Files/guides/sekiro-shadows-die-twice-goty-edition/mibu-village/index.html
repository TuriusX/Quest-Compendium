Mibu Village

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Post-Guardian Ape

Mibu Village is an isolated, decaying settlement afflicted by an unnatural thirst and populated by regenerating villagers. Players must traverse the village and the river to defeat the mist-shrouded spirit O'Rin of the Water and the Corrupted Monk illusion guarding the Wedding Cave Door. Inside the cave lies the Shelter Stone, a vital ingredient required to reach the Fountainhead Palace.

Items

  • Gourd Seed: Found under a glowing tree surrounded by praying villagers near the center of the village
  • Shelter Stone: Inside the shrine past the True Corrupted Monk's spectral arena
  • Red Carp Eyes: Dropped by glowing red-eyed carp swimming in the lake after obtaining the Mibu Breathing Technique

Secrets

  • There is an attic accessible via a shinobi crawlspace beneath the floor of the priest's house across the lake, leading to Water of the Palace and unique dialogues.

  • Exiled Memorial Mob: Mottled Purple Gourd, Dragon's Blood Droplet, Treasure Carp Scales, and crafting materials

Tips

  • Snap Seeds can be used against the spectral Corrupted Monk to deal heavy posture damage and stun her up to three times.
  • You can bypass many of the endlessly respawning villagers by sprinting along the rooftops or wading through the river instead of engaging directly.
