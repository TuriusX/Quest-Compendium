Ashina Outskirts

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Early game progression

Ashina Outskirts is the first major zone Wolf traverses after waking up in the Dilapidated Temple, serving as an introduction to combat, stealth, and vertical grappling traversal. Players make their way through ruined fortifications and encampments toward Ashina Castle, confronting several mini-bosses and gaining access to memory travel. The zone culminates in a battlefield arena fight against Gyoubu Oniwa outside the castle gate.

Items

  • Shinobi Prosthetic: Dilapidated Temple, given directly by the Sculptor
  • Loaded Shuriken (Shuriken Wheel): Outskirts Wall - Gate Path, upper floor of the damaged gatehouse just past the idol
  • Young Lord's Bell Charm: In a destroyed building between Gate Path and Stairway, given by the old woman Inosuke's mother
  • Robert's Firecrackers: Purchased for 500 sen from the Crow's Bed Memorial Mob atop the high cliff near Gate Path
  • Gourd Seed: Dropped by General Naomori Kawarada near Gate Path
  • Gourd Seed: In the chest inside the tall building right after defeating the Chained Ogre
  • Gourd Seed: Purchased for 1,000 sen from the Battlefield Memorial Mob near the castle gate
  • Nightjar Monocular: In the broken lookout tower near the Chained Ogre arena
  • Gokan's Sugar: In the clearing with the grave shrines beneath the bridge leading to the castle
  • Heavy Coin Purse: Carried by various enemies and found in the hidden path leading to the Headless cave

Secrets

  • Crow's Bed Memorial Mob: Grapple up the cliffside near the gatehouse where the cannon enemy stands, then follow the highest rock pinnacles to locate a hidden merchant tent.
  • Headless Cave and Senpou Temple Shortcut: From the bridge path near Underbridge Valley, jump down to the lower branch and follow the cliff edge around to find a dark cavern housing a Headless and a shinobi rotating wall that leads to the Bell Demon's Temple.
  • Great Serpent Stealth Encounter: Drop down into the Underbridge Valley chasm to navigate icy ledges and tall grass, hiding inside a palanquin to stab the giant snake in the eye and clear the path.

Tips

  • Eavesdrop on enemies whenever an icon appears, as guards routinely reveal enemy weaknesses like the Chained Ogre's fear of fire.
  • You can perform a stealth deathblow on most mini-bosses in this area by breaking their line of sight and sneaking behind them to eliminate one of their health nodes immediately.
  • Bring the Young Lord's Bell Charm back to the Buddha statue in the Dilapidated Temple as soon as possible to unlock the Hirata Estate memory.
