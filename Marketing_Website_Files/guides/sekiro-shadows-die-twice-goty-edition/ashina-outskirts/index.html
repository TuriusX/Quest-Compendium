Ashina Outskirts

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Wolf journeys past the castle outskirts and defeats Gyoubu Oniwa ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Wolf starts at the Dilapidated Temple after receiving the Shinobi Prosthetic and journeys across fortified cliffs, shattered gates, and treacherous valleys toward Ashina Castle. Along the way, he navigates past hostile samurai garrisons, evades the massive Great Serpent, and confronts guardian commanders blocking entry. The quest through the outskirts culminates in a battle on the battlefield against the gatekeeper Gyoubu Masataka Oniwa to breach the inner grounds.

Key fights
Gyoubu Masataka Oniwa

Enemies: Gyoubu Masataka Oniwa atop Onikage

Threats: Wide-sweeping spear swings, galloping charge attacks, and sweeping perilous strikes

Weaknesses and resistances: Shinobi Firecracker prosthetic, grapple attacks when the grapple indicator appears over his helmet

Tactics: Grapple onto his spear or helm when he circles away to close distance, use Firecrackers to spook Onikage for free hits, and deflect regular spear thrusts

Rewards: Memory: Gyoubu Oniwa, Mechanical Barrel, 1,000 Sen

Chained Ogre

Enemies: Chained Ogre

Threats: High-damage diving grabs, running tackles, dropkicks, and sweep stomps

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire attacks (Flame Vent) and Oil

Tactics: Circle closely, dodge diagonally through his forward lunges, apply Oil and ignite him with Flame Vent to cause long stuns, and grapple to his horns when thrown

Rewards: Prayer Bead, Shinobi Medicine Rank 1 latent skill
