Following the Divine Dragon fight, the Ministry forces launch a full-scale invasion, setting the Ashina Outskirts ablaze and cutting off direct access from Ashina Castle. Wolf must navigate the burning valley backwards toward the battlefield where Gyoubu was fought, dealing with Interior Ministry elite units. At the ruined battlefield, an optional confrontation against the Demon of Hatred awaits.

Enemies
Demon of Hatred
Weak to Malcontent finger whistle, Suzaku's Lotus UmbrellaSteal / drop Memory: Demon of Hatred, Lapis Lazuli

Massive fiery optional boss with three deathblow markers and sweeping, flame-infused attacks.

Shigekichi of the Red Guard
Weak to Sabimaru, FirecrackersSteal / drop Prayer Bead, Dragon's Tally Board

Heavy armored miniboss equipped with dual blades and a fire-breathing barrel, leading the troops at the stairway.

Red Guard Dual-Katana Shinobi
Weak to Deflection, Puppeteer NinjutsuSteal / drop Ministry Douse Powder, Fulminated Mercury

Lethal agile Ministry scouts patrolling the burning pathways, capable of rapid multi-hit combos.

Red Guard Gunner
Weak to Stealth Deathblow, DeflectionSteal / drop Black Gunpowder, Fulminated Mercury

Ranged Ministry riflemen armed with heavy flintlocks and flamethrowers positioned across barricades.

Tips
  • You can use the Malcontent finger whistle prosthetic up to three times during the Demon of Hatred fight to stun him for long attack windows, ideally saved for his third phase.
  • Suzaku's Lotus Umbrella completely negates all fire damage and knockback from the Demon of Hatred's leaping slam attack.
  • Equip Sabimaru against Shigekichi of the Red Guard to rapidly inflict poison, as Okami descendants and red-armored Ministry forces are susceptible to it.
