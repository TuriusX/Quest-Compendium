Fountainhead Palace

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Post-Divine Dragon ritual

After defeating the Divine Dragon, you obtain the Divine Dragon's Tears and the area transitions to nighttime, closing off the Sanctuary arena. You must return to Ashina Castle, which is now engulfed in a full-scale interior ministry invasion, to progress toward the conclusion of the game. The water and grounds of the palace remain accessible for exploration and cleanup, though the main boss encounter of the zone is completed.

Items

  • Divine Dragon's Tears: Sanctuary, received automatically after performing the Gracious Gift of Tears ritual on the Divine Dragon
  • Memory: Divine Dragon: Sanctuary, obtained immediately upon executing the Divine Dragon

Tips

  • If you missed feeding the Truly Precious Bait to the Great Colored Carp prior to defeating the dragon, you can still return to the Feeding Grounds to complete the questline.
  • You cannot re-enter the Divine Realm boss arena once the dragon has been defeated, so fast travel back out to Ashina Castle via any Sculptor's Idol.
