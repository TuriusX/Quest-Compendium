Ashina Depths

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Post-Guardian Ape

After defeating the Guardian Ape in Sunken Valley, you must travel through Ashina Depths to reach Mibu Village and retrieve the Shelter Stone. In the massive cavern before the Hidden Forest, the headless Guardian Ape reappears and eventually summons a second mate to fight alongside it. Defeating this duo clears the path forward and allows you to sever the immortal ape's ties permanently using the Mortal Blade.

Items

  • Bestowal Ninjutsu: Awarded automatically upon executing the final shinobi deathblow on the Headless Ape's centipede
  • Malcontent's Ring: Dropped by the Shichimen Warrior that appears in the Headless Ape's arena

Secrets

  • Revisit the Headless Ape's arena after defeating the duo and reloading the area to find a Shichimen Warrior standing in the pool of water.

Tips

  • Use the Shinobi Firecracker repeatedly on the brown ape as soon as she appears to build up her posture and eliminate her quickly before focusing on the headless ape.
  • Using the Loaded Spear's drag attack on the headless ape's severed neck when he falls over deals massive posture damage by pulling out the centipede.
  • Make sure to perform the second shinobi deathblow with the Mortal Blade on the twitching corpse after the fight ends to obtain the Bestowal Ninjutsu.
