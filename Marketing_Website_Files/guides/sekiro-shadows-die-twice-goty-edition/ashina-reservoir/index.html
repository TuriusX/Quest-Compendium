The Ashina Reservoir serves as the opening tutorial zone where Wolf wakes up disarmed and must sneak through the outskirts to reunite with the Divine Heir. After the prologue, Wolf returns here with the Shinobi Prosthetic to confront mini-bosses, rescue an NPC, and obtain prosthetic upgrades. Progression involves clearing guards around the gatehouse and two-story watchtower, defeating Seven Achina Spears, and accessing underground or peripheral paths.

Enemies
Seven Achina Spears - Shikibu Toshikatsu Yamauchi
Weak to Mikiri CounterSteal / drop Prayer Bead

Guards the stairs leading up to the Moon-viewing Tower; uses long-reach thrust attacks that can be heavily punished with Mikiri Counter.

Lone Shadow Longswordman
Weak to Fire / DeflectionSteal / drop Prayer Bead

Located inside the cramped bottom of the starting well; delivers high posture-damaging kicks and thrusts in tight quarters.

Ashina Soldier (Spear/Sword/Rifle)
Weak to Stealth deathblowsSteal / drop Pellets, Iron Scrap

Standard guards patrolling the reservoir terraces, gatehouse, and wall paths.

Bell Taro (Hammer/Giant)
Weak to Fire / Blind spotsSteal / drop Pellets

Large brute patrolling the lower trench area, prone to stagger from heavy hits or flame attacks.

Tips
  • You can sneak around the left cliffside of the Moon-viewing Tower to reach Seven Achina Spears from behind for a free initial stealth deathblow, removing half of his health bars immediately.
  • You can execute a plunging deathblow on the Lone Shadow Longswordman by dropping straight down through the hole in the ceiling of the well before initiating combat.
  • Practice the Mikiri Counter timing against Seven Achina Spears, as successfully countering his thrust often triggers a follow-up swipe that can be deflected for massive posture damage.
