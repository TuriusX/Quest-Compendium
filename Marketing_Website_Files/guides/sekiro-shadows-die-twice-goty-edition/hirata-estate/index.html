Hirata Estate

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Early game flashback

Hirata Estate is an optional memory area accessed by offering the Young Lord's Bell Charm to the Buddha statue in the Dilapidated Temple. Wolf investigates the raid on the estate from three years prior to rescue Kuro from bandits and the assassin group known as the Interior Ministry. The path leads through burning estates and bamboo thickets to face Shinobi Hunter Enshin of Misen, Juzou the Drunkard, and Lady Butterfly.

Items

  • Flame Barrel (Flame Vent prosthetic tool material): inside a roaring bonfire in the estate courtyard surrounded by sitting bandits
  • Shinobi Axe of the Monkey (Loaded Axe prosthetic tool material): inside a small shrine in the courtyard houses, which can be eavesdropped on from outside
  • Mist Raven's Feathers (Mist Raven prosthetic tool material): inside a hidden three-story pagoda up the bamboo thicket river path behind a bamboo barrier
  • Hidden Tooth: dropped by the Interior Ministry Shinobi guarding the Mist Raven pagoda, or looted from the pagoda if approached without fighting
  • Gourd Seed: in a chest in the building right behind Juzou the Drunkard
  • Unrefined Sake: given by Nogami Gensai after speaking with him, or found inside the main estate building

Secrets

  • Along the river near the Bamboo Thicket Slope idol, slice through hidden bamboo stalks to find a cave with a vertical wall-jump shaft leading to a pagoda guarded by a Lone Shadow swordsman holding the Mist Raven prosthetic material.
  • In the main building hallway before Lady Butterfly, slash a wall hanging bearing a drawing to reveal a revolving hidden door leading to a secret room containing coin bags and a prayer bead.

Tips

  • Recruit the samurai NPC Nogami Gensai standing in the reeds near Juzou the Drunkard only after you have cleared the surrounding bandit adds by yourself.
  • Throw a Shuriken at Lady Butterfly whenever she leaps into the air and pauses on her tightropes to knock her down for heavy posture damage.
  • You can sprint in wide circles around the arena when Lady Butterfly summons her phantom illusions until they turn into homing butterflies that you can outrun or hide behind pillars to avoid.
