Ashina Castle

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Mid-game progression

Ashina Castle serves as the central hub of the game's mid-game progression, featuring sprawling rooftops patrolled by agile shinobi and deep interior chambers. Players must ascend through the antechamber and dojo to reach the castle roof, where they confront Genichiro Ashina to rescue Lord Kuro. Afterwards, Lord Kuro's room opens up as a major dialogue and quest progression hub directing Wolf toward the items needed for the Immortal Severance ritual.

Items

  • Sabimaru: Found inside a chest in the lower flooded armory of the Ashina Castle interior, guarded by blue-robed samurai
  • Gourd Seed: In a treasure chest located directly past the Chained Ogre mini-boss inside the castle entrance gate
  • Gourd Seed: Inside a chest in the Ashina Castle Antechamber right next to the Idol

Secrets

  • Rotating Shinobi Wall | Between two suits of armor on the upper level of the Upper Tower - Antechamber; lean against it to access a secret room containing a chest with a Prayer Bead.

Shops and people

  • Fujioka the Info Broker: Relocates to the Dilapidated Temple after being saved from Ashina samurai near the castle entrance; sells Gourd Seeds and information memos.

Tips

  • For Genichiro's third phase, jump straight up into the air when he performs lightning strikes, receive the hit while airborne, and tap the attack button before landing to send the shock back for massive posture damage.
  • Equipping the Loaded Shuriken makes traversing the rooftops much easier, as airborne Nightjar take high posture damage and fall instantly when hit mid-jump.
