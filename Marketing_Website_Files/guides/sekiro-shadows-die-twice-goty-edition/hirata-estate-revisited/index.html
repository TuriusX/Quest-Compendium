Hirata Estate (Revisited)

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Late game return

Hirata Estate (Revisited) is accessed by using the Father's Bell Charm at the Buddha statue in the Dilapidated Temple, which requires pursuing the Purification ending steps with Emma. The estate is heavily engulfed in flames, featuring updated high-tier Interior Ministry enemy spawns, Lone Shadow assassins, and altered boss encounters. The primary objective is to defeat the powerful bosses residing here to obtain the Aromatic Flower key item required for the Purification ending.

Items

  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped upon defeating Lone Shadow Masanaga the Spear-bearer near the Bamboo Thicket Slope idol
  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped upon defeating Juzou the Drunkard in the Main Hall courtyard
  • Aromatic Flower MISSABLE: Dropped upon defeating Owl (Father) in the burning Audience Chamber
  • Memory: Foster Father MISSABLE: Dropped upon defeating Owl (Father) in the burning Audience Chamber

Tips

  • Use Gachiin's Sugar and stealth to eliminate or Puppeteer Juzou the Drunkard's Lone Shadow ally before engaging Juzou directly.
  • In the second phase of the Owl (Father) fight, jump over his sweeping flaming owl projectile and immediately prepare to Mikiri Counter his follow-up shadowrush thrust.
  • If you defeat the Divine Dragon in the Fountainhead Palace before acquiring the Father's Bell Charm from Emma, this entire revisit becomes permanently locked out for the current playthrough.
