After completing Fountainhead Palace and defeating the Divine Dragon, Ashina Castle is under final siege by the Ministry, which affects the Dilapidated Temple. The Sculptor is missing from his usual post, having succumbed to his inner flames, leaving Emma alone inside the temple. Players can speak to Emma to learn about the Sculptor's fate, upgrade prosthetics on their own at his workbench, and use the temple as a staging ground before confronting the Demon of Hatred in the outskirts.

Items

0/1

Shops and people
  • Sculptor's Workbench: Allows upgrading Shinobi Prosthetic tools without the Sculptor present
  • Emma: Offers Dragonrot recovery, Gourd Seed upgrades, and dialogue regarding the Sculptor's whereabouts

Tips
  • Examine the glowing idol in the Sculptor's room or the bonfire path leading toward the Ashina Outskirts to track down where the Sculptor went.
