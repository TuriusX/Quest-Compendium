Abandoned Dungeon

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Journeying underground through the infested waterway and bottomless chasm ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

The Abandoned Dungeon is an underground complex below Ashina Castle filled with decaying inmates, crickets, and sinister medical experiments. You explore flooded tunnels and subterranean caves to find an elevator connecting toward Senpou Temple or venture through the Bottomless Hole to reach the depths. Along the way, you can investigate surgeon Doujun's requests, avoid or eliminate rotting test subjects, and clear the apparitions lurking in the dark pits.

Key fights
Shichimen Warrior

Enemies: Shichimen Warrior

Threats: Fires tracking Terror soul orbs, summons spirit beams, teleports, and causes instant-kill Terror buildup

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Divine Confetti, Phoenix's Lilac Umbrella, and the Anti-air Deathblow technique; resistant to non-apparition attacks

Tactics: Consume Pacifying Agent or mottled purple gourd for Terror protection, coat Kusabimaru with Divine Confetti, aggressively close the distance when he teleports, and sprint laterally around spirit beams

Rewards: Ceremonial Tanto (or Bulging Coin Purse on subsequent playthroughs)

Enemies
Decaying Inmate
Weak to Flame Vent or fire attacks (burns prevent resurrection)

Can revive once upon defeat unless killed with fire, and attempts to sprint and grab you

Cricket

Small vermin scattered across floors and walls that attack in groups with minor damage

Shops and people
  • Dungeon Memorial Mob: Sells Mask Fragment: Dragon, a Prayer Bead, Dragon's Blood Droplet, Contact Medicine, Pellets, and Mibu Balloons

Tips
  • Coat your weapon with Divine Confetti before dropping into the lower pit, and use the Phoenix's Lilac Umbrella to deflect apparition beam attacks without accumulating lethal Terror.
