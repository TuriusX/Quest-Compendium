Following the events in Fountainhead Palace, Ashina Castle is besieged by the Interior Ministry and engulfed in flames. Wolf must navigate the burning, heavily fortified grounds, defeat invading elite forces, and make his way through the moat reservoir to the silvergrass field for the final confrontation.

Tips
  • Use the Puppeteer Ninjutsu on the samurai companion standing next to Seven Ashina Spears - Shume Masaji Oniwa to turn the fight into a two-on-one advantage.
  • Most Central Castle idols are disabled upon your return; you must re-attune them by exploring on foot from the Abandoned Dungeon Entrance.
  • In the final encounter with Isshin, stay at mid-range during phases two and three to cleanly bait his jump attack and thrust for guaranteed Mikiri Counters and Lightning Reversals.
