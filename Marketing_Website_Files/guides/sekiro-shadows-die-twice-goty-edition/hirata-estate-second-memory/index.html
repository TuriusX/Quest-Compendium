Hirata Estate (Second Memory)

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Mid-game return

Accessed by using the Father's Bell Charm at the Buddha statue in the Dilapidated Temple, this alternate memory presents Hirata Estate in flames with tougher enemy layouts. Wolf pushes through burning courtyards to confront the Lone Shadow Masanaga, Juzou the Drunkard, and ultimately Owl (Father). Defeating the final boss here yields the key quest item required for the Purification ending.

Items

  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped by Lone Shadow Masanaga the Spear-Bearer outside the burning main hall
  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped by Juzou the Drunkard in the burning courtyard
  • Aromatic Flower MISSABLE: Dropped by Owl (Father) upon defeat in the underground audience chamber
  • Memory: Foster Father MISSABLE: Dropped by Owl (Father) upon defeat in the underground audience chamber

Tips

  • Use a Gachiin's Sugar to sneak behind Juzou's Interior Ministry ally, allowing you to Puppeteer him and turn the fight into a 2v1 advantage.
  • In the second phase against Owl (Father), jump vertically over his flaming owl projectile, then immediately prepare to Mikiri Counter his follow-up shadowrush.
