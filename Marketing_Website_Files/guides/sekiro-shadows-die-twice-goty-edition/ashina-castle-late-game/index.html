Ashina Castle (Late game)

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Late game invasion

Following the Divine Dragon's defeat in Fountainhead Palace, the Ministry forces launch their full-scale final invasion and set Ashina Castle ablaze. The Central Forces push deep into the castle grounds, overrunning Ashina soldiers and cutting off many previous pathways with flames and destroyed bridges. You must traverse the burning district, defeat powerful commanders like Shigekiri Yamauchi and Seven Ashina Spears - Shume Masaji Oniwa, and reach the Silvergrass Meadow via the reservoir moat.

Items

  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped by Seven Ashina Spears - Shume Masaji Oniwa near the Ashina Reservoir
  • Prayer Bead MISSABLE: Dropped by Shigekori Yamauchi (Red Guard samurai general) guarding the stairs leading up to Ashina Castle
  • Lapis Lazuli MISSABLE: Dropped by Demon of Hatred at the Ashina Castle Gate battlefield (accessed via kite from the outskirts bridge)
  • Memory: Hatred Demon MISSABLE: Dropped by defeating Demon of Hatred
  • Dragon Tally Board: Dropped by the True Corrupted Monk earlier, but allows Late Game merchants here to sell infinite Divine Confetti and Ako's Sugar

Tips

  • Use Puppeteer Ninjutsu on the general accompanying Seven Ashina Spears - Shume Masaji Oniwa to turn the fight into a 2-on-1 advantage in your favor.
  • Equipping Suzaku's Lotus Umbrella completely negates the massive fire damage dealt by Ministry flamethrowers and Demon of Hatred attacks.
  • Most idols within Ashina Castle are reset and must be re-communed with after the Ministry's final assault begins.
  • A kite near the broken bridge to the Outskirts allows you to grapple across and travel back to the Ashina Castle Gate to face the Demon of Hatred.
