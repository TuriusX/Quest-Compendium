Senpou Temple, Mt. Kongo

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Mid-game progression

Senpou Temple, Mt. Kongo is an autumn-covered mountain monastery overtaken by corrupt, immortal monks seeking the dragon's heritage. The player traverses temple grounds, cliffs, and subterranean caves to reach the Main Hall and secure the Mortal Blade. Completing the area involves confronting unique mini-bosses and solving the Illusory Hall puzzle to acquire the sacred weapon needed to sever immortality.

Items

  • Gourd Seed: In the first main temple hall surrounded by monks praying in front of an altar
  • Holy Chapter: Infested MISSABLE: At the bottom of the sunken pond near the Temple Grounds idol, accessible once Mibu Breathing Technique is learned
  • Red and White Pinwheel: Sitting near the cliffside plateau right before the Armored Warrior bridge fight
  • White Pinwheel: Resting on the ground at the highest windy cliff ledge reached by wall-jumping past the Shugendo idol
  • Senpou Esoteric Text: Inside the small isolated pagoda reached by crawling through the cave near the Sunken Valley cavern entrance

Secrets

  • The Great Carp cavern and shortcut kite can be operated by backstabbing the rat enemy near the Senpou Temple idol and using the Puppeteer Ninjutsu on him to hold the winch.
  • A hidden cave behind the waterfall in the pond near Temple Grounds leads upward to a pagoda holding the Senpou Esoteric Text.
  • Bell Demon's Temple can be reached early via an illusory wall in the Ashina Outskirts Headless cave, or naturally from Senpou Temple by taking the path down from Shugendo.

Shops and people

  • Kotaro: Can be given pinwheels to direct him to Anayama the Peddler, the Abandoned Dungeon, or the Halls of Illusion.
  • Shugendo Memorial Mob: Sells the Five-Color Rice, Dragon's Blood Droplet, and persimmons near the Shugendo Idol cliffside.

Tips

  • You cannot kill the Armored Warrior with ordinary posture breaks; you must angle his posture deathblow directly toward the broken sections of the balcony walls.
  • The Gourd Seed inside the first building can be grabbed stealthily by sneaking behind the monks rather than fighting the whole group at once.
  • Using the Divine Abduction prosthetic instantly removes regular Senpou monks from the battlefield.
