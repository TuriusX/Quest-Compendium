Senpou Temple, Mt. Kongo is an autumn-covered mountain monastery overtaken by corrupt, immortal monks seeking the dragon's heritage. The player traverses temple grounds, cliffs, and subterranean caves to reach the Main Hall and secure the Mortal Blade. Completing the area involves confronting unique mini-bosses and solving the Illusory Hall puzzle to acquire the sacred weapon needed to sever immortality.

Enemies
Senpou Monk
Weak to Anti-Air Deathblow

Agile martial artists who attack with kicks and staff thrusts; susceptible to rapid aggression.

Armored Warrior
Weak to Knockback off the bridge

Completely immune to standard weapon damage and can only be defeated by filling his posture and kicking him off the edge.

Long-arm Gentipede Sen'un
Weak to FirecrackersSteal / drop Yellow Gunpowder

Fights identically to Centipede Giraffe; deflect his flurry attacks to quickly fill his posture.

Folding Screen Monkeys
Weak to Gachiin's SugarSteal / drop Memory: Screen Monkeys

Four illusory targets requiring environmental traps or stealth execution to defeat.

Straw-hat Assassin
Weak to Deflection

Highly acrobatic dual-blade users who deal rapid posture damage and frequently leap into the air.

Shops and people
  • Kotaro: Can be given pinwheels to direct him to Anayama the Peddler, the Abandoned Dungeon, or the Halls of Illusion.
  • Shugendo Memorial Mob: Sells the Five-Color Rice, Dragon's Blood Droplet, and persimmons near the Shugendo Idol cliffside.

Tips
  • You cannot kill the Armored Warrior with ordinary posture breaks; you must angle his posture deathblow directly toward the broken sections of the balcony walls.
  • The Gourd Seed inside the first building can be grabbed stealthily by sneaking behind the monks rather than fighting the whole group at once.
  • Using the Divine Abduction prosthetic instantly removes regular Senpou monks from the battlefield.
