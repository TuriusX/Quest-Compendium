The Sunken Valley is a rugged, mist-filled gorge reached from the back of Ashina Castle, heavily fortified by shotgun-wielding and sniper-firing Okami-descended clansmen. Wolf must navigate treacherous cliffs, bridge crossings, and a poisonous pool to reach the Gun Fort and beyond into the Bodhisattva Valley. The primary objective is to defeat the Guardian Ape guarding the Lotus of the Palace, a key ingredient for the Immortal Severance ritual.

Enemies
Sunken Valley Clan Rifleman
Weak to Deflect / Sabimaru

Snipes from extreme range; close distance rapidly or use cover to avoid high damage.

Snake Eyes Shirafuji
Weak to Sabimaru / Loaded UmbrellaSteal / drop Prayer Bead

Mini-boss wielding a heavy rifle who attempts an unblockable grab hook; Sabimaru inflicts poison rapidly on her.

Long-arm Centipede Giraffe
Weak to DeflectSteal / drop Prayer Bead, Yellow Gunpowder

Attacks in rapid, rhythmic flurries; successive deflections will break his posture in seconds.

Guardian Ape
Weak to Firecrackers / Loaded SpearSteal / drop Memory: Guardian Ape, Slender Finger

First phase is beast-type vulnerable to firecrackers; second phase uses a sword where deflecting his heavy downward slash exposes his neck to the Loaded Spear drag attack for massive posture damage.

Tips
  • Use the Loaded Umbrella to completely deflect sniper fire when crossing the suspension bridge leading to the Gun Fort.
  • During the Guardian Ape's second headless phase, sprint away immediately when blood fountains from its neck to avoid the Terror-inducing scream.
