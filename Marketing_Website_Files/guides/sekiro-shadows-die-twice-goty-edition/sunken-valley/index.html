Sunken Valley

Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice - GOTY Edition guide · Mid-game progression

The Sunken Valley is a rugged, mist-filled gorge reached from the back of Ashina Castle, heavily fortified by shotgun-wielding and sniper-firing Okami-descended clansmen. Wolf must navigate treacherous cliffs, bridge crossings, and a poisonous pool to reach the Gun Fort and beyond into the Bodhisattva Valley. The primary objective is to defeat the Guardian Ape guarding the Lotus of the Palace, a key ingredient for the Immortal Severance ritual.

Items

  • Gourd Seed: In a small hut surrounded by snipers just before reaching the Under-Shrine Valley Idol
  • Prayer Bead: Found inside a small shrine in the area behind the Sunken Valley Idol, accessible by grappling back and following the lower path
  • Large Fan: Dropped automatically upon defeating the Long-arm Centipede Giraffe mini-boss in the Gun Fort
  • Gun Fort Shrine Key MISSABLE: Found in the library of Ashina Castle near Kuro, required to open the door behind the Long-arm Centipede Giraffe
  • Lotus of the Palace: Located in the cave directly behind the Guardian Ape's arena after defeating it
  • Slender Finger: Dropped automatically by the Guardian Ape upon its defeat
  • Dried Serpent Viscera: Located in the dark cavern below the Bodhisattva Valley, guarded by the Great Serpent's coils

Secrets

  • Reaching the lower cavern beneath the Gun Fort Shrine requires breaking through the loose floorboards in the shrine room to discover a path leading to Divine Confetti and the Poison Pool shortcut.
  • You can sneak past the Great Serpent blocking the shrine with the Dried Serpent Viscera by using Puppeteer Ninjutsu on the nearby shivering monkey to make it dance and lure the snake away.

  • Use the Loaded Umbrella to completely deflect sniper fire when crossing the suspension bridge leading to the Gun Fort.
  • During the Guardian Ape's second headless phase, sprint away immediately when blood fountains from its neck to avoid the Terror-inducing scream.
