The Sunstone Cave

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for the Sunstone with Philippa Eilhart

Geralt enters the ancient elven ruins on the northern coast of Ard Skellig alongside Philippa Eilhart to find the Sunstone. Inside, you must navigate flooded caverns, defeat guardian monsters, and align a series of mirrors to direct a beam of light onto the relic. Once the light puzzles are solved, Philippa claims the Sunstone and you can exit the ruins.

Items

  • Sunstone: Obtained from the central pedestal in the final chamber after aligning the mirrors

Tips

  • Bring Cat potions or keep a torch handy, as several winding corridors off the main path are completely dark.
  • Philippa will repair broken stairs with magic as you follow her through the cavern.
  • Use the Witcher Senses to track the light beam while adjusting the mirrors to ensure the light connects properly to the central altar.
