Crow's Perch

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Seeking the Bloody Baron

Crow's Perch is a fortified wooden settlement in Velen ruled by Phillip Strenger, commonly known as the Bloody Baron. Geralt visits this fortress during his search for Ciri, trading assistance with the Baron's family troubles for information on her whereabouts. While navigating the inner and outer wards, you can pick up various local contracts, challenge inhabitants to Gwent, and access essential crafting services.

Items

  • Letter to Imperator: In a locked chest inside the Baron's private study, accessible during your investigation
  • Voodoo Doll: Inside Tamara's bedroom in the Baron's keep, found during the search for clues

Secrets

  • A submerged cave entrance sits at the base of the hill northwest of Crow's Perch, leading directly up into the fortress gardens through an old well.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Fergus Graem: Journeyman armorer services, armor diagrams, and crafting components
  • Yoana: Journeyman armorer assistance initially; master armorer services upon completing the quest Master Armorers
  • Quartermaster: General crafting materials, basic weapons, and Gwent cards
  • Village Merchant: Basic food, alchemical ingredients, and general sundries located in the lower settlement

Tips

  • Always hit the Baron's keep signpost directly inside the gates in the Complete Edition to avoid the long uphill run from the lower village.
  • Play Gwent against the Bloody Baron as soon as possible, as advancing his storyline can cause him to permanently leave or become unavailable.
