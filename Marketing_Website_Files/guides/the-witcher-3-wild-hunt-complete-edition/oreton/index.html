Oreton

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for the Baron's wife ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Oreton is a lakeside hamlet situated directly south of Crow's Perch and north of Fyke Isle. While exploring the region during the search for the Baron's wife in "Family Matters," this village offers no clues about Anna—who is actually found in Crookback Bog—serving instead as an auxiliary hub for services, contracts, and side encounters. Players can visit the notice board here to pick up regional contracts or consult local craftsmen for trading and Gwent.

Shops and people
  • Blacksmith: Sells runestones, crafting materials, and weapon diagrams (including Diagram: Nilfgaardian longsword and Diagram: Velen longsword)
  • Trader: Sells crafting goods, general wares, and plays Gwent

Tips
  • If the main objective "Family Matters" tells you to investigate all remaining leads to find the Baron's wife, avoid searching Oreton and instead advance the "Ladies of the Wood" storyline in Crookback Bog.
