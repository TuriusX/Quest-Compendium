Temple Isle

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for clues about Dandelion

Geralt visits Temple Isle in Novigrad during the main quest "Broken Flowers" to question Elihal, one of Dandelion's acquaintances. He searches the tailor's home for clues regarding Dandelion's recent activities and whereabouts. Following this thread helps piece together Dandelion's list of lovers and associates across the city.

Items

  • Dandelion's Planner: Elihal's house, on a small table near the entrance

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Witch HunterIgni, Steel Swordnothing can be stolenHostile Novigrad guards that patrol the upper streets of Temple Isle and attack if provoked during certain story sequences.

Shops and people

  • Elihal: Sells various fashionable clothing, masks, and crafting components

Tips

  • Be diplomatic when speaking with Elihal to easily obtain information about Dandelion's recent visits without causing unnecessary friction.
  • Use the nearby fast travel signpost at the entrance to Temple Isle to quickly navigate down into the lower districts of Novigrad after finishing your inquiries.
