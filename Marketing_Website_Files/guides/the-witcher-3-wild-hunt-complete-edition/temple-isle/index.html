Temple Isle

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for clues about Dandelion ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Geralt visits Temple Isle in Novigrad during the main quest "Broken Flowers" to question Elihal, one of Dandelion's acquaintances. He searches the tailor's home for clues regarding Dandelion's recent activities and whereabouts. Following this thread helps piece together Dandelion's list of lovers and associates across the city.

Region
Free City of Novigrad
Levels
Level 12–17
Getting there
Walk across the high bridge from northern Novigrad (Gildorf/The Bits) into Temple Isle, or fast-travel via the Temple Isle or Electors' Square signposts.
Connects to
Electors' Square, Great Temple of the Eternal Fire, the var Attre Villa, the cavern network directly beneath the island cliffs
Related quests
Broken Flowers · Scavenger Hunt: Cat School Gear · Fencing Lessons
Services and people
Rosa and Edna var Attre (Nilfgaardian ambassador’s daughters) · Var Attre estate guards · Preacher / Secret Alchemist (Electors' Square)

The short version

What matters

  • •Questioning Rosa var Attre in the cellar to discover information about Dandelion for the quest Broken Flowers.
  • •Accepting Rosa's fencing invitation so you do not permanently miss out on the secondary quest Fencing Lessons.
  • •Dispelling the cliffside illusion with the Eye of Nehaleni to defeat Mad Kiyan and secure the full set of basic Feline armor diagrams.

Common mistakes

  • •Declining Rosa's request to tutor her after your cellar sparring match, which locks you out of the quest Fencing Lessons for the rest of the playthrough.
  • •Forgetting to turn every statue inward toward the center pit, leaving the underwater grate shut and blocking your path deeper into the caverns.
  • •Failing to loot Mad Kiyan's body after slaying him, missing the Laboratory Log, laboratory key, and four Cat School gear diagrams.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Reaching Temple Isle

    Cross the towering bridge leading out of northern Novigrad from The Bits or Gildorf, or fast-travel straight to the Electors' Square or Temple Isle signposts on this elevated northern district. Check in with the Preacher, who acts as a secret alchemist in Electors' Square near the Great Temple of the Eternal Fire, if you need concoctions. Head south toward the var Attre Villa to pursue your clues regarding Dandelion.

    Tip: Stock up on alchemy goods from the secret merchant at Electors' Square before entering private estates or spelunking.

  2. 2

    Breaching the Var Attre Estate

    Head to the var Attre estate gates, where you will encounter Nilfgaardian guards blocking the path. Inform the sentries that you are the fencing instructor to secure instant, peaceful clearance through the front doors. If you skip or fail the dialogue referencing Zoltan, skirt along the rocky cliff ledge around the back of the property and hop the garden fence into the courtyard.

    Tip: Claiming to be the swordsmanship tutor saves you the trouble of scaling the rear exterior walls.

    Watch out: Missing the proper dialogue forces you into climbing the rocky perimeter ledge behind the residence to get inside.

  3. 3

    Entering the Residence and Heading Below

    Go inside the var Attre house to meet Edna and Rosa var Attre, the daughters of the Nilfgaardian ambassador. After speaking with them, follow their instructions down to the cellar training arena. Grab a Wooden Practice Sword directly from the weapon rack before stepping forward to face Rosa.

    Tip: Remember to equip the wooden sword off the rack so you are ready to spar immediately.

  4. 4

    Sparring and Questioning Rosa

    Engage Rosa in two rounds of fencing in the cellar arena; parry her swings to break her guard or dodge to her flanks to land light strikes. Deplete her health bar to around 50% to trigger a short pause, then lower it again to finish the duel, earning 25 XP and progress in Broken Flowers. Interrogate Rosa about Dandelion, and make sure to accept her request for private outdoor lessons by saying "With pleasure" when she asks.

    Tip: Counter Rosa right as her practice rapier begins its forward swing to easily pierce her defenses.

    Watch out: Declining Rosa's offer for private training permanently locks you out of the secondary quest "Fencing Lessons."

  5. 5

    Entering the Cliffside Sea Caves

    Make your way down to the narrow cliff path along the westernmost house edge directly beneath Temple Isle's cliffs. Use the Eye of Nehaleni to dispel the illusion sealing the sea cave entrance, then head inside where you will encounter wandering Wraiths and a Golem. Trap the Wraiths with Yrden to turn them corporeal, and avoid the Golem's unblockable ground slams using Quen before striking its back.

    Tip: Cast Yrden against the Wraiths to negate their ethereal defense, and coat your blade in Elementa Oil for the Golem.

    Watch out: You cannot open the cavern passage unless you have the Eye of Nehaleni to clear the illusory rock wall.

  6. 6

    Solving the Shrine and Slaying Mad Kiyan

    Reach the central chamber and turn each surrounding statue so that all of them face directly inward toward the middle pit, which lowers the submerged iron grate into the pool. Dive through, then proceed into the laboratory to confront the fallen Cat School Witcher, Mad Kiyan. Disrupt his aggressive swordplay and sign-casting using Aard or Axii, keeping Quen raised to absorb his lunges, then loot his corpse for the Laboratory Log, Key to the laboratory, and diagrams for the Feline Armor, Gauntlets, Trousers, and Boots.

    Tip: Utilize Axii to interrupt Mad Kiyan whenever he attempts to cast Igni or Quen.

    Watch out: Ensure you thoroughly loot Mad Kiyan's remains, or you will leave behind the core diagrams for the Cat School gear set.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Entering the Var Attre Residence

Approaching the estate gates while investigating Dandelion's whereabouts

  • Claim to be the fencing instructor→ Gain immediate, peaceful access through the front gates
  • Fail or skip the instructor dialogue→ Forced to climb around the rear garden wall and cliff ledge to sneak in

Most players pick: Claim to be the fencing instructor

Rosa's Request for Future Training

Talking to Rosa in the cellar arena following your second sparring match

  • Agree to give her lessons→ Unlocks the future secondary quest "Fencing Lessons"
  • Refuse her offer→ Permanently locks out the secondary quest "Fencing Lessons"

Most players pick: Agree to give her lessons

Refusing permanently bars access to the follow-up quest.

Checklists

Key fights
Junior's Guards Courtyard and Secret Passage Ambush

Enemies: Junior's Goons and Thugs

Threats: Melee swarming, heavy two-handed swings, and crossbow bolt fire from the courtyard balcony

Weaknesses and resistances: Susceptible to Aard knockdowns, Igni burn stun, and Axii mind control

Tactics: Funnel the goons through the narrow estate doorway to fight them one by one, then advance into the courtyard and rush the balcony stairs to eliminate crossbowmen

Rewards: Novigradian crowns, blunt axes, rusted swords, key to Junior's secret underground passage, and entry into Whoreson Junior's estate

Hostile Novigrad guards that patrol the upper streets of Temple Isle and attack if provoked during certain story sequences.

Shops and people

Tips
  • Be diplomatic when speaking with Elihal to easily obtain information about Dandelion's recent visits without causing unnecessary friction.
  • Use the nearby fast travel signpost at the entrance to Temple Isle to quickly navigate down into the lower districts of Novigrad after finishing your inquiries.
