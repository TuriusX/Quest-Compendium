Crookback Bog

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for the Crones

Geralt enters the murky swamps of Crookback Bog following the clues from the book "The Ladies of the Wood" to track down Ciri. You must locate a hidden village, interact with an elderly caretaker and a group of orphaned children, and eventually uncover the mysterious and terrifying Crones who rule the bog.

Items

  • She Who Knows: inside the hut in the bog village

Secrets

  • Johnny's cliffside hideout can be found north-east of the bog village, marked by a small cave entrance overlooking the swamp.

Enemies

Tips

  • Use the Witcher Senses along the Trail of Treats to easily follow the carved wooden markers through the swamp without getting lost.
  • Equip Necrophage Oil on your silver sword before wading through the water, as standard swamp encounters here primarily consist of drowners and water hags.
