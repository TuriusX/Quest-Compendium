Land of a Thousand Fables

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Entering the fairytale world

Geralt enters an enchanted illusion created inside an illustrated book to locate and rescue Syanna during the main quest "Beyond Hill and Dale...". To escape this decaying fairytale realm, Geralt and Syanna must track down three magic beans by dealing with corrupted versions of classic fairytale characters and plant them near the giant's castle.

Items

  • Toussaint Relic Steel Sword MISSABLE: Found submerged by following the glowing wisp near the Longlocks tower
  • Magic Ribbon MISSABLE: Won from the Little Flint Girl at her stall by beating her at Gwent or buying it
  • Golden Egg MISSABLE: Obtained from the goose in the Balbina the Goose encounter or retrieved from the goose nest

Secrets

  • Following a floating glowing wisp away from the main path leads into a dark cave containing the remains of a knight, a bonfire, and a unique steel sword that references Dark Souls.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • The Little Flint Girl: Sells tobacco, firecrackers, and the Magic Ribbon

Tips

  • Acquiring the Magic Ribbon from the Little Flint Girl changes the outcome of the main story by preventing Syanna's death later on.
  • You cannot return to the Land of a Thousand Fables once you defeat the Cloud Giant and exit down the well, so explore thoroughly before advancing.
