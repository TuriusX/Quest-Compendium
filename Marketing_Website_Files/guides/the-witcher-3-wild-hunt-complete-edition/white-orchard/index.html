White Orchard

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · The first open-world region

White Orchard is the introductory prologue region where Geralt and Vesemir search for Yennefer while dealing with the local Nilfgaardian garrison. Players can explore freely to complete contracts, clear monster nests, and gather crafting materials before concluding the main quest. Defeating the local Griffin and choosing to leave with Vesemir locks access to this prologue version of the region, advancing the story to Vizima.

Items

  • Viper Steel Sword Diagram MISSABLE: In the crypt inside the cemetery north of the Woesong Bridge, guarded by a Wraith
  • Viper Silver Sword Diagram MISSABLE: Inside the ruined Amavet Fortress castle, guarded by Deserters
  • Temerian Armor Set MISSABLE: Purchased from the merchant Bram near the Woesong Bridge after saving him

Secrets

  • A submerged chest containing valuable loot and crafting components rests beneath the broken bridge at the Ransacked Village.
  • Six Places of Power are scattered across the region; drawing from all six provides early Ability Points and an achievement/trophy.

Shops and people

  • Bram (Merchant): Temerian armor DLC set, horse gear, Gwent cards, crafting supplies
  • Tomira (Herbalist): Alchemy formulas, herbs, crafting ingredients
  • Willis (Dwarven Blacksmith): Weapon and armor repairs, basic crafting, dismantling, early crafting diagrams

Tips

  • Visit all six Places of Power before triggering the final Griffin hunt to maximize free Ability Points early.
  • Craft the Viper School swords as soon as you find their diagrams, as they significantly outclass early starting weapons.
  • Complete all side quests and points of interest before talking to Vesemir at the tavern to leave, as finishing the prologue fails certain active local quests.
