Skellige Isles

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Sailing to Kaer Trolde after Novigrad

Geralt travels across the sea from Novigrad to the Skellige Isles to continue his search for Ciri among the clashing island clans. After shipwrecking on the shores of Ard Skellig, he heads to Kaer Trolde to attend King Bran's wake and investigate a magical cataclysm alongside Yennefer. The region opens up a rugged archipelago filled with maritime contracts, monster hunts, and a succession crisis for the high throne.

Items

  • Aerondight: Not found in Skellige; it is obtained in Toussaint during the Blood and Wine expansion
  • Ursine Witcher Gear Diagrams: Distributed across Skellige in locations such as the ruins of Tuirseach Castle on An Skellig

Secrets

  • Abandoned shipwreck caches marked with floating barrels across the seas hold rare crafting components, armor, and weapons guarded by drowned dead and sirens.
  • A prisoner locked in a sky cell at Kaer Almhult references a famous dwarf from a different fantasy series.

Shops and people

  • Kaer Trolde Armorer: Sells crafting diagrams, master-level armor repairs, and regional treasure maps
  • Yolar: Sells alchemy ingredients and potion recipes at Gedyneith

Tips

  • Use your crossbow while steering a boat to instantly shoot down attacking sirens without letting go of the helm.
  • Purchase the maps from the Kaer Trolde armorer to immediately unlock fast-travel signposts across the distant Skellige islands.
  • Overburdened exploration can be managed by docking your boat directly adjacent to harbor signposts to fast-travel to merchants.
