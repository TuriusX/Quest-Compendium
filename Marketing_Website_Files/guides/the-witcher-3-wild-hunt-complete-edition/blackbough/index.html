Blackbough

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for the Baron's wife
← All areas in this guide

In the quest "Family Matters," tracking the Baron's missing wife Anna leads Geralt northwest of Blackbough to consult the local Pellar, whose protective talisman she previously carried. Outside the hermit's hut near the village, you must first handle a hostile group of the Baron's henchmen harassing the old man before he agrees to divine Anna's fate. After retrieving his runaway goat Princess from the surrounding woods, the Pellar conducts a divination revealing crucial truths about the Baroness's ordeal and pregnancy.

The short version

What matters

  • •Buying Adalbert Kermith’s First and Second Maps from the village merchant and blacksmith to locate Witcher gear.
  • •Gaining the Pellar's cooperation by resolving the standoff in his yard and returning his runaway goat Princess with the Pellar's Bell.
  • •Refusing Margrit's bribe in Wild at Heart so you can uncover the truth about Niellen, obtain Niellen's Key, and acquire a Werewolf Mutagen.

Common mistakes

  • •Slaughtering the Baron's men at the Inn at the Crossroads earlier, which locks out the peaceful alchemical cure solution at the Pellar's hut.
  • •Arriving at the blacksmith's stall in the early morning and assuming he is broken, instead of meditating to midday (12:00 PM) to fix his prompt.
  • •Forgetting to apply fire with Igni or throw Moon Dust bombs at the Level 7 werewolf, allowing its health regeneration to drag out the fight.

What you can skip

  • •Buying standard junk or basic crafting components from the general merchant when you only need his map.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Reach Blackbough

    Head west along the main road from the Crow's Perch bridge crossing to enter this Level 5–7 settlement in Velen. When you arrive at the entrance, inspect the Blackbough Notice Board and grab the fast travel signpost. Then, continue forward into the settlement to begin looking into the local hunts and locating the Pellar.

    Tip: Grab contracts from the board right away to reveal local activities.

  2. 2

    Trade in the Village Center

    Head to the village center stalls to find the General Merchant and the Amateur Blacksmith. Purchase Adalbert Kermith’s First Map from the merchant, and pick up Adalbert Kermith’s Second Map from the blacksmith's forge. Before leaving the center, you can also play Gwent with the blacksmith or pick up food and crafting items.

    Tip: Meditate until midday (12:00 PM) if the blacksmith's talk prompt is inactive during the early morning.

    Watch out: Speak to the blacksmith and his children now if you want their background dialogue about Hanna, as finishing Wild at Heart removes it permanently.

  3. 3

    Take on Wild at Heart

    Walk up to the small cottage in the village where you meet Niellen and Margrit to pick up the quest Wild at Heart regarding Niellen's missing wife, Hanna. After finding Hanna’s remains out in the forest, Margrit offers a bribe to claim wild beasts killed her; refuse her coin and follow the scent to an isolated shack and cave. Wait until nightfall to face the transformed werewolf and his pack of adds, taking down the lesser wolves first before burning the beast with Igni or bombs to negate his regeneration.

    Key fight: The Werewolf

    Tip: Employ Cursed Oil, Moon Dust, or Igni to stop the werewolf from healing when he howls.

    Watch out: Accepting Margrit’s bribe ends the quest early for a small sum of Orens, completely bypassing the secluded cave and the truth of the curse.

  4. 4

    Decide Margrit's Fate

    Once you defeat the beast down to low health, decide whether to protect Margrit by slaying the werewolf outright or stepping aside to let him take his revenge. If you step aside, the werewolf will execute Margrit, step outside, and allow you to finish him off without defending himself. Loot the beast's remains afterward to retrieve the Werewolf Mutagen, a Werewolf Pelt, and Niellen’s Key.

    Tip: Loot Niellen’s Key from the fallen werewolf to access his locked belongings.

    Watch out: Choosing to step aside results in Margrit's death, while intervening keeps her alive, though traumatized.

  5. 5

    Clear the Pellar's Yard

    Follow the wooded path northwest toward the Pellar’s territory until you reach the locked door outside the hut in the Pellar’s Yard. Confront the 3–4 thugs harassing the hermit during Family Matters, choosing to offer Edric an alchemical cure, mind-controlling the leader with Delusion Level 2 Axii, or killing the deserters in combat. After dispersing or slaughtering the gang with your steel sword and crowd control, speak to the holy man to learn about Anna's talisman.

    Key fight: The Baron's Men

    Tip: Using Delusion Level 2 Axii resolves the encounter peacefully while giving you a bonus 35 XP.

    Watch out: You cannot offer an alchemical cure to resolve the standoff peacefully if you slaughtered the Baron's men earlier at the Inn at the Crossroads.

  6. 6

    Rescue Princess in the Woods

    Take the Pellar's Bell given by the hermit and search the northwest woods outskirts to herd his runaway goat, Princess, back to safety during A Princess in Distress. Ring the bell repeatedly to lead the animal, watching for flanking packs of Level 5 wolves and wild dogs along the wooded trail. Keep Princess away from a sleeping Level 6 bear in the woods, using your silver sword and Beast Oil to eliminate any predators that get in your way.

    Key fight: Forest Wolves and Wild Dogs Key fight: Sleeping Forest Bear

    Tip: Use Igni to panic packs of wolves, and deploy Quen or Yrden against the bear while avoiding parries against its vertical swings.

    Watch out: Stay close to the goat, as roaming packs of wild predators will quickly attack her if you leave her unguarded.

  7. 7

    Complete the Divination

    Escort Princess back to the Pellar’s Yard and deliver her safely to the waiting spiritualist. With his goat returned, the Pellar will conduct a divination that uncovers the ordeal of the Baron's missing wife Anna and details regarding her pregnancy. Conclude your business with the herbalist to wrap up your objectives in Blackbough before venturing onward toward Oreton or Midcopse.

    Tip: Browse the Pellar's inventory for alchemy supplies and herbs before departing.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Dealing with the Baron's Men at the Pellar's Yard

Outside the Pellar's locked hut during Family Matters

  • Offer to cure their companion→ Resolves the standoff peacefully and the thugs depart without violence
  • Use Delusion Level 2→ Influences their leader with Axii to leave peacefully, granting 35 bonus experience
  • Threaten them or refuse to leave→ Triggers a battle forcing you to slaughter the Baron's thugs

Most players pick: Offer to cure their companion or use Delusion Level 2

Offering an alchemical cure is unavailable if you previously slaughtered the Baron's men at the Inn at the Crossroads.

Margrit's Offer in the Woods

After locating Hanna's corpse during Wild at Heart

  • Accept the bribe→ Concludes the quest immediately with 55 Orens and 80 experience, leaving Niellen to believe wild beasts killed his wife while the werewolf curse remains secret
  • Refuse the bribe→ Leads you to continue investigating the secluded shack and cave, discovering that Niellen is the werewolf and confronting him at night

Most players pick: Refuse the bribe

Accepting the bribe cuts the contract short and prevents you from obtaining the werewolf's loot.

Margrit's Fate

When the werewolf drops to low health during Wild at Heart

  • Protect Margrit→ Geralt intervenes and slays the werewolf in combat, letting a traumatized Margrit survive
  • Let the werewolf have her→ The werewolf kills Margrit, emerges from the den, and lets Geralt slay him without fighting back

Most players pick: Protect Margrit

Both outcomes leave Niellen dead, but looting his body awards Niellen's Key, a Werewolf Mutagen, and a Werewolf Pelt.

Key fights
The Baron's Men

Enemies: 3–4 Human Deserters/Thugs

Threats: Armed with blunt weapons and two-handed axes

Weaknesses and resistances: Steel Sword, parries/counterattacks, human crowd-control with Axii or Aard

Tactics: Use Steel Sword, parries, counterattacks, and crowd control with Axii or Aard.

Rewards: Rusted weapons, common boots/gloves, minor food items

Forest Wolves and Wild Dogs

Enemies: Packs of Wolves (Level 5) and Wild Dogs

Threats: Flanking and biting during the escort of Princess

Weaknesses and resistances: Beast Oil, Igni

Tactics: Burn through pack health and induce panic with Igni; use fast silver/steel dodges.

Rewards: Raw meat, Wolf Pelts, Dog Tallow

Sleeping Forest Bear

Enemies: Bear (Level 6)

Threats: Powerful swiping blows and heavy vertical swings

Weaknesses and resistances: Beast Oil, Quen, Yrden

Tactics: Absorb blows with Quen, use Quen/Yrden counter-punches, and avoid parrying heavy vertical swings.

Rewards: Bear Pelt, Raw Meat

The Werewolf

Enemies: Werewolf (Level 7) + 3 pack wolves

Threats: Howl triggers rapid health regeneration

Weaknesses and resistances: Cursed Oil, Moon Dust bombs, Devil's Puffball, Igni

Tactics: Dispatch wolf adds first using broad swings or Igni. Hit the werewolf aggressively with fast attacks or ignite with fire to interrupt healing.

Rewards: Werewolf Mutagen, Werewolf Pelt, Niellen’s Key

Shops and people
  • Blacksmith: Sells weapon crafting diagrams, materials, and Cat School gear upgrade maps; offers equipment repairs.
  • Merchant: Sells basic crafting items, food supplies, and witcher gear scavenger maps.
  • The Pellar: Sells alchemy ingredients, crafting components, runestones, and glyphs once aided.

Tips
  • If you previously avoided fighting the Baron’s henchmen at the Inn at the Crossroads, you can peacefully talk down the gang outside the Pellar's hut by offering to cure their comrade Edric, bypassing combat entirely without needing Axii.
