When first visiting this eastern Velen village, you discover that the inhabitants have been slaughtered and two rival groups of looters are quarreling over their remains. You can either provoke them into fighting you or walk away and let the factions butcher one another. Returning later finds the settlement permanently deserted by humans and overrun with scavengers.

Enemies
Peasants
Weak to Steel Sword

Hostile scavengers who argue over the spoils during the unmarked encounter "Looters".

Alghoul
Weak to Necrophage Oil, AxiiSteal / drop Red Mutagen

Tougher scavengers that infest the village after the looters are cleared out.

Devourer
Weak to Necrophage Oil, IgniSteal / drop Devourer's blood

Necrophages that arrive alongside Alghouls to feed on the corpses and detonate right before dying.

Tips
  • Because the necrophages continuously respawn every time you leave and return, this location is an exceptional spot to repeatedly farm rare crafting items like Devourer's blood and Red Mutagens.
