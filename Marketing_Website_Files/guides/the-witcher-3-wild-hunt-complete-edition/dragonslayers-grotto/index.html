Dragonslayer's Grotto

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Exploring the cave during the search for Keira Metz's supplies

Dragonslayer's Grotto is an ancient underground cavern located in Velen northwest of Reardon Manor and southwest of Crookback Bog. Players typically explore it to retrieve the Griffin School Witcher Gear diagrams while navigating subterranean passages filled with monsters and gas hazards. It contains crumbling ruins, hidden chambers, and an encounter with an ancient guardian at the deepest point.

Items

  • Diagram: Griffin steel sword: Inside a chest located in the back chamber after defeating the final boss of the cave
  • Diagram: Griffin silver sword: Inside a chest guarded by the cave's final monster
  • Diagram: Griffin armor: Found inside the chest at the elevated tomb chamber at the cave's end
  • Diagram: Griffin trousers: Inside the chest at the tomb in the deepest part of the cavern
  • Diagram: Griffin boots: Found alongside the other Griffin School diagrams in the final chamber's chest
  • Diagram: Griffin gauntlets: Located within the final chest in the tomb section

Secrets

  • A cracked wall inside the cavern can be identified using Witcher Senses and destroyed using the Aard sign to reveal hidden tunnels.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
WraithSpecter Oil, Yrden, Moon DustHaunts the entrance corridors and chambers, phasing through solid rock to ambush.
EkimmaraVampire Oil, Igni, Devil's PuffballA fast-moving lower vampire serving as the boss in the cavern's final chamber.

Tips

  • Igniting the swamp gas clouds with the Igni sign safely clears the tunnels before walking through, but keep your distance to avoid explosion damage.
  • Use the Yrden sign when fighting Wraiths in the narrow hallways to force them into a tangible form where they take normal damage.
