Dandelion's Inn

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Renovating the inn

Dandelion inherits the Rosemary and Thyme in Novigrad and task Geralt with helping him renovate it into a respectable cabaret. During the quest "Cabaret," you choose the establishment's decorative style, retrieve props, and handle various logistical problems before the grand opening. Once fully refurbished and renamed the Chameleon, it becomes a cozy hub where several key allies gather.

Items

  • Choreographer's Notes: On the upper floor table inside the inn

Secrets

  • The hidden attic space reachable by the ladder on the top floor contains background lore notes and personal trinkets belonging to Dandelion and his previous associates.

Shops and people

  • Zoltan Chivay: Gwent matches, quest dialogue, and advice

Tips

  • Choosing between the theatrical style and the boudoir style during the renovation changes the inn's visual appearance and decor, but it does not negatively impact the quest rewards or story outcome.
