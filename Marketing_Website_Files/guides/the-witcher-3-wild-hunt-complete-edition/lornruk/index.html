Lornruk

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Investigating the abandoned fortress and its wyvern

Lornruk is an abandoned, ruined sea fortress located off the northwestern coast of Velen that is guarded by a flying wyvern. Geralt must bypass the raised drawbridge by diving into an underwater cave system beneath the cliffs to access the interior courtyard. Once inside, you can lower the drawbridge with a winch, slay the wyvern, and loot diagrams and treasure across the ruined ramparts.

Items

  • Diagram: Griffin Silver Sword: chest at the top of the ruined lighthouse tower accessible via a ladder

Secrets

  • Underwater entrance: Dive down into the sea chasm underneath the wooden drawbridge to locate a submerged cavern that leads straight up into the fortress courtyard.

Tips

  • Use the winch located on the fortress side of the drawbridge to lower it, allowing for a fast and direct exit back to the mainland.
  • Drink a Killer Whale potion before diving into the sea beneath the bridge to extend your breath while finding and navigating the submerged cave entrance.
