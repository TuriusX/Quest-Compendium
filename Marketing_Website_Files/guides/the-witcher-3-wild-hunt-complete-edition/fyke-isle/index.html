Fyke Isle

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Investigating the tower for Keira

Geralt travels to Fyke Isle by boat to investigate a deadly curse on a dilapidated tower at Keira Metz's request. Using the Magic Lamp, he uncovers the tragic fate of a young woman named Annabelle and must lift the curse on the structure. The island is also the site of the secondary quest "A Towerful of Mice" and contains several related side encounters.

Items

  • Magic Lamp: equipped in the quick-use slot during the quest "A Towerful of Mice"
  • Annabelle's Remains MISSABLE: tower top floor, given by Annabelle's spirit
  • Alexander's Laboratory Notes: top laboratory floor of the tower, found on desks and shelves

Secrets

  • Submerged smuggler's caches and sunken chests ring the shallow waters around Fyke Isle, containing random armor and weapons.

Tips

  • Equip the Magic Lamp in your consumable pocket and activate it near green glowing ethereal clouds to view spectral flashbacks of the peasants' assault.
  • If you agree to take Annabelle's remains to Graham rather than bringing him to the tower, the plague will spread and cause Graham's death in Oreton.
