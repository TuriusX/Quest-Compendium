Geralt is deeded this dilapidated vineyard estate by Duchess Anna Henrietta as part of his initial payment during the Blood and Wine expansion. By speaking with the majordomo, Barnabas-Basil Foulty, you can invest gold to restore, expand, and personalize the property. Once renovated, it serves as a player home offering unique buffs, gear display stands, an alchemy laboratory, and a peaceful retreat for visitors.

Shops and people
  • Barnabas-Basil Foulty: estate management services, renovations, house tours, and Gwent matches
  • Majordomo-hired Sommelier: sells various regional wines after purchasing the cellar renovation

Tips
  • Sleeping in Geralt's fully upgraded bed grants active bonus perks to vitality, horse stamina, and gained experience points.
  • Fully upgrading the greenhouse and garden allows you to regularly harvest rare herbs, including those needed to craft specialized mutagen transmutation dyes.
  • Upgrade the weapon racks and armor stands early so you have a convenient place to display and store relic-tier Witcher school gear sets.
