Heatherton

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Investigating the village destroyed by the Wild Hunt

Geralt arrives at the village of Heatherton during the quest "The Nilfgaardian Connection" to rendezvous with an imperial agent named Hendrik. Upon arrival, he discovers the entire settlement has been hit by a freak blizzard and completely ravaged by the Wild Hunt. Geralt must eliminate the remaining wild rabid dogs and search Hendrik's hut to uncover clues about Ciri's whereabouts.

Items

  • Hendrik's Key: In the boots of Hendrik's frozen corpse inside his hut
  • Hendrik's Notes: In the hidden cellar of Hendrik's hut, inside an unlocked chest

Secrets

  • A hidden trapdoor beneath a rug inside Hendrik's hut leads to a secret underground room, which is unlocked by pulling an interactive candlestick on the wall.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Rabid DogBeast Oil, Igni, AardSeveral hostile dogs roam the frozen streets and will attack Geralt immediately upon his arrival.

Tips

  • Cast Igni to quickly clear out the pack of rabid dogs, as they are vulnerable to fire and tend to swarm Geralt closely.
  • Use Witcher Senses inside Hendrik's hut to inspect his body and highlight the interactive elements needed to access the cellar.
