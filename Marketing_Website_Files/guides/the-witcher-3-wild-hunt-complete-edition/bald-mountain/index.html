Bald Mountain

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Confronting the Crones

Geralt and Ciri travel to Bald Mountain during a festival to eliminate the remaining Crones and the Wild Hunt general Imlerith. The quest splits your control between Ciri, who enters the roots beneath the mountain to fight the three Crones, and Geralt, who ascends to the summit to duel Imlerith. Completing this area marks the decisive resolution of the Velen Crones storyline and strikes a major blow against the Wild Hunt.

Items

  • Magic Acorn MISSABLE: Found on the corpse of Imlerith at the summit after defeating him
  • Defeated Crones' Loot: Lootable from the corpses of Brewess and Weavess in the cauldron chamber

Secrets

  • Place of Power (Quen) | Located along the ascending mountain path just before reaching the summit arena | missable: no

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
ImlerithIgni, Quen, Northern WindFights in heavy armor with a massive shield before discarding it in phase two for rapid, teleporting two-handed hammer attacks.
The Crones (Whispess, Brewess, Weavess)Ciri's Blink, fast dash dodgesFought simultaneously using Ciri; focusing down Brewess or Whispess first makes dodging their telegraphed AoE attacks much easier.
FugasQuen, Hanged Man's VenomA Sylvan gatekeeper guarding the pathway to the upper peak who uses fire attacks and heavy melee sweeps.

Tips

  • Do not leave the summit arena immediately after killing Imlerith; loot his body right away to acquire the Magic Acorn before triggering cutscenes.
  • You can consume the Magic Acorn yourself from your inventory for 2 free Ability Points, or give it to the villagers at the base of the mountain.
  • During Geralt's fight with Imlerith in his second phase, dodge immediately after he teleports rather than rolling, as his recovery time after a hammer slam gives you an open window to attack his back.
