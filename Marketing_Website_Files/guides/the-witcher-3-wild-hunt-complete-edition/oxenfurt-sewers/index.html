Oxenfurt Sewers

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Investigating the monster for the Hearts of Stone expansion ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Geralt enters the sewer tunnels beneath Oxenfurt via a town well to track a dangerous monster on contract from Olgierd von Everec. Inside, he reunites with the field medic Shani, investigates a slaughtered Redanian soldier squad, and navigates the contaminated waterways to confront the Toad Prince.

Region
Novigrad / Velen map (underneath the city of Oxenfurt, Redania)
Levels
32+
Getting there
Climbed down via an open well with a ladder located in the central square/marketplace of Oxenfurt, near the Oxenfurt Harbor and outside the Borsodi Auction House.
Connects to
Surface marketplace/wellhead of Oxenfurt, A beach north of Novigrad
Related quests
Evil's Soft First Touches

The short version

What matters

  • •Drink Golden Oriole (especially Superior) during the Toad Prince confrontation so the boss's toxic clouds heal you instead of draining your life.
  • •Loot the deceased Redanian captain in the side chamber across from the floor pit to retrieve both his orders and the lower gate key.
  • •Use Yrden traps, Northern Wind bombs, or Dancing Star bombs to lock down the Toad Prince's erratic leaps and strike its sides.

Common mistakes

  • •Leaving uncollected items or notes in the tunnels before the boss fight; defeating the Toad Prince permanently locks the area and ejects Geralt via cutscene.
  • •Carelessly leaping into the floor pit without aiming for the deep water pool below, which inflicts severe fall damage.
  • •Standing directly in front of the Toad Prince without Quen active, leaving you vulnerable to massive toxic spit clouds and crushing belly-flop slams.

What you can skip

  • •Skipping the captain's corpse entirely by jumping down the deep water pit if you prefer to bypass key hunting and gated loot.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Enter the Sewers

    Head to the central marketplace of Oxenfurt near the harbor and Borsodi Auction House, then climb down the open well's ladder to reach the upper sewer tunnels. Wade forward into the flooded corridor, where you must draw your silver sword to cut down an ambush of level 32–33 Drowners. Activate your Witcher Senses immediately after the skirmish to spot fresh female footprints pressed into the muck, then follow them deeper into the passage.

    Tip: Coat your blade with Necrophage Oil and use Igni to incinerate grouped Drowners quickly.

  2. 2

    Meet Shani and Breach the Wall

    Follow the tracks until you discover Shani examining the bodies of slain Redanian soldiers, which triggers a conversation with your old friend. Once you finish talking, turn toward the locked iron grate and inspect the adjacent masonry with your Witcher Senses to find a structurally compromised barrier. Knock this fragile partition down with a blast of the Aard Sign to gain entry into the neighboring torchlit chamber.

  3. 3

    Secure the Key or Drop Below

    Step through the blown-out wall toward the burning torch and look across from the massive hole in the floor to locate the fallen Redanian soldier captain. Loot the captain's corpse to acquire the sewer gate key alongside his written orders, then return to unlock the iron grate for a safe descent to the lower tunnels. Alternatively, you can bypass the key entirely by leaping straight down through the gaping floor cavity into the deep water pool below.

    Tip: Unlocking the gate rather than leaping through the pit allows you to view all dialogue with Shani and collect minor loot down the gated path.

    Watch out: If you choose to jump through the floor hole, ensure you hit the deep water; failing to do so inflicts heavy fall damage, and bypassing the corpse permanently skips the captain's key and orders.

  4. 4

    Prepare the Monster's Bait

    Upon reaching the lower sewer level, eliminate a second pack of aggressive Drowners lurking in the contaminated water. Smash through the wooden barrier with your weapon or signs to step into the massive, cavernous cistern chamber. Proceed deeper to find the body of a deceased maiden, identified as Olgierd's cook, and examine her wounds to deduce that the beast craves dead flesh and strong alcohol. Insist that Shani evacuate back to the surface for her own safety, then set the spiked bait and meditate while you wait for the beast to arrive.

  5. 5

    Slay the Toad Prince

    After meditatively baiting the beast, face the monstrous Toad Prince by keeping Quen active to absorb jump-slams, rolling away from toxic spit streams, and freezing or trapping the beast with Northern Wind and Yrden so you can slash its flanks. Cut the beast down to trigger a cutscene where Geralt succumbs to the monster's toxins, is captured, and is removed from the underground lair permanently.

    Key fight: The Toad Prince

    Tip: Drink Superior Golden Oriole prior to the fight so the boss's venomous spit and lingering gas clouds replenish your vitality rather than depleting it.

    Watch out: Defeating the boss immediately triggers a cutscene that expels you to a distant beach north of Novigrad and locks the sewers forever, meaning any uncollected items inside are lost permanently.

Key fights
The Toad Prince

Enemies: The Toad Prince (Cursed One)

Threats: Deals heavy poison damage via venomous spit and toxic clouds, performs long-range tongue whips/pulls, high-impact ground-slam leaps with knockback, and belly stomps that repel melee attacks

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak against Golden Oriole potion (turns poison damage into healing), Yrden Sign, Igni Sign, Cursed Oil, Northern Wind, and Dancing Star bombs; resistant to frontal melee attacks and high-stagger physical strikes

Tactics: Drink Superior Golden Oriole to nullify and convert its poison aura and spit into healing, trap it in place using Yrden or freeze it with Northern Wind to open an attack window, avoid staying directly directly beneath or behind it to prevent knockback stomps, and circle to its flanks using Igni or Dancing Star to exploit its extreme vulnerability to burning

Rewards: Experience points (progresses the main quest "Evil's Soft First Touches" directly into the Ofieri capture cutscene)
