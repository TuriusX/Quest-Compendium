Oxenfurt Sewers

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Investigating the monster for the Hearts of Stone expansion

Geralt enters the sewers beneath Oxenfurt during the Hearts of Stone quest "Evil's Soft First Touches" to track down a deadly beast lurking in the depths. Along the way, he encounters Shani and Redanian soldiers who are also hunting the creature, leading to a confrontation with a giant cursed toad. Navigating the foul corridors requires burning away toxic clouds, defeating drowners, and using Witcher Senses to follow the monster's trail to its lair.

Items

  • Sewer Key: On the corpse of a Redanian soldier inside the sewer tunnels

Tips

  • Consume Superior Golden Oriole potion before fighting the Toad Prince to heal from the toxic poison clouds rather than taking heavy damage.
  • Use Aard or Igni to ignite or clear hazardous gas pockets in the tunnels before walking through them.
