Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Entering the Isle of Mists: Fails unresolved secondary quests in Velen, Novigrad, and Skellige; locks out recruiting allies for the Full Crew achievement and permanently voids missable Gwent cards tied to characters like the Baron, Lambert, or Zoltan.
  • Dead Man's Party wedding reception ending: Leaving the barn without participating in all ten wedding activities permanently locks the "Let the Good Times Roll!" achievement.
  • Borsodi Auction House bidding sequence during "Open Sesame!": Leaving the auction room without winning the brass figurine, painting, and spectacles locks out the "Shopaholic" achievement.
  • The Night of Long Fangs (Blood and Wine): Branching paths determine whether Geralt is imprisoned or awarded the cross; proceeding without a manual backup save prevents achieving both "King of the Clink" and "Last Action Hero" in a single playthrough.

Recommended order
  1. Start a fresh game directly on Death March! difficulty without ever lowering the combat settings.
  2. In White Orchard and Nilfgaardian Garrison, win your first Gwent game in the tavern, buy every starter card, and draw out all Place of Power bonuses before leaving.
  3. Visit the Royal Palace in Vizima and Vizima Castle Grounds to challenge the nobleman in the courtyard to secure his Foltest card.
  4. Arrive at Hanged Man's Tree in Velen, check the notice boards, and head to the Inn at the Crossroads to purchase local Gwent cards and rescue merchants.
  5. Clear quests around Crow's Perch and Crow's Perch (Underground), making sure to beat the Bloody Baron at Gwent before he departs or perishes.
  6. Assist Keira Metz at Keira Metz's Hut, Elven Ruins, Fyke Isle, and the Tower of Mice, ensuring you convince her to travel to Kaer Morhen instead of fighting her.
  7. Navigate Crookback Bog, Downwarren, and Whispering Hillock Cave while resolving the Crones' storyline without missing contract encounters.
  8. Clean up contracts and points of interest across Blackbough, Oreton, Lindenvale, Frischlow, Lornruk, Mulbrydale, Toderas, Dragonslayer's Grotto, and Hangman's Alley.
  9. Head to Novigrad, sweeping Hierarch Square, Temple Isle, and Dandelion's Inn; win the High Stakes tournament at the Passiflora and ensure Triss helps free the mages.
  10. Progress through Oxenfurt and Oxenfurt Sewers to complete the Beast of Honorton and Oxenfurt Drunk contracts.
  11. Travel to Skellige Isles; visit Kaer Trolde Harbor, Kaer Trolde Castle, Lofoten, Garden of Freya, Undvik, and Spikeroog to finish the kingmaking questline and recruit allies for Brothers in Arms.
  12. Gather all allies at Kaer Morhen for the Full Crew achievement before triggering the journey to the Isle of Mists.
  13. Ascend Bald Mountain and later explore The Sunstone Cave during the final battle sequences against the Wild Hunt to finish the base campaign on Death March.
  14. Begin Hearts of Stone: visit the Herbalist's Hut and Borsodi Auction House (buying all three auction lots for Shopaholic), attend the wedding to complete every single activity, and confront Olgierd's phantoms all at once at the Von Everec Estate before finishing at the Temple of Lilvani.
  15. Travel to Toussaint via Beauclair; shoot Golyat in the eye with a crossbow, renovate Corvo Bianco, and earn a flawless victory at the Tourney Grounds.
