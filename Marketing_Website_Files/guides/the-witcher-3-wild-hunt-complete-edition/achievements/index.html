Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Boarding the skiff to enter the Isle of Mists: Full Crew becomes permanently impossible if any recruit quest is incomplete, and several character-specific Gwent cards for Card Collector become permanently unobtainable.
  • Concluding the quest "Reason of State" or proceeding past "Battle Preparations" without triggering it: Assassin of Kings becomes permanently impossible.
  • Midnight ending of the wedding in the Hearts of Stone quest "Dead Man's Party": Let the Good Times Roll! becomes permanently impossible.
  • Leaving the auction floor in "Open Sesame!" without purchasing the painting, glasses, and statuette: Shopaholic becomes permanently impossible.
  • Defeating Iris's Greatest Fear without having all six ethereal shadows active at once in "Scenes From a Marriage": When It's Many Against One… becomes permanently impossible.
  • Leaving the Painted World before restoring all faded memories during "Scenes From a Marriage": Curator of Nightmares becomes permanently impossible.
  • Defeating Golyat at the Dulcinea Windmill without hitting his optical slit with a crossbow bolt: David and Golyat becomes permanently impossible on that save.
  • Choosing between seeking the Unseen Elder or retrieving the ribbon in the Land of a Thousand Fables during "The Night of Long Fangs": Mutually locks out either Last Action Hero (retrieving the ribbon and saving Syanna) or Kling of the Clink (letting Syanna perish by ignoring the ribbon) if a manual save is not made beforehand.

Recommended order
  1. Start a fresh campaign on Death March! difficulty to stack all campaign difficulty achievements in a single playthrough.
  2. In White Orchard, learn Gwent, buy cards from the innkeeper immediately, defeat the scholar at the tavern, and fulfill all local contracts before speaking with the Nilfgaardian Garrison commander.
  3. Travel across Velen to Crow's Perch, Keira Metz's Hut, Fyke Isle, and Crookback Bog; defeat every fistfight champion without losing a round and secure Keira as an ally without killing her.
  4. Head into Novigrad and Oxenfurt to advance side storylines, win all matches during the High Stakes tournament at the Passiflora, complete the King Radovid assassination conspiracy quests, and finish Triss Merigold's requests.
  5. Sail to the Skellige Isles, help resolve the succession dispute between Cerys and Hjalmar an Craite to crown a ruler, recruit all required allies for the defense of Kaer Morhen, and win all regional fistfights and horse races.
  6. Before entering the Isle of Mists, verify that all missable recruitment and Gwent acquisition quests are finished to unlock Full Crew and avoid locking out Card Collector.
  7. Complete the main story assault at Kaer Morhen and the final conflicts through Bald Mountain and Skellige while keeping the difficulty locked on Death March! to pop the base game completion achievements.
  8. Progress through the Hearts of Stone expansion in north-eastern Novigrad and Oxenfurt; participate in all wedding events during Dead Man's Party, buy out every item at the Borsodi Auction House, provoke all nightmare clones at once in the Painted World, and restore all memories at the Von Everec Estate.
  9. Journey south to Toussaint; kill the giant Golyat with a crossbow bolt to the eye right away, resolve the vineyard dispute between Coronata and Vermentino peacefully, display all five chivalric virtues, win the grand knightly tourney, and create a hard save before entering Syanna's search to earn both ending-dependent achievements via reloading.
  10. Clear remaining non-missable combat milestones, witcher gear diagrams, mutagen developments, and contract clean-ups across all maps.
