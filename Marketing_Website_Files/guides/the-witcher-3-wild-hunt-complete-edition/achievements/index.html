The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition achievement guide

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Boarding the boat at the Isle of Mists cutoff: Permanently fails incomplete regional side quests, locking out the "Full Crew" achievement if recruits are not gathered, and voids any unfinished prerequisites for "Assassin of Kings" and missable Gwent cards tied to those NPCs.
  • Completing "Dead Man's Party" without finishing every wedding event: Permanently locks out the "Shopaholic" and "Let the Good Times Roll" achievements from Hearts of Stone.
  • Defeating the Iris nightmare clones sequentially instead of waking all six at once in the Von Everec Estate: Permanently locks out the "When It's Many Against One…" achievement.
  • Killing Golyat without a direct crossbow eye shot at the mill in Toussaint: Permanently misses the "David and Golyat" achievement for that playthrough.
  • Failing any of the tournament competitions during "The Warble of a Smitten Knight": Permanently misses the "A Knight to Remember" achievement.
  • Advancing past "The Night of Long Fangs" without keeping a manual backup save: Permanently prevents acquiring both mutually exclusive ending achievements ("Last Action Hero" and "Kling of the Clink") in a single run.

Recommended order
  1. Start a fresh campaign on Death March difficulty to clear all difficulty achievements simultaneously without lowering it at any point.
  2. Explore White Orchard thoroughly, purchasing initial Gwent cards from the tavern keeper and Elsa, and play the scholar at the tavern to secure the first missable card.
  3. Progress through Velen and Novigrad, completing Keira Metz's questline peacefully to send her to Kaer Morhen, attending the Vegelbud masquerade ball to win the three exclusive tournament Gwent cards, and pursuing Radovid's conspiracy side quests.
  4. Complete the Skellige succession questline by choosing to back either Cerys or Hjalmar, and recruit all required allies (Keira, Triss, Roche, Ves, Zoltan, Hjalmar, Ermion) across regions for Kaer Morhen.
  5. Secure all remaining base-game Gwent cards and win the high-stakes tournament at the Passiflora before triggering the Isle of Mists story transition.
  6. Complete the main story through the Battle of Kaer Morhen and finish Radovid's assassination questline ("Reason of State") to wrap up base-game story milestones.
  7. Begin the Hearts of Stone expansion, making a hard save before attending the wedding during "Dead Man's Party" to complete all festive activities, purchasing all auction house items, and provoking all six Olgierd nightmare clones simultaneously at the Von Everec Estate.
  8. Start the Blood and Wine expansion, shoot Golyat directly in the eye with a crossbow during his opening boss fight, win every competition at the Tourney Grounds flawlessly, display the five chivalric virtues, and craft Grandmaster Witcher gear.
  9. Create a manual save during "The Night of Long Fangs" before choosing between the Unseen Elder's path and Syanna's fairy tale path; complete one path to obtain its specific ending achievement, then reload to finish the alternative branch.
  10. Clean up any remaining cumulative combat, contract, and exploration achievements across Velen, Novigrad, Skellige, and Toussaint in post-game free roam.
