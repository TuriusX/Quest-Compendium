The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition achievement guide

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Boarding the boat to The Isle of Mists: Permanently locks out uncompleted side quests required for "Full Crew", Keira Metz's questline ("Friends With Benefits"), Skellige succession quests ("Kingmaker"), and several one-time Gwent card opportunities.
  • Main quest "Blindingly Obvious" (shoving Dijkstra instead of bribing him): Permanently locks out the assassination subquest line and the "Assassin of Kings" achievement.
  • Main quest "Capture the Castle" / "The Night of Long Fangs" (Blood and Wine): Permanently locks you into the DLC finale, mutually locking out either "Last Action Hero" or "Kling of the Clink" without a prior manual save.

Recommended order
  1. Start a new game on Death March difficulty without lowering it at any point.
  2. Progress through White Orchard and Royal Palace in Vizima, learning Gwent early and purchasing every card from innkeepers and merchants.
  3. Explore Velen and Novigrad, completing regional Witcher Contracts, fist fights, horse races, and side quests.
  4. Complete Keira Metz's questline in Velen amicably ("Friends With Benefits") and convince her to go to Kaer Morhen for the "Full Crew" achievement.
  5. Win the Gwent tournament at the Passiflora in Novigrad during "High Stakes" ("Gwent Master") and collect tournament/quest-exclusive cards during "A Matter of Life and Death".
  6. Assist Dijkstra, Roche, and Thaler in Novigrad through "A Deadly Plot" and complete the Skellige ruler storyline ("Kingmaker").
  7. Recruit all required allies across Velen, Novigrad, and Skellige before initiating "The Isle of Mists" to unlock "Full Crew".
  8. Complete "Reason of State" late in the base game campaign to unlock "Assassin of Kings".
  9. Complete the main story to unlock all story-related achievements and the Death March completion achievements.
  10. Start Hearts of Stone: ensure you participate in all wedding events during "Dead Man's Party" ("Shopaholic"), purchase all auction items ("Let the Good Times Roll!"), and defeat all Iris von Everec nightmares at once ("When It's Many Against One").
  11. Start Blood and Wine: kill Golyat with a crossbow shot to the eye upon entering Toussaint ("David and Golyat").
  12. Prove the five chivalric virtues across Toussaint side quests to earn Aerondight ("Embodiment of the Five Virtues") and win the tourney with flawless victories ("A Knight to Remember").
  13. Create a manual backup save during "The Night of Long Fangs" before choosing your path.
  14. Branch 1: Follow Syanna to the Land of a Thousand Fables, retrieve the ribbon to keep her alive, and finish the story to obtain the medal ("Last Action Hero").
  15. Branch 2: Reload the save, visit the Unseen Elder or neglect obtaining Syanna's ribbon so she perishes, completing the sequence to spend time in Toussaint Prison ("Kling of the Clink").
