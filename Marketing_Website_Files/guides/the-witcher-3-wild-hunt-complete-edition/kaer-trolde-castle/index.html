Kaer Trolde Castle

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Attending the wake for King Bran

Geralt travels to Kaer Trolde to attend the royal wake for the deceased King Bran alongside Yennefer. During the gathering, you can socialize with Skellige chieftains, participate in drinking and fistfight activities, and sneak into Ermion's workshop to locate the stolen Mask of Uroboros.

Items

  • Formal Skellige Attire MISSABLE: Chest in Yennefer's guest room in Kaer Trolde
  • Mask of Uroboros: Secret laboratory behind Ermion's study
  • Hjalmar's Steel Sword MISSABLE: Rewarded by winning the drinking contest against the Skellige warrior in the banquet hall

Enemies

Earth ElementalDimeritium Bomb, Elementa OilGuards Ermion's secret vault and attacks when you approach the Mask of Uroboros

Tips

  • Equip formal Skellige clothes before meeting Yennefer at the banquet doors to earn her approval and unique dialogue.
  • During the dialogue race against Cerys to the summit above the castle, cut corners on the snowy trail to easily beat her.
