Elven Ruins

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Exploring with Keira Metz ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Geralt enters these subterranean elven ruins alongside the sorceress Keira Metz to track down a mysterious elven mage connected to Ciri. Players must navigate through flooded corridors, toxic gas chambers, and portals while solving symbol riddles left by the elf. The exploration culminates in battles against the Wild Hunt, dispelling illusory barriers, and optionally recovering an elven magical artifact for Keira.

Region
Velen (No Man's Land)
Levels
5–6
Getting there
South/southwest of Midcopse, near the southern coastline (Spitfire Bluff / Lake shore), reached directly by teleporting with Keira Metz from her hut or by traveling manually to the cave entrance.
Connects to
Midcopse, Southern shoreline near Spitfire Bluff
Related quests
Wandering in the Dark · Magic Lamp
Services and people
Keira Metz (trader)

The short version

What matters

  • •Agree to help Keira with the Magic Lamp quest so you can claim both the Magic Lamp artifact and access the hidden Place of Power.
  • •Acquire the Eye of Nehaleni in the mage's study to dispel illusory walls throughout the ruins and the rest of Velen.
  • •Dive to the bottom of the flooded tunnels and the Kelpie cistern to secure sunken crafting and alchemy diagrams.

Common mistakes

  • •Attacking Nithral while he is encased in his frost shield; switch immediately to eliminating his summoned hounds to let Keira drop the barrier.
  • •Examining the Hound or Octopus symbols in the Octagonal Cistern; only interact with the Kelpie symbol to avoid explosive traps and Wraith spawns.
  • •Lighting the Four Guardians braziers in the wrong order; ignite them 3rd, 2nd, 4th, 1st from left to right to prevent hostile Wraiths from appearing.

What you can skip

  • •You can bypass the side-passage Gargoyle fight entirely if you prefer to proceed directly to the blizzard rifts.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Cave Entrance to the Flooded Tunnels

    After teleporting from Keira Metz's hut or walking to the cave south/southwest of Midcopse, follow Keira to an overlook where you witness Wild Hunt warriors and a Navigator opening rifts. Step into Keira's unstable portal in the First Portal Chamber to reach the submerged cave, where you must fight off Drowners. Before climbing out, dive to the bottom of the flooded tunnels to retrieve sunken chests containing crafting and alchemy diagrams.

    Key fight: Drowners

    Tip: Stagger Drowners using Aard and burn them with Igni.

    Watch out: If you hesitate or refuse to step through the portal, it will close, forcing you onto side paths that bypass the submerged cave's underwater chests.

  2. 2

    The Rat Chamber to the Toxic Gas Hall

    Rush along the tunnels to locate Keira Metz stranded on an elevated ledge in the Rat Chamber, surrounded by endless rodent swarms. Once you destroy the two rat nests and reunite, proceed into the First Projection Chamber, where an elven mage's projection plays above a hall covered in flammable sulfur gas and guarded by Wraiths. Before moving forward, look for Swallow symbols to find the safe path through the gas.

    Key fight: Rats & Rat Nests Key fight: Wraiths

    Tip: Destroy both rodent nests instantly with Igni or bombs like Grapeshot and Dancing Star to stop the rats from endlessly respawning.

    Watch out: Avoid using Igni against the Wraiths while standing inside the sulfur gas, as it will ignite the fumes and damage Geralt.

  3. 3

    The Octagonal Cistern and Guardian Antechamber

    Enter the Octagonal Cistern to view a second projection directing Ciri toward Kelpie, surrounded by various wall markings beside a deep water abyss. Dive to the pool's bottom to loot an underwater chest resting next to the Kelpie engraving, then examine the Kelpie (Horse) symbol to unlock the passage. Drop down into the wide laboratory antechamber ahead, where you must destroy an ancient elven Guardian Golem barring your way.

    Key fight: Golem (Guardian)

    Tip: Maintain Quen and dodge around to the Golem's rear flank while applying Elementa Oil.

    Watch out: Examining the Hound or Sea Serpent/Octopus markings instead of the Kelpie symbol triggers an explosion and summons hostile Wraiths.

  4. 4

    The Blizzard Rifts and Nithral Boss Fight

    Bypass or defeat an optional Gargoyle in the side passage, then follow Keira through a series of freezing blizzard rifts where you must eliminate Wild Hunt Hounds while staying strictly inside her protective golden bubble. Enter the Final Chamber to confront the Wild Hunt warrior Nithral, who attacks with a heavy two-handed axe. When Nithral encases himself in an impenetrable healing shield, defeat the summoned hounds swiftly so Keira can lower the barrier, then finish him off to acquire leveled Wild Hunt gear, Dark Steel Plate or ore, and alchemical mutagens.

    Key fight: Wild Hunt Hounds & Blizzard Rifts Key fight: Nithral

    Tip: Apply Elementa Oil to your blade and avoid stepping outside Keira's protective dome during the rift crossing.

    Watch out: Never strike Nithral while his frost shield is active, as it will reflect your damage back onto you.

  5. 5

    The Mage's Study to the Magic Lamp Tomb

    Search the mage's study for notes regarding Ciri, then inspect the hidden doorway illusion at the back to receive the Eye of Nehaleni from Keira. When Keira asks for your assistance near the exit, agree to help her with the Magic Lamp quest and enter the statue puzzle room. Ignite the four statue braziers in the order of 3rd, 2nd, 4th, and 1st from left to right to unlock the sealed door, allowing you to claim the Magic Lamp from the shrine inside.

    Tip: Solving the statue puzzle correctly on the first attempt earns 100 XP and prevents extra combat.

    Watch out: Declining to help Keira causes the Magic Lamp quest to fail immediately and seals off both the Magic Lamp and the ruins' deepest chambers.

  6. 6

    The Hidden Upper Cavern

    Return to the area behind the Magic Lamp riddle chamber and climb the stone ledges. Head up to the high ledge to draw from a Place of Power granting an ability point and boosting Yrden. Defeat the secondary Golem guarding the chamber beyond to loot chests containing diagrams and leveled gear before departing for the southern shoreline near Spitfire Bluff.

    Tip: Yrden and Dimeritium Bombs make short work of the secondary Golem.

    Watch out: If you refused to help Keira with the Magic Lamp quest earlier, the pathway to this Place of Power and its guarded treasure remains permanently inaccessible.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Entering Keira's Portal

In the First Portal Chamber after Keira casts a portal

  • Step through portal→ Sends Geralt into flooded caves with Drowners and sunken chests with diagrams
  • Wait or refuse→ Skips the Drowners' cavern, requiring Geralt to take the side paths directly toward Keira

Most players pick: Step through portal

Cistern Symbol Riddles

In the Octagonal Cistern when selecting symbols

  • Inspect Kelpie (Horse)→ Safely progresses through the ruins
  • Inspect Hound or Sea Serpent/Octopus→ Triggers an explosion and summons hostile Wraiths/illusions

Most players pick: Inspect Kelpie (Horse)

Assisting Keira with the Magic Lamp

Near the ruins exit following the investigation of the mage's study

  • All right, I'll help→ Begins the Magic Lamp quest, unlocks the statue chamber, awards 100 XP, and allows access to a Place of Power, guarded loot, and later side quests
  • Got to get back→ Leaves immediately, failing Magic Lamp, causing Keira to leave, and locking Geralt out of the Magic Lamp, the Place of Power, and the guarded treasure

Most players pick: All right, I'll help

Point of no return: declining fails the quest and permanently blocks access to the Place of Power

Statue Puzzle Braziers

Inside the sealed chamber during Magic Lamp

  • Light the 3rd, 2nd, 4th, and 1st statues from left to right→ Solves the riddle and unseals the tomb door
  • Light statues in the wrong order→ Extinguishes lit braziers and spawns two hostile Wraiths

Most players pick: Light 3rd, 2nd, 4th, 1st from left to right

Key fights
Rats & Rat Nests

Enemies: Swarming rats pouring from two rodent nests

Threats: Swarm Keira rapidly and interrupt her shields

Weaknesses and resistances: Igni, Bombs (Grapeshot, Dancing Star)

Tactics: Destroy both nests instantly using Igni or standard bombs to halt infinite spawns.

Rewards: XP for nest destruction

Drowners

Enemies: Drowners

Threats: Melee swarms

Weaknesses and resistances: Necrophage Oil, Igni, Aard

Tactics: Easily staggered with Aard and burned with Igni.

Rewards: Drowner Brains, Water Essence

Wraiths

Enemies: Wraiths

Threats: Teleporting behind Geralt, ethereal invulnerability, igniting surrounding swamp gas

Weaknesses and resistances: Specter Oil, Yrden, Moon Dust

Tactics: Trap inside Yrden circles to force corporeality; avoid using Igni in sulfur gas areas.

Rewards: Specter Dust, Infused Shards

Golem (Guardian)

Enemies: Elven Guardian Golem

Threats: Highly armored, heavy sweeping swings, charging stomps, corner traps

Weaknesses and resistances: Elementa Oil, Dimeritium Bombs, Quen

Tactics: Use Quen, dodge behind its rear flank, and avoid corner traps.

Rewards: Golem Heart, Infused Dust, crafting loot

Wild Hunt Hounds & Blizzard Rifts

Enemies: Wild Hunt Hounds

Threats: Freezing frost bites, continuous lethal blizzard damage outside Keira's shield

Weaknesses and resistances: Elementa Oil, Igni, Dimeritium Bombs

Tactics: Stay strictly inside Keira's protective golden bubble to avoid blizzard damage while killing incoming hounds.

Rewards: Hound claws, Frost essence

Nithral

Enemies: Nithral

Threats: Parry-breaking two-handed Wild Hunt axe, impenetrable healing frost shield with damage reflection, summoned hounds

Weaknesses and resistances: Elementa Oil, Quen, Igni, Axii

Tactics: Do not attack him while his shield is active; defeat summoned hounds quickly so Keira brings down his barrier.

Rewards: Leveled Wild Hunt gear/swords, Dark Steel Plate/ore, Alchemical mutagens
