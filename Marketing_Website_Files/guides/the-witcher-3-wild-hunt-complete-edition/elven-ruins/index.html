Elven Ruins

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Exploring with Keira Metz

Geralt accompanies the sorceress Keira Metz into underground elven ruins in Velen while pursuing a lead on Ciri and the mysterious masked elven mage. You must navigate dark tunnels, overcome magical hazards and Wild Hunt projection riddles, and survive an ambush by Wild Hunt warriors. Completing this expedition concludes the main quest "Wandering in the Dark" and initiates Keira's optional request quest "Magic Lamp".

Items

  • Note from Unknown Witcher MISSABLE: inside a chest in an underwater tunnel accessible after the gas-filled cavern
  • Magic Lamp: retrieved from the altar in the final puzzle room during the quest "Magic Lamp"
  • Eye of Nehaleni: given automatically by Keira Metz to dispel illusory walls within the ruins

Secrets

  • Dispelling an illusory wall near the gargoyle chamber with the Eye of Nehaleni reveals a hidden stash containing ancient elven weaponry and high-grade crafting components.
  • Solving the four-statue riddle in the side cavern by igniting the braziers in the order described in the elven poem opens the locked door containing the Magic Lamp.

Tips

  • Igniting the swamp gas clouds with the Igni sign safely burns away pockets of toxic vapor before entering them, but keep your distance to avoid catching in the blast.
  • Stay close inside Keira Metz's protective magical shield dome during the White Frost blizzard sequence to avoid rapid health degeneration.
  • Do not attack Nithral while his magical bubble shield is active, as striking it causes massive knockback and severe damage to Geralt.
