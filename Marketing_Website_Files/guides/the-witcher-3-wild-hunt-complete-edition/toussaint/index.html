Toussaint

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Arriving in the duchy for the Blood and Wine expansion

Geralt arrives in the sunny, idyllic Duchy of Toussaint after accepting a contract from Duchess Anna Henrietta to hunt a mysterious beast. Upon arrival, you fight a giant windmill-bound foe, investigate initial crime scenes, and unlock the Corvo Bianco vineyard estate. The area introduces Grandmaster Witcher gear hunts, knightly tourneys, and mutagen mutations alongside the main vampiric mystery.

Items

  • Aerondight MISSABLE: Acquired from the Hermit at Lac Céleste after proving the five chivalric virtues
  • Corvo Bianco Vineyard deed: Awarded by Duchess Anna Henrietta during the early main questline

Secrets

  • Unseen Elder's lair entrance | Hidden on the southern cliffs near the shores of Lake Celavy, accessible only during the late-game Blood and Wine choices | missable: yes

Shops and people

  • Grandmaster Smith Lafargue: Crafts Grandmaster-tier Witcher gear and sells high-end crafting components
  • Corvo Bianco Majordomo: Manages vineyard upgrades such as herb gardens, stable improvements, and bed bonuses

Tips

  • Upgrade your vineyard's bed, library, and stables early to receive passive buffs whenever you rest at Corvo Bianco.
  • Complete the quest "Turn and Face the Strange" as soon as it appears via a courier's letter to unlock the powerful expanded mutation skill tree.
  • Displaying chivalric virtues in side quests and dialogues is necessary to claim the Aerondight silver sword.
