The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · The game's opening tutorial

This is the dream prologue sequence where Geralt wakes up in the Witcher fortress alongside Yennefer. You must find the bedroom key to get dressed, meet Vesemir in the courtyard, and complete the basic movement and combat tutorials with Ciri.

Items

  • Bedroom Key MISSABLE: On a small table to the left of the closed wooden door inside Geralt's starting bedroom
  • A Tome on Kings and Rulers MISSABLE: On a wooden desk near the balcony inside Geralt's starting bedroom
  • Hunting Boots MISSABLE: Inside a chest next to the dressing screen in Geralt's starting bedroom

Secrets

  • You can interact with a wooden bowl and pitcher on a dresser in Geralt's bedroom to trigger a short hygiene cutscene before getting dressed.

Tips

  • Use Witcher Senses to easily spot the bedroom key glow on the table if you cannot find it immediately.
  • You can choose to skip the running race or combat drills with Vesemir if you want to finish the tutorial faster.
