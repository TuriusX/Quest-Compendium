Undvik

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Searching for Hjalmar an Craite

Geralt travels to the desolate island of Undvik to locate Hjalmar an Craite and help him slay the Ice Giant as part of the Skellige succession questline. You will track Hjalmar's ill-fated expedition across frozen ruins, abandoned villages, and sea caves littered with giant-ravaged ships. The journey culminates in a confrontation with the Ice Giant in his mountain lair to avenge the fallen warriors.

Items

  • High Quality Forge Tools: Clan Tordarroch Forge inside a chest guarded by a rock troll
  • Vrygheff: Inside a chest in the tower ruins near the giant's lair

Secrets

  • Inside the derelict ship near the shoreline, you can find a survivor named Octo who has lost his mind and speaks only to severed heads.

Tips

  • You can find and free Folan from a cooking pot inside a troll cave before heading to the giant's lair so he can assist you in the final battle.
  • During the battle with the Ice Giant, free Vigi the Loon from his cage if you want him to fight alongside you, or leave him if you want to avoid him rushing into combat.
  • Use the Hornwall Horn found during the investigation to immediately knock flying sirens to the ground.
