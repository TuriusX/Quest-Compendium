Vizima Castle Grounds

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Exploring the palace gardens

The palace garden in the Royal Palace in Vizima is an outdoor courtyard accessible during the main quest "Imperial Audience." Players can explore the grounds to uncover a hidden chamber, search various containers, and play Gwent with a Nilfgaardian nobleman seated on a bench.

Items

  • Rodeo's notes: Found inside a hollow stone wall in the palace courtyard garden

Secrets

  • Pressing a loose brick on the courtyard garden wall reveals a hidden alcove containing a skeleton and a chest.

Shops and people

  • Nilfgaardian Nobleman: Offers a game of Gwent; defeating him awards the Foltest: The Siegemaster leader card.

Tips

  • Use Witcher Senses in the courtyard to easily spot the interactable loose stone on the garden wall.
  • Play Gwent against the Nilfgaardian Nobleman early, but be prepared for a challenge as he plays a high-strength Nilfgaardian deck with multiple Spy cards.
