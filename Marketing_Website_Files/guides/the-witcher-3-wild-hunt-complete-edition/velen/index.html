The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Arriving in the war-torn region

Geralt enters the grim, swampy landscape of No Man's Land to pursue leads concerning Ciri's whereabouts. You must investigate connections involving the Bloody Baron at Crow's Perch and a local witch operating in the area. Navigating this dangerous wilderness involves clearing monster dens, resolving local village contracts, and balancing volatile regional factions.

Items

  • Letter to John Geermere: Found on a corpse inside the tavern at the Inn at the Crossroads

Secrets

  • A submerged chest containing random gear and crafting materials can be found directly beneath the ruined wooden bridge south of the Inn at the Crossroads.

Shops and people

  • Innkeeper at the Inn at the Crossroads: Sells food, alcohol, and basic Gwent cards
  • Quartermaster at Crow's Perch: Sells crafting diagrams, crafting components, and armor repair kits

Tips

  • Do not draw your weapon or engage in unnecessary aggression while speaking with the Baron's men inside the Inn at the Crossroads to avoid hostile encounters across Crow's Perch.
  • Keep Necrophage Oil applied to your silver sword frequently, as the vast majority of threats lurking in Velen's wetlands are vulnerable to it.
