Kaer Trolde Harbor

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition guide · Arriving in Skellige

Kaer Trolde Harbor is the primary port and commercial hub of Ard Skellig where Geralt first arrives by ship after washing ashore. Players visit the harbor to regroup with Yennefer, access varied merchants, and begin key quests before ascending the bridge to the fortress above.

Items

  • Notice Board notices: On the wooden notice board near the harbor center
  • Gwent Cards: Available to purchase from Jonas the Innkeeper inside the New Port Inn
  • Skellige style clothing: Sold by the tailor near the docks

Secrets

  • Sunken cargo chests containing crafting materials and coin can be looted under the harbor docks by diving beneath the wooden walkways.

Shops and people

  • Jonas the Innkeeper: Sells food, alcohol, and Gwent cards inside the New Port Inn
  • Armorer: Offers armorer services and crafting diagrams near the harbor square
  • Blacksmith: Sells weapons and offers repair and crafting services along the docks
  • Herbalist: Sells alchemy ingredients and potion formulas along the waterfront path

Tips

  • Purchase and read the local travel maps from the harbor merchants to automatically unlock undiscovered fast travel signposts across the Skellige archipelago.
  • Equip the doublet and trousers purchased from the harbor tailor before attending the wake at Kaer Trolde to avoid Yennefer's disapproval.
