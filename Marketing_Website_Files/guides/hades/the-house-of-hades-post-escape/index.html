The House of Hades (Post-escape)

Hades guide · Returning to the hub after dying or escaping
← All areas in this guide

Returning to the House of Hades after a successful escape serves as the central hub between runs, allowing Zagreus to converse with shades and gods, upgrade his capabilities, and interact with the denizens of the Underworld. You can spend collected resources like Darkness, Gemstones, and Chthonic Keys to unlock permanent upgrades and customize the hub's appearance. Once you are prepared for the next attempt, you head back to Zagreus's bedchambers and jump out the window to begin another run.

Achievements here
The Family SecretSuccessfully escape the underworld 10 times to complete the main storyline and view the end credits.
The Useless TrinketSuccessfully escape the Underworld on Heat 8 or higher, then interact with the first unveiled statue in the courtyard given by Skelly.
Dark ReflectionsComplete successful escape attempts having used every single red and green talent on the Mirror of Night at least once.
Harsh ConditionsSuccessfully clear an escape attempt with each of the 15 different Pact of Punishment conditions active at least once.
Skelly's Last LamentationsSuccessfully escape the Underworld on Heat 16 or higher, then interact with the second unveiled statue awarded by Skelly.

Shops and people
  • House Contractor: Work orders, chamber decorations, run upgrades, and music tracks in exchange for Gemstones and Diamonds
  • Wretched Broker: Resource trades exchanging Chthonic Keys, Nectar, Gemstones, and high-tier materials for other currencies
  • Head Chef: Sells Darkness, Gemstones, and other rewards in exchange for caught river fish

Tips
  • Always speak to every resident with an exclamation point above their head, as progressing relationships is essential for unlocking Weapon Aspects and Companions.
  • Turn in any fish caught during your escape attempt to the Head Chef before leaving, as keeping them serves no purpose and turning them in immediately grants resources.
  • Check the Wretched Broker's limited-time offers after every return to trade surplus resources for rare items like Ambrosia or Titan Blood.
