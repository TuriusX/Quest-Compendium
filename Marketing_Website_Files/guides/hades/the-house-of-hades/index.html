The House of Hades

Hades guide · The hub area where Zagreus begins his journey
← All areas in this guide

The House of Hades serves as the central hub where Zagreus returns after dying or completing an escape attempt. Here, you can speak with various Chthonic deities and shades, upgrade your stats and weapon aspects, unlock cosmetics, and prepare your build before embarking on your next run. Once you are ready, you step into the courtyard to select an Infernal Arm and jump out the window to begin in Tartarus.

Enemies
Skelly

Training dummy located in the courtyard who stands still so you can safely test attack combinations, casts, and boons

Shops and people
  • House Contractor: Sells structural renovations, visual decorations, and run-enhancement work orders in exchange for Gemstones and Diamonds
  • Wretched Broker: Offers bartering services to trade various currencies like Darkness, Gemstones, Nectar, and Chthonic Keys
  • Head Chef: Accepts caught fish in exchange for crafting resources and currency rewards

Tips
  • Always talk to every character with an exclamation point above their head before leaving, as progressing dialogue advances key narrative threads and unlocks weapon aspects.
  • You can pet Cerberus in the main hall between escape attempts.
  • Practice moves and test new weapon aspects on Skelly in the courtyard to gauge damage output before committing to a run.
