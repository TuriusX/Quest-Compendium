Tartarus

Hades guide · The first underworld biome Zagreus attempts to escape
← All areas in this guide

Tartarus is the first biome Zagreus must navigate during his escape attempts from the Underworld, characterized by dungeon chambers, soul urns, and green-lit stone corridors. Players clear randomly generated encounter rooms of shades and traps, collecting boons and resources while progressing toward exit doors that show the next room's reward preview. The biome ends in a boss arena at Chamber 14, where Zagreus must defeat one of the Fury sisters to ascend to Asphodel.

Enemies
Lout

Fast-moving melee brawler that charges directly at Zagreus.

Wringer

Creeping hand-like enemy that attempts to grab and immobilize Zagreus.

Spitter

Stationary or slow-moving head that launches ranged projectile orbs.

Thug

Slow, high-health bruiser that executes heavy, wide-radius club strikes.

Witch

Ranged caster that fires spreads of slow-moving magical projectiles.

Brimstone

Floating crystal enemy that tracks Zagreus with a continuous firing beam.

Skull-Imp

Weak minion spawned from Skull-Dropper waves or bloodless clusters.

Doomstone

Mini-boss monolith that summons waves of Skull-Imps and fires beam sweeps.

Megaera

First Fury sister boss who uses whip slashes, dash strikes, and circular bullet-hell patterns.

Alecto

Fury sister boss alternative who charges rage meter, summons tracking buzzsaws, and lays whip traps.

Tisiphone

Fury sister boss alternative who casts room-narrowing mist, fires projectile walls, and teleports.

Shops and people
  • Charon: Sells boons, healing items, Centaur Hearts, Darkness, Gemstones, and Pom of Power slices in exchange for Obols.
  • Sisyphus: Friendly shade NPC found in special story rooms; offers a choice between free Health, Darkness, or Charon's Obols alongside Bouldy.
  • Well of Charon: Intermittent interactable basin offering temporary multi-chamber buffs and consumables for Obols.

Tips
  • Knocking enemies into chamber walls or pillars deals extra impact damage, quickly dispatching unarmored Tartarus foes.
  • Use the spikes, explosive urns, and pressure plates in Tartarus rooms against enemies rather than avoiding them entirely.
  • Tartarus traps and urns can be triggered safely from range using cast abilities or ranged basic attacks.
