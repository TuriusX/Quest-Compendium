Route 114

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Fallarbor from Lavaridge

Route 114 connects Fallarbor Town in the north to Meteor Falls in the south. Players must pass through here while pursuing Team Magma and Team Aqua toward Meteor Falls. The path starts with grassy terrain and bridges before climbing up rocky crags.

Items

  • TM28 (Dig): Inside the Fossil Maniac's house, given by the kid inside
  • TM05 (Roar): Given by a gentleman with a Poochyena near the northern entrance of the route
  • Energypowder: On the ground behind smashable rocks on the lower path
  • Protein: High ledge on the rocky southern cliffside requiring Rock Smash

Secrets

  • Hidden Revive found by checking a rock near the base of the waterfall | use the Itemfinder or interact with the solitary rock
  • Hidden Carbos tucked away in a dead-end rock formation on the southern mountain path | interact with the rock

Tips

  • Bring Pokémon with Rock Smash and Surf to fully explore the route and collect all available items.
  • The Fossil Maniac's cave on this route eventually expands into the Desert Underpass in the post-game, allowing you to obtain the second fossil.
