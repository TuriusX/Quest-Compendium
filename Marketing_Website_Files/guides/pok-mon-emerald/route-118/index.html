Route 118

Pokémon Emerald guide · Heading east from Mauville

Route 118 connects Mauville City in the west to Route 119 in the north and Route 123 in the east, divided by a central river. Crossing the water requires the move Surf, after which the player encounters Steven Stone, who speaks with them before departing. This route acts as a gateway opening up the eastern, rainy half of the Hoenn region.

Items

  • Good Rod: Given by a Fisherman standing on the eastern shore of the river
  • Hyper Potion: Lying on the ground near the grass on the eastern side of the route
  • Sitrus Berry: Found on the Berry soft-soil patch on the eastern side of the route

Secrets

  • Hidden Heart Scale | Buried in the sand near the water's edge on the western side of the river

Tips

  • Make sure you have the Dynamo Badge and HM03 Surf taught to a Pokémon, as you cannot reach the eastern side of Route 118 without surfing.
  • Talking to the Fisherman on the east bank is essential early on if you want to fish up Pokémon like Carvanha.
