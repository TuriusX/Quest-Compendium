Route 102

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Petalburg

Route 102 connects Oldale Town in the east to Petalburg City in the west. It introduces your first standard Trainer battles, as well as berry patches and a wider variety of wild Pokémon. Head west through the grass and paths while defeating trainers to reach Petalburg City.

Items

  • Potion: On the ground in the grass patch near the eastern entrance
  • Pecha Berry: Growing in the soft soil patch near the Boy on the route
  • Oran Berry: Growing in the soft soil patch near the Boy on the route

Tips

  • Ralts can be caught in the grass here, but it has a very low encounter rate, so expect to search for a while if you want one for your team.
  • Pick the Oran and Pecha Berries from the soil patch and plant new ones if you want to farm more later.
