Dewford Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · Second gym challenge

Dewford Town is an isolated island community reached via Mr. Briney's boat from Route 104. Players challenge Brawly, the Fighting-type Gym Leader, and explore the nearby Granite Cave to deliver a letter to Steven. Defeating the Gym awards the Knuckle Badge and allows the use of Flash outside of battle.

Items

  • Old Rod: Received from a Fisherman standing near the Gym
  • Silk Scarf: Given by an NPC inside the house located in the north of town
  • Knuckle Badge: Rewarded by Gym Leader Brawly upon defeat
  • TM08 (Bulk Up): Rewarded by Gym Leader Brawly upon defeat

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Gym Leader BrawlyFlying, PsychicUses Machop, Meditite, and Makuhita; Makuhita can hit hard with Bulk Up and Arm Thrust.

Shops and people

  • Dewford Town Pokémon Center: Free healing service and PC access
  • Trendy Phrase NPC: An NPC outside Dewford Hall who changes the town's "trendy phrase" when spoken to

Tips

  • Catching a Flying-type like Taillow or Wingull on early routes makes the Dewford Gym battle significantly easier.
  • You must deliver the Letter to Steven in Granite Cave north of town before Mr. Briney will offer to sail you to Slateport City.
