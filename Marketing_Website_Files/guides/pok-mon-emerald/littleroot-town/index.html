Littleroot Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · Starting the journey

Littleroot Town is the quiet starting town where you arrive inside a moving truck to settle into your new home. After setting your clock and introducing yourself to your rival next door, you must head north to Route 101 to rescue Professor Birch and choose your starter Pokémon. Once Birch is saved, you return here to receive your starter permanently and later collect the Pokédex and Running Shoes.

Items

  • Potion: Withdrawn from the Item Storage PC in your bedroom
  • Running Shoes: Given by your Mom outside your house after defeating your rival on Route 103
  • Pokédex: Given by Professor Birch in his laboratory after defeating your rival on Route 103
  • Poké Balls (x5): Given by your rival inside Professor Birch's lab after defeating them on Route 103
  • Amulet Coin: Given by your Mom after defeating Gym Leader Norman in Petalburg City

Shops and people

  • Mom: Fully heals your party whenever you speak with her inside your house

Tips

  • Setting the wall clock in your bedroom activates the internal time system, which influences daily events and tides throughout the game.
  • Holding the B button while wearing the Running Shoes lets you travel twice as fast, but running inside buildings is not allowed.
