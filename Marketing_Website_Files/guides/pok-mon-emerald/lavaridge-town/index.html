Lavaridge Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · Fourth gym challenge

Lavaridge Town is a hot springs settlement situated at the southern base of Mt. Chimney, reachable after descending the Jagged Pass. The main objective here is to defeat the Gym Leader Flannery to earn the Heat Badge, which allows the use of HM04 Strength outside of battle. After challenging the Gym, the player receives the Go-Goggles from the rival, enabling access to the desert on Route 111.

Items

  • Charcoal: Inside the local Herb Shop, given by an elderly man on the right side
  • Wynaut Egg: Given by the elderly woman standing beside the outdoor hot spring pool

Secrets

  • Hidden Ice Heal | Buried inside the southern outdoor hot spring pool, located by checking the middle tile on the far left edge of the water | missable: no

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Gym Leader FlanneryWater, Ground, RockUses Numel, Slugma, Camerupt, and an Overheat-focused Torkoal with the White Herb item

Shops and people

  • Lavaridge Herb Shop: Bitter medicinal herbs: Energypowder, Energy Root, Heal Powder, Revival Herb
  • Lavaridge Town Poké Mart: Standard adventuring supplies: Great Ball, Super Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal, Awakening, Super Repel, X Speed

Tips

  • Navigating Flannery's Gym requires dropping into sand trap holes and emerging through linked geysers, with several trainers popping out to ambush you from beneath the sand.
  • The bitter herbs sold at the Herb Shop are much cheaper than standard restorative items, but using them lowers the target Pokémon's friendship.
