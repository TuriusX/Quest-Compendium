Route 106

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Dewford

Route 106 is an ocean route connecting Route 105 to Dewford Town. When first visiting, Mr. Briney transports you here via his boat so you can deliver the Letter to Steven. Later in the game, you can return to fully explore its waters using Surf and check the entrance of Granite Cave.

Items

  • Protein: On the sandy beach near Granite Cave, accessible after getting Surf

Secrets

  • Hidden Poké Ball | On a rock on the beach near the entrance to Dewford Town, inspectable directly or using the Itemfinder

Tips
