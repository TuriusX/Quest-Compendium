Slateport City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Delivering the Devon Parts

Arriving via Mr. Briney's boat, you must deliver the Devon Goods to Captain Stern in Slateport City. Before you can reach him at the Oceanic Museum, you must first visit Stern's Shipyard to learn his whereabouts, after which Team Aqua will clear out of the museum entrance. Inside the museum, you pay a small entry fee, find Captain Stern on the second floor, and defeat two Team Aqua Grunts to finally deliver the parts.

Items

  • Soft Sand: Talk to the girl with the inner tube in the far top-left section of the beach
  • Soda Pop (6-pack): Defeat all Trainers inside the Seashore House on the beach and talk to the owner
  • Powder Jar: Talk to the woman in the Contest Hall near the Berry Blender
  • Poké Ball: Talk to the Sailor NPC standing outside the Oceanic Museum before entering
  • TM46 (Thief): Talk to the Team Aqua Grunt standing on the ground floor of the Oceanic Museum

Secrets

  • Stardust | Hidden in the sand in the far upper-right corner of the beach; interact with the sand tile directly in the corner | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Slateport Market Dolls Vendor: Sells Azurill Doll, Marill Doll, and Skitty Doll for Secret Bases
  • Slateport Market Incense Vendor: Sells Sea Incense and Lax Incense
  • Slateport Market TM Vendor: Sells TM10 (Hidden Power) and TM43 (Secret Power)
  • Seashore House Owner: Sells individual bottles of Soda Pop for 300 Poké Dollars after you beat all Trainers inside

Tips

  • The Oceanic Museum costs 50 Poké Dollars to enter, but if you carry 0 money, the receptionist lets you in for free.
  • Don't forget to talk to the Team Aqua Grunt on the first floor of the museum before defeating the grunts upstairs, or you'll miss getting TM46 (Thief) easily.
