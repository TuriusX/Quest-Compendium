Route 110

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Mauville

Route 110 connects Slateport City to Mauville City and serves as a major north-south corridor. The Seaside Cycling Road runs overhead, while the lower path winds through tall grass and past the Trick House. Midway through the route, you encounter your rival for a battle before being allowed to proceed to Mauville.

Items

  • Dire Hit: On the ground along the lower path near the southern section
  • Elixir: On the ground in the tall grass section south of the rival encounter
  • Itemfinder: Given by your rival May or Brendan after defeating them on the route
  • Nanab Berry: Growing on soft soil patches north of the Trick House

Secrets

  • Hidden Poké Ball | Hidden in a cuttable tree alcove along the lower path | missable: no
  • Hidden Revive | Hidden on an empty tile on the path just south of Mauville City | missable: no

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Rival Brendan or MayElectric, Grass, or Fire (depending on starter)Battles you with a starter Pokémon, a Wingull or Pelipper, and a Shroomish, Lombre, or Numel

Shops and people

  • Trick Master: Offers the Trick House challenge puzzles that unlock as you earn more Gym Badges

Tips

  • You cannot enter the elevated Seaside Cycling Road until you obtain the Mach Bike or Acro Bike in Mauville City.
  • Be prepared to heal before passing under the Cycling Road midway through the route, as the rival will ambush you unexpectedly.
