Route 116

Pokémon Emerald guide · Searching for the Devon researcher

Route 116 extends east from Rustboro City towards Rusturf Tunnel. A Devon researcher informs the player that a Team Aqua Grunt stole his Devon Goods and fled into the tunnel. The player must battle their way east past various trainers to enter Rusturf Tunnel and retrieve the stolen goods.

Items

  • Repel: On the path near the entrance from Rustboro City
  • X Special: In the grass patch south of the main path
  • Ether: Behind a Cut tree on the northern ledge
  • Potion: Near the eastern entrance leading into Rusturf Tunnel

Secrets

  • Hidden Super Potion | Located behind the Cut tree in the northern section; interact with the bare spot to find it
  • Hidden Black Glasses | Hidden in the elevated rocky section accessible after exiting Rusturf Tunnel later

Tips

  • Teach the HM Cut to a Pokémon to access the northern ledges for extra items and wild Pokémon encounters.
  • You can catch Taillow or Nincada in the tall grass here, which are useful additions to your early-game roster.
