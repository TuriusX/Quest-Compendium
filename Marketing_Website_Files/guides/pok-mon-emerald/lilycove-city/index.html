Lilycove City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Shopping and Team Aqua hideout

Lilycove City is a major coastal hub housing the massive Lilycove Department Store, the Move Deleter, and the Lilycove Museum. Your rival challenges you to a battle in front of the department store before you can enter Team Aqua's hideout. Clearing the Team Aqua Hideout on the eastern cove is required to disperse the Wailmer blocking the water route toward Mossdeep City.

Items

  • HM02 (Fly): Awarded by your rival after defeating them outside the Lilycove Department Store
  • Master Ball MISSABLE: Deep inside the Team Aqua Hideout, in a room filled with four item balls, two of which are disguised as Pokémon
  • Max Elixir MISSABLE: Team Aqua Hideout, found within the teleport puzzle rooms
  • Nest Ball MISSABLE: Team Aqua Hideout, found within the teleport puzzle rooms
  • Rare Candy MISSABLE: Team Aqua Hideout, found within the maze of teleporters
  • TM44 (Rest): Given by a man inside a house on the eastern cliffside of town
  • Berry: Given daily by an old gentleman standing near the Move Deleter's house

Secrets

  • Hidden Heart Scale | Beach shoreline on the eastern edge of the city, hidden in the sand | interact with the empty sand tile
  • Hidden PP Up | Team Aqua Hideout, hidden on a machine/pod in the submarine dock area | interact with the tile

Shops and people

  • Lilycove Department Store 1F: Lottery Corner ticket once per day
  • Lilycove Department Store 2F: General trainer goods including Poké Balls, Potions, and Escape Ropes
  • Lilycove Department Store 3F: Stat-boosting battle items like X Attack and permanent vitamins like Protein, Iron, and Carbos
  • Lilycove Department Store 4F: Powerful offensive and defensive TMs such as Fire Blast, Blizzard, Thunder, and Hyper Beam
  • Lilycove Department Store 5F: Secret Base decorations, mats, posters, and dolls
  • Lilycove Department Store Rooftop: Vending machines selling Fresh Water, Soda Pop, and Lemonade, as well as occasional rooftop sales
  • Move Deleter: Forgets any known move for a Pokémon, including HM moves, free of charge

Tips

  • You must infiltrate and clear the Team Aqua Hideout before the Wailmer pod blocking Route 124 will move away.
  • Be sure to collect the Master Ball in the hideout before finishing the Aqua plotline there, as the hideout becomes inaccessible later in the game.
