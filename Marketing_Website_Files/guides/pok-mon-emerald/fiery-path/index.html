Fiery Path

Pokémon Emerald guide · Crossing to Route 113

Fiery Path is an underground cave tunnel connecting the southern and northern sections of Route 112. It serves as an alternative route to bypass Mt. Chimney while the Cable Car is blocked by Team Magma. To explore the deeper parts containing boulder puzzles and extra items, you must return later with HM04 Strength.

Items

  • Fire Stone: Northwest corner of the cave beyond the Strength boulders
  • TM06 (Toxic): At the end of the Strength puzzle path in the northwest

Tips

  • On your first trip through, you cannot push the boulders blocking the side path, so simply head straight north to exit toward Route 113.
  • Bring a Pokémon that knows Strength once you have the Heat Badge to access the Fire Stone and TM06.
