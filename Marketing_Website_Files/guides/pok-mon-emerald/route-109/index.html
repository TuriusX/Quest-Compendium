Route 109

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Slateport

Route 109 is a coastal water route and beach located directly south of Slateport City. Players typically arrive here via Mr. Briney's boat from Dewford Town before gaining access to Surf. It features several beach-going trainers to battle and the Seashore House, an indoor venue offering battles and rewards.

Items

  • Soft Sand: Given by a Tuber on the beach near the Seashore House
  • PP Up: In the western sandbar area, accessible around the water's edge

Secrets

  • Ether | Hidden in the sand in the southern section of the beach; use the Itemfinder or check isolated sand tiles | missable: no
  • Heart Scale | Hidden in an isolated sand tile near the water's edge on the beach | missable: no
  • Revive | Hidden in the sand near the umbrellas on the beach | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Seashore House Owner: Rewards the player with a 6-pack of Soda Pop after defeating all trainers inside the Seashore House
  • Seashore House Vending Counter: Sells individual bottles of Soda Pop to restore 60 HP

Tips

  • Defeat all three trainers inside the Seashore House to receive free Soda Pops, which are among the most cost-effective healing items in the early game.
  • Return here after obtaining the HM for Surf to battle the Swimmers stationed in the deep water south of the beach.
