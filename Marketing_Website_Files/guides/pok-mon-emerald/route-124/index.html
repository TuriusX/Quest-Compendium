Route 124

Pokémon Emerald guide · Diving and surfing to Mossdeep

Route 124 is a sprawling ocean route connecting Lilycove City directly to Mossdeep City. It features vast deep-water trenches that require HM08 Dive to fully explore, navigate underwater mazes, and locate sunken items. While traveling this route, trainers can also visit the Diving Treasure Hunter's house located on a small island.

Items

  • Red Shard: Underwater in the southern trench
  • Blue Shard: Underwater floor in a trench area
  • Yellow Shard: Underwater floor in the route trenches
  • Green Shard: Underwater floor in the route trenches

Secrets

  • Hidden Shards underwater | Use the Itemfinder or press the interact button on empty rocky indentations and patches while diving

Shops and people

  • Diving Treasure Hunter: Trades evolutionary stones (Fire Stone, Water Stone, Thunder Stone, and Leaf Stone) in exchange for Red, Blue, Yellow, and Green Shards

Tips

  • Bring a Pokémon with both Surf and Dive to fully access all items and trainer battles across the surface and seabed.
  • Catch a Relicanth in the underwater seaweed patches here, as it is a mandatory requirement alongside Wailord to open the Sealed Chamber later.
