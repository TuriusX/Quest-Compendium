Rusturf Tunnel

Pokémon Emerald guide · Rescuing Peeko

Rusturf Tunnel connects Rustboro City and Verdanturf Town, but it is initially blocked by breakable rocks. During your first visit from the Rustboro side, you enter to rescue Mr. Briney's Wingull, Peeko, from a Team Magma Grunt. Defeating the grunt recovers both the stolen Peeko and the Devon Goods, clearing the way for your main quest progression.

Items

  • Poké Ball: Lying on the ground near the Rustboro entrance before the rocky blockage
  • Devon Goods: Recovered from the Team Aqua Grunt upon defeating him

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Team Aqua GruntFighting, Ground, Steel, PsychicUses a single Level 11 Poochyena

Tips

  • Bringing a Fighting-type Pokémon like Makuhita or a Pokémon that knows Rock Smash makes quick work of the Grunt's Poochyena.
  • Return to Rustboro City immediately after defeating the grunt to receive the Great Ball from the Devon Researcher and the Letter and PokéNav from Mr. Stone.
