Route 119

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Fortree

Route 119 is a long, rainy route featuring tall grass, bridges, and the Weather Institute. You must defeat Team Magma inside the Weather Institute to rescue the scientists and obtain Castform. After clearing the building, you will battle your rival just before reaching Fortree City to earn the HM for Fly.

Items

  • Castform: Received as a gift from the scientist inside the Weather Institute after clearing Team Magma
  • HM02 (Fly): Awarded by your rival after defeating them on the bridge past the Weather Institute
  • Rare Candy: In the tall grass section on the southern half of the route, accessible near the riverbank
  • Elixir: Found on the ground in the tall grass area south of the Weather Institute
  • Hyper Potion: Located on the ground behind the Weather Institute
  • Leaf Stone: Located across the river, reachable using Surf on the northern section of the water

Secrets

  • Feebas can be caught by fishing with any rod on exactly six randomly determined water tiles along the river; these tiles change whenever the trendy phrase in Dewford Town is altered.
  • Hidden Ultra Ball located in a rock near the riverbank in the southern portion of the route.

Shops and people

Tips

  • The very tall grass on Route 119 cannot be traversed while riding a Bicycle, so you must travel through it on foot.
  • You can rest in the bed on the first floor of the Weather Institute to completely heal your party at any time.
  • Acro Bike rails and waterfalls along the river require specific gear or later HMs, meaning you will need to backtrack later to explore every corner of the route.
