Route 121

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Lilycove

Route 121 connects Route 120 directly to Lilycove City and provides southern water access to Mt. Pyre. It features the Hoenn Safari Zone entrance, numerous wild grass patches, and several Trainer battles. You will also witness Team Magma or Team Aqua members moving east toward Mt. Pyre as you travel through.

Items

  • Zinc: On the ground past a Cut tree near the western entrance
  • Revive: Located in the grassy area south of the Safari Zone building
  • Carbos: In the northeastern section along the path toward Lilycove City
  • Aspear Berry (x2): Picked from the soft soil patch south of the Safari Zone
  • Rawst Berry (x2): Picked from the soft soil patch south of the Safari Zone
  • Chesto Berry (x4): Picked from the soft soil patch south of the Safari Zone
  • Nanab Berry (x2): Picked from the soft soil patch south of the Safari Zone

Secrets

  • Hidden Full Heal | Hidden in a rock located along the southern path leading to the water | use the Itemfinder or inspect the isolated rock
  • Hidden Max Revive | Hidden in the grass patch directly east of the Safari Zone entrance | use the Itemfinder to pinpoint the exact tile in the grass
  • Hidden Nugget | Hidden in the narrow dead-end path behind the Cut trees near the west side | inspect the wall at the end of the cleared path

  • Safari Zone Entrance Attendant: Admission into the Safari Zone for 500 Pokédollars and 30 Safari Balls

Tips

  • You must obtain the Pokéblock Case from the Contest Hall in Slateport City before the attendant will let you enter the Safari Zone.
  • Carry a Pokémon with Cut to reach the northern side paths containing helpful items and to bypass portions of tall grass.
