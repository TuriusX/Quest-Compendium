Fortree City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Sixth gym challenge

Fortree City is a treetop settlement where treehouses are connected by rope bridges. The path to the local Pokémon Gym is initially blocked by an invisible obstacle that requires you to obtain the Devon Scope from Steven on Route 120. Once the invisible Kecleon is revealed and dealt with, you can challenge Gym Leader Winona for the Feather Badge.

Items

  • TM10 (Hidden Power): Given by an elderly woman in the northernmost treehouse if you answer her right-hand guessing game correctly three times
  • Mental Herb: Given by a girl in the house next to the Pokémon Center who loves Wingull
  • Feather Badge: Awarded by Gym Leader Winona upon defeating her
  • TM40 (Aerial Ace): Awarded by Gym Leader Winona upon defeating her

Secrets

  • Invisible Kecleon blocking the Gym | Use the Devon Scope on the space directly south of the Fortree Gym to reveal a wild Kecleon, clearing the path whether you catch, defeat, or flee from it

  • Secret Base Furniture Shop (West House): Sells various desks and chairs for Secret Bases
  • Secret Base Furniture Shop (East House): Sells various mats and posters for Secret Bases

Tips

  • Equip Ice-type and Electric-type moves before challenging the Gym, as all of Winona's Flying-type Pokémon are vulnerable to them, with several taking 4x damage from Ice or Electric.
