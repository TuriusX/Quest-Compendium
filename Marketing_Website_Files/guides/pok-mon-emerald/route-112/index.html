Route 112

Pokémon Emerald guide · Accessing the cable car

Route 112 connects Route 111 with Mt. Chimney and the Jagged Pass, as well as the fiery detour path Fiery Path. On your first visit, access to the Mt. Chimney Cable Car is blocked by Team Magma grunts, requiring you to navigate through the Fiery Path to reach Fallarbor Town. You can return later after completing events in Fallarbor and Meteor Falls to ride the Cable Car up to the summit of Mt. Chimney.

Items

  • Rawst Berry: Berry patch located on the western side of the route near the entrance to Fiery Path
  • Pecha Berry: Berry patch located on the western side of the route near the entrance to Fiery Path
  • Nugget: On a ledge accessed by descending the rocks from the Cable Car station

Tips

  • If you want to ride the Cable Car on your first visit, you will find it blocked by two Team Magma grunts; take the Fiery Path tunnel just to the left instead.
  • You can use the Mach Bike or Acro Bike to hop or ride down the ledges south of the Cable Car station for a quick shortcut back to Route 111.
