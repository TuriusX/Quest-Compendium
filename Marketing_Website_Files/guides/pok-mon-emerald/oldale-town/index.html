Oldale Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · First stop after leaving home

Oldale Town is the first town visited after leaving Littleroot Town via Route 101. Players must pass through heading north to Route 103 to find and battle their rival, May or Brendan. Afterward, returning here allows progression west onto Route 102 toward Petalburg City.

Items

  • Potion: Received from the Mart clerk standing near the town entrance after speaking to him

Shops and people

  • Pokémon Center: Heals your party Pokémon completely for free
  • Poké Mart: Poké Ball, Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal

Tips

  • The path leading west to Route 102 is initially blocked by a person sketching footprints; you must defeat your rival on Route 103 and report back to Professor Birch before this path opens.
  • Be sure to heal your Pokémon at the Pokémon Center before heading north to face your rival.
