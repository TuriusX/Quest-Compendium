Mauville City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Third gym challenge

Mauville City is a major crossroads in central Hoenn featuring the region's third Pokémon Gym, a Bike Shop, and the Mauville Game Corner. To challenge the Gym Leader, you must first defeat your rival Wally outside the Gym doors. Defeating Wattson earns you the Dynamo Badge, unlocks the use of Rock Smash outside of battle, and opens up travel north toward Fallarbor Town or east toward Fortree City.

Items

  • Mach Bike or Acro Bike: Rydel's Cycles, given by Rydel when speaking to him
  • HM06 (Rock Smash): House southeast of the Gym, given by the Rock Smash Guy
  • Coin Case: House next to the Poké Mart, traded by a woman in exchange for a Harbor Mail
  • Basement Key: Given by Wattson in the center of town after gaining Surf and returning later
  • TM24 (Thunderbolt): Given by Wattson as a reward for shutting down the generator in New Mauville
  • TM34 (Shock Wave): Reward from Gym Leader Wattson after defeating him

Secrets

  • Hidden X Speed | Located in the narrow path just north of the Mauville Gym sign | interact directly with the tile to find it

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Gym Leader WattsonGroundUses Electric-type Pokémon: Voltorb (Lv. 20), Electrike (Lv. 20), Magneton (Lv. 22), and Manectric (Lv. 24)

Shops and people

  • Poké Mart: Poké Ball, Great Ball, Potion, Super Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal, Awakening, X Speed, X Attack, X Defend, Dire Hit, Guard Spec.
  • Mauville Game Corner: Sells Coins, TMs (TM32 Double Team, TM29 Psychic, TM35 Flamethrower, TM24 Thunderbolt, TM13 Ice Beam), and decorative Dolls
  • Rydel's Cycles: Allows you to freely exchange between the Mach Bike and the Acro Bike at any time

Tips

  • Ground-type Pokémon such as Geodude or Marshtomp are immune to Electric attacks and make Wattson's Gym significantly easier.
  • You can buy the Harbor Mail required for the Coin Case trade at the Slateport City Poké Mart.
