Sootopolis City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Eighth gym challenge

Sootopolis City is located inside a massive volcanic crater reachable only by surfing and diving through an underwater cavern. During the climax of Pokémon Emerald's story, Groudon and Kyogre battle in the center of the city, requiring you to summon Rayquaza from the Sky Pillar to quell them. Once the weather crisis is resolved, you can challenge the local Gym Leader, Juan, to earn your final badge.

Items

  • HM07 Waterfall: Inside the Cave of Origin entrance area
  • TM31 Brick Break: Inside the northwest house, received from a Black Belt
  • TM03 Water Pulse: Given by Gym Leader Juan after defeating him
  • Rain Badge: Given by Gym Leader Juan upon his defeat
  • Wailmer Doll: Given by a woman inside a house on the eastern side of the city

Tips

  • The puzzle in the Sootopolis Gym requires you to walk on every ice tile once and only once to reach the next platform; stepping on a tile twice breaks it and drops you to the lower floor.
  • Bring Pokémon with Grass-type moves or high physical offense to deal with Gym Leader Juan's Water-type team, especially against Whiscash.
