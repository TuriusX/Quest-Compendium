Fallarbor Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · Visiting the town

Fallarbor Town is a small settlement covered in volcanic ash located in the northwest corner of the Hoenn region. Trainers visit this town to access Route 114, discover the whereabouts of Professor Cozmo, and use the services of the Move Tutor. It serves as a vital resting stop between the ash-strewn Route 113 and the path leading to Meteor Falls.

Items

  • Nugget: Given by Professor Cozmo in his house after rescuing him and returning the Meteorite from Mt. Chimney

Secrets

  • Hidden Nugget | Inspect the crater in the western section of the town | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Poké Mart: Poké Ball, Great Ball, Super Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal, Awakening, Fire Stone, Escape Rope, Super Repel
  • Move Tutor: Teaches a Pokémon a previously learned move in exchange for one Heart Scale

Tips

  • Save any Heart Scales you find across Hoenn so you can use the Move Tutor located in the house on the western edge of town.
  • Be sure to talk to Lanette inside the Pokémon Center to unlock her special PC storage system options before heading to her house on Route 114.
