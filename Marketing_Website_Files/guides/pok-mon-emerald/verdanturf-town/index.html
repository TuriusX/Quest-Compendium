Verdanturf Town

Pokémon Emerald guide · Visiting the town

Verdanturf Town is a peaceful, pollen-rich town nestled in central Hoenn. Players typically arrive here through Route 117 to tackle the Battle Tent and explore Rusturf Tunnel to clear the rock blockage. It also serves as the residence of the Pokémon Contest hall's replacement in Emerald, offering Normal Rank Battle Tent challenges.

Items

  • Contest Pass: Obtained inside the Battle Tent from the attendant
  • Mach Bike or Acro Bike: Rydel's Cycles on Route 110 via Verdanturf route access, but in town Wanda gives access to HM04/HM06 progress

Shops and people

  • Poké Mart: Poké Ball, Great Ball, Potion, Super Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal, Awakening, Repel, Fluffy Tail, Nest Ball
  • Battle Tent: Normal-tier challenge where you choose three rental Pokémon to battle through three consecutive matches
  • Move Deleter's House (Route 115 / Lilycove note - none here) / Wanda's House: Wanda's boyfriend is stuck in Rusturf Tunnel; talk to them to follow up on the tunnel quest

Tips

  • Completing the boulder-smashing task in the adjacent Rusturf Tunnel unlocks the HM04 Strength reward from Wanda's boyfriend and opens a quick shortcut back to Rustboro City.
  • You must obtain the Contest Pass from the reception desk at the Battle Tent in this town before you can enter any Pokémon Contests across the Hoenn region.
