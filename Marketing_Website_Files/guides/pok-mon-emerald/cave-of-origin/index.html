Cave of Origin

Pokémon Emerald guide · Awakening the legendary Pokémon

The Cave of Origin is located inside Sootopolis City and serves as a key story location during the weather crisis caused by Kyogre and Groudon. In Pokémon Emerald, you navigate deep into the cave to meet Wallace, who asks where Rayquaza might be located. Wallace then leaves for Sky Pillar once you provide the correct answer, clearing the path to resolve the legendary conflict.

Items

  • HM07 (Waterfall): Ground floor inside the deepest room (B4F), lying on the floor

Shops and people

  • Wallace: Offers dialogue to advance the main story by answering where Rayquaza is located

Tips

  • In Pokémon Emerald, you do not battle Kyogre or Groudon inside this cave; your only objective is speaking to Wallace and picking up HM07 Waterfall.
  • Bring Repels to navigate the cave quickly without constantly fighting wild encounters.
