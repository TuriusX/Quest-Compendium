Route 104

Pokémon Emerald guide · Traveling to Rustboro

Route 104 is a coastal route divided into southern and northern sections separated by Petalburg Woods. When heading to Rustboro City, trainers pass through the southern beach, journey through the forest, and emerge onto the northern section featuring the Pretty Petal Flower Shop. The route contains several early-game trainer battles, berry patches, and Mr. Briney's cottage.

Items

  • Poké Ball: On the beach in the southern section
  • Oran Berry: Picked from soft soil patches outside the Pretty Petal Flower Shop
  • Pecha Berry: Picked from soft soil patches outside the Pretty Petal Flower Shop
  • Cheri Berry: Picked from soft soil patches in the southern section
  • Potion: Near the entrance to Petalburg Woods in the northern section

Secrets

  • Hidden Heart Scale | In the sand on the beach in the southern section, found by checking suspicious empty tiles near the water
  • Hidden Super Potion | Tucked in the grassy area on the northern side, found by interacting with the empty tile at the dead end

Tips

  • Battle Rich Boy Winston and Lady Cindy whenever possible, as they yield exceptionally high prize money for this early stage of the game.
  • You cannot cross the water between the northern and southern halves directly until you obtain Surf later in the game.
