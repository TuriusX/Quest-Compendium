Rustboro City

Pokémon Emerald guide · First gym challenge

Rustboro City is a major metropolis that serves as the location of the player's first Gym challenge against Roxanne. Players must also interact with the Devon Corporation and recover stolen Devon Goods from Team Aqua to proceed. After earning the badge, the player unlocks the ability to use Cut in the overworld.

Items

  • HM01 Cut: Inside the Cutter's House to the left of the Pokémon Center, given by the man inside
  • Quick Claw: Given by a teacher inside the Pokémon Trainer's School
  • Stone Badge: Awarded by Gym Leader Roxanne upon defeat
  • TM39 Rock Tomb: Awarded by Gym Leader Roxanne upon defeat
  • PokéNav: Received on the top floor of Devon Corporation from Mr. Stone after recovering the Devon Goods

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
GeodudeWater, Grass, Fighting, GroundRoxanne uses two Geodudes; they rely on Defense Curl and Rock Throw
NosepassWater, Grass, Fighting, GroundRoxanne's ace Pokémon; high physical Defense and can use Rock Tomb to lower Speed

Shops and people

  • Rustboro Poké Mart: Poké Ball, Potion, Super Potion, Antidote, Parlyz Heal, Escape Rope, Repel, X Speed, X Attack, X Defend
  • In-game Trade (Boy in house next to Gym): Trades a Makuhita for a Slakoth

Tips

  • Starting with Treecko or Mudkip makes Roxanne's Rock-type team simple, but Torchic users should evolve it into Combusken to learn Double Kick or catch a Marill, Lotad, or Wingull.
