Space Center

Pokémon Emerald guide · Defending against Team Magma

Team Magma has invaded the Mossdeep Space Center to steal rocket fuel for their plans at Mt. Chimney. You must fight your way up through grunts on the ground floor to reach the upper level. Once on the second floor, you team up with Steven Stone in a multi battle to defeat Magma Admin Tabitha and Magma Leader Maxie.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Team Magma GruntWater, Ground, FightingMultiple grunts appear on the first and second floors using Pokémon like Mightyena, Golbat, and Numel.
Magma Admin Tabitha and Magma Leader MaxieWater, Ground, FightingFought in a 2v2 Tag Battle alongside Steven Stone; their team consists of Camerupt, Mightyena, and Crobat.

Shops and people

  • Steven Stone: Teams up with you using Metang, Skarmory, and Aggron to defeat Maxie and Tabitha

Tips

  • In the multi battle with Steven, avoid using wide-area moves like Earthquake or Surf unless your partner's active Pokémon is immune, as they will hit Steven's Pokémon.
  • Bring strong Water-type or Ground-type Pokémon to counter the multiple Fire- and Rock-type Pokémon fielded by Team Magma.
  • Visit Steven's house in north-west Mossdeep City after saving the Space Center to receive the HM08 Dive from him.
