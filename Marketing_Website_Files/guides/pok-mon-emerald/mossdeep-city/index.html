Mossdeep City

Pokémon Emerald guide · Seventh gym challenge

Mossdeep City is an island city hosting Hoenn's seventh Gym, led by the psychic twins Tate and Liza. Players must navigate the Gym's warp/tile puzzles, defeat Team Magma at the Mossdeep Space Center alongside Steven, and obtain the Mind Badge. After resolving the Team Magma conflict, Steven gives the player the HM for Dive in his house.

Items

  • Super Rod: Inside the fisherman's house on the cliff on the north side of the city
  • HM08 (Dive): Inside Steven's house from Steven after defeating Team Magma at the Space Center
  • King's Rock: Given by a boy near the Space Center
  • Sun Stone: Inside the Mossdeep Space Center from a Sailor on the first floor
  • Net Ball: Inside a house in the northwest, given by a boy
  • TM04 (Calm Mind): Awarded by Gym Leaders Tate and Liza after defeating them

Secrets

  • Hidden Revive located on the rock just outside the Mossdeep Gym entrance by interacting with it.

Shops and people

  • Mossdeep Poké Mart: Sells Ultra Balls, Net Balls, Dive Balls, Hyper Potions, Max Potions, Full Heals, Revives, and Max Repels

Tips

  • The Mossdeep Gym battle is a Double Battle; bring Pokémon with Dark-, Water-, or Ghost-type attacks and consider using Surf to hit both foes simultaneously.
  • Be aware that all four of Tate and Liza's Pokémon have the Levitate ability, making Ground-type attacks like Earthquake ineffective against them.
