Mt. Pyre

Pokémon Emerald guide · Retrieving the Orb
← All areas in this guide

Mt. Pyre is a sacred burial mountain on Route 122 that players visit to halt Team Aqua's raid on the summit. The player navigates an indoor monument section filled with trainers and wild Ghost-type Pokémon to reach the foggy exterior paths leading to the peak. At the summit, after fighting through Team Aqua grunts and encountering Archie, the player receives a vital Orb from the elderly shrine guardians to help control the legendary Pokémon.

Tips
  • Equip a Lead Pokémon with a Cleanse Tag or use Repels if you want to avoid constant random encounters with wild Ghost-type Pokémon inside the tower.
  • Ghost-type attacks like Shadow Ball operate as Physical attacks in Generation III, making them ideal for Pokémon with high Attack stats rather than Special Attack.
