Route 123

Pokémon Emerald guide · Exploring south of Mt. Pyre

Route 123 is a long horizontal path connecting Route 118 in the west to Route 122 and Mt. Pyre in the east. The eastern side features one-way ledges leading downward, while the western side is famous for the Berry Master's house and an extensive berry garden. Navigating the route allows players to collect large quantities of rare berries and battle numerous trainers.

Items

  • Ultra Ball: Found on the ground on a ledge in the eastern section of the route
  • Elixir: Found on the ground along the pathway
  • Calcium: Found on the ground behind a cuttable tree
  • Rare Candy: Found on the ground past a Cut tree on the western portion

Secrets

  • Hidden Revive | Use the Itemfinder near the ledges on the eastern side | missable: no
  • Hidden PP Up | Use the Itemfinder in an empty patch near the Berry Master's garden | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Berry Master: Gives away two random berries once per day from a pool of rare varieties
  • Berry Master's Wife: Gives away a specific berry once per day if told certain special phrases, or a common berry otherwise

Tips

  • Bring a Pokémon that knows Cut, as several item balls and shortcut paths are blocked by cuttable trees.
  • Because the eastern section consists of one-way ledges descending from Mt. Pyre, you must re-enter from Route 122 if you want to explore different ledge tiers that you missed.
