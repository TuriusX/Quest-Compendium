Shoal Cave

Pokémon Emerald guide · Exploring near Mossdeep

Shoal Cave is an icy coastal cavern located north of Mossdeep City that shifts between high tide and low tide based on the internal real-time clock. Players explore the flooded chambers by Surfing during high tide and navigate frozen lower depths during low tide. The primary goal is collecting Shoal Salts and Shoal Shells across both tide phases to craft the Shell Bell, while also obtaining the NeverMeltIce and TM07 (Hail).

Items

  • Shoal Salt (x4): Scattered in salty deposits around the cave, accessible only during low tide
  • Shoal Shell (x4): Scattered in shell beds throughout the cave water, accessible only during high tide
  • Rare Candy: In the high-tide area in the room accessed by Surfing north
  • Big Pearl: On the ground in the high-tide cave area
  • Ice Heal: Found on the ground in the lower frozen room during low tide
  • NeverMeltIce: In the deep ice room at the bottom of the cave during low tide
  • TM07 (Hail): Found in the lowest frozen room accessed via a ladder during low tide
  • Focus Band: Given by a Black Belt NPC in the lower cave rooms during low tide

Secrets

  • Hidden ice slide puzzle in the lowest frozen room; slide across the patches of ice in the correct directions to reach NeverMeltIce and TM07.

Tips

  • The tide changes every six hours in real time (High tide: 9:00–14:59 and 21:00–2:59; Low tide: 3:00–8:59 and 15:00–20:59), requiring visits at two different times of day to gather all materials.
  • Bring Pokémon with Surf and Strength, as both HM moves are required to navigate the paths and move boulders during low tide.
  • If the internal cartridge battery has run dry, the tide will be frozen permanently at whichever state the game saved in, preventing access to the other tide's items.
