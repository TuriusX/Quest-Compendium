Dollet

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · The SeeD field exam mission

Squall, Zell, and Seifer are deployed by Balamb Garden to assist the Dollet Dukedom against the invading Galbadia Republic army. You advance through town to the comm tower, where you confront Galbadian forces, acquire the GF Siren, and flee back to the beach from an autonomous spider-tank. Your mission performance determines your starting SeeD rank and starting salary.

Items

  • The Occult Fan I: Obtained in the Dollet pub during later visits, but during the exam you cannot access it

Secrets

  • Draw the GF Siren from Elvoret during the comm tower roof battle; if missed here, she cannot be drawn again until the final dungeon of the game.

Enemies

Tips

  • Do not let Seifer get knocked out before the comm tower, as keeping him alive maintains your mission conduct score.
  • When escaping X-ATM092, avoid entering combat unnecessary times to keep your escape score high, and never run left into the screen blind.
  • While the game instructs you to run from X-ATM092, you can actually destroy it repeatedly with Lightning-junctioned attacks for AP and a bonus item, though running to the beach triggers a special cutscene.
