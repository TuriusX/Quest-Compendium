Trabia Garden (Trabia Garden: Squall visits the ruins of his childhood home)

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit to find items

Squall returns to the devastated Trabia Garden to reflect on his memories and search for valuable supplies left behind among the debris. The facility is severely damaged from Galbadia's missile strike, and the surviving students are scattered around the grounds attempting repairs. Exploring the various screens allows the party to uncover hidden magazines, junction items, and unlisted draw points.

Items

  • Weapons Monthly August Issue: On the ground near the center gargoyle statue at the main plaza fountain
  • Timber Maniacs: On the floor of the graveyard screen on the right side

Secrets

  • Hidden Aura draw point | Inspect the rock near the statue in the courtyard pond | interact while facing the center of the water
  • Hidden Life-stream / Life draw point | Located in the graveyard area near the center gravestones | search around the bottom-left area of the cemetery
  • Hidden Thundaga draw point | Found near the cracked computer console inside the damaged classroom | interact with the right side of the room

Tips

  • Trabia uses distinctive, fast-paced local Triple Triad rules including Plus and Random, so save outside before challenging any student to avoid spreading unfavorable rules.
