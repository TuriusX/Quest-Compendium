Esthar - Great Salt Lake

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Squall travels toward Esthar

Squall and his party traverse this massive crystalline lake bed while carrying Rinoa toward the secluded, technologically advanced nation of Esthar. The journey involves navigating across large skeleton remains and cliffside paths until discovering a hidden optical camouflage barrier. After defeating the undead boss guarding the exit, the party gains access to the futuristic city of Esthar.

Items

  • Meteor Draw Point MISSABLE: located on the skeleton path near the start of the lake
  • Thundaga Draw Point MISSABLE: on the cliffside path just past the massive ribcage

Secrets

  • Hidden passage entrance | Walk all the way to the right side of the screen at the deep cliff area until bumping into an invisible wall, then press the confirm button to reveal a hidden cloaked platform

Tips

  • Equip the Zombie status or Holy magic to your elemental defenses to mitigate the heavy undead damage in this area.
  • Since the boss Abadon is undead, using the Revive ability or tossing an X-Potion or Phoenix Down at it will deal fatal or massive damage immediately.
