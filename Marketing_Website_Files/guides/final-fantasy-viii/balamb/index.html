Balamb

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Boarding the train to Timber

Squall, Zell, and Selphie head to Balamb Town to catch the train for their first official SeeD mission to Timber. Before boarding, you can explore the town, visit Zell's house, challenge card players, and stock up on supplies. Buying a train ticket at the station for 3,000 Gil triggers a cutscene and advances the story.

Items

  • Timber Maniacs issue: Inside the Balamb Hotel guest room on the bedside table

Secrets

  • Card Queen can be challenged near the train station entrance to begin her multi-region questline and modify local card rules
  • Zell's mother in her living room will play Zell's own character card during Triple Triad matches

Shops and people

  • Balamb Shop: Potion, Phoenix Down, Antidote, Eye Drops, Soft
  • Junk Shop: Weapon remodeling services
  • Balamb Station Ticket Conductor: Train ticket to Timber for 3,000 Gil

Tips

  • Challenge Zell's mother to Triple Triad to win the unique Zell Card before leaving the continent.
  • Resting at the Balamb Hotel costs 100 Gil and is required to access the hotel room where the Timber Maniacs magazine is located.
