Shumi Village

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit for the Shumi sidequest

Shumi Village is a secluded underground settlement located on Trabia's northern continent, accessible via an elevator inside a dome. Visiting here allows Squall and his party to undertake a multi-part sidequest assisting the Shumi Sculptor in carving a statue of Laguna. Completing the tasks around the village reveals lore about the Shumi life cycle and rewards the player with valuable items, including a Phoenix Pinion.

Items

  • Phoenix Pinion: Received from the Elder after completing the statue-building questline
  • Status Guard: Received from the Master Fisherman's assistant after completing the extended village tasks

Secrets

  • Ultima Draw Point | Located near the entrance of the village, accessible by paying 5,000 Gil to the Shumi guarding it (can be drawn repeatedly after enough steps)
  • Draw Point (Blizzaga) | Hidden near the pond inside the village

Shops and people

  • Shumi Hotel: 100 Gil to rest and fully restore HP
  • Shumi Village Shop: Sells basic recovery items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Softs

Tips

  • Inspect the Timber Maniacs magazine inside the Shumi Artisan's house if you are collecting them, but be aware reading it can affect Laguna's scenes.
  • Finding the five stones for the sculptor requires searching specific spots across the village: the blue stone behind the statue, the wind stone by the hotel, the life stone near the elder's house tree, the shadow stone under the elevator, and the water stone near the pond.
