Fire Cavern

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · The SeeD field exam

The Fire Cavern serves as a preliminary trial for Squall's SeeD exam, located east of Balamb Garden. Accompanied by Quistis, Squall must navigate the fiery caves within an allotted time limit to reach the inner sanctum. Defeating the boss at the end rewards the player with the Guardian Force Ifrit.

Items

  • Ifrit Card: Defeat the boss Ifrit at the end of the cavern

Tips

  • Choosing the 10-minute timer at the entrance awards the highest score for the Judgement category of the SeeD exam, but requires quickly moving through the dungeon without lingering.
  • Junction the Shiva Guardian Force to Squall or Quistis and equip Ice magic to their Elemental Attack to deal heavy damage to every enemy in this dungeon.
  • Stock up on Blizzard magic from the draw point outside the cavern or from Buel enemies inside to prepare for the boss fight against Ifrit.
  • Scan Ifrit during the battle to unlock unique dialogue interactions between him and the party.
