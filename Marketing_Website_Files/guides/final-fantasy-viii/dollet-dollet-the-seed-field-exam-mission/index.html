Dollet (Dollet: The SeeD field exam mission)

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit to play cards

During the SeeD exam mission in Dollet, Squall and his squad are tasked with clearing Galbadian soldiers and securing the town before heading to the Communications Tower. Although on an active mission, players have limited opportunities to interact with locals and play Triple Triad. Exploring side areas or wasting too much time can impact the final SeeD exam score.

Secrets

  • Checking the fountain in the town square repeatedly can reward small items or trigger brief NPC dialogues.
  • Talking multiple times to a soldier cornered by an Anacondaur near the bridge allows you to intervene, altering mission points.

Tips

  • Avoid lingering or wandering too far off the mission path, as running down the clock or taking detours lowers your SeeD Conduct and Attitude rank.
  • Stock up on offensive magic by drawing Cure, Thunder, and Fire from early Galbadian soldiers before reaching the radio tower.
