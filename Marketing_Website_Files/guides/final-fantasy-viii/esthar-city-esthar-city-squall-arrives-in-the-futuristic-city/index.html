Esthar - City (Esthar - City: Squall arrives in the futuristic city)

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit to shop and play cards

Squall and his party arrive in the technologically advanced city of Esthar and are brought to the presidential palace for an audience with Odine. Before proceeding to the laboratory or leaving the city, you can freely explore the complex elevated pathways, ride the transport platforms, and browse the unique shopping mall. This visit gives you the chance to unlock essential city shops, gather regional magazines, and play Triple Triad with the locals under the Lunar card rules.

Items

  • Combat King 004 MISSABLE: Presidential Palace, lying on the floor in the hallway outside Odine's office
  • Occult Fan IV MISSABLE: Presidential Palace, talk to the Presidential Aide inside the secretary room, then inspect the magazine pile near the entrance after the aide leaves

Secrets

  • Repeatedly visiting Cheryl's Shop at the Esthar Shopping Mall will eventually trigger a gift of a Rosetta Stone from the owner.

Shops and people

  • Cloud's Shop: High-grade recovery items, status recovery remedies, and tents
  • Don Juan's Shop: Standard recovery items, Phoenix Downs, and battle consumables
  • Karen's Shop: Standard general store selling potions, remedies, and phoenix downs
  • Esthar Pet Shop: Gf ability items, pet status medicine, and Pet Scrolls (Volumes 1 through 6)
  • Cheryl's Shop: Usually closed; repeatedly attempting to enter can reward a free Rosetta Stone
  • Esthar Shop!!!: Rare specialty shop run by Johnny; usually closed and requires repeated attempts to enter
  • Esthar Book Store: Sells various volume sets of Weapons Monthly, Pet Pals, and Combat King

Tips

  • Be sure to browse every storefront at the Esthar Shopping Mall at least once so they are permanently registered and accessible later through the GF Call Shop ability.
  • Esthar uses the Lunar card rules by default, including the difficult Same/Plus/Random rules, so take caution before initiating Triple Triad matches here.
  • Rent a car from the city exit vehicle station to quickly travel across the vast Esthar continent world map without triggering random monster encounters.
