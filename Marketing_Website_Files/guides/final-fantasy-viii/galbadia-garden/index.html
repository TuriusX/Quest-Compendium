Galbadia Garden

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Meeting with Headmaster Martine

Squall and his party arrive at Galbadia Garden by train after escaping the events in Timber. You explore the campus, rendezvous with Quistis, and head to the 2nd-floor reception room to meet Headmaster Martine to receive your mission orders for Deling City.

Items

  • Lifesforbidden: Found in the main lobby near the central light structure as a hidden draw point
  • Haste: Found in the athletic field area on the track as a hidden draw point
  • Double: Found on the second floor near the reception area as a hidden draw point

Secrets

  • The Galbadia Garden Triple Triad player on the right side of the inner ring plays using the Trabia trade rules, allowing you to manipulate and spread regional card rules early.

Tips

  • Irvine Kinneas joins your party permanently during the briefing sequence in this area, giving you access to all six main party members.
  • Talk to your party members wandering throughout the garden to trigger unique background dialogue and progress the story triggers required to see Martine.
