Tomb of the Unknown King

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional quest during the Deling City mission

Squall and his party are sent to this ancient maze-like tomb northeast of Deling City to retrieve a fallen student's ID number as an entry exam for General Caraway. Exploring deeper into the tomb is optional, but it allows players to challenge and recruit the Guardian Force Brothers. To complete the optional objective, the party must operate water control mechanisms across the maze before confronting the bosses in the central chamber.

Items

  • Student's Gunblade / ID: dropped on the ground in the entrance hallway just past the doorway

Secrets

  • The location displays a mini-map in the corner, but pressing the Select button toggles a full-screen layout view that also shows the party's current coordinates and facing direction to help navigate the labyrinth.

Tips

  • A foolproof way to navigate the maze without getting lost is to turn right at every single intersection, which naturally leads to all switch mechanisms and back to the center.
  • Casting Float on Sacred and Minotaur prevents them from absorbing ground-based Earth energy to restore HP and removes their immunity to Earth attacks.
  • Drawing from the bosses during combat is essential, as Minotaur and Sacred hold useful restorative and elemental spells like Double and Protect.
