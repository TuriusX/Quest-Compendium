Squall, Zell, and Seifer arrive by boat at the harbor after completing the SeeD field exam in Dollet. Zell departs temporarily to go see his mother, leaving Squall to return to Balamb Garden to wait for the exam results. Before taking the car or walking back to the Garden, players can explore the town, talk to Zell's mother, or challenge locals to card games.

Secrets

0/2

Shops and people
  • Balamb Shop: Sells standard restorative items including Potion, Phoenix Down, and Antidote
  • Balamb Junk Shop: Weapon remodeling service for upgrading characters' weapons with the required materials

Tips
  • Speak to Zell's mother at her house in the residential district to see unique dialogue before heading back to Balamb Garden.
