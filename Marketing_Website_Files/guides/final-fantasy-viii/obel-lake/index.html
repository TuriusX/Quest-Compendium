Obel Lake

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit for the sidequest

Obel Lake is an optional location on the Galbadia continent where the party can trigger a lengthy worldwide scavenger hunt sidequest. Interacting with the center of the lake allows you to talk to a mysterious shadowy creature after humming or throwing rocks. The creature provides a series of cryptic clues that direct the player to travel across the world map to uncover rare treasures and Easter eggs.

Secrets

  • Singing creature interaction | Repeatedly choose to hum at the lake until a shadowy being surfaces to converse with you and provide clues.
  • Rock skipping mechanic | Choosing to throw rocks repeatedly at the lake will eventually result in skipping many times, which unlocks special clues and prompts a Mr. Monkey encounter in Dollet.

Shops and people

  • Shadow at Obel Lake: Offers clues and riddles instead of goods, directing you to various hidden locations across the world.

Tips

  • You must physically step into the peninsula jutting into the center of Obel Lake and press the confirm button to prompt the interaction menu.
