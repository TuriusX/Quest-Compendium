Centra Ruins

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional quest to find Odin and Tonberry

Centra Ruins is an optional dungeon located on the Centra continent where players can recruit the Guardian Force Tonberry and obtain the summon Odin. A 20-minute countdown starts upon entering, requiring players to solve environmental puzzles to reach and defeat Odin before time runs out. The area is also used to defeat dozens of Tonberry enemies to trigger the Tonberry King boss fight.

Items

  • Odin Card: Defeat Odin in battle

Secrets

  • Activating the elevator requires climbing the left ladder at the first triangular ruin, operating the machinery inside the tower room, and then stepping on the altar in the main hall.
  • Opening Odin's chamber requires taking a ruby eye from a gargoyle on the roof, climbing a spiral staircase to place it in another gargoyle, noting the numerical code revealed, and then returning both eyes to the first gargoyle to enter the code.

Tips

  • Defeating Odin does not reset the area, so you can leave and re-enter without a timer to farm Tonberries at your own pace.
  • Junction high Str stats and use Squall's Renzokuken to quickly defeat regular Tonberries before they slowly advance on your party.
  • Mugging Odin yields a Luck-J Scroll, so ensure someone has the Mug ability equipped before initiating the battle.
