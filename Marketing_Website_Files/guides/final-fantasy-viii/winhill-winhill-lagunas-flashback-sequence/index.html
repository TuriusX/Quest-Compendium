Winhill (Winhill: Laguna's flashback sequence)

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit to find items

This flashback sequence places the player in control of Laguna living a quiet life in Winhill alongside Kiros, Raine, and Ellone. Laguna must patrol the village perimeter to fight off invading monsters and then return to Raine's house to rest. Exploring the village during this dream segment yields a hidden stash of Gil and sets up key character relationships.

Items

  • Gil (3,000) MISSABLE: Inside the room on the second floor of the mansion, investigate the desk

Tips

  • Junction Laguna's party carefully before wandering too far down the paths, as combat initiates automatically as you patrol the town entrances.
  • Be sure to grab the hidden Gil in the mansion during this sequence, as you will not be able to obtain it from this exact event state later.
