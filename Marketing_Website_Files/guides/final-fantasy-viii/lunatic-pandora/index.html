Lunatic Pandora

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Squall's team infiltrates the structure

Squall's party infiltrates the Lunatic Pandora structure after it is activated over Tears' Point, pursuing Seifer and Sorceress Adel. Players must navigate three distinct elevator shafts, ladders, and various catwalks while confronting Galbadian defenses. The mission culminates in boss battles against Fujin, Raijin, and Mobile Type 8 before confronting Seifer at the top.

Items

  • Power Wrist MISSABLE: Found behind a hatch accessed via elevator 03
  • Spinkick MISSABLE: Found in a small alcove near the central elevator hub

Secrets

  • Three hidden item caches (including a LuvLuv G and Phoenix Pinion) can only be retrieved if Laguna set off the corresponding boulder traps and checked loose panels during the Centra Excavation Site dream sequence.

Tips

  • Equip Sleep and Confuse junction defenses on your party members before climbing toward the upper floors to counter status ailments.
  • Draw the Holy magic spell from Fujin during the boss confrontation, as it provides a major stat boost when junctioned.
