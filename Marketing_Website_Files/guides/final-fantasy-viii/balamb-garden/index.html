Balamb Garden

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · The game begins here

Balamb Garden is a military academy where the protagonist Squall Leonhart trains to become an elite mercenary known as a SeeD. At the start of the game, Squall must attend class, obtain his first Guardian Forces, and prepare for the SeeD field exam in Dollet. The area serves as the central hub throughout much of the early game and contains study facilities, a training center, and residential quarters.

Items

  • Occult Fan I MISSABLE: Library, on the bookshelf in the back corner
  • Weapons Monthly, First Issue: Training Center, on the ground near the save point screen

Secrets

  • Examine Squall's study desk terminal in the classroom at the start of the game to receive the Guardian Forces Quezacotl and Shiva.
  • Challenge the Trepe Groupie #1 sitting at a table in the 2F cafeteria to win the Quistis Triple Triad card.

Tips

  • Talk to the man near the 2F hallway elevator early on to receive your first set of Triple Triad playing cards.
  • Avoid lingering in the Training Center at very low levels, as running into a T-Rexaur can quickly wipe out an unprepared party.
  • Draw the Sleep spell from Caterchipillars in the Training Center and cast it on a T-Rexaur to safely damage it without taking heavy physical hits.
