D-District Prison

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Squall and team are imprisoned after the failed assassination

Following the failed assassination of Sorceress Edea, Squall and his allies are detained inside an underground cylindrical facility. Players switch between Zell's group as they retrieve their weapons and Squall as he endures interrogation. The objective is to rescue Squall, regroup, and ascend through the floors while surviving an automated floor-drilling sequence to escape to the surface.

Items

  • Combat King 001 MISSABLE: On Floor B1, talk to the prisoner cell sitting near the back, obtained from him after interacting
  • Pet Nametag MISSABLE: Floor B10, won as a random card game prize from the inmate
  • Str Up MISSABLE: Floor B11, won as a random card game prize from the inmate
  • HP Up MISSABLE: Floor B5, challenge the card player inside the cell to win it

Secrets

  • You can purchase the Character Ability "Upgrade Card" functions from the card player on Floor B10 for 300 Gil to unlock additional display features for the card-playing minigame.

Tips

  • Zell begins without weapons, but his unarmed punch attacks deal full physical damage in combat while retrieving the party's equipment.
  • Draw the Sleep spell from soldiers and cast it on difficult mechanical enemies to safely cast high-tier Lightning magic without taking counterattacks.
  • Running past the perimeter catwalk on the highest floor requires heading right across the exterior bridge during the vehicle drilling sequence to trigger the exit scene.
