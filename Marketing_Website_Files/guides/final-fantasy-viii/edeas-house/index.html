Edea's House

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Squall and team confront Edea

Squall and his party visit Edea's House on the Centra continent after the conflict between Balamb Garden and Galbadia Garden. The party learns the truth about their shared childhood at the orphanage and discusses the threat posed by the sorceress Ultimecia. Players can also explore the grounds, find hidden draw points, and play Triple Triad with Edea and Cid.

Items

  • Weapons Monthly July Issue: On the floor inside the house behind the beds

Secrets

  • Curaga draw point located outside near the playground equipment.
  • Dispel draw point hidden inside the house near the entrance doorway.
  • Sorceress Edea card can be won by playing Triple Triad against Edea Kramer.
  • Seifer card can be won by playing Triple Triad against Headmaster Cid Kramer.

Tips

  • Be cautious when playing Triple Triad here, as the Centra card rules include difficult variants such as Same/Plus and Random.
  • Make sure to thoroughly check the bedroom floor behind the beds to avoid missing the Weapons Monthly July Issue.
