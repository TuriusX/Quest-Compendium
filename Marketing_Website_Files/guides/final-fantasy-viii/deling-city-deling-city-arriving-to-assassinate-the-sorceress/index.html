Deling City (Deling City: Arriving to assassinate the Sorceress)

FINAL FANTASY VIII guide · Optional visit to play cards

Squall and his party return to Deling City from the Tomb of the Unknown King to finalize and execute the assassination plot against Sorceress Edea. Before reporting to General Caraway to begin the mission briefing, you have free roam of the city streets to shop, draw magic, and challenge key NPCs to Triple Triad. Once you enter Caraway's mansion and trigger the briefing, you are locked into the climax of Disc 1.

Secrets

  • Draw point for Thundara hidden on the pedestrian walkway near the central fountain square.
  • Draw point for Fira located along the perimeter screens of the city streets.

Shops and people

  • Deling City Hotel: Offers rest to recover HP for 100 Gil; save point located inside the guest room.
  • Deling City Item Shop: Sells standard restorative items including Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-curing remedies.
  • Deling City Junk Shop: Weapon upgrade service to remodel character weapons if you possess the required materials and Gil.

Tips

  • Challenge General Caraway to Triple Triad inside his mansion to win the unique Rinoa Card, but be prepared to intentionally lose the Ifrit Card to him first if you wish to trigger the Card Queen's questline.
  • Ensure all Triple Triad games and city shopping are completed before speaking to Caraway to begin the plan, as the party divides into two teams and cannot explore freely once the parade begins.
