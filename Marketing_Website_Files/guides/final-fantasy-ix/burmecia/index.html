Burmecia

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The rain-soaked city under attack

Burmecia is the ruined, rain-drenched realm of the dragon freaks under invasion by Alexandria's forces. Zidane and his companions must navigate the destroyed city's multi-level corridors, evade Dan-type enemies, and push upward toward the royal palace. Upon reaching the inner sanctum, you witness a plot confrontation involving Kuja and Queen Brahne, concluding in a mandatory battle against Beatrix.

Items

  • Cancer: Behind an overturned cart in the first screen of the city
  • Soft: Chest inside the upper bedroom room near the cracked walkway
  • Germinas Boots: Chest behind a pillar in the large plaza
  • Ether: Chest located on the balcony path above the square
  • Phoenix Down: Chest inside the treasury room guarded by Black Mages
  • Tent: In the supply room behind the large bell
  • Mythril Spear: Embedded in the statue in the vault; requires solving the bell jump

Secrets

  • Bell Rang Event: Ring the massive bronze bell in the courtyard by running under it to cause an item chest to drop down from above.
  • Soldier Rescue: You can rescue a Burmecian family from falling debris in the upper rooms; doing so alters their dialogue and location later in the game.

Shops and people

  • Atla: Moogle who sells standard consumable items like Potions, Hi-Potions, Softs, and Tents
  • Stiltzkin: Sells a bargain bundle containing a Soft, Hi-Potion, and Ether for a discounted price

Tips

  • Equip the "Jelly" ability on party members to prevent petrification caused by the Ironite's attacks.
  • Do not waste major attack items or high-tier offensive MP on Beatrix; the battle ends on a script timer or damage threshold with an unavoidable defeat.
  • Stealing the Mythril Sword from Beatrix is difficult due to low steal rates, but it provides a massive early-game weapon upgrade for Steiner later.
