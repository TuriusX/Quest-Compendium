Desert Palace

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The trap set by Kuja

Regent Cid must successfully complete a minigame against a guarding hedgehog pie to retrieve the hourglass key and free Zidane's captured party members. The party then explores the palace by lighting a series of bloodstones and braziers to solve puzzles and unlock sealed pathways. The dungeon culminates in a confrontation with Kuja's defense mechanism, the Valia Pira.

Items

  • Promist Ring MISSABLE: Found inside the bloodstone located directly on the main staircase in the entrance hall
  • Fairy Earrings MISSABLE: Obtained from a bloodstone on the upper platform in the library after lighting the left brazier
  • Anklet MISSABLE: Found in the bloodstone on the far right balcony overlooking the sandpit
  • Shield Armor MISSABLE: Obtained from a bloodstone on the left path with the gargoyle statues
  • N-Kai Armlet MISSABLE: Retrieved from the bloodstone in the central courtyard after activating the nearby candles
  • Black Hood MISSABLE: Found in the bloodstone located in the east corridor near the staircases
  • Venetia Shield MISSABLE: Obtained from the final bloodstone just before the teleporter to the boss chamber

Secrets

  • Lighting all bloodstones and braziers throughout the palace weakens the boss Valia Pira by stripping it of various elemental and status buffs.

Tips

  • During Cid's key-retrieval minigame, only tap the button when the monster's back is turned, and stop completely the instant it begins to swivel around.
  • Since physical equipment and party members were split prior to Oeilvert, equip this magic-heavy team with reflect rings or Auto-Reflect to turn the enemies' offensive magic against them.
