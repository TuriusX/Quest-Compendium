Lindblum

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Arriving at the Grand Castle and city

Zidane and the party arrive at the massive, airship-powered city of Lindblum to seek an audience with Regent Cid Fabool IX. After exploring the city's three distinct districts and the castle, Zidane participates in the annual Festival of the Hunt before departing for Gizamaluke's Grotto.

Items

  • Ether: Business District, inside the church belfry
  • Hi-Potion: Business District, near the fountain in the main plaza
  • Glass Armlet: Grand Castle, guest room chest
  • Bronze Vest: Grand Castle, guest room chest
  • Steepled Hat: Theater District, inside Michael's studio
  • Leather Cap: Industrial District, near the air-cab station
  • 163 Gil: Theater District, clock tower area near the entrance to Tantalus hideout

Secrets

  • Reaching the belfry of the Business District church requires climbing the pole near the altar to access the hidden upper walkways.
  • Autograph hunting in the Theater District allows Zidane to obtain an Autograph and Moogle Suit from the actor Lowell.

Shops and people

  • Business District Weapon Shop: Daggers, Mage Masher, Mythril Dagger, Broadsword, Iron Sword, Javelin, Rod, Fork, Leather Hat, Bronze Gloves
  • Business District Item Shop: Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Echo Screen, Soft, Antidote, Eye Drops, Tent
  • Dragon's Gate Merchant: Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Tent

Tips

  • Allow Freya to win the Festival of the Hunt to receive the Coral Ring, which absorbs Lightning-element attacks and helps tremendously in upcoming boss fights.
  • Synthesize new weapons and armor at the Business District Synth Shop before competing in the Festival of the Hunt.
  • Use the internal Aircab system to travel quickly between the Castle, Business District, Theater District, and Industrial District.
