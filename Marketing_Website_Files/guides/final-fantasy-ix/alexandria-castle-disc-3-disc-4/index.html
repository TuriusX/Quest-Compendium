Alexandria Castle (Disc 3) (Disc 4)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Returning to the castle after Terra

Alexandria Castle serves as a major hub and shelter for displaced citizens in the late game after the mist returns to the Mist Continent. With the city partially rebuilt, party members can explore the castle grounds to check in on survivors and gather valuable late-game equipment. It is also an important location for wrapping up side quests, including challenges with the Nero family and watching late-game Active Time Events.

Items

  • Opal: Castle hallway bookshelf
  • Sapphire: Left side of the castle entrance courtyard
  • Amethyst: Guard room on the ground floor
  • Peridot: Top of the left castle tower

Secrets

  • Running the Nero Family quest in the Tantalus hideout in town resets and advances every time an event or story trigger occurs in Alexandria Castle or Memoria, yielding a Protect Ring if completed.
