Lindblum (Disc 3) (Disc 4)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Returning for the strategy meeting

On Disc 4, Lindblum acts as a major hub where you can finalize preparations, restock on high-end items, and access various mini-games before confronting the final dungeon. While the story requires you to breach the Shimmering Island to enter Memoria, visiting Lindblum allows you to synthesize powerful gear and handle end-game trading.

Items

  • Elixir: Theater District, search the clock inside Michael's studio
  • 340 Gil: Business District, examine the rubble near the fountain square
  • Lapis Lazuli: Guest Room in Lindblum Castle, inspect the drawer next to the beds

Secrets

  • Visit the synthesis shop in the Business District on Disc 4 to forge ultimate equipment, including Zidane's powerful The Tower dagger, if you have gathered the required synthesis materials.
  • If you saved the Card Freak in Lindblum, you can challenge various high-level Tetra Master players across the Theater and Business Districts to obtain rare monster and boss cards.

Shops and people

  • Lindblum Synthesis Shop: Synthesizes end-game gear such as The Tower, Angel Flute, Grand Cross, and Thief Hat
  • Lindblum Weapon Shop: Sells broad end-game equipment and weapons including Zorlin Shape, Rune Tooth, and Heavy Lance
  • Lindblum Item Shop: Sells basic recovery supplies including Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Soft, and Echo Screen

Tips

  • Make sure to bring Ore and other raw ingredients back here to synthesize rare add-ons before heading through the portal above the Shimmering Island.
  • Use the Lindblum air-cab network or the Grand Castle lift to quickly navigate between districts without wasting travel time on foot.
