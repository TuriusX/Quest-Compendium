Iifa Tree

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The source of the Mist

The party visits the Iifa Tree on the Outer Continent after receiving permission to break the seal from the dwarf village of Conde Petie. You descend along the massive tree's roots and use an elevator platform to reach the core and put an end to the generation of Mist. At the bottom, you discover the true nature of the tree and must defeat the Soulcage boss to clear the area.

Items

  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Behind a root on the winding descent path
  • Hi-Potion MISSABLE: Hidden along the edge of the path while descending the roots
  • Ether MISSABLE: In a chest tucked into an alcove on the descent path
  • Healing Salve MISSABLE: On the downward path before reaching the moving elevator
  • Lamia's Flute MISSABLE: On the central elevator platform structure
  • Remedy MISSABLE: Along the lower root pathways near the bottom
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Chest found on the lower roots near the base
  • Brigandine MISSABLE: Chest located on the tree roots near the bottom area

Tips

  • Because almost every enemy in this area is undead, a single Phoenix Down or Elixir instantly defeats regular undead encounters.
  • Avoid using Fire magic or Fire-elemental attacks on Soulcage, as ignite triggers a powerful counterattack called Fire Blades.
  • Equip the Body Temp and Jelly abilities before exploring the tree to protect against Freeze and Petrify.
