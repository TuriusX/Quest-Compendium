South Gate

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Passing through the gate toward Burmecia

Zidane's party passes through the Bohden Arch of South Gate on their journey toward the Lindblum border and Burmecia. After the gatekeeper allows the party to pass, you can speak with the travelers taking a break and recover your party's status before continuing through the gate.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: hidden near the cart on the right side of the gate area
  • Ether MISSABLE: located along the walkway near the parked carriage

Secrets

  • Inspect the stream of water flowing on the left side of the archway to fully restore the party's HP and MP for free.

Shops and people

  • Part-time Worker Mary: sells recovery items including Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Antidotes

Tips

  • Make sure to drink from the spring on the left to top off your party's health and magic without consuming your own items before heading onto the world map.
