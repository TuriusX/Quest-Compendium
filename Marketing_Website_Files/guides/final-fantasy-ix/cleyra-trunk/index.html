Cleyra Trunk

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Climbing the giant tree

Zidane and his companions scale the inside of the giant Cleyra tree to reach the settlement at the summit. The party navigates winding paths, sand-filled caverns, and levers that alter the sand flow to open the way upward. This area becomes permanently inaccessible later on when Cleyra is destroyed.

Items

  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: In a chest on the right side of the first screen at the entrance
  • Magician Shoes MISSABLE: In an alcove behind a root wall on the second screen
  • Ice Staff MISSABLE: In a chest hidden behind a central pillar on the path above the entrance
  • Needle Fork MISSABLE: In a chest inside a hollow cavern reached before activating the sand lever
  • Tent MISSABLE: In a chest near the hole in the tree trunk looking out over the sandstorm
  • Flame Staff MISSABLE: In a chest near the sand lever mechanism
  • Desert Boots MISSABLE: In a chest along the path after diverting the sand flow
  • Remedy MISSABLE: In a chest tucked against the wall near the vine climbing section
  • Mythril Vest MISSABLE: In a chest on the ledges leading toward the sand whirlpools
  • Potion MISSABLE: In a chest along the upper tree paths
  • Elixir MISSABLE: In a chest on a ledge accessible after stopping a sand pit

Secrets

  • You must pull a lever deeper in the trunk to redirect the sand flow, which fills previously empty pits and creates new walking paths to reach the upper summit.

Tips

  • Equip Quina with the Needle Fork found here to allow physical attacks to inflict the Petrify status.
  • Sand whirlpools will swallow Zidane if you walk into the center; tap the confirm button rapidly to escape if caught.
  • Freya can use Reis's Wind to grant the entire party the Regen status, which helps sustain the team during the climb.
  • Collect every treasure chest on the way up, as you cannot return to the trunk once Cleyra's story events conclude.
