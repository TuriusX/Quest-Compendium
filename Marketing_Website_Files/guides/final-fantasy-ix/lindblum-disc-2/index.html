Lindblum (Disc 2)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Returning after the destruction

Following Alexandria's assault on Lindblum with the eidolon Atomos, Zidane and the party awaken in the devastated city. They must speak with Regent Cid in the throne room to plan their next move, obtain funds, and gather information on Brahne's weapons. The main objective is to restock supplies and then exit via the Dragon's Gate to head toward the Fossil Roo passage.

Items

  • 1,307 Gil MISSABLE: Left corner near the upper railing in Lindblum Castle's guest bedroom
  • Lapis Lazuli MISSABLE: Guest bedroom of Lindblum Castle, found on the floor after speaking to Regent Cid
  • Lindblum Card MISSABLE: Hidden in the rubble outside the ruined synthesis shop in the Business District
  • Tent: In the Theater District inside Michael's studio
  • 2,680 Gil MISSABLE: Base of the destroyed bell tower in the Theater District
  • 340 Gil MISSABLE: Inside the wreckage near the statue in the Business District
  • Tent: Dragon's Gate area, hidden behind the platform near the exit

Secrets

  • Visit the hideout in the Theater District to find chests that have been restocked with items including an Ore and a Phoenix Pinion.

Shops and people

  • Alice: Sells restorative items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Tents in the Business District square
  • Weapon Shop Vendor: Sells weapons and armor out of the rubble in the Business District, including Dagger, Mage Staff, Steepled Hat, and Glass Armlet
  • Dragoon Vendor: Located near the Dragon's Gate, selling items like Potions and Tents before you exit to the Mist Continent

Tips

  • Be sure to search every district before heading down through the Dragon's Gate, as several debris-strewn piles contain gil that cannot be obtained later.
  • You will receive 3,000 Gil from Regent Cid during the storyline cutscenes, which is intended to help re-equip your party from the makeshift street vendors.
