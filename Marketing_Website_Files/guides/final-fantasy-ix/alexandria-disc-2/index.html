Alexandria is reached via the gargant system during a state of turmoil as Queen Brahne uses Eidolons to attack Cleyra and extract power. The party must sneak into the castle to rescue Princess Garnet from Zorn and Thorn before escaping on the gargant again. Time is limited during the central dungeon sequence, requiring quick navigation and preparation for multiple boss battles.

Enemies
Zorn
Steal / drop Partisan, Stardust Rod

Fights alongside Thorn; attack him whenever he charges magic to nullify their combo attack.

Thorn
Steal / drop Mythril Armor, Mythril Armlet

Fights alongside Zorn; hit him whenever he prepares to cast magic to prevent their combined spell.

Beatrix
Steal / drop Survival Vest, Ice Brand, Thunder Gloves

Survive her attacks until she finishes the fight with Climhazzard.

Bandersnatch
Weak to Fire

Canine beast encountered multiple times during the castle escape sequence.

Type C
Weak to ThunderSteal / drop Tent

Mechanical enemy faced by Steiner and Marcus while breaking out of the swinging cage.

Alexandria Soldier
Steal / drop Potion

Standard guard encountered during the castle infiltration.

Tips
  • Equip the Blood Sword on Steiner to reliably drain massive health back and sustain himself in his solo and Beatrix-assisted fights.
  • After freeing Garnet, you face a 30-minute countdown to reach the underground altar before she is permanently trapped, so avoid unnecessary backtracking.
  • Marcus shares his base stat growth bonuses with Eiko, so any leveling you did with him prior to this section provides a stat carryover.
