Dragon's Gate

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Departing Lindblum for Gizamaluke's Grotto

Dragon's Gate is the lower northern exit of the Lindblum Castle elevator system leading out to the Mist Continent's northern plateau. Players visit here to head toward Gizamaluke's Grotto after the Festival of the Hunt concludes and Cid provides party supplies. It serves as the final preparation point before stepping out onto the overworld map.

Items

  • Tent: In the recessed alcove behind the platform stairs

Shops and people

  • Moon Maiden Merchant: Sells standard restorative items like Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Tents
  • Moogle (Monev): Provides standard save and tent services, and handles Mognet delivery

Tips

  • Save your game and rest at the Moogle before leaving, as the overworld enemies on the way to Gizamaluke's Grotto hit noticeably harder than those near the South Gate.
