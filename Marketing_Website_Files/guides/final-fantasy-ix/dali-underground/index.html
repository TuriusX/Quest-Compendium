Dali Underground

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Investigating the black mage production

Zidane, Vivi, and Steiner investigate the hidden production facility beneath the village of Dali after discovering a trapdoor in the windmill. The party uncovers an underground assembly line manufacturing mindless Black Mage infantry from mist. They rescue Vivi from a transport crate and eventually escape aboard a cargo airship after confronting the automated guardian Black Waltz No. 2.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: In a chest inside the first underground room near the lift
  • Eye Drops MISSABLE: In a chest on the walkway behind the spinning wheel
  • Ether MISSABLE: In a chest on a raised platform near the packaging area
  • Leather Hat MISSABLE: In a chest near the large storage tanks
  • Iron Helm MISSABLE: In a chest hidden behind stacked barrels near the exit

Secrets

  • Interacting with the pulley system inside the windmill machinery before going completely down into the dungeon drops a chest containing a Phoenix Down.

Enemies

Tips

  • Do not use magic against Black Waltz No. 2, as it triggers an immediate and heavy magical retaliation against the caster.
  • Equip the Mage Masher on Zidane and use the Soul Blade ability to inflict Silence on Black Waltz No. 2, preventing its most dangerous spells.
  • Ensure you steal all equipment from Black Waltz No. 2, especially the Magus Hat, before finishing the battle.
