Following the devastating attack by Bahamut and Kuja, Zidane and his companions regroup in the ruined streets of Alexandria to search for Dagger. After navigating the wreckage and reuniting the party, the story continues as the team departs to pursue Kuja.

Secrets

0/1

Shops and people
  • Artemicion: Saves your game and allows access to the Mognet mail network amidst the ruined streets

Tips
  • Many areas of the lower town are blocked off by rubble and debris during this visit, narrowing down the paths you can take to locate Dagger.
  • Be sure to thoroughly search the ground for glowing "!" pickup indicators in the wreckage before boarding the boat to the castle, as the town changes again on later discs.
