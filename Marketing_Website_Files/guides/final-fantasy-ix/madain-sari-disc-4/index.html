Madain Sari (Disc 4)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Visiting the village for Eiko's story

Zidane returns to Madain Sari on Disc 4 to uncover the mystery surrounding Eiko's summoner heritage and past. The village is peaceful, allowing the player to engage in an optional sidequest in the Eidolon Wall that reveals Eiko's true birth name. Exploring the village on this final disc unlocks rich lore about the extinct summoner tribe rather than mandatory story progression.

Items

  • Elixir: Inspect the hidden spot behind Dagger's resting area inside Eiko's house

Secrets

  • Eiko's Real Name sidequest: Speak to Lani at the porch of Eiko's house, head to the Eidolon Wall to find a hidden message, walk alternating clockwise and counterclockwise between the entrance and a chime until your HP/MP restores, then read the wall inscriptions to reveal her birth name.

Shops and people

  • Morrison: Tent, Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Echo Screen, Soft, Antidote, Eye Drops, Magic Tag

Tips

  • Doing the Eidolon Wall puzzle on Disc 4 is completely optional, but walking back and forth to hear the chimes fully restores the party's HP and MP for free.
