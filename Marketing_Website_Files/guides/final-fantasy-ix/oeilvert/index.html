Oeilvert

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The ancient ruins

Zidane and his selected party travel here by airship on Kuja's orders to retrieve the Gulug Stone. An anti-magic barrier permeates the entire facility, preventing the party from casting spells or using summons. Players must navigate the ancient hologram displays to unlock the lower chamber and defeat the guardian boss.

Items

  • Remedy MISSABLE: Chest to the right outside the entrance doors
  • Rising Sun MISSABLE: Chest on the far left side of the entrance hall
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Chest located on the upper platform in the planetarium room
  • Diamond Sword MISSABLE: Chest at the bottom of the stairs in the holographic face room
  • Shield Armor MISSABLE: Chest on the upper walkway in the starship display room
  • Power Vest MISSABLE: Chest in the room with the four light projectors
  • Feather Boots MISSABLE: Chest tucked near the projectors in the light room
  • Gulug Stone: Obtained automatically after defeating the boss Ark

Tips

  • Do not bring spellcasters like Vivi, Dagger, or Eiko into Oeilvert, as the anti-magic field completely disables magic and summons.
  • Form a purely physical team consisting of characters like Zidane, Steiner, Freya, and Amarant.
  • Equip the Auto-Potion and Clear-Headed abilities on all party members, as items are your primary healing method and enemies frequently inflict Confuse.
