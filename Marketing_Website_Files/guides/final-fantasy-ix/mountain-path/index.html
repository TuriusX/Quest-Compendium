Mountain Path

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The path to Madain Sari

The Conde Petie Mountain Path is traversed by Zidane's party after leaving Conde Petie to reach Madain Sari on the Mist Continent's neighboring Outer Continent. Along the path, the party meets the young summoner Eiko Carol, who joins the team and helps navigate the giant roots. Players must navigate winding root pathways, deal with wandering Oglops, and defeat the boss Hilgigars guarding the path's exit.

Items

  • Blue Stone: Embedded in a shrine carving along the root pathway on the western side
  • Red Stone: Embedded in a shrine carving accessed by climbing a long vine path
  • Yellow Stone: Embedded in a wall carving on the upper eastern path
  • Green Stone: Embedded in a wall carving near the giant tree trunk
  • Moonstone: Received from the large stone statue at the rest stop after inserting all four colored stones (Blue, Red, Yellow, Green)
  • Remedy: In a treasure chest hidden behind a giant leaf near the path intersection
  • Tent: In a treasure chest along the upper wooden branches
  • Ether: In a treasure chest located along the climbing root network

Secrets

  • Four colored stones scattered throughout the mountain path can be slotted into the mysterious carved stone face near the central rest area to produce a rare Moonstone accessory.

  • Stiltzkin: Sells a bundle containing a Magic Tag, Tent, and Ether for 666 Gil

Tips

  • Equip the Float spell or Earth-negating/absorbing equipment prior to challenging Hilgigars to completely nullify his lethal Earthquake counterattack.
  • Hilgigars carries the Fairy Flute, a powerful early-game instrument for Eiko, but its steal rate is notoriously low; be prepared for a long battle if attempting to steal it.
