Alexandria

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Exploring the town before the play

Vivi explores the streets of Alexandria while heading to the castle to see the Tantalus theater troupe perform "I Want to Be Your Canary." After discovering his ticket is a counterfeit, Vivi teams up with a mouse-kid named Puck to sneak into the castle by roof-hopping. Along the way, players can participate in minigames, gather hidden items, and explore the bustling town before the main events begin.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: street near the starting screen, hidden in rubble on the left
  • 9 Gil MISSABLE: inside the house on the starting screen, in the cabinet
  • Potion MISSABLE: main square, inside the shop near the counter
  • Eye Drops MISSABLE: in the alley near the tavern, hidden behind a crate
  • Zombie Card MISSABLE: inside the bell tower, up the ladder on the platform
  • Goblin Card MISSABLE: found on the ground in the residential area
  • Fang Card MISSABLE: hidden in the rubble outside the synthesis shop
  • Lizardman Card MISSABLE: inside the tavern near the tables

Secrets

  • Jump rope minigame: Speak to the jumping girls in the town square as Vivi to play a timing minigame for various card and Gil rewards.
  • Ringing the bell: Climb the ladder in the church/bell tower and pull the rope to ring the bell, knocking loose several hidden Tetra Master cards.

Shops and people

  • Alexandria Weapon Shop: Dagger, Mage Staff, Broadsword
  • Alexandria Item Shop: Potion, Phoenix Down, Antidote, Eye Drops, Soft

Tips

  • Fully explore all alleys and enter every open house before talking to the ticket booth attendant, as some areas become restricted once the plot advances.
  • Talk to the alleyway cat and locate the lost kitten for a small child in town to receive a card reward.
