Crystal World

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The final area before the end of the game

The Crystal World is the final sub-area found at the very end of Memoria, accessible through the mysterious vortex. Here, the party travels along crystalline platforms, encounters the source of all life, and engages in the final sequence of boss battles to decide the fate of the universe.

Enemies

NecronElixir (x4)The true final boss fought immediately after Trance Kuja, notorious for using Grand Cross to inflict random status ailments

Tips

  • Prepare your party's passive support abilities to resist status effects like Confuse, Freeze, Heat, Stone, and Mini before initiating the final confrontations.
  • Equip Wind-absorbing or Wind-nullifying gear such as Ribbon or Protect Ring to nullify Necron's devastating Blue Shockwave and reduce damage from Neutrino.
  • You can backtrack to the save sphere in Memoria before stepping into the final platform where the boss gauntlet begins.
