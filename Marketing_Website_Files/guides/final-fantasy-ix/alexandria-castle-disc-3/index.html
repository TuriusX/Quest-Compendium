Alexandria Castle (Disc 3)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The coronation and subsequent attack

Garnet prepares for her royal coronation as the new ruler of Alexandria, but the kingdom is violently attacked by Kuja and Bahamut shortly after. Players manage the defense by giving orders to the Knights of Pluto, fight through waves of invading monsters, and unite Garnet and Eiko to summon Alexander atop the castle turret.

Items

  • Opal MISSABLE: Castle guest room, near the foot of the bed
  • Peridot MISSABLE: Left side of the castle entrance lobby during the coronation preparations
  • Sapphire MISSABLE: Right side of the castle entrance lobby during the coronation preparations
  • Topaz MISSABLE: Castle hallway leading toward the library during coronation preparations
  • Angel Bless MISSABLE: Awarded by Beatrix if you assign all Knights of Pluto to their ideal defensive duties

Secrets

  • Assign the Knights of Pluto correctly during the siege: send Blutzen and Kohel to gather info, Weimar and Haagen to protect townspeople, Breireicht and Laudo to make contact with Lindblum, and Doebon and Mullenkedheim to prepare the big cannons to receive an Angel Bless.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
MistodonFire, HolyPotion, Hi-PotionUndead monster vulnerable to curative magic and Life spells, fought in consecutive battles during the siege.

Tips

  • Equip Beatrix and Steiner with undead-slaying abilities or Undead Killer before facing the waves of Mistodons to end battles swiftly.
  • Remove all equipment from Beatrix before her final sequence ends, as she will not remain in the permanent party.
