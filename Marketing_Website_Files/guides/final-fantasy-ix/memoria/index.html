Memoria

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The memory-filled path to the Crystal World

Memoria is the penultimate dungeon accessed via the portal above the Iifa Tree after Terra's destruction. The party travels backward through the collective memories of the universe, re-experiencing ancient history and defeating guardians based on the four elemental Chaos fiends. The ultimate goal is to navigate through to the Crystal World and confront Kuja.

Items

  • Kain's Lance MISSABLE: In the "Past" screen near the base of the tower
  • The Tower MISSABLE: Screen with the clock face, concealed in the ruins on the right
  • Angel Flute MISSABLE: On the "Memory Path" screen with the stone castle path, hidden along the edge
  • Rune Tooth MISSABLE: Screen with the stone staircases, hidden near the corner
  • Mace of Zeus MISSABLE: On the floating platform screen with stairs leading upward, near the left railing

Secrets

  • Hades can be found hidden behind the rocks to the right of the fountain in the "Birth" room; defeat him to unlock synthesis of top-tier equipment like the Pumice and Tin Armor.

Shops and people

  • Hades: Synthesizes ultimate equipment including Pumice, Tin Armor, Save the Queen, and Robe of Lords

Tips

  • Equip Body Temp before the fight with Maliris to prevent the Freeze status, and be prepared to survive her final attack, Raining Swords, when she dies.
  • Many powerful character-specific ultimate weapons are hidden invisibly on screens throughout Memoria; search along ledges and corners by pressing the action button even if an exclamation mark does not appear.
