Black Mage Village

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Visiting the village to find the black mages

The party arrives at the hidden Black Mage Village in the Conde Petie Mountain Path area to investigate the sentient black mages living there. You explore the settlement, watch Active Time Events, and advance the story by talking to the villagers and visiting the cemetery and chocobo stable. Afterward, you return to the inn to trigger key conversations before leaving to continue through Conde Petie.

Items

  • Elixir: inside the chocobo stable, hidden on the floor near the shed
  • Ether: behind the counter in the item shop
  • Gitan's Boots: hidden near the waterwheel walkway outside the workshop
  • 2,000 Gil: on the upper ladder landing inside the workshop
  • 84 Gil: on the floor near the entrance pathways

Secrets

  • Mr. 237's ladder secret | climb the ladder inside the synthesis shop and inspect the rafters to eavesdrop on the shopkeeper below

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Softs, and basic status-curing items
  • Black Mage Village Synthesis Shop: mid-game gear, including Mage Staff, Survival Vest, Madhura Harp, and extension equipment
  • Black Mage Village Inn: rest for 100 Gil and access a save point

Tips

  • Watch all available Active Time Events here, as they give significant backstory for Vivi and Dagger's reflections on their journeys.
  • Synthesize new equipment and upgrade your mage staves before heading out, as this shop offers gear not available in earlier towns.
