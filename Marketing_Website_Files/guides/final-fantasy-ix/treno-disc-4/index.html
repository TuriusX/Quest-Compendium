Treno (Disc 4)

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Visiting for the final card tournament and synthesis

Treno serves as a major hub on Disc 4 for late-game shopping, final synthesis, and mini-games. Players can challenge a brand-new, powerful monster in the weapon shop cellar and participate in an ongoing Tetra Master card tournament at the Card Stadium. The Treno Auction House also continues to run, offering some of the best high-end equipment and key items.

Items

  • Rebirth Ring: Reward from Queen Stella in the mansion after handing in all 12 standard Stellazzio coins
  • Hammer: Reward from Queen Stella after finding and handing in the 13th Stellazzio (Ophiuchus)
  • Ribbon: Reward for winning the optional Tetra Master tournament at the Card Stadium on Disc 4
  • Circlet: Won as the defeat reward against the cellar monster Behemoth in the weapon shop

Secrets

  • The 13th Stellazzio, Ophiuchus, can be found in the exact spot in Quan's Dwelling where you previously found the Scorpio coin, but only after turning in the first 12 Stellazzio to Queen Stella in Treno.

Shops and people

  • Treno Auction House: Auctions rare accessories and gear such as the Thief Glove, Promist Ring, Magician Robe, Pearl Rogue, and Feather Boots.
  • Treno Synthesis Shop: Crafts high-tier gear including the Thief Glove, Robe of Lords, Tin Armor, Grand Helm, and various ultimate armors provided you have the materials.
  • Treno Weapon Shop: Sells mid-to-high tier standard equipment including Defender, Holy Lance, Scissor Fangs, and various defensive armor pieces.

Tips

  • Equipping Auto-Life or Counter on your chosen fighter makes surviving the Behemoth's Meteor counterattack in the weapon shop cellar much easier.
  • If you are synthesizing the Tin Armor at the synthesis shop, be aware that it requires the Hammer, which prevents you from viewing an extra scene in the game's ending.
