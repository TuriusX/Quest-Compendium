Pandemonium

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The final dungeon on Terra

Pandemonium serves as the stronghold of Garland on Terra, accessed after the events in Bran Bal. Zidane must first navigate a series of solo battles with incoming party assistance before reforming the team to traverse light-switching and teleporter puzzles. The dungeon concludes with a gauntlet of major boss encounters against Amdusias, Abadon, Shell Dragon, Silver Dragon, Garland, and Kuja.

Items

  • Holy Mote MISSABLE: In the maze area with the light bridge controls
  • Carabini Mail MISSABLE: Found inside a chest in the room with the teleporter elevator maze
  • Battle Boots MISSABLE: Inside a chest located along the teleporter pathways
  • Elixir MISSABLE: In a chest reached via the teleportation pads

Enemies

Tips

  • Equip the party with Wind-reducing or Wind-absorbing gear before heading to the platform at the top of Pandemonium to neutralize the Silver Dragon's Twister attack.
  • Equip the Auto-Reflect ability on physical fighters for the battle against Kuja to bounce his single-target spells back at him.
  • Once Terra begins to collapse following the Kuja battle, Pandemonium becomes permanently inaccessible, making all missed treasures and encounters unobtainable.
