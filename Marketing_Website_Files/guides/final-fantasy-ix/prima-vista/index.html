Prima Vista

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The airship crash in Evil Forest

Following the crash into the Evil Forest, Zidane wakes up inside the wreckage of the theater ship Prima Vista. He must regroup with Baku, duel his leader in the cargo hold to earn permission to leave, and prepare to venture into the forest to rescue Garnet.

Items

  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Chest inside the crashed cargo room near where Steiner is held
  • Potion MISSABLE: Chest on the lower deck corridor
  • Ether MISSABLE: Chest inside the cabin where Blank is resting
  • Rubber Helm MISSABLE: Chest near Baku in the main bridge area

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
BakuHi-Potion, Iron SwordDuel fought in the cargo hold where Baku frequently trips and loses a turn

Shops and people

  • Cinna: Sells basic restorative items including Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Antidotes
  • Mosco: Offers basic save and tent services using the Mognet communication system outside the wreckage

Tips

  • Be sure to steal the Iron Sword from Baku during the duel, as it gives Steiner a solid weapon upgrade very early in the game.
  • Search every accessible room of the ship for items before leaving into the forest, as the crash site becomes permanently inaccessible later.
