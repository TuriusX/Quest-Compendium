Ice Cavern

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · Traveling toward the Mist Continent

The party enters the Ice Cavern to climb through the mountains and escape the Mist on their way to the Lindblum plateau. Zidane explores the freezing corridors while awakening party members trapped by an icy wind, ultimately facing Black Waltz No. 1 and his summoned beast. The player must navigate icy paths and utilize Vivi to melt frozen obstacles to find paths and treasure.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the first screen with bifurcated paths
  • Potion MISSABLE: Inside a chest located along the icy wall on the western path
  • Ether MISSABLE: Inside an ice formation that Vivi melts for you
  • Elixir MISSABLE: In a chest accessed after sliding down an icy ramp
  • Mage Masher MISSABLE: Inside a chest melted free by Vivi near the deeper part of the cavern
  • Leather Wrist MISSABLE: In a chest sitting along the snowy ledges
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: In a chest revealed after Vivi melts an icy stalagmite barrier

Secrets

  • Several chests in the cavern are entombed in ice and must be freed by interacting with them so Vivi can cast fire magic on them.

Tips

  • A freezing blizzard wind periodically blows across certain rooms; touching it triggers an instant battle against a monster.
  • Zidane must fight Black Waltz No. 1 and Sealion solo, so make sure he enters the fight with full HP and uses Trance to defeat them quickly.
  • Steal the Mage Masher from the earlier chest to equip Zidane with better attack power and the Flee ability before fighting the boss.
  • You cannot return to the Ice Cavern once you exit onto the Mist Continent overworld, making all uncollected treasures missable.
