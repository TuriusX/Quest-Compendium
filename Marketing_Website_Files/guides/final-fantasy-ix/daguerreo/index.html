Daguerreo

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The optional hidden library and synthesis shop

Daguerreo is an isolated multi-level sanctuary of scholars hidden within an island plateau cavern accessible only via the airship or a mountain-climbing Chocobo. Visitors can synthesize high-level equipment, rest at an inn, exchange ore, forge weapons, and challenge a scholar to Tetra Master games. It also serves as the location for obtaining Freya's ultimate weapon, upgrading an Eidolon's power, and consulting the "Four-Armed Man" about treasure ranks.

Items

  • Capricorn: Right side of the entrance hall on the ground by the water
  • Elixir: Middle level, inspect the pile of books on the right balcony
  • Elixir: Middle floor canal path accessible after lowering the left lift platform

Secrets

  • Lowering the left entrance elevator using a rod and three holes in the wall opens a pathway to an equipment synthesis artisan.
  • Changing the names of your party members by showing the Namingway Card to the scholar walking on the middle level library path.
  • Earning the Rank S Medal and discovering the true identity of Gilgamesh by speaking to the Four-Armed Man once your treasure hunter rank is at max.
  • Obtaining Freya's ultimate weapon, the Dragon's Hair, by using a mountain Chocobo to dig at the ocean crack just outside the Daguerreo plateau on the world map.
  • Exchanging Ore for Aquamarines at the dragon statue on the top level once you place a Gem inside it.

Shops and people

  • Daguerreo Synthesis Shop: Synthesizes high-end gear including Sargatanas, Thief Hat, Robe of Lords, and Angel Earrings
  • Daguerreo Weapon Shop: Sells weapons like Asura's Rod, Hamelin, Octagon Rod, and Zollet Elves
  • Daguerreo Item Shop: Sells restorative consumables including Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-healing remedies
  • Innkeeper: Offers a full party HP/MP rest for 100 Gil

Tips

  • Visit Daguerreo as soon as you acquire the Blue Narciss or an upgraded Chocobo to acquire powerful mid-to-late-game synthesis gear early.
  • Equipping the Sargatanas synthesized here allows Zidane to petrify enemies instantly using the Soul Blade ability.
