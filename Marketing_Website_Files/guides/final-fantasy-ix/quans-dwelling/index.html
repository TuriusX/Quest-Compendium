Quan's Dwelling

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The optional cave near Treno

Quan's Dwelling is a small, quiet cave located near Treno that was once the home of Quan and his adopted grandson, Vivi. Players can visit this cavern early in Disc 2 to collect treasure, drink from a restorative spring, and discover significant backstory regarding Vivi and Quina. Later in the game, returning with both Vivi and Quina in the party unlocks exclusive cutscenes and additional rewards.

Items

  • Ether: Inside a pot on the upper cave ledge near the spring
  • Ether: Inside the cabinet on the lower floor of the dwelling

Secrets

  • Resting Spring | Drink directly from the pool of water at the cave ledge to completely restore the party's HP and MP for free.
  • Quan's Fishing Spot / Foam on the Ocean | After obtaining an upgraded Chocobo, examine the edge of the viewing platform outside Quan's Dwelling to jump into the sea for a hidden ocean treasure dive using a Dead Pepper.
  • Vivi and Quina Kitchen Scene | Bring both Vivi and Quina here on Disc 3 or Disc 4 to trigger a cutscene revealing Quan's ghost and Quan's culinary perspective on Vivi.
  • Running Shoes | Search the clock on the wall on the balcony after triggering the Vivi and Quina cutscene with Quale.

Tips

  • Use Quan's Dwelling as a free recovery station while grinding levels or fighting in the card tournament in nearby Treno.
