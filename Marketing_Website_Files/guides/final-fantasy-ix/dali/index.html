Dali

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The village with the chocobo farm and underground factory

The party arrives in the tranquil farming village of Dali to rest and hide after escaping the Evil Forest. While staying at the inn, Princess Garnet tries to practice acting like a commoner, and Vivi wanders the village before being abducted by mysterious villagers. Zidane explores the town and ventures into the hidden underground production facility to rescue Vivi and uncover the source of hostile Black Mages.

Items

  • Potion: Chest inside the Inn's bedroom
  • Antidote: Chest inside the Inn's bedroom
  • 120 Gil: Search the overturned cart near the windmill
  • Eye Drops: Search the shed near the chocobo pen outside the windmill
  • Potion MISSABLE: Chest on the first underground screen near the lift
  • Eye Drops MISSABLE: Chest on the storage area screen in the underground facility
  • Ether MISSABLE: Chest on the storage area screen in the underground facility
  • Leather Wrist MISSABLE: Chest hidden behind barrels in the underground factory
  • Iron Helm MISSABLE: Chest reached via the elevated pulley in the underground machinery area
  • Aries: Search behind the windmill crankshaft on the first floor

Secrets

  • Mayor's Key: Return to Dali during the Card Tournament in Treno on Disc 3 when the mayor is away to search his desk, find his key, and unlock the locked door in the windmill shed for the Burman Coffee and 30,000 Gil.

Tips

  • Kick the pulley machine inside the underground area to make a chest drop down from the ceiling before continuing.
  • Do not use offensive magic against Black Waltz No. 2, as it triggers an immediate teleport and powerful counterattack.
