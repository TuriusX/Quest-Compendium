Evil Forest

FINAL FANTASY IX guide · The party's first journey and petrification

Following the crash of the theater ship Prima Vista, Zidane enters this dangerous wood to rescue Princess Garnet from parasitic monsters. The party navigates deeper into the thicket, rescues Garnet, and flees as the entire forest turns to stone. This dungeon serves as the opening trial where Steiner and Vivi permanently join your active party.

Items

  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: In a chest near the trunk of a tree on the path towards the spring
  • Potion MISSABLE: In a chest located in the clearing with the healing spring
  • Ether MISSABLE: In a chest hidden behind foliage near the entrance clearing
  • Rubber Helm MISSABLE: Dropped or awarded along the path deeper into the woods

Secrets

  • Drinking from the forest spring fully restores the party's HP and MP, allowing for easy leveling before the boss.
  • A concertina-playing moogle named Monty pops out of a tree trunk near the spring when Zidane approaches.

Shops and people

  • Blank: Sells basic consumable supplies such as Potions and Phoenix Downs before entering the deep forest

Tips

  • Equip Zidane with the Bandit ability to make stealing the Iron Helm from Plant Brain much easier.
  • Have Vivi cast Fire on Plant Brain to deal heavy damage and exploit its elemental weakness.
  • Do not use Zidane's Free Energy while Prison Cage holds an ally, as the shared damage can kill them.
  • Collect all chests before triggering the escape sequence, as the forest becomes inaccessible after petrification.
