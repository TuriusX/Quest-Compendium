Pittsburgh

The Last of Us™ Part I guide · Ambushed by hunters upon arrival
← All areas in this guide

Joel and Ellie drive into Pittsburgh, where they are violently ambushed by a local group of hostile survivors known as Hunters who stage fake injuries to trap passersby. After crashing their truck inside a convenience store, Joel and Ellie must fight off waves of attackers and navigate the barricaded, hunter-controlled city streets. The objective is to eliminate the ambushers, clear the nearby multi-story garage building, and search for a way out of the area.

Enemies
Hunter
Weak to Headshots, stealth takedowns, molotovsSteal / drop Ammo, crafting supplies

Armed with melee weapons and pistols; they will flank your position if you stay behind the crashed truck.

Tips
  • Do not stay pinned inside the crashed truck at the start of the ambush; move immediately into the adjacent shop counters to flank enemies as they approach.
  • Save your shivs for the locked Shiv Door on the upper floor of the garage rather than using them on human takedowns.
