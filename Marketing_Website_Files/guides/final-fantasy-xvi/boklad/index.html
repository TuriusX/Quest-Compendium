Boklad

FINAL FANTASY XVI guide · The mission to find the Dominant of Fire
← All areas in this guide

Clive, Jill, and Torgal arrive in the vibrant trading market of Boklad in Dhalmekia on their journey toward Mount Veludd. To secure passage into the mountains, you must gather local intelligence, help find stolen goods, and negotiate with the leaders of the market.

Items

0/1

Shops and people
  • Goetz: basic consumables and weapon crafting services
  • Boklad Merchant: weapons, armor, accessories, and crafting materials

Tips
  • Speak to the various merchants and inspect the marketplace stalls thoroughly, as side quests become available here after progressing the main story.
  • Make sure to upgrade and reforge Clive's gear at Goetz's forge before setting out from the market toward the volcanic paths ahead.
