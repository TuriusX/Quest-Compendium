Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete the main story through Episode 16 on Casual difficulty or higher to unlock the full map network, vehicles, monorails, and equipment.

  2. Step 2

    Connect every shelter to the Chiral Network across all regions, including Ciudad Nudo del Norte, The Bokka, F2 South Distribution Center, Heartman's Lab, East Fort Knot, and South Fort Knot.

  3. Step 3

    Build infrastructure by reconstructing road segments and monorail lines, while restoring three mines to facilitate high-volume transport.

  4. Step 4

    Earn 5-star connection ratings at every prepper facility and knot city, including The Artist, Western Environmental Observatory, The Lone Commander, The Inventor, The Data Scientist, The Pioneer, The Mechanic, The Chronobiologist, The Fisherman, and The Tar Therapist.

  5. Step 5

    Achieve S ranks on 30 standard orders (10 delivery, 10 recovery, 10 elimination/destruction) on Casual difficulty or higher for "Seasoned Porter", and boost all porter categories to grade 40.

    Also in this step: Seasoned Porter

  6. Step 6

    Complete all auxiliary tasks and combat side challenges, including the Ghost Hunter investigation, Headless Riders riddle, defeating the Lord of the Tar Lake, and fabricating all unlocked gear.
