The Lone Commander

DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH guide · Remote military outpost linked in western Australia ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

The Lone Commander is a remote prepper outpost tucked into the elevated mountains of southwestern Australia. After arriving on orders to deliver a dismantled firearm, Sam assists the reclusive sniper by retrieving lost personal mementos and military gear from hazardous terrain. Strengthening connection levels with the outpost integrates the shelter into the Chiral Network and provides critical non-lethal stealth armaments.

Region
Southwestern Australia
Levels

Getting there
From The Government's Base, travel uphill across the mountain cliffs directly through the BT territory using a ladder, or take the longer bypass around the rear of the peaks past the Brigand camp and Smoke Hill mine
Connects to
The Government's Base, Smoke Hill mine, Western Environmental Observatory
