Wiz Chocobo Post

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 3

Wiz Chocobo Post is a rustic outpost in the Duscae region where Noctis and his companions can unlock the ability to rent and ride Chocobos. Players meet the proprietor Wiz and take on the hunt "Friends of a Feather" to track down a rogue Behemoth terrorizing the area. Once the threat is dealt with, Chocobos become accessible across the world and additional side quests become available here.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
DeadeyeGreatswords, Shields, FireA one-eyed Behemoth fought during the quest "Friends of a Feather" to unlock Chocobo rentals.

Shops and people

  • Wiz: Mobhunts, local food, and basic information
  • Chocobo Rent-a-Bird Kiosk: Chocobo rental whistles starting at 50 Gil per day
  • Item Shop: Curative items, Chocobo greens, and Chocobo race medals

Tips

  • Complete the side quest "Friends of a Feather" as soon as you reach this area in Chapter 3 to dramatically speed up world exploration.
  • Resting at the caravan at Wiz Chocobo Post provides a safe resting spot and updates your fast travel return point in central Duscae.
