Fociaugh Hollow

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 3

Fociaugh Hollow is a cavernous dungeon located in the Leide and Duscae border region that Noctis and his companions explore during Chapter 3 to acquire the Mark of the Ramuh. You must navigate narrow tunnels, slip through tight crevices, and defeat lurking daemons to reach the deepest chamber. Upon reaching the end of the cave, Noctis touches the glowing stone to form a covenant with Ramuh and acquire his summoning power.

Items

  • Thieves' Way: on the ground in a dead-end tunnel off the main path

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
ImpDaggers, LightFast-moving daemon that attacks in swarms and can steal your items.
MindflayerDaggers, LightPowerful daemon boss waiting at the depths of the cave that inflicts Confusion.
Thunder BombDaggers, IceExplodes when damaged over time or left unchecked; resists lightning elemental attacks.

Tips

  • Bring weapons or spells imbued with Light or Ice damage, as almost all enemies in this cavern are daemons susceptible to these elements.
  • Equip accessories that protect against Confusion to prevent party members from turning on each other during the fight with the Mindflayer.
  • Flashlight illumination from your party's gear helps deter some smaller daemons in the dark, narrow tunnels.
