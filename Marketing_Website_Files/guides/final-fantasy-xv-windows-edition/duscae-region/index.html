Duscae Region

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 3

Arriving in Duscae opens up the central portion of Lucis, where Noctis and his companions pursue the power of the Archaean, Titan. The party investigates the presence of the Empire, aids Wiz at the Chocobo Post to unlock chocobo riding, and explores the Disc of Cauthess.

Items

  • Megalixir: Inside the Disc of Cauthess along the main path
  • Chocobo Whistle: Rewarded automatically after completing the quest "A Behemoth Undertaking" at Wiz Chocobo Post

Secrets

  • The dungeon of Malmalam Thicket lies deep in southwest Duscae, harboring a Royal Tomb and the Scepter of the Pious at its summit.

  • Wiz Chocobo Post Item Shop: Curative items, Chocobo greens, and camping supplies
  • Coernix Station - Cauthess Diner: Local hunts, area information, and stat-boosting regional meals

Tips

  • Complete the sidequest to defeat Deadeye as soon as you reach Wiz Chocobo Post, as chocobos drastically reduce exploration time across Duscae.
  • Avoid venturing near the giant Catoblepas in the central lakes until you are significantly leveled up, as their stomp attacks can instantly down your party early on.
