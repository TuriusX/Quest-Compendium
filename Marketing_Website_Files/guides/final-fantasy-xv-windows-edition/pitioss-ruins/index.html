Pitioss Ruins

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 15

Pitioss Ruins is an optional endgame platforming dungeon hidden in the northern mountains of Cleigne that can only be reached using the Regalia Type-F. Noctis must explore the dungeon entirely alone during the night, solving intricate environmental puzzles across rotating rooms, tilting platforms, and spike-filled corridors. Unlike other dungeons, there is no combat inside, making navigation, timing, and precision jumping the sole challenges.

Items

  • Black Hood: On the ground directly in front of the statue exit at the end of the dungeon
  • Genji Gloves: On the navel of the large tilted golden statue in the underworld section

Secrets

  • Shortcut switches and breakable walls throughout the dungeon remain unlocked permanently once triggered, allowing you to exit to the surface, save your game, and resume without losing overall puzzle progression.

Tips

  • Do not use the fast-travel "Return to entrance" map option unless you have already unlocked a physical shortcut back to your current section, or you will have to re-do puzzles.
  • Noctis takes an extra step after landing a jump, so perform a regular melee weapon attack in mid-air to halt forward momentum and drop straight down onto narrow platforms.
  • Save your game immediately after successfully landing the Regalia Type-F on the narrow landing strip outside the dungeon to avoid repeating the tricky flight sequence if you crash.
