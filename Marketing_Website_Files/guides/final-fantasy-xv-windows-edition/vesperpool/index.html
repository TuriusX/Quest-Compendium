Vesperpool

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 7

The Vesperpool is a vast, forested wetland region located in northwestern Cleigne that becomes accessible during Chapter 7. Noctis and his companions travel here to investigate the Steyliff Grove ruins alongside Aranea Highwind in order to find mythril for repairing their ship. The area is rich in high-level wildlife, resource deposits, and exceptional fishing spots.

Items

  • Mythril Ore: Found deep inside the Steyliff Grove dungeon during the main quest

Enemies

Tips

  • Complete the optional fishing quest chain from Navyth here, as the Vesperpool harbors some of the most challenging fish in Lucis, including the Liege of the Lake.
  • Aranea temporarily joins your party for the duration of the Steyliff Grove expedition, providing strong aerial attacks and access to her Highwind technique.
