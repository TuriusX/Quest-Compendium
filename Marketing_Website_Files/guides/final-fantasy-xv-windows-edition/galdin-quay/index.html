Galdin Quay

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 1

Galdin Quay is a scenic seaside resort where Noctis and his companions travel to catch a ferry to Altissia, only to find the boat service suspended. To secure passage forward, the party must meet the journalist Dino on the pier and run an errand to fetch him a gemstone from the nearby crags. Once Dino's quest is complete, players stay at the resort hotel to advance the main story to the end of Chapter 1.

Items

  • Mineral Deposit: On the rock face along the shore near the jetty
  • Fishing Spot: Vannath Coast: At the end of the long wooden pier structure south of the restaurant

Shops and people

  • Mother of Pearl: High-end restaurant offering expensive meals that grant powerful combat stat buffs
  • Dino: Gemstone questgiver who trades his reporter connections to arrange a boat ride in exchange for raw garnets
  • Bob (Seagull Produce): Grocer selling cooking ingredients and local fish products
  • The Quayside Cradle: Hotel offering lodging that provides a 2x multiplier to all accumulated experience points

Tips

  • Sleep at The Quayside Cradle before completing Chapter 1 to double the massive chunk of EXP you earn from Dino's story quest.
  • Stop by the pier's tackle shop and fishing spot to stock up on basic fishing gear and level up Noctis's fishing skill early.
