Lestallum

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 3

Noctis and his companions travel to the bustling cliffside city of Lestallum in Chapter 3 to regroup and follow leads on the Archaean, Titan. While exploring the market streets, Noctis experiences persistent severe headaches that drive the party to investigate the Disc of Cauthess. Players can take on various side quests, shop for gear, and rest at the local hotel to progress the main storyline.

Shops and people

  • Culless Sparom: Culinary ingredients including local meats, spices, and produce
  • Taelpar Crag Mineral and Gem Exchange: Ores, minerals, and accessory components
  • Arms Vendor: Assorted upgraded weapons including Greatswords, Swords, Daggers, and Firearms
  • JM Market: Cooking ingredients such as Lestallum Carrots and seasonings

Tips

  • Staying at The Leville hotel in Lestallum grants a 1.5x experience multiplier to all banked EXP when resting.
  • Look for Vyv at the scenic viewpoint overlooking the crater to unlock a high-paying side quest chain involving taking landscape photographs.
  • Resting in Lestallum triggers a scene where Gladio asks Noctis to go for an early morning jog, giving an opportunity to gain easy AP.
