Altissia

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 8
← All areas in this guide

The party arrives in the water city of Altissia aboard the Royal Vessel to seek an audience with First Secretary Camelia Claustra. Players must navigate the canal network via gondolas, progress the main story through negotiations regarding the Leviathan summit, and explore the city's alleys before triggering the climactic trial.

Shops and people
  • Maagho: Sells local Lucian food recipes, ingredients, and information on local hunts
  • JM Market: Sells restorative items, crafting components, and cooking ingredients
  • Arena Exchange Booth (Totomostro): Trades arena medals earned from the monster colosseum for exclusive rewards including car decals, fishing reels, and accessories

Tips
  • Use the gondola transport network and keep track of boat routes on your mini-map, as the multi-tiered stairs and canals can make navigating Altissia on foot very disorienting.
  • Resting at the Leville hotel allows you to choose between standard rooms and an expensive luxury suite that grants a massive 3.0x experience point multiplier when tallying your stored EXP.
