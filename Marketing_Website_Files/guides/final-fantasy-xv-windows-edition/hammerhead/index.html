Hammerhead

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 1

Hammerhead is an outpost and service station in the Leide region where Noctis and his companions push the broken-down Regalia at the start of Chapter 1. Cindy Aurum assists with repairing the car while sending the party on several introductory errands, and Cid Sophiar is introduced as a mechanic who can upgrade weapons. The area serves as the party's initial home base, offering rest facilities, early-game hunts, and various shops to prepare for exploration.

Items

  • Fire Crest: On the rooftop of the garage; climb the metal ladder around the back of the service station building
  • Dagger: Lying on the ground near the trash cans beside the main garage building

Secrets

  • Resting in the caravan parked behind the diner grants a 1.2x EXP multiplier, which can be accessed repeatedly throughout the game for a modest fee.

Shops and people

  • Hammerhead Diner (Tipster): Sells local food, provides local information that marks ingredient and treasure spots on the map, and issues mob hunts
  • Mini-mart (Culless Ornery): Sells basic curative items (Potions, Antidotes, Phoenix Downs) and car customization supplies
  • Weapon Vendor: Sells starter armaments such as the Broadsword, Two-Handed Sword, Javelin, and Daggers

Tips

  • Always talk to the tipster at the diner upon arriving to mark nearby mineral deposits, food ingredients, and points of interest on your mini-map.
  • Inspect the blue-glowing spots scattered across the ground around the parking lot and perimeter fence to pick up miscellaneous junk and basic building materials for free.
