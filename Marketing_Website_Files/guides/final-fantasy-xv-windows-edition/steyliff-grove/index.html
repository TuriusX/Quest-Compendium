Steyliff Grove

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 7

Steyliff Grove is an ancient ruin dungeon located in the Vesperpool region that Noctis and his companions explore during Chapter 7. Accompanied temporarily by Aranea Highwind, the party must navigate through underground chambers filled with Daemons to retrieve Mythril Ore for the Regalia. The entrance only opens at night, so Noctis must arrive between 20:00 and 04:00 to gain access.

Items

  • Mythril Ore: Dropped on the ground in the boss chamber after defeating Quetzalcoatl
  • Star Pendant: Ground pickup in the lower passageways

Secrets

  • Menace Beneath Lucis Dungeon Entrance | A sealed vault door found at the deepest point of Steyliff Grove, unlocked in the post-game using the Seal-Breaker's Key obtained from Ezma.

Tips

  • Aranea Highwind joins as a guest party member here; make frequent use of her high-damage Tech bar ability, Highwind, to clear out Daemon packs.
  • Because Steyliff Grove is heavily infested with Daemons, equipping Light-element weapons or keeping prompt access to Ice-element spells provides a significant advantage.
