Leide

FINAL FANTASY XV WINDOWS EDITION guide · Chapter 1

Noctis and his companions begin their journey stranded in the arid region of Leide after the Regalia breaks down. While Cindy works on repairing their car at Hammerhead, the party takes on local odd jobs and hunts to earn gil. The ultimate goal in this opening segment is to travel east across the desert to Galdin Quay to catch a ferry to Altissia.

Items

  • Fire Crest: Inside the garage at Hammerhead resting on an oil drum
  • Megalixir: Hidden behind the diner building at the Hammerhead outpost

  • Hammerhead Item Shop: Potions, Hi-Potions, Antidotes, and basic curatives

Tips

  • Complete bounty hunts at Takka's diner to quickly earn gil and experience before heading to Galdin Quay.
  • Resting in the camper trailer at Hammerhead grants an experience multiplier, making it better for leveling up than basic campsite resting.
