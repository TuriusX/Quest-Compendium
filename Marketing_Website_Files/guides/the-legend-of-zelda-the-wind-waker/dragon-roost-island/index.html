Dragon Roost Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · First arrival to meet Prince Komali

Link arrives at Dragon Roost Island following the guidance of the King of Red Lions to retrieve Din's Pearl. Inside the Rito aerie, he must speak with the Rito Chieftain and deliver a letter from Medli to Prince Komali to help calm the distressed dragon Valoo. After consoling the prince, Link helps Medli reach the entrance of the Dragon Roost Cavern to uncover the trouble atop the mountain.

Items

  • Wind Waker: Received from the King of Red Lions on the beach upon arriving at the island
  • Delivery Bag: Bought or received from the mailbox / Rito post office area upon arriving
  • Father's Letter: Given by Medli inside the Rito aerie to deliver to Prince Komali
  • Bottle: Given by Medli at the pond near the entrance to Dragon Roost Cavern after helping her reach the higher ledge

Secrets

  • The Wind Shrine at the back of the island contains a stone tablet that teaches Link the Wind's Requiem after he conducts with the Wind Waker.

Tips

  • Throw water jugs at the dried Bomb Flowers blocking paths and gaps to revive them so they can be picked up and detonated.
  • Pay close attention to wind direction when throwing Medli up to the higher ledge near the mountain entrance, as the wind must blow toward the ledge for her to reach it.
