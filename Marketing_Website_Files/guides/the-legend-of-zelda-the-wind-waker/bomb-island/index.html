Bomb Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Notable island

Bomb Island is a volcanic island in sector F5 featuring a rock formation shaped like a bomb and a cavern beneath a heavy boulder. Link explores this island to trade with the Traveling Merchant and enter a subterranean cave guarded by Magtails. Inside the cave, Link must navigate lava and solve puzzles with Magtails to claim a Piece of Heart.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: Inside the underground cavern after dropping Magtails onto floor switches to open the chest

Secrets

  • Lift the heavy black boulder on top of the island using the Power Bracelets to reveal a hole leading to a lava-filled cave.
  • Salvage the sea floor directly southwest of the island using Triforce Chart 2 to find Triforce Shard 2.

Tips

  • Hit Magtails with the Skull Hammer or sword to safely curl them into balls before picking them up to place on pressure switches.
