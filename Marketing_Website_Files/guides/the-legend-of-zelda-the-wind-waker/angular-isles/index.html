Angular Isles

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Notable island

Angular Isles consists of block-shaped stone islands that challenge Link to solve block-pushing puzzles to reach high ledges. Players can climb the large island by maneuvering blocks to collect a piece of heart and enter a secret cavern beneath a large stone head. The surrounding waters also hide treasure sunken beneath the sea.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: In a treasure chest at the very top of the main island after solving the block climbing puzzle
  • Silver Rupee: In a chest inside the subterranean secret cavern beneath the heavy stone head
  • Piece of Heart: Salvaged from the ocean floor nearby using Triforce Chart 5 / Treasure Chart 15

Secrets

  • Lift the heavy black stone head using the Power Bracelets to reveal a hole leading to a secret cavern with a block puzzle.

Tips

  • If you push a block incorrectly while attempting to scale the island, jumping into the water or walking far enough away will reset the puzzle.
