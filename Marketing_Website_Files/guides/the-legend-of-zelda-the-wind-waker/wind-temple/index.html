Wind Temple

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · After awakening Makar

Link navigates the Wind Temple alongside the Korok sage Makar, using Makar's ability to plant trees and fly over obstacles to advance. Together, they activate a massive central updraft fan and unlock deeper chambers to reach the temple's boss. Defeating Molgera restores the power to repel evil within the Master Sword.

Items

  • Iron Boots: Found earlier in the Ice Ring Isle, but essential for stepping on heavy floor switches and anchoring Link against wind gusts inside the temple
  • Hookshot: In a chest inside the northern room of the upper level, guarded by a Darknut
  • Heart Container: Drops after defeating the boss Molgera in the deepest basement arena
  • Wind God's Crest / Master Sword Power Restoration: Awarded in the boss room after defeating Molgera and conducting Makar
  • Dungeon Map: Found in a treasure chest in the large multi-story central chamber
  • Compass: Found in a chest in the tall room with spiked floor traps and air currents
  • Big Key: Inside a treasure chest on the lowest basement level, reached by solving a Hookshot and fan puzzle

Tips

  • Equip the Iron Boots when walking through strong wind tunnels or when pulling down spring-loaded switches to prevent being blown backward.
  • Use the Command Melody to take control of Makar so he can fly across gaps and plant seeds in soft dirt patches to grow trees and unlock doors.
  • If Makar is captured by Floormasters and locked in a jail cell, you must locate the switch to free him before you can complete the temple.
  • Use the Hookshot to attach to Makar's planted trees to pull Link across large chasms.
