Diamond Steppe Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Triforce Chart location

Diamond Steppe Island consists of tall, sheer rock spires that cannot be scaled by climbing alone. Link must use the Hookshot on palm trees to ascend to the top and enter a warp pot puzzle inside the island's cavern. Solving the maze of warp pots rewards the player with the chart needed to locate a Triforce Shard.

Items

  • Triforce Chart #6: Inside the chest at the end of the warp pot maze inside the island (replaced with Triforce Shard #6 in the HD version)
  • Piece of Heart: In a sunken treasure chest retrieved via Treasure Chart #23 directly adjacent to the island

Secrets

  • Joy Pendant | Found inside one of the pots inside the cavern maze reached by falling down the correct warp pot | missable: no

Tips

  • You must obtain the Hookshot from the Wind Temple before you can scale the island's exterior spires.
  • Look into the warp pots before jumping in, as some are occupied by Floormasters waiting to grab you.
