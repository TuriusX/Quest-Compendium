Pawprint Isle

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Notable island

Pawprint Isle is an island shaped like a dog or bear paw print that features a small cave hidden inside a hollow shell. Inside the underground grotto, players navigate ChuChu-infested tunnels to find treasure chests and a subterranean exit leading to another part of the island. A Wizzrobe lookout platform and a Beedle shop ship also reside around the island's waters.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: Inside the underground cavern behind a boulder, in a chest guarded by Red ChuChus
  • Silver Rupee: Inside the underground cave in a chest accessible through a crawlspace

Tips

  • Smash through the cracked boulder at the main dome with Bombs or the Skull Hammer to gain access to the underground cavern.
  • You will need the Hookshot to reach the Piece of Heart located inside the treasure chest atop the tallest paw pad.
