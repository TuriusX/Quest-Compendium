Earth Temple

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · After awakening Medli

Link and the newly awakened Earth Sage, Medli, enter the subterranean temple beneath Headstone Island to restore the Master Sword's power to repel evil. You must command Medli to fly, step on switches, and reflect beams of light to solve puzzles and banish darkness. Completing the dungeon culminates in defeating Jalhalla to fully awaken Medli's prayers and partially repower the Master Sword.

Items

  • Mirror Shield: Inside a treasure chest in the large basement room after solving the light-reflection puzzle
  • Dungeon Map: In a treasure chest reached in the early rooms after using Medli to glide across a gap
  • Compass: In a treasure chest found after defeating all enemies in a side chamber filled with fog
  • Big Key: Inside a treasure chest in a basement puzzle room guarded by Redeads and Blue Bubbles
  • Heart Container: Drops onto the arena floor after defeating the boss Jalhalla

Secrets

  • Blue warp cauldrons scattered through the temple can be opened by blowing up the boulders blocking their tops with Bombs to quickly travel between dungeon sections.
  • Several dark stone sun-face statues can be completely dissolved by using the Mirror Shield to shine reflected light on them, revealing hidden passages, switches, or chests.

Tips

  • Play the Command Melody while standing near Medli to take direct control of her movement and harp-reflector ability.
  • You can pick up and carry Medli while running, and jumping off a ledge while holding her allows Link to glide for a short distance.
  • If Link is afflicted by a Blue Bubble's curse, play the Song of Passing or wait out the timer to recover your ability to use weapons.
