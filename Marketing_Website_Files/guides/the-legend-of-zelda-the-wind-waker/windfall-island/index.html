Windfall Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · First arrival after leaving Outset

Link arrives at Windfall Island aboard the King of Red Lions to search for a sail so he can navigate the Great Sea. While here, he can free a captive from the island's jail and purchase the necessary sail from a traveling merchant. After acquiring the sail, Link can set off toward his next destination.

Items

  • Sail: Purchased from Zunari in the island's market square
  • Tingle Tuner: Given by Tingle inside the island's jail after opening his cell
  • Tingle's Chart: Given by Tingle inside the island's jail after opening his cell
  • Picto Box: Found at the end of the crawlspace maze behind the crate in Tingle's jail cell

Secrets

  • Pushing aside the wooden crate in Tingle's jail cell reveals a crawlspace maze leading to the Picto Box.

Shops and people

  • Zunari: Sail

Tips

  • Smash the pots inside the jail cell and around town to gather enough rupees to buy the sail from Zunari.
