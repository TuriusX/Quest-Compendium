Horseshoe Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Triforce Chart location

Horseshoe Island is shaped like a crescent with several small holes where Link must play a golf-like mini-game using the Deku Leaf to blow nuts into the holes. Successfully getting the nuts into the holes clears obstacles so Link can progress across the island. Completing this path grants access to an underground cavern that houses a Triforce Chart.

Items

  • Triforce Chart #8: Inside the island's underground cavern, obtained after defeating the enemies within
  • Treasure Chart #28: In a treasure chest on the small outer island reachable by gliding with the Deku Leaf

Secrets

  • Underground cavern | Drops down a hole inside the final area on the main island path, blocked until the nuts are cleared

Tips

  • Use the Deku Leaf to aim and blow the nuts into the small hollows to lower the spiked vines blocking the path.
