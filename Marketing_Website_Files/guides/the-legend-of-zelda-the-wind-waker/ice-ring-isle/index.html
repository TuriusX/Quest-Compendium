Ice Ring Isle

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Notable island
← All areas in this guide

Ice Ring Isle is a frozen island surrounded by a biting blizzard that damages Link over time. Link must disable the freezing winds using Fire Arrows to gain access to the interior cavern. Inside, navigating the slippery ice paths leads to a key item needed to explore volcanic areas.

On this page:ItemsEnemiesTips

Items

0/2

Enemies
Keese

Flying enemies encountered inside the cavern that can be frozen.

ChuChu

Red and Green ChuChus appear around the island and its cave.

Tips
  • Shoot a Fire Arrow into the freezing blast of air blowing from the dragon head to temporarily stop the blizzard and start a countdown timer.
  • Wear the Iron Boots inside the cave to walk steadily across slippery ice paths and avoid being blown back by wind gusts.

Not listed for this area: secrets, shops and people.
