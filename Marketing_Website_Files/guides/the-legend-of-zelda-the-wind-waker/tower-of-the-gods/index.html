Tower of the Gods

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Third dungeon

Link navigates a sunken divine trial using the King of Red Lions to traverse shifting water levels on the lower floor. Inside, he acquires the Hero's Bow and guides mystical statues to unlock the upper levels and face the trial's guardian. Ringing the bell at the pinnacle of the tower reveals the path to the sunken realm of ancient Hyrule.

Items

  • Dungeon Map: inside a treasure chest in the flooded lower floor reached by burning away wooden boards with a torch
  • Compass: inside a treasure chest on the second floor after defeating all enemies in the room
  • Hero's Bow: awarded from a treasure chest after defeating the Darknut miniboss
  • Small Key: found in a chest that spawns after defeating two Armos Knights on the first floor
  • Small Key: inside a treasure chest on the second floor guarded by floating Armos statues
  • Big Key: found in a chest reached by guiding the final stone statue across the upper level puzzle rooms
  • Heart Container: appears in the boss chamber after defeating Gohdan

Tips

  • When the water level recedes on the first floor, you can pick up nearby pots and sticks to carry fire over to unlit wall torches.
  • Use the Command Melody to take direct control of the stone Servants of the Tower and navigate them across floor switches.
  • If you run out of arrows or bombs during the boss battle with Gohdan, the boss will sneeze pickups onto the platform.
