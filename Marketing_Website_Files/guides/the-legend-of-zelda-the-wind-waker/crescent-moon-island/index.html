Crescent Moon Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Triforce Chart location

Crescent Moon Island is an island shaped like a crescent moon, situated in the Great Sea at coordinate E1. Players visit this island primarily to open a treasure chest containing Triforce Chart #10 (or a Triforce Shard in the HD version). The island is also guarded by enemies and occasionally visited by the Ghost Ship under specific moon phases.

Items

  • Triforce Chart #10: Inside the treasure chest on the center of the island
  • Piece of Heart: Salvaged from the ocean floor nearby using Treasure Chart #11

Secrets

  • Blue ChuChu found on the island, which yields Blue Chu Jelly for potion-making when defeated
  • A submerged treasure chest containing Silver Rupee (200 Rupees) can be hauled up using Treasure Chart #9
