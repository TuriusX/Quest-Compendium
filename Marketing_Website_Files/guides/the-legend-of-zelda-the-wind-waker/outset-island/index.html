Outset Island

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Beginning of the game

Outset Island is Link's peaceful home island where his journey begins on his birthday. After receiving his green clothes, Link must obtain a sword to rescue a girl who fell into the island's forest, triggering an event where his sister Aryll is kidnapped by a giant bird. To leave the island and rescue her, Link joins the pirate crew led by Tetra.

Items

  • Hero's Clothes: Given to Link by his Grandmother on the second floor of their home
  • Telescope: Given to Link by Aryll at the lookout tower
  • Hero's Sword: Given by Orca in his dojo after successfully completing his combat training
  • Hero's Shield: Given to Link by his Grandmother inside their home before boarding the pirate ship
  • Bait Bag: Purchased from Beedle's Shop Ship docked near the island

Secrets

  • Crawl through a small opening underneath Link's house to find a treasure chest containing an Orange Rupee (worth 100 Rupees).

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
Red BokoblinJoy PendantDrops its wooden club or shield, which Link can pick up and use temporarily as a weapon.

Shops and people

  • Beedle's Shop Ship: Bait Bag, All-Purpose Bait, Hyoi Pears
  • Orca: Offers sword training to teach basic combat moves and gives the Hero's Sword

Tips

  • Catching the stray pigs roaming around the island for the resident woman rewards Link with Rupees.
  • Practice side-hops, backflips, and parry attacks with Orca before heading into the forest to get used to the sword combat mechanics.
