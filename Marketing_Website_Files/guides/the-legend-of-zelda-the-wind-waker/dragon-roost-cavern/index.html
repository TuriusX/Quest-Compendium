Dragon Roost Cavern

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · First dungeon

Dragon Roost Cavern is the first dungeon in the game, situated inside the volcano on Dragon Roost Island. Link enters the cavern to find out what is tormenting the Sky Spirit Valoo and causing him to lash his tail. Inside, you must navigate lava pits, burn wooden barricades, and climb to the summit to defeat the boss.

Items

  • Dungeon Map: Found in a treasure chest in the second room of the first floor
  • Compass: Found in a treasure chest in a room on the second floor guarded by Kargarocs
  • Small Key: Locked in a chest or dropped by defeated enemies in multiple puzzle rooms
  • Grappling Hook: Awarded after defeating the Mini-Boss, a Moblin, in the upper portion of the dungeon
  • Big Key: Found in a treasure chest on the exterior summit area accessible using the Grappling Hook
  • Heart Container: Drops upon defeating the dungeon boss, Gohma
  • Din's Pearl: Given to Link by Prince Komali outside after completing the dungeon

Secrets

  • Water pots can be thrown onto magma spouts to create temporary solid platforms that let you reach high ledges or cross gaps.
  • Using the Grappling Hook on the hanging pendant of the Boss Door unlatches the lock to reveal the arena entrance.

Tips

  • You can light a Boko Stick on fire at any torch and carry it to burn down wooden barricades or ignite unlit sconces.
  • If you leave a room or restart, lit Boko Sticks cannot be carried across loading zones, so look for a local fire source nearby.
  • Use the Grappling Hook on enemies to steal valuable spoil items, such as Joy Pendants from Bokoblins and Golden Feathers from Kargarocs.
