Seven-Star Isles

The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker guide · Triforce Chart location

Seven-Star Isles is a sector of the Great Sea located at coordinates F1, named after its group of small, barren rock formations. The primary objective here is to defeat a cluster of Wizzrobes and their summoned minions atop several wooden platforms to reveal a treasure chest. Completing this combat challenge awards a chart used in the Triforce quest.

Items

  • Triforce Chart #5 (GameCube) / Triforce Shard (Wii U): Inside a treasure chest that appears on a wooden platform after defeating all the Wizzrobes stationed in the area
  • Piece of Heart: Salvaged with the Grappling Hook from a glowing light ring in the sea after obtaining Treasure Chart #16

Secrets

  • A twelve-eyed Big Octo dwells in the waters of this sector, marked by a flock of seagulls circling overhead; defeating it yields a Piece of Heart.

Tips

  • Use the Fire Arrow or Ice Arrow to defeat the elevated Wizzrobes quickly from long range before boarding their lookout platforms.
