Don Corneo's Mansion

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Rescuing Tifa and Aerith

Cloud infiltrates Don Corneo's mansion in Wall Market disguised as a woman to rescue Tifa and find out what Shinra is planning. Depending on Cloud's disguise choices, Don Corneo selects either Cloud, Tifa, or Aerith to be his companion for the night. After interrogating Corneo in his bedroom, the Don drops the party through a trapdoor into the sewers.

Items

  • Ether MISSABLE: In the basement room where the lackeys are hanging out, underneath the stairs
  • Hyper MISSABLE: In the torture room on the second floor where Tifa or Aerith may be held

Tips

  • If Don Corneo chooses Cloud, you will find yourself in his bedroom right away and can search behind the bed for an item before talking to him.
  • If Corneo chooses Tifa or Aerith instead of Cloud, defeat the lackeys in the basement or changing room to regroup with the party before confronting the Don.
  • Don Corneo drops the party into a boss fight against Aps immediately following the interrogation, so heal everyone during the dialogue before giving the final prompt.
