Sleeping Forest

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Path to the Forgotten Capital

The Sleeping Forest is an ancient woodland on the northern continent that serves as the gateway to the Forgotten Capital. Without the Lunar Harp, the forest loops infinitely, preventing the party from reaching the deeper sections. Once the Lunar Harp is obtained from Bone Village, walking into the forest awakens it and opens the way forward.

Items

  • Kjata MISSABLE: floating around the trees in the second screen
  • Water Ring: chest hidden behind the trees in the third screen

Secrets

  • The red Kjata Summon Materia floats through the canopy of the second screen and must be grabbed by timing your interaction as it zips across the path.

Tips

  • You must dig up the Lunar Harp at Bone Village before entering, or the forest will indefinitely loop on the first screen.
  • Keep your finger near the confirm button on the second screen so you do not miss catching the floating Kjata Materia as it passes by.
