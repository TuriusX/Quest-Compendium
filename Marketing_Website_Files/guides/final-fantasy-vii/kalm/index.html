Kalm

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · After leaving Midgar

Kalm is the first peaceful town the party reaches after escaping Midgar across the grassy world map. Upon entering the inn, Cloud gathers the group upstairs and recounts his past memories of Sephiroth and the Nibelheim incident. Once the flashback sequence concludes, Cloud receives the PHS item, allowing party switching on the world map before heading toward the Chocobo Farm.

Items

  • PHS: automatically given by party members upon leaving the inn after the flashback
  • Ether: cabinet beneath the stairs in the house to the right of the inn
  • Ether: inside the cabinet on the second floor of the house next to the inn
  • Peacemaker: chest on the top floor of the house on the far right side of town
  • Guard Source: chest hidden under the spiral staircase in the house on the far right side of town

Secrets

  • The locked cabinet on the second floor of the house near the town entrance cannot be opened until much later in the game.
  • An elderly man living in the house on the right side of town will trade rare Materia and an airship-gold chocobo for three specific map items much later in the game.

Shops and people

  • Kalm Item Shop: Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Antidote, Eye Drop, Tent
  • Kalm Weapon Shop: Mythril Saber, Cannon Ball, Shotgun, Mythril Clip, Bronze Bangle, Silver Armlet
  • Kalm Materia Shop: Earth Materia, Fire Materia, Ice Materia, Lightning Materia, Restore Materia, Heal Materia

Tips

  • Be sure to fully explore every house in town and loot all the cabinets before heading east toward the Chocobo Farm.
