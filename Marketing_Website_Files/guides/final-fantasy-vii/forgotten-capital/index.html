Forgotten Capital

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Confronting Sephiroth and the end of Disc 1

Cloud and the party follow Aerith to the ancient city to stop Sephiroth from using the Black Materia. The party rests in a seashell house before navigating deep into the crystalline underground altar where Aerith is praying. After a confrontation with Sephiroth and a boss battle, Disc 1 comes to a close.

Items

  • Comet Materia: In an upper opening inside the spiraling shell-like structure in the center path
  • Magic Source: In a chest inside the entrance path house near the beds
  • Aurora Armlet: In a chest accessed by going right from the central hub and navigating the leafy path
  • Guard Source: In a chest on the path leading toward the sunken crystal altar
  • Elixir: Behind the central pedestal in the lower dwelling area
  • Water Ring: In a chest in the back of the fish-shaped house on the left path

Secrets

  • Resting in the bed on the top floor of the seashell house on the left path will trigger the story sequence allowing you to proceed to the altar at night.
