Cave of the Gi

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Exploring the cave beneath Cosmo Canyon

Bugenhagen opens the sealed door at Cosmo Canyon, leading Cloud and Red XIII into the subterranean cave to confront the spirits of the Gi tribe. The party must navigate oil-slicked caverns, bypass stone face gates, and defeat the undead boss at the end to learn the truth about Red XIII's father, Seto.

Items

  • Added Effect Materia: Resting on the ground along the pathway in the second major cavern screen
  • Black M-phone: Found inside a treasure chest along the upper path of the first cavern screen
  • Ether: Found in a treasure chest on the lower path of the first cavern screen
  • X-Potion: Inside a treasure chest tucked away in the second cavern area
  • Fairy Ring: Found inside a treasure chest in the cavern tunnels
  • Gravity Materia: Dropped onto the ground after defeating the boss Gi Nattak

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Gi NattakHoly, Fire, and healing items/magicBoss of the cave who is accompanied by two Soul Fires and is instantly killed by restorative items like an X-Potion or Phoenix Down due to undead status
Gi SpectorHoly and healing items/magicUndead humanoid spirit that can inflict status ailments and is easily dispatched with recovery magic
SneakyIceInsect-like enemy that crawls along the tunnel paths

Tips

  • Walking instead of running across the oily patches on the ground prevents the party from slipping into spiked pits.
  • Red XIII is a mandatory party member for the entirety of this dungeon.
  • Using an X-Potion or Phoenix Down directly on Gi Nattak ends the boss fight immediately.
