Mt. Nibel

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Traveling toward Rocket Town

Cloud and the party navigate the treacherous paths and interior cavern of Mt. Nibel after passing through Nibelheim on their way to Rocket Town. The area requires navigating rocky ridges, kicking down ladders to create shortcuts, and passing through a Mako reactor interior. Along the way, the party encounters the boss Materia Keeper before exiting to the northern continent.

Items

  • Rune Blade: In a chest on the mountain path exterior near the first bridge
  • Plus Barrette: In a chest inside the cavern's upper pathway
  • Powersoul: In a chest reached via a hidden path in the cavern
  • Elixir: In a chest found in the winding cavern pathways
  • Jem Ring: In a chest on the upper ledge overlooking the cavern entrance
  • Counter Materia: Found on the ground right where the Materia Keeper boss was standing after defeating it
  • All Materia: In a chest located in the upper caverns reached by extending a ladder

Tips

  • Equip the Enemy Skill Materia before fighting the Materia Keeper to learn the powerful lightning spell Trine.
  • Be sure to kick down the folded ladders on the upper cavern bridges to easily access lower paths and backtrack if necessary.
