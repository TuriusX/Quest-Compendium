Midgar

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Returning to infiltrate the city

Cloud and the party parachute into Midgar near the end of Disc 2 to shut down the Mako Cannon before Hojo can fire it. The team must navigate the underground train tunnels, defeat the Turks and the Proud Clod, and ascend the Sister Ray to confront Hojo.

Items

  • Aegis Armlet MISSABLE: Chest on the winding track path before entering the tunnels
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Chest found on the train tracks near the start of the infiltration
  • Starlight Phone MISSABLE: In a chest in the winding underground duct area
  • Max Ray MISSABLE: Chest on the left side of the platform where the team regroups before going into the train tunnels
  • Behemoth Horn MISSABLE: Chest on the stairs inside the Shinra Building when backtracking south through the tunnels
  • Pile Banger MISSABLE: Found on the upper floors of the Shinra Building when backtracking
  • Master Fist MISSABLE: Found in the Shinra Building workout room when backtracking
  • Glowgger MISSABLE: Chest on the path leading up toward the Sister Ray
  • Missing Score MISSABLE: Chest on the stairway up to Sister Ray with Barret in the active party

Secrets

  • You can turn south in the train tunnel maze and run all the way back to Sector 8 to re-enter the Shinra Headquarters building to collect missed items.

Tips

  • Bring Barret in your active battle party when climbing the Sister Ray stairs, or his ultimate weapon Missing Score will not appear.
  • The Turks will offer an option to avoid fighting them entirely if you completed the Wutai pagoda side quest earlier in the game.
