Cosmo Canyon (Post-Disc 1)

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Observing the Planet's defense

The party returns to Cosmo Canyon on Disc 2 to consult with Bugenhagen regarding the Planet's impending crisis after the barrier at the Northern Crater is raised. Bugenhagen joins the team temporarily to guide Cloud and the others toward the Forgotten Capital, unlocking his observatory's planetarium mechanism for further revelations. On Disc 3, returning here triggers an emotional scene involving Bugenhagen's final moments and grants Red XIII his ultimate weapon.

Items

  • Limited Moon: Received from Bugenhagen in his observatory on Disc 3 with Red XIII in the party
  • Full Cure Materia: Found in the back room of the Item Shop once Bugenhagen removes the seal later in the game

Secrets

  • Returning to the sealed room behind the Item Shop late in the game reveals the Full Cure Materia, an Elixir, and a Magic Source lying on the floor.

Shops and people

  • Tiger Lily (Weapon Shop): Sells Butterfly Edge, Tiger Fangs, Heavy Vulcan, Prism Staff, Silver Rifle, Pinwheel, Green Megaphone, and various armor sets.
  • Materia Shop: Sells MP Plus, HP Plus, Restore, Heal, Revive, Fire, Ice, Lightning, and Earth Materia.

Tips

  • Be sure to bring Red XIII to the observatory after the events beneath Midgar on Disc 3 to obtain his ultimate weapon, the Limited Moon.
