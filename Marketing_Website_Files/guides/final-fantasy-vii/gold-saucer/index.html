Gold Saucer

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Searching for Barret's past

Cloud and the party visit the massive amusement park Gold Saucer seeking a Keystone and answers regarding Barret's past. While exploring the attractions, they meet the fortune-telling robot Cait Sith, who forces himself into the group as a playable character. The visit turns dark when a slaughter at the Battle Square is blamed on a man with a gun-arm, resulting in the party being cast down into the Corel Prison.

Secrets

  • GP Exchange NPC | Occasionally, a man appears near the save point at the Station entrance offering to sell GP directly for Gil at an exchange rate of 100 Gil per 1 GP.

Shops and people

  • Station Ticket Booth: Sells a single-entry pass for 3,000 Gil or a lifetime Gold Ticket granting unlimited entry for 30,000 Gil.
  • Wonder Square Exchange Counter: Trades earned GP for various prizes, including items, accessories, and Materia like Gil Plus and Exp Plus.
  • Speed Square Attraction: Offers high-score minigame rewards for shooting targets on the roller coaster, including Cloud's Umbrella weapon.

Tips

  • Buying the lifetime Gold Ticket for 30,000 Gil as early as possible will save significant Gil and hassle, as you will return to the Gold Saucer multiple times throughout the game.
  • Minigames in the Wonder Square, such as the basketball-style Super Dunk, provide a consistent way to earn GP without relying on random NPC spawns.
