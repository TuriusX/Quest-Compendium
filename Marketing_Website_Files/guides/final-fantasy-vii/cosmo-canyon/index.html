Cosmo Canyon

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Learning about the Planet and Red XIII's past

The party visits Cosmo Canyon, the homeland of Red XIII, to repair the broken buggy and learn about the study of Planet life known as Study of Planet Life or Valley of the Fallen Star. Bugenhagen guides the group through the observatory to explain the Lifestream and the fate of the dying Planet before opening the sealed Cave of the Gi. Conquering the cavern reveals the truth behind Red XIII's father, Seto, inspiring Red XIII to continue the journey to save the world.

Items

  • Added Effect Materia MISSABLE: Cave of the Gi, lying on the ground along the path
  • Gravity Materia: Cave of the Gi, dropped after defeating the boss Gi Nattak
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Cave of the Gi, found inside a treasure chest
  • Seraph Comb: Cosmo Canyon, given by Bugenhagen to Red XIII after defeating Gi Nattak

Secrets

  • The Turtle's Paradise Newsletter No. 5 is posted on the wall beside the inn desk.
  • Full Cure Materia can be retrieved on Disc 2 or 3 in the back room of the item shop once the blocked door opens.

Shops and people

  • Cosmo Canyon Item Shop: Sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Ethers, and status recovery items
  • Cosmo Canyon Materia Shop: Sells HP Plus Materia, MP Plus Materia, and basic magic Materia
  • Cosmo Canyon Weapon Shop: Sells mid-game equipment upgrades for party members, including weapons with improved Materia growth

Tips

  • Walk slowly instead of running across the oily patches of floor in the Cave of the Gi to avoid slipping into spiked walls and taking damage.
  • Equip the Added Effect Materia paired with an elemental or status Materia to grant either offensive status strikes or defensive status immunities.
