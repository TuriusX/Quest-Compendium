Bone Village

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Searching for the path to the Sleeping Forest

Cloud and the party arrive at this excavation site on the northern continent while pursuing Sephiroth and Aerith toward the Forgotten Capital. You must hire excavation workers to dig up the Lunar Harp, which is required to awaken and clear a path through the Sleeping Forest. After uncovering the harp, you can proceed northeast into the forest, or return later to dig for rare treasures.

Items

  • Lunar Harp: excavated on the upper plateau, generally near the tent or smoke

Secrets

  • Barmell Nut | excavated on the upper level | missable: no
  • Key to Sector 5 | excavated on the lower level near the crashed plane after Disc 1 | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Bone Village merchant: sells Diamond Bangle, Rune Armlet, Circlet, Silver Glasses, and Headband
  • Excavation Foreman: offers to set up digging staff for 100 gil per worker and detonates the search charge

Tips

  • Place your excavation workers across different elevations to triangulate the exact dig spot before detonating the explosive.
  • Watching the direction the workers turn their heads after the explosion points directly toward where the hidden item is buried.
