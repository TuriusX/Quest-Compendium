Gongaga

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Visiting the reactor and Aerith's hometown

Gongaga is an optional forest settlement featuring a destroyed Mako Reactor that Cloud's party can explore after crossing the ocean with the buggy. The party runs into the Turks, Reno and Rude, for a boss fight in the jungle path, and can discover lore about Zack Fair by visiting the village and talking to his parents. Exploring the ruined reactor provides valuable Materia, while the village houses weapon and accessory vendors.

Items

  • Titan Materia: Found in the ruined reactor core by inspecting the debris after the Turks encounter
  • Deathblow Materia: Lying on the ground near a path fork in the jungle area
  • White M-phone: Inside a treasure chest in the village house containing Zack's parents
  • X-Potion: Found in a treasure chest inside the ruined building at the Gongaga Village entrance

Secrets

  • Zack Fair dialogue cutscene: Bring Aerith and Tifa into the small house on the southeast side of Gongaga Village to speak with the elderly couple, triggering a scene about Zack's past.

Shops and people

  • Gongaga Weapon Shop: Sells Hardedge, Grand Glove, Atomic Scissors, Heavy Vulcan, Diamond Pin, Silver M-phone, Striking Staff, and Slash Edge.
  • Gongaga Accessory Shop: Sells Peace Ring, Star Pendant, Silver Glasses, Talisman, and Headband.
  • Gongaga Item Shop: Sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Soft, Maiden's Kiss, Cornucopia, Antidote, and Tent.

Tips

  • Be sure to grab the Titan Materia from the blown-out reactor core immediately after the scene with Scarlet and Tseng, before leaving the screen.
  • You can use the buggy to cross the shallow river shallows south of Gongaga to easily return to the Gold Saucer and Corel areas.
