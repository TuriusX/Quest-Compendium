Nibelheim

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Investigating Cloud and Sephiroth's past

Cloud and the party arrive in Nibelheim during the present day, only to discover the town completely rebuilt and populated by strange cloaked figures who deny the fire ever happened. The party must search the town and the Shinra Mansion to learn the truth about Cloud's and Sephiroth's shared past and follow Sephiroth's trail toward Mt. Nibel.

Items

  • Twin Viper: Inside a chest in the Shinra Mansion
  • Enemy Launcher: Inside a chest on the upper floor of the Shinra Mansion
  • Silver M-phone: Inside a chest in the Shinra Mansion
  • Platinum Fists: Inside a chest in the Shinra Mansion
  • Odin Summon Materia MISSABLE: Dropped by Lost Number inside the Shinra Mansion safe
  • Key to the Basement: Found inside the opened safe in the Shinra Mansion
  • Destruct Materia: Thrown at Cloud by Sephiroth in the basement library

Secrets

  • Vincent Valentine recruitment: Unlock the safe on the upper floor of the Shinra Mansion (combination: Right 36, Left 10, Right 59, Right 97 without passing the numbers), defeat the boss Lost Number to obtain the Key to the Basement, then unlock the crypt door in the basement and talk to the man sleeping in the coffin until he joins.
  • Tifa's Piano and Final Limit Break: Playing the correct melody on Tifa's piano later in the game (with Tifa in the party during Disc 2 or 3) awards her ultimate Limit Break manual, Final Heaven, and an Elemental Materia.

Shops and people

  • Nibelheim Item Shop: Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Tents, and basic status curatives

Tips

  • When inputting the combination for the Shinra Mansion safe, you have a strict 20-second time limit and you must stop exactly on each number without overshooting, or the safe will fail to open.
  • Talk to every black-cloaked person in town, as several of them will give you free items such as Potions, Ethers, or Luck Sources.
