Shinra Building

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Infiltrating the headquarters to rescue Aerith

Cloud, Barret, and Tifa infiltrate the Shinra Electric Power Company headquarters in Midgar to rescue the captive Aerith Gainsborough. They must scale the floors of the tower either by sneaking up the emergency stairs or fighting through the front entrance, navigate through corporate and laboratory floors, and retrieve keycards to ascend. After confronting Shinra executives and finding Aerith, the party is captured, leading to a bloody escape following the sudden appearance of Sephiroth.

Items

  • Elixir MISSABLE: Found in the emergency stairwell during the climb
  • Ether MISSABLE: Found in the emergency stairwell during the climb
  • Star Pendant MISSABLE: Found in the gym area on Floor 62
  • Four Slots MISSABLE: Given by Mayor Domino on Floor 62 if you guess his password on the first attempt
  • Keycard 65 MISSABLE: Rewarded on Floor 62 after correctly solving Mayor Domino's password puzzle
  • Midgar Parts MISSABLE: Found inside chests in the model room on Floor 65 to assemble the Midgar diorama
  • Enemy Skill Materia: Found inside the laboratory specimen tank after defeating Sample: H0512 on Floor 68
  • Poison Materia: Left on the floor of the laboratory specimen tank after the battle with Sample: H0512
  • Carbon Bangle MISSABLE: In a chest inside the Floor 67 laboratory specimen room

Secrets

  • You can enter the vent on Floor 64 and climb through the duct system to Floor 66 to eavesdrop on the Shinra executive conference.
  • If you inspect the vending machine on Floor 64 and bang on it, it jams; returning to this machine on the revisit to the Shinra Building in Disc 2 yields the Mind Source and Speed Source items.

Tips

  • If you choose to sneak up the emergency stairs instead of taking the lobby elevator, you avoid all random battles, but the climb takes several minutes of continuous running.
  • Equipping the Steal Materia on Floor 67 or 68 to steal the Hardedge from a SOLDIER:3rd grants Cloud a huge attack boost this early in the game.
  • Cloud fights Rufus alone, so ensure he has healing magic and a Restore Materia equipped before going out to the roof.
  • Long-range Materia or ranged weapons must be equipped on characters fighting Hundred Gunner, as melee attacks without Long Range cannot connect.
