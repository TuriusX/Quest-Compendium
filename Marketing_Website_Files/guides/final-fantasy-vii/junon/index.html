Junon

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Searching for a way to cross the ocean

Cloud and the party arrive at Under Junon seeking passage across the sea to the Western Continent. After saving Priscilla from a monster at the beach and resuscitating her, she loans her dolphin friend to launch Cloud up to Upper Junon. Once in the upper city, Cloud disguises himself as a Shinra soldier to infiltrate Rufus Shinra's welcoming parade and board the cargo ship.

Items

  • Shiva Materia: Given by Priscilla at Under Junon as a reward for saving her
  • Enemy Skill Materia: Found in the Respectable Inn classroom basement in Upper Junon
  • 1/64 Shinra Soldier: Found in the Respectable Inn classroom basement in Upper Junon
  • Mind Source: Found in a locker inside the soldier barracks in Upper Junon
  • 5,000 Gil MISSABLE: Awarded by the commander for achieving a TV broadcast rating of 51% or higher during the parade mini-game
  • Force Stealer MISSABLE: Awarded by Rufus for scoring over 100 points during the send-off rifle drill mini-game

Secrets

  • You can activate an alarm button on the wall in the hallway connecting Upper Junon sections to trigger encounters with high-level enemies like Slaps and Machine Golems early.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
BottomswellWindBoss fought at the beach in Under Junon that traps party members in water bubbles.

Shops and people

  • Under Junon Weapon Shop: Sells Mythril Saber, Cannon Ball, Shotgun, Premier Rod, Bronze Knuckle, and Giant Glove

Tips

  • During the resuscitation mini-game, wait until Cloud takes about ten breaths before pressing the action button to revive Priscilla efficiently.
  • Cast magic or long-range physical attacks on the Water Column bubbles Bottomswell creates to free trapped party members before they drown.
