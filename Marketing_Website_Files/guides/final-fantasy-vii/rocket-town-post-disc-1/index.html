Rocket Town (Post-Disc 1)

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Attempting to stop the rocket launch

Cloud and the party return to Rocket Town on Disc 2 to stop Shinra from launching the rocket loaded with Huge Materia into Meteor. After fighting through Shinra forces to reach the launch pad, you must defeat Rude and board the rocket before it blasts off into outer space. Inside the cockpit and cargo hold, you must solve a console puzzle to secure the Huge Materia and escape via an escape pod with Cid.

Items

  • Huge Materia MISSABLE: inside the rocket's fuel chamber console
  • Fourth Bracelet MISSABLE: reward from the old man staring at the rocket after speaking with him multiple times

Secrets

  • Unlock the Huge Materia container inside the rocket by inputting the code: Circle, Square, X, X (PlayStation default controls).

  • Weapon Shop: sells Shotgun, Master Fist, Platinum Fist, Butterfly Edge, and various mid-tier armors and accessories
  • Item Shop: sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-healing consumables

Tips

  • You have a strict time limit of three minutes inside the rocket to input the keypad code, retrieve the Huge Materia, and return to the cockpit.
  • Equip the Steal Materia before facing Rude on the rocket gantry to grab the powerful Ziedrich armor from him.
