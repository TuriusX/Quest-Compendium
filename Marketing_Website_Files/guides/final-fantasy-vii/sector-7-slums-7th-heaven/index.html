Sector 7 Slums - 7th Heaven

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Meeting Tifa and planning the next mission

Cloud arrives at AVALANCHE's hideout, the 7th Heaven bar, reuniting with his childhood friend Tifa. Inside, Barret pays Cloud for the first bombing run, and the group plans their upcoming assault on the Sector 5 Reactor. Before heading out, Cloud can rest, speak with the team, and purchase supplies around the Sector 7 Slums.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: Received from Tifa during the conversation at the bar if Cloud takes a drink

Secrets

  • You can interact with the pinball machine to access the hidden basement elevator used as AVALANCHE's command center.

Shops and people

  • 7th Heaven Bartender (Tifa): Drinks offered during story dialogue sequences

Tips

  • Rest up and purchase plenty of Potions and Phoenix Downs from the shops in the Sector 7 Slums outside the bar before boarding the train to Sector 5.
  • Be attentive to dialogue choices with Tifa inside the bar, as your answers influence her hidden affection score for the later Gold Saucer date.
