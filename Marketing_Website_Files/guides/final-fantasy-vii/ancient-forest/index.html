Ancient Forest

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Optional forest near Cosmo Canyon

The Ancient Forest is an optional puzzle-heavy area located on a high plateau near Cosmo Canyon. It can be reached either by breeding a Green, Black, or Gold Chocobo, or by defeating Ultimate Weapon late in the game so its explosion creates a natural land bridge. Players must navigate four screens by catching small fauna like insects and frogs to trigger carnivorous pitcher plants, vines, and snapping traps to bridge gaps.

Items

  • Spring Gun Clip: Found in a treasure chest on the first screen
  • Slash-All Materia: Found lying on the ground behind a giant hollow tree trunk on the final screen
  • Typoon Materia: Found floating inside the hollow trunk in the final screen
  • Minerva Band: Found inside a chest on the third screen reached via the high treetops
  • Apocalypse: Found inside a chest on the final screen
  • Elixir: Found in a chest on the third screen
  • Supershot ST: Found in a chest on the second screen

Secrets

  • The Apocalypse triple-growth sword is reached by clearing the final screen's puzzle and walking around the upper path into the clearing.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
KelzmelzerIceSmall snail-like creature that inflicts Poison.

Tips

  • If you place a frog into a pitcher plant, stand on it quickly before it spits the frog back out and propels you forward.
  • Giant red snapping traps will snap shut and deal heavy damage if you walk into them; drop an insect or frog into them first to safely walk across.
  • If you run out of creatures or get stuck on a puzzle, press the Cancel button or step on the screen exit to reset the fauna on that screen.
