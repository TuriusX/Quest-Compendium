Mideel

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Seeking Cloud in the village

After obtaining the airship Highwind, the party travels to the hot springs village of Mideel in search of the missing Cloud. Speaking to the locals and visiting the clinic leads to finding Cloud in an incapacitated state due to severe Mako poisoning. Tifa chooses to remain by his side at the clinic while the rest of the party continues their journey under a new temporary leader.

Items

  • Elixir MISSABLE: Inspect the shed behind the weapon shop; when finding a rusty key, attempt to unlock the back door of the weapon shop, tell the merchant the truth about breaking the key, and he will give you this item
  • "Contain" Materia MISSABLE: Feed a Mimett Green to the baby white Chocobo running around the village, then scratch it behind its ears
  • Curse Ring MISSABLE: Sneak behind the counter in the accessory shop and inspect the door/secret area at the back, then follow the merchant's prompt

Shops and people

  • Mideel Weapon Shop: Sells late-game weapons for party members including Crystal Sword, Crystal Glove, A·M Cannon, and Partisan
  • Mideel Materia Shop: Sells high-tier magic Materia including Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth, Restore, Heal, Revive, and Barrier
  • Mideel Accessory Shop: Sells status-protecting accessories including Peace Ring, Headband, Ribbon (during second visit only, but basic protection gear initially), and Talisman
  • Mideel Item Shop: Sells consumables including Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Ether, and status cures

Tips

  • Buy Mimett Greens from the Chocobo Sage or the Chocobo Farm before heading to Mideel, or buy Sylkis Greens to feed the baby Chocobo for the Contain Materia.
  • Pick up all village-exclusive items during this first visit, because Mideel will suffer severe structural destruction later in the story.
