Costa del Sol

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Arriving on the new continent

Cloud and the party arrive in the sunny resort town of Costa del Sol via cargo ship from Junon to continue their pursuit of Sephiroth on the western continent. After disembarking, you can explore the town, purchase supplies, relax on the beach to speak with Hojo, or rest at the inn before heading out to Mt. Corel.

Items

  • Ifrit Materia MISSABLE: Picked up automatically after defeating Jenova-BIRTH on the cargo ship right before arriving
  • Motor Drive: Inside the basement of the villa offered for sale by the wealthy man
  • Fire Ring: Inside the basement of the villa offered for sale by the wealthy man
  • Power Source: Inside the basement of the villa offered for sale by the wealthy man

Secrets

  • The luxury villa in town can be purchased by Cloud for 300,000 Gil from the man lounging in the bedroom, which permanently allows the party to rest there for free.
  • Kicking a soccer ball at Red XIII while he is on the beach will make him jump and react in irritation.

Shops and people

  • Costa del Sol Materia Shop: Sells Seal Materia, Transform Materia, Heal Materia, and Restore Materia
  • Costa del Sol Weapon Shop: Sells Platinum Dagger, Carbon Bangle, and Four Slots
  • Costa del Sol Item Shop: Sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Antidote, Eye Drops, Soft, and Tent

Tips

  • Rest at the inn or purchase the villa before leaving, as the journey through Mt. Corel ahead involves several long stretches without a free place to heal.
