Sector 5 Slums - Aerith's House

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Meeting Aerith and staying the night

After falling from Sector 5's plate into the church, Cloud escorts Aerith through the slums back to her residence. Inside, Cloud meets her adoptive mother Elmyra and agrees to stay the night before heading toward Sector 7 alone. During the night, Cloud must sneak past Aerith's bedroom without making a sound to avoid waking her.

Items

  • Cover Materia: Resting on the ground in Aerith's garden outside her house
  • Ether: In the small patch of flowers in the garden right next to Aerith's house
  • Potion: Hidden in the garden path leading to Aerith's house

Tips

  • Hold the walk button and walk slowly when leaving the second-floor bedroom, as running will cause the floorboards to creak and make Aerith stop you.
