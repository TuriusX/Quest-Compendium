Midgar Underground

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · The tunnels beneath the city

Cloud and the party infiltrate the Midgar Underground railway network during the return to Midgar on Disc 2 to stop Hojo. The team must navigate a series of winding train tunnels and maintenance shafts, culminating in a final showdown against the Turks and the giant mech Proud Clod. Progressing down the tracks eventually leads toward the Sister Ray cannon winding up at the Sector 8 Mako Cannon platform.

Items

  • Aegis Armlet MISSABLE: on the ground in the train tunnel section while running south
  • Elixir MISSABLE: on the path in the Midgar Underground tunnel tracks
  • Power/Speed/Magic/Guard Source MISSABLE: found in various chests scattered across the tunnel routes
  • Starlight Phone MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest along the maintenance crawlspaces
  • Max Drive MISSABLE: found in a chest in the winding underground Sector 8 tunnels

Secrets

  • Running backward (south) down the train tracks away from the destination leads to dead ends containing several hidden treasure chests and the W-Item Materia.
  • W-Item Materia | run all the way down to the southernmost dead end of the Sector 8 train tunnel | missable: yes

Tips

  • Equipping Steal materia before the Turks battle allows you to obtain rare armor pieces: the Ziedrich, Minerva Band, and Touph Ring.
  • You must head south down the tracks immediately upon entering the tunnels to get the W-Item Materia, as you cannot return here later in the game.
  • Destroying Proud Clod's Jammer Armor will prevent it from using its materia-jamming capabilities, making the rest of the fight much easier.
