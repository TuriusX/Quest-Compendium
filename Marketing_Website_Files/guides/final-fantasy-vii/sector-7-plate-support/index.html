Sector 7 Plate Support

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Climbing the pillar to stop the Turks

Cloud, Tifa, and Aerith arrive at Sector 7 to find Shinra attacking the support pillar in an attempt to crush the slums below. Aerith takes Marlene to safety while Cloud and Tifa climb the winding stairs, fighting through waves of Shinra troops to aid Barret at the summit. At the top, the party confronts Reno to stop the plate detonation sequence before making a desperate escape.

Items

  • Ether MISSABLE: In a treasure chest along the ascending stairway pathway

Tips

  • Equip the Poison Materia to hit Reno with Bio, as he is vulnerable to the status effect and it will chip away at his HP while you manage his Pyramid attacks.
  • Use a basic physical attack from an unaffected ally to shatter the Pyramid Reno traps a character in.
  • This entire sequence is a point of no return for the Sector 7 Slums; make sure you have collected everything from the train graveyard and slum district before finishing the climb.
