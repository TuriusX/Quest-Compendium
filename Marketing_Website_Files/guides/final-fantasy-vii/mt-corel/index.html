Mt. Corel

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Traveling toward the Gold Saucer

The party traverses the rocky heights and abandoned railway tracks of Mt. Corel to reach the impoverished town of North Corel. Along the tracks, Cloud must lower a bridge and optionally navigate crumbling rails to collect hidden treasures before reaching Barret's hometown. After a cold reception from the villagers in North Corel, the team boards a ropeway tram heading straight to the Gold Saucer.

Items

  • Wizard Staff: In a treasure chest on the winding mountain path before reaching the railway tracks
  • Star Pendant: In a treasure chest along the tracks near the first drawbridge
  • W-Machine Gun: Inside a chest found by falling through the tracks into the lower cavern
  • Turbo Ether: Found in a treasure chest reached by falling through a specific hole in the train tracks
  • Mind Source: Found in a chest in the lower cavern area accessed by falling through the railway
  • Power Source: Found in a treasure chest on the tracks near the switch control hut
  • Tent: In a chest inside the switch control shed where Cloud lowers the track bridge
  • Phoenix Down (x10): Placed in a bird's nest up a cliff path near the railway bridge

Secrets

  • When climbing the cliff above the railway bridge, choosing to take the treasure from the bird's nest gives 10 Phoenix Downs, but forces a battle with the protective Cokatolis parent birds.
  • Stepping on certain weakened wooden sections of the elevated train tracks causes Cloud to fall, but rapidly pressing the confirm button while falling allows him to steer toward hidden treasure chests in the caverns below.

Shops and people

  • North Corel Weapon Shop: Sells Carbon Bangle, Force Stealer, Butterfly Edge, and Silver Rifle
  • North Corel Item Shop: Sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Ether, Antidote, Eye Drops, and Tent

Tips

  • Equip the Steal materia on a fast party member to steal a Diamond Pin from Bagnadrana, which serves as a powerful weapon upgrade for Red XIII at this point in the game.
  • If you miss jumping to a treasure chest when falling through the railway tracks, you can walk back up and drop through the hole again as many times as needed.
