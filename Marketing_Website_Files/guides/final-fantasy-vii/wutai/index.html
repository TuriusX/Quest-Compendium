Wutai

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Optional side quest area

Wutai is an optional Asian-themed continent and settlement accessible once you have the Tiny Bronco, where Yuffie steals your party's Materia upon arrival. You must chase Yuffie throughout the town, deal with the Shinra Turks, and rescue both Yuffie and Elena from Don Corneo. Later, you can enter the Five-Story Pagoda with Yuffie alone to fight a series of solo bosses and earn her ultimate limit break.

Items

  • MP Absorb Materia: Chest in the secret room behind the folding screen in the Wutai shop
  • Swift Bolt: Found in the chest hidden inside the Turtle's Paradise restaurant
  • Leviathan Materia: Awarded for defeating Godo on the top floor of the Five-Story Pagoda
  • All Materia: Chest in the basement room of the house next to the entrance of Wutai
  • All Creation: Awarded for defeating Godo on the top floor of the Five-Story Pagoda
  • Magic Shuriken: Chest in the secret stash room in the house next to the town entrance
  • Hairpin: Chest in the Wutai Da-chao statue mountain path

Secrets

  • The Turtle's Paradise flyer #6 is posted on the wall inside the basement of Yuffie's house.
  • Inspecting the vase outside the Turtle's Paradise after Yuffie runs away reveals Yuffie hiding inside it.

Shops and people

  • Wutai Weapon Shop: Sells Murasame, Diamond Knuckle, Striking Staff, Slash Edge, Heavy Lance, Rocket Punch, and Wind Slash
  • Wutai Item Shop: Sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Hyper, Tranquilizer, Tent, and various elemental attack items

Tips

  • Because your Materia is stolen during the first visit, rely on items and weapons with high raw attack power until you resolve the Don Corneo incident.
  • Equip Yuffie with the Enemy Skill Materia when fighting Godo in the Pagoda so she can learn the Enemy Skill "Trine" if you missed it earlier.
  • Use the "Added Effect" Materia paired with "Time" or "Rest" on Yuffie's weapon to inflict status ailments easily during the Five-Story Pagoda trials.
