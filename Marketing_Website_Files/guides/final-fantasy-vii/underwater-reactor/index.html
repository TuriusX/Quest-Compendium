Underwater Reactor

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Retrieving the Huge Materia from the ocean floor

Cloud and the party infiltrate the underwater Mako reactor beneath Junon to stop Shinra from loading the third Huge Materia onto a submarine. After descending through an elevator and a glass ocean tunnel, you fight through reactor docks, confront Reno, and defeat the mechanical boss Carry Armor. Once victorious, you commandeer a red submarine, complete an underwater torpedo minigame, and secure the Huge Materia.

Items

  • Leviathan Scales MISSABLE: chest located inside the submarine dock passage before the boss arena
  • Scimitar MISSABLE: chest on the catwalk just before the submarine dock where the Huge Materia is loaded
  • Battle Trumpet MISSABLE: chest in the dock area leading toward the submarine

Secrets

  • Ghost Hand item | can be stolen from the Ghost Ship enemy encountered in the underwater glass tunnel or reactor pathways

Tips

  • Focus all single-target attacks on Carry Armor's main body or quickly destroy its arms using powerful Lightning or magic attacks before both of your companions get held captive.
  • Equipping the Morph Materia allows you to morph the Ghost Ship enemy into a Guide Book, which can later be traded in Kalm for the Underwater Materia.
  • You cannot return to the submarine docks once you complete the submarine battle, so be sure to open every treasure chest along the catwalks before boarding the vessel.
