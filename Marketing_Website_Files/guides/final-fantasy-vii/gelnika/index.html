Gelnika

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Exploring the crashed plane
← All areas in this guide

The Sunken Gelnika is a crashed Shinra transport plane located on the ocean floor, accessible exclusively via the submarine in Disc 2 and Disc 3. Inside, Cloud and the party navigate the flooded corridors and cargo bays while dealing with powerful mutated creatures and an optional boss encounter with the Turks. The wreck holds several endgame weapons, Limit Break manuals, and powerful Materia, making it a premier late-game exploration and training spot.

Tips
  • Equipping Yuffie with her Conformer weapon and the Morph Materia allows her to deal full physical damage while using Morph, turning every regular enemy here into permanent stat-boosting Source items.
  • Save your game at the save point in the entrance room before exploring deeper, as the standard enemies here hit exceptionally hard compared to most overworld foes.
