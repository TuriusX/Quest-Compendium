Sector 1 Mako Reactor

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · The second mission to bomb the reactor

Cloud, Barret, and Tifa infiltrate the Sector 5 Mako Reactor (often confused with the first mission, but the second reactor bombing targets Sector 5). The party descends through the facility to plant an explosive on the core before attempting to escape. Their retreat is interrupted by Shinra forces, leading to a confrontation with the Air Buster boss on the catwalks.

Items

  • Potion MISSABLE: Chest on the platform inside the core chamber
  • Ether MISSABLE: Chest located on the walkway leading down to the reactor core

Secrets

  • Three-button simultaneous press minigame | Press the action button at the exact same moment as Barret and Tifa to unlock the security door on the catwalk | missable: yes
  • Air Buster parts tampering | Terminal consoles found in the preceding maintenance rooms allow you to remove units from the Air Buster, weakening its capabilities during the fight (Note: Remake only; in the original game, no such console exists)

Tips

  • Lightning magic (Bolt) is extremely effective against all mechanical enemies in this reactor, including the boss.
  • Position your characters on opposite sides of the Air Buster so physical attacks from the rear deal double damage.
