Sector 7 Slums

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Returning to the 7th Heaven bar

After blowing up the Sector 1 Mako Reactor and riding the train back, Cloud and AVALANCHE arrive at their hideout inside the 7th Heaven bar in the Sector 7 Slums. Players explore the town, visit the bar to meet Tifa, receive payment for the mission, and rest before the next assignment. The visit also provides access to the beginner's tutorial hall and local shops to gear up.

Items

  • Potion: Lootable chest located on the upper floor of the Beginner's Hall
  • All Materia: Lootable orb lying on the floor in the Beginner's Hall next to the dog
  • Ether: Lootable chest inside the weapon shop, accessible via the small lift

Secrets

  • Punching the pinball machine inside the 7th Heaven bar gives a tiny Easter egg reaction, and using its lift allows Cloud to descend into AVALANCHE's secret basement meeting room.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells basic consumables including Potion, Phoenix Down, and Antidote
  • Weapon Shop: Sells Iron Bangle and basic early-game weapons
  • Materia Shop: Sells Restore Materia and basic elemental magic Materia like Fire, Ice, and Lightning

Tips

  • Visit the Beginner's Hall on the second floor above the weapon shop to collect a free All Materia and learn foundational combat mechanics.
  • Be sure to talk to Tifa when she asks about Materia to ensure you receive the Materia tutorial and setup prompts before departing on the next mission.
