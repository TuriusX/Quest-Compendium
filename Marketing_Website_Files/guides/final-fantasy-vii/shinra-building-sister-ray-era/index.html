Shinra Building (Sister Ray Era)

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Returning to the headquarters

During the late-game raid on Midgar, Cloud and his party can return to the Shinra Headquarters to scavenge powerful equipment and ultimate weapons. You gain access via the underground train tunnels and Midgar streets before heading up the Sister Ray cannon. Many floors are deserted, but select upper floors contain crucial end-game weapons and items for certain party members.

Items

  • Master Fist MISSABLE: Found in a locker on the 59th floor
  • Pile Banger MISSABLE: Found inside a locker in the gym locker room on the 64th floor
  • HP Shout MISSABLE: Found inside one of the lockers on the 64th floor
  • Behemoth Horn MISSABLE: Found on the stairwell between the 67th and 68th floors
  • Glow Lance MISSABLE: Found in the chest inside the Research Room on the 68th floor
  • Mind Source MISSABLE: Found on the 63rd floor inside the vents/rooms

Secrets

  • You can purchase a restorative item from the broken vending machine on the 64th floor gym by banging on it during this visit, yielding an item if you interacted with it during your first visit in Disc 1.
  • Checking the gym lockers on the 64th floor during this raid yields Cait Sith's ultimate weapon, the HP Shout, which was previously inaccessible during the first visit.

Enemies

Tips

  • Enter the front doors of the Shinra Building immediately from the Midgar street level rather than rushing straight to the Sister Ray, as this is your only chance to gather these high-tier weapons.
  • The elevator can be used to quickly travel between the lower lobby and the upper floors, bypassing the long emergency stairwell.
