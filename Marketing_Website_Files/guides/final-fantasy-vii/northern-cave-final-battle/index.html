Northern Cave (Final Battle)

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · The final journey to the core

The party descends to the core of the planet deep inside the Northern Cave to confront Sephiroth and stop Meteor. After assembling at the bottom, Cloud and his companions engage in a multi-stage final confrontation consisting of Jenova-SYNTHESIS, Bizarro∙Sephiroth, Safer∙Sephiroth, and a scripted 1-on-1 duel. Defeating Safer∙Sephiroth triggers the ending sequence of the game.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Jenova-SYNTHESISThe first encounter in the final gauntlet; begins a countdown to cast the devastating Ultima spell when low on health.
Bizarro∙SephirothCan be fought by up to three separate parties depending on prior actions; destroying the core ends the battle.
Safer∙SephirothThe true final boss; uses powerful status attacks like Pale Horse, inflicts Super Nova, and casts Heartless Angel to drop HP to 1.
SephirothA scripted duel inside Cloud's mind where Cloud automatically counters with Omnislash or attacks to win.

Tips

  • Equip Ribbon accessories on your main party members to neutralize the devastating status effects inflicted by Safer∙Sephiroth's Super Nova and Pale Horse.
  • If Bizarro∙Sephiroth splits your team into multiple parties, make sure materia and equipment are distributed across reserve members beforehand.
  • Cast Big Guard, Wall, or Regen immediately after Safer∙Sephiroth uses Heartless Angel to survive subsequent attacks.
