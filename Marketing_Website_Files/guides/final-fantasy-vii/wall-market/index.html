Wall Market

FINAL FANTASY VII guide · Searching for Tifa and infiltrating Don Corneo's mansion

Cloud arrives in Wall Market to locate Tifa after she was seen being taken toward Don Corneo's mansion. To gain entry into the mansion and reach Corneo, Cloud must disguise himself as a woman by gathering various disguise items from the town's residents and shops. Once properly dressed, Cloud and Aerith infiltrate the mansion to rescue Tifa and interrogate Don Corneo about Shinra's plans.

Items

  • Cotton Dress MISSABLE: Obtained from the boutique tailor after talking to his father at the bar and selecting specific options
  • Silk Dress MISSABLE: Obtained from the boutique tailor by choosing "soft" and "shimmery" when speaking to his father at the bar
  • Satin Dress MISSABLE: Obtained from the boutique tailor by choosing other dialogue options at the bar
  • Blonde Wig MISSABLE: Won at the gym by defeating the athlete Big Bro in a squat minigame
  • Dyed Wig MISSABLE: Received at the gym if you tie or lose slightly in the squat minigame
  • Wig MISSABLE: Consolation prize received at the gym if you lose badly in the squat minigame
  • Diamond Tiara MISSABLE: Obtained from the Materia shop owner after staying at the inn and buying the most expensive item from the vending machine
  • Ruby Tiara MISSABLE: Obtained from the Materia shop owner after buying the mid-tier item from the vending machine
  • Glass Tiara MISSABLE: Obtained from the Materia shop owner after buying the cheapest item from the vending machine
  • Sexy Cologne MISSABLE: Acquired in the restaurant by ordering a meal and saying the food was "alright" or "good" to receive a pharmacy coupon, then exchanging it for digestif and giving it to the sick person in the bar's bathroom
  • Ether: Found in a chest in the weapon shop's back room

Secrets

  • If Cloud collects the best possible set of disguise items (Silk Dress, Blonde Wig, Diamond Tiara, Sexy Cologne, and mystery panties from the Honeybee Inn), Don Corneo chooses Cloud instead of Tifa or Aerith.

Tips

  • Save your game before starting the squat minigame at the gym so you can reload if you fail to win the Blonde Wig.
  • Don Corneo drops Cloud, Tifa, and Aerith down a trapdoor into the Sewers after the interrogation, initiating an immediate boss fight with Aps.
