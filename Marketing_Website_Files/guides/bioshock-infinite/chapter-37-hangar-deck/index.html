Chapter 37 - Hangar Deck

BioShock Infinite guide · Booker repels assault waves aboard the vessel's mooring platform ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Booker DeWitt and Elizabeth fight across the outer decks of Father Comstock's flagship airship, the Hand of the Prophet. Booker must clear enemy blockages from cargo Sky-Lines while fighting off waves of Vox Populi forces boarding from aerial barges. After surviving the onslaught and freeing the rails, Booker rides the Sky-Line upward toward the vessel's upper decks.

Secrets

0/1

Key fights
Sky-Line Clearance Siege

Enemies: Vox Populi forces, Volley Gun troops, and Motorized Patriots

Threats: High-volume suppression fire from Crank Guns, explosive Volley Gun bombardment, and enclosed barge drop-offs

Weaknesses and resistances: Back gear vulnerability on Motorized Patriots; human Vox susceptible to crowd-control Vigors and environmental tosses

Tactics: Ride the surrounding Sky-Lines to maintain elevation, deploy Undertow to fling heavy enemies overboard, and focus fire on the Motorized Patriots' engines

Rewards: Clears the Sky-Line track, granting passage to the airship's upper levels

Enemies
Vox Populi

Foot soldiers who board in waves from gunships and barges, wielding standard firearms and heavy weaponry like Volley Guns.

Motorized Patriot
Weak to Back-mounted gear mechanismSteal / drop Crank Gun

Heavily armored robotic units that continuously advance while firing automatic weapons.

Tips
  • Use the Undertow Vigor to sweep enemy soldiers straight off the sides of the airship for instant, low-cost eliminations.
