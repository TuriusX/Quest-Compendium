Soldier's Field

BioShock Infinite guide · The amusement park area
← All areas in this guide

Booker DeWitt and Elizabeth arrive at Soldier's Field, a patriotic amusement park, aiming to reach the First Lady airship to escape Columbia. After the gondola power is cut, Booker must navigate the park to the Hall of Heroes to retrieve the Shock Jockey Vigor to restore power. The area introduces the Bucking Bronco Vigor and sets up major combat encounters across plazas and exhibits.

Spawns on high ground or distant airships; attacks with slow-moving but high-damage explosive rockets.

Shops and people
  • Dollar Bill: Health kits, salts, and basic weapon ammunition
  • Minuteman's Armory: Upgrades for the Pistol, Machine Gun, Shotgun, and Carbine
  • Veni Vidi Vigor: Upgrades for Vigors including Possession and Devil's Kiss

Tips
  • Have Elizabeth summon tears such as the automated Turret or the Freight Hook to gain high-ground advantage and draw fire during the large courtyard firefight.
  • Use Possession on the Motorized Patriot to turn its devastating Crank Gun against other enemies, buying time to safely target its weak spot on its back.
  • Look above the walkways for Skyline rails; riding them gives you access to high flanking routes and allows you to perform lethal Skyline strikes on grounded foes.
