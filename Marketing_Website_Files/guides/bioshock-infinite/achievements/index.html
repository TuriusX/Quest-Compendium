Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Boarding the gondola at Battleship Bay: Returning to Lighthouse, Raffle Square, Comstock Center Rooftops, Monument Island Gateway, Monument Island, and Battleship Bay to collect missed Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, or Infusions.
  • Boarding the First Lady airship at Return to Soldier's Field: Returning to Soldier's Field and Hall of Heroes to collect missed Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, or Infusions.
  • Boarding the First Lady airship at Return to Finkton: Returning to Finkton Proper, Shantytown, and The Bull House to collect missed Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, or Infusions.
  • Entering Comstock House from Downtown Emporia: Returning to Port Prosperity and Downtown Emporia to collect missed Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, or Infusions.
  • Boarding the Hand of the Prophet at The Atrium: Returning to Comstock House and The Atrium to collect missed Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, or Infusions.
  • Reaching the final encounter at Hangar Deck: Freely exploring the Hand of the Prophet and completing story-related collectible achievements before finishing the game.
  • Purchasing any item or ammo from a Dollar Bill vending machine in 1999 Mode: The Scavenger Hunt achievement for the remainder of that playthrough.

Recommended order
  1. Play through the campaign on Medium or Hard while picking up all Voxophones, Kinetoscopes, Telescopes, and Infusions, and working on weapon and Vigor combat achievements.
  2. Clean up any remaining weapon, Vigor, and miscellaneous combat achievements via Chapter Select.
  3. Complete the campaign on 1999 Mode without purchasing any items or upgrades from Dollar Bill vending machines to unlock the final difficulty achievements.
