Prey achievement guide

How to unlock every achievement, which ones you can miss, and the best order to get them all. Tick them off as you go.

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Locking the Cargo Bay blast door during "Shipping and Receiving": Cargo Bay survivors will die if breached without defenses, locking out "Do No Harm" and "I and Thou"
  • Entering the escape pod with Alex or allowing Dahl's shuttle to leave early: Aborts access to remaining Talos I sectors, failing all active survivor quests
  • Completing "The Repo Man" and triggering the Apex Typhon's arrival: Station-wide traversal becomes heavily restricted, corrupting certain areas and permanently despawning several human NPCs
  • Activating the Nullwave Device or Self-Destruct Key at the climax: Reaches the ending sequence, permanently ending the current playthrough with no free-roam post-game

Recommended order
  1. Playthrough 1 (Human Abilities Only): Explore thoroughly, rescue every human survivor, track all crew members, read all emails, and finish without installing any Typhon Neuromods.
  2. TranStar Apartment to Hardware Labs: Acquire the Disruptor Stun Gun, hunt for emails/employee bracelets, and prepare non-lethal tools for mind-controlled humans.
  3. Talos I Exterior to G.U.T.S.: Scan your first enemies, navigate zero-g sectors carefully, and track floating corpses for crew roster achievements.
  4. Arboretum to Crew Quarters: Save all mind-controlled survivors in the Fitness Center and Cafeteria, resolve the cook impostor situation, and recover personal audio logs.
  5. Deep Storage to Life Support: Escape confinement, restore primary environmental systems, and neutralize the Water Treatment Facility mimic infestation.
  6. Power Plant to Cargo Bay: Reboot the station reactor, ensure survivor safety in Cargo Bay A/B by arming the turrets, and complete side objectives for Sarah Elazar.
  7. Shuttle Bay to Talos I Bridge: Prevent Dahl from assassinating key staff, incapacitate Dahl non-lethally with the Stun Gun/Disruptor, and save Igwe and Mikhaila.
  8. Arboretum & Exterior (Endgame): Secure the Apex Typhon scan targets, prepare the Nullwave or Self-Destruct protocol, and perform a manual save before the climax.
  9. Playthrough 1 Conclusion: Earn "Do No Harm", "I and Thou", "Split Affinity" (Human), and alternate endings via reloading the final save.
  10. Playthrough 2 (Typhon Abilities Only + Kill Everyone): Install only Typhon Neuromods, hunt down every living human on Talos I for "I and It", and trigger the opposite station destruction choice.
  11. Playthrough 3 (No Needles): Complete the main story on an easy difficulty setting without installing a single Neuromod (Human or Typhon).
  12. Cleanup: Farm lingering combat-specific trophies (Mimic kills, environmental kills, zero-g hazards) via earlier manual saves or a quick extra run.
