Cargo Bay

Prey guide · Defending against the Typhon breach
← All areas in this guide

Morgan arrives in the Cargo Bay to find human survivors barricaded inside Cargo Bay A, led by Sarah Elazar. To proceed to Life Support and secure the survivors, Morgan must fortify Cargo Bay B with turrets and open the blast doors to eliminate the Typhon breach. You can either fulfill Elazar's request to set up turrets to help defend the group or take on the alien forces independently.

Weak to Kinetic debris thrown at them, explosive canistersSteal / drop Typhon Organs

Small suicidal organisms that swarm toward any fast-moving object or sound

Tips
  • Fabricate at least three functioning turrets and deploy them in the designated defensive zone in Cargo Bay B before opening the door to minimize survivor casualties.
  • Throw small physical objects or luggage into groups of Cystoids to detonate them safely from a distance before opening the main blast door.
  • Use the GLOO Cannon to freeze incoming Phantoms inside the doorway so the deployed turrets can gun them down without taking damage.
