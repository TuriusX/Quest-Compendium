Points of no return

  • Entering the Unity: Resets the game world into New Game Plus, temporarily resetting outposts, faction quest progress, and relationships (though achievements can still be earned in the new cycle).

Recommended order
  1. Begin the campaign on Vectera and progress through the Kreet Research Lab to unlock basic story progression achievements and reach New Atlantis.
  2. Report to The Lodge to join Constellation, recruit companions, and start working on companion affinity achievements through active conversations and shared exploration.
  3. Travel back to the Argos Extractors Mining Outpost to recruit Lin and Heller for crew assignment achievements.
  4. Complete the early main story missions visiting Nova Galactic Staryard, Indum II, and The Scow while gathering resources and establishing your first outpost network for extraction and cargo link achievements.
  5. Visit Akila City and enter The Rock to complete the entire Freestar Collective faction storyline for its associated quest achievements.
  6. Travel to Neon, head to Ryujin Tower, and play through the full Ryujin Industries espionage questline to earn all corporate quest achievements.
  7. Infiltrate or join the Crimson Fleet at The Key and infiltrate SY-920 during the UC Vanguard and Crimson Fleet storyline, completing the chain to secure the fleet faction achievements.
  8. Go to Cydonia on Mars to fulfill United Colonies faction quests and craft items, modify weapons, and build ships at the local technician stations.
  9. Progress through the late campaign at NASA Launch Facility and Masada III, ensuring you unlock all starborn powers, collect organic/inorganic resources, and survey star systems.
  10. Enter the Unity to complete the main story, unlocking the final campaign achievements, and use New Atlantis (New Game Plus), The Key (New Game Plus), Akila City (New Game Plus), Cydonia (New Game Plus), or Masada III (New Game Plus) only if you still need remaining levels, crafting, or general cumulative milestones.
