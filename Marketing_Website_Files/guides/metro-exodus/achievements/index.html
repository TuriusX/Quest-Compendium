Points of no return

  • Driving the railcar to the train in Volga: Exploring Volga side content, finding collectibles, and rescuing prisoners
  • Lowering the drawbridge in The Bridge: Any remaining Volga achievements, collectibles, and Duke surviving (Duke achievement)
  • Entering the elevator after fighting the cannibal leader in Yamantau Bunker: All Yamantau Bunker collectibles and the Righteous Vengeance achievement
  • Starting the assault on the oil rig in Caspian: Free exploration of the Caspian desert, local side quests, and finding missed collectibles
  • Reaching the top floor of the oil rig in Caspian: Damir staying with the crew (Damir achievement) and remaining Caspian achievements
  • Using the zipline after speaking to the church leader in Taiga: The Pioneer Camp area, early Taiga collectibles, and the Forest Child achievement progress
  • Entering the boat to navigate the marsh swamp in Taiga: The Pirate Camp area, middle Taiga collectibles, and Alyosha avoiding injury (Alyosha achievement)
  • Dropping into the underground metro tunnel from the surface in Dead City: Surface Dead City collectibles and the Guide achievement
  • Entering the Institute extraction sequence in Dead City: Remaining Dead City collectibles and all Good Ending requirements (Your Destination achievement)

Recommended order
  1. Playthrough 1 on Reader or Normal difficulty, focusing on the Good Karma path, keeping all crew members alive (Duke, Damir, Alyosha), finding all 71 Diary Pages and 21 Postcards, and earning area-specific action achievements.
  2. Clean up any missed combat or situational achievements via Chapter Select if necessary before starting the next playthrough.
  3. Playthrough 2 on Ranger Hardcore difficulty, focusing on surviving, managing resources, avoiding unnecessary combat, and completing the game to unlock the bad ending and final difficulty achievements.
