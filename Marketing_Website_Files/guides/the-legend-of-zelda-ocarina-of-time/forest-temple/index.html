Forest Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · First adult dungeon to save the Forest Sage

Link enters this abandoned mansion deep within the Sacred Forest Meadow to awaken Saria as the Forest Sage. To reach the deepest chamber, you must hunt down and defeat the four Poe Sisters to restore the elevator's colored flames. Along the way, you must navigate twisting hallways, outdoor courtyards, and rotating rooms to unlock the lair of Phantom Ganon.

Items

  • Fairy Bow: Defeat the Stalfos mini-boss in the central courtyard room
  • Dungeon Map: Defeat the Blue Bubble in the room directly after the entrance hall
  • Compass: Open the treasure chest in the high alcove of the outdoor courtyard garden
  • Boss Key: Defeat the Floormaster in the rotating room and drop through the hole
  • Small Key: Inside a treasure chest on the tree branch in the very first room
  • Small Key: Dropped by an enemy in the outdoor courtyard garden after draining the well
  • Small Key: Found in the twisted corridor room after untwisting the hallway with an arrow switch
  • Small Key: Dropped by Floormaster in the checkerboard ceiling room
  • Small Key: Defeat the two Stalfos in the room preceding the Fairy Bow

Secrets

  • Gold Skulltula | On the vines in the entrance room; kill it with the Hookshot before climbing
  • Gold Skulltula | On the wall of the outdoor courtyard garden reachable using the Hookshot
  • Gold Skulltula | On the island in the drained well; drop down and grab the token
  • Gold Skulltula | On the high ledge near the red Poe sister's painting room
  • Gold Skulltula | Inside the rotating basement chamber before the boss door, hidden behind a rotating stone wall

Tips

  • If you ever get stuck in the twisted hallways, shoot the golden eye switch above the door to straighten or twist the room again.
  • Look up at the ceiling in the room with the falling checkerboard grid; safe spots under the holes in the tiles allow you to avoid getting crushed.
  • You must destroy the three small hands immediately after defeating a Floormaster, or they will merge back into a full-sized monster.
