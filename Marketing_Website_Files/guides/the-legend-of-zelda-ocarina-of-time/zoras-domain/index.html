Zora's Domain

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Meeting King Zora to gain access to Zora's Fountain

Link visits this aquatic cavern as a child to ask King Zora for the Spiritual Stone of Water. After winning a mini-game to retrieve the Silver Scale, Link must dive into Lake Hylia to find a message in a bottle from Princess Ruto. Showing this letter to King Zora causes him to slowly slide aside, opening the entrance to Zora's Fountain.

Items

  • Silver Scale: Reward for completing the Diving Game at the top of the waterfall
  • Piece of Heart: In a chest behind the waterfall that appears after lighting all four unlit torches at the bottom of the cavern with a Deku Stick

Secrets

  • Gossip Stone located beneath the throne room staircase | Hit it with various weapons to make it bounce, stretch, or launch into the air

Shops and people

  • Zora Shop: Sells Fish, Deku Nuts, Deku Sticks, Arrows, and Recovery Hearts (as child) or Zora Tunic (as adult)
  • Diving Game Zora: Offers the diving mini-game at the top of the falls for 20 Rupees

Tips

  • Use a Deku Stick to carry fire from the torch in front of King Zora down the stairs and through the shallow water quickly before it burns out to light all torches for the Piece of Heart.
  • King Zora moves very slowly when clearing the path to Zora's Fountain, but you can wait or leave the area to skip waiting.
