Kakariko Village (Adult Era)

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Investigating the village after the Forest Temple

After completing the Forest Temple, adult Link visits Kakariko Village to find a safe haven connecting to Death Mountain and the Graveyard. You can use newly acquired adult equipment, such as the Hookshot, to reach rooftops, obtain collectibles, and prepare for upcoming temples. The village also serves as a hub to trade items in the adult trading sequence and deposit collected Gold Skulltula tokens in the House of Skulltula.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: Given by the man sitting on a rooftop, reached by Hookshooting onto the roof of the potion shop or using the Longshot
  • Pocket Egg: Given by the Cucco Lady standing near the center of the village to begin the adult trading sequence
  • Cojiro: Given by the Cucco Lady after hatching the Pocket Cucco and using it to wake Talon in the center house
  • Odd Mushroom: Given by the master craftsman's son in the Lost Woods, but initiated via Cojiro from Kakariko
  • Stone of Agony: Reward from the House of Skulltula inside the village after collecting 20 Gold Skulltula tokens
  • Giant's Wallet: Reward from the House of Skulltula inside the village after collecting 30 Gold Skulltula tokens
  • Piece of Heart: Reward from the House of Skulltula inside the village after collecting 50 Gold Skulltula tokens

Secrets

  • Hookshoot the wooden plank on the roof of the tall windmill/well complex to reach the roof of the purple-roofed house where a man sits and awards a Piece of Heart.
  • A Gold Skulltula clings to the high wooden support beam of the village watchtower, which can be reached with the Hookshot.

Shops and people

  • Kakariko Potion Shop: Red Potion, Green Potion, Blue Fire, Fairy, and Fish
  • Granny's Potion Shop: Located behind the standard potion shop, accessible via the rooftop garden later in the trade sequence
  • Bazaar: Hylian Shield, Arrows, and Recovery Hearts
  • Shooting Gallery: Bow target minigame for 20 Rupees offering the Quiver upgrade upon a perfect score

Tips

  • Use the Hookshot on the high ladder of the village watchtower to climb up quickly without needing to circle around.
  • Wake Talon inside the center house with the Pocket Cucco as soon as it hatches so you can advance the adult trade quest promptly.
