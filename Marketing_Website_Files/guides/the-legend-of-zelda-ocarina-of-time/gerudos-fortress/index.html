Gerudo's Fortress

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Rescuing the carpenters and gaining access to the desert

Link infiltrates the sprawling stronghold to free four captive Hyrulean carpenters held in scattered prison cells. After defeating the Gerudo guards guarding each cell and speaking to all four workmen, Link earns the respect of the Gerudo and gains permission to cross the Haunted Wasteland.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: inside a treasure chest on the fortress rooftop reachable via the Longshot or Hover Boots
  • Piece of Heart: earned as a reward for scoring 1,000 points at the Horseback Archery Range
  • Biggest Quiver: earned as a reward for scoring 1,500 points at the Horseback Archery Range
  • Gerudo Membership Card: given by the Gerudo leader Nabooru's second-in-command after freeing all four carpenters
  • Small Key: dropped by defeating the Gerudo warrior near each carpenter's cell

Secrets

  • A Gold Skulltula is located high on the large target wall at the Horseback Archery Range, visible and reachable with the Longshot at night.
  • A Gold Skulltula sits on an exterior wall of the upper fortress ramparts, retrievable at night with the Longshot.

Shops and people

  • Horseback Archery Range Attendant: 20 Rupees per attempt at the Horseback Archery minigame

Tips

  • Stun roaming fortress guards from a distance with the Fairy Bow to move past them safely without triggering an arrest.
  • Use the Longshot on the wooden beam above the fortress dungeon window to easily escape the cell if captured.
  • Equipping the Gerudo Membership Card permanently stops guards from attacking or jailing Link throughout the fortress grounds.
