Kokiri Forest

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Starting the game and meeting the Great Deku Tree

Link wakes up in his home village and is tasked by Navi to visit the Great Deku Tree. Before Mido will grant passage to the tree, Link must explore the village to obtain a sword and a shield. Once equipped, Link speaks to Mido and gains access to the path leading to the game's first dungeon.

Items

  • Kokiri Sword: inside a treasure chest at the end of the Forest Training Center maze
  • Deku Shield: Kokiri Shop

Secrets

  • Blue Rupee behind Mido's house | walk behind Mido's house near the village perimeter to collect a hidden blue rupee
  • Heart Piece on the pond | jump across the floating square platforms on the water near the shop to reach a Piece of Heart

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Shops and people

  • Kokiri Shop: Deku Shield (40 Rupees), Deku Nuts (5 for 15 Rupees), Deku Sticks (1 for 10 Rupees), Recovery Heart (10 Rupees)

Tips

  • You can cut tall grass or throw rocks scattered around the village to quickly farm the 40 Rupees needed for the Deku Shield.
  • Talk to the Know-It-All Brothers in their house if you need explanations on basic game mechanics like targeting and movement.
