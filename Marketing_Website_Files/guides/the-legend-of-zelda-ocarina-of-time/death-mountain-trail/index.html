Death Mountain Trail

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Climbing the mountain to reach Goron City

The lower stretch of Death Mountain Trail leads Link from the Kakariko Village entrance up to the doors of Goron City. Link must navigate a steep, winding incline while avoiding rolling boulders to deliver the letter to the Goron patriarch. Later, with the help of Bomb Flowers found along the path, Link can blast open the blocked entrance to Dodongo's Cavern located just below.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: Above the entrance to Dodongo's Cavern, accessible by dropping down from the cliff above

Secrets

  • A bombable cracked wall near the entrance from Kakariko Village hides a Fairy Fountain containing a Piece of Heart.
  • Planting a Magic Bean in the soft soil patch right outside Dodongo's Cavern creates a flying leaf platform in the adult era that transports Link to higher cliffs.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Shops and people

  • Goron by Dodongo's Cavern: Offers advice regarding Bomb Flowers and Dodongo's Cavern

Tips

  • Backwalking or side-hopping lets you climb the steep slopes much faster than regular forward running.
  • You cannot pick up the wild Bomb Flowers growing along the trail until you acquire the Goron's Bracelet from Darunia inside Goron City.
