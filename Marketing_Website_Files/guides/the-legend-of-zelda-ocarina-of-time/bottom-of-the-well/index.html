Bottom of the Well

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Mini-dungeon to obtain the Lens of Truth

As child Link, enter the dried well in Kakariko Village to explore a dark, trap-filled underground mini-dungeon. Navigate illusory walls and water channels to reach the central chamber and defeat the mini-boss. The primary goal is to retrieve the Lens of Truth so you can reveal hidden objects and fake walls throughout the game.

Items

  • Lens of Truth: Chest in the mini-boss room guarded by Dead Hand
  • Compass: Chest in a room guarded by an enemy in the northern section
  • Dungeon Map: Chest in a room off the main perimeter corridor

Secrets

  • The dried fountain symbol in the main corridor drains the water when you stand in front of it and play Zelda's Lullaby.
  • Illusory walls that look like solid stone can be walked straight through throughout the main outer hallway.

Tips

  • Falling through hidden pitfall traps in the central room drops you into a poison-filled basement floor.
  • Look at the textures on the wooden beams and wall segments, as slight discoloration or lack of collision often signals an illusory wall even before getting the Lens of Truth.
  • You must play the Song of Storms inside the Windmill as an adult to drain the well, but you can only crawl inside the entrance as child Link.
