Zora's River

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Traveling to Zora's Domain

Zora's River is a winding canyon waterway that connects Hyrule Field to Zora's Domain. As child Link, you must navigate high ledges, waterfalls, and river currents to reach the domain entrance behind a waterfall. You can also pick up cuccos near the entrance to reach elevated platforms or grab a magic bean to access high places later.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: on a high wooden platform near the middle of the river path reached by throwing a Cucco across the river
  • Piece of Heart: on a high plateau near the final waterfall entrance, reached by carrying a Cucco from the river mouth
  • Piece of Heart: awarded by the frogs on the log after playing the Song of Storms for them
  • Piece of Heart: awarded by the frogs on the log after winning their bug-eating bugle game

Secrets

  • Playing Zelda's Lullaby while standing on the triforce mark in front of the large waterfall opens the passage into Zora's Domain
  • A circle of small stones near the river mouth conceals a hidden grottos revealed by using a bomb or the Stone of Agony

Tips

  • Grab the Cucco standing near the start of the river to easily glide across gaps to hard-to-reach ledges and Heart Pieces.
  • Plant a Magic Bean in the soft soil patch right next to the bean salesman to easily traverse the river as an adult.
