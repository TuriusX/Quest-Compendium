Zora's Fountain

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Finding the Great Fairy and entering Jabu-Jabu's Belly

Zora's Fountain is the sacred reservoir behind Zora's Domain that serves as the dwelling place of the deity Lord Jabu-Jabu. As a child, Link must offer a fish to Jabu-Jabu to be swallowed inside his belly, the game's third dungeon. The area also conceals a Great Fairy Fountain accessible by blasting away a cracked boulder on an island shore.

Items

  • Farore's Wind: Inside the Great Fairy Fountain, given by the Great Fairy after playing Zelda's Lullaby

Secrets

  • Great Fairy Fountain | Located behind a cracked brown boulder on an island near the southeast edge of the water, accessed by blowing up the boulder with a Bomb | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Lord Jabu-Jabu: Ingests a fish dropped from a bottle and swallows Link, granting access to Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly

Tips

  • You must catch a live Fish in a bottle from Zora's Domain or elsewhere before visiting Lord Jabu-Jabu, as dropping it right before his mouth is the only way to open the dungeon.
