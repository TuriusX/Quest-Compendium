Dodongo's Cavern

Link enters this cavern to clear out the monsters starving the Goron tribe and to claim the Spiritual Stone of Fire. Inside, he navigates lava-filled chambers, uses Bomb Flowers to blast open blocked paths, and lowers the giant stone Dodongo skull to reach the lair of King Dodongo.

Enemies
Dodongo
Weak to Tail or internal explosion from bombs

Breathes fire and turns slowly, leaving its vulnerable tail exposed to sword slashes.

Baby Dodongo
Weak to Any standard weapon attack

Burrows out of the ground in swarms and explodes violently shortly after being struck.

Lizalfos
Weak to Slingshot or jump slashes with the sword

Fights in pairs by leaping across platforms and striking with swords.

Armos
Weak to Bombs or Bomb Flowers

Dormant statues that come to life when touched, hopping toward Link before flashing and detonating when damaged.

Beamos
Weak to Bombs thrown at its base

Rotating stationary defense turret that tracks Link and fires a continuous laser beam when he draws near.

Keese
Weak to Deku Seeds or sword strikes

Flying bats that can catch fire from lava and burn Link's Deku Shield if they make contact.

King Dodongo
Weak to Bombs and sword strikes

Dungeon boss that inhales before breathing fire; throw a bomb into its mouth to stun it and expose its head to attacks.

Shops and people
  • Business Scrub: Deku Sticks for 15 Rupees
  • Business Scrub: Deku Nuts for 20 Rupees
  • Business Scrub: Deku Shield for 50 Rupees

Tips
  • Equip the Hylian Shield throughout this dungeon to prevent flaming Keese and fire attacks from burning your wooden Deku Shield.
  • Throw bombs or Bomb Flowers directly into the glowing eye sockets of the giant Dodongo skull from the bridge above to unlock the mouth leading to the boss.
  • Roll immediately after striking a Baby Dodongo to clear the blast radius of its post-mortem explosion.
