Dodongo's Cavern

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Second dungeon to obtain the Goron's Ruby

Link enters this cavern to clear out the monsters starving the Goron tribe and to claim the Spiritual Stone of Fire. Inside, he navigates lava-filled chambers, uses Bomb Flowers to blast open blocked paths, and lowers the giant stone Dodongo skull to reach the lair of King Dodongo.

Items

  • Bomb Bag: Found inside a large treasure chest guarded by two Lizalfos on the upper floor
  • Dungeon Map: Located in a treasure chest in the room to the right of the main central hub
  • Compass: Located in a treasure chest in the northern room behind a row of Armos statues
  • Heart Container: Appears in the boss room after defeating King Dodongo
  • Goron's Ruby: Awarded by Darunia outside the cavern after completing the dungeon

Secrets

  • Gold Skulltula behind a bombable wall in the southeastern corner of the first floor central room.
  • Gold Skulltula located on the upper floor behind a bombable wall in the room with the descending spiked trap.
  • Gold Skulltula clinging to a high wall above the stairs created by chain-reacting Bomb Flowers.
  • Gold Skulltula resting on a vine wall directly behind the giant Dodongo skull on the upper floor.
  • Gold Skulltula hidden inside an alcove on the upper level reached by riding the central pillar elevator.

Shops and people

  • Business Scrub: Deku Sticks for 15 Rupees
  • Business Scrub: Deku Nuts for 20 Rupees
  • Business Scrub: Deku Shield for 50 Rupees

Tips

  • Equip the Hylian Shield throughout this dungeon to prevent flaming Keese and fire attacks from burning your wooden Deku Shield.
  • Throw bombs or Bomb Flowers directly into the glowing eye sockets of the giant Dodongo skull from the bridge above to unlock the mouth leading to the boss.
  • Roll immediately after striking a Baby Dodongo to clear the blast radius of its post-mortem explosion.
