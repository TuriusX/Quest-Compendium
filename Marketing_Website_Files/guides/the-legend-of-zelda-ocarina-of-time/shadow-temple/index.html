Shadow Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Fourth adult dungeon to save the Shadow Sage

Located behind the Graveyard in Kakariko Village, the Shadow Temple requires you to utilize the Lens of Truth to reveal illusions, hidden passages, and invisible foes. You must navigate treacherous traps, guillotines, and bottomless pits using the Hover Boots to ultimately confront Bongo Bongo and awaken Impa as the Shadow Sage.

Items

  • Hover Boots: Obtained from a large chest guarded by a Dead Hand in the temple's early section
  • Dungeon Map: Found in a chest in a room filled with hidden scythe traps
  • Compass: Found in a chest inside a room with falling spike ceilings
  • Boss Key: Located in a room where you must burn a large wooden bird statue with Din's Fire or a fire arrow
  • Heart Container: Drops after defeating the dungeon boss, Bongo Bongo

Secrets

  • Gold Skulltula | Inside a cell in the room with the giant guillotines; pull the hidden hookshot target down with the Lens of Truth
  • Gold Skulltula | Behind the giant spinning pot with skull faces; smash the pot using a Bomb
  • Gold Skulltula | In the room with the invisible moving platforms near the ghost ship; located on the back wall near a ledge
  • Gold Skulltula | Found behind a hidden wooden partition in the room with the giant wooden spikes that fall from the ceiling

Tips

  • Keep the Lens of Truth mapped to a readily accessible button, as countless walls, floors, and platforms throughout this dungeon are completely invisible without it.
  • Equip the Hover Boots to cross gaps between floating platforms and to walk over pit traps without falling.
  • Play the Sun's Song immediately when entering rooms with ReDeads or Gibdos to permanently freeze them in place.
  • During the Bongo Bongo boss fight, equip the Hover Boots to avoid being bounced into the air by the drum beats while aiming your bow.
