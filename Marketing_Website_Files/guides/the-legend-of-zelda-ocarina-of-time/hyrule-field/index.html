Hyrule Field

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Traveling to Hyrule Castle

Hyrule Field connects Kokiri Forest to Hyrule Castle Town and several other regions across the kingdom. Link must cross the vast open plain during his first journey to seek an audience with Princess Zelda at Hyrule Castle. Along the way, he must navigate the terrain during the day or night while avoiding hostile creatures roaming the plains.

Items

  • Heart Piece: Inside a hidden grotto near the entrance to Lake Hylia, accessible by bombing the ground near a tree
  • Gold Skulltula Token: Inside a tree near the entrance to Kakariko Village, retrieved by rolling into the trunk

Secrets

  • A hidden hole in the ground containing a scrub merchant appears when using a bomb near the lone tree outside the entrance to Lake Hylia.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Traveling Business Scrub: Sells a Deku Shield or Deku Nuts inside a hidden grotto near Lake Hylia

Tips

  • Crossing the drawbridge into Hyrule Castle Town requires arriving before nightfall, as the bridge lifts and locks Link outside until dawn.
  • Running backward or side-hopping covers ground faster across the open field than standard forward jogging.
