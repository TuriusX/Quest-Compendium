Spirit Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Fifth adult dungeon to save the Spirit Sage

The Spirit Temple is the desert dungeon where Link must conquer trials as both a child and an adult to awaken the Spirit Sage. Child Link navigates small crawlspaces to retrieve the Silver Gauntlets, while Adult Link uses those gauntlets to move massive stones and acquire the Mirror Shield. Sunlight-reflection puzzles culminate in a climb to the temple's colossal statue to face the twin witches Koume and Kotake.

Items

  • Silver Gauntlets: In a treasure chest on the outside balcony of the colossus, retrieved by Child Link
  • Mirror Shield: In an ornate treasure chest dropped after defeating an Iron Knuckle as Adult Link
  • Compass: In a treasure chest in the child section room where you collect silver rupees
  • Dungeon Map: In a treasure chest in the early adult section after activating a switch with the Hookshot
  • Boss Key: In a treasure chest guarded by a Beamos and time-based switch in the adult section
  • Heart Container: Drops after defeating Twinrova at the end of the dungeon

Secrets

  • Playing Zelda's Lullaby in front of the Triforce symbol in the main central room causes a treasure chest to drop from above.
  • Bombing the wall with cracks near the rolling boulder room reveals a hidden room containing an enemy and a chest.

Tips

  • You must enter as Child Link first, as Adult Link cannot fit into the initial crawlspace that leads to the dungeon's first half.
  • The Mirror Shield reflects both natural sunlight to melt sun faces and magical elemental attacks fired by Koume and Kotake.
  • You can use Nayru's Love to avoid taking damage from Iron Knuckle axes while practicing sword spacing.
