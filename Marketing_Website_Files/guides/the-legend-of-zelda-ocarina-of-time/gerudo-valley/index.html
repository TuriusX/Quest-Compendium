Gerudo Valley

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Crossing the bridge to reach the desert

Gerudo Valley serves as the rugged gateway between Hyrule Field and the Gerudo's Fortress. As a child, the broken bridge is guarded, but as an adult, Link must find a way across the chasm to infiltrate the thieves' territory. Below the bridge lies a fast-flowing river that leads into Lake Hylia, containing a few hidden collectibles.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: In a wooden crate on a high ledge near the waterfall below the bridge
  • Piece of Heart: Behind the waterfall in a small alcove accessed by dropping into the river

Secrets

  • A silver rock hides a secret grottto behind the tent as an adult, requiring the Megaton Hammer to break.
  • Cucco floating allows Child Link to reach the ledges and riverbed below without dying from the fall.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
OctorokReflected projectiles or arrowsSpits rocks at Link while he swims in the river below.

Shops and people

  • Running Man: Challenges Link to an unwinnable footrace to the bridge in the lost woods
  • Carpenters: Offer dialogue regarding the broken bridge and the desert entrance

Tips

  • Use Epona to easily jump across the broken bridge as an adult.
  • If you lack Epona, use the Longshot on the wooden bridge posts to cross the gap as an adult.
  • Falling into the river sweeps you directly to Lake Hylia, providing a one-way trip there.
