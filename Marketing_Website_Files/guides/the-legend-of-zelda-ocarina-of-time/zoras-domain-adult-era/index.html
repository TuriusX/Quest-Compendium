Zora's Domain (Adult Era)

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Finding the domain frozen in ice

Returning to Zora's Domain as an adult reveals that the entire cavern has been frozen over under a thick sheet of red-tinted ice. Link must navigate the frozen waters to access King Zora, free him using Blue Fire obtained from the Ice Cavern, and gain the Zora Tunic to proceed to the Water Temple. The path behind King Zora leads outward toward the frozen Zora's Fountain.

Items

  • Zora Tunic: Given by King Zora after thawing him with Blue Fire

Secrets

  • Gold Skulltula | High up on the frozen waterfall wall; grab the token using the Longshot or Hookshot

Enemies

EnemyNotes
NoneThe interior of Zora's Domain is completely devoid of regular enemies during this visit

Shops and people

  • King Zora: Thawing him with Blue Fire grants the Zora Tunic and unlocks access to Zora's Fountain
  • Zora Shop: Frozen in red ice; thawing the shop entrance allows you to purchase general supplies and an expensive Zora Tunic if needed

Tips

  • You must head behind the throne room into Zora's Fountain and conquer the Ice Cavern first to acquire the Blue Fire needed to thaw anything here.
  • Bring several empty bottles to the Ice Cavern so you can scoop up extra Blue Fire to thaw both King Zora and the entrance to the Zora Shop.
