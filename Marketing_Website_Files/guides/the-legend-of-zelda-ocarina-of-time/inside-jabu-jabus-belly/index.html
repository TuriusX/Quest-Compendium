Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Third dungeon to obtain the Zora's Sapphire

Link enters the sacred patron deity of the Zoras to rescue Princess Ruto and retrieve the third Spiritual Stone, the Zora's Sapphire. You must carry and cooperate with the stubborn young princess to weigh down switches and navigate the creature's organic passages. Inside, you defeat parasitic tentacles and a mini-boss to claim the Boomerang before facing the bio-electric monster Barinade.

Items

  • Boomerang: inside a treasure chest guarded by Stingrays after destroying the Red Tentacle
  • Dungeon Map: inside a treasure chest appearing after eliminating all enemies in a room past the Blue Tentacle
  • Compass: inside a treasure chest awarded after defeating the Big Octo mini-boss
  • Heart Container: floating near the center of the boss chamber after defeating Barinade
  • Zora's Sapphire: awarded by Princess Ruto during the cutscene immediately following the dungeon's completion

Secrets

  • Gold Skulltula | on the wall in the rising water room that leads to the boss door; lower the water to strike it with the Boomerang
  • Gold Skulltula | on a vine wall near the platform puzzle in the lower basement levels; retrieve its token using the Boomerang

Shops and people

  • Princess Ruto: carried companion who acts as a heavy weight for blue floor switches

Tips

  • You can throw Princess Ruto onto platforms or across gaps to hit switches or advance puzzles before jumping across to meet her.
  • If you lose Princess Ruto, she will usually reappear in the room where you initially met her or near the pit you dropped through.
  • Boxes found in the dungeon can be used to permanently weigh down blue floor switches when Princess Ruto is no longer with you.
  • The Boomerang is effective at stunning electric enemies from a safe distance without taking recoil shock damage.
