Fire Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time guide · Second adult dungeon to save the Fire Sage

Link enters Death Mountain Crater as an adult to free the imprisoned Gorons and defeat the dragon Volvagia residing inside the Fire Temple. Navigating the dungeon requires finding keys by releasing Gorons from their cells and climbing through chambers filled with lava and fire traps. Completing the temple awakens Darunia as the Sage of Fire and rewards Link with the Fire Medallion.

Items

  • Megaton Hammer: inside a chest guarded by a Flare Dancer on the upper floors
  • Dungeon Map: inside a large chest in a room off the main entrance hall
  • Compass: inside a chest in the maze-like room on the third floor
  • Boss Key: inside an elevated chest accessible by pounding a totem pillar down with the Megaton Hammer
  • Heart Container: on the arena floor after defeating the boss Volvagia

Secrets

  • Playing the Song of Time near floating blue time blocks removes or moves them to reveal hidden doors or Gold Skulltulas.
  • Playing the Scarecrow's Song in the large boulder maze summons Pierre to reach a high ledge leading up to hidden Gold Skulltulas.

Tips

  • Wear the Goron Tunic throughout the dungeon to prevent the heat timer from appearing in high-temperature rooms.
  • Striking rusty floor switches with the Megaton Hammer activates doors and opens locked Goron cells.
  • When Volvagia flies into the air to breathe fire or drop ceiling rocks, Link can hang off the ledge of the arena platforms to avoid taking damage.
