New Delsta

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · The bustling, neon-lit capital of the Brightlands

New Delsta is a grand, bustling metropolis in the Brightlands that serves as the starting location for the thief Throne. Players can explore its wealthy town square, the crowded theater district, the gritty Backstreets, and the nearby harbor. It contains numerous side stories, valuable gear to procure, and access to key early-game facilities like the Game Parlor.

Items

  • Stiletto: Chest on the rooftop walkway in the Backstreets
  • Wind Spirit Stone: Chest tucked in an alleyway in the eastern theater district
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest near the docks at New Delsta: Anchorage

Secrets

  • Behind a screen near the Game Parlor in the Backstreets, a hidden passage leads underground into the Abandoned Waterway, a higher-level dungeon with valuable treasures.

Shops and people

  • New Delsta Provisioner: Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, Olive of Life, and Herb of Clamor
  • New Delsta Armorer: Iron Sword, Iron Dagger, Bronze Vest, Round Shield, and Leather Hat

Tips

  • Switch to nighttime and use Throne's Steal ability to take items from the rich patrons around the theater district without spending money.
  • Use Osvald's Scrutinize or Castti's Inquire on townspeople in New Delsta to reveal hidden items scattered throughout the streets and buildings.
