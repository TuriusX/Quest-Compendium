Abandoned Waterway: Castti's Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Castti's Chapter 3

Castti travels deep into the Abandoned Waterway beneath the town of Winterbloom during Chapter 3 of her tale to uncover the tragic truth behind Eir's Apothecaries and Malaya. Players navigate flooded stone corridors using a small wooden canoe to cross water passages while battling local pests and undead creatures. The objective is to reach the deepest chamber where Castti confronts her painful memories in an extended story sequence following an arduous dungeon crawl.

Items

  • Ice Soulstone (L): In a treasure chest located along a northern walkway reached by disembarking from the canoe
  • Inspiriting Plum Basket: Found inside a chest on a secluded stone ledge accessible only via the water channels
  • Imperial Vest: In a chest guarded on an elevated walkway near the midsection of the dungeon
  • Cleansing Leaf: Inside a chest situated on a dead-end path near the lower-level water docks

Tips

  • Take advantage of Castti's Concoct ability using Cleansing Seeds and Plums to frequently regenerate SP for the party across long battles.
  • Equipping accessories that protect against Freezing helps counter the cold-element attacks frequently dealt by aquatic and ice-type enemies in this waterway.
