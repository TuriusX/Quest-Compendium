Southern Timberain Trail

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Partitio's Chapter 2

Southern Timberain Trail connects the Western Montwise Pass to the city of Timberain in the Leaflands region. Players traverse this forested path to reach Timberain during the late-game chapters, notably Partitio's Chapter 2 and Hikari's Chapter 5. The trail features high-level wilderness encounters and leads toward the grand capital city.

Items

  • 15,000 Leaves: Chest on a dirt ledge reached via the eastern wooden ramp
  • Olive of Life (L): Chest tucked behind trees south of the main stone bridge
  • Energizing Pomegranate (L): Chest on the elevated northern riverbank

Secrets

  • A submerged river passage accessible by canoe leads to a hidden treasure chest tucked under the stone overpass.

Tips

  • Equipping the Cleric's Evil Ward passive skill helps safely retreat from high-threat encounters if passing through this route before reaching the recommended level.
  • Bring a Thief or Hunter to reliably exploit Dagger and Bow vulnerabilities common among the local wildlife.
