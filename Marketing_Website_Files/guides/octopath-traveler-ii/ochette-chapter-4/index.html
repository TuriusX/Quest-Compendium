Ochette, Chapter 4

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · The final confrontation with the dark beast

Ochette travels to the island of Nameless Village to prevent the Night of the Scarlet Moon from consuming Toto'haha. She confronts the corrupted partner beast she did not pick at the start of her journey. Defeating the boss clears Ochette's main story and concludes her tale.

Items

  • Herb of Light: In a treasure chest along the path toward the Altar of the Sun King

Tips

  • The final boss continually summons dark minions to lock its weaknesses; prioritize breaking and eliminating the adds to expose the boss again.
  • Equipping accessories that protect against unconsciousness or blind will significantly ease the pressure during the boss's boosted phases.
