Sacred Guard Headquarters

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Temenos's Chapter 2

The Sacred Guard Headquarters is a key location visited during Temenos's Chapter 2, located within the town of Canalbrine. Temenos investigates the interior to uncover information regarding the suspicious death of the Pontiff and interact with members of the order. After completing the story events here, players proceed to further investigate the clues leading outward into the surrounding areas.

Items

  • Herb of Healing: Chest inside the barracks living quarters
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest located along the hallway on the main floor

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Sacred GuardSword, LightHealing GrapeFought via Temenos's Coerce Path Action to reveal information

Tips

  • Use Temenos's Coerce action on stationed guards to break their shield points and reveal hidden items or story-relevant information.
  • Equipping a Scholar or skills that hit multiple shield points will make breaking Coerce targets during nocturnal interrogations much quicker.
