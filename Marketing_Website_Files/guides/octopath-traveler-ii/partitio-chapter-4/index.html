Partitio, Chapter 4

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Partitio auctions for the steam engine to end poverty

Partitio arrives in Oresrush and travels to Roque Island to attend the grand auction for the steam engine's manufacturing rights, carrying the 80 billion leaves he amassed. When business magnate Roque attempts to rig the deal and deploy his ultimate weapon, Partitio and his companions must infiltrate the factory and defeat Roque's mechanical steam train. Victory secures the rights to mass-produce the steam engine for common benefit and concludes Partitio's main storyline.

Tips

  • Bring party members capable of multi-hit physical and elemental shield breaks, particularly Lightning and Bow attacks, to quickly dismantle the Steam Tank Obsidian before its cannons charge.
  • Partitio's Latent Power "Huminate" restores maximum BP immediately, which allows him to repeatedly use fully boosted divine skills or Donate BP to allies every turn.
