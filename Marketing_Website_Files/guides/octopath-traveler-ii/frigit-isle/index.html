Frigit Isle

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Osvald's Chapter 1

Frigit Isle is a harsh, snow-swept penal colony where the scholar Osvald is wrongfully imprisoned for the murder of his family. During Osvald's Chapter 1, you complete daily labor routines in the labor tunnels while secretly gathering materials for a prison break alongside your cellmate Emerald. The chapter culminates in scoping out escape routes and taking down Warden Davts to acquire the key needed to facilitate your escape.

Items

  • Herb of Healing MISSABLE: Labor tunnels, found in a chest along the main mining track
  • Inspiring Plum MISSABLE: Labor tunnels, in a treasure chest tucked in a dead-end side shaft
  • Empty Waterskin MISSABLE: Labor tunnels, obtained by scrutinizing or shaking down an inmate during work details

Tips

  • Use Osvald's Mug path action during nighttime to defeat specific targets and claim necessary quest components for your escape.
  • Osvald starts with fire and ice elemental magic, so exploit elemental weaknesses on the guards to break them before they deal heavy physical damage.
