Montwise

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · A city of scholars and libraries

Montwise is an academic city built into a mountain in the Crestlands, renowned for its grand library and scholarly pursuits. Visitors can explore the town, study within the Library of Montwise, and venture into the connected Underground Laboratory during specific story chapters. It serves as a key location in the story paths for characters such as Osvald and Hikari.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone (M): In a treasure chest located in the lower western section of Montwise
  • Inspiriting Plum Basket: In a chest inside the Library of Montwise

Shops and people

  • Montwise Provisioner: Sells standard restorative consumables, status-cleansing herbs, and travel items
  • Montwise Armorer: Sells mid-game equipment, including scholarly staves, armor, and accessories

Tips

  • Scrutinize, Inquire, or Coerce the scholars throughout the library to uncover hidden item locations and expand knowledge-based town perks.
  • Bring Path Actions suited for night-time information gathering and nighttime knockouts (like Castti or Throné) to access guarded back rooms in the archives.
