Beasting Village: Hikari's Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Ochette's Chapter 2

Beasting Village is the starting point for Ochette's journey on the island of Toto'haha, not Hikari's story, but Hikari can visit here to progress shared or side events. Players exploring during Ochette's Chapter 2 paths visit this settlement to prepare, speak with the beastling residents, and gather supplies before heading out into the wild reaches of the island.

Items

  • Herb of Clamor: chest near the animal pen in the southern section of the village
  • Healing Grape: wooden chest tucked behind a hut on the western ridge

Shops and people

  • Beasting Village Armorer: sells basic shields, light armor, and mid-tier starting weapons suited for the Toto'haha region
  • Beasting Village Provisioner: sells standard recovery items including Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, and status curatives

Tips

  • Bring Path Actions like Hikari's Challenge or Coquer, or Ochette's Provoke, to interact with beastling NPCs blocking doorways or holding unique local items.
