Timberain

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Partitio's Chapter 2

Timberain is a fortified castle town in the Leaflands region featuring a town square, a court area, and Timberain Castle. While Partitio's Chapter 2 actually takes place in Clockbank, Timberain itself serves as the climactic setting for Castti's Chapter 4. Visitors come here to navigate the political heart of the Leaflands, upgrade mid-to-late game gear, and challenge the castle grounds.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells high-tier mid-to-late game equipment including the Imperial Lance, Platinum Sword, and Quartz Rod
  • Provisioner: Sells standard restorative items including Healing Grape (M), Inspiring Plum (M), and various status-curing herbs
  • Inn: Offers a full HP and SP recovery for the party for a standard fee

Tips

  • Bring Castti and a party geared for wind and poison resistance when preparing for major story battles in this area.
  • Use Path Actions like Inquire or Scrutinize on the town guards to reveal hidden item stashes scattered around the castle gardens and courthouse.
