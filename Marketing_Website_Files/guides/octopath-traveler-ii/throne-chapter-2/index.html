Throne, Chapter 2

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Throne hunts the Father and Mother in the New Delsta
← All areas in this guide

Throné's Chapter 2 splits into two distinct paths, Mother's Route in Oresrush and Father's Route in Winterbloom. Following Mother's trail requires sneaking through the foundries of Oresrush to uncover the whereabouts of the Blacksnakes' cruel matron. Tracking Father leads to Winterbloom, where Throné must navigate snowy alleyways and confront an associate known as the Snowhare.

Bergomi
Weak to Sword, Dagger, Bow, Light

Boss fought at the end of Father's Route in Winterbloom; summons subordinates to shield his weaknesses.

Thug
Weak to Sword, Axe, Fire

Found throughout the Abandoned Foundry during Mother's Route.

Tips
  • Complete both Father's Route and Mother's Route in whichever order you prefer, as both are required to unlock Throné's Chapter 3.
  • Use Throné's nighttime talent "Blessing of Darkness" at the start of boss encounters like Bergomi to automatically buff your party's physical attack, elemental attack, and speed.
