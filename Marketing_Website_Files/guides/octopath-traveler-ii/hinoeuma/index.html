Hinoeuma is an arid desert region characterized by rolling sands, ancient ruins, and the sovereign nation of Ku. It serves as the primary setting for the warrior Hikari's quest to reclaim his homeland and features settlements like Ryu and Sai. Travelers visiting the region can cross expansive sand dunes, discover forgotten tombs, and engage in high-stakes regional trade.

Enemies
Sandworm
Weak to Sword, Dagger, Ice, Light

Capable of inflicting blindness with its sand-based physical attacks.

Desert Drake
Weak to Polearm, Bow, Ice, Dark

Flying reptilian foe found in the outer sands that attacks with wind and physical sweeps.

Tips
  • Bring party members capable of curing Blindness when crossing the Hinoeuma dunes, as many native monsters frequently reduce your accuracy.
  • Switch between day and night in Ku and Sai to find wandering scholars and guards holding high-tier weapon information and hidden armaments via Path Actions.
