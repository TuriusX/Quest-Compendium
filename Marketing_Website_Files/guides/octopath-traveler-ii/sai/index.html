Sai

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · A desert city known for its trade and markets

Sai is a tiered desert settlement located in the Hinoeuma region, split between bustling merchant quarters and an impoverished residential district. It plays an important role as a chapter location for Agnea and Castti, offering several side stories and trading opportunities. Players can recruit useful NPCs, discover high-tier path action items, and find pathways leading into the dangerous Decaying Temple.

Items

  • Wind Soulstone (M): Chest located on the upper terrace near the town wall
  • 6,000 Leaves: Inside a treasure chest hidden in the southern residential area

Secrets

  • The entrance to the Decaying Temple can be reached via a winding path extending east and south from the town outskirts, containing powerful foes and unique gear.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Restorative items, status-cleansing herbs, and travel essentials
  • Armorer: Mid-tier desert armor, shields, and weapons such as swords and polearms

Tips

  • Many residents in Sai carry valuable accessories and crafting materials, making Path Actions like Entreat, Steal, or Mug especially profitable here.
