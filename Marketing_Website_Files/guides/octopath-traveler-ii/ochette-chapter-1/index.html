Ochette, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Ochette leaves Toto'haha to hunt the legendary beasts

Ochette's journey begins in Beasting Village on Toto'haha, where she is raised by the beastling warden Juvah. After choosing her lifelong animal companion, she investigates an encroaching disaster known as the Night of the Scarlet Moon and sets out to sea to track down three legendary creatures.

Don't miss

  • Companion Choice | Ochette must choose between the Malamaowl (Mahina) or the Lajackal (Akalà) near the start of the chapter; the choice is permanent and the unchosen animal cannot be acquired later.

Items

  • Jerky: Obtained automatically during the meat-processing tutorial

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Shadowy BruteSword, Dagger, Axe, Lightning, LightBoss encountered in the Tombs of the Wardenbeasts that summons Dark Remnants during battle

Tips

  • Capturing monsters in battle does not consume items and can be used indefinitely, making captured beasts a free and versatile source of elemental and physical damage.
  • Excess captured monsters can be prepared into Jerky or other meat items to heal HP/SP or recruit townspeople using Ochette's daytime Path Action, Befriend.
  • Mahina primarily targets elemental weaknesses (Fire, Ice, Lightning), while Akalà targets weapon weaknesses (Sword, Polearm, Dagger), providing reliable shield breaks throughout Ochette's entire story.
