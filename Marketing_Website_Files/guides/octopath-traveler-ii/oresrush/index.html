Oresrush

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Partitio's Chapter 1

Oresrush is a rugged Wildlands mining town that serves as the starting location for Partitio the Merchant. During his Chapter 1, Partitio witnesses the town rise into prosperity and fall into hardship under the silver mine owner, leading him to recruit townsfolk and confront the mine boss. Players explore the town, purchase essential gear, hire companions with Partitio's Purchase/Hire Path Actions, and head into the nearby mines.

Items

  • Inspiration Axe: inside a treasure chest located behind the tavern
  • Healing Grape: inside a treasure chest on the wooden walkways of the town
  • Olive of Life: inside a treasure chest near the mine entrance

Secrets

  • Hidden path to an equipment chest | walk around the back of the houses on the upper terrace to find an obscured trail leading behind the buildings

Shops and people

  • Oresrush Provisioner: sells standard early-game consumables like Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, and basic equipment
  • Oresrush Weapon Shop: sells iron-tier weapons such as Iron Sword, Iron Polearm, Iron Axe, and basic shields

Tips

  • Use Partitio's daytime Path Action "Purchase" on townsfolk to pick up recovery items and crafting materials cheaper than store prices.
  • Use Partitio's night Path Action "Hire" to recruit a townsperson with offensive skills to aid you in combat for the upcoming boss battle.
  • Take advantage of Partitio's Latent Power "Business Acumen" in combat to instantly fill his BP gauge to maximum for burst damage or breaking shields.
