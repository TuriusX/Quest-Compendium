Oresrush

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · A dusty, industrious town in the Wildlands

Oresrush is a rugged silver mining settlement located in the Wildlands region. It serves as the starting location for Partitio the Merchant's storyline, introducing his origin under his father Papp and the villainous landowner Roque. Outside of Partitio's introductory chapter, players can visit to recruit several townspeople, browse the local market, and pick up side quests.

Items

  • Light Nut: In a chest inside the foundry building on the upper level
  • Inspiriting Plum: In a treasure chest near the tavern
  • Healing Grape: In a wooden chest behind the general store

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
GiffSword, Bow, Fire, LightningBoss encountered in Partitio's Chapter 1 alongside his two footmen

Shops and people

  • Oresrush Provisioner: Restorative items including Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, and status curatives
  • Oresrush Armorer: Basic iron equipment, daggers, bows, and lightweight mining armor
  • Oresrush Tavern: Offers rumor-sharing, party reorganization, and tavern meals

Tips

  • Partitio can Purchase items from local miners and townsfolk during the day, which often yields cheaper gear and ores before you enter the nearby mines.
  • Hiring an NPC follower with combat skills in Oresrush makes Partitio's early solo chapter significantly easier to manage against groups of bandits.
