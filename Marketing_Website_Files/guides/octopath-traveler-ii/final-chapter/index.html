Guides / OCTOPATH TRAVELER II

Final Chapter

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · The eight travelers unite to face the ultimate evil

After completing all eight travelers' individual storylines and all Crossed Paths, the party gathers around a campfire in the Southern Cropdale Trail to begin the unified epilogue. The travelers venture across Solistia to extinguish the eternal night by reigniting four sacred flames before confronting the dark god Vide.

Don't miss

  • After lighting the final sacred flame, the world is plunged into eternal night and standard town interactions become unavailable until the final sequence concludes, so finish open-world exploration and equipment prep beforehand.

Items

  • Book of Night: Dropped by Arcanette during the chapter

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
ArcanetteSword, Dagger, Bow, Ice, LightFought atop the Duskbarrow ruins to relight one of the sacred flames.
VideSword, Axe, Staff, Lightning, LightThe first phase of the final encounter; attacks with summoned tentacles that must be destroyed to unlock its weaknesses.
Vide, the WickedThe final boss encounter where the battle shifts between both split four-member parties.

Tips

  • You must use all eight travelers across the final sequence, so make sure every party member is appropriately leveled and equipped rather than focusing only on a main four.
  • Equip the Support Skill "A Step Ahead" from the Inventor job across your parties to ensure immediate actions at the start of multi-phase final battles.
