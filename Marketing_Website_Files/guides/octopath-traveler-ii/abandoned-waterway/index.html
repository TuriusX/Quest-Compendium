Abandoned Waterway

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Castti's Chapter 1

The Abandoned Waterway is a dungeon accessed from Canalbrine during Castti's Chapter 1 story. Castti explores this foul sewer beneath the town alongside the apothecary Malaya to discover the source of a deadly toxin afflicting the citizens. You must navigate the murky paths, battle waterborne vermin, and reach the depths to defeat the Plagued Doran and cleanse the water supply.

Items

  • Herb of Healing: Chest found along the stone pathway near the entrance
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest situated on a dead-end platform on the lower wooden catwalk
  • Cleansing Leaf: Chest tucked along the path leading toward the central channel
  • Light Nut: Chest located near the southern ledge on the way to the save point

Tips

  • Keep Castti stocked with Soothing Dust and Cleansing Leaves, as Plagued Doran inflicts poison on the party that can quickly drain your HP if left untreated.
  • Use Castti's Latent Power "Every Day's a Dose" during the boss encounter to concoct multi-target healing or buffing mixtures without expending your valuable ingredients.
  • Malaya accompanies you as a guest during this dungeon and will automatically provide healing and combat support, easing item management during regular encounters.
