Southern Wellgrove Trail

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Castti's Chapter 2

Southern Wellgrove Trail connects the town of Wellgrove with the eastern wilderness and the path toward Oresrush. It is an outdoor wilderness area featuring paths through woods and small hills, traversed during Castti's story in the Leaflands. Players will encounter moderate-level wild beasts and gather basic supplies as they approach Wellgrove.

Items

  • Olive of Life (M): Found inside a treasure chest located along the south-central path
  • 5,600 Leaves: Found in a treasure chest tucked away along a winding dead-end cliff

Secrets

  • Concealed trail behind the foliage leading to a hidden chest containing valuable equipment on the eastern section of the map

Tips

  • Prepare Wind and Bow attacks to quickly break the most common avian and beast shields found along this trail.
  • If you travel along this trail at night, have Throne or Temenos in the party to take advantage of nocturnal buffs and automated debuffs against tough wilderness encounters.
