Nameless Village

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Ochette's Chapter 2

The Nameless Village is a secluded beastling settlement located on the island of Toto'haha that serves as a key location for Ochette's Chapter 2 - Cateracta Route. During her story here, Ochette must converse with the locals using food to gain their trust and locate the resting place of the sacred beast Cateracta. Outside of her story, players can explore the village to obtain valuable local gear and recruit beastling residents via Path Actions.

Items

  • Fire Pebble: Inside a treasure chest on the wooden walkways on the eastern side of the village
  • Olive of Life: Inside a treasure chest tucked near a hut in the western section

Secrets

  • Path to the Wandering Wood: Heading north past the chief's hut leads directly into the Wandering Wood dungeon, where Cateracta resides.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing Grape, Inspiring Plum, Purifying Dust, and basic consumable restorative items

Tips

  • The beastling villagers do not speak the common tongue, so use Ochette's Path Action to feed them Jerky to earn their assistance and learn information.
  • Bring an Apothecary or items that cure Poison, as the surrounding Toto'haha wilds and the nearby Wandering Wood contain many venomous creatures.
