Ku

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · The fortified castle town of the Hinoeuma region

Ku is a heavily fortified desert settlement in the Hinoeuma region, split into the residential Castle Town and the elevated Castle Ku. It serves as the starting location and homeland for the warrior Hikari Ku, as well as the site of his dramatic fall and eventual return. Players come here during Hikari's story line to engage in palace intrigue, purchase equipment, and challenge martial NPCs.

Items

  • Light Nut: Chest located inside a building in Castle Town
  • Plum Basket: Chest found along the wall fortifications

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells early to mid-game swords, polearms, and warrior equipment
  • Provisioner: Sells standard consumables including Healing Grapes and Inspiring Plums

Tips

  • As Hikari, take advantage of his Path Action Challenge on town guards and townsfolk throughout Ku to learn powerful skills early on.
  • Return to Ku after completing Hikari's chapter to acquire high-tier gear and learn potent martial abilities from the surviving generals and masters.
