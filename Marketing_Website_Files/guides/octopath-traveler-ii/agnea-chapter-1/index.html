Agnea, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Agnea leaves Cropdale to become a star

Agnea Bristni dances at the local festival in Cropdale to fulfill her dream of becoming a world-famous star like her mother. After gathering festival goods and learning her sister Pala made a coin purse for her journey, she ventures up the Path to the Festival Grounds to confront a troublesome monster. Defeating the boss clears her way to set out on her worldwide tour.

Items

  • Wind Pebble: Chest near the save point on the eastern edge of Cropdale
  • Healing Grape: Chest located off a hidden path on the Path to the Festival Grounds

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Forest MarmotDagger, Axe, WindCommon rodent enemy found on the outskirts of Cropdale
DuorduorDagger, Axe, Fire, WindChapter boss accompanied by Boarling adds that it can summon

Tips

  • Use Agnea's Path Action, Allure, to recruit a townsperson before heading to the festival grounds so they can be summoned to deal extra damage in combat.
  • Focus down the Boarlings during the Duorduor boss fight to minimize incoming damage before breaking Duorduor with Dagger or Wind attacks.
