Cropdale Forest

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Agnea's Chapter 1

Cropdale Forest is the first dungeon explored during Agnea's Chapter 1, situated just outside Cropdale. Agnea ventures through this woodland path alongside an Entreated village companion to gather ingredients for a festival dance troupe costume. The trail leads deeper into the woods where a territorial predator awaits as the chapter boss.

Items

  • Healing Grape: In a treasure chest near the beginning of the winding forest path
  • Inspiriting Plum: In a treasure chest located along a clearing off the main route
  • Wind Soulstone: In a treasure chest situated on a northern branch of the woods

Tips

  • Entreat an NPC from Cropdale to follow Agnea before heading deep into the forest so you can summon them to assist in combat.
  • Duorduor summons smaller boars during battle; defeat the adds quickly with area-of-effect skills so you can focus break attacks on the boss.
