Clockbank

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · An industrial city in the Brightlands

Clockbank is a bustling, steam-powered industrial city situated in the Brightlands region. It features prominently in Partitio the Merchant's story line (Chapter 2) as well as Castti the Apothecary's story. Players can recruit new companions, purchase advanced mid-game gear, and explore both the upper factory districts and the lower industrial areas.

Items

  • Empowering Lychee (M): Found inside a treasure chest on the rooftop catwalks of the industrial district
  • Physical Belt: In a chest inside a locked house on the outskirts, accessible by using a Path Action on the guard at the door

Shops and people

  • Clockbank Armorer: Upgraded mid-game weapons and armor such as the Clockwork Mail, Great Bow, and Heavy Spear
  • Clockbank Provisioner: Healing Grapes (M), Inspiring Plums (M), and various Concocting ingredients for Castti

Tips

  • Use Partitio's Hire action on local factory workers and engineers here to gain bonuses to purchasing discounts and merchant skill damage.
  • Switching between Day and Night in Clockbank reveals different factory workers with distinct Path Action rewards, including hidden item discoveries revealed via Scrutinize, Inquire, or Coerce.
