Canalbrine

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · A coastal town serving as a major port

Canalbrine is a prosperous port town in the Harborlands region that serves as the starting location for Castti the Apothecary. Players visit this town to experience Castti's opening story, access ferry routes across the sea, and recruit various local NPCs using Path Actions.

Items

  • Plum Basket: Carried by an NPC in the town center, acquired via Path Actions

Secrets

  • Waterway Chest | Board a canoe from the town's wooden docks to paddle under the northern bridges and reach an otherwise inaccessible treasure chest.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Restorative items including Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, and basic status-curing herbs
  • Armorer: Low-to-mid tier weapons and defensive equipment suited for the early game
  • Anchorage: Ferry service granting travel to the eastern continent port of New Delsta Harbor

Tips

  • Use Castti's Inquire Path Action during the day or Osvald's Scrutinize to reveal hidden items scattered throughout town and secure discounts at the local inn.
