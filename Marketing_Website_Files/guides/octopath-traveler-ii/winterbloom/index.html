Winterbloom

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · A snowy town in the Winterlands

Winterbloom is an icy town located in the Winterlands region featuring an upper residential tier and a lower market area. It serves as the setting for Throne's Chapter 2 (Father's Route) and Castti's Chapter 2 (Winterbloom Route). Players visit to advance these character storylines, explore side quests, and investigate the Melancholy Botanist's garden.

Items

  • Empowering Lychee (M): In a treasure chest located in the eastern portion of the town
  • Ice Soulstone (M): In a treasure chest near the frozen riverbank in the lower area
  • Physical Attire: Inside a chest in the residential manor in the upper district

Secrets

  • Behind a snowed-in cottage on the outskirts, you can guide or hire an NPC to access a hidden cellar containing a treasure chest.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing Grape, Inspiring Plum, Purifying Seed, Cleansing Leaf, Herb of Light, Herb of Healing
  • Armorer: Platinum Sword, Ice Glaive, Quartz Dagger, Heavy Bow, Platinum Shield, Fur Hood, Fur Coat

Tips

  • Bring Castti or Osvald with Path Actions ready at night to easily scavenge information and uncover hidden items throughout the town's residences.
  • You will need to resolve Throne's or Castti's chapter objectives here to unlock full access to certain blocked interiors in the upper district.
