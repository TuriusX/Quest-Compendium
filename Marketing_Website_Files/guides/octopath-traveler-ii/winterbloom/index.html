Winterbloom

OCTOPATH TRAVELER II guide · Osvald's Chapter 2

Winterbloom is a snowy town located in the Winterlands region where Osvald travels during his main narrative to track down his former colleague and enemy, Harvey. While visiting, Osvald investigates leads regarding Harvey's whereabouts and confronts Lady Clarissa to uncover the truth behind his family's tragedy. Players should utilize path actions to scrutinize townsfolk for information and explore the town and its outskirts for valuable gear before advancing the plot.

Items

  • Ice Soulstone (M): Found inside a treasure chest located in the eastern portion of Winterbloom
  • Inspiriting Plum: Located inside a treasure chest near the residences in town

Secrets

  • Thieves' Guild | Enter the abandoned building on the upper level of town to find a hidden passage leading to the underground Thieves' Guild, where you can acquire the Thief secondary license.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Sells standard restorative consumables including Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, and Olives of Life
  • Armorer: Sells mid-tier equipment such as weapons and armor suitable for the Winterlands chapter level range

Tips

  • Use Osvald's Scrutinize action on the local townspeople to reveal hidden items and gain discounts at the local inn.
  • Bring Fire and Lightning skills into battles around this area, as many local humanoid and beast enemies are susceptible to elemental attacks.
