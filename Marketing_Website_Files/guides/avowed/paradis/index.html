Paradis

Avowed guide · Primary port city and capital hub within Dawnshore, visited during early main quests
← All areas in this guide

Paradis is the primary capital port settlement of Dawnshore, divided into sub-districts and serving as the starting urban hub of the Living Lands.

Region
Dawnshore
Levels
Levels 1–5
Getting there
After completing the prologue at Fort Northreach / Claviger’s Landing, cross into Dawnshore and reach Paradis’s main gates via the main quest Message from Afar.
Connects to
Shantytown, Cistern / Undercity, Emerald Stair Gatehouse
Related quests
Message from Afar · The Animancy Method · An Untimely End · Mapping the Living Lands · Precious Light · Escape Plan · Dawntreader · Food Theft
Services and people
Ambassador Falscen Hylgard · Marius · Sanza · Lynna · Sapiento de Falva · Remei Gardiola · Ofryc · Captain Aelfyr / The Steel Garrote

The short version

What matters

  • •Sparing Ygwulf nets you Adra, gold, Ygwulf's Key, and the Writ of Passage, ensuring peaceful passage through Emerald Stair.
  • •Helping Leoflaed and Glaedwine flee during Escape Plan secures valuable crafting supplies and the Gambeson of the Grand Empire later in Thirdborn.
  • •Buying both 100-copper treasure maps from Lynna and Sanza reveals valuable stashes across the region early on.

Common mistakes

  • •Reporting the fleeing couple to Captain Aelfyr for 470 XP locks you out of the Gambeson of the Grand Empire and upsets companion Kai.
  • •Killing Ygwulf solely for the Blackwing Armor permanently forfeits the Writ of Passage and provokes future rebel retaliation.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Arrive at the Southern City Gates and Recover

    When you arrive at the Southern City Gates to deliver the Emperor's orders via Message from Afar, a cutscene triggers where an unseen attacker shoots you with an arrow. After you resurrect, the quest An Untimely End begins. You will recover from your wounds inside Liviana’s Clinic in Southern Paradis, where town stairs and the Administration District Beacon are waiting directly outside. Before heading into the upper districts, speak with Remei Gardiola nearby to start Escape Plan, and browse Lynna's wares to buy the Treasure Map: Intimidating Feline Codpiece for 100 copper. While in the area, climb the scaffolding platforms at the Paradis lighthouse to pull Captain Henqua's Spoils from a skeleton.

    Tip: Tag the Administration District Beacon immediately outside the clinic for convenient fast travel later.

  2. 2

    Ascend to Hightown and the Embassy

    Head up the grand stairs toward Hightown to report to Ambassador Falscen Hylgard in the Ambassador’s Quarters of the Aedyran Embassy. Outside the embassy, speak with Ofryc to take on Dawntreader, which grants the Delver's Caution Ring upon completion. Pay attention to Captain Aelfyr and the Steel Garrote forces who patrol the surrounding gates and High Market area.

    Tip: Speak to Ofryc outside the embassy grounds before going inside so you can track Dawntreader alongside your main duties.

    Watch out: Turning in Leoflaed and Glaedwine to Captain Aelfyr later will permanently get them killed or captured and cause companion Kai to strongly disapprove.

  3. 3

    Recruit Marius at The Grinning Balarok

    Make your way to the tavern known as The Grinning Balarok to find the dwarven ranger Marius caught in a bar altercation. Intervene to fight off the agitated patrons harassing him, using melee kicks, blocks, or crowd control in the cramped indoor quarters to keep the thugs grouped for his arrows. After the skirmish concludes, speak with Marius to enlist him as a permanent companion.

    Key fight: The Grinning Balarok Brawl

    Tip: Group the brawlers together so Marius's ranged shots can pick them off rapidly in the tight room.

  4. 4

    Trade in the Market District

    Explore the merchant stalls across the Market District and enter Sanza's Emporium in Hightown to purchase the Treasure Map: Woedica's Inheritance for 100 copper. While browsing, you can also consult Sanza regarding cartography requests and pick up the quest Precious Light from Sapiento de Falva.

    Tip: Purchase both available maps early so you can track down extra loot while clearing regional objectives.

  5. 5

    Investigate the Pearl District

    Advance into the Pearl District past wealthy homes and courtesans toward the Temple of Ondra, which sits above the entrance to the Cistern. If you are completing Escape Plan, search a marked Pearl District estate to loot the Thirdborn Land Deeds off Meiko's corpse right beside an indoor pond. To secure the Gambeson of the Grand Empire later in Thirdborn, pay off or intimidate the harbor smuggler Captain Soldis and deliver the papers to help Leoflaed and Glaedwine escape, which also earns Softwood, Pelts, and Iron Chunks. Cut through the smugglers, criminal enforcers, and snipers guarding the waterway entrances beneath the temple to gain entry to the undercity.

    Key fight: The Pearl District Boss Battle / Cistern Ruffians

    Tip: Command Marius to mark and disrupt walkway crossbow snipers while applying Shock or Frost abilities to lock down heavy clubbers in narrow hallways.

    Watch out: If you report the refugees to Captain Aelfyr instead of helping them flee, you forfeit the Gambeson of the Grand Empire gifted in Thirdborn.

  6. 6

    Confront Ygwulf in the Cistern

    Delve into the Cistern beneath the Temple of Ondra to confront your shooter, Ygwulf, for An Untimely End. If you choose revenge, battle Ygwulf by trapping him in corners with companion abilities to neutralize his smoke concealment and high evasion, which lets you loot the unique Blackwing Armor from his body. Alternatively, spare his life to receive Adra, skeyt gold, Ygwulf's Key, and the Writ of Passage, which prevents hostile ambushes from Emerald Stair rebels later.

    Key fight: Ygwulf

    Tip: Use heavy crowd control or stagger effects to keep Ygwulf pinned down during combat.

    Watch out: Sparing Ygwulf forfeits the Blackwing Armor, while killing or arresting him forfeits the Writ of Passage and turns regional rebels hostile.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Confronting Ygwulf

Tracking down the sniper in the Cistern below the Temple of Ondra during "An Untimely End"

  • Spare Him→ Receive Adra, skeyt, Ygwulf's Key, and the Writ of Passage, ensuring rebels in Emerald Stair remain peaceful
  • Kill Him→ Fight and loot the unique Blackwing Armor from his body, but lose the Writ of Passage and face hostile rebels later
  • Turn Him In→ Surrender him to the Steel Garrote for imprisonment, provoking future rebel retaliation and angering anti-imperial factions

Most players pick: Spare Him

Fate of the Fleeing Refugees

Resolving the situation of Leoflaed and Glaedwine during "Escape Plan"

  • Help Them Escape→ Secure passage via Captain Soldis to earn crafting materials, allowing the couple to reach Thirdborn safely and later grant the Gambeson of the Grand Empire
  • Report Them→ Hand them over to Captain Aelfyr to receive 470 XP as the Steel Garrote captures or kills the pair, permanently losing them

Most players pick: Help Them Escape

Reporting them triggers strong disapproval from Kai
