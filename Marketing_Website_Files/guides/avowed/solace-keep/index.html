Solace Keep

Solace Keep is a fortified dwarven settlement situated in Galawain's Tusks that acts as an access point toward The Garden.

Region
Galawain's Tusks
Levels
22–26
Getting there
Reached from Shatterscarp via the Galawain's Tusks Gatehouse, then following the main northern road to the keep's fortified outer gate and raised drawbridge.
Connects to
Shatterscarp, Galawain's Tusks Gatehouse, The Garden
Related quests
Our Dreams Divide Us Still · Mapping the Living Lands: Galawain's Tusks · Boundaries of Antiquity · Chorus of the Lost · Beetle Matriarch · Congasar & Crusta · Urvolnau · The Sporeking
Services and people
Warden Kosmin · Komendant Mihala · Councilor Kostya · Quartermaster Yorgu · Forgemaster Dela · Chef Kimitru · Renato Varga

The short version

What matters

  • •Opening the Sluice Gates in the Undercroft preserves the keep, keeps every merchant available, and earns you the unique Bastion of Solace shield from Komendant Mihala.
  • •Buying Faith and Conviction, the Refined Palate Ring, Huntmaster's Spoils Clues / Totem Fragments, and Treasure Map #2 from keep vendors prior to completing the subterranean quest.
  • •Focusing down Skeleton Priests first during the Undercroft battle so they cannot heal the skeletal warriors while you focus Fire and Crush attacks on Sibelius.

Common mistakes

  • •Siding with Kostya to destroy the winch gears, or walking away without acting; both choices cause magma to flood the settlement, permanently locking out all merchants, Mihala, and the Bastion of Solace shield.
  • •Careless movement during the battle in the Lava Tubes; getting shoved or stepping into magma pools results in instant death.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    The Outer Drawbridge & Watchtower

    Follow the main northern road from the Galawain's Tusks Gatehouse until you reach the chasm before the massive stone drawbridge. Head into the outer watchtower cells to discover the detained Aedyran cartographer, Renato Varga. Resolve his situation to acquire his personal cartographic notes before approaching the bridge.

    Tip: Resolve Renato's situation right away to secure his regional cartography notes before entering the fortress.

  2. 2

    Main Gate & Entry Hall

    Approach the raised span and speak with the sentry, Warden Kosmin, bringing Marius along to clear the dialogue and lower the gate. Pass through the portal into the main entry hall to gain entry to the fortress interior.

    Tip: Bring Marius to handle the dialogue smoothly with Warden Kosmin at the gate.

  3. 3

    Upper Courtyard, Market & Mess Hall

    Explore the central grounds, which house the communal barracks, administrative offices, and a Shrine to Woedica. Visit Forgemaster Dela at Abydon's Forge to purchase unique equipment, stop by Chef Kimitru for culinary items, and browse the wares of Quartermaster Yorgu beside the Bounty Board. Be sure to purchase all necessary wares and maps from these vendors before advancing the local political crisis.

    Tip: Stock up on vendor inventories early, as upcoming story events put all shop access in jeopardy.

    Watch out: Failing to buy items from Dela, Kimitru, and Yorgu now will permanently forfeit them if the fortress is subsequently destroyed.

  4. 4

    The Council Chambers

    Enter the Council Chambers to meet with the Warden Council, where conservative leader Komendant Mihala and reformist Councilor Kostya debate tradition, trade, and path access toward The Garden. Listen to both sides as they seek to resolve their standoff and direct you beneath the mountain to secure access forward.

    Tip: Pay close attention to Mihala and Kostya's arguments regarding the mountain's mechanisms before descending into the depths.

  5. 5

    The Subterranean Lava Tubes / Undercroft

    Descend beneath the mountain into a hazardous complex of molten rock pathways, ancient machinery, winch assemblies, and sluice controls. Fight your way through rebellious wardens, Kostya's loyalist troops, teleporting shadows, and skeletal hordes backed by their healing priests, culminating in a showdown against Sibelius. Overcome the encounter, then choose the fate of the keep: open the sluice gates to safely redirect the magma and preserve the stronghold, destroy the winch gears to flood the settlement, or walk away and let Kostya ruin the mechanism.

    Key fight: Solace Keep Lava Tubes/Undercroft

    Tip: Prioritize eliminating Skeleton Priests immediately to stop them from sustaining the skeletal barbarians, and utilize Fire or Crush damage against Sibelius while minding instant-kill magma pits.

    Watch out: Destroying the winch gears or walking away results in magma engulfing Solace Keep, permanently locking out all interior merchants, losing Mihala, and forfeiting the Bastion of Solace shield.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

The Magma Flow Mechanism

During the climax of "Our Dreams Divide Us Still" in the Lava Tubes under the fortress

  • Open the Sluice Gates→ You side with Mihala, routing the molten rock safely away to preserve Solace Keep, keep all local merchants accessible, receive a positive ending slide, and earn the Bastion of Solace shield
  • Destroy the Winch Gears→ You side with Kostya, unleashing magma that burns down the settlement, permanently seals the interior, loses access to all keep vendors, and forfeits the unique shield
  • Walk Away→ Kostya breaks the assembly regardless, causing the keep to be destroyed, forfeiting the shield reward, and barring you from entering the ruined stronghold

Most players pick: Open the Sluice Gates

Point of no return: destroying the gears or walking away permanently locks out all interior services, merchants, and items.
