The Garden

The Garden is the endgame, point-of-no-return region located at the heart of the Living Lands.

Region
The Living Lands
Levels
20–25+

Entered during the main quest "Our Dreams Divide Us Still" via the Lava Tubes underneath Solace Keep in Galawain’s Tusks.
Connects to
Lava Tubes, Dawnshore / Paradis
Related quests
Our Dreams Divide Us Still · The Heart of the Living Lands · Pulling Back the Veil

Woedica · Sapadal · Kai · Giatta · Marius · Yatzli

The short version

What matters

  • •Collecting the four Wildwalker set pieces (Armor, Boots, Gloves, Ring) scattered through the shrine, aqueduct, and route caches.
  • •Navigating the submerged aqueduct tunnel to recover the Sarcophagus Key and backtracking to open the early golden sarcophagus for the Spiritmender Armor.
  • •Farming Pristine Maegfolc in the Inner Complex for Severed Maegfolc Arms and legendary crafting materials.
  • •Sapadal's fate choice, which awards either Sapadal's Fury or Woedica's Call before sending you to Dawnshore.

Common mistakes

  • •Rushing into Sapadal's Prison and completing the main quest objective before fully exploring, permanently locking yourself out of all missable items and secrets.
  • •Using piercing or slashing attacks on mechanical Maegfolc instead of shock spells, armor-shredding skills, or crush/impact damage.
  • •Forgetting to bring Yatzli and fire abilities, leaving you unable to dispel the Godless Shrine illusion or burn through aqueduct brambles.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Garden Entrance & Enclosed Grove Camp

    When you arrive via the Lava Tubes underneath Solace Keep during the quest "Our Dreams Divide Us Still", establish your base at the Enclosed Grove Camp alongside Kai, Giatta, Marius, and Yatzli. As you explore this initial overgrown ruin early on, keep an eye out for a golden sarcophagus locked behind a glowing seal containing the Spiritmender Armor. Note its location now, as you will need to backtrack here once you obtain its key deeper in the ruins.

    Tip: Keep Yatzli in your party to handle magical barriers and Kai or fire spells to burn through briars and webs.

    Watch out: Do not complete the final prison event before returning to open the golden sarcophagus, or it will be permanently lost.

  4. 4

    Broken Aqueduct

    Navigate the multi-level ruined waterworks of the Broken Aqueduct and branch off the beaten path to loot the Wildwalker Boots. Disarm traps and burn down briars along a web-covered side path to claim the Brooch of Spreading Flame, then locate the Voice Memory inside the southern ruined structure. Head past the chest to a circular web-lined pool, swim through a submerged archway tunnel to the left branch, burn wall brambles with fire, freeze the water to cross, and scale the high ledge to loot the Sarcophagus Key off an exposed skeleton overlooking the aqueduct.

    Key fight:

    Tip: Use fire attacks or grenades to rapidly clear away Dreamthrall Moon Spiders, spiderlings, and thick web hazards.

    Watch out: Ensure you backtrack to open the early golden sarcophagus with the Sarcophagus Key before passing the point of no return.

  5. 5

    Overlook Camp to Inner Complex

    Rest at the Overlook Camp waystone before descending into the heavily fortified halls of the Inner Complex. Inside, confront elite Pristine Maegfolc defending the interior corridors and defeat them to harvest Severed Maegfolc Arm(s). Push past the mechanical sentries toward the threshold of the inner sanctum, ensuring every side objective, cache, and backtrack is completed before breaking the seal.

    Key fight:

    Tip: Severed Maegfolc Arms can be collected from defeated Pristine Maegfolc as valuable high-tier crafting reagents.

    Watch out: Crossing beyond into the inner sanctum to resolve the prison seals this area permanently, locking any missed loot.

  6. 6

    Sapadal's Prison

    Step into the inner sanctum to confront the imprisoned god Sapadal alongside Woedica's binding edict. You must choose Sapadal's fate: either shatter the bindings to free Sapadal—granting you Sapadal’s Fury, healing the Dreamscourge, and keeping Sapadal bonded to you and the land—or destroy Sapadal to align with Woedica, gaining Woedica’s Call, severing Sapadal's dream influence, and internalizing the deity's power. Whichever path you choose, a route unlocks leading directly to Dawnshore to decide the final siege at Paradis.

    Watch out: Choosing either fate permanently closes the Garden dungeon and advances the endgame narrative to Dawnshore and Paradis.

Sapadal's Fate

When reaching Sapadal's Prison and facing Woedica's binding edict

  • Free Sapadal→ You obtain Sapadal’s Fury, keep Sapadal linked to you and the region to heal the Dreamscourge, and unlock the route to Dawnshore
  • Destroy / Kill Sapadal→ You side with Woedica, receive Woedica’s Call, eliminate Sapadal's dream influence over the Living Lands, absorb or cut off its essence, and open the way forward to Paradis

Area completion is a point of no return for missed region loot.

Key fights

Enemies: Damaged Maegfolc, Pristine Maegfolc, Putrid/Arisen/Decaying Skeletons (Monks, Fighters, Wizards, Rangers, Priests), Dreamthrall Swarmers, Dreamthrall Elder Black Bears, Dreamthrall Moon Spiders/Spiderlings

Threats: Maegfolc have lethal physical sweeps, shield barriers, and high stagger thresholds; Skeleton Priests and Wizards buff the frontline and use heavy AoE spells and healing; Spiders and Beasts are aggressive pack rushers that inflict poison and Dreamscourge buildups

Weaknesses and resistances: Maegfolc are vulnerable to heavy crush/impact attacks, shock damage, and armor-shredding abilities (resistant to piercing/slashing); Spiders and Beasts are cleared quickly with fire or wide AoE crowd-control

Tactics: Rush down skeleton magic and elemental users first; bring Yatzli for dispelling energy fields and illusions; bring Kai or fire-based spells to clear webs and burn briar barricades; use freeze effects on water sections for platforming shortcuts or tactical choke points

Rewards: High-tier legendary crafting/enchanting reagents, raw essence, gems, and Severed Maegfolc Arms
