Galawain's Tusks

Avowed guide · Mountainous volcanic region unlocked during "Our Dreams Divide Us Still"
← All areas in this guide

Galawain's Tusks is the late-game fourth major region of the Living Lands, characterized by a harsh, mountainous volcanic terrain scarred by lava flows, ashfall, and ruins.

Region
Galawain's Tusks
Levels
Level 18–25+ gear tier scale
Getting there
Journey north from Shark's Teeth / Shatterscarp and enter through the Galawain's Tusks Gatehouse over the northern boundary bridge during main quest 'Our Dreams Divide Us Still'
Connects to
Shatterscarp, The Garden, Lava Tubes
Related quests

Quartermaster Yorgu · Bulti

The short version

What matters

  • •Side with Councilor Mihala in the Lava Tubes to preserve Solace Keep, protect its dwarven residents, and secure direct quest rewards.
  • •Weigh your choice in "A Cure for Rage" between taking the unique greataxe Limb-Taker by slaying Grakohr or the Stoneheart amulet by concocting his cure.
  • •Drop off the right-hand ledge facing the Bridge to the Garden to snag the Antler of the Persevering Stag before leaving the region.

Common mistakes

  • •Entering the deep endgame threshold of the Lava Tubes and The Garden before finishing your exploration; this permanently locks you out of the Umbral Needle, Sentinel Mail, Battletrance, and the God Totem items.
  • •Attacking heavily armored Stone Beetles and Warden Ironbreakers with standard physical slashes; crack their defenses much faster using heavy power attacks, blunt maces, or frost and corrosion magic.

What you can skip

  • •Do not support Councilor Kostya's plan to flood Solace Keep with lava, as it needlessly destroys the fortress hub and yields zero tangible compensation.
  • •Avoid walking away from Kostya in the Lava Tubes, as he floods the keep anyway and leaves you with nothing.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Cross the Gatehouse into the Volcanic Frontier

    Cross the northern boundary bridge from Shark's Teeth into Galawain's Tusks Gatehouse during "Our Dreams Divide Us Still" to settle your business from Shatterscarp and gain entry. As you step out onto the ash-covered trails, stay alert for hazardous molten lava puddles that inflict harsh fire tick damage.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Break the guard of heavily armored Warden Ironbreakers and Stone Beetles with maces, heavy power strikes, or frost and corrosion magic rather than basic slashes.

  2. 2

    Investigate the Sallow Steppe and Grakohr’s Cave

    Head onto the trail through the Sallow Steppe to discover an ambushed caravan and the den of the sick ogre, Grakohr. During "A Cure for Rage," choose between brewing a remedy to pacify him and free the captured merchant Gilyn, or killing him on the spot.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Focus down ranged Xaurip Screamers first to prevent enemy buff sequences, and use frost spells or ranger slow-time precision aiming to safely kite ogre rushes.

    Watch out: Brewing the cure permanently locks you out of the greataxe Limb-Taker, while slaying Grakohr forfeits the Stoneheart amulet.

  3. 3

    Clear the Ash Forest and Northern Cavern

    Push through Amelia’s Camp into the Ash Forest, an ash flat populated by predatory beasts and dwarven outcasts. Scour the volcanic ruined complexes for Battletrance, and scale the eastern ledges of the Pargrun Cache tower to drop through its roof opening for Sentinel Mail. Once you buy the treasure map The Blade that Hungers from Bulti in Solace Keep, check the northernmost rim inside a cave directly north of the forest to loot a chest.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Choke point swarms of beetles and creepers into narrow corridors to clear them efficiently with area damage.

    Watch out: All unique loot across the region permanently becomes unobtainable if you proceed into the final deep section of the Lava Tubes and The Garden during "Our Dreams Divide Us Still."

  4. 4

    Breach the Southern Approach and Solace Keep

    Advance past the Southern Guard Tower and Drawbridge, checking lower-level cells across the chasm to locate the captured cartographer Renato. Enter Solace Keep to find Chief Engineer Gabral, Warden Enunta, and the opposing faction leaders Councilor Mihala and Councilor Kostya across the Council Chamber, Elevator, and Upper Shrines. Before leaving the fortress grounds, inspect the Galawain Shrine southwest of the keep to claim the Totem Core, and speak with Quartermaster Yorgu for totem clues or Bulti on the upper shrine level for maps.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Silence or burst down Steel Garrote War Wizards immediately to prevent devastating area bombardment while their shielded Champions engage you in melee.

    Watch out: Make sure to purchase The Blade that Hungers from Bulti before moving toward the endgame descent.

  5. 5

    Cleanse the Slumbering Fields and Ekidan Workshop

    Travel to the Slumbering Fields, a rare verdant expanse sheltering ancient Godless aqueducts, and proceed into the Ekidan Workshop during "Boundaries of Antiquity." To free the prisoner Raluka and restore the water flow, choose between negotiating with the Delemgan to cleanse the poisoned source inside the ruins or killing the Delemgan.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Watch your positioning around environmental lava pockets while dealing with hostile automations and corruption.

  6. 6

    Defend the Living Archives

    Head toward the ancient dwarven library known as the Living Archives, which has come under hostile assault. Secure the site by cutting through Steel Garrote contingents, including their thaumaturges and corrupted dreamthralls.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Interrupt the casting of Thaumaturges before their crowd-control abilities disrupt your party setup.

  7. 7

    Descend the Bridge to the Garden and Decide Solace Keep's Fate

    March southeast to the Bridge to the Garden, making sure to drop down off the ledge to the right side facing the bridge to grab the Antler of the Persevering Stag. Venture deep into the Lava Tubes to confront Inquisitor Lodwyn, then resolve the fate of Solace Keep: side with Mihala to raise the lava gates and save the settlement, or align with Kostya to destroy the winch gears and submerge the fortress in molten rock.

    Key fight: Regional Encounters

    Tip: Use slow-time abilities or frost grimoire magic to keep mobile threats from knocking you into environmental lava.

    Watch out: Siding with Kostya or simply walking away results in the total destruction of Solace Keep and deprives you of quest rewards, while entering the final Garden passage locks out all uncollected regional gear.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Fate of Grakohr

Visiting Grakohr’s Cave during "A Cure for Rage"

  • Slay Grakohr immediately→ You defeat the ogre in combat, rescue Gilyn, and loot the unique greataxe Limb-Taker
  • Brew the Cure→ Grakohr consumes the remedy, becomes peaceful, lets Gilyn go unharmed, and grants you the unique Stoneheart amulet

Choosing one outcome locks you out of the unique item rewarded by the other

Water Supply of Slumbering Fields

Interacting with the Delemgan during "Boundaries of Antiquity"

  • Ally with the Delemgan→ You purify the ruined Ekidan Workshop without destroying nature, clearing the aqueduct and securing the peaceful release of the prisoners
  • Slay the Delemgan→ You eliminate the Delemgan by force to rescue Raluka directly and manually reroute the water network

Fate of Solace Keep

Deep inside the Lava Tubes during "Our Dreams Divide Us Still"

  • Side with Mihala→ You raise the lava gates to thwart Kostya's scheme, preserving Solace Keep along with its dwarven inhabitants and earning Mihala's thanks alongside direct quest rewards
  • Side with Kostya→ You smash the winch gears, causing lava to flood and obliterate Solace Keep to eradicate the Dreamscourge while earning no tangible compensation
  • Abandon Kostya→ You depart without acting, letting Kostya flood and destroy the fortress on his own with no rewards granted

Most players pick: Side with Mihala

Pushing into the deep Lava Tubes and The Garden marks a point of no return for missed region content

Tactics: Silence or eliminate Xaurip Screamers and Steel Garrote War Wizards first to prevent buff cycles and heavy ranged bombardment. Utilize ranger slow-time precision aiming or frost grimoire spells to kite heavy ogre charges and group beetle swarms into choke points.
