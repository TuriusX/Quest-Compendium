Points of no return

  • Entering the final confrontation quest at the conclusion of the Living Lands campaign: Locks out access to previous regions, open exploration, and all unfinished side quests or companion tasks.

Recommended order
  1. Complete the prologue in The Shattered Passage, learning core combat mechanics and gathering early equipment.
  2. Explore The Coastal Reach thoroughly, completing side quests, unlocking regional fast-travel points, and recruiting available companions.
  3. Advance the main narrative into The Shattered Scarp while pursuing companion questlines and resolving major faction dilemmas carefully.
  4. Sweep all three regions for remaining combat challenges, unique boss bounties, and equipment upgrade achievements before the climax.
  5. Pass the final point of no return to finish the main story and unlock the ending-related achievements.
