Fior mes Ivèrno

Avowed guide · Major animancer settlement and central town within the Emerald Stair
← All areas in this guide

Fior mes Ivèrno is an Italian/Vailian-inspired hillside settlement and the central hub of the Emerald Stair, renowned as a sanctuary for animancy and soul-science research.

Region
Central Emerald Stair
Levels
8–14
Getting there
Following the main story out of Dawnshore, enter Emerald Stair via the regional transit gate and follow roads leading straight toward the central hill.
Connects to
The Wildwoods, Maxim's Claim, Rolling Crags, Truffle Farm and agricultural terraces, Naku Tedek ruins
Related quests
Ancient Soil · Steel Resolve · Debt of Blood · Emerald Stair Bounty Contracts
Services and people
Emerald Stair Bounty Board · blacksmiths · animancy material purveyors · potion-brewers · general goods vendors

The short version

What matters

  • •Buying the Treasure Map: Practical Pockets from Ector Brewer for 660 copper before progressing the main story.
  • •Locating Salvius's Lab Secret Entrance east of the northern gate to claim the Shoes of the Glimmering Merchant.
  • •Clearing out the paladins inside the Waterfall Cave prior to finishing Ancient Soil to keep the settlement intact and unlock the 'Fior Extinguisher' achievement.

Common mistakes

  • •Heading directly to the Naku Tedek ruins or accepting a bribe from the paladins, which causes the Steel Garotte to burn Fior mes Ivèrno to the ground and permanently fails local board bounties.
  • •Trying to batter through Steel Garotte Paladins without corrosive spells or piercing weapons to crack their disciplined shields and heavy armor.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Approach the Southern Gate

    Pass through the Emerald Stair Gatehouse east of Paradis with your Writ of Passage, traveling northwest past Maxim’s Claim and Coastal Farms to reach the settlement's entrance. Speak with Ranger Dorso and Captain Aelfyr of the Steel Garrote stationed outside the barrier before stepping inside.

  2. 2

    Audience at Piazza mes Vidarro

    Head up to the Piazza mes Vidarro and enter the Governor's Estate to conduct your envoy business. Report directly to Governor Ignassi Vidarro to advance the quest The Animancy Method.

  3. 3

    Visit the Castell Family Lab

    Make your way into the Animancy Workshop to rendezvous with animancer scholar Giatta Castell. Here, converse with her fellow researchers Ivette, Remi, Elia Rosell, and Monto Pastor regarding their ongoing soul-science and field experiments.

  4. 4

    Grab Bounties and Hit the Tavern

    Check the Notice Board in The Town Square to collect regional postings, then step into The Bone & Boar Tavern. Meet with Lieutenant Fidelio inside the pub, and seek out religious devotee Rossana Fallo in the northeastern quarter of town.

  5. 5

    Browse Marketplace Stalls

    Head to the marketplace vendors at The Gifted Magpie, Porrya & Vellino, and browse their stock of gear and supplies. Speak with trader Ector Brewer to purchase the Treasure Map: Practical Pockets for 660 copper before departing.

    Watch out: Don't finish the main quest Ancient Soil at Naku Tedek without dealing with the Steel Garotte in advance, or the town will be razed and Ector Brewer's stall lost permanently.

  6. 6

    Investigate the Docks

    Walk down toward the Fior mes Ivèrno Docks along the supply barge waterfront. Speak with Ranger Casagrada stationed at the water to follow up on your local side investigations.

  7. 7

    Uncover Salvius's Hidden Lab

    Head outside the settlement along the exterior city wall situated directly east of the northern gate to discover Salvius's Lab Secret Entrance. Read the Animancer’s Log to reveal the path into the hidden rival lab and secure the Shoes of the Glimmering Merchant.

  8. 8

    Clear the Waterfall Cave Garrison

    Before completing the quest Ancient Soil at Naku Tedek, locate the hidden Steel Garotte Waterfall Cave and confront the Steel Garrote Paladins & Enforcers. Strip their heavy defenses using piercing attacks, lightning, or corrosive magic, and disrupt their healing to avoid the town falling into Occupied Town Ruins.

    Key fight: Waterfall Cave Garrison Key fight: Occupied Town Ruins

    Tip: Use piercing damage and lightning or corrosive spells against the paladins, and keep your distance from two-handed enforcers while interrupting paladin heals.

    Watch out: Taking a bribe, walking away, or progressing straight to Naku Tedek without clearing this cave leads to the town being destroyed and locks you out of unfinished local side quests and bounties.

The Fate of Fior mes Ivèrno

Prior to finishing "Ancient Soil" at Naku Tedek

  • Discover and eliminate the Steel Garotte in their hidden Waterfall Cave→ Fior mes Ivèrno is saved from destruction, keeping its residents and vendors alive, preserving town access, and earning the secret achievement "Fior Extinguisher"
  • Head directly to Naku Tedek without killing the paladins, or accept a bribe/leave after talking to them in the cave→ Steel Garotte forces burn the settlement to the ground, killing or exiling the animancers, wiping out vendor stalls, and failing remaining local side quests and bounties

Most players pick: Discover and eliminate the Steel Garotte in their hidden Waterfall Cave

Finishing "Ancient Soil" before dealing with the paladins serves as a point of no return that destroys the settlement.
