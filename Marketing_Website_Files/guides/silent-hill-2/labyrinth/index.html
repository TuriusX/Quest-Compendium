Labyrinth

SILENT HILL 2 guide · Navigating the underground maze
← All areas in this guide

James descends into the subterranean Labyrinth, a vast and surreal network of rusted corridors divided into three main zones: the Rotten Area, Desolate Area, and Ruined Area. To progress, you must explore these sections to manipulate a giant rotating cube puzzle at the central hub, rescue Maria, and confront Eddie Dombrowski.

Enemies
Lying Figure

Spits acidic bile from a distance and scrambles across the floor when knocked down.

Mannequin

Hides in blind spots or behind walls to ambush James when he passes.

Bubble Head Nurse

Attacks with steel pipes or knives; best dealt with by staggering with a firearm shot before closing in with melee.

Mandarin

Hangs from the slatted iron mesh ceilings and floors, striking from below with its tentacle-like arms.

Abstract Daddy

Moves aggressively in confined corridors, charging forward to grapple James.

Pyramid Head

Pursues James through specific sections of the maze and cannot be killed during regular corridor encounters; run away and keep your distance.

Tips
  • Pay close attention to the symbols on the rotating cube at the central hub, as twisting it changes the doorways and unlocks access to new zones.
  • Listen carefully for heavy metallic footsteps or dragging noises to anticipate Pyramid Head stalking the maze corridors.
  • Look down at the floor grating often; Mandarins lurk underneath and can hit you through the mesh if you stand still.
  • Conserve your Rifle and Shotgun ammunition as much as possible for the enclosed boss fights against Abstract Daddy and Eddie Dombrowski.
