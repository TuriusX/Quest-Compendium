Points of no return

  • Entering the courtyard of Wood Side Apartments: You cannot return to the initial east/west streets of Silent Hill to collect missed Memos, Photos, or Glimpses of the Past.
  • Exiting Blue Creek Apartments via the final grandfather clock puzzle: Blue Creek Apartments becomes permanently inaccessible for the rest of the playthrough.
  • Entering Brookhaven Hospital after meeting Maria: The western half of South Vale (Silent Hill South Side) becomes locked off, along with any uncollected street items.
  • Solving the Chained Box and leaving Brookhaven Hospital (Otherworld): Brookhaven Hospital and its Otherworld variant cannot be re-entered.
  • Jumping down the consecutive holes into Toluca Prison from the Historical Society: The Silent Hill Historical Society and upper South Vale are no longer accessible.
  • Completing the Labyrinth and boarding the rowboat at the dock: Toluca Prison, the Labyrinth, and all previous mainland areas are permanently locked out.
  • Using the Lakeview Hotel elevator with all personal belongings placed into the shelf: The Lakeview Hotel shifts into the Otherworld variant, making standard hotel rooms and missed items unreachable.
  • Defeating the final boss in Lakeview Hotel (Otherworld): The playthrough ends; all uncollected items, unfinished combat challenges, and ending prerequisites are permanently locked.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete Playthrough 1 on Standard or Hard combat difficulty, thoroughly exploring every area to collect all Memos, Glimpses of the Past, and Strange Photos while aiming for the "Leave" ending and achieving combat-specific trophies.

  2. Step 2

    Ensure you complete area-specific missable tasks in Playthrough 1, such as breaking all glass in specific sections, avoiding damage during specific boss encounters, and viewing key optional events.

  3. Step 3

    Start Playthrough 2 on New Game+ on Light/Standard difficulty to gather NG+ exclusive items needed for the new and alternative endings ("In Water", "Maria", "Rebirth", "Dog", "UFO", "Bliss", and "Stillness").

  4. Step 4

    Complete Playthrough 3 (or combine with Playthrough 2) focusing on finishing the game in under 10 hours and without using ranged weapons (melee only) for the remaining speed and combat restriction achievements.

  5. Step 5

    Reload strategic manual saves made prior to key decision points or ending triggers to unlock any remaining ending achievements.
