Points of no return

  • Entering the Subway Train after restoring power in Raccoon City Streets: Blocks access to Raccoon City Streets, Subway Station, Subway Power Substation, Sewers, Sewers (Upper Level), Gun Shop Kendo, and Construction Site collectibles.
  • Exiting the Raccoon City Police Department as Carlos: Blocks access to Raccoon City Police Department and Raccoon City Police Department (Upper Floors) collectibles and locks.
  • Completing the Hospital lobby siege as Carlos: Blocks Carlos's Hospital (Interior) and Hospital (Courtyard) areas, weapon parts, and Carlos-specific safes.
  • Using the underground elevator to enter Underground Storage as Jill: Permanently seals the Raccoon City Hospital areas.
  • Riding the final elevator up into NEST 2: Locks out Underground Storage and previous areas entirely.
  • Triggering the final boss fight in NEST 2 (Lab 2): Prevents backtracking to earlier NEST 2 rooms and NEST 2 (Lab 1) to retrieve missed collectibles.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1Step 1

    Complete an initial playthrough on Assisted or Standard difficulty while obtaining all Charlie Dolls, Files, Locks, and weapon upgrade parts.

  2. Step 2Step 2

    Farm zombie kills and specific weapon kill records in safe room checkpoints (such as the Hospital defense section or NEST 2) to earn Points.

  3. Step 3Step 3

    Purchase bonus Shop items using Points, prioritizing the RAI-DEN, Infinite MUP Pistol, Recovery Coins, Iron Defense Coins, and the Infinite Rocket Launcher.

  4. Step 4Step 4

    Complete a Hardcore difficulty playthrough with the Infinite Rocket Launcher to unlock Nightmare difficulty.

  5. Step 5Step 5

    Complete a Nightmare difficulty playthrough with the Infinite Rocket Launcher to unlock Inferno difficulty.

  6. Step 6Step 6

    Complete an Inferno difficulty playthrough without saving more than 5 times and achieving an S-Rank, while combining restrictions for completing the game without opening the item box and using one or fewer recovery items.

  7. Step 7Step 7

    Clean up any remaining combat-specific achievements (such as "Two for One Deal" or "Electric Slide") via story saves.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Electric Slide: Collect all three warehouse fuses and restore power to the lift in under 5 minutes.

    Also in this step: Two For One Deal
