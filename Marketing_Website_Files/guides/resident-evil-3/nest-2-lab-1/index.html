NEST 2 (Lab 1)

Resident Evil 3 guide · Exploring the laboratory levels
← All areas in this guide

After riding the freight elevator down from the Spencer Memorial Hospital underground storage area, Jill enters NEST 2 through Level 1. The objective is to secure the vaccine synthesis materials by acquiring an override flash drive, restoring laboratory power, and retrieving antigen and adjuvant samples. This initial tier connects directly to the lower incubation rooms and leads toward the vaccine synthesis chamber.

Pale Head
Weak to Fire (Flame Rounds) / High kinetic damage

Fast-regenerating, skinless zombies that require concentrated firepower or incineration to stay down.

Tips
  • Do not waste standard handgun ammunition on Pale Heads, as their flesh regenerates faster than regular bullets can reliably kill them.
  • Pick up the laboratory map in the main Level 1 Safe Room console room to reveal locked electronic door routes immediately.
