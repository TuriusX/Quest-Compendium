Raccoon City Police Department

Resident Evil 3 guide · Carlos enters the station to find Dr. Bard
← All areas in this guide

Playing as Carlos Oliveira alongside Tyrell Patrick, you enter the Raccoon City Police Department shortly after the outbreak to locate S.T.A.R.S. member Dr. Bard. You must navigate the main hall, west wing offices, and upper corridors to reach the S.T.A.R.S. office, clearing out zombies and Lickers along the way. Your primary objectives are to bypass locked doors using key items and breach the Shower Room wall with a detonator and battery.

Licker
Weak to High-damage weapons, grenades, and head/exposed brain

Blind enemy with keen hearing, encountered outside the S.T.A.R.S. office corridor and after the shower wall explosion.

Tips
  • Brad Vickers outside the front doors can be lured near explosive or open spaces to make taking him down safer, ensuring you can loot his ID Card.
  • You can walk slowly to sneak right past Lickers undetected, saving precious assault rifle ammunition and avoiding high-damage attacks.
  • Combine the Electronic Gadget with the Battery to assemble the Detonator needed to blow open the Shower Room wall.
