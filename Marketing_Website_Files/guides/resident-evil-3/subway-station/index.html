Subway Station

Resident Evil 3 guide · Meeting Carlos and the UBCS team
← All areas in this guide

Jill is brought to the Redstone Street subway station by Carlos Oliveira to meet the surviving members of the UBCS platoon led by Mikhail Victor. After Mikhail asks for Jill's help to get the train operational so they can evacuate civilians, she heads up to street level to restore power to the tracks. The subway station concourse serves as an initial hub and safe room containing an Item Box and a Typewriter.

Enemies

Weak to Headshots

Several dormant or walking zombies appear in the street area right outside the station shutter once you proceed upward.

Shops and people
  • Mikhail Victor: Directs Jill to find the substation and restore power to the tracks
  • Carlos Oliveira: Accompanies Jill to the train, provides initial briefing, and gives communications via radio

Tips
  • The Item Box and Typewriter in the concourse allow you to safely store unnecessary items and save your progress before opening the shutter to Downtown.
  • Don't miss the Charlie Doll tucked beside the newspaper stands before exiting the station gates onto the street.
