Forgotten Crossroads (Infected Crossroads)

Hollow Knight guide · Returning to access new paths after gaining abilities

The Forgotten Crossroads transforms into the Infected Crossroads after you kill your first Dreamer or obtain the Monarch Wings. The area is overgrown with volatile orange pustules, blocking several main paths and turning ordinary creatures into aggressive, bloated mutants. Navigating requires finding alternate routes around blocked hallways while dealing with high-damage, exploding threats.

Tips

  • Many vertical transition shafts are completely walled off by orange pustules, so check your map and use detour tunnels through the sub-caves to travel between Dirtmouth and Greenpath.
  • Stay at maximum melee distance or use Shade Soul when finishing off swollen enemies, as their death explosions inflict two masks of damage.
  • Tram access to the Resting Grounds and Deepnest remains fully functional and serves as a reliable bypass around heavily blocked central rooms.
