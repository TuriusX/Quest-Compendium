Forgotten Crossroads

Hollow Knight guide · The first major region explored after Dirtmouth

The Forgotten Crossroads is the first underground area you explore directly beneath the town of Dirtmouth. Your primary objective here is to defeat the False Knight to earn the City Crest, find the Shaman to obtain the Vengeful Spirit spell, and unlock transit options. Later in the game, the entire area becomes the Infected Crossroads, blocking certain paths and introducing more dangerous enemies.

Items

  • Forgotten Crossroads Map: Bought from Cornifer in a lower central cavern
  • City Crest: Dropped by the False Knight in the center of the region
  • Vengeful Spirit: Gifted by the Snail Shaman in the Ancestral Mound on the right side of the crossroads
  • Vessel Fragment: Found on the far western path guarded by a Brooding Mawlek
  • Mask Shard: In the far western room with flying Aspid Hunters, reached via platforming
  • Mask Shard: Rescued from the giant grub room after defeating the Gruz Mother and platforming
  • Hot Spring: Located in a cavern on the upper right side to heal masks and regenerate Soul quickly
  • Simple Key: Sold by Sly after you rescue him from a derelict house in the southeastern corner

Secrets

  • A hidden breakable wall in the room to the left of the False Knight arena allows you to bypass the second phase of the False Knight fight entirely.
  • A breakable wall in the long elevator shaft leading to Dirtmouth contains a hidden Soul Totem and Geo cache.

Shops and people

  • Cornifer: Sells the Forgotten Crossroads region map, along with the Quill and basic Wayward Compass charm if missed earlier
  • The Last Stag: Offers fast-travel transportation to other unlocked Stag Stations for a small Geo fee
  • Salubra: Sells various utility Charms and Charm Notches in her shop in the lower southeastern section, accessed via Mothwing Cloak

Tips

  • Always follow the paper trail on the ground and listen for humming to easily find Cornifer's location when entering new rooms.
  • You can bounce off of pink spikes and certain hard surfaces by slashing downward with your nail to reach areas earlier than intended.
