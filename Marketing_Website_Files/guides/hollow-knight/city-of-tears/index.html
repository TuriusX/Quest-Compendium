City of Tears

Hollow Knight guide · Reached after obtaining the Mantis Claw

The City of Tears is the sprawling, rain-drenched heart of Hallownest, divided into an eastern and western side. You enter by inserting the City Crest into the grand gates, explore flooded corridors and lavish spires, and encounter key utility NPCs like the Nailsmith and Relic Seeker Lemm. Key objectives here include upgrading your Nail, unlocking the central stagway, and infiltrating the Soul Sanctum to gain a crucial movement and combat spell.

Items

  • Desolate Dive: Soul Sanctum, awarded immediately upon defeating the Soul Master
  • Spell Twister: Soul Sanctum, found in a high ceiling chamber full of Mistake enemies before the boss room
  • Simple Key: Below the City Storerooms near the Royal Waterways entrance, guarded by Winged Sentries

Secrets

  • Nailsmith's basement breakable wall: To the far left of the Nailsmith's exterior hut, the ground and side wall can be broken to reveal a hidden Geo cache and Wanderer's Journal.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Nailsmith: Upgrades the damage of your Nail in exchange for Geo and Pale Ore
  • Relic Seeker Lemm: Buys Wanderer's Journals, Hallownest Seals, King's Idols, and Arcane Eggs in exchange for Geo
  • Tuk: Sells Rancid Eggs in exchange for Geo in the waterways access area below the city

Tips

  • Equip the Fragile Greed charm when exploring the grand houses, as the wealthy noble husks drop significantly more Geo than standard enemies.
  • Use Desolate Dive on loose, shaking floors marked with cracked glass or rattling stones to reveal shortcuts and sub-areas.
