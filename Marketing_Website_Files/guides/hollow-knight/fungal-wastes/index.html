Fungal Wastes

Hollow Knight guide · Reached after passing through Queen's Station

The Fungal Wastes is a damp, spore-choked subterranean region filled with bouncy purple mushrooms and acid hazards. Players travel through this area to unlock the Mantis Claw wall-jump ability in the Mantis Village and gain access to the City of Tears. Navigating the zone requires bouncing off environmental objects using the downward nail strike and dealing with territorial mantis warriors.

Items

  • Mantis Claw: Located in the Mantis Village within a dedicated chamber on the upper western side
  • Dashmaster: Found underneath a large stone statue below the Mantis Village near the entrance to the Royal Waterways
  • Spore Shroom: Located in a lower cavern near the border between the Fungal Wastes and the Queen's Gardens, requiring wall jumping to reach

Secrets

  • Bretta can be rescued behind a breakable wall in the lower section of the wastes near the Dashmaster charm location.
  • A lore tablet and hidden geo cache can be found behind a false wall high above the main path leading toward the Mantis Village.

Shops and people

  • Leg Eater: Sells the Fragile Heart, Fragile Greed, and Fragile Strength charms, and repairs them if broken
  • Cloth: An NPC found fighting hostile bugs who offers dialogue and later moves to other locations

Tips

  • Downward slashing (pogoing) on the purple bouncy mushrooms grants extra jump height needed to cross large gaps and acid pools.
  • Defeating the Mantis Lords boss at the bottom of the village causes all surviving Mantis enemies in the area to become passive and bow to you.
