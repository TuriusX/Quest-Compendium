Mantis Village

Hollow Knight guide · A sub-area within the Fungal Wastes

Mantis Village is a hazardous enclave deep in the Fungal Wastes governed by a proud, hostile tribe of mantises. Navigating through its lower chambers requires flipping switches to unlock the Mantis Claw, which is essential for wall-jumping. After descending into the lowest chamber, the player can challenge the Mantis Lords, which earns the respect of the tribe and unlocks a path into Deepnest.

Items

  • Mantis Claw: In the upper-west portion of the main village structures on an altar
  • Mark of Pride: Inside the treasure room in the northeast corner of the village, unlocked only after defeating the Mantis Lords
  • Hallownest Seal: Found inside a chest in the Mantis Lords' treasure room

Secrets

  • A Lore Tablet is hidden behind a breakable wooden wall near the upper pathways of the village.

Tips

  • Upgrade your weapon with the Nailsmith in the City of Tears before fighting the Mantis Lords to make the battle significantly shorter and easier.
  • Once the Mantis Lords are defeated, the normal mantis enemies throughout the village will bow to you and will no longer attack unless struck first.
