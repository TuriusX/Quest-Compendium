Dirtmouth

Hollow Knight guide · The first town reached after the tutorial

Dirtmouth is the quiet, fading town above Hallownest that serves as the central hub of your journey. You can speak with Elderbug, access a bench to save, open shops as you rescue merchants from the ruins below, and enter the well to begin exploring the Forgotten Crossroads. Later in the game, returning here allows you to access fast travel stations, summon your shade, and host the Grimm Troupe.

Items

  • Simple Key: Sold in Sly's shop inside Dirtmouth
  • Lumafly Lantern: Sold in Sly's shop inside Dirtmouth
  • Wayward Compass: Sold in Iselda's shop inside Dirtmouth
  • Quill: Sold in Iselda's shop inside Dirtmouth
  • King's Idol: Found inside the house of Bretta after rescuing her from the Fungal Wastes
  • Mask Shard: Sold in Sly's shop inside Dirtmouth
  • Vessel Fragment: Sold in Sly's shop inside Dirtmouth

Secrets

  • Opening the stone door on the far right of town requires a Simple Key to meet Confessor Jiji
  • Ringing the bell at the Stag Station on the left side of town opens the roof hatch and unlocks fast travel back up to the surface
  • Lighting the Nightmare Lantern in the Howling Cliffs brings the Grimm Troupe tents to the far left side of Dirtmouth

Shops and people

  • Elderbug: Offers lore, hints about nearby areas, and friendly conversation throughout your adventure
  • Iselda: Sells Wayward Compass, Quill, maps, map markers, and pins once unlocked after finding Cornifer below
  • Sly: Sells various Charms, Mask Shards, Vessel Fragments, Simple Keys, and the Lumafly Lantern after being rescued from the Forgotten Crossroads
  • Salubra: Accessible through the Forgotten Crossroads shaft to the far bottom-right; sells Charms, Charm Notches, and Salubra's Blessing
  • Confessor Jiji: Summons your lost Shade from anywhere in the kingdom in exchange for a Rancid Egg once her stone door is opened
  • Tuk: Found in the Royal Waterways, not Dirtmouth; do not confuse with Confessor Jiji

Tips

  • Make sure to talk to Elderbug whenever you return; he reacts to major events and provides helpful hints on where to go next.
  • Purchase the Quill and Wayward Compass from Iselda as soon as possible to make mapping Hallownest manageable.
  • If you die and leave your Shade in a dangerous or hard-to-reach location, bring a Rancid Egg to Jiji's hut on the far right to retrieve it safely.
