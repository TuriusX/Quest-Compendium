Deepnest

Hollow Knight guide · Reached after obtaining the Mantis Claw

Deepnest is a dark, maze-like subterranean zone infested with arachnid enemies and hazardous environment traps. Players explore the area to find the Dreamer Herrah the Beast, locate powerful upgrades, and rescue various captured NPCs. The Lumafly Lantern is practically essential here due to the extreme darkness throughout the region.

Items

  • Tram Pass: Inside the Ruined Tramway in an abandoned tram car
  • Pale Ore: Guarded by Nosk in its lair behind a breakable wall near the hot spring
  • King's Idol: Carried by Zote the Mighty trapped in webs near the top-left section
  • Spore Shroom: Behind a breakable wall in a cavity between Deepnest and the Fungal Wastes
  • Weaversong: Found in the Weaver's Den, deep within the upper areas of the region
  • Vessel Fragment: Found in the Garpede tunnel requiring precise downward strikes to bounce across spikes
  • Mask Shard: Found in a small room behind breakable structures near the Mantis Lords entrance

Secrets

  • Nosk's Lair can be found by breaking a hidden cracked wall just west of the Deepnest hot spring.
  • Weaver's Den entrance is concealed behind a breakable wall covered in thick spider webs near the upper-middle section.
  • Midwife's room hides behind a breakable wall in the lower passages below the Distant Village.

Tips

  • Buy the Lumafly Lantern from Sly in Dirtmouth before entering, or navigating the pitch-black rooms will be nearly impossible.
  • Listen carefully for chittering noises to avoid being surprised by collapsing floors that drop you into enemy ambushes.
  • Use the Desolate Dive or Descending Dark spell for invincibility frames when trapped in tight tunnels with Stalking Devouts.
