Greenpath

Hollow Knight guide · Reached after obtaining the Vengeful Spirit

Greenpath is an overgrown, acidic region reached from the Forgotten Crossroads after defeating the False Knight and learning the Vengeful Spirit spell. Navigating this area requires leaping over pools of acid while pursuing the mysterious cloaked figure, Hornet. Defeating Hornet here grants the Mothwing Cloak, enabling a horizontal dash that opens up the rest of the kingdom.

Items

  • Mothwing Cloak: Dropped upon defeating Hornet at the far western edge of the area
  • Hunter's Journal: Given by the Hunter in his lair located in the eastern portion of the area
  • Thorns of Agony: Located inside a maze of thorns in the lower section, guarded by Charged Lumaflys
  • Great Slash: Taught by Nailmaster Sheo inside his hut located at the far southwest end
  • Shape of Unn: Granted by the slug deity Unn in the Lake of Unn beneath Sheo's hut, accessible with Isma's Tear

Secrets

  • Massive Moss Charger ambush room containing a hidden passage to the Journal entry for the Mossy Vagabond.
  • Hidden breakable ceiling in the room with the first Moss Knight that conceals a cache of Geo.

Shops and people

  • Cornifer: Sells the Greenpath map for 60 Geo near the initial entrance from the Crossroads
  • Zote the Mighty: Needs to be rescued from the jaws of a Vengefly King in the northwestern canopy

Tips

  • Strike down on the shells of Durandoos and pink acid buds to pogo across deadly acid pools safely.
  • Listen for Cornifer's humming and follow the loose paper pages scattered on the floor to track down his hiding spots.
  • Hornet lunges forward and throws her needle; jump straight up to easily dodge the needle and strike her as it returns.
