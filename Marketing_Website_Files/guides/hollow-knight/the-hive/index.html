The Hive

Hollow Knight guide · Reached from Kingdom's Edge

The Hive is an isolated bee-inhabited sub-area hidden within Kingdom's Edge, coated in amber and dripping with honey. The Knight explores winding golden caverns, encounters territorial bee husks, and reaches the chamber of Hive Knight. Defeating the boss grants access to the area's primary reward, the Hiveblood charm.

Items

  • Hiveblood: awarded after defeating Hive Knight at the deepest point of The Hive
  • Mask Shard: embedded in a wall reachable by baiting a Hive Guardian to break the cracked amber
  • Grub: trapped in a jar near the ceiling of a vertical shaft guarded by bees

Secrets

  • The entrance to The Hive is concealed behind a breakable wall in Kingdom's Edge, near the tram station, requiring a spell or nail strike to reveal.

Shops and people

  • Vespa: offers dialogue and lore about the fate of the Hive once Hive Knight is defeated

Tips

  • Use Hive Guardians to smash cracked amber barriers that your nail cannot break to reach hidden rooms and items.
  • Equipping Hiveblood lets you regenerate the last lost mask of health over time, which is especially useful for challenging platforming sections elsewhere in Hallownest.
