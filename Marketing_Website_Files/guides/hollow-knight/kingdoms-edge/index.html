Kingdom's Edge

Hollow Knight guide · Reached after obtaining the Monarch Wings

Kingdom's Edge is a desolate, ash-covered expanse situated on the far eastern border of Hallownest. Players explore deep vertical shafts and acidic pits to reach the Colosseum of Fools and confront Hornet for a second time. Defeating Hornet allows access to the Cast-Off Shell, where you can retrieve the King's Brand to unlock sealed doors throughout the kingdom.

Items

  • King's Brand: Inside the Cast-Off Shell after defeating Hornet
  • Quick Slash: Located in a hidden room deep in the lower-right section near Oro's hut, behind a breakable wall guarded by Great Hoppers
  • Pale Ore: Awarded for completing the Trial of the Conqueror in the Colosseum of Fools

Secrets

  • The Hive's entrance is located behind a breakable wall next to a cluster of Hiveling bees near the tram station.
  • Nailmaster Oro's training room can be found at the very bottom right of the area, accessible by descending the vertical shafts.

Tips

  • Use Great Slash or Dash Slash to instantly defeat Primal Aspids before they have a chance to shoot their spread projectiles.
  • Equip the Spore Shroom or Desolate Dive/Descending Dark charms in the Colosseum of Fools to manage cramped floor hazards and flying enemies.
