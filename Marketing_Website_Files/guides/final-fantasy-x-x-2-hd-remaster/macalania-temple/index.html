Macalania Temple

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The temple and the confrontation with Seymour

The party arrives at Macalania Temple to deliver Jyscal Guado's sphere, which reveals Seymour's crimes, leading directly to a confrontation with Maester Seymour. After defeating Seymour and escaping the temple through the Cloister of Trials, Yuna earns the summon Shiva, and the party must flee Guado pursuers on the frozen lake.

Items

  • Lucid Ring MISSABLE: In a chest inside the Great Hall on the left side before entering the Cloister
  • Mega-Potion MISSABLE: In a chest inside the Great Hall on the right side
  • 5,000 Gil MISSABLE: In a chest inside the living quarters/monks' room in the temple
  • Phoenix Down x2 MISSABLE: In a chest inside the living quarters/monks' room in the temple
  • Elixir MISSABLE: In a chest in the temple entrance hallway
  • Level 1 Key Sphere: Reward for defeating Seymour and Anima
  • Luck Sphere: In a chest on the Cloister of Trials floor revealed using the Destruction Sphere

Secrets

  • Destruction Sphere reward: Place the Destruction Sphere into the slot on the lower level of the Cloister of Trials to reveal a hidden path that leads to a chest containing a Luck Sphere, which is required to unlock the bonus in Baaj Temple later.

Tips

  • Talk to Seymour with Tidus and Yuna using the "Trigger Command" during battle to boost Tidus's Strength and Yuna's Magic Defense.
  • Steal from the Guado Guardians immediately to strip their ability to use Auto-Potion items.
  • Summon the new Aeon, Shiva (labeled ?????? at first), to counter Anima, and use her Blizzara on herself to heal.
  • Cast Shell and the appropriate Nul-element spells with Yuna to easily negate Seymour's multi-hit magic attacks.
