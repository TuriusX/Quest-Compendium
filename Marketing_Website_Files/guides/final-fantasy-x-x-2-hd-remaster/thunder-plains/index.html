Thunder Plains

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The storm-ridden plains

The party traverses a hazardous wasteland constantly struck by lightning while heading north toward Macalania. Players must dodge lightning strikes across the southern and northern fields and take shelter at the midway agency.

Items

  • Phoenix Down x2: In a treasure chest near the path in the southern section
  • Hi-Potion x2: In a treasure chest in the southern section
  • 5,000 Gil: In a treasure chest along the western perimeter of the southern section
  • Water Ball: In a treasure chest near the path in the northern section
  • Ether: In a treasure chest in the northern section
  • Remedy: In a treasure chest in the northern section
  • Yellow Ring MISSABLE: Received from Rikku inside the Travel Agency
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XIV MISSABLE: Handed over by Rin inside the Travel Agency when asked how your Al Bhed is going

Secrets

  • Venus Sigil | Dodge 200 consecutive lightning bolts without leaving the screen or saving, then open the reward chest outside the Travel Agency
  • Qactuar Stone activation | Pray with the square button at three glowing Qactuar stones across the plains to release Qactuar spirits into the area
  • Spirit of the Qactuar | After activating the stones, follow the ghost of a Qactuar in the southern section to a ruined lightning tower and pray to reveal a chest containing Kimahri's celestial weapon, the Spirit Lance

  • Rin's Travel Agency Shop: Sells weapons, armor, Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and status-clearing items

Tips

  • Press the confirm button the exact split-second the screen flashes white to successfully dodge a lightning bolt.
  • Equip armor with Lightning Ward or Lightning Proof to significantly cut down magic damage taken in regular encounters.
  • Equip gear with the No Encounters ability when attempting the 200 consecutive lightning dodges to prevent random battles from breaking your streak.
  • A small crater near a lightning tower in the southern plains triggers lightning strikes consistently every time you step into it, making rhythm timing easier for dodging.
