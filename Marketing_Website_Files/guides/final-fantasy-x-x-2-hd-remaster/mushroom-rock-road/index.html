Mushroom Rock Road

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The operation against Sin

Yuna and her guardians arrive at Mushroom Rock Road to witness Operation Mi'ihen, a joint military assault against Sin staged by the Crusaders and the Al Bhed. You must navigate the winding cliff paths to reach the command center, speak with key officers, and prepare for battle. The operation ends in disaster, leading to a direct confrontation with a piece of Sin before you can move on to Djose.

Items

  • 1,000 Gil: In a treasure chest near the beginning of the road, tucked behind a rock formation
  • Remedy: In a chest off to the side on the winding lower valley path
  • Hi-Potion: Inside a treasure chest on the second elevator platform ledge
  • Serpentine Ring: Found in a chest at the dead end of a high cliff ledge
  • Mega-Potion: Inside a chest located near the command center staging area
  • Al Bhed Primer Vol. X: Found on a small dead-end ledge hidden behind a Crusader near the elevator lifts

Secrets

  • Godhand Celestial Weapon spot | A hidden lower valley area accessible much later via the airship by entering the password "GODHAND" in the coordinates menu | missable: no
  • Rusty Sword | An antique sword embedded in the ground on a side path off the command plateau, used later to unlock Auron's Masamune Celestial Weapon | missable: no

Tips

  • Use Wakka to inflict Silence or Darkness on the head of Sinspawn Gui to significantly reduce the incoming damage to the party.
  • Equip Auron with armor that protects against Poison or Darkness prior to the Sinspawn Gui battle.
  • Auron's Armor Break will not affect Sinspawn Gui's main body while its arms are fully intact.
