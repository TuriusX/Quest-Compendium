Moonflow

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The river crossing with the shoopuf

The party travels to the Moonflow to cross the river using a giant creature called a Shoopuf. While crossing underwater, Tidus and Wakka must fight an Al Bhed war machine to rescue Yuna. After reaching the north bank, the party recruits Rikku as a permanent guardian before moving onward to Guadosalam.

Items

  • Lv. 1 Key Sphere: in a chest on the Moonflow South Bank path before the dock
  • X-Potion: in a chest on the Moonflow South Bank path
  • Magicite x2: in a chest on the Moonflow South Wharf
  • Phoenix Down x2: in a chest on the Moonflow South Wharf
  • Ether: in a chest on the Moonflow North Bank path
  • Mega-Potion: in a chest on the Moonflow North Bank path
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XI: on the ground near the terminal path on the North Bank
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XII: on the ground at the North Bank dock area

Secrets

  • Dragon Scale x4 can be obtained by talking to the Hypello at the South Wharf terminal.

Shops and people

  • Oaka XXIII: Sells standard restorative items, Phoenix Downs, and specialized weapons/armor with various status-inflicting or status-warding abilities.

Tips

  • Cast Haste on both Tidus and Wakka during the Extractor battle to deal enough damage to interrupt its Aqua Shooter.
  • Use Rikku's Steal command immediately after she joins to gather useful synthesis items and eliminate Machina enemies in one hit.
