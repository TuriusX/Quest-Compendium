FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The Al Bhed base under attack

Tidus and the party arrive at the Al Bhed sanctuary, Home, only to find it under siege by the Guado and fiends. The party must fight their way through burning corridors, rescue the captive summoners, and discover the truth about the Final Summoning before escaping on an airship. This entire location is permanently destroyed after the events here, making all items and Al Bhed Primers within it permanently missable.

Items

  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XIX MISSABLE: On the ground outside the entrance before going inside
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XX MISSABLE: On a bed in the Living Quarters hallway
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XXI MISSABLE: At the end of the Main Corridor on the floor
  • Friend Sphere MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the Living Quarters unlocked by translating an Al Bhed riddle
  • Special Sphere MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the Living Quarters unlocked by selecting correct Al Bhed words
  • Skill Sphere MISSABLE: Inside a chest in the Summoners' Sanctum area

Secrets

  • The compilation spheres in the Living Quarters offer two distinct puzzles entirely written in Al Bhed that yield rare spheres (Friend Sphere and Special Sphere) if deciphered.

Tips

  • Do not rush toward the airship before picking up Al Bhed Primers XIX, XX, and XXI, as you cannot return to Home once you leave.
  • Use Silence or Darkness on Guado Guardians to reduce their ability to heal themselves and buff their fiend companions.
