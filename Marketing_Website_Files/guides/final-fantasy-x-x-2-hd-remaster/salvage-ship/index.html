Salvage Ship

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The Al Bhed ship where Tidus is rescued

Tidus is fished out of the ocean by an Al Bhed crew led by Rikku and forced into salvage work under the sea. You explore the deck of the vessel, unlock the Sphere Grid tutorial, and dive to an underwater ruin to power up a sunken airship. After completing the salvage operation and defeating a boss, Sin attacks the vessel and sweeps Tidus away to Besaid.

Items

  • Al Bhed Primer Vol. I MISSABLE: Sitting on top of crates on the right side of the main deck
  • Potion x3 MISSABLE: Talk to the Al Bhed standing on the left side of the upper deck

Secrets

  • You can use the Al Bhed Compilation Sphere near the save sphere to sync unlocked Primers from other save files.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes

Shops and people

  • Al Bhed on the lower deck: Potions

Tips

  • Don't forget to grab the Al Bhed Primer Vol. I before speaking with Rikku to dive, as the ship cannot be revisited once you leave.
  • Rikku's Use command can throw Grenades during battles here, dealing heavy area-of-effect damage to quickly wipe out groups of Piranhas.
