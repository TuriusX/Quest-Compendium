Calm Lands

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The vast plains leading to Mt. Gagazet

The Calm Lands is an expansive open plain connecting Macalania Woods to Mt. Gagazet where high summoners historically battled Sin. Players can engage in chocobo training mini-games, capture fiends for the Monster Arena, and acquire powerful celestial armament materials. It also serves as the crossroads leading to the optional Sunken Cave and the entrance to Gagazet.

Items

  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XXIII: In the far northwestern section of the plains, lying on the ground near the edge of the chasm
  • Rusty Sword: Lodged in the ground on a secluded cliff ledge near the entrance to the Sunken Cave in the northeast
  • Nebilim: Dropped as a reward inside the Monster Arena after capturing one of every fiend throughout the Calm Lands

Secrets

  • Obtaining the Cloudy Mirror requires winning the Chocobo Trainer mini-game to gain access to riding chocobos, then examining a yellow chocobo feather in the southeast to leap down to the Remiem Temple entrance.
  • Finding Yojimbo requires descending into the Cavern of the Stolen Scarred / Sunken Cave beneath the northern bridge, defeating the summoner within, and paying his negotiation fee.
  • Accessing Tidus's Celestial Weapon, the Caladbolg, requires winning the Catcher Chocobo mini-game with a time under 0.0 seconds to make the guard blocking the narrow ramp in the northwest move away, then opening the crest with the Celestial Mirror.

Shops and people

  • Monster Arena Proprietor: Sells Capture weapons customized to harvest live fiends across Spira
  • Calm Lands Central Travel Agency: Sells standard restorative items including Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Antidotes

Tips

  • Buy Capture weapons for your frontline physical attackers immediately upon opening the Monster Arena so you can capture enemies as you cross the plain.
  • Equip armor with Confuseproof or Berserkproof to survive random ambush encounters with wild Malboros.
  • Avoid moving birds rather than aggressively chasing balloons in the Catcher Chocobo mini-game, as collisions incur a severe time penalty.
