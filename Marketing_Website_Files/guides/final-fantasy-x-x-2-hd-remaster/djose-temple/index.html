Djose Temple

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The temple and the Chamber of the Fayth

Djose Temple is the lightning-aspected trial area where Yuna completes her Cloister of Trials to earn the Aeon Ixion. The party arrives here after crossing the Djose Highroad, solving a puzzle primarily based on powering electrical pedestals with spheres. Afterward, Yuna sleeps at the inn before the journey continues toward the Moonflow.

Items

  • Ether: In a chest inside the living quarters on the left side of the temple lobby
  • Mega Phoenix: In a chest in the living quarters on the right side of the temple lobby
  • Ability Sphere x4: In a chest located outside behind the temple exterior wall on the far left
  • Magic Sphere: Inside the Chamber of the Fayth in a treasure chest
  • Agility Sphere: Inside the Chamber of the Fayth in a treasure chest
  • Rematch: Inside the Chamber of the Fayth, awarded to Wakka

Secrets

  • Destruction Sphere Treasure: Insert the Destruction Sphere into the pedestal in the Cloister of Trials to break the seal near the exit stairs, revealing a chest containing an Agility Sphere.

Tips

  • You must acquire the Destruction Sphere treasure here if you plan to unlock the secret Aeon Anima later in the game.
  • In the Cloister, pushing the pedestal across the electrified floor gap creates a stepping-stone path to reach the far side of the room.
  • Inspect the pedestals in the upper gallery room to push them into place, completing the lightning circuit that powers the elevator.
