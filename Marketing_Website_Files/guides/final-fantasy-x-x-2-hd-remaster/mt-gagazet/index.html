Mt. Gagazet

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The mountain path leading to the summit

Mt. Gagazet is the sacred homeland of the Ronso tribe, which the party must traverse through harsh blizzards to reach Zanarkand. Along the mountain path, Kimahri faces his old rivals Biran and Yenke in a solo battle before the party continues toward a confrontation with Seymour. Players must push through heavy encounters and prepare for intense boss fights prior to entering the mountain's cave system.

Items

  • 20,000 Gil: In a treasure chest hidden in a small alcove along the early winding mountain trail
  • Mega-Potion: In a treasure chest located along the main snowy path
  • Braska's Sphere: Dropped on the path just past the area where you fight Biran and Yenke
  • Defending Bracer: In a treasure chest tucked away along the snowy route before the summit
  • HP Sphere: In a treasure chest hidden at the end of a dead-end pathway on the mountain trail
  • Lv. 4 Key Sphere: In a treasure chest situated on a narrow side trail near the top of the outdoor pass

Secrets

  • Kimahri can use Lancet to learn multiple Ronso Rages (Thrust Kick, Doom, Fire Breath, Aqua Breath, Self-Destruct, Stone Breath, White Wind, and Mighty Guard) directly from Biran and Yenke during their duel.

Tips

  • In Kimahri's duel against Biran and Yenke, their stats scale based on Kimahri's current stats, making it manageable even if you have not leveled him heavily.
  • Equip armor with Zombie Ward or Zombieproof for the Seymour Flux battle to counter his Lance of Atrophy and Full-Life instant-kill combo.
  • Use Bio or Rikku's Poison concoctions on Seymour Flux, as he is susceptible to Poison damage throughout the battle.
