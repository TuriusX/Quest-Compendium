Kilika Woods

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The path through the forest to the temple

The party travels through this lush forest to reach Kilika Temple so Yuna can pray to the Fayth. Along the way, players learn how to handle agile, armored, and elemental enemies, culminating in a mandatory boss battle against Lord Ochu or Geneaux. The area serves as an early-game combat tutorial zone where Kimahri can also learn a new Ronso Rage.

Items

  • Potion: In a treasure chest just off the main path near the entrance
  • Antidote: In a chest found along the eastern pathway
  • Scout: In a chest awarded or found on the path leading north toward the temple
  • Elixir: Given by the Crusaders if you defeat the optional boss Lord Ochu
  • Luck Sphere: In a treasure chest hidden in the clearing behind Lord Ochu

Secrets

  • Lord Ochu | A powerful, sleeping plant creature resting off the main clearing that can be fought optionally or bypassed entirely.

Tips

  • Have Kimahri use Lancet on Ragora to learn the Ronso Rage ability "Seed Cannon".
  • If you choose to fight the sleeping Lord Ochu, use Lulu's Fire magic and inflict Sleep or Darkness to survive its devastating physical attacks.
  • Talk to the Crusader guards stationed around the woods to receive free restorative items like Potions.
