Zanarkand Dome

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The interior of the dome and the final trial

The party enters the Zanarkand Dome to complete the final trial in the Cloister of Trials and meet with Lady Yunalesca. You must solve shape-matching floor puzzles across two rooms and defeat the Spectral Keeper before descending to Yunalesca's chamber. Here, the truth behind the Final Summoning is revealed, culminating in a three-phase boss fight against Yunalesca.

Items

  • Sun Crest: In a treasure chest located in the back of the Great Hall where you fought Yunalesca (down the northern stairs after the battle)

Secrets

  • Returning to the Cloister of Trials after acquiring the airship reveals seven white square puzzles to solve in both rooms, which opens the way to the Destruction Sphere treasure containing the Magistral Rod.

Tips

  • Do not cure the Zombie status on all three active party members during the second and third phases of the fight against Yunalesca, as Zombie status is the only way to survive her Mega-Death attack.
  • Grab the Sun Crest from the chest immediately after defeating Yunalesca before leaving the room, otherwise the extremely difficult optional boss Dark Bahamut will block access to it later in the HD Remaster.
  • Use the Hastega and Protect spells against Spectral Keeper, and position characters on separate glyph platforms using the "Move" command to minimize multi-target counterattack damage.
