This is the final dungeon inside Sin, consisting of the City of Dying Dreams and the crystalline Dream's End. Players navigate crumbling ruins, collect floating crystals, and step into the core to confront Braska's Final Aeon and Yu Yevon. Once you enter the final portal past the save sphere, you pass a point of no return.

Enemies
Great Malboro
Weak to FireSteal / drop Celestial Mirror / Remedy

Ambushing parties frequently with Bad Breath; First Strike is essential to avoid party-wide status effects.

Behemoth King
Steal / drop Twin Stars

Casts Meteor as a final counterattack upon dying, dealing massive damage to the entire front line.

Adamantoise
Steal / drop Stamina Spring

High physical defense and health; vulnerable to Armor Break and Piercing weapons.

Barbatos
Steal / drop Star Curtain

Very high HP and defense, counters physical attacks with Mortar.

Demonolith
Weak to HolySteal / drop Petrify Grenade

Frequently counters attacks with Breath, which can inflict Petrify on the whole party.

Gemini (Sword)
Steal / drop Light Curtain

Fights alongside the Club variant and can unleash heavy physical combination attacks.

Gemini (Club)
Steal / drop Star Curtain

High strength fiend that acts in tandem with the Sword variant.

Land Worm
Weak to Water / IceSteal / drop Stamina Spring

Possesses high HP and uses Earthquake to hit the entire party for earth damage.

Wraith
Weak to HolySteal / drop Farplane Shadow

Uses high-tier black magic and Doom; vulnerable to physical attacks and Holy.

Tips
  • Equip armor with Auto-Med or Confuse, Berserk, and Poison protection to counter the frequent status ailments inflicted by fiends here.
  • Have a character equip a weapon with the First Strike ability so you can switch to a summoner or flee if ambushed by a Great Malboro.
  • Cast Auto-Life or summon an Aeon to deliver the finishing blow to Behemoth King to survive its lethal Meteor counter.
  • Make a separate hard save at the final save sphere in Dream's End before stepping into the central structure, as you cannot save after triggering the final boss sequence.
