Sin: The Nucleus

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The final area inside Sin

This is the final dungeon inside Sin, consisting of the City of Dying Dreams and the crystalline Dream's End. Players navigate crumbling ruins, collect floating crystals, and step into the core to confront Braska's Final Aeon and Yu Yevon. Once you enter the final portal past the save sphere, you pass a point of no return.

Items

  • Four-on-One: inside a treasure chest on the right path in the City of Dying Dreams
  • Defeater: inside an alcove in the City of Dying Dreams
  • Elixir: inside a chest found along the central route of the City of Dying Dreams
  • Mega-Potion: inside a treasure chest along the pathway before the tower
  • Phantom Ring: inside a chest in the City of Dying Dreams
  • Special Sphere: inside a treasure chest in the City of Dying Dreams
  • Lv. 3 Key Sphere: inside a chest in the City of Dying Dreams
  • Master Sphere MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest in the final crystalline room after collecting ten items

Secrets

  • Touching ten glowing crystals while dodging falling icicles in Dream's End spawns a chest containing a Master Sphere.
  • Stepping on a specific glyph on the floor in the City of Dying Dreams reveals an alcove with a hidden treasure chest.

Tips

  • Equip armor with Auto-Med or Confuse, Berserk, and Poison protection to counter the frequent status ailments inflicted by fiends here.
  • Have a character equip a weapon with the First Strike ability so you can switch to a summoner or flee if ambushed by a Great Malboro.
  • Cast Auto-Life or summon an Aeon to deliver the finishing blow to Behemoth King to survive its lethal Meteor counter.
  • Make a separate hard save at the final save sphere in Dream's End before stepping into the central structure, as you cannot save after triggering the final boss sequence.
