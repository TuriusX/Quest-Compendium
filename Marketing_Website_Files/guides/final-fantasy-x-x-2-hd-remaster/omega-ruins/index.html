Omega Ruins

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · Optional high-level dungeon found via Airship

The Omega Ruins is an optional endgame cavern accessible by inputting coordinates into the Airship's navigation map. The dungeon consists of two mazelike tiers filled with exceptionally powerful fiends and two bosses. Players venture here to obtain ultimate armor crafting materials, obtain Rikku's Level 4 Key Spheres, capture deadly fiends for the Monster Arena, and defeat Omega Weapon.

Items

  • Warmonger: inside a treasure chest on the upper tier
  • Teleport Sphere: inside a treasure chest on the upper tier
  • Friend Sphere: inside a treasure chest on the upper tier
  • Magic Sphere: inside a treasure chest on the upper tier

Secrets

  • Accessing the dungeon requires manually entering the coordinates X: 69-75 and Y: 33-38 on the Airship search screen.
  • Opening the clusters of treasure chests found in the dungeon triggers a gambling minigame; opening a chest without triggering a Mimic battle multiplies the rewards of subsequent chests up to 99 Warp Spheres, but finding a Mimic resets the streak.

Tips

  • Equip a weapon with the First Strike auto-ability on a frontline party member so you can immediately switch to Yuna to summon an Aeon or use Flee before a Great Malboro can use Bad Breath.
  • Equip armor with Confuseproof and Stoneproof, as many fiends here inflict sudden petrification and confusion that can quickly wipe your party.
  • Rikku's Gillionaire ability on the Godhand should be active when defeating Mimics to double their payout to 100,000 Gil per fight.
