Macalania Woods

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The forest leading to the lake

The party travels through the shimmering Macalania Woods toward the frozen Macalania Lake while chasing Auron and fleeing Guado pursuers. Navigating the winding crystalline paths leads to a clearing where the party sets up camp before encountering a massive elemental sphere. This area also serves as a crucial hub for the celestial mirror quest and the butterfly-hunting minigame.

Items

  • Sleepy Cait Sith: South area, chest hidden behind trees on the lower path
  • 2000 Gil: Central area, treasure chest sitting in plain sight along the path
  • Phoenix Down x3: Central area, treasure chest along the winding path
  • Remedy: North area, treasure chest tucked along the upper branch
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XV: North area, on the ground near the save sphere before the Lake exit
  • Lucid Ring: Lake Road, chest sitting directly beside the save sphere

Secrets

  • The Cloudy Mirror can be transformed into the Celestial Mirror by bringing it to the great crystal in the woods after reuniting the lost boy with his parents at the campsite.
  • Touching glowing blue butterflies starts a timed minigame where you must collect blue butterflies while avoiding red butterflies to win rare sphere items.

Tips

  • Use magic of the opposing element on Spherimorph immediately after it casts a spell (e.g., hit with Blizzard if it casts Fire).
  • Auron can learn the Armor Break ability around this time to easily bypass the sturdy shells of Murussu.
  • Running into red butterflies during the butterfly minigame triggers an immediate, unskippable battle and wastes precious seconds.
