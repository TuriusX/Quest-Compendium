Sin: Sea of Sorrow

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The first interior area of Sin

This is the first sub-area encountered inside Sin, consisting of a network of dark ledges surrounded by swirling purple water. The party must navigate through the branching paths and defeat the boss, Land Worm, located at the far northern end to reach the next area. A save sphere is available near the entrance to rest and return to the airship if needed.

Items

  • Phantom Ring: Inside a chest situated on an eastern path segment
  • Special Sphere: Inside a chest found on a dead-end pathway on the western side
  • Elixir: Inside a chest located along the central-eastern path
  • Wizard Knight: Inside a chest near the northern exit ledge

Tips

  • Equip armor with Confuseproof and Berserkproof, or ensure someone has First Strike to swap in a summon, to survive surprise attacks from Great Malboro.
  • Capture Behemoth King with an Overdrive attack or capture weapon to safely avoid its deadly dying Meteor counter.
  • Use the save sphere near the entrance to teleport back aboard the Fahrenheit to purchase supplies or travel across Spira.
