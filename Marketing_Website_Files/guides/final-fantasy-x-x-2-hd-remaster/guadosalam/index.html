Guadosalam

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The home of the Guado

Guadosalam is the otherworldly city of the Guado where the party stops en route across Spira and meets Maester Seymour in his mansion. After a pivotal storyline sequence involving Seymour's proposal and an introduction to the Farplane, the party exits toward the Thunder Plains. Players should explore the area, visit the Farplane for extra character scenes, and collect chests before moving on.

Items

  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XIV: Behind the pillar and wall in the center passage of the main level, near the inn entrance
  • Hi-Potion: Inside the Guado house on the west side of the main level, in a chest
  • Mega-Potion: Inside Seymour's Mansion, on the upper landing to the right
  • Elixir: Inside Seymour's Mansion, on the upper floor behind a pillar
  • 3000 Gil: In the Farplane path area, tucked behind the ramp
  • Moon Crest: Inside the Farplane itself, on the left side of the screen in a chest (accessible after the main storyline visit)

Secrets

  • Re-entering the Farplane later reveals Lulu and Wakka having optional conversations with spirits, and gives access to the chest containing the Moon Crest.
  • Inspecting the glowing sphere in Seymour's living room upstairs unlocks a hidden movie scene regarding the history of Zanarkand.

Shops and people

  • O'aka XXIII: Sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Antidotes, Softs, and equipment suited for the upcoming Thunder Plains (including Lightning Ward and Lightning Strike gear)
  • Guado Item Vendor: Sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Eye Drops, Antidotes, Softs, and basic elemental battle items

Tips

  • Buy Lightning Ward or Lightning Proof armor from O'aka or customize your gear now, as the very next area is the Thunder Plains.
  • You must talk to every member of the party outside Seymour's Mansion and inside the Farplane to trigger the next story event.
