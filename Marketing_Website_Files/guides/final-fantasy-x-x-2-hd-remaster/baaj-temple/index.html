Baaj Temple

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · Tidus's first location after the attack

Tidus awakens alone in the flooded ruins of this ancient structure after being displaced from Zanarkand by Sin. To escape the cold, he must explore the submerged corridors, gather materials to light a campfire, and survive attacks from local fiends. After an encounter with a massive aquatic fiend and a rescue by mysterious cloaked figures, Tidus leaves the temple area on an airship.

Items

  • Potion: Chest directly in the water near where Tidus first swims into the temple exterior
  • Hi-Potion: Chest located along the southern edge of the flooded circular plaza area outside the entrance
  • X-Potion: Chest hidden behind the rubble in the northern passage of the flooded exterior plaza
  • Ether: Chest inside the central antechamber room, tucked in the corner to the right of the stairs
  • Potion: Chest found inside the small room on the right where Tidus locates the Flintstones

Enemies

Tips

  • During the boss fight against Klikk, use Rikku's Steal command continuously to stock up on Grenades, which deal heavy fixed damage when thrown.
  • Interact with the unlit campfire in the center of the interior room to trigger the objectives for finding both the Withered Bouquet and the Flintstones.
  • Do not worry about the locked chamber doors guarded by unlit statues; this temple is a location you will return to much later in the game via the airship.
