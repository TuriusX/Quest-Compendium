FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The underwater prison beneath Bevelle

Yuna awakens separated from her guardians inside a maze-like subterranean dungeon beneath Bevelle and must navigate the passages to find her companions. Meanwhile, Tidus, Wakka, and Rikku are plunged into an underwater section where they must swim through submerged channels and defeat an undead dragon. Reunited at the surface exit, the party faces a boss duel before escaping to the Highbridge.

Items

  • Mega-Potion MISSABLE: Chest in the northern section of the maze corridors
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Chest located in a southern dead-end of the labyrinth
  • White Magic Sphere MISSABLE: Inside a chest reached by stepping on teleporter pads in the maze
  • Lucid Ring MISSABLE: Chest found inside the room where Kimahri is encountered
  • Avenger MISSABLE: Chest found in the underwater passage with Tidus, Wakka, and Rikku
  • Rematch MISSABLE: Chest located near a save sphere in the underwater channel

Secrets

  • Stepping on the arrow pads embedded in the floor will activate hidden teleportation pathways that warp Yuna to locked side rooms containing treasure chests.

Tips

  • Because Evrae Altana has the Undead status, using healing items or recovery spells directly against it will inflict massive damage.
  • For Yuna's aeon duel against Isaaru, charging aeon Overdrive gauges prior to the battle allows you to unleash high-damage attacks immediately when each opposing aeon is summoned.
  • Equip armor with Zombie Ward or Zombieproof during the underwater sequence to protect against status effects caused by Evrae Altana.
