Remiem Temple

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The optional temple in the Calm Lands

Remiem Temple is an optional location found across a fallen bridge on the east side of the Calm Lands, reachable only while riding a Chocobo. Yuna can challenge Belgemine to an escalating series of aeon duels, race a Chocobo on the outside track for valuable prizes, and unlock the hidden aeon Magus Sisters behind a sealed door. After defeating all of Belgemine's aeons and sending her to the Farplane, you will obtain the Moon Sigil required for Yuna's Celestial Weapon.

Items

  • Cloudy Mirror: awarded to the player upon winning the Remiem Temple Chocobo Race for the first time
  • Blossom Crown: reward from Belgemine for defeating her aeon Spatha (Bahamut)
  • Moon Sigil: awarded after defeating all of Belgemine's aeons (ending with the Magus Sisters) and performing the Sending
  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XXIV: lying on the ground along the outer Chocobo racing path, near the northwest edge

Secrets

  • The Magus Sisters aeon can be obtained by unlocking the Chamber of the Fayth using both the Flower Sceptre and the Blossom Crown once all other aeons have been acquired.
  • Winning the Chocobo Race while opening specific numbers of treasure chests without hitting poles rewards rare synthesis items, including Wings to Discovery, Megalixirs, and Underdog's Secrets.

Shops and people

  • Belgemine: offers aeon duels for valuable item rewards and provides the Sending event after defeating her entire roster

Tips

  • Inspect the yellow feather on the ground near the broken bridge in the southeast Calm Lands while mounted on a Chocobo to jump across to the entrance path.
  • For the Chocobo race 5-chest challenge, memorize the shortest path to the central goal without brushing against any rainbow poles, as hitting a pole forfeits the chest rewards.
