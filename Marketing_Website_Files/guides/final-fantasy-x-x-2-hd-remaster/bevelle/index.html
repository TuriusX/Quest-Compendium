Bevelle

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The capital city and temple

The party infiltrates the high-tech holy capital to crash the wedding of Yuna and Seymour Natus. After fighting along the highbridge, you navigate the moving glyph platforms of the Bevelle Cloister of Trials to obtain the Aeon Bahamut. Following the trials, the party is captured, put on trial by Maester Mika, and separated into the Via Purifico dungeon beneath the city.

Items

  • Knight Lance MISSABLE: inside a chest along the pathway during the Bevelle Cloister of Trials
  • HP Sphere MISSABLE: inside the destruction sphere chest blocking the exit of the Bevelle Cloister of Trials
  • Lucid Ring: inside a chest on the Via Purifico underwater path with Tidus, Wakka, and Rikku
  • Avenger: inside a chest in the Via Purifico teleporter puzzle area with Yuna

Secrets

  • Opening the Destruction Sphere chest containing the HP Sphere in the Bevelle Cloister of Trials is mandatory to unblock the exit, making the temple's hidden treasure impossible to miss.

Tips

  • Equip armor with Zombie Ward or Zombie Proof for the Seymour Natus boss fight, as he frequently combos the Zombie status with Full-Life for an instant KO.
  • You can never return to the Bevelle Cloister of Trials once you leave, so ensure you have looted the Knight Lance chest before stepping onto the final platform.
  • Yuna's summons can easily defeat Isaaru's aeons in the Via Purifico duel by filling their Overdrive gauges before entering the fight.
