Airship

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The party's base of operations

The Airship (Fahrenheit) serves as the player's primary mobile base of operations during the latter portions of Final Fantasy X. Players can speak to Cid on the bridge to navigate to previously visited locations or unlock new destinations across Spira. It also contains rest cabins, a merchant, and access to side quests including the Celestial Weapons and optional bosses.

Items

  • Al Bhed Primer vol. XXIV: Inside the Airship's living quarters on the floor

Secrets

  • Inputting coordinates using Cid's navigation system reveals secret locations such as Baaj Temple, the Sanubia Sands ruins, and Besaid Falls.
  • Entering specific Al Bhed passwords into the navigation menu unlocks hidden areas, including "GODHAND" to find Rikku's Celestial Weapon, "VICTORIOUS" to find Rikku's Victorious armor, and "MURASAME" to obtain Auron's Murasame weapon.

Shops and people

  • Rin: Sells restorative items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and basic equipment

Tips

  • Speak to Rin in the Airship corridor after gaining free flight to receive Al Bhed Primer vol. XXV if it was missed in earlier Al Bhed Home areas.
  • Use the save sphere located on the bridge to freely heal your party and immediately access Blitzball tournaments.
