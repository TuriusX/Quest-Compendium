Djose Highroad

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The path leading to Djose Temple

The Djose Highroad is a rocky, coastal path connecting the Mushroom Rock Road to the Djose Temple. Along this stretch, Yuna and her guardians meet wandering Crusaders and summoners who share items, information, and encouragement following the disastrous battle against Sin. The main objective is simply to follow the linear highway northward toward the bridge leading to the temple grounds.

Items

  • Phoenix Down x2: In a treasure chest on the left side of the road, early along the path
  • Bright Guard: In a treasure chest hidden behind a rock formation on the right side of the path
  • Ether: In a treasure chest nestled on the left side near the middle of the highroad
  • Mega Phoenix: In a treasure chest located along the stone path approaching the temple bridge

Secrets

  • Speak to the traveling NPC Crusaders and monks walking down the road; several of them will give you free items including a Soft, a Hi-Potion, and Gil.

Tips

  • Equip armor with Stone Ward or Stoneproof, or keep plenty of Softs ready to cure petrification from the Basilisk's gaze.
  • Kimahri can use Lancet on the Basilisk fiends along this road to learn the Stone Breath Ronso Rage overdrive.
  • Rikku's use of Steal on Basilisks rewards you with Petrify Grenades, which instantly petrify and shatter many non-boss fiends when used in battle.
