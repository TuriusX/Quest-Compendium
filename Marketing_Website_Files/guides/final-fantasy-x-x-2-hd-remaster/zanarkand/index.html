Zanarkand

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The opening sequence and blitzball stadium

Sin attacks Dream Zanarkand in the middle of a blitzball match, causing massive destruction across the city. Tidus meets Auron outside the stadium and fights through waves of Sinscales to reach the source of the chaos. After battling Sinspawn Emmes, the duo is pulled into Sin, marking the end of the game's prologue.

Items

  • Potion x2 MISSABLE: Given by the crowd of fans outside Tidus's houseboat if spoken to before the match
  • Potion MISSABLE: Acquired by interacting with the woman fan near the save sphere outside the stadium

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
SinscaleFireDefeated automatically or easily dispatched with single physical attacks from Tidus and Auron.
Sinspawn AmmesUses Demi to reduce current HP of both characters by 25%; defeated quickly by using Auron's Bushido Overdrive Dragon Fang followed by standard attacks.
Sinscale (Boss Form)Group of Sinscales encountered after Ammes; targeting the glowing Sinscale on the tanker causes it to explode, instantly ending the battle.

Tips

  • You cannot open the main menu or access inventory until after the Zanarkand prologue concludes and Tidus washes up at the Baaj Temple ruins.
  • The enemies during the road sequence infinitely respawn, but you gain no AP from them, so run forward instead of lingering to grind.
  • Triggering Auron's "Dragon Fang" Overdrive requires inputting Down, Left, Up, Right, L1, R1, Circle, Cross within the time limit for maximum damage.
