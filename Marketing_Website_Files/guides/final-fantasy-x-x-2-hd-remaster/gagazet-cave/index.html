Gagazet Cave

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The underwater cavern inside the mountain

After defeating Seymour Flux on Mount Gagazet, the party enters this cavernous submerged tunnel network. To proceed through the cave, players must complete two trials: Wakka's First Trial of Gagazet (hitting a target with a blitzball) and Rikku's Second Trial of Gagazet (swimming to color-coded holes). The exit leads up to the summit path overlooking the ruins of Zanarkand.

Items

  • Fortune Sphere: In a treasure chest located in a dead-end submerged path
  • Return Sphere: Found in a treasure chest on the submerged paths
  • Recovery Ring: In a chest on dry land along the upper branches of the cavern
  • Defending Bracer: Inside a treasure chest on dry ground within the cavern
  • Star Pendant: Found in a treasure chest reached after completing Wakka's blitzball trial
  • Pep Talk: Located in a treasure chest revealed by completing the trials

Secrets

  • First Trial of Gagazet: Interacting with the center underwater pedestal starts a mini-game where Wakka throws a blitzball at a spinning target; timing it to hit the glowing center activates a mechanism and uncovers a chest.
  • Second Trial of Gagazet: An underwater puzzle requiring Tidus, Wakka, and Rikku to swim into three separate glowing holes matched to their respective character sphere colors (green, yellow, and blue) to reveal an exit staircase.

Tips

  • Only Tidus, Wakka, and Rikku are available in party formations during the submerged swimming sections of the cave.
  • Equip armor with status protection like Confuse Ward or Confuseproof to mitigate threats from Mandragora fiends.
  • Capture Behemoth with a capture weapon carefully, as its dying counter-attack deals heavy damage to your party.
