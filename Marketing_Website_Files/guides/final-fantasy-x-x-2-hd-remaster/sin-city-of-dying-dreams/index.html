Sin: City of Dying Dreams

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The second interior area of Sin

This is the second inner area of Inside Sin, characterized by crumbling ruins and strange organic matter. You must traverse these submerged streets, activate glyphs to uncover hidden alcoves, and climb toward the Core. It acts as the final gauntlet of random encounters before entering the Tower of the Dead and facing the final storyline bosses.

Items

  • Phantom Ring: Inside a treasure chest submerged near the start of the area
  • Special Sphere: Inside a chest found on a broken platform on the right side of the main path
  • Wizard Knight: Found in an alcove unlocked by touching a hidden glyph on the wall
  • Lv. 4 Key Sphere: Hidden in a ruined structure accessible by climbing a fallen pillar
  • Defending Bracer: Chest inside the upper platform reached after unlocking the floor sigil
  • Megalixir: Located inside a chest near the stairs leading to the next screen

Secrets

  • Touching an engraved glyph hidden on an ancient wall causes a hidden pathway to rise, allowing access to high platforms containing rare equipment.
  • Defeating ten enemies in the sunken courtyard area causes an elevated stone walkway to lower, granting access to a secluded treasure chest.

Tips

  • Equip armor with Confuseproof and Berserkproof, or keep First Strike on a weapon to immediately swap in a character to deal with Ambush attacks from Great Malboro.
  • Teach Yuna the spell Holy or equip weapons with the Piercing auto-ability to deal efficiently with armored fiends like Barbatos and Adamantoise.
  • You can use Provoke on a Defender or Demonolith to shut down their dangerous status-inflicting counterattacks.
