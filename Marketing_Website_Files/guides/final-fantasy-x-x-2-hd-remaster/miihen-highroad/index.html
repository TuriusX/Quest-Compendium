Mi'ihen Highroad

FINAL FANTASY X/X-2 HD Remaster guide · The long road leading north from Luca

The party travels along this long road north from Luca toward the Moonflow while learning about Lord Mi'ihen, the Crusaders, and chocobos. Along the way, you stop at an agency to rest, encounter a monster threatening the chocobos, and unlock chocobo riding for faster exploration. You can choose to proceed straight up the main path or explore the lower Oldroad loop to find additional treasures and encounters.

Items

  • Ice Brand: In a chest hidden behind a ruined wall on the Oldroad North screen
  • Heat Lance: Inside a chest found on the Oldroad South screen near the dead end
  • Fortune Sphere: Rewarded by an NPC traveler when talked to along the central highroad path
  • Scout: Inside a treasure chest on the southern section of the road before reaching the travel agency
  • Thunder Blade: In a chest located in the Newroad area past the agency

Secrets

  • Yellow feathers on the ground allow you to jump with a rented chocobo to reach secluded ledges containing chests, such as the Heat Lance and Saturn Crest.
  • Defeating the Chocobo Eater by pushing it off the cliff earns extra AP, saves the chocobo breeder, and grants free chocobo rentals for the area.

Shops and people

  • Rin's Travel Agency: Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, Antidotes, Eye Drops, Softs, and basic elemental equipment
  • Traveling Merchant Oaka XXIII: Restorative items, equipment, and an opportunity to lend him gil to lower his future shop prices

Tips

  • Rent a chocobo after completing the travel agency segment to avoid all random encounters and collect otherwise unreachable chests.
  • Use Auron's Armor Break or Power Break on the Chocobo Eater to significantly lower its offensive threat and defensive stats.
