Chapter 2: A Bloody Reunion

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Ichiban is released from prison in 2019 and confronts Masumi Arakawa ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Following his release from an 18-year prison sentence, Ichiban Kasuga returns to a drastically altered Kamurocho, navigating through the sewers and infiltrating Heian Tower to confront his former patriarch.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Prison Release and the Cemetery

    After your 18-year sentence ends in early 2019, cutscenes introduce Koichi Adachi and an accidental afro perm at a barber, sending you toward the cemetery outskirts. There, you spot Masumi Arakawa from afar and must battle two level 4 bodyguards: eliminate the low-health Maruyama immediately with a single strike, then finish Kaneda using Tenacious Fist while guarding through his Rage taunts.

    Key fight: Arakawa Family Bodyguards (Cemetery)

    Tip: Target Maruyama first so you only have to deal with Kaneda's baton attacks and taunts.

  2. 2

    Tracking Leads Across Kamurocho

    Walk across the city to find the former Arakawa Family Office shuttered, then head to the nearby cigarette stand to speak with Michiyo. Defeating the bat-wielding enforcer Kishi triggers directions to Children's Park, where you step in alone to save Ushio from two Omi Alliance thugs and learn that the Omi now run the town.

    Key fight: Arakawa Enforcer (Cigarette Stall) Key fight: Omi Alliance Thugs (Children’s Park)

    Tip: Grab a nearby street cone around the corner during Kishi's fight to inflict high physical burst damage.

  3. 3

    Rescuing Nick and Regrouping

    Push toward the ruins of Shangri-La while dodging Omi patrols, stepping in to rescue Nick Ogata from two street thugs in an alley. Nick gives you a smartphone as thanks, allowing you to rendezvous with Adachi at Theater Square before he guides you to a subterranean manhole by Theater Alley.

    Key fight: Street Harassers (Nick Ogata Rescue)

    Tip: Knock out the level 2 thug with regular strikes before breaking the level 5 thug's defense with Tenacious Fist.

  4. 4

    Infiltrating the Kamurocho Sewers

    Descend into the Kamurocho Underground B1F, where you and Adachi fight three successive groups of Newbie Yakuza, Bomb Bugs, and Unboxers. Scour the tunnels for a Mysterious Mushroom along the right wall past the first battle, take the right fork for a Hardball, defeat an optional trio guarding a dead-end to secure a Mysterious Leaf, and check a late corner for the Dirty Tenugui accessory before hitting the phone save point and free HP/MP recovery station.

    Key fight: Kamurocho Underground Encounters

    Tip: Hit grounded foes with basic attacks for automatic critical hits to save MP, and use Adachi's Beatdown or Slick Tongue against trios.

    Watch out: Do not climb the exit ladder if prompted with "Head up" until you grab all four sewer pickups, as entering the tower permanently locks you out of this area.

  5. 5

    Breaching Heian Tower and the Rooftops

    Scale the ladder into Heian Tower's security monitoring office to recover a Staminan X and Half-Eaten Bento, then smash through three heavily guarding security guards with Adachi's blunt skills and Tenacious Fist. Battle across the rooftops against four Omi Alliance members until a structural collapse separates you from Adachi, leaving you to use a second rooftop recovery station before taking down two level 7 rage-inflicting Omi thugs in the hall outside the banquet room.

    Key fight: Heian Tower Infiltration Waves

    Tip: Break through the initial security guards' high defenses with Adachi's blunt abilities and Tenacious Fist.

    Watch out: Loot the monitoring office right away, as these recovery items are permanently missable once you finish the chapter.

  6. 6

    Duel with Jo Sawashiro

    Enter the main banquet hall to confront the Arakawa leadership and engage in a solo duel against level 8 Jo Sawashiro. He unleashes slashing cane-sword attacks that cause bleed, and whenever he adopts his defensive stance, you must avoid basic physical hits to dodge catastrophic counter-damage, relying instead on thrown items like hardballs or regular guards while keeping your HP above 50%.

    Tip: Thrown items like the Hardball bypass Sawashiro's counter-stance safely while exploiting vulnerabilities.

    Watch out: Attacking Sawashiro with standard strikes during his defensive posture triggers a devastating counterattack that can instantly defeat you.

Key fights
Arakawa Family Bodyguards (Cemetery)

Enemies: Kaneda (Lv. 4) and Maruyama (Lv. 4)

Threats: Kaneda uses a baton that inflicts Rage (forcing Ichiban to use regular basic attacks)

Tactics: Burst Maruyama first (he possesses very low health and falls in 1–2 hits). Use Tenacious Fist on Kaneda while timing Perfect Guards.

Rewards: Small XP, Yen, early combat tutorial completion

Arakawa Enforcer (Cigarette Stall)

Enemies: Kishi (Lv. 5)

Threats: High base melee damage using a baseball bat

Tactics: Target him immediately with a nearby street weapon (e.g., kicking/grabbing the nearby traffic cone with a basic melee hit) or use Tenacious Fist.

Rewards: Basic XP, Yen

Omi Alliance Thugs (Children’s Park)

Enemies: Two Omi Family Grunts (Lv. 4–5)

Tactics: Focus them down sequentially; neither has hyper armor. Spam Tenacious Fist.

Rewards: Cutscene trigger saving Ushio's camera

Street Harassers (Nick Ogata Rescue)

Enemies: Level 2 Thug & Level 5 Thug

Tactics: Defeat the Level 2 thug with standard attacks, then focus Tenacious Fist on the Level 5 enemy.

Rewards: Nick Ogata’s smartphone

Kamurocho Underground Encounters

Enemies: Newbie Yakuza (Lv. 4–5), Bomb Bugs (Lv. 4), and Unboxers (Lv. 5) encountered in pods of 2–3

Threats: Bomb Bugs possess explosive throwing attacks; Unboxers execute multi-hit punch combos

Tactics: Adachi joins your lineup here. Use Kasuga’s strikes while utilizing Adachi’s high raw physical power to clear mobs without overspending MP.

Rewards: XP, loose change, crafting materials (e.g., insect/bug items, dirty iron)

Heian Tower Infiltration Waves

Enemies: 3x Heian Tower Security Guards, followed by 2x Omi Members, then 4x Omi Members on the roof, followed by two Level 7 Omi enforcers

Threats: The roof fight features heavy crowd numbers where Adachi is eventually split off from the party

Tactics: Use Adachi’s Slick Tongue to draw aggression or debuff guards before he departs. After losing Adachi, use the recovery node before opening the double doors to face two final Level 7 Omi enforcers.

Rewards: Story progression, access to the Chapter Boss
