Chapter 5: The Liumang Man

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Investigating Soapland Otohime Land and crossing paths with Saeko and the Yokohama Liumang
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga and his party investigate Nonomiya's murder across Restaurant Row and Romance Street, eventually uncovering Mabuchi's counterfeit printing operation at the Yokohama Trading Company.

Region
Isezaki Ijincho, Yokohama (Red Light District, Restaurant Row / Chinatown fringes, Romance Street, Jinnai Station, and Yokohama Trading Company warehouse area past Hamakita Park)
Levels
Levels 14–17
Getting there
Starts directly after the Chapter 4 finale at the funeral home / Otohime Land. Fast travel is done via the Yokohama Taxi network and Jinnai Station train line to access outer industrial sectors.
Connects to
Red Light District (Otohime Land), Restaurant Row / Chinatown fringes, Romance Street, Jinnai Station, Yokohama Trading Company warehouse area past Hamakita Park
Related quests
Substory 12 (How to be a Part-time Hero) · Substory 13 (Nowhere to Go But Up)
Services and people
Saeko Mukoda · Hiro · Sumire Sawa · Kanbe & Ririka (Hello Work) · Eri Kamataki · Yokohama Taxi network · Jinnai Station train line

The short version

What matters

  • •Unlocking the Romance Workshop lets you forge initial equipment like the Self-Proclaimed Hero's Bat, Rugged Cane, and Carbon Police Baton into top-tier EX weapons.
  • •Activating the Part-Time Hero feature back-calculates earlier achievements to immediately boost Kasuga's Personality traits.
  • •Speaking to Ririka at Hello Work unlocks the job terminal, allowing you to reassign party roles to customize elemental coverage.

Common mistakes

  • •Rushing into the final office cutscene at the Yokohama Trading Company before picking up all safes and smashing the wooden crates locks you out of Glass Panes, Nails, and Wires.
  • •Spreading attacks evenly across the six Yokohama Liumang ambushers in Restaurant Row allows them to easily overwhelm Saeko and Nanba; eliminate them one by one or sweep them with Pyro Belch.

What you can skip

  • •You can steer entirely clear of the Kung Fu Disciples and Pressured Cookers by sprinting north and west along Isezaki Road during your escape from Restaurant Row.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Attend the Funeral and Meet Saeko

    Following the Chapter 4 finale, head to the funeral hall near Otohime Land in the Red Light District alongside Nanba and Adachi to pay respects to Nonomiya. Saeko Mukoda approaches you here, joining your active roster permanently in the Barmaid role.

  2. 2

    Unlock Part-Time Hero near Restaurant Row

    Head north toward Restaurant Row, where you will be stopped outside the Seagull Cinema near the southern highway entrance to start Substory 12 (How to be a Part-time Hero). Defeat the Menacing Men using standard strikes or Hero batting attacks, then brawl with the Mysterious Masked Man Guy. Knock this masked opponent down to land guaranteed critical hits, or bypass his physical resistance with Nanba's fire and pigeon magic to unlock Hiro's Part-Time Hero app.

    Key fight: Menacing Men Key fight: Mysterious Masked Man Guy (Part-time Hero Rep)

    Tip: Activating Part-Time Hero instantly evaluates all milestones completed since Chapter 1, granting an immediate bulk surge across Kasuga's Personality traits.

  3. 3

    Interrogate Restaurant Row and Ambush

    Proceed into the alleyways of Restaurant Row and speak with the three marked NPCs to extract leads. Once you finish questioning them, six Yokohama Liumang Thugs ambush your party. Target them down individually with blunt strikes or sweep through grouped hostiles using Nanba's Pyro Belch before they can overpower Saeko or Nanba.

    Key fight: Restaurant Row Liumang Ambush
  4. 4

    Visit Romance Workshop While Saeko Infiltrates

    Track Zheng to Lin Lin Hostess Bar, where Saeko disguises herself as an employee and temporarily departs your active party to secure access. Kill time by heading to Romance Street to start Substory 13 (Nowhere to Go But Up), introducing you to Sumire Sawa and the Romance Workshop. You can invest funds here to expand craftable gear blueprints (Rank 2 for 200,000 Yen, Rank 3 for 2,000,000 Yen, Rank 4 for 20,000,000 Yen).

    Tip: Unlocking the workshop allows you to reforge starter gear—such as Kasuga's Self-Proclaimed Hero’s Bat, Nanba's Rugged Cane, and Adachi's Carbon Police Baton—along upgrade paths that reach top-tier EX weapons.

  5. 5

    Raid Lin Lin Hostess Bar and Evade Patrols

    Wait for Saeko's call, then enter Lin Lin Hostess Bar to confront Zheng and his escorts, learning of the Yokohama Trading Company. Have Adachi absorb aggro while Saeko weakens Zheng's offensive stats, allowing Nanba to exploit his fire and bludgeon weaknesses. After winning, evade the Kung Fu Disciples and Pressured Cookers prowling the streets by running north and west along Isezaki Road back toward Otohime Land.

    Key fight: Lin Lin Hostess Bar - Zheng Rematch Key fight: Escape from Restaurant Row
  6. 6

    Unlock Job Changes and Business Management

    In the morning, visit Hello Work to consult Kanbe for an employment cover story, then speak with Ririka to unlock the job-switch system. You can freely reassign your party members between roles like Hero, Host, Enforcer, Barmaid, and Idol to adjust stats and elemental coverage. Afterward, head outside near Otohime Land to meet Eri Kamataki, which unlocks the Ichiban Confections Business Management minigame.

  7. 7

    Warehouse Infiltration and Counterfeit Ring

    Travel to Jinnai Station to take the train past Hamakita Park to the Yokohama Trading Company warehouse for your first shift, then regroup at Otohime Land before returning at night. Infiltrate the facility's office level and clear out two consecutive waves of Level 17 guards and Liumang mercenaries using fire attacks such as Nanba's Pyro Belch. Besting both waves triggers the confrontation with Akira Mabuchi and reveals his counterfeit printing operation, concluding the chapter.

    Key fight: Yokohama Trading Company Warehouse Finale

    Watch out: Do not trigger the office cutscene discovering Mabuchi's counterfeit operation until you have smashed every crate and grabbed all safes in the warehouse, or their materials will be permanently lost.

Romance Workshop Investment

Speaking to Sumire Sawa at Romance Workshop

  • Pay 200,000 Yen→ Upgrades the workshop to Rank 2, expanding available crafting blueprints for weapons and armor
  • Pay 2,000,000 Yen→ Upgrades the workshop to Rank 3, further widening crafting recipes for weapons and armor
  • Pay 20,000,000 Yen→ Upgrades the workshop to Rank 4, granting maximum access to equipment crafting blueprints

Most players pick: Pay 200,000 Yen

Key fights
Menacing Men

Enemies: 3x Street Thugs (Sugawara, Asada, Masuda; Lv. 15)

Weaknesses and resistances: Physical damage (Blunt / Blade)

Tactics: Straightforward brawl; use Kasuga’s standard attacks or Hero batting skills to clear quickly.

Rewards: Exp, Yen, introductory progression into Substory 12

Mysterious Masked Man Guy (Part-time Hero Rep)

Enemies: Lv. 15 Masked Man

Threats: High HP, guards frequently, resists standard physical attacks.

Tactics: Avoid raw basic attacks; use Nanba’s pigeon/fire magic skills or knockdowns to score guaranteed critical strikes while he is prone on the floor.

Rewards: Unlocks Part-Time Hero Smartphone App and Quests

Restaurant Row Liumang Ambush

Enemies: 6x Yokohama Liumang Thugs (Lv. 14)

Threats: High enemy count; can swarm low-defense members like Saeko or Nanba.

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire / Blunt

Tactics: Focus fire to defeat them one by one rather than spreading damage evenly. Nanba’s Pyro Belch is ideal for sweeping adjacent targets.

Rewards: Yen, generic crafting materials

Lin Lin Hostess Bar - Zheng Rematch

Enemies: Zheng and his escorts

Threats: Heavy melee rushdown and guard break moves from Zheng.

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire and Bludgeon attacks

Tactics: Use Adachi to draw aggro with defensive skills; use Saeko’s offensive debuffs to reduce attack power before burning him down with Nanba.

Rewards: Exp, accessory/consumable drops

Escape from Restaurant Row

Enemies: Kung Fu Disciples, Pressured Cookers

Tactics: Can be fought or steered around by running north/west along Isezaki Road. Use area-of-effect skills if drawn into alley skirmishes.

Yokohama Trading Company Warehouse Finale

Enemies: Two consecutive back-to-back waves of Yokohama Trading Company guards / Liumang mercenaries (Lv. 17)

Threats: Guarded room, heavy mob density, higher defense than previous street encounters.

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Fire

Tactics: Enter with full MP. Have Nanba use Pyro Belch or elemental weapon skills crafted from Romance Workshop. Maintain Saeko's healing/support to keep Kasuga topped off.

Rewards: Large EXP payout, unlocked Chapter 5 Cleared Trophy/Achievement (The Liumang Man / Soap on a Rope), safe prompt to save before Chapter 6
