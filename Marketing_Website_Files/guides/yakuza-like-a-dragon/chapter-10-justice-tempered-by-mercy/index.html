Chapter 10: Justice Tempered by Mercy

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Tracking down Mabuchi and defending the Geomijul territory alongside Joon-gi Han
← All areas in this guide

Following the destruction of the Geomijul, Kasuga and company team up with Joon-gi Han to infiltrate Keinishiki in Restaurant Row, rescue Tianyou Zhao, and defeat Mabuchi and Ishioda.

Region
Isezaki Ijincho
Levels
27 – 32
Getting there
Starts at the Homeless Camp along the riverbank, then cross town toward Restaurant Row / north of Seagull Cinema to enter the alleys leading to Keinishiki.
Connects to
Homeless Camp, Commercial District, Restaurant Row, Keinishiki / Qing Jin
Related quests
Chapter 10: Justice Tempered by Mercy
Services and people
Recovery points at the Homeless Camp, Keinishiki kitchen, and Keinishiki upper floor · Item Storage box

The short version

What matters

  • •Collecting all gear safes in Keinishiki, including the Tactical Helmet, Iron Gloves, Wonder Gown, Tactical Boots, Magical Sandals, and Tactical Vest.
  • •Utilizing the recovery points located at the Homeless Camp, Keinishiki kitchen, and the upper floor waiting area before bosses.
  • •Exploiting Mabuchi's Electricity and Blunt vulnerabilities and Ishioda's Gun, Ice, and Fire weaknesses.

Common mistakes

  • •Leaving behind ground-floor, kitchen, or meat locker loot, since Keinishiki cannot be re-entered after finishing the infiltration until post-game Premium Adventure.
  • •Focusing single-target attacks on Mabuchi or Ishioda first instead of wiping out their waves of escort adds with wide AoE attacks.
  • •Rushing past the storage box and recovery point on the upper floor before verifying Kasuga's equipment and health for the back-to-back boss fights.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Interrogate Ogasawara and defend the Homeless Camp

    Speak with Ogasawara twice at the riverside camp to question him about Bleach Japan and Ryo Aoki, which triggers an ambush by Omi Alliance and Bleach Japan enforcers. Joon-gi Han joins your party during the scene to help you eliminate the attackers. Use the camp's recovery point immediately after the battle before heading across town toward Restaurant Row.

    Key fight: Bleach Japan / Omi Alliance Grunts

    Tip: Use area-of-effect skills like Hero bat swings to eliminate the low-level grunts quickly, leaving Satake for last.

  2. 2

    Breach the Restaurant Row Alleys

    Navigate north of Seagull Cinema toward Restaurant Row and approach the map marker to trigger a scene with the Yokohama Liumang. You will immediately face two successive enemy waves, beginning with six Liumang members and concluding with Zheng alongside his two bodyguards. Fight your way through to Keinishiki's exterior entrance once Zheng is brought down.

    Key fight: Restaurant Row Gatekeepers & Zheng

    Tip: Zheng lacks knockdown resistance, so knock him down with Joon-gi Han or Adachi to land guaranteed critical strikes while he is grounded.

  3. 3

    Secure the Keinishiki Lobby and Stairwell

    Defeat the outdoor greeting waves, then enter Keinishiki and wipe out the front-desk host and guards on the ground floor. Before chasing the fleeing host upstairs, search the floor and grab the Gold Butterfly by the front door, Almond Jelly in the southwest, and the nearby Restorative Medicine, Roasted Chestnuts, and Toughness ZZ. Head up the stairs to crack the safe on the mid-stairwell for a Tactical Helmet, then snatch the Burn Ointment and Astringent Gauze from the upper landing.

    Key fight: Keinishiki Interior Trash Mobs & Chefs

    Watch out: All items inside Keinishiki become permanently inaccessible for free pickup once the infiltration is cleared until post-game Premium Adventure.

  4. 4

    Clear the Kitchen and Meat Locker

    Enter the commercial kitchen and defeat the squad of chefs and kitchen workers. Scour the kitchen prep counters to gather Chinese Sticky Rice, Hot Pepper, Liquid Nitrogen Spray, Potato, Onion, Garlic, Shark Fin Bun, and Roasted Chestnuts, then utilize the kitchen's recovery point. Step into the adjoining cold storage meat locker to open a safe containing Iron Gloves, then grab Sturdy Plastic under the hanging carcass, Full-grain Leather on the west wall, and Mysterious Stone on the east wall.

    Key fight: Keinishiki Interior Trash Mobs & Chefs

    Tip: Target the kitchen chefs with Ice skills from Host or Hostess jobs to exploit their core weakness.

  5. 5

    Pass the Tiger Enclosure and Prepare for Mabuchi

    Descend the stairs near the trash to grab Mango Pudding, then fight through the balconies and stairwells while collecting Chinese Detoxers, a Golden Mantis, a Brainwash Gas Ball, and a Wonder Gown from a safe. Pick up the Raw Ruby Ore beside the live tiger enclosure gate, then clear the remaining guards as you ascend to the upper floor. On the open pre-boss landing, collect Tauriner++, Chinese Sticky Rice, a Handmade Meat Bun, and two safes holding Tactical Boots and Magical Sandals; then grab Staminan XX, a Premium First Aid Kit, and a Tactical Vest from the final safe beside the recovery point and item storage box before examining the double doors.

    Tip: Rest at the recovery point and deposit spare gear into the item box before interacting with the double doors.

    Watch out: Choosing to head through the double doors locks you into back-to-back boss encounters with no way to turn back.

  6. 6

    Defeat Akira Mabuchi and Reiji Ishioda

    Pass through the doors to view the cutscene introducing Yosuke Tendo, which immediately transitions into battle against Akira Mabuchi and four Liumang elites. After bringing Mabuchi down, Reiji Ishioda ambushes your group alongside seven Omi Alliance reinforcements. Withstand the assault until Tianyou Zhao arrives to rescue the party, concluding the chapter.

    Key fight: Boss: Akira Mabuchi Key fight: Boss: Reiji Ishioda

    Tip: Clear Mabuchi's adds with AoE skills and shock him with Joon-gi Han or Kasuga's Thundercrack Bat; on Ishioda's fight, open with Nanba's Essence of Pyro Prison to instantly decimate the seven Omi thugs.

    Watch out: Keep Kasuga guarded whenever Ishioda prepares his charge attacks to prevent fatal Stun and Fear combos.

Key fights
Bleach Japan / Omi Alliance Grunts

Enemies: Satake (Lv. 28), Furuya (Lv. 27), Yamane (Lv. 27), Tashiro (Lv. 27), Nozawa (Lv. 27)

Threats: Focused melee damage on single party members

Tactics: Area-of-effect skills (Hero bat swings, Hostess spreads) clear the lower-level grunts quickly; defeat Satake last. Use the homeless camp recovery spot immediately after.

Restaurant Row Gatekeepers & Zheng

Enemies: Wave 1: 6x Yokohama Liumang members (Lv. 27); Wave 2: Zheng (Lv. 28–29) and 2 Liumang bodyguards

Threats: Zheng possesses high HP and heavy swings

Weaknesses and resistances: Lacks knockdown resistance

Tactics: Use knockdown skills (e.g., Joon-gi Han or Adachi) followed by guaranteed critical downed attacks.

Keinishiki Interior Trash Mobs & Chefs

Enemies: Restaurant guards, chefs, and kitchen workers (Lv. 27–29)

Weaknesses and resistances: Chefs are notably weak to Ice skills (Hostess / Host jobs)

Boss: Akira Mabuchi

Enemies: Akira Mabuchi (Lv. 31) + 4 Liumang elite thugs (Lv. 30)

Threats: Mabuchi wields curved twin blades, high agility, and rapid slashes that inflict heavy Bleed

Weaknesses and resistances: Electricity and Blunt damage; susceptible to Poison (e.g., Nancy-Chan summon) and Blind/Accuracy debuffs

Tactics: Clear the 4 adds immediately using wide AoE (Hero’s Fearless Command buff into multihit physical/lightning attacks, or Clerk’s Thumbtack Scatter). Shock Mabuchi using Joon-gi Han (Lightning Bandages / Hitman gun and stun abilities) or Kasuga with the Thundercrack Bat.

Rewards: Large chunk of EXP/Job EXP, high cash payout

Boss: Reiji Ishioda

Enemies: Reiji Ishioda (Lv. 31) + 7 Omi Alliance reinforcements (Lv. 28–30)

Threats: Ishioda opens with a special counter posture and heavy hits that inflict Stun and Fear. High count of surrounding adds.

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Ice, Fire, and Gun attacks

Tactics: Use Nanba's newly unlocked special attack (Essence of Pyro Prison) on turn one to wipe out or cripple the 7 Omi thugs across the field. Debuff Ishioda's attack and defense, then strike his Ice/Fire vulnerabilities while keeping Kasuga guarded against Ishioda's stun charges.

Rewards: Story clear progression, unlocks Chapter 10 trophy, Zhao rescues the party, and new Chinatown/Hamakita substories open up
