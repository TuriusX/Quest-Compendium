Chapter 15: To the Pinnacle

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Storming the Millennium Tower in Kamurocho to face Aoki and conclude Ichiban's journey ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga and his party infiltrate the Millennium Tower to defeat Yosuke Tendo and put an end to Governor Ryo Aoki's ambitions in the climactic final chapter.

Region
Kamurocho (Tokyo), Millennium Tower
Levels
Level 57–60+ (Job Level 28+)
Getting there
From Earth Angel in the Champion District, head southwest to New Serena on Tenkaichi Street to rest, then walk to the front doors of the Millennium Tower on Taihei Boulevard the following morning.
Connects to
Isezaki Ijincho (Yokohama), Sotenbori (Osaka), Kamurocho Underground Dungeon
Related quests
Chapter 15: To the Pinnacle

The short version

What matters

  • •Cast Peerless Resolve on Kasuga during the Tendo fight so he survives fatal one-shot attacks with 1 HP.
  • •Use lightning attacks and Nancy-chan's poison summon to dismantle Yosuke Tendo's massive health bar.
  • •Utilize the mid-level and Floor 54 elevator Recovery Points to top off HP and MP before major encounters.

Common mistakes

  • •Entering the Millennium Tower underleveled; stay above level 57 and job level 28 before passing the point of no return.
  • •Neglecting instant-death protection on Kasuga against Tendo, which will lead to a sudden Game Over.

What you can skip

  • •Spending excess turns killing all of Aoki's summoned guards, since he just summons more with Call a Friend.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Final Preparations in Kamurocho

    Begin by confronting Governor Ryo Aoki during his street campaign speech, then meet Nick Ogata at Earth Angel in the Champion District to finalize your plan. Afterward, head southwest to New Serena on Tenkaichi Street to speak with Date and rest for the night. The following morning, walk to the front entrance of the Millennium Tower on Taihei Boulevard.

    Tip: If you still need levels or gear, choose "Wait" at the entrance to travel back to Ijincho, Sotenbori, or the Kamurocho Underground Dungeon.

    Watch out: Selecting "OK" or "Enter" locks you inside the tower permanently until the credits roll.

  2. 2

    Storming the Millennium Tower Lobby

    Step through the front doors to battle the initial wave of Tokyo Omi Alliance guards holding the entrance. Search the ground floor thoroughly for ground pickups and unlock the safe before proceeding upward. Once cleared, head up toward the higher floors.

    Key fight: Tower Gauntlet (Trash Mobs)

    Tip: Area-of-effect skills like Fulminating Forecast or Essence of Mayhem clear these crowds quickly without wasting excess MP.

    Watch out: Every item here is missable if you clear the story without picking them up.

  3. 3

    Fighting Through the Office Floors

    Push through Floor 13, fighting Tokyo Omi hitmen stationed among office partitions, corridors, and server stations. Check every office corner, corridor, and safe you encounter to gather valuable crafting resources, medicine, and upper stat boosters. Continue advancing toward the stairwells once the floor is clear.

    Key fight: Tower Gauntlet (Trash Mobs)

    Tip: Break areas along the mid-level emergency stairs feature Recovery Points where you can completely restore your party's HP and MP.

  4. 4

    Ascending the Sky Restaurant

    Navigate through the fine-dining area on Floor 54 to reach the elevator foyer leading toward the heliport transition. Defeat the remaining Omi guards blocking your path to the lift. Before stepping onto the elevator, stop at the nearby Recovery Point to restore the party and make final preparations.

    Key fight: Tower Gauntlet (Trash Mobs)

    Tip: Choose to hold back at the elevator prompt if you need to swap jobs or adjust accessories before the summit.

    Watch out: Taking the elevator commits you to three consecutive boss battles.

  5. 5

    Defeating Yosuke Tendo

    Ride the elevator to the Penthouse's Arakawa Family Office to confront Ryudo Family Patriarch Yosuke Tendo. Exploit his weaknesses to piercing knives and electricity, and summon Nancy-chan on your first turn to poison him. Keep Kasuga protected with Peerless Resolve to endure his deadly God's Right Hand punch when he powers up below half health.

    Key fight: Boss: Yosuke Tendo (Ryudo Family Patriarch)

    Tip: Keep a dedicated Idol like Saeko or Eri constantly casting group heals every single turn.

    Watch out: Failing to keep Peerless Resolve or instant-death protection on Kasuga risks an immediate Game Over if Tendo targets him with his lethal blow.

  6. 6

    Clashing with Ryo Aoki

    Pursue Ryo Aoki as he retreats with elite Tokyo Omi Alliance bodyguards. Focus your attacks directly on Aoki using blunt physical strikes and lightning while using wide electrical skills to handle his summoned reinforcements. Once Aoki's health drops to critical levels, watch the cutscene as he flees toward the building's exterior steps.

    Key fight: Boss: Ryo Aoki & Tokyo Omi Reinforcements

    Tip: Target Aoki directly rather than wasting turns on his guards, as he will repeatedly summon replacements with Call a Friend.

  7. 7

    The Final Duel

    Chase Aoki down to the campaign stage outside for a final one-on-one showdown against Masato Arakawa. Stripped down to your basic Freelancer class, trade blows using standard punches and brawling skills against his weak offense and minor healing. Defeat him to trigger the ending cutscene, rolling the credits and completing Ichiban's journey.

    Key fight: Final Boss: Masato Arakawa (1-on-1 Duel)

    Tip: Mash basic attacks and Freelancer brawling skills, as Arakawa's offense is extremely weak.

Checklists

Don't miss 0/27

Key fights
Tower Gauntlet (Trash Mobs)

Enemies: Tokyo Omi Alliance members, High-ranking yakuza, and singular Sujimon mobs (Levels 57–58)

Tactics: Group attacks such as Fulminating Forecast (Fortuneteller), Essence of Mayhem (Hero), or Eri/Zhao area-of-effect skills clear crowds quickly without wasting excess MP. Use recovery points between floors to replenish.

Boss: Yosuke Tendo (Ryudo Family Patriarch)

Enemies: Yosuke Tendo (Level 60)

Threats: Massive HP pool and heavy physical damage. Resists standard physical/blunt strikes. Below 50% HP, enters cutscene with dragon aura and charges 'God's Right Hand' / deadly punch skills capable of inflicting an instant KO (targets Kasuga for an immediate Game Over).

Weaknesses and resistances: Piercing / knife attacks, Electricity; vulnerable to Poison

Tactics: Have Kasuga cast and maintain Peerless Resolve (Hero skill) to survive any lethal blow with 1 HP. Equip Kasuga and healers with accessories like Curse Substitute to prevent instant death effects. Summon Nancy-chan (Poundmates) on turn one to apply poison. Keep a dedicated healer (Idol class like Saeko or Eri) to spam group heals every round.

Boss: Ryo Aoki & Tokyo Omi Reinforcements

Enemies: Ryo Aoki accompanied by multiple elite Tokyo Omi Alliance bodyguards

Threats: Aoki commands guards with offensive and defensive buffs, and casts 'Call a Friend' to summon more elite guards when existing ones fall.

Weaknesses and resistances: Aoki is weak to Physical/Blunt attacks and Electricity

Tactics: Ignore Aoki's summons initially or dispatch them with large electric AoE skills (e.g., Fulminating Forecast). Burst Aoki down directly. Once Aoki's HP drops to critical, transitions immediately into a cutscene where he flees down the steps.

Final Boss: Masato Arakawa (1-on-1 Duel)

Enemies: Masato Arakawa

Threats: Arakawa attempts basic attacks and small self-heals (~135 HP), but his offensive power is very low. Kasuga is restricted to his basic Freelancer (Freeter) set.

Tactics: Simple brawling; mash regular punches and Freelancer brawling skills.

Rewards: Clears the story; awards trophy/achievement 'Thank You From All the Staff' and creates post-game Game Clear Save file (unlocking Premium Adventure and post-game Final Millennium Tower dungeons).

Not listed for this area: secrets, enemies, shops and people.
