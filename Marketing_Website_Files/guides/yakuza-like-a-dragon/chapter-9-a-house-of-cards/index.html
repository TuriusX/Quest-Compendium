Chapter 9: A House of Cards

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Investigating the Omi Alliance's infiltration and battling to preserve the Yokohama balance
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga and his party defend Hamako's brothel and race to the Geomijul Stronghold to stop an Omi Alliance and Bleach Japan breach led by Reiji Ishioda and Nanba.

Region
Isezaki Ijincho, Yokohama (Commercial District/Hamako’s brothel area, Koreatown, Fukutokucho, and the Geomijul Stronghold)
Levels
Levels 24–27
Getting there
Starts directly after Chapter 8 inside the Survive Bar in the Bar District
Connects to
Bar District, Commercial District, Koreatown, Fukutokucho, Geomijul Stronghold
Related quests
Substory 23: Warmest Wishes · Substory 26: The Masked Murderer · Substory 28: Tour Guide Turmoil · Substory 29: The Michio Minefield
Services and people
Shady merchant selling Gold Keys

The short version

What matters

  • •Unlocking the permanent Recovery Point and Item Box on the 2nd floor of Survive Bar.
  • •Buying Gold Keys from the shady merchant in Koreatown/Ijincho to open gold safes across the city.
  • •Equipping Ice abilities on your party before entering Eomeoni's Vow to exploit Nanba's weakness in the final boss battle.

Common mistakes

  • •Attacking Ishioda with standard melee strikes in the counterfeiting facility, which triggers his devastating counter stance.
  • •Forgetting to have all party members Guard when the Wrecking Ball Crane raises its ball, leading to wipeout-level crushing damage.
  • •Leaving your party setup or jobs unprepared at Eomeoni's Vow, since both party switching and job changing are locked inside the facility.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Surviving and Regrouping

    Starting immediately after Chapter 8 inside the Survive Bar in the Bar District, head up to the Survive Bar (Upstairs Area) to review background records on Governor Ryo Aoki. Doing so unlocks permanent access to the 2nd-floor Recovery Point and Item Box for your party. Before heading out into the Commercial District, take advantage of these new facilities to heal up and manage your inventory.

    Tip: Use the newly unlocked second-floor recovery spot to top off your health and MP for free.

  2. 2

    Defending Hamako's Brothel

    Travel to Hamako’s brothel in the Commercial District, where you will find Yamato Totsuka and rogue Seiryu Clan members waiting outside. Eliminate the lower-level clan members first with wide-area physical or blade attacks to stop them from interrupting your turns, then hammer Totsuka with Kasuga's bat attacks, blunt force, fire, or electric skills. Victory clears the street and rewards you with the Fortune Dice accessory.

    Key fight: Yamato Totsuka & Seiryu Clan Grunts

    Tip: Totsuka is particularly weak to blunt, fire, and electric moves, so Kasuga's core bat abilities will deal heavy damage.

  3. 3

    The Koreatown Infiltration

    Proceed toward the Koreatown Outskirts, where you will find a staged Bleach Japan protest made up of disguised Omi Alliance operatives lining the streets. While navigating through Koreatown and Ijincho during this midpoint sequence, keep an eye out for a shady merchant selling Gold Keys that can unlock gold safes scattered across Yokohama. You can also pick up side activities like Substory 28 (choose "Red Dragon" for Passion, "Blue Sign" for Intellect, and "Ultra Packed Super Bun" for Style) or Substory 26 near Eomeoni's Vow (select the top option to investigate the masked butcher at night).

    Tip: Purchase Gold Keys from the shady merchant as soon as you spot him so you can start cracking gold safes across the city.

    Watch out: In Substory 28, answering Ayami's questions incorrectly will miss out on specific personality stat boosts.

  4. 4

    Breaching the Front Gates

    Approach the Front Gates of Geomijul, where Sota Kume confronts your party alongside an angry demonstration mob of disguised Omi thugs. Thin out the five Bleach Japan members immediately using sweeping moves like Eri's Thumbtack Scatter or Kasuga's Mega/Giga Swing, then focus standard blunt attacks onto Kume to claim the Fluorite Bracelet. Once they fall, the path ahead opens toward the stronghold's perimeter.

    Key fight: Sota Kume & Disguised Bleach Japan (Omi Goons)

    Tip: Wide area-of-effect skills will prevent the large crowd of grunts from flanking your party.

  5. 5

    Demolition Standoff

    Move to the Construction Site / Street Outside Stronghold, where Reiji Ishioda hijacks a Wrecking Ball Crane to smash through the Geomijul gates. Batter the heavy machinery using blunt and electric skills; once its health falls by roughly one-third, Ishioda will tumble out of the cab stunned, giving you a window to unleash your strongest single-target burst attacks before he climbs back in. Be ready at all times to command every party member to Guard whenever the crane hoists the wrecking ball high into the air.

    Key fight: Heavy Demolition Machinery: Wrecking Ball Crane

    Tip: Ishioda is vulnerable to massive burst damage while lying stunned on the pavement outside the cab.

    Watch out: Failing to Guard when the crane raises its wrecking ball will result in catastrophic crushing damage across your entire team.

  6. 6

    Entering the Burning Compound

    Head around to the back door entry at Eomeoni’s Vow (Korean Restaurant) to gain access to the burning Geomijul compound. Use the Item Box and prep spot inside the restaurant to finalize your team layout, items, and gear. Make sure your party is fully prepared here, because party switching and job changes become completely locked out once you cross into the inner Geomijul facility.

    Tip: Stock up on recovery items and set jobs with Ice skills (such as Hostess) before heading inside.

    Watch out: Entering the facility locks you out of changing jobs or swapping party members, leaving you stuck with your current lineup for the boss battle.

  7. 7

    Clash in the Counterfeiting Facility

    Enter the Geomijul Interior (Counterfeiting Facility) just as Seong-hui and her operatives destroy the printing machinery while Ishioda, Nanba, and Omi forces break in. Eliminate the two Lv. 25 Bleach Japan reinforcements immediately to stop them from calling endless backup, then take down Nanba using Ice attacks to put an end to his fiery breaths and debuffs. Finally, burn, freeze, or shock Ishioda with ranged skills, elemental magic, or Poundmates—avoiding standard melee attacks that trigger his counter stance—and keep Kasuga's health capped so Ishioda's sub-50% handgun bursts do not instantly defeat him, earning you the Passion Bandana.

    Key fight: Boss: Reiji Ishioda & Yu Nanba

    Tip: Nanba is weak to Ice abilities, making Hostess skills exceptionally useful for bursting him down quickly.

    Watch out: Do not use basic melee attacks against Ishioda, or he will counterattack your party members for severe damage.

Key fights
Yamato Totsuka & Seiryu Clan Grunts

Enemies: Yamato Totsuka (Lv. 25), 4x Seiryu Clan Members (Lv. 24)

Threats: High group numbers capable of interrupting actions

Weaknesses and resistances: Totsuka: Blunt (Bat attacks), Electric, Fire; Grunts: Blade/Slash, Physical

Tactics: Clean out the low-HP clan members using wide-area physical/blade attacks before focusing high-damage electric skills and Kasuga's bat moves on Totsuka.

Rewards: Fortune Dice, EXP, Yen

Sota Kume & Disguised Bleach Japan (Omi Goons)

Enemies: Sota Kume (Lv. 25), 5x Bleach Japan Members (Lv. 24)

Threats: Crowd pressure and mob flanking

Weaknesses and resistances: Kume: Blunt / Physical (fist and bat)

Tactics: Thin the mob immediately with area attacks (such as Eri's Thumbtack Scatter or Kasuga's Mega/Giga Swing); focus standard blunt combos onto Kume.

Rewards: Fluorite Bracelet, EXP, Yen

Heavy Demolition Machinery: Wrecking Ball Crane

Enemies: Wrecking Ball Crane (Lv. 26) operated by Reiji Ishioda

Threats: Sweeping area attacks and high burst damage when raising the crane into the air

Weaknesses and resistances: Electric

Tactics: Target the machinery with heavy blunt and electric skills. When health drops by roughly one-third, Ishioda is knocked out of the cab and temporarily stunned; pile heavy single-target burst onto him before he re-enters. When the crane lifts the wrecking ball high, have all party members use Guard to mitigate massive crushing damage.

Boss: Reiji Ishioda & Yu Nanba

Enemies: Reiji Ishioda (Lv. 27), Yu Nanba (Lv. 27), 2x Bleach Japan Reinforcements (Lv. 25)

Threats: Bleach Japan extras prioritize calling in backup. Nanba uses wide debuffs and elemental fire (Rancid Breath, Pyro Breath). Ishioda counters melee strikes and deals devastating burst damage with his handgun (Gunshot). When below 50% HP, Ishioda fires repeatedly and gains aggressive turns.

Weaknesses and resistances: Ishioda: Fire, Ice, Electric; Nanba: Ice

Tactics: 1. Burn down the Lv. 25 extras instantly to stop summoning spam. 2. Take down Nanba next using ice abilities (e.g., Hostess skills) to remove incoming AoE burns and debuffs. 3. Keep Kasuga’s HP topped off to avoid being one-shot by Ishioda’s gunshots. Avoid basic melee attacks that trigger Ishioda's counter stance; use strong ranged/elemental skills or high-impact Poundmates.

Rewards: Passion Bandana, major EXP/Job EXP
