Chapter 6: Ignition

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Exploring the Underground and dealing with the Seiryu Clan's internal tensions ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga escapes Akira Mabuchi's underground hideout, navigates the Yokohama Underground sewers to ground level, and crosses the Restaurant Row rooftops to confront Mamoru Takabe.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Escape the Liumang Holding Cell

    When you wake up strapped to a chair after Mabuchi departs, choose to break your chains by force or consult Nanba, Saeko, and Adachi before a masked stranger frees you. Step into the holding cell to loot the silver safe for a Slapjack, search between cardboard boxes for a Throwing Knife, grab a Spider from the corner debris, and crack two briefcases near the corridor exit for Staminan X (x2). In the first drainage passageway outside, you must face Foreman Yan and his two crewmen in a solo brawl.

    Key fight: Escape Fight: Foreman Yan & Workers

    Tip: Knock out Worker Mo and Worker Meng first with Tenacious Fist to limit incoming damage before focusing on Yan.

    Watch out: You fight this battle entirely solo with no equipped weapons in the Freelancer stance, so time your Perfect Blocks carefully.

  2. 2

    Explore Yokohama Underground B3F (First Half)

    Having regained your party and gear, proceed through the lower sewers while fighting off Turncoat Titillators, Automoburglars, Technomancers, Escapists, Sluggers, and Clobberjacks. Loot the west dead-end briefcase for a Toughness Light, open silver safes on the north wall for High Cut Shoes and the south dead-end alcove for an Embroidered Jacket, and gather floor sparklies in the north fork room for Mysterious Mushrooms, Mysterious Carrots, and Mysterious Leaf. Collect Comforting Medicine from the east wall, open the safe in the north alcove near the intersection for a Steel Frame, grab Poseidon Power and Tauriner from southeast wall briefcases, and check the corner for a Thick Hide.

    Key fight: Roaming Dungeon Encounters (B3F & B2F)

    Tip: Clear Automoburglars quickly with thunder-based attacks or stuns, and wipe clustered Escapists and Sluggers using Nanba's Pyro Belch or Kasuga's Mega Swing.

    Watch out: Always guard when Clobberjacks pull out dynamite to avoid massive area-of-effect explosive damage.

  3. 3

    Climb to the B2F Rest Area Checkpoint

    Before leaving B3F via the exit stairs, open the nearby silver safe to obtain a Titanium Anklet and search the adjacent debris to pick up a Staminan Light, Mysterious Stone, and Quality Lumber. Ascend to the Yokohama Underground B2F rest area, where you will spot a recovery point and a lone silver safe standing in the middle of the room. Approach the safe prepared for an ambush rather than standard treasure.

    Key fight: Mimic Trap: Sneak-Thief / Shaman

    Tip: Heal fully at the nearby Recovery Point before touching the silver safe, then burst down the target with single-target physical attacks.

    Watch out: The central silver safe is a mimic trap housing Sneak-Thief Otsubo; opening it initiates a sudden high-defense fight.

  4. 4

    Descend Through the B3F Return Tunnels

    Head back down into the B3F return section and open the silver safe positioned behind the first enemy pack to claim a Longcoat. Continue through the lower tunnels to reach the path leading up to the construction basin.

    Key fight: Roaming Dungeon Encounters (B3F & B2F)

    Tip: Use standard blunt strikes or physical skills to disrupt Technomancers and bring down Turncoat Titillators.

  5. 5

    Defeat the Excavator and Escape to the Bar District

    Enter the B2F Construction Basin to confront Foreman Yan operating heavy machinery. After dismantling the excavator and Yan, proceed behind the construction pit and climb the exit ladder to emerge safely in the Bar District.

    Key fight: Boss: Heavy Machinery / Excavator

    Tip: Target the excavator strictly with Electric skills to exploit its sole elemental weakness.

    Watch out: Do not climb the exit ladder behind the construction pit until you have collected all dungeon items, as climbing it permanently locks you out of the area.

Don't miss 0/28

Key fights
Escape Fight: Foreman Yan & Workers

Enemies: Foreman Yan (Lv. 17), Worker Mo (Lv. 16), Worker Meng (Lv. 16)

Threats: Kasuga fights completely solo with weapons unequipped and is forced into his base Freelancer stance.

Weaknesses and resistances: Workers have low defense and are vulnerable to physical brawling.

Tactics: Knock out Mo and Meng first using Tenacious Fist to reduce incoming attacks, then focus on Yan. Time Perfect Blocks carefully.

Rewards: Party members and standard gear are restored immediately upon victory.

Roaming Dungeon Encounters (B3F & B2F)

Enemies: Turncoat Titillators (Lv. 15), Automoburglars (Lv. 15), Technomancers (Lv. 16), Escapists (Lv. 17), Sluggers (Lv. 17), Clobberjacks (Lv. 17)

Threats: Automoburglars can steal items or deal heavy blunt hits; Clobberjacks pull out sticks of dynamite to deal explosive AoE damage.

Weaknesses and resistances: Turncoat Titillators: Blunt/Physical, Fire, Ice (resists non-elemental blade strikes); Automoburglars: Electric; Technomancers: Physical skills, Blunt; Escapists & Sluggers: Fire.

Tactics: Use standard physical or sweep attacks on Turncoat Titillators. Clear Automoburglars with stun or thunder-based skills. Disrupt Technomancers' casting quickly. Use Nanba's Pyro Belch or Kasuga's Mega Swing on clustered Escapists and Sluggers to wipe them in 1–2 turns. Ensure all characters guard against Clobberjacks' dynamite.

Mimic Trap: Sneak-Thief / Shaman

Enemies: Sneak-Thief / Shaman / Otsubo

Threats: High-defense surprise enemy hiding inside the central safe.

Tactics: Heal at the adjacent Recovery Point before interacting with the safe. Focus single-target physical bursts down on him.

Rewards: Treasure Displeasure trophy

Boss: Heavy Machinery / Excavator

Enemies: Heavy Machinery Excavator operated by Foreman Yan, Foreman Yan

Threats: Excavator Phase: High crushing defense, massive sweeping physical damage with scoop arm, high stagger resistance, resists most blunt and status ailments. Yan Phase: Yan engages on foot.

Weaknesses and resistances: Excavator: Electric only (neutral to Blade). Yan: Fire, status stuns.

Tactics: Excavator Phase: Apply stat debuffs (Attack Down / Defense Down), keep party HP topped up, and target the arm with Electric damage skills. Yan Phase: Focus fire Yan directly using Nanba's Pyro Belch or fire items until defeated.

Rewards: Crowbar, substantial XP, allows party exit to surface Yokohama
