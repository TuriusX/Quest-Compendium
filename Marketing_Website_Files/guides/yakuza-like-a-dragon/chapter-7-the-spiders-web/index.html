Chapter 7: The Spider's Web

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Uncovering the counterfeit money operation run by the Geomijul syndicate
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga and his allies infiltrate the Geomijul Hideout across dilapidated apartments and rooftops to uncover Mabuchi's betrayal, discovering the Ijin Three's counterfeit currency operation before confronting Joon-gi Han.

Region
Ijincho (Yokohama)
Levels
Level 20–23
Getting there
Following the ceasefire with Tianyou Zhao, head to the objective marker in Koreatown to meet a mysterious pink-haired woman in Hyakukei Alley, who leads the party into an alleyway apartment complex.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Alleyway Entrance

    After your truce with Tianyou Zhao, head to Hyakukei Alley in Koreatown and speak to the pink-haired guide standing near the entrance. Before selecting "I'm ready" to proceed, search directly east of her to crack open a silver safe. Choosing to go inside will immediately lock you into the complex.

    Tip: Choose "Not yet" if you still need to purchase supplies or level up your party before heading inside.

    Watch out: Warning: Telling the guide "I'm ready" locks you out of the city streets and into the hideout gauntlet.

  2. 2

    First Floor Ambush Room & Hallway

    Head inside to trigger two back-to-back waves against Geomijul brawlers like Kim, Ko, Moon, Song, and Park. Once the room is clear, check the northwest floor shine, search the east wall near the blocked exit for a second shine, grab the briefcase by the south wall, take the hallway item, and open the safe sitting at the south dead end.

    Key fight: Geomijul Brawlers (Interior Waves)

    Tip: Use Ichiban's Mega Swing or Nanba's Pyro Breath to wipe out the lower-level grunts with blade or fire damage before focusing down Kim.

    Watch out: Warning: The entire hideout is a one-time story area; any missed floor shines, briefcases, or safes are permanently lost once you finish the chapter's hideout run.

  3. 3

    Internal Stairwells & Second Floor

    Fight through the patrol as you climb past debris into the second-floor rooms. Search the shelves, loot the north and south briefcases, open the silver safe, and grab the items on the stairs and upper landing before moving out toward the roof.

    Tip: Pick up the Luxury Yakiniku Bento from the stairs shine to keep your recovery supplies stocked.

  4. 4

    Rooftops & Power-Wire Crossing

    Step out onto the rooftop, navigating around barricades and fallen planks near a green traffic cone before crossing the heavy overhead electric power cables. Check the barricade area for loose pickups, unlock the corner safe, grab the item sitting over the barrels, and intentionally drop down the gap on the right side of the stairs to loot the drop-off ledge before climbing back up.

    Tip: Make sure to deliberately fall through the right-side gap near the stairs so you do not miss the Raw Sapphire Ore and other items on the lower ledge.

  5. 5

    The Rooftop Ambush

    As you push across the rooftops, activate the Active Search reticle mechanic to scan the surrounding area. Locate and highlight all 6 concealed Geomijul sentries positioned along the vantage points, then eliminate the remaining combatants in the skirmish that follows.

    Key fight: The 6 Ambushers (Rooftop Wire Crossing)

    Tip: Spotting all 6 snipers through the targeting reticle neutralizes them immediately, making the ensuing mop-up battle trivial.

  6. 6

    Inner Corridor to Seong-hui's Chamber

    Push through the remaining defensive perimeter until you reach the heavy double door leading to Seong-hui's chamber. Approach the door to trigger the prompt asking if you wish to proceed into the printing facility.

    Tip: Fully heal your entire party and equip poison-curing items before interacting with the double doors.

    Watch out: Warning: Selecting "Proceed" at the double doors locks you into major story cutscenes, permanently removes Nanba from your active party for the fight, and throws you straight into the boss battle.

  7. 7

    Boss Confrontation: Joon-gi Han

    Watch the cutscene uncovering the counterfeit yen printing press, lose Nanba from your party, and face off against Joon-gi Han. Keep HP topped off to withstand his high critical rate, Divine Shot, and poison effects while exploiting his weaknesses until he goes down, after which you defeat the fleeing enforcers and escape to the homeless camp.

    Key fight: Boss: Joon-gi Han (Level 23)

    Tip: Assign Saeko to the Idol job to keep the party healed with Redressing, have Adachi tank hits, buff offense with Ichiban's Brave Command, and strike Joon-gi Han with blunt knockdowns and electric damage.

Key fights
Geomijul Brawlers (Interior Waves)

Enemies: Geomijul combatants (Kim Lv. 21, Ko Lv. 17, Moon Lv. 18, Song Lv. 18, Park Lv. 18)

Weaknesses and resistances: Blade / Knife and Fire attacks

Tactics: Use Ichiban’s Mega Swing or area elemental skills (e.g., Nanba's Pyro Breath) to wipe out the lower-level grunts quickly before bursting down Kim.

Rewards: Yen, basic crafting scrap, XP

The 6 Ambushers (Rooftop Wire Crossing)

Enemies: Geomijul scout snipers and combatants (Levels 18–20)

Tactics: Neutralized immediately upon spotting all six via the reticle mechanic, followed by a clean-up skirmish.

Boss: Joon-gi Han (Level 23)

Enemies: Joon-gi Han (Level 23)

Threats: High agility, multiple actions per turn, high critical hit rate, Divine Shot (piercing bullet damage that cannot be perfectly dodged), and Poison status effects. Nanba is disabled for this battle.

Weaknesses and resistances: Blunt knockdowns, attack debuffs, and electric damage

Tactics: Have Saeko as an Idol to keep HP topped off and use Redressing; use Adachi to tank; use Ichiban to buff party offense with Brave Command; bring poison curing medicine or high-grade First Aid Kits.

Rewards: Massive EXP payout, substantial Yen, high-grade armor/accessory drops

Not listed for this area: enemies, shops and people.
