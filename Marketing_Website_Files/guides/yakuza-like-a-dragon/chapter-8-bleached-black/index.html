Chapter 8: Bleached Black

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Dealing with Bleach Japan protests and the fallout within the Ijin Three ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Chapter 8 follows Ichiban Kasuga as he tracks Nanba to Bleach Japan's office in the Hakuryo Building, culminating in a clash with Akira Mabuchi.

Region
Isezaki Ijincho (Yokohama)
Levels
Level 23–25
Getting there
Unlocked automatically upon completing Chapter 7: The Spider's Web; movement between objectives is on foot or via Taxi stops around Isezaki Ijincho.
Connects to
Koreatown (Geomijul territory), Carriage Highway / Hakuryo Building, Chinatown & Jinnai Station areas
Related quests
Chapter 8: Bleached Black · Part-Time Hero

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating Akira Mabuchi to acquire the Chainmail armor and a massive payout of Yen, character XP, and Job XP.
  • •Exploiting Mabuchi's weaknesses to blade attacks, ice, electricity, and poison to cut through his fiery Heat phase.
  • •Utilizing area-of-effect skills and environmental office props against Mabuchi's four advance guard members.

Common mistakes

  • •Attacking Shin with basic non-blunt skills instead of Kasuga's bat attacks, allowing him to inflict crippling shock and stun statuses.
  • •Leaving Kasuga unguarded against Mabuchi's high-damage strikes once the boss activates his fiery Heat aura below 50% HP.
  • •Forgetting to assign Saeko to the Idol job to provide reliable group healing during the boss encounter.

What you can skip

  • •Standard melee attacks against Akira Mabuchi while he is holding his defensive parry stance.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Receive Your Objective at the Seiryu Clan Headquarters

    Inside the Reception Room at Seiryu Clan Headquarters, watch the briefing concerning the Great Wall of Muscle and the counterfeit currency network. Chairman Ryuhei Hoshino will then instruct you to track down Nanba, who has gone missing. Once the discussion concludes, prepare your gear and head outside into Isezaki Ijincho.

    Tip: Ensure your party is around Level 23–25 before pushing ahead.

  2. 2

    Speak with Joon-gi Han in Koreatown

    Make your way to Koreatown and locate Joon-gi Han standing along Breeze Street / Fukutokucho, directly outside the Geomijul Stronghold. Speak with him to discover that Nanba has likely headed to Bleach Japan's local branch. Pick the top dialogue option to confirm your readiness to proceed, or select the alternate choice if you still wish to explore the city first.

    Tip: If you want to finish Part-Time Hero activities or buy supplies, back out of the prompt to freely roam before committing.

  3. 3

    Head to the Hakuryo Building

    Travel to the northwest portion of Isezaki Ijincho either on foot or by hailing a taxi to Carriage Highway. Approach the Hakuryo Building, which houses the regional branch of Bleach Japan. Prepare your party for combat before stepping toward the front door.

    Tip: Taking a taxi to Carriage Highway saves significant travel time compared to walking from Koreatown.

  4. 4

    Clear the Entrance Ambush

    As you attempt to enter the Hakuryo Building, Former Geomijul Officer Shin ambushes your party at the front entrance. Target him immediately with heavy single-target blunt physical attacks, such as Kasuga's default bat strikes, to wear down his health quickly. Focus all your offensive power to defeat him before his high-damage shock strikes inflict the volt or stun status effects.

    Key fight: Fight 1: Entrance Ambush

    Tip: Shin is particularly weak to blunt physical strikes, making standard bat attacks extremely effective.

    Watch out: Shin's electrical strikes deal heavy damage and can stun your party members if the fight drags on.

  5. 5

    Fight Through the Advance Guard

    Take the Hakuryo Building elevator up to the 2nd/upper floor to confront Sota Kume, Hajime Ogasawara, and Nanba. Akira Mabuchi suddenly interrupts the encounter and sics his subordinates on you in the tight corridor. Deploy broad area-of-effect skills like Kasuga's Mega Swing or Eri's Thumbtack Scatter, and smash office debris into the four Yokohama Liumang members (Xiang, Fu, Meng, and Yu) to wipe them out.

    Key fight: Fight 2: Mabuchi’s Advance Guard

    Tip: Environmental objects cluttering the office floor can be utilized for strong debris strikes against the clustered enemies.

    Watch out: Avoid wasting single-target skills here, as the four enemies can overwhelm you with combined physical attacks if not cleared together.

  6. 6

    Inspect the Inner Office

    Step into the inner Bleach Japan office and examine the photographs displayed across the desk and wall. This triggers a story cutscene showing that Bleach Japan's founder is Ryo Aoki, also known as Masato Arakawa. Regroup and fully heal your party immediately after the revelation finishes.

  7. 7

    Defeat Akira Mabuchi

    Face off against Akira Mabuchi in the Hakuryo Building's upper floor to conclude the chapter. Exploit his weaknesses to electricity, ice/water, and blade strikes (such as Eri's Shadow Stitch), or call Nancy-chan to afflict him with Poison. When Mabuchi assumes his parry stance, do not use standard melee strikes, and instead cast magic, apply buffs, or use party-wide recovery skills with Saeko as an Idol; guard Kasuga whenever Mabuchi ignites his fiery Heat aura at 50% HP. Defeating him rewards you with Yen, massive Job and character XP, and Chainmail armor.

    Key fight: Boss Fight: Akira Mabuchi

    Tip: Summoning Nancy-chan to inflict Poison steadily drains Mabuchi's health while he is defensive.

    Watch out: Basic melee strikes against Mabuchi during his counter stance will be deflected, prompting an immediate counterattack.

Key fights
Fight 1: Entrance Ambush

Enemies: Former Geomijul Officer Shin (Lv. 24)

Threats: High-damage volt/stun shock attacks

Weaknesses and resistances: Blunt physical damage (Kasuga’s default bat attacks)

Tactics: Focus fire with heavy single-target physical skills to burn him down quickly and prevent shock status effects.

Fight 2: Mabuchi’s Advance Guard

Enemies: 4 Yokohama Liumang Members — Xiang (Lv. 24), Fu (Lv. 23), Meng (Lv. 23), Yu (Lv. 23)

Threats: Close quarters and combined physical dog-piling

Weaknesses and resistances: Standard crowd control and broad physical/AoE skills

Tactics: Use wide area-of-effect skills like Kasuga’s Mega Swing or Eri’s Thumbtack Scatter, and take advantage of office debris for environmental strikes.

Boss Fight: Akira Mabuchi

Enemies: Akira Mabuchi (Lv. 25)

Threats: Counter/Defensive Stance deflecting standard melee strikes and triggering counterattacks; activates a fiery Heat aura around 50% HP increasing offensive power and attack speed

Weaknesses and resistances: Blade / Knife damage, Ice / Water damage, Electricity, and susceptible to Poison

Tactics: Keep Saeko (Idol) ready for party-wide healing. Guard on Kasuga if targeted during Heat state. Use blade skills like Eri's Shadow Stitch. Summon Nancy-chan to inflict Poison. During parry stance, avoid basic physical attacks and instead buff, heal, or cast magic/skills.

Rewards: Large XP/Job XP payout, Yen, and Chainmail armor
