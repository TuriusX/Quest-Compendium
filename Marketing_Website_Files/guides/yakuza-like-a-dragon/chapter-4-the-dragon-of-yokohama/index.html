Chapter 4: The Dragon of Yokohama

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Teaming up with Adachi, seeking work at Hello Work, and discovering the Hero job
← All areas in this guide

Chapter 4 follows Kasuga, Nanba, and the returning Adachi as they cross the river bridge to pull a legendary bat from the pavement and head to Hello Work for legal employment.

Region
South / Central Isezaki Ijincho (Commercial District & Misaki Town border)
Levels
Recommended Level 8–11
Getting there
Hamako’s brothel boarding house (Kasuga’s room), situated across the river bridge directly west/southwest of Hello Work
Connects to
Hello Work, Otohime Land
Related quests
Substory #2 Who’s That Sujimon!? · Substory #6 It's The Thought That Counts
Services and people
Adachi · Nanba · Director Kaneko / Staff at Hello Work · Kobayashi

The short version

What matters

  • •Unlocking Kasuga's Hero job class and acquiring the Rusty / Barbed Bat during the river bridge sequence.
  • •Opening the silver Hello Work Safe inside the facility before proceeding through the main office lobby doors.
  • •Speaking with Director Kaneko inside Hello Work to gain the story referral leading to Otohime Land.

Common mistakes

  • •Focusing attacks on Slugger Otsuka right away instead of taking out Kusano and Hashiguchi first, leaving your party open to heavy incoming damage and Fear incapacitation.
  • •Neglecting to guard when Otsuka charges his heavy hit, which can devastate an unguarded character's health.

What you can skip

  • •Worrying about permanently losing possessions taken by Pro Pickpockets, as all stolen items return upon victory.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Regroup at the Boarding House

    Start Chapter 4 inside Kasuga's room at Hamako’s brothel boarding house, which sits across the river bridge directly west/southwest of Hello Work. Here, Adachi reunites with your crew and rejoins as an active combatant alongside Nanba. Prepare your gear before venturing out into South / Central Isezaki Ijincho toward your next destination.

    Tip: Ensure your party is around the recommended level range of 8–11 before setting out.

  2. 2

    Cross the River Bridge

    Head toward Hello Work by crossing the river bridge, where a cutscene triggers as Kasuga spots a legendary weapon stuck firmly in the ground. You will watch Kasuga pull the embedded weapon from the concrete to claim it for himself.

  3. 3

    Clash with the Street Thugs

    Immediately after pulling the weapon, you are thrust into combat against Slugger Otsuka (Lv. 10), Assassin Kusano (Lv. 9), and Hands Thrower Hashiguchi (Lv. 9). During this confrontation, Kasuga's job automatically switches to Hero, introducing full Sujimon transformation visuals to battle. Focus down Kusano and Hashiguchi first to cut incoming damage, then exploit Otsuka's weakness to Fire using Nanba’s Pyro Belch while making sure to guard when Otsuka winds up his heavy attack.

    Key fight: Street Thugs

    Tip: Nanba's Pyro Belch deals severe elemental damage to Otsuka due to his fire vulnerability.

    Watch out: Otsuka can inflict Fear, a status condition capable of completely incapacitating party members for several turns.

  4. 4

    Approach Hello Work

    Proceed to the front steps of the Hello Work employment office, where men recognizing Adachi confront you outside. Before or after this encounter, you can spot Kobayashi standing on the block's northeast corner right outside the facility, who serves as a potential recruit for Business Management.

    Tip: Take note of Kobayashi's position outside if you plan to hire him for Business Management later.

  5. 5

    Repel the Reckless Men

    Battle the ambushers outside Hello Work: Break-and-Entryman Kawahara (Lv. 10) alongside Pro Pickpocket Minagawa (Lv. 11) and Pro Pickpocket Abe (Lv. 11). Focus down the two pickpockets first using Adachi's physical strike skills and Nanba’s pigeon or fire abilities to eliminate targets one at a time.

    Key fight: Reckless Men

    Tip: Any items swiped by the pickpockets are automatically recovered once you win the fight.

    Watch out: Leaving the pickpockets alive allows them repeated opportunities to steal inventory items mid-battle.

  6. 6

    Enter Hello Work and Secure a Posting

    Walk inside the Hello Work building and check the area immediately before entering the main office lobby to locate a silver safe. Once inside the lobby, speak with Director Kaneko and the Hello Work staff to receive your next employment referral at Otohime Land.

    Tip: Look out for the silver safe in the hallway just prior to stepping through the lobby doors.

Key fights
Street Thugs

Enemies: Slugger Otsuka (Lv. 10), Assassin Kusano (Lv. 9), Hands Thrower Hashiguchi (Lv. 9)

Threats: Otsuka is bulky and can inflict the Fear status ailment, which can incapacitate party members for several turns.

Weaknesses and resistances: Otsuka is weak to Fire.

Tactics: Take out the Lv. 9 henchmen (Kusano and Hashiguchi) first to reduce incoming party damage. Use Nanba’s Pyro Belch skill to deal heavy elemental damage to Otsuka. Guard when Otsuka charges his heavy hit.

Rewards: Unlocks the Hero job class for Kasuga and shifts combat aesthetics into full Sujimon transformation mode.

Reckless Men

Enemies: Break-and-Entryman Kawahara (Lv. 10), Pro Pickpocket Minagawa (Lv. 11), Pro Pickpocket Abe (Lv. 11)

Threats: The Pro Pickpockets will attempt to steal items/valuables from your inventory mid-fight.

Tactics: Prioritize the Pickpockets early to minimize lost items (stolen items are automatically recovered upon battle victory, but defeating them early avoids nuisance). Use Adachi’s physical strike skills and Nanba’s pigeon/fire skills to focus down one target at a time.

Not listed for this area: secrets, enemies, shops and people.
