Chapter 12: The End of the Yakuza

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Traveling to Sotenbori, Osaka, for the historic joint dissolution of the Tojo Clan and Omi Alliance
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga and his party travel from Ijincho to Sotenbori and infiltrate the Omi Alliance Headquarters, facing legendary legends and participating in the historic dissolution of the yakuza.

Region
Sotenbori (Osaka) and the instanced Omi Alliance Headquarters
Levels
Recommended Level 45–50
Getting there
After raising 3,000,000 yen in Ijincho (Yokohama) and handing it over to Chairman Hoshino at the Seiryu Clan Headquarters, Ichiban and the party depart for Osaka, beginning near The Grand cabaret club on the west end of Sotenbori Street.
Connects to
Yokohama (Isezaki Ijincho), Omi Alliance Headquarters
Related quests
Part-Time Hero
Services and people
Yappi-kun · Kani Douraku · Gyu-Kaku · Zuboraya · Kinryu Ramen · Club SEGA · Casino · Gambling Den

The short version

What matters

  • •Completing Sotenbori Battle Arena floors to reach Level 45–50 and claim milestone rewards like the Floor 18 Curse Substitute and Dragon gear.
  • •Grabbing the two floor Briefcases and opening both Silver Safes inside the ante-room right before opening the Double Samurai Armor Door.
  • •Defeating Goro Majima and Taiga Saejima to acquire high EXP, cash payouts, and their Poundmates summons.

Common mistakes

  • •Entering the Omi Alliance HQ under Level 45, which leaves you dangerously underpowered for the dual boss encounter.
  • •Leaving Ichiban below 90% HP without guarding against Majima and Saejima, which can cause an instant game over from their joint combo strike.
  • •Rushing past the ante-room into the boss chamber without picking up the two Briefcases and unlocking both Silver Safes.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Arrival in Sotenbori

    After handing over 3,000,000 yen to Chairman Hoshino at the Seiryu Clan Headquarters in Yokohama, you reach Osaka and appear outside The Grand on the west end of Sotenbori Street. Discovering that Mitsu fails to answer his phone, you are free to explore local attractions including Kani Douraku, Gyu-Kaku, Zuboraya, Kinryu Ramen, Club SEGA, the Casino, Gambling Den, Shogi parlor, Golf center, Mahjong, and Part-Time Hero activities.

  2. 2

    Conquer the Sotenbori Battle Arena

    Head over to meet the robotic receptionist Yappi-kun to access the 30-floor combat challenge at the Sotenbori Battle Arena. Take time to train until your team reaches the recommended level range of 45–50 before attempting any story objectives. You can obtain valuable milestone gear here, such as a Curse Substitute on Floor 18 alongside assorted high-tier weapons and Dragon equipment.

    Tip: Train Saeko as an Idol to ensure party-wide recovery spells are ready, and prep jobs like Host, Nanba, or Zhao with Fire and Ice/Cold skills.

  3. 3

    Infiltrate the Omi Alliance Headquarters

    Approach a Taxi Stand and choose to travel to the Omi Alliance Headquarters to launch the night infiltration. You will promptly be ambushed in the entry area by an Omi Alliance Security Guard Squad consisting of Kasai, Niwa, and Watanabe. Target their blade and bullet weaknesses with broad attacks like Joon-gi Han's Divine Shot to wipe them out quickly.

    Key fight: Omi Alliance Security Guard Squad

    Watch out: Confirming your taxi ride locks you directly into the Omi Alliance Headquarters dungeon sequence.

  4. 4

    Clear the Corridors to the Double Armor Door

    Advance down the Omi Alliance HQ Infiltration Hallways toward the inner sanctum, ascending the stairs and opening consecutive doors. You will reach the ante-room positioned directly in front of the Double Samurai Armor Door. Before crossing the threshold, loot the two Briefcases off the floor and unlock the pair of Silver Safes using Silver Keys.

    Watch out: Both the Briefcases and Silver Safes are permanently missable if you push through the boss doors without collecting them.

  5. 5

    Clash with the Tojo Legends

    Unseal the Double Samurai Armor Door to trigger a confrontation against Goro Majima, who creates three Doppelgängers, followed by Taiga Saejima joining the fray. Target Majima and his illusions with Fire attacks like Nanba or Zhao's Essence of Pyro Prison, then strike Saejima with Cold/Ice skills or Water Poundmates once Majima falls. Keep Saeko casting Magical Song and ensure Ichiban guards or maintains above 90% HP to survive their lethal joint team combo attack.

    Key fight: Dual Legend Boss Fight: Goro Majima & Taiga Saejima

    Tip: Defeating these legends rewards massive EXP, Job EXP, cash, and unlocks both Goro Majima and Taiga Saejima as Poundmates summons.

    Watch out: If Ichiban is caught unblocking with reduced health during their coordinated tag-team assault, he will be instantly killed.

  6. 6

    The Grand Dissolution Ceremony

    Head inside the Inner Reception Room, where Masumi Arakawa and Daigo Dojima reveal the scheme to disband both the Tojo Clan and the Omi Alliance. Speak to a Taxi the following day to attend the ceremony in the Omi Alliance HQ Main Hall, where Masaru Watase and Daigo Dojima announce the dissolution. Rebel traitors will riot, forcing you to wipe out six Omi Henchmen in a brawl using wide-hitting skills like Essence of Orbital Laser, Giga Swing, or Divine Shot.

    Key fight: The Grand Dissolution Brawl

    Watch out: Speaking to the taxi driver to return the next morning marks your final point of no return for completing the chapter's storyline.

Key fights
Omi Alliance Security Guard Squad

Enemies: Kasai (Lv. 48), Niwa (Lv. 47), Watanabe (Lv. 47)

Threats: Low-to-moderate; serves as a check to confirm party readiness.

Weaknesses and resistances: Gun and Blade attacks

Tactics: Use Joon-gi Han's Divine Shot or wide AOE attacks to wipe them out quickly.

Dual Legend Boss Fight: Goro Majima & Taiga Saejima

Enemies: Goro Majima (Lv. 50), Majima Clones (Doppelgängers) (Lv. 48 x3), Taiga Saejima (Lv. 50)

Threats: Rapid blade slashes inflicting Bleed, multiple clones overwhelming party members with multi-hit bleeding strikes, buffed offense/defense, and a deadly joint team combo attack that easily one-shots Ichiban if unblocked.

Weaknesses and resistances: Goro Majima & Clones: Fire. Taiga Saejima: Ice/Cold (Water).

Tactics: Set Saeko as an Idol for dedicated party healing like Magical Song. Use Nanba or Zhao to exploit Majima's Fire weakness with AOE fire spells like Essence of Pyro Prison to burst down clones. Keep Ichiban above 90% HP or guard immediately if bosses get consecutive turns. Use Host skills or Poundmates with Ice/Cold damage to defeat Saejima once Majima is eliminated.

Rewards: Large sum of EXP/Job EXP, high cash payout, and unlocks Goro Majima and Taiga Saejima summons via Poundmates.

The Grand Dissolution Brawl

Enemies: Amassed Omi Alliance Faction Traitors (6x Omi Henchmen, Lv. 47–48)

Threats: Standard mob stats

Weaknesses and resistances: Susceptible to heavy AOE physical and magical strikes

Tactics: Clean up with large area skills such as Essence of Orbital Laser, Giga Swing, or Divine Shot.

Not listed for this area: secrets, enemies, shops and people.
