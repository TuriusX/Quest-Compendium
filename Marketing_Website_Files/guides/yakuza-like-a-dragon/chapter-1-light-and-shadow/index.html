Chapter 1: Light and Shadow

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · The prologue introducing Ichiban Kasuga in Kamurocho on New Year's Eve 2000
← All areas in this guide

Chapter 1: Light and Shadow serves as a linear tutorial prologue set in Kamurocho on New Year's Eve, 2000, following Ichiban Kasuga through his daily tasks for the Arakawa Family before taking the fall for a crime.

Region
Kamurocho, Tokyo (December 31, 2000)
Levels
Level 1
Getting there
Follows the historical prologue cutscenes set in 1971; exploration is cordoned strictly to relevant streets guiding between objectives.
Related quests
Chase and apprehend Ushio · Check in with Mama Michiyo in the Champion District · Clear the issue at Shangri-La (retrieve plunger) · Collect money at Public Park 3 · Meet Mitsu at Theater Square and report to the Arakawa Family Office · Walk with Patriarch Arakawa to Theater Square for Peking Duck · Return to the office, receive your orders, and eat a final meal at Akaushimaru
Services and people
Cigarette shop clerk · Akaushimaru

The short version

What matters

  • •Choose between helping out Mama Michiyo (+Kindness) or scolding her (+Passion) to boost your preferred Personality stat.
  • •Learn basic combat fundamentals including offensive MP Skills, item use, and Perfect Guard timing during the scripted skirmishes.
  • •Take advantage of environmental items like bikes and cones during street brawls to inflict easy extra damage.

Common mistakes

  • •Searching Kamurocho for keys to crack safes; the required silver and gold keys are only obtainable much later in Yokohama.
  • •Worrying about missing shop completion lists or stock in Kamurocho 2000, as unpurchased consumables have zero impact on completion records.

What you can skip

  • •Do not bother buying extra consumables from local stores, as unbought inventory will be abandoned when the chapter ends.
  • •Ignore all locked silver and gold safes across Kamurocho since their opening keys cannot be obtained in this chapter.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Apprehend Ushio

    After the 1971 opening scenes place Level 1 Ichiban Kasuga in Kamurocho on December 31, 2000 with your starting ¥1,500, clear the QTE pursuit through the streets to corner Ushio in an alleyway. Follow the on-screen tutorial prompts to deplete his HP with basic attacks, test basic recovery items, and practice Perfect Guard against his slow, weak strikes.

    Key fight: Street Thug Ushio

    Tip: Time your button presses during his windups to master Perfect Guard with minimal incoming damage.

  2. 2

    Visit the Champion District and Shangri-La

    Navigate the cordoned streets to Mama Michiyo's Snack Bar in the Champion District, where you must choose how to respond when she takes a spill. Head to Shangri-La on Taihei Boulevard to inspect the plumbing trouble, and keep an eye on Ichiban's phone as the single early-game Table Talk/Party Chat tutorial prompt triggers automatically.

    Tip: Choosing "Help Out" awards +Kindness, whereas selecting "Scold Sternly" grants +Passion to your Personality stats.

  3. 3

    Fetch the Plunger

    Make your way toward Nakamichi Alley, where a group of street punks confronts you with basic attacks disabled so you must spend MP on Ichiban's offensive combat Skills. Once the punks drop their yen and EXP, step onto Nakamichi Street and speak with the cigarette shop clerk at the counter to obtain the Plunger.

    Key fight: Street Punks

    Tip: Line up your Skills as instructed to defeat the entire punk mob swiftly.

  4. 4

    Collect the Debt at Public Park 3

    Head to Public Park 3 to back up your junior Mitsu in a debt-collection brawl against delinquent debtors. Mix your regular attacks and MP skills, positioning near loose signs, cones, or bicycles so Kasuga automatically weaponizes them.

    Key fight: Collection Brawl

    Tip: Stand directly adjacent to street objects before hitting attack inputs to turn them into improvised weapons.

    Watch out: Do not waste time hunting for silver and gold keys to unlock Kamurocho's safes, as these keys are only found much later in Yokohama.

  5. 5

    The Final Hours in Kamurocho

    Regroup at Tenkaichi Street Gate, meet Mitsu at Theater Square, and report to the Arakawa Family Office before accompanying Patriarch Masumi Arakawa back to Theater Square to buy Peking Duck. Return to the office to receive orders to take the fall for Captain Jo Sawashiro, then visit Akaushimaru on Tenkaichi St. for a final meal before turning yourself in at the police station.

    Watch out: Turning yourself in permanently closes the 2000 Kamurocho prologue, rendering all unbought shop consumables irrelevant.
