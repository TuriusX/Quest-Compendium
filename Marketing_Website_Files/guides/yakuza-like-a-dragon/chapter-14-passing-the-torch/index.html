Chapter 14: Passing the Torch

Yakuza: Like a Dragon guide · Confronting Reiji Ishioda and preparing for the final confrontation in Kamurocho ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Kasuga pursues Kume and Ishioda across Isezaki Ijincho, facing Kazuma Kiryu in a trial at Geomijul HQ before defeating Reiji Ishioda and Mirror Face in the Bar District warehouse.

Region
Isezaki Ijincho (Yokohama)
Levels
55–57
Getting there
You start in Ijincho. Fast travel is handled via standard taxi stops across the city.
Connects to
Kamurocho, Kamurocho Underground Dungeon
Services and people
Yi Xing Tang · Romance Workshop · Survive Bar

The short version

What matters

  • •Bring a female party member like Saeko in the Idol job against Kiryu, as he will never target women.
  • •Keep cold and ice elemental attacks on hand to exploit Kiryu's main weakness during his boss trial.
  • •Target Mirror Face first in the final battle using Gun and Blade abilities while pausing offense whenever Surveillance is active.

Common mistakes

  • •Attacking Ishioda with basic chip damage or non-knockdown attacks; he will automatically punish you with counter gunshots that inflict Fear.
  • •Hitting Kazuma Kiryu with male physical fighters while he holds his Rush counter or sub-25% Dragon's Gaze stance.
  • •Forgetting to apply Kasuga's Peerless Resolve during the Kiryu battle, leaving him vulnerable to an unexpected one-shot defeat.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Ambush Outside Survive Bar

    Step out of Survive Bar into the alleyway to trigger an immediate fight against Akita and his Tokyo Omi Alliance subordinates. Because the alley is narrow, launch area-of-effect Fire or Electric skills early to wipe out the tightly clustered grunts, then finish off the leader.

    Key fight: Tokyo Omi Alliance (Survive Bar Alleyway)

    Tip: Blast the clustered group with Fire or Electric AoE skills right away to thin the pack.

  2. 2

    Cornering Kume at the Hakuryo Building

    Head north through Ijincho toward the Hakuryo Building near Part-time Hero HQ to pursue Kume. You will run into Muraoka and five guarding Omi grunts blocking the way, an encounter that gets interrupted by the arrival of Kazuma Kiryu once resolved.

    Key fight: Tokyo Omi Alliance (Hakuryo Building)

    Tip: Clear out the surrounding level 53 grunts with Fire AoE abilities before focusing your heavy single-target attacks on Muraoka.

  3. 3

    Boss Trial at Geomijul HQ

    Travel to Koreatown and visit Geomijul HQ, where you must confirm the prompt to enter before facing Kazuma Kiryu in a nighttime boss trial. Take advantage of Kiryu's refusal to attack women by deploying female party members like an Idol Saeko for risk-free healing, and bombard his Ice weakness with skills like Sprechgesang while avoiding physical strikes during his Rush counters, Beast furniture throws, or Dragon of Dojima stance. Defeating him rewards you with the Dragon Binding and Empty Cough Drop Tin before story scenes unfold in the Monitor Room.

    Key fight: Kazuma Kiryu

    Tip: Apply Peerless Resolve on Kasuga early to ensure a sudden high-damage Heat attack does not cause an instant game over.

    Watch out: Attacking Kiryu with physical attacks while his Rush counter stance or sub-25% HP Dragon's Gaze is readied will trigger devastating counter damage.

  4. 4

    Breaching the Bar District Warehouse

    Cross Fukutoku Bridge back into the Bar District to find Reiji Ishioda's hideout, confirming the prompt outside when you are prepared to engage. Six Ishioda Family grunts guard the exterior entrance and must be eliminated with high-tier AoE attacks before you can step inside.

    Key fight: Ishioda Family Grunts (Exterior)

    Tip: Grouped foes can be rapidly cleared using your strongest multi-target skills.

    Watch out: Confirming the prompt locks you into the warehouse sequence, so visit Romance Workshop or Yi Xing Tang beforehand if you need gear upgrades or restocking.

  5. 5

    Clearing the Warehouse Anteroom

    Move through the interior anteroom of the warehouse, where another squad of five Ishioda Family grunts stands between you and the inner sanctum. Obliterate this defensive line with your top AoE abilities, and take time to restore your party's HP and MP before opening the inner office door.

    Key fight: Ishioda Family Grunts (Anteroom)

    Tip: Fully replenish your party's health and magic points prior to unlocking the inner sanctum door.

  6. 6

    Showdown with Ishioda and Mirror Face

    Step into the inner office to battle Reiji Ishioda alongside Mirror Face. Focus down Mirror Face first with Gun and Blade attacks while holding off strikes when his Surveillance stance is active, then exploit Ishioda's Fire, Ice, and Electric weaknesses with burst magic, knockdowns, or paralysis to avoid triggering his retaliation shots; taking Ishioda down awards the Jewel Bracelet and wraps up the chapter.

    Key fight: Reiji Ishioda & Mirror Face

    Tip: Use skills with knockdown or paralysis like the Enforcer's Paralysis Prongs on Ishioda to bypass his automatic counterattacks.

    Watch out: Hitting Mirror Face while Surveillance is active or striking Ishioda without knocking him down will provoke harsh counterattacks, especially below 50% HP when Ishioda buffs his attack and fires point-blank gunshots.

Key fights
Tokyo Omi Alliance (Survive Bar Alleyway)

Enemies: Akita (Lv. 52) + 5 Tokyo Omi grunts: Nitta, Kubo, Mori, Shintani, Honma (Lv. 51)

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire, Electric

Tactics: Enemies bunch together tightly in the narrow alley; wipe them out quickly using area-of-effect (AoE) elemental skills.

Tokyo Omi Alliance (Hakuryo Building)

Enemies: Muraoka (Lv. 54) + 5 Tokyo Omi grunts: Otsuki, Osada, Ohara, Hayashida, Yanagizawa (Lv. 53)

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire

Tactics: Use AoE fire abilities on the cluster of Lv. 53 grunts before focusing single-target heavy damage onto Muraoka.

Kazuma Kiryu

Enemies: Kazuma Kiryu (Lv. 57)

Threats: Kiryu never targets female party members. Brawler (100%–75% HP) uses heavy physical strikes. Rush (75%–50% HP) gains evasion and a counter stance against male physical attackers. Beast (50%–25% HP) uses heavy furniture attacks and is immune to grab attacks. Dragon of Dojima (Sub-25% HP) gains Dragon’s Gaze counter stance and executes high-damage Heat attacks.

Weaknesses and resistances: Ice / Cold (neutral to Electric; highly resists most physical types)

Tactics: Cast Kasuga’s Peerless Resolve early to protect against a sudden knockout. Run female party members (such as Saeko as an Idol) as safe, uninterrupted healers/debuffers. Exploit Host/Hostess ice skills (Sprechgesang, etc.) and avoid attacking physically while Dragon's Gaze or counters are readied.

Rewards: Dragon Binding, Empty Cough Drop Tin

Ishioda Family Grunts (Exterior)

Enemies: 6 Ishioda family members (Lv. 55)

Tactics: Clear grouped targets using high-tier AoE skills.

Ishioda Family Grunts (Anteroom)

Enemies: 5 Ishioda family members (Lv. 55)

Tactics: Clear grouped targets using high-tier AoE skills and recover HP/MP before opening the inner sanctum door.

Reiji Ishioda & Mirror Face

Enemies: Reiji Ishioda (Lv. 57), Mirror Face (Lv. 55)

Threats: Mirror Face uses Surveillance, preparing a severe knife counter against whoever strikes him before his next turn. Reiji Ishioda uses gun attacks that inflict Fear, and automatically counters attacks that do not knock him down. At 50% HP, Ishioda buffs attack power and enhances his counter to point-blank gunshots.

Weaknesses and resistances: Reiji Ishioda: Fire, Ice, Electric (resists physical/bullet). Mirror Face: Blade, Bullet.

Tactics: Focus down Mirror Face first with Joon-gi Han’s bullet skills or bladed attacks (Dealer, Bodyguard) while holding off attacks when Surveillance is active. Against Ishioda, avoid low-damage basic/chip attacks; rely on burst elemental magic, knock-down skills, or paralysis moves like Enforcer's Paralysis Prongs.

Rewards: Jewel Bracelet
