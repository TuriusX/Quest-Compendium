Shepard and squad board a disabled Collector cruiser to search for technical data and a way to navigate the Omega 4 Relay safely. Once inside the central terminal, EDI discovers the derelict condition is an ambush set by the Collectors. You must fight your way back out against waves of Collector forces and Praetorians before the ship's systems crush the Normandy's shuttle.

Don't miss 0/3

Enemies
Collector Trooper
Weak to Biotics, fire, Warp

Basic foot soldier vulnerable once its barrier is removed

Collector Guardian
Weak to Fire, Warp, Shield-draining powers

Deploys a mobile energy shield and has enhanced defenses compared to standard troopers

Collector Assassin
Weak to Sniper fire, Warp

High damage ranged threat armed with a particle beam or sniper rifle

Harbinger (Possessed Collector)
Weak to Warp, Incinerate, heavy weapons

Constantly possesses regular Collectors, regenerating barriers and hurling biotic blasts

Scion
Weak to Concentrated heavy weapon fire, armor-piercing rounds, Warp

Tanky mutated enemy firing long-range shockwaves that flush you from cover

Praetorian
Weak to Heavy weapons, armor-piercing ammo, Warp, Incinerate

Flying behemoth surrounded by armor and barriers that charges and executes an area-of-effect ground slam

Abomination
Weak to Headshots, long-range fire, Cryo

Melee husks that sprint toward the squad and explode violently upon death

Tips
  • On the floating platform ambush, focus down the Scion or possessed Collector as fast as possible to avoid being overwhelmed by area-of-effect stagger attacks.
  • When fighting the Praetorian, constantly rotate around the central structural columns backwards to maintain distance and break its line of sight during laser attacks.
  • Infiltrators and Soldiers should strongly consider taking the Widow sniper rifle at the weapon station, while Vanguards benefit greatly from the Claymore shotgun.
