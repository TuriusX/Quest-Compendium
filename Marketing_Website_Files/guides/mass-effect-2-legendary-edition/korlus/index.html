Korlus

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · During Grunt's recruitment

Shepard travels to the junk planet Korlus to track down a warlord named Okeer and recruit a krogan for the team. You must fight through the Blue Suns mercenary group and defective krogan clones through wrecked starships. The mission ends with a battle against the mercenary leader Jedore and retrieving an intact tank-bred krogan.

Items

  • Sniper Rifle Damage MISSABLE: Researched at a terminal inside a shipping container shortly after meeting the wounded mercenary
  • Krogan Vitality MISSABLE: Researched at a terminal located in Okeer's laboratory
  • Heavy Weapon Ammo MISSABLE: Scanned from a PDA on the floor in the room just before exiting to the final landing pad

Tips

  • Keep your distance from charging Krogan Berserkers and focus on burning down their armor to stop their health regeneration.
  • In the final arena, prioritize eliminating the YMIR Mech while using the central structure to break line of sight with Jedore's rocket attacks.
