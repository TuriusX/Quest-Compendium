Omega

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · Recruiting Archangel and Mordin

Commander Shepard travels to the lawless asteroid station Omega to recruit the brilliant salarian scientist Dr. Mordin Solus and the vigilante known as Archangel. Shepard must navigate a quarantined district ravaged by a deadly plague to reach Mordin's clinic, and then infiltrate a mercenary strike team operating in the lower wards to reach Archangel's fortified stronghold. Completing both recruitments secures essential squadmates, unlocks the Normandy's main research terminal, and opens up the rest of the galaxy.

Items

  • M-15 Vindicator Assault Rifle MISSABLE: On a couch in Archangel's stronghold, right near where Garrus is positioned
  • M-9 Tempest Submachine Gun MISSABLE: In the clinic area during Mordin's recruitment mission, lying on a table in the mercenary-held rooms
  • Assault Rifle Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: Downloaded from a wall terminal inside Archangel's base
  • Heavy Weapon Ammo Upgrade MISSABLE: Hacked from a computer terminal in the plague district near Mordin's clinic
  • Submachine Gun Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: Found on a datapad inside the mercenary quarters during the Archangel recruitment line

Secrets

  • You can sabotage the Blue Suns heavy gunship before the Archangel siege begins by speaking with the mechanic repairing it and choosing the Renegade interrupt.
  • Siding with or sparing Patriarch by convincing him to run or helping him face assassins resolves his feud with Aria without directly fighting the station's former ruler yourself.

Shops and people

  • Harrot's Emporium: Upgrades, armor pieces, and general merchandise run by an elitist Elcor merchant
  • Omega Market (Kenia): Starship fuel, probe bundles, and minor station equipment
  • Morlan's Famous Shop: Medi-gel capacity upgrades, armor pieces, and assorted tech items

Tips

  • Take the Renegade interrupt to zap the Blue Suns mercenary gunship mechanic with a datapad to cut the gunship's health in half for the upcoming boss fight.
  • Bring squadmates with Incinerate, Warp, or Incendiary Ammo to both missions to counter Vorcha health regeneration and burn through Blood Pack armor.
  • Close the blast doors in Archangel's basement quickly before enemy reinforcements swarm your flanks and overwhelm your position.
  • Speak with Aria T'Loak at Afterlife before exploring Omega to gain vital mission leads, local lore, and access to secure areas.
