Horizon

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · First Collector attack

Commander Shepard investigates the human colony Horizon, which has gone completely silent following an assault by the Collectors. The squad must fight through waves of Collector forces and Husks to reach the colony's defensive space cannon and drive off the Collector cruiser. After activating the defense grid and clearing the final landing pad, Shepard has a tense reunion with an old squadmate from the first game.

Items

  • Heavy Weapon Ammo MISSABLE: In a datapad inside the first colony building Shepard breaches
  • M-443 Particle Beam MISSABLE: Found lying on the ground near an inactive defense turret inside the settlement
  • Biotic Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: In a research terminal inside the quarantine lab building before reaching the space cannon
  • Heavy Skin Weave Upgrade MISSABLE: In a research terminal located near the transmitter array where Mechanic Delan is hiding

Tips

  • Take out Husks quickly with Area of Effect powers or knockbacks, as removing their armor makes them instantly susceptible to instant-kill gravity effects like Pull.
  • When fighting the Praetorian at the defense cannon, kite it continuously around the central platform structure to break its line of sight and avoid its beam attack.
  • Destroy regular Collectors before the Collector General possesses them, or focus fire on Harbinger immediately with heavy weapons to halt his aggressive advance.
