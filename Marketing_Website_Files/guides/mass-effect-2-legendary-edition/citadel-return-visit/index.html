Citadel (Return visit)

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · Return visit for side missions

The Citadel can be revisited throughout the game to wrap up side assignments and progress squad loyalty missions, primarily involving Thane Krios and Garrus Vakarian. You will navigate Zakera Ward across Levels 26, 27, and 28 to interact with contacts, deliver requested items, and resolve ongoing civilian disputes. There is no direct combat in the public wards, so focus on dialogue, purchases, and tracking down key objectives.

Items

  • Krogan Sushi Datapad: On the ground near the fish merchant on Zakera Ward Level 28
  • False Positive ID MISSABLE: Found during Garrus's loyalty mission or on Level 27 near the transport terminal

Shops and people

  • Rodam Expeditions: Heavy weapon ammo upgrades, sniper rifle damage upgrades, and heavy pistol damage upgrades
  • Saronis Applications: Tech damage upgrades and terminal bypass modules
  • Citadel Souvenirs: Space Hamster, model ships for the Normandy, and decorative items
  • Zakera Cafe: High-grade ingredients needed for the Normandy's Mess Sergeant Gardner

Tips

  • Talk to the shopkeepers before buying items to ask for a discount, which can be secured using Paragon or Renegade persuasion options.
  • You must leave the Citadel and return later to check in on the Krogan looking for fish in the Presidium to conclude his running joke encounter.
