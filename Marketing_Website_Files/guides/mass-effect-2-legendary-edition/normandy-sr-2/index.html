Normandy SR-2

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · Throughout the game

The Normandy SR-2 serves as Commander Shepard's mobile base of operations, crew quarters, and starship throughout the campaign. Between missions, you can interact with squadmates, conduct research upgrades, manage loadouts, check private terminals, and plot star chart navigation from here.

Items

  • Space Hamster: Illium / Citadel pet stores, kept in Shepard's Cabin on Deck 1
  • Model Ship - Normandy SR-1: Various Citadel / Omega vendors, displayed in Shepard's Cabin on Deck 1
  • Prothean Relic: Shepard's Cabin desk (received if the Firewalker DLC mission is completed)

Secrets

  • Feeding the aquarium fish in Shepard's Cabin after every single mission keeps them alive; if Kelly Chambers is romanced or befriended enough, she will offer to feed them for you.
  • Interacting with the Space Hamster repeatedly in Shepard's Cabin causes it to peek out of its cage and emit a high-pitched squeak.

Shops and people

  • Kelly Chambers: Ship updates, feeding Shepard's fish, and alerting Shepard when squadmates want to speak
  • Mordin Solus (Tech Lab): Research Terminal providing ship upgrades, squad upgrades, and weapon augmentations using harvested resources

Tips

  • Visit every squadmate across all four decks after completing main story missions and recruitment tasks, as new dialogue branches, upgrades, and loyalty missions unlock as the story progresses.
  • Prioritize purchasing ship upgrades suggested by squadmates via the Tech Lab terminal (Tali's Shields, Garrus's Thanix Cannon, and Jacob's Heavy Ship Armor) to ensure everyone survives the final mission approach.
  • Use the Advanced Training option at the Tech Lab research terminal to gain a bonus combat power unlocked from loyal squadmates.
  • Check Shepard's private terminal frequently on the Combat Information Center (CIC) deck to read messages that grant credits, unlock planetary coordinates, and advance personal subplots.
