Purgatory

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · During Jack's recruitment

Commander Shepard visits the prison ship Purgatory to purchase the release of the biotic criminal Subject Zero, also known as Jack. Warden Kuril betrays Shepard in an attempt to capture them instead, prompting Jack to break free and rampage through the facility. Shepard must fight through the prison security forces, reach the docking bay, and defeat Kuril to successfully recruit Jack.

Items

  • Heavy Weapon Ammo MISSABLE: Next to the door leading into the first cell block after Kuril springs his trap
  • Shotgun Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: On a terminal in the security control room overlooking the cryo bays
  • Credits MISSABLE: Wall safe in the security office near the beginning of the prison section

Tips

  • Shoot the three power conduits powering Warden Kuril's protective energy field before attempting to damage him directly.
  • Bring squadmates with abilities tailored for crowd control and anti-shield or anti-armor damage, such as Overload and Warp.
  • Keep your distance from FENRIS and YMIR mechs to avoid lethal explosion damage upon their destruction.
