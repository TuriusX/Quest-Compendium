Citadel

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · First visit after Omega

After leaving Omega, Commander Shepard visits the Citadel's Zakera Ward to seek an audience with the Council and gather supplies. Players explore Levels 26, 27, and 28 to browse commercial shops, recruit potential allies, and acquire weapon and ship upgrades. The area is a non-combat hub focused on conversations, shopping discounts, and picking up secondary assignments.

Shops and people

  • Ratch's Wares: Sells Krogan Vitality upgrade, Focuser (armor piece), and Pyjak Meat on Zakera Ward Level 26
  • Saronis Applications: Sells Tech Damage upgrade and Asari-made armor components on Zakera Ward Level 26
  • Sirta Supplies: Sells Medi-Gel Capacity upgrade and Life Support webbing armor on Zakera Ward Level 26
  • Rodam Expeditions: Sells Heavy Pistol Damage and Sniper Rifle Damage upgrades on Zakera Ward Level 27
  • Zakera Cafe: Sells High-Grade Provisions required for the Normandy's mess sergeant on Zakera Ward Level 27
  • Harrot's Emporium: Sells Visor (armor piece) and Heavy Weapon Ammo upgrade on Zakera Ward Level 28

Tips

  • Talk to shopkeepers and use Charm or Intimidate dialogue options to secure permanent discounts before buying expensive upgrades.
  • Forgis the Bartender at the Dark Star Lounge serves Shepard a drink that knocks them out, waking them up near the restrooms.
  • You can purchase Pyjak Meat from Ratch's Wares to lure and kill troublesome varren at the nearby game terminal.
