Lazarus Station

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · Prologue mission

Commander Shepard awakens inside the Cerberus research facility Lazarus Station during an attack by security mechs. You must equip yourself, regroup with Cerberus operatives Jacob Taylor and Miranda Lawson, and fight through the base to escape on a shuttle.

Items

  • M-3 Predator Heavy Pistol: Found on a crate immediately after waking up and leaving your medical bay room
  • Medi-gel MISSABLE: In a medical station on the wall in the corridor shortly after picking up the heavy pistol
  • M-4 Shuriken Machine Pistol: Placed on a desk inside the first office area where you encounter active combat mechs
  • Wall Safe Credits MISSABLE: A hackable wall safe located in the office room shortly before meeting Jacob
  • Datapad Credits MISSABLE: Found on a desk in the server room shortly after joining up with Jacob Taylor
  • Wall Safe Credits MISSABLE: Found inside the room where you meet the Cerberus operative Wilson
  • Medi-gel MISSABLE: Inside the medical station on the wall next to Wilson when you first find him injured

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
LOKI MechShields, Overload, Rapid weapon fireFragile security androids that advance toward your position and fire basic submachine guns

Tips

  • Destroying a LOKI Mech with a headshot will cause its power core to overload, triggering an explosion that damages nearby enemies.
  • Use Jacob's Pull power against unshielded mechs to lift them into the air, completely disabling them and leaving them vulnerable to gun fire.
  • Be sure to hack both wall safes and retrieve datapad credits before speaking to Miranda and boarding the shuttle, as you cannot return to Lazarus Station once you leave.
