Illium (Return visit)

Mass Effect 2 (Legendary Edition) guide · Return visit for loyalty missions

Shepard returns to Illium to pursue the loyalty missions for crew members Miranda Lawson and Thane Krios. The hub serves as the staging ground to track down Miranda's informant Lanteia and hunt down Thane's estranged son Kolyat. Completing these personal requests secures the squadmates' focus and loyalty ahead of the suicide mission.

Items

  • Submachine Gun Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: Hackable terminal located in the cargo transfer area during Miranda's loyalty mission
  • Heavy Pistol Damage Upgrade MISSABLE: Terminal near the VIP lounge offices during Thane's loyalty mission
  • Medi-Gel Capacity Upgrade MISSABLE: Medical terminal in the transit terminal during Miranda's loyalty mission

Tips

  • During Thane's mission in the catwalks, keep visual contact on Talid constantly and immediately update Thane through the comms before the target timer expires.
  • Bring squadmates specializing in Warp and Overload to strip down the combined Shields and Barriers favored by Eclipse mercenaries.
  • Taking the Renegade interrupt during the confrontation with Lanteia allows you to eliminate several Eclipse ambushers before the firefight begins.
