The Rice Fields

Gothic 1 Remake guide · Exploring the outskirts of the New Camp
← All areas in this guide

The Rice Fields are an agricultural area situated directly outside the entrance to the New Camp, serving as a primary source of food and moonshine production. It is worked by oppressed peasants who harvest the paddies while Rice Lord's enforcers patrol the perimeter.

Enemies
Bloodfly

Aggressive flying insect frequently encountered around the damp outskirts and water bodies near the fields.

Scavenger

Flightless predatory bird roaming the terrain and grassy approaches leading up to the paddies.

Lurker

Amphibious creature dwelling near the waters and riverbanks bordering the farmland.

Tips
  • Avoid picking fights with Lefty and the other enforcers early on until your character has obtained proper armor and basic combat training.
