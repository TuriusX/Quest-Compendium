Agrabah

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit to the world
← All areas in this guide

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive in Agrabah to reunite with Aladdin and Jasmine, only to find the city troubled by Heartless and Pete scheming with Jafar's lamp. Sora journeys into the desert to the Cave of Wonders to foil Pete's plot and stop the rampaging elemental Heartless. After clearing the trials of the cave and defeating the twin bosses Volcanic Lord and Blizzard Lord, the city is temporarily saved.

Enemies
Fortuneteller
Weak to Ice

Floats in the air casting ice and fire magic; can be knocked down with aerial combos.

Silver Rock

Small Heartless that bounces around and explodes in a burst of light; defeat quickly before it detonates.

Fat Bandit

Immune to frontal physical attacks while guarding; hit it from behind or deflect its fireball attack.

Luna Bandit

Agile Heartless wielding dual scimitars; frequently leaps around and performs spinning slash attacks.

Volcanic Lord
Weak to Ice

Elemental boss fought alongside Blizzard Lord; attack it with Blizzard magic to deal heavy damage.

Blizzard Lord
Weak to Fire

Elemental boss fought alongside Volcanic Lord; attack it with Fire magic to quickly deplete its health.

Shops and people
  • Peddler's Shop: Potions, Ethers, Tent, and basic accessory and armor equipment

Tips
  • When fighting the Fat Bandit, wait for it to shoot a fireball and use Guard to deflect it back to stun the enemy.
  • Equip Blizzard magic on a shortcut to target the Volcanic Lord, and Fire magic to target the Blizzard Lord during the final boss battle.
  • You can use the reaction command "Air Siphon" when near the Chasm of Challenges elevator or certain puzzle devices to quickly traverse heights or trigger mechanisms.

Not listed for this area: secrets.
