Halloween Town

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit to the world

Sora, Donald, and Goofy reunite with Jack Skellington, who is acting as Santa's bodyguard while awaiting an assignment from Christmas Town. After following Jack through the tree door into Christmas Town, they track down Lock, Shock, and Barrel, who have stolen Santa's presents at Maleficent's behest. The visit concludes with an escort quest to guide Sally to Santa and a boss battle against the resurrected Prison Keeper, followed by the toy monster Oogie Boogie.

Items

  • Navigational Map: In a treasure chest located in the Guillotine Square area
  • Christmas Town Map: In a treasure chest found near the entrance of Christmas Town

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Prison KeeperBoss fought in Guillotine Square that periodically swallows Lock, Shock, or Barrel to gain different attack patterns.
Oogie BoogieBoss fought in the Toy Factory conveyor area where Sora must use Reaction Commands on the factory switches to raise Oogie's platform and knock him down.
Wight KnightAgile Heartless that leaps around and attacks with claw swipes.
Toy SoldierHoliday-themed Heartless that wields firearms and a bayonet to shoot and march into Sora.

Tips

  • Equipping Jack Skellington in your active party unlocks his Limit attack, "Applause, Applause," which is very effective for crowd control in the narrow streets.
  • During the Prison Keeper boss fight, use the "Inside-Out" Reaction Command when prompted to force the boss to spit out whichever trick-or-treater it has swallowed.
