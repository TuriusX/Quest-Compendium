Hollow Bastion

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive at Hollow Bastion, reuniting with Leon, Yuffie, Aerith, and Cid as part of the Hollow Bastion Restoration Committee. After an ambush in the marketplace and learning about the town's defensive state, the party heads to Merlin's House where they obtain the Baseball Charm and the ability to summon Chicken Little. Merlin also grants Sora access to the 100 Acre Wood through the magic storybook, opening up the world map to allow exploration of other worlds.

Items

  • AP Boost: Marketplace, inside a chest on the elevated ledge
  • Hi-Potion: Marketplace, inside a chest near the stairs leading toward Merlin's House
  • Mythril Shard: Marketplace, in a chest near the entrance
  • Tent: Merlin's House, inside a chest by the corner
  • Baseball Charm: Merlin's House, received from Merlin during the story cutscene
  • 100 Acre Wood Book: Merlin's House, received automatically from Merlin to access Winnie the Pooh's world

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
ShadowFireSmall, basic Heartless that can melt into the floor to avoid incoming attacks.
SoldierBasic Heartless that attacks with physical claw swipes and spinning charges.
DuskBasic Nobody that shifts its body to evade attacks and retaliates with twisting whip motions.

Tips

  • You will encounter both Heartless and Nobodies during this visit; remember to use the "Sliding Dash" or "Reversal" Reaction Command when facing Dusks to stun them and get behind them safely.
