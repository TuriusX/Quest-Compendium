The World That Never Was

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's final journey

Sora and his companions arrive at the Organization XIII stronghold to rescue Kairi and Riku and put an end to Xemnas's plans. You must fight your way through the neon-lit city streets and ascend the Castle That Never Was, defeating the remaining Organization members along the way. The journey culminates in a multi-stage final battle against Xemnas to save the worlds.

Items

  • Mythril Stone: Fragment Crossing, inside a treasure chest on the street level
  • Mythril Crystal: Memory's Skyscraper, inside a treasure chest
  • Two Become One: Memory's Skyscraper, obtained automatically after defeating Roxas
  • Oblivion: Proof of Existence, obtained via a cutscene before entering the upper castle
  • Cosmic Belt: Nothing's Call, inside a treasure chest on the walkway

Secrets

  • The Way to the Garden entrance is revealed in the Proof of Existence room after defeating the required Organization bosses, opening the path to the Garden of Assemblage where data rematches await.

XemnasThe final boss; the battle spans several phases, ending in an armored form and a two-player sequence with Sora and Riku.

Shops and people

  • Moogle: Sells basic recovery items, synthesis services, and gear upgrades

Tips

  • Equipping Two Become One forces Sora into either Final Form or Anti-Form, making it the most efficient Keyblade to use for unlocking Final Form before the final encounters.
  • In the final phase against Xemnas, mash both the attack button (Sora) and the Reaction Command button (Riku) simultaneously during the laser dome attack to block all incoming beams.
