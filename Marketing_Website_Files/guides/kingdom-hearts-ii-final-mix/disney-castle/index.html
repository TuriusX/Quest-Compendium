Disney Castle

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive at Disney Castle after receiving word of trouble, only to discover the realm has been invaded by Heartless. Queen Minnie tasks Sora with escorting her safely through the Audience Chamber to the Throne Room to investigate the source of the corruption. Once at the throne, you access the cornerstone chamber and find the dark door leading into the Timeless River world.

Items

  • Mythril Shard: Courtyard, inside a treasure chest located along the perimeter path
  • Star Recipe: Courtyard, inside a treasure chest on the elevated hedges
  • AP Boost: Courtyard, inside a treasure chest tucked near the hedge maze area
  • Mythril Stone: Courtyard, inside a treasure chest near the garden area
  • Blizzaga Stone: Courtyard, inside a treasure chest on a high ledge accessible via hedges
  • Torn Pages: Library, inside a treasure chest
  • Disney Castle Map: Gummi Hangar, inside a treasure chest near the save point

Enemies

EnemyNotes
ShadowBasic Heartless that sinks into the floor to become invulnerable.
SoldierBasic ground Heartless that attacks with kicks and spinning lunges.
Minute BurglarsSmall, agile Heartless that drop Munny and leap around the area.
Bolt TowerTall stationary Heartless that fires electricity and grabs Sora; use Reaction Commands to counter.

Tips

  • During Queen Minnie's escort sequence in the Audience Chamber, stay close to her and use her "Faith" Reaction Command to blast away groups of Heartless quickly.
  • Queen Minnie's "Call" Reaction Command can be used when she is separated from you to direct her back toward Sora's position.
  • Don't miss the Torn Pages chest in the Library, as it is needed to unlock portions of the 100 Acre Wood book.
