Beast's Castle

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive at Beast's Castle to find the Beast acting aggressive and hostile toward his staff. To uncover the reason behind his anger, Sora explores the castle, helps Belle and the enchanted servants, and restores Beast's senses. The visit concludes with a battle against the Heartless Thresholder and the possessed Shadow Stalker/Dark Thorn in the Ballroom.

Items

  • AP Boost: Chest located in the Courtyard
  • Hi-Potion: Chest located in the Parlor
  • Tent: Chest in Belle's Room
  • Mega-Potion: Chest in the West Hall
  • Mythril Shard: Chest located in the Secret Passage
  • Mythril Stone: Chest located in the Dungeon
  • Mega-Ether: Chest in the West Wing

Enemies

EnemyNotes
ShadowBasic Heartless that sinks into the floor to avoid attacks.
Large BodyHeavy Heartless immune to frontal physical attacks; strike it from behind or deflect its charges.
Hook BatAerial Heartless that swoops down; can be caught and swung using a Reaction Command.
Gargoyle KnightStone warrior that attacks with a large sword; can turn into an invulnerable statue.
Gargoyle WarriorAirborne stone gargoyle that attacks from above; also turns into stone periodically.
ThresholderLiving door Heartless guarding the Dungeon; must be attacked to force open its mouth and release the Possessor.
Dark ThornAgile boss fought in the Ballroom that can turn invisible and swing from the chandelier.

Tips

  • During the Thresholder fight, use the "Release" Reaction Command when the door's core is exposed to stun it.
  • Against Dark Thorn, use the Reaction Command "Sliding Dash" and follow-ups when prompted to knock it out of invisibility and drop the chandelier on it.
  • Use Cogsworth's Lantern to melt the ice blocks blocking paths in the Secret Passage and Dungeon.
  • When Beast is enraged in his room, use the "Charge" and "Wake Up" Reaction Commands to snap him out of his trance.
