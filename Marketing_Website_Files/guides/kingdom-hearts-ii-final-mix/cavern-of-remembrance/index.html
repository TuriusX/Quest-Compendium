Cavern of Remembrance

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's second visit to Hollow Bastion

The Cavern of Remembrance is an optional endgame dungeon introduced in the Final Mix version, accessible via a hidden passage in Hollow Bastion's Postern. It features high-level variants of Heartless and Nobodies and requires high-level Drive Form Growth abilities to traverse its various physical obstacles. Reaching the deepest chamber, the Garden of Assemblage, unlocks portal battles against powerful data recreations of all thirteen Organization XIII members.

Items

  • AP Up: Cavern of Remembrance: Depths, inside a treasure chest on an elevated platform
  • Remembrance Gem: Cavern of Remembrance: Mineshaft, inside a treasure chest along the ascending path
  • Manifest Illusion: Cavern of Remembrance: Mining Area, inside a treasure chest reachable via Glide
  • Non-existence Hint: Garden of Assemblage, inside a treasure chest near the data portals

Tips

  • Maximize all Drive Form Growth abilities—High Jump, Quick Run, Dodge Roll, and Aerial Dodge—before attempting to fully navigate the cavern, as several gaps and ledges cannot be cleared without them.
  • Equipping Magnet and Thunder spells makes clearing the mandatory multi-wave battle rooms inside the Transport to Remembrance significantly more manageable.
  • Drive Forms cannot be used in the engine room steam-vent sections if an anti-form or restriction occurs, so practice clearing the platforming obstacles strictly with standard mobility abilities.
