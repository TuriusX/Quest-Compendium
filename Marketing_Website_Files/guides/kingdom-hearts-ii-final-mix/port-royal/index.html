Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive at the harbor town of Port Royal and join forces with Captain Jack Sparrow and Will Turner. They set sail on the Black Pearl to rescue Elizabeth Swann and break the cursed Aztec gold's power from Captain Barbossa's pirate crew. Sora must chase the undead pirates across the sea, defend the ships, and defeat Barbossa in the depths of the Isla de Muerta.

Enemies
Undead Pirate
Weak to Fire, Blizzard, Thunder (varies by weapon type)

Immune to all damage when in moonlight, so lure them into the dark to make them vulnerable.

Air Pirate

An aerial Heartless that swoops down to attack and can be knocked down with aerial combos.

Captain Barbossa

Accompanied by the Illuminator Heartless; defeat the Illuminator first to restore full visibility and keep moonlight from protecting Barbossa.

Tips
  • Undead Pirates cannot be damaged while standing in moonlight; draw them into the shadows or blast them with magic that disrupts their state before attacking.
  • During the cannon defense sequence on the Interceptor, prioritize clearing explosive barrels dropped onto the deck before they detonate.
