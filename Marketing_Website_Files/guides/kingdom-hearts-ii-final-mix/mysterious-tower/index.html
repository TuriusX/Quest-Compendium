Mysterious Tower

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive at the Mysterious Tower by train and meet up with Pete and several Heartless outside the tower. After ascending the staircases to Master Yen Sid's study, Sora learns about the threat of Organization XIII and receives new clothes along with the ability to use Drive Forms. Completing the visit grants Sora the Star Seeker Keyblade and opens up the Gummi Ship routes to other worlds.

Items

  • Potion: Chest located outside on the path near the train entrance
  • Hi-Potion: Chest located on the path outside the tower
  • Ether: Chest located on the outside path leading to the tower entrance
  • Ether: Chest inside the tower along the Wayward Stairs
  • Mythril Shard: Chest located on the Wayward Stairs
  • Mythril Stone: Chest located on the Wayward Stairs
  • Tower Map: Large chest inside Master Yen Sid's study
  • Star Seeker: Reward from Yen Sid after completing the wardrobe cutscene
  • Valor Form: Granted by the fairies Flora, Fauna, and Merryweather alongside Sora's new clothes

Enemies

EnemyNotes
ShadowBasic Pureblood Heartless that can sink into the floor to become invulnerable.
SoldierAgile Emblem Heartless that attacks with kicks and spinning spin dashes.

Tips

  • Reading the red book on Yen Sid's desk allows you to review the story prologue and basics of the game's lore.
  • Valor Form requires 3 Drive Gauges to activate and temporarily removes Donald from the party to let Sora dual-wield Keyblades.
