Twilight Town

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Roxas's prologue
← All areas in this guide

Roxas spends his final summer vacation days running errands and investigating supernatural occurrences with his friends in Twilight Town. He eventually uncovers the truth about his existence, the simulated town, and his connection to Sora. The player must complete daily tasks, minigames, and battles with Dusks and Axel to progress the story until Sora awakens.

Don't miss 0/3

Enemies
Dusk

Basic Nobody enemy that slithers along the ground and uses the Reversal reaction command to dodge and counter.

Creeper

Small Nobody capable of transforming its shape into weapons to attack.

Axel

Boss fought with Dual Wield Keyblades in the final stages of the prologue inside the simulated Old Mansion basement.

Twilight Thorn

Giant Nobody boss fought inside the Station of Awakening that requires multiple reaction commands like Break Raid to defeat.

Tips
  • Completing the Grandstander or Mail Delivery odd jobs on Day 2 is generally the fastest way to earn the munny required to advance the story.
  • Winning the struggle tournament matches against Hayner, Vivi, and Setzer awards Roxas the Champion Belt accessory.
  • Spamming the Reversal reaction command against Dusks makes Roxas completely invincible while sliding behind them for an easy opening.
