Olympus Coliseum

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit

Sora, Donald, and Goofy visit the Underworld to find Hercules, who has grown weary, and end up running afoul of Hades. After breaking an Underworld curse with the help of Auron, they rescue Megara and protect the Coliseum from an attack. To clear the visit, the party must fight their way through the Underworld, complete Phil's urn-breaking mini-game, and defeat Demyx's water clones and Pete.

Items

  • Mythril Shard: Chest in the Cave of the Dead: Entrance
  • AP Boost: Chest in the Cave of the Dead: Valley of the Dead
  • Mythril Stone: Chest in the Underworld Caverns: Atrium

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

PeteDisney villain who uses a protective barrier, throws explosive bowling-ball-style shockwaves, and must be defeated to complete the visit.

Shops and people

  • Moogle Shop: Sells consumable recovery items such as Potions and Ethers, and offers item synthesis services.

Tips

  • Inside the Underworld prior to obtaining the Olympus Stone, Sora is afflicted with an Underworld curse that drains HP, prevents Drive Forms, and restricts MP recovery.
  • Use the "Free-Finish" reaction command against Large Bodies to quickly stun them and deal massive damage from behind.
  • Equipping Fire magic is effective against Demyx's water clones to clear them out quickly and beat the countdown timer.
