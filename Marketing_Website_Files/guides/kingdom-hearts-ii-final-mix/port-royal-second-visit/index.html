Port Royal (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's second visit to the world

Sora and his companions return to Port Royal to investigate a cursed Grim Reaper Nobodies crisis connected to the stolen Aztec gold medallions. Jack Sparrow rejoins the party to board the Black Pearl, pursue the ship of the cursed pirates, and retrieve the medallion pieces to lift the curse. The visit culminates in a boss fight against the Grim Reaper at the Port Royal harbor, requiring the use of medallions to render it vulnerable.

Items

  • AP Boost: Harbor, chest near the dock warehouse
  • Mythril Shard: Town, inside a chest in the town square
  • Mythril Gem: Isla de Muerta: Rock Face, chest in the cavern path
  • Orichalcum: Ship Graveyard: Seadrift Row, inside a chest aboard the ruined wreckage
  • King's Recipe: Ship Graveyard: The Interceptor's Hold, inside a treasure chest

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Grim ReaperMagic (Fire/Blizzard/Thunder to stagger while vulnerable)Boss fought twice during this visit; steals the Aztec medallion to become invincible until hit with dropped medallions.
GamblerUses mini-game reaction commands like "Stop Dice" and "Game"; failing causes Sora to turn into a card or dice.
Air PirateAerial Heartless that swoops down with sword attacks and resists physical knockback mid-combo.
Cannon GunHeartless that shoots projectile bombs from afar and swings its cannon barrel at close range.

Tips

  • During the second Grim Reaper fight, use Jack Sparrow's "Bone Smash" reaction command or cast magic to force the boss to drop stolen Aztec medallions, then interact with the chest to return them.
  • When Gamblers initiate their reaction command mini-games, quickly select "Stop Dice" or "Game" (O) instead of (X) to win prizes and avoid being transformed.
  • Blizzard magic is effective for quickly freezing and interrupting crowds of Pirate Nobodies and Heartless during the skirmishes on board the ship.
