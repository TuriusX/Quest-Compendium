Pride Lands

KINGDOM HEARTS II FINAL MIX guide · Sora's first visit to the world

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive transformed into animals and team up with Nala to locate Simba, the rightful king of the Pride Lands. After tracking Simba down to the Oasis and helping him overcome his guilt, the party returns to Pride Rock to challenge Scar and restore the balance of the Circle of Life.

Items

  • Energy Stone: Pride Rock (inside the King's Den chest)
  • AP Boost: The Savannah (chest in the wide grassy plains)
  • Mythril Gem: Elephant Graveyard (chest in the area)
  • Torn Pages: Oasis (chest near the waterfall)

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Living BoneA large skeletal quadruped Heartless that can carry a shaman rider; hitting the rider first helps disorient it.
Rapid ThrusterSmall flying Heartless that gather in massive swarms; easily dispatched with wide-range attacks or Magnet magic.
Shenzi, Banzai, and EdFireHyena trio fought as a boss encounter; focus on one hyena at a time to reduce incoming attacks.
ScarFireAgile boss who uses dark lightning and swift claw strikes; utilizing the Dash ability helps evade his sweeping rush attacks.

Tips

  • Sora's Lion Form replaces normal Drive Forms with boosted ground mobility and unique combat abilities like Dash, making traversing wide areas much faster.
  • Using the Magnet spell against large groups of Rapid Thrusters will instantly cluster and defeat them, generating large amounts of HP and MP orbs.
