Marukyu Striptease

Persona 4 Golden guide · Rise's dungeon

The Marukyu Striptease is the third major Midnight Channel dungeon, manifested from Rise Kujikawa's inner anxieties about her idol career and public image. The Investigation Team must navigate through its neon club-themed floors to save Rise before the fog sets in. Reaching the end triggers a dramatic narrative sequence featuring back-to-back boss battles against Shadow Rise and Shadow Teddie.

Tips

  • Bring Personas that can cast Mediarama and have party members equipped to heal or prevent silence, as Shadow Teddie uses heavy ice and status-inflicting attacks.
  • You do not need to reduce Shadow Rise's HP to zero; the fight automatically transitions into a cutscene after she drops below a certain health threshold and scans the party.
  • Guard immediately with all characters whenever Shadow Teddie spends a turn using "Mind Charge" followed by "Ultra Charge," as his subsequent "Nihil Hand" deals lethal damage if unblocked.
  • Return to the top floor after completing the dungeon to defeat the optional boss, Amorous Snake, which leaves behind a powerful weapon for Teddie on the floor.
