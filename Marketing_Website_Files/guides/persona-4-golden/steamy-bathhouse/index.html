Steamy Bathhouse

Persona 4 Golden guide · Strategy guide for the second dungeon and Shadow Kanji boss fight

The Steamy Bathhouse is the second major dungeon in the TV World, unlocked after gathering information around Inaba about Kanji Tatsumi. You must ascend through 11 floors, navigating steam-filled corridors and locked doors, to reach the top. Defeating Shadow Kanji at the end rescues Kanji and adds him to the Investigation Team.

Deadlines

  • June 4 | Clear the Steamy Bathhouse and defeat Shadow Kanji before consecutive days of rain trigger a Game Over.

Social links

  • Chie Satonaka (Chariot): Available on the school rooftop or Central Shopping District on certain days; spending time increases physical damage output and unlocks follow-up attacks.
  • Yukiko Amagi (Priestess): Unlocked in early May; spend time with her on the school 1F hallway or shopping district to strengthen her healing and fire capabilities.
  • Yosuke Hanamura (Magician): Available on the school 2F hallway or Junes to boost his Wind magic and cooperative combat abilities.

Worth doing

  • Read books in your room at night to raise social stats such as Courage, Knowledge, or Diligence.
  • Work part-time jobs available at the Town Bulletin Board in Northern Shopping District to earn money and raise social stats.
  • Tend to the garden outside the Dojima Residence once vegetable seedlings become available to harvest restoration items.

Items

  • Bathhouse Key: Defeat the Daring Gigas mini-boss on Floor 7

Tips

  • Bring Persona affinities or accessories that resist or nullify Electricity, as Shadow Kanji frequently casts Swift Strike and Mazio/Zionga.
  • Take out Nice Guy before attacking Shadow Kanji, as Nice Guy constantly heals and buffs the boss.
  • Use Red Wall on Chie or have her guard often during the boss fight, because she is weak to Electricity and will grant the boss extra turns if struck.
  • Return to Floor 11 after clearing the dungeon on a later date to defeat an optional boss, Intolerant Officer, to acquire Kanji's weapon, the Iron Plate, and boost your Courage.
