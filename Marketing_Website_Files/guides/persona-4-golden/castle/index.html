Castle

Persona 4 Golden guide · Yukiko's dungeon

Yukiko's Castle is the first major dungeon in the TV World, where the Investigation Team ventures to rescue Yukiko Amagi before the fog sets in. Players must navigate an eight-floor labyrinth filled with Shadows, locked doors, and teleportation traps. Reaching the top floor leads to the boss encounter against Shadow Yukiko to save her and secure her Persona.

Items

  • Glass Key: Floor 2, obtained by interacting with Chie after triggering the locked door
  • Suzaku Feather: Floor 8, found on the throne after defeating the optional boss Contrarian King

Secrets

  • Returning to Floor 8 after completing the dungeon reveals an optional boss, the Contrarian King, guarding a powerful weapon for Yukiko.

Tips

  • Floor 5 contains a teleportation puzzle where walking into certain hallways sends you backward, which requires opening the map to identify the correct route.
  • Floor 7 contains a locked door that requires finding and defeating the mini-boss Shadow, Tough Guy, on the same floor to obtain the key.
  • Shadow Yukiko summons a helper named Chivalrous Prince who is weak to Electric attacks and can cast healing magic on the boss.
  • When Shadow Yukiko uses the skill White Wall to boost her resistance to Ice, switch Chie to standard physical attacks until the barrier wears off.
