October: Secret Laboratory

Persona 4 Golden guide · Approaching midterms and saving Naoto
← All areas in this guide

October opens with the continuation of the Secret Laboratory dungeon rescue mission and leads directly into Midterm exams. Securing Naoto Shirogane early frees up valuable time to study, tend garden crops, and advance Social Links before school events lock your schedule. After the rescue and exams, school life briefly transitions toward the upcoming Culture Festival preparations.

On this page:Don't missDeadlinesSocial linksWorth doingEnemiesTips

Don't miss 0/1

Deadlines
  • October 5 | Clear the Secret Laboratory and defeat the boss to save Naoto Shirogane or game over occurs.
  • October 13 | Finish all exam preparation, as Midterm exams run from October 14 through October 19.

Social links

0/3

Worth doing
  • Study at the bedroom desk at night on rainy days or before the exams to raise Knowledge.
  • Work the nighttime Origami folding or Envelope making part-time jobs at the room desk to boost Diligence.
  • Read books in the bedroom at night to complete reading achievements and boost social stats.

Enemies
Shadow Naoto
Weak to Fire

Casts Mute, utilizes physical and elemental attacks, and shifts affinities using Element Zero.

Dominating Machine
Weak to Lightning

Sub-boss found on B4F of Secret Laboratory that buffs its own attack power.

Power Castle
Weak to Wind

Hits hard with physical attacks; knock down quickly with Garu skills.

Tips
  • Equip accessories or bring items that cure Mute and Silence before facing the dungeon boss on floor B9F.
  • You cannot engage in daytime Social Links during exam week (October 14–19), but you can still use the evenings to read or work.

Not listed for this area: items, secrets, shops and people.
