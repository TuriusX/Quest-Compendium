Magatsu Inaba

Persona 4 Golden guide · Adachi's dungeon in December

Magatsu Inaba is the December dungeon accessible through the TV World where the Investigation Team pursues Tohru Adachi to put an end to the serial murders. Navigating the distorted town street layout eventually leads players into Magatsu Mandala, a multi-floor interior area culminating in major boss confrontations. Clearing this dungeon triggers a time skip straight to December 25, requiring careful planning of your calendar before finishing it.

Secrets

  • You must clear Magatsu Inaba floor 2 by interacting with the bottom-center pit, which redirects you into Magatsu Mandala floor 1, before you can break the barrier protecting Adachi.
  • Magatsu Mandala World 6 forces you to defeat the Envious Giant mini-boss to remove the barrier preventing access to the final floors.

Tips

  • Do not defeat Adachi early in the month if you have pending Social Links, as defeating him automatically skips the in-game calendar forward to December 23–25.
  • The optimal day to clear Magatsu Inaba to maximize free time without missing holiday events is December 22.
  • Equip armor or accessories that grant resistance to instant-kill Light and Dark spells, as several enemies and Adachi employ Hama and Mudo skills.
  • Bring items or skills like Dekaja to immediately remove the boss's Heat Riser buffs during the sequential Adachi and Ameno-sagiri battles.
