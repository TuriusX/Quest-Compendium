November: Heaven

Persona 4 Golden guide · Nanako is kidnapped and rescued

Following Nanako's kidnapping on November 5, the Investigation Team pursues Taro Namatame into the TV World's Heaven dungeon to rescue her. You must reach the 10th floor and defeat the dungeon boss before your deadline while balancing daytime activities during this rainy month.

Deadlines

  • November 20 | Defeat the boss at Heaven floor 10 to rescue Nanako and prevent a Game Over.

Don't miss

  • Completing the Heaven dungeon early advances the calendar immediately to November 22, skipping all remaining free daytime and evening slots in this stretch; clear the dungeon on November 20 to maximize free time.

Social links

  • Justice (Nanako Dojima): Max out before November 3 to avoid missing the end of her Social Link.
  • Hierophant (Ryotaro Dojima): Max out before November 3; he becomes unavailable once Nanako is kidnapped.
  • Aeon (Marie): Must reach Rank 10 before clearing Heaven or early December to unlock the third-semester Golden epilogue and Hollow Forest dungeon.
  • Jester (Tohru Adachi): Advance to Rank 6 before November 1 to allow his Social Link to proceed automatically later.

Secrets

  • Defeating the optional boss Lost Okina on Floor 10 after clearing the dungeon yields the Chakra Ring and boosts your Courage stat.

Tips

  • Equipping fire-resistant or nullifying armor prevents party members from suffering heavy damage when Kunino-sagiri manipulates elemental attributes.
  • Pack plenty of status-recovery items or bring a Persona with Amrita, as the boss frequently inflicts status ailments across the entire party.
