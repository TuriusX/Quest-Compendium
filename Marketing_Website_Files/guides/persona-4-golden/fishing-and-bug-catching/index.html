Fishing and Bug Catching

Persona 4 Golden guide · Guide to obtaining the fishing rod and catching rare fish

Bug catching and fishing allow you to gather bait and catch fish required for the Fox Hermit Social Link, item exchanges, and the Master Angler achievement. You can fish at the Samegawa Riverbank once you obtain the Fishing Rod, and later at Shichiri Beach once you acquire the River Rod and Deep-Wayfarer rod upgrade. Rainy days drastically change water conditions and make rare fish, including the River Guardian and Sea Guardian, appear more frequently.

Don't miss

  • The River Rod and River Guardian must be obtained before you can obtain the Deep-Wayfarer and catch the Sea Guardian, which is required to max the Hermit Social Link before the game ends.

Worth doing

  • Catch bugs at the Tatsuhime Shrine trees once per day during the daytime or evening, with bug yield affected by your timing on the swing prompt.
  • Fish at the Samegawa Riverbank or Shichiri Beach to spend an afternoon catching fish for requests, trading with the Shiroku Pub lady, or completing the Fox's requests.

Items

  • Tatsuhime Ladybug: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching
  • Meiou Cricket: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching
  • Yaso Locust: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching
  • Daimyo Snail: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching
  • Inaba Jewel Beetle: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching
  • Genji Beetle: Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching, particularly later in the year or at night
  • Red Goldfish: Samegawa Riverbank using bread crumbs or bugs as bait
  • Genji Ayu: Samegawa Riverbank using Daimyo Snail or other common bait
  • Amber Seema: Samegawa Riverbank using Yaso Locust
  • Inaba Trout: Samegawa Riverbank using Meiou Cricket
  • Huge Fish: Samegawa Riverbank using Inaba Jewel Beetle
  • Guardian: Samegawa Riverbank on rainy or snowy days using Inaba Jewel Beetle
  • Sea Guardian: Shichiri Beach on rainy or snowy days using Genji Beetle

Secrets

  • To get the standard Fishing Rod, obtain the Bug Net from the boy at Tatsuhime Shrine by speaking to the lady at Shiroku Pub at night for Fishhook breadcrumbs, feed them to the river fisherman's request, and bring the fishhook to the old man at Samegawa Flood Plain.
  • To unlock ocean fishing at Shichiri Beach, ride your scooter to the beach three times, choose the option to fish, talk to the old man at the Samegawa Riverbank about the sea, and show him a caught River Guardian to receive the Deep-Wayfarer rod.

Shops and people

  • Bug Catching Boy at Tatsuhime Shrine: Trades caught bugs for hints or gives clues on what bait appears in different weather
  • Shiroku Pub at night: Trades fish for valuable weapons, armor, and gems

Tips

  • Rain and snow create rippling, splashing water circles at the fishing spots, which is the visual indicator that a Guardian is on the line.
  • If the water ripple effect does not appear when the float hits the water, pull your line back in immediately to save your bait and try again until the Guardian ripple effect triggers.
  • Reading the fishing books "Expert Fishing" and "Catching Bugs" increases the number of fish caught per session and improves the bug catching mini-game.
