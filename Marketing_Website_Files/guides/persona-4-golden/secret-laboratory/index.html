Secret Laboratory

Persona 4 Golden guide · Naoto's dungeon in September

The Secret Laboratory is the TV World dungeon accessed in September to rescue Naoto Shirogane before the fog sets in. It spans nine underground floors styled after a futuristic sci-fi testing facility, featuring locked security doors that require hunting down keycards on other floors. Reaching the lowest level leads to a boss showdown against Shadow Naoto.

Items

  • Research Card: Defeat the mini-boss Dominating Machine on B4F
  • Leader Card: Found in a chest on B6F behind a door unlocked by defeating the Government Agent

Enemies

Tips

  • Bring Personas that resist or nullify Fire and Mudo skills, as Shadow Naoto relies on strong elemental attacks and instant-death mechanics.
  • Shadow Naoto uses an ability called Element Zero, which strips all elemental resistances from your party members.
  • Return to the bottom floor after finishing the dungeon and securing the rescue to fight an optional boss, the Extreme Vessel, to obtain an increase in Courage and a weapon for Naoto.
