Yukiko's Castle

Persona 4 Golden guide · Strategy guide for the first dungeon and Shadow Yukiko boss fight

Yukiko's Castle is the first full dungeon in the TV World, where the Investigation Team must rescue Yukiko Amagi. You navigate eight floors of winding castle hallways, encounter mid-bosses, and face Shadow Yukiko at the top. Preparing Personas with Ice and Lightning skills will give you a major advantage throughout the dungeon.

Deadlines

  • April 29 | Defeat Shadow Yukiko on the 8th floor before rain falls continuously to prevent Yukiko from dying.

Don't miss

  • Returning to defeat the optional boss later | If you do not revisit the castle after clearing it to fight Contrarian King, you will miss out on Yukiko's powerful early-game weapon, the Suzaku Feather.

Social links

  • Chie Satonaka (Chariot): Available on the school rooftop or in the Central Shopping District on days without rain; spend time with her to unlock battle perks like follow-up attacks.

Worth doing

  • Study at the desk in your room at night to raise your Knowledge stat, which helps with upcoming school exams.
  • Read books bought from Yombokudo bookstore in the Central Shopping District during evening free time to boost social stats.

Items

  • Glass Key: Obtained on the 5th floor after defeating the mini-boss Shadow Knight

Shops and people

  • Daidara Metalworks: Sells weapons, armor, and accessories crafted from materials you sell him after TV World explorations.
  • Shiroku Store: Sells recovery items, status ailment curatives, and basic battle consumables during the day.

Tips

  • Fuse the Persona Senri before fighting Shadow Yukiko, as Senri naturally nullifies Fire damage and learns Media to keep the party healed.
  • Guard with Chie whenever Shadow Yukiko uses the skill "White Wall" or casts "Red Wall," as she will frequently follow up with a powerful party-wide Fire attack that exploits Chie's weakness.
  • Running out of SP is common early on; purchase TaP Soda from the vending machine in the Central Shopping District North each week to restore a small amount of SP in a pinch.
