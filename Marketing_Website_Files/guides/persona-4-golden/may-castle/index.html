May: Castle

Persona 4 Golden guide · Investigating Yukiko's Castle and forming the Investigation Team

In late April and early May, you must finish rescuing Yukiko Amagi from Yukiko's Castle before the prolonged rainy period ends. Once she is rescued and Golden Week concludes, she officially joins the party and you can re-enter the TV World to challenge an optional boss in the castle. The Investigation Team formally forms, opening up new daytime activities, part-time jobs, and the next dungeon arc.

Deadlines

  • April 29 | Defeat Shadow Yukiko and rescue Yukiko Amagi before the rain starts on April 30 to prevent a Game Over.

Don't miss

  • Contrarian King optional boss quest | Do not miss clearing Yukiko's Castle again after Yukiko recovers; defeating the boss grants a powerful weapon for Yukiko.

Social links

  • Priestess (Yukiko Amagi): Automatically established on May 17 after she fully recovers and returns to school.
  • Jester (Tohru Adachi): Visit Junes on available days/evenings starting in May to initiate and rank up his Social Link.
  • Aeon (Marie): Visit the Velvet Room on available non-rainy days starting in late April/May to hang out with her.

Worth doing

  • Read books in your room at night to boost Social Qualities or improve efficiency for studying and fishing.
  • Work part-time jobs available via the Central Shopping District bulletin board to earn money and raise Social Qualities.
  • Tend to the garden outside the Dojima Residence once vegetable seedlings become available in May.

Items

  • Suzaku Feather: Defeat the Contrarian King on the top floor of Yukiko's Castle after Yukiko joins

Secrets

  • Revisit the 8th floor of Yukiko's Castle after Yukiko's rescue to fight the Contrarian King; inspect the throne room afterward for Yukiko's weapon.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Contrarian KingIceHits extremely hard with physical attacks like Rampage; use Red Wall and keep buffs/debuffs active.

Shops and people

  • Daidara Metalworks: Sells improved weapons, armor, and accessories as you sell materials dropped by Shadows.
  • Shiroku Store: Sells recovery items during the day and trades gems for specialized weapons and items at night.

Tips

  • Fuse Personas that resist or nullify Fire to make the Shadow Yukiko encounter much easier to survive.
  • Bring plenty of revival and healing items when returning to fight the Contrarian King, as his Rampage skill can easily wipe your party.
