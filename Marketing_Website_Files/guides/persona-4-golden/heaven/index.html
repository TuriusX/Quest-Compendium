Heaven

Persona 4 Golden guide · Dungeon details for Heaven

Heaven is the dungeon where the Investigation Team attempts to rescue Nanako Dojima from her kidnapper. It spans 10 floors and features a serene, cloudy aesthetic that contrasts with the emotional stakes of the story. Completing this dungeon will dramatically shift the tone and progression of the game heading into late autumn.

Deadlines

  • November 20 | Defeat the dungeon boss to rescue Nanako Dojima, otherwise the game ends in an immediate Game Over

Don't miss

  • Adachi's Jester Social Link | Must reach Rank 6 before clearing Heaven, as November 1st to November 20th contains the final opportunities to hang out with him for this arc

Secrets

  • Returning to Floor 10 after completely clearing Heaven spawns the optional boss Lost Okina, which rewards the player with the Chakra Ring and an increase to Courage when examined after defeating it

Tips

  • Equip accessories that prevent Confusion and Enervation, especially for the Floor 7 mini-boss Natural Dancer.
  • During the Kunino-sagiri boss fight, enter the Guard stance when he uses Control to turn party members against you, or use elemental breaks to pierce his absorption barriers.
  • Fuse Personas with Null, Repel, or Drain affinities across multiple elements to counter Kunino-sagiri's Quad Converge, which drastically amplifies a single chosen element's damage.
