Heaven

Persona 4 Golden guide · Nanako's dungeon in November

Heaven is a sprawling dungeon located in the TV World accessible during November to rescue Nanako Dojima. The area consists of 10 floors with a clear aesthetic of clouds and stairs reaching upward toward a grand summit. Reaching the top floor triggers a storyline confrontation with the kidnapper, Taro Namatame.

Items

  • Chakra Ring: Heaven Floor 10 after defeating the optional boss Lost Okina

Secrets

  • Defeating the main boss and returning to Heaven on a later date reveals a new optional boss, the Lost Okina, waiting at the top floor.

Tips

  • Complete this dungeon by November 20th, but avoid finishing it on the very first day if you want to maximize daytime Social Links, as clearing it advances the calendar directly to the evening of November 22nd.
  • Bring Personas with diverse elemental coverage and multi-target break or defensive skills to handle Kunino-sagiri's elemental control shifts.
  • Equipping accessories or using spells that grant evasion against a character's own weakness is helpful, because Kunino-sagiri will brainwash party members to attack you with their signature affinities.
