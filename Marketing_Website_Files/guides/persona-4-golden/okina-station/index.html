Okina Station

Persona 4 Golden guide · Visiting the city area

Okina Station is a modern city hub unlocked after taking the scooter out for multiple long-distance trips. It features unique shops, costume stores, movie theaters, and a coffee shop that provide unique upgrades outside of Inaba. You can spend free daytime periods here to boost party members' stats or advance specific side quests.

Shops and people

  • Croco Fur: Sells various cosmetic costumes and outfits for the investigation team.
  • Chagall Cafe: Serves coffee that allows the protagonist to extract a skill card from an equipped Persona.
  • Cinema 30: A movie theater where you can invite party members to level up their Personas and increase their stats.
  • Crane Game: A prize machine outside the station where you can spend money to win plush dolls for your bedroom.

Tips

  • Visiting the movie theater with party members raises their Persona's stats permanently, making it a great way to boost allies you regularly use in combat.
  • Taking a drink at Chagall Cafe with a Persona holding a skill card icon will safely extract the card into your inventory without losing the Persona.
