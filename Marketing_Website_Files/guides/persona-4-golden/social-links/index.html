Social Links

Persona 4 Golden guide · Comprehensive guide to unlocking and ranking up all character relationships

Social Links represent bonds forged with other characters that grant bonus experience when fusing Personas of the corresponding Arcana. In Persona 4 Golden, maxing out Social Links also unlocks ultimate Persona fusions and grants party members enhanced combat abilities and Persona evolutions. Managing your daily calendar between daytime activities, school clubs, part-time jobs, and evening interactions is essential for completing as many links as possible in a single playthrough.

Deadlines

  • November 1 | Maximize the Justice (Nanako) and Hanged Man (Naoki) links or progress them significantly, as Nanako's link becomes unavailable due to story events shortly after this date
  • November 17 | Reach Rank 6 with the Jester (Tohru Adachi) link before this date, as ranks beyond 6 advance automatically through specific story events later
  • December 3 | Reach Rank 10 with the Aeon (Marie) link before clearing the December dungeon to unlock the bonus hollow forest dungeon and the golden epilogue

Don't miss

  • The Aeon Social Link (Marie) must reach Rank 10 before defeating the boss of the December dungeon, or the bonus winter dungeon and true Golden ending will be locked out
  • The Jester Social Link (Tohru Adachi) must reach Rank 6 by November 17, and specific dialogue choices must be made during December to transition it into the Hunger Arcana

Social links

  • Fool (Investigation Team): Advances automatically throughout the story as the mystery progresses
  • Magician (Yosuke Hanamura): Speak with him at Yasogami High School on the 2nd floor hallway or Junes on days off starting in mid-April
  • Priestess (Yukiko Amagi): Speak with her on the 1st floor hallway of Yasogami High or in the shopping district after rescuing her from the TV world
  • Empress (Margaret): Visit the Velvet Room after May 19 with Knowledge at rank 3 or higher, then fuse specific Personas with requested skills
  • Emperor (Kanji Tatsumi): Speak to the female student on Yasogami High 2F, then find Kanji in the Practice Building 1F to initiate the link after his dungeon is cleared
  • Hierophant (Ryotaro Dojima): Spend time with Dojima at the Dojima Residence during evenings when he is home and not on duty
  • Lovers (Rise Kujikawa): Speak with her on Yasogami High 1F hallway or the Shopping District South on days off after her dungeon is cleared
  • Chariot (Chie Satonaka): Speak with her on the school rooftop or at the Shopping District South on non-school days starting in mid-April
  • Justice (Nanako Dojima): Interact with Nanako at the Dojima Residence during evenings when Dojima is away, requiring high Expression
  • Hermit (Fox): Visit the Tatsuhime Shrine starting on May 5 and fulfill Fox's side quests to lower the cost of SP recovery in the TV world
  • Fortune (Naoto Shirogane): Speak to the man in black at Shopping District North with high Courage, get the card, then talk to Naoto at school with high Knowledge
  • Strength (Daisuke Nagase / Kou Ichijo): Join either the Basketball or Soccer club in Yasogami High starting in late April
  • Hanged Man (Naoki Konishi): Speak to him multiple times at Yasogami High 1F near the infirmary after June 9 with sufficient Understanding
  • Death (Hisano Kuroda): Reach Rank 4 in the Devil link to unlock the Riverbed area, then talk to the old woman at the Samegawa Floodplain on Sundays and holidays
  • Temperance (Eri Minami): Apply for the Day Care part-time job at the town bulletin board and take the bus to work on specified afternoons
  • Devil (Sayoko Uehara): Apply for the Hospital Janitor part-time job at the town bulletin board and work evenings, requiring high Diligence
  • Tower (Shu Nakajima): Apply for the Tutor part-time job on the town bulletin board with high Understanding and work evenings
  • Star (Teddie): Advances automatically through the storyline as the investigation continues
  • Moon (Ai Ebihara): Reach Rank 4 in the Strength link, then speak with Ai at Yasogami High 1F with high Courage to become sports manager
  • Sun (Yumi Ozawa / Ayane Matsunaga): Join either the Drama or Band club at Yasogami High starting in late April
  • Jester/Hunger (Tohru Adachi): Find Adachi at Junes during the day or the Shopping District South at night starting in May
  • Aeon (Marie): Visit the Velvet Room and choose to spend time with Marie on available afternoons starting in late April

Worth doing

  • Work part-time jobs via the Shopping District bulletin board to raise social stats and unlock the Temperance, Devil, and Tower links
  • Draw fortune slips at the Tatsuhime Shrine during free daytime slots to gain Social Link points with characters who are not yet ready to rank up
  • Spend evenings at the Tatsuhime Shrine catching bugs or trading gems with the Ghost Lady at the shrine

Tips

  • Always carry a Persona matching the Arcana of the person you are hanging out with to gain extra affinity points during dialogue choices
  • Prioritize party member Social Links early to unlock essential combat perks like surviving fatal blows, curing status ailments, and follow-up attacks
  • Rain prevents most outdoor Social Links from meeting, making rainy days ideal for clearing dungeons or eating the mega beef bowl at Aiya to raise multiple stats
