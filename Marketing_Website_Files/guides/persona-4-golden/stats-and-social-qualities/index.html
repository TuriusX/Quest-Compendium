Stats and Social Qualities

Persona 4 Golden guide · How to raise Courage, Diligence, Knowledge, Expression, and Understanding

The protagonist has five personal parameters: Courage, Diligence, Knowledge, Expression, and Understanding, each leveling from Rank 1 to Rank 5. Increasing these stats is mandatory to start or advance certain Social Links, apply for specific part-time jobs, and unlock special dialogue choices. Balancing stat gains with reading books, part-time jobs, and diner visits is vital throughout the school year.

Social links

  • Tower (Shu Nakajima): Requires Rank 5 Understanding to begin via the Tutor part-time job on the Central Shopping District bulletin board.
  • Moon (Ai Ebihara): Requires Rank 3 Courage to invite her out after advancing the Strength Social Link to Rank 4.
  • Fortune (Naoto Shirogane): Requires Rank 5 Knowledge and Rank 5 Courage to initiate after receiving the card from the man in black in the Shopping District.
  • Justice (Nanako Dojima): Requires high Expression to advance past intermediate ranks.
  • Hanged Man (Naoki Konishi): Requires Rank 3 Understanding to unlock through talking to him multiple times at school.

Worth doing

  • Visit the Aiya Diner in the Central Shopping District on rainy days to attempt the Mega Beef Bowl Challenge, which grants random increases across several stats at once.
  • Work the Envelope Construction part-time job from the town bulletin board at your bedroom desk at night to raise Diligence.
  • Work the Translation part-time job from your bedroom desk at night to raise Expression, which requires a baseline of Knowledge to accept.
  • Work the Tutor part-time job at night to raise Expression and Understanding, though it requires maximum Understanding to accept.
  • Work the Day Care part-time job on bus stop days to raise Understanding while progressing the Temperance Social Link.
  • Work the Hospital Janitor part-time job at night to raise Courage while progressing the Devil Social Link, though it requires Diligence to accept.
  • Work the Origami Crane folding part-time job at your bedroom desk at night to raise Understanding, though it requires a baseline of Diligence to unlock.
  • Study at the desk in your bedroom or at the school library after school to raise Knowledge, with rainy days yielding higher gains.
  • Read stat-boosting books purchased from the Yokusendo Bookstore at your bedroom sofa to permanently increase stats or boost the efficiency of stat-raising activities.
  • Eat strange or spoiled food from the Dojima residence fridge when available at night to raise Courage at the cost of evening time.

Tips

  • Prioritize reading the books "Expert Study Methods" and "Hyperspeed Reading" as soon as they become available at the bookstore to drastically reduce the time spent reading other books.
  • Save the Mega Beef Bowl Challenge at Aiya for rainy days when outdoor Social Links are unavailable, as it boosts three to four stats in a single evening.
  • Answering classroom questions correctly raises Knowledge without consuming any time, while volunteering to answer when classmates struggle can raise Expression.
