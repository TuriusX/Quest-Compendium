Yomotsu Hirasaka

Persona 4 Golden guide · Dungeon details for Yomotsu Hirasaka

Yomotsu Hirasaka is the true final dungeon of Persona 4 Golden, accessed only on the very last day of the game. It consists of nine floors populated by the strongest regular Shadows in the game, culminating in the final confrontation with the mastermind behind the fog. Clearing this dungeon is required to achieve the True Ending and Golden Epilogue.

Deadlines

  • March 20 | You must reach the 9th floor and defeat the final boss before leaving the TV World, as this is the final playable day of the game.

Don't miss

  • Accessing Yomotsu Hirasaka | When prompted to return home on March 20, choose not to leave, visit the Junes Food Court to gather the Investigation Team, speak with Dojima at the riverbank, speak with Igor in the Velvet Room to receive the Orb of Sight, and finally talk to the gas station attendant in the Shopping District.

Items

  • Celestial Brooch MISSABLE: Yomotsu Hirasaka Floor 3 (in a chest after defeating the mini-boss Neo Minotaur)
  • Space Badge MISSABLE: Yomotsu Hirasaka Floor 6 (in a chest after defeating the mini-boss Sleeping Table)

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Neo MinotaurMini-boss found guarding the path on Floor 3; heavily relies on potent physical attacks.
Sleeping TableHamaMini-boss found guarding the path on Floor 6; uses Fear and Ghastly Wail to instantly kill party members.
Ameno-sagiriThe first form of the final sequence on Floor 9, fought before the true form awakens.
IzanamiThe true mastermind encountered on Floor 9; uses powerful Almighty, elemental magic, and status-inflicting skills.
Izanami-no-OkamiThe ultimate form of the final boss, fought immediately after Izanami is forced to reveal her true nature.

Tips

  • Equip accessories or Personas that nullify or protect against Fear before initiating the mini-boss fight against Sleeping Table on Floor 6.
  • Ensure you carry plenty of SP restorative items or utilize the Fox's healing services at the dungeon entrance, as this entire dungeon must be conquered in a single in-game day.
