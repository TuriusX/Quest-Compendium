Yomotsu Hirasaka

Persona 4 Golden guide · The final dungeon in the True Ending

Yomotsu Hirasaka is the true final dungeon of the game, accessed only on March 20th if you uncover the mastermind behind the Fog. The Investigation Team must ascend all nine floors to confront the origin of the Midnight Channel. Defeating the boss at the top resolves the entire conflict and unlocks the True Ending.

Items

  • Soma MISSABLE: Rare golden chests on floors 1 through 8
  • Bead Chain MISSABLE: Regular or golden chests throughout the dungeon

Secrets

  • You must talk to every Max Social Link character on March 20th, choose not to return home, inspect the Junes elevator, visit the Riverbank, and enter the Velvet Room to receive the Orb of Sight before this dungeon unlocks.

Tips

  • Fuse the Persona Yoshitsune and max out Hassou Tobi before entering, as it deals massive physical damage against both mid-bosses and the final boss.
  • Floor 3 and Floor 6 are blocked by barrier doors that require defeating a mid-boss on each respective floor to proceed.
  • Bring Goho-M items or use the Traesto skill so you can return to the entrance and heal via the Fox before challenging the top floor.
