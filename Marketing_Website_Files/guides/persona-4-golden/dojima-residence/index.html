Dojima Residence

Persona 4 Golden guide · Living with Uncle Dojima

The Dojima Residence serves as the protagonist's primary home and base of operations during his year in Inaba. Here, you can study, read books, work on part-time jobs, check the fridge, and garden with Nanako. It is also the main location to interact with Ryotaro Dojima and Nanako Dojima to advance their respective Social Links.

Items

  • Various food items: Inside the refrigerator on select evenings

Secrets

  • Checking the refrigerator on certain nights allows you to cook boxed lunches that, if prepared correctly, can be shared with school friends the next day to boost their Social Link.

Shops and people

  • Nanako Dojima: Offers the Justice Social Link interactions and helps with gardening
  • Ryotaro Dojima: Offers the Hierophant Social Link interactions at the living room table

Tips

  • Always check the living room calendar and the refrigerator in the evening, as leftover food can raise your Courage or provide boxed lunch opportunities without passing time.
  • Using the desk in your bedroom to study on rainy nights provides a larger boost to your Knowledge stat than on clear evenings.
  • Resting on the sofa in your room to read requires you to finish every chapter of a book to gain its stat boosts or gameplay perks.
