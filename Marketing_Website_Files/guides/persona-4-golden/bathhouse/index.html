Bathhouse

Persona 4 Golden guide · Kanji's dungeon

The Steamy Bathhouse is the second major dungeon, unlocked to rescue Kanji Tatsumi from his Shadow before the fog sets in. Players must ascend through 11 floors containing steam-filled corridors, locked gates, and tough mid-bosses. Reaching the top floor triggers a boss fight against Shadow Kanji and his two bodyguards.

Items

  • Bathhouse Key: Dropped by the mid-boss Daring Gigas on the 7th floor

Tips

  • Bring Personas with Ice and Fire skills to quickly exploit the weaknesses of Shadow Kanji's companions during the main boss fight.
  • Return to the 11th floor after clearing the dungeon to defeat the optional boss, Intolerant Officer, to obtain Kanji's Iron Plate weapon.
  • Watch out for enemies that cast Poison and Enervation; carry Dis-Poison and Sedatives or ensure your healer has Energy Shower.
