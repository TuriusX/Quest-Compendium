February: Hollow Forest

Persona 4 Golden guide · Rescuing Marie from the Hollow Forest

On February 13, the Investigation Team travels into the Hollow Forest to save Marie before the dungeon disappears. You must clear the entire dungeon in a single calendar day with unique resource-draining mechanics. Completing this dungeon is necessary to unlock the epilogue and the path to the true Golden ending.

Deadlines

  • February 13 | Complete the Hollow Forest in its entirety, as you are only given this single day to rescue Marie.

Don't miss

  • Access to the Hollow Forest | Must max Marie's Aeon Social Link to Rank 10 before December 24 and ask Margaret to search for Marie when prompted on January 2.
  • Golden Epilogue | If Marie is not rescued on February 13, the game skips directly to the normal departure ending in March without the extended epilogue.

Tips

  • Your team's SP is cut in half after every battle inside the Hollow Forest, making normal SP preservation useless.
  • All equipped armor, weapons, and standard inventory items are locked away upon entering; you must rely exclusively on gear and consumables found inside chests.
  • Use physical skills that cost HP for regular battles rather than elemental spells to conserve your draining SP pools.
  • Open every chest you find to collect special dungeon-only accessories that restore SP each turn.
  • Bring Rise's Social Link up to max beforehand, as her passive support abilities will constantly heal and restore SP regardless of dungeon restrictions.
  • During the Kusumi-no-Okami fight, use the Break items or elemental Break skills found in the dungeon to remove her innate elemental resistances.
