Part-Time Jobs

Persona 4 Golden guide · Guide to available jobs and their benefits for stats and money

Part-time jobs in Persona 4 Golden allow the protagonist to earn money, raise social stats, and initiate specific Social Links. Job applications become available on the Town Bulletin Board in the Central Shopping District starting on April 23.

Social links

  • Temperance (Eri Minami): Apply for the Day Care Assistant job on the bulletin board and attend work on Monday, Friday, or Saturday (non-rainy days).
  • Tower (Shu Nakajima): Apply for the Tutor job on the bulletin board (requires high Understanding) and work at night on Tuesday, Thursday, or Saturday.
  • Devil (Sayoko Uehara): Apply for the Hospital Janitor job (requires Diligence) and work at the hospital on Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday nights.
  • Death (Hisano Kuroda): Encountered at the Samegawa Flood Plain on non-rainy Sundays and holidays after progressing the Devil (Sayoko Uehara) Social Link to Rank 4.

Worth doing

  • Envelope Construction | Work at the work desk in your bedroom to earn money and increase Diligence; requires no prerequisites and is available day or night.
  • Translating | Work at the desk in your room to raise Expression and earn money; requires basic Knowledge to apply.
  • Origami Crane Folder | Work at your bedroom desk to raise Understanding; unlocked after reaching a Diligence threshold.

Tips

  • Do not overlook the at-home crafting jobs early on, as Envelope Construction is a quick way to build the Diligence needed to apply for the Hospital Janitor position.
  • Working at the Day Care, Hospital, or Tutoring jobs advances daytime or nighttime slots, but each session grants both social stat points and progress toward their respective Social Links.
