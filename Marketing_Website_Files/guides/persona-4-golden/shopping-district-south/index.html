Shopping District, South

Persona 4 Golden guide · Exploring the town

The Central Shopping District, South is a primary hub street in Inaba featuring several key retail shops, service locations, and Social Link contacts. Players visit this area to buy weapons, purchase healing items, accept quests from residents, and access the Velvet Room. It serves as a central junction connecting the Dojima Residence, Junes, and the northern part of the shopping district.

Shops and people

  • Daidara Metalworks: Sells weapons, armor, and accessories made from materials you sell from the TV World
  • Shiroku Store: Sells recovery items and status cures by day; operates as Shiroku Pub by night
  • Velvet Room: Managed by Igor and Margaret for Persona fusion, Persona Compendium access, and Social Link quests
  • Capsule Machine: Dispenses random miscellaneous items in exchange for yen
  • Vending Machines: Sell SP-restoring canned drinks in limited weekly batches

Tips

  • Check the vending machines every Monday to buy out all SP-restoring drinks, as their stock resets weekly.
  • Visit the bulletin board near the bus stop to view and apply for various part-time jobs.
  • Give Margaret materials and fused Personas with requested skills at the Velvet Room entrance to advance the Empress Social Link.
  • Trade gems obtained from Shadows to the Shiroku Pub hostess at night to acquire unique weapons and items.
