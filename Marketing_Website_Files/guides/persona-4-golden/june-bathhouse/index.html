June: Bathhouse

Persona 4 Golden guide · Rescuing Kanji Tatsumi from the Steamy Bathhouse

During early June, the team must navigate the Steamy Bathhouse dungeon to rescue Kanji Tatsumi from his Shadow. Once Kanji is saved, the game transitions into mid-term exams preparation and opens up new evening activities and daytime Social Links. Completing the rescue early leaves plenty of rainy and clear days to raise Social Links and social stats before the next story arc begins.

Deadlines

  • June 4 | Defeat Shadow Kanji on the 11th floor of the Steamy Bathhouse before continuous rain causes a Game Over.

Don't miss

  • Hermit Rank Up | The Fox's quests require catching specific fish or speaking to NPCs across specific days, which can stall progress if ignored during rainy days.

Social links

  • Kanji Tatsumi (Emperor): Available starting June 9 by speaking to a female student on the 2nd floor of Yasogami High classroom building, then finding Kanji in the Practice Building 1F.
  • Rise Kujikawa (Lovers): Cannot be started yet, but story cutscenes introducing her begin later in June.
  • Margaret (Empress): Advanced by fusing requested Personas with specific skills at the Velvet Room once the protagonist is level 17 or higher.

Worth doing

  • Work the Translator or Envelope folding part-time jobs at your room's desk in the evening to raise Expression and Diligence.
  • Read books in your room at night to raise social stats or speed up future reading sessions.
  • Study at the school library after class or at your desk at night to raise Knowledge ahead of the upcoming exams.

Items

  • Bathhouse Key: Obtained on the 7th floor of the Steamy Bathhouse after defeating Daring Gigas
  • Iron Plate: Steamy Bathhouse 11F after defeating the optional boss Intolerant Soloist later in the month

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Nice GuyIceBuffs Shadow Kanji and casts healing spells; weak to Ice attacks.
Tough GuyFireUses physical attacks and defends Shadow Kanji; weak to Fire attacks.
Daring GigasWindMini-boss encountered on the 7th floor of the Steamy Bathhouse; guards the key to proceed.

Shops and people

  • Shiroku Pub: Operates at night at Shiroku Store, trading gems collected in dungeons for special weapons and armor.

Tips

  • Focus down Nice Guy first during the Shadow Kanji boss encounter to prevent him from healing the boss and buffing its offense.
  • Bring Personas that resist or nullify Physical and Electric damage to survive Shadow Kanji's powerful area-of-effect attacks.
