January: Ski Trip

Persona 4 Golden guide · Golden exclusive ski trip and winter events

In early February, the Investigation Team embarks on an overnight ski trip exclusive to Persona 4 Golden. This event serves as the narrative bridge into the Hollow Forest bonus dungeon if Marie's Social Link has been completed. During the trip, you can spend evening events with your friends and explore a mysterious cabin before being drawn into the next mystery.

Deadlines

  • February 5 | Complete Marie's Social Link to Rank 10 and speak to Margaret in the Velvet Room on January 2 to unlock the Hollow Forest rescue sequence during the ski trip.

Don't miss

  • Ghost Story Evening Scene | During the cabin stay on the ski trip, choose which character to investigate the noise with to view exclusive romantic or comedic dialogue scenes.
  • Hollow Forest Dungeon Access | If Marie is not maxed out by early January or Margaret is not asked to search for her, the ski trip ends normally and the game skips directly to the March ending sequence.

Worth doing

  • Free daytime Social Link progression | Spend the days of January prior to the ski trip maxing out lingering school and party Social Links before daytime free time ends.

Tips

  • Pack winter clothing and gear up beforehand, as the ski trip triggers directly from school calendar events and transitions straight into the February story dungeon.
