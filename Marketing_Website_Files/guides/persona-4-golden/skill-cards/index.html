Skill Cards

Persona 4 Golden guide · How to obtain and use cards to customize Persona abilities

Skill Cards in Persona 4 Golden allow you to teach specific skills directly to any Persona in your current roster. They can be registered with Marie in the Velvet Room so you can repurchase infinite copies later for a yen fee. Understanding how to acquire and duplicate these cards makes optimizing your team's skillsets significantly easier.

Worth doing

  • Take a Persona with a card icon on its skill list to the Chagall Cafe in Okina City to extract that skill as a Skill Card

Items

  • Skill Card: Shuffle Time sword cards in dungeons

Secrets

  • Personas with a blank card icon next to a skill will yield that specific Skill Card upon leveling up or when visiting Chagall Cafe in Okina City

Shops and people

  • Marie: Skill Card Registration and Repurchase

Tips

  • Always register a newly found Skill Card with Marie before using it, ensuring you never permanently lose access to that ability.
  • Extracting a Skill Card at Chagall Cafe consumes a daytime time slot, so plan visits on days when key Social Links are unavailable.
