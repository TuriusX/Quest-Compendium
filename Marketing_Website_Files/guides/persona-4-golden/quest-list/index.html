Quest List

Persona 4 Golden guide · Walkthrough for all optional side quests given by NPCs

Side quests in Persona 4 Golden are optional requests given primarily by schoolmates, townspeople across Inaba, and the Fox at Tatsuhime Shrine. Most quests require gathering specific materials dropped by shadows in the TV World, fetching items from other NPCs, or answering trivia questions. Completing them rewards cash, usable items, weapons, or raises your Social Link rank with the Hermit.

Deadlines

  • January 15 | Complete any remaining NPC side quests before this date, as normal free-roaming daytime exploration ends shortly after.
  • February 5 | Complete Quest 69 to acquire the final Hermit rank before the dungeon events conclude the gameplay loop.

Don't miss

  • Quest 14: A Wish for A Diet Cocktail | Requires talking to the slender woman in Okina City and trading a Soul Drop, accessible only after obtaining the scooter.
  • Quest 34: Acquire Hard Boots | Requires selling 5 Thick Hides dropped by Dancing Hands in the Steamy Bathhouse to Daidara before the boots can be bought and delivered.
  • Quest 40: Acquire Fashionable Dishes | Requires farming 3 Fashionable Trays dropped by Elegant Turkeys in the Secret Laboratory, which become unavailable if the dungeon is cleared without gathering them.
  • Quest 48: Acquire a Crystal Ball | Requires obtaining a Crystal Ball dropped by Constancy Relics in the Heaven dungeon before finishing that dungeon block.

Social links

  • Hermit: Complete the sequential Fox Shrine requests (Quests 07, 09, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, and 69) and report back to the Fox to rank up.

Worth doing

  • Talk to the Fox at Tatsuhime Shrine to read the wooden ema plaques and unlock new Hermit Social Link requests.
  • Visit Okina City via scooter once unlocked to find unique quest givers outside the cinema and clothing boutique.
  • Speak to the Funky Student on the 2F hallway of Yasogami High to answer series of riddle quests (Quests 04, 16, 21, 28, 38).

Items

  • Angler's Set MISSABLE: Reward for completing Quest 15 from the old man at the Samegawa Flood Plain
  • Leather Keyring: Obtained from the student in Yasogami High Practice Building 1F for Quest 01

Shops and people

  • Daidara Metalworks: Crafts materials dropped from TV World shadows into quest-required weapons and armor.
  • Shiroku Pub: Trades Daidara bug materials for specialized gem equipment during evening hours.

Tips

  • You must talk to a quest giver to accept the quest before the relevant TV World shadows will begin dropping the requested materials.
  • Multiple quests require materials from previously cleared dungeons, so plan TV World trips to clear several fetch requests in a single run.
  • Rainy days change NPC locations and prevent many outdoor quest givers from appearing, so handle school and outdoor requests on sunny or cloudy days.
