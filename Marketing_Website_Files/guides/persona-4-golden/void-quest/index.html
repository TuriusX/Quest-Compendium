Void Quest

Persona 4 Golden guide · Mitsuo's dungeon

Void Quest is the retro video-game-themed dungeon created inside the TV world by Mitsuo Kubo. You must navigate a labyrinth with shifting teleporters, locked doors, and mini-bosses across ten chapters to reach and defeat Shadow Mitsuo. Clearing this dungeon is required to rescue Mitsuo and continue the main story.

Items

  • Orb of Darkness: Chapter 7, awarded after defeating the sub-boss Killing Hand
  • Chakra Ring: Chapter 10, found in a chest in the boss room after defeating the optional boss Escapist Soldier

Secrets

  • After Mitsuo is defeated, returning to Chapter 10 unlocks an optional boss named Escapist Soldier guarding a powerful accessory.

Tips

  • On Chapter 7, you must defeat the mini-boss Killing Hand to acquire the Orb of Darkness, which unlocks the door to Chapter 8.
  • Bring Personas that can hit multiple targets with high physical or almighty damage to shatter Mitsuo the Hero's shell before he finishes casting spells.
  • Watch out for floor-warping tiles on Chapter 3 and Chapter 7 that turn your character around or teleport you backward.
