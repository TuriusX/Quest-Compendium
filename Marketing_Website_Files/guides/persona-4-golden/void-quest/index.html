Void Quest

Persona 4 Golden guide · Strategy guide for the fourth dungeon and Shadow Mitsuo boss fight

The Investigation Team enters the Midnight Channel to rescue Mitsuo Kubo from a retro video game-styled dungeon called Void Quest. You must navigate ten floors, solving a key-and-door puzzle along the way, to confront and defeat Shadow Mitsuo. Clearing this dungeon halts Mitsuo's actions and advances the investigation into the serial murders.

Deadlines

  • August 12 | Rescue Mitsuo Kubo by defeating the boss on Chapter 10 of Void Quest or the game ends.

Don't miss

  • Summer Festival invitation | Accept a phone call invitation on August 20 from a compatible Social Link to attend the festival on August 21; missing the call or rejecting it forfeits the exclusive scene and festival book.
  • The Hypnotist book | Available for purchase at the Y Bookstore in the Central Shopping District starting August 9; buy it before the end of the year to complete the book reading achievement.
  • Short-Fused Mantis | Catch at the Tatsuhime Shrine bug catching spot; needed for the Guardian fishing questline later in the month.

Social links

  • Teddie (Star): Automatically ranks up as you progress the story through August and clear Void Quest.
  • Nanako Dojima (Justice): Spend time with Nanako at the Dojima residence in the evenings when she is available and your Expression rank meets her requirements.

Worth doing

  • Work on your garden outside the Dojima Residence during daytime or evening slots to grow useful healing items.
  • Read books in your room at night to raise your Social Stats (Courage, Knowledge, Diligence, Understanding, Expression).

Items

  • Orb of Darkness: Chapter 7 of Void Quest

Secrets

  • Escort Search bonus boss | After defeating Shadow Mitsuo, revisit Chapter 10 at a later date to fight an optional boss, after which you can inspect the room to acquire the Algernon weapon for Naoto.

Shops and people

  • Shiroku Store (Central Shopping District): Sells recovery items, status healing medicine, and Goho-M escape items.
  • Daidara Metalworks (Central Shopping District): Crafts weapons and armor in exchange for materials dropped by Shadows in Void Quest.

Tips

  • Bring items or Persona skills that cure Fear, as Shadow Mitsuo frequently inflicts Fear and follows up with Ghastly Wail to instantly kill afflicted party members.
  • When Shadow Mitsuo starts rebuilding the "Mitsuo the Hero" shell, deal as much burst damage as possible in that turn to disrupt the construction and knock him down.
