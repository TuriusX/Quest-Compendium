Shopping District, North

Persona 4 Golden guide · Accessing the shrine

The Central Shopping District, North is a commercial street in Inaba that contains several local shops, bulletin boards, and the path to the Tatsuhime Shrine. Players visit this area to accept side quests, buy food or materials, interact with Social Links, and fulfill fox requests at the shrine.

Shops and people

  • Chinese Diner Aiya: Offers rainy-day Mega Beef Bowl challenges and various stat-raising meals
  • Souzai Daigaku: Sells croquettes and skewers that heal HP or SP
  • Marutake Hobby Shop: Provides assembly kits to build models in your room
  • Tatsuhime Shrine: Home to the Fox Hermit Social Link, an ema board for quests, and fortune slips

Tips

  • Eating the Mega Beef Bowl at Chinese Diner Aiya on rainy days increases multiple social stats at once.
  • You can catch bugs at the shrine trees or pull fortune slips at the shrine to advance Social Link affinity without spending time.
