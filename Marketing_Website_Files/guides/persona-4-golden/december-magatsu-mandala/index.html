December: Magatsu Mandala

Persona 4 Golden guide · Finding the culprit and confronting Adachi

Following the events of early December where the true culprit behind the murders is deduced, the Investigation Team enters the TV World to pursue Tohru Adachi. The dungeon consists of two intertwined sections, Magatsu Inaba and Magatsu Mandala, culminating in back-to-back boss fights. Navigating the dungeon requires breaking barriers across specific floors before finally confronting the culprit.

Deadlines

  • December 22 | Clear Magatsu Inaba and defeat the boss by this day to view the Christmas Eve event with a romantic partner, as clearing on December 23 or 24 skips directly to the 25th.
  • December 24 | The absolute hard deadline to clear Magatsu Inaba; failing to beat the boss by today triggers an automatic game over.

Don't miss

  • Christmas Eve Event | Clear the dungeon on or before December 22; defeating the boss triggers an immediate time skip to December 25, so waiting until December 23 or 24 forfeits the date entirely.
  • Jester Arcana Level 8 | When deciding to pursue Adachi, choose to visit him alone in the TV World to transform the Jester Arcana into the Hunger Arcana and progress it automatically toward max rank.

Social links

  • Hunger (formerly Jester): Automatically advances to Rank 9 and Rank 10 later in the story if Rank 8 was triggered by confronting Adachi alone.

Worth doing

  • Finishing party member Social Links to unlock their third-tier Personas during the remaining free days.
  • Reading remaining unread books at your room desk on free evenings to work toward the Compulsive Reader trophy.

Secrets

  • Magatsu Mandala World 6 Barrier | The door to the final area is sealed until you defeat the Envious Giant mini-boss located on Magatsu Mandala World 6.

Tips

  • Finishing the dungeon immediately fast-forwards the calendar to December 25, so spend as many days as possible maxing Social Links before fighting the boss on December 22.
  • Bring Personas with high Agility or evasion against Dark and Light skills, as random encounters in Magatsu Mandala frequently cast instakill spells.
  • Stock up on Patra items or equip accessories that grant charm resistance, as status ailments become very frequent in the deeper floors.
