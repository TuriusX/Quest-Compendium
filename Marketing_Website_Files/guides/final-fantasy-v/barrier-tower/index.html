Barrier Tower

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Scaling the tower to disable the barrier

The party infiltrates this tower using Cid's submarine in order to destroy the antenna generator powering the magical barrier around Exdeath's Castle. The tower features a split path with two treasure-lined staircases and a warp point at the summit. Once the generator boss is defeated, the tower is destroyed, making the entire location permanently inaccessible.

Items

  • 9,000 Gil MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the 2nd floor
  • 18,000 Gil MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the 7th floor
  • Blood Sword MISSABLE: In a treasure chest on the 10th floor, triggers a monster-in-a-box encounter with Red Dragons

Enemies

AtomosDark MatterThe boss at the summit who slowly drags incapacitated party members toward its mouth to permanently remove them from the battle.

Tips

  • Since Atomos only begins vacuuming up characters who are dead, keeping all four party members alive—or reviving fallen allies immediately—prevents them from being permanently swallowed.
  • Inflicting Sleep on Atomos using the Spellblade ability Sleep or the White Magic spell Sleep can render the boss completely unable to act.
  • This dungeon sinks into the sea immediately upon defeating Atomos, so collect all treasure chests before interacting with the generator at the top.
