Surgate Castle

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Kingdom in the second world

Surgate Castle is the home fortress of King Kelger, located in the Second World. The party visits the castle to seek Kelger's aid and learns how to obtain the Levitation spell Float to bypass hazardous terrain in X-Death's castle. Exploring the castle allows players to solve a brief book-sorting puzzle in the library to unlock hidden treasure rooms.

Items

  • Float: in King Kelger's quarters, found inside a book on the table after speaking with him
  • 5000 Gil: chest in the lower storeroom reachable after clearing the library puzzle
  • Hermit's Robe: chest in the hidden storeroom

Secrets

  • Returning the misplaced books on the library desks to their proper alphabetical shelves opens a pathway leading down to the castle's treasury and storerooms.
  • Entering the courtyard and interacting with the hidden passage near the castle walls leads to a secluded crate containing a Speed Shake.

Tips

  • Be sure to acquire the Float spell here before attempting the Island Shrine or X-Death's Castle, as it prevents damage from lava and damaging floor tiles.
