Jachol Cave

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Cave near the desert

Jachol Cave is an optional cavern located near the town of Jachol on the southwestern continent of the first world. Exploring deep into the cave leads past switch-puzzle corridors to a long, empty tunnel that connects directly into the basement of Castle Bal.

Items

  • Shuriken: in a treasure chest in the back chamber after the switch room
  • Tent: in a treasure chest in the back chamber after the switch room
  • Blitz Whip: in a treasure chest in the back chamber after the switch room

Secrets

  • In the room filled with skull switches, pressing the single switch that remains after the others disappear unlocks the door to the treasure room.
  • Climbing the sheer wall at the end of the long interior path leads straight into Bal Castle's basement, granting early access to the Odin summon battle in World 3.

Enemies

Tips

  • Skull Eaters in this cave grant 5 Ability Points (ABP) per encounter, making this one of the premier ABP grinding spots in World 1.
  • Skull Eaters can instantly wipe out characters with high physical damage; use the Geomancer's !Gaia ability to defeat them safely without triggering counterattacks.
