The party sails through this narrow waterway using the dragon-pulled ship unlocked at the pirate hideout. As you navigate the canal, a whirlpool traps the vessel and forces a confrontation with an aquatic monster. Defeating the boss clears the passage, but a scripted event separates the party from the dragon and washes the ship toward the ship graveyard.

Enemies
Karlabos
Weak to LightningSteal / drop Potion

Boss fought at the whirlpool that attacks using Tentacle and Tailscrew to reduce a target's HP to single digits

Sucker
Weak to LightningSteal / drop Potion

Common aquatic enemy appearing in groups along the canal

Octokraken
Weak to LightningSteal / drop Potion

Cephalopod encounter vulnerable to electrical magic

Tips
  • Equip the Black Mage with the Thunder spell to exploit the elemental weakness shared by all enemies and the boss in this area.
