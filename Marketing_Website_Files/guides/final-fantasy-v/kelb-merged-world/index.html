Kelb (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the village after the merge

Kelb remains accessible in the Merged World after the worlds combine, though there are no major mandatory story events located here. Players can return to this werewolf village to purchase equipment and use the local services if they did not do so earlier. It serves primarily as a peaceful rest stop and transit point in the northwestern region of the map.

Shops and people

  • Armor Shop: sells Gaia Gear, Tiger Mask, Hypno Crown, Bone Mail, Power Sash, Diamond Plate
  • Weapon Shop: sells Killer Bow, War Hammer, Daimyo's Katana, Radiant Wand, Fire Rod, Ice Rod, Thunder Rod
  • Magic Shop: sells Poison, Drain, Bio, Flare, Mini, Esuna, Reflect, Curaga, Shell, Silence
  • Item Shop: sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Maiden's Kiss, Eye Drops, Antidote, Soft, Tent

Tips

  • The well-known trick of having the werewolf in the top house restore your party with mutton is still available if you need free healing.
