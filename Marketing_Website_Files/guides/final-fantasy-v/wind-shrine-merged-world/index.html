Wind Shrine (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the shrine after the merge

In the Merged World, the Wind Shrine stands abandoned with no active crystals or story objectives remaining. Players can optionally return here to fight low-level monsters or speak with the remaining scholar. It serves primarily as a nostalgic landmark with no major bosses or new key items.
