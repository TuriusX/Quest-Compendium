Ancient Library

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Researching the crystals

The party visits the Ancient Library to rescue Mid, the grandson of the engineer Cid, who is trapped in the depths beneath the shelves. Navigating the shifting bookcases requires solving path puzzles and fighting monsters to reach the lowest chamber. After defeating the boss Byblos, Mid is rescued, allowing Cid to modify the Fire-Powered Ship so the party can travel more freely.

Items

  • Ether: In a treasure chest found along the moving bookcase pathways
  • Stealth Switch: In a hidden treasure chest reached via a secret path in the bookshelves

Secrets

  • Examine the books on top of the shelves in the main entrance area to trigger humorous or informative reading prompts.

Tips

  • Bring a character with the Learning ability equipped to learn several useful Blue Magic spells such as Level 5 Death, Aero 2, and Magic Hammer.
  • Cast fire-elemental magic or use Ifrit against Byblos to exploit his weakness and defeat him quickly.
