Karnak (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the town after the merge

Karnak can be revisited in the Merged World after the worlds combine, though the nearby castle remains destroyed. The town functions as an accessible shopping and recovery hub where players can purchase gear and high-tier magic from earlier in the game. It is a quiet stop primarily used for stocking up on supplies or picking up spells missed during World 1.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: sells Mythril Sword, Great Sword, War Hammer, Javelin, Kunai, Moonring Blade
  • Armor Shop: sells Mythril Shield, Iron Helm, Bronze Armor, Brigandine, Kenpo Gi, Silver Glasses
  • Item Shop: sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Maiden's Kiss, Soft, Antidote, Eye Drops
  • Inn: costs 10 GP to fully restore HP and MP
  • Magic Shop: sells Fire 2, Blizzard 2, Thunder 2, Cure 2, Raise, Antidote, Sleep, Confuse

Tips

  • This is a safe and convenient location to purchase Mid-tier elemental spells (Fira, Blizzara, Thundara) or basic equipment if you neglected to buy them in World 1.
