Karnak Castle

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Escaping the exploding castle

After the fire crystal shatters, the party is locked in the castle dungeon as a 10-minute self-destruct timer starts ticking down. You must backtrack up through the burning halls to the entrance, battling forced encounters and looting high-tier treasure chests before time runs out. Escaping before the timer hits zero is mandatory to prevent a Game Over.

Items

  • 2000 Gil MISSABLE: In a treasure chest in the east wing basement
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest on the basement level
  • Ribbon MISSABLE: Inside the treasure chest guarded by a monster-in-a-box on the upper floor
  • Shuriken MISSABLE: In a treasure chest located in the castle storehouse
  • Main Gauche MISSABLE: In a monster-in-a-box chest along the escape route
  • Elf Cape MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest on the castle ramparts
  • Esuna MISSABLE: In a treasure chest in the dungeon area

Tips

  • Running from battles using the Thief's Flee or Smoke ability saves crucial time on the 10-minute countdown.
  • Learn the Blue Magic spell Death Claw from Iron Claw at the castle exit before finishing the battle.
  • Equip the Thief's Sprint ability to double your movement speed through the corridors.
