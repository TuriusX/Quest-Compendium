Interdimensional Rift

FINAL FANTASY V guide · The final dungeon of the game

The Interdimensional Rift is the final dungeon of the game, accessed through the Void at the center of the world map. The party must traverse several distinct sub-areas resembling earlier locations, defeat Exdeath's strongest servants, and reach the core of the Void to face Neo Exdeath.

Items

  • Ragnarok: Defeat Shinryu inside the treasure chest in the final crystal cloister
  • Apocalypse: Dropped by the boss Necrophobe in the final area

Secrets

  • The ultimate super-boss Shinryu is hidden inside a fake treasure chest located in the crystalline section just before the final save point.
  • The super-boss Omega roams around visibly in the cave/waterfall section of the Rift and initiates battle upon physical contact.

Tips

  • Equip Coral Rings on every character before opening the chest containing Shinryu to nullify its opening Tidal Wave.
  • Cast Float on the entire party before walking into the battle with Catastrophe so he wastes turns casting 100 Gs instead of attacking.
  • Using the Spellblade ability with Thundaga combined with Rapid Fire is an extremely effective way to defeat Omega.
  • The Movers in the final crystal area do not drop EXP, making them ideal for mastering jobs without leveling up.
