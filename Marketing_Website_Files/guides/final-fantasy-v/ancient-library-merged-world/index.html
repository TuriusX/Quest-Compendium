Ancient Library (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the library after the merge

After the worlds merge, you return to the Ancient Library to reunite with Cid and Mid and learn about the Sealed Weapons. Following the conversation on the roof, the library is pulled into the Void and permanently destroyed. You must escape westward toward the desert quickly before the library vanishes from the world map.

Items

  • Sealed Tome pages: Received automatically during the scene on the rooftop

Secrets

  • The rooftop scene triggers the library's destruction, making any uncollected items or missed events from previous visits impossible to access afterward.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
NoneNo random encounters take place inside the library during this brief visit.

Shops and people

  • Scholars: Free HP/MP restoration via the restorative jar

Tips

  • Finish any business in the surrounding region and heal up before speaking to the scholars on the roof, as you will not be able to return to the library once the cutscene ends.
