Castle Exdeath

FINAL FANTASY V guide · The first major confrontation

Castle Exdeath is visited in the second world and initially appears as a dead end until the illusion is broken by Kelger. The party must climb the nightmarish fortress to confront Exdeath at the top before he executes his plan. Navigating the castle involves operating skull switches, crossing lava hazards, and fighting several boss encounters.

Items

  • Hayate Bow MISSABLE: Treasure chest in the upper dungeon floors
  • Ice Shield MISSABLE: Treasure chest found past the illusion room
  • Kotetsu MISSABLE: Treasure chest found inside the castle
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Treasure chest in a side room

Secrets

  • Stepping on a skull switch in the moving floor room stops the bridge, letting you access the central chests.
  • Walking into a dead end on the third floor triggers an event where Kelger uses his remaining strength to dispel Exdeath's illusion, revealing the true castle.

Tips

  • Make sure to equip the Thief's Steal ability during the Gilgamesh battle to acquire the missable Genji Helm.
  • Equipping the Geomancer's "Find Pits" or "Light Step" abilities protects the party from the damage floors in the lower levels.
  • Defeating the optional boss Carbuncle awards the Carbuncle summon spell.
  • Castle Exdeath cannot be revisited after completing World 2, so collect all treasures before defeating Exdeath.
  • Make sure you collected all treasure chests inside Exdeath's Castle in World 2, as the interior is completely inaccessible once the worlds merge.
  • Do not approach the castle until you are ready to view the cutscenes involving Exdeath and the Void, though you will be able to leave the area afterward to finish sidequests.
