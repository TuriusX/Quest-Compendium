Phoenix Tower

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Climbing the tower for the summon

The Phoenix Tower is an optional 30-floor dungeon located in the southwestern desert of the Merged World, accessible only by black chocobo. Players must climb all 30 floors without encountering any save points while choosing between urns and walls to progress and gather treasures. Reaching the top triggers a cutscene with Lenna and Hiryuu that awards the Phoenix summon spell.

Items

  • Phoenix: Reaching the top (30th floor) after the story sequence
  • 5,000 Gil: In an urn on the 5th floor
  • 10,000 Gil: In an urn on the 10th floor
  • 15,000 Gil: In an urn on the 15th floor
  • 20,000 Gil: In an urn on the 20th floor
  • 25,000 Gil: In an urn on the 25th floor
  • Avenge Bow: In the left urn on the 30th floor
  • Ribbon: In the right urn on the 30th floor

Secrets

  • The stairs to the next floor on floors without urns are hidden inside one of the wall sections; examine the left or right wall indented arches to reveal the way up.
  • Opening any treasure pot triggers an immediate fight against a Magic Pot, but giving it Elixirs causes it to flee and yield 100 Ability Points (ABP).

Tips

  • Bring a large supply of Elixirs if you intend to open the urns, as feeding the Magic Pots is one of the fastest ABP grinding methods in the game.
  • Cast Teleport or use the Flee command if random encounters become too dangerous, as Teleport instantly evacuates you from the tower.
  • Ensure you have a Thief or the Secret Paths ability equipped to make spotting the hidden door mechanisms much easier.
