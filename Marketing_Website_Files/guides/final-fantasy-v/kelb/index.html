Kelb

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Village of the Werewolves

Kelb is a village populated by werewolves, located on Galuf's World. Upon entering, the party is ambushed by werewolves until Galuf's friend Kelger recognizes him, welcomes the party into his manor, and eventually opens the northern gate to allow passage to the Valley of Dragons.

Items

  • Requiem (Bard song): learned from the werewolf dancing in the northeast house
  • Cornucopia: check the pot in the upper-left corner inside Kelger's manor

Secrets

  • Press the bottom-left well in town, and an old man will pop out asking for a frog (Kornago); capturing a Kornago outside and bringing it to him rewards you with the Kornago Gourd.

Shops and people

  • Magic Shop: sells Level 3 White, Black, and Time magic spells
  • Armor Shop: sells Gaia Gear, Sage's Surplice, Wizard's Hat, Green Beret, Mythril Helm, Mythril Armor, Mythril Shield, and Mythril Gloves
  • Weapon Shop: sells Main Gauche, Mythril Sword, Mythril Spear, Killer Bow, and Silver Harp

Tips

  • Inspect the werewolf cooking inside the northern house to receive a free full party restore and eight free potions.
  • Don't miss learning the Requiem song here, as it deals massive non-elemental damage to undead enemies for a very low MP cost.
