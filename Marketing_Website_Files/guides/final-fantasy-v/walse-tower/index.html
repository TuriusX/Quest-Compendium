Walse Tower

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Protecting the Water Crystal

Bartz and his party scale the tower to reach the top where the Water Crystal is housed, attempting to stop King Walse from using an amplifier to force the crystal's power. Along the way, the party witnesses a meteorite crash nearby, and upon reaching the crystal chamber, they must fight the monster Garula before the Water Crystal shatters. The tower begins sinking into the sea after the crystal breaks, forcing an escape sequence back to the surface.

Items

  • Silk Robe MISSABLE: Treasure chest on the 4th floor
  • Maiden's Kiss MISSABLE: Treasure chest on the 5th floor
  • Silver Armlet MISSABLE: Treasure chest on the 8th floor
  • Ether MISSABLE: Treasure chest on the 9th floor

Secrets

  • A vine on the 4th floor allows climbing up along the outside of the tower to reach otherwise inaccessible ledges containing treasure chests.

Tips

  • Equip the party with the Toad spell or stockpile Maiden's Kisses before climbing, as the Elf Toads frequently inflict the Toad status.
  • Using the Knight's Guard or Protect ability against Garula helps mitigate the heavy physical damage from its counterattacks.
  • Collect every treasure chest on the way up, as the tower collapses into the sea after the crystal shatters and cannot be explored again in the same state.
