Istory

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Village of the Chocobo

Istory is a peaceful village in the northwestern area of the world map, accessible in the first world after obtaining the black chocobo or airship. Players visit to purchase high-level elemental magic and to acquire an optional summon magic spell in the surrounding falls.

Items

  • Toad MISSABLE: In the pasture area behind a fence, received after chasing a frog into the water
  • Flame Ring MISSABLE: Hidden in the flower beds near the village entrance

Secrets

  • Chocobo Village access: Walk around the northwest forest surrounding Istory on the world map to encounter and defeat the Ramuh summon battle.

Shops and people

  • Magic Shop: Sells Level 3 Black Magic (Fire 2, Blizzard 2, Thunder 2) and White Magic (Cure 2, Raise, Protect)
  • Armor Shop: Sells Iron Armor, Iron Shield, Iron Helm, Kenpo Gi, and Silver Armlet

Tips

  • Make sure to buy the Toad spell and collect the Flame Ring before triggering the end of World 1, as the village cannot be revisited in later worlds.
