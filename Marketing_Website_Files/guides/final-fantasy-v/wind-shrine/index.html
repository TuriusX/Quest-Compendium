Wind Shrine

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Investigating the wind's disappearance

The party travels to the Wind Shrine northeast of Tycoon to find out why the wind has stopped blowing and to locate King Tycoon. Inside, they navigate through monster-filled floors until reaching the top chamber where the Wind Crystal shatters. After defeating the boss Wing Raptor, the party receives the crystal shards, unlocking their first set of jobs.

Items

  • Tent: 1F, in a chest in the room to the left
  • Broadsword: 2F, in a chest in the southern room
  • Leather Cap: 3F, in a chest
  • Staff: 4F, in a chest

Secrets

  • On 1F, talk to the wounded Tycoon chancellor to receive 5 Potions.
  • On 2F, there is a recovery spring in the far left room where the party can fully restore HP and MP.

Tips

  • Do not attack Wing Raptor while its wings are closed, or it will counter with Wing Gale to drastically reduce party HP.
  • Save your game and rest using a Tent at the save point on 4F before facing the boss on the top floor.
