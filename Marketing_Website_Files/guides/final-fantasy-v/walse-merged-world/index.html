Walse (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the town after the merge

After the worlds merge, Walse Castle has sunk into the sea and cannot be entered directly from the surface, but Walse Town remains accessible on the world map. While no mandatory story events occur here in the Merged World, players can return to pick up equipment or spells from the local shops that may have been skipped earlier. The sunken castle beneath the waves can eventually be explored later once submarine travel is unlocked.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Battleaxe (650 Gil), Long Sword (480 Gil), Dagger (300 Gil)
  • Armor Shop: Iron Armor (1000 Gil), Iron Shield (390 Gil), Iron Helm (315 Gil), Kenpo Gi (450 Gil), Silver Armlet (300 Gil)
  • Magic Shop: Blizzard (150 Gil), Thunder (150 Gil), Poisona (300 Gil), Silence (280 Gil), Protect (280 Gil), Chocobo (300 Gil)
  • Item Shop: Potion (50 Gil), Phoenix Down (1000 Gil), Gold Needle (500 Gil), Maiden's Kiss (60 Gil), Eyedrops (50 Gil), Antidote (30 Gil)

Tips

  • If you missed the level 1 White, Black, or Time Magic spells earlier in the adventure, the shops in Walse Town still stock their original inventory.
