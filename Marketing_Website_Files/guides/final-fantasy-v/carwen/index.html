Carwen

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Harbor town visited before heading toward the mountains ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Carwen is a port town visited after escaping the Ship Graveyard. The party explores the settlement to re-equip, learn that ships are stranded due to dying winds, and receive word of King Tycoon ascending North Mountain toward a stranded dragon.

  • Armor: Bronze Helm (250 Gil), Bronze Shield (290 Gil), Cotton Robe (300 Gil), Copper Cuirass (350 Gil), Bronze Armor (400 Gil)
  • Magic: Sleep (300 Gil), Silence (280 Gil), Protect (280 Gil)

  • Inn: Rest for the party (10 Gil)

Tips
  • Equip the hidden Frost Rod on a Black Mage or break it as an item in battle to deal high ice damage against North Mountain enemies.
