Castle Tycoon

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Returning home

Upon obtaining the wind drake, the party can return to Castle Tycoon to rest and initiate story scenes focused on Lenna and Faris. Exploring the castle at this point reveals numerous previously locked or missed treasure rooms stocked with equipment and restorative items. After triggering the emotional reunion cutscene in the royal quarters, the party can fully raid the castle vaults before heading toward the next destination.

Items

  • Ashura MISSABLE: in a treasure chest within the castle's storeroom
  • Shuriken MISSABLE: in a treasure chest located in the castle vaults
  • Diamond Bell MISSABLE: in a hidden room accessed via a switch in the castle
  • Cabin MISSABLE: in a treasure chest inside the castle's interior rooms
  • Ether MISSABLE: in a pot/chest within the castle interior
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: found in a storage container inside the castle

Secrets

  • Secret treasury passage | Search the walls on the lower levels and hidden switch pathways to access rooms packed with katanas, bells, and shurikens.
  • Nighttime campfire sequence | Sleeping in Lenna's room triggers a private cutscene between the sisters Faris and Lenna reminiscing about their childhood and their father.

Shops and people

  • Chancellor: offers free full rest and recovery for the entire party when spoken to inside the castle

Tips

  • Loot every treasure chest in Castle Tycoon during World 1, as the castle becomes completely inaccessible later in the game.
