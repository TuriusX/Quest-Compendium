Solitary Island Temple

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Exploring the optional dungeon

The Solitary Island Temple is a storyline dungeon accessible in the merged world via the airship or submarine, located on an isolated landmass. The party traverses vents, traps, and conveyor-like pathways to reach the depths and retrieve one of the four legendary stone tablets. Conquering the temple unseals the second set of sealed weapons in Kuza Castle and grants access to the powerful fork tower events.

Items

  • Rising Sun: In a treasure chest found along the path through the temple
  • Protect Ring: Found in a treasure chest on the lower levels
  • Second Tablet: Obtained automatically after defeating the boss at the end of the temple

Tips

  • When fighting the boss Wendigo, use single-target attacks rather than multi-target spells, as hitting a fake image triggers a harsh counterattack and reshuffles their positions.
  • Bring a character with the Thief's "Find Pits" or "Light Step" passive abilities to safely navigate hidden pitfall traps and damaging grates throughout the structure.
