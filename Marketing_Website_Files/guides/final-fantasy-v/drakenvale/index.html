Drakenvale

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Mountain of the dragons
← All areas in this guide

The party climbs this treacherous mountain to search for the Dragon Grass needed to cure Hiryu, Lenna's dying wind drake. Along the way, the party must navigate rocky paths covered in bone fields and face the summit's overgrown flora. At the peak, you must fight and defeat the corrupted Dragon Grass boss to secure a healthy clipping.

Enemies
Dragon Zombie
Weak to Fire, HolySteal / drop Dragon Fang

An undead dragon that can afflict party members with deadly status conditions.

Drippy
Steal / drop Hi-Potion

Uses Magic Hammer to deplete a party member's MP by half.

Bonemail
Weak to Holy, FireSteal / drop Hi-Potion

An undead skeletal foe that appears frequently on the bony mountain paths.

Dragon Grass
Steal / drop Elixir

The boss at the summit that summons Dragon Flowers to inflict various status ailments on your party.

Tips
  • Equipping the Bone Mail gives massive defense, but it makes the wearer undead, causing healing spells and items to damage them instead.
  • Blue Mages can learn the Blue Magic spell "Magic Hammer" from Drippy by manipulating it or letting it cast the move on them.
  • Heavy multi-target physical attacks or powerful summon magic like Titan can quickly eliminate the Dragon Flowers summoned during the boss battle.
