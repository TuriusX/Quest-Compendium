Karnak

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Town near the Fire Crystal

Karnak is a technologically advanced town powered by the Fire Crystal, situated near Karnak Castle and the Fire-Powered Ship. Upon first entering, attempting to buy items at the shops causes the party to be arrested and thrown into Karnak Castle's dungeon. After the events in the Fire-Powered Ship and Castle, the town becomes fully accessible for shopping and learning lore.

Items

  • Flame Rod: Hidden in a barrel behind the weapon and armor shops, accessible by walking around the exterior path
  • Plumed Hat: Found in a barrel in the outdoor area near the upper-left of town
  • Potion: In a barrel near the entrance of the town

Secrets

  • The piano for Karnak is inside the pub on the second floor; playing it is required to master the Piano Master sidequest.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Mythril Sword, Great Bow, Mythril Dagger, and various rods
  • Armor Shop: Sells Mythril Armor, Mythril Shield, Mythril Helm, Kenpo Gi, and Silver Armlet
  • Magic Shop: Sells Level 3 Black, White, and Time Magic including Fira, Blizzara, Thundara, Cura, Raise, Gravity, and Haste

Tips

  • Buy multiple elemental rods (especially Flame and Frost Rods) here, as breaking them in combat deals massive magical damage to bosses in the upcoming Fire-Powered Ship and Karnak Castle.
  • Do not worry about the steep discount or being arrested when you first shop here, as it is a mandatory story event that moves the plot to the prison.
