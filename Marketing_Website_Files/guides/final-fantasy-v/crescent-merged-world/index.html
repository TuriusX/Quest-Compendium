Crescent (Merged World)

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Visiting the town after the merge

After the worlds merge, Crescent remains accessible on its island and serves primarily as an optional stop to collect late-game rewards. You can visit the Bard's house to learn new piano skills and songs that were previously unavailable. Additionally, checking back here allows you to obtain powerful equipment by interacting with the town's remaining inhabitants.

Items

  • Power Harp: inside the Bard's house, rewarded for playing the piano

Secrets

  • Playing the piano inside the Bard's house after mastering all previous world pianos unlocks the Sinewy Etude and Hero's Rhyme songs from the Bard.

Shops and people

  • Magic Shop: Life, Curaga, Shell, Protect, Esuna, Blink
  • Armor Shop: Power Armlet, Silver Armlet, Diamond Armlet, Circlet, Black Cowl, Tiger Mask
  • Weapon Shop: Flame Tongue, Icebrand, Blood Sword, Kotetsu, Morning Star, Air Knife

Tips

  • Make sure you have played every other piano throughout both worlds before visiting the Bard here, or he will not teach you the final song.
