Lix

FINAL FANTASY V guide · Bartz's hometown

Lix is Bartz's secluded hometown, surrounded by mountains and accessible after acquiring the black chocobo in World 1. Visiting this village triggers several childhood flashback scenes regarding Bartz's parents, Stella and Dorgan, and provides access to exceptionally cheap supplies. It eventually becomes inaccessible later in the story when the area is consumed by the Void.

Items

  • Tempting Harp MISSABLE: Inside Bartz's old childhood home, inspect the table or desk to trigger a flashback with the scholar
  • Music Box item/song (Alluring Air / Romeo's Ballad) MISSABLE: Play the pink music box inside Bartz's childhood house, then speak to the bard in town

Secrets

  • Resting in Bartz's childhood home triggers a cutscene with his mother Stella and gives you a free full rest.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells standard items like Potions, Hi-Potions, and Ethers at half the standard purchase price
  • Magic Shop: Sells Ninjutsu scrolls (Fire Scroll, Water Scroll, Lightning Scroll) and Esuna

Tips

  • Stock up heavily on consumable ninja throwing scrolls and basic healing supplies here, as the vendor prices are discounted by 50% compared to other towns.
  • Visit Lix during World 1, as the town becomes permanently unavailable in the merged world (World 3) when it gets swallowed into the Rift.
