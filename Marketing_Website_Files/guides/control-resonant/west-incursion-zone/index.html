West Incursion Zone

CONTROL Resonant guide · Investigated to deal with reality distortion and track the incursion fault ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

In this distorted sector of Manhattan, Dylan Faden tracks an Altered World Event disturbance known as the Patterning and looks for traces of Jesse. Progression requires completing the quest "The Incursion Fault" to unlock the Gravity Shift traversal mechanic, which allows navigating flipped architecture and crossing the chasm to the Patterned Theater to confront a major boss.
