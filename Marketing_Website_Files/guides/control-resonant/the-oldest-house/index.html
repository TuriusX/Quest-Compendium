The Oldest House

CONTROL Resonant guide · Dylan awakens and retrieves Aberrant during the prologue ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Dylan Faden is brought out of his long coma when Jesse Faden embeds the Aberrant into his chest right before she vanishes. Guided by the Board, Dylan must claim this transforming melee weapon and choose an initial form inside his cell. Afterwards, he fights through invading Hiss in Central Executive to break out of the facility and reach Manhattan.

Achievements here
Assigned to AcceptUnlocks automatically during the opening prologue inside The Oldest House when Dylan pulls the Aberrant weapon from his chest.
Sibling ReunionFinish the main story quest The House in Distress by returning to the Oldest House and completing the Fault Ritual.
The First LinkComplete the story mission The Hedron Link by rescuing Pope and destroying the first Pattern Link to the Oldest House.
Astral AdeptInteract with the large pillar in The Gap to fully upgrade any single Aberrant Form.
Stop it at the SourceProgress through the main story mission "Sever the Hedron Links" and destroy all the Hedron Links.
Graffiti ArtistFinish the entire mural in The Gap by defeating all six Resonants and beating the main story.
Dog's Best FriendFind the Mysterious Dog and retrieve her missing toy in all regions, then visit her in The Gap to adopt her.
