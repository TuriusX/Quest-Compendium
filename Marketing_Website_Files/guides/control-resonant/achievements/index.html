Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Completing the karaoke sequence in the side mission "The Party Won't Stop" with incorrect lyrics: "Be What You Want to Be" achievement becomes locked out for the rest of that playthrough without reloading a previous manual save

Recommended order
  1. Begin the campaign in The Oldest House and Downtown; grab early upgrades, clear initial tutorial objectives, and interact with the Downtown terminal Q&A.
  2. Progress through Central to establish the FBC Field Office, obtain the Level 1 Keycard, and activate regional travel points.
  3. Advance through the Evacuation Zone story missions to rescue Arish, acquire key traversal upgrades, and access the FBC task board at the Field Office.
  4. Explore the West Incursion Zone to acquire the Shift traversal power from the Parking Garage Fault and defeat the regional Resonant (The Dancer).
  5. Head into the Underpass and subterranean routes to unlock Reach, conquer the Physicist Resonant, and finish the local Fault Rituals.
  6. When tackling the optional side quest "The Party Won't Stop", ensure you choose the correct song lyrics during the karaoke performance to secure the one missable achievement ("Be What You Want to Be").
  7. Finish Pope's Research questline and proceed past the final story threshold to defeat the Hedron.
  8. In post-game free roam, backtrack through all previous zones to wrap up remaining regional Resonants, max out Aberrant forms, clear Mold incidents, and finish 20 FBC tasks.
