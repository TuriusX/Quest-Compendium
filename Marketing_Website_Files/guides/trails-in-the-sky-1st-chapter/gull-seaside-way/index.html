Gull Seaside Way

Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter guide · Walking along the coast toward Ruan and the orphanage ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

This coastal highway connects the City of Ruan to Manoria Village, Mercia Orphanage, and the trail leading to Jenis Royal Academy. During Chapter 2, Estelle and Joshua traverse this route to reach the orphanage alongside Kloe and handle bracer monster extermination requests. Players navigate paved sections and sandy shores while fighting aquatic monsters and collecting shorefront treasures.
