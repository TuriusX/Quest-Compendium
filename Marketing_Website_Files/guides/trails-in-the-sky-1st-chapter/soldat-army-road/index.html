Soldat Army Road

Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter guide · Approaching the military base after deciphering Dorothy's photo ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Estelle and Joshua travel north along this secured military road connecting Ritter Roadway to Leiston Fortress to investigate Dorothy's photograph during the main quest "Prof.'s Whereabouts". After approaching the main gates and being rebuffed by Major Cid, an orbal blackout occurs, prompting the party to turn around to formulate an infiltration plan.

Enemies
Armored Rabbit

Can unleash the deadly physical move Hippity-Bob after becoming excited.

Bane Cobra

Frequently calls for reinforcements to overwhelm the party with poisonous attacks.

Creepy Sheep

Uses attacks such as Baaaad Touch and can raise a party member's attack power while lowering their defense.

Cronocider
Weak to Earth, Water

Heavily armored foe that casts Golden Armor to boost parameters and can inflict Faint.

Grass Hopper

Inflicts minor speed-down effects with regular strikes and can lick wounds to recover health.

War Mantis
Weak to Fire, WindSteal / drop Prickly Seed

Possesses Cancel Cutter to interrupt art casting.

Tips
  • When asked an investigative deduction question by Joshua after leaving the fortress gates, select that the Royal Guardsmen are being framed to earn full bonus BP.
