Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter achievement guide

How to unlock every achievement, which ones you can miss, and the best order to get them all. Tick them off as you go.

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Rolent Checkpoint (Verne Gate): Locks out all Rolent region sidequests, missable dialogues, and unopened regional treasure chests.
  • Bose Checkpoint (Krone Pass): Prevents return to the Bose region, permanently locking out incomplete Bose quests and missed Carnelia volumes.
  • Ruan Checkpoint (Air-Letten Gate): Permanently closes off the Ruan region and Lake Valleria, locking out the fishing achievement and remaining Ruan chests.
  • Zeiss Checkpoint (Sanktheim Gate): Seals off the Zeiss region and central factory requests.
  • Entering the Final Dungeon beneath Grancel Castle: Final boundary past which all shops, Grancel city sidequests, Quartz syntheses, and Carnelia weapon exchanges become permanently inaccessible.

Recommended order
  1. City of Rolent | Rolent, Prologue - Clear all guild noticeboard tasks and hidden requests with full bonus points, examine every opened chest twice for chest messages, collect Carnelia chapters 1–2, and defeat Shining Poms for mass sepith.
  2. City of Bose | Bose, Chapter 1 - Pick up Carnelia chapter 3 before entering the mine, complete all sidequests and inspect the hijacked airliner fully for bonus Bracer Points, and defeat the Amberl Tower Shining Poms.
  3. City of Ruan | Ruan, Chapter 2 - Complete all local quests and collect Carnelia chapters 4–7; at Lake Valleria, ensure you fish all 10 times to unlock "Hobbyist".
  4. City of Zeiss | Zeiss, Chapter 3 - Collect Carnelia chapters 8–9, complete all regional assignments without advancing the story prematurely, and take Olivier back directly during the story detour to secure "The Perfect Package".
  5. City of Grancel | Grancel, Final Chapter - Purchase or trade for the final Carnelia volumes (10 & 11) to trade for the ultimate weapon, obtain all missing Quartz, reach max Bracer rank (Junior Bracer Rank 1), and hoard at least 3,000 of every sepith type.
  6. City of Grancel | Grancel, Final Chapter (Endgame Prep) - Defeat 2nd Lieutenant Lorence during the encounter preceding the final dungeon ("Preemptive Powerhouse"), then create a permanent backup save right before assembling your final assault squad.
  7. City of Grancel | Grancel, Final Chapter (Cleanup & Endings) - Form an all-female support party (Schera/Kloe/Tita) to enter the dungeon for "Ladies' Man," reload your backup save and assemble an all-male support team (Olivier/Agate/Zin) for "Reverse Harem," and defeat the final boss encounter without triggering any S-Crafts to unlock "S-Craft?
