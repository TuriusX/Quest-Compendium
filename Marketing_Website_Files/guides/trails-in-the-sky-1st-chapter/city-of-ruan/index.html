Estelle and Joshua arrive in the seaside port of Ruan to report to the local Bracer Guild branch, only to be swept into regional investigations spanning the harbor and surrounding areas. Players navigate across the city's signature drawbridge, take on jobs from the request board, and unravel local criminal disturbances before departing for the next territory.

Don't miss 0/5

Shops and people
  • O'Neil Duty-Free Shop: Sells accessories, miscellaneous wares, and copies of the Liberl News
  • Joan Arms & Guards: Sells armor and weaponry upgrades for party members

Tips
  • Always check the Bracer Guild bulletin board with receptionist Jean and visit the local general store whenever major events wrap up so you do not miss fleeting quest deadlines or new issues of the regional newspaper.
