Hades II achievement guide

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • None: There are no missable achievements or points of no return; all tasks, prophecies, and resources can be pursued infinitely across repeated runs.

Recommended order
  1. The Crossroads: Complete initial introductions, select your first Nocturnal Arm, unlock the cauldron, and prepare for your first descent into the Underworld.
  2. Erebus: Learn basic combat patterns, gather Moly, confront Hecate, and advance early Underworld progress.
  3. Oceanus: Navigate the watery depths, collect Lotus, face the Siren band (Scylla and the Sirens), and upgrade basic Arcana Cards.
  4. The Fields of Mourning: Explore open layouts, gather Myrtle, unlock side encounters, and defeat the Infernal Beast (Cerberus).
  5. Tartarus: Traverse the deepest chambers, survive Chronos's stronghold, battle Chronos, and secure your first Underworld victory.
  6. The Surface: Perform the Permeation of Witching-Wards incantation at the cauldron to unlock the surface path and lift the wards.
  7. City of Ephyra: Gather Moss and spirit pylon charges, navigate the corrupted streets, and defeat Polyphemus the Cyclops.
  8. The Rift of Thessaly: Board the naval warships, brave encounters with sea monsters, gather Driftwood, and defeat Charybdis and Eris.
  9. The Crossroads (Post-run return): Brew high-tier cauldron incantations, gift Nectar and Ambrosia to develop bonds, unlock all Weapon Aspects, and summon Animal Familiars.
  10. Erebus (Subsequent runs): Fulfill Fated List prophecies, harvest resources using all gathering tools, and clear Charon's gold and shop requirements.
  11. Oceanus (Subsequent runs): Optimize boon combinations, activate Chaos Trials via the Pitch-Black Stone, and trigger unique mid-run dialogue events.
  12. The Fields of Mourning (Subsequent runs): Uncover all hidden reward caches, finish Olympian relationship milestones, and experiment with advanced Boon duos.
  13. Tartarus (Subsequent runs): Activate higher Fear ranks using the Oath of the Unseen, defeat Chronos repeatedly with every Nocturnal Arm, and complete endgame prophecies.
  14. City of Ephyra (Subsequent runs): Clear city wards efficiently under time constraints, maximize surface gathering, and farm resources for remaining cosmetic and functional incantations.
  15. The Rift of Thessaly (Subsequent runs): Defeat Eris with all weapons, finish remaining surface trials on high Fear levels, and complete all remaining entries in the Fated List of Minor Prophecies.
