Oceanus

Hades II guide · The second region of the Underworld
← All areas in this guide

Oceanus is the flooded, industrial second region of the Underworld reached after clearing Erebus. Melinoë must fight through submerged chambers filled with slippery currents and marine-themed automata to reach the surface access or continue deeper. Navigating the water hazards and avoiding knockbacks into industrial machinery is essential for survival here.

Achievements here
Witch of the DepthsDefeat Scylla and the Sirens to successfully clear Oceanus.
Breaking Up the BandUse the Moon Hex to resurrect one of the defeated Sirens during the boss fight against Scylla.

Items

Enemies
Pinhead

Explodes upon death in a wide radius, which can trigger chain reactions among other hazards.

Needle

Fires rapid-burst projectile spreads from long range across flooded arenas.

Scylla and the Sirens

Region guardian encounter composed of Scylla on lead vocals, Roxy on drums, and Jetty on guitar.

Shops and people
  • Charon: Sells boons, healing items, Pom of Power slices, and crafting reagents in exchange for Gold Crowns
  • Narcissus: Offers a choice of vanity gifts that grant boons, resources, or restorative items

Tips
  • Beware of the slippery water currents flowing across the floor, as they constantly pull Melinoë toward traps and enemy attack zones.
  • Target Roxy the drummer first during the Scylla encounter to eliminate her arena-wide ranged shockwaves early.
