Tartarus (Harabah)

Persona 3 Reload guide · September onwards

Harabah is the fifth major block of Tartarus, opening from Floor 173 up to Floor 226 after the events of early September and expanding further in November. Your objective is to climb through both tiers of the block, defeat the border Floor Guardians, and rescue lost civilians trapped within. Reaching the barriers on Floor 198 and Floor 226 serves as your progression milestones for the late-game months.

Items

  • Nihil Blade: Chests on Harabah floors
  • Homunculus: Rare golden chests on Harabah floors
  • Twilight Fragment: Glowing spots on Harabah floors and border platforms

Secrets

  • Rare golden-tinted Greed Shadows appear randomly on these floors; corner them by predicting their escape path at intersections to initiate combat for massive EXP and valuable coin drops.
  • Finding Monad Doors scattered throughout Harabah allows you to fight powerful boss-tier Shadow pairings to unlock major Tarot cards and high-tier equipment chests.

Tips

  • Enemies in Harabah heavily favor physical strike skills and elemental ma-dynes, so fuse high-level Personas that possess null or absorb affinities against physical damage.
  • Take full advantage of the Shift mechanic in standard encounters to cycle through party members and systematically exploit different elemental weaknesses in a single turn.
  • Save several Twilight Fragments to open the large Monad Passage doors found at the end of sections for the most valuable equipment drops in this tier.
