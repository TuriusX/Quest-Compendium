Club Escapade

Persona 3 Reload guide · June onwards

Club Escapade is an entertainment venue located in the Paulownia Mall that is accessible exclusively during the evening once your Courage reaches rank 2 (Ordinary). It hosts several important social services, vendors, and the Fortune Social Link, making it a critical evening destination. Players visit this area to buy valuable combat software, trade materials for rare gear, and advance social interactions.

Shops and people

  • Suspicious Man (Info Broker): Sells gameplay notes, Tartarus floor data, and URL access notes usable at the dorm shared PC
  • Mayoido Antiques (accessible via Club Escapade dance floor): Crafts powerful custom equipment and stat-boosting items using Tartarus gems and base personas
  • Luxury Bartender: Sells high-priced drink items that grant various stat-boosting or recovery effects

Tips

  • Increase your Courage stat to at least rank 2 early in the game so you can gain access past the bouncer at the club's entrance.
  • Visit the monk sitting on the upper lounge level during Thursday through Sunday nights to progress the Tower Social Link (Mutatsu) after unlocking him via the Strength Social Link.
  • Check the Info Broker's stock regularly as he adds new URL notes whenever major calendar events and Tartarus blocks unlock.
