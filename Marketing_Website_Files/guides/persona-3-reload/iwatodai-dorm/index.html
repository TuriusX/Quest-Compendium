Iwatodai Dorm

Persona 3 Reload guide · April onwards

Iwatodai Dorm serves as the main base of operations and living quarters for the protagonist and SEES members. Here, you can access your private room to study or sleep, interact with teammates to trigger hangout events, and use various communal facilities on each floor. You also return here after nighttime activities or Tartarus explorations to progress the calendar day.

Items

  • Umugi Potage: Purchase from the vending machine on the 2nd floor
  • Durian Soda: Purchase from the vending machine on the 3rd floor

Secrets

  • Starting in late May, you gain access to the dorm's rooftop garden, allowing you to plant and harvest various crops with your party members for unique consumable items.

Shops and people

  • 2nd Floor Vending Machine: Canned drinks that restore small amounts of SP or HP
  • 3rd Floor Vending Machine: Novelty canned sodas and juices that restore small amounts of HP

Tips

  • Using the 1st floor shared kitchen, reading together, or watching DVDs with teammates in the lounge permanently boosts their combat characteristics and raises your Social Stats.
  • Running software on the shared PC in the 1st floor lounge is an effective way to raise your Academics, Charm, or Courage, or to unlock new site notes for Tartarus.
