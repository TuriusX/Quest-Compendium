Tatsumi Port Island

Persona 3 Reload guide · May onwards

Tatsumi Port Island serves as the main hub where the protagonist spends daytime and evening hours outside of Tartarus. Players interact with party members, establish and level up Social Links, raise Social Stats, and purchase supplies for dungeon crawling. Starting in May, more locations and activities unlock, including exam preparation, new social circles, and club activities.

Items

  • Umugi Special: Wakatsu Kitchen at Iwatodai Strip Mall
  • Seafood Fried Rice: Wakatsu Kitchen at Iwatodai Strip Mall
  • Mystery Burger: Wild-duck Burger at Iwatodai Strip Mall
  • Weekend Burger: Wild-duck Burger at Iwatodai Strip Mall (available on weekends/holidays)
  • Prodigy Platter: Chagall Cafe at Paulownia Mall
  • Pheromone Coffee: Chagall Cafe at Paulownia Mall
  • Special Takoyaki: Octopia stand at Iwatodai Strip Mall
  • Muscle Drink: Aohige Pharmacy at Paulownia Mall
  • Medicine: Aohige Pharmacy at Paulownia Mall
  • Revival Bead: Aohige Pharmacy at Paulownia Mall
  • Odd Morsel: Shinshoudo Antiques at Paulownia Mall (opens in July)

Secrets

  • The Twilight Fragment dispenser near the bottom of the stairs in the Paulownia Mall fountain plaza awards fragments by examining blue sparkles scattered across the island.
  • Interacting with the shrine lady at Naganaki Shrine while carrying specific sums of money allows for fortune readings that boost Social Link affinity with a designated character.

Shops and people

  • Aohige Pharmacy: Sells healing items, status recovery medicine, and dungeon exploration consumables at Paulownia Mall.
  • Shinshoudo Antiques: Crafts special weapons, armor, and skill cards using Tartarus gemstones and Personas (available starting mid-July).
  • Police Station (Officer Kurosawa): Sells melee weapons, armor, footwear, and accessories at Paulownia Mall, updating inventory as the calendar advances.
  • Net Shopping (Sunday TV): Sells exclusive item bundles every Sunday through the TV in the Iwatodai Dorm lounge.
  • Game Panic: Offers arcade games at Paulownia Mall that boost the protagonist's Social Stats or Persona parameters depending on the daily rotation.
  • Be Blue V: Relives persona skill adjustments and allows party members to relearn forgotten skills (opens later in the summer).
  • Club Escapade: Hosts an informant who sells software notes unlocking dorm features, website URLs, and access to fortune telling at Paulownia Mall (requires Courage).

Tips

  • Always take advantage of the Saturday sale at Aohige Pharmacy for discounts on healing supplies before doing long weekend Tartarus excursions.
  • Working part-time at Chagall Cafe or Be Blue V on evenings where you have no night Social Links raises both Social Stats and your wallet.
  • Checking your shared dorm PC with software purchased from the Club Escapade informant permanently unlocks benefits like new menu items or stat gains.
