June

Persona 3 Reload guide · Midterm exams and the Priestess shadow

June brings the first major operation to rescue Fuuka Yamagishi inside Tartarus during the full moon, as well as the unlocking of Tartarus's second block, Arqa. Midterm exam results are posted early in the month, and Fuuka subsequently replaces Mitsuru as the team's battle support navigator. Use this month to push through Arqa, raise social stats, and initiate new school-based Social Links.

Deadlines

  • 6/8 | Prepare your party and gear before the full moon operation to rescue Fuuka and confront the Empress and Emperor Shadows.

Don't miss

  • 6/12 | Speak to Mitsuru on the first floor of the high school after placing top of the class on the May midterms to receive your reward (Incense cards).

Social links

  • Fuuka Yamagishi (Priestess): Available after mid-June once she joins SEES; requires Courage rank 6 (Badass) to initiate in the 2F hallway of Gekkoukan High.
  • Keisuke Hiraga (Fortune): Join the Culture Club (Art, Music, or Photography) on the school's 1F to unlock this Social Link.
  • Yuko Nishiwaki (Strength): Walk home with her twice after Track/Swimming/Kendo practice to formally establish the Strength link.
  • Chihiro Fushimi (Justice): Speak to her on four separate school days outside the Student Council room, choosing reassuring responses, to initiate the Justice link.

Worth doing

  • Work part-time at Chagall Cafe or Screen Shot Cinema on free evenings to earn money while boosting Charm, Courage, or Academics.
  • Study at the dorm lounge desk or eat at Wakatsu Kitchen to steadily increase Academics for future exam periods and Social Link requirements.

Tips

  • During the full moon boss battle on 6/8, Fuuka will scan weaknesses manually; swap between physical attackers and magic casters depending on whether you are targeting the Empress or Emperor.
  • Tartarus's second block (Arqa) opens up further following the full moon, so prioritize reaching the border floor before the next full moon deadline.
