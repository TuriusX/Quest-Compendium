October

Persona 3 Reload guide · The school festival and the confrontation with the next full moon shadow
← All areas in this guide

October brings a major narrative turning point beginning with the Full Moon operation on October 4th against the shadows Fortune and Strength. Midterm exams take place from October 13th to October 17th, requiring high Academics to place top in class. Following the exams, the Gekkoukan High School Culture Festival takes center stage alongside the opening of Tartarus block 4's upper half.

Don't miss 0/2

Deadlines
  • October 4 | Defeat the Full Moon shadows Fortune and Strength at Port Island Station.
  • October 13 | Reach Academics Rank 6 (Genius) before this date to ace the Second Term Midterms.

Social links

0/2

Worth doing
  • Visit the Game Parade arcade at Paulownia Mall during free evenings to boost Persona stats via the claw machines or stat-boosting arcade games.

Enemies
Fortune

Uses the Wheel of Fortune mechanic to spin for random buffs, debuffs, or damage on both allies and enemies.

Strength

Pairs with Fortune during the Full Moon; deals heavy physical damage and can inflict status ailments.

Tips
  • Fortune's roulette mechanic requires timed button presses; aim for the green zones to ensure the positive effect applies to your party rather than the bosses.
  • Report to Mitsuru at school on October 19 if you placed highest on the midterms to claim powerful accessory and Incense rewards.
