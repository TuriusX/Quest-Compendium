Twilight Fragments

Persona 3 Reload guide · Locations and uses for this resource

Twilight Fragments are a rare crystalline currency used to unlock locked treasure chests in Tartarus and fully heal your party at the entrance clock. You obtain them primarily by discovering them as sparkling pickups around the real-world town, progressing Social Links to earn rewards from Elizabeth, and occasionally finding them inside Tartarus. Efficiently budgeting their use is vital for keeping up your supplies and exploring deeper blocks in a single run.

Shops and people

  • Tartarus Entrance Clock: Fully restores party HP and SP for 7 Twilight Fragments
  • Locked Tartarus Treasure Chests: Rare equipment, skill cards, and consumable materials for 1 to 3 Twilight Fragments depending on chest size

Tips

  • Speak to Elizabeth outside the Velvet Room door at Paulownia Mall frequently, as she rewards you with bundles of Twilight Fragments as a reward for advancing your Social Link ranks.
  • Avoid spending Twilight Fragments on the Tartarus entrance clock early on, and instead save them exclusively for locked chests to acquire powerful gear and fusion materials.
