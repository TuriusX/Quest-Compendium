Naganaki Shrine

Persona 3 Reload guide · May onwards

Naganaki Shrine is a daytime exploration area where you can start and advance daytime Social Links. You can interact with Maiko in the playground area to rank up the Hanged Man Arcana and the dying young man Akinari on Sundays for the Sun Arcana. The area also features fortune-drawing and offering facilities to boost affinity or social stats.

Shops and people

  • Shrine Offertory Box: Donate 100 yen or 10,000 yen during the day to gain Academics points
  • Fortune Slip Box: Draw a relationship fortune for 100 yen during daytime to gain Social Link points with a chosen character
  • Inari Shrine (Clone Cards): Duplicate a Skill Card by offering it here and picking up the copies on subsequent days

Tips

  • Make sure you give Maiko Weird Takoyaki and Mad Bull to initiate the Hanged Man Social Link when she asks for food and a drink.
  • Akinari Kamiki appears at the shrine bench on Sundays, but you must reach Rank 3 in the Hanged Man Social Link and retrieve his missing Fountain Pen before his Sun Social Link can begin.
  • Drawing a relationship fortune at the shrine does not consume time if done on a free afternoon, making it a great way to push a Social Link to its next rank without wasting an event.
