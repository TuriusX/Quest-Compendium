Tartarus (Yabbashah)

Persona 3 Reload guide · June onwards

Yabbashah is the third major block of Tartarus, accessible starting in June after the full moon operation. It spans floors 70 to 118, divided into two distinct halves with thematic purple-hued, metallic pathways. Your primary objective is to push through the Gatekeeper mini-bosses, rescue lost civilians, and reach the barrier at the top before the next full moon.

Secrets

  • Locked Treasure Chests | Open with Twilight Fragments scattered across Tatsumi Port Island to claim top-tier armor, weapons, and accessories.
  • Monad Doors and Monad Passages | Defeat the tough shadow encounters inside these glowing red doors to obtain tarot cards, high-value materials, and reveal sections of the floor map.

Tips

  • Bring Akihiko and Yukari along to effortlessly cover electric and wind weaknesses while keeping healing consistent.
  • Conserve your Twilight Fragments exclusively for multi-fragment locked chests containing character-specific gear rather than single-fragment chests.
  • Watch for Elizabeth's notifications regarding missing people wandering into Yabbashah, and rescue them before the upcoming full moon to claim rewards at the police station.
