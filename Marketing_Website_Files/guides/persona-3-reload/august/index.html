August

Persona 3 Reload guide · Summer vacation events and the Hierophant/Lovers shadows

August spans the summer vacation, providing complete daytime freedom without school attendance. The Full Moon operation on August 6 tasks SEES with investigating an abandoned hotel to eliminate the Hierophant and Lovers Arcana Shadows. Afterwards, the month is ideal for catching up on out-of-school Social Links, evening stats, Tartarus exploration, and participating in the Summer Film Festival.

Deadlines

  • August 6 | Full Moon operation at the Shirakawa Boulevard love hotel to defeat the Hierophant and Lovers Shadows.
  • August 31 | End of summer vacation and the final day of the Summer Film Festival at Port Island Station.

Don't miss

  • Koromaru walking events | Skipping evening walks when other SEES members offer to join forfeits unique dialogue and Social Link points.
  • Summer Film Festival invitations | Between August 17 and August 31, friends will invite you to daytime movies that raise social stats and award Social Link points without spending a regular hangout; refusing or missing the invitation forfeits the bonus.

Social links

  • Sun (Akinari Kamiki): Available on Sunday daytime at Naganaki Shrine if Academic is rank 4 and Hanged Man is rank 3 or higher.
  • Star (Mamoru Hayase): Available on non-rainy days at Iwatodai Station strip mall if Courage is rank 4.
  • Hanged Man (Maiko Oohashi): Available at Naganaki Shrine on daytime non-school days.
  • Hermit (Maya / "Innocent Sin Online"): Available daytime on your desk computer in the dorm on days without school.
  • Tower (Mutatsu): Available Thursday through Sunday nights at the Club Escapade lounge once initiated via Yuko Nishiwaki and Courage rank 2.
  • Devil (President Tanaka): Available Tuesday and Saturday nights at Paulownia Mall after giving him 40,000 yen over three separate encounters (requires Hermit rank 4 and Charm rank 2).

Worth doing

  • Watch movies during the Summer Film Festival at Screen Shot in Port Island Station to gain Social Stat points and boost Social Link affinity.
  • Tend to the rooftop garden at the Iwatodai Dorm with party members during evenings to yield stat-boosting crops and deepen character bonds.
  • Work part-time at Chagall Cafe or Blue Seal in Paulownia Mall during free evenings to earn money and increase social stats.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
HierophantReflects Strike, Slash, and Pierce; relies on heavy electric and physical attacks; vulnerable to debuffs.
LoversUses charm skills and status ailments; vulnerable to fire/ice/elec/wind neutral damage, nullifies nothing, but carries no elemental weakness.

Tips

  • Equip anti-Charm accessories (such as the Narcissus Bow or Narcissus Flower) on your active party before facing the Lovers Shadow on August 6 to prevent your party members from turning on each other.
  • Since school-based Social Links are entirely unavailable throughout August, prioritize non-school daytime Links like Hierophant (Old Couple), Hanged Man (Maiko), Star (Mamoru), and Sun (Akinari).
