December

Persona 3 Reload guide · The final showdown with the shadows and the truth behind the Dark Hour

December marks the critical turning point where the party discovers the true nature of the Dark Hour and confronts Ryoji Mochizuki about the Fall. Players must explore Tartarus's Harabah block to reach the barrier at Floor 226 before the end of the month. On December 31st, a momentous decision determines whether the story reaches an early bad ending or proceeds toward the true conclusion in January.

Deadlines

  • December 31 | Make the choice regarding Ryoji's fate in your room; choosing to spare him is required to unlock the final month of the game and pursue the true ending.

Don't miss

  • True Ending path | On December 31, refuse Ryoji's offer and choose to let him live; agreeing to kill him triggers the Bad Ending immediately and ends the game prematurely.
  • Missing Persons rescue | Multiple civilians go missing into Tartarus during December, including one on December 22; rescue them all before the full moon deadline on December 31 to claim your rewards from Officer Kurosawa.

Social links

  • Empress (Mitsuru Kirijo): Available during daytime after school if you have maxed Academics; meet her on the 1st floor of Gekkoukan High outside the faculty office.
  • Aeon (Aigis): Unlocks automatically in January, so spend December daytime hours prioritizing school-based Social Links that may soon become unavailable.

Worth doing

  • Visit Club Escapade at night to buy URLs from the Informant to unlock new software functions and shared computer benefits at the dorm.
  • Tend to the rooftop garden with party members at the dorm during the evening to yield high-tier recovery vegetables and raise social stats.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Mayoido Antiques: Sells powerful fusion equipment, stat incenses, and skill cards in exchange for Tartarus gemstones.
  • Shinshoudo Antiques / Officer Kurosawa: Exchanges rescue rewards and sells updated late-game weapons and armor at Paulownia Mall.

Tips

  • Do not leave the Tartarus climb to the last minute; reach Floor 226 early in the month so you can devote remaining daytime slots to finishing school Social Links before winter break.
