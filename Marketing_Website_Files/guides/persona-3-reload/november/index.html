November

Persona 3 Reload guide · The investigation into the culprit and the final full moon operations

November marks the culmination of the Shadow-hunting mission with the 12th and final Full Moon operation on November 3. Following that battle, shocking narrative revelations reshape the conflict, leading into the Kyoto school trip and a quiet tension ahead of late November. Players must prepare for difficult boss battles, progress Tartarus to Floor 172 before the end of the month, and carefully schedule Social Links as the school semester winds down.

Deadlines

  • November 3 | Defeat the Hanged Man Shadow on the Moonlight Bridge during the Full Moon Operation.
  • November 30 | Reach Floor 172 in the Tziah block of Tartarus and defeat any pending missing persons requests before the upcoming December full moon cycle.

Don't miss

  • Junpei's Linked Episode | On November 7, speak to Junpei at school or the dorm to buy a flower for Chidori; if you miss supporting his visits to Chidori, his character resolution and Persona evolution are altered.
  • Shinjiro's final Linked Episode step completion | Must have been pursued in prior months; November will fully close out his availability due to story events that occurred on October 4.

Social links

  • Chariot (Kazushi Miyamoto): Available on weekdays at school; this is a prime time to finish this link before sports club availability wanes late in the term.
  • Justice (Chihiro Fushimi): Available on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays outside the Student Council room; advance now to maximize daytime availability.
  • Tower (Mutatsu): Available Thursday through Sunday nights at Club Escapade; great way to spend free evenings when team members are unavailable.
  • Devil (President Tanaka): Available Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday nights at Paulownia Mall; requires no matching Persona to rank up.

Worth doing

  • Participate in the Kyoto School Trip events from November 17 to November 20 to enjoy exclusive character scenes and story downtime.
  • Study at the dorm console or library in late November to prepare for the upcoming second-semester final exams in mid-December.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Hanged ManThe 12th major arcana boss fought on November 3; summons Devious Maya minions and drops bombs that detonate unless eliminated quickly.
Natural DancerBoss located on Floor 171 of Tartarus; uses heavy Wind skills, Charm status effects, and Tetrakarn, requiring status protection gear.

Tips

  • Bring items or Persona skills that cure Charm (such as Amrita Drop or Me Patra) for the late Tziah Tartarus guardians, especially Natural Dancer.
  • Do not neglect daytime school Social Links during November, as exam study weeks and story events will drastically cut down your available days at Gekkoukan High.
