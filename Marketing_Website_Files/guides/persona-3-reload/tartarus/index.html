Tartarus

Persona 3 Reload guide · April onwards

Tartarus is the massive labyrinthine tower that appears during the Dark Hour, serving as the main dungeon where SEES fights Shadows and investigates the anomaly. Players explore its randomly generated floors, fight enemies, rescue trapped civilians, and defeat floor bosses to reach border floors that block further progress until specific story dates. Your goal across the year is to continually climb toward the top, gather materials and EXP, and prepare the party for the Full Moon operations.

Items

  • Twilight Fragment: Glowing blue points scattered on various Tartarus floors and around Port Island
  • Snuff Soul: Found in breakable object clusters and standard treasure chests
  • Chewing Soul: Found inside locked treasure chests opened with Twilight Fragments

Secrets

  • Monad Doors appear as glowing red doorways starting in the Arqa block, containing high-level Shadows guarding rare materials and equipment.
  • Rare Shadows resembling gold hands scurry away on sight; cornering and defeating them yields large amounts of EXP and high-value selling items.
  • The Reaper emerges on a floor if the party lingers in one place for too long, pursuing you with overwhelming combat power.

Shops and people

Tips

  • Always strike Shadows from behind on the field to gain an ambush advantage, allowing your team to act first and exploit weaknesses immediately.
  • Break all glowing blue crystals and destructible objects on every floor to collect free loot and Twilight Fragments without spending SP.
  • After winning a battle via an All-Out Attack, Shuffle Time is guaranteed, letting you pick up extra EXP, money, healing, or new Persona cards.
  • Coordinate your party members' direct commands in the tactics menu to prevent them from wasting SP on resisted affinities.
  • Missing person notices appear on the police bulletin board periodically; rescue these citizens inside Tartarus before the next Full Moon to earn rewards and avoid losing them permanently.
