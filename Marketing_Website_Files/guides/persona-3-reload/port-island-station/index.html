Port Island Station

Persona 3 Reload guide · April onwards

Port Island Station is a central transit and commercial hub that unlocks early in the game for daytime and nighttime visits. It features shops, restaurants, and hangout spots, as well as an entrance to the Station Outskirts where more shady establishments reside. You will visit this area to increase Social Stats through dining or working part-time, advance Social Links, and purchase goods.

Shops and people

  • Screen Shot Movie Theater: Sells daytime movie tickets that increase specific Social Stats depending on the day's showing
  • Shirakawa Boulevard Beef Bowl Shop: Offers beef bowl meals that raise Academics, along with a nighttime part-time job opportunity
  • Flower Shop (Rafflesia): Sells various flowers and plant nutrients that can be given as gifts or used for dorm gardening
  • Shinshinto Boxing Gym: Located in the outskirts alleyway; offers daytime work or training sessions that increase stats like Charm and Courage

Tips

  • Check the movie theater schedule frequently, as weekend screenings provide substantial stat boosts that help with early-game Social Link prerequisites.
  • Working the nighttime part-time jobs available around the station raises both your Social Stats and your wallet without consuming Tartarus exploration time.
