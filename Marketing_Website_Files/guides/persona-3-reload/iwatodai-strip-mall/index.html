Iwatodai Strip Mall

Persona 3 Reload guide · April onwards

The Iwatodai Strip Mall is a multi-floor commercial district offering various restaurants, specialty food stalls, and a network shop terminal. It serves as a primary hub for raising social stats during daytime or nighttime through affordable meals and study spots. Players also visit this location to advance specific Social Links and find materials required for Elizabeth's requests.

Shops and people

  • Wild-duck Burger: Fast food meals that increase Courage, including the Mystery Burger challenge available on certain days
  • Wakatsu Kitchen: Seafood meals that increase Knowledge
  • Hagakure Ramen: Specialty ramen bowls that increase Charm
  • Aoba Drugstore: Healing items, status condition cures, and occasional request-related goods
  • Net Cafe Chavez: Sells software notes and digital URLs used on the dormitory shared computer to unlock passive combat buffs and stat-boosting methods
  • Beef Bowl Shop: Late-night meals that boost Academics, which also becomes the site of a part-time job opportunity
  • Book On On: Bookstore where the Hierophant Social Link (Bunkichi and Mitsuko) is initiated and advanced during the daytime

Tips

  • Check the net cafe regularly for new software notes, as they unlock permanently useful perks such as harvesting bonuses and extra combat techniques.
  • Prioritize eating at the various restaurants at night when free time is more abundant than during busy school afternoons.
