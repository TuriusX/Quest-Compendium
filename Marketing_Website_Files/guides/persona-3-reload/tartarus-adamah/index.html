Tartarus (Adamah)

Persona 3 Reload guide · November onwards

Adamah is the sixth and final regular block of Tartarus, accessible starting in January leading up to the promised day. Climbing to the top requires clearing floors 227 to 263 and defeating multiple Gatekeeper bosses along the way. Reaching the final boundary prepares the team for the ultimate battle atop Tartarus on January 31st.

Tips

  • Save all your remaining Twilight Fragments to unlock the high-tier locked chests scattered across Adamah, as they contain endgame gear and rare materials.
  • Fully clear the block and reach the barrier on Floor 263 before January 31st so you can spend the final day resting or wrapping up Social Links rather than rushing the climb.
  • Take advantage of Monad Doors and Monad Passages found throughout Adamah to obtain major Persona fusion materials and catch party members up in levels quickly.
  • Keep a broad spread of elemental skills and Dekaja ready on your main Personas, as many late Adamah Gatekeepers heavily buff themselves before attacking.
