Part-Time Jobs

Persona 3 Reload guide · Overview of available jobs and their benefits

Part-time jobs in Persona 3 Reload allow the protagonist to earn money while simultaneously raising Social Stats during free time slots. Players can sign up for available positions by checking the bulletin board inside the Paulownia Mall station corridor. Working shifts passes the respective day or night time period, making jobs a viable alternative to other stat-raising activities.

Worth doing

  • Work at Chagall Cafe in Paulownia Mall on available nights to earn money while increasing Charm and Courage.
  • Work at Be Blue V in Paulownia Mall on days off and select daytime slots to earn money while increasing Academics and Charm.
  • Work at Screen Shot cinema in Port Island Station on Sundays or days off to earn money while boosting Courage and Academics.

Tips

  • Prioritize part-time jobs over standard stat activities if you are low on yen, as jobs yield both cash rewards and dual Social Stat points.
  • Check the bulletin board in the Paulownia Mall corridor whenever new opportunities unlock to see updated work schedules and prerequisites.
