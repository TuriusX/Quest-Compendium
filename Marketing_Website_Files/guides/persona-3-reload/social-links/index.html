Social Links

Persona 3 Reload guide · Guide to unlocking and ranking up all character relationships

Social Links represent bonds forged with schoolmates, acquaintances, and figures around Tatsumi Port Island. Ranking these up grants bonus experience when fusing Personas of the matching Arcana and unlocks ultimate Persona fusions at Rank 10. Prioritize daytime school links during the semester, as evening time slots are much less crowded and school links become unavailable during vacations and exam weeks.

Don't miss

  • School-based Social Links are unavailable during exam weeks, holidays, and school breaks; failing to prioritize them over off-campus daytime links can leave you unable to max out all bonds before the final day on January 31.
  • Akinari Kamiki (Sun Arcana) requires finding his Red Fountain Pen and can only be advanced on Sunday daytimes; ignoring him early can make it impossible to finish all 10 ranks before the end of the year.

Social links

  • Fool (SEES): Advances automatically as you progress through the main story.
  • Magician (Kenji Tomochika): Available on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays in Class 2-F at Gekkoukan High starting in late April.
  • Priestess (Fuuka Yamagishi): Available in the 2F hallway outside the classrooms after rescuing her; requires Courage Rank 6 (Badass) to initiate.
  • Empress (Mitsuru Kirijo): Available on 1F near the faculty office starting in late November; requires Academics Rank 6 (Genius) to initiate.
  • Emperor (Hidetoshi Odagiri): Available on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays in the Student Council Room after joining the Disciplinary Committee.
  • Hierophant (Bunkichi and Mitsuko): Available Tuesday through Sunday daytimes at the Bookworms bookstore in Iwatodai Strip Mall; requires retrieving a Persimmon Leaf from the Gekkoukan High corridor tree.
  • Lovers (Yukari Takeba): Available in Class 2-F on Mondays, Wednesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays starting in late July; requires Charm Rank 6 (Charismatic) to initiate.
  • Chariot (Kazushi Miyamoto): Available in Class 2-F on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays to attend Track team practice starting in late April.
  • Justice (Chihiro Fushimi): Available outside the Student Council Room on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays; talk to her several times to build trust before the link officially begins.
  • Hermit (Maya / "Innocent Sin Online"): Available on free daytime Sundays and holidays by accessing the laptop inside your room at the dorm.
  • Fortune (Keisuke Hiraga): Available in the 1F hallway on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays to attend Art Club once club recruitment opens in June.
  • Strength (Yuko Nishiwaki): Available in the 2F hallway on Wednesdays and Saturdays; talk to her and walk her home twice after Track practices to unlock her link.
  • Hanged Man (Maiko Oohashi): Available at Naganaki Shrine on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays; requires giving her a Weird Takoyaki from Octopia and a Mad Bull drink from the dorm vending machine.
  • Death (Pharos): Advances automatically during cutscenes on specific calendar dates as the story proceeds.
  • Temperance (Andre Laurent Jean "Bebe" Geraux): Available on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Fridays in the Home Economics Room; requires Chariot Rank 3 and Academics Rank 2 to initiate.
  • Devil (President Tanaka): Available at Paulownia Mall on Tuesday and Saturday evenings; requires Hermit Rank 4, Charm Rank 3, and handing him a total of 40,000 yen over three separate nights.
  • Tower (Mutatsu): Available at Club Escapade on Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings; requires Chariot Rank 3, Courage Rank 4, and serving drinks to patrons for the bartender to gain entry to his booth.
  • Star (Mamoru Hayase): Available at Iwatodai Strip Mall on Wednesdays, Fridays, and Sundays starting in early August; requires Courage Rank 4 to speak with him.
  • Moon (Nozomi Suemitsu): Available at Paulownia Mall daytime; requires Charm Rank 2, Hermit Rank 3, and answering his food quiz correctly while giving him an Odd Morsel.
  • Sun (Akinari Kamiki): Available at Naganaki Shrine exclusively on Sunday daytimes; requires Hanged Man Rank 3, Academics Rank 4, and retrieving his Red Fountain Pen from Koromaru.
  • Judgement (Nyx Annihilation Team): Advances automatically as you scale the final block of Tartarus (Adamah) in January after making the pivotal story decision on December 31.
  • Aeon (Aigis): Available in Class 2-F starting on January 8; can be advanced almost every school day up until the final week of January.

Worth doing

  • Spend free evenings with male SEES members at the Iwatodai Dorm (cooking, gardening, reading, watching DVDs) to unlock Link Episodes, which function similarly to Social Links and grant exclusive Persona fusions.
  • Visit the relationship fortune-teller at Club Escapade in the evening or use the Naganaki Shrine relationship shrine during the day to gain points toward a Social Link that is not yet ready to rank up.

Tips

  • Always carry a Persona of the matching Arcana in your inventory when spending time with a Social Link to gain a 50% multiplier on affection points earned from dialogue choices.
  • Matching Personas are not required for Devil and Sun, as their events rank up automatically every single time you hang out with them.
  • Prioritize raising your three Social Stats (Courage, Charm, Academics) during evening free time early in the year so you meet the prerequisites for party member Social Links (Fuuka, Yukari, and Mitsuru) as soon as they become available.
