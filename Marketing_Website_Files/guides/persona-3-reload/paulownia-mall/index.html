Paulownia Mall

Persona 3 Reload guide · April onwards

Paulownia Mall is a major commercial hub accessible during both daytime and nighttime throughout the game. It houses various shops, dining spots, and entertainment facilities essential for preparing for Tartarus, boosting Social Stats, and fusing Personas. Players will frequently visit this area to buy equipment, fuse Personas in the Velvet Room, and spend evening time on stat-raising activities.

Shops and people

  • Velvet Room Door: Access to Igor and Elizabeth for Persona fusion, Persona Compendium, and quests
  • Aoba Pharmacy: Sells healing items, status recovery medicine, and stat-buffing items
  • Shinshoudo Antiques: Crafts special weapons, armor, and accessories using materials found in Tartarus, and trades gems for cards and items
  • Police Station: Sells weapons, armor, and accessories that update as you progress through the game
  • Chagall Cafe: Offers coffee that raises Charm and provides a part-time job during evenings to increase stats and earn yen
  • Mandragora Karaoke: Provides a singing room activity that raises Courage during the day or evening
  • Game Parade: Features various arcade games that increase specific Social Stats or boost the stats of currently equipped Personas
  • Club Escapade: Nightclub hosting an informant who sells software notes, a fortune teller who alters Tartarus conditions, and a daytime Social Link NPC
  • Be Blue V: Offers a service to recover your party members' forgotten skills and improve Personas later in the game

Tips

  • Check the arcade lineup at Game Parade daily, as different games appear on specific days of the week to boost different Persona stats or Social Stats.
  • The Police Station offers discounted prices on weapons and armor on Mondays, making it the best day for major gear upgrades.
