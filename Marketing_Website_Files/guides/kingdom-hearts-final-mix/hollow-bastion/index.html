Hollow Bastion

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · First visit after Neverland

Sora arrives at Hollow Bastion after Neverland and temporarily loses the Keyblade, requiring him to rely on the Beast to navigate the castle grounds. Sora eventually reclaims the Keyblade with his friends' help, ascends the castle to confront Maleficent, and faces Riku in a climactic duel. To save Kairi's heart, Sora turns the Keyblade of heart upon himself and transforms into a Shadow before escaping the world.

Items

  • White Trinity: Castle Gates, right after taking the rising platform from the Rising Falls
  • Flare Cadenza: Waterway, inside a floating chest reached by freezing bubbles or hitting switches
  • Ray of Light: Library, obtained by collecting and placing the missing books on the shelves
  • Oblivion: Grand Hall, chest located on a high ledge directly above the dark portal after defeating Riku

Secrets

  • Library Book Puzzle: Collect the misplaced books ("Khama", "Theon", etc.) scattered around the room and return them to their matching shelves to unlock the upper doors and lower a platform.
  • Waterway Ice Puzzle: Use the Blizzard spell on the geysers of water in the Waterway to freeze them into solid stepping stones, allowing access to elevated platforms and chests.

Tips

  • While Sora is armed only with the Wooden Sword, rely on Beast's high damage and invulnerability to defeat enemies.
  • Cast Gravity magic on the floating chests held aloft by magic blocks to bring them down to ground level.
  • Equip Glide or High Jump to easily cross the large gaps and ascending lift puzzles surrounding the exterior of the castle.
