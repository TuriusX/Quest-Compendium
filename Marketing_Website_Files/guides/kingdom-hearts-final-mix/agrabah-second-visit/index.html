Agrabah (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit for the Cave of Wonders

Sora and the party return to Agrabah's Cave of Wonders after pursuing Jafar and finding Princess Jasmine in danger. Navigating through the deeper subterranean chambers leads to the Lamp Chamber for back-to-back boss battles against Jafar and Genie Jafar. Sealing Agrabah's Keyhole concludes the world's main storyline and yields new magic and Trinity power.

Items

  • Ray of Light: Awarded upon defeating Genie Jafar in the Lamp Chamber
  • Ansem's Report 1: Dropped after defeating Genie Jafar in the Lamp Chamber
  • White Fang: Treasure chest located in the Entrance area of the Cave of Wonders
  • Mythril Shard: Treasure chest located in the Silent Chamber
  • Torn Page: Found inside a chest in the Dark Chamber

Secrets

  • Touch the submerged, glowing statues in the Bottomless Hall to unlock the sealed water gate leading into the Silent Chamber and Hidden Room.
  • Strike the pillar structures in the Hidden Room to collapse the wall in the Hall above, revealing the pathway to the Lamp Chamber.

Enemies

Tips

  • In the first battle against Jafar, cast Blizzard to exploit his elemental vulnerability and use high-elevation platforms to reach him when he floats out of melee range.
  • During the Genie Jafar encounter, ignore the giant boss entirely and focus all lock-ons and attacks solely on Iago, who flies around clutching the black magic lamp.
  • Equip fire-resistant accessories such as Fire Rings to mitigate the lava damage and magma boulders hurled during the Genie Jafar confrontation.
