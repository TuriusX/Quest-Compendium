Destiny Islands

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · The prologue of the game

Destiny Islands serves as the prologue world where Sora explores his home and interacts with his childhood friends. Sora gathers supplies to build a raft with Riku and Kairi before a dark storm consumes the islands. After the storm hits, Sora obtains the Keyblade, defeats a massive Heartless, and is separated from his home.

Items

  • Cloth MISSABLE: Found on the wall inside the treehouse on the first island area
  • Rope MISSABLE: Lying on the beach near the wooden platform where Tidus hangs out
  • Log MISSABLE: Found on the seashore near the raft or on the small islet reached by the bridge
  • Fish MISSABLE: Swimming in the ocean water around the main beach and near the small islet
  • Drinking Water MISSABLE: Collected from the small spring located inside the waterfall cavern
  • Coconut MISSABLE: Dropped from palm trees when struck with a wooden sword
  • Seagull Egg MISSABLE: Located at the top of a palm tree on the small islet reached via the wooden bridge
  • Mushroom MISSABLE: Found inside the Secret Place cavern, in the hollow behind the boulder in the Cove, and inside the watchtower area
  • Pretty Stone MISSABLE: Found in the ocean floor or by moving rocks in the Cove area
  • Protect Chain MISSABLE: Awarded for defeating Riku in a race across the island

Secrets

  • You can push a large boulder in the Cove area to reveal an opening leading to a hidden Mushroom.
  • Exploring the Secret Place at the base of the tree reveals cave drawings made by Sora and Kairi.

Enemies

Tips

  • You can duel Riku, Wakka, Selphie, and Tidus on the first day to grind Tech Points and level up before leaving the island.
  • Parrying Wakka's blitzball back at him awards Tech Points and temporarily stuns him.
  • Choosing to race Riku allows you to win a Protect Chain, which serves as a defense-boosting accessory.
  • During the Darkside boss fight, you can defeat the summoned Shadows to collect HP balls if your health gets low.
