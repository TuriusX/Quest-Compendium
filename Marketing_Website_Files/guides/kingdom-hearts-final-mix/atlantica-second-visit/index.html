Atlantica (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit for the Ursula's Revenge event

Sora and companions return to Atlantica after Ursula steals King Triton's trident, pursuing her deep into the ocean depths. You must navigate through the sea trenches to Ursula's Lair to confront her in her giant form and reclaim the trident. Defeating Giant Ursula restores peace to the ocean, unlocks the path to seal the world's Keyhole, and earns Sora a new magic upgrade.

Items

  • Ansem's Report 3: Rewarded automatically after defeating Giant Ursula
  • Thunder upgrade (Thundara): Rewarded automatically after defeating Giant Ursula
  • Crabclaw: Obtained from King Triton in Triton's Palace after sealing the Keyhole

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Giant UrsulaBack of the headTargets the party with sweeping arm strikes, lightning bolts, and water vortices; swim behind her head to attack safely.

Tips

  • Equipping accessories with high Thunder resistance, such as the Thunder Ring line, drastically reduces the damage taken from Giant Ursula's arena-wide lightning strikes.
  • Use Aero magic at all times during the Giant Ursula fight to reduce incoming damage while staying close enough to hit the back of her neck.
  • Mermaid Kick is essential during the final battle to quickly swim away when Ursula prepares to inhale water and pull Sora into her mouth.
