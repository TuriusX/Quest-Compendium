Halloween Town

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · First visit after Monstro

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive in Halloween Town disguised as monsters and team up with Jack Skellington to help Dr. Finkelstein build an artificial heart for the town's Halloween dance. After the key ingredients are retrieved, Lock, Shock, and Barrel steal the heart to take to Oogie Boogie, forcing Sora to pursue them to Oogie's Manor. To complete the world, players must defeat Lock, Shock, and Barrel, confront Oogie Boogie inside the torture chamber, and then destroy the giant manor itself.

Items

  • Forget-Me-Not: Search the gravestone near the base of the spiral hill in Moonlight Hill
  • Jack-in-the-Box: Inside a treasure chest on the cemetery balcony accessible through the tomb entrance
  • Holy Pumpkin keychain: Received automatically from Jack Skellington in the Research Lab after defeating Oogie Boogie

Secrets

  • Lighting the platform furnace in Moonlight Hill with Fira makes the platform rise, granting access to an elevated bridge leading to the bridge area outside Oogie's Manor.
  • Ringing the doorbell of the house in Guillotine Square three times causes it to dispense a prize.

Tips

  • Equipping Jack Skellington in your active party provides strong offensive magic, making crowd control against flying Heartless much easier.
  • During the first Oogie Boogie fight, stand on the glowing floor switch that matches Oogie's partition when the roulette wheel stops to lift your gate directly to him.
