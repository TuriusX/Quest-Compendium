End of the World

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Final world visited

End of the World is the final world visited in Kingdom Hearts Final Mix, formed from the remnants of worlds consumed by darkness. Sora, Donald, and Goofy traverse fractured landscapes of previously visited worlds, battle waves of powerful Heartless, and confront Chernabog before reaching the final rest point. The journey concludes at the remnants of Destiny Islands and the abyss beyond, leading to the multi-phase final confrontation with Ansem.

Items

  • Megalixir: Chest at Gate to the Dark
  • AP Up: Chest in the World Terminus (Traverse Town section)
  • Defense Up: Chest in the Giant Crevasse
  • Power Up: Chest in the Giant Crevasse
  • Elixir: Chest in the Final Dimension
  • Omega Weapon: Awarded automatically after defeating Chernabog
  • Superglide: Shared ability unlocked after defeating Chernabog

Tips

  • Equip the Superglide shared ability immediately after defeating Chernabog to make navigating the deep sections of the Giant Crevasse much easier.
  • Use the Final Rest save point to prepare fully before walking through the final door, as entering it initiates the point of no return for the final boss sequence.
  • Cast Aerora or Aeroga before engaging Chernabog to deflect incoming flame pillars and reduce sustained damage while airborne.
  • Tinker Bell is an invaluable summon in the grueling combat gauntlet inside the Final Dimension, as she provides continuous health regeneration without disappearing.
