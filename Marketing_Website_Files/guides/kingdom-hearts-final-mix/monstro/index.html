Monstro

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Fifth world visited after Traverse Town

Sora and his companions are swallowed by the massive whale Monstro while traveling between worlds in the Gummi Ship. Inside, they reunite with Pinocchio and Geppetto, chase down Riku, and must navigate the confusing, maze-like internal chambers. The main objective is to defeat the Parasite Cage boss inside Monstro's Stomach and Throat to escape.

Items

  • High Jump: Obtained after defeating the Parasite Cage for the first time in the Stomach
  • Watergleam: Found in a chest inside the Mouth area atop Geppetto's ship after obtaining High Jump

Secrets

  • Geppetto's Ship Chests: Returning to the Mouth area with High Jump allows you to leap onto the roof and upper structures of Geppetto's boat to reach previously inaccessible treasure chests.

Shops and people

  • Geppetto: Offers shelter inside his ship with a Save Point

Tips

  • Falling into the green digestive acid in the Stomach drains Sora's HP continuously, so fight from the platforms whenever possible.
  • Look at the colored organic doorways leading between Chambers 1 through 6 to avoid running in circles through Monstro's interior.
