Guides / KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX

Deep Jungle

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Third world visited after Traverse Town

Sora is separated from Donald and Goofy upon crash-landing in a dense jungle and is rescued by Tarzan. The group reunites and searches for missing gorillas, eventually clashing with the hunter Clayton who seeks the gorillas' nesting ground. After defeating Clayton and a stealthy Heartless, Sora locks the world's Keyhole inside the Waterfall Cavern and acquires the jungle slider mini-game.

Items

  • Jungle King: Rewarded automatically after sealing the Keyhole in the Waterfall Cavern
  • White Fang: Inside a chest found in the Tree House rafters
  • Mythril Shard: Rewarded for completing the slide puzzle experiment in the Camp: Tent area
  • Cure: Upgrades Sora's magic roster after defeating Clayton and the Stealth Sneak

Secrets

  • Bell in the Tree House: Striking the hanging bell in the Tree House multiple times causes it to drop items, including Potions and synthesis materials.
  • Camp Lab Experiment: Interacting with the laboratory table inside the Camp Tent allows Sora to perform an experiment by casting the matching elemental magic, yielding item rewards.

Enemies

Tips

  • Equipping Tarzan can be very useful for healing early on, as his default ability allows him to restore party HP without consuming Sora's MP.
  • When Clayton mounts the Stealth Sneak, target the chameleon first; depleting its health knocks Clayton to the ground and leaves him vulnerable.
  • You can bypass swinging on the vines in the Hippo's Lagoon area entirely by jumping directly across the hippos' backs once they surface.
