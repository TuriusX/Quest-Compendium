100 Acre Wood

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · First visit after Hollow Bastion

Returning here after clearing Hollow Bastion allows Sora to use the final Torn Pages to complete the storybook. Playing the remaining mini-games reunites Winnie the Pooh with his friends and unlocks valuable rewards. Completing the book also seals the world's Keyhole and turns the book into a completed item.

Items

  • EXP Ring: awarded after guiding Tigger and Roo through the bouncing game on the Muddy Path page
  • Sora's Cheer ability: received upon completing the Muddy Path page mini-game
  • Curaga upgrade: received after talking to Owl once all Torn Pages are completed and the world Keyhole is locked
  • Nature Spark: obtained from the Muddy Path page area

Secrets

  • Extinguish the fire in the fireplace inside Pooh's house using Blizzard magic to reveal an item.
  • Strike the chimney on the roof of Pooh's house from the outside to make an item fall out into the room.

Tips

  • Equipping items or abilities that increase Sora's jump height or aerial mobility can make navigating the Bouncing Spot much easier.
  • There are no hostile Heartless to fight anywhere inside the 100 Acre Wood book, making it completely combat-free.
