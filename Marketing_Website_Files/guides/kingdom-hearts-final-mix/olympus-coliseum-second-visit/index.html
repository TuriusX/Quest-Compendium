Olympus Coliseum (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit for the Hades Cup

Sora and companions return to Olympus Coliseum late in the game after sealing Hollow Bastion to participate in the ultimate tournament, the Hades Cup. Completing this massive 50-seed gauntlet pits the party against numerous bosses including Hades and the Rock Titan, unlocking powerful spells, weapons, and special time trial and solo modes. It is also the venue for optional end-game bouts against the Ice Titan and Sephiroth once requirements are met.

Items

  • Lionheart: Awarded for defeating Leon and Cloud simultaneously at Seed 10 of the Hades Cup
  • Graviga: Upgraded magic spell received as a reward for completing all 50 seeds of the Hades Cup
  • Trinity Upgrade (Yellow Trinity): Rewarded after defeating Hercules in the Hercules Cup (if not completed prior to or alongside the Hades Cup sequence)
  • Diamond Dust: Awarded after defeating the Ice Titan in the Gold Match
  • One-Winged Angel: Awarded after defeating Sephiroth in the Platinum Match

Secrets

  • Re-entering the Hades Cup allows you to select specific starting seeds (Seed 49, 39, 29, 19, or 9) once you have cleared them, making it easy to practice against late-tournament bosses without repeating the whole gauntlet.
  • Defeating the full 50-seed Hades Cup unlocks two new trial modes for it: Sora Alone (solo challenge) and Time Attack (complete the tournament within a time limit).

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
HadesBlizzardCasts fiery cone and beam attacks that make him invulnerable while moving; use Blizzard magic to extinguish his flames and stun him.
Rock TitanExtremely slow and towering boss; stagger him by attacking his feet until he collapses, then climb or jump onto his heads to deal massive damage.
Ice TitanFireOptional Gold Match boss; deflect his launched icicles back at him with Guard or weapon swings to stun him and deplete his health.
SephirothOptional Platinum Match boss featuring extreme speed, sweeping sword range, and the deadly Sin Harvest attack that reduces Sora's HP to 1 and MP to 0.

Shops and people

  • Phil: Offers registration for tournaments including the Hades Cup, Gold Match, and Platinum Match

Tips

  • Set Strike Raid or Superglide to your shortcuts/abilities for the Sephiroth fight, as Strike Raid grants invincibility frames and Superglide allows you to cross the arena quickly to interrupt Sin Harvest.
  • Equip the Guard ability for the Ice Titan encounter, as reflected projectiles deal the vast majority of the damage against him.
