Guides / KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX

Neverland

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · First visit after Halloween Town and Atlantica

Sora and the party are captured aboard Captain Hook's pirate ship, where they team up with Peter Pan to rescue Wendy and Kairi. After fighting through the ship's interior and taking back the deck, Sora unlocks the ability to fly to defeat Captain Hook and seal the world's Keyhole at the Clock Tower.

Items

  • Curaga upgrade: Talk to Aerith in the Hollow Bastion Library after sealing Hollow Bastion (requires Neverland's Navi-G piece)
  • Raven's Claw: Clock Tower: inside one of the light doors on an hour matching the door number
  • Protect Chain: Hold: inside a treasure chest on top of the suspended cargo net

Secrets

  • The Clock Tower has 12 numbered doors that open one by one based on every full real-world hour elapsed on the game timer, each rewarding rare items.
  • Phantom optional boss fight becomes accessible at the Clock Tower by speaking to Tinker Bell at the Ship's Cabin save point after sealing Hollow Bastion.

Tips

  • Equipping Sora with aerial combo-boosting abilities before the deck fight makes flying combat against Captain Hook and the flying Heartless significantly easier.
  • Use the yellow Trinity Mark in the Ship's Hold to drop a ladder, opening a convenient shortcut between the lower decks and the upper cabins.
  • In the boss fight against Captain Hook, casting Fire or knocking him off the ship forces him to scramble back to avoid the Crocodile, giving you time to heal.
