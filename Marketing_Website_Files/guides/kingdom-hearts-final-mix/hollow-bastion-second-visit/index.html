Hollow Bastion (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit for the Sephiroth and Ice Titan events

Sora and his companions return to Hollow Bastion after sealing its keyhole to speak with the restoration committee members in the Library. After securing the Castle Gates area, you locate the final world entrance through the Dark Depths to continue to the End of the World.

Items

  • Firefly: Castle Gates, chest on a floating debris platform
  • AP Up: Library, accessible by speaking with Belle after completing the Princesses' gathering

Secrets

  • Speaking with Belle in the Library after sealing the Keyhole rewards Sora with the Divine Rose keychain.
  • Talking to the reconstructed Princesses of Heart in the Chapel reveals deep lore regarding the worlds and unlocks Fire arts upgrades.

Tips

  • Divine Rose obtained from Belle has high base power and critical hit rate, making it one of the strongest physical Keyblades for late-game combat.
  • Equip the MP Rage and Second Chance abilities before engaging the Behemoth at the Castle Gates to easily survive its lightning discharges.
