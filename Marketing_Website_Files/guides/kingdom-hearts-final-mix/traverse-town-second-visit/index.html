Traverse Town (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit to unlock new areas

Sora and his companions return to Traverse Town with the Navi-G Piece found in Deep Jungle to have Cid inspect it. Exploring behind the alleyways and the Gizmo Shop reveals the Hidden Waterway, an old bell tower, and a direct path to the Second District's high rooftops. RINGING the town bell three times summons the Reverse Armor boss and unlocks access to the warp holes leading to the next set of worlds.

Items

  • Earthshine: Hidden Waterway, received from Leon after speaking with him
  • Navi-G Piece: Third District, dropped by Reverse Armor after defeat
  • Camping Set: Gizmo Shop roof, found inside a treasure chest reached via the chimneys

Secrets

  • Ringing the bell above the Gizmo Shop three times reveals the district's hidden Keyhole on the fountain plaza below.
  • Speaking to Merlin in his Mystical House unlocks magic trials and allows Sora to begin examining the Old Book using Torn Pages.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Guard ArmorFought initially in the fountain plaza before transforming into its second form.
Reverse ArmorThe inverted form of Guard Armor that flies and separates its limbs to attack independently.
Yellow OperaAirborne Heartless casting Thunder spells that appear frequently across the districts during this return.

Shops and people

  • Cid: Installs the newly discovered Navi-G Piece into the Gummi Ship
  • Moogle Synthesis Shop: Unlocked above the Accessory Shop once the Green Trinity mark in the shop is activated; crafts unique accessories and weapons

Tips

  • Use Red Trinity marks found in the Alleyway and water channel to open shortcuts and expose hidden chests.
  • Target Reverse Armor's hands and feet first to stop its ranged assaults and stun the main body.
