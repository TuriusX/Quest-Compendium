Sora and his companions return to Traverse Town with the Navi-G Piece found in Deep Jungle to have Cid inspect it. Exploring behind the alleyways and the Gizmo Shop reveals the Hidden Waterway, an old bell tower, and a direct path to the Second District's high rooftops. RINGING the town bell three times summons the Reverse Armor boss and unlocks access to the warp holes leading to the next set of worlds.

Enemies
Guard Armor

Fought initially in the fountain plaza before transforming into its second form.

Reverse Armor

The inverted form of Guard Armor that flies and separates its limbs to attack independently.

Yellow Opera

Airborne Heartless casting Thunder spells that appear frequently across the districts during this return.

Shops and people
  • Cid: Installs the newly discovered Navi-G Piece into the Gummi Ship
  • Moogle Synthesis Shop: Unlocked above the Accessory Shop once the Green Trinity mark in the shop is activated; crafts unique accessories and weapons

Tips
  • Use Red Trinity marks found in the Alleyway and water channel to open shortcuts and expose hidden chests.
  • Target Reverse Armor's hands and feet first to stop its ranged assaults and stun the main body.
