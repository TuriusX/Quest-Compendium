Halloween Town (Second Visit)

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Return visit for the Oogie's Manor event

Sora and his companions return to Halloween Town after Lock, Shock, and Barrel steal the artificial heart, pursuing them to Oogie's Manor. After ascending the manor to defeat Oogie Boogie inside his torture chamber, the villain merges with the structure itself, requiring Sora to destroy the giant manor's dark orbs to seal the world's Keyhole.

Items

  • Holy Circlet: Manor Ruins, inside a chest on the ground after Oogie's Manor collapses

Secrets

  • The Red Trinity at the entrance of Oogie's Manor is permanently destroyed once the manor collapses, so activating it beforehand is required to obtain its contents.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
LockFights alongside Shock and Barrel inside the manor's playroom before the boss encounter.
ShockAgile boss trio member encountered in the playroom of Oogie's Manor.
BarrelHeavy boss trio member supporting Lock and Shock inside the playroom.
Oogie BoogieFought on a giant roulette wheel where players must step on glowing buttons to elevate the platform to strike him.
Giant Oogie BoogieA massive environmental boss requiring Sora to navigate the manor's external terrain and shatter dark energy orbs.

Tips

  • During the Giant Oogie Boogie battle, equip the Glide ability to easily maneuver along the manor's rib-like structures and reach every dark orb.
  • Extinguish the Gargoyle lantern flames using Fire magic in the graveyard to reveal hidden chests while returning to the manor.
