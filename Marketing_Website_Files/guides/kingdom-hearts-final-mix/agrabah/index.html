Guides / KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX

Agrabah

KINGDOM HEARTS FINAL MIX guide · Fourth world visited after Traverse Town

Sora, Donald, and Goofy arrive in Agrabah to search for the Keyhole and Princess Jasmine while assisting Aladdin. The group navigates the desert city and the Cave of Wonders to stop Jafar and his Heartless forces from taking control of the lamp. After navigating the underground ruins and defeating Jafar in multiple forms, Sora locks the world's Keyhole.

Items

  • Ray of Light: Treasure chest in the Treasure Room within the Cave of Wonders

Secrets

  • Activating a high monkey statue with Abu in the Cave of Wonders opens up previously sealed passages across the chasm.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
BanditArmed Heartless that deflects frontal physical attacks with scimitars.
Fat BanditHeavy Heartless that absorbs frontal physical strikes and breathes fire; attack from behind.
Pot SpiderDisguises itself as an ordinary breakable pot before ambushing you.
JafarFloats out of melee reach while casting spells, requiring Sora to navigate elevated platforms to hit him.
Genie JafarBlizzardTarget the lamp held by Iago rather than Jafar's giant genie body to deal damage.

Tips

  • Equip Blizzard-resistant accessories or the Dodge Roll ability to avoid the environmental hazards and long-range fire magic in the Cave of Wonders.
  • Use the High Jump shared ability, acquired by progressing in this world, to reach previously inaccessible rooftops in the Agrabah bazaar.
