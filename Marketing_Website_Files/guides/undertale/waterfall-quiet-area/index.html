Waterfall (Quiet Area)

Undertale guide · The player explores the deeper, more peaceful sections of Waterfall
← All areas in this guide

After the bridge collapse, Frisk awakens in the quiet, lower cavern of Waterfall on a bed of golden flowers. You navigate through darker, reflective waterways, solve the piano puzzle, and make your way toward the Blook Family ghost farm and quiet residential crossway. It offers a breather between chases, focusing on atmosphere, light puzzle-solving, and quiet environmental storytelling.

Enemies
Mad Dummy
Weak to Guided cotton attacks directed into it

Boss fight where you must redirect magic cotton projectiles to strike the dummy body until it takes damage.

Shyren

Can be peacefully resolved by repeatedly choosing to hum with her until a concert takes place.

Aaron

Flexing three times causes him to flex his way completely out of the battle screen.

Woshua

Asking it to clean you initiates an attack with green cleansing water drops that heal and spare it.

Shops and people
  • Gerson: Sells Sea Tea, Crab Apple, Cloudy Glasses, and Torn Notebook

Tips
  • Purchase the Torn Notebook and Cloudy Glasses from Gerson if you struggle with bullet evasion, as they increase invulnerability frames after taking hits.
  • Giving the small statue an umbrella shields it from dripping water, revealing the hidden music-box melody required to solve the piano puzzle.
