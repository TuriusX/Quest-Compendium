Wellspring

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Tressa Chapter 3

Tressa arrives in the desert town of Wellspring to follow up on a rare treasure called the Eldrite, only to have it snatched away by a band of thieves. She must gather information from the townsfolk, locate the perpetrators, and chase them into their hideout at the Caves of Maiya. Outside of Tressa's storyline, Wellspring serves as an essential mid-game desert town offering strong equipment, side stories, and access to Sunshade-region routes.

Items

  • Imperial Vestments: In a chest inside the house on the far east side of the town
  • Olive of Life (L): In a treasure chest hidden behind the tent near the center of the settlement
  • 9,000 Leaves: In a chest located in the western portion of town near the guard post

Secrets

  • Concealed chest path behind the guard's residence | Follow the narrow alleyway behind the house in the northwest section of town to reach a tucked-away chest containing a Silver-filled Pouch

Shops and people

  • Wellspring Provisioner: Sells standard consumables such as Healing Grapes (M), Inspiriting Plums (M), and Purifying Dust
  • Wellspring Armorer: Sells mid-tier gear including the Veteran's Spear, Spirit Bow, and Elemental Robe

Tips

  • Use Tressa's Purchase or Therion's Steal on the town guards to obtain high-grade spears and shields before taking on the nearby cave dungeon.
  • Bring armor with high physical defense or abilities like Hresume/Shackle Foe, as the enemies and bosses associated with this chapter deal heavy physical damage.
