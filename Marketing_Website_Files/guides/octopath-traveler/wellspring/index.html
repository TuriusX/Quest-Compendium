Wellspring

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A desert town in the Sunlands

Wellspring is an oasis settlement located in the Sunlands desert, serving as a haven with water and armed guards. It is the setting for Chapter 3 of Olberic's story, where he comes seeking Erhardt, as well as a stop during Chapter 3 of Ophilia's story. Players can take on side quests, challenge or scrutinize townspeople, and gear up before heading into the nearby caverns.

Items

  • Imperial Vestments: Stolen or purchased from an NPC in town
  • Revitalizing Jam: Chest inside a house in town

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing items, status recovery herbs, and basic travel supplies
  • Weapon Shop: Mid-tier weapons including swords, spears, and elemental staves, alongside defensive armor

Tips

  • Inquire or Scrutinize the town guards to reveal hidden items scattered throughout town and to gain advantages in battle.
  • Bring Olberic or H'aanit to challenge or provoke the guards blocking doorways to gain access to locked houses with treasure chests.
