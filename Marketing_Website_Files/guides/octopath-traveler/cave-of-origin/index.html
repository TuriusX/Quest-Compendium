Cave of Origin

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Ophilia Chapter 1

The Cave of Origin is a dungeon situated just outside the town of Flamesgrace where Ophilia journeys during Chapter 1. Her objective is to navigate the freezing caverns to reach the sacred altar at the depths and ignite the lantern for the Kindling ritual. Inside, she must face a dark beast that guards the innermost sanctum.

Tips

  • Bring a summoned townsperson using Ophilia's Guide path action to provide extra attacks and cover damage types she lacks.
  • Prioritize eliminating the Dark Wisps as soon as they appear, as their self-destruct attack can heavily damage Ophilia.
