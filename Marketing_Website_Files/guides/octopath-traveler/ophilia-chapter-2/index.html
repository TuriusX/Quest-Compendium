Ophilia, Chapter 2

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A trial of faith in the town of Stillsnow

Ophilia arrives in the snowy town of Stillsnow to fulfill her duties on the Kindling pilgrimage, where she meets a troubled young boy named Emil who is being bullied. To help Emil and complete the ritual of the flame, she must use Guide to escort an essential NPC and venture deep into the Tomb of the Imperator.

Items

  • 1,500 Leaves: Red chest hidden behind a residence in the southern section of Stillsnow
  • Shadow Soulstone (M): Chest inside the Tomb of the Imperator
  • Spirit Bow: Chest located on an upper ledge in the Tomb of the Imperator
  • Healing Grape (M): Chest along the Path to the Tomb of the Imperator

Tips

  • Equip the Warrior's "Inspiriting Flame" or accessories that grant resistance against Terror, as Hrodvitnir will regularly afflict your party members with Terror to block their use of Boost points.
  • Ophilia must use her Path Action Guide to lead Dan to Emil to progress the chapter's storyline in Stillsnow before gaining access to the dungeon.
