Cobbleston

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A quiet village in the Highlands region

Cobbleston is a remote mountain village in the Highlands region that serves as the starting location for Olberic the warrior. Players visiting here can experience Olberic's Chapter 1 story, explore the surrounding Mountain Pass, and challenge local villagers using Olberic's Path Action.

Items

  • Healing Grape: Inside a treasure chest located in the village square near the entrance
  • Inspiring Plum: Inside a wooden chest tucked beside a house in the northern section of the village

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Sells basic consumable supplies including Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, and basic early-game equipment
  • Inn: Offers a full recovery of HP and SP for a small fee of leaves

Tips

  • Use Olberic's Challenge Path Action against town watchmen and villagers to earn easy early-game experience and test out combat skills.
