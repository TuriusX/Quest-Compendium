Everhold

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A city of theater in the Flatlands

Everhold is a cliffside city located in the Flatlands region, renowned for its grand amphitheater and rich dramatic arts culture. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 4 of Primrose's story, where she seeks final vengeance against the mastermind behind her father's murder. Players visiting the city can explore the theater grounds, recruit powerful townspeople, and challenge the nearby high-level optional dungeon.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone (L): Hidden in a chest near the stairs leading toward the upper amphitheater area
  • Revitalizing Jam: Located in a treasure chest tucked away behind the coliseum seating

Tips

  • Bring strong Light and elemental area-of-effect attacks to handle the illusions and puppet minions summoned during the boss battle in the amphitheater.
  • Many residents in Everhold possess high-level Path Action requirements; ensure your party level and Path Action success rates are boosted before attempting to Inquire, Scrutinize, Guide, or Allure them.
