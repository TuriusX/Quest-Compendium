Ophilia Clement, a cleric of the Order of the Sacred Flame in Flamesgrace, takes her adoptive sister Lianna's place in the Kindling pilgrimage to spare her from grief. To begin her journey, Ophilia uses her Guide Path Action to recruit a townsperson and heads into the Cave of Origin. Inside, she must reach the altar and defeat the Guardian of the First Flame to light the Sacred Lantern.

Don't miss 0/1

Items

0/6

Enemies
Guardian of the First Flame
Weak to Sword, Dagger, Staff, Ice, Light

Boss of the chapter that summons Dark Wisps and charges an ultimate attack

Dark Wisp
Weak to Spear, Bow, Fire, Light

Minion summoned by the Guardian; explodes when its health is depleted

Shops and people
  • Flamesgrace Provisioner: Healing Grape, Inspiriting Plum, Olive of Life, Herb of Clamor, Plum Nut
  • Flamesgrace Armorer: Iron Staff, Bronze Vest, Round Shield, Linen Robe

Tips
  • Guide the Whispering Milliner or another townsperson before heading into the Cave of Origin so you can summon them for physical attacks and shield breaks.
  • Focus down the Dark Wisps summoned by the Guardian quickly, as Ophilia's Holy Light can easily hit their Light weakness.
