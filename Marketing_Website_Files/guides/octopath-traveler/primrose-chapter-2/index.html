Primrose, Chapter 2

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A confrontation at the theater in Noblecourt

Primrose travels to Noblecourt in search of Left-Handed Man Rufus, one of the three men with the crow tattoo who murdered her father. She reunites with her childhood friend Nathalia and infiltrates the town's theater during a performance to confront Rufus. Prepare for a boss encounter against Rufus and his guards at the conclusion of the theater confrontation.

Items

  • Imperial Vestments: Noblecourt: inside a treasure chest located in the theater area
  • Revitalizing Jam: Noblecourt: treasure chest behind the stage inside the theater

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
RufusSword, Staff, Lightning, LightUses physical attacks and can boost his attack power, while summoning minions to support him.
Obsidian OfficerDagger, Axe, Fire, WindGuards who accompany Rufus during the battle and protect his weaknesses.

Tips

  • Silence or break the Obsidian Officers quickly so you can focus attacks on Rufus without being overwhelmed by multiple physical strikes.
  • Primrose's Dark element and buffs pair well with a Scholar or Warrior sub-job to exploit Rufus's physical and elemental vulnerabilities.
