Olberic, Chapter 2

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A duel in the arena of Victor's Hollow

Olberic travels to Victor's Hollow in search of the mercenary Erhardt, only to learn that clues about his whereabouts are tied to the town's colosseum. To gain an audience with the arena's champion, Olberic must register for the tournament and defeat several challengers in consecutive gladiatorial duels. The chapter concludes with a fierce final battle against the reigning arena combatant, Gustav.

Tips

  • Equip Olberic or your physical damage dealer with Slash and Physical Defense boosting accessories, as the arena battles heavily favor raw physical offense.
  • Ensure you clear the minions in each tournament round first, as letting them buff their respective leaders or stack debuffs on your party will quickly overwhelm your defenses.
  • Gustav frequently employs terror-inducing attacks; bring a supply of Herb of Clamor or equip an Articulate Stone to keep your party able to use Boost Points.
