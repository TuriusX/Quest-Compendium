Riverford

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Olberic Chapter 4

Olberic arrives in Riverford to track down Werner, the man responsible for the fall of the Kingdom of Hornburg. Players must investigate the heavily oppressed town, make contact with the local resistance movement, and eventually storm the local stronghold. Preparing for intense physical combat and multi-target duels is essential for clearing the chapter's challenges.

Items

  • Imperial Vest: Chest located in the lower district near the river edge
  • Revitalizing Jam: Chest situated in the residential square behind a stone building

Secrets

  • Hidden weapon stash can be revealed by inquiring or scrutinizing the former soldier standing near the lower plaza tavern.

Shops and people

  • Riverford Armorer: Sells high-tier physical gear including Imperial Armor, Rune Glaives, and Platinum Shields.
  • Riverford Provisioner: Sells standard restorative consumables such as Healing Grape (M) and Inspiriting Plum (M).

Tips

  • Ensure Olberic has high physical defense and access to multi-hit skills before triggering town duels, as Werner's guards deal heavy burst damage.
  • Equip Terror-resistant accessories or stock up on Herb of Clamor, as Werner's forces rely heavily on inflicting Terror to lock your Boost points.
