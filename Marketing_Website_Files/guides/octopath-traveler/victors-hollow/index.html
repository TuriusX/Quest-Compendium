Victor's Hollow

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A bustling town in the Woodlands

Victor's Hollow is a bustling town located in the Woodlands region, renowned throughout Orsterra for its fighting arena. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 2 of Olberic's story as well as Chapter 3 of Tressa's tale. Visitors can explore the town square, climb up to the arena district, purchase upgraded gear, or take on several arena-related side stories.

Items

  • Shadow Bow: Inside a chest in the arena district

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Restorative items, status cures, and basic consumables
  • Armorer: Mid-tier weapons, shields, body armor, and helms appropriate for Level 20–30 parties

Tips

  • Bring Olberic or H'aanit to take advantage of Challenge or Provoke, as several arena combatants and town guards can be fought for entrance or side quests.
  • Inquire or Scrutinize the spectators and gladiators around the arena to reveal hidden item stashes and unlock discounts at the local shops.
