Victors Hollow

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Olberic Chapter 2

Olberic arrives in Victors Hollow to participate in the prestigious arena tournament in hopes of finding clues about Erhardt's whereabouts. To enter the grand tournament, Olberic must first prove his martial worth by tracking down and defeating a formidable local gladiator. The chapter centers entirely on arena combat, culminating in a three-stage tournament against diverse, skilled opponents.

Items

  • 5,000 Leaves: Inside a treasure chest tucked near the arena entrance pathway

Tips

  • Equip Olberic with accessories that boost physical defense and physical attack, as all three arena rounds feature purely physical threats.
  • Focus down the minions accompanying Joshua and Gustav immediately to reduce the number of actions you have to withstand each round.
