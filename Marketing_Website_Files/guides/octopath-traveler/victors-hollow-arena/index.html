Victors Hollow Arena

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Olberic Chapter 2

This is the combat arena located inside Victor's Hollow where Olberic participates in a tournament during Chapter 2 of his story. Olberic must fight through a sequence of gladiatorial rounds against distinct champions to advance the plot. Between each arena bout, the player is returned to the staging grounds to heal, adjust equipment, and prepare for the next opponent.

Tips

  • Equip Olberic with accessories that boost physical defense and physical attack, as every opponent in this tournament focuses heavily on physical damage.
  • Make sure Olberic has access to secondary jobs providing Dagger and Bow capabilities (such as Thief or Hunter) to exploit the weaknesses of Joshua and Archibold easily.
  • Purchase plenty of Healing Grapes and Inspiring Plums beforehand so you can freely sustain HP and SP during these prolonged single-character battles.
