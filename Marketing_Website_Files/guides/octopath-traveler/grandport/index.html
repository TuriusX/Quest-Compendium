Grandport

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A major trade hub in the Coastlands

Grandport is a bustling, wealthy harbor metropolis located in the Coastlands region that hosts the annual Great Auction. It serves as the setting for Tressa's Chapter 4, where she brings her journal to compete in the bidding contest. Visitors can explore the main town, harbor docks, and the Great Auction arena to purchase powerful end-game equipment or tackle high-level side stories.

Items

  • Forbidden Bow: Purchased or stolen from an NPC standing inside the Great Auction grounds
  • Pom-Pom Vest: Inside a treasure chest on the eastern side of the docks area

Shops and people

  • Armorer (Grandport): Sells high-tier Coastlands equipment, including Platinum weapons and sturdy armor pieces
  • Provisioner (Grandport): Sells standard restorative items, including Healing Grapes (M) and Energizing Pomegranates

Tips

  • Many citizens in the auction grounds and harbor hold exceptional end-game gear and battle items; bring Therion to Steal or Tressa to Purchase from nearly every NPC here.
  • A blocked doorway in the town can be unblocked by using Olberic's Challenge or H'aanit's Provoke on the guard to access chests inside the house.
