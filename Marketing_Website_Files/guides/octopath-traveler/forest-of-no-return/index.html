Forest of No Return

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Alfyn Chapter 4

The Forest of No Return is a high-level danger zone located near Orewell in the Cliftlands. It serves as the final dungeon for Alfyn's Chapter 4 storyline, where he ventures deep into the woods to confront the apothecary Ogen and face the final threat. Players must navigate winding wooded paths while dealing with dangerous status-inflicting monsters before triggering the chapter's climax.

Items

  • Imperial Armor: Inside a red treasure chest tucked along a winding side path in the forest
  • Conscious Stone: In a treasure chest located along an offshoot path
  • 20,000 Leaves: In a treasure chest hidden behind dense foliage along the eastern paths

Secrets

  • A secluded path behind thick tree cover on the eastern side leads directly to a high-tier chest containing a substantial amount of Leaves.

Tips

  • Many enemies here, as well as the chapter boss, inflict debilitating physical and mental ailments such as Poison and Unconscious, so equip the Conscious Stone and have Alfyn concoct status-clearing remedies.
  • Bring strong elemental attacks covering Ice and Light to quickly break the bulkier woodland monsters that inhabit the area.
