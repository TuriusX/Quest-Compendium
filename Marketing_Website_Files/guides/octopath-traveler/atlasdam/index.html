Atlasdam

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Cyrus Chapter 1

Atlasdam is a scholarly town in the Flatlands and the setting for Cyrus's Chapter 1 story. Cyrus investigates the disappearance of a rare tome from the Royal Academy of Fine Arts archives, which leads him into the Subterranean Study beneath the palace. After defeating the scholar responsible for the theft, Cyrus decides to embark on a journey across Orsterra to seek the legendary book "From the Far Reaches of Hell."

Items

  • Healing Grape: Inside a chest in the Royal Academy archives
  • Inspiriting Plum: Inside a chest in the eastern part of the Atlasdam palace grounds
  • Wind Nut: Inside a chest on the path leading to the Subterranean Study
  • Light Nut: Inside a chest located in the Subterranean Study dungeon

Secrets

  • Hidden passage in the Royal Academy archives that opens a route downward into the Subterranean Study after Cyrus deduces the clues during his investigation

Tips

  • Use Cyrus's Path Action Scrutinize on the citizens and guards around the Royal Academy to gather the mandatory clues needed to identify the book thief.
  • Russell starts charging a powerful fire spell during combat; break his shield points before the countdown finishes to cancel the attack.
