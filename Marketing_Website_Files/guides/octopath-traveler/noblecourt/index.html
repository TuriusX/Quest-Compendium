Noblecourt

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · An academic town in the Flatlands

Noblecourt is an affluent town located in the Flatlands that serves as the setting for Therion's Chapter 2 and Primrose's Chapter 2. Divided into lower and upper districts, visitors can pursue main character storylines, gather information via Path Actions, and accept various side stories.

Items

  • Heavy Coin Pouch: Lower district, inside the tavern in a treasure chest
  • 10,000 Leaves: Upper district, inside a treasure chest on the eastern terrace

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Restorative items, status cures, and basic travel supplies
  • Armorer: Mid-tier weapons including swords, daggers, axes, and corresponding defensive gear

Tips

  • Bringing Cyrus or Alfyn allows you to Inquire or Scrutinize the townspeople to reveal hidden item locations and expand inventory at the local armory.
  • Therion is required to initiate his Chapter 2 storyline here, which involves infiltrating the Orlick mansion located in the upper district.
