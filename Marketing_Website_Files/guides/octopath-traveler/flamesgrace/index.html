Flamesgrace

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A snowy town in the Frostlands

Flamesgrace is a snowy settlement located in the Frostlands and serves as the starting location for Ophilia Clement's Chapter 1 story. It is home to the Sacred Flame, and players who visit can initiate Ophilia's tale, advance into the Cathedral, and prepare for the nearby dungeon, the Cave of Origin.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone: Inside a treasure chest on the eastern outskirts of the town
  • Inspiriting Plum: In a treasure chest near the path ascending to the Cathedral

Shops and people

  • Flamesgrace Provisioner: Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, Olive of Life, Herb of Clamor, and basic starter gear
  • Flamesgrace Armorer: Iron Sword, Iron Lance, Iron Dagger, Iron Axe, Iron Bow, Wooden Staff, and early defensive apparel

Tips

  • Use Ophilia's Path Action, Guide, on an NPC in town so they can aid you in battle, which is especially useful during her early boss fight.
  • Inquire or Scrutinize townspeople in Flamesgrace to reveal hidden item stashes scattered across the settlement and Cathedral grounds.
