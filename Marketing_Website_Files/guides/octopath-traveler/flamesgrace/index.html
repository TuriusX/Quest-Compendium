Flamesgrace

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Ophilia Chapter 1

Flamesgrace is a snowy mountain town where Ophilia begins her journey to undertake the Kindling pilgrimage in place of Lianna. Players visit the Cathedral to advance the story, guide an NPC, and eventually head into the Cave of Origin to face Chapter 1's boss.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone: Inside a treasure chest located in the graveyard behind the Cathedral
  • Olive of Life: Inside a treasure chest on the eastern path in Flamesgrace
  • Herb of Clarity: Inside a treasure chest located near the entrance to the Cathedral interior

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Sells basic healing items such as Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, and Olives of Life, along with low-tier equipment
  • Innkeeper: Offers a full HP and SP recovery for the party for a fee of 16 leaves

Tips

  • Use Ophilia's Guide Path Action to recruit a townsperson to summon in battle for extra damage and shield-breaking capability during Chapter 1.
  • Break the Dark Wisps summoned by the boss immediately before they self-destruct and deal heavy dark damage to the entire party.
