Rippletide

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Therion Chapter 1

Rippletide is a small seaside fishing village in the Coastlands region that serves as Tressa's starting hometown rather than Therion's. Therion visits this town later in the game or when exploring the world to recruit Tressa and gather items from its residents. In this settlement, players can interact with townsfolk, trigger Tressa's Chapter 1 story, and shop for basic travel supplies.

Items

  • Healing Grape: Chest located near the docks on the western side of town
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest located tucked near a house in the northern section of town

Secrets

  • Hidden passage leading behind the merchant stalls on the east side reaches a secluded chest.

Shops and people

  • Rippletide Provisioner: Sells basic consumables including Healing Grapes, Inspiriting Plums, and Olive of Life
  • Rippletide Armorer: Sells basic iron and bronze tier weapons and armor suited for low-level Coastlands travel
  • Rippletide Inn: Restores all HP and SP for the party for a small fee of leaves

Tips

  • Therion's Chapter 1 takes place in Bolderfall in the Cliftlands, so visit Rippletide primarily to recruit Tressa and Steal valuables from the townsfolk early on.
