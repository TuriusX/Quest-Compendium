Rippletide

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A coastal town in the Coastlands

Rippletide is a small seaside fishing village located in the Coastlands region. It serves as the starting location for the merchant Tressa Colzione and is the hub for her Chapter 1 story. Players visiting the town can purchase supplies, interact with local villagers using Path Actions, and head toward the nearby Caves of Maiya.

Items

  • Healing Grape: Chest behind the weapon shop
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest near the docks to the east
  • 1,000 Leaves: Hidden item located near the village beach, revealed via Inquire or Scrutinize

Shops and people

  • Rippletide Provisioner: Healing Grape, Inspiriting Plum, Plum Basket, Olive of Life, Herb of Clamor
  • Rippletide Armorer: Iron Sword, Iron Spear, Dagger, Iron Axe, Wooden Bow, Wooden Staff, Round Shield, Iron Armor

Tips

  • Tressa's Purchase path action can be used immediately in town to buy unique items, including equipment and quest items, from various townsfolk.
  • You can find money dropped on the ground when entering new areas using Tressa's talent, Eye for Money.
