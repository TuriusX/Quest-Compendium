Town of S'warkii

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · H'aanit Chapter 1

S'warkii is a secluded village nestled in the deep forests of the Woodlands region and serves as the starting town for the hunter H'aanit. During Chapter 1, H'aanit prepares to track down a rampaging beast threatening the surrounding woods while reminiscing about her master, Z'aanta. Players can explore the village to stock up on gear, recruit or challenge locals using Path Actions, and head toward the Whispering Mill to continue the story.

Items

  • Herb of Clamor: Chest near the village entrance on the left
  • Olive of Life: Hidden chest tucked behind a tree in the northern part of the village

Secrets

  • Concealed trail on the eastern edge of town leads behind a tent to a hidden chest containing an invigorating nut.

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, Purifying Seeds, and basic restorative items
  • Armorer: Low-level axes, bows, and basic armor suitable for the early Woodland chapter
  • Tavern: Restores party HP and SP, allows changing active party members, and lets you re-hear tales

Tips

  • Use H'aanit's Path Action "Provoke" on town NPCs blocking doorways to gain access to locked cabins containing treasure chests.
  • Linde, H'aanit's companion snow leopard, can be summoned infinitely in battle at no cost and can hit either sword or polearm weaknesses depending on the skill chosen.
