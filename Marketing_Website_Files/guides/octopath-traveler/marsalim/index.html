Marsalim

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · The desert capital in the Sunlands

Marsalim is the grand, fortified royal capital of the Sunlands ruled by King Khalim. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 4 of H'aanit's story, where she seeks to lift the petrification curse on her master Z'aanta. Players can also explore the Marsalim Catacombs nearby, complete high-level side stories, and utilize Path Actions on the city's heavily guarded nobility and soldiers.

Items

  • High House Armor: Chest inside the Marsalim Palace
  • Imperial Vestments: Chest inside the Marsalim Palace
  • Enchanted Sword: Chest inside Marsalim Catacombs

Shops and people

  • Marsalim Provisioner: High-tier restorative items, Large Soulstones, and status-curing herbs
  • Marsalim Armorer: Advanced mid-to-late game weapons and armor pieces

Tips

  • Many royal guards in the palace hold powerful equipment and high-tier items via Therion's Steal or Tressa's Purchase, but they require high levels or reputation to acquire successfully.
  • Bring Olberic or H'aanit at high levels to Challenge or Provoke the guards blocking several palace doorways to access hidden chests.
