Orewell

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A town in the Cliftlands

Orewell is a secluded, arid mountain town situated in the upper Cliftlands region. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 4 of Alfyn the Apothecary's story, where he confronts an outbreak and seeks a cure. Visitors will find standard town amenities, path action opportunities with the locals, and the trailhead leading to the Forest of Rubeh.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone (L): In a treasure chest hidden in the southeastern area of the town
  • Purifying Dust: Inside a treasure chest on the elevated ledge near the eastern cliffside

Secrets

  • Path to the Forest of Rubeh | Exit through the northwest opening of town to reach the high-level dungeon required for Alfyn's Chapter 4 climax

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Restorative items including concocting materials, Healing Grapes (M), and basic status curatives
  • Armorer: Mid-to-late game weapons and armor, particularly heavier defensive equipment suitable for high-level areas

Tips

  • Inquire or Scrutinize the townspeople to reveal hidden item locations scattered across rooftops and cliff edges, as well as to discount inn stays.
  • Stock up on Concoct ingredients such as Purifying Dust and Soothing Dust here before heading into Alfyn's Chapter 4 boss fight.
