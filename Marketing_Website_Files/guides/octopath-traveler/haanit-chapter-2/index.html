H'aanit, Chapter 2

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A hunt for the Lord of the Forest in Stonegard

H'aanit arrives in Stonegard in search of Master Z'aanta and follows a lead regarding the scholar Natalia. To save the surrounding wilderness from creeping petrification, H'aanit ventures into the Spectacle Hollow to hunt down the Lord of the Forest.

Tips

  • The Lord of the Forest changes its vulnerabilities throughout the battle; keep an assortment of weapon and elemental options ready.
  • Bring items or skills that cure Unconscious, as the Lord of the Forest's Pollen attack can stun multiple party members.
