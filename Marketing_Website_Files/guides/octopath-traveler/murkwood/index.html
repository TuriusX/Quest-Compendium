Murkwood

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Alfyn Chapter 1

The Murkwood is a forested dungeon located in the Riverlands where Alfyn travels during his first chapter to find a remedy for a venomous snake bite. The path winds through thick trees and across wooden bridges leading deep into the woods. At the heart of the forest, Alfyn must defeat the Blotted Viper to harvest the venom needed to save Nina.

Items

  • Herb of Clarity: chest on a path branching south near the entrance area
  • Olive of Life: chest located along the northern winding path
  • Inspiriting Plum: chest tucked away on an elevated wooden ridge
  • Essence of Plum: chest on the trail leading toward the boss clearing

Tips

  • Keep Alfyn stocked with Antidotes or use his Vivify and First Aid skills frequently, as enemies here and the boss regularly inflict poison.
  • Alfyn's Concoct ability can easily hit weaknesses or heal through the Blotted Viper's damage over time.
