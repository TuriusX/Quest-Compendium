Stonegard

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A historic town in the Woodlands

Stonegard is a multi-tiered settlement divided into three sections: main town, Sunshade-facing ramps/slopes, and the Stonegard Valleys. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 2 of H’aanit's tale as well as Chapter 3 of Cyrus's story. Players visiting can also recruit townspeople, take on multiple side quests, and gear up with mid-game equipment.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone (M): In a treasure chest located in the eastern portion of Stonegard Valleys
  • 5,000 Leaves: In a treasure chest hidden on the upper terrace of Stonegard

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing Grape (M), Inspiring Plum (M), Energizing Pomegranate, Olive of Life (M), various curatives
  • Armorer: Mid-tier weaponry and armor including Crescent Knife, Sturdy Shield, and Heavy Armor

Tips

  • Bring Cyrus or Alfyn to scrutinize or inquire every resident across all three tiers of town to uncover hidden item locations and discount rates at the inn.
  • A blocked doorway in the Valleys section can be accessed by challenging or provoking the guard stationed in front of it using Olberic or H'aanit.
