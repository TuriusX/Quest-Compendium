Stonegard

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · H'aanit Chapter 2

H'aanit arrives in Stonegard pursuing rumors regarding her master, Z'aanta, and the legendary beast Redeye. She must investigate the whereabouts of the scholar Suzrenah, requiring her to question locals and use Provoke to bypass obstacles. After uncovering the trail, she proceeds toward the Spector Hollow dungeon to confront the forest beast Nathan.

Tips

  • Use H'aanit's Provoke Path Action against the blocking guard outside the scholar's residence to progress the story.
  • Stock up on items or equipment that prevent Terror before facing Nathan in the nearby dungeon.
