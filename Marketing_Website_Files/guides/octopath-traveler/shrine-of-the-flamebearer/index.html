Shrine of the Flamebearer

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Ophilia Chapter 4

The Shrine of the Flamebearer is the final dungeon in Ophilia's Chapter 4, located beyond the town of Wispsmill. Ophilia journeys into the heart of the shrine to reclaim the Sacred Flame and rescue Lianna from the influence of Mattias. You must navigate the cavernous path, battling cultists and dark beasts, leading directly to the chapter's climactic multi-phase boss fight.

Items

  • 30,000 Leaves: In a treasure chest along the western path of the lower cavern
  • Revitalizing Jam: In a chest near a hidden alcove on the eastern side of the cavern
  • Imperial Vestments: In a chest guarded on an elevated ledge in the mid-section

Secrets

  • A concealed pathway through the cavern wall near the first save point leads to a chest containing high-tier restorative items.

Tips

  • Equip accessories that grant resistance against Unconscious and Silence, as Mattias and his minions regularly inflict these afflictions.
  • Bring strong Light and physical damage dealers, as Mattias consistently retains a vulnerability to Light throughout several of his shield-shift phases.
