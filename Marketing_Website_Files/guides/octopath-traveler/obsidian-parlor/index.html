Obsidian Parlor

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Cyrus Chapter 2

The Obsidian Parlor is a secluded chamber beneath the town of Quarrycrest where Cyrus tracks down the translated forbidden tome. In this dungeon, Cyrus confronts Headmaster Yvon to uncover the truth behind the stolen grimoire and the murders in town. Navigating through the guards leads directly to a confrontation with Yvon and his summoned minions.

Items

  • Fire Stone (L): In a treasure chest located along the upper walkways of the parlor
  • 5,000 Leaves: In a treasure chest hidden in an alcove before reaching the final door

Enemies

Tips

  • Cyrus's Fire and Light elemental spells hit the weaknesses of both Yvon and his minions simultaneously, making multi-target Scholar spells ideal for breaking shields quickly.
  • Defeat Yvon's Bone minions as early in the fight as possible, as Yvon remains locked from weakness breaks until they are cleared from the field.
