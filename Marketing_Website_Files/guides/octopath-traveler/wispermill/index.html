Wispermill

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A village in the Flatlands

Wispermill is an eerie, windmill-dotted rural village situated in the western reach of the Flatlands. It serves as the primary setting for Chapter 4 of Ophilia's journey, where she discovers the town has fallen under the sway of a sinister cult. Players visit this settlement to expose the corrupted leadership, complete local side quests, and access the dangerous dungeon hidden beneath the central windmill.

Don't miss

  • None

Items

  • Purifying Dust: Inside a treasure chest hidden in the southeastern field near the entrance
  • Wind Soulstone (L): Inside a chest located behind a windmill in the upper area of the town

Secrets

  • Ebisu's Lair entrance | The entrance to the Shrine of the Starseer is accessed via a path leading out from the western edge of the Flatlands route connecting directly near Wispermill (Western Wispermill Flats).
