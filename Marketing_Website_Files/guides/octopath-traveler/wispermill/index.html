Wispermill

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Ophilia Chapter 4

Wispermill is a seemingly quiet windmill town in the Flatlands that serves as the main setting for Ophilia's Chapter 4. Ophilia arrives pursuing the cult of the Order of the Black Flame, only to discover the entire village has fallen under the sway of their fanatical teachings. Players must investigate the strange behavior of the brainwashed townsfolk before proceeding to the nearby Ebony Grotto to rescue Lianna.

Items

  • Firestorm Amulet: In a treasure chest located behind the church near the back of the village
  • Imperial Vestments: In a treasure chest hidden beside a windmill along the western path
  • Inspiriting Plum Basket: In a chest inside one of the villager homes in the lower section of town

Secrets

  • Behind the large central windmill, there is an obscured path leading to a hidden chest containing a Purifying Dust.

Shops and people

  • Wispermill Provisioner: Offers healing supplies including Healing Grape (M), Inspiriting Plum (M), Purifying Seed, and Revival Herb
  • Wispermill Armorer: Sells high-tier mid-to-late game equipment including Imperial Lances, Rune Bows, and Elemental Robes

Tips

  • The villagers are hostile and refuse to speak normally until you progress Ophilia's story events, so you must rely on Scrutinize or Inquire on accessible targets to uncover what happened to the town.
  • Equipping items or accessories that grant Light and Dark resistance is recommended here, as enemies and the forthcoming chapter boss utilize heavy elemental attacks of these types.
