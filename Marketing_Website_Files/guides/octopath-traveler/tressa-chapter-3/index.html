Tressa, Chapter 3

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Tressa visits Grandport for the auction

Tressa arrives in the seaside town of Victor's Hollow in the Woodlands seeking a master artisan she met previously, Leon Bastralle, to learn more about the merchant's craft. She agrees to hunt down the dangerous beast known as the Venomtooth Tiger inside the Forgotten Grotto in order to obtain Eldrite for him. After confronting the beast and returning the stone, Leon shares his past as a pirate and gives Tressa an invaluable artifact: a dragon's vest.

Items

  • 1,000 Leaves: Victor's Hollow, inside a chest behind a wooden fence
  • Shadow Nut: Victor's Hollow, chest in the central plaza area
  • 15,000 Leaves: Forgotten Grotto, inside a chest on an upper pathway
  • Shadow Soulstone (L): Forgotten Grotto, inside a chest hidden down a southern side path
  • Dragon's Vest: Awarded automatically after defeating the chapter boss and talking to Leon

Enemies

Tips

  • Equip accessories like the Antidote Stone or concoct poison-curing items, as the Venomtooth Tiger frequently poisons the entire party.
  • Bring an Apothecary to cast Empoison or use Concoct to cleanse ailments and keep the party sustained during prolonged status affliction phases.
