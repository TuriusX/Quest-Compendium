Primrose, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A dancer seeks revenge in Sunshade

Primrose Azelhart works as an exotic dancer in Sunshade while secretly hunting for the men with the crow tattoo who murdered her father. After sighting one of the marked men, she uses her charms to access the Sunshade Catacombs to pursue him, turning against her cruel master Helgenish. Guide a besotted gentleman using Primrose's Path Action, navigate the Sunshade Catacombs, and defeat Helgenish in boss combat.

Items

  • Eye Opener: Sunshade, inside the tavern
  • Shadow Soulstone: Sunshade Catacombs, in a chest
  • SP Nut: Sunshade Catacombs, in a chest

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
HelgenishDagger, Bow, Ice, LightSummons Helgenish's Footmen during the battle and buffs them
Helgenish's FootmanSword, Dagger, Staff, DarkAccompany Helgenish and attack Primrose

Shops and people

  • Sunshade Provisioner: Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, Daggers, and basic dancer equipment

Tips

  • Use Primrose's Allure Path Action on a high-strength townsperson before heading into the Catacombs so you can summon them to deal heavy damage or break shields.
  • Primrose's Moonlight Waltz deals substantial dark-elemental damage and is very effective for hitting weaknesses in the Catacombs.
  • When Helgenish summons his footmen, focus on eliminating them quickly or using multi-target attacks so Primrose does not get overwhelmed.
