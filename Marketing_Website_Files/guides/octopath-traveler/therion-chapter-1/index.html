Therion, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A thief is blackmailed in Bolderfall

Therion arrives in the cliffside town of Bolderfall targeting the legendary treasures hidden inside the Ravus Manor. After using his Steal path action to gain access disguised as a merchant, he infiltrates the mansion dungeon only to be caught and coerced by the butler Heathcote into recovering three stolen Dragonstones.

Items

  • Wind Soulstone: In a chest inside the Ravus Manor infiltration path
  • SP Nut: In a chest located in Bolderfall

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
HeathcoteSword, Dagger, Fire, Ice, LightningSummons a Ravus Guard; watch out for his swift dagger attacks
Ravus GuardSword, LightAccompanies Heathcote during the boss fight

Tips

  • Use Therion's Steal ability on townspeople in Bolderfall prior to infiltrating the manor to stock up on healing items and upgrade your gear for free.
  • Therion is the only character who can open locked purple treasure chests, making it worthwhile to explore every corner of Bolderfall and the Ravus Manor.
