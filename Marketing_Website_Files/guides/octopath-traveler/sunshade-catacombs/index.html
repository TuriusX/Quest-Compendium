Sunshade Catacombs

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Primrose Chapter 1

Primrose ventures deep into the Sunshade Catacombs beneath the pleasure city of Sunshade to confront her abusive master, Helgenish. She must navigate through narrow, torch-lit cavern corridors filled with hostile creatures and guards. At the deepest point of the catacombs, Primrose faces Helgenish and his lackeys to win her freedom and begin her quest for revenge.

Items

  • Old Coin: Chest in an alcove along the main path
  • Inspiriting Plum: Chest on an elevated ledge reached via stairs
  • Shadow Soulstone: Chest tucked away in an eastern side tunnel
  • Healing Grape: Chest located near the final save point before the boss chamber

Tips

  • Use Primrose's Moonlight Waltz to exploit dark weaknesses or deal reliable neutral elemental damage against enemies with high physical defense.
  • Bring an Allured townsperson from Sunshade into the catacombs to provide extra attacks and absorb hits during combat.
  • Helgenish can inflict the Blind status on party members, so rely on Primrose's elemental skills or use an Allured summon's attacks when physical strikes keep missing.
