Olberic, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A fallen knight seeks purpose in Cobbleston

In Cobbleston, the fallen knight Olberic serves as a village watchman under the pseudonym Berg until bandits threaten the peace. After defending the town and discovering a lead regarding the traitor who destroyed his homeland, he pursues the raiders to the Brigands' Den. Defeating the bandit leader Gaston pushes Olberic to leave the village to seek out his former comrade, Erhardt.

Items

  • Healing Grape: Chest near the village entrance of Cobbleston
  • Olive of Life: Chest on the path leading up to the watchtower in Cobbleston
  • Inspiring Plum: Chest inside the house at the top of Cobbleston
  • 1,000 Leaves: Chest hidden behind a stone archway in North Cobbleston Gap
  • Healing Grape: Chest along the main trail in North Cobbleston Gap
  • Wind Soulstone: Chest off a side path in the Mountain Pass
  • Light Soulstone: Chest in the upper cavern paths of Brigands' Den
  • Bronze Helm: Chest located deep in the Brigands' Den

Tips

  • Use Olberic's Boosted "Level Slash" with a sword to quickly clear out groups of bandits and break multiple enemies at once.
  • During the duel tutorials in Cobbleston, rely on "Abide" to increase your physical attack power before unleashing fully boosted standard attacks or skills.
