Saintsbridge

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A bridge town in the Riverlands

Saintsbridge is a scenic settlement in the Riverlands constructed across a prominent river and linked by a central stone bridge. It serves as the primary stage for Ophilia's Chapter 2 and Alfyn's Chapter 3 story quests, while also hosting a grand cathedral. Players can restock equipment, investigate local gossip for hidden items, and recruit helpful NPCs across its three sections.

Items

  • Heavy Bow: In a chest inside the provisioner's house in Saintsbridge
  • 5,000 Leaves: In a chest located in the eastern section of Saintsbridge

Secrets

  • Inquiring or Scrutinizing the knowledge-bearing NPCs around the town and cathedral reveals several hidden item markers on the ground, yielding items like concocting ingredients and restorative nuts.

Shops and people

  • Saintsbridge Armorer: Assorted mid-tier weapons, armor, and shields suited for level 20–30 parties

Tips

  • Bring both Alfyn and Cyrus to maximize your ability to gather information and locate every hidden item scattered between the town entrance, upstream area, and the cathedral.
  • Use Therion to Steal or Tressa to Purchase valuable accessories and concoction materials directly from the children and guards stationed near the river bridge.
