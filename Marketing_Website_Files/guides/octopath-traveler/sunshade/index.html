Sunshade

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A desert town in the Sunlands

Sunshade is a bustling, decadent desert town located in the Sunlands region. It serves as the starting location for the dancer Primrose, where her quest for vengeance against the men marked with the crow tattoo begins. Players visiting the town can explore the tavern and local markets while navigating Primrose's opening storyline.

Items

  • Shadow Soulstone: Inside a treasure chest located in the Sunshade tavern
  • Dagger: Inside a treasure chest on the eastern side of town

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
HelgenishSwords, Daggers, Ice, LightBoss of Primrose's Chapter 1 who commands underlings in battle

Shops and people

  • Provisioner: Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, Olive of Life, and basic status-curing items
  • Armorer: Low-tier daggers, swords, staves, and basic defensive armor suitable for Chapter 1 characters

Tips

  • Primrose's Path Action, Allure, allows you to recruit townspeople to aid you in combat and unleash additional attacks.
  • Helgenish will summon additional footmen to shield him; focus on eliminating his minions first to lower his defenses.
