Sunshade

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · Primrose Chapter 1

Sunshade is a desert town in the Sunlands region known for its bustling nightlife, taverns, and pleasure houses. In Chapter 1, the dancer Primrose plots her revenge against the men who murdered her father while working under a cruel master named Helgenish. Players must explore the town, use Primrose's Allure Path Action to gather allies and manipulate targets, and eventually pursue Helgenish to the Sunshade Catacombs.

Items

  • Inspiration Stone: In a treasure chest located down an alleyway in the eastern section of town
  • Old Coin: In a treasure chest near the entrance path to the Sunshade Catacombs
  • Herb of Clamor: In a treasure chest found near the tavern exterior

Shops and people

  • Sunshade Armorer: Sells basic early-game equipment including daggers, swords, light armor, and shields
  • Sunshade Provisioner: Sells standard restorative items including Healing Grapes, Inspiring Plums, and status curatives

Tips

  • Use Primrose's Allure Path Action on strong townspeople early so you have an offensive summon to help deal heavy damage and break shields during boss battles.
  • Primrose's Night Ode skill deals Dark elemental damage to all foes, making it excellent for clearing groups of enemies quickly in the adjacent catacombs.
