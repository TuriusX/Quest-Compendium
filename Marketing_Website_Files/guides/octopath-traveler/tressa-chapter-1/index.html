Tressa, Chapter 1

OCTOPATH TRAVELER™ guide · A merchant leaves her home in Coastlands

Tressa Colzione starts her journey in the seaside village of Ripplerock when pirates rob arriving merchants. She purchases a mysterious journal from an enigmatic sea captain named Leon and sets off to the Caves of Maiya to recover the stolen goods. After confronting the pirate duo Mikk and Blacc, she resolves to travel the wider world to find her true treasure.

Tips

  • Tressa's Purchase path action allows you to obtain useful equipment and restorative items directly from townspeople, often unlocking gear better than standard shop stock early on.
  • Use Tressa's Hired Help skill in boss battles to summon Mercenaries, granting an immediate defense boost to the entire party while breaking sword-weak enemies.
  • In the battle against Mikk and Blacc, prioritize defeating one boss quickly to stop their coordinated offensive and defensive buffs.
