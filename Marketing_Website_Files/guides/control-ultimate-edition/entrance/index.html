Entrance

CONTROL Ultimate Edition guide · Arrive at the Oldest House and find the Director's office
← All areas in this guide

Jesse Faden enters the Federal Bureau of Control headquarters, the Oldest House, searching for answers about her past. The building appears completely abandoned until she encounters the janitor, Ahti, who directs her to take the elevator up to the Director's office. After entering the office, Jesse finds Director Zachariah Trench dead on the floor and claims his Service Weapon.

Shops and people
  • Ahti: Provides directions to the Director's office via the janitor's elevator

Tips
  • You cannot run or draw a weapon yet; focus on exploring the lobby and picking up documents before heading to the elevator.
  • Follow the janitor's directions straight to the elevator corridor, as side doors on the lower level are locked during this initial sequence.
