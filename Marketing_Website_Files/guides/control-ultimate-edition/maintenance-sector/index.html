Maintenance Sector

CONTROL Ultimate Edition guide · Travel to the Maintenance sector to fix the Power Plant
← All areas in this guide

Jesse travels to the Maintenance Sector during the mission "Unknown Caller" to lift the Sector lockdown by repairing the NSC Power Plant. You must speak to the janitor Ahti, venture into the ventilation and coolant systems to solve clogs, and restore power to the cooling pumps. Once the coolant and energy converters are fixed, you return to the Power Plant control room to lift the lockdown.

Achievements here
Insular TelekinesisComplete the side mission "A Good Defense" in the Maintenance Sector to bind the Home Safe Object of Power.
Volatile DebrisUnlock the Shield Burst upgrade in the ability tree and use it to kill 10 enemies.
Rush JobUnlock the Shield Rush ability in The Foundation and use it to kill 100 enemies.

Hiss Charged

Kamikaze enemies that fly or sprint toward Jesse and explode on impact.

Shops and people
  • Control Point (Astral Constructs): Crafts Service Weapon forms, weapon mods, and personal mods using Source and gathered materials.
  • Ahti: Provides janitorial side missions from the task board in his office that reward crafting materials and mods.

Tips
  • Shoot or throw Launch objects at Hiss Charged before they get close, as their explosions deal massive damage and trigger chain reactions.
  • Use the Launch ability to clear the glowing yellow matter clogs blocking the Coolant Pumps and NSC Converters instead of wasting ammunition.
