Maintenance Sector

CONTROL Ultimate Edition guide · Jesse enters the sector to fix the power plant
← All areas in this guide

Jesse ventures into the Maintenance Sector to repair the NSC Power Plant and stabilize the Oldest House's lockdown. Guided by the janitor Ahti, she must navigate hazardous, industrial zones to clear clogs in the coolant pumps and restart the power converters. Along the way, Jesse claims new Objects of Power that unlock vital traversal and combat abilities.

Achievements here
Insular TelekinesisComplete the side mission "A Good Defense" in the Maintenance Sector to bind the Home Safe Object of Power.
Volatile DebrisUnlock the Shield Burst upgrade in the ability tree and use it to kill 10 enemies.
Rush JobUnlock the Shield Rush ability in The Foundation and use it to kill 100 enemies.

Enemies
Hiss Guard

Common foot soldier armed with standard firearms.

Hiss Ranger

Armored soldier carrying shotguns or automatic weapons that utilizes aggressive tactics.

Hiss Sniper

Long-range threat targeting Jesse with high-damage, laser-sighted sniper rifles.

Hiss Charged

Kamikaze enemy that runs directly at Jesse and explodes upon impact.

Hiss Trooper

Heavy armored unit equipped with a mini-gun or rocket launcher that requires heavy Launch attacks to break shields.

The Clog
Weak to Launch attacks on its glowing tentacle nodes

Massive tentacle-based entity obstructing the coolant and waste systems that must be destroyed to restore plant operations.

Shops and people
  • Ahti: Offers janitorial side missions from his task board in the Janitor's Office that reward crafting materials, mods, and ability points

Tips
  • Pick up radioactive power cores with Launch and slot them into marked sockets to restore power to blast doors and machinery.
  • Keep moving during encounters with Hiss Charged to prevent their explosive attacks from cornering you in narrow corridors.
