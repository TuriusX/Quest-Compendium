Recommended order
  1. Complete the main story through the Executive, Maintenance, Research, and Containment sectors while acquiring all base supernatural abilities.
  2. Respond to Bureau Alerts and complete Board Countermeasures alongside story progression to work toward their cumulative requirements early.
  3. Clear all side missions across the Oldest House to unlock all ability upgrades, hidden locations, and optional bosses.
  4. Enter The Foundation DLC via the Hotline Chamber to explore the Crossroads, Cave System, Warehouse, Canyon Rim, and Astral Collision for all DLC-specific quests and collectibles.
  5. Enter the AWE DLC via the Investigations Sector elevator, clearing Active Investigations, the Fra Mauro AWE, the Eagle Limited AWE, and Bright Falls AWE.
  6. Clean up any remaining cumulative combat tasks, weapon forms, material crafting, and unvisited hidden areas in free roam.
