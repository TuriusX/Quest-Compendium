Far Harbor

Fallout 4 guide · traveling to the island for the detective case

The Sole Survivor travels by boat to the island of Far Harbor after taking a missing person case from the Valentine Detective Agency regarding Kasumi Nakano. Upon arrival at the harbor town, the player assists the locals in defending the gate before venturing inland to uncover the conflicts between the Harbormen, the Children of Atom, and the synth refuge of Acadia. The area features heavy radioactive fog, unique mutations, and branching story choices that determine the fate of the island's factions.

Items

  • The Fishmonger's Catch: Sold by Allen Lee at his gun shop in the town of Far Harbor
  • The Captain's Feast: Rewarded by Teddy Wright upon completing the quest "The Rite of Passage" in Far Harbor
  • Islander's Almanac (issue #1): Located inside the Last Plank tavern on the counter next to Old Longfellow

Shops and people

  • Allen Lee: Weapons, ammunition, armor, and weapon modifications at his outdoor weapon shop in the town of Far Harbor
  • Brooks: General goods, junk, and basic supplies from his stall in the town of Far Harbor
  • Mitch: Food, drinks, and lodging inside The Last Plank tavern in the town of Far Harbor

Tips

  • Bring Nick Valentine as your companion, as he possesses extensive unique dialogue and critical backstory ties to Acadia and the main questline.
  • Pack ample RadAway, Rad-X, or power armor, as the ambient fog covering much of the island steadily inflicts radiation damage.
