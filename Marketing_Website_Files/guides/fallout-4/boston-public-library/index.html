Boston Public Library

Fallout 4 guide · exploring the library ruins

The Boston Public Library is an abandoned pre-war landmark occupied by a fierce battle between Super Mutants and automated defense systems. Players can explore the reading rooms and archives while helping or avoiding the Protectrons and Turrets holding off the mutant invaders. The location features a unique book return terminal where overdue books found across the Commonwealth can be redeemed for various prizes.

Items

  • Intelligence Bobblehead: Located in the northwest mechanical room inside the library, resting on a computer console next to a terminal
  • Massachusetts Surgical Journal: Located in the terminal room near the subway entrance on a computer desk
  • Stealth Boy: Inside a terminal in the book return area, redeemable for 100 book return tokens

Secrets

  • The library can be entered without picking an Advanced lock by going through the Copley metro station entrance connected directly to the library basement.

Shops and people

  • Book Return Terminal: Exchanges Overdue Books for tokens to buy magazines, a Stealth Boy, Fusion Cores, and various crafting materials

Tips

  • Do not damage the Protectrons or wall turrets initially, as they will draw fire from the Super Mutants and make clearing the interior much easier.
  • Collect every Overdue Book you find throughout the Commonwealth before visiting, as you can turn them all into the terminal here for instant prize tokens.
  • Hacking the library curator terminal allows you to access facility intercoms and maintain control over the defense turrets.
