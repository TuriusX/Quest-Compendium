Croup Manor

Fallout 4 guide · clearing the ghouls

Croup Manor is a ruined, ghoul-infested seaside mansion located on the Nahant peninsula. To turn the location into an allied settlement, you must clear out all the feral ghouls inside and outside the house, including those locked in the basement. Once every ghoul on the property is dead, you can activate the workshop on the ground floor.

Items

  • Croup Manor Key: Carried by Theodore Croup in the basement
  • Mini Nuke: In the basement next to a mattress and steamer trunk

Secrets

  • The basement cellar can be accessed via a novice-locked floor hatch inside the manor or using the key dropped by the named ghoul inside.

Tips

  • Look out for ghouls that play dead on the floor and porch; shoot them from a distance before they stand up.
  • After clearing the basement, make sure you double-check the cliff edges and coastline around the house if the settlement workshop remains locked.
