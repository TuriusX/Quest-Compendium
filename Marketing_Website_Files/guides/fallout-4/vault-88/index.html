Vault 88

Fallout 4 guide · exploring the underground construction site

Vault 88 is an unfinished subterranean vault introduced in the Vault-Tec Workshop add-on, entered through Quincy Quarries. After clearing out raiders and helping Overseer Barstow, the Sole Survivor unlocks the workshop to clear massive cave-ins and construct a custom vault settlement. Exploring the expansive sector tunnels allows you to secure the perimeter against native creatures, connect power, and conduct experimental prototype tests on dwellers.

Items

  • Control Board: Found inside an active workshop station in each unopened sector (North, East, South)
  • Overseer's Desk blueprint: Unlocked automatically in the workshop menu during the quest "A Model Citizen"

Secrets

  • Massive radioactive scrap piles deep in the tunnels can be scrapped in workshop mode to permanently remove high radiation zones while yielding massive supplies of nuclear material.

Tips

  • Scrap the limestone wall blockages within workshop mode to open up passages into the North, East, and South expansion sectors.
  • You must find and insert the specific Sector Control Board into the sub-workshops before you can build or scrap in those outlying cavern zones.
  • The main cavern features an enormous natural water pool in the East Sector where high-capacity industrial water purifiers can be placed.
