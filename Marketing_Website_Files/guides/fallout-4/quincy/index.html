Quincy

Fallout 4 guide · southern Commonwealth, after the Quincy massacre

Quincy is a ruined town in the southeastern Commonwealth that has been completely overtaken by Gunners following the massacre of its former Minutemen and civilian residents. Players can battle through heavily fortified street-level barricades, multistory ruins, and an elevated freeway network to eliminate hostile command personnel and scavenge high-tier combat gear. The settlement serves as a major environmental storytelling hub detailing Preston Garvey's tragic backstory and the betrayal of the Minutemen by Clint.

Items

  • Guns and Bullets issue #4: Elevated freeway level, resting on a desk near a computer terminal within Clint's personal encampment
  • Big Boy (Fat Man): Dropped by the named Gunner boss Baker, situated atop the church scaffolding and roof
  • Good Intentions: Carried and dropped by Clint on the elevated highway section
  • Quincy Police Department Key: Looted from Tessa's corpse or found on top of a terminal inside the police station

Secrets

  • Preston Garvey's former defensive headquarters and emergency bunker entrance can be accessed through a hidden basement hatch beneath the broken floor of the town church.
