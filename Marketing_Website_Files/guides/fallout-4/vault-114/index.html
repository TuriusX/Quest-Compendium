Vault 114

Fallout 4 guide · rescuing Nick Valentine from the mobsters

The Sole Survivor enters Vault 114 beneath Park Street Station to rescue the kidnapped detective Nick Valentine during the quest "Unlikely Valentine". The unfinished vault has been repurposed as a hideout by Skinny Malone and his gang of Triggermen. Fighting or talking your way out with Nick completes the rescue and advances the main storyline.

Items

  • Speech Bobblehead: Resting on a desk directly behind Nick Valentine in the overseer's office where he is held
  • Astoundingly Incredible Tales #8: Located on a container in the blocked, unfinished living quarters hallway
  • Overseer's Office Key: Carried by Dino, who is guarding the entrance to the overseer's office

Tips

  • You can hack the terminal near Dino to activate the protectron, which can distract or eliminate nearby Triggermen for you.
  • With high Charisma, you can convince Darla to turn on Skinny Malone or abandon the fight entirely, avoiding a shootout at the exit.
  • Triggermen heavily favor Submachine Guns, so looting them provides a large supply of .45 caliber ammunition early in the game.
