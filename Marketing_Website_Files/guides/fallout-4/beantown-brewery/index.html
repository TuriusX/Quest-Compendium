Beantown Brewery

Fallout 4 guide · clearing out the raiders

Beantown Brewery is an industrial raider stronghold located south of the Charles River that can be cleared out for general loot or as part of the quest "Confidence Man" to rescue the kidnapped radio host Vadim Bobrov. Players must fight through multiple interior catwalks and brewing rooms, taking out regular raiders, attack dogs, and their leader, Tower Tom. Once the raiders are eliminated and Vadim is freed from the holding cell if the quest is active, you can thoroughly loot the brewery before exiting.

Items

  • Picket Fences: In the brewery foreman's office near the top catwalks, resting on the floor beside an upturned green trunk and mattress
  • Gwinnett Stout holotape recipe: Inside the terminal-locked office at the catwalk level, next to the terminal on a small table
  • Tower Tom's terminal password: On the body of the raider boss Tower Tom

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Tower TomHeadThe raider boss of the facility, typically armed with a hunting rifle and armored chest piece, fighting from the elevated walkway.
RaiderHeadStandard raiders patrol the ground level, catwalks, and brewing vats with low-tier firearms and pipe guns.
Attack DogLegsPatrols the brewery floor alongside the raiders and will rush in to pin the player in melee.

Tips

  • Watch out for tripwires and floor traps placed along the narrow walkways and stairs leading up to Tower Tom's office.
  • If you are doing the quest "Confidence Man", ensure you clear the upper level carefully so crossfire does not hit Vadim Bobrov inside his cell.
