Poseidon Energy

Fallout 4 guide · clearing the raider base

Poseidon Energy is a large power plant located in the southeastern Commonwealth that has been converted into a heavily fortified raider stronghold. The interior is filled with raiders, automated turrets, and Mirelurks in the flooded sub-levels. To clear the base, you must navigate multi-level catwalks, disable the security systems, and eliminate the raider boss Cutthroat.

Items

  • Endurance Bobblehead: On the central elevated metal office desk in the main reactor room
  • Tesla Science Magazine: On a desk next to a broken terminal in the catwalk office overlooking the reactor area
  • Fusion Core: Inside the generator room on the lowest level
  • Poseidon Energy Key: Carried by the raider leader, Cutthroat

Secrets

  • A submerged cooling intake pipe on the exterior leads directly into the flooded sub-levels, providing a stealthy alternate entrance that bypasses perimeter turrets.

Tips

  • Bring RadAway or a Hazmat suit, as the water-filled sub-levels and turbine chambers emit significant radiation.
  • Hack the master terminal in the main control room to turn the internal ceiling turrets against the raiders.
