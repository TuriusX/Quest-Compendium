Concord

Fallout 4 guide · fighting raiders and the Deathclaw

You visit Concord early in the main quest "When Freedom Calls" to investigate gunfire and rescue trapped settlers inside the Museum of Freedom. You must clear out the hostile raiders inside and outside the museum, retrieve a suit of Power Armor from the roof, and defeat a powerful Deathclaw that emerges into the streets. Once the threats are eliminated, you can talk to Preston Garvey to help the survivors relocate safely to Sanctuary.

Items

  • Perception Bobblehead: On a desk next to a broken terminal in the room on the top floor of the Museum of Freedom where Preston Garvey and the survivors are holding out
  • T-45 Power Armor: Abandoned on the roof of the Museum of Freedom, accessed by going through the crashed vertibird
  • Minigun: Mounted on the crashed vertibird on the roof of the Museum of Freedom
  • Laser Musket: Lying on the pavement outside the front entrance of the Museum of Freedom next to a dead Minuteman
  • Fusion Core: Inside a generator in the locked basement utility room of the Museum of Freedom
  • RobCo Fun (magazine containing the Atomic Command holotape): On the desk with the terminal next to the door in the Museum of Freedom's upper office room

Tips

  • Enter the nearby open buildings or shops to shoot the Deathclaw from the doorway, as its massive model cannot fit through standard doors.
  • Focus your gunfire on the Deathclaw's soft belly rather than its armored head to deal critical damage.
  • The Laser Musket found outside the museum can be cranked multiple times before firing to release a single, high-damage burst that is great for opening combat on tough targets.
