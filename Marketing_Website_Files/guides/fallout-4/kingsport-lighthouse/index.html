Kingsport Lighthouse

Fallout 4 guide · clearing the Children of Atom

Kingsport Lighthouse is a coastal settlement location occupied by hostile Children of Atom cultists and a captive Glowing One. Players must eliminate all cultists, the Glowing One trapped at the top of the lighthouse, and clear out the docks to unlock the workbench. Once cleared, the location can be claimed and developed as a player settlement.

Items

  • Wasteland Survival Guide #6: On a small table inside the lighthouse, near the top level staircase

Tips

  • Equip a Hazmat Suit or Power Armor before attacking to completely negate the heavy radiation damage dealt by the cultists' Gamma Guns.
  • Beware of the dock area below the house, as several mines are planted along the pathways and stairs leading down to the water.
  • The Glowing One trapped in the lantern room emits a constant radiation pulse, so kill it quickly or take it out from range through the doorway.
