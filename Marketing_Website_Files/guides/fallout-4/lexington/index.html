Lexington

Fallout 4 guide · passing through on the way to Corvega

Lexington is a sprawling suburban ruin located directly north of the Corvega Assembly Plant, serving as a dangerous transitional hub on your way there. The town square and streets are contested ground filled with large packs of Feral Ghouls and heavily armed Raiders. Taking high ground or skirting the edges is often necessary for low-level players trying to survive the trek toward the plant.

Items

  • Fat Man: Carried by the Raider in Power Armor on the overhead walkway near the town center
  • Mini Nuke: Carried by the Raider in Power Armor on the overhead walkway near the town center

Tips

  • Listen carefully for the high-pitched whistle of an incoming Mini Nuke when entering the central streets, as the Power Armor Raider has long sightlines.
  • You can use the outer drainage ditches and rail lines to bypass the hazardous central crossfire entirely if you want to reach Corvega without fighting through the town.
