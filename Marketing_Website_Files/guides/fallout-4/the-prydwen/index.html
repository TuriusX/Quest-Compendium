The Prydwen

Fallout 4 guide · arriving on the Brotherhood airship

The Prydwen serves as the massive airborne command center and main base of operations for the Brotherhood of Steel in the Commonwealth. Upon arrival, you attend an introductory address by Elder Maxson and report to key officers aboard the ship to officially integrate into their ranks. The airship features living quarters, medical facilities, research labs, and a flight deck equipped with power armor stations.

Items

  • Fusion Core: Inside the generator room on the lower main deck
  • Maxson's Battlecoat MISSABLE: Worn by Elder Arthur Maxson, obtainable by killing him
  • Final Judgment MISSABLE: Carried by Elder Arthur Maxson, obtainable by killing him
  • T-60 Power Armor: Awarded by Proctor Ingram on the main deck after completing your introductory tour

Shops and people

  • Proctor Teagan: Power armor mods, heavy weapons, ammunition, Brotherhood gear, and miscellaneous supplies
  • Knight-Captain Cade: Medical supplies, addiction curing, radiation removal, and health restoration

Tips

  • Check in with Proctor Teagan to take radiant procurement quests, and talk to Proctor Quinlan to turn in Technical Documents for extra caps.
  • A full set of Brotherhood T-60 power armor is assigned to you on the main deck once you finish speaking to the division heads.
  • Walking around the catwalks outside the command deck carries a risk of falling to the airport tarmac below, so watch your footing or wear power armor to negate fall damage.
