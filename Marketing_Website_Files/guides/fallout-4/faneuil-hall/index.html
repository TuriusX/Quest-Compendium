Faneuil Hall

Fallout 4 guide · clearing the super mutants

Faneuil Hall is a multi-story historic landmark in central Boston that has been turned into a heavily fortified outpost by super mutants. To clear the location, you must fight through barricades outside, ascend through multiple interior levels connected by scaffolding and stairs, and eliminate the mutants and hounds occupying the building all the way to the attic and roof. It serves as an active hostile zone and is also visited during the Railroad quest line.

Items

  • Total Hack: On the second floor above the main hall, resting on the podium in the center of the room
  • Live & Love: On a small table next to a sofa inside the top-floor attic area

Tips

  • Listen carefully for the beeping sound of a Super Mutant Suicider both when approaching the plaza outside and when entering the ground-floor interior.
  • Sneak through the main hall and avoid triggering tripwires and noise-making bone chimes that alert the upper-level snipers to your position.
  • You can shoot the Suicider's right arm from long range inside the main hall to clear a large cluster of enemies on the ground level in a single blast.
