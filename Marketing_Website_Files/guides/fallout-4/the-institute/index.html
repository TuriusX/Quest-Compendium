The Institute

Fallout 4 guide · entering the underground facility

The Institute is an advanced subterranean research facility that serves as the headquarters of the Institute faction, reached via molecular teleportation. Inside, the Sole Survivor meets Father, explores distinct divisions focused on robotics, bioscience, synth retention, and advanced systems, and decides whether to ally with or oppose the organization. Peaceful exploration and faction quest progression occur here unless the player initiates combat or becomes permanently hostile with the Institute.

Items

  • Institute Core Magazine (Astoundingly Awesome Tales) MISSABLE: Outside the living quarters on a balcony table in the main concourse
  • Syringer: On a medical cart inside the BioScience division
  • Synth Uniform: Found in the personal living quarters assigned to the player

Secrets

  • Abandoned FEV Lab | Accessed through a locked terminal door in the BioScience division, containing holotapes detailing the Institute's super mutant experiments.

Shops and people

  • Synth Requisition Officer: Sells Institute-themed gear, ammo, fusion cells, and the unique weapon Experiment 18-A.
  • Dean Volkert: Offers medical services and radiation removal inside the BioScience division.

Tips

  • Tag fast-travel points quickly by speaking with all four division heads, which unlocks fast travel directly in and out of the Institute.
  • Becoming hostile to the Institute immediately locks you out of the facility and fails all pending Institute quests permanently.
