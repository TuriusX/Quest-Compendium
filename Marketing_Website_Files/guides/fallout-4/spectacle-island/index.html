Spectacle Island

Fallout 4 guide · claiming the island settlement

Spectacle Island is a massive, water-isolated settlement location inhabited by swarms of Mirelurks. To claim the workbench, you must restore power by flipping a breaker in a derelict tugboat and then activating a sonic transmitter in the metal shack near the radio tower. Activating the boat generator provokes high-level Mirelurks, including a Mirelurk Queen, while the transmitter drives them away.

Items

  • Luck Bobblehead: Inside a metal locker placed next to a steamer trunk inside the pilot house of the southern derelict tugboat

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Mirelurk QueenSpawns from the water near the southern tugboat immediately after flipping the boat's circuit breaker
Mirelurk Deep KingLurks around the island and attacks alongside the queen during the power restoration process
Mirelurk HunterPatches of the island are infested with them, and they attack aggressively when power is switched on

Tips

  • Prepare for high radiation exposure while swimming out to the island by using a Hazmat Suit, Power Armor, or the Aquaboy/Aquagirl perk.
  • Follow the overhead power lines on poles from the tugboat generator directly to the radio shack to easily navigate without getting lost.
  • Flipping the switch inside the metal radio shack immediately causes remaining Mirelurks to flee in panic due to the high-frequency sound, making combat unnecessary if you can sprint inside and activate it quickly.
