Fort Hagen

Fallout 4 guide · hunting Kellogg

Fort Hagen serves as the primary base of operations for the mercenary Conrad Kellogg during the main quest "Reunions." You must infiltrate the fortified building, navigate down through the underground command center, and eliminate Kellogg to advance the search for your son. Exiting the facility via the roof triggers a major narrative event involving the arrival of the Brotherhood of Steel.

Items

  • Guns and Bullets issue #2: On an oval dining table inside the Fort Hagen Command Center kitchen area
  • Energy Weapons Bobblehead: In the Command Center, between two computer banks on a desk in the kitchen/mess hall area
  • RobCo Fun (Pip-Fall game): Next to Kellogg's terminal on a desk in the room where you confront him
  • Kellogg's Pistol: Carried by Kellogg
  • Kellogg's Outfit: Carried by Kellogg
  • Cybernetic Brain Augmenter: Looted from Kellogg's body
  • Fat Man: Inside the Fort Hagen Command Center armory behind an Expert-locked door or accessed via terminal
  • Mini Nuke: Inside the Fort Hagen Command Center armory

Tips

  • Bring explosives or a high-spread weapon to deal with Kellogg when he uses a Stealth Boy to conceal himself.
  • You can hack or lockpick the armory before the final confrontation room to secure a Fat Man and Mini Nuke to use against Kellogg.
  • Watch out for laser tripwires and fragmentation mines placed in the stairwells leading to the Command Center.
  • Take the elevator straight to the roof after defeating Kellogg to trigger the arrival of the Brotherhood of Steel's Prydwen.
