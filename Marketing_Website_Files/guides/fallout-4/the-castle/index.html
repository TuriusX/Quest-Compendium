The Castle

Fallout 4 guide · reclaiming the Minutemen base

The Castle is a star-shaped coastal fortification that served as the former headquarters of the Minutemen. The Sole Survivor travels here during the quest "Taking Independence" to help Preston Garvey and a squad of Minutemen wipe out a Mirelurk infestation and clear out egg clutches across the walls and courtyard. Reclaiming the fort unlocks it as a major settlement and later allows access to its sealed armory and a powerful artillery support system.

Items

  • Guns and Bullets issue #5: On a small table next to the radio transmitter in the middle of the courtyard
  • The Last Minute: Sold by Ronnie Shaw inside the Castle armory after finishing the quest "Old Guns"
  • Minutemen General's Uniform and Hat: Looted from the remains of General McGann in the Castle tunnels behind the armory security door

Secrets

  • Castle Armory | Unlocked during the quest "Old Guns" by clearing rubble in the western bastion, traversing the underground tunnels past traps and a hostile sentry bot, and bypassing terminal-locked blast doors.

Shops and people

  • Ronnie Shaw: Sells unique Gauss rifle "The Last Minute", weapons, ammunition, Minutemen gear, and general supplies once the Castle armory is unlocked.

Tips

  • Bring heavy explosive weaponry or a missile launcher specifically to deal with the Mirelurk Queen, as she possesses a massive health pool and strong armor.
  • Destroy every Mirelurk egg clutch in the courtyard and along the ramparts before the Queen arrives to prevent hatchlings from swarming you during the main boss fight.
  • Use the sturdy interior hallways and battlements as cover to block the Mirelurk Queen's acid projectile volleys.
