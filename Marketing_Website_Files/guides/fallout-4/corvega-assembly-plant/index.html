Corvega Assembly Plant

Fallout 4 guide · clearing out the raider gang

The Corvega Assembly Plant is a massive industrial dungeon in Lexington overrun by a large raider gang led by Jared. Clearing out these raiders is frequently assigned by Preston Garvey as an early Minutemen recruitment quest for nearby settlements like Tenpines Bluff. To complete the objective, you must navigate the complex interior or roof to hunt down and eliminate Jared.

Items

  • Repair Bobblehead: Located outside on the highest catwalk encircling the southwest blue sphere tank on the roof
  • Grognak the Barbarian: Inside the main assembly room in the elevated office where Jared is found, resting on Jared's terminal desk
  • Corvega Storage Key: Carried by Jared, or found in a desk near his terminal

Secrets

  • Beneath the factory floor lies an extensive subterranean maintenance tunnel system containing feral ghouls, accessible via trapdoors and sewer pipes leading out to Lexington.

Tips

  • Approaching the factory from the rooftops of Lexington lets you snipe exterior raiders and turrets before heading inside.
  • Look for hanging oil lanterns and fuel spills on the assembly floor; shooting them will ignite groups of raiders and save ammunition.
  • Use Jared's terminal to activate the factory's protectron to assist you in clearing nearby raiders.
