Diamond City

Fallout 4 guide · arriving at the Great Green Jewel

Diamond City is a fortified settlement built inside the ruins of Fenway Park, serving as the primary commercial and narrative hub in the Commonwealth. Upon first arrival, you assist the reporter Piper Wright in bluffing her way back through the entrance gate, unlocking the city interior. Inside, you can gather information about your missing son, recruit companions, take on side quests, and trade with various market vendors.

Items

  • Wasteland Survival Guide #9: On the counter inside Gorski Cabin or wait, inside the lower-level Dugout Inn on a table in the back room
  • Grognak the Barbarian: Inside Piper's house (Publick Occurrences) on top of the desk upstairs
  • Robo-Co Sales & Service Magazine: Inside the Science! Center on a table near the back

Shops and people

  • Arturo Rodriguez: Weapons, ammunition, armor mods, and the unique Fat Man named Big Boy
  • Myrna: General goods, junk components, and shipments of crafting materials at the surplus store
  • Becky Fallon: Various clothing and basic apparel at Fallon's Basement
  • Solomon: Chem items, medical supplies, and aid goods
  • Doctor Sun: Healing services, rad removal, facial reconstruction referrals, and medical supplies
  • Polly: Meat, butchered animal products, and cooking ingredients
  • Sheng Kawolski: Purified water sold from the central reservoir

Tips

  • Talk to the local guards to have various Commonwealth locations and potential faction headquarters marked directly on your Pip-Boy map.
  • You can purchase a permanent player home called Home Plate from Mayor McDonough's secretary, Geneva, for 2,000 caps.
  • Listen to Diamond City Radio on your Pip-Boy or visit the station trailer near the back bleachers to initiate a quest chain aiding the timid DJ, Travis Miles.
