Mass Pike Interchange is a multi-level Gunner fortification built across a section of collapsed highway. Players must take a yellow personnel elevator to reach the elevated road, eliminate heavy Gunner defenses, and defeat their commanding officer. Clearing the site requires navigating traps, automated turrets, and high-damage enemy explosives.

Items

0/2

Enemies
Gunners
Weak to HeadSteal / drop Combat armor, laser weapons, ballistic weapons

Stationed both on the ground perimeter and throughout the upper highway scaffolding.

Gunner Commander
Weak to HeadSteal / drop Power armor pieces, heavy weapon (often a missile launcher or Fat Man)

Patrols the top level near the shack, typically wearing a partial frame or suit of Power Armor.

Machinegun Turrets
Weak to Combat Inhibitor

Mounted to walls and guard posts overlooking the lower approach and elevator platforms.

Assaultron
Weak to LegsSteal / drop Fusion cells

High-level Gunner variant that may patrol the upper highway; capable of stealth and powerful head-laser blasts.

Tips
  • Watch out for laser tripwires and fragmentation mines rigged to doorways and ramps on the upper highway platforms.
  • Take out the Gunner Commander quickly from long range to prevent them from firing heavy explosive ordnance down the narrow catwalks.
