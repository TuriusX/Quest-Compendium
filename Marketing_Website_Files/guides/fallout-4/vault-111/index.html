Vault 111

Fallout 4 guide · escaping the cryo-stasis chamber

The Sole Survivor wakes up from cryogenic stasis to find the other Vault 111 residents deceased and must escape to the surface. You navigate dark corridors, battle giant roaches, and retrieve basic survival gear to open the exit vault door. The sequence ends when you use your Pip-Boy to activate the platform lift to the Commonwealth wasteland.

Items

  • 10mm Pistol: On the desk inside the Overseer's office
  • Cryolator: Inside a Master-locked display case mounted on the wall in the Overseer's office
  • Pip-Boy 3000 Mark IV: On the ground next to the skeleton near the Vault 111 exit control console
  • Red Menace Holotape Game: Inserted into the Recreation Terminal in the kitchen/cafeteria area
  • Security Baton: Found on a desk shortly after exiting the cryo-chamber room

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
RadroachHeadRadroach meatWeak pests that can be easily dispatched with a melee weapon to conserve ammunition

Tips

  • You cannot pick the Master lock to get the Cryolator right away, so remember to return to Vault 111 later once you unlock the Master Locksmith perk or recruit a companion who can pick it for you.
  • Search all open cryo-pods and lockers along the hallways, as they contain Stimpaks and bobby pins that are crucial for early-game survival.
