Desert Palace

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Second dungeon

The Desert Palace is the second dungeon of the Light World, located in the Desert of Mystery. Link must navigate a mix of indoor sandy rooms and an outdoor cliff ledge to reach the back section of the temple. The goal is to obtain the Power Glove and defeat the Lanmolas to claim the Pendant of Power.

Items

  • Power Glove: inside the Big Chest in the front section of the palace
  • Map: inside a chest in the northern room of the palace's first section
  • Compass: inside a chest surrounded by pots in the first section
  • Big Key: inside a chest guarded by Popos and laser eyes in the west wing
  • Pendant of Power: awarded after defeating the boss, the Lanmolas
  • Heart Container: drops upon defeating the Lanmolas

Tips

  • Stand in a corner or hold your shield facing outward to deflect or block the room's Flying Tiles until they exhaust themselves.
  • Watch the flying dirt particles on the sand during the Lanmola boss fight to predict where the worms will breach the surface.
  • You must exit through a southwest doorway out to an outdoor ledge to reach the northern entrance for the second half of the palace.
