Skull Woods

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Third Dark World dungeon

Skull Woods is the third dungeon of the Dark World, unique for having multiple interconnected entrances scattered throughout an outdoor forest area. Link must explore several underground segments, manipulate switches, and burn open the final rib cage entrance using the Fire Rod to reach the boss. Defeating Mothula inside the final section rewards Link with the third Crystal.

Items

  • Fire Rod: Inside a treasure chest located in the underground section accessible via the skull entrance with a pull-lever trapdoor
  • Big Key: Inside a treasure chest in the western underground section reached by dropping through an outdoor pit
  • Map: Inside a treasure chest in the first underground section entered from the south
  • Compass: Inside a treasure chest located in the northern underground segment

Enemies

MothulaFire RodThe dungeon boss that flies across a room with moving spike traps and conveyor belt floors while shooting ring beams.

Tips

  • Keep moving constantly in rooms where shadows appear on the floor to prevent Wallmasters from grabbing you.
  • The Fire Rod is the most effective weapon against Mothula, but manage your Magic Meter carefully with Green Potions so you do not run out.
  • You must use the Fire Rod to burn the skeletal head blocking the final underground entrance in the northern outdoor forest.
