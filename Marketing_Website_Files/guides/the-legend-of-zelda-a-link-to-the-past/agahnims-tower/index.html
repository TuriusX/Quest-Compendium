Agahnim's Tower

After obtaining the Master Sword, Link breaches the magical barrier at the top of Hyrule Castle to stop the wizard Agahnim. Navigating several dark and trap-filled rooms, Link confronts Agahnim as he banishes Princess Zelda to the Dark World. Defeating the wizard in a duel of reflected magic causes him to draw Link into the Dark World atop the Pyramid of Power.

Enemies
Agahnim
Weak to Master Sword, Bug Catching Net

Cannot be harmed directly; his magic energy balls must be deflected back at him.

Blue Knight
Weak to Sword

Standard patrolling guard armed with a sword.

Red Spear Knight
Weak to Sword

Castle guard that attacks by throwing spears at Link.

Chasupa
Weak to Sword

Bat-like enemy lurking in the darkened rooms of the tower.

Tips
  • Break the magical seal blocking the tower entrance by striking it with the Master Sword.
  • The Lantern is essential for illuminating multiple dark floors inside the tower.
  • When fighting Agahnim, avoid his blue spread attacks, as they shatter into lightning bursts that cannot be reflected.
  • You can reflect Agahnim's magic projectiles using the Bug Catching Net instead of the Master Sword.
