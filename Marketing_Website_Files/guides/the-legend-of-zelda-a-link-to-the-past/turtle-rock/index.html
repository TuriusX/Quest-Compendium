Turtle Rock

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Seventh Dark World dungeon

Turtle Rock is the seventh dungeon in the Dark World, accessed by striking stakes outside its entrance in a specific order and using the Quake Medallion on the turtle-shaped pad. Inside, Link must navigate dark rooms, lava pits using the Cane of Somaria to create platforms, and complex track networks to rescue the final maiden, Princess Zelda. Defeating the boss Trinexx yields a Heart Container and frees Zelda, opening the path to Ganon's Tower.

Items

  • Cane of Somaria: Inside a big chest in the central chamber requiring the Big Key
  • Big Key: Dropped by a chest that appears after defeating two Fire Serpents/Chains in a spiked, switch-puzzle room
  • Compass: Found in a treasure chest in the western section of the dungeon
  • Dungeon Map: Found in a treasure chest in the northern room near the entrance
  • Heart Container: Dropped by the boss Trinexx upon defeat

Secrets

  • Exiting the dungeon through a southern doorway onto a high ledge outside reveals a cave containing a Piece of Heart inside a maze of invisible floor tiles.

Tips

  • Bring plenty of Green Potions or Blue Potions, as the Fire Rod, Ice Rod, and Cane of Somaria heavily drain your Magic Meter during navigation and the Trinexx boss battle.
  • You must have the Ice Rod and Fire Rod in your inventory before entering, as Trinexx cannot be defeated without both.
