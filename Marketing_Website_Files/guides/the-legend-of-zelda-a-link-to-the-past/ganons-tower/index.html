Ganon's Tower

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Final Dark World dungeon

Ganon's Tower is the massive final dungeon in the Dark World, accessible only after rescuing all seven maidens from the Dark World crystals. Inside, you must navigate multiple branching floors, defeat rematches with previous Light World bosses, and face Agahnim a second time. Defeating Agahnim here reveals Ganon and unlocks the path to the final showdown at the Pyramid of Power.

Items

  • Red Mail: Located in a treasure chest in the basement area reached through the western path
  • Big Key: Located in a chest in the basement labyrinth on the eastern path

Tips

  • Bring the Magic Powder to transform several annoying dungeon enemies into peaceful Slimes (Bubbles).
  • Keep the Hookshot and Pegasus Boots ready, as several rooms require Hookshotting across wide gaps and dashing across conveyor floors.
  • When fighting Moldorm, use the Pegasus Boots dash sparingly and stay near the center to avoid sliding off the edges when struck.
  • Store multiple Fairies or Blue Potions in your bottles, as the gauntlet of boss rematches and high enemy damage can rapidly deplete your hearts.
