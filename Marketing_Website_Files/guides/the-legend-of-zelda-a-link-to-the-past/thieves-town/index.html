Thieves' Town

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Fourth Dark World dungeon

Thieves' Town is the fourth dungeon of the Dark World, located under the gargoyle statue in the Village of Outcasts. Link navigates its multi-floor maze to rescue a captive maiden and retrieve the dungeon's treasure. Bringing the rescued maiden into the boss room reveals her true form as the boss, Blind the Thief.

Items

  • Big Key: In the basement guarded by Stalfos and traps
  • Compass: In a chest on the first floor
  • Dungeon Map: In a chest on the first floor
  • Titan's Mitt: Inside the big treasure chest on the basement level
  • Heart Container: Rewarded upon defeating Blind

Secrets

  • Bombing the cracked floor on the upper floor allows sunlight to shine down into the basement room directly below.

Tips

  • Use bombs on cracked sections of the floor on the entrance level to let sunlight penetrate into the boss chamber below.
  • Blind's floating heads will bounce around the room shooting fireballs; focus your attacks on the main body while constantly moving to dodge the heads.
