Palace of Darkness

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · First Dark World dungeon

The Palace of Darkness is the first dungeon Link tackles in the Dark World after defeating Agahnim. Link must navigate a maze of warp tiles, darkened rooms, and cracked walkways while utilizing the Hammer to progress. Defeating the boss at the end rewards Link with the first of seven Maiden Crystals.

Items

  • Magic Hammer: Inside the big chest in the central basement chamber
  • Map: In a chest inside the entrance hall
  • Compass: In a chest guarded by Anti-Fairies in the western wing
  • Big Key: Inside a chest found in the dark maze room in the eastern wing
  • Crystal 1: Rewarded after defeating the dungeon boss, Helmasaur King

Secrets

  • In the basement room with the conveyor belt, bombing the lower-left cracked wall reveals a hidden room containing several chests with Rupees.
  • In the central northern room with the telepathic tile, bombing the cracked floor on the right side opens a hole leading down to the platform with the Magic Hammer chest.

Tips

  • You must pay Kiki the monkey 100 Rupees outside to unlock the entrance to this dungeon, and you must not take damage on the path leading there or he will run away.
  • Keep the Bow and Arrow equipped in the dark maze room to easily snipe green and red Goriyas from across the barriers.
  • The Magic Hammer can break Helmasaur King's mask in just a few hits if you prefer to save your Bombs.
