Ice Palace

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Fifth Dark World dungeon

The Ice Palace is the fifth dungeon of the Dark World, located within Lake Hylia and accessed by utilizing a warp tile found by lifting a heavy Dark World rock. Navigating this treacherous, frozen labyrinth requires melting ice obstacles, sliding across slick floors, and solving complex block puzzles across multiple descending basement floors. Defeating the boss at the bottom rescues the fifth maiden and awards another Crystal.

Items

  • Blue Mail: inside the Big Chest located on basement floor B5
  • Compass: in a treasure chest on basement floor B2
  • Dungeon Map: in a treasure chest on basement floor B1
  • Big Key: in a treasure chest on basement floor B4

Tips

  • Equip the Fire Rod before entering, as it is strictly required to melt Freezors and crack Kholdstare's frozen shell.
  • You can utilize the Hookshot on wooden chests, blocks, and certain statues to pull Link safely across slippery ice platforms.
  • The Cane of Somaria can create a red block to hold down floor switches, allowing you to bypass or simplify the dungeon's infamous sliding block puzzle.
