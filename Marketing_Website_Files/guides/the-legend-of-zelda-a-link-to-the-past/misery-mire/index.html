Misery Mire

The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past guide · Sixth Dark World dungeon

Misery Mire is the sixth dungeon of the Dark World, located beneath the storm clouds of the Mire of Misery swamp. Link must traverse flooded basement rooms, light torches in dark corridors, and manipulate crystal switches to navigate deeper into the complex. Conquering the dungeon requires defeating Vitreous to rescue the sixth maiden.

Items

  • Cane of Somaria: Inside a chest found on the basement level, accessible by navigating the northern maze-like corridors
  • Big Key: Inside a treasure chest on the basement level after solving a switch and torch puzzle
  • Compass: Found in a treasure chest in the basement rooms
  • Dungeon Map: Found in a chest in the early eastern section of the dungeon
  • Heart Container: Drops upon defeating the dungeon boss, Vitreous

Tips

  • The Cane of Somaria is required to complete later dungeons, such as Turtle Rock and Ganon's Tower, so do not leave without collecting it.
  • Keep the Magic Meter filled with Green Potions, as you will need the Fire Rod to light unlit torches in dark rooms to open sealed doors.
  • Watch the shadows on the floor to avoid being ambushed and captured by falling Wallmasters.
  • During the boss fight with Vitreous, equip the Hookshot or standard sword to safely pull or slash away the smaller eyeballs while avoiding lightning strikes.
