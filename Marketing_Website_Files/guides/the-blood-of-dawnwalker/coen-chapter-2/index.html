Coen, Chapter 2

The Blood of Dawnwalker guide · Transformed into a day-and-night hybrid, Coen confronts the carnage of his burning village ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Awaking as a hybrid Dawnwalker after surviving the sunlight, Coen accompanies the herbalist Anca back into the ruins of their scorched village, Laslea Glen. To track down his abducted family, you investigate Coen's burned homestead using Focus Mode and utilize witchcraft to interrogate the spirit of a fallen soldier with Compel Soul. Finally, clearing the heavy rubble obstructing the village gates consumes the remaining daytime segments and grants access to the open world of Vale Sangora.
