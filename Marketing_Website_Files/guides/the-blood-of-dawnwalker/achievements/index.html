Points of no return

  • Escaping via the Coward's Path gate across the ravine in Briar Sloughs: Triggers the premature retreat ending and immediately ends the campaign.
  • Reaching Day 31 before rescuing Coen's family: Causes the family to be permanently sacrificed, failing the 30-day rescue requirement and locking out family-dependent ending resolutions.
  • Breaching the inner sanctum of Brencis's Stronghold during Coen, Chapter 13: Locks you into the final encounter sequence and locks the story into a single mutually exclusive ending path for that save file.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Start the playthrough on Duelist difficulty preset to secure the Uphill Battle completion requirement.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Uphill Battle: Start the story on Duelist difficulty and finish the game while successfully rescuing Coen's family before the time limit expires.
  2. Step 2

    Complete the introductory prologue in Laslea Glen and Coen, Chapter 1, getting accustomed to the 30-day time management system and omniblock combat mechanics.

  3. Step 3

    Progress through Coen, Chapter 2 into the wider open map of Vale Sangora, visiting early settlements and synchronizing local scout towers.

  4. Step 4

    Explore Altenmarkt and push through Coen, Chapter 4, tackling local Monster Lairs and Court Activities while actively leveling combat skill trees.

  5. Step 5

    Traverse the Briar Sloughs into Coen, Chapter 6; create a backup manual save near the Coward's Path shrine at night to cross the ravine chasm and trigger the early retreat ending for "A Hero They Deserve", then immediately reload.

    Don't miss in this step
    • A Hero They Deserve
  6. Step 6

    Advance through Coen, Chapter 7 and make entrance into Svartrau City to unlock regional exploration milestones and advance urban questlines.

  7. Step 7

    Complete Coen, Chapter 8 and infiltrate the Svartrau Cathedral district, steadily raising regional infamy across Vale Sangora toward peak level.

  8. Step 8

    Clear Coen, Chapter 10 and Coen, Chapter 11 while finishing all time-sensitive side quests and rescuing Coen's family well before the 30-day limit expires.

  9. Step 9

    Gather at the Silty Mug Inn to finalize alliances, companion-specific objectives, and unlock high-tier vampiric, witchcraft, and swordmastery ultimate perks.

    Also in this step: Mastery

  10. Step 10

    Reach Brencis's Stronghold and create a dedicated manual save slot prior to breaching the inner sanctum.

  11. Step 11

    Complete Coen, Chapter 13 on Duelist difficulty to trigger an ending and unlock Uphill Battle alongside The Blood of Dawnwalker.

    Also in this step: The Blood of Dawnwalker

  12. Step 12

    Reload the manual save at Brencis's Stronghold to trigger each mutually exclusive storyline ending, then clean up any remaining cumulative combat feats, transports, flags, and level 50 grinding.
