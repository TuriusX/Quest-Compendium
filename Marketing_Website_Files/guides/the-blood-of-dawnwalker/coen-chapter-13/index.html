Coen, Chapter 13

The Blood of Dawnwalker guide · Coen breaks through the fortress outer gates before the thirty-day deadline lapses ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

In the assault on the vampire stronghold Greifberg Castle, Coen storms the fortress to confront Brencis before the thirty-day limit expires. You can either bypass the barred front entrance at night using Shadowstep to scale the perimeter walls or infiltrate through the lower side door. Once inside the courtyard, you must cut through garrisoned forces and push deeper into the inner keep and dungeons.
