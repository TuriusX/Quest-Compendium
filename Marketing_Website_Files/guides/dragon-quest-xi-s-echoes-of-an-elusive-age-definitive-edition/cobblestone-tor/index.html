Cobblestone Tor

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The trial of manhood

Cobblestone Tor serves as the opening tutorial trial where the Hero ascends the sacred peak with Gemma and her faithful dog Sandy to offer prayers for their coming-of-age ceremony. Along the way, the Hero rescues the village youth Cole from a pair of monsters before scaling the summit's rock face. At the top, an ambush by a great winged predator triggers the sudden awakening of the Luminary's lightning powers.

Items

  • Pot Lid: In a treasure chest hidden directly behind the stone shrine south of the cave entrance at the Tor's base
  • Medicinal Herb: Sparkly spot on the ground at the top of a climbable cliff ledge inside the cavern
  • Copper Ore: Sparkly spot on the floor near the cavern path leading toward the exit
  • Pink Pine: Sparkly spot sitting near a tree by the cliff edge on the mountain face
  • Pink Pine: Sparkly spot next to the tree near the precipice at the summit

Secrets

  • A lower recess inside the cave path hides destructible pots holding 5 gold coins and an additional Medicinal Herb.

Tips

  • Equip the Cobblestone Greatsword for quick one-hit knockouts against early enemies, or stick to the Cobblestone Sword paired with the off-hand Pot Lid if you prefer extra defense.
  • Gemma regularly restores the Hero's health during combat, so you can freely focus on aggressive physical strikes and fire magic without stopping to heal.
