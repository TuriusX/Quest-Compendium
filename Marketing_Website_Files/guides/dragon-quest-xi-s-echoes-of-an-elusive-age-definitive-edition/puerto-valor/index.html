Puerto Valor

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Meeting the nobleman

The party arrives at Puerto Valor seeking a ship to cross the sea and must gain passage through the town's water gate. To open the sea gate, you must meet the local nobleman, Don Rodrigo, who resides in a large villa on the south side of town. Sylvando refuses to accompany the party into the mansion, leaving the Hero to resolve the travel permits with the Don.

Items

  • Magic Water: Inside a treasure chest on the seaside terrace behind the hotel
  • 1,000 gold coins: Inside a wardrobe in the master bedroom of Don Rodrigo's mansion

Secrets

  • Casino entrance | Enter the town's large building to the north to access the casino, allowing you to play slot machines and poker early in the game | missable: no

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells medicinal herbs, antidotal herbs, holy water, and chimaera wings
  • Weapon Shop: Sells early-game gear including the Silver Rapier, Cast-Iron Claymore, and Battle-Fork
  • Armour Shop: Sells defensive equipment including Iron Armour, Turtle Shell, and Iron Cuirass

Tips

  • Sylvando will temporarily leave the active party while you visit Don Rodrigo's manor, so make sure your party formation and inventory are sorted beforehand.
  • You can exchange casino tokens for valuable early-game equipment and recipes at the prize exchange counter before continuing your voyage.
