Dundrasil Region

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Visiting the ruins of the kingdom

Following the events in Octagonia, the party travels through the Dundrasil Region to uncover the truth behind the fallen kingdom. Navigating a well leads inside the ruined fortress, where the Luminary conducts a memorial ceremony alongside Rab and learns crucial details about his royal heritage. After evading Heliodorian soldiers who corner them among the ruins, both Rab and Jade officially become permanent companions.

Items

  • Drasilian Sovereign: Chest on an isolated islet far to the west across the water, requiring a flying mount
  • Rod of Rapidity: Treasure chest inside one of the damaged structures near the campsite
  • Dress Like a Drasilian: Treasure chest accessed by crawling beneath a low rock pile near the grave site within the city ruins
  • Mini Medal: Treasure chest hidden directly behind the remote cabin on the southwestern edge of the region

Secrets

  • A dry well next to a blue slime serves as an underground passageway into the otherwise blocked-off inner castle grounds.
