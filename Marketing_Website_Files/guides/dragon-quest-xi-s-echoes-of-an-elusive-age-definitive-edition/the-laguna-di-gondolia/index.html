The Laguna di Gondolia

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the port city

The Laguna di Gondolia is a waterlogged marshland connecting the Gallopolis region to the coastal city of Gondolia. To reach the city, you must navigate wooden walkways, fight through wetland monsters, and occasionally cross water hazards. You will also encounter a campsite along the main path to rest, save, and forge gear before entering Gondolia.

Items

  • Pitch Pearl: Sparkly spot in the northern marsh waters
  • Evencloth: Sparkly spot under the elevated walkways
  • Warm Water: Sparkly spot on the edge of the shallow water
  • Mini Medal: Treasure chest located on a raised platform reached by riding a mount

Secrets

  • Midnight Hitter recipe book | Found inside a treasure chest on an isolated platform reachable via a glowing Midnight Hitter mount | missable: no

Tips

  • Look for monsters surrounded by a yellow sparkle; defeat them in battle to commandeer them as mounts to leap onto high ledges you cannot otherwise reach.
  • Bring plenty of Antidotal Herbs or keep the Squelch spell ready, as several native marsh monsters can inflict the poison status.
