The Laguna di Gondolia

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the port city

The party arrives in this marshy wetland region from the Gallopolis Region checkpoint on their journey toward the canal port of Gondolia. Navigating the winding walkways and marsh pools requires defeating shiny monsters to take advantage of their flying mounts to reach isolated platforms.

Items

  • Seed of Skill: Inside a treasure chest on an isolated water island in the northeastern marsh, reached by riding a flying monster mount
  • I Love Iron Armor: Inside a treasure chest on a grassy dead-end platform near the middle platforms approaching Gondolia, accessible via a monster mount

Secrets

  • Defeating a sparkling Midnight Horknight allows the Luminary to mount its flying hornet steed and hover across deep water to reach otherwise inaccessible islets holding rare treasures.

Tips

  • Heavy-hitting greatsword abilities help bypass the tougher physical defense of the Crabber Dabber Doo monsters found along the waterways.
