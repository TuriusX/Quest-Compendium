The Costa Valor

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the castle

The Costa Valor is a scenic coastal region connecting the Laguna di Gondolia checkpoint to the resort town of Puerto Valor. The party travels through this area along the main road to reach Puerto Valor Castle and secure a ship. While passing through, you can explore the beaches and grassy cliffs for materials, battle aquatic monsters, and rest at the campsite.

Items

  • Coralite: along the shoreline on the southern beach
  • Slipweed: sparkling spot near the grassy path on the western cliffs
  • Crimson Coral: sparkling spot in the shallow water near the southern shore

Secrets

  • Follow the southeastern beach to the water's edge to reach a hidden treasure chest resting near the cliff wall.

Tips

  • Use your horse to dash through wandering monsters along the main path if you want to reach Puerto Valor quickly without unnecessary battles.
  • Rest at the campsite to save your progress and forge improved gear at the Fun-Size Forge before heading into the town of Puerto Valor.
