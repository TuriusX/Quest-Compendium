The Fortress of Fear

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The final assault on Mordegon

The party storms the floating stronghold of the Lord of Shadows, navigating multiple levels connected by teleportation portals and external balconies. Along the way, you must defeat Mordegon's top lieutenants to unlock pathways leading to the throne room at the summit. The assault culminates in a two-phase showdown against Mordegon and his monstrous draconic form.

Items

  • Super Seed of Skill: Level 1, inside a treasure chest in the western corridor
  • Meteorite Bracer: Level 3, found inside a treasure chest on the southern exterior platform
  • Liquid Metal Slime Shield: Level 2, inside a treasure chest behind an electric barrier that must be deactivated

Secrets

  • The entire exterior and layout of the Fortress of Fear vanishes after completing the main story, but any missed unique treasures and recipe books wash up or reappear in the post-game at the Havens Above.

Tips

  • Defeat glowing rideable monsters like the Dragon Rider to take flight and reach otherwise inaccessible floating platforms containing high-tier loot.
  • Equip accessories that provide high darkness resistance, such as Devil's Tails or Skull Rings, to significantly mitigate Mordegon's most lethal attacks.
  • When fighting the final form, focus offensive fire on the tail (Lord of Shadows) first, as its disruptive spellcasting and buffs make it the deadlier threat.
