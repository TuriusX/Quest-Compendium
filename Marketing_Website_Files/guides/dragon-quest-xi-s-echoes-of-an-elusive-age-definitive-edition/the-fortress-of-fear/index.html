The Fortress of Fear

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The final assault on Mordegon

Guided by the flying whale Cetacea, the Luminary uses the newly forged Sword of Light to dispel the protective dark veil enveloping this flying citadel. Players navigate its multi-level dungeon, solve floor switches, and utilize rideable monster mounts to bypass obstacles while climbing to the upper reaches. The primary mission is to vanquish the sentinel Indignus and ultimately confront and defeat the Lord of Shadows, Mordegon.

Items

  • Recipe Book: Pride of the Valkyries: Found inside a treasure chest located in the southwestern room on Level 1
  • Liquid Metal Jacket: Located inside a treasure chest tucked beneath the staircase on Level 1
  • Molten globules x3: Found in a treasure chest within the south room of the eastern chamber on Level 1
  • Technicolor Dreamcloth x3: Found inside a treasure chest in the southernmost room on Level 2
  • Chronocrystal: Obtained from an exterior balcony area accessed through the western exit of Level 2

Secrets

  • Slot machines in the Level 3 south wing contain hidden rewards as well as monster traps; the eastern machine on the northern wall awards 1,000 casino tokens, while the others are Token Taker mimics.

Tips

  • Do not stress over missed treasure chests here, as any unopened containers from the citadel safely relocate to The First Forest Whale Way Station in the post-game.
  • Use the statue located near the entranceway following the Indignus fight, as it serves as the only designated save marker across the entire fortress.
