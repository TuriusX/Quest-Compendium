Cobblestone (Act 3)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Returning to the past

Upon returning to Cobblestone in Act 3, Gemma asks the Luminary to help restore their destroyed hometown by enlisting four key recruits from across Erdrea. Once you bring back Derk, Vince, Noah, and the beast Jörmun, the settlement is fully reconstructed into a flourishing community. Finishing this reconstruction unlocks an elite endgame item shop and makes Gemma eligible for marriage after completing a trial in the postgame.

Items

  • Gemma's Eternal Charm: Reward from Gemma after fully restoring the village
  • Cobblestone Clobber: Inside the wardrobe of the Luminary's restored childhood house
  • Single Phial: Treasure chest located in the cave on the east edge of town beside Jörmun

Shops and people

  • Derk's Shop: High-tier gear and items including Berserker's Blade, Knife of Strife, Cane of Compassion, Rod of Paradise, Anchor of Rancour, Aristocrat's Axe, Pirate King's Coat, Metal King Jacket, Metal King Armour, Freebooter's Gloves, Holy Talisman, Chronocrystal, and Pep Pop

Tips

  • Stock up on Pep Pop from Derk's shop here, as it provides an immediate way to force the entire active team into a Pepped Up state for quick level grinding and rare item hunting.
