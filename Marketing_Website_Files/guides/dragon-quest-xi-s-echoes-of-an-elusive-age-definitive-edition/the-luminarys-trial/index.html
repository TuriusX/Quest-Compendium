The Luminary's Trial

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The post-game time travel event

This end-game challenge in Drustan's Labyrinth takes the party through the Citadel of Spite, a structure echoing the layout of the Fortress of Fear. Explorers must traverse multiple levels, defeat rideable monsters to ascend the central shaft, and unseal barriers to reach the final chamber. At the conclusion, the party faces trial bosses within a turn limit to earn legendary equipment recipes and special wishes from Drustan.

Items

  • Erdwin's Tunic: Inside a treasure room on Level 1 unlocked after pressing the switch on Level 4
  • Drustan's Armour: Inside the sealed north treasure room on Level 1
  • Serenica's Surplice: Inside the sealed north treasure room on Level 1
  • Erdwin's Coronet: Inside a treasure chest on Level 3
  • Recipe book: Big Hitters of the Battlefield: Inside a treasure chest located on Level 4
  • Recipe book: If I Could Turn Back Time: In a chest on the western side of Level 4
  • Recipe book: Ye Manifold Methods of Mighty Drustan: Found under the stairs inside a treasure chest on Level 1

Secrets

  • A slot machine monster called Gold Grabber can be discovered disguised as regular casino equipment, and hitting a glowing switch on Level 4 lowers the force field locking the central treasure room on Level 1.
