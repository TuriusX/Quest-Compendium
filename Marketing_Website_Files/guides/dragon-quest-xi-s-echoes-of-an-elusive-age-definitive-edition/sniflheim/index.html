Sniflheim

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the kingdom

Upon arriving at Sniflheim via ship, the party discovers the entire kingdom and its inhabitants encased in magical ice. Players must bypass the frozen front entrance by using the Magic Key on the western red door to enter town, consult Queen Frysabel near the fire, and set out into the Sniflheim Region toward The Hekswood to break the curse.

Items

  • Magic Beast Hide: In barrels situated at the eastern end of the Sniflheim harbor dock
  • Seed of Deftness: Inside a barrel near the western outer wall of the town church
  • Permasnow: In a breakable pot outside the structure at the northeastern corner of town

Secrets

  • Red Door shortcut: Access to the frozen settlement is completely sealed at the front gates, requiring you to travel along the outer western periphery to discover a red-locked door that opens with the Magic Key.

Tips

  • Equip cold-resistant equipment or Anti-Freeze Earrings purchased at the periphery campsite before venturing into the harsh icy plains outside the kingdom.
