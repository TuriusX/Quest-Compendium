Sniflheim

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the kingdom

Upon arriving at the frozen kingdom of Sniflheim, the party discovers that the entire city and its citizens are encased in solid ice except for Queen Fryske. You must speak with the Queen in the castle's throne room to learn about the ice witch Krystalinda and begin the quest to find a way to break the curse. This leads the Luminary outside to the Snaerfelt and the Royal Library in search of a solution.

Items

  • Mini Medal: inside a treasure chest located in the basement level of Sniflheim Castle
  • Platinum Ore: inside a wardrobe in the residential quarters of Sniflheim Castle
  • 3,000 gold coins: inside a treasure chest on the second floor of the Sniflheim inn
  • Savvy Sapphire: inside a pot in the royal bedchamber of Sniflheim Castle

Secrets

  • The Royal Library Key can be acquired from Queen Fryske in the castle after speaking with her about the frozen state of the kingdom, which unlocks the massive library located in the Snaerfelt.
  • A locked Ultimate Key door inside the castle basement hides rare treasures, which cannot be opened until much later in the game when the key is obtained.

Shops and people

  • Wandering Priest: offers church services such as saving game progress and curing status ailments

Tips

  • The main city shops and residential doors are completely frozen shut during this first visit, so ensure your equipment and items are stocked before heading into the freezing wild areas outside.
  • You cannot access the majority of Sniflheim Castle's facilities until you defeat the boss threatening the region and lift the ice curse.
