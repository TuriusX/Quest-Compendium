Gallopolis Region

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Approaching the desert kingdom

This expansive desert realm connects The Hotto Steppe with the bustling city of Gallopolis and Laguna di Gondolia. Adventurers pass through the dunes to reach the sultan's domain, establish camps, and prepare for encounters with strong local wildlife. Exploration involves collecting crafting materials, checking outposts, and later hunting dangerous targets across the sands.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest on the southern edge of the desert dunes

Secrets

  • Hidden golden variant Golden Globe appears randomly when engaging Cactiball enemies in battle.

  • Roving Emporium: Sells armaments and gear including Rapier, Cast-iron claymore, Poison moth knife, Crucerang, Iron lance, Battle whip, Silver platter, and Bronze shield.

Tips

  • Opponents here hit considerably harder than those in the previous region, so rest at campsites and upgrade equipment at the roving merchant before pushing too far south or north.
