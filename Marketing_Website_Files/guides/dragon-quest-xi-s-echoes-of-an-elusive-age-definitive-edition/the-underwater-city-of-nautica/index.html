The Underwater City of Nautica

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the ruins

The Luminary awakens alone in the submerged domain of Nautica transformed into a fish following the fall of Yggdrasil. After meeting Queen Marina in her palace and observing visions through her pearl, the protagonist discovers the undersea settlement under sudden siege from Mordegon's monstrous forces. You must navigate the aquatic levels and bite the fisherman's hook dangling from above to escape back to the surface.

Items

  • Seed of Skill: inside a treasure chest located beside the sunken shipwreck in the lower part
  • Drasilian Sovereign: retrieved from a sparkly spot in the lower part reachable by swimming upward
  • Elfin Elixir: found in a treasure chest positioned on the southwest raised platform of the main level
  • Zombiesbane: inside a treasure chest on an elevated platform southeast of the Royal Terrarium
  • Mini Medal: inside a treasure chest resting close to the building northwest of the inn

Secrets

  • Michelle's diary can still be found and read on the bookshelf inside her residence.

Tips

  • Swim up onto the elevated stone ledges and columns of water across the ruins to collect chests that were completely inaccessible on foot during the earlier visit.
