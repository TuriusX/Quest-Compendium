Phnom Nohn (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Investigating the village

Upon arriving during Act 2, the Hero and Rab find Phnom Nonh subdued and drained of cheer under the influence of the monster Avarith. You must visit Da's house to learn what is troubling the villagers and son, confront the beast to restore everyone's spirits, and witness Sylvando's joyful parade.

Items

  • Extra Mural: Sold at the village Item Shop

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Care Rivière, Protective Pendant, Extra Mural, and basic curative items
  • Weapon Shop: Cautery Sword, Carbon Steel Claymore, Sword Breaker
  • Armour Shop: Light Shield, Steel Shield, Silver Tiara

Tips

  • Pick up the side quest "Worth its Salt" from the man near the top of the steps leading toward the church square while investigating the town.
