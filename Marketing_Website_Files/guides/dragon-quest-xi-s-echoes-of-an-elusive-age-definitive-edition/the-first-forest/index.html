The First Forest

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the altar

The Luminary and company journey through this lush ascent from the Arborian Highlands to bring the six orbs to the altar at the forest summit. Placing the orbs upon the sacred altar creates a path of light leading directly up to the World Tree, Yggdrasil.

Items

  • Saint's Ashes: Inside a treasure chest on an elevated northern ledge reached by scaling a vine near the entrance
  • How to Outdo the Undead: Inside a treasure chest located inside the monster-infested hollow trunk passage
  • Ethereal Stone: Inside a treasure chest sitting just beyond the far exit of the trunk cave tunnel
  • Savvy Sapphire: Inside a treasure chest tucked away at a dead end in the southeast portion of the lower area
  • Enchanted Stone: Inside a treasure chest near the base of the small waterfall by the southern campsite
  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest concealed directly underneath the altar at the summit

Secrets

  • A treasure chest hiding a Mini Medal sits tucked directly under the staircase platform of the orb altar at the summit, out of plain view until you check below the structure.

Tips

  • Do not ascend the stairs to place the orbs at the summit altar before grabbing the Mini Medal chest tucked beneath it, and rest at the northern campsite to fully recover before advancing.
