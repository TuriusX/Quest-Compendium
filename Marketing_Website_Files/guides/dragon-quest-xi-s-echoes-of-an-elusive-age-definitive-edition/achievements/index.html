Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • None: Nothing is permanently missable. All side quests, collectibles, accolades, and achievements can be completed during the post-game.

Recommended order
  1. Act 1 - Journey from Cobblestone to Yggdrasil: Complete the main story progression across early regions, recruit your party members, and unlock foundational story achievements.
  2. Interlude & Act 2 - Reclaim the World: Experience character interludes, regroup with party members across Erdrea, storm the Fortress of Fear, and defeat Mordegon.
  3. Act 3 - Post-Game & Rebuilding: Revisit Erdrea, complete post-game story events, rebuild Cobblestone, and unlock access to the ultimate trials.
  4. Clean-up & Accolades: Finish all Tickington Pastword quests, complete the Wheel of Harma trials, forge the Supreme Sword of Light, reach level 99, acquire all appearance-altering outfits, and defeat Calasmos.
