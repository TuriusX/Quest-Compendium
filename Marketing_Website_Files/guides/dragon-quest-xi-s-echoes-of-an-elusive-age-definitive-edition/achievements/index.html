Points of no return

  • None: Nothing is permanently missable. All side quests, collectibles, accolades, and achievements can be completed during the post-game.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1Act 1 - Journey from Cobblestone to Yggdrasil

    Complete the main story progression across early regions, recruit your party members, and unlock foundational story achievements.

    Also in this step: The World Tree, A Momentous Decision, Master of the Skies

  2. Step 2Interlude & Act 2 - Reclaim the World

    Experience character interludes, regroup with party members across Erdrea, storm the Fortress of Fear, and defeat Mordegon.

  3. Step 3Act 3 - Post-Game & Rebuilding

    Revisit Erdrea, complete post-game story events, rebuild Cobblestone, and unlock access to the ultimate trials.

  4. Step 4Clean-up & Accolades

    Finish all Tickington Pastword quests, complete the Wheel of Harma trials, forge the Supreme Sword of Light, reach level 99, acquire all appearance-altering outfits, and defeat Calasmos.
