Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Climax of Act 1 at Yggdrasil: World map layout and monsters temporarily change for Act 2; however, all items and quests remain accessible later.
  • End of Act 2 at The Fortress of Fear: The citadel vanishes upon defeating the final boss of Act 2, but all missed treasures wash up at the pebble in the First Forest Whale Way Station.
  • Reaching the Tower of Lost Time in the post-game: Transitions the world state into Act 3, but does not lock you out of any achievements, items, or accolades.

Recommended order
  1. Start in Cobblestone, complete the coming-of-age ceremony at Cobblestone Tor, and trigger the opening story progression toward Heliodor.
  2. Travel across the Heliodor Region to enter Heliodor and Heliodor Castle, then escape through the Heliodor Sewers.
  3. Traverse The Manglegrove and The Emerald Coast to reach Hotto, clearing The Hotto Steppe and The Cryptic Crypt with your growing party.
  4. Continue to the Gallopolis Region, explore Gallopolis and The Gallopolis Sands, and conquer the Slayer of the Sands.
  5. Cross The Laguna di Gondolia into Gondolia, secure your ship, and set sail across The Inland Sea.
  6. Dock at Puerto Valor and explore The Costa Valor, then journey into the Dundrasil Region and explore The Dundrasil Ruins.
  7. Visit Soltco, The White Coast, L'Academie de Notre Maitre des Medailles, and The Champs Sauvages on the path to Phnom Nohn and The Mount Huji.
  8. Voyage past The Outer Cape to the northern lands of Sniflheim and The Sniflheim Region, braving The Hekswood and The Snaerfelt.
  9. Reach The First Forest and scale Yggdrasil to conclude Act 1 and initiate the global transformation.
  10. Begin Act 2 rallying allies at The Last Bastion, re-exploring Cobblestone (Act 2) and Cobblestone Tor (Act 2).
  11. Venture into The Dundrasil Ruins (Act 2) to unlock the Luminary's hidden potential.
  12. Sail The Southern Sea, explore The Insula Australis, and dive deep into Nautica and The Underwater City of Nautica.
  13. Pass through The Zwaardrust Region, ascend to The Battleground to claim rare forging materials, and revisit The Champs Sauvages (Act 2), Phnom Nohn (Act 2), and The Mount Huji (Act 2).
  14. Infiltrate The Fortress of Fear to confront Mordegon and conclude Act 2.
  15. Enter Act 3's post-game timeline, rebuild Cobblestone (Act 3), and unlock the mysterious trials in Havens Above.
