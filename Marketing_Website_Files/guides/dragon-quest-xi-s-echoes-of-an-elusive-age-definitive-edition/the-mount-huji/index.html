The Mount Huji

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The volcano dungeon

Mount Huji is an active volcano dungeon located just outside the village of Hotto. Players visit the mountain during Act 2 and post-game to uncover the secret of the rampaging dragon Tatsunaga, confront it deep within the caldera, and later access the Crucible to forge the legendary Sword of Light.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Caldera - Lower Area, inside a treasure chest located in the center that is blocked by a breakable boulder
  • Fire Ball: Caldera - Lower Area, inside a treasure chest on the northern platform
  • Sage's Elixir (x4): Caldera - Lower Area, inside a treasure chest along the eastern pathway before the dragon's lair
  • Chest of Hotto stuff: Caldera, at the far end of the overlook reached via the mountain's Secret Entrance

Secrets

  • A secluded mountain trail accessed through the children's secret hideout ladder leads to a cliff overlook above Tatsunaga's lair, where a golden sparkly spot containing the Chest of Hotto stuff can be recovered for the quest "The Legendary Treasure of Legend."
