The Champs Sauvages (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the ruins

After escaping Mount Pang Lai and passing through Costa Valor, the Luminary and Rab reach the monster-infested wilderness of The Champs Sauvage. While journeying southward toward Phnom Nonh and the Nhou Wat ruins, a storyline sequence triggers on the central river crossing involving Sylvando leading a flamboyant entourage. Players must navigate past upgraded fiends to reach the southern settlements, revisit L'Académie, and secure new monster mounts to access previously unreachable ledges.

Items

  • Sunny Citrine: In a treasure chest on an isolated island in the northwest lake, reachable by using a rideable flying monster mount

Secrets

  • Defeating a sparkling Honeyhead Horknight enables the Luminary to commandeer its bee mount, allowing flight across the water to access previously cut-off islands, hidden shoreline foraging spots, and the northwestern treasure chest.

Tips

  • Take advantage of rainy weather or use resting intervals at campsites to control weather conditions, as specific monsters like umbrella variants and particular ingredient nodes only spawn under wet or night conditions.
