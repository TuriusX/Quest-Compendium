The Last Bastion

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The Luminary awakens in the ruins of Cobblestone

Following the fall of Yggdrasil, the Luminary awakens to find his home village of Cobblestone transformed into a fortified refugee camp known as the Last Bastion. Guided by Gemma and his mother Amber, the hero reunites with King Carnelian and joins forces with Sir Hendrick to defend the stronghold against attacking monsters. To progress, the player must repel the enemy vanguard outside the northern gates and defeat the commander assaulting the camp.

Items

  • Mini medal: Inside a treasure chest located in the ruins near King Carnelian's royal tent
  • Recipe book: The Frills of Foundation: Inside a chest situated in the southern part of the settlement near the riverbank
  • Seed of skill: Inside a pot found within Amber's rebuilt shelter

  • Item Shop Merchant: Sells curative supplies such as Strong Medicine, Chimaera Wings, and Holy Water

Tips

  • Sir Hendrick fights alongside the hero as an uncontrollable guest companion during the invasion battle, frequently dealing heavy damage and providing emergency healing.
  • Be sure to visit the Church nun stationed in the center of the camp to save your progress before speaking to the guards at the northern barricade.
