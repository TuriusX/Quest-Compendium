Cobblestone

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The Luminary's hometown

Cobblestone is the peaceful rural hometown where the Luminary grew up under the care of his adoptive mother, Amber. After completing the traditional coming-of-age climb up Cobblestone Tor, the Hero returns home, learns of his heritage, and departs on his journey. In the post-game, players can complete tasks across Erdrea to rebuild the ruined village into a thriving sanctuary with high-end shops.

Items

  • Seed of Agility: Inside a breakable pot located at the top of a tall ladder near the village entrance
  • Cypress Stick: Inside a red treasure chest tucked behind the Mayor's house
  • Plain Clothes: In the wardrobe inside the Hero's home
  • Pink Pine: Sparkly gather spot found on the roof of the southernmost house

Shops and people

  • Cobblestone Item Shop (Early Game): Cypress Stick, Pot Lid, Bandana, Wayfarer's Clothes, Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb
  • Derk's Shop (Post-Rebuild): Berserker's Blade, Knife of Strife, Cane of Compassion, Rod of Paradise, Anchor of Rancour, Aristocrat's Axe, Pirate King's Coat, Metal King Jacket, Metal King Armour, Freebooter's Gloves, Holy Talisman, Perfect Panacea, Mystifying Mixture, Pep Pop, Chronocrystal

Tips

  • Smash every barrel and check the rooftops in village houses early on to gather starting gold and healing items before heading out to Heliodor.
