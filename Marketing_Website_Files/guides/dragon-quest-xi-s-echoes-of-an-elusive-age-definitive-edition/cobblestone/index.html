Cobblestone

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The Luminary's hometown

Cobblestone is the peaceful hometown of the protagonist, nestled in a quiet valley within the Heliodor region. After completing the coming-of-age ceremony on the Tor, the protagonist discovers his identity as the Luminary and prepares to depart for the capital. Players explore the village to gather resources, say farewell to neighbors, and trigger the opening narrative quests.

Items

  • Ambergris: In a wardrobe inside the protagonist's house
  • Plain Clothes: In a wardrobe inside Gemma's house
  • 10 Gold Coins: Inside a pot behind the church
  • Cypress Stick: In a treasure chest located in the small shed near the village stream
  • Medicinal Herb: Inside a barrel near the village stables
  • Antidotal Herb: Inside a pot on the southern balcony of the elder's house

Secrets

  • Climb the ladder behind the village church onto the roof to find a sparkling spot containing Chimera Wing.

Shops and people

  • Village Item Shop: Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb, Holy Water, Chimera Wing

Tips

  • Jump into the stream and search the shallows beneath the bridges for sparkling foraging nodes before departing the village.
  • Return to your mother's house after your meeting with the village elder to rest and receive your departure gifts.
