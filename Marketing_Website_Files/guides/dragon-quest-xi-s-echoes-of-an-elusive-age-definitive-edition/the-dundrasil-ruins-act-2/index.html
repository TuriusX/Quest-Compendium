The Dundrasil Ruins (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the Luminary's grandfather

The Luminary and Rab return to the ruins of Dundrasil in search of the spirit of King Irwin, the Luminary's father. Descending into the underground cavern, they discover Irwin trapped in a nightmare created by the demon Gloomnibis. Defeating the monster frees Irwin's soul and allows the Luminary to unlock powerful hidden potential in his skill tree.

Items

  • Devil's Tail: In a treasure chest found in the underground dungeon beneath the ruins
  • Sage's Elixir: Inside a jar in the subterranean ruins

Tips

  • Gloomnibis heavily relies on dark elemental magic and sleep-inducing attacks, so equip accessories like Rousing Rings to prevent incapacitation.
  • Defeating the boss here expands the Luminary's character builder, unlocking the Luminary branch skills such as Quadraslash.
