The Dundrasil Ruins (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the Luminary's grandfather ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Following rumors about a tormented ghost knight, the Hero and Rab return to Dundrasil to uncover an underground dungeon beneath the ruined castle. Inside, the Luminary confronts the Restless Knight—the corrupted spirit of his father, King Irwin—and enters his memories to defeat the demon tormenting him. Conquering these nightmarish trials permanently restores and unlocks the Hero's hidden Luminary skill panel powers.

Enemies
Restless Knight

Story boss encountered in the dungeon chamber beneath Dundrasil; uses sword techniques, curse attacks, and can reduce maximum HP.

Gloomnivore
Weak to Ice

Nightmare boss encountered deep within Irwin's tragic memories; acts multiple times per round, casts Kazam, inflicts sleep, and uses Malevolent Miasma.

Tips
  • Prioritize equipping sleep-resistant accessories such as Rousing Rings or Moonbeam Bombshells before confronting the Gloomnivore, as its sleep tactics and Malevolent Miasma can quickly incapacitate party members.
