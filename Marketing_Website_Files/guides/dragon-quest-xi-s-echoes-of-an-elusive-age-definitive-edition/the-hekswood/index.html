The Hekswood

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Chasing the witch

The Luminary ventures into this frozen, winding forest from The Snærfelt to confront the witch's beast guardian and discover who has frozen Sniflheim. After an intense blizzard separates the hero from his allies, he must fight his way north toward the clearing where the creature and Heliodorian soldiers await.

Items

  • Seed of Agility: Inside a treasure chest at the eastern dead-end near the central crossroad
  • Platinum Protection: Inside a treasure chest at the southwest dead-end reached by circling from the central crossroads
  • Sage's Elixir: Inside a treasure chest on the pathway north of the central crossroads
