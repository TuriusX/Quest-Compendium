The Manglegrove

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to Gallopolis

The party must navigate through this dense, winding jungle as they make their way forward on their journey. Progression requires investigating the area around a broken bridge, viewing a mystical Yggdrasil root memory, and eliminating the culprit to repair the path forward.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest situated on a raised ledge down an eastern path near the entrance
  • Economies of Scale: Found in a treasure chest located inside the Woodcutter's Hut at the campsite
  • Single Phial: In a treasure chest located along a pathway southeast of the central campsite

Secrets

  • A glowing Yggdrasil root hidden down a dead-end pathway past the Woodcutter's Hut reveals a vision of a transformed woodcutter and unlocks the path toward a concealed boss.
  • A weather cow stands in a clearing north of the Woodcutter's Hut; interacting with it provides an advance forecast of the jungle's climate conditions.

Shops and people

  • Traveling Merchant: Sells standard equipment and restorative supplies, including Copper Swords, Copper Choppers, Bronze Knives, Boomerangs, Scale Shields, and Antidotal Herbs.

Tips

  • Stock up on Antidotal Herbs or keep Squelch handy before venturing deep into the swampy trails, as Bubble Slimes frequently poison your characters.
