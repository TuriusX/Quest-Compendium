The Sage's Trial

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The second trial of the post-game

The Sage's Trial is the second post-game challenge accessed through the central portal in the Trial Isle. It is split into two distinct sub-areas: the Salt Flats (based on the Laguna di Gondolia map) and the Hoarder's Keep (based on Gyldenhall). Navigating the shifting layouts leads to the Ample Cinnabar, where you fight the second dragon boss to earn a wish from the Watcher.

Items

  • Crimsonite: Hoarder's Keep, treasure chest on the second floor
  • Dashing Druid: Hoarder's Keep, treasure chest on the third floor behind a slot door
  • Minerva's Tiara: Hoarder's Keep, treasure chest in the western vault
  • Sceptre of Over-Rivalry Recipe Book: Hoarder's Keep, treasure chest on the top floor near the throne room

Secrets

  • Gold glow enemies on the Salt Flats can be mounted; riding a dragon-type monster allows you to fly across poisoned swamps and reach otherwise unreachable high ledges.

Tips

  • Equipping poison-resistant accessories like Catholicon Rings will neutralize the status damage suffered in the marshy waters of the Salt Flats.
  • Bring strong multi-target Ice or Light spells, as most machine and fiend enemies roaming the golden corridors of Hoarder's Keep take severe damage from them.
