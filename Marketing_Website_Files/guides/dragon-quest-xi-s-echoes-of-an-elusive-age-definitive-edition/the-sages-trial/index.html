The Sage's Trial

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The second trial of the post-game

Entered via the northern seedling in the Temple of Tranquility on Trial Isle, this post-game gauntlet takes players through redesigned versions of the First Forest and the Gyldenhall named The Fierce Forest and Hoarder's Keep. Navigating disconnected teleporters, one-way barriers, and floor pitfalls leads to a rematch trial boss, Unfathomable Anger, deep in the Realm Between Worlds. Overcoming the boss within the required turn limit allows the player to claim a wish from the ancient hero Drustan.

Items

  • Serenica's Circlet: In a treasure chest near a broken bridge in the northeastern section of The Fierce Forest
  • Über Agate of Evolution: Inside a treasure chest situated within a cave in the lower section of The Fierce Forest
  • What a Wonderful World Tree: Found in a treasure chest on the high northwest plateau of The Fierce Forest
  • Mini Medal: A treasure chest containing three medals located at the base of the waterfall north of the base campsite in The Fierce Forest
  • The Fandom of the Opera: Located inside a treasure chest near a dead-end path in the southwestern middle level of The Fierce Forest
  • Drustan's Shield: Inside a treasure chest on the northern end of Level 2's eastern side in Hoarder's Keep
  • Gold Bar: Inside a treasure chest on Level 2 in the southwest chamber of Hoarder's Keep
  • Eternal Elegance: Found in a treasure chest near the ground-floor south entrance on Level 1 in Hoarder's Keep

Secrets

  • A warp sigil located directly behind the Summit Altar in The Fierce Forest transports players directly to the final bridge exit, bypassing every teleportal puzzle in the forest.

  • In Hoarder's Keep on Level 3, drop through the eastern floor pitfall near the blocked portal to plunge directly to Level 1, saving significant traversal time.
