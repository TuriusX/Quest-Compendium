Nautica

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the Pearl Harp

After obtaining Lorelei's Harp (sometimes called the Pearl Harp) in Lonalulu, the party dives into the light column in the sea to reach the sunken undersea kingdom of Nautica. Your main goal here is to enter the palace and speak with Queen Marina, who bestows the Green Orb upon the Luminary.

Items

  • Water Sedge: Sparkly spot on the sea floor near the arrival area / docked ship
  • Mini Medal: Treasure chest situated behind Queen Marina's palace
  • Mini Medal: Treasure chest located northwest of the Item Shop
  • Green Orb: Received directly from Queen Marina during your audience inside the palace

Secrets

  • Michelle's diary can be found and read inside her house situated north of the weapon shop.
  • After concluding your business with Queen Marina, a mermaid appears just outside the palace gates offering the side quest "Up Where They Walk".

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells items and accessories, notably the Coral Hairpin
  • Weapon Shop: Manned by a shark clerk who cannot trade with you during your initial visit due to the language barrier
  • Mermaid Bank Clerk: Offers banking services to store and retrieve your gold

Tips

  • You do not need to spend money at an inn here; speak to the pink-marked mermaid near town to completely replenish the entire party's HP and MP free of charge.
