The Champs Sauvages

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to Phnom Nohn

The Luminary and his companions arrive in The Champs Sauvages via the southern sea crossing, traveling north toward the painted village of Phnom Nonh. The party must traverse a large marshland area filled with cliffs, rope bridges, and high plateaus to reach their destination. Along the way, players can explore the southern campsite, access mysterious ruins, and navigate around dangerous high-level monsters.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest on an island in the southern river
  • Pitch Pearl: Sparkly spot found in the shallow water of the southern marsh
  • Love Essence: Inside a treasure chest near the path heading toward the Promontory Hermit's cave
  • Saint's Ashes: Inside a treasure chest behind a locked red door in the southern ruins

Secrets

  • A secluded path behind a waterfall in the western section leads to an otherwise hidden treasure chest.

Tips

  • Look for glowing rideable monsters to reach high, isolated plateaus that are inaccessible on foot.
  • Resting at the southern campsite is recommended to save progress and recharge MP before tackling the tough encounters near Phnom Nonh.
