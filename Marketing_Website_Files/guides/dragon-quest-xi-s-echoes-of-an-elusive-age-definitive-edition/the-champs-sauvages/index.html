The Champs Sauvages

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to Phnom Nohn

This expansive, jungle-like region connects coastal landings and inland paths directly to the town of Phnom Nonh. While journeying southward to reach the town, players navigate winding waterways, rocky cliffs, and monster-filled paths. Travelers frequently stop to manage gear at campsites or seek out rare materials before entering Phnom Nonh.

Items

  • Mini Medal: inside a treasure chest located in the corner to the west of the southern campsite
  • Enchanted Stone: inside a treasure chest reached by ascending stairs in the rocky ravine south-west of the northern campsite

Secrets

  • Five hidden archery targets are scattered across the cliffs, trees, and waters of the region; shooting all five and reporting to the Bodkin archer at the north campsite rewards 4 Seeds of Therapeusis.
  • A fortune-telling weather cow stands near the ruins and circular water path west of Phnom Nonh; speaking with it reveals upcoming rain conditions.
