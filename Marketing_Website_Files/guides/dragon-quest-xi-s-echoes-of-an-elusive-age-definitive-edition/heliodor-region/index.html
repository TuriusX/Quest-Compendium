Heliodor Region

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the capital

The Luminary travels through this sweeping countryside after departing Cobblestone to reach the royal capital of Heliodor. The player must navigate northward along the paths, battling local wildlife and gathering materials on their way to audience with the King. A campsite is located along the western path where the party can rest, save, and access basic supplies before entering the city gates.

Items

  • Medicinal Herb: In pots outside the small rest cabin near the campsite
  • Antidotal Herb: Inside a wooden barrel by the wooden shack south of the Heliodor gates
  • Single Phial of Holy Water: In a treasure chest hidden in the southeastern cul-de-sac
  • Red Eye: In a treasure chest located on a raised hill near the western area
  • 80 Gold Coins: Inside a treasure chest tucked away near the eastern forested ridge

Tips

  • Use your horse to dash across the wide plains and trample smaller field monsters to bypass combat entirely without losing time.
  • Break glowing mineral deposits and sparkle nodes across the map to stockpile early crafting components for later use at the Fun-Size Forge.
  • Stop at the campsite to pray at the sacred statue before entering Heliodor to ensure your autosave point is updated and your HP/MP is full.
