The Cryptic Crypt

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Retrieving the Red Orb

The Luminary and Erik travel deep into this underground dungeon beneath the ruins of the Kingsbarrow to reclaim the stolen Red Orb. To advance through the lower levels, the party must navigate trapped pitfalls and operate stone levers that lower protective barriers. Reaching the innermost sanctum triggers a battle against a pair of demonic guardians known as the Grim Gryphons.

Items

  • Magic water: Inside a treasure chest on the southern side of the central floor
  • Feathered cap: Found in a treasure chest in the subterranean prison area
  • Mini medal: Found inside a treasure chest located in an alcove on the lower floor
  • Seed of agility: Smashed from an unsealed urn in the corridor leading to the main chamber

Secrets

  • Stepping deliberately into certain cracked floor tiles drops the party down into otherwise unreachable cell blocks on the floor below containing treasure chests.

Tips

  • Make sure to utilize the campsite located just inside the dungeon entrance to rest, forge gear, and save your game before delving deeper.
  • In the boss battle against the two Grim Gryphons, focus all single-target attacks on one gryphon at a time to quickly reduce incoming damage.
