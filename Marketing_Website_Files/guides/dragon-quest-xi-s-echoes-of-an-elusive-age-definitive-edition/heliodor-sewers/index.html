Heliodor Sewers

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Escaping the castle

The Luminary navigates the underground waterway with Erik to evade pursuing castle guards after breaking out of the dungeon. After a wooden bridge gives way, they plunge deeper into the caverns and find themselves fleeing a colossal Black Dragon. The escape concludes with both characters jumping off a cliff edge into the river far below.

Items

  • Medicinal Herb: at a sparkly spot located to the right near a locked door shortly into the sewers

Secrets

  • Defeating the Black Dragon during the first chase sequence unlocks the special Dragon Slayer accolade.

Tips

  • You can crouch and duck behind large stacks of wooden crates to slip past patrolling soldiers without getting dragged into combat.
  • During the Dragon's Lair chase, keep sprinting straight toward the screen and swerve around debris and panicked monsters to avoid entering a battle.
