The Emerald Coast

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Journeying toward the next town

The Emerald Coast is an expansive outdoor region traversed after escaping Heliodor Castle through the sewers and Manglegrove. The Luminary and Erik pass through these coastal meadows and cliffs while journeying south toward the town of Hotto. The area features a central campsite near a river and serves as a major hub for hunting, gathering materials, and learning mounted combat.

Items

  • Flintstone: In a sparkling gather spot on the ground near the northern canyon exit
  • Red Eye: Inside a treasure chest hidden behind a waterfall along the river
  • Gold Ore: Inside a treasure chest placed on a high ledge accessible via climbing
  • Wakerobin: Sparkling gather spot in the open meadow near the central campsite
  • Copper Ore: In a blue mineral deposit on a rock face along the northern path

Secrets

  • Steal a monster mount from an Eggoskeleton along the cliff paths to leap up high rocky platforms that are otherwise completely unreachable.

Shops and people

  • Roving Emporium merchant at the campsite: Sells basic recovery items such as Medicinal Herbs and Antidotal Herbs, along with starter equipment.

Tips

  • Rest at the Emerald Coast campsite to restore all party HP and MP for free and use the Fun-Size Forge to upgrade your starter weapons.
  • Ride your horse at full speed through groups of smaller enemies to knock them away and avoid entering battle.
