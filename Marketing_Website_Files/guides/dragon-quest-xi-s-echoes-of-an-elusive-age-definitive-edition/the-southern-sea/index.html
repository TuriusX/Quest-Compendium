The Southern Sea

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Sailing to find allies

After regaining access to the Salty Stallion, the Luminary sails the southern waters of Erdrea to locate his separated companions. Traveling through these sea lanes connects you to several coastal destinations, including Gondolia, Puerto Valor, and The Strand. Players can engage in maritime battles and check shiny ocean spots while navigating toward their next story objectives.

Tips

  • Watch for flocks of seabirds circling over open water, as they mark salvage points where you can fish out items.
  • Open the sea menu while sailing to rest inside the ship's cabin, which completely restores the party's HP and MP at no cost.
