DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the casino city

Puerto Valor (often approached from the Costa Valor region near Soltico) is a coastal resort town famous for its vast casino, luxury villas, and bustling beach. Players visit to gain access to the open sea via Don Rodrigo's sluice gate and to take a break playing minigames. Advancing the story here requires visiting Don Rodrigo's mansion to petition for passage through the canal locks.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest on the beach along the western coast of town
  • Recipe Book: Down the Hatch: Inside a bookshelf in the bar/restaurant area
  • Bunny Suit: Purchased at the Puerto Valor Casino exchange counter for casino tokens

Secrets

  • The Puerto Valor Casino features a special high-roller area on the upper floor that is initially blocked off and only unlocks later in the game.

Shops and people

  • Puerto Valor Casino Token Exchange: Sells valuable gear, materials, and recipe books in exchange for tokens won at slots, poker, or roulette
  • Armor Shop: Sells mid-game protective gear, shields, and helmets appropriate for the journey across the sea

Tips

  • Spend some time at the poker or slot tables to acquire the Platinum Sword or Platinum Greatsword early, which significantly boosts party damage output.
  • Don Rodrigo's mansion is located in the northernmost section of the city, which is where you must go to progress the main storyline and open the sea gates.
