The Disciple's Trial

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The first trial of the post-game

The Disciple's Trial is the first challenge within Drustan's Labyrinth during the post-game, accessed by touching the western sapling on Trial Isle. It requires the party to navigate dangerous, rearranged renditions of previous dungeons—specifically The Cruel Crypt and The Eerie Valley—to reach the trial boss. Defeating Blind Hatred at the end within 25 actions completes the trial and lets the party claim a wish from Drustan.

Items

  • Kings of the Rings: The Cruel Crypt, inside a treasure chest on the lower level reached by falling through an eastern hallway floor trap
  • Erdwin's Bracelet: The Cruel Crypt, inside a chest located down the southwest hallway after dropping through the floor into the pit room
  • Lumen Essence: The Eerie Valley, inside a treasure chest found at the Phoenix's Nest in the northeast
  • Drustan's Helm: The Eerie Valley, inside a treasure chest situated at a dead end within the eastern cave section
  • A Hero's Book of Better Weapons: The Eerie Valley, inside a treasure chest positioned in the southeastern section near the wall of light
  • Mini Medal x3: The Eerie Valley, inside a treasure chest located atop a high ledge along the northern riverbank requiring a flying mount

Secrets

  • A Vicious Mimic posing as a treasure chest along the path to the exit in The Cruel Crypt; defeat it to obtain a Seed of skill.

Tips

  • In The Eerie Valley, defeat a glowing Vicious Dread Dragooner to commandeer its flying drake mount, which allows you to bypass ground routes and soar straight to high ledges containing rare chests.
  • To defeat Blind Hatred under the 25-turn constraint, pre-buff party members like Erik and the Hero with Oomphle and apply Sap on the boss before delivering high-burst physical skills.
