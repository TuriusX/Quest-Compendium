The Disciple's Trial

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The first trial of the post-game

The Disciple's Trial is the first of three trials located within the mysterious realm accessible through the glowing seal in the Havens Above. You must navigate two sub-areas—the Cruel Crypt and the Eerie Valley—to reach the trial boss, Blind Hatred, at the Sunshade Temple. Defeating the boss within the required turn limit allows you to claim one wish from the trial's master.

Items

  • Sunburst Ell: Cruel Crypt, inside a treasure chest located in the dead end of the central lower chambers
  • Sainted Soma: Eerie Valley, inside a treasure chest on an elevated ridge accessible by riding an enemy mount
  • Recipe book "A Guide to Godly Get-Up": Sunshade Temple, inside a treasure chest in the courtyard area

Secrets

  • Certain blocked ledges and toxic terrain in the Cruel Crypt and Eerie Valley can only be safely bypassed by defeating glowing demon mounts and riding them.

Tips

  • Bring strong Area-of-Effect skills like Veronica's Magic Burst or the Hero's Gigagash to clear out Blind Hatred's shadows immediately so they do not drain your turn count.
  • You do not need to fight every regular monster on the way; avoid unnecessary battles to conserve MP and reach the Sunshade Temple at full strength.
