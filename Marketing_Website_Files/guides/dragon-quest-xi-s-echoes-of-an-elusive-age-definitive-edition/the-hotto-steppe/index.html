DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the Cryptic Crypt

The Luminary and Erik journey through this arid, volcanic region to track down Veronica's missing sister and retrieve stolen magic. Travelers must head west toward the Cryptic Crypt while navigating rugged canyons, monster-filled paths, and a campsite that offers respite.

Items

  • Copper Ore: In mineral deposit nodes throughout the rocky cliffs
  • Lava Lump: Sparkling spots near lava pools and rock formations
  • Feathered Cap: Inside a treasure chest on the high ground south of the campsite

Tips

  • Rest at the campfire in the southern area to save your game and forge new gear using the Fun-Size Forge before heading into the Cryptic Crypt.
  • Use Erik's Half-Inch skill on Robber Rabbits to secure Bunny Tails for an early-game boost to your item drop rates.
