The Dundrasil Ruins

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Learning about the Luminary's past

In this location, the party traverses the remains of the fallen kingdom by entering a well to access the inner grounds and following Rab to a memorial altar. Major cutscenes reveal the tragic events that destroyed Dundrasil and unveil the Luminary's true royal heritage. Afterwards, pursuing Heliodorian soldiers trigger an escape sequence that eventually brings Jade and Rab into the active party.

Items

  • Dress Like a Drasilian: Found in a treasure chest reached by ducking underneath fallen debris east of the memorial altar
  • Drasilian Sovereign: Found inside a breakable barrel on the path continuing past the memorial site
  • Strong Medicine: Picked up at a sparkly spot on the ground near the memorial headstone
  • Mini Medal: Concealed in a breakable pot positioned along a ledge inside the entrance area

Secrets

  • A secluded chamber containing a treasure chest is accessed by crawling through an opening beneath a collapsed stone pillar near the gravesite.
