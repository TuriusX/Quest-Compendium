Cobblestone (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The Luminary returns to his hometown

During Act 2, the Luminary discovers that the ruined ruins of Cobblestone have been converted into "The Last Bastion," a heavily fortified refugee camp led by King Carnelian and Hendrik. You must navigate the encampment to reunite with the surviving villagers, speak with the King, and defend the settlement against invading monster forces.

Items

  • Mini Medal: inside a treasure chest located in King Carnelian's royal tent
  • Obsidian Blade: inside a treasure chest near the armor shop tent
  • Seed of Skill: found in a pot near the entrance of the refugee camp
  • 3,000 gold coins: inside a treasure chest near the southern watchtower

Secrets

  • The roots of Yggdrasil near the village's high cliff path can be inspected to witness memories explaining how Hendrik and the surviving villagers escaped the fall of Heliodor.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells medicinal herbs, strong medicine, holy water, and chimera wings to aid during the siege
  • Weapons & Armor Shop: Sells upgraded Act 2 basic equipment including Broadswords, Battleaxes, and Iron Armor sets
  • Church Priest: Offers confession, divination, resurrection, and save-game recording near the northern side of the camp

Tips

  • Fully prepare your equipment and rest at the inn before approaching the fortress exit, as an unskippable multi-phase battle against monster waves begins immediately once you head out to the barricades.
  • Hendrik fights alongside you as an automated guest party member during the siege; let him draw enemy aggression and deal heavy single-target blows while you focus on group healing and crowd control.
