Cobblestone (Act 2)

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The Luminary returns to his hometown

In Act 2, the Luminary discovers that Cobblestone has been converted into an armed stronghold known as The Last Bastion, serving as the final refuge for survivors. You reunite with Gemma, your mother Amber, and the King of Heliodor before heading to the front lines just outside town to defend it alongside Hendrik. Once the assault is repelled and you confer with the King, you make preparations here before setting out toward the Heliodor Region.

Items

  • 3000 gold coins: In a treasure chest on the wooden rampart/raised walkway west of the King's royal tent
  • Wristorative: Inside a treasure chest located behind the locked red Magic Key door
  • Mini Medal: In a barrel found among the ruins of Dunstan and Gemma's house near the northern entrance
  • Sage's Elixir: Inside a pot situated on the upper rampart/roof area near the cannon of the Luminary's house (reached by climbing the adjacent watchtower ladder)
  • Special Antidote: In a barrel placed outside the King's royal pavilion tent
  • Special Medicine: In a barrel near the southern base of the hill where the royal tent stands
  • Magic Water: Inside a barrel on the northern section of the wooden scaffolding along the ridge northeast of the shop
  • 200 gold coins: In a barrel at the southeastern end of the wooden scaffolding northeast of the shop

Secrets

  • A free resting place remains usable at the bed located amidst the wreckage of the Luminary's ruined house.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Headless HonchoMini-boss encountered during the battle just outside the Bastion's northern barricade
ChasmonautMonster fighting as support alongside the Headless Honcho at the northern gates

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells weapons including Cypress Stick, Zombiesbane, and Bastard Sword
  • Armour Shop: Sells armor and gear including Magic Shield, Iron Headgear, and Heavy Armour
  • Item Shop: Sells items including Holy Talisman, Strong Medicine, and Strong Antidote

Tips

  • If you missed opening the locked red door with the Magic Key during Act 1, be sure to unlock it now to claim the Wristorative accessory.
  • Take advantage of resting for free in your childhood home's intact bed to restore full HP and MP without spending gold.
