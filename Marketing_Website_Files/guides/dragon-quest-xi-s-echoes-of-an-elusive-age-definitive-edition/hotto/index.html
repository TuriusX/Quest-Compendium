Hotto

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Seeking the seer

The Luminary and Erik travel to Hotto looking for a renowned seer to learn more about the Luminary's destiny. While investigating the village, the party encounters Veronica outside the steam baths, setting off a search for her missing sister in the nearby crypts.

Items

  • 200 gold coins: Inside a treasure chest on the rooftop accessed via stairs on the western side of town
  • Single phial of holy water: Smashed pot inside the items shop
  • Single sprig of pretty betsy: Wardrobe inside a residence near the bar
  • Single chunk of copper ore: Barrel near the blacksmith forge in the northern part of town

Secrets

  • A treasure chest is hidden behind the outdoor counter of the bar, accessible by walking around the wooden partitions.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Bronze Sword, Cast-Iron Claymore, Bronze Knife, Divine Dagger, Bronze Feather, and Oaken Club
  • Armour Shop: Sells Leather Shield, Scale Shield, Leather Cap, Feathered Cap, Wayfarer's Clothes, and Leather Dress
  • Item Shop: Sells Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb, Holy Water, and Chimaera Wing
  • Steam Baths: Pay a small fee of gold to relax in the hot springs and fully restore HP and MP

Tips

  • Resting at the Hotto steam baths is an inexpensive way to completely recover party HP and MP instead of staying at the inn.
  • Look inside the local homes to find recipe books on bookshelves that teach basic forge crafting recipes for early equipment.
