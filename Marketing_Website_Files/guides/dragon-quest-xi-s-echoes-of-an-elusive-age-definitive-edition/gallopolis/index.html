DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · The horse race and the prince

When the party arrives at the desert sultanate to obtain the Rainbough, Prince Faris begs the Luminary to secretly take his place in the prestigious horse race. Following the competition, the Luminary must journey with Faris and the performer Sylvando into the northern sands to face the menacing Slayer of the Sands.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest hidden directly behind the building south of the church
  • So You Want to Be a Witch: On the bookshelf inside the inn

Secrets

  • A woman standing in an alleyway between the weapon and armour shops offers a classic Puff-Puff interaction.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Slayer of the SandsFireEmploys Kasap, physical strikes, and a sand-swirling move capable of inflicting confusion.

Shops and people

  • Gallopolis City Armour Shop: Bronze armour, Dancer's costume, and assorted defensive gear

Tips

  • During the horse race, hold the dash button the moment the countdown number disappears to trigger an early speed burst.
