The Zwaardrust Region

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the ruins of the kingdom

The party arrives in the Zwaardrust Region while journeying across the continent toward the royal ruins. Here you find the Warrior's Rest Inn and the remnants of a fallen kingdom guarded by monsters. The area acts as a crucial crossroads leading south to Laguna di Gondolia and north toward Dundrasil.

Items

  • Purple Eye: Chest hidden in the southern ruins area behind a locked gate accessible later
  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest at the Warrior's Rest Inn
  • Recipe Book: An Affectionate Look at Arms: Found in a bookshelf at the Warrior's Rest Inn

Tips

  • Use the Warrior's Rest Inn as your regional base camp to freely heal, save at the church, and craft gear on the Fun-Size Forge.
  • Look for sparkling harvest points along the wheat fields to gather valuable crafting ingredients like Fresh Milk and clusters of Wheat.
