The Zwaardrust Region

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Traveling to the ruins of the kingdom

The Luminary and party travel to this devastated region after setting sail from Gondolia, following the path towards Octagonia. You can rest and regroup at The Warrior's Rest Inn, explore the poisonous ruins of the fallen kingdom, and later return with the Magic Key or during the post-game to reveal buried secrets.

Items

  • 1500 gold coins: inside a treasure chest located across a stone arch at the far western path past the ruins
  • Purple Eye: inside a treasure chest reached by crossing a fallen log over a poison pool near the western ruins stairway
  • Recipe Book: Smithing with Steel: found by checking a bookshelf upstairs in The Warrior's Rest Inn
  • Mini Medal: inside a treasure chest out on the balcony of The Warrior's Rest Inn
  • Purple Orb: inside a treasure chest behind the red Magic Key door down the eastern stairs of the ruins
  • Recipe Book: Classy Clobber for Kingly Kids: inside a treasure chest behind the Magic Key door down the eastern stairway of the ruins

Secrets

  • A hidden Mini Medal buried in the very center of the wheat field directly west of The Warrior's Rest Inn, discovered by searching when an arrow appears above your feet.

  • The Warrior's Rest Inn: Resting services and church saving, along with traveling merchant goods

Tips

  • Accept the quest "A Path to Paradise" from the priest behind the inn, sleep until nightfall, and have the Luminary and Serena use the Pep Power Care Prayer against a Deadnaut to easily claim the Robe of Serenity.
