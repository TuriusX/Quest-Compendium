The Gallopolis Sands

DRAGON QUEST® XI S: Echoes of an Elusive Age™ - Definitive Edition guide · Fighting the Slayer of the Sands

The Luminary travels to the far corners of the Gallopolis Sands alongside Prince Faris and the Sultan's soldiers to confront the beast terrorizing the desert. Upon reaching the Celestial Sands near the northern ruins, you must face the colossal Slayer of the Sands in a multi-stage story battle. Defeating this monstrosity proves Faris's supposed bravery to his kingdom and earns you the Crackshot Key.

Items

  • Duneberry: Sparkly spot south of the Celestial Sands campsite
  • Glass Frit: Sparkly spot near the central oasis
  • Copper Ore: Mining deposit on the eastern cliffs

Tips

  • Equipping your frontline fighters with Sand Spit-resistant or confusion-resistant gear will heavily counter the Slayer of the Sands' most debilitating attacks.
  • Set Prince Faris to support during the boss fight as he occasionally throws items or distracts the beast, giving you free turns to heal.
