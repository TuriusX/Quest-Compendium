Great Bay Coast

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After learning the New Wave Bossa Nova

After learning the "New Wave Bossa Nova", you can summon the giant turtle sleeping near the Marine Research Lab by playing the melody while standing on the pedestal in front of him. Using the Hookshot on the palm tree on the turtle's back transports Link directly to the Great Bay Temple. The coast also serves as the staging ground for several optional Heart Pieces and side objectives using newly acquired abilities.

Items

  • Piece of Heart MISSABLE: Inside the Oceanside Spider House, awarded by the man at the entrance if cleared on the First Day
  • Piece of Heart: Given by the fisherman at the islands in the ocean after completing his jumping mini-game
  • Giant Wallet MISSABLE: Awarded by the man at the entrance of the Oceanside Spider House on the First Day if you have not already obtained the Piece of Heart

Secrets

  • Hookshot targets located atop the high rock pillars in the bay allow access to the fisherman's island minigame and the entrance to the Waterfall Rapids.

Tips

  • Play the New Wave Bossa Nova on the Zora guitar (Ocarina of Time while transformed as Zora Link) to awaken the giant turtle.
  • The Oceanside Spider House near the shore requires fire to burn away cobwebs, so bring Fire Arrows or a method to produce fire before entering.
