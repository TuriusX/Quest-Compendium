Termina Field

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · First cycle

Termina Field is the central hub connecting Clock Town to the four outlying regions of Termina. During the first three-day cycle as Deku Link, access to the surrounding perimeter is blocked by guards until the moon's tear is obtained and the gate opened. Once outside the walls, the area contains wandering enemies and paths leading toward Swamp, Mountain, Ocean, and Canyon.

Secrets

  • A hollow tree near the Southern Swamp path holds a Piece of Heart resting among its branches, reachable by climbing a nearby vine.
  • A hidden hole in the ground near the north exit contains two Dodongos that award a Piece of Heart once both are defeated.
  • Four underground Gossip Stone grottos are scattered around the field; playing the Sonata of Awakening to the large stone in each hole changes their color to match and rewards a Piece of Heart.

Tips

  • Avoid letting the large bird near Milk Road touch you, as getting robbed forces you to buy back your gear at the Curiosity Shop in Clock Town.
  • You cannot leave Clock Town to explore Termina Field until you regain your human form or acquire the proper pass from the guard.
