Great Bay Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After obtaining the Hookshot

In this ocean dungeon, Link must manipulate water flow by turning color-coded valves to access new areas and power the central waterwheel. Players frequently switch between swimming as Zora Link and utilizing the Hookshot and Ice Arrows to traverse water-filled corridors and pipes. The dungeon culminates in a battle against the giant aquatic creature Gyorg to rescue the Zora boss soul and claim the area's remains.

Items

  • Dungeon Map: Inside a treasure chest on a high ledge in the second main room, accessed using the Hookshot
  • Compass: Inside a treasure chest found underwater in a room filled with spiked mines and water currents
  • Ice Arrows: Awarded from a large treasure chest after defeating the mini-boss Wart
  • Boss Key: Inside a treasure chest located in a flooded room beneath water currents, reached via a green pipe route
  • Stray Fairies (x15) MISSABLE: Dispersed throughout pots, skulltulas, bubbles, and chests across the dungeon rooms
  • Gyorg's Remains: Rewarded upon defeating the boss Gyorg in the dungeon's final chamber
  • Heart Container: Dropped onto the boss room platform after defeating Gyorg

Tips

  • Ice Arrows can freeze Octoroks into solid blocks or freeze patches of water to create temporary stepping stones across large pools.
  • Equipping the Great Fairy's Mask helps draw out Stray Fairies trapped in out-of-reach bubbles without wasting arrows.
  • Follow the direction of the colored pipes along the ceilings and floors to track which water pumps and gates have been activated.
