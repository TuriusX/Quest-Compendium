Clock Town

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · First cycle

Trapped as a Deku Scrub, you arrive inside Clock Town with only three days remaining until the moon crashes down. You must navigate the town, retrieve your stolen Ocarina of Time from the Skull Kid atop the Clock Tower, and learn the Song of Time to reset the three-day cycle. Completing these tasks will return you to your human form and properly begin your adventure.

Items

  • Stray Fairy: Floating above the pool in the Laundry Pool or inside the East Clock Town tavern area during specific hours
  • Moon's Tear: Obtained at the Astral Observatory after looking through the telescope at the Skull Kid
  • Land Title Deed: Received from the Business Scrub in South Clock Town by trading him the Moon's Tear
  • Ocarina of Time: Dropped by the Skull Kid atop the Clock Tower just before the time runs out
  • Deku Bubble attack: Learned after returning the Stray Fairy to the Great Fairy of Magic in the Fairy Fountain in North Clock Town

Secrets

  • Pop the floating balloon in North Clock Town using a Deku Bubble to reveal Jim, who challenges you to a hide-and-seek minigame with the Bombers' Secret Society of Justice.
  • Catching all five hidden Bombers in Clock Town gives you the password required to access the hidden underground waterway leading to the Astral Observatory.

Shops and people

  • Business Scrub: Trades the South Clock Town Deku Flower and a Land Title Deed in exchange for a Moon's Tear
  • Clock Town Bank: Stores your Rupees across cycles, guarded by the Banker in West Clock Town

Tips

  • Spin into the wooden crates and cut the grass throughout town to easily gather Rupees as a Deku Scrub.
  • The entrance to the top of the Clock Tower in South Clock Town only opens at midnight on the Final Day.
  • Use the Deku Flower in South Clock Town to launch into the air and land on the Clock Tower ledge once midnight strikes on the Final Day.
