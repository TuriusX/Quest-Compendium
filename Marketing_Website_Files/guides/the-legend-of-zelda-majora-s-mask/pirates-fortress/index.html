Pirates' Fortress

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After infiltrating the fortress

Link navigates the interior courtyards and rooms of the Gerudo Pirates' headquarters to retrieve four stolen Zora eggs. He must avoid being captured by patrolling pirate guards while solving puzzles and taking down elite Gerudo warriors. After claiming the eggs in bottles and securing the Hookshot, Link can leave the fortress to return the eggs to the Marine Research Lab.

Items

  • Hookshot: Inside a treasure chest in the central upper chamber guarded by an elite pirate, accessed after shooting a beehive through a high grating
  • Zora Egg (x4): Submerged in large fish tanks located in separate guarded chambers throughout the fortress interior
  • Piece of Heart: In a treasure chest located inside a prison cell requiring a switch puzzle to unlock

Secrets

  • Shooting the Desbreko fish inside the Zora egg aquariums allows Link to sink safely to the bottom and collect the egg without taking damage.
  • Shooting a beehive on the ceiling of the pirates' planning room through a high window with an arrow causes bees to attack, forcing the pirates out of the room.

Tips

  • Equipping the Stone Mask makes Link completely invisible to all patrolling Gerudo guards, making navigation through the fortress trivial.
  • Bring four Empty Bottles before entering the fortress interior so you can collect all four Zora eggs in a single trip.
  • Use the Pictograph Box to snap a clear, close-up photo of an active female pirate, as it can be traded later to the Fisherman for the Seahorse.
