Zora Hall

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After arriving at Great Bay Coast

Zora Hall is the home of the Zora tribe and the band The Indigo-Go's, located off the shore of the Great Bay. In this area, Link disguises himself as the fallen guitarist Mikau to interact with band members, rehearse, and help restore the voice of the singer Lulu. Addressing the band's troubles and participating in musical sessions awards Pieces of Heart.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: In Japas's room, play the jam session notes with him as Zora Link, then play the completed song for Evan in his room as regular Link
  • Piece of Heart: Use fire to light all four unlit torches in the rehearsal room where Lulu resides

Secrets

  • Read Mikau's diary entries in his private room by using the Hookshot or Zora fins to climb up to the upper ledge, which provides half of the jam session melody.

Shops and people

  • Zora Shop: Green Potion, Hero's Shield, Arrows

Tips

  • You must present the completed jam melody to Evan as regular Link, because Evan is too proud to accept songwriting suggestions from fellow band members like Mikau.
