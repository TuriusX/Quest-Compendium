Goron Village

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After arriving at Snowhead

Goron Village is initially frozen over in a harsh winter, but melts back to spring after you defeat Goht at Snowhead Temple. While frozen, you learn the Goron Lullaby from the Goron Elder's crying child to open the path to Snowhead. Once spring arrives, you can clear the Goron Powder Keg challenge and access Biggoron to buy massive explosives.

Items

  • Goron Lullaby: Learned from the crying Goron child inside the Goron Shrine by playing the Lullaby Intro
  • Piece of Heart: Located inside the Goron Shrine; light all torches to make the chandelier spin, then roll into the jars to break them and find the piece
  • Powder Keg: Awarded by Medigoron inside the giant cave shop after successfully completing his boulder-clearing test
  • Huge Quiver: Won by scoring a perfect 50 points in the Swamp Shooting Gallery, but requires a Powder Keg from here to access the Powder Keg certification to blast open the boulder for Romani Ranch

Secrets

  • Melting the frozen Goron Elder on the path outside the village with Hot Spring Water will prompt him to teach you the Lullaby Intro.
  • Defeating Goht melts the ice across the village, revealing Medigoron's bomb workshop and a Deku Scrub merchant selling to Gorons.

Shops and people

  • Goron Village Deku Scrub: Green Potion for 10 Rupees; trades you his Deku Flower for the Swamp Title Deed
  • Medigoron: Sells Powder Kegs to certified Gorons for 100 Rupees each (first one is given for completing his test)
  • Goron Shop (Frozen): Red Potion (80 Rupees), Arrows (40 Rupees), Bombs (40 Rupees)
  • Goron Shop (Spring): Red Potion (50 Rupees), Arrows (40 Rupees), Bombs (40 Rupees)

Tips

  • Hot Spring Water cools down and turns into ordinary water quickly, so roll as a Goron to transport it swiftly from the cavern to the frozen Elder.
  • You must be in Goron form to carry and throw Powder Kegs, as they are too heavy for normal Link.
