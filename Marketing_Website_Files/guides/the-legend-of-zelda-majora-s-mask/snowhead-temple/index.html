Snowhead Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After learning the Goron Lullaby

After playing the Goron Lullaby to put Biggoron to sleep, Link ascends the blizzard-swept path to enter Snowhead Temple. Inside, Link must manipulate a giant central pillar, melt ice blocking pathways, and use Goron rolling across precarious platforms to reach the mechanical boss. Defeating the boss clears the freezing curse over the mountain, bringing spring to the Snowhead region.

Items

  • Fire Arrows: Defeat the Wizzrobe miniboss on the upper floor
  • Dungeon Map: Inside a treasure chest in the large chamber on the first floor
  • Compass: Inside a treasure chest in a side room surrounded by ice blocks
  • Boss Key: In an upper room reached after punching away segments of the central pillar
  • Goht's Remains: Defeat the temple boss, Goht
  • Heart Container: Spawns in Goht's chamber after defeating the boss

Secrets

  • Stray Fairy | Underneath the central pillar's basement floor platform, inside a bubble popped with a projectile
  • Stray Fairy | Inside hidden recesses in the central room's walls, revealed by looking around with the Lens of Truth

Tips

  • Use the Goron Mask's punch to knock out the colored cylindrical segments of the central pillar to alter its height across different floors.
  • The Great Fairy Mask is vital here because its hair will shimmer if a Stray Fairy is in the room, and it pulls floating fairies directly to you.
  • In the Goht battle, use Goron rolling to match the boss's speed and crash into it once your spikes emerge, but watch out for stalactites and bombs dropped in the track.
