Southern Swamp

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After obtaining the Ocarina of Time

Southern Swamp is the gateway to the Woodfall region, initially choked with poisonous purple water that hurts Link unless he navigates in Deku form or via boat. Link must rescue the kidnapped witch Koume in the nearby Woods of Mystery, obtain the pictograph box, and clear a boat tour path to gain access to the Deku Palace. After defeating the Woodfall Temple boss, the swamp's waters are purified, opening up minigames and optional rewards.

Items

  • Pictograph Box: Swamp Tourist Information building, given by the guide inside
  • Red Potion: Magic Hags' Potion Shop, given by Kotake to heal Koume (or inside an empty bottle)
  • Piece of Heart: Top of the trunk of the massive Deku-faced tree along the path to the palace
  • Piece of Heart: Swamp Shooting Gallery, awarded for achieving a perfect score
  • Piece of Heart: Given by the Swamp Tourist Information guide after showing him a good pictograph of the Deku King
  • Piece of Heart: Above the Swamp Tourist Information building roof, accessed via Deku Flower
  • Land Title Deed: Trade the Moon's Tear to the Deku Scrub in front of the Swamp Tourist Information building

Secrets

  • Inside the hollow of the giant tree in the middle of the swamp is a swarm of bats and a Gold Skulltula token door.

Shops and people

  • Swamp Tourist Information: Swamp Cruise ride (free after saving Koume) and Pictograph Contest
  • Magic Hags' Potion Shop: Red Potion, Green Potion, and Blue Potion (if ingredients provided)
  • Swamp Shooting Gallery: Archery minigame offering quiver upgrades and a Heart Piece for high scores
  • Business Scrub: Sells Green Potion for 30 Rupees to Deku Link, or normal potions to Hylian Link

Tips

  • Water hurts Human Link while the swamp is poisoned, but Deku Link can skip across the surface for up to five hops without taking damage.
  • You must finish the Woods of Mystery path following the monkey to reach Koume before taking the cruise boat through the deeper waters.
  • Defeating Odolwa in Woodfall Temple purifies the swamp water, allowing Link to swim safely and access the Koume Boat Target minigame.
