Southern Swamp

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After obtaining the Ocarina of Time
← All areas in this guide

The Southern Swamp serves as the gateway to the Woodfall region, initially covered in hazardous purple poison that damages Link unless he is traversing via boat or lily pads. To proceed toward Woodfall Temple and rescue the Deku Princess, Link must heal the injured witch Koume with a Red Potion to restore boat cruise services and navigate deep into the Deku Palace. After cleansing the swamp by defeating the boss of Woodfall Temple, the poison clears entirely, unlocking new mini-games and scenic access throughout the waterways.

Spider hanging from webs along paths and trees

Shops and people
  • Swamp Tourist Information Center: Boat Cruise tickets and Pictograph contest entry
  • Magic Hags' Potion Shop: Red Potion, Green Potion, and Blue Potion (after giving Mushrooms to Kotake)
  • Swamp Shooting Gallery: Archery mini-game for Rupees that yields quiver upgrades and a Piece of Heart

Tips
  • Avoid touching the purple water directly as human Link; use the Deku Scrub form to hop across the surface lily pads safely.
  • You can bring a pictograph of the Deku King to the Tourist Information Center man to earn a Piece of Heart.
  • The swamp's poisonous water will be completely purified once Odolwa is defeated inside Woodfall Temple.
