Ikana Canyon

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After obtaining the Garo's Mask

Ikana Canyon is a desolate, cursed valley of the undead located in eastern Termina. After using the Garo's Mask to access the cliffs, Link must restore the flow of water to drive away the Gibdos, explore the Ancient Castle of Ikana, and enter Stone Tower Temple. Along the way, Link helps troubled spirits, learns the Elegy of Emptiness, and obtains the Mirror Shield.

Items

  • Gibdo Mask: Inside the Music Box House after playing the Song of Healing for Pamela's father
  • Mirror Shield: Beneath the Well after giving the required trade items to the Gibdos
  • Piece of Heart: Ikana Graveyard on the Second Night after ordering the Stalchildren to dig up a grave
  • Piece of Heart: Ancient Castle of Ikana on an exterior pillar reached by activating switch platforms
  • Piece of Heart: Spirit House (Poe Hut) by defeating the four Poe Sisters within the time limit while taking little damage

Secrets

  • The dried-up riverbed can be refilled by playing the Song of Storms inside the spring cave to banish the evil spirit Flat's brother, Sharp.
  • Playing the Song of Storms or the March of the Music Box House causes Pamela to emerge and leave the house unlocked, allowing you to sneak inside.

Tips

  • Wearing the Gibdo Mask or Captain's Hat prevents ReDeads and Gibdos in the area from paralyzing Link.
  • Stock up on trade items like Magic Beans, Spring Water, Fish, and Bombs before entering Beneath the Well to satisfy the Gibdo guards quickly.
