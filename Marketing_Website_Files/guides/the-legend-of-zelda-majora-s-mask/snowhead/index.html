Snowhead

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After learning the Goron Lullaby
← All areas in this guide

Having learned the Goron Lullaby, ascend the winding mountain path to Snowhead while using Goron rolling jumps to cross wide chasms. Equip the Lens of Truth to reveal Biggoron generating the blizzard, then play the lullaby to lull him to sleep and clear the path to the summit. Once the giant clears the way, climb to the peak to activate the Owl Statue outside the temple.

Enemies
Biggoron
Weak to Goron Lullaby

Invisible without the Lens of Truth; creates a relentless blizzard that blocks access to the summit until put to sleep.

White Wolfos
Weak to Fire, standard attacks

Burrows under the snow along the mountain path and leaps out to attack when you draw near.

Real Bombchu
Weak to Shield deflect, Arrows, BombsSteal / drop Bombs

An aggressive mechanical rodent that rushes Link and detonates on impact.

Shops and people
  • Great Fairy of Wisdom: Fully heals Link and doubles his maximum magic capacity when reunited with her Stray Fairies

Tips
  • Strike the Owl Statue at the summit immediately after bypassing Biggoron so you can warp directly to the peak in future cycles.
  • Build up spiked speed while Goron rolling on the straightaways to ensure you clear the snow ramp jumps over the deep gorges.
