Snowhead

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After learning the Goron Lullaby

After learning the Goron Lullaby, Link returns to the blizzard-swept path of Snowhead to confront the source of the fierce winds. Playing the lullaby reveals and pacifies a giant invisible Goron, opening the way up to Snowhead Temple. Once inside, Link must thaw the frozen temple, navigate a massive central pillar mechanism, and defeat Goht to restore spring to the mountain region.

Items

  • Fire Arrows: Defeat the Wizzrobe mini-boss inside Snowhead Temple
  • Goht's Remains: Defeat the boss Goht in Snowhead Temple
  • Heart Container: Drops after defeating Goht in Snowhead Temple
  • Stray Fairy: 15 scattered throughout Snowhead Temple inside bubbles, chests, and crates

Secrets

  • Equip the Lens of Truth along the blizzard pathway to see Biggoron, the giant Goron creating the windstorm.
  • A Great Fairy Fountain is located beneath the entrance ramp to Snowhead Temple, accessible once all 15 Stray Fairies from the dungeon are collected.

Shops and people

  • Great Fairy of Courage: Enhanced Magic Meter capacity reward for returning all 15 Stray Fairies from Snowhead Temple

Tips

  • Transform into Goron Link and roll with full magic spikes engaged to continuously ram into Goht during the boss fight.
  • Fire Arrows consume magic, so keep an eye on your magic meter by breaking the jars scattered around the Goht boss arena.
