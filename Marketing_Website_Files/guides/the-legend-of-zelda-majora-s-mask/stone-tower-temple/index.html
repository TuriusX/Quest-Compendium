Stone Tower Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask guide · After obtaining the Elegy of Emptiness
← All areas in this guide

Stone Tower Temple is the fourth and final main dungeon, located in the Ikana Canyon region. Link must navigate a complex, mechanical labyrinth by flipping the entire temple upside down using Light Arrows fired at an external crystal emblem. Conquering the dungeon requires defeating the Twinmold bosses to claim Twinmold's Remains and free the final trapped Giant.

Tips
  • Firing a Light Arrow at the red jewel switch located near the temple's entrance flips the entire dungeon upside down.
  • Use the Elegy of Emptiness to spawn shell statues on weighted floor switches to keep doors unlocked.
  • Equip the Mirror Shield to redirect beams of sunlight at sun faces or light-sensitive blocks to remove obstacles.
  • Equipping the Giant's Mask against Twinmold drains your magic meter continuously, so bring Chateau Romani to grant infinite magic.
