Zeffo (Return Visit)

STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order™ guide · Returning to unlock the Tomb of Miktrull
← All areas in this guide

Cal returns to Zeffo following the events on Kashyyyk, tasked with infiltrating the Imperial Dig Site and unlocking the Tomb of Miktrull. Navigating deeper into the planet requires using newly unlocked abilities to bypass previous Imperial roadblocks and environmental barriers. Upon reaching the dig site, you must defeat Imperial forces to access the tomb entrance and continue Cere's mission.

Enemies
Purge Trooper (Electrohammer)
Weak to Parrying attacks with precise timing or dodging heavy unblockable ground slams

Employs slow, high-damage strikes with wide reach that can break Cal's guard quickly.

Purge Trooper (Staff)
Weak to Force Push or parrying combo chains

Quick melee fighter capable of countering standard attacks and recovering quickly from stagger.

Skungus
Weak to Explodes when struck or pulled into enemies with Force Pull

Volatile plant that can be launched at Imperial squads or destructible walls.

Tips
  • You can use Force Pull to grab exploding Skungus plants and launch them directly into groups of Stormtroopers or Purge Troopers for massive area damage.
  • Use Overcharge with BD-1 on red panels inside the Imperial Dig Site to disable laser barriers and redirect powered ziplines to open shortcuts.
