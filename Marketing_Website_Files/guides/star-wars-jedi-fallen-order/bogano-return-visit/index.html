Bogano (Return Visit)

STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order™ guide · Returning to retrieve the Holocron
← All areas in this guide

Cal Kestis returns to Bogano after journeying across the galaxy with the Astrium needed to open the ancient Vault. Upon entering the Vault and securing the Jedi Holocron, Trilla Suduri ambushes Cal, steals the Holocron, and forces him to escape back to the Stinger Mantis through newly deployed Imperial forces.

Achievements here
The Holocron AwaitsRe-enter the ancient Vault on Bogano and retrieve Eno Cordova's Jedi Holocron. This unlocks automatically as part of the story.

Enemies
Trilla Suduri (Second Sister)
Weak to Parrying her telegraphed strike combinations and interrupting her unblockable charges with Force Push or Force Slow

Boss fight encountered inside the Vault after retrieving the Holocron; the fight ends via cutscene rather than depleting her entire health bar

Imperial Purge Trooper
Weak to Parrying melee combos and depleting their stamina block

Armed with electrostaffs or electrohammers, guarding paths outside the Vault as Imperial reinforcements land

Stormtrooper Commander
Weak to Deflecting blaster bolts or utilizing Force Pull

Stations outside the Vault supported by standard Stormtrooper squads during Cal's retreat

Tips
  • After Trilla flees with the Holocron, the route back to the Stinger Mantis is filled with hostile Imperial squads and Purge Troopers, so conserve Stims during the Vault duel.
  • Use Force Pull followed by an immediate strike to quickly eliminate Imperial commanders and regular Stormtroopers to avoid being surrounded on the narrow pathways of Bogano.
