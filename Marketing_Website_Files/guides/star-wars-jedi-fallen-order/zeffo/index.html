Zeffo

STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order™ guide · First visit to investigate the ancient tomb
← All areas in this guide

Cal arrives on the windswept planet Zeffo to investigate the ancient Tomb of Eilram following clues left by Master Eno Cordova. You navigate imperial-occupied settlements, windswept ruins, and solve wind-and-sphere environmental puzzles inside the tomb. Along the way, Cal unlocks the Force Push ability before escaping back to the Mantis to follow the next lead.

Achievements here
Kicking BackApproach a Phillak goat creature from behind so it kicks Cal, then immediately perform an Evasive Kick to strike it back.

Tips
  • Wind vents in the Tomb of Eilram can be toggled via nearby switches to push large metal spheres into designated floor sockets.
  • Look for cracked or glowing blue walls inside the tomb, which can be shattered once you unlock Force Push.
  • Keep an eye out for glowing yellow crates, but remember they require the Scomp Link to slice open if BD-1 has not been upgraded yet.
