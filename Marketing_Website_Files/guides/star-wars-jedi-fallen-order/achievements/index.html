Recommended order
  1. Complete the prologue on Bracca and arrive on Bogano, focusing on learning core parry and dodge mechanics.
  2. Progress through the story on Zeffo, exploring the Tomb of Eilram to unlock the Force Push ability.
  3. Travel to Kashyyyk to free the Wookiees, then return to Zeffo to explore the Tomb of Miktrull and gain Force Pull.
  4. Revisit Kashyyyk to climb the Origin Tree, unlock the double jump (Jedi Flip), and recruit the companion terrarium seeds.
  5. Head to Dathomir, explore until reaching the Tomb of Kujet, and subsequently visit Ilum to rebuild your lightsaber and gain all blade colors.
  6. Return to Bogano to open the Vault using all acquired Force abilities and BD-1 upgrades.
  7. Before the finale, free-roam to achieve 100% holomap exploration and collect all chests, secrets, seeds, and scans across Bogano, Zeffo, Kashyyyk, and Dathomir.
  8. Complete combat-specific achievements (such as kicking a Phillak, severing an Oggdo tongue, and precision evading) during your cleanup phase.
  9. Travel to Fortress Inquisitorius and complete the final story mission to unlock the remaining achievements.
