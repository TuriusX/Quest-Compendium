Persona 5 Royal guide · Okumura's Palace and the rise of the Phantom Thieves' fame

Following the Hawaii school trip, Morgana clashes with the group and runs off, leading the Phantom Thieves to pursue him into Okumura's Spaceport Palace. As Haru Okumura joins the team, public support surges to dangerous heights, culminating in a mission to change Sugimura and Kunikazu Okumura's desires. Players must balance advancing critical third-semester prerequisite Confidants with tackling one of the game's strict time-limit dungeons.

Deadlines

  • October 10 | Send the calling card and steal Kunikazu Okumura's Heart before the time limit expires.

Don't miss

  • School trip souvenirs | In Hawaii on 9/10, purchase exclusive gifts from local vendors, as these shops are inaccessible after the trip ends.
  • Hawaiian date with a romance partner | On 9/10, you can spend the evening with Kawakami, Hifumi, Makoto, or Ann, but only if their Confidant rank is at Rank 5 or higher (or maxed in Kawakami's case) prior to the trip.

Social links

  • Councilor (Takuto Maruki): Available on select weekdays at Shujin Academy; prioritize raising him toward Rank 9 to ensure access to the third semester.
  • Justice (Goro Akechi): Hang out with him at Kichijoji during evenings to raise his Confidant toward Rank 8 before mid-November.
  • Faith (Kasumi Yoshizawa): Hang out with her at Kichijoji to work toward capping her first-half limit at Rank 5.

Worth doing

  • Visit the Jazz Jin club in Kichijoji on Sunday evenings to grant a teammate permanent stat boosts or unique combat skills.

Items

  • Black Rock: Untouchable in Shibuya

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Used Clothing Store "Furugi no Neuchi" in Kichijoji: Exchanges soot-covered armor washed from dungeons for points, armor pieces, and accessories.

Tips

  • Baton Pass chains and single-target elemental consumable items are crucial for beating the timed waves during the boss battle in Okumura's Palace.
