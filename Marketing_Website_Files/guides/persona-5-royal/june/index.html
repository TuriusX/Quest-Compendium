June

Persona 5 Royal guide · Madarame's Palace and the introduction of Yusuke

June marks the transition between finishing the investigation of Madarame's Museum and the beginning of the hunt for the mafia boss Junya Kaneshiro. During this month, Yusuke Kitagawa officially joins the Phantom Thieves, the team deals with the aftermath of Madarame's change of heart, and Makoto Niijima begins closely tailing the protagonist. You must investigate the student body and Shibuya's underworld to track down Kaneshiro's operations, leading into the infiltration of his floating bank Palace.

Deadlines

  • June 5 | Send the Calling Card and steal Ichiryusai Madarame's treasure to force his confession before the deadline expires.

Don't miss

  • Free time on June 6 through June 17 | This period has several locked story sequences and investigation days, so ensure you handle necessary shopping and preparation on your few open days before the next infiltration arc.

Social links

  • Yusuke Kitagawa (Emperor): Available starting June 18 in the Shibuya Underground Walkway; requires Proficiency Rank 2 to initiate.
  • Ann Takamaki (Lovers): Available on open days in Shibuya Underground Mall; requires Kindness Rank 2 to proceed beyond Rank 2.
  • Ryuji Sakamoto (Chariot): Available after school near the 2F classroom or Shibuya arcade; raise him to Rank 7 as soon as possible to unlock Insta-kill.
  • Sadayo Kawakami (Temperance): Available on Friday and Saturday nights via the Leblanc payphone after completing the Operation Maidwatch event and talking to her outside the faculty office.

Worth doing

  • Attend the DVD rental shop in Central Street to rent and watch movies that boost specific social stats in Leblanc.
  • Work at the Triple Seven convenience store in Shibuya on days ending in 7 (June 17 or June 27) to advance the associated Mementos request line.
  • Clean Leblanc or brew coffee on free evenings to gain Kindness or Charm without spending money while generating useful SP-recovery items.

Items

  • Black Rock: Sold at the Stoneon jewelry shop in Kichijoji

Shops and people

  • Untouchable (Airsoft Shop) in Shibuya: Sells upgraded melee weapons, firearms, and protective gear for your party members.
  • Clinic (Tae Takemi) in Yongen-Jaya: Sells medicine and accessories, including the SP Adhesive accessories once your Confidant rank is high enough.

Tips

  • Prioritize leveling Sadayo Kawakami's Temperance Confidant to gain daytime chore services and free up extra night slots.
  • Bring personas matching the Arcana of the Confidants you plan to hang out with to earn extra relationship points for every dialogue choice.
