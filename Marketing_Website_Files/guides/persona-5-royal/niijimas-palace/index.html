Niijima's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · October, the sixth dungeon

Niijima's Palace manifests as a sprawling, high-stakes casino representing the twisted perception of prosecutor Sae Niijima. The Phantom Thieves must navigate rigged casino games, an arena, and high-limit areas to accumulate enough coins to reach the manager's floor. Along the way, the party must solve various security puzzles and expose the casino's fraudulent odds to pave the path to the Treasure.

Items

  • Red Envy Seed MISSABLE: Behind a locked vault door in the Staff Area of the casino
  • Green Envy Seed MISSABLE: On an elevated platform reached by the Grappling Hook in the Slot Room dice maze
  • Blue Envy Seed MISSABLE: High above the Battle Arena behind an elevator shaft accessible via Grappling Hook

Secrets

  • Vent shortcuts inside the Staff Area bypass powerful wandering security shadows and lead straight to backroom terminals.

Tips

  • Equip accessories or Personas that resist or nullify Physical attacks before entering the Battle Arena sequence, as Joker must fight alone.
  • Do not spend time attempting to win the rigged slot machines or dice games normally; search for the maintenance rooms and back corridors to manipulate the odds in your favor.
