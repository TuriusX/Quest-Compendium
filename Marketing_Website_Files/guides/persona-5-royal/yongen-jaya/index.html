Yongen-Jaya

Persona 5 Royal guide · April, the start of the game

Yongen-Jaya is the quiet Tokyo residential neighborhood where the protagonist resides under the care of Sojiro Sakura during the probation period. In April, this serves as the primary home base for unwinding in the evening, performing routine chores, and building initial social stats. Players learn to navigate between the local station, the backstreets, and Cafe Leblanc while unlocking neighborhood facilities.

Items

  • Yon-G-Wash Voucher: on the floor near the laundromat during early exploration

Secrets

  • Chalkboard drawing in the attic of Cafe Leblanc; interact with it early on to find faint doodles left by past visitors.

Shops and people

  • Secondhand Shop Yumenoshima: sells a broken CRT TV and cheap secondhand household goods
  • Supermarket (Super Muramasa): sells cheap food, drink, and cooking ingredients
  • Takemi Medical Clinic: offers healing items, medicine, and initiates Tae Takemi's Confidant
  • Yongen-Jaya Batting Cages: provides a minigame activity to improve Proficiency and earn batting prizes
  • Coin Laundromat: allows washing soiled armor retrieved from Palaces for a small fee

Tips

  • Clean up your Leblanc attic room thoroughly early in April to uncover study space, workout areas, and make room to place home appliances.
  • Inspect the public payphone and neighborhood vending machines regularly for cheap recovery sodas that restore SP.
