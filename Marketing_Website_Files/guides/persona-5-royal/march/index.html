March

Persona 5 Royal guide · The conclusion of the Phantom Thieves' journey

March covers the epilogue and farewells of Persona 5 Royal after resolving the third semester conflicts. You spend your final days saying goodbye to all maxed-out Confidants across Tokyo before returning home. There are no Palace infiltrations, combat encounters, or standard time management slots remaining.

Deadlines

  • March 19 | Say your goodbyes to all maxed Confidants across the city to receive their farewell gifts before heading to Leblanc for the final evening

Don't miss

  • Farewell Gifts | You must manually travel to and interact with every maxed-out Confidant on March 19; skipping any Confidant prevents you from receiving their specific gift for New Game Plus

Items

  • Farewell Gifts MISSABLE: Given by each maxed Confidant upon speaking to them on March 19

Tips

  • Farewell gifts carry over into New Game Plus and unlock some or all of that Confidant's abilities as soon as you re-establish their rank 1 in the next playthrough.
  • Use the fast travel map on March 19 to locate your Confidants easily by checking which map icons show available characters.
