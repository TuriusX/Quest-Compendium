Mementos (Third Semester)

Persona 5 Royal guide · February, the path to the final battle

During the third semester, an entirely new deep section of Mementos named the Path of Da'at opens up beneath the previous lowest depths. The Phantom Thieves must explore this distorted zone to reach its lowest platform to secure the route and prepare for the confrontation with Takuto Maruki. Navigating these floors allows you to collect high-tier stamps, farm ultimate materials, and trigger endgame requests.

Items

  • Cogna-thread MISSABLE: Random glowing search objects throughout the Path of Da'at
  • Blank Card MISSABLE: Random item boxes and search objects throughout the Path of Da'at

Secrets

  • Stamp Platforms | Located at the waiting platform on every floor transition, as well as hidden behind destructible dead-end walls that require driving through them to reveal Jose's stamp podiums

Shops and people

  • Jose: Exchanges accumulated third semester Flowers for high-grade recovery items and cognitive materials, and redeems collected Stamps to alter Da'at's cognition for higher EXP, money, or item drop yields

Tips

  • Since Jose's shop carries end-game skill accessories and rare recovery consumables, max out the item drop rate cognition at his stamp stand first to optimize supply hoarding.
  • Running into Shadows using Morgana's dash (Insta-kill mechanic) works on underleveled enemies here as long as your party level is high enough, making it the most efficient way to grind EXP and Flowers before the final Palace infiltration.
