Madarame's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · May, the second dungeon

The Phantom Thieves infiltrate the museum-themed Palace of the fraudulent artist Ichiryusai Madarame to steal his distorted desires. Yusuke Kitagawa awakens his Persona, Goemon, inside the Palace and officially joins the party. The team must navigate security barriers, solve artistic puzzles, and overcome Madarame's multi-part boss portrait to trigger his change of heart.

Items

  • Red Will Seed MISSABLE: In a hidden room accessed via the Grappling Hook above the exhibition gallery
  • Green Will Seed MISSABLE: Behind an exhaust vent in the Treasure Hall area reached using the Grappling Hook
  • Blue Will Seed MISSABLE: Guarded by a powerful mini-boss behind a laser grid in the upper gallery

Secrets

  • Golden Tsubo treasure demons spawn inside Madarame's Palace and require non-elemental physical critical hits or Lucky Punch to knock down due to having no elemental weaknesses.
  • Security control terminals reveal passcodes for electronic doors, one of which refers to the exact date Madarame took credit for a specific pupil's artwork.

Tips

  • Bring Personas with multi-target elemental spells to efficiently exploit the weaknesses of the four distinct facial portraits during the Madarame boss fight.
  • Fuse or recruit a Persona that knows Lucky Punch, such as Obariyon, to secure knockdowns against Treasure Demons in this Palace.
  • The True Sayuri painting puzzle requires checking the background coloration and the branch details rather than just the figure's posture.
