Yongen-Jaya (The True Ending)

Persona 5 Royal guide · February, the day before departure

On March 19, the day before Joker leaves Tokyo, you visit Yongen-Jaya to say your final goodbyes to local residents and maxed-out confidants. Speaking with characters whose Confidant ranks reached Rank 10 will reward you with special farewell gifts that unlock their perks early in New Game Plus. Once you finish saying farewell to everyone in Yongen-Jaya and around town, you return to Leblanc to end the day and trigger the departure sequence.

Items

  • Coffee Beans MISSABLE: Received automatically from Sojiro Sakura inside Cafe Leblanc if the Hierophant Confidant is Rank 10
  • Recipe Note MISSABLE: Received automatically from Sadayo Kawakami outside Cafe Leblanc if the Temperance Confidant is Rank 10
  • Sakura Wrap MISSABLE: Received automatically from Futaba Sakura inside Cafe Leblanc if the Hermit Confidant is Rank 10

Shops and people

  • Takemi Medical Clinic: Tae Takemi offers farewell dialogue and gives the Dog Tag gift item if the Death Confidant is Rank 10

Tips

  • Do not interact with the front door of Leblanc to go to sleep until you are completely finished visiting everyone across Tokyo, as doing so advances the story and ends the free-roam period permanently.
