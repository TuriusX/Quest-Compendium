February

Persona 5 Royal guide · The final days of the third semester

February contains the final playable days of the third semester storyline, concluding the infiltration of Maruki's Palace. Once the infiltration route is secured and the Calling Card is sent, the calendar rapidly moves forward through story cutscenes leading to the game's ending.

Deadlines

  • February 2 | Secure the infiltration route to the treasure in Maruki's Palace to avoid a bad ending.
  • February 3 | Send the Calling Card and defeat Takuto Maruki in his Palace.

Don't miss

  • True Ending | Reject Maruki's deal whenever prompted on February 2 or February 3; accepting his reality triggers an alternate ending.
  • Third-Tier Persona Evolutions | Reach Rank 10 with phantom thief confidants before February 2 so you can spend a daytime slot hanging out with them in early February to unlock their ultimate Personas.
  • Farewell Gifts | On March 19 (unlocked by completing February successfully), speak to all maxed Confidants across Tokyo to receive items that grant bonuses in New Game Plus.

Worth doing

  • Visit the Jazz Jin in Kichijoji on free evenings to boost party members' stats or teach them specific skills before the final encounter.
  • Work out at the Shibuya gym using imported protein to maximize Joker's HP and SP pools for the final boss battle.

Tips

  • Fuse powerful ultimate Personas like Yoshitsune, Maria, or Futsunushi before February 3 to prepare for the multi-phase final boss battle.
  • Stock up on SP recovery items and status-cleansing items, as the ruler of the final Palace utilizes party-wide debuffs and high-damage multi-elemental attacks.
