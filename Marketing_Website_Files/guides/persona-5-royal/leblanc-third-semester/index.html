Leblanc (Third Semester)

Persona 5 Royal guide · January, the start of the third semester

Leblanc serves as your home base in the third semester starting in January, where reality has noticeably warped around you. You can still use the cafe's facilities to boost social stats, craft infiltration tools, and brew coffee or make curry. Morgana's human form also appears here during the early days of this semester, triggering key story interactions.

Items

  • Overseas Coffee Beans: Received downstairs by inspecting the coffee siphon to brew Master Coffee
  • Phantom Waffle MISSABLE: Left on the small table or bar after certain events in January

Secrets

  • Interacting with the TV downstairs on specific days in January broadcasts unique third-semester news reports reflecting the altered reality.

Tips

  • Use your attic bed at night to sleep or exercise to permanently increase your maximum HP and SP before heading into the new Palace.
  • You can still invite your confidants over or spend time with them downstairs to prep for upcoming Palaces, though prioritizing your party members' third-tier Persona awakenings should take precedence.
