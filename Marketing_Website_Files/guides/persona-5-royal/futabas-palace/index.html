Futaba's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · Strategy for the Pyramid of Wrath dungeon

The Phantom Thieves infiltrate the Pyramid of Wrath to change the heart of Futaba Sakura and cure her shut-in trauma. You must navigate trapped corridors, solve binary and hieroglyph puzzles, and disable ballistas to reach the treasure room. After securing the infiltration route, send the calling card to trigger the aerial boss battle against the cognitive beast.

Deadlines

  • August 21 | Steal Futaba's Heart before this date or face a game over.

Don't miss

  • Red Will Seed | Located behind a grapple point in the Chamber of Rejection before jumping across floating platforms; missable if the Palace is completed without grabbing it.
  • Green Will Seed | Found in the Chamber of Guilt by crawling through a hidden duct near the binary code puzzle; missable if the Palace is completed without grabbing it.
  • Blue Will Seed | Guarded by the mini-boss Decarabia in the Chamber of Sanctuary; missable if the Palace is completed without grabbing it.

Social links

  • Councilor (Takuto Maruki): Available on select school-break days; advance him toward Rank 9 to unlock the third semester later in the game.
  • Justice (Goro Akechi): Hang out with him in Kichijoji in the evenings when available to secure progress toward his Rank 8 requirement.

Worth doing

  • Visit the Jazz Jin club in Kichijoji during the evening with a teammate to raise their stats or level up their Persona.
  • Work part-time at the Crossroads bar in Shinjuku at night to boost Charm and advance Chihaya's or Ohya's storylines.

Items

  • Crystal of Wrath MISSABLE: Formed automatically once all three Will Seeds in the Palace are gathered

Secrets

  • Shoot the giant ballistas located throughout the upper chambers to smash distant stone walls, creating shortcuts and opening paths forward.

Shops and people

  • Jose (Mementos): Trades stat-boosting incense, recovery items, and accessories in exchange for flowers and stamps found in Mementos.

Tips

  • Bring plenty of Relax Gel or items that cure Despair, as the boss and several dungeon enemies use status effects that cause affected party members to die after three turns.
  • Use physical or gun-focused party members for the first phase of the boss fight, because the boss flies out of melee range until knocked down by the ballista.
