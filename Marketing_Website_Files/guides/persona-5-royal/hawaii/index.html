Hawaii

Persona 5 Royal guide · August, the school trip

The protagonist travels to Hawaii with Shujin Academy from September 7th to September 11th for the annual school field trip. During this story-driven sequence, free time is limited to wandering a small beachfront area, shopping for unique gifts, and choosing a companion to spend free time with.

Items

  • Tiki Keychain MISSABLE: Bought at the Hawaiian gift shop
  • Hawaiian Ring MISSABLE: Received during the evening hangout if you choose Ann
  • Hibiscus Bookmark MISSABLE: Received during the evening hangout if you choose Makoto
  • Choco Truffles MISSABLE: Received during the evening hangout if you choose Hifumi
  • Mai Tai Glass MISSABLE: Received during the evening hangout if you choose Kawakami
  • Round Bamboo Shell Pendant MISSABLE: Received during the evening hangout if you hang out with Ryuji

Shops and people

  • Resort Gift Shop: Exclusive accessory souvenirs and Hawaiian regional goods

Tips

  • You must have reached Confidant Rank 5 or higher with female options (such as Makoto, Ann, or Hifumi) prior to the trip to spend the September 10th evening with them.
  • The unique accessories given by your date provide strong stat boosts for this point in the game, so pick the companion whose item best suits your playstyle.
