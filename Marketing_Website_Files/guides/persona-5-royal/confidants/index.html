Confidants

Persona 5 Royal guide · Guide to social links, perks, and availability

Confidants represent your social links in Tokyo, granting combat perks, fusion bonuses, and daily quality-of-life abilities as their ranks increase. Managing your calendar to spend time with the right allies while balancing your five Social Stats is the core loop of daily life. Certain Confidants are mandatory to prioritize if you wish to unlock the third semester story arc.

Deadlines

  • November 17 | Takuto Maruki (Councillor) must reach Rank 9 to unlock the third semester
  • November 17 | Goro Akechi (Justice) must reach Rank 8 to see his full confidant story and unlock additional third semester scenes
  • December 22 | Kasumi Yoshizawa (Faith) must reach Rank 5 to allow her confidant to continue into the third semester

Don't miss

  • Unlocking the third semester Palace and story requires Takuto Maruki to be Rank 9 before he leaves the school on November 18; if missed, the game ends in December.
  • Goro Akechi's Rank 7 and 8 must be completed before November 18, and specific dialogue choices must be selected across ranks 7, 8, and late-game story scenes to unlock his ultimate Persona and post-credits scene.
  • Kasumi Yoshizawa's Faith Confidant caps at Rank 5; failing to reach Rank 5 before late December permanently locks out Ranks 6 through 10.
  • Sadayo Kawakami's Rank 8 request in Mementoes must be cleared to progress her to Rank 10 before November, or you miss out on her post-Palace evening free time ability.
  • Toranosuke Yoshida (Sun) becomes unavailable after mid-November due to election events, requiring you to finish his link early.

Social links

  • Takuto Maruki (Councillor): Available at Shujin Academy practice building on school days after May 13; grants SP restoration and status cure abilities.
  • Sadayo Kawakami (Temperance): Initiate after the maid operation event by using the payphone in Yongen-Jaya; allows you to slack off in class and explore at night after Metaverse visits.
  • Chihaya Mifune (Fortune): Located in Shinjuku at night; pay 100,000 yen for the holy stone to start her quests, granting stat and confidant affinity-boosting readings.
  • Hifumi Togo (Star): Found in the Kanda church during evenings once Charm is high enough; allows tactical mid-battle party swapping.
  • Ryuji Sakamoto (Chariot): Hang out at school or Shibuya arcade; Rank 7 unlocks the Insta-kill ability for fast Mementos grinding.
  • Yuuki Mishima (Moon): Progresses entirely through completing Mementos requests received via text messages; boosts party-wide EXP gain.
  • Toranosuke Yoshida (Sun): Work at the Shibuya beef bowl shop twice to unlock speechmaking with him in Station Square on Sunday nights; boosts negotiation rewards.

Worth doing

  • Visit Chihaya Mifune for an Affinity Reading to gain points with any Confidant without passing game time.
  • Order Sadayo Kawakami's housekeeping service at night to brew coffee, make curry, or craft infiltration tools while you spend time elsewhere.

Items

  • Holy Stone: Purchased from Chihaya Mifune in Shinjuku for 100,000 yen to initiate the Fortune link

Secrets

  • Bringing a Persona of the matching Arcana when hanging out with a Confidant gives a bonus point to almost every positive dialogue choice.

Tips

  • Prioritize Chihaya Mifune to Rank 5 as early as possible so you can use her daily readings to avoid non-ranking hangout sessions.
  • Prioritize Ryuji Sakamoto to Rank 7 early to gain Insta-kill, which allows you to dash directly through lower-level shadows in Mementos for instant EXP, money, and Personas.
  • Do not spend time hanging out with a Confidant if the game warns you that your bond will not deepen yet; use Fortune readings or the Meiji Shrine instead.
