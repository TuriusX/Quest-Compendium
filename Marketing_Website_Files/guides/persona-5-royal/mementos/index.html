Mementos is a sprawling, randomly generated dungeon representing the collective unconscious of the general public. Players explore its various depths by driving the Morgana bus to defeat targeted Shadows for requests, farm experience, collect items, and stamp platforms. New areas of Mementos unlock sequentially as you secure infiltration routes and clear the main story Palaces.

Worth doing
  • Complete multiple side requests (requests received via Mishima's texts or overheard on the street) in a single trip to save calendar time.

Enemies
The Reaper

Spawns if the party lingers on any floor for several minutes without moving between floors; extremely dangerous with high damage and no elemental weaknesses.

Shops and people
  • Jose: Sells recovery items, incense, and accessories in exchange for Flowers; trades Cognition changes (boosted EXP, money, or items) for Stamps

Tips
  • Maximize Ryuji's Chariot Confidant to Rank 7 to obtain the Insta-kill ability, allowing you to instantly defeat lower-level Shadows and gain their EXP, money, and Personas simply by dashing into them with the bus.
  • Flowers disappear entirely whenever you leave Mementos, so spend all accumulated flowers at Jose's shop before returning to the real world.
  • Weather conditions in the real world affect Mementos; visiting during torrential rain or pollen warning days increases rare item spawns, treasure demons, and afflicted enemies.
