Mementos

Persona 5 Royal guide · May, the first visit

The Phantom Thieves make their mandatory first infiltration into Mementos during early May to investigate Tokyo's shared Palace of the public. Morgana transforms into a bus to carry the party through the Qimranut area as they track down their first target, the shadow of Natsuhiko Nakanohara. After defeating the target and learning the basics of Mementos exploration, the team finds their path further down blocked and returns to the real world.

Tips

  • Shoot or slash the glowing red cubes found scattered on the subway tracks to collect free crafting materials and consumable items.
  • Do not linger too long on any single floor of Mementos, or the lethal Reaper will appear and chase you down.
  • Ambushing Shadows while driving the Morgana Car gives your party an immediate advantage turn in battle.
