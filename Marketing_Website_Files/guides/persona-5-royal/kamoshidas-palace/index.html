Kamoshida's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · April, the first dungeon

The protagonist and Ryuji accidentally stumble into a cognitive castle rule by their abusive gym teacher, Suguru Kamoshida, where students are treated as slaves and victims. Alongside Morgana and Ann, they form the Phantom Thieves of Hearts to infiltrate the fortress and secure an infiltration route to the treasure. To change Kamoshida's heart in the real world, the team must steal the physical manifestation of his desires before the school expulsion deadline.

Items

  • Red Lust Seed MISSABLE: Room behind a grapple point in the East Building 3F
  • Green Lust Seed MISSABLE: Behind an elevator shaft accessed from the central tower rafters
  • Blue Lust Seed MISSABLE: Guarded by a powerful shadow behind a painting in the Treasury

Secrets

  • Kamoshida's giant portrait in the Central Tower swings open as a shortcut once unlocked via a hidden switch room.
  • Grappling up into the rafters of the Central Tower allows players to entirely bypass heavily guarded ground hallways.

Shops and people

  • Untouchable: Model weapons, protective armor, and infiltration gear sold by Munehisa Iwai in Shibuya.
  • Takemi Medical Clinic: Restorative healing medicines and SP recovery accessories sold by Tae Takemi in Yongen-Jaya.

Tips

  • Secure the infiltration route early so you have plenty of days left to send the calling card and rank up Confidants.
  • Craft Lockpicks at your home workbench before entering the Palace to open locked treasure chests.
  • Keep a Persona with Ice and Electric skills on Joker to hit the weaknesses of the tough guard captains like Berith and Archangel.
  • Safe Rooms can be used to fast travel across different sections of the castle and completely restore your party's stamina by saving.
  • Collect all three Will Seeds to fuse the Crystal of Lust, which grants the wearer access to the healing spell Diarama.
