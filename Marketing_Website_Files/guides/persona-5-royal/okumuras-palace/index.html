Okumura's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · September, the fifth dungeon

The Phantom Thieves infiltrate the cosmic space station Palace of Kunikazu Okumura to steal his treasure and reform him. You must navigate corporate-themed security zones, solve factory assembly puzzles, and navigate zero-gravity airlock networks. The infiltration culminates in a strict timed battle against successive waves of robotic corporate subordinates.

Items

  • Red Seed of Greed MISSABLE: Facility Passageway area behind an air vent and platforming path
  • Green Seed of Greed MISSABLE: Export Line cargo hold area after navigating moving crane arms
  • Blue Seed of Greed MISSABLE: Central Factory area guarded by an elite enemy before the final elevator

Secrets

  • Airlock puzzle terminal overrides in the Transfer Space allow you to toggle the flow of zero-gravity tubes to access isolated loot platforms.

Tips

  • Prepare multi-target elemental consumables or single-target items to easily execute Baton Pass chains during the final boss wave assault.
  • Equip accessories that grant Haru, Morgana, and Makoto extra single-target elemental coverage to match the boss minions' varying weaknesses.
  • Use the factory arm control terminals to break defective production lines and forge new traversal paths through the industrial sector.
