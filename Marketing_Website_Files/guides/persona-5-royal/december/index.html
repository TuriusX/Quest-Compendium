December

Persona 5 Royal guide · The final confrontation and the path to the true ending

December covers the infiltration and confrontation within Shido's Palace, the end-of-term school exams, and the dive into the Depths of Mementos. Resolving the events at the end of Mementos requires making critical dialogue choices to avoid a bad ending and secure the path toward the third semester. This month represents the final opportunity to complete vanilla Confidants before the timeline shifts into the Royal-exclusive content.

Deadlines

  • December 18 | Secure the treasure route and steal Masayoshi Shido's Heart in his Palace
  • December 24 | Reach the bottom of the Depths of Mementos and defeat the Holy Grail

Don't miss

  • Access to the Third Semester | Secure rank 9 with Takuto Maruki before November 18; on December 24, refuse Igor's / the God of Control's offer to maintain the world as it is
  • Goro Akechi's third-tier Persona and true ending scenes | If Akechi was Rank 8 before November 18, select the dialogue option "I want to keep our promise" when lying in bed on the night of December 24

Worth doing

  • Study in the Leblanc cafe booth or the school library during the first half of the month to prepare for final exams starting on December 20.

Items

  • Soma MISSABLE: Locked treasure chest in the Depths of Mementos

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Masayoshi ShidoMulti-phase boss fight where he shifts between mounted forms, physical powerhouses, and elemental reflect phases; prepare physical shields and party-wide buffs
Holy GrailSever the supply lines immediately when they appear in the first phase, otherwise the boss continuously heals to full health

Tips

  • December 24 features back-to-back dungeon crawling from the Depths of Mementos directly to the Qliphoth World; stock up heavily on SP restoration items and team-revival consumables beforehand.
  • Caroline stands outside the Velvet Room entrance in the Depths of Mementos and fully restores the party's HP and SP for free whenever spoken to.
