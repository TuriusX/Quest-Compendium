August

Persona 5 Royal guide · Summer vacation, beach trip, and Futaba's Palace

August spans the summer vacation period, dedicated primarily to clearing Futaba Sakura's Palace (Pyramid of Wrath) and awaiting her awakening. With no school sessions throughout the month, players have full daytime and evening freedom to secure the treasure route, send the calling card, and advance Confidants. The month concludes with a long stretch of story events leading up to the Phantom Thieves' trip to the beach.

Deadlines

  • August 21 | Steal Futaba's Heart by securing the infiltration route and sending the calling card before this date to avoid a game over.

Don't miss

  • Summer festival invitation | On August 29, a female Confidant may invite you to the festival, or friends will default to hanging out; accept the phone invite immediately if you want the exclusive festival scene and special accessory.
  • Justice Confidant (Goro Akechi) Rank 6 | Available during late August at Leblanc; make sure to advance him when he appears in the cafe in the evenings to keep his Royal-exclusive storyline on track.

Social links

  • Councillor (Takuto Maruki): Available outside the practice building on select days; prioritize reaching Rank 5 before late summer to stay on track for the third semester unlock.
  • Justice (Goro Akechi): Found inside Cafe Leblanc during select evenings in late August; advance to Rank 6 when prompted.
  • Hermit (Futaba Sakura): Unlocks automatically as a story event toward the very end of August, establishing Rank 1 before manual rank-ups become available in September.

Worth doing

  • Work part-time at the Crossroads bar in Shinjuku on Sunday nights to gain Charm and advance toward unlocking the Devil Confidant.
  • Read books in Cafe Leblanc during evenings without leaving the cafe to boost social stats without using up external travel opportunities.
  • Brew coffee or craft infiltration tools at night in Leblanc to raise stats or restock SP-recovery items.

Items

  • Black Stone: Sold at the Stoneon shop in Kichijoji

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Stoneon (Kichijoji): Sells blank accessories and crafting materials, including Black Stones for Persona itemization.
  • Chihaya Mifune (Shinjuku): Offers Affinity and Luck readings to boost daytime and evening Confidant point gains without spending time.

Tips

  • Secure the infiltration route to the treasure early in the month so your daytime party members remain available for afternoon Confidant hangouts.
  • Always carry a Persona matching the Arcana of the Confidant you plan to meet to maximize bond points earned from dialogue choices.
