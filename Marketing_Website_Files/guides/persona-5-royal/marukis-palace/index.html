Maruki's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · Strategy for the final dungeon

This Palace serves as the final infiltration mission exclusive to the third semester, located inside an idyllic, psychological research laboratory. Joker, Akechi, and Sumire join forces with the rest of the Phantom Thieves to secure an infiltration route and steal the treasure. Players must navigate psychological questionnaires, light-refracting cable puzzles, and high-level Shadow encounters to confront Takuto Maruki.

Deadlines

  • February 2 | Send the calling card and steal Maruki's treasure to prevent his altered reality from becoming permanent

Don't miss

  • True Ending | You must reject all of Maruki's offers to accept his false reality on both January 9 and February 2 to remain on the path to the true ending

Social links

  • Faith (Sumire Yoshizawa): Reach rank 5 before the third semester to unlock ranks 6 through 10, then hang out with her during daytime free slots in January

Worth doing

  • Spend daytime slots speaking with Phantom Thief teammates who have maxed Confidants to awaken their powerful third-tier Personas

Items

  • Melancholy Seed (Red): Inside the laboratory monitor room, reach the upper platforms using the grappling hook
  • Melancholy Seed (Green): In the research facilities, climb down into a hidden vent system near the quiz chambers
  • Melancholy Seed (Blue): In the twilight corridor area behind a locked door guarded by an elite Shadow

Secrets

  • Crystal of Melancholy | Collect all three Will Seeds and take the resulting accessory to Jose in Mementos to upgrade it into the Ring of Melancholy

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Jose: Sells recovery items and upgrades the Palace's Will Seed accessory in exchange for flowers and stamps in Mementos

Tips

  • Equip accessories or Personas that resist or nullify Bless and Curse skills, as late-palace mini-bosses and the ruler heavily rely on these elements.
  • Take advantage of Baton Pass chains during the final boss fight to eliminate Maruki's tentacles before targeting Azathoth and Maruki directly.
