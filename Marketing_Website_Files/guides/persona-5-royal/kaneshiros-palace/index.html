Kaneshiro's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · Strategy for the Bank of Gluttony dungeon

The Phantom Thieves infiltrate Junya Kaneshiro's floating bank Palace to stop his extortion of high school students. Makoto Niijima awakens her Persona and joins the party, providing essential Nuclear damage and healing. You must disable security locks, enter PIN codes in the vault, and defeat Shadow Kaneshiro and his Piggytron machine.

Deadlines

  • July 9 | Secure the infiltration route and send the Calling Card to steal Kaneshiro's treasure before Makoto is forced into debt.

Social links

  • Priestess (Makoto Niijima): Unlocks automatically following the Palace infiltration period once Makoto joins the Phantom Thieves; requires Knowledge rank 3 to hang out at Shujin Academy.
  • Justice (Goro Akechi): Available in Kichijoji during evenings; advance this Confidant early to meet the third semester prerequisites.
  • Councilor (Takuto Maruki): Available at Shujin Academy practice building on select days; rank up toward the level 9 requirement needed for third semester access.

Items

  • Bank of Gluttony Will Seed 1: Behind a locked security gate in the Banker Pass area, reachable by grappling up to an open vent
  • Bank of Gluttony Will Seed 2: In the underground vault area, accessible by climbing up pipes and grappling through an elevated opening
  • Bank of Gluttony Will Seed 3: Behind a vault door guarded by a mini-boss in the deepest section of the vault

Secrets

  • The four PIN codes needed in the vault are hidden in scrap notes and spell out English words mapped to keypad numbers: RICH (0102), REAP (0931), HUGE (2319), and GOLD (1841).

Tips

  • Makoto's Persona, Johanna, excels at Nuclear magic; combine it with status effects like Burn, Freeze, or Shock to trigger Technical damage against enemies without standard weaknesses.
  • Save all the torn notebook pages you find around the vault rooms to easily reference the alphanumeric cipher needed to decode the PINs.
