Kaneshiro's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · June, the third dungeon

In this Palace, the Phantom Thieves infiltrate a massive floating bank to steal the distorted desires of the mafia boss Junya Kaneshiro. The team must navigate heavily surveilled office spaces, disable security barriers, and solve code puzzles deep within the underground vault. Makoto Niijima awakens to her Persona here and joins the party to help bring down the corrupt syndicate.

Items

  • Red Will Seed MISSABLE: Inside the Bankers Passageway, found beyond an elevated vent reached using the Grappling Hook
  • Green Will Seed MISSABLE: Inside the Laundering Office, accessed by grappling up to an open balcony after navigating security lasers
  • Blue Will Seed MISSABLE: Inside the Underground Vault, found behind a heavy vault door guarded by a powerful shadow mini-boss

Secrets

  • Destroying floating security cameras and electrical fuse boxes throughout the bank lowers the Palace's security level and disables laser grids blocking major pathways.

Tips

  • Bring Makoto into the active party as her Nuclear skills hit common technical triggers and expose multiple enemies in this dungeon.
  • Memorize the keywords found in scraps of paper within the Vault to convert letters into the numerical keypad codes (RICH, REAP, HUGE, GOLD).
  • During the boss fight against Piggytron, throw expensive consumable items to distract Kaneshiro whenever he starts charging his lethal rolling attack.
