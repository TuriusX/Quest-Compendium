Shibuya (The True Ending)

Persona 5 Royal guide · February, the day before departure

On the day before leaving Tokyo in the True Ending route, the protagonist visits Shibuya to say his final goodbyes to local Confidants and allies. Speaking with maxed Confidants here grants special farewell gifts that unlock powerful abilities immediately upon starting New Game Plus. After finishing your rounds throughout the city, return to Leblanc to proceed to the departure day.

Items

  • Shogi Piece MISSABLE: Received from Hifumi Togo in the church area accessed via the map, though she can also be found at Shibuya Station
  • Cheering Whistle MISSABLE: Received from Toranosuke Yoshida at the Station Square podium if his Confidant rank was maxed
  • Autographed Recipe MISSABLE: Received from Sadayo Kawakami outside the Station Square entrance if her Confidant rank was maxed
  • Gun Muffler MISSABLE: Received from Shinya Oda at the Gigolo Arcade in the Central Street area if his Confidant rank was maxed
  • Tarot Card MISSABLE: Received from Chihaya Mifune in Shinjuku, though her travel prompts are accessible while navigating Tokyo districts

Tips

  • Track down and talk to every single maxed Confidant available in the district before heading home, as missing a conversation forfeits their NG+ perk item entirely.
  • Do not rush through dialogue prompts when exiting Shibuya; confirm you have visited everyone in other districts before ending the day.
