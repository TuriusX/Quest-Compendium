Shido's Palace

Persona 5 Royal guide · December, the seventh dungeon

In Shido's Palace, the Phantom Thieves infiltrate a massive luxury cruise ship sailing across a sunken Tokyo to obtain the politician Masayoshi Shido's heart. To access the bridge and confront Shido, the party must explore the vessel's sprawling decks and VIP facilities to collect five recommendation letters from elite passengers. Progress requires solving navigation puzzles that involve turning into mice to slip through small vents and avoiding watchful guards.

Items

  • Red Envy Seed MISSABLE: Inside a treasure room in the Port Hall, reached by grappling to upper scaffolding
  • Green Envy Seed MISSABLE: Behind an exterior locked chamber on the Starboard Deck, accessible by climbing the ship's outer scaffolding
  • Blue Envy Seed MISSABLE: Deep within the Side Deck area, guarded by an elite Shadows miniboss

Secrets

  • Returning to the rest lounge bartender after obtaining the Restaurant VIP's letter triggers a dialogue that rewards the party with special recovery drinks.

Tips

  • While transformed into mice by the golden statues, combat is forbidden and getting caught by Shadows immediately triggers a battle with disadvantage, so stay strictly to the vents.
  • Equipping gear or accessories that nullify Fear and Despair will significantly reduce the danger posed by the cognitive mini-bosses holding the recommendation letters.
  • Bring Personas with diverse magical affinities or high-tier Almighty spells, as several mini-bosses in this Palace lack elemental weaknesses.
