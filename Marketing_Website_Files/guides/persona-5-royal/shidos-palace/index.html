The Phantom Thieves infiltrate Masayoshi Shido's Palace, which takes the form of a massive cruise ship sailing through a sunken Tokyo. To open the route to the main assembly hall, you must navigate multiple passenger decks and acquire five recommendation letters by defeating key VIP figures. Once the infiltration route is secured, send the calling card and return to confront Shido.

Don't miss 0/5

Deadlines
  • December 17 | Send the calling card and secure Shido's treasure before the deadline to avoid a game over.

Social links

0/1

Worth doing
  • Work on lingering Confidants in Tokyo | Prioritize party member Confidants to unlock their second-tier Personas before the final confrontation.
  • Visit the Velvet Room for fusion and itemization | Craft high-tier Personas with strong single-target skills and passive defensive buffs to prepare for the five VIP sub-bosses and the multi-phase final boss.

Secrets

0/2

Enemies
Baphomet
Weak to Bless

Uses heavy Fire and Curse magic; clear quickly using Bless attacks.

Kali
Weak to Gun

High physical attack power; knock down with Gun skills to avoid severe party damage.

Cerberus
Weak to Ice

Deals heavy Fire damage; vulnerable to Ice skills.

King Frost

Absorbs Ice; relies on heavy Ice spells and physical attacks.

Surt
Weak to Ice

Absorbs Fire; hit its Ice weakness to trigger an All-Out Attack.

Dakini

Has no elemental weaknesses; use technicals or high-crit physical skills to knock down.

Tips
  • Transformation switches toggle the ship's mouse traps, so plan your route to hit switches only when you need to walk through doors as humans.
  • As mice, you cannot fight and will be instantly ambushed if caught by patrolling shadows, so use cheese scents and cover to sneak past them.
  • The battle against Shido consists of multiple phases where his affinities and attack styles change completely, so carry Personas that resist physical and all elements.
  • Keep Dekaja and Matarunda ready during the duel against Akechi to negate his powerful stat buffs and lower his high damage output.
