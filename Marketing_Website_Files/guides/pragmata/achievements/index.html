Points of no return

  • Launching the escape craft in Sector 06: Central Port: Free-roam story campaign progression locks until post-story; however, all missed areas, collectibles, and combat challenges remain fully accessible post-game via Unknown Signal Mode, so no achievements become permanently impossible.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1Play through the main story on Normal difficulty, exploring Sector 01

    Solar Power Plant, Sector 02: Mass Production Array, Sector 03: Terra Dome, Sector 04: Lunum Mines, and Sector 06: Central Port while interacting with Diana at every visit to the Shelter.

  2. Step 2

    Use the post-story Unknown Signal Mode to revisit all five sectors via Escape Hatches, finishing all combat challenges, simulator tasks, and cleaning up any remaining collectibles.

  3. Step 3

    Start a fresh New Game run on Lunatic difficulty and complete the campaign to finish the 100% list.
