Guides / FINAL FANTASY VI

Thamasa

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The party investigates the secluded village of magic users to track down the missing Espers.

Terra and Locke travel to this isolated settlement in search of the missing Espers, only to discover that the villagers are descendants of the Mage Warriors who conceal their magical heritage. After meeting Strago and Relm, the heroes must rescue Relm from a blazing mansion with Strago's help before traveling toward the nearby mountains to find the Espers. Peace talks conducted later in the village are violently disrupted when Kefka attacks the town, kills General Leo, and converts the arriving Espers into Magicite.

Items

  • Eye Drops: inside a barrel situated next to the eastern outside wall of the Item Shop
  • Gold Needle: inside a barrel directly beside the exterior of the Relic Shop
  • Echo Screen: inside a barrel sitting outside the village elder's residence in the northwest
  • Green Cherry: inside an outdoor barrel standing next to a home in the northeastern area
  • Phoenix Down: inside an exterior barrel located to the right side of the inn
  • Memento Ring: hidden within the wall space next to the table on the second floor of Strago's house
  • Flame Rod MISSABLE: inside a chest accessed through a right-side doorway within the Burning Home
  • Ice Rod MISSABLE: inside a chest located through the second right-side doorway inside the Burning Home

Secrets

  • Inspecting the blank bedroom wall to the left of the table upstairs in Strago's house reveals an invisible cache containing a Memento Ring.
  • Speaking with Strago and Relm causes the innkeeper to lower the nightly lodging fee from an exorbitant 1,500 gil down to a single gil.

Tips

  • Buy an Ice Rod at the local weapon shop and snap it as an item in battle to exploit Flame Eater's Ice weakness for overwhelming burst damage.
  • Remove all equipped Espers and Relics from Shadow prior to sleeping at the inn, as he slips away from the group once the fire begins.
  • Equip spellcasters like Terra or Celes with the Mystery Veil available at the armor shop to enjoy substantial defensive and magic stat enhancements.
