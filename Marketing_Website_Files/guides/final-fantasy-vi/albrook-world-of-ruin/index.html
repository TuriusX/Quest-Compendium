Albrook (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Celes travels to the mainland

After arriving from the Solitary Island on a makeshift raft, Celes reaches Albrook, the first mainland port town visited in the World of Ruin. The surviving residents live under Kefka's tyrannical rule and the threat of the Light of Judgment, offering crucial hints about a wandering martial artist (Sabin) seen heading toward Tzen. Players can explore the devastated town to gather rumors, unearth overlooked items, and purchase upgraded weapons, armor, and provisions before journeying onward.

Items

  • Hi-Potion: barrel situated directly next to the entrance of the Inn
  • Ether: large pot located inside the Weapons Shop
  • Elixir: wall clock inside the room connecting the Pub and Relic Shop
  • Teleport Stone: crate on the docks near the port entrance

Secrets

  • The two treasure chests placed inside the Armor Shop remain permanently empty throughout the entirety of the game and contain no items.

Shops and people

  • Weapons Shop: Flametongue (7000 Gil), Icebrand (7000 Gil), Thunder Blade (7000 Gil)
  • Armor Shop: Golden Shield (2500 Gil), Priest's Miter (3000 Gil), Green Beret (3000 Gil), Golden Helm (4000 Gil), Golden Armor (10000 Gil)
  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion (300 Gil), Ether (1500 Gil), Phoenix Down (500 Gil), Holy Water (300 Gil), Remedy (1000 Gil), Sleeping Bag (500 Gil), Smoke Bomb (300 Gil), Teleport Stone (700 Gil)
  • Relic Shop: Sprint Shoes (1500 Gil), Gigas Glove (5000 Gil), Earring (5000 Gil), Barrier Ring (500 Gil), Mythril Glove (700 Gil), Knight's Code (1000 Gil), Reflect Ring (6000 Gil), Jeweled Ring (1000 Gil)
  • Inn: Rest and full recovery (300 Gil)

Tips

  • Restock on consumable supplies and consider picking up elemental swords or defensive gear before traveling north to Tzen, as Celes must navigate the wilderness without any companions.
