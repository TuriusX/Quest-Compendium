Ancient Castle

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Exploring the ruins beneath Figaro

The Ancient Castle is an optional dungeon buried deep beneath the sands, accessible by intentionally stopping Figaro Castle midway through its journey under the desert. Exploring the ruined stronghold leads to potent equipment, a dragon boss battle, and the petrified remains of an ancient monarch and queen. Investigating the throne room and the hidden chambers beneath rewards players with the Odin magicite, which can optionally be upgraded into Raiden.

Items

  • Wing Edge: Chest in the small treasure room off the main courtyard
  • Punisher: Chest in the eastern courtyard area
  • Blizzard Orb: Chest in the castle interior hallway
  • Gold Hairpin: Chest in the interior room leading toward the throne
  • Master's Scroll MISSABLE: Dropped upon defeating the Blue Dragon in the courtyard library
  • Odin Magicite: Interact with the petrified king sitting on the main throne
  • Raiden Magicite MISSABLE: Examine the petrified queen in the hidden basement beneath the castle, which permanently transforms the Odin magicite

Secrets

  • Step five paces south from the right-hand throne in the throne room and press the confirm button to reveal a hidden staircase leading down to the queen's sanctuary.
  • Reading the diary in the queen's underground bedchamber reveals the backstory of the war a thousand years ago and hints at the location of her hidden tear.

Tips

  • Equip Water-nullifying or Water-absorbing gear such as the Paladin's Shield or Force Shield before engaging the Blue Dragon.
  • Consider keeping the Odin magicite for a while before upgrading it to Raiden, as Odin is the only magicite that provides a +1 Speed stat boost on level up.
