Tzen

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Celes arrives just as Kefka strikes the town with the Light of Judgment and finds Sabin.

Upon arriving in Tzen in the World of Ruin, Kefka strikes the town with the Light of Judgment, damaging a mansion and trapping a young child inside. Celes discovers Sabin physically holding up the collapsing building and must rush through the monster-infested interior to rescue the child within a six-minute time limit. Once Celes safely flees the structure with the child, the house collapses completely and Sabin officially rejoins the party.

Items

  • Healing Rod MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest at the northern end of the hallway on the 1st floor of the collapsing house
  • Ether MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest at the far western dead end on the 1st floor of the collapsing house
  • Holy Rod MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest within the center bedroom on the 1st floor of the collapsing house
  • Hyper Wrist MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest in the southwestern room on the 1st floor of the collapsing house
  • Magicite Shard MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest in the northeastern section of the collapsing house basement
  • Blood Sword MISSABLE: Inside a treasure chest in the southernmost room of the collapsing house basement

Secrets

  • Seraph Magicite can be obtained by navigating behind the Relic Shop into the northeastern tree line to speak with a hidden scoundrel, who offers the Magicite for just 10 Gil if it was not purchased during the World of Balance.

Shops and people

  • Inn: Free lodging and full party restoration during the crisis (350 Gil once the house collapses)
  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion, Ether, Green Cherry, Phoenix Down, Echo Screen, Holy Water, Sleeping Bag, and Super Ball
  • Weapon Shop: Kaiser Knuckles, Venom Claws, Flametongue, Icebrand, Thunder Blade, and Burning Fist
  • Armor Shop: Golden Shield, Beret, Tiger Mask, Golden Helm, Power Sash, and Golden Armor
  • Relic Shop: Dragoon Boots, Thief's Bracer, Black Belt, Alarm Earring, Sniper Eye, Peace Ring, Jeweled Ring, and Amulet
  • Shady Man behind Relic Shop: Seraph Magicite for 10 Gil (if not previously bought for 3,000 Gil)

Tips

  • Equip Celes with a Ribbon or a Jeweled Ring before stepping into the house, as petrification results in an instant Game Over when solo.
  • Sleep at the Inn for free prior to talking with Sabin to enter the burning mansion with maximum HP and MP.
  • Equip Sprint Shoes to move quickly through the mansion and escape from random encounters if the six-minute timer begins running low.
  • Exploit the Undead nature of the Nightwalkers by targeting them with a Phoenix Down or casting Fire spells to wipe them out instantly.
