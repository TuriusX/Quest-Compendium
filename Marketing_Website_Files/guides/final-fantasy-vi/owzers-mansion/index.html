Owzer's Mansion

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · investigating the haunted art gallery in Jidoor to recruit Relm

In the World of Ruin, the party investigates an affluent manor in Jidoor that has been shrouded in darkness after its owner commissioned Relm to paint a portrait. Players must navigate an eerie basement dungeon populated by aggressive paintings and floating treasure chests before reaching the studio. Defeating the evil entity Chadarnook expels the evil spirit possessing the painting, allowing Relm to rejoin the group and providing the Lakshmi magicite.

Items

  • Lakshmi Magicite: inside the bookcase in Owzer's art studio after defeating Chadarnook

Secrets

  • Overcome the stairway repulsion ward by reading the diary and turning on the lamp switch beside the entrance stairs.
  • Collect the Emperor's Letter clue pointing toward Phoenix Cave by inspecting Emperor Gestahl's portrait two times.
  • Reveal the secret doorway down to the basement by examining the portrait of the woman in the gallery and defeating the Mistys.
  • Reach the secluded Lich Ring chest by moving into the alcove tucked directly behind the three alternating doors.
  • Bring down each hovering treasure chest by walking straight under its shadow to defeat an ambush.
  • Create the final passage to the save point by interacting with the armored painting and defeating Still Life.

Tips

  • Cease all offensive actions whenever Chadarnook transitions into the Goddess form, waiting for the Demon form to reappear before striking with Fire or Holy.
  • Cast Vanish over your party members upon entering the basement to render them immune to the frequent physical attacks thrown by Blade Dancers.
  • Bring Locke along to steal rare animal suits such as the Nutkin Suit, Moogle Suit, and Chocobo Suit from the enemies residing here.
