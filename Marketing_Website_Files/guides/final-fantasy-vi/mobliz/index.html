Mobliz

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Celes and Sabin locate Terra living underground to protect war orphans after the cataclysm.

Following the cataclysm, Celes and Sabin find that the settlement of Mobliz has been reduced to rubble, with surviving orphans living in an underground shelter cared for by Terra. Players must protect the children by driving off the rampaging monster Humbaba and eventually return with an airship to follow a hidden pathway to recruit Terra back into the party.

Items

  • Elixir: inside the grandfather clock on the upper level of the carrier pigeon post office
  • Tintinnabulum MISSABLE: received from the injured soldier upon sending five consecutive deliveries to Lola
  • Phoenix Down: inside the outdoor barrel sitting beside the ruins of the western house
  • Hi-Ether: inside an urn tucked away in the concealed subterranean room where Katarin rests
  • Fenrir: magicite given by a child after defeating Humbaba during the first invasion in the World of Ruin

Secrets

  • A hidden passageway located behind the bookcase in the western home, found by following the pet dog into the room.
  • A free resting bed located in the back room of the former relic shop, allowing party recovery without an inn fee.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
HumbabaPoisonCasts potent lightning spells, attacks with Solar Plexus, and uses Humbaba Breath to eject party members from combat before Terra enters.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Dried Meat, Potion, Hi-Potion, Eye Drops, Green Cherry, Phoenix Down, Sleeping Bag, Tent
  • Weapon Shop: Mythril Knife, Mythril Sword, Mythril Claws, Kotetsu
  • Armor Shop: Buckler, Heavy Shield, Plumed Hat, Magus Hat, Bandana, Iron Helm, Kenpo Gi, Iron Armor

Tips

  • Purchase several pieces of Dried Meat from the local shop early on so you can feed and recruit Gau once you encounter him on the Veldt.
  • Equip lightning-absorbing or resistant armor before facing Humbaba, and target the creature with Poison-element spells like Bio to exploit its primary weakness.
