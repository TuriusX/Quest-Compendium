Colosseum

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Winning items and recruiting Shadow

The Dragon's Neck Colosseum in the World of Ruin allows you to wager items to fight randomized single combatants for upgraded gear or rare relics. A single party member is chosen to battle automatically without direct player control. Wagering the Ichigeki (Striker) dagger here is also the required step to face and permanently recruit Shadow if he survived the Floating Continent.

Shops and people

  • Chief Chupon: Wager items to initiate one-on-one battles in the arena

Tips

  • Party members fight automatically in the Colosseum, so remove useless magic spells or equip them with relics like the Berserker Ring or Genji Glove to optimize their basic attacks.
  • If you save your game right outside the Colosseum on the airship or world map, you can safely reload if you lose a valuable wagered item.
