Darill's Tomb

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Setzer leads the party into his late friend's crypt to restore and raise the airship Falcon.

Setzer leads the party into this subterranean crypt to retrieve his departed friend's buried airship, the Falcon. Players must explore flooded corridors, trigger switches to raise water levels, and navigate past traps and undead monstrosities. Slaying the chariot-mounted boss Dullahan opens the passage to the underground hangar, allowing the party to take flight and travel the World of Ruin freely.

Items

  • Genji Helm: inside a treasure chest located in the southeastern chamber of the second basement floor (B2)
  • Crystal Mail: inside a treasure chest reached through the southwestern doorway on the second basement floor (B2)
  • Regal Gown: inside a treasure chest at the foot of the southwestern stairs on the third basement floor (B3)
  • Growth Egg: inside a treasure chest located within a hidden chamber accessed through the eastern wall of the third basement floor (B3)
  • Dragon Claws: dropped by the Angler Whelk inside the monster-in-a-box chest north of the save point
  • Man-Eater: inside a treasure chest situated to the east in the chamber right before the boss's room

Secrets

  • Concealed corridor to the Growth Egg | Walk directly right through a hidden opening in the eastern wall just south of the Regal Gown chest on the third basement floor (B3).

Tips

  • Equip the party with Amulets or Ribbons before entering to prevent the frequent zombification inflicted by the tomb's undead enemies.
  • Unequip Setzer's Viper Darts before fighting in this dungeon, as their instant-death proc will fully heal undead targets.
  • Have Celes use her Runic command consistently during the Dullahan encounter to absorb harmful spells such as Blizzaga and Holy.
  • Defeat both the head and shell of Angler Whelk at the exact same moment to obtain two pairs of Dragon Claws instead of just one.
