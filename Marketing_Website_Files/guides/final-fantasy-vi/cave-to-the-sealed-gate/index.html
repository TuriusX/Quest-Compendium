Cave to the Sealed Gate

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The heroes navigate subterranean magma passages to reach the gate dividing the two worlds.

The Returners navigate magma-filled underground tunnels past the Imperial Observation Post to locate the gateway between the human world and the Esper realm. The party must solve bridge and switch puzzles across lava pits while fighting off dangerous undead creatures to reach the inner sanctum. At the gate, Terra reaches out to communicate with the Espers, triggering an uncontrolled escape of enraged magical beings into the world.

Secrets

  • Hidden floor items buried under the grand staircase on B3F, revealed through dialogue after beating the Ninja summoned by pulling the right wall lever.
  • A secret treasure room containing the Ultima Weapon and Magicite Shards, revealed by activating floor pressure switches across the lower B3F pathways.
  • A pathway extension to the western Genji Glove chest that appears when activating a switch hidden in the middle of a bridge on B3F.

Tips

  • Equipping Gaia Gear on party members converts the lethal Lifeshaver attack from Outcasts into party healing.
  • Terra must be present in your party, or the game will refuse to let you enter the cave.
  • Use Phoenix Downs or the Raise spell to instantly eliminate undead enemies like the Zombie Dragon and Outcast.
  • Loot every treasure and ground item during your excursion, as the cave becomes permanently inaccessible once the Floating Continent ascends.
