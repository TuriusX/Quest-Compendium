Guides / FINAL FANTASY VI

Magitek Research Facility

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The team finds imprisoned Espers drained for power and receives their Magicite before fleeing.

The party infiltrates the Empire's laboratory in Vector to discover that Espers are being held in vats and drained of their life force to fuel Magitek weapons. After conquering the guardian Number 024, the heroes witness the dying Espers transform into Magicite, prompting Celes to sacrifice her standing to help the others escape Kefka. Guided by Cid, the remaining heroes flee through the facility's railway on a mine cart while battling pursuing imperial contraptions.

Items

  • Stoneblade MISSABLE: concealed in an obscure alcove behind the wall directly south of the leftmost Esper containment tank

Secrets

  • A concealed treasure chest holding a Stoneblade can be found by walking into the dark wall space directly below the westernmost Esper tube.

Tips

  • Remove all equipment and relics from Celes before activating the switch in the Esper chamber, as she departs from the party immediately after the ensuing scene.
  • Cast Sleep or Imp on Number 024 to lock it down completely and prevent it from altering elemental attributes with Barrier Change.
