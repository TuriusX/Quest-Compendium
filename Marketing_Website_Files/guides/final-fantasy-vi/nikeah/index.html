Guides / FINAL FANTASY VI

Nikeah

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Sabin, Cyan, and Gau arrive at the port town to board a ship across the sea.

After navigating the currents of the Serpent Trench, Sabin, Cyan, and Gau wash ashore at the port town of Nikeah to rest and replenish their supplies. Players can browse multiple market stalls for fresh equipment, rest up, and speak with townsfolk at the pub for local rumors. Once ready to proceed, talking to the sailor waiting by the ship at the harbor allows the group to set sail across the sea toward South Figaro.

Items

  • Elixir: Inside the grandfather clock in the northeast room of the inn

Secrets

  • A comedic interaction involving a flustered Cyan can be activated by talking to the dancer standing inside the pub during Sabin's scenario.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Mythril Claw, Kotetsu, and Mythril Spear
  • Armor Shop: Heavy Shield, Plumed Hat, Magus Hat, Bandana, Iron Helm, Kenpo Gi, Silk Robe, and Iron Armor
  • Item Shop: Potion, Hi-Potion, Echo Screen, Smoke Bomb, Green Cherry, Phoenix Down, Sleeping Bag, and Tent
  • Relic Shop: Silver Spectacles, Star Pendant, Fairy Ring, and White Cape
  • Innkeeper: Overnight stay to fully restore HP and MP for 150 Gil
  • Chocobo Stable: Chocobo rental for 80 Gil

Tips

  • Buy a spare Mythril Spear from the weapon stall so Edgar will have an upgraded weapon ready when the parties reunite at Narshe.
