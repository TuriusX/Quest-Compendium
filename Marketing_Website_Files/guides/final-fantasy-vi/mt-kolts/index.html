Mt. Kolts

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · While traversing the mountain route, where the party defeats Vargas and Sabin joins ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Terra, Edgar, and Locke journey through the trails and caverns of Mt. Kolts to reach the Returners' hideout. Along the mountain route, a mysterious shadow follows the team before confronting them as Duncan's rogue pupil, Vargas. Players must battle Vargas and his beasts until Sabin arrives to eliminate him with a martial arts Blitz technique and join the group.

Enemies
Zaghrem
Weak to IceSteal / drop Bandana

A martial artist appearing inside the caves who attacks with swift physical jumping strikes.

Trillium
Weak to FireSteal / drop Potion, Remedy

A hostile flora found on the open mountain slopes that inflicts poison with Poison Touch.

Gorgias
Weak to FireSteal / drop Potion, Hi-Potion

A charging boar enemy in the caverns that deals heavy single-target gore damage and uses the group-hitting Snowstorm.

Cirpius
Steal / drop Antidote, Potion

A flying bird found along exterior pathways whose Rage ability can grant Hastega.

Ipooh
Weak to FireSteal / drop Hi-Potion

A pair of wild bears guarding Vargas at the beginning of the boss encounter.

Vargas
Weak to PoisonSteal / drop Potion, Mythril Claws

A martial arts master who knocks the party away with Blizzard Fist, requiring Sabin to defeat him in a one-on-one duel.

Tips
  • Use Edgar's Auto Crossbow continuously throughout Mt. Kolts to eliminate full waves of monsters without draining MP.
  • Attempt to steal from Zaghrem encounters in the caves to pick up Bandana headgear early for Locke and Terra.
  • Keep Antidotes on hand or cast Terra's Poisona promptly to prevent lingering poison inflicted by Trillium enemies from depleting party health between battles.
  • When Sabin takes over the duel against Vargas, select Blitz and input Left, Right, Left to execute Raging Fist and finish the battle immediately.
