South Figaro Cave (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · traversing subterranean tunnels to reach the submerged castle

After pursuing the Crimson Robbers and their leader Gerad from South Figaro, the party enters this cavern to reach Figaro Castle, which became trapped beneath the desert sands during the cataclysm. By watching the thieves use food to position a resident turtle as a stepping stone across the recovery pool, the group uncovers an unmapped passage. Navigating these underground tunnels leads straight into the prison cells of the submerged castle.

Items

  • Hero's Ring MISSABLE: inside a chest located down the western stairs of the main cavern, provided the chest was left untouched in the World of Balance
  • Hi-Ether MISSABLE: inside a chest nestled in a far eastern alcove of the central cavern, provided the chest was left unopened in the World of Balance
  • X-Potion MISSABLE: inside a chest atop the elevated platform in the southwestern corner of the cavern, provided the chest was left unopened in earlier visits

Tips

  • Protect your party with Peace Rings or Ribbons, as several enemies lurking in these tunnels frequently inflict the Confuse status.
  • Sabin's fire-based Blitz technique, Rising Phoenix, swiftly dispatches packs of undead foes such as Humpty and Cruller.
