Triangle Island

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Being swallowed by the Zone Eater

Triangle Island in the World of Ruin is home to the Zone Eater monster, which swallows the entire party into an underground cavern when encountered. Navigating the belly of the beast requires jumping across bridges and avoiding green-cloaked roaming figures that knock the party down into the sub-levels. Reaching the deepest chamber allows the party to recruit the mimic Gogo.

Items

  • Magical Brush: Inside a treasure chest on the upper bridge walkway
  • Fake Mustache: Inside a treasure chest reached by dropping through the floor from the bridges above
  • Zephyr Cloak: Inside a treasure chest along the bridge pathways
  • Hero's Ring: Inside a treasure chest on the bridge pathways
  • Pinwheel: Inside a treasure chest on the bridge pathways
  • Thunder Shield: Inside a treasure chest on the bridge pathways
  • Red Jacket: Inside a treasure chest on the bridge pathways

Secrets

  • Reaching the end of the dungeon requires intentionally getting knocked off the moving bridges by the walking green-cloaked men into a pit below, granting access to the path leading to Gogo's room.

Tips

  • Do not defeat the Zone Eater on the surface; simply defend and let it use Inhale on each party member until the entire group is swallowed to enter the dungeon.
  • Equip Sprint Shoes to make jumping between the moving platforms and dodging the walking figures much easier.
  • If you step on the save point inside, you can cast the Warp spell or use a Warp Stone to return to the surface of Triangle Island at any time.
