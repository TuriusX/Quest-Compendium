In the World of Ruin, navigating to Triangle Island and deliberately allowing the Zone Eater monster to inhale every party member transports you inside its subterranean belly. Players must dodge roaming green men along narrow bridges and sprint beneath a crushing ceiling trap to explore the caves. At the deepest point of the dungeon, speaking to the cloaked figure will recruit the mimic Gogo to the party.

Enemies
Zone Eater

Inhales party members individually; letting it devour the entire team enters the dungeon instead of causing a Game Over.

Amduscias
Weak to Poison

Casts Booty Shake to inflict Confuse and can counter the Throw ability by throwing items back.

Baalzephon
Weak to Fire

Absorbs every element other than fire and appears as an uncommon encounter.

Covert
Weak to Holy

Powerful ninja-type enemy that counters Throw attacks.

Kamui
Weak to Lightning, PoisonSteal / drop Holy Water

Can afflict characters with the Zombie status via Zombie Touch.

Shambling Corpse
Weak to Fire, Holy

Undead foe that drains vitality using the Life Shaver attack.

Wartpuck
Weak to Fire

Common monster lurking throughout the belly chambers.

Tips
  • In the crushing ceiling corridor, keep moving forward between drops and immediately duck into chest openings or floor crevices to avoid being instantly wiped out.
