Narshe (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Recruiting Mog and Umaro

The mining town of Narshe has been completely abandoned and overrun with dangerous monsters in the World of Ruin. Players can navigate the mines through Arvis's residence to find Mog waiting in the Moogle cave, who then offers to help recruit his companion Umaro. Venturing beyond the snowfields and leaping into the Yeti's Cave leads to a battle against Umaro, who joins the party when ordered by Mog.

Items

  • Molulu's Charm: Examined on the wall spot where Mog was standing in the Moogle Cave
  • Midgardsormr: Carved inside the bone carving in Umaro's Cave
  • Gauntlet: Chest accessed via a pitfall in Umaro's Cave
  • Cursed Shield: Given by a sick man in the house north of the Relic Shop (opened by Locke)
  • Ragnarok: Received as either magicite or a sword from the weapon shopkeeper behind the locked counter

Secrets

  • Searching the exact wall location Mog stood in front of reveals Molulu's Charm, an accessory that stops all random monster encounters.

Tips

  • Ensure Mog is active in the party when speaking to Umaro following the boss fight, otherwise Umaro will not join.
