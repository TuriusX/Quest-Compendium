Narshe (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Recruiting Mog and Umaro ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

The mining town of Narshe has been completely abandoned and overrun with dangerous monsters in the World of Ruin. Players can navigate the mines through Arvis's residence to find Mog waiting in the Moogle cave, who then offers to help recruit his companion Umaro. Venturing beyond the snowfields and leaping into the Yeti's Cave leads to a battle against Umaro, who joins the party when ordered by Mog.
