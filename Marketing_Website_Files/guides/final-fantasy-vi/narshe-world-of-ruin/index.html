Narshe (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Recruiting Mog and Umaro

In the World of Ruin, Narshe is frozen over, abandoned by its townsfolk, and infested with monsters. Locke is required to pick the locked doors in town to access hidden houses and the weapon shop's treasures. You can recruit Mog in his former home, defeat the frozen Esper Valigarmanda on the mountain cliff, and venture into the northern mines to battle and recruit the yeti Umaro.

Items

  • Moogle Charm: Mog's living space in the Arvis/Moogle cave, examine the wall where Mog was standing
  • Ragnarok (Sword or Magicite) MISSABLE: Weapon shop back room, choose between the magicite shard or the blade from the elderly smith
  • Cursed Shield: House with a sick man in the lower-right section of town, talk to the bedridden man

Secrets

  • Blood Sword | Enter the hidden passage in the back of the item shop after unlocking it with Locke
  • Paladin's Shield | Fight and survive 256 battles with the Cursed Shield equipped to break its curse and transform it into the strongest shield in the game

Enemies

Tips

  • Bringing Mog to the Yeti's Cave is strictly required to recruit Umaro after defeating him; without Mog, Umaro will not join the party.
  • Equipping the Moogle Charm on Mog disables all random encounters, allowing you to explore Narshe, open chests, and bypass dangerous mine enemies with zero danger.
  • The Cursed Shield inflicts multiple status ailments and elemental weaknesses on the user; mitigate this by equipping a Ribbon and using characters who can rely on Magic or Tools.
