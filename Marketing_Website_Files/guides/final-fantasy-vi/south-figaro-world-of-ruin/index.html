South Figaro (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Searching for party members

After arriving on the mainland in the World of Ruin, Celes and her companions visit South Figaro to pick up the trail of their lost allies. The town is largely occupied by traveling thieves and refugees, and Gerad—a man bearing an uncanny resemblance to Edgar—can be found staying at the local inn. Speaking with Gerad and following him to the docks allows the party to secure passage to Figaro Cave, continuing the search for Edgar.

Shops and people

  • South Figaro Inn: 1 GP to rest and recover HP/MP
  • South Figaro Weapon Shop: Trident, Morning Star, Boomerang, Poison Rod, Fire Rod, Ice Rod, Thunder Rod
  • South Figaro Armor Shop: Gold Shield, Gold Helm, Gold Armor, Power Sash
  • South Figaro Item Shop: Potion, Hi-Potion, Revivify, Remedy, Phoenix Down, Sleeping Bag, Tent

Tips

  • Talk to the thief leader named Gerad on the second floor of the inn to trigger the next step in finding Edgar and recovering Figaro Castle.
  • Do not leave town without visiting the docks; boarding the ferry behind Gerad is required to reach Figaro Cave and reunite with Edgar.
