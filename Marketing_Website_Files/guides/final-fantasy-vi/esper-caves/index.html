Guided by Strago, the party traverses the cavernous paths and cliffside slopes of the western mountains near Thamasa to track down the refugee Espers and negotiate peace. Along the way, the group discovers ancient statues honoring the Warring Triad and must repel another ambush by Ultros with the help of Relm. After navigating a series of floor pitfalls, the party reaches the inner sanctum where the Espers agree to terms of reconciliation.

Enemies
Adamankary
Steal / drop Golden Shield

High physical defense reptile found in the caves that can eject party members with Snort.

Ultros
Weak to Fire, LightningSteal / drop White Cape

Recurring boss who ambushes the party at the statues and uses abilities like Magnitude 8 and Aqua Breath.

Tips
  • Hold off on quickly defeating Venobennu packs on the mountain slopes so Strago can witness and learn the White Wind Lore.
  • Avoid standard physical strikes against Adamankary due to its towering defense; eliminate it quickly using tier-2 magic spells instead.
