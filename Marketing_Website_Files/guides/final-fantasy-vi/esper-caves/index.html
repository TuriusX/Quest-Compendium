Guides / FINAL FANTASY VI

Esper Caves

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Guided by Strago, the group treks into the western mountain caverns to locate the Espers and negotiate peace.

Guided by Strago, the party traverses the cavernous paths and cliffside slopes of the western mountains near Thamasa to track down the refugee Espers and negotiate peace. Along the way, the group discovers ancient statues honoring the Warring Triad and must repel another ambush by Ultros with the help of Relm. After navigating a series of floor pitfalls, the party reaches the inner sanctum where the Espers agree to terms of reconciliation.

Items

  • Healing Rod MISSABLE: In a treasure chest located directly west of the main cave entrance
  • Chocobo Suit MISSABLE: In an outdoor chest on the northern mountain cliffs, reached by dropping through the northern trapdoor and heading north
  • Tabby Suit MISSABLE: In an outdoor chest reached by falling through the northern trapdoor and exiting through the southwestern doorway
  • X-Potion MISSABLE: In a chest inside a lower chamber reached by stepping through the eastern trapdoor

Secrets

  • Concealed rock tunnel | In the first cavern chamber, players can bypass the dead-end western loop by walking into a partially obscured corridor cut into the eastern wall to access the mountain slopes.

Enemies

Tips

  • Hold off on quickly defeating Venobennu packs on the mountain slopes so Strago can witness and learn the White Wind Lore.
  • Avoid standard physical strikes against Adamankary due to its towering defense; eliminate it quickly using tier-2 magic spells instead.
