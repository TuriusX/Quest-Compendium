FINAL FANTASY VI guide

Every area in story order, with item checklists, secrets, missables and enemy weaknesses.

  • Narshe
    At the opening of the game when Terra, Biggs, and Wedge infiltrate the city in Magitek armor
  • Narshe Mines
    When Terra flees the local guards through the caverns and is aided by Locke and the Moogles
  • Figaro Castle
    When Locke escorts Terra to seek aid from King Edgar before Kefka attacks
  • South Figaro Cave
    Right after fleeing Figaro Castle to cross the mountains toward the eastern lands
  • South Figaro
    After emerging from the cave to purchase supplies and gather information on the Empire
  • Mt. Kolts
    While traversing the mountain route, where the party defeats Vargas and Sabin joins
  • Returners' Hideout
    Upon reaching the resistance base to confer with Banon about opposing the Empire
  • Imperial Camp
    Sabin sneaks through the Imperial base while trying to make his way back.
  • Doma Castle
    Cyan defends the stronghold before Kefka poisons the waters during the siege.
  • Phantom Train
    The party boards and fights through the spirit train ferrying souls to the afterworld.
  • The Veldt
    Sabin and Cyan traverse the wild plains and recruit the feral boy Gau.
  • Serpent Trench
    Sabin's team travels the underwater ocean currents using a diving helmet to reach civilization.
  • Nikeah
    Sabin, Cyan, and Gau arrive at the port town to board a ship across the sea.
  • Zozo
    The heroes explore the lawless, rainy city to find the transformed Terra and Ramuh.
  • Opera House
    The party puts on a performance to intercept the gambler Setzer and obtain his airship.
  • Vector
    The infiltration team enters the Imperial capital to breach the Magitek Research Facility.
  • Magitek Factory
    The heroes navigate conveyor belts and trash chutes inside the industrial plant to reach the testing labs.
  • Magitek Research Facility
    The team finds imprisoned Espers drained for power and receives their Magicite before fleeing.
  • Imperial Base
    The party travels through the Empire's military outpost to access the trail to the Sealed Gate.
  • Cave to the Sealed Gate
    The heroes navigate subterranean magma passages to reach the gate dividing the two worlds.
  • Imperial Palace
    The party visits the throne room in Vector to participate in a peace banquet hosted by Emperor Gestahl.
  • Albrook
    Locke and Terra arrive at the port town to board General Leo's vessel bound for Crescent Island.
  • Thamasa
    The party investigates the secluded village of magic users to track down the missing Espers.
  • Burning Home
    The heroes enter a blazing residence in Thamasa alongside Strago to save Relm.
  • Esper Caves
    Guided by Strago, the group treks into the western mountain caverns to locate the Espers and negotiate peace.
  • Blackjack
    The party takes to the skies on Setzer's airship and repels an airborne assault from the Imperial Air Force.
  • Floating Continent
    The party traverses the elevated landmass to stop Gestahl and Kefka from disturbing the Warring Triad.
  • Solitary Island
    Celes awakens from a one-year coma and leaves on a raft after Cid falls ill.
  • Albrook (World of Ruin)
    Celes travels to the mainland
  • Tzen
    Celes arrives just as Kefka strikes the town with the Light of Judgment and finds Sabin.
  • Crumbling House
    Celes rushes inside the damaged manor on a timer to rescue a child while Sabin braces the roof.
  • Mobliz
    Celes and Sabin locate Terra living underground to protect war orphans after the cataclysm.
  • Nikeah (World of Ruin)
    Searching for party members
  • South Figaro (World of Ruin)
    Searching for party members
  • South Figaro Cave (World of Ruin)
    traversing subterranean tunnels to reach the submerged castle
  • Figaro Castle (World of Ruin)
    Moving the castle to the Ancient Castle
  • Kohlingen
    The castle surfaces near this village, where the team finds a grieving Setzer inside the tavern.
  • Darill's Tomb
    Setzer leads the party into his late friend's crypt to restore and raise the airship Falcon.
  • Maranda
    The crew touches down near town after following a carrier pigeon seen during the Falcon's maiden flight.
  • Mt. Zozo
    The group scales the mountain caves, defeats the Storm Dragon, and reunites with Cyan.
  • Cave on the Veldt
    delving into the caverns to rescue an injured comrade
  • Thamasa (World of Ruin)
    Searching for Strago and Relm
  • Jidoor (World of Ruin)
    Investigating the Owzer's Mansion
  • Owzer's Mansion
    investigating the haunted art gallery in Jidoor to recruit Relm
  • Colosseum
    Winning items and recruiting Shadow
  • Narshe (World of Ruin)
    Recruiting Mog and Umaro
  • Yeti's Cave
    Recruiting Umaro
  • Fanatics' Tower
    Climbing to recruit Strago
  • Ebot's Rock
  • Triangle Island
    Being swallowed by the Zone Eater
  • Zone Eater's Interior
    Recruiting Gogo
  • Ancient Castle
    Exploring the ruins beneath Figaro
  • Phoenix Cave
    directing two separate parties through the dungeon to reunite with Locke
  • Kefka's Tower
    invading the final stronghold with three teams to confront Kefka
  • Kefka's Tower: Final Battle
    the summit of Kefka's Tower: the Warring Triad, the four-tier final gauntlet and Kefka
