Doma Castle

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Cyan defends the stronghold before Kefka poisons the waters during the siege.

Cyan defends Doma Castle against an Imperial assault until Kefka poisons the stronghold's water supply, wiping out King Doma, Cyan's family, and the castle garrison. In the World of Ruin, resting inside a castle bedroom with Cyan pulls the party into his Dreamscape to defeat his inner demons and unlock all of his Bushido techniques. Once the nightmare is resolved, you must search the throne room to claim the Alexander Magicite left in the aftermath.

Items

  • Remedy MISSABLE: inside a pot in the southeast chamber during the poison siege
  • Hi-Ether: inside a treasure chest in the western room off the main corridor
  • X-Potion: inside a treasure chest located in Cyan's living quarters
  • Phoenix Down: inside a treasure chest in the eastern storehouse reached via the walls
  • Prayer Beads: inside a treasure chest in the eastern storehouse reached via the walls
  • Alexander: resting directly upon the royal seat in the throne room after completing the Dreamscape

Secrets

  • A hidden Elixir can be collected by examining the clock located inside the room west of the main hallway.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
CaptainThe Imperial officer leading the gate breach, who fights Cyan in single combat and can drop a Black Belt.

Tips

  • Fight at least one Imperial Soldier along the castle ramparts before challenging the Captain so their encounter is unlocked for Gau on the Veldt.
  • You must include Cyan in your active four-person party and sleep in the castle's guest beds to trigger the Dreamscape sequence in the World of Ruin.
