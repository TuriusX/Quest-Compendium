Kohlingen

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The castle surfaces near this village, where the team finds a grieving Setzer inside the tavern.

Kohlingen is a small town where the party visits during both eras to uncover key character backstories and recruit party members. In the World of Ruin, Figaro Castle surfaces nearby in the desert, allowing players to enter the tavern and convince a grieving Setzer to rejoin the cause. The village functions as the final staging ground to restock supplies and upgrade weapons before traveling southwest to Darill's Tomb to uncover the airship Falcon.

Items

  • Elixir: Inside the grandfather clock in Rachel's house in the northwest
  • Green Beret: Inside a treasure chest reached by circling around and entering through the back door of the herbalist's house

Secrets

  • Hidden flashback cutscenes explaining Locke's tragic romance with Rachel can be viewed by leading Locke into the northwestern house and down into the herbalist's basement.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Darts, Dice, Viper Darts, Enhancer, and Golden Spear
  • Armor Shop: Diamond Shield, Priest's Miter, Green Beret, Diamond Helm, Diamond Vest, and Diamond Armor
  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion, Ether, Antidote, Phoenix Down, Holy Water, Remedy, Sleeping Bag, and Tent
  • Innkeeper: A room for the night for 200 Gil
  • Unequip Specialist NPC: Free service to strip equipment from non-active companions or the entire party
  • Shadow: Offers mercenary recruitment for 3,000 Gil at the cafe during the World of Balance

Tips

  • Purchase a generous supply of Holy Water from the local item shop before heading out, because the undead monsters filling Darill's Tomb regularly inflict Zombie status.
