Fanatics' Tower

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Climbing to recruit Strago

You visit this magic-only spire to snap Strago out of his cultist trance by bringing Relm along to talk to him outside the tower. Entering the tower forces your party into a magic-only restriction where standard combat commands are unavailable without specific relics. Climbing to the pinnacle yields the valuable Soul of Thamasa relic and triggers a boss battle against the MagiMaster.

Items

  • Safety Bit: In a treasure chest found within the second doorway room
  • Air Anchor: In a hidden chest within the room revealed behind the secret doorway
  • Genji Shield: In a treasure chest found within the third doorway room
  • Stunner: In a treasure chest found within the fourth doorway room
  • Soul of Thamasa: In a treasure chest at the pinnacle of the tower

Secrets

  • Air Anchor chest room | In the room with the Stunner, inspect the wall directly to the right of the treasure chest to trigger a switch, which reveals an extra doorway outside leading to the Air Anchor.

Tips

  • Cast Reraise on your party before defeating the MagiMaster, or use the Paladin's Shield to survive his dying Ultima cast.
  • Equip the Berserker Ring on a character with the Master's Scroll or powerful weapon to bypass the magic-only restriction through automated physical attacks.
  • Casting Berserk on the MagiMaster prevents him from changing barriers and locks his attack pattern into standard physical strikes.
  • Keep the Float status active on all party members to completely evade damaging Earth attacks like Quake.
  • Equip the Moogle Charm relic on Mog to walk all the way to the top of the tower without encountering random cultist battles.
