Nikeah (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Searching for party members

Celes and her companions arrive at the port town of Nikeah while searching for their scattered allies across the World of Ruin. In the local pub, you encounter a man named Gerad who strongly resembles Edgar leading a gang of thieves. Following Gerad onto his boat allows the party to cross the sea and pursue him toward South Figaro and the Figaro Castle engine room.

Shops and people

  • Armor Shop: Diamond Shield, Diamond Helm, Diamond Vest, Diamond Armor
  • Weapon Shop: Rune Blade, Flame Sabre, Blizzard, Thunder Blade, Enhancer
  • Relic Shop: Sprint Shoes, Black Belt, White Cape, Guard Ring, Fairy Ring, Barrier Ring
  • Item Shop: Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Revivify, Remedy, Sleeping Bag, Tent

Tips

  • Talk to the thieves in the pub first, then speak to Gerad, and finally follow them to the harbor to board the ship bound for South Figaro.
  • Upgrade your equipment here if you need gear, as the shops stock decent mid-tier Diamond armor and elemental blades for the World of Ruin.
