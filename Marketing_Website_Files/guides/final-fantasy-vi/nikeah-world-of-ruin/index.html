Nikeah (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Searching for party members

In the World of Ruin, this port settlement is visited while searching for allies and tracking the immobilised Figaro Castle. Inside the local pub, you encounter a band of thieves talking about raiding the submerged castle, followed by meeting their leader "Gerad"—who looks identical to Edgar. After speaking with him repeatedly through the market, you sneak aboard their ferry at the docks to sail across the ocean toward South Figaro.

Items

  • Elixir: inside the grandfather clock in the town inn

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion (300 gil), Ether (1,500 gil), Gold Needle (150 gil), Phoenix Down (500 gil), Holy Water (300 gil), Remedy (1,000 gil), Sleeping Bag (500 gil), Tent (1,200 gil)
  • Weapon Shop: Rune Blade (7,500 gil), Flametongue (7,000 gil), Icebrand (7,000 gil), Thunder Blade (7,000 gil), Enhancer (10,000 gil)
  • Armor Shop: Diamond Shield (3,500 gil), Priest's Miter (3,000 gil), Green Beret (3,000 gil), Diamond Helm (8,000 gil), Gaia Gear (6,000 gil), Power Sash (5,000 gil), Diamond Vest (12,000 gil)
  • Relic Shop: White Cape (5,000 gil), Angel Wings (8,000 gil), Zephyr Cloak (7,000 gil), Gale Hairpin (8,000 gil), Hyper Wrist (8,000 gil), Prayer Ring (4,000 gil), Amulet (5,000 gil), Prince Ring (3,000 gil)
  • Inn: Rest and full party recovery (150 gil)

Tips

  • Buy an Enhancer from the weapon merchant here if you have spare funds, as it offers a great magic and defense boost for Celes or Terra.
