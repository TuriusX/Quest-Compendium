Floating Continent

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The party traverses the elevated landmass to stop Gestahl and Kefka from disturbing the Warring Triad.

The party ascends this jagged airborne landmass to confront Emperor Gestahl and Kefka before they can harness the power of the Warring Triad statues. Players must navigate shifting terrain, deadly fiends, and the ancient guardian Ultima Weapon before witnessing the cataclysmic event that shatters the world. Once the continent begins to destabilize, the group must sprint through a collapsing path under a strict countdown timer to reach the airship.

Items

  • Murasame MISSABLE: Found in a blue orb along the northern dead-end path near the continent's entrance
  • Sasuke MISSABLE: Found in a blue orb guarded by Gigantos on the eastern side of the continent
  • Beret MISSABLE: Found in a blue orb tucked into an alcove shortly before the final save point
  • Elixir MISSABLE: Found in a blue orb located along a lower detour during the timed escape sequence

Secrets

  • Rescuing Shadow for the World of Ruin by selecting the wait option at the airship jump prompt until roughly five seconds remain on the escape timer

Tips

  • Equip Gaia Gear on party members to absorb earth attacks such as Quake from Ultima Weapon and reverse the damage from Misfit's Lifeshaver.
  • Cast Rasp and Osmose on Ultima Weapon to safely defeat it by draining its MP rather than triggering its deadliest damage phases.
  • Equip Sprint Shoes during the escape countdown to move past falling terrain and reach the airship with plenty of time to spare.
