Jidoor (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Investigating the Owzer's Mansion

In the World of Ruin, visit Owzer's Mansion at the north end of Jidoor to find it haunted by monsters infesting the paintings. Explore the mansion's gallery to rescue Relm and recover the magicite Lakshimi (Starlet). Defeating the boss Chadarnook frees both Owzer and Relm, allowing her to rejoin the party.

Items

  • Torage Ring / Moogle Charm: examine the bookshelf in the hidden gallery room behind the Emperor Gestahl portrait
  • Magicite Lakshimi (Starlet): dropped on the floor in Owzer's room after defeating Chadarnook
  • Potion: search the bucket in the basement storage room

Secrets

  • Examine the painting of Emperor Gestahl twice in the mansion's basement gallery to uncover a hidden door leading deeper inside.
  • In the hallway with moving doors, time your interaction to enter the door when it appears in front of you to reach the inner sanctum.

Tips

  • Equip Reflect Rings on your party members before fighting Chadarnook to bounce back the Demon form's powerful black magic.
  • Stop attacking immediately whenever Chadarnook shifts into the Goddess form, waiting patiently until it transforms back into the Demon.
  • Equip Relm with the Fake Mustache relic to turn her Sketch ability into the far more reliable Control command.
