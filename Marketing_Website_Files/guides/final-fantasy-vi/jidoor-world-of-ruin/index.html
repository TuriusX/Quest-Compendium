Jidoor (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Investigating the Owzer's Mansion

The party visits Owzer's Mansion in Jidoor during the World of Ruin to rescue Relm and free the wealthy art collector from a supernatural menace. After lighting the dark manor and navigating hidden passageways behind haunted paintings, the party must defeat the possessing entity Chadarnook to save Relm and acquire the Esper Lakshmi.

Items

  • Moogle Suit: Inside a treasure chest through the left door in the basement hallway
  • Lich Ring: Inside a hidden treasure chest tucked at the end of the passage directly behind the three shifting doors
  • 293 Gil: Inside the leftmost floating chest in the basement treasure room
  • Potion: Inside the second floating chest from the left in the basement treasure room
  • Ether: Inside the third floating chest from the left in the basement treasure room
  • Gold Needle: Inside the rightmost floating chest in the basement treasure room
  • Lakshmi: Obtained as magicite by examining the glowing point in Owzer's bedroom after defeating Chadarnook

Secrets

  • Inspecting the portrait of Emperor Gestahl twice in the second-floor gallery uncovers the Emperor's Letter, which gives a clue regarding the Phoenix Cave.

Shops and people

  • Jidoor Weapon Shop: Sells the Man-Eater, Crystal Sword, Partisan, and Sniper
  • Jidoor Armor Shop: Sells the Circlet, Black Cowl, Crystal Helm, and Black Garb
  • Jidoor Item Shop: Sells Hi-Potions, Ethers, Phoenix Downs, Holy Water, Remedies, Sleeping Bags, Tents, and Teleport Stones

Tips

  • Chadarnook's goddess form absorbs Holy and Water while heavily countering attacks, so hold high-damage Holy and Fire abilities until the demon form materializes.
