The Veldt

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Sabin and Cyan traverse the wild plains and recruit the feral boy Gau.

After washing up from Baren Falls, Sabin and Cyan explore the expansive grasslands of the Veldt and encounter the wild child Gau. To recruit him, the party must visit the nearby town of Mobliz to purchase Dried Meat and feed it to Gau when he appears following a random encounter. Gau subsequently leads the companions to Crescent Mountain to uncover a diving helmet needed to journey across the Serpent Trench.

Items

  • Berserker Ring: Treasure chest located in the northwestern section of the Cave on the Veldt
  • Tigerfang: Concealed treasure chest guarded by the Death Warden in the Cave on the Veldt
  • Ichigeki: Treasure chest inside the southern switch chamber of the Cave on the Veldt

Shops and people

  • Mobliz Item Shop: Offers Dried Meat for 150 Gil, alongside basic restorative items like Potions and Green Cherries needed for survival and recruiting Gau.

Tips

  • Never attack Gau when he pops onto the screen after combat; instead, immediately select Dried Meat from your inventory and feed him to ensure he joins your party without running away.
