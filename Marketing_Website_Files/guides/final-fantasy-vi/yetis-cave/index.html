Yeti's Cave

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Recruiting Umaro

Yeti's Cave is a hidden cavern beneath the snow-covered cliffs of Narshe, accessed after defeating the Valigarmanda esper and jumping into the chasm behind it. Inside, the party must navigate pitfalls and minefield traps to find the skull carving holding the Midgardsormr esper, which provokes an attack from the yeti Umaro. Once defeated, Umaro will officially join the party if Mog is present in the active group to order him to comply.

Items

  • Midgardsormr Magicite: Embedded in the eye socket of the large bone carving at the back of the cave
  • Gauntlet: In a treasure chest located in the upper cavern tunnels
  • X-Ether: In a chest inside the cavern pathways

Enemies

Tips

  • Bring Mog in your four-person party before interacting with the bone carving, or you will not be able to recruit Umaro after defeating him.
  • Equipping Fire-elemental weapons or spells makes quick work of Umaro, as he is inherently weak to Fire.
  • Falling through specific pitfall traps drops the party into lower cavern rooms containing the chests and bridges leading back to Umaro's lair.
