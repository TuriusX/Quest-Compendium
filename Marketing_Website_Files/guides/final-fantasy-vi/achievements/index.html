FINAL FANTASY VI achievement guide

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Escaping the Floating Continent: World of Balance areas and unencountered monster formations become permanently inaccessible.
  • Entering the escape airship on the Floating Continent without waiting for Shadow: Shadow dies permanently, locking out his recruitability, equipment, and related Bestiary encounters.
  • The Emperor's Banquet at the Imperial Palace: Unearned banquet points cannot be redone, locking the player out of imperial storehouse rewards.
  • Committing to the descent in Kefka's Tower: Final Battle: Final boss tiers must be defeated; free exploration requires reloading an external save file.

Recommended order
  1. Narshe - Complete the opening raid, record early Bestiary entries, and escape through the back cave.
  2. Narshe Mines - Evade guards via the light path, defeat Ymir, and loot all early chests and hidden items.
  3. Figaro Castle - Recruit Edgar, purchase tools, interact with all NPCs, and submerge the castle to escape.
  4. South Figaro Cave - Navigate the subterranean tunnel while grabbing all initial chests and cataloging local enemies.
  5. South Figaro - Search town barrels and pots for hidden items, meet Shadow, and gear up before heading east.
  6. Mt. Kolts - Fight through the mountain paths, defeat Vargas using Sabin's Pummel blitz, and enter the hideout.
  7. Returners' Hideout - Speak with Banon, grab hidden room items, and launch the split-scenario river raft sequence.
  8. Imperial Camp - Sabin's scenario; infiltrate the outpost, defeat officers, collect tent treasures, and duel Kefka.
  9. Doma Castle - Witness Cyan's backstory, battle escaping soldiers, and fight your way past the perimeter.
  10. Phantom Train - Board the haunted locomotive, recruit ghosts, raid dining cars, and outrun the train engine.
  11. The Veldt - Reconnect with Gau, leap onto enemy formations to catalog Rages, and pick up Dried Meat.
  12. Serpent Trench - Swim through the undersea current, exploring alternate cavern paths to collect every chest.
  13. Nikeah - Restock supplies, search port containers for collectibles, and board the ship to South Figaro.
  14. Zozo - Climb the deceptive towers, solve the clock puzzle for the Chainsaw, and retrieve Terra and early Magicite.
  15. Opera House - Perform the opera without stage errors, duel Ultros on the rafters, and board Setzer's airship.
