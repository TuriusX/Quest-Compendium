Points of no return

  • Boarding the raft at Lethe River: Locking out the World of Balance encounters in South Figaro Cave.
  • Completing the Magitek Research Facility and escaping Vector: Permanent closure of the Magitek Factory interior and its unique bestiary spawns and chests.
  • Opening the Sealed Gate at the eastern cave: Cave to the Sealed Gate collapses and becomes permanently inaccessible along with any missed chests and monster entries.
  • Initiating the Floating Continent escape sequence without waiting for Shadow: Permanent death of Shadow, locking out his World of Ruin recruitment and his throw command/interceptor achievements.
  • Fleeing the Floating Continent to transition into the World of Ruin: Entire World of Balance map, exclusive overworld/dungeon chests, and region-locked bestiary monsters become permanently lost.
  • Choosing between the Ragnarok Esper or the Ragnarok Sword in Narshe (WoR): Permanently locks you out of the unpicked option for that playthrough.
  • Defeating the final boss at the top of Kefka's Tower: Triggers the game's finale and ending credits.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1Narshe

    Clear the opening sequence, open the chests in the Arvis/school/mines area, and note down all early bestiary encounters.

    Also in this step: Mystifying Woman, Defend the Esper

  2. Step 2Narshe Mines

    Navigate the mines during the escape and opening defense; defeat all unique enemies here for the bestiary.

  3. Step 3Figaro Castle

    Purchase Edgar's initial Tools (Auto Crossbow, Noiseblaster, Bioblaster) toward the Tool achievement, talk to NPCs, and submerge the castle.

  4. Step 4South Figaro Cave

    Clear all enemies to register bestiary entries before exiting into the Figaro region.

  5. Step 5South Figaro

    Collect every hidden item and open every accessible chest throughout the town before the imperial blockade.

  6. Step 6Mt. Kolts

    Navigate the mountain paths, defeat Vargas, recruit Sabin, and learn his first Blitzes toward the Blitz achievement.

  7. Step 7Returners' Hideout

    Collect all chests/hidden items, refuse Banon's request three times if you want the Genji Glove (or accept for the Gauntlet), and set off down the Lethe River.

    Also in this step: Prevailing Hope

  8. Step 8Imperial Camp

    During Sabin's scenario, loot all chests and fight every officer/soldier formation for the bestiary before the camp disperses.

  9. Step 9Doma Castle

    Experience Cyan's story scenario and collect all available treasures in the castle.

    Also in this step: Master Samurai

  10. Step 10Phantom Train

    Board the train, defeat the unique ghostly encounters for the bestiary, recruit the Ghost, and defeat the Phantom Train boss.

  11. Step 11The Veldt

    Encounter various monster packs with Gau, use the Leap command to add Rages toward the Rage achievement, and retrieve Gau.

    Also in this step: Master of the Wild

  12. Step 12Serpent Trench

    Traverse the underwater trench pathways, collecting chests along the way and securing sea-life bestiary entries.

  13. Step 13Nikeah

    Restock, gather hidden items, visit every building to register the location map requirement, and take the ferry.

  14. Step 14Zozo

    Climb the tower, solve the clock puzzle for Edgar's Chainsaw, defeat Dadaluma, and obtain the first set of Magicite espers.

    Also in this step: Imparted Power

  15. Step 15Opera House

    Complete the opera performance without failing Celes's lines, defeat Ultros on stage, and recruit Setzer aboard the airship.

    Also in this step: Taking Off
