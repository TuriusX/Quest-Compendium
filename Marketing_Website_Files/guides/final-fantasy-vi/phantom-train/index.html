Phantom Train

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The party boards and fights through the spirit train ferrying souls to the afterworld.

Following their escape through the Phantom Forest, Sabin, Cyan, and optionally Shadow mistakenly climb aboard a supernatural locomotive tasked with hauling deceased souls to the afterlife. Because the entry doors seal behind them, the companions must battle westward through the passenger cars and disconnect pursuing coaches to reach the locomotive engine. Once the party operates the steam pressure switches at the front, they must defeat the runaway train in direct combat to halt its journey and return to the living world.

Items

  • Tent MISSABLE: Caboose interior compartment, hidden on the floor tile in the northwest corner
  • Earring MISSABLE: Dining car, found inside a treasure chest reached by going outside and re-entering the car through its western door
  • Tent MISSABLE: Car 7 western private compartment, located in a hidden spot in the northwest corner near the table
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Car 8 western compartment, retrieved from the easternmost chest in the room
  • Sniper Eye MISSABLE: Car 8 western compartment, retrieved from the second chest from the right
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Car 8 western compartment, retrieved from the third chest from the right
  • Hyper Wrist MISSABLE: Car 8 western compartment, obtained as a guaranteed reward after opening the leftmost chest and defeating the monster within

Secrets

  • A fully restorative meal can be enjoyed by walking up to the center dining table in the dining car, completely healing the party's HP, MP, and abnormal statuses.
  • Pulling the mechanical lever situated in the northeast corner of the caboose provokes an amusing comedic reaction from Cyan regarding modern technology.

  • Ghost Merchant: Potions, Hi-Potions, Antidotes, Green Cherries, Phoenix Downs, Sleeping Bags, and Shurikens

Tips

  • Because the Phantom Train boss is classified as an undead creature, using a Phoenix Down directly on it causes an instant knockout.
  • Equipping the Earring found in the dining car onto Sabin significantly amplifies the damage of his magic-based Blitz techniques, such as Aura Cannon.
  • Speaking to the peaceful ghost in the caboose allows it to join your party as a fourth member, providing an extra target for attacks and access to the one-time instant-kill skill Possess.
  • Sabin can perform his Meteor Strike Blitz on the entire locomotive during the boss encounter to slam the train upside-down.
