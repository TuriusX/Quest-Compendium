Zozo

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The heroes explore the lawless, rainy city to find the transformed Terra and Ramuh.

The party journeys to the crime-ridden, rain-soaked city of Zozo in search of Terra after her Esper transformation causes her to fly away from Narshe. To locate her, the heroes must navigate vertical rooftops and dilapidated apartment buildings filled with aggressive cutthroats and chronic liars. Reaching the highest chamber requires defeating the martial artist Dadaluma, after which the Esper Ramuh entrusts the party with Magicite shards and vital lore.

Items

  • Chainsaw: Inside a hidden chamber revealed behind the clock on the ground floor of the Inn
  • Ether: In a treasure chest located in a rooftop room atop the Pub building
  • Brigand's Glove: In a chest inside the topmost room of the Relic Shop building prior to jumping across to the next tower
  • Ether: Hidden within one of the ceramic pots on the upper floor of the western building
  • Hi-Potion: Hidden inside a ceramic pot next to the other container on the upper floor of the western building
  • Burning Fist: Inside a chest situated in a side room while climbing the exterior stairs of the main tower
  • Hermes Sandals: Inside a chest on the left side of the upper chamber where Terra is kept
  • X-Potion: Inside a chest on the right side of the upper chamber where Terra is kept
  • Ramuh Magicite: Lying on the floor in the uppermost sanctuary after the dialogue with Ramuh concludes
  • Kirin Magicite: Lying on the floor in the uppermost sanctuary after the dialogue with Ramuh concludes
  • Siren Magicite: Lying on the floor in the uppermost sanctuary after the dialogue with Ramuh concludes
  • Cait Sith Magicite: Lying on the floor in the uppermost sanctuary after the dialogue with Ramuh concludes

Secrets

  • Hidden Chainsaw room | Enter the building labeled "Inn," interact with the clock, and input the time 6:10:50 (deducible by taking the opposite of the statements told by the deceitful townsfolk) to open a doorway on the right.

  • Prevent Dadaluma from triggering his devastating knife-throwing counters by relying on command skills such as Edgar's Tools, Sabin's Blitz, or Locke's Mug rather than the standard Attack or Magic commands.
