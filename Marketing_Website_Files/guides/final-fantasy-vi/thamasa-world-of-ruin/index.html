Thamasa (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Searching for Strago and Relm

Returning to Thamasa in the World of Ruin reveals that both Strago and Relm are missing from their home. Speaking with the villagers provides clues that Relm headed toward the cave in the Veldt and Strago joined a strange cult. This town acts as an informational hub and gear check before setting out to rescue the two mages.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Darts, Dice, Shuriken, Fuma Shuriken (Shadow Edge), and elemental rods
  • Armor Shop: Sells Mystery Veil, Circlet, Bard's Hat, Dark Gear, Tao Robe, and Earth Shield
  • Relic Shop: Sells Silver Spectacles, Fairy Ring, Amulet, White Cape, Jewel Ring, and Barrier Ring
  • Item Shop: Sells Potion, Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Revivify, Remedy, Soft, Holy Water, and Tent
  • Inn: Rest for 1 GP

Tips

  • Buy several Mystery Veils at the Armor Shop, as they are among the best magic-boosting headgear for female characters in the game.
  • You must discover Relm's fate at the Cave in the Veldt before you can recruit Strago at the Fanatics' Tower.
