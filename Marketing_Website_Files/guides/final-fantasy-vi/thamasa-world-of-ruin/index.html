Thamasa (World of Ruin)

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Searching for Strago and Relm

When you visit Thamasa in the World of Ruin after clearing the Cave in the Veldt, the injured survivor (Shadow or Relm) is brought here to rest before disappearing toward Dragon's Neck Coliseum. Later, visiting the village with Strago and Relm in your party triggers an event where Strago discovers his old friend Gungho has been injured by the monster Hidon. This unlocks the quest to travel to Ebot's Rock just north of Thamasa to hunt down Hidon.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion, Ether, Phoenix Down, Holy Water, Remedy, Smoke Bomb, Sleeping Bag, Tent
  • Weapon Shop: Da Vinci Brush, Gravity Rod, Holy Rod, Viper Darts, Golden Spear, Man-Eater, Shuriken, Fuma Shuriken
  • Armor Shop: Mystery Veil, Circlet, Black Cowl, Luminous Robe, Diamond Vest
  • Relic Shop: Barrier Ring, Fairy Ring, Reflect Ring, Jeweled Ring, Princess Ring, Protect Ring, Peace Ring, Angel Ring
  • Innkeeper: Offers full rest for 1 Gil

Tips

  • Bringing both Strago and Relm in your active party is mandatory to activate the scene with Gungho and open up the dungeon at Ebot's Rock.
