Narshe Mines

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · When Terra flees the local guards through the caverns and is aided by Locke and the Moogles

Tips

  • Unequip Terra's Mythril Knife and Buckler before the floor collapses so Locke can immediately wear them in the battle that follows.
  • Leave one Silver Lobo alive while fighting Guard Leader to prevent him from using his lethal Charge ability.
  • Avoid opening the two treasure chests in these mines now, as leaving them untouched causes them to transform into superior gear later in the game.
  • Move moogles equipped with ranged weaponry into the back row to cut down the physical damage they take from advancing troops.
