Returners' Hideout

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Upon reaching the resistance base to confer with Banon about opposing the Empire

After crossing Mt. Kolts, the group reaches the resistance base to confer with the rebel leader Banon, who asks Terra to assist their fight against the Empire. Terra explores the refuge, speaks with her companions, and delivers her decision to Banon near the cavern entrance. Word of an Imperial invasion soon interrupts the meeting, compelling the group to evacuate through the Lethe River.

Items

  • Hi-Potion MISSABLE: Banon's chambers in the southeastern treasure chest
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Bedroom where Terra wakes up, inside the lone treasure chest
  • Air Knife MISSABLE: Storeroom in the bottom-right treasure chest
  • Phoenix Down MISSABLE: Storeroom in the bottom-left treasure chest
  • Knight's Code MISSABLE: Storeroom in the northern treasure chest
  • Antidote MISSABLE: Storeroom inside a bucket
  • Ether MISSABLE: Storeroom inside a pot beside the bucket
  • White Cape MISSABLE: Hidden room reached via a concealed passage on the right wall of the storeroom
  • Green Cherry MISSABLE: Dining area inside a pot beside an armor statue
  • Gauntlet MISSABLE: Awarded by Banon if Terra accepts his request to become their beacon of hope
  • Genji Glove MISSABLE: Awarded by a Returner if Terra refuses Banon's request three consecutive times

Secrets

  • A concealed pathway in the right wall of the storeroom behind the stacked crates leads into a secluded alcove containing a treasure chest with a White Cape.
  • Examining the northern edge of the meeting table reveals a discarded paper scrap; choosing to leave it in place triggers an extra scene where Banon vents about littering during the strategy council.

Shops and people

  • Item Shop Merchant: Sells Potions, Hi-Potions, Ethers, Eye Drops, Echo Screens, Sleeping Bags, Tents, and Sprint Shoes
  • Returner Innkeeper: Offers free lodging that fully restores party HP and MP

Tips

  • Reject Banon's plea three times in a row to secure the Genji Glove rather than the Gauntlet, granting the ability to dual-wield weapons for significantly greater physical damage output.
  • Take advantage of the hideout merchant to purchase Hi-Potions, as this base is the first vendor in the game that carries them.
  • Shift Banon into the back row before stepping onto the Lethe River raft, because his low durability makes him vulnerable to sudden knockouts that cause an immediate Game Over.
