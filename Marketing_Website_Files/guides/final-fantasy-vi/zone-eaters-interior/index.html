Zone Eater's Interior

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Recruiting Gogo

You access this secret cavern by allowing a Zone Eater enemy on Triangle Island in the northeast corner of the map to use its Engulf ability on your entire party. Inside, navigate downward through obstacle-filled rooms, including a timing puzzle with wandering men who bump you off ledges, to reach the master mimic Gogo. Speak to Gogo at the deepest point of the dungeon to recruit them into your party and use the save point to exit.

Tips

  • Equip the Sprint Shoes relic to make timing your movements past the wandering men in the obstacle room much easier.
  • If a wandering green man pushes you off a ledge, you fall to the floor below rather than dying, allowing you to walk back up and retry.
  • Gogo's unique command Mimic allows them to duplicate the exact previous action performed by any party member, including magic, abilities, and item usage.
