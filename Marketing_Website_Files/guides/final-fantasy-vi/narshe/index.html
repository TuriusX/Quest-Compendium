Narshe

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · At the opening of the game when Terra, Biggs, and Wedge infiltrate the city in Magitek armor

Imperial soldiers Biggs and Wedge escort an enslaved young woman in Magitek armor into Narshe to retrieve a frozen Esper unearthed deep within the mines. The player must push north through the snow-covered streets while clearing out local defenders, navigate the mine tunnels, and overcome the guardian monster Ymir to reach the frozen creature.

Items

  • Hi-Potion: Dropped by the boss Ymir upon defeat in the deep mine tunnel

Enemies

Tips

  • Move all three characters into the back row through the party menu, which halves incoming physical strikes while Magitek beams continue to deal unreduced damage.
  • Cease all offensive moves when Ymir withdraws its head into the shell and spend those turns using Healing Force instead of risking a lethal counterattack.
  • Clear out multi-target groups of regular enemies immediately by using Terra's unique Bio Blast Magitek ability.
