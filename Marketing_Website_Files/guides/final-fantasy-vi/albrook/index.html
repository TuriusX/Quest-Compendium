Albrook

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Locke and Terra arrive at the port town to board General Leo's vessel bound for Crescent Island.

Terra and Locke travel to the Imperial port town of Albrook to board General Leo's ship bound for Crescent Island in search of the Espers. Upon reporting to General Leo at the harbor and reuniting with Celes and Shadow, the party is instructed to spend the night at the local inn. After nighttime events conclude, return to the vessel at the docks the next morning to set sail across the sea.

Items

  • Hi-Potion: inside a barrel outside, to the right of the entrance to the Inn
  • Ether: inside an urn located within the Weapon Shop
  • Elixir: inside a clock in the back corridor connecting the Pub and the Relic Shop
  • Teleport Stone: inside a crate by the entrance to the docks in the southeastern part of town

Secrets

  • A grandfather clock in the passageway between the Pub and Relic Shop contains a hidden Elixir when inspected.
  • The two chests located inside the Armor Shop can be opened, but both are completely empty.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sakura, Bastard Sword, Kiku-ichimonji, Venom Claws, Shuriken, Flame Scroll, Water Scroll, and Lightning Scroll
  • Armor Shop: Twist Headband, Priest's Miter, Mythril Vest, Ninja Gear, and White Dress
  • Relic Shop: Silver Spectacles, Peace Ring, Sniper Eye, Amulet, Earring, and Reflect Ring
  • Item Shop: Hi-Potion, Ether, Eyedrops, Remedy, Holy Water, Phoenix Down, Tent, and Teleport Stone
  • Inn: lodging for 300 Gil (provided free of charge during General Leo's mission)

Tips

  • Purchase multiple Reflect Rings from the local Relic Shop to bounce spells back at magical enemies in upcoming areas.
  • General Leo covers the cost of the party's lodging at the Inn during this storyline segment, allowing you to fully recover and trigger the necessary story events without spending Gil.
  • Shadow joins the active party during the voyage, so inspect his gear and assign him an Esper before heading into the wild areas on Crescent Island.
