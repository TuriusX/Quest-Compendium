South Figaro

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · After emerging from the cave to purchase supplies and gather information on the Empire

Terra, Locke, and Edgar reach South Figaro to gather intelligence on imperial activity and replenish their equipment after fleeing Figaro Castle. The party can explore the town, discover hidden passages beneath an affluent resident's mansion, and shop for upgraded gear and Edgar's tools. Once business is concluded, the party continues northeast toward Mt. Kolts and the Returners' hideout.

Items

  • Potion: upper barrel directly to the left of the Chocobo Stables near the entrance
  • Green Cherry: top wooden crate situated to the right of the Chocobo Stables
  • Gold Needle: crate in the far southwestern corner along the town waterfront
  • Antidote: barrel placed along the southern dock area
  • Eye Drops: upper crate resting beside the Antidote barrel near the southern dock
  • Potion: barrel nestled in the alley between the Weapon Shop and Armor Shop
  • Teleport Stone: southeastern barrel resting on the elevated ramparts overlooking town
  • Phoenix Down: barrel placed outside the western entrance of the affluent resident's mansion
  • Elixir: grandfather clock inside the storeroom accessible behind the affluent resident's mansion

Secrets

  • Speaking to the quiet mercenary sitting with his hound in the Cafe initiates a sequence where you register the name Shadow.

Shops and people

  • South Figaro Weapon Shop: Dagger, Mythril Knife, Mythril Sword, Great Sword, Noiseblaster, Bioblaster
  • South Figaro Armor Shop: Buckler, Heavy Shield, Hairband, Plumed Hat, Cotton Robe, Kenpo Gi
  • South Figaro Relic Shop: Sprint Shoes, Silver Spectacles, Star Pendant, Jeweled Ring, Knight's Code
  • South Figaro Item Shop: Potion, Antidote, Gold Needle, Eye Drops, Echo Screen, Phoenix Down, Sleeping Bag, Tent
  • Chocobo Stables: rentable Chocobos to travel the overworld without monster encounters

Tips

  • Skip spending Gil to sleep at the local inn, since Duncan's Cabin just north on the world map allows the team to rest and fully heal for free.
  • Purchase Sprint Shoes at the Relic Shop to double party exploration movement speed.
  • Consider leaving outdoor containers untouched—such as the southwest Gold Needle crate and central Potion barrel—because they upgrade into an Elixir and an X-Potion later in the World of Ruin.
  • Buy a Great Sword for Terra at the Weapon Shop to drastically enhance her physical attack power for the road ahead.
