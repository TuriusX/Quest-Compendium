Magitek Factory

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · The heroes navigate conveyor belts and trash chutes inside the industrial plant to reach the testing labs.

The Returners infiltrate the Empire's industrial core to rescue captured Espers whose magical energies are being drained for weapon development. Players must navigate a tangled network of conveyor belts, garbage chutes, and piping to reach the inner research laboratories and escape via minecart. The mission culminates in collecting Magicite remnants, witnessing Kefka's deceit, and fleeing Vector aboard Setzer's airship.

  • Ether MISSABLE: Inside a chest accessed through the eastern pipe on the entrance platform
  • X-Potion MISSABLE: Inside a chest positioned right at the exit of the first conveyor belt
  • Thunder Blade MISSABLE: Inside a chest found on the landing next to the second conveyor belt
  • Remedy MISSABLE: Inside a chest situated south of the Thunder Blade platform before riding the next belt
  • Dragoon Boots MISSABLE: Inside a chest located in the far southwestern corner of the second room
  • Golden Shield MISSABLE: Inside a chest within a small room reached by climbing the southwestern catwalk stairs
  • Tent MISSABLE: Inside a chest sitting next to the small stairs in the southeastern catwalk area
  • Golden Armor MISSABLE: Inside a chest in a hidden room accessed through a doorway along the southern wall near the Tent
  • Golden Helm MISSABLE: Inside a chest in an obscured chamber along the southern wall west of the Golden Armor
  • Zephyr Cloak MISSABLE: Inside a chest in a secluded storage room reached through a pipe tucked among northeast crates
  • Icebrand MISSABLE: Inside a chest sitting beside the Zephyr Cloak in the crate-accessible northeast pipe room
  • Stoneblade MISSABLE: Inside a hidden chest tucked beyond the lower-left glass specimen tube in the research lab

Secrets

  • An obscured doorway exists along the darkened southern wall near the Tent chest, opening up hidden rooms that house the Golden Armor and Golden Helm.
  • A secret passage in the Magitek Research Facility extends south and west from the bottom-left glass specimen tank to reveal a hidden chest containing the Stoneblade.

Tips

  • Most mechanical and infantry enemies inside have high physical Defense, so prioritize Lightning spells, Sabin's Blitzes, and Edgar's Tools over basic attacks.
  • Adjust party levels so no character is at a multiple of 4 or 5, preventing Trappers from wiping the squad with Level 4 Flare or Level 5 Death.
  • Have Locke attempt to steal the rare Kazekiri sword from Number 128 during the minecart sequence before destroying the boss's main body.
  • Fight at least one Flan in the disposal pit and collect all treasures immediately, as the entire facility is permanently blocked off once you leave Vector.
