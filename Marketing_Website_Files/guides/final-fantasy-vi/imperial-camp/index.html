Guides / FINAL FANTASY VI

Imperial Camp

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · Sabin sneaks through the Imperial base while trying to make his way back.

Items

  • Star Pendant MISSABLE: Inside the chest located in the northeastern tent near the entrance
  • Mythril Glove MISSABLE: Inside the southwestern tent, stored in the chest on the right side
  • Green Beret MISSABLE: Inside the southwestern tent, awarded as a guaranteed drop after opening the left chest and slaying the Satellite
  • Barrier Ring MISSABLE: Inside a hidden northern tent accessed by jumping off the ridge directly behind the southwestern tent

Secrets

  • A hidden ridge behind the southwestern tent lets your party leap down to an otherwise inaccessible area housing a tent with a Barrier Ring.
  • Choosing to kick the stuck treasure chest in the first tent prompts a fight with Dobermans, which is the only place in the story to register them for Gau's Veldt encounters.

Tips

  • Grab every chest before confronting Kefka for the third time, as triggering that encounter permanently advances the story and destroys your chance to explore the camp.
  • Always choose to kick the stubborn treasure chest instead of hitting it so you do not permanently miss the Doberman encounter required for Gau's complete Rage list.
