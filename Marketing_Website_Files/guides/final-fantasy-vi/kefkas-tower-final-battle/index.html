Kefka's Tower: Final Battle

FINAL FANTASY VI guide · the summit of Kefka's Tower: the Warring Triad, the four-tier final gauntlet and Kefka

Reaching the summit of the tower requires dividing the party across three paths to defeat the statues of the Warring Triad. Stepping on the final three floor switches triggers the ultimate encounter against the multi-tiered Statue of the Gods and the ascended Kefka. Before combat begins, players arrange their entire party lineup, with reserve members stepping in to replace fallen characters between battle tiers.

Tips

  • Place Strago in the party confronting Fiend to learn the Force Field Lore, as this boss is the sole monster in the game to use it.
  • In the first tier of the final battle, eliminate Visage before destroying both arms to prevent it from casting a dying Quake.
  • Target Lady first during the third tier of the statue to prevent her from constantly reviving Rest with Arise.
  • Frontload your combat roster with your four strongest heroes equipped with Ribbons to handle the majority of the final gauntlet uninterrupted.
