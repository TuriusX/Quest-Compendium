Feymarch

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party enters the land of the Eidolons

The Feymarch is the mystical subterranean realm of the Eidolons, reachable through a cave in the Underworld. Cecil's party visits to speak with the Eidolon royalty and seek their aid in the battle against Golbez. By challenging the ruling Eidolons in combat, Rydia can gain powerful new summons for her arsenal.

Items

  • Rat Tail: Found in a treasure chest in the small treasure room reached by a hidden path
  • Yoichi Bow: In a chest inside the Feymarch's treasury

Secrets

  • Hidden passages exist within the walls of the Feymarch that lead to a secluded room containing several high-tier treasure chests, including the Rat Tail.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells high-level gear including the Whip, Blitz Whip, and various arrows
  • Armor Shop: Sells Aegis Shield, Minerva Bustier, and other high-end armor pieces

Tips

  • Walking over the glowing floor panels in the town causes damage to the party unless Float is cast beforehand.
  • Speak to the Queen and King of the Eidolons (Asura and Leviathan) in the central library to initiate optional boss fights that grant them as summons.
