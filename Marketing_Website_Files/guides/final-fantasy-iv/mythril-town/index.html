Mythril Town

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party visits to purchase equipment

Mythril Town, also known as the Village of Silver, is an isolated settlement populated by tiny folk such as dwarves, toads, and pigs. Accessible by the hovercraft, it serves as a shopping stop where the party can purchase powerful Mythril gear to upgrade Cecil and other compatible fighters.

Secrets

  • Hidden Mythril items buried in the grass | Inspect the grassy patches in the northern section of town to find loose Mythril gear

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Mythril Sword, Mythril Knife, and Mythril Staff
  • Armor Shop: Sells Mythril Shield, Mythril Helm, Mythril Armor, and Mythril Gloves
  • Item Shop: Sells standard restorative items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Gold Needles

Tips

  • To reach the town early, you must transport the hovercraft using the Enterprise airship and land it near the archipelago of small islands where the town resides.
