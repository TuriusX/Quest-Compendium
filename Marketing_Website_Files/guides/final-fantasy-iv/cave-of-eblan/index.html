Cave of Eblan

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party travels through to reach the Tower of Babil

The party traverses the Cave of Eblan to reach the underground entrance of the Tower of Babil. Inside, the party discovers the surviving refugees of the Kingdom of Eblan who have set up a temporary settlement. Cecil and his companions also encounter Prince Edge here, who joins the party after attempting to fight Rubicante alone.

Tips

  • The enemies in this cave hit hard, so make sure to equip the ice-elemental gear purchased from the refugee shops to exploit local weaknesses.
  • Speak to the wounded ninjas and refugees in the settlement to gain access to free resting spots and background lore before venturing deeper into the passage toward the Tower of Babil.
