Mist

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The village is destroyed by the bomb ring

Cecil and Kain arrive at the isolated village of Mist to deliver the Carnelian Signet given to them by the King of Baron. Upon entering the village, the package bursts open, releasing hostile Bombs that engulf the entire town in flames and destroy the houses. Cecil and Kain discover the young summoner Rydia, triggering a fight and a subsequent earthquake that splits the party.

Items

  • Tiara: hidden in a path through the trees on the eastern side of the village after it is rebuilt
  • Clothes: inside a treasure chest in the village
  • Ruby Ring: inside a treasure chest in the village

Secrets

  • Hidden pathway | walking through the dense treeline on the northeast side of the rebuilt village leads to hidden treasure chests

Tips

  • Do not expect to use any shops during your initial story arrival, as the village is immediately incinerated upon your entry.
  • You will return to Mist later in the game once you have access to an airship, allowing you to access new items and dialogue in the reconstructed village.
