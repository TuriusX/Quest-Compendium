Tower of Zot

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The party ascends to rescue Rosa and defeat Golbez's minions

Cecil leads the party up the tower to rescue Rosa from Golbez in exchange for the Earth Crystal. After trading the crystal, the party must fight the Magus Sisters and Barbariccia before escaping the collapsing structure.

Items

  • Flame Mail MISSABLE: Chest on the 2nd floor
  • Flame Sword MISSABLE: Chest on the 5th floor
  • Hell Claw MISSABLE: Chest on the 5th floor

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes

BarbaricciaLightningnothing reliableSurrounds herself with a tornado barrier that must be broken by Kain's Jump.

Tips

  • Equip the Flame Mail and Flame Sword found here, but be mindful of enemies that absorb fire elements.
  • Have Kain Jump whenever Barbariccia enters her tornado state to cancel the barrier and prevent her counterattacks.
  • You cannot return to the Tower of Zot after completing it, so collect all treasure chests before reaching the top floor.
