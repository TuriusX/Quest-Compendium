The party enters the Tower of Babil from the Underworld to prevent Golbez's forces from firing the giant supercannon at the dwarves. Cecil, Yang, Rydia, and Rosa must fight their way up several floors, confront Doctor Lugae, and reach the cannon control room. The mission concludes with a sacrifice to stop the artillery, forcing the surviving members to escape to the surface.

Don't miss 0/3

Enemies
Flamehound
Weak to IceSteal / drop Bomb Fragment

Uses the powerful fire attack Blaze to damage the entire party

Lugae
Steal / drop Potion

Fights alongside his creation Barnabas and can fuse with it if Barnabas falls first

Lugaborg
Steal / drop Elixir

Doctor Lugae's mechanical second form who uses devastating attacks like Laser

Tips
  • Equip Cecil with the ice-elemental gear found in chests on the lower floors to exploit the fire-aligned enemies throughout the tower.
  • Have Rosa cast Silence on Doctor Lugae during the boss encounter to prevent him from inflicting status ailments on the party.
  • Be sure to collect all treasure chests on this ascent because the lower underground section of the tower cannot be re-entered later in the game.
