Land of Summons

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party explores the town within Feymarch

The Land of Summons, located in the Underworld, is the hidden subterranean home of the Eidolons. Cecil and his party can visit to explore, buy powerful equipment, and challenge the rulers of the Eidolons to earn new summons for Rydia. It can be accessed through a cave southwest of the Dwarven Castle that requires the Float spell to cross damaging floors safely.

Items

  • Phoenix Down: Treasure chest on the first floor
  • Hi-Potion: Treasure chest on the first floor
  • Cottage: Treasure chest on the second floor
  • Poison Axe: Treasure chest in the hidden treasure room accessed via the warp tile
  • Kiku-ichimonji: Treasure chest in the hidden treasure room accessed via the warp tile
  • Defender: Treasure chest in the hidden treasure room accessed via the warp tile
  • Rat Tail: Treasure chest in the hidden treasure room accessed via the warp tile

Secrets

  • Stepping onto the dark teleport pad near the entrance transports the party to a secluded treasury containing powerful weapons and the Rat Tail.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Defender, Fire Whip, and Angel Arrows
  • Armor Shop: Sells Aegis Shield, Genji Shield, Genji Helm, Genji Armor, and Genji Gloves
  • Item Shop: Sells Hi-Potion, Phoenix Down, Ether, Dry Ether, Cottage, and Remedy

Tips

  • Cast Float on the party before walking around the town to avoid taking damage from the glowing floor tiles.
  • Bring the Rat Tail found in the treasure room to the collector in the Mythril Cave near Silvera in the Overworld to exchange it for Adamantite.
  • Speaking with Queen Asura and King Leviathan inside the royal chamber initiates optional boss battles that grant Rydia their summoning powers upon defeat.
