Fabul Castle

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The party defends the castle from the Red Wings

Cecil and his companions arrive in Fabul to warn King Fabul of an impending attack by Baron's fleet. The party agrees to defend the castle and fights a consecutive series of battles against invading forces on the castle parapets and interior halls. After defending the throne room, the Red Wings seize the Wind Crystal, kidnap Rosa, and leave the party to regroup and depart by ship.

Items

  • Potion: Chest in the West Tower 1F
  • Tent: Chest in the West Tower 2F
  • Demon Shield: Chest in the West Tower 3F

Secrets

  • Stepping on a hidden switch on the floor near the throne reveals a passage to three hidden chests containing a Spider Silk, a Hermes Sandals, and a Bacchus's Wine.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
CaptainLeads groups of Baron troopers and commands them during the defense battles.
Baron SoldierBasic infantry fought alongside Captains; will attack recklessly if the Captain is defeated first.
GargoyleWinged monster deployed by Baron during the castle defense waves.

Shops and people

  • Fabul Weapon and Armor Shop: Sells equipment such as the Fire Claw, Ice Claw, Lightning Claw, and Demon armor pieces
  • Fabul Item Shop: Sells basic consumable supplies including Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Tents

Tips

  • Equip Cecil with the best defensive gear available and keep Edward in the back row to prevent him from taking heavy physical damage during the gauntlet of battles.
  • Do not use your most valuable recovery items on the final cutscene confrontation against Kain in the throne room, as that battle is scripted.
