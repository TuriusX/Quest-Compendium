Magnetic Cave

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The party retrieves the Earth Crystal from the Dark Elf

The party travels to the Magnetic Cave northeast of Troia to recover the Earth Crystal from the Dark Elf. Metallic armor and weapons are paralyzed here by a strong magnetic field, severely restricting what gear Cecil and others can safely wield. Once inside the innermost chamber, Edward uses his Twin Harp from Troia to play a melody that breaks the Dark Elf's magnetic hold, allowing you to re-equip standard gear and defeat him.

Items

  • Fairy Claws: In a treasure chest on floor B2F
  • Elixir: In a treasure chest on floor B3F
  • Spider Silk: In a treasure chest on floor B4F
  • Earth Crystal: Obtained automatically upon defeating the Dark Elf in the Crystal Room

Secrets

  • On floor B3F, walk through the false wall on the right side of the main path to access a hidden alcove containing chests.

Tips

  • Equip Cecil with a bow and non-metallic arrows or unequip his metallic gear entirely so he can act before the magnetic field is lifted.
  • Yang and Cid are excellent damage dealers here because claws and wooden hammers do not trigger the magnetic restriction.
  • Save your game at the save point on floor B3F or B4F before entering the Crystal Room to confront the Dark Elf.
