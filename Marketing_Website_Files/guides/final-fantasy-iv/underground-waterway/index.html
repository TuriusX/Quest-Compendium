Underground Waterway

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The path taken to reach Damcyan

Cecil and Rydia travel through this subterranean cavern alongside Tellah to reach the kingdom of Damcyan. The path consists of several flooded basement floors and waterfalls connected by stairways. Navigating the caves requires fighting water-dwelling monsters and culminates in an exit leading directly to the outskirts of Damcyan Castle.

Items

  • Potion: B1F, chest on a southern ledge
  • Tent: B1F, chest in the central pathway
  • Ether: B2F, chest on the eastern walkway
  • Ice Rod: B2F, chest hidden down a secluded path
  • Feathered Cap: B3F, chest near the northern stairs

Secrets

  • Hidden passage on B1F | Walk through the east wall on B1F to reach a secluded area with treasure chests.

Tips

  • Tellah has access to high-level elemental magic, making him ideal for targeting the elemental weaknesses of the monsters here.
  • Resting at the save point on B3F allows you to use a Tent to fully restore the party before tackling the Octomammoth boss fight.
