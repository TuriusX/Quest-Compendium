Mist Cave

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Cecil and Kain travel through to reach Mist

Cecil and Kain journey through the Mist Cave to deliver the Carnelian Signet to the village of Mist on the King of Baron's orders. Inside, an ominous voice warns the duo to turn back before challenging them to a fight near the exit. Defeat the cave's guardian to reach the exit and proceed onward to Mist.

Items

  • Potion: Chest on an elevated ledge in the first open cavern area
  • Eye Drops: Chest located along the pathway leading toward the upper section
  • Tent: Chest found on a side path before reaching the boss chamber

Secrets

  • A hidden passageway through the east rock wall near the center of the cave leads directly to an isolated treasure chest.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes

Mist DragonThe boss of the cave; it transforms into an invulnerable mist form during battle.

Tips

  • Cease all attacks immediately when the Mist Dragon turns into mist, as any strike will trigger a devastating Breath counterattack that hits the entire party.
  • Use Kain's Jump ability while the Mist Dragon is in its solid physical form to deal heavy burst damage.
