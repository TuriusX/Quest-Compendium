Lunar Subterrane

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party explores the final dungeon

Cecil and his party venture deep beneath the Moon's surface through the Lunar Subterrane to reach the final chamber where the evil entity Zeromus resides. The dungeon is a massive, multi-floor gauntlet containing the strongest random encounters and the game's ultimate equipment guarded by optional super bosses. Navigating through multiple hidden paths and sub-areas, the party must defeat the bosses to prepare for the final confrontation.

Items

  • Murasame: B3F, found inside a treasure chest guarded by the boss White Dragon
  • Crystal Shield: B5F, found inside a treasure chest
  • Crystal Mail: B5F, found inside a treasure chest guarded by Behemoth
  • Crystal Helm: B5F, found inside a treasure chest
  • Crystal Gloves: B5F, found inside a treasure chest
  • Protect Ring: B5F, found inside a treasure chest
  • White Spear: B5F, inside a chest guarded by the boss Plague (Plague Horror)
  • Ribbon: B7F, inside a chest guarded by two Lunasaurs
  • Ragnarok: B7F, inside a chest reached via a warp tile and guarded by the boss Dark Bahamut
  • Masamune: B8F, inside a chest guarded by the boss Ogopogo
  • Elixir: B1F, found in a treasure chest
  • Fuma Shuriken: B5F, found in a treasure chest

Secrets

  • B7F contains an invisible path across the void that leads to a save room and the warp point to the Ragnarok sword altar.
  • B5F has several false walls that allow walking through the rock face to reach chests containing the Crystal equipment set.

Tips

  • Equip Cecil and Rosa with Ribbons found in B7F to prevent Zeromus and other endgame encounters from inflicting lethal status ailments.
  • Cast Float on the entire party before walking onto floors in lower depths to avoid taking continuous damage from floor traps.
  • During the Plague fight for the White Spear, cast Haste on your strongest physical attackers to defeat it before the 10-second Death counter reaches zero.
  • In the battle against Dark Bahamut, cast Reflect on party members to bounce Megaflare back at the boss.
