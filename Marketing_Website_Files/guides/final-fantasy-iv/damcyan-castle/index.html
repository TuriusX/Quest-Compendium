Damcyan Castle

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Cecil arrives to find the castle under attack

Cecil and Rydia arrive at Damcyan Castle just as the Red Wings finish a devastating aerial bombardment. Inside, they discover that Anna, Tellah's daughter, has been mortally wounded, leading to a grief-fueled confrontation with Edward. After the tragic events unfold, Edward joins the party and provides a Hovercraft to cross the nearby shoals.

Items

  • Tent: In a chest on the 1st floor of the castle
  • Potion: In a chest on the 1st floor of the castle

Secrets

  • Walk along the exterior perimeter wall on the eastern side of the first floor to access a hidden doorway leading to the dungeon treasury chests.

Tips

  • Do not leave the castle right away after Edward joins; search the lower floor and dungeon for recovery items and equipment before boarding the Hovercraft.
  • You will not encounter random enemy battles while exploring Damcyan Castle during this visit.
