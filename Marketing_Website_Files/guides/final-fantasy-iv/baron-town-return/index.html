Baron Town (Return)

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · Party visits the town to get the Hovercraft

Cecil and his companions return to the town of Baron to infiltrate the castle and obtain an airship, but find the town heavily guarded. Entering the local inn leads to an encounter with Yang, who is suffering from amnesia under the influence of Baron's forces. Defeating Yang restores his memory, after which he gives the party the Baron Key to unlock previously locked doors in town and enter the castle through the waterway.

Items

  • Baron Key: Received from Yang after defeating him and the guards in the inn

Secrets

  • Enter the waterway canal in the northwest part of town and walk along the water down to a hidden clearing on the eastern edge to find hidden items.

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
Baron GuardPotionFought alongside Yang inside the inn; easily dispatched with physical attacks

Shops and people

  • Inn: Free resting after the encounter with Yang inside
  • Baron Item Shop: Sells standard restorative items such as Potions, Hi-Potions, Phoenix Downs, and Gold Needles

Tips

  • Use the Baron Key on the locked door in the weapon/armor shop to access hidden treasure chests.
  • Use the Baron Key on the locked door in the western part of town to gain access to the underground waterway leading into Baron Castle.
