Baron Castle

FINAL FANTASY IV guide · The game begins here with Cecil's mission

Baron Castle is the starting location of the game where Cecil returns after stripping the Mysidians of the Water Crystal. Stripped of his command of the Red Wings by the King, Cecil prepares to journey to the village of Mist alongside Kain. Players must explore the castle, speak with key allies like Rosa and Cid, rest in Cecil's quarters, and receive the Carnelian Signet before departing.

Items

  • Ether: Pot inside the east tower, first floor
  • Tent: Chest inside the east tower, second floor
  • Potion: Chest inside the east tower, second floor

Secrets

  • Press a small hidden switch on the wall near the entrance of the eastern guardhouse to open a doorway leading to several chests in the basement.

Shops and people

  • Cecil's Bed: Free full HP and MP restoration when Cecil rests in his bedchamber in the western tower

Tips

  • Speak to Rosa and Cid before heading to Cecil's bed in the western tower, as resting triggers the story sequence to advance to the next morning.
  • Be sure to loot the accessible chests inside the towers and outer courtyard before leaving, giving Cecil and Kain a supply buffer for the road to Mist.
