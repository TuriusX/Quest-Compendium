  • None: Chapter Select unlocks upon beating the game, allowing all missed items, odd jobs, minigames, and achievements to be completed without starting a new save file.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete the main story on Normal or Easy, focusing on enjoying the narrative while completing local side quests (Odd Jobs), World Intel tasks (Towers, Fiend Intel, Lifesprings, Divine Sanctuaries), and Queen's Blood matches as each region opens (Grasslands, Junon, Corel, Gongaga, Cosmo Canyon, Nibel, and the Meridian Ocean).

  2. Step 2

    In Junon during Chapter 4, recruit all Midgar Seventh Infantry units and earn the highest performance evaluation during the parade to secure the "Stealing the Show" trophy.

    Also in this step: Stealing the Show

  3. Step 3

    Throughout Chapters 1 to 12, maintain high relationship status with all party members (dialogue choices, synergy attacks, and region odd jobs) to prepare for the Gold Saucer date in Chapter 12 and work toward "Critica-Link".

  4. Step 4

    Reach Chapter 12 and 13 to unlock full open-world mobility with the Tiny Bronco; backtrack to finish all regional mini-games at maximum rank (including Chocobo Racing, Fort Condor, Run Wild, Queen's Blood, and Cactuar Crush) to collect all Hotel Del Sol collectibles for Johnny's Treasure Trove.

  5. Step 5

    Defeat all secret regional bosses and summon challenges via Chadley's Combat Simulator, unlock and conquer Gilgamesh Island, and craft the Genji equipment.

  6. Step 6

    Clear Chapter 14 to finish the main story, unlocking Chapter Select and Hard difficulty.

  7. Step 7

    Use Chapter Select to reach Level 70, master all weapon abilities, max out all essential Materia, and complete any remaining dates or relationship requirements.

  8. Step 8

    Complete all remaining Combat Simulator brutal and legendary solo challenges, including the "Rulers of the Outer Worlds" and "To Be a Hero" trials.

  9. Step 9

    Play through all 14 chapters on Hard difficulty using Chapter Select to collect all remaining manuscript folios and unlock the final story-related Hard mode achievements to obtain the Platinum Trophy.
