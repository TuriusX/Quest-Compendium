Scarstu Debris Field

Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart guide · Visiting Zurkie's Gastropub for the first time
← All areas in this guide

Rivet travels to Zurkie's Gastropub in the Scarstu Debris Field to meet an informant who knows how to contact the Resistance. Upon arrival, weapons are disabled inside the pub, forcing Rivet to enter the Battleplex arena to win parts for her ship and earn the trust of Pierre Le Fer. Completing the introductory Bronze Cup challenge against the Spargas forces rewards the Hurlshot and allows Rivet to progress.

Enemies
Pierre Le Fer

A pirate captain who sends waves of goons at Rivet in the arena rather than fighting directly in this visit

Spargas Goon

Basic pirate foot soldiers that rush in groups using melee strikes and blasters

Spargas Bouncer

Heavily armored brute pirates that fire explosive mortar rounds and leap toward you

Shops and people
  • Ms. Zurkon: Sells weapons, ammunition upgrades, and armor restocking right outside the entrance to Zurkie's Gastropub
  • Battleplex Arena Terminal: Offers the Bronze Cup battle challenges that award Raritanium, Bolts, weapons, and armor pieces

Tips
  • Use the arena challenges to level up newly acquired weapons rapidly, as defeated waves grant full experience without penalty on defeat.
  • Weapons are holstered automatically while inside the pub, so explore freely to chat with patrons and listen to the jukebox without combat danger.
