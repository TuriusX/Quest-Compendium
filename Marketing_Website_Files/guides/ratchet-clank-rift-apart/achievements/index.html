Recommended order
  1. Play through the Prologue in Megalopolis while learning the basic mechanics.
  2. Clear Nefarious City, ensuring you defeat enemies while wall-running and gather CraiggerBears, Spybots, and Gold Bolts.
  3. Explore Sargasso to rescue the Morts, complete Trudi's optional questline, and collect your first pocket dimension armors.
  4. Travel to Scarstu Debris Field to complete early Battleplex arena challenges for the Zurkon Jr. trophies and acquire the Pixelizer.
  5. Roam Savali using the hoverboots, activate the dimensional shrines, complete the Lorbs quest, and solve Clank's anomaly puzzles.
  6. Progress through Blizar Prime, hitting Blizon crystals to shift dimensions, completing the Chef Tulio side quest, and collecting the CraiggerBear.
  7. Clear Torren IV by completing the rails sequence, earning the Hurlshot, and grabbing all local collectibles.
  8. Explore Cordelion, using the dimensional crystals to switch between the derelict and functional states while looting the underwater section.
  9. Complete Ardolis by passing the pirate trials, solving Glitch's virus terminals, and grabbing the local armor pieces.
  10. Infiltrate Viceron to free the prisoners, making sure to hit the CraiggerBear in the detention cell block.
  11. Make Return Visits to Sargasso, Scarstu Debris Field, Savali, Blizar Prime, Torren IV, Cordelion, Ardolis, and Viceron to clean up any remaining Gold Bolts, Spybots, Lorbs, and Battleplex arena matches.
  12. Trigger the point of no return at Megalopolis (Return Visit) to finish the main campaign and defeat the final boss.
  13. Start Challenge Mode (New Game+) to visit the first vendor and purchase the Bouncer and Pixelizer to finish unlocking all weapons.
