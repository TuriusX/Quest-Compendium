Castle Battlements

Resident Evil 4 guide · Fighting El Gigante
← All areas in this guide

In Chapter 8, Leon must navigate the Castle Battlements while an Armored El Gigante bombards the pathways from a distant tower by hurling massive debris. Leon must duck behind cover, drop down into the lower fortification levels, and raise a counterweight cannon to clear rubble and defeat the giant. Once the cannon is raised to the top level, Leon can aim and fire it directly at the giant to eliminate the threat and blast open the exit gate.

Enemies
Armored El Gigante
Weak to Cannon fire

Pelts Leon with boulders from afar; cannot be damaged effectively with standard firearms, requiring a direct hit from the battlements cannon.

Zealot
Weak to Head, exposed PlagaSteal / drop Random resources or Pesetas

Patrols the interior and exterior walkways, attempting to grapple Leon or push him into the giant's bombardment zones.

Shield Zealot
Weak to Exposed feet, explosivesSteal / drop Random resources or Pesetas

Carries wooden shields that block frontal fire; vulnerable to shotguns at close range or rifle shots to the exposed legs.

Tips
  • Time your sprints across open roof sections right after a volley of debris shatters, as the giant takes several seconds to ready another boulder.
  • You do not need to fight every Zealot on the upper open walkways; the giant's flying debris will crush them if they are caught in the impact area.
  • Aim the raised cannon directly at the Armored El Gigante first to kill it instantly, then swivel the cannon to destroy the reinforced exit gate.
