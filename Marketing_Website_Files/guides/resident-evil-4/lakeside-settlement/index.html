Lakeside Settlement

Resident Evil 4 guide · Arrival in the village
← All areas in this guide

Leon travels through the Lakeside Settlement during Chapter 1 and must navigate past hostile Ganados and bear traps to enter the large house at the back. Inside the house's basement, he discovers a tied-up Luis Serra after moving a bookcase and fending off a surprise attacker. This area is revisited by boat in Chapter 4, opening access to locked doors, side paths, and the Forest Altar.

Tips
  • Tread carefully in the tall grass and mud around the shacks, as several bear traps are hidden along the main pathways.
  • Do not shoot the hanging lantern over the well until you shoot the wooden prop to drop the well cover, or the treasure inside will fall into the filth and lose value.
