Cape Howl (Another World)

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Visiting the grave of Serge

Serge arrives here shortly after crossing over to Another World, discovering a gravestone bearing his own name on the cliff's edge. While he is reeling from the discovery, the Acacia Dragoons led by Karsh arrive to capture him, initiating a sequence of battles. Kid makes her first appearance during the fray to aid Serge, after which the player must choose whether to allow her to join the party.

Items

  • Bone: Found by inspecting the glowing spot near the cliff edge path

Tips

  • Agreeing to let Kid join right after the battle provides Serge with the Astral Knox (Tele-Porter) and her equipment immediately, but declining her three times allows you to recruit Leena and the dog Poshul back in Arni Village instead.
  • Target Solt and Peppor first during the boss fight to reduce the number of physical attacks hitting your party each turn before focusing down Karsh.
