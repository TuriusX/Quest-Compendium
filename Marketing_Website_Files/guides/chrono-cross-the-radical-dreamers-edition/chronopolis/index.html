Chronopolis

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Entering the research facility

Chronopolis is a high-tech research facility located within the Sea of Eden in Another World. Serge and his party infiltrate the complex to unravel the mysteries behind the timeline shifts and reach the central computer. You must navigate multiple floors, deactivate automated security systems, and make your way to the top to confront Fate.

Items

  • Yellow Brooch: Treasure chest on the 1st floor dock area
  • Rainbow Shell: Chest in the dock area reached via elevator/walkway
  • Nostrum: Chest on the 2nd floor
  • White Brooch: Chest in the 3rd floor office
  • Rainbow Shell: Chest on the 4th floor laboratory
  • Card Key: Received from the researcher projection after defeating PolisPolice

Secrets

  • Releasing the sub-level security lock: Return to the dock area and pilot the small cleanup robot into the water ducts to access the computer terminal that unlocks the staircase to B1.

Tips

  • Equip Black elements and Trap elements against the Fate boss to absorb or counter its countdown cycle of devastating spells.
  • Talk to the hologram ghosts wandering the corridors to learn deep lore explaining the connection between El Nido, Chrono Trigger, and the year 2400 AD.
