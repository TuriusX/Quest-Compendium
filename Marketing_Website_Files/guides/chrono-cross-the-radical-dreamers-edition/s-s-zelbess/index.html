S.S. Zelbess

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Boarding the ghost ship
← All areas in this guide

Serge and his party board the cruise cruise ship S.S. Zelbess in the Home World to find a way into the Sea of Eden. You must deal with Captain Fargo, clear out a sudden monster infestation on the Grand Slam stage, and help Nikki put on a concert to purify Marbule. Navigating the ship involves clearing out obstacles, turning into a cat briefly, and challenging Fargo at the casino.

Achievements here
Grand ChampionEnter the monster-battling arena tournament hosted by Janice aboard the S.S. Zelbess and win all three rounds.
Immoral CompassSpin the compass pointer in the S.S. Zelbess casino minigame to land on the top prize and accumulate 10,000 points.
