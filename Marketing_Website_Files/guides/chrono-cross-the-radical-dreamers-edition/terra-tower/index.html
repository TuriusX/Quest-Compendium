Terra Tower

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Ascending the tower of the Dragon God

Terra Tower is the final major dungeon of the game, raised into the sky after the defeat of the Six Dragons. Serge and his party must ascend through its floating ruins, confront the guardian manifestations of the elements, and reach the summit. At the top, you defeat the TimeDevourer in its dragon form to obtain the Chrono Cross element.

Items

  • Rainbow Axe MISSABLE: In a chest on the exterior stairways of the tower
  • Betta Carotene MISSABLE: In a chest inside the library area
  • Pack of Lies MISSABLE: Found in a chest in the winding branch-like paths
  • Holy Light MISSABLE: In a chest along the ascent path
  • UltraNova MISSABLE: Found in a treasure chest in the ruined structures

Tips

  • Equip the elemental absorption Plate armor matching the innate element of each elemental guardian to negate their strongest attacks.
  • Bring Trap elements for high-tier summons and powerful elements like Holy Light or UltraNova when battling the elemental bosses.
  • A Prophet of Time appears inside the library room to explain deep lore regarding the history of Chrono Cross and Chrono Trigger.
  • Before engaging the final sequence atop Terra Tower, ensure you have the Chrono Trigger item and the completed Chrono Cross element if you intend to pursue the true ending.
