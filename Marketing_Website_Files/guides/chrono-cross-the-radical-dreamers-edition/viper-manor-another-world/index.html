Viper Manor (Another World)

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Infiltrating the manor as Lynx

Lynx returns to Viper Manor in Another World, which has been occupied and overrun by Porre military forces. Your primary objective is to infiltrate the manor to rescue Riddel from custody before she can be executed. Navigating the fortress requires sneaking through the lower levels, sewers, and private quarters while battling Porre soldiers.

Items

  • Prison Key: Defeat the guards holding Riddel in the manor dungeon
  • Parlor Key: Found inside the bucket in the underground cell area

Secrets

  • Check the hidden treasure niche behind the dragon statue in the upper office to find equipment left behind by the Acacian dragoons.

Tips

  • Equipping anti-Black or anti-Yellow elements helps mitigate the high physical and tech damage dealt by the Porre military units.
  • Revisit the laboratory and library rooms during this rescue operation to pick up neglected supplies, as patrol routes leave several side doors unguarded.
