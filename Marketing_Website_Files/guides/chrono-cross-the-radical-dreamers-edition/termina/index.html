Termina

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Arriving at the port town

Serge and his companion arrive in the bustling port town of Termina to find a way to infiltrate Viper Manor. While exploring, you can recruit several different characters and learn about the local culture and current political climate. Your primary objective here is to choose one of three specific guides who will help you break into the manor grounds.

Items

  • Profiteers Purse: Inside the secret room behind the counter in the weapon smith's house
  • Tea for Three frame: Speak to the fortune teller after examining the small plant pot beside her tent
  • Hero's Medal MISSABLE: Found in the grass near the grave of the town hero at the top of the cliff stairs, or given by Pierre depending on interactions
  • Brass Pick MISSABLE: Given to you by Korcha at the docks if you talk to him with Leena in your party

Secrets

  • You can enter the smithy's back room by talking to the smith Zappa and inspecting the inner door to find hidden treasure.
  • Inspecting the statue of General Viper in the plaza causes the shoe-shine boy to offer to polish Serge's shoes for a small fee, opening minor extra dialogue.

Shops and people

  • Termina General Store: Sells consumable recovery items such as Tablets, Antidotes, and basic offensive element materials
  • Zappa's Smithy: Forges bronze and iron weapons, armor, and helmets using raw materials and gold
  • Lisa's Element Shop: Sells basic consumable elements and status-healing consumables

Tips

  • You must choose strictly one guide out of Guile, Pierre, or Nikki to infiltrate Viper Manor, and your choice permanently locks you out of the other two for the rest of this playthrough.
  • Talk to Glenn near the entrance flower stall to trigger an interaction that sets up his character before you decide on your manor route.
