Shadow Forest

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Serge enters the underground caverns beneath the manor ruins ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Shadow Forest serves as an infiltration route to the underground cavern systems and well beneath Viper Manor. Traveling through requires tracking down Nikki, navigating watery passages, and luring small creatures to clear away a massive sleeping beast. In Home World, the subterranean channels leading to the manor ruins can also be accessed to find rare items and complete side objectives like recruiting Funguy.

Key fights
Zoah, Solt, and Peppor

Enemies: Zoah, Solt, Peppor

Threats: Heavy physical blows from Zoah alongside element-teaching combos from Solt and Peppor

Weaknesses and resistances: Yellow (Zoah), Yellow (Solt and Peppor)

Tactics: Focus attacks to eliminate the weaker squires Solt and Peppor first before countering Zoah with Green element magic

Rewards: Copper, Tablet, Knee Pad

Enemies

Tips
  • In the remaster, you can activate the built-in battle boost or speed-up features to quickly kite the colored monsters without getting caught by roaming woodland enemies.
