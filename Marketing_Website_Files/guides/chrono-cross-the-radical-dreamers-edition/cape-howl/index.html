Cape Howl

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Meeting Kid and fighting Karsh

After crossing into Another World, Serge visits Cape Howl to inspect the grave of his alternate self. He is ambushed by Karsh and the Acacia Dragoons, but Kid arrives to join forces with him in battle. Defeating the soldiers allows the party to safely leave the cliff and proceed to Arni Village.

Items

  • Bone: Found inside a treasure chest on the lower ledges of the cliff

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes

SoltYellowIvory HelmetAccompanies Karsh and takes actions based on Peppor's instructions.
PepporYellowIvory MailFights alongside Karsh and prompts Solt during battle.

Tips

  • Focus down Solt and Peppor first to cut down incoming attacks before turning your focus to Karsh.
  • Kid will ask to join Serge's party immediately after the fight; you can accept her offer now or decline if you want to recruit Leena in Arni Village instead.
