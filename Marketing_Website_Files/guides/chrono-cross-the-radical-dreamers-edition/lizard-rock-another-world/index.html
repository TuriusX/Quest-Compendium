After arriving in Another World and finding that Serge's existence is unrecognized, you travel through this rocky pass to reach the mainland. Unlike Home World, there are no Komodo Pups to hunt, and the valley is patrolled by wild reptiles and birds instead. Your objective is simply to fight your way through the screens and exit north toward Cape Howl or south toward Arni.

Items

0/3

Enemies
SandScorpion
Weak to GreenSteal / drop Feather

Hard-hitting arachnid that can inflict poison.

Beach Bum
Weak to BlueSteal / drop Bone

Soft shelled creature that frequently casts low-level status and attack elements.

Komodo Pup
Weak to BlueSteal / drop Fang

Wild lizard roaming the area, significantly weaker than its Home World counterparts.

Opah Fish
Weak to RedSteal / drop Bone

Floating fish enemy usually encountered in watery paths.

Tips
  • Since Serge is alone during this trek until other party members join, equip defensive elements like Cure and keep his HP high after every battle.
  • You do not need to solve the rock-dropping or chasing puzzles from Home World to navigate through this version of the area.
