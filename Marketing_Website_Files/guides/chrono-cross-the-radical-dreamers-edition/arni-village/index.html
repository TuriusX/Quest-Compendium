Arni Village

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Start of the game

Arni Village is the peaceful hometown of the protagonist, Serge, where the story begins. Serge must speak with his mother Marge and his friend Leena, who tasks him with collecting Komodo Scales at Cape Opassa. Before departing for the cape, exploring the village allows you to gather equipment, meet early allies, and learn basic game mechanics.

Items

  • Sea Skiff Frame MISSABLE: Speak to the fisherman in the room beneath the village elder's house
  • Komodo Scale MISSABLE: Given by a fisherman standing on the docks if you choose the second dialogue option during your conversation
  • Ivory Helmet: Found inside a chest in the room beneath the village chief's house
  • Heckran Bone: Search under the bed inside the village chief's house

Secrets

  • Feed the Komodo Dragon pet in the cage at the back of the village 100 times to receive the Iron Pan weapon.
  • Give the Heckran Bone to Poshul, the dog running around the village square, to recruit her to your party immediately.

Shops and people

  • Arni Merchant: Sells early elements including Cure, Photon, Fireball, AquaBeam, Bushwhacker, and MagmaBurst
  • Arni Blacksmith: Can forge basic Bronze equipment and weapons using materials found in the wild

Tips

  • Accepting the task from Leena at the village docks sets the main objective to hunt three Komodo Dragons at Cape Lizard.
  • Talking to the poet on the lower path reveals background world lore and foreshadows the parallel dimension theme.
