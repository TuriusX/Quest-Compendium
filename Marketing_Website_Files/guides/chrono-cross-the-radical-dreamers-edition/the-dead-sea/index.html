The Dead Sea

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Entering the ruins of the future

Serge and his party enter the Dead Sea in Home World to find the frozen wasteland of a destroyed future and reach the Sea of Eden. You must navigate across ruins of ruined cities, a derelict highway, and an ancient research tower to reach the frozen flame. At the deep core of the area, you confront the apparitions of Crono, Marle, and Lucca before battling Miguel to release the distortion.

Items

  • Medical Fruit MISSABLE: Chest in the Highway ruins
  • Hell-Bound MISSABLE: Chest inside the City ruins
  • Resistance Belt MISSABLE: Chest along the path in the Tower of Geddon
  • Station Pass MISSABLE: Found on a ghostly train passenger inside the Tower of Geddon
  • Screwy MISSABLE: Inside the theater room of the Tower of Geddon
  • HolyLight MISSABLE: Trappable element from the boss Miguel

Secrets

  • You can activate an arcade machine inside the mall ruins in the Tower of Geddon to hear classic sound effects and music cues from Chrono Trigger.

Tips

  • Equip the Trap element HolyLight before confronting Miguel to safely absorb one of his most devastating attacks and acquire the rare level 6 element.
  • The entire Dead Sea area is permanently destroyed and inaccessible after defeating Miguel, so collect all chests before fighting him.
  • Bring strong Black element users like Lynx or Harle to deal heavy damage against Miguel's White innate.
