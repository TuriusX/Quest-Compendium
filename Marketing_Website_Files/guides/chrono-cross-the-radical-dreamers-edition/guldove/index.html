Guldove

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Seeking the Hydra Humour

Serge awakens here in the clinic after escaping Viper Manor, learning that Kid has been poisoned by Lynx's dagger. To save her life, you must make a critical story decision to either seek out the Hydra Humour or refuse to help, which determines your route and available recruits. You need to gather information from the village's Shaman and prepare for the journey ahead.

Items

  • Astral Knox: Given by Korcha at the docks after speaking with him about Kid's condition
  • Pelvic Bone: Received from the trader near the dock after examining her wares and speaking to her

Shops and people

  • Guldove Element Shop: Sells offensive and curative Elements including Cure, Bushwhacker, and Fireball
  • Guldove Equipment Shop: Sells Bronze and Iron tier weapons and armors

Tips

  • The dialogue choice given when talking to Korcha regarding whether to save Kid branches the recruitment paths permanently for this playthrough, so choose deliberately.
  • Be sure to talk to Macha and Mel around the village to learn details that will unlock additional character recruitments later in the story.
