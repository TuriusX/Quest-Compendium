Hydra Marshes

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Hunting the Hydra

Serge travels to the Another World version of Hydra Marshes on the "Save Kid" route to harvest Hydra Humour to cure Kid's poison. You must navigate toxic waterways and defeat the territorial dwarf clan to reach the last surviving Hydra. Defeating the Hydra secures the cure and completes the primary objective of this storyline branch.

Items

  • Hydra Humour MISSABLE: Dropped automatically after defeating the Hydra boss
  • Beeba Flute: Given by a Beeba NPC after defeating it in combat
  • Safety Gear: Given by an explorer near the entrance

Tips

  • Equip Green innate elements such as Bushwhacker or AeroSaucer, as most enemies and the Hydra itself are Yellow innate.
  • Bring plenty of Antidotes or brace with the Green element Purify, as the swamp enemies frequently inflict the Poison status effect.
