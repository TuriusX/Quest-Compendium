Lizard Rock

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Searching for Komodo scales

Serge travels to Lizard Rock to hunt down three Komodo Pups and gather Komodo Scales for Leena. Each pup must be cornered or trapped using different environmental puzzles across the cliffs and waterways. Defeating all three prompts the arrival of Mama Komodo, the area's boss.

Items

  • Bone: Found inside a treasure chest on the lower beach path
  • Ivory Helmet: Inside a chest atop the high ledge accessible via the green rock path
  • Tablet: Found inside a chest near the cavern tunnel

Enemies

Tips

  • To catch the first Komodo Pup, block its escape route through the lower hollow log using a heavy rock.
  • For the second Komodo Pup running along the upper loop, wait on the ledge above and jump down directly onto it.
  • Equip Serge and your companions with Red innate elements like Fireball, as Mama Komodo and the pups are Blue innate and take extra damage from fire.
