Terra Tower (Another World)

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Returning for the final battle

Serge and his party ascend this resurrected ancient floating city to stop the corruption of the Dragon God and defeat the Time Devourer. You must solve path puzzles, defeat six elemental mini-bosses representing the merged Dragon Gods, and reach the pinnacle to face the ultimate threats. Climbing through the tower's interior and ruined past visions leads directly into the climax of the game.

Items

  • Betta Carotene MISSABLE: In a chest inside the ruins section
  • Pack of Lies MISSABLE: In a treasure chest along the ascending branch path
  • Defender MISSABLE: Chest found on the upper platforms before reaching the frozen library
  • Rainbow Shell MISSABLE: Chest in the library ruins area
  • Spectral Glove MISSABLE: Found in a chest along the exterior walkways
  • Earth Charm MISSABLE: Inside a chest guarded near the yellow elemental pathway
  • Holy Light MISSABLE: Treasure chest hidden behind the roots in the lower ascent

Secrets

  • Equipping the Chrono Cross element on Serge allows you to unlock the true ending during the final confrontation by playing the elemental colors in the exact sequence revealed in the final dungeon.
  • Inspecting the floating glowing orbs scattered across the tower ruins reveals environmental lore and projections left behind by the ancient Chronopolis scientists and Belthasar.

Tips

  • Equip the elemental brooches or plates you steal or find here to absorb specific high-level magic cast by the elemental dragon bosses.
  • Return to the world map via the teleporter beam at the base if you need to forge Rainbow equipment before triggering the final sequence of battles.
  • Use summons or high-tier opposite-element magic to quickly eliminate each of the six elemental guardian bosses guarding the path up the spire.
