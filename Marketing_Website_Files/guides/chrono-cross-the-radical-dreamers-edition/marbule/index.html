Marbule

CHRONO CROSS: THE RADICAL DREAMERS EDITION guide · Visiting the village of the demi-humans

Marbule is the home of the demi-humans and exists in two starkly different states across the parallel worlds. In Another World, it lies abandoned and overrun by phantom lagoon monsters, while in Home World, its citizens were displaced to work on the S.S. Zelbess before a concert helps restore the village. Visiting here is critical for lifting the curse on the demi-humans and acquiring powerful endgame summons and equipment.

Items

  • Master Hammer MISSABLE: Purchased from the merchant demi-human inside the cave after Marbule's restoration in Home World
  • Black Carib Tear: Inside a treasure chest in the village

Secrets

  • Take Fargo to speak with his alternate counterpart aboard the S.S. Zelbess to initiate the concert that enables clearing the ghosts from Marbule in Another World.
  • Defeat the Black Dragon sleeping in Marbule's cave in Another World while it is awake to claim the Black Relic and BlackPlate.

Tips

  • You must defeat all nine phantom enemies in Another World's Marbule without leaving the screen after the concert triggers, or the quest will not properly clear.
  • Bring Green innate elements and characters to easily clear the Lagoonates infesting the cursed village.
