Lemoyne

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the state containing Rhodes and Clemens Point

Lemoyne is a southern state featuring the dusty plantation town of Rhodes, the sprawling swamps of Bayou Nwa, and the industrial city of Saint Denis. The Van der Linde gang sets up camp at Clemens Point along Flat Iron Lake during Chapter 3, becoming entangled in a blood feud between the Gray and Braithwaite families. Players can hunt unique swamp wildlife, track down serial killer clues, capture bounties, and uncover the historical wealth of crumbling antebellum estates.

Items

  • Semi-Automatic Shotgun: Hidden in the basement of Chez Porter, accessed via a ladder inside the homestead
  • Double-Action Revolver: Inside a lockbox under the bed at Lonnie's Shack south of Emerald Station
  • Lancaster Repeater: In the gunsmith's basement in Rhodes, rescued during a robbery interaction
  • Rare Rolling Block Rifle MISSABLE: Dropped by a sniper in the barn loft during the Chapter 3 mission "Magicians for Sport"
  • Catherine Braithwaite's Brooch: Found on the charred corpse of Catherine Braithwaite at the ruins of Braithwaite Manor after the mission "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern"

Secrets

  • The Strange Man's cabin can be found in the swamps of Bayall Edge, containing a painting that progressively finishes as Arthur's story advances.
  • A tiny church sized for miniature inhabitants is hidden off the main road in Lakay within Bayou Nwa.
  • A Confederate gold bar is hidden inside a lockbox within the burned ruins of the Braithwaite Manor main house.
  • The Ghost of Agnes Dowd appears on foggy nights in the Bluewater Marsh, crying and shouting out dialogue that plays across several encounters.

Shops and people

  • Rhodes Gunsmith: Firearms, ammunition, weapon customizations, and hunting supplies
  • Rhodes General Store: Provisions, tonics, clothing, and hunting gear
  • Rhodes Fence: Black market goods, crafting recipes, weapon upgrades, and purchasable dynamite
  • Saint Denis Trapper: Custom clothing, reinforced equipment, and unique saddles crafted from perfect pelts
  • Saint Denis Tailor: High-end outfits, fine coats, vests, and exclusive city apparel

Tips

  • Equip a shotgun whenever traveling through Bayou Nwa at night, as Night Folk will rush you quickly without showing up as red dots on the radar until they attack.
  • Avoid sprinting on horseback through shallow swamp waters to prevent your mount from bucking you off near hidden American Alligators.
  • When riding through southern Lemoyne, watch the road ahead for tipped-over wagons or burning carts, as they reliably indicate a Lemoyne Raider ambush.
