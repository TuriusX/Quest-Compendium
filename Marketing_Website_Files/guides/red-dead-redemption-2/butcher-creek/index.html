Butcher Creek

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 6, visited during missions near Annesburg

Butcher Creek is an impoverished, insular settlement located in the Roanoke Ridge region of New Hanover, situated along the Kamassa River. The locals are heavily deformed and sick due to industrial runoff, making them extremely hostile and distrusting toward outsiders. Players primarily engage with this area through the Stranger mission strand "The Wisdom of the Elders," investigating the source of the village's curse.

Items

  • Wide-Blade Knife: Found stuck into a wooden post in an abandoned mine shaft near Butcher Creek (associated with "The Wisdom of the Elders")

Secrets

  • Glowing pentagram under the wooden cabin floorboards at the edge of the village, visible only between 4:00 AM and 5:00 AM.

Tips

  • Do not loot fallen bodies within sight of the villagers, as this immediately triggers town-wide hostility and causes them to swarm you with melee weapons and shotguns.
  • You will quickly lose Honor if you fight the residents, so leave the settlement immediately if you accidentally antagonize them rather than shooting back.
