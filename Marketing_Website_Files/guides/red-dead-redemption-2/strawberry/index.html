Strawberry

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 1, John Marston's arrival

John Marston arrives in Strawberry with Abigail and Jack looking to start a clean, lawful life while seeking honest work. In town, John visits the local general store to ask about employment, which eventually directs him to a job opening at Pronghorn Ranch. While in town, players can explore the mountain settlement, pick up minor tasks, and use the local amenities.

Items

  • Wanted Poster: Hanging on the wall inside the Strawberry Sheriff's office

Secrets

  • Underneath the porch of the Strawberry General Store, John can look through a small barred cellar window to discover an illegal moonshine operation, which unlocks the ability to rob the store's side business.

Shops and people

  • Strawberry General Store: Provisions, canned goods, tonics, ammo, and clothing
  • Strawberry Welcome Center: Lodging, baths, and beds to rest
  • Strawberry Stables: Horses, saddlery, horse care items, and stabling services

Tips

  • Avoid drawing weapons or galloping recklessly through the narrow, crowded wooden bridges in town to prevent incurring an unnecessary bounty early in the Epilogue.
