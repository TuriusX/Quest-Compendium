Scarlet Meadows

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the region surrounding Rhodes

Scarlet Meadows is a sun-baked, red-dirt region in Lemoyne dominated by the town of Rhodes, the Braithwaite Manor plantation, and Caliga Hall. During Chapter 3, the Van der Linde gang sets up camp nearby at Clemens Point and tries to manipulate the two feuding local plantation families. Players spend their time navigating town disputes, dealing with hostile local factions, exploring abandoned battlegrounds, and robbing stagecoaches.

Items

  • Lancaster Repeater: Basement gunsmith robbery in Rhodes
  • Semi-Automatic Shotgun: Inside the basement of the house at Watson's Cabin, northwest of Rhodes across the region border
  • Catherine Braithwaite's Brooch MISSABLE: Found on Catherine Braithwaite's charred body at Braithwaite Manor after the mission "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern"

Secrets

  • You can find a hidden moonshine still and a captive boy calling for help through a barred window in the Rhodes Gunsmith basement, which unlocks a robbery prompt when investigated.
  • Gold Bar located inside a lockbox underneath the burned rubble of Braithwaite Manor after Chapter 3.

Shops and people

  • Rhodes Gunsmith: Firearms, weapon upgrades, ammunition, and gun cleaning oil
  • Rhodes General Store: Canned goods, tonics, clothes, and provisions
  • Rhodes Fence: Buys stolen valuables and stagecoaches; sells crafting recipes, dynamite, and weapons
  • Rhodes Station Post Office: Train tickets, mail delivery, and bounty payment services

Tips

  • Avoid drawing weapons or loitering near the front gates of Braithwaite Manor and Caliga Hall, as their armed guards will shoot on sight without a warning period.
  • You cannot use weapons in the town of Rhodes for much of Chapter 3 while Arthur is serving as an undercover deputy for Sheriff Gray.
