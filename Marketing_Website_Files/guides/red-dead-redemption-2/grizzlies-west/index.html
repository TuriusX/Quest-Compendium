Grizzlies West

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 1, the snowy mountain region

Grizzlies West is a freezing, snow-covered mountain territory that serves as the setting for Chapter 1, beginning at the abandoned mining settlement of Colter. Arthur Morgan and the Van der Linde gang must survive a brutal blizzard, hunt for food, and raid an O'Driscoll camp before moving to the heartlands. Free-roaming in this area requires warm clothing to prevent health core drain, and the rugged terrain makes travel slow and dangerous.

Items

  • White Arabian Horse: Along the western bank of Lake Isabella
  • Cobalt Petrified Wood: Hidden inside a chest on an abandoned cart near the northwest edge of Lake Isabella
  • Mining Helmet: Found inside the abandoned mine shaft at Mount Hagen next to a corpse

Secrets

  • The Frozen Settler can be found at the high peak of Mount Shann holding a panoramic map in their frozen hands.
  • Defuser bones and an airplane crash site can be discovered hidden in the snow near the summit of Mount Hagen.

Tips

  • Always store a winter outfit on your horse so you can switch into warm clothes to stop your health core from draining when visiting this region later in the game.
  • Look along the western shore of Lake Isabella early in free roam to tame the White Arabian, which is one of the fastest wild horses available.
