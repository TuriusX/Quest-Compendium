Bluewater Marsh

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the region north of Rhodes

Bluewater Marsh is a swampy, humid region situated in the Lemoyne territory directly north of Saint Denis and Bayou Nwa. The area is dense with dangerous wildlife, rare orchids, and mysterious night encounters. Players frequent the marshes to hunt exotic birds, gather crafting plants, and confront the unsettling local ambushers.

Items

  • Night Fragrant Orchid: growing on tree trunks scattered throughout the marsh trees
  • Spider Orchid: clinging to bald cypress and mangrove tree bark across the wetlands
  • Rat Poison: located inside the abandoned, decrepit structures around the marsh borders

Secrets

  • The Ghost of Agnes Dowd, a spectral woman who appears on foggy nights between 9 PM and 3 AM, can be heard weeping and watched speaking from a distance across multiple stages.

Tips

  • Avoid moving through the water on foot at night, as both submerged alligators and sudden Night Folk ambushes become significantly harder to spot.
  • Whistle frequently to keep your horse calm and close, but be prepared for it to buck you off if an alligator gets within striking distance.
