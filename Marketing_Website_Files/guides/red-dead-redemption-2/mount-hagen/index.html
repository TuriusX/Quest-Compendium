Mount Hagen

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 2, the final confrontation

Mount Hagen serves as the site for the final story mission, "American Venom," where John Marston, Sadie Adler, and Charles Smith hunt down Micah Bell. You must fight your way up a snowy, narrow mountain pass guarded by numerous members of Joe and Cleet's gang before reaching the summit. The mission ends in a final standoff at the peak outside a small wooden cabin.

Items

  • Micah's Revolver: Looted from Micah Bell's body near the cabin on the summit after completing the story

Tips

  • Equip warm winter clothing before heading up the mountain, as John will otherwise suffer cold weather penalties to his Health Core.
  • Bring a high-powered scoped rifle like the Carcano or Rolling Block Rifle to eliminate snipers entrenched along the high ridges.
  • Return to the peak of Mount Hagen after the end credits roll to retrieve the unique revolver from the frozen remains.
