Colter is an abandoned mining town in the snowy Grizzlies West that serves as the Van der Linde gang's temporary camp during Chapter 1. Here, Arthur and the gang recover from the botched Blackwater heist, hunt for food, and prepare their raid on Cornwall's train before the snow melts. Players can return here in free roam later in the game to find the settlement completely deserted.

Tips
  • Bundle up in warm clothing when revisiting Colter later in the game, as Arthur will steadily lose health core stamina in the extreme cold.
  • You cannot engage in weapons customization or standard hunting trades in Colter during Chapter 1, as camp facilities are entirely locked down until Horseshoe Overlook.
