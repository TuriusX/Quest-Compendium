Pronghorn Ranch

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 1, John's employment

Pronghorn Ranch serves as the primary setting for Epilogue Chapter 1, where John Marston works as a hired ranch hand under the alias Jim Milton. Players complete various manual labor chores and ranch-defense story missions here to earn an honest living and support John's family. The area acts as a safe settlement before John eventually purchases his own land at Beecher's Hope.

Tips

  • Performing daily chores around Pronghorn Ranch, such as milking cows and moving hay bales, increases John's Dead Eye experience and raises his Honor.
