Tall Trees

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 2, hunting and missions

Tall Trees is a heavily forested, mountainous region in West Elizabeth populated by redwood-like pines and dangerous predators. In the Epilogue, John Marston can freely explore the area to gather resources, track high-tier game, and complete ranch hand chores nearby. It also serves as the staging ground for several late-game missions, including hunting down remaining gang threats and tracking down Charles Smith.

Items

  • Trunk Loot: Inside the ruined cabin cellar at Bear Claw
  • Grizzly Bear Pelt: Harvested from grizzly bears roaming northern Tall Trees

Secrets

  • The Devil's Cave, located at the far western edge past the Upper Montana River, contains a recluse hermit who claims to be the devil.
  • Bear Claw Camp is an abandoned, dilapidated settlement in eastern Tall Trees filled with skeletons and ruined cabins hinting at a past slaughter.

Tips

  • Keep a shotgun or high-powered rifle equipped on your shoulder at all times while traveling off-road here due to instant cougar and bear ambushes.
  • Look up into the trees at night when searching for Skinner Brother camps, as they often post scouts in elevated hunting platforms.
  • Clear the Skinner Brother ambush camps promptly to loot unique crafting supplies and protect your travel routes between Beecher's Hope and Blackwater.
