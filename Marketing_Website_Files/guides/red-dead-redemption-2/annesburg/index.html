Annesburg

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 6, the mining town near Beaver Hollow

Annesburg is a grim, polluted coal-mining settlement in Roanoke Ridge that serves as a central hub during Chapter 6 due to its close proximity to Beaver Hollow. Players visit the town for story missions involving the local miners, the Downes family, and Cornwall Kerosene & Tar interests. It offers basic town services alongside a heavily guarded industrial mine complex that responds aggressively to trespassing.

Items

  • Cigarette Card (Fauna of North America - Blue Jay): On the table on the upper platform of the coal-loading facility near the train tracks
  • Cigarette Card (Prominent Americans - Cornelius Palmer): Inside a desk in the mining company's main operations office
  • High Velocity Ammo: On wooden crates stacked near the town's train station platform

Secrets

  • The coal mine tunnel system can be infiltrated from the rear hillside via ventilation shafts and cart tracks without immediately alerting the town guards.

Tips

  • Avoid sprinting through the center of town with drawn weapons or riding recklessly near the rail lines, as local lawmen and guards have very low tolerance for disturbances.
