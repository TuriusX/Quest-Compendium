Saint Denis (Chapter 6)

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 6, returning for missions and the final confrontation

Arthur returns to Saint Denis in Chapter 6 following the gang's return from Guarma, dealing with his worsening tuberculosis and the gang's collapse. You will visit the city to receive a medical diagnosis from the local clinic, meet Sadie Adler at a saloon to plan John Marston's prison break, and encounter Edith Downes in the slums. The city features high law presence, and certain storyline choices here heavily alter Arthur's Honor level before the endgame.

Items

  • Doctor's Diagnosis Letter: Acquired automatically after visiting the Saint Denis clinic during "A Fork in the Road"

Secrets

  • Blind Man Cassidy can be encountered on the road northwest leading out of Saint Denis during Chapter 6, offering final, prophetic foreshadowing about Arthur's fate for one dollar.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Saint Denis Doctor's Office: Medicine, tonics, and health cures
  • Doyle's Tavern: Alcohol, food, and card games
  • Saint Denis Tailor: High-end outfits, vests, coats, and hats
  • Saint Denis Fence: Purchase lock breakers, throwing weapons, crafting recipes, and sell stolen valuables

Tips

  • Completing the Chapter 6 optional Honor mission strand "Do Not Seek Absolution" with Edith Downes in the Saint Denis slums provides significant massive positive Honor boosts toward your ending.
  • Arthur's health core drains at an increased rate throughout Saint Denis in Chapter 6 due to his illness; keep high-tier food and potent tonics stocked to maintain your cores.
