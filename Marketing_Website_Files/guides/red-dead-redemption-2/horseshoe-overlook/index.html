Horseshoe Overlook

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 2, the gang's first main camp

Horseshoe Overlook serves as the Van der Linde gang's first full camp location after descending from the snow in Colter. Here, Arthur Morgan can participate in camp chores, initiate story missions with various gang members, and begin exploring the open world of New Hanover. It is also where players can start upgrading the camp's facilities and Arthur's personal lodgings using the camp ledger.

Items

  • Camp Ledger MISSABLE: next to the camp tithe box behind Dutch's tent
  • Double-Barreled Shotgun: mounted above Arthur's bed inside his tent

Secrets

  • Camp chore stat boosts | Complete chores around camp like carrying hay bales, chopping wood, or hauling water sacks to subtly increase Arthur's Dead Eye experience and camp morale.

Shops and people

  • Pearson: crafting satchel and camp cosmetic upgrades from pristine animal pelts
  • Camp Provisions Wagon: basic food and tonics restocked using the camp ledger

Tips

  • Contribute funds to the camp ledger early to unlock the map upgrade at Arthur's tent, which grants the ability to fast travel from any personal wilderness camp.
