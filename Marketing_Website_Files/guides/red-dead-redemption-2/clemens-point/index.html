Clemens Point

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the gang's second main camp

Clemens Point serves as the Van der Linde gang's base of operations during Chapter 3, situated on a scenic peninsula along the shores of Flat Iron Lake in Lemoyne. Players can undertake numerous main story missions, interact with camp members, play minigames, and purchase camp upgrades here. The camp is abandoned at the end of the chapter following escalating conflicts with the local Gray and Braithwaite families.

Items

  • Five Finger Fillet Table MISSABLE: Under the canopy near the center of camp
  • Pearson's Crafting Tools MISSABLE: At Pearson's butcher station near the chuck wagon
  • Dutch's Phonograph MISSABLE: Outside Dutch's tent near the water's edge

Secrets

  • Camp Boat | Earned by purchasing the boat ledger upgrade, allowing Arthur to row out onto Flat Iron Lake to fish or explore nearby islands.

Shops and people

  • Pearson: Satchel upgrades, camp cosmetic crafting, and meat provisioning
  • Leopold Strauss: Camp ledger and camp upgrade purchases
  • Camp Scout or Cook: Free stews, coffee, and basic camp provisions

Tips

  • Use Clemens Point's shoreline access to catch sturgeon and bass directly from camp without traveling to distant fishing spots.
  • Complete camp companion activities, like fishing with Kieran or hunting with Javier, before progressing the main story, as they become unavailable once the gang moves.
