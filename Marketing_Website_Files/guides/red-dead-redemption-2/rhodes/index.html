Rhodes

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the gang's second town hub

Rhodes is a dusty, southern town in Scarlett Meadows dominated by the bitter feud between the Braithwaite and Gray families. In Chapter 3, Dutch's gang attempts to secretly play both sides against each other while Arthur temporarily takes on the role of a local deputy. Outside of story missions, players can use the town's full range of amenities, including a gunsmith, saloon, train station, and fence just north of town.

Items

  • Lancaster Repeater: Basement of the Rhodes Gunsmith

Secrets

  • Captive in the Gunsmith basement | Peer through the barred basement window on the outside wall of the Rhodes Gunsmith to trigger a robbery prompt where you force the shopkeeper to free the boy.

Shops and people

  • Rhodes Gunsmith: Firearms, weapon upgrades, ammunition, and cleaning supplies
  • Rhodes General Store: Tonics, hunting provisions, clothing, and cigarettes
  • Rhodes Saloon (The Parlor House): Food, whiskey, beer, and Blackjack table
  • Rhodes Train Station: Train tickets, mail delivery, and bounty payment services
  • Seamus at Emerald Ranch (nearby Fence): Fencing stolen stagecoaches, jewelry, and selling crafting recipes (located slightly north of Scarlett Meadows/Rhodes)

Tips

  • You are legally barred from using weapons inside the town of Rhodes during most of Chapter 3 due to Arthur acting as a local deputy.
  • A rare silver pocket watch and loose money clips can be quietly looted from the second floor of the Rhodes train station without attracting the law if unobserved.
