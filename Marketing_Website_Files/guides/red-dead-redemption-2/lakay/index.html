Lakay

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 5, the gang's hideout in the swamp after returning from Guarma

Lakay is an abandoned, dilapidated stilt village hidden deep within the Bayou Nwa swamps that serves as the Van der Linde gang's temporary hideout directly following the Guarma chapter. Arthur reunites with the surviving gang members here before the camp is violently attacked by Pinkerton agents using a Gatling gun. After defending the settlement in the mission "Fleeting Joy," the gang quickly abandons Lakay to relocate to Beaver Hollow.

Items

  • Cat Skull Mask: Inside the small, submerged shack located at the northern edge of the settlement, mounted on the back wall

Tips

  • Use the heavy fog and Dead Eye during the Pinkerton assault to quickly target the agent operating the Maxim gun before he shreds your cover.
  • Avoid sprinting blindly through the water surrounding Lakay at night, as both alligators and hostile Night Folk use the murky swamp vegetation for concealment.
