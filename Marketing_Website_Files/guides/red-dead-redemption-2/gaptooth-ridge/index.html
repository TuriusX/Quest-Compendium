Gaptooth Ridge

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 2, exploring New Austin

Gaptooth Ridge is the westernmost region of New Austin, characterized by arid red-rock canyons, mines, and desolate desert expanses. In Epilogue Chapter 2, John Marston can freely explore this frontier to hunt legendary game, clear Del Lobo hideouts, and discover abandoned industrial sites. The major landmarks here include Tumbleweed, Gaptooth Breach, Rathskeller Fork, and Solomon's Folly.

Items

  • Otis Miller's Revolver: inside a chest in Rattlesnake Hollow, north of Cholla Springs near the ridge border
  • Shark Tooth Trinket: inside a lockbox on the shore near the wrecked boat at the far southwestern tip of the map

Secrets

  • The remains of a Jesuit Missionary can be found with a unique letter near a rock formation directly southwest of the Sea of Coronado.

Shops and people

  • Tumbleweed Gunsmith: firearms, weapon customizations, and ammunition
  • Tumbleweed Butcher: buys animal pelts and meat, sells basic provisions
  • Tumbleweed General Store: provisions, horse tonics, hunting supplies, and outfits

Tips

  • Always keep a high-powered rifle equipped in this region because cougars spawn with little warning in the scrubland outside Tumbleweed.
  • Clearing the miners and Del Lobo gang out of Gaptooth Breach allows you to loot the mine carts and office shack without getting pinned down by heavy rifle fire.
