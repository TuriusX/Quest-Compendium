Guarma

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 5, the island where the gang is shipwrecked

After a failed bank robbery in Saint Denis, Arthur Morgan and several gang members wash ashore on the tropical island of Guarma. The gang is stripped of their usual gear and must fight alongside local revolutionaries against a brutal military regime to secure a boat back to the mainland. The area is heavily restricted, heavily guarded, and impossible to return to through normal gameplay once the chapter concludes.

Items

  • Sun Hat MISSABLE: Looted from local soldiers or guards in Cinco Torres during combat encounters
  • Rare Shotgun MISSABLE: Leaning against the wall inside the cellar during the mission "A Kind and Benevolent Despot"

Secrets

  • Guarma unique compendium animals | The island contains several exclusive snakes, boobies, and macaws that can only be studied and tracked while exploring Guarma before leaving | missable: yes

Tips

  • Do not stray too far past the designated mission boundaries or invisible snipers and instant-alert patrols will kill you immediately.
  • Study and hunt the exclusive birds and snakes immediately between early missions if you want 100% compendium completion, as you cannot return later.
  • Conserve your ammunition and loot every fallen soldier, as you do not have access to your regular horse inventory or weapons locker while on the island.
