Valentine

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 2, the gang's first town hub

Valentine is a muddy, bustling livestock town in the Heartlands that serves as the Van der Linde gang's primary urban hub during Chapter 2. Players can take on story missions, participate in bar fights, access standard town amenities, and clear out minor bounties here. It offers an early introduction to side activities like poker, sheep herding, and robbery opportunities.

Items

  • Schofield Revolver: Inside a weapon case in the backroom of the Doctor's Office during a robbery
  • Special Miracle Tonic Pamphlet: Inside a lockbox placed beneath a wooden camp platform near the Valentine stockyards
  • Nevada Hat: Hidden behind the cascading water of the granite falls just north of Valentine

Secrets

  • The Valentine Doctor's Office hosts an illegal O'Driscoll operation; peer through the rear security window to unlock a side-business robbery opportunity.
  • A serial killer clue containing a map piece is pinned inside the mouth of a severed head, discovered under the railway tracks south of town.

Shops and people

  • Valentine General Store: Provisions, tonics, hunting gear, and outfit upgrades
  • Valentine Gunsmith: Firearm purchases, ammo, weapon customizations, and gun cleaning
  • Valentine Doctor's Office: Health medicine, curatives, and miracle tonics
  • Valentine Hotel: Rentable beds to sleep in and hot baths with assistance options
  • Valentine Stables: Horse purchases, horse care supplies, saddle upgrades, and boarding services
  • Smithfield's Saloon: Hot meals, whiskey, beer, haircuts from the barber, and poker tables

Tips

  • Wear a bandana before robbing the doctor's illegal backroom to help conceal your identity, and flee immediately to avoid lawmen blockading the exit.
  • The livestock auction pens are an easy spot to farm positive honor by greeting townspeople and workers consecutively.
