Roanoke Ridge is the rugged, densely forested northeastern region of New Hanover where the Van der Linde gang establishes their final camp at Beaver Hollow in Chapter 6. Players navigate a deteriorating gang dynamic while dealing with the coal town of Annesburg, the contaminated village of Butcher Creek, and the predatory Murfree Brood. Key activities include hunting rare game around Elysian Pool and Brandywine Drop, exploring mines and caves, and completing pivotal late-game stranger missions.

Murfree Brood
Weak to Headshots with high-powered weaponsSteal / drop Broken pocket watches, cheap tonics, and basic crafting materials

Highly territorial savage hill folk who set pitfall traps and launch sudden close-quarters machete ambushes in the woods.

Shops and people
  • Annesburg Gunsmith: Firearms, weapon modifications, and ammunition
  • Annesburg Train Station Post Office: Mail delivery, train tickets, and bounty payment services

Tips
  • Keep a lantern or miner's hat equipped when exploring the deep caves behind Elysian Pool or near Beaver Hollow, as natural light is completely absent.
  • Watch the minimap carefully for sudden red markers indicating Murfree Brood pit traps or ambushes along forested paths at night.
