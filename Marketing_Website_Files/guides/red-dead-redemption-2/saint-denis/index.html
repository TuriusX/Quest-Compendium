Saint Denis

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 4, the gang's base of operations in the city

Saint Denis serves as the primary urban setting in Chapter 4, where the Van der Linde gang plots high-stakes heists while staying nearby at Shady Belle. Players can explore industrialized streets, interact with high society, and engage in numerous unique side missions and urban activities. The city features the highest concentration of vendors, unique encounters, and law enforcement in the game.

Items

  • Algernon's Revolver: Awarded by Algernon Wasp in his greenhouse shop after completing all stages of the "Duchesses and other Animals" exotics requests
  • Midnight's Pistol MISSABLE: Dropped by Billy Midnight on the roof of the train during the "The Noblest of Men, and a Woman" mission line departing from the Rhodes/Saint Denis area
  • Ornate Dagger: Looted from the Saint Denis Vampire in the alleyway near the church after solving all five mysterious wall writings

Secrets

  • The Saint Denis Vampire can be confronted in an alley south of the cathedral at midnight after inspecting five specific scrawled writings on walls throughout the city.
  • Knock on the door at the back of the Doctor's office to find an illegal high-stakes gambling operation, which can be robbed through a specific dialogue prompt with the physician.
  • An illegal poker game can be robbed above the Saint Denis Gunsmith by examining the metal security door inside the store and forcing the clerk to open it.

Shops and people

  • Saint Denis Tailor: High-end clothing, accessories, and pre-made formal outfits unavailable in rural general stores
  • Saint Denis Trapper: Custom crafted garments, saddles, and gear upgrades in exchange for pristine animal pelts and legendary animal parts
  • Saint Denis Fence: Purchase of stealth weapons, crafting recipes, lock breakers, and the ability to sell stolen valuables
  • Saint Denis Barber: Haircuts, beard trims, styling pomade, and clean shaves
  • Saint Denis Gunsmith: Firearms, weapon customizations, ammunition, and cleaning supplies

Tips

  • Ride your horse at a trot or walk along crowded sidewalks and market streets, as bumping into pedestrians easily triggers a disturbing the peace or assault charge from nearby police.
  • You can pay off bounties for any territory at the Saint Denis Post Office inside the central train terminal.
  • Use the city's extensive network of interconnected rooftops and fire escapes to easily evade law enforcement sightlines when escaping a crime scene.
