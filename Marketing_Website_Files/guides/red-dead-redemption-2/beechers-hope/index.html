Beecher's Hope

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 2, John's new homestead

Beecher's Hope is the plot of land John Marston purchases to build a permanent home and ranch for his family. After securing a bank loan and clearing out squatters, John, Uncle, and Charles build a ranch house and barn using pre-cut materials. Once constructed, the ranch serves as John's primary base of operations where you can do daily chores, rest, interact with family, and launch the final missions of the story.

Items

  • Squirrel Statue: On top of the fireplace mantle in the main house
  • John's Gambler Hat: Inside the wardrobe in the main bedroom

Secrets

  • Complete all hunting requests for Mrs. Hobbs to place the Squirrel Statue on the mantle, which triggers a recurring hide-and-seek minigame around the ranch where Abigail repeatedly hides it.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Laramie Gang memberHeadshotsPocket watch, pocket changeHostile gang members who harass John and must be driven off the property during the purchase phase.

Shops and people

  • Feed Sack and Water Pump: Supplies used to perform chores around the ranch

Tips

  • Completing daily ranch chores like pitching manure, milking cows, and carrying grain sacks raises John's Honor and awards small amounts of cash deposited in the ranch ledger.
  • Sleeping in John and Abigail's bed fully restores all of John's Health, Stamina, and Dead Eye cores.
  • Fast travel can be initiated directly from the map located in John's bedroom once the house is fully constructed.
