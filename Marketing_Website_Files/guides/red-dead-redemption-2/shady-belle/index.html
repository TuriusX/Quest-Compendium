Shady Belle

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 4, the gang's temporary base before moving to Saint Denis

Shady Belle is an abandoned plantation mansion in the bayou of Lemoyne that serves as the Van der Linde gang's camp throughout Chapter 4. Arthur and John clear out the hostile Lemoyne Raiders residing here in Chapter 3 before relocating the camp from Clemens Point. The area provides access to main story missions that transition the gang's focus toward Saint Denis and the southern swamplands.

Items

  • Cigarette Card (Famous Gunslingers - Jack Hall): On a table in the downstairs room near the entrance of the main house
  • Naval Compass MISSABLE: In the boathouse on an old workbench

Secrets

  • You can find a lockbox containing money and valuables hidden under a wooden bunk inside the mausoleum structure on the property grounds.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Lemoyne RaidersHeadshots with rifles, shotguns at close rangeConfederate notes, pocket watches, ammoHostile Southern militia members who control the mansion during the initial mission to take it over.

Shops and people

  • Pearson: Camp stew, animal provisions, satchel and camp cosmetic upgrades
  • Leopold Strauss: Camp ledger donations and contribution box management

Tips

  • Don't linger too far into the shallow marsh water directly surrounding Shady Belle, as American Alligators frequently wait near the banks and can ambush Arthur.
  • Fast travel can be initiated directly from Arthur's bed and map on the second floor of the mansion once the camp map upgrade has been purchased from the ledger.
