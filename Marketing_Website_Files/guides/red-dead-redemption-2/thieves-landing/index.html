Thieves Landing

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Epilogue Chapter 2, exploring New Austin

Thieves Landing is an outlaw haven in eastern New Austin controlled entirely by the Del Lobo gang. Players visiting during the Epilogue will find a hostile settlement with no standard town law, where any violence immediately triggers a shootout with dozens of gang members. It is a prime location for engaging in sustained combat, looting gang bodies, and using the town's operational Fence.

Items

  • Various provisions and ammo: scattered inside the boathouses, docks, and warehouses

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Del Lobo gang memberheadshotscash, ammunition, pocket watchesThey populate the entire town and will open fire collectively if you draw a weapon, bump into them, or loot in plain sight.

Shops and people

  • Fence: black market goods, crafting recipes, weapon modifications, and purchasing stolen valuables

Tips

  • You will not receive a New Austin bounty for killing the Del Lobo outlaws here, making it one of the best locations for completing combat-related challenges.
  • Equip a shotgun or fast-firing repeater before antagonizing the residents, as enemies will quickly swarm your position from elevated catwalks and surrounding shacks.
