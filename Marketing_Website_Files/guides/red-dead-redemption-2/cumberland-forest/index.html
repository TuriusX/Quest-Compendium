Cumberland Forest

Red Dead Redemption 2 guide · Chapter 3, the region near the oil refinery

Cumberland Forest is a rugged, wooded territory in New Hanover situated directly between Valentine and the Ambarino border. The area serves as the backdrop for story encounters involving the Cornwall Coal & Tar refinery and offers dense woodland hunting grounds. Players frequent the region to hunt large game, evade ambushes, and navigate through the cliffs between the northern mountains and the heartlands.

Items

  • Ram Horn Trinket: Fencing the legendary bighorn ram horn to a fence after hunting the animal in the western woods of the region
  • High Velocity Ammo Pamphlet: Inside a lockbox hidden under floorboards near ruined structures northeast of the oil company

Secrets

  • The dinosaur bone found embedded in the rockface along the southern edge of Cumberland Forest overlooking the valley.

Tips

  • Craft and carry potent predator bait if hunting in the forest, as thick tree cover makes spotting predators before they charge difficult.
  • Maintain weapon cleanliness and load express rounds before riding near the refinery, as guard reinforcements respond aggressively to any weapon discharge.
