The Light Elf Sanctuary

God of War guide · Deep within Alfheim
← All areas in this guide

The Light Elf Sanctuary is an optional island area located on the eastern side of the Lake of Light in Alfheim. Kratos and Atreus can explore its ruins by solving light crystal and root-clearing puzzles to unlock gates and pathways. Traversing this sanctuary rewards you with valuable gear, resources, and lore before continuing the main quest.

Enemies
Dark Elf Warrior
Weak to Stun damage via bare-handed attacks and Atreus's arrows

Standard agile infantry that can fly short distances and shroud the area in darkness.

Tökkblökkr (Dark Elf Lord / Elite)
Weak to Bare-handed combat to build up stun quickly

Tougher variant wielding a staff that fires blinding, exploding light blasts.

Tips
  • Look for angles where multiple glowing red vine cores align in a straight line so you can slice through them all with a single throw of the Leviathan Axe.
  • Keep an eye out for hanging explosive pots in the corridors to detonate against groups of Dark Elves before engaging them in melee.
