Tyr's Temple

God of War guide · Exploring the central hub of the Lake of Nine
← All areas in this guide

Tyr's Temple serves as the central hub of the Lake of Nine, connecting to various realm towers and hosting the Realm Travel Room inside. Players return here frequently as the water level drops to access new pathways, discover lore, and travel to other realms. Exploring the exterior decks and lower maintenance areas reveals legendary chests, puzzle chambers, and access points to the bridge mechanisms.

Shops and people
  • Brok's Shop: Upgrades Kratos's weapons and armor, crafts new gear, and offers resurrection stones from the main central bridge workshop.

Tips
  • Return to the exterior balconies of the temple after both water drops to find newly accessible boat docks and treasure chests that were previously submerged.
  • Use Atreus's Shock Arrows to shatter the red crystalline sap blocking side doors along the lower corridors of the bridge.
