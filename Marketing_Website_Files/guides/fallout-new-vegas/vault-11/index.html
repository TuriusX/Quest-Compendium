Vault 11

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the wasteland

Vault 11 is an abandoned Vault-Tec shelter located west of Boulder City, driven by a social experiment where dwellers were told they had to sacrifice one resident annually to survive. You explore the ruined facility to piece together its tragic backstory through audio logs, terminal entries, and campaign posters. Reaching the sacrificial chamber deep below the clinic and living quarters triggers the climax of the vault's dark experiment.

Items

  • Speech Skill Book (Tales of a Junktown Jerky Vendor): In the living quarters, sitting on a bed inside an unflooded room in the lower levels
  • Vault 11 Jumpsuit: On various skeletons and in dressers throughout the living quarters

Secrets

  • The Sacrificial Chamber terminal reveals the Vault-Tec mainframe's automated congratulations, rewarding the dwellers for choosing not to sacrifice a human life, but it arrived after almost the entire population had killed each other over elections.

Tips

  • Bring an automated weapon with armor-piercing rounds or pulse grenades, as the ambush inside the Sacrificial Chamber traps you in close quarters with heavily armored robots and turrets.
  • High Science or Lockpick skills allow you to bypass submerged corridors and hack terminals to unlock access to the overseer's office and lower sublevels.
