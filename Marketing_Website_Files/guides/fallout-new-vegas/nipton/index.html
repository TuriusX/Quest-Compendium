Nipton

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Traveling east from Mojave Outpost

Nipton is a former mining settlement recently raided, slaughtered, and burned by Caesar's Legion in a staged lottery. Players arrive to find crucified NCR personnel, surviving lottery contestants, and an encounter with the Legion officer Vulpes Inculta. While here, you can investigate the town hall, trigger the quest "Cold, Cold Heart," and search the ruined buildings for loot while dealing with feral dogs and traps.

Items

  • Big Book of Science: Inside the Nipton Town Hall, lying on the mayor's desk on the top floor
  • Pugilism Illustrated: Inside the mayor's house on the desk in the bedroom
  • Dean's Purity: Inside the general store, on the store counter next to the cash register

Secrets

  • The Mayor's Steamer Trunk on the top floor of the Town Hall contains valuable supplies and bottle caps, unlocked using the Mayor's key found on the desk nearby or picked with Lockpick skill.

Shops and people

  • Boxcars: Offers quest information for "Booted" and "Wheel of Fortune"

Tips

  • Do not attack Vulpes Inculta or his Legion escort unless you want an early game Legion hit squad sent after you repeatedly.
  • Watch your step inside the residential houses, as several are rigged with frag mines and tripwire shotgun traps.
  • You can speak to Oliver Swanick, the winning lottery contestant sprinting away near the town entrance, though he does not offer trade or a quest.
