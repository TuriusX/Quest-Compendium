Primm

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Shortly after leaving Goodsprings

Primm is a divided desert town whose residents have taken refuge in the Vikki and Vance Casino while Escaped Convicts hold the Bison Steve Hotel across the street. The Courier tracks Benny's trail here to recover the stolen Platinum Chip and can resolve the town's crisis by finding a new sheriff to restore order. Players can also repair the damaged eyebot ED-E in the Nash residence to recruit a versatile companion early in the game.

Items

  • ED-E: Inside the Mojave Express building, resting on a counter
  • Pugilism Illustrated: Inside the Bison Steve Hotel, on the floor of the ground-floor gift shop
  • Dean's Prowess: Inside the Sheriff's house, on a bookshelf in the living room
  • Vance's 9mm SMG MISSABLE: Inside the display case safe in the Vikki and Vance Casino

Secrets

  • The broken Eyebot ED-E inside the Mojave Express office can be permanently recruited as a companion early by repairing it with 65 Repair, 55 Science (followed by 35 Repair), or spare parts (3 scrap metal, 2 sensor modules, 1 scrap electronics).
  • Vance's unique 9mm submachine gun is stolen from its display case in the Vikki and Vance Casino; pass a Speech check with Primm Slim to find out it was stolen, then track down Pauline and Sammy Wins to get the safe combination and retrieve it.

Tips

  • Disarm the tripwires and floor traps placed inside the Bison Steve Hotel lobby and hallways to avoid taking heavy limb damage before engaging the convicts.
  • For the quest "My Kind of Town," you can choose the NCR (requires speaking to Lieutenant Hayes), Meyer (from the NCR CF, requires pardon), or Primm Slim (requires 30 Science or a spare fission battery and conductor) as the town's new sheriff.
  • You can rescue Deputy Beagle in the Bison Steve Hotel by sneaking or fighting your way into his room, and pickpocket his journal immediately to advance the main quest without having to talk to him.
