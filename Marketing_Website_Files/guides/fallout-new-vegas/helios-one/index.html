Helios One

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Traveling north from Novac

HELIOS One is a pre-war solar power plant controlled by the New California Republic. Players are tasked by Fantastic and Ignacio Rivas with restoring the facility's power network in the quest "That Lucky Old Sun." The final decision allows the player to distribute power across the Mojave Wasteland or activate the plant's orbital defense weapon.

Items

  • Archimedes II rangefinder (Euclid's C-Finder power) MISSABLE: Activated at the terminal in the solar plant tower
  • Big Book of Science: Inside the plant's building, on a table in the room with the terminal operated by Ignacio Rivas

Secrets

  • Siding fully with the NCR or redirecting power to Freeside will make the orbital strike function unusable for the Euclid's C-Finder, whereas directing full power to Archimedes II unlocks the weapon for exterior use.

Tips

  • Repairing the broken generator inside the plant requires a Repair skill of 35 or a piece of Scrap Metal.
  • You can use the terminal inside the main building to reprogram the security robots to attack each other or deactivate them entirely with a sufficient Science skill.
