Lucky 38 (Post-House activation)

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Returning to the Lucky 38

Returning to the Lucky 38 penthouse after installing the Platinum Chip initiates the next stage of Mr. House's plans, during which he demonstrates the Securitron Mark II upgrade on his large screen. You must speak with Mr. House to receive your upgraded suite access and your subsequent faction-related story objectives. If you decide to betray him at this point, you can bypass his mainframe terminal to access his true physical chamber in the control room.

Items

  • Snow Globe - The Strip: On a curved counter to the left of the cash registers in the Cocktail Lounge

Tips

  • Speak to Jane on the penthouse floor after the demonstration to turn in any collected Snow Globes for 2,000 caps each before taking hostile action against House.
  • Unlocking the terminal to the basement antechamber will permanently turn all Securitrons inside the Lucky 38 hostile, so prepare for heavy automatic fire before hacking it.
