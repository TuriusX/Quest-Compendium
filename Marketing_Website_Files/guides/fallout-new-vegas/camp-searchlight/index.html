Camp Searchlight

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the eastern wastes

Camp Searchlight is a former NCR base that was turned into an intensely radioactive ruin after Caesar's Legion detonated a dirty radiation weapon in the center of town. You will navigate the irradiated ruins to put down irradiated NCR ghouls, recover dog tags, and explore several abandoned structures including two basements and an elementary school. The location is the main staging ground for the NCR quests "We Will All Go Together" and "Eye for an Eye."

Items

  • Knock-Knock: Held by a dead NCR trooper inside the Searchlight Fire Station restrooms
  • Golden Boxing Gloves: On a desk in the East Church basement

Secrets

  • Logan's locked supply cache: Inside the Searchlight Police Station and the Searchlight Elementary School are storage cellars containing supply crates that can only be unlocked with Logan's assistance or keys tied to the "Wheel of Fortune" quest.

Tips

  • Equip an Advanced Radiation Suit or space suit and take Rad-X before entering, as ambient radiation across the entire camp is persistently high.
  • Speak to First Sergeant Astor on the road just west of town to start "We Will All Go Together" before clearing the ghouls so you can collect their dog tags efficiently.
