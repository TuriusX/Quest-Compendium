Sunset Sarsaparilla Headquarters

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the wasteland

The Sunset Sarsaparilla Headquarters is an abandoned bottling facility and corporate office infested with malfunctioning security robots and feral ghouls. It serves as the primary turn-in location for the quest "The Legend of the Star," where players collect Sunset Sarsaparilla blue star bottle caps. Exploring the facility reveals corporate lore, logistical terminal entries, and the ultimate reward behind the blue star cap legend.

Items

  • Pew Pew: Next to the body of Allen Marks in the prize room unlocked after turning in 50 blue star caps
  • Sunset Sarsaparilla Deputy Badge: In the prize room unlocked after turning in 50 blue star caps
  • Star Caps (approx. 3): Scattered loose throughout the offices and factory floor, including on desks and near bottling equipment

Tips

  • Bring Pulse grenades, Pulse mines, or weapons chambered in Pulse rounds to easily deal with the high number of security robots inside.
  • You must collect and turn in 50 Sunset Sarsaparilla blue star bottle caps to Festus before the prize room door unlocks.
  • Failing Festus's trivia game yields no penalty; you can talk to him repeatedly until the conversation progresses.
