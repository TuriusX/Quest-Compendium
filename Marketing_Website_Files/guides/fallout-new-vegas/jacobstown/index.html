Jacobstown

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Seeking the Super Mutants

Jacobstown is a secluded alpine haven in the Charleston Mountains founded by Marcus for peaceful Super Mutants and Nightkin. Players visit this retreat to recruit the companion Lily Bowen and investigate a cure for the Nightkin's schizophrenia-like affliction caused by Stealth Boy addiction. The settlement also serves as an objective in several companion quests and contains non-violent resolutions to outside conflicts.

Items

  • Snow Globe - Mt. Charleston: On the reception desk near the entrance inside the Jacobstown lodge
  • Grognak the Barbarian: Inside the right-side bungalow in the front row, lying on a table next to a bed

Tips

  • Recruiting Lily Bowen grants the Stealth Girl perk, which doubles the duration of Stealth Boys and increases sneak attack critical damage.
  • Avoid attacking the mercenaries outside the town entrance during "Unfriendly Persuasion"; using Speech or paying them preserves your peaceful standing with Jacobstown.
