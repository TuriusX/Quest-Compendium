Mojave Outpost

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Traveling south from Primm

The Mojave Outpost is a fortified New California Republic checkpoint situated in the southwestern corner of the Mojave Wasteland. Players can recruit the companion Rose of Sharon Cassidy here, resolve travel permits for caravans, and take on side quests clearing local threats. It serves as a major waypoint before heading east along the southern edge of the map toward Nipton.

Items

  • Duck and Dodge: Inside the Mojave Outpost headquarters building, on the bottom shelf of a wooden bookcase

Shops and people

  • Lacey: Repairs gear, trades general caravan goods, food, drinks, and ammunition
  • Major Knight: Repairs weapons and armor up to 100% condition for caps

Tips

  • Talk to Ranger Jackson to get the quest "Can You Find It in Your Heart?" which tasks you with clearing giant ants along the nearby highway for an easy NCR reputation boost.
  • You can recruit Rose of Sharon Cassidy (Cass) here as a companion once you complete her associated questline involving the Crimson Caravan Company.
