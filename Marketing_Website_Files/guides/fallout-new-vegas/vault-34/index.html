Vault 34

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the wasteland

Vault 34 is a heavily flooded, irradiated Vault that served as the ancestral home of the Boomers before they fled to Nellis Air Force Base. Navigating its labyrinthine halls requires draining flooded corridors using submerged terminal passwords and dealing with ghoulified residents to reach the reactor and the armory. Inside, you can resolve the reactor crisis by either saving trapped survivors or stabilizing the Vault's power to aid the NCR Sharecropper Farms.

Items

  • All-American: On an overturned table on the floor inside the Vault 34 Armory cache
  • Vault 34 Armory Key: Found on the corpse of the Ghoul Overseer inside the Overseer's Office
  • Pulse Gun: Locked inside a Very Hard footlocker in the Vault 34 Armory
  • Underwater Breather: Worn by the player if obtained from Jack at Nellis Air Force Base prior to exploring

Secrets

  • Activating the terminal in the reactor room presents a choice to either vent the radiation into the trapped dwellers' quarters to save the Sharecropper Farms' water supply, or reroute the power to unlock the doors and save the trapped Vault survivors.

Tips

  • Bring plenty of RadAway and Rad-X, as the ambient radiation in Vault 34 is pervasive and accumulates rapidly throughout the entire facility.
  • Search submerged desks in the flooded technical and security areas to find drowned technicians carrying the passwords needed to drain the water.
