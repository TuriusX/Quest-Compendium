Camp Golf

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the Lake Mead area

Camp Golf is an NCR military base established inside the pre-War House Resort on the shore of Lake Mead. The area serves as the headquarters for the NCR Rangers in the Mojave, as well as the home of the undisciplined "Misfits" squad. Visiting players can resolve issues for the Misfits, complete tasks for Chief Hanlon, or explore the grounds for supplies and skill books.

Items

  • Guns and Bullets: Inside the House Resort on the upper floor, sitting on a table in the hallway
  • Ranger Sequoia: Carried by Chief Hanlon on the resort's outdoor balcony

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Sergeant McCredie: Sells standard NCR armory supplies, ammunition, and weapon maintenance items inside the House Resort

Tips

  • Complete the quest "Flags of Our Foul-Ups" to improve the Misfits squad via training, psycho, or cheating on their records for different quest rewards and NCR fame.
  • You can acquire Chief Hanlon's unique Ranger Sequoia without violence by waiting until the quest "Return to Sender" concludes with him committing suicide, allowing you to safely loot his corpse.
