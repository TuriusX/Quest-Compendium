Camp McCarran

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Arriving in the New Vegas area

Camp McCarran serves as the primary headquarters for the New California Republic in the Mojave Wasteland, located inside the pre-war McCarran International Airport. The area is dense with major NCR side quests, including interrogating a captured Legion centurion, hunting Fiend leaders, and exposing a spy within the ranks. Players can also utilize the secure Monorail located inside the terminal to travel directly onto the New Vegas Strip without paying the credit check.

Items

  • Cram Opener MISSABLE: Carried by Little Buster, who rests near the outdoor shooting range
  • Marksman Carbine: Found leaning against a rack of weights inside the armory in the terminal building
  • NCR Engineer Jumpsuit: On a workbench inside the terminal maintenance area near Chief Technician Betsy

Secrets

  • You can board the Monorail to the Strip without NCR reputation or hostility if you wear a disguise of NCR armor to fool the guards at the platform door.

Shops and people

  • Daniel Contreras: Sells high volumes of surplus ammunition, weapon modifications, and weapons from his supply closet in the terminal concourse
  • Farber: Offers food items and chef services in the terminal mess hall

Tips

  • Complete the quest to discover the spy before attempting to use the Monorail, or diffuse the bomb inside the train quickly, otherwise the Monorail will be permanently destroyed.
  • Bring Fiend leader heads directly to Major Dhatri outside the terminal to claim significant cap bounties during the quest "Three-Card Bounty."
