Ranger Station Golf

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the Lake Mead area

Ranger Station Golf is a major New California Republic military post situated inside the House Resort on the shores of Lake Mead. It serves as the primary base for the NCR veteran rangers and the headquarters for Chief Hanlon. Players visit this location to advance military-related quests, interact with the underperforming Misfits squad, and investigate Hanlon's misleading radio transmissions.

Items

  • Hunting revolver: Carried by Chief Hanlon on the balcony of the main building
  • Ranger Sequoia MISSABLE: Carried by Chief Hanlon, which can be acquired if he commits suicide or is killed
  • Tumblers Today skill book: Inside the main resort building, on the desk in the small office on the upper floor

Secrets

  • The Misfits' footlocker | Found inside the Misfits' tent, containing personal effects and gear that can be utilized during the quest "Flags of Our Foul-Ups"

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
NCR Veteran RangerHeadNCR Ranger combat armor, Anti-materiel rifleOnly hostile if the player attacks the NCR or becomes vilified by the faction

Shops and people

  • Doctor Tart: Medical services, healing, and radiation removal located inside the resort clinic

Tips

  • Complete the quest "Flags of Our Foul-Ups" by either training the Misfits through legitimate skill checks or cheating their records on the resort's terminal to easily gain NCR reputation.
  • Do not confront Chief Hanlon about his falsified reports during "Return to Sender" until you are certain how you want the quest to end, as speaking with him directly determines his fate and the fate of the station.
