Vault 22

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the wasteland

Vault 22 is an overgrown vault overrun by deadly mutated plants and creatures resulting from agricultural experiments gone wrong. You explore the decaying multi-level structure to locate lost Brotherhood of Steel research data and help the NCR or Followers of the Apocalypse. The core task involves navigating through locked bulkheads, fixing the main elevator, and ultimately finding a way to destroy the spore contamination.

Items

  • AER14 prototype: On Level 5 - Pest Control, next to a skeleton in the common area blocked behind locked doors
  • Vault 22 cave keycard: Level 4 - Common Areas, inside a room on a desk near the living quarters

Tips

  • Bring a weapon capable of long-range fire damage or a flamer, as the mutated plant enemies are particularly vulnerable to burning.
  • You can repair the central elevator with a Repair skill of 50 or by finding the replacement parts on Level 3, saving significant travel time between floors.
  • When igniting the gas on Level 5 to destroy the spores, step inside the sealed maintenance room or take shelter around a heavy bulkhead to avoid dying in the explosion.
