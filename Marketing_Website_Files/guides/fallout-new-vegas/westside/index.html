Westside

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the outskirts of New Vegas

Westside is a walled, low-income community situated west of the New Vegas Strip, notable for operating independently of both the NCR and Mr. House. Players visit this settlement to recruit the companion Arcade Gannon at the local clinic, investigate local water theft, and assist a cooperative farming operation. Key locations include The Casa Madrid Apartments, The Thorn entrance, and the Westside Co-op.

Items

  • Boxing Times: Inside the Casa Madrid Apartments, in the room belonging to Sweet Jill, sitting on a dresser
  • Grognak the Barbarian: Inside Westside, resting on a table inside the shack belonging to Ma Mayberry

Secrets

  • Entering the open manhole located on the southern edge of Westside leads directly into The Thorn, a subterranean combat arena operated by Red Lucy.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Klamath Bob: Sells food, alcohol, and general supplies at Bob's Liquor.
  • Marco: Sells fresh produce and cooking ingredients at the Westside Co-op.
  • Doctor Anderson: Provides medical healing, radiation removal, addiction treatment, and sells basic medical supplies at the Casa Madrid clinic.

Tips

  • Speak to Anderson at the clinic with Arcade Gannon in your party to trigger unique dialogue and gain approval toward Arcade's personal quest.
  • Passing a high Speech or Intelligence check when dealing with Hector during "The White Wash" resolves the water diversion issue peacefully without turning Westside residents hostile.
