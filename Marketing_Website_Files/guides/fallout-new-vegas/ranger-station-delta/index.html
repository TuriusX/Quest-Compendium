Ranger Station Delta

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the eastern wastes

Ranger Station Delta is an NCR outpost nestled on a cliffside ridge overlooking Nelson and Boulder City in the eastern Mojave. It serves as an observation post where NCR Troopers and Rangers monitor nearby Legion troop movements. Players visit to explore, trade, or deliver updated radio security codes during the quest "Return to Sender".

Items

  • Big Book of Science: In the lower tent on a small table

Enemies

NCR TrooperHeadService Rifle, NCR Trooper ArmorStandard guards patrolling the area; only hostile if the NCR turns against you

Shops and people

  • Comm Officer Melissa: Caravan games and general military chatter

Tips

  • During the side quest "Return to Sender", you must interact with Comm Officer Melissa inside the communications tent to deliver the radio codes.
  • If you plan on attacking Nelson, Ranger Station Delta provides a clear elevated viewpoint to snipe Legion sentries before engaging them at close range.
