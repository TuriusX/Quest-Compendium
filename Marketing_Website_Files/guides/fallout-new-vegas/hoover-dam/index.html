Hoover Dam

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Visiting the dam exterior

The exterior of Hoover Dam is a massive, fortified pre-war hydroelectric structure serving as the NCR's military headquarters in the Mojave Wasteland. Visitors can traverse the main road running across the top of the dam, explore the intake towers, and access the visitor center and interior power plants. It is also the site of the game's climactic final battle, where the fate of the Mojave is decided.

Items

  • Chinese Army: Spec. Ops. Training Manual: Inside the Hoover Dam Visitor Center, resting on the reception desk
  • Sunset Sarsaparilla star bottle cap: Inside Hoover Dam Intake Tower 01, sitting on a counter near a terminal

Shops and people

  • Quartermaster Bardon: Sells massive bulk quantities of various ammunition types, weapon mods, and high-end military armor

Tips

  • Avoid wearing Caesar's Legion faction armor on the dam exterior unless you want the entire base of NCR troopers and snipers to immediately open fire.
  • The intake towers along the road have operational elevators that allow you to bypass long interior walks to quickly reach lower levels of the power plants.
