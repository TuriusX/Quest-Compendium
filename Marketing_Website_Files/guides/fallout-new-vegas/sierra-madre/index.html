Sierra Madre

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Starting Dead Money DLC

You are stripped of your gear and forced by Father Elijah to assemble three companions and break into the legendary Sierra Madre Casino. You must navigate a toxic villa filled with deadly red Cloud and hostile Ghost People while keeping your bomb collar from detonating. The primary goal is to gather the companions, trigger the gala event, and unlock the casino doors to confront the secrets of the vault.

Items

  • Holorifle: Added to your inventory automatically upon waking up at the Sierra Madre fountain
  • Dean's Electronics MISSABLE: Inside Dean Domino's hideout on a desk
  • Automatic Rifle: Found inside the Villa police station armory
  • Bear Trap Fist: Scattered throughout the Villa streets and carried by Ghost Trappers
  • Sierra Madre Snow Globe MISSABLE: On the upper ledge of the Villa fountain plaza above the clinic

Secrets

  • Dean's Secret Stashes can be found across the Villa by looking for hand-painted white handprints on walls, pointing to nearby hidden suitcases.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Ghost PersonDismemberment or decapitationGhost Harvester RemainsThey will continually get back up unless a limb or head is completely destroyed.

Shops and people

  • Sierra Madre Vending Machine: Dispenses food, chems, weapon mods, and ammunition in exchange for Sierra Madre Chips once unlocked with found codes

Tips

  • Radios and speakers emit a high-pitched tone that causes your bomb collar to beep; destroy them or run past them before your collar explodes.
  • Avoid lingering in the red Cloud, as it constantly drains your health over time.
  • Look for Return Codes for the Sierra Madre Vending Machines to convert junk items, such as pre-War money and clothing, into valuable chips.
