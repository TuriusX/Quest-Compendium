The X-22 Botanical Garden is an overgrown greenhouse facility in the Big MT featuring mutated vegetation and research stations. Players explore the facility to retrieve the biological research station upgrade for the Sink and various seed samples. The exterior and interior are patrolled by hostile plant-based creatures and Spore Carriers.

Items

0/2

Enemies
Spore Carrier
Weak to Fire

Camouflaged humanoids that rush in close range to melee attack

Spore Plant
Weak to Fire

Immobile plant hazards that spit ranged projectiles at approaching targets

Tips
  • Use fire-based weapons like the Flamer or incinerators to dispatch the Spore Plants and Spore Carriers quickly.
  • Look closely along the ground before advancing, as Spore Carriers often crouch completely still to ambush you.
