Goodsprings

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Starting the game

Goodsprings is the starting settlement where the Courier awakens in Doc Mitchell's house after surviving an execution attempt. Players can learn survival basics from Sunny Smiles, explore the town buildings, and resolve a brewing conflict between the locals and the escaped convict gang known as the Powder Gangers. Choosing to protect the town or aid the Powder Gangers establishes the Courier's early faction reputation and concludes the introductory section.

Items

  • 9mm Pistol: Handed directly to you by Doc Mitchell during character creation
  • Vault 21 Jumpsuit: Given by Doc Mitchell before leaving his house
  • Varmint Rifle: Given by Sunny Smiles behind the Prospector Saloon to start the shooting tutorial
  • Laser Pistol: In a locked hard-level gun case inside the Goodsprings Cave
  • Snow Globe - Goodsprings: Sitting on a small tombstone in the Goodsprings Cemetery near the open grave
  • Sunset Sarsaparilla Star Bottle Cap: Inside the abandoned ranch house east of town, on a table near an empty bottle
  • Sunset Sarsaparilla Star Bottle Cap: On the counter inside the Goodsprings general store near the cash register
  • Salesman Settler: In a broken crate in the abandoned schoolhouse
  • Chinese Army: Spec. Ops. Training Manual: In the Goodsprings house east of Doc Mitchell's, hidden inside a low bookshelf

Secrets

  • You can dig up the graves in Goodsprings Cemetery with a shovel to obtain minor loot, though doing so carries a small Karma loss.
  • Victor's Shack contains a terminal that reveals he was explicitly monitoring you on behalf of an unseen employer.

Shops and people

  • Doc Mitchell: Basic medical healing, addiction removal, and radiation treatment
  • Chet (Goodsprings General Store): General supplies, ammunition, weapons, weapon modifications, and leather armor
  • Trudy (Prospector Saloon): Basic food, drink, and minor medical items

Tips

  • Talk to Sunny Smiles at the Prospector Saloon right after leaving Doc Mitchell's to receive a Varmint Rifle and free target practice.
  • Fix the broken radio inside the Prospector Saloon with a high enough Repair skill to gain early experience and town favor.
  • Avoid traveling directly north from Goodsprings past the cemetery early on, as high-level Cazadores and Deathclaws block the path.
  • During the quest "Ghost Town Gunfight," you can ask townspeople like Chet, Doc Mitchell, and Easy Pete for supplies using speech and skill checks to strengthen the town's militia.
