REPCONN Headquarters

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Exploring the wasteland

REPCONN Headquarters is the corporate office building of the REPCONN Aerospace company, occupied by automated security robots. Exploring the building allows the Courier to scavenge high-tech loot, discover corporate lore, and access the top-floor executive suites. Gaining access requires either fighting through the security systems or printing security cards and passing facial/voice recognition checks.

Items

  • Q-35 Matter Modulator: Inside a locked display case in the locked shipping and receiving room on the ground floor; accessible through an Average lockpick door or by hacking a terminal on the third floor to unlock it
  • Big Book of Science: On a shelf in the gift shop storage room on the first floor
  • Dean's Purity (Electronics skill book): In the third-floor office area, on a desk in the room with the terminal controlling the shipping room safe
  • REPCONN office mobile security card: On a desk in the main office area on the second floor
  • REPCONN executive security card: Inside an executive briefcase on the third-floor conference table

Secrets

  • You can bypass security combat entirely on the first and second floors by interacting with the Tour Guide robot, obtaining an official tour badge, or having high Luck (7+) to guess the password "Ice cream" when prompted by security terminals.

Tips

  • Bring Pulse Grenades, Pulse Mines, or weapons chambered in Pulse slugs, as all hostile entities inside the building are robotic.
  • A high Science or Lockpick skill allows you to skip the security card hunt and crack the doors directly.
