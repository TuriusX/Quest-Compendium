The Strip (Post-House activation)

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Returning to the Strip

After upgrading the Securitrons with the Platinum Chip in Mr. House's bunker, returning to the Strip showcases upgraded Mark II Securitrons patrolling the streets. You must return to the Lucky 38 to continue the main storyline by reporting your success to Mr. House or following an alternative faction path. This state also triggers the initial outreach from major factions, where ambassadors or couriers may confront you to secure your allegiance.

Items

  • The Strip Snow Globe: On a curved desk on the main floor of the Lucky 38 cocktail lounge

Tips

  • If you plan on betraying Mr. House for Yes Man or another faction, now is a key turning point to access his control room using the penthouse terminal.
  • Approaching the Lucky 38 after leaving with the upgraded Securitrons active typically prompts Vulpes Inculta or Alerio to present the Mark of Caesar, pardoning past crimes against the Legion.
