The Divide

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Starting Lonesome Road DLC

The Divide is a shattered, storm-ravaged landscape that serves as the setting for the Lonesome Road add-on. Players must navigate treacherous ruins, collapsed highways, and underground silos to reach the Courier Ulysses. Along the way, you must manage severe radiation, detonate unexploded warheads, and confront heavily mutated wildlife and hostile Marked Men.

Items

  • ED-E's Upgraded Arc Welder: Inside the Hopeville Missile Base silo, found on a work bench near where you first reactivate the local ED-E unit
  • Laser Detonator: Found on a console inside the Hopeville Missile Base silo
  • Riot Gear: Inside the Hopeville Men's Barracks armory closet, or purchased from Commissary terminals
  • Advanced Riot Gear: Located on a dead NCR trooper on the upper floor of the Crow's Nest sniper overlook
  • Elite Riot Gear: Found on a corpse on the top level of the Third Street Municipal Building
  • Red Glare: In Marked Men camp armories, high-tier loot containers, or purchased from Commissary terminals
  • Industrial Hand: Found inside a footlocker in the Marked Men camp near the High Road, or purchased from Commissary terminals
  • Scribe Blameless journal entry MISSABLE: Inside an Eyebot pod near the collapsed overpass in the Hopeville area

Secrets

  • Unexploded Warheads | Detonating these hidden nuclear warheads across the canyon using the Laser Detonator clears rubble, reveals hidden caves, and counts toward the "Warhead Hunter" challenge.
  • Ralphie the Robot posters | Five collectible promotional posters are scattered across The Divide, including locations inside the Hopeville Missile Base and the Sunstone Tower.

Shops and people

  • Commissary Terminals: Sells ammunition, weapon mods, high-tier armor, and supplies, while possessing a large pool of caps for repairing and trading equipment.

Tips

  • You can leave The Divide and return to the Mojave Wasteland at any time through the canyon entrance without failing the DLC questline.
  • Fire flares or flashbangs directly at Tunnelers to temporarily blind and scatter them when your position is overrun.
  • Keep a safe distance behind sturdy cover before firing the Laser Detonator at unexploded nuclear warheads to avoid lethal radiation and blast damage.
