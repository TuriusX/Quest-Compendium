Big MT

Fallout: New Vegas guide · Starting Old World Blues DLC

You arrive in the Big MT crater via a crashed satellite drive-in movie projector and immediately have your heart, brain, and spine surgically replaced by the Think Tank scientists. To return to the Mojave Wasteland, you must complete research tasks, recover stolen personal items, and confront Doctor Mobius at the Forbidden Zone. The main base of operations is The Sink, which acts as a central player home with interactive, upgradable personalities.

Items

  • Sonic Emitter: Given by Doctor Klein in the Think Tank during the opening dialogue
  • K9000 Cyberdog Gun: Found on a console in the X-8 Research Center or given during main quests
  • Stealth Suit Mk II: Located inside the X-13 Testing Facility
  • Christine's CoS Silencer Rifle: Leaning against the wall inside Little Yangtze's ruined sniper building near the northern exit
  • Elijah's Advanced LAER: Found on the roof of the Signal Hills Transmitter
  • Transportalponder: Awarded upon completing the Old World Blues main questline

  • The Sink Central Intelligence Unit: Energy weapons, ammunition, weapon mods, doctor supplies, and repair services

Tips

  • Bring plenty of pulse slugs, pulse grenades, or the Paladin Toaster, as almost all difficult enemies in the crater are mechanical.
  • Upgrade the Sink personality modules as soon as you find their holotapes to unlock water purification, crafting converters, and plant cloning.
  • You cannot leave Big MT and return to the Mojave until you finish the main questline of Old World Blues.
