Gibson Scrap Yard is a small junkyard located north of Novac run by Old Lady Gibson and her pack of guard dogs. It serves as a key location to gather parts for the quest "Come Fly With Me" and to trigger a recording log for ED-E's companion quest. Players visit primarily to purchase salvage, repair gear, and recruit or advance companions.

Shops and people
  • Old Lady Gibson: Sells various scrap metal, miscellaneous junk, ammunition, and provides weapon repair services

Tips
  • Bring ED-E with you when first speaking to Old Lady Gibson; mentioning the word "HELIOS" or "ARCHIMEDES" during her dialogue triggers one of ED-E's hidden audio logs for the quest "ED-E My Love."
