Half-Life 2 achievement guide

How to unlock every achievement, which ones you can miss, and the best order to get them all. Tick them off as you go.

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Exiting the train station courtyard at the start: Throwing the can at the metrocop and putting it in the trash can become impossible for that chapter run.
  • Leaving Ravenholm via the mines: Finishing the district with only the Gravity Gun becomes permanently impossible for that run.
  • Stepping onto the rocky surface of the lighthouse base at the end of Sandtraps: Crossing the sand without alerting antlions becomes failed and locked out.
  • Transitioning out of any given chapter: Any Lambda caches not found in that chapter's boundaries require restarting the chapter to register.

Recommended order
  1. Complete City 17 Canals while picking up the early story achievements, knocking down the can, throwing it back at the cop, killing barnacles with explosive barrels, and shooting down the Hunter-Chopper.
  2. Complete Black Mesa East while finding the hidden HEV charger and locating the singing Vortigaunt in the scrap yard.
  3. Complete Ravenholm using only the Gravity Gun to secure the primary missable restriction, while also pinning zombies to walls with the crossbow/props.
  4. Complete Coastal Highway while killing soldiers with the buggy, taking out gunships with the rocket launcher, pinning enemies with the crossbow, and crossing the sand without touching it.
  5. Complete Nova Prospekt while killing enemies using antlion guards and surviving all turret holdout sections without letting your sentries be destroyed.
  6. Complete City 17 Streets by leading your squadmates carefully, destroying striders with the rocket launcher, and taking out sniper nests.
  7. Complete City 17 Plaza (City 17 Uprising) while destroying generators, disabling suppression devices, and protecting your rebellion squad members.
  8. Complete City 17 Nexus by disarming the tripwire laser traps, taking out the remaining snipers, and breaching the core defenses.
  9. Complete the Citadel while vaporizing soldiers with the supercharged Gravity Gun and destroying the dark fusion reactor.
  10. Use chapter select to clean up any missed miscellaneous combat feats, Lambda cache locations, or weapon-specific kills.
