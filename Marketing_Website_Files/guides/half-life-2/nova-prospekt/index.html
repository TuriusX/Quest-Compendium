Nova Prospekt

Half-Life 2 guide · Infiltrating the Combine prison
← All areas in this guide

Gordon Freeman infiltrates the high-security Combine facility Nova Prospekt to rescue Eli Vance and Judith Mossman. You battle through derelict prison cell blocks, train terminals, and fortified checkpoints alongside friendly antlions. The objective is to push deep into the facility while bypassing security barricades, hazards, and automated Combine defenses.

Enemies
Combine Soldier
Weak to Headshots

Standard armed guards utilizing SMGs, shotguns, and frag grenades.

Combine Turret
Weak to Knocking them over using the Gravity Gun

Deployable automated tripods that deactivate permanently once toppled.

Fast Zombie
Weak to Shotgun blasts to the torso or head

Agile undead found in ruined, infested sections of the lower prison.

Poison Zombie
Weak to Explosives or high-damage weapons from long range

Durable zombie that carries and hurls toxic headcrabs at the player.

Manhack
Weak to Physics impacts using the Gravity Gun

Flying rotor drones that can be easily swatted out of the air or pulled and thrown.

Tips
  • Use the Pheropod to direct your antlion squad to trigger tripwires or distract automated turrets ahead of you.
  • Grab deactivated turrets with the Gravity Gun and place them in narrow hallways to mow down incoming Combine reinforcements.
  • Watch for water on the floor in ruined cell blocks, as exposed wiring can electrify it and deal severe environmental damage.
