Bloodmoon Island

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Crossing to the forbidden island

Bloodmoon Island is a corrupted landmass located in the northern waters of Reaper's Coast, reachable across a broken bridge or by ferry. Players journey here to investigate the source of the demonic infestation, locate the Ancestor Tree, and discover the true nature of the Advocate and his master. Key objectives include learning how to breach the Archives, exorcising demons trapped inside ancient vaults, and freeing or silencing the spirits bound to the corrupted island.

Items

  • The Forgotten and the Damned: Found in the Bloodmoon Island Archives on a shelf
  • Ornate Hymnal: Located in the Bloodmoon Island Archives or looted from the demon-possessed Archivist
  • Silver Ingot MISSABLE: Scattered around the island and inside the ancient vaults, used in the nearby forge
  • Bloodmoon Island Map: Dug up near the entrance of the island or found inside the Archives

Secrets

  • The broken bridge to Bloodmoon Island can be safely crossed by casting Spirit Vision to reveal hidden, walkable phantom bridge segments.
  • A hidden hatch to the Bloodmoon Island Archives is buried near the northeastern shore and requires high Wits or a shovel at the exact spot to uncover.

Tips

  • Forge a Silver Lever Shaft at the island's ancient forge using a Silver Ingot before entering the underground vaults to repair the broken levers inside.
  • Reading the Ornate Hymnal with a character who has the Scholar tag allows you to learn the correct taming verse required to open the sealed vaults safely.
  • Do not take the undead Ferryman's boat across without care, as the surrounding water is lethal Deathfog that will instantly kill living party members.
