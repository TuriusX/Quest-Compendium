Arx Prison

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Entering the dungeon

The Arx Prison is located beneath the Magister Barracks, accessible via stairs or hatches within the compound. Players explore this dungeon to locate Lord Arhu, confront Magister interrogators, and find clues related to the whereabouts of Kemm and Lucian's tomb. Navigating the depths involves bypassing locked cells, handling Magisters, and uncovering hidden passageways to the Sewers.

Items

  • Arx Prison Key: Carried by Paladin Warden or lying on the warden's desk inside the prison
  • Execution Orders: Found on the desk in the interrogation room

Secrets

  • A hidden wall brick can be found along the prison corridor that unlocks access to an underground shortcut directly connecting to the Arx Sewers.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Paladin GuardsHostile if you trespass without permission or side against the Paladins
Interrogator MagisterEncountered during the investigation in the lower detention area

Tips

  • Use Spirit Vision inside the cells to reveal the spirits of tortured prisoners who provide context and clues about Magister operations in Arx.
  • Sneaking or utilizing Teleportation helps avoid fighting the heavy concentration of Paladins stationed near the main entrance cells if you do not want to compromise your standing with them.
