Paradise Downs

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the cursed fields

Paradise Downs is a devastated, decay-afflicted region situated in the northeastern quadrant of Reaper's Coast. Players navigate across poisoned farmlands and blighted ruins while confronting Voidwoken incursions, completing Magister-related fallout quests, and tracking down powerful Source masters like Hannag and the advocate-connected forces nearby.

Items

  • Ancient Empire War Drum: Inside the ruined house near the decayed orchard

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

Shops and people

  • Almira: Master-level Source skillbooks and magical gear once aided

Tips

  • Equipping gear or learning spells that grant the Bless status is essential, as large swaths of the terrain are smothered in Necrofire and Cursed Blood that inflict Decay.
  • Bring teleportation skills to bypass ruined bridges and navigate the steep cliffs without walking directly through persistent environmental hazard fields.
