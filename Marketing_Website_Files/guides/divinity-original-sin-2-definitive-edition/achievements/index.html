Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Boarding Lady Vengeance at the end of Hollow Marshes: All unfinished quests, uncollected items, and NPCs left in Fort Joy and Hollow Marshes become permanently inaccessible.
  • Departing Lady Vengeance at the end of Act 2: All unfinished quests, exploration areas, and unique items across Reaper's Coast become permanently inaccessible.
  • Entering the Academy Arena of the One on Nameless Isle: All unvisited god shrines, side quests, and areas across the Nameless Isle become permanently locked.
  • Descending past the Lucian Crypt altar in the Arx Cathedral: The world of Arx locks out, sealing all open urban quests and locking you into the final boss encounters.

Recommended order
  1. The Hold: Choose an Origin avatar, complete tutorial tasks, rescue all companions during the ship disaster, and escape on the lifeboat.
  2. Fort Joy Beach: Recruit initial party companions, acquire the Black Cat follower, and obtain the Teleportation gloves.
  3. Fort Joy Ghetto: Defeat the Fort Joy underground arena for Arena Champion, trigger the confrontation at the front gate to drive off Dallis for Hammered, and remove your Source Collar.
  4. Fort Joy Prison: Navigate the prison sub-levels, rescue Verdas or Delorus, and escape the fortress compound while keeping the Black Cat alive.
  5. Fort Joy: Fully breach the exterior perimeter to register the prison break achievements.
  6. Hollow Marshes: Explore the exterior marsh, find and aid Gareth, clear voidwoken ambushes, and free the cursed winter dragon Slane.
  7. Dark Cave: Infiltrate the vault of Braccus Rex, defeat Trompdoy, shatter soul jars, and obtain the Bless ability.
  8. Gargoyle's Maze: Navigate the labyrinth traps and trials, solve the gargoyle puzzle, and access the tower gates.
  9. Braccus Rex's Tower: Cleanse the cursed well, loot the ancient treasury relics, and conclude exploration of the ruins.
  10. Sanctuary of Amadia: Align with the Seekers, heal the wounded refugees, coordinate the assault on Bishop Alexandar, and board the ship.
  11. The Lady Vengeance: Claim control of the ship, defeat Dallis's boarding party on the deck, and sail into the Hall of Echoes.
  12. Reaper's Coast: Reach the mainland, recruit Tarquin's aid to recover Anathema pieces, and hunt for Meister Siva.
  13. Driftwood: Free Meister Siva, start Spirit Vision rituals, and secure passage into the tavern underbelly.
  14. Driftwood Arena: Win the arena matches while blindfolded to earn the master champion title.
  15. Driftwood Tavern Cellar: Engage with the local undertakers, smuggler rings, and acquire the strange glowing egg.
