Points of no return

  • Boarding Lady Vengeance at the end of Hollow Marshes: All unfinished quests, uncollected items, and NPCs left in Fort Joy and Hollow Marshes become permanently inaccessible.
  • Departing Lady Vengeance at the end of Act 2: All unfinished quests, exploration areas, and unique items across Reaper's Coast become permanently inaccessible.
  • Entering the Academy Arena of the One on Nameless Isle: All unvisited god shrines, side quests, and areas across the Nameless Isle become permanently locked.
  • Descending past the Lucian Crypt altar in the Arx Cathedral: The world of Arx locks out, sealing all open urban quests and locking you into the final boss encounters.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1The Hold

    Choose an Origin avatar, complete tutorial tasks, rescue all companions during the ship disaster, and escape on the lifeboat.

    Also in this step: Thunders of the Upper Deep

  2. Step 2Fort Joy Beach

    Recruit initial party companions, acquire the Black Cat follower, and obtain the Teleportation gloves.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Cat's Out of the Bag!

    Also in this step: Reaper's Eye, The Great Escape, Walk at Liberty

  3. Step 3Fort Joy Ghetto

    Defeat the Fort Joy underground arena for Arena Champion, trigger the confrontation at the front gate to drive off Dallis for Hammered, and remove your Source Collar.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Hero
    • Gladiator: Speak to the Arena Master underneath Fort Joy Ghetto and win the underground gladiatorial tournament.
    • Villain
    • Hammered
  4. Step 4Fort Joy Prison

    Navigate the prison sub-levels, rescue Verdas or Delorus, and escape the fortress compound while keeping the Black Cat alive.

  5. Step 5Fort Joy

    Fully breach the exterior perimeter to register the prison break achievements.

  6. Step 6Hollow Marshes

    Explore the exterior marsh, find and aid Gareth, clear voidwoken ambushes, and free the cursed winter dragon Slane.

    Don't miss in this step
    • A Good and Faithful Servant: Find Gareth under attack by Magisters at the Decrepit Ruins/Braccus' Armoury in the Hollow Marshes and defeat his attackers to rescue him.
    • The Weird Sister
    • Dragonslayer

    Also in this step: His Father's Son

  7. Step 7Dark Cave

    Infiltrate the vault of Braccus Rex, defeat Trompdoy, shatter soul jars, and obtain the Bless ability.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Abound with Blessings: Interact with the statue at the end of Trompdoy's Dark Cave (or in the Hollow Marshes) to be pulled into a realm and meet your higher power.
  8. Step 8Gargoyle's Maze

    Navigate the labyrinth traps and trials, solve the gargoyle puzzle, and access the tower gates.

  9. Step 9Braccus Rex's Tower

    Cleanse the cursed well, loot the ancient treasury relics, and conclude exploration of the ruins.

  10. Step 10Sanctuary of Amadia

    Align with the Seekers, heal the wounded refugees, coordinate the assault on Bishop Alexandar, and board the ship.

    Also in this step: Spira, Spera

  11. Step 11The Lady Vengeance

    Claim control of the ship, defeat Dallis's boarding party on the deck, and sail into the Hall of Echoes.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Seascape: Survive the ambush by Dallis on the Lady Vengeance until Malady completes her teleportation spell without wiping out every hostile enemy.
    • Scholar of Secret Arts: After gaining control of the ship or meeting Tarquin, speak with him cordially and agree to welcome him into the Seekers' service rather than attacking him.
    • Swashbuckler

    Also in this step: Sails That Glance in the Sun, Heaven's Gate

  12. Step 12Reaper's Coast

    Reach the mainland, recruit Tarquin's aid to recover Anathema pieces, and hunt for Meister Siva.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Master: Learn how to expand your Source capacity to three slots by completing quests for any two Source masters across Reaper's Coast.
    • Proud Parent
    • Iniquity Forgiven
    • The Good Doctor
    • Anathema
    • Furious Rebuke

    Also in this step: A Million of Waves, Expert

  13. Step 13Driftwood

    Free Meister Siva, start Spirit Vision rituals, and secure passage into the tavern underbelly.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Isle of the Gods
    • Death in the Pot
    • Infiltration

    Also in this step: The Meistr

  14. Step 14Driftwood Arena

    Win the arena matches while blindfolded to earn the master champion title.

    Don't miss in this step
    • Andabata: Win the arena combat trial while blindfolded, and then defeat the reigning champion Murga to earn the arena title.
  15. Step 15Driftwood Tavern Cellar

    Engage with the local undertakers, smuggler rings, and acquire the strange glowing egg.
