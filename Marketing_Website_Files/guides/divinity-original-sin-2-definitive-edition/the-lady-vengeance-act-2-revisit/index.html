The Lady Vengeance (Act 2 Revisit)

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Returning to the ship

After escaping Fort Joy, the Lady Vengeance remains anchored off the coast of Reaper's Coast throughout Act 2, serving as your primary home base. You return here frequently using the lower deck waypoint to respec attributes, trade, craft, and interact with recruited companions and rescued NPCs. Once you have acquired three Source points and completed the rituals to learn the location of the Council of Academy, speaking to Malady here will trigger departure to the Nameless Isle.

Items

  • Magic Mirror: Lower deck, bottom of the ship
  • Teleporter Pyramid (Red) MISSABLE: Master Cabin, sitting on a desk
  • Teleporter Pyramid (Blue) MISSABLE: Master Cabin, hidden inside a secret chest or bedroom desk
  • Anathema Blade Piece (Broken Shaft) MISSABLE: Tarquin's inventory / quest reward aboard the ship once retrieved

Secrets

  • Tarquin's artifact quest | Handing over both parts of Anathema to Tarquin on the ship's deck unlocks the reforging of the blade in the final act.

Shops and people

  • Tarquin: Necromancer skillbooks, Source orbs, high-tier armor, and weapons on the top deck
  • Fletcher Corbin Day: Arrows, huntsman gear, and bow crafting components on the lower deck (if rescued from the Blackpits)
  • Almira: High-level magical gear, accessories, and scoundrel/witchcraft skillbooks on the upper deck (if rescued and recruited)
  • Han: Fetches rare crafting items and Source materials of your choice upon request

Tips

  • Place one Teleporter Pyramid on the lower deck near the Magic Mirror so you can teleport back and freely respec your characters at any time from anywhere in Reaper's Coast.
  • Any companion not currently in your active four-person party will remain on the Lady Vengeance, allowing you to swap party members before leaving for Act 3.
  • Once you instruct Malady to depart Reaper's Coast, you cannot return to this region, and any unfinished Act 2 quests will automatically fail.
