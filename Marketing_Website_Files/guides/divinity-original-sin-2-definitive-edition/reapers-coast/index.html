Reaper's Coast

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the main region

After escaping Fort Joy, the Godwoken arrive at Reaper's Coast to master their Source abilities and locate powerful Source Masters. You must explore the town of Driftwood, the surrounding wilderness, and Bloodmoon Island to expand your Source capacity and advance toward the Nameless Isle. Along the way, you investigate Magister corruption, uncover ancient dwarven and elven secrets, and assist your companions with their personal quests.

Items

  • Captain's Compass Amulet: Looted from the stranded shipwreck on the southern coastline southwest of Driftwood
  • Teleporter Pyramid (Yellow): Found in the basement of Ryker's Stonegarden mansion
  • Teleporter Pyramid (Red): Inside the hidden laboratory behind the blood altar in Bloodmoon Island's archives
  • Shadow Prince's Signet MISSABLE: Carried by the Shadow Prince located in the lava field near the Cloisterwood

Secrets

  • A hidden entrance to the Undertavern can be found through the trapdoor inside the Black Bull tavern kitchen in Driftwood.
  • A concealed hatch behind a stone wall in Mordus's house in Driftwood leads to his secret cellar, opened by interacting with a nearby wall switch.

Shops and people

  • Trader Bree: Sells skill books and basic crafting gear
  • Trader Haran: Sells physical weapons and heavy armor
  • Eithne: Sells necromancer skill books and rare crafting ingredients
  • Tarquin: Sells high-level armor, weapons, and necromancy supplies

Tips

  • Speak to the ghost of Meister Siva using Spirit Vision inside her home in Driftwood whenever you need guidance on advancing your Source capacity.
  • You only need to persuade or assist two Source Masters across Reaper's Coast to unlock your maximum capacity of three Source Points.
  • Blessed Smoke or teleporting Alice Alisceon down to the nearby cliff near Jahan can make her notoriously difficult encounter much easier to survive.
