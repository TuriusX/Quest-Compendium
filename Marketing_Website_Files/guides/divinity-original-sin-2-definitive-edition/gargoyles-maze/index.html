Gargoyle's Maze

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · The cursed labyrinth
← All areas in this guide

The Gargoyle's Maze is an obstacle-filled outdoor labyrinth located on the southeastern part of Fort Joy island. Navigating the maze involves unlocking gated sections using glowing skull keys, solving pressure plate puzzles, and interacting with cursed historical remnants. Reaching the central tower at the end completes the labyrinth and grants access to Braccus Rex's hidden sanctuary.

Burning Skeleton
Weak to Hydrophic spells / Water / Rain

Resists fire damage and attempts to ignite the party on oil surfaces.

Tips
  • Use Rain skills to extinguish surfaces before moving through courtyards to prevent accumulating continuous burn damage.
  • Keep non-essential party members unchained and parked outside hazards to avoid triggering unwanted pressure plates or traps.
  • You do not need to fight every group of enemies in the maze; movement skills allow you to grab the skulls and reposition safely.
