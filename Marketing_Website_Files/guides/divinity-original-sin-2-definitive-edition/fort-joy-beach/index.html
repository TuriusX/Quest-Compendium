Fort Joy Beach

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Arrival on the island

You awaken washed ashore on the southern coast of Fort Joy after the shipwreck of the Merryweather. Explore the beach to find companions, gather starting resources, and defeat early enemies to earn experience before entering the ghetto. The main path leads north along the sands straight toward the entrance gates of the Fort Joy internment camp.

Items

  • Bedroll: On a sleeping mat near the shoreline campfires south of the entrance gate
  • Teleportation Gloves MISSABLE: Looted from the Saltwater Crocodiles on the secluded beach northwest of the ghetto
  • Yarrow Flower: Growing on the grassy ledge north of the crashed ship wreckage

Secrets

  • Buried chest near the crashed rowboat on the southern edge, revealed by walking near it with high Wits or digging directly with a shovel

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Unnis: Sells low-tier basic crafting supplies and trinkets while hiding in the beach ruins

Tips

  • Always carry a bedroll in your quickbar; using it outside of combat instantly restores your entire party to full health and clears most status effects.
  • You can recruit the Red Prince immediately by heading south along the beach rocks from the starting point.
  • Having a character with the Pet Pal talent allows you to speak to the black cat that begins following you near the camp approach.
