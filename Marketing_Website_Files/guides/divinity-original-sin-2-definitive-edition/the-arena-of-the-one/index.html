The Arena of the One

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Fighting for divinity

Located inside the Academy of the Seven on the Nameless Isle, this is the arena where Godwoken contest each other to claim the Well of Ascension. You must rush to the core, defeat your rivals or former companions who reject your claim, and face the sudden intervention of Dallis the Hammer and the God-King's forces. After Dallis destroys the mantle of divinity, you must escape the collapsing chamber while fighting off summoned Source Titans.

Items

  • The Swornbreaker MISSABLE: Carried by the automaton guardian or found near the ascension platform ruins

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Dallis the HammerAppears in dragon form alongside Lucian's cloaked figure to interrupt the ascension before you can claim it
Malady's Mirror/Shadow ChampionsFormer companions who did not join your active party will be revived as opponents by the God-King if not swayed
Source TitanMassive incarnation summoned to slow your escape as the Arena fills with lava and collapses

Tips

  • Prepare mobility skills like Tactical Retreat, Cloak and Dagger, or Phoenix Dive before interacting with the entrance so you can race quickly to the Well of Ascension.
  • If you lack party members who are completely loyal, they will turn hostile right before entering the final chamber to claim divinity for themselves.
  • Do not spend all your Source Points and ultimate cooldowns on the rival Godwoken, as a second consecutive phase against Dallis's summons immediately follows.
  • Use Teleportation and Nether Swap to bypass barriers and race ahead of rival Godwoken attempting to reach the Well first.
