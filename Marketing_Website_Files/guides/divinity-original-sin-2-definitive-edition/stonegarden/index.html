Stonegarden

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the graveyard

Stonegarden is the sprawling graveyard of Reaper's Coast filled with ancient tombs, wandering spirits, and the buried dead. Players must explore crypts, confront necromancers, and assist or purge restless souls to gather Source, artifacts, and clues for multiple ongoing quests. Careful interaction with mechanisms and Spirit Vision is essential to unlocking many of the area's subterranean chambers.

Items

  • Anathema Blade Piece (Broken Haft) MISSABLE: Inside the crypt of Johanna Hath, locked behind a puzzle mechanism
  • Ancient Empire Artifact MISSABLE: Carried by the spirit of the Lizard Ambassador in the Lizard consulate section
  • Livewood Crossbow MISSABLE: Received from the elf spirit Fletcher Corbin Day after dealing with the necromancer nearby
  • Vulture Armor Feather (Cursed) MISSABLE: On the altar of the undertaker Digger next to the ritual site

Secrets

  • Touching the buttons on the wall in Johanna Hath's crypt in the correct sequence opens the secret passage into the tomb where her sarcophagus and Anathema component lie.
  • Using Spirit Vision near the crying dog and the grave of Paul Fletcher reveals the master's ghost, allowing you to peacefully resolve the encounter using Pet Pal.
  • Casting Teleportation or jumping into the locked mausoleum of the Surpass family allows access without needing Thievery, bypassing the ornate gate entirely.

Tips

  • Cast Spirit Vision continuously throughout Stonegarden, as nearly every grave and mausoleum hosts an interactable spirit with a quest or background lore.
  • Have Pet Pal active on a party member before approaching the resting hound Andras, as talking to him unlocks alternate outcomes before violence breaks out.
  • Bring heavy pots or crates to Johanna Hath's tomb to weigh down pressure plates if you lack sufficient party members to trigger the secret door.
