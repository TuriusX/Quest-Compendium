Hollow Marshes

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · The wilderness outside the fort

The Hollow Marshes is the wild region outside Fort Joy where players search for a way off the island. You must navigate hostile Magister patrols and corrupted wildlife to find the hidden Sanctuary of Amadia and ally with the Seekers. The ultimate goal is securing Purging Wands to destroy Shriekers and taking Lady Vengeance, the Magister flagship.

Items

  • Tyrant's Stride MISSABLE: Inside the Ancient Passage connected to the Decrepit Ruins, interact with the statue while having at least 15 Wits
  • Tyrant's Helm MISSABLE: Found inside the vault in the Decrepit Ruins behind the cursed switch puzzle
  • Hands of the High Judge MISSABLE: Carried by Magister Houndmaster in the marsh approach or looted from High Judge Orivand
  • Purging Wand: Looted from the Witch Radeka inside her thorned cave along the eastern coast

Secrets

  • The Decrepit Ruins entrance hidden behind a stone door near the burning pigs, requiring Bless on the lever and Source on the shrine to access the Tyrant's artifact.
  • Madman's Enclave hidden through the Dark Cavern along the southern beach, filled with illusionary walkways and the soul jars of Braccus Rex's necromancers.

Shops and people

  • Gareth: Skill books for Warfare and Geomancer after rescuing him from the Ruined Castle
  • Gratiana: Aerotheurge skill books and refills Purging Wand charges at the Sanctuary of Amadia
  • Bahara: Huntsman skill books, perched atop the watch platform at the entrance of the Sanctuary of Amadia
  • Kerban: Summoning skill books and basic crafting gear inside the Sanctuary of Amadia encampment

Tips

  • Clear the Gargoyle's Maze early to reach the Tower of Braccus Rex for high-tier equipment and a well that provides guaranteed jewelry when paying gold.
  • You must eliminate the Shriekers guarding the harbor using either a Purging Wand or the dragon Slane before your party can safely assault the Magister docks.
  • Bless the flaming pigs to remove their curse; one pig named Feder will run to the Sanctuary of Amadia, where bathing in the holy pool permanently turns her into a unique trader.
