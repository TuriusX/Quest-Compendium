Braccus Rex's Tower

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the wizard's ruins

This ruined tower located past the Gargoyle's Maze on the southeast coast of Fort Joy contains the remnants of Braccus Rex's personal chambers and vault. Players enter to locate magical relics, decipher the fate of the necromancers bound here, and find an escape route toward the Sanctuary of Amadia. Completing the puzzle rooms within requires manipulating the environment, interacting with cursed objects, and handling soul jars.

Items

  • Heart of the Tyrant: Looted from the stone sarcophagus in the central burial chamber
  • Hands of the Tyrant MISSABLE: Located inside the tomb in the Gargoyle's Maze courtyard right before entering the tower
  • Captain's Jaunty Hat MISSABLE: Found inside a buried pirate chest inside the ruined tower chamber if following the Threads of a Curse questline

Secrets

  • A hidden hatch in the floor of the ruins leads directly to the Madman's Enclave / Dark Cavern, revealed by stepping near it or passing a Wits check.
  • Interacting with the ancient stone tomb requires high Physical Strength (around 18 Strength) to force open without relying on specific spells.

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
TrompdoyTrompdoy's RingEncountered across multiple illusionary copies within the cavern below the tower who cast offensive spells.

Tips

  • Bring Source Points or a Purging Wand if you intend to interact with the cursed levers and mechanisms in the vault.
  • Smash the soul jars found in the lower chamber to immediately destroy their corresponding necromancer copies encountered across Fort Joy.
  • Equip all pieces of the Tyrant's set together to remove the negative curses each individual piece inflicts on your character.
