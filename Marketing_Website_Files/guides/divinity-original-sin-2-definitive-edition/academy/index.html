Academy

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Entering the central temple

The Academy of the Seven is the final major location on the Nameless Isle where Godwoken prepare to ascend to Divinity. Players must resolve personal loyalties with their companions, unravel the Academy's secrets, and align the Eternal conductors to unlock the central Arena of the One.

Items

  • Blade of the Swornbreaker MISSABLE: Located in the Treasury (unlocked via the portal maze in the side room), placed on a central pedestal
  • Ancient Key: Found on the body of an Ancient Automaton in the Great Hall

Secrets

  • Spirit of Seneschal | Can be activated by inserting a Phase Capacitor into the terminal located near the Library, unlocking lore and training services

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Eternal ProtectorWaterGuards the Ancient Forge/Cabinet area alongside several automaton reflections
Voidwoken ShriekerStationary turret enemies guarding the entry approaches, must be destroyed using Purging skills or the Source Wand

Shops and people

  • The Seneschal: Offers attribute and skill-boosting lessons at the cost of other stats once awakened

Tips

  • Ensure you speak to all party companions in the entrance hall to secure their allegiance, as anyone not persuaded will turn hostile later.
  • Phase Capacitors found throughout the area can be placed on electric floor tiles to conduct the circuits and open the Barrier to the Arena.
