Arx

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Arriving at the city

After the crash of the Lady Vengeance, you arrive on the outskirts of Arx where Paladins and Voidwoken are engaged in a massive battle at the city gates. Securing the gate grants you entry into the bustling final hub city of the game, where multiple major companion and divine questlines converge. Your primary goal here is to investigate the Divine Order's secrets, resolve Lucian's legacy, and uncover the path into the Lucian's Tomb beneath the city cathedral.

Items

  • Anathema MISSABLE: Rewarded by Tarquin outside the Stonegarden/Cathedral area after giving him both components in previous acts
  • Falone Girt: Sold by the merchant Trader Friel near the Arx town square
  • Dûx Téramorte: Looted from a hidden chest in Kemm's Garden

Secrets

  • The entrance to Lord Kemm's Vault is revealed by pulling a hidden mechanical lever hidden among the flowerbeds and water pump inside Kemm's Garden.
  • Beneath the toy shop of Toyseller Sanders, a hidden hatch leads to a basement containing the materials needed to create the Amulet of the Toymaker and obtain the Scroll of Atonement.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Voidwoken TunnelerAppears during the bridge battle at the Arx main entrance, capable of burrowing and dealing massive physical and Earth damage.
Lord Arhu's TormentorsCultists of the God King that channel torture beams into Lord Arhu within Kemm's Vault; disrupting them frees Arhu.

Shops and people

  • Trader Aravae: Sells high-level Finesse armor and Huntsman/Rogue skill books in the Arx city center
  • Toyseller Sanders: Offers unique quest-related dialogue, source-infused toys, and high-tier crafting ingredients

Tips

  • Cast Spirit Vision frequently inside the Magister Barracks and Kemm's estate to spot hidden ghosts that provide vital passcodes and crime-scene clues.
  • Keep the Green Tea item crafted via Lady Kemm's tea leaves in your hotbar, as it reduces all Action Point costs by 2 for several turns.
