Driftwood Arena

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Fighting in the underground pit

The Driftwood Arena is an underground fighting ring located beneath the Black Bull tavern where players can test their combat prowess. To become the local champion, you must first win a blindfolded match against a squad of combatants and then defeat the reigning champion, Murga. Winning both battles earns you experience, unique gear, and the title of Champion of Driftwood.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
MurgaUses Scoundrel skills, turns invisible, and fights alongside a pet Voidwoken in the second phase.
The Undefeated VoidwokenSummons smaller Voidlings and inflicts heavy physical and magic damage during the championship bout.
Arena CompetitorA squad of diverse fighters fought while your party is afflicted with the Blind status.

Shops and people

  • Arrashne: Sells Scoundrel skill books and thief equipment inside the arena lounge.

Tips

  • In the first challenge, the Blind status severely restricts attack range; using summons, teleportation skills, and surface spells helps bypass the aiming penalty.
  • After winning the arena, you can visit the Driftwood undertaker to get an audience with the arena's overseers and progress related questlines.
  • Position high-ground ranged characters on the wooden scaffoldings before triggering dialogue to start the fight.
