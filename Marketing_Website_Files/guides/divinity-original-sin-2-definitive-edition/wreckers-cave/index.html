Wrecker's Cave

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · The spider-infested cavern

This cavern on the Reaper's Coast is a hideout for Mordus and his Voidwoken-worshipping wreckers. Exploring the upper chambers triggers an ambush where Voidwoken abduct party members, scattering them into isolated lower chambers. You must regroup your party, navigate through the spider-infested tunnels, and defeat Mordus to acquire his Source amulet and learn his teachings.

Items

  • Mordus' Amulet: Dropped by Mordus in his ritual chamber
  • Glowing Idol of Rebirth MISSABLE: Looted or pickpocketed from the undead spider Dorotya's questline, but can be kept and recharged using Resurrection Scrolls in your inventory
  • Free Fall Skillbook MISSABLE: Found in the ruins of the lower cavern shipwreck
  • Letter from the Dwarven Queen MISSABLE: Inside the private quarters behind Mordus' throne chamber

Secrets

  • A cracked wall in the lower flooded tunnels can be blasted with explosives or bludgeoned with a heavy weapon to reveal a hidden passage leading straight to the central cavern.
  • A submerged chest resting off the docks near Mordus' chamber can be spotted with high Wits and raised using Telekinesis or the Teleportation skill.

Tips

  • You can use the Pyramids of Teleportation or waypoint fast-travel to reunite your party immediately after the Voidwoken Lurkers scatter you across the lower depths.
  • Bring high-mobility skills like Tactical Retreat, Cloak and Dagger, or Spread Your Wings to bypass the spiked barricades and vertical cliffs that block the separate starting cells.
  • If you leave Mordus alive after beating him down to low health, you can interrogate him for Source mastery before deciding whether to execute him.
