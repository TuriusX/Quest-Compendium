Sanctuary of Amadia

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · The hidden refuge

The Sanctuary of Amadia is a hidden, peaceful campsite in the Hollow Marshes that serves as the refuge for the Seekers fleeing the Magisters. Here, players can remove their Source Collars, heal wounded refugees, acquire skill books, and plan their assault on the Magisters to secure a ship off the island.

Items

  • Tyrant's Heart: Worn by the statue inside the mysterious shrine ruins in the camp's upper alcove
  • Purging Wand: Awarded by Gratiana or found as part of her quest involving the vault

Secrets

  • Speaking to the stone statue of Amadia while praying with a character that passes her dialogue checks or is blessed will reveal a pool of healing water beneath it.

Shops and people

  • Gratiana: Sells Aerotheurge and Priest-themed skill books and services the player's Source Collar needs
  • Bahara: Sells Huntsman skill books and archery equipment from her perch near the entrance
  • Kerban: Sells Summoning skill books and crafting materials near the middle campfire
  • Simone: Sells Hydrosophist skill books and potion ingredients
  • Exter: Sells Scoundrel skill books, lockpicks, and thieving equipment

Tips

  • Healing the three wounded Seekers lying on cots near the waypoint (using restoration spells or potions) will help convince Leya to remove your entire party's Source Collars without needing to rescue Gareth first.
  • Bring Lohse to speak directly with the statue of Amadia for unique deity interactions that grant temporary blessings.
  • You must rescue the Seeker leader Gareth from the nearby Decrepit Ruins before the refugees can make their final offensive move toward the Lady Vengeance.
