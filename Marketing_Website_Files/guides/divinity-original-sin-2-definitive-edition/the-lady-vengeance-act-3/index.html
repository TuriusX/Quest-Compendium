The Lady Vengeance (Act 3)

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Arriving at the Nameless Isle

Upon arriving at the waters of the Nameless Isle, the ship serves as your mobile base camp before you disembark to explore the island. You can respec your party, trade with surviving companions, converse with your crew, and prepare gear before heading ashore into the heart of the act. Once you are ready, use the ship's rowing boat on the main deck to disembark onto the beach of the Nameless Isle.

Items

  • Magic Mirror: Lower deck of the ship
  • Teleporter Pyramid: On the lower deck or inside the crew quarters if previously retrieved

Shops and people

  • Tarquin: High-level skill books, weapons, armor, and crafting ingredients
  • Almira: High-level skill books and magical accessories if she was rescued in Act 2
  • Fletcher Corbin Day: Weapons, bows, and fletcher supplies if recruited in Act 2

Tips

  • You can use the Lady Vengeance's rowboat or Waypoint Shrine to freely return to the ship throughout your time on the Nameless Isle until you enter the Academy.
  • Fletcher Corbin Day can upgrade the level of specific unique items sold to him between Act transitions, making this a good time to review your inventory.
  • Visit the Magic Mirror on the lower deck before heading ashore to adjust attributes, combat abilities, and talents to handle the sudden power spike of enemies on the island.
