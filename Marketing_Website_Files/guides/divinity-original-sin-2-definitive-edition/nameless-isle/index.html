Nameless Isle

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the island

The Godwoken arrive on the Nameless Isle to reach the Council of the Seven and claim their place as the new Divine. Exploring the island requires uncovering the alignments of the seven shrines—determining which gods favor the Sun and which favor the Moon—to unlock the central Lunar Gate. Along the way, players navigate the warring factions of the Black Ring and the Divine Order while uncovering the ancient origins of the Eternal race.

Items

  • Shaft of the Swornbreaker MISSABLE: Located inside the Ancient Temple in the possession of the mechanical watchers
  • Blade of the Swornbreaker MISSABLE: Carried by the Sallow Man inside his hidden cave council chamber

Secrets

  • The Ancient Temple hidden beneath the storm cloud can be entered by interacting with the lightning shrines or praying at Rhalic's temple to disperse the storm.
  • A hidden cave entrance to the Sallow Man's war room is concealed by an illusion near the crash site and troll encampment, dispelled using the Cowl of True Sight or an illusory wall interaction.
  • Finding all seven god alignments can be skipped entirely by using Phase Capacitors on the Lunar Gate altar puzzle or by picking the lock on the side entrance.

Tips

  • Keep at least two Phase Capacitors in your inventory, as they are required to power the ancient machinery throughout the island and the Council of the Seven.
  • You can use the Swornbreaker to free your companions or yourself from the Covenant with the God King later, so ensure you craft it before leaving the act.
  • Completing the Lunar Gate alignment requires placing the god totems to either the Sun or the Moon based on clues found across each racial shrine.
