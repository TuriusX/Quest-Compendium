Blackpits

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the industrial area

The industrial section of the Blackpits is a heavily guarded oil extraction site run by the White Magisters and their enslaved Sourcerers. Players arrive here to investigate Magister operations, rescue Gwydian Rince from execution atop the wooden gallows, and secure access to the deeper mines. Navigating the docks and oil platforms requires managing vast environmental hazard surfaces while dealing with patrols and ambush squads.

Items

  • Captain Trippel's Orders MISSABLE: Carried by White Magister Jonathan on the gallows platform
  • Ancient Key: Inside the Magister barracks building near the harbor area

Secrets

  • A locked cellar door inside the ruined house near the wooden gates leads to an underground workshop where Magisters conceal Source weapons and notes.

Tips

  • Cast Bless on the necrofire surfaces surrounding the central gallows to convert them into holy fire, preventing Gwydian Rince from burning to death.
  • Teleport Gwydian Rince inside the sturdy storage shed near the gallows platform and block the doorway with heavy crates to keep his AI safe from Voidlings.
  • Stock up on Rain, Armor of Frost, and Restoration scrolls before initiating combat around the oil rigs to constantly replenish Magic Armor against pervasive burn surfaces.
