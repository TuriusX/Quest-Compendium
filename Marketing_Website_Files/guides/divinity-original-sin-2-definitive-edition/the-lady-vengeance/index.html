The Lady Vengeance

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Boarding the ship to leave the island

After escaping Fort Joy, you board the living ship named the Lady Vengeance, which is under Magister control and held stationary. You must explore the decks, defeat Magister Ranley and her cohorts, and uncover the ship's control phrase from Dallis's stateroom to make the ship sail. Once freed, you face an assault by Dallis the Hammer and must protect Malady until she can cast a ritual spell to escape.

Items

  • Teleporter Pyramid: On a desk inside Dallis's stateroom
  • Strange Gem: Looted from Magister Ranley on the lower deck
  • Dusty Tome: Inside Dallis's stateroom on a desk

Tips

  • Your primary objective during Dallis's ambush is to keep Malady alive for a set number of turns rather than defeating all enemies.
  • Cast Fortify, Magic Shell, and Haste on Malady to help her survive the assault from Dallis's minions.
  • Reading the Dusty Tome with a scholar character unlocks the password needed to interact with the ship's figurehead and control the vessel.
  • If you reduce Dallis or her cloaked figure to low health during the ambush battle, the fight ends immediately and Dallis drops her unique weapon.
