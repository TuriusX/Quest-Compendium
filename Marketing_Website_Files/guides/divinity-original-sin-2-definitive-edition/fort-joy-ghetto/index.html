Fort Joy Ghetto

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Exploring the prison camp

Fort Joy Ghetto is the makeshift prison camp where Sourcerers are held under Magister control, serving as the central hub of Act 1. Here you can recruit companions, investigate routes to breach or bypass the inner fortress, and acquire your first full sets of equipment and skill books. Navigating social dynamics with gang bosses, enslaved elves, and watchful guards is key to surviving and preparing your escape.

Items

  • The Sparkler Card MISSABLE: Carried by Dallis's guard or found on the card players on the battlements near the main gate
  • Teleportation Gloves: Looted from the Saltwater Crocodiles located on the secluded beach northwest of the ghetto
  • Yarrow Flower: Growing near the cliffside paths and ruins around the ghetto perimeter
  • Migo's Ring MISSABLE: Given by Migo on the southern beach if handed a Yarrow Flower, or looted from his corpse
  • Saheila's Crest MISSABLE: Rewarded by Saheila inside the Caverns after rescuing Amyro from Griff's cage

Secrets

  • Digging up the concealed mound near the shrine of Lucian in the square opens an underground entrance directly into the Withermoore ruins.
  • A hidden alcove high on the southern cliffside can be reached by using Teleportation to move a party member up to a concealed wooden scaffold containing a chest.
  • The hatch hidden under the bedroll in the elf cave leads to the ancient subterranean ruins where Lord Withermoore is trapped.

Shops and people

  • Hilde: Scoundrel skill books, lockpicks, daggers, and leather armor in Griff's camp
  • Kalias: Warfare skill books, shields, and melee weapons inside the Caverns
  • Maol: Geomancer skill books, located on the southern shoreline
  • Rezik: Hydrosophist skill books, wandering near the center of the camp
  • Stingtail: Pyrokinetic skill books, resting on the beach southwest of the camp
  • Gawin: Aerotheurge skill books, located near the camp's central thoroughfare
  • Nebora: Summoning skill books and crafting materials, stationed near the main entrance
  • Doctor Leste: Polymorph skill books, tending to a wounded patient near the fortress walls

Tips

  • Acquire the Teleportation Gloves early from the crocodiles, as they allow you to access multiple secluded ledges, pull unreachable chests, and create alternate escape routes.
  • Do not let Sebille speak to Stingtail if you want to buy Pyrokinetic skill books from him, as she will murder him during her personal dialogue.
  • Giving Migo a Yarrow Flower resolves his encounter peacefully, granting a ring that casts Restoration and opening a quest with Magister Yarrow on the battlements.
  • Keep a shovel or a Lizard character in your party at all times, as numerous dirt mounds containing buried treasure and secret entrances are scattered across the ghetto.
