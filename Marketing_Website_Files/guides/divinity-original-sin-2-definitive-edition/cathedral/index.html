Cathedral

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Entering the final sanctuary

The Cathedral in the city of Arx serves as the entry point to the Path of Blood and the final sanctuary. Players must bypass the Path of Blood's purity trial or use an alternate hidden mechanism to unlock the descent below. Completing the puzzles in the crypt beneath the Cathedral allows the party to enter Lucian's tomb and proceed to the game's finale.

Items

  • Amulet of the Toyseller MISSABLE: Acquired from Toyseller Sanders in Arx to activate the Path of Blood mechanism
  • Scroll of the Source Barrier MISSABLE: Acquired from Toyseller Sanders in Arx to unveil the entrance hatch

Secrets

  • Path of Blood bypass: Instead of taking the purity test at the statue of Lucian, place the Amulet of the Toyseller and read the Scroll of the Source Barrier at the hidden switch on the pillar to reveal the hatch to the crypt.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
PuppetsInfinite waves of puppet enemies spawn in the crypt puzzle room until all Source barrier levers spelling "POWER" are correctly pulled.

Tips

  • Bring Source Infusion or Source spells into the crypt puzzle room, as you must bless the floor and utilize Source to activate the pipe and altar mechanism.
  • A character who has never killed, stolen, lied, or used Source can pass Lucian's purity test directly, though it is usually far easier to use Toyseller Sanders' bypass method.
