Ryker's Mansion

Divinity: Original Sin 2 - Definitive Edition guide · Visiting the necromancer's home

Ryker's Mansion is located at the Stonegarden graveyard on Reaper's Coast. Players meet the necromancer Ryker, who offers to teach Source Mastery in exchange for retrieving an ancient tablet from the Blackpits. The mansion also hides a private basement sanctum where Ryker conducts sacrificial rituals.

Items

  • Ancient Stone Tablet MISSABLE: Ryker's Private Study in the basement
  • Spidersilk Gloves: Desk in Ryker's ground floor study

Secrets

  • A hatch to Ryker's basement is hidden beneath a rug in his ground floor office, revealed by interacting with a nearby lever.
  • A secret door behind a bookshelf in the basement opens by pulling the correct lever among several decoys on the walls.

Enemies

EnemySteal / dropNotes
RykerRyker's staff and high-tier necromancy lootTeleports around the arena, summons guardian minions, and sacrifices chained souls to empower his magic.

Shops and people

  • Ryker: Necromancer skill books, crafting ingredients, and high-level gear

Tips

  • Do not refuse Ryker's request outright upon returning with the tablet if you wish to receive his Source point upgrade before fighting him.
  • Talking to the mansion's animals using the Pet Pal talent reveals warnings about Ryker's true nature and hints about hidden passages.
