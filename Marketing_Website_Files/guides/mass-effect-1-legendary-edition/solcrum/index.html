Solcrum

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · Side mission exploration

Solcrum is an uncharted, heavily irradiated moon orbiting the gas giant Dholen in the Han system of the Voyager Cluster. Commander Shepard travels here during the "UNC: Hostile Takeover" assignment to eliminate the crime boss Dora Groves inside his heavily guarded mercenary base. Navigating the surface requires driving the Mako while contending with severe Level 2 Hazard radiation that limits time spent on foot.

Items

  • Asari Writings MISSABLE: Crashed Probe located in the mountainous terrain northwest of the landing zone
  • Turian Insignia MISSABLE: Inside a Malfunctioning Probe situated in the south-central portion of the map

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
MercenaryBiotics, anti-personnel roundsGuards the exterior grounds and patrols inside the subterranean hideout

Tips

  • Monitor your Level 2 Hazard meter constantly when exiting the Mako, as the intense solar radiation will quickly deplete your shields and kill your squad.
  • Take out the mercenary snipers on the exterior ridges using the Mako's cannon before disembarking to enter the mercenary compound.
