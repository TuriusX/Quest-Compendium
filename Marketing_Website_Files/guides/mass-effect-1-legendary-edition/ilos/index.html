Ilos

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · After Citadel under attack

Shepard lands on the lost Prothean world of Ilos to pursue Saren and prevent him from activating the Citadel relay for Sovereign. You must fight through Geth defenses on foot to disable the security lockdown, speak with the Prothean VI Vigil, and then drive the Mako through the Conduit trench before it closes.

Secrets

  • Armory cache behind security doors | Found in the upper levels of the Courtyard by hacking the secondary security terminal before descending into the archives.

Tips

  • Do not exit the Mako during the trench run toward the Conduit; the countdown timer leaves very little room for delay.
  • Use the Mako's cannon and machine gun to eliminate the Geth Colossus and Armatures guarding the Conduit ramp rather than engaging them on foot.
  • Exhaust all dialogue options with the VI Vigil to receive the data file needed to override Saren's Citadel master control, as well as crucial lore details.
  • Once you drop down the elevator shaft in the Mako after speaking to Vigil, you are locked into the endgame sequence with no way to return to the Normandy.
