Mavigon

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · Side mission exploration

Mavigon is an uncharted, frozen world located in the Han system of the Gemini Sigma cluster. Landing here allows Commander Shepard to navigate treacherous mountainous terrain in the Mako to investigate an unlicensed Syndicate base. Entering the subterranean facility involves neutralizing a fortified group of mercenary syndicate operatives.

Items

  • Molybdenum Deposit: Surveyable mineral deposit located in the mountainous terrain
  • Cobalt Deposit: Surveyable mineral deposit found on the planet surface
  • Med Kit: Mounted on the wall inside the subterranean syndicate bunker
  • Storage Locker: Placed along the perimeter walls inside the pirate base

Tips

  • Mavigon has a Level 2 Extreme Cold hazard on its surface, which will deplete your life support meter rapidly if you exit the Mako for extended periods.
  • Use the Mako's cannon to eliminate the mercenary snipers guarding the exterior of the hideout before disembarking to enter the compound.
  • Watch out for sniper lasers inside the main base room; utilize the entrance hallway as a choke point to draw enemies out safely.
