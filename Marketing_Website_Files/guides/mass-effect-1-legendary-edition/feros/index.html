Feros

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · Arrival at Zhu's Hope

Commander Shepard arrives at the Zhu's Hope colony on the planet Feros to investigate Geth attacks on the ExoGeni facility. The squad must immediately secure the landing pad and speak with colony leader Fai Dan to understand the settlement's critical situation. After defending the colony from initial assaults, Shepard takes on tasks to restore vital survival resources to the colonists before advancing deeper into the corporate complex.

Tips

  • Talk to Macha Doyle, Davin Reynolds, and May O'Connell before leaving the area to start the three major colony support assignments (Water, Food, and Power).
  • Keep the colonists alive whenever possible, as your choices here contribute to the overall Zhu's Hope survival rating later in the mission.
  • When fighting Geth Snipers, stay behind solid cover until their laser sights move away from your squad to avoid devastating one-shot shield drains.
