Noveria

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · After recruiting Liara and completing Feros

Commander Shepard travels to the frozen corporate research world of Noveria to track down Matriarch Benezia. After dealing with local bureaucracy and corporate corruption at Port Hanshan to secure a garage pass, Shepard journeys by Mako through a blizzard to reach the Peak 15 research facility. Within Peak 15, Shepard must restore the facility's disabled virtual intelligence, purge an infestation of escaped Rachni, and confront Benezia.

Items

  • Garage Pass MISSABLE: Port Hanshan: Obtained from Gianna Parasini after completing the Lorik Qui'in questline, or from Administrator Anoleis directly
  • Maintenance Hatch Key MISSABLE: Port Hanshan: Inside Administrator Anoleis's office safe
  • Mako: Noveria docking garage: Stationed at the vehicle bay after acquiring the garage pass

Secrets

  • Lorik Qui'in Infinite Morality Exploit | In Port Hanshan, after retrieving Lorik Qui'in's evidence, Shepard can repeatedly convince him to testify using Charm or Intimidate, which awards Paragon or Renegade points continuously each time the dialogue tree is looped.

Shops and people

  • Petozi: Hanar merchant in Port Hanshan selling general gear, weapon upgrades, armor, and omni-tools.
  • Opold: Hanar merchant in Port Hanshan who sells equipment and offers an illicit smuggling assignment for an illegal weapon mod.

Tips

  • Bring Liara T'Soni in your squad for unique and emotionally resonant dialogue during the confrontation with Matriarch Benezia.
  • In the Peak 15 Mira Core puzzle, you must transfer data blocks between three pillars; the classic Tower of Hanoi solution requires moving the blocks one at a time so that a larger block is never placed on top of a smaller one.
  • At Rift Station, you can either synthesize a cure for the poisoned scientists to gain peaceful entry to the lower labs or fight your way past the guards.
  • Using Incendiary Ammo against Rachni prevents them from regenerating health and quickly dispatches their armor.
