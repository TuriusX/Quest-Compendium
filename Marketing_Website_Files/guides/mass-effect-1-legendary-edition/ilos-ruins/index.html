Ilos Ruins

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · During Ilos mission

Shepard and the squad arrive on the mysterious Prothean world of Ilos to pursue Saren Arterius before he can use the Conduit. You must fight through heavy Geth resistance on foot across the Courtyard and Upper Ruins to access the security controls. Deactivating the security lockdown opens the blast doors below, allowing you to return to the Mako and proceed deeper into the planet.

Items

  • Armature Foot Locker MISSABLE: In the lower plaza near the disabled elevator in the Courtyard
  • Secure Crate MISSABLE: Inside the security station next to the console used to deactivate the lockdown

Tips

  • You cannot return to the Normandy or any previous planets once you land on Ilos, so ensure your squad equipment is finalized before landing.
  • Use Damping or Sabotage on the Geth Armatures early in the engagement to prevent them from firing their siege cannons.
  • Take advantage of the heavy stone pillars and broken Prothean architecture for cover against the elevated snipers.
