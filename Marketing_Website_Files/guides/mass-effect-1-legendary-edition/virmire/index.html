Virmire

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · After Noveria

Shepard and the Normandy crew are dispatched to Virmire to assist an undercover Salarian Special Tasks Group unit pinned down by Geth forces. You discover Saren's base of operations, which houses a cloning facility to breed an army of Krogan cured of the genophage. You must assault the base, destroy the breeding facility, and make critical choices regarding the fate of squadmates.

Items

  • Cinch (Armory License) MISSABLE: Sold by Commander Rentola at the Salarian base camp before launching the assault
  • Kassa Fabrication (Armory License) MISSABLE: Sold by Commander Rentola at the Salarian base camp before launching the assault
  • Sirta Foundation (Armory License) MISSABLE: Sold by Commander Rentola at the Salarian base camp before launching the assault

Secrets

  • Assisting Captain Kirrahe's strike team during the base infiltration (disrupting communications, destroying satellite uplinks, shooting Geth flyers, and hacking base alarms to direct Geth to the far side) ensures Kirrahe survives the mission and appears in later games.

Shops and people

  • Commander Rentola: Salarian quartermaster at the STG camp offering basic weapons, armors, mods, and three distinct manufacturer armory licenses

Tips

  • High Charm or Intimidate ranks, or completing Wrex's family armor assignment beforehand, are required to stand down Wrex during the confrontation on the beach without resorting to killing him.
  • You will be forced to choose between saving Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko during the final detonation phase; the squadmate you do not rescue dies permanently.
  • Disable the anti-aircraft guns as early as possible inside the facility to give the Normandy safe extraction clearance.
