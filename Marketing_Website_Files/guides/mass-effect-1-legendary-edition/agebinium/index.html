Agebinium

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · Side mission exploration

Agebinium is a mountainous world in the Amazon system of the Voyage Cluster where Commander Shepard investigates an Alliance distress beacon. Following the signal leads to an abandoned mining facility that turns out to be an ambush trap set by a mercenary named Elanos Haliat. You must disable a rigged nuclear warhead, escape the facility, and eliminate Haliat and his pirate forces.

Items

  • Heavy Weapon Locker: Inside the mine facility near the trapped Alliance probe

Tips

  • Once you interact with the Alliance probe inside the mine, a countdown starts on a nuclear warhead; immediately examine the bomb and either spend Omni-gel or complete the bypass mini-game to disarm it.
  • You cannot leave the mine the way you entered because the entrance is sealed during the ambush; look for the secondary tunnel exit near the back of the facility.
  • Use the high vantage point when exiting through the back tunnel to snipe Elanos Haliat's mercenaries before engaging them at close range.
