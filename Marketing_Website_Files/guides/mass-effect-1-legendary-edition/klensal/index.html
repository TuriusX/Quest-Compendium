Klensal

Mass Effect 1 (Legendary Edition) guide · Side mission exploration

Klensal is a harsh, mountainous planet located in the Dis system of the Hades Gamma cluster. Commander Shepard lands here via the Mako to investigate an illegal mercenary syndicate facility during the assignment "UNC: Hostile Takeover". You must eliminate the mercenary forces stationed outside and breach the syndicate hideout to neutralize their leader.

Items

  • Maelstrom Armor Upgrades MISSABLE: Inside a secure storage crate within the syndicate base
  • League of One Medallion: Found in an ancient debris salvage site on the surface
  • Rare Earths: Beryllium: Located at a mineral deposit in the mountainous terrain
  • Heavy Metal: Platinum: Found at a surveyable mineral node on the planet's surface

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Mercenary SniperBioticsPositioned on elevated ground outside the hideout and dangerous at range.
MercenaryTech abilitiesStandard syndicate ground forces stationed both outside and inside the underground base.
Syndicate BossStasis or LiftTough biotically capable mercenary leader encountered at the back of the underground base.

Tips

  • Use the Mako's cannon to wipe out the initial wave of mercenaries and their turrets guarding the base exterior before going in on foot.
  • Inside the syndicate hideout, funnel approaching mercenaries through the doorway using crowd control biotics like Lift or Singularity to avoid getting surrounded.
