Pickham

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Town of thieves and information

The party travels to the lawless town of Pickham to seek out the information broker Brains to track down Dhoulmagus. While searching for him, Medea and the wagon are stolen, leading to a confrontation in the local pub and a deal with the eccentric thief Dodgy Dave. To get the horse back and earn Brains's help, the party must retrieve the Venus' Tear from the nearby Swordsman's Labyrinth.

Items

  • Rennet powder: Inside a pot in a house in the northern part of town
  • Red mould: Inside a barrel near the slum area
  • Waterweed mould: Inside a barrel in the lower alleys
  • Seed of life: Inside a pot near the watchtower area
  • Magic water: Inside a pot in the rooftop settlement area
  • Bunny tail: Inside a cabinet in the fortune teller's house
  • Chain whip: Inside a treasure chest behind a locked door accessible with the Thief's Key

Secrets

  • Dodgy Dave operates a special barter shop in his lounge, trading rare weapons, armor, and items in exchange for specific concoctions created through the Alchemy Pot.

Shops and people

  • Weapon Shop: Sells Steel broadsword, Iron axe, Holy lance, and Crossbow
  • Armor Shop: Sells Leather cape, Dancer's costume, Iron armor, and Light shield
  • Item Shop: Sells Medicinal herb, Antidotal herb, Holy water, and Chimaera wing
  • Church: Offers saving, resurrection, and curing of status ailments

Tips

  • Breaking pots and rummaging through barrels on the town's rooftop walkways yields a significant number of alchemy ingredients early on.
  • Talk to the barkeep and the patrons at the town's central tavern immediately after Medea disappears to trigger the search for the wagon thief.
