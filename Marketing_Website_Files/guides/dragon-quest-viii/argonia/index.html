Argonia

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

Following the events at the Dark Ruins, the party returns to Argonia to find that the Great Bazaar has officially opened in the town square. King Clavius grants the party permission to take the Magic Mirror from the castle treasury to assist in your quest. After obtaining the mirror and learning it has lost its power, you must locate the Seer's spring to restore it.

Items

  • Magic Mirror: Argonia Castle, inside the treasure vault on the fourth floor

Shops and people

  • Great Bazaar Armor Stall: Heavy Armour, Silk Bustier, Magic Vestment, Silver Cuirass
  • Great Bazaar Weapon Stall: Battle-Fork, Partisan, Silver Mail, Iron Axe, Light Shield
  • Great Bazaar Item Merchant: Chimaera Wing, Holy Water, Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb, Moonwort Bulb

Tips

  • The Great Bazaar only remains open between your first visit after the Dark Ruins and the defeat of the boss at Arcadia, after which the merchants relocate inside the town building.
  • Don't forget to purchase a Scholar's Specs from the bazaar to use in several high-tier Alchemy Pot recipes.
