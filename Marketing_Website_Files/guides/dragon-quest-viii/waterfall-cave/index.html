Waterfall Cave

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · First dungeon to find the crystal ball

This is the first dungeon in the game, located south of the town of Farebury. The Hero and Yangus enter to retrieve the Crystal Ball for the fortune teller Kalderasha. At the bottom, you must defeat the boss Geyzer to claim the Crystal Ball and clear the area.

Items

  • Map of Waterfall Cave: In a treasure chest on the first basement floor (B1) near the entrance
  • Medicinal Herb: In a treasure chest on B1
  • Leather Hat: In a treasure chest on B2
  • Copper Sword: In a treasure chest on B3
  • Crystal Ball: Dropped after defeating the boss at the bottom of the cave (B3)

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
GeyzerFrizz and Sizzcannot stealBoss of the dungeon who uses physical claw attacks, Sizz, and a Cursed Mist that can waste a character's turn.

Tips

  • Reach at least Level 4 or 5 with the Hero so you learn the Heal spell before challenging the boss.
  • Yangus's Psyche Up command is very effective against the boss to deal heavy damage with a single charged strike.
