Riverside Chapel

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Save point and rest stop

The Riverside Chapel is a small waystation situated beside a river on the path between the Ruined Abbey and the town of Ascantha. It functions primarily as a safe rest area where the party can heal, save progress, and gather their bearings. Outside, you can find a pier with a ferryman that connects the chapel area to the river route.

Items

  • Tough guy tattoo: Inside the church bedroom in a dresser
  • Holy water: Inside the church bedroom in a dresser

Shops and people

  • Innkeeper: Offers a full party rest and recovery for 10 gold coins
  • Priest: Provides standard church services including Confession (saving the game), Divination, and Resurrection

Tips

  • Break all the pots and barrels located directly behind and around the chapel exterior to grab some quick common crafting ingredients and gold coins.
