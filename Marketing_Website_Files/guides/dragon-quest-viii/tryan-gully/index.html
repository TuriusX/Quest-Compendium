Tryan Gully

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

Tryan Gully is a secluded sanctuary where humans, elves, and monsters coexist peacefully deep in the eastern mountains. The party travels here to obtain the Darktree Leaf and the Godbird's Sceptre after learning of Leopold's path of destruction. Speaking with the elf leader Raya advances the story and provides essential guidance for tracking down the possessed dog.

Items

  • Darktree Leaf: Given by Raya inside her dwelling in the trunk
  • Yggdrasil Dew: Inside a barrel in the tavern area
  • Elfin Elixir: In a treasure chest inside Raya's room
  • Premium Mould: Found in a pot inside the monster kitchen area

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells items including Moonwort Bulb, Water Feather, and various basic concoctions
  • Equipment Shop: Sells powerful mid-to-late game weapons and armor such as the Great Bow and Heavy Armour

Tips

  • Use the Darktree Leaf from your inventory while on the world map to track the current flight path of Sir Leopold.
  • You must fly using the Godbird's soul to access the elevated gorge entrance that leads to Tryan Gully.
