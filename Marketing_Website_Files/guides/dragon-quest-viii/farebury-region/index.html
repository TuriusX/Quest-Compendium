Farebury Region

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Field area surrounding Farebury

This is the opening wilderness area of the game surrounding the town of Farebury and leading south toward the Waterfall Cave. The party must explore the region to gain experience, gather starter equipment, and complete the fortune teller Kalderasha's quest to retrieve the Crystal Ball. It connects Farebury to the Waterfall Cave and the checkpoint path leading to the Alexandria Region.

Items

  • Medicinal Herb: inside a pot near the entrance of the Waterfall Cave
  • 82 Gold Coins: inside a treasure chest located south of Farebury
  • Boxer Shorts: inside a treasure chest on a path south of Farebury

Secrets

  • A friendly Hammerhood stands atop a hill southwest of Farebury and will give advice without attacking.

Tips

  • Return to the inn in Farebury to heal rather than burning through restorative herbs in the early levels.
  • Monsters become significantly stronger and more dangerous in this region once night falls.
