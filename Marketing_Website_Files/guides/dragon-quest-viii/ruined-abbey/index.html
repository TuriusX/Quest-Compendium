Ruined Abbey

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Dungeon where you fight the Abbot's shadow

The Ruined Abbey is an underground dungeon beneath Maella Abbey, accessed through a secret passage along the riverbank using the World Cloister Ring. The party navigates the monster-infested ruins to reach Abbot Francisco's quarters and investigate Marcello's suspicions. At the end of the dungeon, the party faces the boss Tortured Soul before surfacing inside the abbey grounds.

Items

  • Iron Nails: inside an old dresser in the lower living quarters
  • Bronze Shield: inside a treasure chest on the lowest basement floor
  • Mini Medal: inside a breakable barrel on the path through the ruins

Tips

  • Keep a supply of Antidotal Herbs on hand, as several poisonous floors and enemies in the dungeon can rapidly drain your health.
  • This area is one of the earliest spots to hunt Metal Slimes; use Yangus's Hatchet Man or multi-hit physical skills to defeat them before they run.
