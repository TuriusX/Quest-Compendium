Dragovian Path

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Post-game

The Dragovian Path is an arduous post-game dungeon unlocked by loading your cleared save file and examining the altar at the Dragon's Graveyard. It consists of multiple winding subterranean and cavernous floors populated by brutal end-game monsters. Navigating to the end of the path leads directly into the Dragovian Sanctuary, the village of the Dragovian tribe.

Items

  • Liquid metal shield: inside a treasure chest on B1
  • Seed of skill: inside a treasure chest on B2
  • Thanatos' shield: inside a treasure chest on B3
  • Elfin elixir: inside a treasure chest on B4
  • Metal king spear: inside a treasure chest on B4

Tips

  • Equip Holy Talismans or accessories that protect against instant-death spells, as monsters like Pandoran's Box frequently cast Thwack.
  • Yangus's Stainless Steal Sickle can be used on Pandoran's Boxes here for a chance to steal rare Orichalcum.
  • Keep the Hero and Angelo ready to cast Kazing, as standard random encounters here can easily defeat party members in a single turn.
