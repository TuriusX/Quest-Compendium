Dragon Graveyard

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

The Dragon Graveyard is a cavernous desert dungeon that unlocks as part of the Baccarat casino succession questline after completing the Dark Ruins. The party must escort either Cash or Carrie to the sacred trial chamber deep inside the skeletal remains. At the conclusion of the dungeon, you defeat a pair of bosses to restore power to Baccarat and reopen its casino.

Items

  • Dragon dung: Pot near the campsite inside the cavern
  • Mini medal: Pot inside the skeletal ribcage section
  • Yggdrasil dew: Treasure chest in the lower labyrinth pathways
  • Dragon scale: Bag hanging inside the skeleton caverns

Tips

  • Metal King Slimes rarely spawn inside the Dragon Graveyard, making it a prime location to farm experience using Yangus's Executioner or the Hero's Lightning Thrust.
  • You must rest at the campsite near the entrance with Cash or Carrie before proceeding into the depths of the trial.
  • Equipping Dragon Shields or armor that mitigates fire and cold breath will significantly reduce incoming damage from the dragon-type foes throughout the cavern.
