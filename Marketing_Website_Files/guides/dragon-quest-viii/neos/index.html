Neos is a holy island dominated by a massive statue of the Goddess and serves as the headquarters for the high priests of the Goddess religion. Players visit here during the search for Marcello and the Lord of the High Priests, and the town eventually becomes the site of a pivotal plot confrontation. Later in the game, the town is devastated during a major story event, rendering its original layout inaccessible.

Shops and people
  • Neos Weapon Shop: Steel Broadsword, Holy Silver Rapier, Bandana, Iron Helmet, Light Shield
  • Neos Armor Shop: Full Plate Armour, Silver Mail, Dancer's Costume, Cloak of Evasion

Tips
  • Collect every town treasure and purchase any exclusive gear before confronting the scepter's wielder later in the story, as the settlement will be destroyed.
  • The exterior overworld of Neos is one of the premier mid-game grinding spots for gold due to frequent encounters with Gold Golems.
