Tower of Alexandria

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Dungeon to find the magic mirror

The Tower of Alexandra is a dungeon located east of the village of Alexandria that the party explores to find Jessica Albert. Navigating the tower involves climbing multi-tiered staircases, solving a puzzle with statues using Munchie, and reaching the top floor to uncover the truth about Alistair's death.

Items

  • 11 gold coins: In a treasure chest on the first floor
  • Holy water: In a treasure chest on the second floor
  • Moonwort bulb: In a pot on the third floor
  • Seed of agility: In a treasure chest on the fourth floor
  • 22 gold coins: In a barrel on the fifth floor

Secrets

  • On the top floor, send Munchie through a small hole in the wall to push down a face statue's rotating mechanism, which unlocks the staircase leading to the roof.

Tips

  • Equip the Boomerang on the Hero to quickly clear the large swarms of weaker bats and moles encountered in this tower.
  • Ensure you inspect the face of the towering statue on the roof to trigger the cutscene with Jessica and Alistair's spirit.
