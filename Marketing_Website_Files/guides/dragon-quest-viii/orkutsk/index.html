Orkutsk

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

Orkutsk is a snow-covered, multi-tiered circular town in the northern continent that serves as a central hub during the blizzard storyline. The party arrives here seeking the herb specialist Marek on the orders of High Priest Rolo and Kalderasha. After discovering Marek is not in his room, players must head out to the Herb Grotto to find him.

Items

  • Chilly cheese: in a pot inside Marek's underground quarters
  • Mini medal: in a barrel located in the basement central plaza
  • Mini medal: inside a pot in the underground item storehouse
  • Ruby of protection: inside a wardrobe in the underground residential quarters

Shops and people

  • Orkutsk Weapon Shop: sells Bastard Sword, Blizzard Blade, Swallowtail, and Battle Fork
  • Orkutsk Armour Shop: sells Velvet Cape, Silver Cuirass, Dragon Shield, and Iron Headgear
  • Orkutsk Item Shop: sells Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb, Holy Water, and Chimaera Wing
  • Orkutsk Inn: offers rest to restore HP and MP for the party

Tips

  • The exterior and interior are laid out in concentric rings; using the underground central hub is much faster for navigating between shops than walking the outer snow paths.
  • Be sure to purchase a Blizzard Blade at the weapon shop, as it is an excellent weapon for Jessica or the Hero and is also a key ingredient for high-level Alchemy recipes.
