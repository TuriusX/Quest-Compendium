Savella Cathedral

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

After completing the Dark Ruins, Savella Cathedral becomes a key hub where you can access the Lord High Priest's residence using the elevator. Important story cutscenes occur here involving Marcello, High Priest Rolo, and the Lord High Priest as the scepter's influence spreads. You visit this holy island to advance the main plot before being directed toward your next major destination.

Items

  • Mini medal: In a pot inside the residence elevator room
  • Mini medal: In a cabinet inside the Lord High Priest's residence
  • Sage's robe: Inside a wardrobe on the second floor of the Lord High Priest's quarters

Shops and people

  • Item Shop: Sells Holy Water, Chimaera Wing, Moonwort Bulb, and status-curing items
  • Church: Offers saving, resurrection, curse removal, and divination
  • Inn: Restores all party members' HP and MP for a gold fee

Tips

  • The elevator leading up to the isolated high rock cliff is guarded and only functions after reaching this specific stage in the main story.
  • Remember to check the armoire and chests inside the Lord High Priest's house as soon as you gain access to avoid backtracking later.
