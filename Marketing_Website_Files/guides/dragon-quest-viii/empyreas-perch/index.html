Empyrea's Perch

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

Empyrea's Perch is a mountain summit located on the isolated Godbird's Eyrie plateau, accessible first in the World of Darkness and later in the Light World. The party must climb the dark mountain to confront the legendary Godbird Empyrea, learn the truth about her stolen egg, and eventually scale the light version to save her offspring from Gemon. Defeating the boss atop the Light World version grants the party the Godbird's Soulstone, unlocking airborne travel across the world map.

Items

  • Map of the Godbird's Eyrie: in a chest near the entrance path inside the mountain cave network

Tips

  • Gemon regularly summons subordinate monsters and uses powerful fire breath, so keep Insulatle active and focus damage on Gemon rather than endlessly clearing his adds.
  • Empyrea in the Dark World can use Disruptive Wave, meaning you should only build Tension to moderate levels before attacking rather than aiming for 50 or 100 every time.
