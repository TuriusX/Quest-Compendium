Swordsman's Labyrinth

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Dungeon to find the Venus' Tear

The Swordsman's Labyrinth is an ancient subterranean tomb explored to retrieve the legendary jewel known as the Venus' Tear for Red. To navigate deeper into the ruins, you must navigate traps, cross narrow stone bridges, and solve a stone face statue puzzle to reveal the hidden staircase. At the very bottom awaits the guardian spirit Trap Box, who must be defeated to claim the treasure.

Items

  • 11 Gold Coins: In a pot on Level 1
  • Antidotal Herb: In a pot on Level 1
  • Mini Medal: In a breakable pot on Level 2
  • Red Mould: In a pot on Level 2
  • Mini Medal: Inside a treasure chest on Level 4
  • Venus' Tear: In the treasure chest behind the boss on Level 4

Secrets

  • The hidden staircase to the lowest level is uncovered by pushing the giant stone face statue along its floor track until it slots into the square opening in the bridge.

Tips

  • Equipping your party members with status-curing items like Angel Cheese or stocking plenty of Panaceas is crucial for countering Trap Box's party-wide confusion breath.
  • Bring along a healthy supply of Antidotal Herbs or keep the Squelch spell ready, as several poison-inflicting enemies inhabit the corridors.
  • Inspect the holes in the wall carefully throughout the upper floor to avoid activating arrow traps while traversing the walkways.
