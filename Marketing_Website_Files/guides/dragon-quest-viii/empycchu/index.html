Empycchu

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

The party arrives in the isolated village of Empycchu in the southwest after sailing through the newly opened sea arch near the Godbird Eyrie. The residents revere the divine bird Empyrea and explain that her shadow has been sighted roaming the nearby plateau. To progress, the party must speak with the village chief and then pursue the bird's shadow outside to reach the Dark World.

Items

  • Fur Hood: inside a pot in a villager's hut
  • Seed of Strength: found inside a pot near one of the huts
  • Rennet Powder: inside a pot in the village
  • Fresh Milk: inside a pot within the village
  • Red Mould: inside a bag hanging on a wall in a residence
  • Waterweed Mould: inside a bag hanging in a residence
  • Cowpat: inside a barrel in the village

Shops and people

  • Empycchu Item Shop: Sells Dragon Dung, Rennet Powder, Fresh Milk, Red Mould, Waterweed Mould, Premium Mould, and Moonwort Bulb

Tips

  • Break all pots and inspect every hanging sack inside the homes, as this village is rich in rare alchemy ingredients like moulds and rennet powder.
  • Talk to the village chief in the main building to trigger the appearance of Empyrea's giant shadow in the fields south of the village.
