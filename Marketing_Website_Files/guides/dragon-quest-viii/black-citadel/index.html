Black Citadel

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

The Black Citadel is the massive floating fortress raised by Rhapthorne after the seal at the Godbird Eyrie is completely broken. The party must scale the labyrinthine city to reach the central throne room and confront the Lord of Shadows. After defeating Rhapthorne's physical form, the citadel begins to collapse, requiring the party to fight through several bosses during a timed escape sequence.

Items

  • Orichalcum MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest on the exterior rooftops
  • Ruinous Shield MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest within the lower circular maze area
  • Thanatos' Shield MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest in the residential district
  • Sage's Stone MISSABLE: inside a treasure chest located along the escape route
  • Yggdrasil Leaf MISSABLE: inside a pot within the ruined interior rooms

Secrets

  • Unlocking the central elevator shortcut creates a direct path from the entrance plaza to the upper heights, which becomes critical during the escape sequence.

Tips

  • Collect every treasure chest and breakable container before initiating the battle with Rhapthorne, as the entire Black Citadel collapses and becomes permanently inaccessible once you escape.
  • Keep the Sage's Stone in the inventory of a character who lacks multi-target healing spells, such as Yangus, to provide free party-wide HP restoration during long battles.
