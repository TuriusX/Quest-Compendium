Simpleton

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Town near the abbey

Simpleton is a run-down, seedy settlement located downstream from Maella Abbey, primarily known for its lively and unruly pub. You visit this town to look for Angelo after getting turned away at the abbey gates, which triggers a bar brawl that progresses the main storyline.

Items

  • Mini medal: in a barrel inside the pub
  • Plain clothes: in a wardrobe on the second floor of the inn

Shops and people

  • Weapon and armor merchant: sells Rapier, Iron Lance, Farmer's Scythe, Leather Shield, and Scale Armor
  • Innkeeper: offers rest for the party for a small fee per person
  • Church priest: offers game saving, revival, curing poison, and removing curses

Tips

  • Speak to the hooded man gambling at the table inside the pub to advance the story and meet Angelo.
  • Be prepared for an unavoidable fistfight with rowdy thugs in the bar, so heal your party at the inn before triggering the cutscene.
