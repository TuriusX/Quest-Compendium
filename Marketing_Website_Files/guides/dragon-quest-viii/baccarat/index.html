Baccarat

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

After the events at the Dark Ruins, Baccarat becomes the hub for a succession feud between Cash and Carrie following Mr. Golding's death. You can visit the mansion to choose which sibling to back as a bodyguard, unlocking the quest to the Dragon Graveyard. Successfully completing their trial resolves the dispute and reopens the town's casino.

Items

  • Rennet powder: barrel in the mansion kitchen pantry
  • Fresh milk: pot in the mansion kitchen pantry
  • Mini medal: cabinet on the top floor of the mansion
  • Mini medal: barrel on the raised walkway behind the mansion
  • Gold ring: cabinet in a ground-floor guest room of the mansion
  • Garter: cabinet in the top floor room of the inn
  • Mini medal: pot in the inn cellar
  • Ruby of protection: cabinet inside Cash and Carrie's mansion bedroom

Shops and people

  • Innkeeper: Resting for 18 gold coins per person
  • Church priest: Saving game data, curing status ailments, and reviving party members
  • Weapon Shop: Sells weapons including the Falcon Knife, Steel Broadsword, and Battle-Fork
  • Armor Shop: Sells protective gear including the Coral Hairpin, Dancer's Costume, Light Shield, and Iron Helm

Tips

  • Speak to both Cash and Carrie in the mansion to listen to their offers before deciding which one to escort to the Dragon Graveyard.
  • Completing the sibling trial directly restores the Baccarat casino, granting access to high-value prizes like the Gringham Whip and Liquid Metal Armor.
