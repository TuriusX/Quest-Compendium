Port Prospect

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Coastal town where you take the ship

Port Prospect is a coastal port settlement that connects the starting region to the southern continent of Peregrin Quay. To board the ship, the party must first defeat a sea monster boss named Khalamari, who is disrupting maritime travel. Once the beast is dealt with, the ferry opens up, allowing the party to set sail and continue the main story.

Items

  • Chimaera wing: in a barrel near the inn
  • Holy water: inside a pot in the lighthouse building
  • Seed of strength: inside a pot near the docks

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
KhalamariFrizz / Fire attacksnone guaranteedBoss encountered immediately upon attempting to board the ferry for the first time.

Shops and people

  • Weapon and Armor Shop: Sells Stone Axe, Iron Lance, Farmer's Scythe, Leather Armor, Scale Shield, and Bronze Helmet
  • Item Shop: Sells Medicinal Herb, Antidotal Herb, Holy Water, and Chimaera Wing
  • Innkeeper: Offers rest for the party to fully restore HP and MP for a small fee

Tips

  • Prepare for the boss fight against Khalamari before talking to the ferry attendant, as the battle begins automatically once you set sail.
  • Equip Jessica and your physical attackers with weapons bought from the local shop to keep up with the jump in enemy defense across the sea.
