Trodain Castle

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · The cursed castle

The party returns to the cursed home of King Trode and Princess Medea, which has been overgrown by thorny brambles and frozen in time by Dhoulmagus. The objective is to navigate the ruinous castle halls to access the library and find a way to reach the mystical Moonshadow Realm. Inside the library, waiting until nightfall reveals a magical portal in the moonlight that leads to Ishmahri's domain.

Items

  • Magic Key: Inside a treasure chest on the 3rd floor
  • Templar's Sword: In a chest in the royal quarters
  • Gold Ring: In a dresser in the living quarters

Secrets

  • The library door at the back of the castle is initially locked from the inside, requiring you to enter the castle via the front gates, navigate through the bramble-choked upper corridors, and descend into the library from above to unlock it.

Tips

  • Trodain Castle's interior is one of the best locations at this point in the game to hunt Liquid Metal Slimes using skills like Yangus's Hatchet Man or Hero's Thunder Thrust.
  • Once you unlock the library's back doors from the inside, you can access the Moonshadow Realm door directly from the exterior without fighting your way through the castle again.
