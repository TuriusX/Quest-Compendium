Princess Minnie's Castle

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

Princess Minnie's Castle is an island fortress where Princess Minnie trades valuable equipment and items in exchange for Mini Medals you collect throughout your journey. After clearing the Dark Ruins, you can return here periodically to turn in your growing medal collection for powerful mid-to-late game milestone rewards. Outside of medal exchanges and looting the castle's internal chambers, the location serves as a peaceful safe haven with no hostile encounters inside.

Items

  • Mini Medal: Inside a barrel located in the outdoor area near the castle entrance
  • Mini Medal: Inside a pot in the small room adjacent to the throne room
  • Double-Edged Sword: Inside a locked treasure chest behind the ultimate lock in the castle treasury

Secrets

  • The treasury behind the throne room contains several high-tier chests that require the Ultimate Key to open.

Shops and people

  • Princess Minnie: Offers cumulative rewards such as the Staff of Divine Wrath, Gold Nugget, Meteorite Bracer, Miracle Sword, and Sacred Armour for handing over Mini Medals

Tips

  • Always zoom back to Princess Minnie immediately whenever your total Mini Medal count reaches the threshold for the next tier of equipment.
