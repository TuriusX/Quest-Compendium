Mole Hole

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Dungeon to find the Moonshadow Harp

The Mole Hole is an underground cavern located north of Castle Ascantha. The party enters this dungeon to retrieve the stolen Moonshadow Harp from a rogue group of moles. Navigating through the winding dirt tunnels leads to a subterranean boss battle against Don Mole and his soul-stirring musicians.

Items

  • Mini medal: Level 2, inside a pot located in the underground mole settlement
  • Stone hardhat: Level 3, found inside a treasure chest along the western path
  • Seed of life: Level 3, inside a treasure chest in the final cave section before the boss
  • Moonshadow Harp: Dropped after defeating the boss at the lowest level

Tips

  • Don Mole's performance can confuse both your party and his own minions, so equipping status-resistant gear or having items to cure confusion is vital.
  • Don Mole and his Soul Moles are susceptible to Snooze, making sleep magic an effective way to control the battlefield.
