Guides / DRAGON QUEST VIII

West Trodain Region

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Field area leading to the castle

This rugged wilderness connects the Port Prospect/Peregrin Quay coastline through a mountain tunnel up to the cursed seat of Castle Trodain. You pass through this area to reach the overgrown royal castle in order to retrieve the magical Moonshadow Harp. The land is fraught with significantly tougher enemies than earlier regions, signaling a noticeable jump in difficulty.

Items

  • Magic water: Inside a treasure chest located along the western cliffside path
  • Gold coin: Inside a treasure chest off the road leading toward Castle Trodain

Secrets

  • Slime Hill can be seen from high ridges in this region, but the plateau itself is entirely unreachable until much later in the game when you gain the ability to fly.

Tips

  • Stop by the deserted church resting near the path to save your progress and heal before entering the dangerous grounds of Castle Trodain.
  • Equip Yangus with an Iron Axe and use Helm Splitter often, as many monsters in this region have heavily fortified physical defense.
