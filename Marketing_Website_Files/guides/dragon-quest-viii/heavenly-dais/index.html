Heavenly Dais

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · Post-game

The Heavenly Dais is the final post-game dungeon located beyond the Dragovian Sanctuary. Players traverse cloud-like paths and floating ruins to reach the summit and challenge the Lord of the Dragovians. Completing the trials here unlocks powerful Dragovian equipment and enhancements for the hero.

Items

  • Dangerous bustier: Inside a treasure chest on the winding cloud paths
  • Mini medal: Inside a treasure chest along the ascent
  • Orichalcum: Found in a treasure chest on the later paths of the dais

Tips

  • Cast Insulatle immediately when fighting the dragon trials to mitigate massive elemental breath damage.
  • Bring plenty of Elfin Elixirs and Yggdrasil Leaves, as boss encounters at the summit are back-to-back with no healing in between.
