Arcadia

DRAGON QUEST VIII guide · After the Dark Ruins

After confronting Dhoulmagus at the Dark Ruins, the party travels to the stone-walled city of Arcadia in pursuit of the scepter's next victim. You must assist the haughty sorcerer Dominico, who fears an attack by the possessed Jessica, by retrieving the Kran Spinels to power his defensive barrier. Completing this storyline restores Jessica to your party and unlocks the ability to use the Godbird's Sceptre in later events.

Items

  • Rennet powder: Inside a pot in a lower-tier house in Arcadia
  • Mini medal: Inside a barrel near the fountain square
  • Magic water: Found inside a wardrobe on the second floor of Dominico's mansion
  • Dragon dung: Search the pots located near the secret outdoor path
  • Gold ring: Found inside a treasure chest within Dominico's private quarters
  • Mini medal: Smashed out of a pot in Dominico's garden area

Secrets

  • The Kran Spinels are not hidden in Arcadia itself, but in the nearby Kran Mound dungeon north of the city, requiring you to navigate the subterranean puzzles to retrieve them for Dominico.

Shops and people

  • Arcadia Weapon Shop: Sells Swordsman's Epee, Platinum Sword, Dragon Slayer, and Bandit Axe
  • Arcadia Armor Shop: Sells Magic Vestment, Cloak of Evasion, Dragon Shield, and Iron Headgear
  • Arcadia Item Shop: Sells Medicinal Herb, Strong Medicine, Antidotal Herb, Holy Water, and Chimaera Wing

Tips

  • Visit Dominico's mansion at night to loot cabinets and pots with fewer guard interactions interrupting your exploration.
  • Equipping fire-resistant armor such as the Magic Vestment before triggering the confrontation against Jessica will drastically reduce the damage she deals with her Frizzle spells.
