Smouldering Lake

DARK SOULS III guide · After entering the Catacombs of Carthus

Smouldering Lake is an optional underground expanse located beneath the Catacombs of Carthus, characterized by a massive lake constantly bombarded by a giant triple-shot ballista. Players must navigate the hazards of the lake and delve into the labyrinthine Demon Ruins beneath it to disable the siege weapon and defeat the Old Demon King.

Items

  • Large Titanite Shard: scattered across the lake bed
  • Shield of Want: found on a corpse in the open lake area near the giant worm
  • Chaos Gem: dropped by a Crystal Lizard in the lower corridors of the Demon Ruins
  • Izalith Pyromancy Tome: located near the Old King's Antechamber bonfire behind an illusory wall
  • Quelana Pyromancy Tome: found against a dead Fair Lady spider corpse behind an illusory wall in the Demon Ruins
  • Estus Shard: found behind an illusory wall near the Demon Ruins bonfire
  • Undead Bone Shard: dropped by the Carthus Sandworm upon defeat
  • Undead Bone Shard: found on a corpse in the flooded hallway with Basilisks in the Demon Ruins
  • Black Knight Sword: found on a corpse resting on a broken architectural ledge in the Demon Ruins
  • Fume Ultra Greatsword: dropped by Knight Slayer Tsorig upon defeat in the lava chamber of the Demon Ruins

Secrets

  • The wooden suspension bridge before High Lord Wolnir in the Catacombs can be broken to form a ladder that leads downward to the Smouldering Lake entrance.
  • A destructible brick wall on the lake perimeter can be shattered by baiting the giant ballista's projectiles into it, revealing the Speckled Stoneplate Ring.
  • Multiple illusory walls are hidden throughout the Demon Ruins, concealing bonfires, pyromancy tomes, and shortcuts.

Tips

  • The giant ballista fire can be used to kill the Carthus Sandworm effortlessly if you stand behind the worm and let the incoming bolts hit its body.
  • You can disable the giant ballista entirely by reaching the mechanism at the highest peak of the ruins and interacting with the central lever.
  • Equipping high fire-resistance gear, the Flame Stoneplate Ring, and casting Flash Sweat is necessary to survive walking across the lava to collect items in the lower ruins.
