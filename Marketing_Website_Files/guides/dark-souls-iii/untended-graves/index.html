Untended Graves

DARK SOULS III guide · After defeating the Consumed King

This dark, alternate version of the Cemetery of Ash is reached after conquering Oceiros, the Consumed King. Players must navigate past powerful enemies in pitch darkness to fight Champion Gundyr and access the dark Firelink Shrine. Inside, you can collect key items and an alternate ending component.

Items

  • Ashen Estus Ring: Found in the starting basin where you first awaken in the Cemetery of Ash
  • Titanite Slab: Dropped by the dark Firelink Shrine Handmaid or found behind the coiled sword bonfire
  • Eyes of a Fire Keeper: Found behind an illusory wall where Irina of Carim normally sits in the shrine
  • Coiled Sword Fragment: In the central pit of the dark Firelink Shrine where the bonfire would usually be
  • Chaos Blade: Outside the dark Firelink Shrine where the Sword Master normally stands

Secrets

  • Illusory wall behind the chest after the Oceiros boss fight leads directly into the Untended Graves.
  • Illusory wall in the dark Firelink Shrine where Irina of Carim sits hides the Eyes of a Fire Keeper.

Shops and people

  • Shrine Handmaid (Dark Firelink): Sells the Priestess Ring, Artorias's Armor set (Wolf Knight Set), and twinkling titanite

Tips

  • Bring a torch or the Cast Light sorcery to navigate the pitch-black waters and avoid getting ambushed.
  • Champion Gundyr is extremely susceptible to parrying, which can make his relentless melee combos manageable.
