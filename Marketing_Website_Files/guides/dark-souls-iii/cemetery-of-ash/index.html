Cemetery of Ash

DARK SOULS III guide · Beginning of the game

The Cemetery of Ash serves as the starting tutorial area where players awaken from their graves as the Ashen One. Players learn fundamental combat and exploration mechanics while navigating through undead hollows. The path culminates in a boss fight against Iudex Gundyr, which unlocks the way forward to Firelink Shrine.

Items

  • Ashen Estus Flask: On a corpse sitting near a fountain at the start of the area
  • Coiled Sword: Looted from the body of Iudex Gundyr after defeating him
  • Titanite Scale: Dropped by the Ravenous Crystal Lizard in the side path
  • East-West Shield: On a corpse resting against a tree near the tutorial messages

Secrets

  • A flooded side path to the right of the main trail contains a developer warning sign and leads to a powerful Ravenous Crystal Lizard.
  • You can jump off the path leading toward Firelink Shrine onto an adjacent coffin ledge to retrieve a Soul item.

Tips

  • In the second phase of the Iudex Gundyr fight, stay glued to his backside to avoid the chaotic hitbox of his dark serpent form.
  • Firebombs found on a ledge overlooking the arena can deal massive damage to Iudex Gundyr's second phase.
  • You can practice parrying on the Hollows or Gundyr's halberd attacks to make early combat significantly easier.
