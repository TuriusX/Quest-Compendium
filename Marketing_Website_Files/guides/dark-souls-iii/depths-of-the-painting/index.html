Depths of the Painting

DARK SOULS III guide · After progressing from the Snowy Mountain Pass
← All areas in this guide

The Depths of the Painting is a frigid, icy valley at the lowest point of the Painted World of Ariandel, accessed by descending the giant frozen tree roots beneath the rope bridge. It features a frozen lake bed, an optional boss encounter, and a pathway leading back upward into a hollow tower. Players come here to defeat the optional boss for a unique weapon and the item required to unlock Undead Match PvP.

Items

0/4

Enemies
Champion's Gravetender
Weak to Bleed, LightningSteal / drop Champion's Bones, Valorheart

Fights alongside regular wolves before summoning the Gravetender Greatwolf when at low health

Gravetender Greatwolf
Weak to Fire, Bleed

Enters the battle when the Gravetender loses about half his health and enters a hyper-aggressive state when low on health

Giant Crab
Weak to Strike, Fire

Roams the frozen expanse and can burrow into the snow to ambush

Tree Woman
Weak to Fire

Plant-like enemies found near the Snap Freeze spell that cast frost-based spells or grab the player

Tips
  • Burn the Champion's Bones at the Firelink Shrine bonfire to unlock Undead Match multiplayer arena combat.
  • Focus on bursting down the human Champion's Gravetender before the Gravetender Greatwolf enters the fight to avoid dealing with both threats simultaneously.
  • Fire damage quickly staggers both the Greatwolf and regular wolves in this snowy expanse.
