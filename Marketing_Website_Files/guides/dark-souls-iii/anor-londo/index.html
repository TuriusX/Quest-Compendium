Anor Londo

DARK SOULS III guide · After passing through Irithyll of the Boreal Valley

Anor Londo is a frozen, ruinous remnant of the legendary city of the gods that players enter immediately after rotating the spiral tower bridge in Irithyll. The main goal is to infiltrate the cathedral, unlock the front gates, and face Aldrich, Devourer of Gods, one of the Lords of Cinder. Clearing this area is mandatory for progressing the main storyline.

Items

  • Giant's Coal: On the corpse of the Giant Blacksmith in the side room to the left of the main cathedral stairs
  • Aldrich's Ruby: Dropped by the Deep Accursed monstrous spider inside the main cathedral hall
  • Estus Shard: Inside a chest located along the perimeter wall of the ground floor in the main cathedral hall
  • Sun Princess Ring: On the floor in the room directly above the Aldrich, Devourer of Gods boss arena
  • Cinders of a Lord (Aldrich): Dropped directly upon defeating Aldrich, Devourer of Gods

Secrets

  • The Darkmoon Tomb entrance at the bottom of the spiral tower mechanism is concealed behind an illusory wall disguised as a statue of Lord Gwyn.

Shops and people

  • Anri of Astora: Offers summon assistance via a summon sign near the main cathedral double doors to help defeat Aldrich in their world.

Tips

  • In Aldrich's second phase, the arrow rain tracks your position continuously, so sprint in wide circles to avoid taking massive damage.
  • You can unlock the cathedral's giant front doors using the lever on the ground floor to create a direct shortcut from the bonfire.
