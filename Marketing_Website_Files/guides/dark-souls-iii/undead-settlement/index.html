Undead Settlement

DARK SOULS III guide · After defeating Vordt of the Boreal Valley

After arriving from the High Wall of Lothric, you must navigate through a sprawling, dilapidated village populated by hollowed villagers, evangelists, and cage-carrying beasts. The primary objective is to make your way through the settlement to the Road of Sacrifices, with an optional path leading to the Curse-rotted Greatwood boss. Along the way, you can recruit several key NPCs and open up shortcuts through the dilapidated sewer network.

Items

  • Loretta's Bone: Hanging in a corpse sac on the balcony of the first interior house before the burning tree plaza
  • Mortician's Ashes: In the graveyard near the white birch tree where the giant shoots greatarrows
  • Grave Key: Available for purchase from the Shrine Handmaid after giving her the Mortician's Ashes
  • Estus Shard: At the base of the burning tree surrounded by praying hollows and an Evangelist
  • Undead Bone Shard: On a wooden platform off a broken ledge in the graveyard pelted by giant arrows
  • Flynn's Ring: On a corpse at the top of a wooden tower on the rooftop after defeating the Fire Demon
  • Chloranthy Ring: Dropped onto a wooden ledge accessed by jumping from the roof near the Mirrah set in the Fire Demon courtyard

Secrets

  • Join the Mound-makers covenant early by climbing into the cage on the back of the non-hostile saw-wielding giant in the ravine before triggering the Curse-rotted Greatwood fight.
  • Shoot down hanging corpses holding loot with arrows or firebombs from the ledges above.

Shops and people

  • Yoel of Londor: Offers sorceries and allows you to "Draw True Strength" up to five times to gain free levels, initiating the Hollow storyline.
  • Cornyx of the Great Swamp: Teaches pyromancies and upgrades the Pyromancy Flame after being rescued from a hanging cage above the cliff bonfire.
  • Irina of Carim: Sells miracles after being freed from her locked cell in the sewers using the Grave Key.
  • Giant of the Undead Settlement: Sells peace by offering friendship and a White Branch, causing him to target enemies instead of you with his greatarrows.

Tips

  • Befriend the giant at the top of the tower before exploring the graveyard to make retrieving the items there much easier.
  • Equip a torch in the sewer tunnels to clear off the parasitic maggot bleed buildup inflicted by leeches.
