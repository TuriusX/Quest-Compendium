Grand Archives

DARK SOULS III guide · After defeating the Dragonslayer Armour

The Grand Archives is a multi-story library that serves as the final path leading to the high ramparts of Lothric Castle. You must unlock the front doors, navigate the curse-inflicting library interior, and ascend to the rooftop pathways to face the final Lord of Cinder. Unlocking shortcuts and surviving rooftop ambushes leads to Prince Lothric and Younger Prince Lorian.

Items

  • Grand Archives Key: On Black Hand Gotthard's corpse directly in front of the main entrance doors
  • Gotthard Twinswords: On Black Hand Gotthard's corpse directly in front of the main entrance doors
  • Crystal Scroll: Dropped by the Crystal Sage located inside the archives
  • Estus Shard: On the archives roof, found on a balcony area guarded by Gargoyles
  • Undead Bone Shard: Sitting in a chair on the wooden beams near the roof of the library
  • Titanite Slab: Rewarded after pulling a lever behind a bookshelf on the upper library floor
  • Titanite Slab: Dropped by the Golden Winged Knights on the domed rooftop
  • Titanite Slab: Found at the bottom of the elevator shaft shortcut outside the Twin Princes' boss room

Secrets

  • Dunk your head into the large wax vats found throughout the archives to gain a wax coating that temporarily prevents the ghostly curse hands from inflicting curse.
  • Illusory wall located on the upper floor of the archives; striking it reveals an Outrider Knight guarding the Soul Stream sorcery.
  • On the high roof near the birdcage, you can drop into the cage and onto the rafters below to reach the Blessed Gem and Hunter's Ring.

Shops and people

  • Orbeck of Vinheim: Sells advanced sorceries if given the Crystal Scroll obtained in this area

Tips

  • Use the Rapport pyromancy on the three Golden Winged Knights on the roof to make them fight each other for easy souls and upgrade materials.
  • Dip your head in the wax basins before exploring new sections of the interior to neutralize the curse hands extending from the bookshelves.
  • Unlock the large elevator to the right of the Twin Princes' boss fog gate to establish a direct shortcut back to the Grand Archives bonfire.
