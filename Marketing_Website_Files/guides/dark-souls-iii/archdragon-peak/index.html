Archdragon Peak

DARK SOULS III guide · After using the Path of the Dragon gesture

Archdragon Peak is an optional, late-game secret area accessed by performing the Path of the Dragon gesture near a petrified dragon statue in the Irithyll Dungeon. Players traverse ruined mountain structures to fight the Ancient Wyvern, summon-wielding serpent-men, and eventually the Nameless King. Rings, dragon transformation stones, and ashes needed for Titanite upgrades can be gathered throughout the area.

Items

  • Soul of the Nameless King: Rewarded automatically upon defeating the Nameless King
  • Dragon Stone Ring: Along the path near the first bonfire after entering the area
  • Twinkling Dragon Head Stone: Found at the altar at the very top of the peak after clearing the serpent-man gauntlet
  • Dragon Chaser's Ashes: Found on a corpse on the rocky path leading up to the peak altar

Secrets

  • Ring of Steel Protection | Found on a stone ledge outside near the Ancient Wyvern boss arena by walking along the outer perimeter platform |
  • Calamity Ring | Use the Path of the Dragon gesture at the altar with dragonkin statues near the Dragon-Kin Mausoleum bonfire |

Tips

  • You do not need to fight the Ancient Wyvern head-on; run past the serpent-men through the arena to find the high scaffolding above its head for an instant-kill plunging attack.
  • Giving the Dragon Chaser's Ashes to the Shrine Handmaid unlocks the ability to purchase infinite Titanite Chunks, Twinkling Titanite, and Titanite Scales.
  • Ringing the great bell permanently changes the weather to a stormy overcast sky and unlocks the fog gate to the Nameless King fight, but prevents you from accessing a few ground areas swallowed by the clouds.
