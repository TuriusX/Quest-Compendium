Road of Sacrifices

DARK SOULS III guide · After exiting the Undead Settlement

Exiting the Undead Settlement elevator leads directly into this forested, marshy expanse connecting multiple major paths. You must traverse through flocks of transformed avian hollows and outposts of hostile exile warriors to reach the next regions. Exploring the flooded lower grounds leads both to the Cathedral of the Deep via the Crystal Sage boss and further downward into Farron Keep.

Items

  • Brigand Twindaggers: Found on a corpse in the flooded marsh area, defended by an NPC wielding them
  • Sellsword Twinblades: Found in the ruins guarded by a Black Knight on the path toward Farron Keep
  • Golden Falcon Shield: Found on a body resting on a rocky ledge on the edge of the path before reaching the marsh
  • Morne's Ring: Found below the stone bridge arch after dropping down near the cliffside path
  • Grass Crest Shield: Located on a corpse by the tree trunks in the flooded waters near the giant crabs
  • Fallen Knight Armor Set: Located on a corpse resting in the water near the two exile watchdogs
  • Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome: Located in the water near the giant crabs in the central marsh
  • Braille Divine Tome of Carim: Found below the bridge ledge near the Morne's Ring drop-off
  • Sorcerer Armor Set: Found on a body inside the ruins along the path leading to the Crystal Sage
  • Sage's Ring: Found in the flooded room beneath the ruins where Cornyx's pyromancy tome is nearby
  • Estus Shard: Found on the left side of the ruins entrance where the two hostile exile warriors patrol

Secrets

  • Dropping down a gap near the stone bridge ledge along the first path reveals an isolated grotto containing Morne's Ring and the Braille Divine Tome of Carim.
  • Slaying the giant hostile crabs in the marsh yields the Great Swamp Ring upon their first defeat.

Shops and people

  • Anri of Astora: Advances their questline upon speaking with them at the Halfway Fortress bonfire
  • Horace the Hushed: Gives the player the Blue Sentinels covenant pledge upon interaction at the Halfway Fortress bonfire

Tips

  • Equipping fire-infused weapons will panic the Corvians and Lycanthropes, temporarily interrupting their aggressive combo attacks.
  • Deep marsh water slows your running speed and prevents rolling normally, but using the Quickstep weapon skill allows you to move through it without penalty.
  • You can lure the two hostile exile warriors guarding the Farron Keep ladder to the hole and let them fall down to their deaths.
