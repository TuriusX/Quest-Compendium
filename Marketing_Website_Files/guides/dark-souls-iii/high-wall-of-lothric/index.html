High Wall of Lothric

DARK SOULS III guide · After warping from Firelink Shrine

You arrive at the High Wall of Lothric via the bonfire at Firelink Shrine to begin your journey toward the Lords of Cinder. Navigate through ramparts infested with hollows, petrified pilgrims, and a fire-breathing dragon to unlock shortcuts back to earlier bonfires. Your primary goals are to obtain the Small Lothric Banner from High Priestess Emma and defeat Vordt of the Boreal Valley to gain access to the Undead Settlement.

Items

  • Cell Key: Located in the lower levels of the workshop beneath the Tower on the Wall bonfire, down a ladder in a room guarded by a Greirat-sized hollow and dogs
  • Estus Shard: On an anvil in the large room beneath the Tower on the Wall bonfire, guarded by several hollow soldiers and dogs
  • Astora Straight Sword: In a chest inside the room above the workshop, accessible by dropping down through broken rafters
  • Raw Gem: Dropped by a Crystal Lizard located on the rooftops past the Tower on the Wall bonfire
  • Small Lothric Banner: Received from High Priestess Emma inside the cathedral opposite Vordt's boss fog gate
  • Way of Blue MISSABLE: Received from High Priestess Emma after speaking with her again following receiving the banner

Secrets

  • You can free the merchant Greirat of the Undead Settlement from his locked cell using the Cell Key found in the workshop.
  • You can trigger the late-game boss Dancer of the Boreal Valley early by killing High Priestess Emma and approaching the basin behind her altar.

Shops and people

  • Greirat of the Undead Settlement: Agrees to sell items at Firelink Shrine once unlocked from his cell beneath the Tower on the Wall bonfire.

Tips

  • Shoot the fire-breathing wyvern perched on the ramparts with a bow from below until it reaches low health to force it to fly away, unlocking safe access to the items it guards.
  • Fire damage, such as from Firebombs or a fire-infused weapon, causes the Pus of Man enemies to writhe in pain and temporarily stop attacking.
