Irithyll of the Boreal Valley

DARK SOULS III guide · After crossing the bridge from the Catacombs
← All areas in this guide

Players arrive in this snowy, moonlit city after defeating High Lord Wolnir and must present the Small Doll to pass the entrance barrier. Navigating the streets and sewers leads upward toward the central cathedral to defeat Pontiff Sulyvahn. Beyond the church, the path crosses an open courtyard populated by sleeping Giants and continues up the rooftops toward Anor Londo.

Enemies
Sulyvahn's Beast
Weak to Fire, BleedSteal / drop Pontiff's Right Eye

Attacks on the bridge upon entering; will drop down into the water below if skipped initially.

Pontiff Knight
Weak to Frost, DarkSteal / drop Pontiff Knight weapons and armor

Wield curved swords or scythes, capable of fast multi-hit combos and ranged magic waves.

Fire Witch
Weak to Physical, DarkSteal / drop Immolation Tinder, Fire Witch set

Uses tall fire pillars from afar and casts tracking flames along the ground.

Irithyllian Slave
Weak to Fire, Lightning

Can turn semi-invisible and wields cleavers or shoots soul darts.

Silver Knight
Weak to Lightning, FrostSteal / drop Silver Knight set, Titanite Shards

Stationed with Dragonslayer Greatbows across the high rooftops past the courtyard.

Pontiff Sulyvahn
Weak to Parrying, Fire, ThrustSteal / drop Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn

Dual-wields a Greatsword of Judgment and Profaned Greatsword, summoning a phantom clone in phase two.

Shops and people
  • Greirat the Thief: Can be sent here to scavenge; will die unless saved by either Siegward of Catarina or Unbreakable Patches.
  • Archdeacon McDonnell: Found in the Water Reserve chamber; offers membership in the Aldrich Faithful covenant.

Tips
  • A Small Doll looted from the Cathedral of the Deep is strictly required to pass the barrier on the bridge.
  • Parrying Pontiff Sulyvahn's opening jumping slash makes the first phase significantly easier to manage.
  • Light the Central Irithyll bonfire immediately after crossing the bridge to avoid re-fighting Sulyvahn's Beast if you die.
