Lothric Castle

DARK SOULS III guide · After defeating the Lords of Cinder

Lothric Castle is accessed by climbing the ladder behind the altar in the High Wall of Lothric after placing the Basin of Vows. Players must navigate fortified battlements guarded by high-ranking Lothric Knights and wyverns to reach the upper reaches of the castle. Defeating the Dragonslayer Armour at the end of the castle allows access to the Grand Archives.

Items

  • Sunlight Medal: On a corpse in the room near the Lothric Castle bonfire
  • Twinkling Titanite: Looted from various corpses along the castle walls and dropped by Crystal Lizards
  • Titanite Scale: Looted from multiple chests and corpses across the battlements
  • Undead Bone Shard: On a corpse below the bridge guarded by the two wyverns
  • Estus Shard: Down the stairs from the room housing the Winged Knight, on a corpse
  • Red Tearstone Ring: On a balcony outside the Dragonslayer Armour boss fog gate

Secrets

  • Illusory wall located behind the Winged Knight room that leads to the Outrider Knight and the Soul Stream sorcery room.
  • Illusory wall in the room near the Dragon Barracks that reveals the Winged Knight set and Sacred Bloom Shield.

Tips

  • Use firebombs or fire-infused weapons against the Pus of Man growths in the dragons' feet to quickly eliminate the wyverns.
  • Kill the Lothric Priests first before engaging the Lothric Knights to prevent them from constantly healing their allies.
  • Watch out for projectile barrages from the Pilgrim Butterflies during the Dragonslayer Armour fight, especially in the second phase.
