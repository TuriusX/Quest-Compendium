Irithyll Dungeon

DARK SOULS III guide · After passing through Irithyll of the Boreal Valley

Irithyll Dungeon is a sprawling, subterranean prison complex accessible after navigating the flooded manor section of Irithyll of the Boreal Valley. Players must navigate cramped cell blocks and toxic lower depths while dealing with enemies that actively reduce maximum health. The dungeon serves as the primary route to the Profaned Capital, while also containing pathways and keys to rescue trapped NPCs.

Items

  • Jailer's Key Ring: At the end of a corridor in the Profaned Capital side leading back to the dungeon cells
  • Old Cell Key: Found in a sewer pipe infested with basilisks near the giant
  • Estus Shard: Inside a chest in the room guarded by a Peeler next to the first open cell block
  • Xanthous Ashes: In a cell in the lower room containing numerous jailers
  • Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring: Beyond a locked gate opened using the Jailbreaker's Key
  • Dark Clutch Ring: Found inside a Mimic in the upper cell block
  • Dragon Torso Stone: Found on a cross-legged dragon corpse outside on the cliffside path

Secrets

  • Performing the "Path of the Dragon" gesture next to the meditative dragon statue on the outdoor balcony teleports you to Archdragon Peak.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
JailerLightningStares at the player with a lantern to rapidly reduce their maximum health bar.
WretchFirePale, distorted humanoids that cling to walls or lurk in cells to ambush players.
Lycanthrope HunterFireWanders inside a locked cell; carries a large wooden cross on its back and attacks aggressively.
Sewer CentipedeFireMulti-legged creatures that lie dormant in the sewer water before attacking.
MimicFire, Undead Hunter CharmDisguised as treasure chests throughout the dungeon; attacks with bites and kicks when opened.

Shops and people

  • Karla: Rescued from a cell on the lowest level using the Jailer's Key Ring; relocates to Firelink Shrine to teach dark sorceries and pyromancies.

Tips

  • Line-of-sight breaks will stop the Jailers from draining your maximum HP, allowing your bar to eventually refill.
  • Keep Undead Hunter Charms handy to put the numerous Mimics to sleep and loot them without fighting.
