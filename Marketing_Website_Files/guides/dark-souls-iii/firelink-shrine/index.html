Firelink Shrine

DARK SOULS III guide · Immediately after the tutorial

Firelink Shrine acts as the central hub area where you can level up, upgrade equipment, and interact with various non-player characters. Upon arrival, you must plant the Coiled Sword from the tutorial boss into the central fire pit to activate the bonfire and unlock fast travel to High Wall of Lothric. As you progress through the game, recruited NPCs will gather here to offer merchant services, lore dialogue, and side quests.

Items

  • Homeward Bone: Found in a small alcove along the interior walkway
  • East-West Shield: Outside the shrine, lying near the entrance stairs on a corpse
  • Uchigatana: Outside to the left of the main entrance, dropped by the Sword Master
  • Master's Attire: Outside to the left of the main entrance, dropped by the Sword Master
  • Master's Gloves: Outside to the left of the main entrance, dropped by the Sword Master
  • Estus Shard: On the interior rafters accessed via the roof
  • Covetous Silver Serpent Ring: Behind an illusory wall near the rafters accessed via the roof

Secrets

  • You can access the upper roof without buying the Tower Key by sprinting and executing a precise jump off the bent tree trunk near the shrine's exterior entrance.
  • Strike the false wall located at the end of the upper walkway in the rafters to reveal a hidden chest containing the Covetous Silver Serpent Ring.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessSteal / dropNotes
Sword MasterBleedUchigatana, Master's Attire, Master's GlovesAn aggressive hostile NPC wielding a katana who guards the path outside the shrine's left exit.

Shops and people

  • Fire Keeper: Levels up your character in exchange for souls.
  • Shrine Handmaid: Sells basic consumables, arrows, simple armor, and items unlocked by turning in Umbral Ashes.
  • Andre the Blacksmith: Upgrades weapons, infuses equipment, repairs durability, and reallocates or reinforces Estus Flasks.
  • Hawkwood the Deserter: Gives dialogue, lore hints, and gifts a Heavy Gem after certain milestones.
  • Ludleth of Courland: Offers lore and performs Soul Transposition once given the Transposing Kiln.

Tips

  • Plant the Coiled Sword in the central ash pit immediately upon entering to activate fast travel to the next zone.
  • If the Sword Master is too difficult to defeat in fair melee combat, you can lure him to the edge of the cliff to make him fall off.
