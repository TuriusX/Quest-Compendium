Profaned Capital

DARK SOULS III guide · After passing through Irithyll Dungeon

The Profaned Capital is a sunken, flame-scorched city that connects directly from Irithyll Dungeon. Players must navigate a toxic swamp populated by monstrosities, unlock pathways back to the dungeon, and cross a heavily guarded causeway to face Yhorm the Giant. Defeating Yhorm claims his Cinders of a Lord and progresses the main quest.

Items

  • Storm Ruler: Located right next to Yhorm the Giant's throne inside the boss arena
  • Profaned Flame (Pyromancy): Dropped by the Monstrosity of Sin enemies in the lower swamp building
  • Logan's Scroll: Dropped by the hostile Court Sorcerer NPC on the roof of the swamp structure
  • Wrath of the Gods: Found on a corpse inside the building directly behind the Court Sorcerer roof
  • Undead Bone Shard: Located on a corpse resting by the central Profaned Capital bonfire

Secrets

  • Releasing Siegward of Catarina | Obtain the Old Cell Key from Irithyll Dungeon, leap from the Court Sorcerer roof into an open window, and unlock the cell to free him and receive a Titanite Slab

Shops and people

  • Siegward of Catarina: Joins the battle against Yhorm the Giant if his questline was advanced, bringing his own Storm Ruler

Tips

  • Equip the Storm Ruler immediately upon picking it up in the boss arena and fully charge its weapon art to stagger Yhorm.
  • Wear poison-resistant gear or carry Blooming Purple Moss Clumps when navigating the deep mire beneath the ruins.
