This subterranean maze connects Farron Keep to the Boreal Valley, filled with reanimated skeleton warriors, rolling boulder traps, and bottomless drops. You must navigate through the winding corridors and break a rickety wooden suspension bridge to unlock access to the Smouldering Lake below. Finally, interact with an ominous skull chalice in the deepest chamber to face High Lord Wolnir and proceed to Irithyll.

Enemies
High Lord Wolnir
Weak to Blessed weapons

Attack the glowing gold bracelets on his wrists to shatter them and deal massive damage before his dark fog engulfs you.

Carthus Swordsman
Weak to Strike damage, Blunt weaponsSteal / drop Sharp Gem

Agile skeletons that can inflict bleed, jump around erratically, and occasionally turn invisible during their dodge rolls.

Skeleton Ball
Steal / drop Undead Bone Shard

Giant rolling spheres of bones that crush anything in their path; they break apart upon hitting walls if their controlling hat-wearing skeletons are killed.

Anri of Astora (Hollow)
Steal / drop Anri's Straight Sword

If Horace was killed in Smouldering Lake and you told Anri his location, Anri turns hostile in the catacombs.

Shops and people
  • Anri of Astora: Progresses their storyline and inquires about the whereabouts of Horace the Hushed

Tips
  • Blunt and strike weapons deal massive bonus damage to skeletons and consistently knock them into pieces, preventing them from counterattacking.
  • Skeletons with glowing white eyes will revive once after being killed unless slain with a Blessed weapon or by breaking their body while collapsed.
  • Lure the rolling skeleton balls into groups of standard skeleton enemies to easily crush them without wasting weapon durability or FP.
