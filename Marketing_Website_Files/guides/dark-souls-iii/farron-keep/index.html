Farron Keep

DARK SOULS III guide · After passing through the Road of Sacrifices

Farron Keep is a vast, murky poison swamp situated below the Road of Sacrifices. The primary objective is to extinguish three ritual flames atop elevated stone structures to unlock the massive stone door leading to the Abyss Watchers. Navigating the deep sludge heavily restricts movement and continuously builds up the poison status effect.

Items

  • Sage's Scroll: Near the perimeter wall guarded by several Rotten Ghrus, close to the Keep Ruins bonfire
  • Golden Scroll: In a small cave in the deep poison swamp area, guarded by Basilisks
  • Estus Shard: Next to a fallen tower base near the first extinguishable ritual flame
  • Undead Bone Shard: Inside a small, sunken stone tower filled with slugs near the Keep Ruins bonfire
  • Sunlight Talisman: Near the sunken bridge structure in the deep swamp
  • Nameless Knight Set: On a corpse resting in the deep water near the center of the swamp
  • Dragon Crest Shield: At the top of the central fortress structure, reached via an elevator near the Old Wolf of Farron, guarded by a Stray Demon
  • Coal: Farron Coal: Located in a hallway guarded by a Black Knight, on the perimeter leading toward the Road of Sacrifices

Secrets

  • Illusory Wall | Attack or roll into the illusory wall on the upper tier of the stone structure housing the Old Wolf of Farron to reveal the Dreamchaser's Ashes |

Shops and people

  • Old Wolf of Farron: Offers the Watchdogs of Farron covenant and associated rank rewards

Tips

  • Use the Quickstep weapon art on daggers to bypass the movement penalty while traversing the deep swamp sludge.
  • Equip armor with high poison resistance and keep Purple Moss Clumps assigned to your quick-use inventory slot.
  • The Darkwraiths outside the final boss gate will fight the Rotten Ghrus on the hill, allowing you to let them thin each other out safely.
