Farron Keep

Farron Keep is a vast, murky poison swamp situated below the Road of Sacrifices. The primary objective is to extinguish three ritual flames atop elevated stone structures to unlock the massive stone door leading to the Abyss Watchers. Navigating the deep sludge heavily restricts movement and continuously builds up the poison status effect.

Enemies
Rotten Ghru
Weak to Fire

Common beast-like humanoid that attacks with spears, daggers, or shields; can inflict poison.

Elder Ghru
Weak to Fire

Massive bipedal creatures that wield tree trunks and summon tracking red skull projectiles.

Basilisk
Weak to Dark

Small hopping reptiles that spew dense clouds capable of causing instant death via Curse.

Corvian
Weak to Fire

Raven-like humanoids near the entrance that sprout wings and shriek when alerted.

Darkwraith
Weak to StrikeSteal / drop Dark Sword, Cracked Red Eye Orb

Two red-eyed knights that patrol the rampart path toward the boss, fighting Ghrus in their path.

Stray Demon
Weak to StrikeSteal / drop Soul of a Stray Demon

A large, stone-skinned mini-boss atop the fortress elevator who uses a massive greathammer and grab attacks.

Abyss Watchers
Weak to LightningSteal / drop Soul of the Blood of the Wolf, Cinders of a Lord

The Lord of Cinder boss of the area, featuring a multi-opponent sword duel followed by a fire-imbued second phase.

Shops and people
  • Old Wolf of Farron: Offers the Watchdogs of Farron covenant and associated rank rewards

Tips
  • Use the Quickstep weapon art on daggers to bypass the movement penalty while traversing the deep swamp sludge.
  • Equip armor with high poison resistance and keep Purple Moss Clumps assigned to your quick-use inventory slot.
  • The Darkwraiths outside the final boss gate will fight the Rotten Ghrus on the hill, allowing you to let them thin each other out safely.
