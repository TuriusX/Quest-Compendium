DARK SOULS III achievement guide

How to unlock every achievement, which ones you can miss, and the best order to get them all. Tick them off as you go.

Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Defeating the Abyss Watchers before obtaining 5 Dark Sigils from Yoel: Yoel dies prematurely, Yuria never appears, permanently locking the "Usurpation of Fire" ending and the "Master of Miracles" / "Master of Expression" items tied to Yuria for that playthrough.
  • Offering a Pale Tongue to Rosaria's Fingers before finishing Sirris' questline: Sirris becomes permanently hostile, locking you out of the Silvercat Ring and the Cat Ring / Darkmoon Loyalty rewards for that playthrough.
  • Killing the hidden assassin at Church of Yorshka: Breaks the wedding ritual, locking the "Usurpation of Fire" ending and the chameleon/marriage progression for that playthrough.
  • Defeating Pontiff Sulyvahn and Abyss Watchers before summoning Yellowfinger Heysel: Yellowfinger Heysel's summon sign disappears, permanently locking the "Proper Bow" gesture for that playthrough.
  • Informing Anri of Horace's location before killing Horace in Smouldering Lake: Horace kills Anri, permanently failing Anri's questline and locking the "Usurpation of Fire" ending for that playthrough.
  • Defeating Pontiff Sulyvahn without sending Siegward to the kitchen or giving Patches Siegward's armor: Greirat dies permanently upon scavenging in Irithyll, failing his shop inventory progression for that playthrough.
  • Interacting with the bonfire or summoning the Fire Keeper after defeating the Soul of Cinder: Locks the game into that specific ending cutscene, preventing the triggers for the other two ending achievements until a future playthrough.

Recommended order
  1. Cemetery of Ash: Defeat Iudex Gundyr and light the bonfire.
  2. Firelink Shrine: Meet the Crestfallen Warrior for the "Collapse" gesture, talk to Leonhard, and level up with Fire Keeper.
  3. High Wall of Lothric: Rescue Greirat, acquire the Small Lothric Banner from Emma, join the Way of Blue, and defeat Vordt of the Boreal Valley.
  4. Undead Settlement: Recruit Yoel of Londor; defeat the Fire Demon with Siegward for the "Toast" and "Sleep" gestures; join the Mound-Makers covenant before fighting the boss; rescue Cornyx and Irina; defeat the Curse-rotted Greatwood for the Transposing Kiln.
  5. Firelink Shrine: Receive five free level-ups (Dark Sigils) from Yoel before proceeding, prompting Yuria of Londor to spawn; interact with Sirris of the Sunless Realm.
  6. Road of Sacrifices: Meet Anri and Horace at the Halfway Fortress bonfire to receive the Blue Sentinels insignia; recruit Orbeck of Vinheim; defeat the Crystal Sage.
  7. Cathedral of the Deep: Meet Siegward at the cleansing chapel well; trigger Patches' encounter inside the cathedral for the "Prostration" gesture; buy Siegward's armor from Patches and return it to Siegward; discover Rosaria's Bedchamber to join Rosaria's Fingers (do not donate Pale Tongues if pursuing Sirris' quest this cycle); defeat Deacons of the Deep.
  8. Farron Keep: Extinguish the three flames; join the Watchdogs of Farron; summon Londor Pale Shade for the "Duel Bow" gesture; summon Yellowfinger Heysel for the "Proper Bow" gesture (requires a Pale Tongue offering, conflicting with Sirris; complete Sirris in NG and Heysel in NG+ or vice versa); defeat Abyss Watchers.
  9. Catacombs of Carthus: Speak with Anri twice; break the wooden rope bridge to access the ladder; defeat High Lord Wolnir.
  10. Smouldering Lake: Kill Horace the Hushed in the watery cave before informing Anri; defeat Old Demon King; collect spell tomes and estus upgrades.
  11. Irithyll of the Boreal Valley: Assist Sirris on the bridge to earn the Silvercat Ring and Mail Breaker; speak with Anri at Church of Yorshka (leave the disguised pilgrim alive for the Usurpation ending); save Greirat via Siegward or Patches; defeat Pontiff Sulyvahn; join Aldrich Faithful covenant.
  12. Irithyll Dungeon: Rescue Karla; collect the Old Cell Key; rescue Siegward from his cell.
  13. Profaned Capital: Defeat Yhorm the Giant alongside Siegward; acquire the Cinders of a Lord.
  14. Anor Londo: Complete the Dark Sigil wedding ceremony in the hidden chamber of the Darkmoon Tomb for the Usurpation of Fire ending; join Blades of the Darkmoon with Company Captain Yorshka; defeat Aldrich, Devourer of Gods.
  15. Lothric Castle: Place the basin to enter; help Sirris defeat Hodrick outside the Greatwood arena; defeat Dragonslayer Armour.
