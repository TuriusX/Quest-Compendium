The Ringed City

DARK SOULS III guide · After interacting with the bonfire at the Kiln of the First Flame

After interacting with the bonfire at the Kiln of the First Flame, you are transported to the Dreg Heap, the introductory section leading to the Ringed City DLC. You will navigate crumbling ruins collapsing into the abyss, fight Murkmen and Angels, and push downward to reach the Demon Prince boss fight. Defeating the Demon Prince allows you to raise an envoy banner and be carried directly into the Ringed City proper.

Items

  • Aquamarine Dagger: Found on a corpse on the high terrace just past the first Harold Legion Knight in the Dreg Heap
  • Murky Hand Scythe: Found in the first library building after falling through the stained-glass window in the Dreg Heap
  • Ring of Favor +3: Located on a giant branch protruding over the swamp in the Dreg Heap, near the two Pyromancer Desert Pyromancer Zoey
  • Chloranthy Ring +3: Found on a balcony near the statue of Gwyn knighted by a Primordial Serpent, accessed by dropping onto a lower walkway
  • Ring of the Evil Eye +3: Dropped by an infested corpse behind a hidden wall inside the dragon bridge tower in the Ringed City
  • Havel's Ring +3: Dropped on a ledge guarded by a Ringed Knight below the chasm bridge in the Ringed City
  • Ringed Knight Paired Greatswords: Dropped by the Ringed Knight wielding paired greatswords outside the church containing the Spear of the Church boss fight
  • Bloodlust-compatible Titanite Slabs: Several are found throughout, including one awarded by Lapp after completing his quest and one in the poison swamp cave guarded by a Harold Knight

Secrets

  • The Purging Monument can be accessed by solving the "Show Your Humanity" riddle near the Ringed City Streets bonfire, which requires using the Chameleon spell or a White Preacher Arm / Young White Branch in the swamp to turn into a black humanity apparition and standing before the wall carving.
  • Midir can be knocked down from the chasm bridge encounter, which unlocks the hidden boss room behind an illusionary statue in the elevator shaft connecting the Ringed Inner Wall bonfire.

Shops and people

  • Stone-humped Hag: Sells embers, arrows, divine blessings, and the Splintering Lightning miracle; leaves ashes upon death.
  • Amnesiac Lapp: Gives away Titanite Slabs and Siegbräu throughout his progression questline across the DLC areas.

Tips

  • Falling long distances onto ash piles will prevent all fall damage throughout the Dreg Heap.
  • Use the environment and ruined walls to block the Angel's holy barrage while you hunt down the grotesque pupa controlling it.
  • Stay in front of Darkeater Midir and attack his head rather than attacking his legs or tail to bait readable attacks and deal full damage.
