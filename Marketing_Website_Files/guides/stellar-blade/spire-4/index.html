Spire 4

Stellar Blade™ guide · The orbital elevator mission
← All areas in this guide

Eve travels to the orbital elevator Spire 4 to reach the Nest and confront the Elder Naytiba. The mission involves fighting through an infested space-elevator complex, navigating high-altitude exterior platforms, and facing powerful elite bosses.

Achievement guide and roadmap →

Enemies
Belial

An agile multi-limbed Elite Naytiba equipped with scythe-like blades encountered multiple times throughout the ascent.

Karakuri

An Elite Naytiba with puppet-like aesthetics that attacks using rapid kicks, ribbons, and energy projectiles.

Democrawler

A massive Naytiba fought near the upper structures of the spire, requiring ranged attacks and timed dodges to hit exposed weak spots.

Tips
  • Pack plenty of ranged ammunition for your drone, as the exterior sections and the Democrawler encounter heavily favor gunplay.
  • Equipping Gear that speeds up Beta or Burst Energy regeneration helps tremendously with the gauntlet of bosses found across the spire.
