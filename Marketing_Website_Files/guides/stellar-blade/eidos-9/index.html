Eidos 9

Stellar Blade™ guide · Visiting the ruined city with Adam
← All areas in this guide

Eidos 9 is a flooded, partially submerged ruined coastal city visited late in the game alongside Adam and Lily. Eve explores the vertical structures, uses submerged rail slides, and navigates rooftops to reach Lily's secret atelier. Reaching the end yields key story revelations and unlocks access to the game's secret ending path.

Tips
  • Take advantage of the yellow painted ledge markers and roller-coaster-like rail grinds to easily track intended platforming routes.
  • Once you finish Eidos 9 and leave for Spire 4, you can no longer return to this area during the current playthrough.
