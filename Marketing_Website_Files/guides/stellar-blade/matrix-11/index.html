Matrix 11

Stellar Blade™ guide · Journey through the subway tunnels
← All areas in this guide

Eve traverses the collapsed underground subway network and flooded train tunnels of Matrix 11 to track down an Alpha Naytiba. You must navigate derelict carriages, solve basic water-draining puzzles, and survive ambushes across the dark rail lines. The mission culminates in an intense boss battle against Stalker followed by Juggernaut deeper within the facility.

Enemies
Skull Hunter

Highly aggressive melee Naytiba that leaps around train compartments; parrying its combo chains is vital.

Hive

Stationary spawning mass attached to walls or train cars; destroy it quickly to stop infinite enemy spawns.

Stalker
Steal / drop Weapon Core

Ferocious quadrupedal Alpha Naytiba encountered in the train depot that uses heavy charging attacks and rock flings.

Juggernaut
Steal / drop Weapon Core

Massive hammer-wielding boss encountered deep in the matrix that deals heavy area-of-effect blunt damage.

Tips
  • Use your drone scanner frequently in the subway tunnels, as Naytibas often cling to train ceilings or hide behind doors waiting to ambush you.
  • When fighting the Stalker, maintain medium distance to easily read its charging wind-ups, and save your Beta Skills to interrupt its unblockable attacks.
