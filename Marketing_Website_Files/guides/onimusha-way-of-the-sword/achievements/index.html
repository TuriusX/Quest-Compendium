Points of no return

  • Talking to Takamura to start Eastern Kyoto (The Clash at Rendaino): Free-roam access across Kyoto, open-world side quests, and prior map areas for the duration of the finale until completing the story or reloading.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete the prologue, then explore Kiyomizu-dera Temple and Eastern Kyoto while learning parry counters and unlocking early Oni Vision abilities.

  2. Step 2

    Progress through story missions including Kiyomizu-dera Temple (The Curse of Kiyomizu-zaka Slope), Eastern Kyoto (Windswept Kennin-ji), and Nijo-jo Castle into the Underground Laboratory while rescuing local citizens.

  3. Step 3

    Advance through Kyoto Imperial Palace, Eastern Kyoto (Bloodbath on the Bridge), Eastern Kyoto (A Tempest at the Temple), and Eastern Kyoto (Okuni's Kabuki Dance), picking up unlocking side quests and Oni Armaments.

  4. Step 4

    Venture through Mount Oe, Eastern Kyoto (A Haunted Distraction), Eastern Kyoto (Storm Chaser), and Arashiyama while gathering collectibles and upgrading gear.

  5. Step 5

    Stop before triggering Eastern Kyoto (The Clash at Rendaino) to mop up all 18 Chance Encounters, 8 Mysteries of Kyoto, 36 Lion Dogs, Genma Notes, and full equipment upgrades across all regions.

  6. Step 6

    Initiate Eastern Kyoto (The Clash at Rendaino), breach Shanaoh Castle, defeat the final boss, and complete your initial run.

  7. Step 7

    Reload pre-final free roam via continue if needed, or start New Game+ on Carnage difficulty and finish the Boss Rematch mode to unlock all remaining achievements.
