Kiyomizu-dera Temple (The Curse of Kiyomizu-zaka Slope)

Onimusha: Way of the Sword guide · Musashi returns up the slope to clear the Malice Barrier and purge corruption ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Musashi returns toward the Kiyomizu grounds wielding his newly mastered Oni Strength to rip down red Malice Barriers that previously barricaded pathways. Across the corrupted slope, you traverse rooftops to cut down Malice Plants, eliminate surrounding Genma, and seal open Rifts to lift the persistent miasma. The excursion culminates in a clash against the defiled demon Byakue – Hundred Defilements before the route is fully cleared.
