Normandy SR-2

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Throughout the game

The Normandy SR-2 serves as Commander Shepard's primary mobile base of operations and hub between missions throughout Mass Effect 3. Players use the ship to consult the Galaxy Map, manage War Assets, upgrade weapons, and converse with crew members to advance personal subplots and romances. Regularly exploring each deck after major assignments is essential for triggering new dialogue, interactions, and shipboard scenes.

Items

  • Space Hamster MISSABLE: Deck 4 Sub-deck / Engineering, scurrying on the floor near the lower storage area
  • Model Ship: Alliance Cruiser: Deck 3 Crew Quarters, on a table near the sleeper pods
  • Model Ship: Destiny Ascension: Deck 2 Captain's Cabin, on a shelf
  • Model Ship: Geth Cruiser: Deck 5 Cargo Bay / Shuttle Bay, on a workbench near the requisitions terminal

Secrets

  • Reclaiming the Space Hamster from the Engineering lower sub-deck requires Shepard to chase it on foot and quickly interact with it as it darts across the floor; once caught, it can be displayed in the Captain's Cabin.

Shops and people

  • Procurement Terminal: Deck 5 Shuttle Bay
  • Weapon Modification Station: Deck 5 Shuttle Bay
  • Med-Bay / Dr. Chakwas or Dr. Michel: Deck 3 Med-Bay

Tips

  • Check the Galaxy Map terminal on the Combat Information Center (Deck 2) frequently to monitor War Asset updates and read incoming private messages on Shepard's private terminal.
  • Visit every deck after each Priority mission, as companions routinely move between compartments to interact with one another rather than staying at their designated stations.
  • EDI's core on Deck 3 Crew Deck offers regular philosophical dialogue opportunities throughout the campaign that influence her personal development.
