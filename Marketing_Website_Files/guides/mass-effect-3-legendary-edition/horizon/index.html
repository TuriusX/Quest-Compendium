Horizon

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Following the Thessia mission

Commander Shepard travels to the Sanctuary facility on Horizon to investigate the mysterious Cerberus refugee haven and track down Henry Lawson. The squad discovers Cerberus was using the facility to conduct horrific experiments to control Husks and process refugees into Reaper ground troops. You must fight through corrupted Cerberus forces and Reaper troops to confront Henry Lawson and save Miranda Lawson if proper steps were taken previously.

Items

  • M-12 Locust MISSABLE: Found on a crate inside the main reception area near the entrance
  • M-99 Saber MISSABLE: Found inside a pod/display in the processing laboratory area after interacting with a console
  • Pistol Power Magnifier MISSABLE: Found on a shelf in the laboratory area
  • Shotgun Blade Attachment MISSABLE: Found on a dead Cerberus scientist near the lab terminal controls

Tips

  • Bring squadmates equipped with Armor-Piercing Ammo, Warp, or Incinerate, as the majority of enemies in the final courtyard and facility rely heavily on armor.
  • Miranda's survival strictly depends on prior choices: you must have warned her about Kai Leng via private comms, given her access to Alliance resources, and maintained her loyalty from Mass Effect 2.
  • In the final courtyard battle against multiple Banshees and Brutes, keep moving around the perimeter and use the central building's walls to break line of sight against Banshee warp blasts.
