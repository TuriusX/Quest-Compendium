Perseus Veil

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Geth Dreadnought mission

Commander Shepard infiltrates a massive Geth Dreadnought in the Perseus Veil to disable the Reaper signal controlling the geth fleet. The team navigates through airlocks, maintenance pathways, and the ship's main battery cannon to reach the operational core. After reaching the bridge and liberating Legion (or the Geth VI), Shepard must sprint back to the shuttle bay as the Quarian fleet fires on the vessel.

Items

  • Arc Pistol MISSABLE: On a console in the Operations room where you examine geth data consoles
  • Geth Pulse Rifle MISSABLE: On the floor near a console in the room just before the main battery access
  • Shotgun Smart Choke MISSABLE: On a catwalk ledge during the traverse through the main battery chamber
  • Sniper Rifle Enhanced Scope MISSABLE: On a computer terminal inside the operations center before reaching the drive core

Tips

  • In the main battery chamber, the massive shockwaves from the cannon firing will periodically strip your shields and deal damage; duck behind solid pillars whenever you hear the charging sound.
  • Bring squadmates with Overload, Disruptor Ammo, or Arc Grenades, as almost all enemies in this mission are protected by shields.
  • Combat Drone, Decoy, or Sabotage are exceptionally powerful on this ship to distract or turn the heavily shielded geth forces against one another.
