Arrae

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Optional mission after the Citadel

Commander Shepard travels to a hidden research facility on the planet Arrae to evacuate defecting Cerberus scientists and their families. The mission involves fighting through Cerberus assault forces to secure the complex, re-establishing anti-air defenses, and escorting the shuttles safely off-world. Former Normandy crew member Jacob Taylor is stationed here assisting the refugees and fights alongside Shepard during the evacuation.

Items

  • M-25 Hornet MISSABLE: On a crate inside the comms control room before heading to the exterior roof
  • Shotgun High-Caliber Barrel MISSABLE: On a table in the medical bay area inside the facility
  • Sniper Rifle Piercing Mod MISSABLE: In the exterior radar array area on the catwalks
  • Pistol Piercing Mod MISSABLE: Inside the main central lab area on an equipment table

Tips

  • Bring squadmates with Overload or Reave to quickly strip the barriers off Phantoms and shields off Centurions before they can close in.
  • During the roof defense segment, immediately shoot down the Combat Engineers before they finish deploying their heavy turrets.
  • Speak to Jacob Taylor and Dr. Brynn Cole before overriding the satellite dish to secure extra dialogue and ensure full War Asset readiness.
