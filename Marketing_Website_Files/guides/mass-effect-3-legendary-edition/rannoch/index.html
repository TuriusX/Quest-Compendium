Rannoch

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Following the Geth Dreadnought mission

Shepard lands on the quarian homeworld of Rannoch to locate and destroy the Reaper base broadcasting control signals to the geth fleet. Commander Shepard must ascend a cliffside complex defended by geth forces, designate the base for an orbital strike, and face a Reaper Destroyer in a laser-targeting showdown. Completing this mission forces a critical, story-defining decision regarding the fate of both the quarian and geth species.

Items

  • Geth Plasma Shotgun MISSABLE: On a platform next to a low wall early in the mission
  • Reaper Code Fragment MISSABLE: On a console inside the base structure near the Reaper core access
  • Kassa Fabrication Chestplate MISSABLE: On a walkway ledge before riding the elevator to the targeting area

Tips

  • When painting the Reaper Destroyer with the targeting laser, strafe side-to-side to bait the beam away, then hold the trigger to charge the strike.
  • Achieving peace between the geth and quarians requires a very high Paragon or Renegade score alongside specific choices carried over from Mass Effect 2 and earlier Mass Effect 3 missions.
