Thessia

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Following the Rannoch arc

Shepard travels to the asari homeworld of Thessia to retrieve a Prothean artifact stored inside the Temple of Athame. Alongside asari forces, the squad fights through Reaper ground forces occupying the falling city to reach the temple. After uncovering the Prothean VI within the relic, Kai Leng arrives to steal the data, leading to a mandatory mission-ending defeat.

Items

  • M-98 Widow MISSABLE: On a sniper nest platform early in the mission near dead asari snipers
  • Asari Command Console MISSABLE: On the ground inside an outpost structure along the approach to the temple
  • Umbra Visor MISSABLE: Found on the ground in the courtyard area just before entering the temple

Tips

  • Bring Liara along for this mission, as she provides unique dialogue, personal reactions to her homeworld's fall, and interacts directly with the temple consoles.
  • Keep away from Banshees at all costs during the final exterior courtyard battle, utilizing the expansive perimeter to kite them while whittling down their barriers.
  • The battle against Kai Leng cannot be won; focus on taking down his shields and clearing the gunship attacks to trigger the scripted retreat.
