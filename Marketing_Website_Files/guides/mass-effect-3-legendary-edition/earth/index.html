Earth

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Prologue

Commander Shepard is relieved of duty in Vancouver when the Reapers suddenly launch an all-out planetary invasion. Guided by Admiral Anderson, Shepard must navigate the collapsing defense committee building and adjacent rooftops to board the SSV Normandy. The main objective is to survive the initial Reaper assault, learn basic combat mechanics, and escape Earth to rally galactic aid.

Items

  • M-8 Avenger: Picked up automatically from the body of a fallen soldier while escaping the damaged building with Anderson
  • M-3 Predator: Found on the ground near the radio console before holding off Husks awaiting extraction
  • Med-Kit MISSABLE: Mounted on the wall inside the ruined hallway just before climbing outside onto the rooftop ledges
  • Thermal Clips: Scattered along the rooftop barricades during the final stand while waiting for the Normandy

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
CannibalHeadshotsReaper-modified Batarian shock troopers capable of consuming fallen corpses to regenerate armor.
HuskHeadshotsCybernetic melee-only zombies that rush in groups; vulnerable to any crowd-control powers or quick melee attacks.

Tips

  • Don't linger when the Reaper destroyer fires on the rooftop; sprint straight along the catwalks to avoid instant death from falling debris.
  • Use heavy melee when Husks get into close quarters, as it can kill them outright without wasting ammunition.
  • Fire at the fuel tanks near the barricades during the final wave to wipe out clusters of Cannibals simultaneously.
