Sur'Kesh

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Following the mission on Menae

Commander Shepard travels to the Salarian homeworld of Sur'Kesh to secure a fertile female Krogan capable of curing the Genophage. Upon arriving at the STG research facility, Cerberus forces launch an assault to prevent the cure from ever being deployed. You must fight through the laboratory complex to escort the pod carrying the Krogan female safely to the extraction zone.

Items

  • Pistol High-Caliber Barrel MISSABLE: On a shelf in the quarantine lab where Eve is kept before the attack begins
  • Shotgun Spare Ammo MISSABLE: On a console in the upper walkways during the initial Cerberus ambush
  • Assault Rifle Extended Barrel MISSABLE: Near a terminal along the catwalks after fighting through the first wave of Cerberus troops
  • Scorpion MISSABLE: Sitting on a console in the security station overlooking the courtyard
  • Sentry Interface MISSABLE: On a desk in the office area during the ascent through the labs
  • Armax Arsenal Gauntlets MISSABLE: Sitting on a crate in the secondary quarantine staging corridor

Tips

  • Bring squadmates with tech powers like Overload to quickly strip the shields off Centurions, Engineers, and the Atlas mech.
  • The pod carrying Eve has an integrity meter; prioritize taking out Cerberus troops attacking the pod control terminals to prevent mission failure.
  • Interact with the Yahg specimen in the holding cell early in the mission to trigger unique ambient dialogue.
