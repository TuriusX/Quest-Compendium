Grissom Academy

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Optional mission after the Citadel

Commander Shepard responds to an emergency distress call from Grissom Academy to rescue biotic human students before Cerberus captures them. You must fight through the station's classrooms, halls, and courtyards to rendezvous with Kahlee Sanders and secure the surviving biotic students. After clearing the landing bay of heavy Cerberus forces, the survivors evacuate to safety.

Items

  • M-96 Mattock MISSABLE: On a bench in the classroom near the beginning of the mission
  • M-22 Eviscerator MISSABLE: Next to a student barricade in the transit hallway after meeting Kahlee Sanders
  • Serrice Council Chestplate MISSABLE: On a bench in the outdoor arboretum
  • Asari Tech Armor Upgrade MISSABLE: Inside a terminal in the technical lab area before the outdoor courtyard

Tips

  • This entire mission is strictly time-sensitive and becomes permanently unavailable if you complete the Priority: Citadel II mission before visiting Grissom Academy.
  • Jack appears as an instructor leading the biotic students if she survived the Suicide Mission in Mass Effect 2; otherwise, an unfamiliar replacement instructor takes her place.
  • Shoot the canopy glass of the unoccupied Atlas Mech in the courtyard to hijack it and turn its heavy firepower against Cerberus forces.
