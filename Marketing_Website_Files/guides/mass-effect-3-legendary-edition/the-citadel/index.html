The Citadel

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · First visit after Mars

Commander Shepard arrives at the Citadel immediately following the mission on Mars to address the Council about the Reaper threat and secure aid for Earth. During this first visit, you need to speak with the Council in the Presidium, visit your injured Virmire survivor squadmate in Huerta Memorial Hospital, and rally early support. It serves as an introduction to Citadel navigation, early shopping terminals, and several ambient side quests.

Items

  • M-8 Avenger: Purchased at the Kassa Fabrication terminal in the Presidium Commons if not upgraded or acquired earlier

Shops and people

  • Sirta Supplies: Medigel capacity upgrades and armor pieces at the shop terminal in Huerta Memorial Hospital
  • Kassa Fabrication: Armor components and weapon mods at the Presidium Commons terminal
  • Cipritine Armory: Turian-manufactured armor pieces and weapon mods at the Presidium Commons terminal
  • Batarian State Arms: Shotgun and assault rifle mods, as well as weapon upgrades, located in the Presidium Commons
  • Kanala Exports: Asari and Hanar armor pieces, along with Citadel defense-related items, located in the Presidium Commons
  • Elkoss Combine Arsenal Supplies: Basic weapon modifications and budget-tier armor pieces in the Presidium Commons

Tips

  • Visit your injured companion in Huerta Memorial Hospital as early as possible to progress your relationship and set the stage for their later recovery.
  • Buy the Sirta Supplies Medigel capacity upgrade immediately at Huerta Memorial Hospital to increase your overall survivability in subsequent combat missions.
  • Eavesdrop on civilian conversations throughout the hospital, docks, and commons to trigger early ambient side quests that reward War Assets.
  • Check in with the human councilor and Udina's office in the Presidium Embassies to gather crucial context and unlock initial diplomatic interactions.
