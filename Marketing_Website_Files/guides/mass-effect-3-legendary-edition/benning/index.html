Commander Shepard travels to the human colony world of Benning on a rescue operation to extract civilian survivors pinned down by Cerberus forces. The squad must fight through residential city streets, locate the surviving civilians, and protect them until the extraction shuttle arrives.

Don't miss 0/4

Enemies
Assault Trooper
Weak to Shields / Biotics

Basic Cerberus infantry armed with assault rifles.

Centurion
Weak to Shields

Squad leader capable of laying down smoke screens and throwing grenades.

Combat Engineer
Weak to Shields

Deploys stationary automated turrets that deal heavy damage.

Nemesis
Weak to Shields

Cerberus sniper marked by a red targeting laser; uses stealth to reposition.

Tips
  • Use the environment's high ground and balconies to counter Nemesis snipers before they can line up shots on your squad.
  • Destroy or disable Combat Engineers immediately before they can deploy automated turrets, or use Sabotage/Overload to turn the turrets against Cerberus.
  • Look around the civilian plaza for discarded dog tags before interacting with the final survivor group to fulfill a Citadel fetch quest later.
