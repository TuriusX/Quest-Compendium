Benning

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Optional mission

Commander Shepard travels to the human colony world of Benning on a rescue operation to extract civilian survivors pinned down by Cerberus forces. The squad must fight through residential city streets, locate the surviving civilians, and protect them until the extraction shuttle arrives.

Items

  • M-25 Hornet MISSABLE: Near the civilian survivor group's barricade on the ground
  • Pistol Caliber Compression System MISSABLE: On the street near a civilian vehicle in the first combat zone
  • Shotgun High-Caliber Barrel MISSABLE: Inside a multi-story residential building on the upper level
  • Cerberus Turret Control Schematics MISSABLE: Found on a terminal in a residential plaza

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Assault TrooperShields / BioticsBasic Cerberus infantry armed with assault rifles.
CenturionShieldsSquad leader capable of laying down smoke screens and throwing grenades.
Combat EngineerShieldsDeploys stationary automated turrets that deal heavy damage.
NemesisShieldsCerberus sniper marked by a red targeting laser; uses stealth to reposition.

Tips

  • Use the environment's high ground and balconies to counter Nemesis snipers before they can line up shots on your squad.
  • Destroy or disable Combat Engineers immediately before they can deploy automated turrets, or use Sabotage/Overload to turn the turrets against Cerberus.
  • Look around the civilian plaza for discarded dog tags before interacting with the final survivor group to fulfill a Citadel fetch quest later.
