Mars

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Immediately following the prologue

Commander Shepard travels to the Mars research facility to retrieve Prothean data critical to building the Crucible. Accompanied by James Vega and Ashley Williams or Kaidan Alenko, Shepard navigates the compromised facility fighting Cerberus forces while racing to reunite with Liara T'Soni. The mission concludes with a confrontation against Dr. Eva Coré to secure the Prothean archives before Cerberus escapes with them.

Items

  • M-23 Katana MISSABLE: Inside a facility hallway near the beginning of the mission on an operational rack
  • M-4 Shuriken MISSABLE: Found in an early control room console area shortly after entering the Mars archives
  • M-15 Vindicator MISSABLE: Sitting on a shelf inside the security control center
  • M-92 Mantis MISSABLE: Resting on the floor near dead personnel prior to entering the railcar chamber
  • Kassa Fabrication Chestplate MISSABLE: Placed on a low table inside the darkened room where environmental controls must be restored
  • Shotgun High-Caliber Barrel MISSABLE: On a workstation desk inside the communication center
  • Sniper Rifle Extended Barrel MISSABLE: Found on the floor in the open vehicle bay area

Secrets

  • A locked decontamination room near the communications hub contains extra weapon mods and credits, accessible by clearing the environmental venting cycle through a nearby terminal console.

Tips

  • Bring biotic powers like Pull or Singularity to easily snatch away the shields of Cerberus Guardians, exposing them to direct fire.
  • Shoot through the thin eye slit in a Guardian's shield to kill them instantly from the front without having to flank.
  • During the final sprint sequence against Dr. Eva Coré, backpedal while continuously firing your sidearm to prevent her from reaching Shepard.
