Lesuss

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Monastery mission

Commander Shepard travels to an Ardat-Yakshi monastery on Lesuss to investigate silence from the facility and prevent the Reapers from harvesting the inhabitants. You fight through waves of Reaper forces, search for surviving asari including Samara's daughters, and ultimately detonate a bomb to purge the facility.

Items

  • Assault Rifle Precision Scope MISSABLE: On the floor near a locked door early in the monastery corridors
  • Pistol Magazine Upgrade MISSABLE: On a desk in an office area after an early Reaper skirmish
  • Sniper Rifle Spare Thermal Clip MISSABLE: Found on a bench in the courtyard garden area
  • Disciple shotgun MISSABLE: On the floor near an elevator entrance after clearing enemies

Tips

  • Listen for the loud, high-pitched shriek to know when a Banshee has spawned, and maintain long distance to avoid its one-hit melee execution.
  • If Samara survived Mass Effect 2, you must quickly complete the Paragon interrupt in the final confrontation with her to prevent her suicide, then choose to spare Falere to resolve the mission peacefully.
  • Bring squadmates with strong anti-armor capabilities, such as Liara for Warp or Garrus for armor-piercing rounds, to handle the multiple Banshees encountered in the grand hall.
