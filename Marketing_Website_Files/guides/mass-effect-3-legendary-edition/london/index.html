London

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Final mission

Earth: London is the final priority mission where Commander Shepard and allied forces launch a desperate assault on the Reaper base to reach the Citadel conduit. Shepard rallies troops at the forward operating base, escorts missile batteries, and survives waves of Reaper forces until the Thanix missiles can disable the Reaper guarding the beam. After securing the launch, Shepard and surviving squadmates make a final charge toward the transport beam to ascend to the Citadel.

Items

  • M-98 Widow MISSABLE: Found inside a destroyed building on the way to the forward base
  • M-12 Locust MISSABLE: Located on a crate near the artillery staging area
  • M-27 Scimitar MISSABLE: Resting on the floor in a ruined corridor before reaching the missile battery
  • M-99 Saber MISSABLE: Found inside the shop building during the final missile defense defense perimeter

Tips

  • Take the time to talk to every squadmate and major ally via the comms console in the forward base before speaking to Anderson, as leaving triggers the point of no return.
  • In the final missile defense standoff, focus on surviving and dodging Banshees rather than clearing every enemy, as waves will spawn indefinitely until the fire controls are ready.
  • Equip heavy-hitting anti-armor weapons or powers like Incinerate, Warp, and Armor-Piercing Ammo, as the vast majority of threats in London possess heavy armor plating.
