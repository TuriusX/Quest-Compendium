Tuchanka (Bomb Site)

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Bomb mission

Shepard and squad land at the ruins on Tuchanka to secure a Cerberus-infiltrated site where a turian antimatter bomb is set to detonate. Lieutenant Victus must be defended while disarming the warhead across multiple defensive waves. The squad must hold off escalating Cerberus forces, culminating in Atlas mechs, to ensure the bomb is safely neutralized.

Items

  • Armax Arsenal Gauntlets MISSABLE: Found on the ground inside a ruined room shortly after navigating the initial trench pathways
  • Shotgun Smart Choke MISSABLE: Lying near debris on the upper ramparts before descending toward the central bomb courtyard
  • Sniper Rifle Concentration Module MISSABLE: Placed on the floor near some rubble in the defensive perimeter surrounding the bomb mechanism

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Assault TrooperShields/HealthStandard Cerberus frontline infantry that attempts to flank along the trenches.
CenturionShields/HealthSquad leader capable of laying down smoke screens and firing smoke grenades to obscure targeting.
Combat EngineerShields/HealthDeploys Sentry Turrets that pin down the squad if not destroyed quickly.
Sentry TurretShields/ArmorHigh-damage stationary guns set up by Combat Engineers; dangerous in open areas.
NemesisShields/HealthLong-range sniper identified by a red targeting laser; can quickly strip shields.
Atlas MechShields/ArmorHeavily armored mech that drops in during the final defense of Victus; vulnerable to anti-armor weaponry and tech powers.

Tips

  • Focus down Combat Engineers before they can fully assemble their Sentry Turrets, as active turrets can tear through your squad on the open walkways.
  • Lieutenant Victus has a health bar during the final sequence; prioritize taking down enemies directly targeting him or the bomb platform.
  • Bring anti-shield powers like Overload for the heavy Cerberus shields, and save high-damage anti-armor weapons or powers for the Atlas mechs at the climax.
