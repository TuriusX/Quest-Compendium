Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Ardat-Yakshi Monastery mission

Commander Shepard investigates an Ardat-Yakshi monastery on the planet Lesuss after communication ceases, aiming to assess the Reaper threat. The squad discovers the Reapers are converting the residents into Banshees and must rescue Samara's remaining daughters. To complete the mission and eliminate the Reaper forces, Shepard must locate and manually detonate a bomb within the Great Hall.

Items

  • Pistol Melee Stunner MISSABLE: Found in the entry courtyard near the start of the monastery
  • Assault Rifle Precision Scope MISSABLE: Located in the early residential corridor on a bench
  • Sniper Rifle Spare Thermal Clip MISSABLE: Found near an Asari corpse in the dormitory wing
  • Shotgun Shredder Mod MISSABLE: Located on the floor in the open garden area
  • Disciple (Shotgun) MISSABLE: Sitting on a table in the office overlooking the courtyard garden
  • Asari Widow Commendation MISSABLE: Datapad retrieved from a bedroom desk to initiate a Citadel side quest

Tips

  • Keep maximum distance from Banshees at all times, as their close-range warp grab bypasses shield gates to instantly kill Shepard.
  • Do not prime the bomb in the Great Hall until you have secured the area and restocked all thermal clips from nearby crates.
  • If Samara survived Mass Effect 2, you can prevent her suicide during the post-battle cutscene by reacting quickly to the Paragon interrupt.
