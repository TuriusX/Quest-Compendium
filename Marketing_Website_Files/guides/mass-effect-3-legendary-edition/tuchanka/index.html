Tuchanka

Mass Effect 3 (Legendary Edition) guide · Following the mission on Sur'Kesh

Commander Shepard travels to the Krogan homeworld of Tuchanka to deploy the cure for the Genophage at the Shroud facility alongside Mordin and the Krogan leadership. The mission involves navigating ancient Krogan ruins, surviving a massive Reaper ambush, and securing the Shroud while dealing with Reaper forces and a monstrous Kalros worm. Success here secures the allegiance of the Krogan armada for the war effort against the Reapers.

Items

  • Death Mask MISSABLE: Found on the ground among rubble in the ancient Krogan city ruins before the final Shroud area
  • Pistol Magazine Upgrade MISSABLE: Located on the ground in the catacombs pathway before reaching the Shroud
  • Shotgun Smart Choke MISSABLE: Resting near a broken pillar in the ruins section
  • Graal Spike Thrower MISSABLE: Propped against debris along the main pathway in the ruins

Secrets

  • Renegade cure sabotage choice | Agree to Dalatrass Linron's secret offer prior to the mission and actively prevent Mordin from dispersing the cure via dialogue or force at the Shroud control console

Tips

  • During the Destroyer confrontation at the Shroud, do not try to fight the Reaper directly; sprint between the two Maw Hammers to activate them while avoiding the Reaper's foot stomps and laser beams.
  • Equip anti-armor ammunition or high-damage biotic powers, as the later stages of this mission feature multiple Brutes and Ravagers in tight arenas.
  • Keep moving constantly during the Kalros sequence, as pausing in the open leaves Shepard vulnerable to the Reaper Destroyer's targeting laser.
