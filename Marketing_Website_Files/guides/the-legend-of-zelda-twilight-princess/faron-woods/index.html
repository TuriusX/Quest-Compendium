Faron Woods

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · First journey into the woods
← All areas in this guide

Link heads into Faron Woods to find a lost monkey and rescue his kidnapped childhood friend, Talo. Navigating through dark, misty pathways and a foggy cave requires obtaining and using a lantern. The sequence ends when Link cuts through a wooden cage to free Talo and the monkey at an enemy camp.

Items

0/2

Enemies
Deku Baba
Weak to Severing its stem with the sword or hitting its open mouthSteal / drop Deku Nut

Snaps at Link from the ground or ceiling and can be sliced upright after it lunges

Keese
Weak to Any sword strike

Flying bats that cluster inside the dark cave passages

Bokoblin
Weak to Sword strikes or shield attacks

Red goblin-like creatures that attack with wooden clubs

Rat
Weak to Any attack

Small pests that scurry along the cave floor in dark areas

Shops and people
  • Coro: Refills lantern oil for 20 Rupees or sells an extra Bottle of lantern oil for 100 Rupees

Tips
  • Use the lantern flame to burn through thick spiderwebs blocking doorways inside the cave.
  • Keep an eye on the lantern fuel gauge; turn it off when you are not in pitch-black areas or burning webs to conserve oil.
