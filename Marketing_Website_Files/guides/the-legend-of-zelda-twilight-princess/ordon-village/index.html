Ordon Village is Link's peaceful hometown where the adventure begins through a series of tutorial tasks and errands for the villagers. Link must help recover a baby cradle, catch a fish to feed a cat, acquire his first equipment, and herd goats before the village is attacked by monsters. After events plunge the area into danger, Link must later return to gather a sword and shield in Wolf form to proceed.

Tips
  • You can use Hawk Grass to summon a hawk to knock down a bee hive, collect the honey, or retrieve the floating baby cradle from the river.
  • As Wolf Link sneaking into the village, use Midna's jumps and follow scent trails while digging under fences to avoid the villagers who will attack you on sight.
  • When herding goats at the Ordon Ranch, shout with the A button while riding Epona to direct them into the barn efficiently.
