Snowpeak Ruins

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Fifth dungeon

Snowpeak Ruins is a decrepit, snowbound mansion that serves as the fifth dungeon, where Link searches for a shard of the Mirror of Twilight. Guided by the friendly Yetis Yeto and Yeta, Link navigates frozen rooms, uses cannons, and recovers ingredients for a restorative soup. The quest culminates in retrieving the Bedroom Key to access the top floor and confront the boss.

Items

  • Ball and Chain: Defeat the Darkhammer mini-boss in the western wing of the mansion
  • Ordon Pumpkin: Chest inside the storage room in the west courtyard
  • Ordon Goat Cheese: Chest inside the northern storage area guarded by Freezards
  • Bedroom Key: Chest in the room accessed with the final key in the northern section of the mansion
  • Heart Container: Rewarded upon defeating the boss, Blizzeta

Secrets

  • Piece of Heart | Destroy the cracked floorboards in the parlor with the Ball and Chain to drop into the locked room below
  • Piece of Heart | Melt the Freezard blocking the door in the courtyard with a cannonball and align the target puzzles inside

Shops and people

  • Yeto: Offers ladles of increasingly restorative soup from his cauldron as Link brings him more ingredients

Tips

  • The Ball and Chain instantly shatters ice enemies and brittle walls, making it the most effective weapon in this dungeon.
  • You can use the cannonball to eliminate distant Freezards by firing the stationary cannons loaded with bombs or cannonballs.
