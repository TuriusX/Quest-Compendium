Lake Hylia

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Searching for the Zora

Upon arriving at Lake Hylia during the twilight search for the Zoras, Wolf Link finds the lakebed completely drained of water and shrouded in Twilight. Link must navigate the dry basin, defeat a Shadow Bulblin shooting fire arrows to ride a Kargarok up Zora's River, and ultimately locate the frozen Zora domain. Later, Link returns here after unfreezing Zora's Domain to collect Tears of Light and restore the Light Spirit Lanayru.

Items

  • Vessel of Light: Received automatically from the Spirit Lanayru at the Lanayru Spring

Tips

  • Use Wolf Link's senses constantly when navigating the dried basin and Lanayru Spring to spot tears, bugs, and digging spots buried in the sand.
  • When fighting Shadow Beast portals alone, take out all but the last two or three individually, then defeat the remaining ones in one spin or charge attack to prevent them from reviving.
