Hyrule Castle (Post-Twilight)

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Final dungeon

Link and Midna breach the barrier surrounding Hyrule Castle to confront Ganondorf and free Princess Zelda. The dungeon requires fighting through both the castle grounds and interior halls using nearly every item acquired throughout the quest. It culminates in a multi-phase final confrontation against Ganondorf to decide the fate of Hyrule.

Items

  • Dungeon Map: In a treasure chest located in the castle's western courtyard
  • Compass: In a treasure chest in the eastern courtyard area after defeating enemies there
  • Big Key: Inside a treasure chest on the high exterior balcony on the path leading toward the throne room
  • Small Key: Inside a treasure chest guarded by a King Bulblin in the castle courtyard

Secrets

  • Graveyard puzzle path: Igniting torches in the rainy graveyard reveals an entrance containing a hidden switch and a treasure chest.
  • Hidden treasure room: Striking a specific painting inside the castle with an arrow drops it from the wall, revealing a target for the Clawshot that leads to upper balconies with extra chests.

Tips

  • Keep the Bow and Clawshot assigned to active buttons, as flying enemies and wall switches appear frequently throughout the castle.
  • Enter the dungeon with full Quivers and maximum Bomb capacity, along with Rare Chu Jelly or Fairy Tears in your bottles for the extended final boss gauntlet.
  • In the final sword duel against Ganondorf, initiate sword clashes by attacking at the same time he does, then mash the indicated button to knock him off balance.
