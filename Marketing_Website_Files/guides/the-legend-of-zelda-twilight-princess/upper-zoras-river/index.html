Upper Zora's River

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Traveling to Zora's Domain

During the main story, Link arrives here as a wolf to reach Zora's Domain by jumping along the high cliff paths with Midna's help. After thawing the domain, water flows through here again, connecting the river to Lake Hylia and opening up side activities. Link can return as a human to play minigames, fish, and explore the riverbanks.

Items

  • Piece of Heart: On a high rock spire in the water near Iza's boat rental; retrieve it using the Clawshot and Iron Boots, or during Iza's river descent minigame
  • Giant Bomb Bag: Given by Iza after completing her second river ride minigame by scoring at least 25 points hitting pots with bomb arrows

Secrets

  • Howling Stone | Located on a high cliff edge near Iza's boat rental cabin; howl the tune as Wolf Link to summon the Golden Wolf elsewhere in Hyrule

Shops and people

  • Iza: Bomb arrow river ride minigame and boat rental service down to Lake Hylia
  • Hena's Fishing Hole: Fishing gear rental and boat access to fish in the nearby secluded pond area

Tips

  • Warp to the Upper Zora's River portal to quickly reach either Zora's Domain, Hena's Fishing Hole, or ride the boat down to Lake Hylia.
  • Clear the boulders blocking the river channel using Bomb Arrows to help Iza reopen her boating minigame.
