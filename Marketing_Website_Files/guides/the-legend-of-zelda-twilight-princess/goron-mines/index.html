Goron Mines

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Second dungeon

The Goron Mines is the second dungeon, located deep inside Death Mountain where the Gorons harvest resources. Link must navigate lava-filled rooms and utilize magnetized surfaces to meet three Goron Elders to assemble the Big Key. The final goal is to reach the heart of the mines to defeat the corrupted Goron patriarch, Darbus, and claim the second Fused Shadow.

Items

  • Hero's Bow: Defeat the sub-boss Dangoro in the central room
  • Piece of Heart: In a chest inside a room with rotating magnetic cranes on the ceiling
  • Piece of Heart: In a chest behind two Dodongos in the room with stone walls that must be destroyed with water/climbing mechanics
  • Dungeon Map: Given by the first Goron Elder, Gor Amato
  • Compass: Given by the second Goron Elder, Gor Ebizo
  • Big Key: Assembled after receiving the three key shards from Gor Amato, Gor Ebizo, and Gor Liggs

Secrets

  • A submerged treasure chest can be opened at the bottom of a pool of water by equipping the Iron Boots to sink.

Shops and people

  • Gor Amato: Gives the first Key Shard and the Dungeon Map
  • Gor Ebizo: Gives the second Key Shard and the Compass
  • Gor Liggs: Gives the third Key Shard and the Bow (via access)

Tips

  • Equip the Hylian Shield before entering the mines to prevent your wooden shield from burning.
  • Walking on blue glowing tracks while wearing the Iron Boots pulls Link up against walls and ceilings.
  • You can use the Iron Boots to step onto floor switches that are too heavy for Link's normal weight to activate.
