Snowpeak Mountain

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Searching for the mansion

Link navigates the frozen, blizzardy slopes of Snowpeak in Wolf form by following the scent of the Reekfish caught in Zora's Domain. The scent trail leads through winding caves, snowy switchbacks, and monster ambushes up to the summit. After speaking with Yeto at the peak, Link snowboards down a frozen ramp to arrive at Snowpeak Ruins.

Items

  • Howling Stone: Found along the path ascending the mountain bluffs before the cave

Secrets

  • Dig spots in the snow during Wolf form reveal hidden red or blue rupees, and a dig point inside a cavern alcove leads underground to an enemy room containing a Piece of Heart.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Yeto: Gives Link half of a pumpkin and allows him to use a frozen sheet of bark to snowboard down to the mansion

Tips

  • Use Wolf Link's senses constantly, as the howling blizzard obscures the path and the Reekfish scent trail will be completely lost without them active.
  • Roll into the tree near Yeto on the summit to dislodge a slab of frozen bark to use as a snowboard.
