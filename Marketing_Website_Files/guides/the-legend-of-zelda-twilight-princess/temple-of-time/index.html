Temple of Time

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Sixth dungeon

Link travels to the past via the Sacred Grove to explore the intact Temple of Time. The goal is to climb to the top of the tower, obtain the dungeon item, and guide an ancient stone statue back down to the ground floor entrance to unlock the boss room. Inside the boss chamber, Link defeats Armogohma to retrieve a shard of the Mirror of Twilight.

Items

  • Dungeon Map: First floor inside a treasure chest flanked by unlit torches
  • Compass: Fifth floor in a chest accessed after defeating Armos and activating switches
  • Small Key: Scattered chests throughout the ascent, such as the spinning platform room on floor 3
  • Dominion Rod: Seventh floor inside a chest guarded by the Darknut mini-boss
  • Big Key: Floor 5 inside a central treasure chest guarded by electrical barriers and traps
  • Piece of Heart: Moving wall corridor on floor 5, requiring the Dominion Rod to move a statue onto a floor switch
  • Piece of Heart: Floor 7 behind an electric barrier, reached by using the Dominion Rod to maneuver a small pot onto a switch
  • Mirror Shard: Dropped after defeating the boss Armogohma
  • Heart Container: Rewarded upon defeating Armogohma in the boss chamber

Secrets

  • Male and female Golden Snail bugs can be found just outside the dungeon entrance in the ruined Temple of Time area of the Sacred Grove.
  • A Poe Soul can be found on floor 3 behind a movable wall; use the Dominion Rod to guide the statue away and slash the Poe with Wolf Link.
  • A Poe Soul is located on floor 7 in the scales room, accessed by balancing the scales with statues to reach the high alcove.
  • A Poe Soul hovers behind a gate near the dungeon entrance on floor 1, requiring the Dominion Rod to guide a statue onto a pressure plate to open the gate.

Tips

  • You can use the Dominion Rod to control small stone pots as well as large statues to weigh down floor switches.
  • When balancing the giant scales on the upper floors, throw small stone statues onto the opposite pan to adjust your elevation.
  • The giant statue Link escorts back down the dungeon can smash through metal gates and stone barriers by swinging its massive stone hammer via the Dominion Rod.
