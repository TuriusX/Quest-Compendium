Sacred Grove

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Searching for the Master Sword

Link travels to the deep woods of the Faron Province in wolf form to locate the blade of evil's bane. Guided by the sound of a horn, he navigates a maze-like forest by pursuing a mysterious entity. After solving a guardian puzzle at the ancient ruins, Link retrieves the Master Sword to break the dark magic binding him.

Items

  • Master Sword: Resting in a stone pedestal at the ruins deep within the grove
  • Piece of Heart: Inside a hollow tree alcove accessible after bombing a boulder near the Master Sword pedestal

Secrets

  • Howling Stone near the grove entrance | Howl the melody as Wolf Link to summon the Golden Wolf later on Death Mountain

Enemies

Tips

  • Follow the glowing light of the lantern and the direction of the horn sounds to find which path Skull Kid took through the maze.
  • For the stone guardian statue puzzle, moving Wolf Link causes one statue to mimic your movement direction while the other mirrors it in reverse.
