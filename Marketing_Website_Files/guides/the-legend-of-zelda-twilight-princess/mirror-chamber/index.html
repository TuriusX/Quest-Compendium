Mirror Chamber

The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess guide · Finding the Mirror of Twilight

The Mirror Chamber is an open-air platform located atop the Arbiter's Grounds where the ancient Sages sealed away Ganondorf. Upon your arrival, the Sages explain the history of the Mirror of Twilight and reveal that Zant broke it into four fragments. You must interact with the pedestal using the Spinner to raise the chamber and expose the base of the broken mirror.

Items

  • Mirror of Twilight (Fragment): Found directly on the pedestal after using the Spinner to raise the stone structure

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Shadow BeastSwords, Midna's dark energy fieldA group drops from a portal as you first enter the area; defeat the final ones together to prevent resurrection

Shops and people

  • Ancient Sages: Offer lore exposition regarding Ganondorf's execution and the fractured state of the Mirror of Twilight

Tips

  • Use Midna's charged circular attack to kill the final two or three Shadow Beasts at the same time so they cannot revive each other.
  • A warp portal is activated in this chamber once the Shadow Beasts are cleared, providing a direct fast-travel point to the Mirror.
