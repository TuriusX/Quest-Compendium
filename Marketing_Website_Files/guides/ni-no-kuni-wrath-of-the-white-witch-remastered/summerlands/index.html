Summerlands

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The field region surrounding Castaway Cove

The Summerlands is the southern coastal region encompassing the port town of Castaway Cove and the waters leading to the Open Sea. Players journey across these sun-soaked shores and surrounding islands to secure travel across the ocean and continue their quest. Exploring the region involves battling aggressive wild familiars and harvesting crafting ingredients scattered across the beaches and cliffs.

Secrets

  • Hidden Forest Glade | Located in the cluster of trees north of Castaway Cove, containing resting NPCs and gathering points.
  • Unmarked Cave | Hidden at the dead end of a mountain crevice along the southern cliffs, concealing treasure chests.

Tips

  • Use Esther to charm and tame the wild Purrloiners in this area, as they evolve into exceptionally fast and strong physical attackers.
  • Keep Fire-element familiar tricks equipped when roaming the mainland, as several native plant and beast enemies are weak to heat.
