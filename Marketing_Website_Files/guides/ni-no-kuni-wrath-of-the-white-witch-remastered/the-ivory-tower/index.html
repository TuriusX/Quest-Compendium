The Ivory Tower

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The final dungeon and home of the White Witch

The Ivory Tower is the massive floating stronghold above the world map where Queen Cassiopeia resides. Oliver and his party must navigate its glowing crystalline hallways, overcome teleportation pads, and defeat powerful mechanical and angelic foes. Reaching the pinnacle culminates in a multi-stage final showdown to bring peace back to the world.

Items

  • Sage's Secret: Blue chest along the winding path on the lower tiers
  • Phoenix Feather: Chest located on the western catwalks
  • Elixir: Chest situated on the middle ring before the final elevator

Secrets

  • Hidden passages and shortcut platforms can be activated by stepping on designated glowing floor panels scattered across the tower's interior rings.

Tips

  • Stock up heavily on recovery items, particularly Phoenix Feathers and MP-restoring provisions, before taking the central lift to the final battles.
  • Many enemies inside the tower are aligned with the Light element, making Dark-element spells and familiars particularly potent throughout the dungeon.
