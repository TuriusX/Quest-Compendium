Hamelin

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The industrial, militaristic kingdom

Hamelin is a steampunk-inspired industrial city ruled by the Emperor of the pig-masked Autonomous Empire of Hamelin. Oliver and his companions arrive here searching for the Great Sage Marcassin and the sky-skimmer, getting caught up in uncovering the Emperor's true identity and healing brokenhearted citizens along the way.

Items

  • Medal of Agility: Inside a green chest requiring Spring Lock, located in an alleyway near the town entrance
  • Steam Pipe: Hidden on the ground behind some crates on the industrial upper walkways
  • Green Tea: Inside a common chest near the hotel in the lower district

Secrets

  • A purple treasure chest sits on a raised platform, which can only be unlocked later in the game once Oliver learns the spell Chest-Open.
  • A hidden ghost NPC named Horace appears near the balcony overlooking the town, giving riddles that reward Oliver with new spells upon solving them.

Shops and people

  • The Hootique (Provisions): Sells recovery items, status-healing medicines, and basic treats for familiars
  • The Owl's Nest (Weapons & Armor): Sells mid-game equipment featuring mechanical gears and reinforced steel gear suited for Hamelin's technological theme
  • The Cat's Cradle (Inn): Offers rest to fully restore HP and MP, and provides the local Swift Solutions notice board for bounty hunts and errant tasks

Tips

  • Keep an eye out for citizens with missing Heart pieces, as several people here require the "Belief" or "Confidence" virtue to resolve story events and side quests.
  • You will need the "Cultivate Plant" and "Spring Lock" spells to access certain elevated and locked treasure chests placed throughout Hamelin's layered streets.
