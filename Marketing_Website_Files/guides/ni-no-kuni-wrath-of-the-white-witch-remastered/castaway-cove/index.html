Castaway Cove

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The coastal town and port

Castaway Cove is a sun-soaked port town where the party travels to secure a ship to cross the Summerlands ocean. While here, Oliver must cure the Governor of his broken heart and recover a stolen letter to earn the right to set sail. Once cleared of story obstacles, this town becomes your home dock and grants access to open sea travel.

Items

  • Fishburger: In a green chest sitting on a boat dock on the east side of town
  • Booster Shoot: Inside a purple chest on a raised wooden walkway, requiring the Spring Lock spell

Secrets

  • Hidden treasure chest behind the Governor's hut accessible by walking along the narrow wooden pier around the back.
  • Horace the ghost boy appears standing on the docks and will give you a new spell if you solve his riddle about the Nazcaän language.

Shops and people

  • The Hootique (Swift Solutions): Accepts completed regional side quests, stamps merit cards, and offers localized bounty hunts.
  • Cawtermaster's Store (Provisions Shop): Sells coastal-tier weapons, armor, accessories, and standard recovery items like iced coffees and sandwiches.
  • The Cat's Cradle: Provides overnight inn resting services to fully replenish HP and MP, and houses a save point.

Tips

  • You must acquire a swimsuit for Oliver from the local market stall before the Governor will grant you an audience.
  • Do not leave port immediately after getting the boat; speak to the locals around the docks to unlock a batch of coastal side quests and errand hunts.
