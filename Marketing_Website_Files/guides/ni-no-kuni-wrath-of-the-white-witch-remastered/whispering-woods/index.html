Whispering Woods is the first true wilderness dungeon Oliver explores shortly after arriving in the Other World. Accompanied by Drippy, Oliver must navigate the winding, mossy paths to reach the clearing of the Elderwood and seek an audience with the Great Sage Father Oak. Along the way, the duo fights off local wildlife and eventually battles a corrupted guardian to cleanse the forest's heart.

Items

0/2

Enemies
Inphant
Weak to FireSteal / drop Sandwich

Weak early-game enemy that attacks with basic physical headbutts.

Sleepeon
Weak to FireSteal / drop Iced Coffee

Often attempts to inflict the Sleep status ailment on party members.

Gruffian
Weak to WindSteal / drop Milk

Aggressive melee foe that hits harder than other common enemies in the woods.

Gladiataur
Weak to FireSteal / drop Chocolate

Boss encountered at the Deep Wood clearing who charges in a straight line.

Tips
  • Keep Oliver at a safe distance and cast the Fireball spell against common enemies here, as most local creatures are weak to Fire.
  • When the boss Gladiataur prepares his rushing attack, run perpendicular to his path to make him crash and leave himself vulnerable.
  • Pick up the glowing green and blue glims that drop during combat to keep Oliver's Health and Mana topped up without using inventory items.
