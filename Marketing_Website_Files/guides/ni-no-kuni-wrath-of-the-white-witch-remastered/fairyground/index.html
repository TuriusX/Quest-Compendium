Fairyground

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The hidden village of the fairies

The Fairyground is the vibrant, comedy-obsessed sanctuary that Drippy calls home, located on the island of No Longer Mine. Oliver and his companions arrive here to acquire the magic needed to bypass Hamelin's guard posts, only to discover that the village's humor has vanished due to broken hearts. The party must perform a stand-up comedy routine at the theater and heal the islanders' hearts to restore the Fairy Godmother to her former, cheerful self.

Items

  • Fishburger: In a green chest located near the swings on the eastern side of the village
  • Chocolate: Inside a treasure chest on the theater's side balcony

Shops and people

  • Hootique (Weapons and Armor): Sells mid-tier equipment suited for the journey toward Autumnia, including elemental robes, comedy-themed equipment, and provisions
  • The Cat's Cradle (Inn): Offers a comedy-filled night of rest to fully restore Oliver and the party's HP and MP

Tips

  • Talk to the comedian fairies backstage to practice and memorize the correct comedic timing and lines before performing the routine in front of the Fairy Godmother.
  • Bring plenty of heart-healing pieces like Restraint, as several fairies around the village are brokenhearted and require essence from your Locket before you can finish the main storyline objectives here.
