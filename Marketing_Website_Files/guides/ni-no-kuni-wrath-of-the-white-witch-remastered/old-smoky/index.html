Old Smoky

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The volcanic mountain dungeon

Old Smoky is an active volcanic dungeon that Oliver and Esther must scale to stop the mountain from erupting and to retrieve King Tom's lost heart piece. Players must navigate winding paths with flowing lava, timed obstacles, and environmental hazards that can damage the party. At the summit, Oliver faces the fiery guardian Magmanizer in order to stabilize the volcano.

Items

  • Phoenix Feather: Green chest located along the winding paths on the lower slopes
  • Milk Chocolate: Red chest near the midway waypoint on the mountain ascent
  • Burning Blade: Red chest found along a detour path on the higher slopes

Tips

  • Use Drippy's "Frostbite" spell or water-based familiar attacks to deal massive damage against the fire-aligned enemies here.
  • Avoid stepping directly into the patches of magma on the ground, as they will cause continuous damage to the lead character.
