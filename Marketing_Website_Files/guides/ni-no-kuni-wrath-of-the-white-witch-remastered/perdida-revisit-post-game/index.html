Perdida (Revisit: Post-Game)

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · Completing post-game errands and quests

Returning to Perdida in the post-game allows you to accept new Conductor quests and wrap up high-level bounty tasks. Derwin appears here to present his final and most demanding research assignment regarding rare monster evolutions. You can also gather late-game heart pieces and complete remaining errands for the local villagers.

Secrets

  • Look around the center of the village near the elder's residence to find Derwin for the ultimate errand in his creature-research questline.

Shops and people

  • Swift Solutions: Bounty hunts, errands, and Merit Card reward redemption
  • Hootique / Peddler: Endgame weapons, high-tier armor, and provision items

Tips

  • Prepare the specific final-tier familiars Derwin requests before speaking to him to finish his questline immediately without redundant travel.
  • Perdida is home to NPCs who regain surplus heart pieces, making it an essential hub to restore missing virtues needed for remaining side quests across the world.
