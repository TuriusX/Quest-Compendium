Ding Dong Dell (Revisit: Post-Game)

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · Completing post-game errands and quests

Ding Dong Dell opens up several high-tier post-game errands and bounty hunts once the main story has been completed and the cleared save file is loaded. Players return here to speak with King Tom to trigger the Conductor questline, as well as pick up local side quests involving familiars and craft requests. It serves as a central hub for resolving the final errands on the Swift Solutions task board and harvesting late-game rewards.

Items

  • Philosofer's Stone: Awarded by King Tom upon accepting Errand 131: The Conductor
  • Mirror of Truth: Given by King Tom during the post-game quest chain to unlock the Conductor's lair

Secrets

  • Speak to King Tom inside Ding Dong Dell Palace after loading clear data to initiate Errand 131, which unlocks the hidden teleporter in the palace ruins leading to the ultimate post-game boss.

Shops and people

  • Swift Solutions: Post-game Bounty Hunts (Task 121–130) and master tier Merit Award stamps
  • The Hootique (Ding Dong Dell Owl Merchant): Standard recovery supplies, provisions, and basic equipment

Tips

  • Accept Errand 131 from King Tom early in the post-game so that you gain access to the Guardian of Worlds portal, allowing you to farm rare materials like Kaleidostones.
  • Check the Swift Solutions board right away, as several newly posted hunts take place around the Summerlands region just outside the city gates.
