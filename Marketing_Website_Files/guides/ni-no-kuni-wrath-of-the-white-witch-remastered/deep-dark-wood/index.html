Deep Dark Wood

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The forest where Oliver rescues Drippy

This is the very first dungeon Oliver explores shortly after entering the Other World and arriving through the gate. You follow Drippy through the forest to learn the fundamentals of movement and combat, culminating in a boss battle against the Guardian of the Woods. Completing this area clears the way to the Rolling Hills and the kingdom of Ding Dong Dell.

Items

  • Iced Coffee: In a treasure chest along the path
  • Sandwich: In a treasure chest along the path

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
JabberFireWeak bird-like creature found early in the woods.
SleepeonFireA small mammalian enemy encountered in the brush.
Guardian of the WoodsFireThe dungeon boss; defends with high physical strength but is weak to Oliver's fireball spell.

Tips

  • Make liberal use of Oliver's Fireball spell against the Guardian of the Woods, as it exploits the boss's elemental weakness.
  • Use the Defend command promptly whenever the Guardian of the Woods winds up its powerful area-of-effect attack, Wind Fall.
  • Stand near the glowing waystones inside the forest to fully replenish your HP and MP before fighting tough enemies.
