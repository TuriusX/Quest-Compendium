Sea of Tears

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The ocean region surrounding the Spirit Isles

The Sea of Tears is an open water expanse situated around the Spirit Isles that you cross using the Sea Cow. While navigating these waters, Oliver and his party encounter various aquatic familiars during shipboard travel. It serves as a maritime passage connecting the surrounding islands to the broader ocean territories.

Tips

  • Equipping fire and storm elemental spells or familiars will allow you to quickly defeat the water-based creatures that board your vessel in this region.
  • When sailing, you cannot steer to avoid monster markers as easily as running on land, so initiate combat on your own terms using long-range familiars.
