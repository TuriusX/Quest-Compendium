Golden Grove

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The forest dungeon filled with autumn leaves

Oliver and Esther enter this vibrant autumn forest to travel toward the desert kingdom of Al Mamoon. The party must restore dried-up spring bulbs to create traversable plant pathways and defeat the Guardian of the Woods. Completing this dungeon rewards Oliver with the ability to cast Nature's Tongue and talk to animals.

Items

  • Quenched Sword: Inside a green chest on the western path
  • Booster Shots: Inside a chest along the southern winding trail
  • Poison-Be-Gone: Inside a treasure chest near the giant mushroom platforms

Secrets

  • Blue and purple locked chests throughout the grove can be opened later once Oliver learns Spring Lock and acquires the master key.

Tips

  • Cast Quench on the glowing red and yellow flower bulbs to make them grow into leaf bridges that bridge gaps between cliffs.
  • Use Esther's Sidestepping ability to keep her safe while controlling Oliver to pelt the Guardian of the Woods with Fire Ball from a safe distance.
