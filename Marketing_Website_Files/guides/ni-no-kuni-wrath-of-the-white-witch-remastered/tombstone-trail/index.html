Tombstone Trail

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The path leading to the Ghostly Gorge

Tombstone Trail is a dark, haunted mountain trail traveled to find the ghost of the former Sage of Autumn to obtain a spell. Oliver and his party must navigate foggy paths by lighting statues with fire magic, avoiding hazardous poison swamps, and navigating tricky stone steps. The journey culminates in a boss fight against a towering, undead spirit at the summit.

Items

  • Candle: Given to Oliver by the spirit outside the cabin near the entrance
  • Spiritstone Armor: Inside a green chest requiring Spring Lock along the upper mountain path
  • Star Drop: Inside a treasure chest on an elevated stone ledge midway through the trail

Tips

  • Cast the spell Secret Ingredient on the candle to light its flame, which is necessary to reveal and follow the ghosts across the dark paths.
  • Avoid stepping into the purple swamp waters along the path, as they will steadily drain your party members' health.
  • Use Fire-based spells and fire trick skills frequently here, as most of the undead and ghostly familiars roaming the trail are weak to Fire.
