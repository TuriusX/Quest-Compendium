Al Mamoon

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The desert kingdom and oasis city

Al Mamoon is an oasis city in the Shimmering Sands ruled by the Cowlipha. Oliver visits to seek the great sage Rashaad to learn new magic, but must first restore the ruler's broken heart.

Items

  • Milk: In a pot next to a stall in the central market
  • Sandwich: In a pot near the entrance fountain
  • Green chest (contains Phoenix Feather): Near the pathway leading to the palace

Shops and people

  • The CawBank: Weapons, armor, and provisions for Oliver and familiars
  • Cat's Cradle: Inn where Oliver can rest and recover HP and MP

Tips

  • Talk to the swift-solutions representative in town to unlock several local bounty hunts and errand quests.
  • You need to extract the piece of heart "Enthusiasm" from a townsperson to cure the Cowlipha's broken heart.
