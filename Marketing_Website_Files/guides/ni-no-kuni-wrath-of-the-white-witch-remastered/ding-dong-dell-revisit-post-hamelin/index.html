Ding Dong Dell (Revisit: Post-Hamelin)

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · Returning to the kingdom to find it under attack

Oliver and his companions return to Ding Dong Dell after completing their business in Hamelin, only to discover the kingdom has been invaded by Prince Marcassin's Porcine forces. You must fight your way through the streets against enemy soldiers to reach the palace. Once inside, you confront the commander leading the assault to liberate the cat kingdom.

Tips

  • Porcine soldiers are mechanical or armored beasts that are generally weak to Fire magic, making Esther's fire familiars and Oliver's fireball spells highly effective.
  • Clear the streets systematically toward Ding Dong Well and the palace gates to avoid getting swarmed by multiple patrols at once.
