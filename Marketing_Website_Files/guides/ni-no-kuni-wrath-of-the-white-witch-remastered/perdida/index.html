Perdida

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The hidden village of the bandits

Perdida is a secluded, fortified settlement set high in the Autumnia mountains, reachable on foot by traversing the perilous Billy Goat's Bluff or swiftly by flying on Tengri. Oliver visits this village in search of the legendary spell "Gateway" to cross back between worlds and to assist the great sage Khulan. While here, you must mend broken hearts among the residents and acquire the Magic Lamp to advance the main story.

Items

  • Magic Lamp: Received from Khulan inside her tent after clearing the story events in Perdida
  • Green chest: In the southern plaza near the pig stalls
  • Purple chest: Tucked behind the tent in the upper residential sector of the village

Secrets

  • The Hidden Hermit in the far northeastern recess of the village only gives you errand tasks once you have advanced the storyline past the fall of Shadar.

Shops and people

  • The Hootique (Perdida Branch): Sells late-game restorative items, sundries, and high-tier defensive armor suited for the harsh climate
  • The Cat's Cradle: Provides overnight resting services to fully heal HP and MP, as well as a Waystone right outside for saving

Tips

  • Cast "Travel" right after discovering Perdida to easily fast-travel directly into the village square rather than hiking up Billy Goat's Bluff every time.
  • Many residents in Perdida suffer from a deficiency of belief, so make sure to carry a spare piece of Belief from elsewhere if you want to complete local side errands quickly.
