Glittering Grotto

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · The cave leading to the Tombstone Trail

The Glittering Grotto is an icy cavern located in the Winter Isles that party members must traverse to reach the northern reaches. The area is filled with slick pathways, frozen hazards, and ice-attuned familiars. At the heart of the cavern, the party must defeat a massive ice dragon named Cerulean to proceed.

Items

  • Milk Chocolate: Blue chest in an icy dead-end cavern on the lower tier
  • Wildflower Ice Cream: Treasure chest situated near a slide shortcut in the midsection
  • Star Drop: Green chest on an elevated ledge reachable by following the path downward
  • Medal of Agility: Red treasure chest located right before the final chamber

Tips

  • Equip fire-based weapons and prepare fire spells like Fireball before entering, as almost every wild familiar is weak to Fire.
  • Slide down the frozen slopes rather than walking when you want to access lower ledges that cannot be reached on foot.
  • Have Oliver or a fire-proficient familiar constantly target Cerulean's fire weakness to interrupt heavy attacks and create gold glims.
