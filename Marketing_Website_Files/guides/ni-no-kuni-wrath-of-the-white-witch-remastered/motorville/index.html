Motorville

Ni no Kuni Wrath of the White Witch™ Remastered guide · Oliver's hometown at the start of the game

Motorville is Oliver's hometown in the real world, serving as the prologue location where his journey begins following the sudden loss of his mother, Allie. You will periodically return here via the Gateway spell to find soulmates in order to mend broken hearts in the other world. The area is completely peaceful, featuring story cutscenes, exploration, and errand-running without traditional monster encounters.

Items

  • Wizard's Companion: Oliver's bedroom in his house, left on the table
  • Wooden Stick: Oliver's bedroom fireplace inside his house

Secrets

  • Examining the fireplace in Oliver's bedroom yields a makeshift wand (the Wooden Stick) after Drippy tells you to search for something resembling a wand.
  • Checking the car in Leila's shop garage triggers nostalgic dialogue with Mark once you advance the early story steps.

Shops and people

  • Leila's Milk Bar: A local grocery and diner owned by Leila, though you do not buy standard RPG battle supplies here; it primarily serves as a story location to interact with Leila.

Tips

  • You cannot engage in battles or tame familiars while in Motorville, so focus entirely on story objectives and locating soulmates.
  • Remember the layout of residents' homes and workplaces, as you will need to revisit specific people like Phil, Mark, and Leila later in the game to fix brokenhearted residents in Ding Dong Dell and beyond.
