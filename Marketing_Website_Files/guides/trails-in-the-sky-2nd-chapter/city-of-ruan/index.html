Estelle and her partner arrive at the seaside hub in Chapter 1 to investigate ghost sightings amidst mayoral election tensions. Players must check in with Jean at the local Bracer Guild branch, explore both sides of the canal, and head to the southern harbor warehouses to deal with the Ravens.
