Points of no return

  • Finishing Grimsel Fortress and reporting to Phylia: Locks off Le Locle Canyon, Balstar Channel, and Saint-Croix Forest indefinitely for the rest of the playthrough.
  • Boarding the airship at Ruan Landing Port at the end of Chapter 1: Expires all Chapter 1 side quests, Gull Seaside Way exploration, and missed items in the Old Schoolhouse.
  • Concluding the central Zeiss conference at the end of Chapter 2: Expires all Zeiss-region quests, Hot Springs Fountainhead events, and Tratt Plains monster exterminations.
  • Entering Grancel Castle for the Chapter 3 banquet: Locks out Grancel City side quests, Grancel Sewers hunting, and ongoing Anton dialogue flags.
  • Clearing the Malicious Mist at Rolent's clock tower in Chapter 4: Forfeits unfinished Rolent requests and missable dialogue with traveling NPCs.
  • Entering the Ancient Dragon's Dwelling inner sanctum in Chapter 5: Expires all Bose region side content, Ravennue Village tasks, and Abandoned Mine pickups.
  • Escaping The Crimson Ark at the end of Chapter 6: Permanent cutoff for the red ark layout, unique enemies, and chests located on the ship.
  • Defeating the guardian atop the fourth tower in Chapter 7: Forever seals the alternate dimensions of Esmelas, Carnelia, Sapphirl, and Amberl towers.
  • Delivering the final Zero Field Generator and reporting to the Royal Keep in Chapter 8: Locks out all remaining surface Liberl side quests, shopping, fishing spots, and city visits.
  • Triggering the elevator to the final core past Liber Ark Tunnels - 3: Seals off the entirety of the Liber Ark residential, industrial, and tunnel maps before the game's finale.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Start Playthrough 1 on Normal or Hard, systematically collecting all 566 chests, all Gambler Jack and Liberl News issues, all recipes, fish, and maxing out all BP by reporting every side quest.

  2. Step 2

    Progress through Le Locle Canyon, Balstar Channel, Saint-Croix Forest, and Grimsel Fortress in the Prologue, securing every hidden chest, defeating Gilbert without losing, and completing all training objectives.

  3. Step 3

    Travel across City of Ruan, Gull Seaside Way, Krone Trail, Aurian Causeway, Sapphirl Tower, Jenis Royal Academy, and the Old Schoolhouse during Chapter 1, initiating the Odyssey of Anton thread and checking every expired request.

  4. Step 4

    Clear Chapter 2 in the City of Zeiss, Tratt Plains Road, Kaldia Tunnel, Soldat Army Road, and Hot Springs Fountainhead, logging all new enemies and collecting Peculiar dishes.

  5. Step 5

    Investigate the City of Grancel and the Grancel Sewers during Chapter 3, progressing Anton's encounters and buying all limited casino rewards and books.

  6. Step 6

    Uncover the mist incidents across the City of Rolent in Chapter 4, following Anton and finishing all local monster bounties.

  7. Step 7

    Hunt down the rampaging threat through the City of Bose, Amberl Tower, Ravennue Village, Ravennue Trail, Abandoned Mine, and Ancient Dragon's Dwelling in Chapter 5.

  8. Step 8

    Infiltrate the Lakeside Laboratory and navigate The Crimson Ark in Chapter 6, ensuring you scan every boss and minion for the Combat Handbook.

  9. Step 9

    Ascend Esmelas Tower, Carnelia Tower, Sapphirl Tower (Chapter 7), and Amberl Tower (Chapter 7) in Chapter 7, gathering all high-tier quartz and chest drops.

  10. Step 10

    Traverse the non-functioning orbal network across City of Bose (Chapter 8), City of Ruan (Chapter 8), City of Zeiss (Chapter 8), City of Rolent (Chapter 8), and City of Grancel (Chapter 8), delivering all Zero Field generators, securing all late-game weapons, and obtaining Senior Bracer A-Rank.

  11. Step 11

    Conquer Liber Ark Tunnels - 1, Residential Block Cradle, Liber Ark Tunnels - 2, Industrial Block, and Liber Ark Tunnels - 3 in the Finale, crafting Zemurian Ore weapons, maxing orbment slots, and logging every final encounter.

  12. Step 12

    Start New Game Plus on Nightmare (or with +75 levels) carrying over data to defeat the final bosses and unlock the remaining difficulty and completion achievements.
