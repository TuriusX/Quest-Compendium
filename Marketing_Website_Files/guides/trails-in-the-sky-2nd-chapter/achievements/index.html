Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Finishing the Balstar Channel trials in Prologue: Le Locle training camp and surrounding wilderness become permanently inaccessible, locking out any missed chests or monster scans.
  • Boarding the scheduled airship at the end of each regional chapter (Chapters 1 to 5): Immediate expiry of active regional side quests, missed Gambler Jack books, and unlearned local cooking recipes.
  • Concluding the lakeside sequence at Valleria Lake in Chapter 6: Permanent cutoff for various standard fish catches and open-world fishing spots prior to the invasion phase.
  • Defeating the final enforcer in the Tetracyclic Towers in Chapter 7: The tower environments collapse, permanently voiding access to their unique enemy scans and internal chests.
  • Boarding the Arseille to fly to the Liber Ark at the end of Chapter 8: Entire surface kingdom of Liberl becomes inaccessible, locking Bracer Rank A, Anton's side narrative, and land-based collection goals.
  • Passing through the barrier leading to the final elevator in the Liber Ark Axis Pillar: Cuts off access to Liber Ark residential districts, data terminals, remaining recipe cooking, and weapon trade-ins.

Recommended order
  1. Le Locle | Estelle, Prologue: Scan every unique monster, open all Balstar Channel and Saint-Croix Forest chests, find all hidden items, and earn maximum BP on training challenges.
  2. City of Ruan | Estelle, Chapter 1: Acquire the fishing rod, catch early fish varieties, inspect all required spots during the lighthouse investigation, collect early Gambler Jack issues, and finish all bulletin and hidden requests before boarding the liner.
  3. City of Zeiss | Estelle, Chapter 2: Hunt monsters in the Carnelia Tower and underground passages, scan every variant, collect Gambler Jack volumes, clear all Zeiss bulletin board jobs, and recruit maximum BP before the seismic incident wraps up.
  4. Royal City of Grancel | Estelle, Chapter 3: Follow Anton's narrative scenes, visit the casino for mini-game achievements, scour the sewers for chests, grab contemporary Gambler Jack issues, and win the tournament matches flawlessly.
  5. City of Rolent | Estelle, Chapter 4: Complete local investigations during the supernatural fog, thoroughly inspect the mist zones, read remaining Gambler Jack volumes, and resolve all optional bounty and side quests before departing.
  6. City of Bose | Estelle, Chapter 5: Track the dragon incidents, fully explore the Ravennue Trail and Nebel Valley, scan all dragon-related beasts, gather recipe books, and keep abreast of limited-time book issues.
  7. Valleria Lake | Estelle, Chapter 6: Fish at every exclusive dock around the lake, achieve the Master Angler rank requirements, trigger all camp character events, clear the lakeside facility, and inspect all chests aboard the subsequent airborne sequence.
  8. Tetracyclic Towers | Estelle, Chapter 7: Ascend the four elemental towers, open all dimensionally sealed chests, fully register all Ouroboros combat handbook entries, and prepare Estelle’s party for the nationwide blackout.
  9. Estelle, Chapter 8: Traverse the non-orbal Liberl by foot, deliver zero-field generators, talk to Anton to advance his saga, complete all local emergencies and monster exterminations across all regions, and achieve Senior Bracer Rank A.
  10. Liber Ark | Estelle, Final Chapter: Collect the ultimate Gambler Jack issue and trade the full set for top-tier weapons, complete the entire recipe catalog and peculiar dishes, open every chest in the Ark, defeat Gilbert without falling, scan the final boss forms, and complete the game.
