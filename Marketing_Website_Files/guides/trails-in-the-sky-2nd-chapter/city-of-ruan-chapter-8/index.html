City of Ruan (Chapter 8)

Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter guide · The bracers arrive in the port town to deliver a generator to the local guild ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Estelle and her companions enter the port city on foot during the nationwide orbal blackout to deliver a Zero Field Generator to Jean at the local guildhouse. Handing over the unit restores local communication lines and prompts Jean to alert the party about troubles in the outlying area, specifically unlocking the hidden rescue scenario at Jenis Royal Academy.

Tips
  • Because parting with a Zero Field Generator strips one of your party members of arts usage and an equipped accessory slot, wrap up any regional bounty hunts outside town before handing the generator to Jean.
