Stormhill

ELDEN RING guide · Traveling north from Limgrave

Stormhill is the windy, elevated plateau north of Limgrave acting as the primary gateway to Stormveil Castle. You will encounter several wandering bosses, pack animals, and key NPCs here, including Roderika at a sheltered shack and Knight Bernahl at a remote warmaster's hut. Navigating the main road takes you through heavily fortified barricades up toward the castle gates.

Items

  • Spirit Jellyfish Ashes: Given by Roderika inside the Stormhill Shack after speaking to her multiple times
  • Stonesword Key: Found on a corpse sitting on the edge of the broken bridge leading north from Stormhill
  • Strength-knot Crystal Tear: Found in a stone basin guarded by a giant on the cliffs near the Stormhill Shack
  • Ash of War: Golden Vow: Dropped by a mounted Godrick Knight patrolling the high cliffs southeast of the Warmaster's Shack
  • Lance: Looted from a corpse on top of the ruined square building near the Deathtouched Catacombs

Secrets

  • The Stormhill Evergaol sits on the southern plateau of the hill and can be entered to fight the Crucible Knight.
  • A Deathbird boss only appears at night in the clearing near the Warmaster's Shack beneath the collapsed divine tower bridge.
  • Night's Cavalry patrols the main paved road leading through Stormhill solely during the nighttime.
  • The Deathtouched Catacombs entrance is tucked into the northern rock face leading down to the ravine east of the Warmaster's Shack.

  • Knight Bernahl: Sells various physical Ashes of War including Stamp (Upward Cut) and Quickstep at the Warmaster's Shack

Tips

  • Lure one of the roaming giants into hitting the cracked, glowing statue on the plains to break it open for upgrade stones.
  • Ride your mount past the heavily guarded trench gate rather than fighting through the ballista fire on foot.
