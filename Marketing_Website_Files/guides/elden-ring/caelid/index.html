Caelid

ELDEN RING guide · The rot-infested region east of Limgrave
← All areas in this guide

Caelid is a scarlet-rot-blighted wasteland situated directly east of Limgrave, devastated by the battle between Malenia and General Radahn. Players navigate deadly swamps, break magical town seals, and traverse the Redmane Castle fortress to trigger the Radahn Festival. The primary objective is venturing to Redmane Castle to challenge Starscourge Radahn and claim his Great Rune.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating Starscourge Radahn unlocks Radahn's Great Rune, the Remembrance of the Starscourge, and opening access to Nokron, Eternal City.
  • •Collecting the Meteorite Staff and Rock Sling in the Street of Sages Ruins provides a premier early-to-midgame spellcasting combination.
  • •Completing Commander O'Neil's fight awards the Unalloyed Gold Needle, which is essential for advancing Millicent and Gowry's questline.

Common mistakes

  • •Engaging the giant dogs and crows without the Beast-Repellent Torch; purchase it from the Isolated Merchant in northern Caelid to keep them passive.
  • •Attacking Gowry in his shack prematurely, which turns him into a pest and ends his spell shop and quest assistance.
  • •Missing Alexander on the Wailing Dunes; talk to him right after Radahn falls while he scours the battlefield to avoid stalling his quest to Mount Gelmir.
  • •Fighting Commander O'Neil on foot in the Heart of Aeonia; remain mounted on Torrent so scarlet rot does not build up from the swamp basin.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Crossing the Border and Rotview Balcony

    When you cross into Caelid along the main road past the Smoldering Church, push east until you reach the clifftop Site of Grace at Rotview Balcony. Beware the Monstrous Dogs and Giant Crows prowling the crimson terrain, or detour south along the cliffs to clear Gael Tunnel. Inside Gael Tunnel, defeat the Magma Wyrm boss to claim the Moonveil katana and locate Iron Fist Alexander behind the rear door before continuing down the main road past Caelem Ruins.

    Tip: Holding a Beast-Repellent Torch in your off-hand keeps Monstrous Dogs and Giant Crows from attacking you.

    Watch out: Defeating the area bosses too early can permanently prevent the NPC invader Anastasia, Tarnished-Eater, from spawning at the Smoldering Church entrance, costing you the Sacred Scorpion Charm.

  2. 2

    Follow the highway south past the fork toward Caelid Highway South and the Cathe

    tip: Gallop behind Decaying Ekzykes's legs with Torrent the moment you see the dragon wind up its expansive airborne or sweeping rot breath attacks.

    Key fight: Decaying Ekzykes

    Tip: Gallop behind Decaying Ekzykes's legs with Torrent the moment you see the dragon wind up its expansive airborne or sweeping rot breath attacks.

  3. 3

    Braving the Swamp of Aeonia

    Ride into the central rot basin of the Swamp of Aeonia and navigate toward the Street of Sages Ruins. Search the rot-flooded tower window corpse to claim the Meteorite Staff, then descend into the underground cellar to loot Rock Sling. Ride out toward the Heart of Aeonia clearing to confront Commander O'Neil and his summoned phantom soldiers to retrieve the Unalloyed Gold Needle and the Commander's Standard halberd.

    Key fight: Commander O'Neil

    Tip: Fight Commander O'Neil entirely atop Torrent to prevent scarlet rot buildup while baiting his summoned crossbow and halberd soldiers away from his aura buffs.

  4. 4

    Gowry's Shack, Sellia, and Church of the Plague

    Ride to the northeastern rim of the swamp to find Gowry's Shack, where Gowry will repair your Unalloyed Gold Needle and reveal how to dispel the seals blocking Sellia, Town of Sorcery. Deliver the repaired needle to Millicent at the Church of the Plague on the eastern cliffs to cure her and receive the Prosthesis-Wearer Heirloom. Return to Gowry to access his sorceries and incantations, but leave him unharmed as you prepare for the southern march.

    Tip: Kindred of Rot pests fire dangerous homing Pest Threads from a distance, but they stagger easily against basic physical and slash attacks.

    Watch out: Attacking Gowry early will transform him into a pest, permanently cutting off Millicent's quest progression and your access to his purchasable magic.

  5. 5

    Impassable Greatbridge and Redmane Castle

    Travel to the far southern end of the highway, cross the Impassable Greatbridge, and enter Redmane Castle. Depending on whether you triggered the Radahn Festival via Altus Plateau or Ranni and Blaidd's quest, either battle the Misbegotten Warrior and Crucible Knight duo for the Ruins Greatsword, or join the assembled festival champions in the courtyard. Speak with Castellan Jerren to commence the festival, pass through the waygate to the Wailing Dunes, and take down Starscourge Radahn alongside your allies to claim Radahn's Great Rune and the Remembrance of the Starscourge.

    Key fight: Starscourge Radahn

    Tip: Take cover behind the battlefield debris to block Radahn's initial gravity arrows, continuously trigger the golden summon signs to send festival allies into the fray, and stay at a distance on Torrent when gravity meteors orbit him in phase two.

    Watch out: Speak to Iron Fist Alexander immediately while he gathers corpses on the Wailing Dunes battlefield after Radahn dies, or you will miss pushing his journey toward Mount Gelmir.

Gowry's Fate

Meeting Gowry at his shack near Sellia

  • Leave him unharmed→ Retain his merchant stock of sorceries and incantations while keeping Millicent's quest progression open
  • Attack him early→ Transforms him into a pest and cuts off both his shop and his involvement in Millicent's storyline

Most players pick: Leave him unharmed

Redmane Castle State

After defeating Starscourge Radahn at the festival

  • Speak with Castellan Jerren in the church until he departs and reload the area→ Restores the hostile dungeon layout with standard guards and makes the Misbegotten Warrior & Crucible Knight duo fight available for the Ruins Greatsword
  • Do not exhaust Jerren's dialogue→ Retains the empty festival state where doors stay closed and the duo boss cannot be challenged

Most players pick: Speak with Castellan Jerren in the church until he departs and reload the area

Triggering the festival earlier despawns the duo boss fight until Jerren is dismissed following Radahn's death

Key fights
Commander O'Neil

Enemies: Commander O'Neil and phantom summons

Threats: Summons spirit crossbowmen and axemen; uses sweeping halberd whirlwinds that release widespread Scarlet Rot

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Piercing and Strike damage; susceptible to Rotten Breath and Fire

Tactics: Ride Torrent to maneuver around his rot storms, quickly eliminate his summoned phantoms, and use hit-and-run attacks between his slow swings

Rewards: Commander's Standard; Unalloyed Gold Needle

Starscourge Radahn

Enemies: Starscourge Radahn

Threats: Fires gravity greatarrows at range; rides a small horse with relentless melee sweeps; summons meteors and enters a crashing meteorite phase transition

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Pierce damage, Scarlet Rot, and Bleed; resistant to Holy

Tactics: Ride across the Wailing Dunes while repeatedly re-summoning fallen festival champions from gold summon signs on the ground; attack with Scarlet Rot effects or long-range spells while Radahn focuses on NPCs

Rewards: Remembrance of the Starscourge; Radahn's Great Rune

Decaying Ekzykes

Enemies: Decaying Ekzykes

Threats: Massive dragon breathing Scarlet Rot clouds over enormous areas; stomps and tail swipes

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Fire and thrust attacks to the head

Tactics: Stay mounted on Torrent to outrun his broad-area scarlet breath, attacking his rear feet when he lands, and retreat immediately when he charges his grounded rot explosion

Rewards: Dragon Heart

Cleanrot Knight
Weak to Fire, StrikeSteal / drop Cleanrot Knight Armor set; Cleanrot Spear; Halo Scythe

Wields thrusting spears and scythes that inflict Scarlet Rot, capable of blocking and piercing attacks.

Kindred of Rot (Pests)
Weak to Slash, FireSteal / drop Pest's Glaive

Insectoid worshippers of rot who fire homing Pest Threads from long range.

Shops and people
  • Gowry: Sells Sorceries including Glintstone Stars, Night Shard, and Night Maiden's Mist after progressing his and Millicent's tasks.
  • Nomadic Merchant (Caelid Highway South): Sells Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook [15], Cracked Pots, and Preserving Boluses.

Tips
  • Prepare Preserving Boluses or the "Flame, Cleanse Me" incantation before exploring the Swamp of Aeonia to counter the Scarlet Rot buildup.
  • Riding Torrent makes you immune to Scarlet Rot while traveling over the surface of the rot swamp, but dismounting or being knocked off will instantly expose you to deep buildup.
