Caelid

ELDEN RING guide · Traveling east from Limgrave

Caelid is a blighted wasteland east of Limgrave, devastated and transformed by the Scarlet Rot following the battle between Malenia and General Radahn. Players explore hazardous swamps, ruined towns, and fortified outposts while preparing for the Radahn Festival held at Redmane Castle. Survival requires managing the pervasive Scarlet Rot buildup and handling aggressive, mutated wildlife.

Items

  • Map: Caelid: Found directly along the main road beneath a monolithic stele just south of the Caelid Highway North Site of Grace
  • Meteorite Staff: Looted from a corpse leaning out the window of a ruined tower in the Street of Sages Ruins
  • Rock Sling: Found in an underground cellar chest within the Street of Sages Ruins
  • Icerind Hatchet: Inside a chest found inside a ruined building in the Temple Quarter within the Dragonbarrow border
  • Moonveil: Dropped by the Magma Wyrm boss inside the Gael Tunnel
  • Greatsword: Located inside the back of an abandoned carriage surrounded by Monstrous Dogs along the road in Northwest Caelid
  • Cross-Naginata: Looted from a corpse in a side cavity near giant land squirts inside the Gael Tunnel
  • Radagon's Soreseal: Found on a corpse in the lower wooden scaffolding area of Fort Faroth
  • Dectus Medallion (Right): Located in a chest atop the highest tower of Fort Faroth
  • Gold Scarab: Rewarded for defeating the Cleanrot Knight duo in the Abandoned Cave

Secrets

  • The magic barrier seals around Sellia, Town of Sorcery can be dispelled by lighting three flames atop stone towers scattered around the town rooftops.
  • Approaching the Beast Sanctuary cliffside in the northeast allows you to jump down root branches to find hidden Dragoncrest Shield Talisman and Cinquedea dagger.
  • An illusory wall inside the Sellia Hideaway cave near the graveyard can be broken to reveal Lusat's body and the Stars of Ruin sorcery.

Shops and people

  • Gowry: Sells night-affinity and rot sorceries after progressing Millicent's questline at Gowry's Shack
  • Isolated Merchant (Dragonbarrow): Sells basic supplies, arrows, and unique equipment
  • Nomadic Merchant (Caelid Highway South): Sells standard equipment, bolts, and crafting materials

Tips

  • Keep a supply of Preserving Boluses or the "Flame, Cleanse Me" incantation on hand at all times to cure Scarlet Rot before it drains your flasks.
  • Riding Torrent through the deep waters of the Swamp of Aeonia prevents your character from accumulating Scarlet Rot buildup.
  • Striking the giant Monstrous Dogs and Crows repeatedly on their heads will quickly break their stance for a critical hit.
