Shadow Keep

ELDEN RING guide · The major legacy dungeon in the center of the map
← All areas in this guide

Shadow Keep is a massive, multi-tiered legacy dungeon occupying the center of the Realm of Shadow, serving as the military headquarters of Messmer the Impaler. Players must navigate its main plaza, the flooded Church District, and the towering Specimen Storehouse to reach Messmer's chamber or uncover hidden exits leading to other regions. Exploring its depths and draining the floodwaters reveals crucial lore, cross paths with multiple NPCs, and unlocks access to the Hinterlands and the Ancient Ruins of Rauh.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating the Golden Hippopotamus and Messmer to claim 2x Scadutree Fragments, Messmer's Kindling, and remembrance rewards.
  • •Lowering the floodgates in the Church District to unlock the Prayer Room, two Ulcerated Tree Spirits, and the passage toward Scadutree Base.
  • •Draining the water and utilizing the 'O Mother' gesture at the Back Gate to access the Hinterland, while using the illusory wall coffin to reach Castle Watering Hole / Ruins of Unte, and the western elevator for Ancient Ruins of Rauh.

Common mistakes

  • •Entering Messmer's chamber prematurely; slaying him before finishing NPC quests locks Freyja out of dropping her gear and despawns the bridge duels.
  • •Summoning Hornsent from outside the boss fog; summon him from the sign inside the arena to avoid inflating Messmer's health bar.
  • •Giving the wrong Iris to Fire Knight Queelign in the Prayer Room; the Iris of Grace awards a Spirit Ash, while the Iris of Occultation yields his weapon.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Main Gate Courtyard

    Ride the Main Gate lift up into the courtyard arena and prepare immediately for a sudden charge by the Golden Hippopotamus. After putting down the beast, collect the Aspects of the Crucible: Thorns incantation along with 2x Scadutree Fragments. Rest at the nearby Site of Grace before climbing the high ramparts patrolled by Messmer Soldiers, Vulgar Militia, and Fire Knights to find the Talisman of Lord's Bestowal along the perimeter.

    Key fight: Golden Hippopotamus

    Tip: Roll forward-right through the hippo's bite grabs and apply heavy strike attacks or Bleed to stagger its head.

    Watch out: Do not summon immediately upon entering the arena, as the boss charges instantly.

  2. 2

    High Bridge and Canal Secrets

    Push through the high ramparts to the central bridge lined with burning boats and pyres, checking along the perimeter and side rooms for the Main-gauche and Fire Knight's Seal. Before crossing to the Specimen Storehouse, descend the stone staircase down to the waterway ladder to find the room housing the Domain of Dragons painting, then strike the plain wall to discover an illusory passage with a stone coffin leading down to the Castle Watering Hole. Returning to the high bridge, you will spot duel summon signs involving Needle Knight Leda and Hornsent.

    Tip: Parrying or using guard counters will easily neutralize the aggressive Fire Knights on the battlements.

    Watch out: Choosing to assist Leda against Hornsent will permanently kill him, while ignoring both or helping Hornsent preserves him for a summons inside Messmer's chamber.

  3. 3

    Lower Specimen Storehouse

    Step into the Storehouse, First Floor central hub Site of Grace, grabbing the Dueling Shield off a corpse on the lower level and the Boltdrake Talisman +3 tucked behind a secret corridor. Speak with Sir Ansbach in his study, and note the western elevator that opens the path toward the Ancient Ruins of Rauh. Ascend to the Storehouse, Fourth Floor Site of Grace, where you can grab a Scadutree Fragment from the altar before heading out along the balcony walkways and battlement paths to bypass roosting bats.

    Tip: Sir Ansbach will study the Secret Rite Scroll if you retrieve it for his research.

    Watch out: Delivering the Secret Rite Scroll to Ansbach can trigger a hostile encounter sign with Leda in the library; siding with Leda permanently eliminates Ansbach from later battles.

  4. 4

    Upper Specimen Storehouse and the Dark Chamber

    Continue up the winding paths to the Storehouse, Seventh Floor to find Redmane Freyja standing by a high ring bookcase. Scale toward the upper gantry to pull the Specimen Mechanism Lever, which rotates the hanging beasts so you can leap onto their limbs to claim the Pearldrake Talisman +3. Ride the topmost elevator up to the Dark Chamber Entrance, where you will confront Messmer the Impaler. Defeating him rewards you with the Remembrance of the Impaler and Messmer's Kindling.

    Key fight: Messmer the Impaler

    Tip: If he is still alive, summon Hornsent from inside the boss room floor so Messmer's maximum health pool does not scale up.

    Watch out: Defeating Messmer before finishing the NPC exchanges involving Freyja's Letter will cause Redmane Freyja to vanish without dropping her unique rewards.

  5. 5

    Church District Drainage

    Access the sunken Church District via the Highroad from Bonny Village, navigating across cathedral roofs, buttresses, and broken domes while avoiding exploding Fire Slimes. Head to the elevated walkway atop the floodgates to turn the turnstile drain mechanism, lowering the water levels across the entire district. Drop down into the drained sunken plaza to defeat two Ulcerated Tree Spirits for upgrade fragments and an Iris of Occultation, then head to the locked Prayer Room to use your Prayer Room Key on weeping Fire Knight Queelign. From this district, you can also access the Tree-Worship Passage leading east to the Scadutree Base.

    Tip: Ulcerated Tree Spirits in the drained courtyard have an extreme vulnerability to Fire damage.

    Watch out: Giving Queelign the Iris of Grace grants his Spirit Ash, whereas giving him the Iris of Occultation yields Queelign's Greatsword; each choice locks out the other item for the playthrough.

  6. 6

    Storehouse Loft and the Back Gate

    Ascend through the Storehouse rafters in the back section to find the Carian Thrusting Shield tucked behind a hidden wall/balcony before descending toward the Shadow Keep, Back Gate Site of Grace. Collect the Scadutree Fragment resting beside the statue inside the chamber, and prepare to face Commander Gaius just outside in the courtyard. Before departing the area, approach the headless statue of Marika in the chamber beside the grace and perform the "O Mother" gesture to slide the statue aside and open the path to the Hinterland.

    Key fight: Commander Gaius

    Tip: Use Torrent or defensive skills like Bloodhound's Step to safely iframe Gaius's opening charge hitbox.

    Watch out: You will be blocked from accessing the Hinterland and Shaman Village if you do not use the 'O Mother' gesture at the headless statue.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Needle Knight Leda vs. Hornsent Duel

On the elevated bridge leading to the Specimen Storehouse prior to defeating Messmer

  • Assist Hornsent (Gold Sign)→ Leda retreats, granting Ash of War: Swift Slash and Leda's Rune; Hornsent remains alive and can be summoned inside Messmer's arena
  • Assist Leda (Red Sign)→ Hornsent is permanently slain, dropping the Falx, Caterpillar Mask, and Braided Cord Robe; Leda later gives you the Lacerating Crossed-Tree Talisman at Highroad Cross and Hornsent's subsequent encounters are canceled
  • Ignore Both Signs→ The duel summon signs vanish once Messmer is defeated; neither sign's exclusive rewards are obtained, but both NPCs survive for later events

Defeating Messmer the Impaler removes both summon signs permanently.

Delivering Ansbach's Letter to Freyja

On the Storehouse Seventh Floor after finding the Secret Rite Scroll and consulting Ansbach

  • Hand over the letter to Freyja→ Freyja rewards you with the Golden Lion Shield and progresses her storyline, enabling her weapon and armor set to be acquired later
  • Keep the letter or defeat Messmer before delivery→ Freyja departs without completing her narrative, permanently missing out on the Golden Lion Shield for the current playthrough

Most players pick: Hand over the letter to Freyja

Slaying Messmer prematurely or failing to pass on the letter causes Freyja to disappear.

Needle Knight Leda vs. Sir Ansbach Duel

In the First Floor Storehouse room after handing over the Secret Rite Scroll, provided Hornsent is alive or was not targeted

  • Help Sir Ansbach (Gold Sign)→ Leda is driven off, rewarding Leda's Rune and Ansbach's Longbow while keeping Ansbach alive for endgame battles
  • Help Leda (Red Sign)→ Ansbach is killed, yielding the Wise Man's Mask, his armor set, and Ansbach's Longbow; Leda awards the Retaliatory Crossed-Tree Talisman, but Ansbach is removed from future fights

Most players pick: Help Sir Ansbach (Gold Sign)

This confrontation only manifests if Hornsent survived or was spared from Leda's earlier pursuit.

Fate of Fire Knight Queelign

In the sunken Church District's Prayer Room, opened with the Prayer Room Key

  • Offer the Iris of Grace→ Queelign becomes the Fire Knight Queelign Spirit Ash, forfeiting Queelign's Greatsword for this playthrough
  • Offer the Iris of Occultation→ Queelign perishes and yields Queelign's Greatsword, preventing you from obtaining his Spirit Ash

Unlocking the chamber requires obtaining the Prayer Room Key by defeating Queelign's invasion at the Church of the Crusade.

Key fights
Golden Hippopotamus

Enemies: Golden Hippopotamus

Threats: Massive charging bite attacks, needle rain, rolling bodyslams, and Crucible light thorns

Weaknesses and resistances: Susceptible to Hemorrhage, Frostbite, and Fire; head takes increased poise and physical damage

Tactics: Stay near its flank rather than straight ahead to avoid the unblockable grab bite; dodge into the charges and attack its head during recovery

Rewards: 2 Scadutree Fragments, Aspects of the Crucible: Thorns

Messmer the Impaler

Enemies: Messmer the Impaler

Threats: High-speed spear thrusts, delayed sweeping flame explosions, unblockable grab attack, and transformation into giant striking abyss serpents

Weaknesses and resistances: Susceptible to Frostbite, Bleed, and Slash damage; highly resistant to Fire

Tactics: Roll inward through his aerial diving attacks to punish lingering recovery windows, stay calm during multi-serpent strikes by dodging sideways, and use flame-resistant talismans

Rewards: Remembrance of the Impaler, Messmer's Kindling

Commander Gaius

Enemies: Commander Gaius

Threats: High-speed boar charges, gravity lance thrusts, spinning gravity blade waves, and airborne dive attacks

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Holy and Fire damage; resistant to Magic and Gravity

Tactics: Position slightly to the side to avoid his tight charge hitbox, roll through lance swings toward his back leg, and punish the downtime following his aerial slam

Rewards: Remembrance of the Wild Boar Rider

Tips
  • Approaching the entrance of Shadow Keep shatters Miquella's Great Rune, triggering dialogue and advancing several major NPC questlines across the realm.
  • Equipping the Flamedrake Talisman +3 and consuming Fireproof Dried Liver greatly mitigates the heavy Fire damage dealt throughout the higher floors and Messmer's chamber.
