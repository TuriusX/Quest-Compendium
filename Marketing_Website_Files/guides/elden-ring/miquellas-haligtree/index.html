Miquella's Haligtree

ELDEN RING guide · Descending the massive tree structure

This late-game legacy dungeon begins as you navigate high, narrow branches of an enormous sacred tree while dodging ranged attacks from canopy dwellers. As you descend toward Haligtree Town, you navigate smaller wooden platforms, rooftops, and prayer rooms guarded by Misbegotten and Rotten Cleanrot Knights. Proceeding to the bottom of the structure leads you to the boss Lorelai, who guards the path downward into Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.

Items

  • Oracle Envoy Ashes: Looted from a corpse on a thin branch hanging off the side of the upper canopy
  • Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone: Looted from a corpse near a statue at Haligtree Town Plaza
  • Pearldrake Talisman +2: Found on a corpse tucked away on a dead-end branch high in the canopy
  • Loretta's Mastery: Dropped upon defeating the boss Royal Knight Loretta

Secrets

  • The platform elevator shortcut near the Haligtree Town Grace can be activated by climbing to the top of the tower on the walkway leading toward the town's lower areas.

Tips

  • Use a ranged weapon or Greatbow to knock dangerous snipers off high branches from a safe distance before crossing.
  • Equipping items that boost Holy damage negation substantially reduces the damage taken from Oracle Envoy bubble barrages.
