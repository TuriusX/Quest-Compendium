Miquella's Haligtree

ELDEN RING guide · The legacy dungeon accessed via the Consecrated Snowfield
← All areas in this guide

Miquella's Haligtree is a massive endgame legacy dungeon accessed by breaking the seal at Ordina, Liturgical Town. Players start on high, narrow tree branches under heavy ranged bombardment before descending into the canopy settlement of Haligtree Town. Progressing through the town and defeating Loretta opens the path downwards into the fortified brace, Elphael.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree to unlock the path and elevator leading down into Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.
  • •Collecting two Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones—one by the Leonine Misbegotten gazebo near Haligtree Town Plaza, and one atop the roof above the descent lift.
  • •Upgrading your survivability and stamina with the Pearldrake Talisman +2 and Viridian Amber Medallion +2.

Common mistakes

  • •Running across the canopy branches while under fire; eliminate bubble-blowing Envoys from range with spells or a bow to prevent lethal knockbacks.
  • •Rushing into the pavilion before the boss arena; take down the Battlemages from stealth or interrupt their spell windups before they cast Cannon of Haima.

What you can skip

  • •Clashing repeatedly with the Giant Oracle Envoy at the canopy's eastern tips once you have retrieved the Envoy Crown.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Haligtree Canopy Branches

    When you arrive at the Haligtree Canopy Site of Grace after teleporting from Ordina, Liturgical Town, drop down along the high, winding limbs suspended in the sky. Pick up Prattling Pate "My Beloved" along the path, and look out for the Envoy Crown on a corpse at the tip of a narrow branch patrolled by a Giant Oracle Envoy. You can carefully drop to an isolated northern branch to grab the Oracle Envoy Ashes from a corpse guarded by clusters of Envoys.

    Tip: Stack Holy damage negation and pick off the Envoys with ranged arrows or spells from afar before stepping onto narrow limbs.

    Watch out: The Giant Oracle Envoy fires holy bubble barrages that can blast you clean off the branches to your death.

  2. 2

    Transition to Haligtree Town

    Continue descending the large branches past regular and flying Giant Ants toward the western edge to find a ladder. Jump from a branch drop onto a solitary high platform near the town transition to claim the Pearldrake Talisman +2. Climb down the ladder into the settlement architecture to reach the Haligtree Town Site of Grace.

    Tip: Use fire damage or guard counters against the Giant Ants to break their stance immediately.

    Watch out: Rushing through this path without baiting ants one at a time can cause you to get shoved off the edge by their sudden lunges.

  3. 3

    Haligtree Town Settlement

    Walk through the residential sector built directly into the trunk, traversing connected walkways, ladders, and rooftops. Loot Stonesword Keys from corpses and balconies, grab a Lost Ashes of War from a ledge overlooking the rooftops, and gather assorted Smithing Stones and Somber Smithing Stones from rooftop corpses and Misbegotten nests. Proceed until you reach the Haligtree Town Plaza Site of Grace.

    Tip: Use heavy poise-breaking weapons, shield counters, or fire damage against the aggressive Misbegotten packs.

  4. 4

    Haligtree Town Plaza and Plaza Pavilion

    From the Haligtree Town Plaza Site of Grace, navigate the rooftops above a rot-sprouting plaza crawling with Miranda Sprouts and snails. Jump across the rooftops to reach a statue gazebo guarded by a Leonine Misbegotten to pick up an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone, and open the chest in an inner chamber guarded by Miranda Sprouts and a Spirit-Calling Snail to acquire the Viridian Amber Medallion +2. Activate the shortcut elevator that connects the lower plaza bridge back up to the Haligtree Town Site of Grace, then advance toward the pavilion bridge guarded by a Leonine Misbegotten and Battlemages.

    Tip: Rush the Battlemages quickly to backstab them or interrupt their heavy spells like Gavel of Haima and Cannon of Haima.

  5. 5

    Haligtree Promenade

    Cross the bridge to enter the wide circular stone arena of the Haligtree Promenade to confront Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree. After defeating her to earn Loretta's War Sickle and Loretta's Mastery, rest at the Haligtree Promenade Site of Grace. Before riding the elevator down to Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree, take the stairs to the right to locate a second Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone on the roof above the lift chamber.

    Key fight: Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree

    Tip: Stick close to Loretta to bait her halberd attacks and dodge toward her right whenever she pulls her bow for Loretta's Mastery.

    Watch out: Missing the stairs to the right before riding the lift will cause you to overlook an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.

Millicent's Fate

After defeating the Putrid Tree Spirit near the Drainage Channel

  • Golden Summon Sign→ Co-op with Millicent against her four sisters to earn the Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, then talk to her and reload the area for the Unalloyed Gold Needle (which can be traded at Malenia's bloom for Miquella's Needle and a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone)
  • Red Summon Sign→ Invade and slay Millicent to earn Millicent's Prosthesis, locking yourself out of Miquella's Needle and the Frenzied Flame cure

Most players pick: Golden Summon Sign

Siding against Millicent permanently forfeits Miquella's Needle and the ability to reverse the Frenzied Flame.

Key fights
Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree

Enemies: Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree

Threats: Casts Loretta's Mastery, Glintblade Phalanx, and charges with a magic-infused polearm

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Pierce damage and Lightning; resistant to Magic

Tactics: Fight at medium-close range to bait melee swings, roll through magic greatbow volleys, and punish her post-swing recovery

Rewards: Loretta's Mastery, Loretta's War Sickle, 200,000 Runes

Enemies
Giant Envoy Oracle
Weak to Fire, HolySteal / drop Envoy's Greathorn

Sits on high branches and casts large, homing bubble salvos that deal heavy damage and knock players off ledges.

Leonine Misbegotten

Highly aggressive melee fighter found in the lower town rooftops.

Battlemage

Haligtree Soldier
Weak to Fire, LightningSteal / drop Haligtree Crest Surcoat

Standard infantry patrolling the settlement walkways and rooftops.

Tips
  • Equip the Spelldrake Talisman and Haligdrake Talisman to significantly reduce damage from high-power bubble barrages and magical snipers along the narrow canopy branches.
