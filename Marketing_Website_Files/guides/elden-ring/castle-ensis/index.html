Castle Ensis

ELDEN RING guide · The legacy dungeon guarding the path to the north ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Castle Ensis is a fortified stronghold spanning the chasm between Gravesite Plain and Scadu Altus. Players must push across fortified bridges, navigate inner barracks, and defeat Glintstone sorcerers and Carian knights to open the main highway north. The fortress culminates in a confrontation with Rellana, Twin Moon Knight, in the castle's highest sanctuary.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Outer Moat & Entry Bridge

    Before crossing the main bridge toward the courtyard, drop down the ledges beneath its left flank to loot a hidden storeroom. Return up and cross the bridge, eliminating the patrolling Carian Troll Knight in the open entryway.

    Tip: Strike the Carian Troll Knight's head or legs with heavy attacks to break its poise for a riposte.

  2. 2

    Lower Courtyard & Storeroom Ramparts

    Clear out the Messmer Soldiers and Noble Sorcerers occupying the courtyard, making sure to check the small right barracks room for runes. Ascend the outer wooden ramparts and follow the watchtower stairs to loot a chest containing the light greatsword Milady.

    Tip: Watch out for sorcery projectiles while ascending the open wooden platforms.

  3. 3

    The Cave Path & Fortified Incline

    Take the natural cave tunnel to circumvent the locked main portcullis. When you emerge onto the rocky slope, push past the Messmer Foot Soldiers and war hounds while avoiding trades with a roaming Lesser Black Knight.

    Tip: Dodge behind the Lesser Black Knight during his heavy swing recoveries to land a backstab.

  4. 4

    Castle Ensis Checkpoint Shortcut

    Rest at the Castle Ensis Checkpoint Site of Grace and grab the Scadutree Fragment resting directly beside Miquella's Cross. Jump down along the cliff ledge near the waterfall to reach and pull the lever that raises the locked portcullis shortcut.

    Watch out: Ensure you open the portcullis shortcut now so you do not have to repeat the cave bypass if you perish later.

  5. 5

    Upper Ramparts & Barracks

    Head up the fortified stone ramp and duck into the side barracks room to defeat the soldiers and a Black Knight guarding a chest with the Loyal Knight's Cookbook. Continue along the upper stone walkway, cutting down Noble Sorcerers as you advance.

    Tip: Use guard counters or lightning damage to dispatch the barracks Black Knight quickly.

  6. 6

    The Courtyard Garden & Chapel

    Defeat the hostile NPC Moonrithyll, Carian Knight in the garden, who drops Moonrithyll's Knight Sword upon death. Step into the chapel behind her to pillage the Wolf Crest Shield from a corpse and open the altar room chest for Rellana's Cameo.

    Tip: Roll through Moonrithyll's Glintblade Phalanxes and punish her colossus smashes with backstabs.

  7. 7

    Rooftop Detours & Secrets

    Leap from the balcony and cliff path outside the chapel down onto the lower battlements to claim the Glintblade Trio spell in a storeroom. Climb the ladder paths to a raised guardhouse tower chest for an Imbued Sword Key, then navigate the outer waterfall tower ledges to grab Ash of War: Wing Stance.

    Tip: Keep this Imbued Sword Key for the Belfry behind the castle if you want an early warp to the Ancient Ruins of Rauh.

    Watch out: Misjudging the drop-downs onto the lower battlement roofs will send you falling to your death.

  8. 8

    Castle-Lord's Chamber Preparation

    Fight through the main hall past a Black Knight to reach the Castle-Lord's Chamber Site of Grace. Outside the mist wall, you can activate the golden summon signs for Needle Knight Leda and Dryleaf Dane if you want co-op support.

    Tip: Equip the Spelldrake Talisman +3 before passing through the mist to mitigate incoming magic barrage damage.

    Watch out: Summoning Leda or Dane will increase the boss's total health pool.

  9. 9

    Ensis Moongazing Grounds

    Step through the fog to battle Rellana, Twin Moon Knight, and defeat her to earn 240,000 Runes alongside the Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight. Once the master of the castle is slain, exit out the rear archway into Highroad Cross in Scadu Altus.

    Key fight: Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

    Tip: Jump over both consecutive moon impacts and the finishing ground blast during her Phase 2 Twin Moons attack.

    Watch out: Pushing directly past the arena into Scadu Altus triggers the breaking of Miquella's Great Rune, which prematurely advances ongoing NPC dialogue steps for Freyja, Moore, and Hornsent.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Waygate Destination Choice (Ancient Ruins of Rauh)

At the Belfry located behind Castle Ensis

  • Use the Imbued Sword Key on the waygate→ You are teleported to an isolated ledge in the Ancient Ruins of Rauh to acquire the Carian Sorcery Sword and a Talisman
  • Save the Imbued Sword Key→ You keep the key for another Belfry elsewhere, delaying acquisition of the Carian Sorcery Sword

Most players pick: Use the Imbued Sword Key on the waygate

Summoning NPCs for Rellana

Outside the fog gate at the Castle-Lord’s Chamber Site of Grace

  • Summon Needle Knight Leda and/or Dryleaf Dane→ You receive allied help in combat and added dialogue flair, but the boss's total health increases
  • Do not summon either NPC→ You face Rellana solo without increasing her maximum health pool

Key fights
Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

Enemies: Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

Threats: Dual-wields light and fire swords, casts Twin Moons shockwaves, Moonblade slashes, and rapid sword combos

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Pierce and Lightning; resistant to Magic and Fire

Tactics: Jump over the shockwaves of her Twin Moons attack, dodge inward on her sweeping dual-blade combos, and punish during recovery windows

Rewards: Remembrance of the Twin Moon Knight; 240,000 Runes

Enemies
Black Knight
Weak to Lightning, PierceSteal / drop Black Steel Greatshield

Tough knight wielding heavy weapons; parrying or poise-breaking is effective.

Carian Sorcerer
Weak to Physical, MagicSteal / drop Glintstone Staff

Fires Glintstone projectiles from range; rush down quickly to interrupt spellcasting.

Giant Manda
Weak to Fire

Lurks in the watery moat below the castle ramparts; deal with it using fire-based attacks.

Tips
  • The Twin Moons spell cast by Rellana hits three consecutive times with expanding shockwaves; timing your jumps instead of rolling is the most reliable way to avoid taking massive magic damage.
