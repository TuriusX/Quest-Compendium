Ainsel River

ELDEN RING guide · An underground region accessible via Liurnia
← All areas in this guide

Ainsel River is a cavernous underground region reached via a deep elevator in eastern Liurnia of the Lakes. It features flooded caverns infested with Giant Ants, ruined Uhl Palace architecture, and an encounter with a Dragonkin Soldier. Navigating through the ruins leads toward the Lake of Rot, while the upper Ainsel River Main branch connects to Nokstella, Eternal City.

The short version

What matters

  • •Map: Ainsel River found on a corpse inside the pillared ruin behind the Malformed Star, next to the Hermit Merchant.
  • •Great Ghost Glovewort stored inside a chest beneath the titan throne after besting the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella.
  • •Miniature Ranni retrieved from the open stone coffin beside the Ainsel River Main Site of Grace to progress Ranni's questline.
  • •Wing of Astel curved sword located inside a chest along the high stone archway of the Uhl Palace Ruins upper terrace.

Common mistakes

  • •Ignoring the prompt to speak with the Miniature Ranni at Sites of Grace; you must attempt conversation three times in a row to break her silence and prevent halting her entire questline.
  • •Attacking Giant Ants directly on their armored headshields; roll past their charges to exploit their fire weakness on their unprotected abdomens.
  • •Hitting Claymen with pierce or slash weapons; switch to strike damage with fire or lightning to overcome their high physical defenses.

What you can skip

  • •Meleeing the Malformed Star while under heavy fire in the lower Uhl Palace Ruins, as you can bypass it or safely dispatch it later from the upper terrace.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Elevator Drop-Off into Ant Tunnels

    When you descend the elevator via the Ainsel River Well in eastern Liurnia, rest at the Ainsel River Well Depths Site of Grace before pressing into the winding cavern system ahead. Then, clear a path through the aggressive Giant Ants and Flying Ants that nest throughout these corridors. Before pushing forward, navigate the branching tunnels near the Uhl Palace Ruins to find a chest holding the Ant's Skull Plate greatshield.

    Tip: Roll past the front charges of the ants to strike their unarmored, fleshy rears with fire damage, which causes them to panic.

  2. 2

    Navigating Sluice Gate to the Sunken Ruins

    When you reach the stone ramparts overlooking the cavern floor, activate the Ainsel River Sluice Gate Site of Grace. Then, carefully advance into the expansive sunken courtyard of the Uhl Palace Ruins lower floor, taking cover behind massive stone pillars from the hanging Malformed Star that snipes at you with gravity rocks and laser beams. Before leaving this lower hall, slip into the pillared ruin directly beneath and behind the monster to locate the Hermit Merchant, the Map: Ainsel River on a corpse, and climb the elevated ruined walls to loot Celestial Dew.

    Tip: Sprint between stone pillars to avoid the Malformed Star's gravity barrages, or pick it off from range by targeting its weak head.

  3. 3

    Downstream Tunnels and Queen Ant Lair

    When you drop down into the lower watery cave past the merchant and the Malformed Star, tag the Ainsel River Downstream Site of Grace. Then, investigate the side passages branching away from the riverbed, dealing with lurking Basilisks and ant nests. Before heading toward the massive ruin hall, search a corpse within these southern ant tunnels to obtain the Immunizing Horn Charm.

    Tip: Equip a Beast-Repellent Torch to prevent deadly Basilisks from spraying their instant Death Blight clouds.

  4. 4

    Slaying the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella

    When you arrive at the massive cavern hall containing the artificial titan remains upon an underground throne, prepare to face the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella. Then, bring down the behemoth to claim 12,000 Runes and the Frozen Lightning Spear Incantation. Before leaving the arena, open the chest hidden directly beneath the giant skeletal throne to claim a Great Ghost Glovewort.

    Key fight: Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella

    Tip: Stick close to the boss's pelvis and strike its weak head with slash damage in Phase 1, then roll forward into its wing dive in Phase 2 while watching out for delayed frozen lightning discharges on the ground.

  5. 5

    Entering Ainsel River Main and Nokstella Path

    When you teleport into the northern section using the Waygate in Renna’s Rise or the stone coffin from Deeproot Depths, rest at the Ainsel River Main Site of Grace and immediately loot the open stone coffin right in front of you for the Miniature Ranni. Then, advance down the flooded upper riverbed through patrols of Claymen, navigating the high upper terraces of the Uhl Palace Ruins to loot a chest on the high stone arch for the Wing of Astel. Before continuing west down the water toward the staircases of Nokstella, Eternal City, sit back down at the Site of Grace to commune with your doll.

    Tip: Use strike damage alongside fire or lightning against the Claymen, as they heavily resist slash and pierce damage.

    Watch out: Bypassing speaking to Miniature Ranni three consecutive times at the Site of Grace halts her quest progression toward the Baleful Shadow and the Lake of Rot.

Resting at a Site of Grace after looting the doll at Ainsel River Main

  • Ignore the doll→ Halts progression on Ranni's storyline
  • Try to converse repeatedly→ The doll remains silent on the first two attempts, then speaks on the third try, orders you to defeat the Baleful Shadow, reveals further dialogue downstream, and continues Ranni's quest toward the Lake of Rot

Key fights
Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella

Enemies: Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella

Threats: sweeping ground slams, claw swipes, charging attacks, and a second-phase transformation featuring blue frozen lightning strikes

Weaknesses and resistances: Strike damage, Pierce damage to the head; highly resistant to Poison and Scarlet Rot

Tactics: Stay beneath or behind its legs during phase one to bait claw strikes that miss; in phase two, retreat when it charges up frozen lightning ground slams and strike its head after it lands

Rewards: Frozen Lightning Spear, 12,000 Runes

Shops and people

Tips
  • Giant Ants can be reliably staggered and knocked out of animations using Fire-infused weapons, Fire Grease, or fire-based spells.
  • Bring Preserving Boluses or the Flame, Cleanse Me incantation when descending further into the southern caverns where Basilisks lurk, as they cause instant Death Blight.
