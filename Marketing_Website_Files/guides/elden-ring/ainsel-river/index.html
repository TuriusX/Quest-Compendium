Ainsel River

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the underground via the Ainsel River Well

The Ainsel River is an underground region accessed initially through the Ainsel River Well in eastern Liurnia. Explorers must navigate through flooded tunnels filled with Giant Ants and ruins inhabited by Claymen to reach the Uhl Palace Ruins. The southern path concludes with a confrontation against a Dragonkin Soldier in a massive submerged chamber.

Items

  • Map: Ainsel River: Inside the Uhl Palace Ruins, found on a corpse resting inside the stone pavilion beneath the giant hanging Malformed Star
  • Immunizing Horn Charm: Looted from a corpse found inside the ant nest chambers in the northern tunnels
  • Celestial Dew: Found on a corpse sitting on the ledge of the ruins overlooking the water in the Uhl Palace Ruins
  • Smithing Stone [4]: Found on several corpses scattered throughout the flooded ruins and ant tunnels
  • Ghost Glovewort [1]: Picked from various spots along the riverbanks near the Ainsel River Well Depths site of grace
  • Frozen Lightning Spear: Dropped immediately upon defeating the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella

Secrets

  • Reaching the upper platforms above the Uhl Palace Ruins requires navigating behind the giant Malformed Star through a hidden doorway along the right wall of the ruin chamber.
  • A giant chest sits behind the throne in the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella arena, containing the Great Ghost Glovewort.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Hermit Merchant: Sells Celestial Dew, Fan Daggers, Gravity Stone Chunks, and the Prisoner set

Tips

  • Bring Fire Grease or fire-based spells to easily dispatch the Giant Ants throughout the initial tunnels.
  • Wear armor or use talismans that boost Robustness to endure the frostbite attacks during the boss fight against the Dragonkin Soldier of Nokstella.
  • The northern section of the Ainsel River (Ainsel River Main) is walled off from this lower route and must be entered later via a Waygate in Renna's Rise.
