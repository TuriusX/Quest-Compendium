Lake of Rot

ELDEN RING guide · An underground area accessed via the Ainsel River questline ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

The Lake of Rot is an underground subterranean basin heavily contaminated with Scarlet Rot that must be traversed to progress Ranni the Witch's questline. Players must raise stone platforms using floor switches across the lake to avoid rotting before reaching the Grand Cloister and descending into Astel's arena.

The short version

What matters

  • •Stepping on the obelisk pressure plates to bring up dry stone platforms from the scarlet fluid.
  • •Opening the chest in the Grand Cloister's lower temple to acquire the Scorpion's Stinger.
  • •Climbing into the stone coffin at the Grand Cloister waterfall to reach Astel, Naturalborn of the Void for Ranni's questline.

Common mistakes

  • •Engaging the Dragonkin Soldier directly in the fluid instead of raising the nearby floor switches first to create dry footing.
  • •Skipping the corpse directly south of the Lake of Rot Shoreside Site of Grace and traversing the basin without the area map.

What you can skip

  • •Fighting the Dragonkin Soldier, Alabaster Lord, and Ulcerated Tree Spirit if you only want to sprint through the switches to the coffin.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Shoreside Arrival

    Descend from Nokstella Waterfall Basin via the lift after dispatching the Baleful Shadow, then rest at the Lake of Rot Shoreside Site of Grace. Head directly south to find a corpse at the water's edge to secure the map fragment. Southeast of this site in the eastern shallows, you can also defeat a lone Ancestral Follower to pick up the Immunizing Horn Charm +1.

    Tip: Loot the corpse on the bank before setting foot in the liquid to easily navigate the cavern.

  2. 2

    Traversing the Scarlet Lake

    Step into the caustic fluid and press the floor pressure switches atop the stone obelisks to raise dry walking platforms across the lake. Head into the southwestern ruins to locate a chest inside an alcove holding the Nomadic Warrior’s Cookbook . Triggering the southwestern switch will also raise an elevated ruin where you can fight an Alabaster Lord to claim the Alabaster Lord’s Sword.

    Tip: Focus the Alabaster Lord with heavy physical hits or backstabs to easily break his low poise.

    Watch out: Wandering the deep liquid without triggering switches will leave you stranded in the scarlet rot without solid ground.

  3. 3

    The Central Basin Ambush

    Move south toward the middle of the lake where a dormant Dragonkin Soldier sits resting in the scarlet waters. Hit the nearby floor switches before starting combat so you have raised platforms to fight on instead of standing in the damaging rot. Slay this behemoth by targeting its vulnerable head to walk away with a hefty rune reward and its signature katana.

    Key fight: Dragonkin Soldier

    Tip: Aim for the head to deal 20% bonus damage and stagger the beast at 120 stance health.

    Watch out: Rushing straight into combat without stepping on the nearby switches will force you to fight while constantly suffering rot buildup.

  4. 4

    Crossing the Southern Ruins

    Navigate toward the southern portion of the lake, jumping across ruined columns to reach a high stone pillar. Plunder the remains resting on this pillar to secure the Mushroom Crown. Continue past the columns to reach the monumental staircase leading down into the subterranean Grand Cloister.

    Tip: Leap carefully across the broken ruin columns to reach the elevated corpse without falling back into the lake.

  5. 5

    The Grand Cloister Complex

    Descend the grand staircase into the submerged temple complex below. Fight the Ulcerated Tree Spirit in the northern basin near the waterfall to obtain a Golden Seed, and loot the ornate chest on the ground floor altar beneath the gathering Pests to acquire the Scorpion’s Stinger. Follow the liquid down to the waterfall's edge where you will find an open stone coffin.

    Tip: Bring fire-based attacks to melt the Ulcerated Tree Spirit quickly near the staircase.

    Watch out: Rushing the ground floor chest recklessly will cause the surrounding Pests to overwhelm you.

  6. 6

    The Coffin Precipice

    Approach the open stone coffin sitting directly on the precipice overlooking the abyss. Climb inside the coffin to view a cutscene that carries you downward into the void. Once you land, step into the arena to confront Astel, Naturalborn of the Void to further Ranni the Witch's questline.

    Key fight: Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

    Tip: Rest and replenish your supplies at the nearby Site of Grace before climbing into the coffin.

    Watch out: Resting inside the coffin immediately transports you into the boss area below, so ensure you have explored the Cloister fully first.

Engaging the Lake of Rot Optional Bosses

While navigating the lake and the Grand Cloister

  • Fight the Dragonkin Soldier, Alabaster Lord, and Ulcerated Tree Spirit→ Defeat them to obtain the Dragonscale Blade, 58,000 Runes, the Alabaster Lord’s Sword, a Golden Seed, and 15,000 Runes
  • Bypass the bosses by sprinting across the switches to the Grand Cloister→ Proceed directly through the area without receiving these weapons, runes, or upgrade items

Most players pick: Fight the Dragonkin Soldier, Alabaster Lord, and Ulcerated Tree Spirit

Entering the Waterfall Stone Coffin

At the waterfall edge inside the Grand Cloister

  • Climb inside the coffin→ Triggers a cutscene transporting you down into the void to fight Astel, Naturalborn of the Void, enabling you to progress Ranni's questline and reach the Moonlight Altar
  • Do not enter the coffin→ Remain in the Grand Cloister and postpone the battle with Astel and Ranni's quest progression

Most players pick: Climb inside the coffin

Point of no return for this specific pathway until you defeat Astel or travel back via the map

Key fights
Dragonkin Soldier

Enemies: Dragonkin Soldier

Threats: Massive reach, sweeping arm swipes, ground slams

Weaknesses and resistances: Slash damage

Tactics: Raise the platforms surrounding it using pressure plates to create solid footing, then attack its legs while avoiding rot exposure

Rewards: Dragonscale Blade

Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

Enemies: Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

Threats: Gravity waves, meteor shower, massive command grab, tail whips

Weaknesses and resistances: Magic resistant; weak to Standard, Strike, and Slash on the head

Tactics: Stay focused on attacking its head, dodge toward it during gravity bursts, and immediately sprint sideways when it channels the meteor volley

Rewards: Remembrance of the Naturalborn

Enemies

Weak to Holy

Kindred of Rot
Weak to Fire, Physical

Attack with homing pest threads and polearms; guard the Grand Cloister.

Tips
  • Equip the "Flame, Cleanse Me" incantation and stock up on Preserving Boluses to quickly purge Scarlet Rot buildup when crossing the liquid.
  • Resting in the stone coffin at the edge of the waterfall in the Grand Cloister will transport you to the arena of Astel, Naturalborn of the Void.
