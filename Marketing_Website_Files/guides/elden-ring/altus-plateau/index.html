Altus Plateau

ELDEN RING guide · Reaching the plateau via the Grand Lift of Dectus

After riding the Grand Lift of Dectus with the two Dectus Medallion halves, the player enters the central golden highlands of the Lands Between. The region serves as the primary gateway toward Leyndell, Royal Capital, as well as Mount Gelmir and the Shaded Castle to the north. Players navigate expansive military encampments, highway crossroads, and scattered ruins while advancing the regional quests of various NPCs.

Items

  • Golden Seed: Found at an illusory golden tree along the main road directly northeast of the Grand Lift of Dectus
  • Map: Altus Plateau: Found on a stone monolith along the northern road leading into the plateau just past the Altus Highway Junction Site of Grace
  • Golden Seed: Found at the base of a phantom tree beside the roadway just north of the Altus Highway Junction Site of Grace

Secrets

  • Illusory wall at the back of the first room on the right in the Perfumer's Grotto dungeon, leading deeper into the cavern system.

Tips

  • Resting at any Site of Grace on the Altus Plateau triggers the start of the Radahn Festival in Redmane Castle down in Caelid.
  • Strike the stairs and walls in the Sealed Tunnel southwest of the outer capital moat to uncover a path down to the Smithing-Stone Miner's Bell Bearing [2].
