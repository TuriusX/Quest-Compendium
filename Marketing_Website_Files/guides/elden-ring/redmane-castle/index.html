Redmane Castle

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the Caelid legacy dungeon

Redmane Castle is a heavily fortified stronghold located on an island in southeastern Caelid, serving as the seat of General Radahn. The state of the castle changes dramatically depending on whether the Radahn Festival has been triggered, which clears out regular enemies and replaces an earlier duo boss encounter with festival attendees. Players visit to defeat Starscourge Radahn to progress the main story and unlock underground areas, as well as clear out the castle outside the festival to claim various combat items.

Items

  • Red-Hot Whetblade: Inside a room guarded by an Iron Virgin, accessible when the festival is inactive
  • Flamberge: Found on a corpse on a balcony near the Pumpkin Head in the central courtyard
  • Armorer's Cookbook [5]: Found inside a room in the castle near the entrance ramparts
  • Ash of War: Flaming Strike: Dropped by a Teardrop Scarab in the graveyard on the cliff path outside the rear castle walls
  • Ruins Greatsword: Dropped by the Misbegotten Warrior and Crucible Knight duo boss
  • Remembrance of the Starscourge: Dropped upon defeating Starscourge Radahn in the Wailing Dunes

Secrets

  • When the Radahn Festival is active, the Misbegotten Warrior and Crucible Knight boss fight in the castle plaza disappears entirely, but it can be restored and fought later by exhausting the dialogue of Witch-Hunter Jerren and resetting the area at a Site of Grace.

Shops and people

  • Witch-Hunter Jerren: Does not sell items, but coordinates the Radahn Festival and acts as the announcer

Tips

  • You can trigger the Radahn Festival early either by activating any Site of Grace on the Altus Plateau or by progressing Ranni the Witch's questline to the point where Blaidd directs you there.
  • During the battle against Starscourge Radahn, you can repeatedly re-summon fallen NPC allies using summon signs scattered across the battlefield as long as the fight continues.
