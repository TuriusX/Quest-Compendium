Gravesite Plain

ELDEN RING guide · The starting region of the Shadow of the Erdtree expansion
← All areas in this guide

Gravesite Plain is the initial open-world zone of the Realm of Shadow where the Tarnished awakens after interacting with the withered arm at Mohgwyn Palace. Players explore ghostflame-ridden fields, find initial cross sites left by Miquella, and seek passage toward Belurat, Tower Settlement or east across the Ellac Greatbridge to Scadu Altus. Key goals include collecting Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ashes to raise the Realm of Shadow blessing level before pushing deeper into major legacy dungeons.

The short version

What matters

  • •Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ashes scattered across roadside crosses and shrines to upgrade your Realm of Shadow blessings.
  • •High-tier early armaments including the Greatsword of Solitude, Backhand Blade, Great Katana, and Beast Claw.
  • •Speaking to Hornsent at Three-Path Cross for the Cross Map to easily track down Miquella's footprints.

Common mistakes

  • •Venturing far into Scadu Altus or nearing Shadow Keep prematurely, which breaks a great rune and locks Moore and Thiollier out of the Black Syrup exchange.
  • •Attempting to trade hits with the Blackgaol Knight instead of using launch skills or guard-counters to exploit his poise vulnerabilities.

What you can skip

  • •Taking on the Ghostflame Dragon or the Furnace Golem immediately if you lack holy damage, strike weapons, or Hefty Fire Pots.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Awakening and Surveying the Plain

    After interacting with Miquella's withered arm at the Cocoon of the Empyrean, you emerge from a subterranean cave through an arch into the open wheat fields to tag the Gravesite Plain Site of Grace. Head northeast along the main path to grab the Map: Gravesite Plain from the roadside stele, then detour into the woods to the southeast to slay Logur, the Beast Claw for his Beast Claw weapon. Across the open field, you will spot a towering Furnace Golem roaming the central plains.

    Key fight: Furnace Golem (Gravesite Plain)

    Tip: When fighting the Furnace Golem, stay on Torrent to jump over its foot-stomp fire shockwaves, staggered hits to its legs will eventually bring it down for a massive critical hit on its faceplate.

  2. 2

    Scorched Ruins and Southern Shrines

    Push northeast from your starting point to explore the Scorched Ruins, using Torrent to leap onto the rooftops to grab the Blade of Mercy from the highest tower chest, and inspecting the eastern cellar for "The Sacred Tower" Painting. Grab the Revered Spirit Ash from the corpse resting on a stone frame along the nearby cliffs, and collect the Backhand Blade from the small sarcophagus shrine in the open forest to the northeast. Follow the southern valley road down toward the cliffs to reach the Church of Consolation, where you must defeat a Black Knight wielding the Black Steel Greathammer to secure two Scadutree Fragments before the beheaded Marika statue.

  3. 3

    Western Cliffs and the Solitude Gauntlet

    Ride into the far northwest meadow near the cliffs to uncover the Western Nameless Mausoleum. Head inside the fog to battle the Blackgaol Knight, whose heavy strikes and phantom waves will test your spacing. Emerging victorious rewards you with his full Solitude Set and the Greatsword of Solitude.

    Key fight: Blackgaol Knight

    Tip: Use guard-counters or launch attacks like Giant Hunt to reliably break the Blackgaol Knight's high poise and disrupt his rapid-fire repeating crossbow.

  4. 4

    The Three-Path Junction and Main Gate

    Follow the highway north from the Scorched Ruins to reach the Three-Path Cross, where you will find a Scadutree Fragment, meet Needle Knight Leda and Redmane Freyja, and receive the Cross Map from Hornsent. Branch along the western fork toward the Main Gate Cross outside Belurat, Tower Settlement to collect another Scadutree Fragment while meeting Sir Ansbach and the merchant Moore. Scale the southern cliff ramps to clear Prospect Town for the Outer God Heirloom and "Incursion" Painting, and search the graveyard above toward the cliff drop for another Scadutree Fragment and Revered Spirit Ash.

    Watch out: Do not venture too far into Scadu Altus or approach the gates of Shadow Keep early, as breaking a great rune locks you out of the Black Syrup and Thiollier's quest exchange.

  5. 5

    The Northern Crossing and Dragon Terraces

    Take the northern route across the Ellac Greatbridge toward Castle Front, grabbing the Ash of War: Savage Lion's Claw from the camp corpse, a Scadutree Fragment by the cross, and a Revered Spirit Ash along the northern ravine cliffs. Ride out across the shallow northern lake to slay the Ghostflame Dragon and loot the Great Katana from a corpse in the water, or slip down the cliffs near the riverbed south of the Ellac Greatbridge to access the Ellac River chasm. Finally, follow the southeastern cliffs to Pillar Path Waypoint to hear the screams of Igon, then advance to Pillar Path Cross to claim another Scadutree Fragment, recruit Thiollier, and trade Moore's Black Syrup by answering "I am weary of life."

    Tip: Target the Ghostflame Dragon's head with strike or holy damage, and mount Torrent to outrun frost-inflicting ghostflame breath when it takes flight.

    Watch out: Choosing "I am weary of life" trades your Black Syrup for Thiollier's Concoction, an optional item that permanently turns the Dragon Communion Priestess into a spirit ash puppet rather than granting her heart and weapon.

Black Syrup Dialogue with Thiollier

At Pillar Path Cross after handing the Black Syrup to Thiollier and being asked why you want it

  • Say "I am weary of life."→ You receive Thiollier's Concoction, which can be administered to the Dragon Communion Priestess later to turn her into a spirit ash summon instead of receiving her weapon and heart
  • Do not select the option→ You do not obtain Thiollier's Concoction

Most players pick: Say "I am weary of life."

Must be completed before advancing deep into Scadu Altus or approaching Shadow Keep gates, which triggers the Great Rune breaking.

Key fights
Furnace Golem (Gravesite Plain)

Enemies: Furnace Golem

Threats: Sweeping fire stomps, jumping area-of-effect fire eruptions, fire kick projectiles

Weaknesses and resistances: Susceptible to heavy stance-breaking hits to the legs and critical strikes to its fallen face

Tactics: Mount Torrent to jump over expanding fire rings after stomps; continually attack both legs until it stumbles and topples over, then execute a critical hit on the fallen faceplate

Rewards: Defeating Flame Crystal Tear, Furnace Visage

Blackgaol Knight

Enemies: Blackgaol Knight

Threats: Repeating crossbow volley, sweeping two-handed greatsword swings with projectile wave of light

Weaknesses and resistances: Vulnerable to heavy poise disruption, parrying, and backstabs

Tactics: Avoid his rapid-fire crossbow bursts at range; roll inward on his sword swings to punish recovery frames, or stagger him repeatedly with high-poise colossals

Rewards: Greatsword of Solitude, Helm of Solitude, Armor of Solitude, Gauntlets of Solitude, Greaves of Solitude
