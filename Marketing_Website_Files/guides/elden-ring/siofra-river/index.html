Siofra River

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the underground via the Mistwood well

Siofra River is an expansive subterranean zone accessed via the Siofra River Well in eastern Limgrave's Mistwood. Exploration centers around activating eight stone obelisks scattered across the ruins to light their corresponding braziers. Lighting every flame unlocks the fog barrier at Hallowhorn Grounds, allowing you to face the Ancestor Spirit boss.

Items

  • Clarifying Horn Charm: Found on a corpse resting on the edge of the elevated ruins near the Siofra River Bank site of grace
  • Golden Seed: Picked up at the base of an illusory tree situated near the ruins north of the Worshippers' Woods site of grace
  • Armorer's Cookbook [6]: Resting on a corpse guarded by Ancestral Followers behind the ruins west of the Siofra River Bank site of grace

Secrets

  • Dragonkin Soldier of Siofra | Located across a hidden cliffside ledge accessible by taking the broken spiritspring or teleport waygate near the northern ruins
  • Elevated ledges of eastern ruins | Accessible by climbing fallen pillars and scaffolding behind the waterfall near the Siofra River Well Depths

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Abandoned Merchant: Sells Larval Tear, Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook [17], Armorer's Cookbook [6], and crafting items inside the wooden rafters above the northern ruins

Tips

  • Keep rolling erratically or break line of sight using trees to avoid the unerring, high-velocity arrow shots from the spectral archers.
  • You must interact with all eight stone pillars to light the steps at Hallowhorn Grounds; each lit pillar adds one glowing flame to the stairs.
