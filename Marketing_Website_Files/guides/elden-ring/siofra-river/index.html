Siofra River

ELDEN RING guide · An underground region accessible via Limgrave
← All areas in this guide

Siofra River is a vast subterranean zone beneath Limgrave and Caelid, illuminated by a starry false sky and filled with ruins, ancient spirits, and claymen. Explorers light eight stone obelisks across the lower riverbed to awaken the Ancestor Spirit resting at Hallowhorn Grounds. The region also features an isolated Dragonkin Soldier boss accessible via a waygate and provides passage north to the Deep Siofra Well elevator leading into Caelid.

Region
Underground (beneath Limgrave and Caelid)
Levels
50-60
Getting there
Take the Siofra River Well elevator situated in the Mistwood of East Limgrave, east of the Minor Erdtree
Connects to
Nokron, Eternal City (upper cliffs, inaccessible from below), Caelid (Deep Siofra Well elevator)
Related quests
Blaidd's search for Nokron (Ranni's questline)

Enemy types
Ancestral Followers · Claymen · Giant Crabs · Land Octopuses · Giant Rats · Dragonkin Soldier · Ancestor Spirit

The short version

What matters

  • •Map: Siofra River, found directly at the base of the Hallowhorn Grounds stairs, maps out the vast underground layout.
  • •Lighting all eight stone obelisks to battle the Ancestor Spirit at Hallowhorn Grounds for 13,000 Runes and the Ancestral Follower Ashes.
  • •Using the waygate atop the collapsed column to reach the isolated terrace for the Dragonkin Soldier, Dragon Halberd, and Marika's Scarseal.
  • •Finding the Abandoned Merchant hidden behind the western waterfall cave to purchase a Larval Tear and Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook .

Common mistakes

  • •Sprinting in straight lines across the central waterways on Torrent, which makes you an easy target for Ancestral Follower snipers whose arrows track movement.
  • •Leaving stone obelisks unlit, which prevents the deer carcass at Hallowhorn Grounds from igniting and locks you out of the Ancestor Spirit boss.
  • •Overlooking the hidden waterfall cave in the western scaffolding, completely missing the merchant and his unique wares.

What you can skip

  • •Engaging the floating electric orbs, as they are fully invulnerable environmental hazards that drop no rewards.
  • •Slugging through every pack of slow Claymen once you have cleared their starting ruins.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Siofra River Well Depths

    Descend the massive elevator inside the domed well in eastern Mistwood, rest at the first Site of Grace, and advance through the flooded ruins past slow-moving Claymen. Seek out the high stone structure in these entrance ruins to slay a Teardrop Scarab for the Oracle Bubbles sorcery before ascending stone steps to a second lift that opens into the starry cavern.

    Tip: Claymen resist piercing blows, so rely on strike weapons or circle around their slow frames for backstabs.

    Watch out: Do not get caught in clusters of slow-moving Claymen when their bubble sorceries begin overlapping.

  2. 2

    Siofra River Bank and Hallowhorn Grounds

    Rest at the Siofra River Bank Site of Grace, collect Map: Siofra River from a corpse leaning on a pillar at the base of the stone stairs, and find the Horn Bow inside a crawl space guarded by skeletal spirits beneath those steps. Inspect Hallowhorn Grounds to note the giant deer carcass surrounded by eight unlit braziers, grab the Inverted Hawk Heater Shield from a cliff corpse by the second obelisk, and hunt the western cliffs behind Ancestral Followers for Armorer's Cookbook .

    Tip: Break sightlines with rocks and trees whenever Ancestral Follower snipers lock onto you, as their Dwelling Arrows track you through sprints.

  3. 3

    Igniting the Flame Pillars and Reaching the Abandoned Merchant

    Traverse the open waterways to find and light all eight stone obelisks while picking off Ancestral Followers, and hunt a Teardrop Scarab on the ledges above the central forest to claim Ash of War: Square Off. Head west toward the cliffs from the waterways to scale wooden scaffolding around ruins, dropping across platforms and entering a waterfall cave to reach the Abandoned Merchant, who sells Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook , a Larval Tear, a Stonesword Key, a Shotel, and Dwelling Arrows.

    Tip: Lighting all eight obelisks ignites the deer bones at Hallowhorn Grounds with blue fire, granting passage to the remembrance boss.

    Watch out: The invulnerable electric orbs drifting through the water pools cannot be destroyed, so ride Torrent past them without engaging.

  4. 4

    The Ancestor Spirit Battle

    Return to Hallowhorn Grounds once all eight flame pillars are burning and touch the glowing blue deer skeleton to teleport into the boss arena. Bring down the Ancestor Spirit by dodging through its sweeping antler charges, punishing its landing recoveries, and striking its staggered body to claim your runes and the Ancestral Follower Ashes.

    Key fight: Ancestor Spirit

    Tip: Stick to the center of the arena during aerial leaps so you do not lose your camera lock, and exploit its vulnerability to fire, holy, and slash damage.

  5. 5

    Worshipers' Woods

    Rest at the Worshipers' Woods Site of Grace, bypass giant crabs, land octopuses, and electric orbs in the northern marsh, and locate Blaidd standing near a cliff edge behind a broken pillar to progress Ranni's quest. Ascend the collapsed pillar or spirit spring atop a half-sunken column along the western/central waters to reach the inactive-looking waygate.

    Tip: Speak to Blaidd beside the cliff edge until he instructs you to confer with Preceptor Seluvis regarding a path to Nokron.

  6. 6

    Elevated Ruin Ledge and Dragonkin Soldier

    Step through the waygate to warp onto an isolated high ruin terrace, then search a room behind the nearby waterfalls for a Teardrop Scarab carrying the Great Oracular Bubble sorcery. Confront the resting Dragonkin Soldier on the terrace, stick to its back legs to avoid wide sweep attacks, and after claiming the Dragon Halberd, loot Marika's Scarseal from a corpse beneath the massive waterfall behind the arena.

    Key fight: Dragonkin Soldier

    Tip: Focus jump heavy attacks on the Dragonkin Soldier's head to trigger posture breaks and critical hits.

    Watch out: Avoid standing directly in front of the Dragonkin Soldier in the water, as ripples disrupt footing and leave you open to sweeping slams.

  7. 7

    Below the Well to Deep Siofra Well

    Pass underneath the soaring aqueduct bridges near the Below the Well Site of Grace, speak to the kneeling Siofra River Spirit, and loot the Clarifying Horn Charm from a corpse at the aqueduct bridge overlook. Snag a Golden Seed from the phantom sapling along the northern trail, then approach the northern elevator and decide whether to insert two Stonesword Keys into the Imp Statue to unlock the lift leading up to northern Caelid.

    Tip: Unlocking the northern well elevator provides early access to Dragonbarrow and the Great-Jar's Arsenal challenge in Caelid.

    Watch out: Spending your two Stonesword Keys is irreversible, so keep them saved if you have no current need to reach northern Caelid.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Activating the Flame Pillars

While exploring the open waters and ruins with the 8 obelisks

  • Light all 8 stone obelisks→ Ignites the deer remains at Hallowhorn Grounds with blue fire to grant entry to the Ancestor Spirit boss arena
  • Leave obelisks unlit→ The path to the Ancestor Spirit remains sealed

Most players pick: Light all 8 stone obelisks

Deep Siofra Well Imp Statue

At the northern lift structure

  • Use 2 Stonesword Keys→ Operates the lift ascending to northern Caelid to reach the Great-Jar and its Great-Jar's Arsenal challenge early
  • Keep the keys→ The northern lift stays locked

Most players pick: Use 2 Stonesword Keys

Key fights
Ancestor Spirit

Enemies: Ancestor Spirit

Threats: Teleporting dashes, sweeping antler strikes, floating soul-fire breath that lingers on the ground

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Fire, Holy, and Slash; resistant to Magic and Status Effects

Tactics: Stay under or behind its torso, dodge through the descending aerial stomp attacks, and hit the head during recovery windows for stance breaks

Rewards: Ancestral Follower Ashes, 13,000 Runes

Dragonkin Soldier

Enemies: Dragonkin Soldier

Threats: Sweeping claw slams, lunging strikes, ground tremors with delayed shockwaves

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Slash and Strike damage to the legs and head; immune to Bleed

Tactics: Stick closely behind its left knee to avoid broad arm sweeps, baiting recovery animations to punish its legs

Rewards: Dragon Halberd, 16,000 Runes

Tips
  • Use the abundant tree cover and stone ruins to break line of sight against the Ancestral Follower snipers, whose spectral arrows have extreme range and tracking.
  • You must interact with and ignite all eight stone obelisks scattered across the open plain before the horned remains at Hallowhorn Grounds will activate the portal to the Ancestor Spirit.
