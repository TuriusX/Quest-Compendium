Mt. Gelmir

ELDEN RING guide · Scaling the mountain after crossing the Altus Plateau

Mt. Gelmir is a harsh, volcanic region west of the Altus Plateau defined by steep cliffs, rope bridges, and battlefields. Players must navigate winding paths and tall rope ladders through military camps and fiery terrain to reach the summit. Reaching the peak allows access to the Ninth Mt. Gelmir Campsite, the crater boss, and the entrance to Volcano Manor.

Items

  • Comet Azur: Found on Primeval Sorcerer Azur, sitting near the cliffs past the Hermit Village
  • Somber Smithing Stone [6]: Looted from a corpse hanging on a wooden chair atop a rock outcrop near the First Mt. Gelmir Campsite
  • Map: Mt. Gelmir: Found on a corpse beneath a road monument pillar just past the Road of Iniquity Site of Grace

Secrets

  • A rainbow stone trail along the cliffside past the ninth campsite prompts an encounter where Patches kicks the player into the gorge below.

Enemies

Tips

  • When scaling the tall cliffs near the First Mt. Gelmir Campsite, look for long rope ladders pinned to the sheer rock walls instead of trying to climb along the jagged ledges.
  • Using the Spiritspring jump near the Ninth Mt. Gelmir Campsite sends you directly into the arena of the Full-Grown Fallingstar Beast, but you can bypass the fight by running across the protruding rock bridge to Volcano Manor.
