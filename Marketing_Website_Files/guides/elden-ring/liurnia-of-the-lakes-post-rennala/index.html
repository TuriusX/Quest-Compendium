Liurnia of the Lakes (Post-Rennala)

ELDEN RING guide · Returning after the Academy

Defeating Rennala allows access to character respecs and unlocks the pathway to proceed further north toward Caria Manor and the Altus Plateau. You can also explore previously locked interactions, advance regional NPC questlines, and eventually unlock the inverted version of the Carian Study Hall once you obtain the key item from Ranni.

Items

  • Cursemark of Death: At the top of the Divine Tower of Liurnia, accessed via the Carian Study Hall after using the Carian Inverted Statue
  • Stargazer Heirloom: Found on the body at the top of the Divine Tower of Liurnia
  • Dark Moon Ring MISSABLE: Inside the locked chest next to Rennala in the Raya Lucaria Grand Library, unlocked using the Discarded Palace Key
  • Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen: Rewarded upon defeating Rennala in the Raya Lucaria Grand Library
  • Great Rune of the Unborn: Rewarded upon defeating Rennala in the Raya Lucaria Grand Library

Secrets

  • Placing the Carian Inverted Statue on the pedestal in the Carian Study Hall flips the entire interior upside down, revealing new walkways and the route to the Divine Tower.

Shops and people

  • Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon: Rebirth (respec stats in exchange for a Larval Tear) and Cosmetics (alter appearance)
  • Enia (Roundtable Hold): Exchanges the Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen for the Carian Regal Scepter or Rennala's Full Moon spell

Tips

  • You must retain at least one Larval Tear in your inventory each time you wish to use Rennala's Rebirth service.
  • The locked chest next to Rennala in the Grand Library cannot be opened until late in Ranni's questline after defeating the Baleful Shadow in Nokstella.
