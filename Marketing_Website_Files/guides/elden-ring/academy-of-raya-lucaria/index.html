Academy of Raya Lucaria

ELDEN RING guide · Storming the second legacy dungeon

The Academy of Raya Lucaria is a sprawling, multi-level sorcery school enclosed by magical barriers in the center of Liurnia of the Lakes. You must navigate its classrooms, courtyards, rooftops, and waterwheel lifts while enduring relentless ranged magic from glintstone sorcerers. Defeating the Red Wolf of Radagon and Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon will earn you a Great Rune and unlock the ability to respec your character.

Items

  • Academy Glintstone Key: On a corpse perched on the chandeliers inside the Church of the Cuckoo, reached via the rooftops
  • Radagon Icon: Inside a treasure chest on the upper balcony above the Debate Parlor Site of Grace
  • Comet: Inside a chest behind an illusory bookcase in the room right before the Debate Parlor
  • Memory Stone: Dropped upon defeating the Red Wolf of Radagon in the Debate Parlor
  • Glintstone Craftsman's Cookbook [5]: Inside a chest in an illuminated room near the Schoolhouse Classroom Site of Grace
  • Karolos Glintstone Crown: Found behind an illusory wall in the Schoolhouse Classroom area, behind a glowing crystal snail
  • Olivinus Glintstone Crown: On a corpse on a balcony accessed by hitting an illusory bookcase in the hallway before the Debate Parlor
  • Twinsage Glintstone Crown: Dropped by a crystal crab wandering the rooftops on the far side of the Debate Parlor courtyard
  • Imbued Sword Key: Looted from a corpse on the rooftops in the section just past the Debate Parlor

Secrets

  • Illusory walls disguised as empty bookshelves are scattered throughout the classrooms near the Debate Parlor; striking them reveals hidden rooms containing sorceries and paths to unique glintstone crowns.
  • Letting the Abductor Virgin at the very bottom of the giant rotating waterwheel lift kill you with its internal pull-in grab attack teleports your character directly beneath Volcano Manor.

Shops and people

  • Iron Fist Alexander: Available for dialogue inside the courtyard if progressed to this point, offering combat dialogue and advancing his questline
  • Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon: Offers "Rebirth" to reallocate your attributes in exchange for a Larval Tear, as well as cosmetic character alteration

Tips

  • Wear armor or use talismans that boost magic damage negation, as the vast majority of threats here deal pure magic damage.
  • Running zig-zag patterns across the long outdoor walkways will easily avoid the vast majority of long-range Glintstone Pebble volleys.
  • In phase one of the Rennala fight, ignore the crawling scholars entirely except for the single glowing student singing in the room; hit them once to disrupt her protective barrier.
  • Rennala possesses extremely high resistance to magic damage, so sorcerers should rely on physical-damage spells like Rock Sling to deplete her health effectively.
