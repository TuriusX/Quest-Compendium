The Academy of Raya Lucaria is a sprawling, multi-level sorcery school enclosed by magical barriers in the center of Liurnia of the Lakes. You must navigate its classrooms, courtyards, rooftops, and waterwheel lifts while enduring relentless ranged magic from glintstone sorcerers. Defeating the Red Wolf of Radagon and Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon will earn you a Great Rune and unlock the ability to respec your character.

Enemies
Glintstone Sorcerer
Weak to Physical damage

Fires homing magic projectiles from range and hits with magic daggers if approached.

Living Jar
Weak to Blunt strike damage

Swings violently and attempts slam attacks; breaks easily to heavy blunt impacts.

Avionette Soldier
Weak to Strike damage and lightning

Erratic flying marionette that dives with scythes and enters a berserk thrashing frenzy when damaged.

Abductor Virgin
Weak to Lightning

Heavily armored wheeled construct that attempts to trap you inside its chassis for massive damage.

Red Wolf of Radagon
Weak to Physical slash and jump attacks

Agile boss that leaps across the arena casting homing glintblade swords and swinging a glowing magic blade.

Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon
Weak to Physical slash and bleed

Remembrance boss whose first phase requires breaking student shields and whose second phase unleashes heavy sorceries and spirit summons.

Shops and people
  • Iron Fist Alexander: Available for dialogue inside the courtyard if progressed to this point, offering combat dialogue and advancing his questline
  • Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon: Offers "Rebirth" to reallocate your attributes in exchange for a Larval Tear, as well as cosmetic character alteration

Tips
  • Wear armor or use talismans that boost magic damage negation, as the vast majority of threats here deal pure magic damage.
  • Running zig-zag patterns across the long outdoor walkways will easily avoid the vast majority of long-range Glintstone Pebble volleys.
  • In phase one of the Rennala fight, ignore the crawling scholars entirely except for the single glowing student singing in the room; hit them once to disrupt her protective barrier.
  • Rennala possesses extremely high resistance to magic damage, so sorcerers should rely on physical-damage spells like Rock Sling to deplete her health effectively.
