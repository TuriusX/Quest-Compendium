Mohgwyn Palace

ELDEN RING guide · Accessing the underground region via Varre's quest or portal

Mohgwyn Palace is a high-level secret underground region steeped in blood, ruled by Mohg, Lord of Blood. Players navigate dark cave tunnels, a vast blood-red swamp filled with giant crows and Albinaurics, and an ancient ruined pavilion leading to the Mausoleum. The ultimate objective is ascending the grand structure to confront the demigod Mohg in his audience grounds.

Items

  • Pureblood Knight's Medal MISSABLE: Obtained from White Mask Varré at the Rose Church to teleport directly here
  • Swarm of Flies: Found on a corpse inside a small cave wall niche off the blood swamp path
  • Haligdrake Talisman +2: Looted from a corpse near a giant bird in the blood swamp
  • Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone: In a chest guarded by a Sanguine Noble just before the elevator to Mohg's arena
  • War Surgeon Set MISSABLE: Dropped by defeating the third Nameless White Mask invader in the blood lake
  • Lord of Blood's Exultation: Dropped by Esgar, Priest of Blood inside the Leyndell Catacombs, not directly in this zone, but synergistic
  • Mohg's Great Rune: Obtained by defeating Mohg, Lord of Blood
  • Remembrance of the Blood Lord: Obtained by defeating Mohg, Lord of Blood

Secrets

  • Teleporter in the Consecrated Snowfield guarded by a Sanguine Noble provides alternative access without doing Varré's questline
  • Invading White Mask Varré through a red summon sign near the Mausoleum Midpoint grace allows you to defeat him for his weapon

  • Imprisoned Merchant: Sells Rune Arcs, Dwelling Arrows, St. Trina's Arrows, and Burred Bolts

Tips

  • Use the Purifying Crystal Tear in your Flask of Wondrous Physick to negate Mohg's lethal Nihil transition curse.
  • Mohg's Shackle can be used twice during the first phase of the boss fight to bind Mohg to the ground.
  • The Palace Approach Ledge-Road Site of Grace is widely considered the best late-game rune farming spot using area-of-effect skills.
  • Defeat all three Nameless White Mask NPC invaders before killing Mohg, or they will permanently stop spawning for the rest of the playthrough.
