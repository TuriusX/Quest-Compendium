Mohgwyn Palace is a high-level secret underground region steeped in blood, ruled by Mohg, Lord of Blood. Players navigate dark cave tunnels, a vast blood-red swamp filled with giant crows and Albinaurics, and an ancient ruined pavilion leading to the Mausoleum. The ultimate objective is ascending the grand structure to confront the demigod Mohg in his audience grounds.

Enemies
Albinauric (Red/Grey)
Weak to Physical, Magic

High density of sleeping enemies, ideal for massive rune farming

Giant Crow
Weak to Pierce, Fire

Extremely aggressive bird patrolling the blood marsh that can be baited off cliffs

Nameless White Mask
Weak to Bleed, Strike

Invades three distinct times in the blood lake as long as Mohg remains alive

Sanguine Noble
Weak to Strike, Heavy Stagger

Dagger-wielding bleed assassins hiding in caves and ruins

Mohg, Lord of Blood
Weak to Bleed, Physical, Hemorrhage

Shardbearer boss who inflicts heavy blood loss and uses a ritual chant

Shops and people
  • Imprisoned Merchant: Sells Rune Arcs, Dwelling Arrows, St. Trina's Arrows, and Burred Bolts

Tips
  • Use the Purifying Crystal Tear in your Flask of Wondrous Physick to negate Mohg's lethal Nihil transition curse.
  • Mohg's Shackle can be used twice during the first phase of the boss fight to bind Mohg to the ground.
  • The Palace Approach Ledge-Road Site of Grace is widely considered the best late-game rune farming spot using area-of-effect skills.
  • Defeat all three Nameless White Mask NPC invaders before killing Mohg, or they will permanently stop spawning for the rest of the playthrough.
