The Abyssal Woods is a haunting, fog-drenched forest located in the Shadow of the Erdtree expansion, accessible through the Darklight Catacombs. Players cannot summon Torrent here and must traverse the murky paths primarily using stealth to evade invulnerable patrolling horrors. Navigating through the woods ultimately leads southwest toward the Midra's Manse dungeon to face the Lord of Frenzied Flame.

Items

0/5

Enemies
Aged One (Winter Lantern)
Weak to Parrying their grab attack

Wandering watchers with giant swollen heads that inflict rapid Madness buildup; they are completely immune to normal damage until you successfully parry their leaping grab, which renders them permanently vulnerable.

Frenzied Inquisitor

Humanoid spellcasters that lurk along the watery trails and cast bursts of frenzy incantations from a distance.

Tips
  • Torrent is terrified of this area and cannot be mounted, forcing all navigation and evasion to be done on foot.
  • Crouch in the tall, amber-glowing bushes to break line of sight and completely sneak past the Aged Ones.
  • Defeating an Aged One after parrying its grab attack causes it to permanently despawn and awards an exclusive talisman on the first kill.
