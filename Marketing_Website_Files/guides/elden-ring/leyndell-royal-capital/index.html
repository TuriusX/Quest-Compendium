Leyndell, Royal Capital

ELDEN RING guide · Entering the capital city after gathering Great Runes

Leyndell, Royal Capital is a sprawling golden city resting at the foot of the Erdtree, accessible after acquiring at least two Great Runes. You must navigate its rooftops, streets, and the wing of a giant petrified dragon to ascend toward the Erdtree and confront Morgott, the Omen King. Completing key events later in the game will transform this zone into an ash-covered wasteland, making many of its original paths and treasures permanently unobtainable.

Items

  • Bolt of Gransax MISSABLE: Found on the massive spear stuck into the side of the palace, reachable by dropping down from the balcony near the Erdtree Sanctuary
  • Seedbed Curse MISSABLE: Found in the fortified manor on the corpse sitting in the replica of the Roundtable Hold's central room
  • Golden Seed: Found at the base of an illusory tree on the main street near the gargoyle encounter
  • Lionel's Armor Set MISSABLE: Found on a bed in the building next to the Lower Capital Church site of grace
  • Black Bow MISSABLE: Found on the rooftops near the Avenue Balcony, reached by descending ladders and climbing adjacent rooftops
  • Coded Sword MISSABLE: Found in the throne room of the Fortified Manor on the lower floor

Secrets

  • Subterranean Shunning-Grounds entrance | Jump into the open well located in the ruined, lower residential section of the city to descend into the sewers below.
  • Colosseum access | Follow the path past the West Capital Rampart up along the roots and cliffs to reach the Royal Colosseum cliffside.

Tips

  • Grab the Bolt of Gransax legendary armament before defeating Maliketh in Crumbling Farum Azula, as it cannot be collected once the city becomes the Ashen Capital.
  • You can bypass several heavily guarded street encounters by climbing across the giant dragon corpse's wing to reach the upper ramparts directly.
