Leyndell, Royal Capital

ELDEN RING guide · The major legacy dungeon and capital city
← All areas in this guide

Leyndell, Royal Capital is a sprawling golden metropolis serving as the seat of the Erdtree and the ruling demigods. Navigating the city requires descending from the outer ramparts through devastated avenues, climbing the petrified wing of the dragon Gransax, and scaling Erdtree roots to reach the royal sanctuary and the throne. Defeating the shardbearer Morgott at the foot of the Erdtree is mandatory to progress the main story and unlock the path toward the Mountaintops of the Giants.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating Godfrey (Golden Shade) awards a Talisman Pouch, while Morgott grants his Great Rune, a Remembrance, and access to the Forbidden Lands via Melina's Rold Medallion.
  • •Collect key armaments like the Bolt of Gransax, Coded Sword, Black Bow, and the Sanctified Whetblade before completing Crumbling Farum Azula.
  • •Reveal the secret at the Radagon statue with Law of Regression to progress Brother Corhyn and Goldmask's questline.

Common mistakes

  • •Handing Boc the Seamster a Larval Tear kills him in Raya Lucaria; speak the Prattling Pate You're Beautiful to keep him alive.
  • •Rushing Crumbling Farum Azula before fully looting Leyndell will bury key weapons, armor sets, and Seedbed Curses under ash permanently.
  • •Rolling through Godfrey's earth stomps usually gets you clipped; jump cleanly over the shockwaves instead.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    East Capital Ramparts

    When you arrive along the parapets, push past Leyndell Soldiers, Pages, and Oracle Envoys to reach the East Capital Rampart grace. Rest here to speak with Boc the Seamster, though Melina will depart your company once you rest inside the city gates. Look for a fortified rampart tower nearby to claim a Seedbed Curse from its highest level.

    Tip: Strike the Oracle Envoys with physical Standard, Slash, or Pierce damage to easily interrupt their holy bubble attacks.

    Watch out: Using a Larval Tear on Boc sends him to Raya Lucaria where he dies; use Prattling Pate You're Beautiful to keep him alive.

  2. 2

    Rooftops and Lower Capital Slums

    Descend the elevator from the ramparts and drop onto the tiled rooftops while fighting off ambushing Fanged Imps. Drop further down into the Lower Capital City and canal slums to battle Rabid Dogs, Slimes, and Leyndell Soldiers. Head inside the Lower Capital Church to grab Lionel’s Armor Set and the Deathbed Dress from the bed, and note the open sewer well that leads down into the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds.

    Watch out: Lionel’s Armor Set and the Deathbed Dress become permanently unobtainable once you defeat Maliketh, the Black Blade in Crumbling Farum Azula.

  3. 3

    Main Avenue to Avenue Balcony

    Battle through high-tier Leyndell Knights, perfumers, and an Erdtree Avatar to unlock the Avenue Balcony grace along the east-west grand thoroughfare. From this area, clear the courtyard holding an Ulcerated Tree Spirit and search the nearby rooftops to locate the Black Bow.

    Tip: Exploit the Erdtree Avatar's extreme vulnerability to Fire damage and poise-breaking hits to drop it quickly.

    Watch out: The Black Bow is permanently lost if you do not collect it before defeating Maliketh, the Black Blade in Crumbling Farum Azula.

  4. 4

    The Corpse of Gransax

    Walk up the massive stone wing and tail of the petrified dragon Gransax to bypass the collapsed avenues and reach the upper defenses. Follow this elevated route past resisting forces until you reach the West Capital Rampart.

    Tip: Climbing the dragon's body is the only direct path to bypass the rubble blocking the main street to the upper city.

  5. 5

    Fortified Manor and Colosseum Hill

    From the West Capital Rampart grace, head south toward the Royal Colosseum past Duelists to grab the Ritual Shield Talisman, or assist Bernahl with the Volcano Manor Assassination against Vargram and Wilhelm inside the Fortified Manor. Explore the manor to retrieve the Sanctified Whetblade, Coded Sword, Two Fingers' Prayerbook, Alberich’s Armor Set, and another Seedbed Curse.

    Tip: Brother Corhyn and Goldmask await along the hillside path south of the West Capital Rampart to continue their quest.

    Watch out: Every unique item inside the Fortified Manor, including the Sanctified Whetblade and Seedbed Curse, is buried in ash once Maliketh is defeated.

  6. 6

    Erdtree Sanctuary and Godfrey

    Ascend the giant tree roots from the ramparts into the palace architecture and enter the Erdtree Sanctuary to defeat the Golden Shade of Godfrey, First Elden Lord. Jump onto the large tree branch to enter the upper level for the Golden Order Principia, or exit west and ride the elevator down to cast Law of Regression before the Radagon statue, and drop onto the colossal spear to retrieve the Bolt of Gransax.

    Key fight: Godfrey, First Elden Lord (Golden Shade)

    Tip: Godfrey's shade is immune to all status effects, so rely on Lightning or Pierce damage and jump over his axe stomp shockwaves.

    Watch out: The Bolt of Gransax is permanently missable if not picked up before defeating Maliketh, the Black Blade in Crumbling Farum Azula.

  7. 7

    Elden Throne and Morgott

    Climb the hanging golden branch past the Queen's Bedchamber grace and make your way to the Elden Throne to defeat Morgott, the Omen King. Summon Melina outside the fog door and trigger Margit's Shackle during his opening phase to hold him vulnerable. After the battle, rest at the Elden Throne grace to meet Melina, who grants the Rold Medallion to guide your route toward the Forbidden Lands.

    Key fight: Morgott, the Omen King

    Tip: Morgott is weak to Slash, Bleed, and Frostbite, and Margit's Shackle can pin him down twice during Phase 1.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Boc's fate

When speaking to Boc the Seamster at the East Capital Rampart Site of Grace

  • Hand over a Larval Tear→ He relocates to the Raya Lucaria Grand Library, transforms into a human, loses the ability to speak, and perishes once you rest
  • Play the "You're Beautiful" Prattling Pate→ He embraces his true looks, stays alive, and continues tailoring your garments indefinitely

Most players pick: Play the "You're Beautiful" Prattling Pate

Giving him the Larval Tear permanently ends his life and severs his tailoring services.

Uncovering the secret of Radagon's statue for Goldmask

After encountering Brother Corhyn and Goldmask on the cliff near the colosseum south of the West Capital Rampart

  • Cast the Law of Regression incantation before the statue and reveal "Radagon is Marika" to Goldmask→ Progresses Goldmask's questline toward earning the Mending Rune of Perfect Order and grants the Golden Order Totality gesture
  • Do not cast the spell or fail to inform Goldmask→ Halts progress on Goldmask and Corhyn's storyline

Most players pick: Cast Law of Regression and report to Goldmask

Defeating Maliketh in Crumbling Farum Azula buries the capital in ash, making this area unreachable if not done beforehand.

Key fights
Godfrey, First Elden Lord (Golden Shade)

Enemies: Golden Shade of Godfrey

Threats: Broad sweeping strikes with a colossal battleaxe and wide-radius ground-slam shockwaves

Weaknesses and resistances: Piercing damage, Lightning; immune to all status effects (Bleed, Frostbite, Poison, Rot)

Tactics: Roll forward into his axe swings to stay behind him, and jump cleanly over his foot-stomp earth shockwaves

Rewards: 80,000 Runes, Talisman Pouch

Morgott, the Omen King

Enemies: Morgott, the Omen King

Threats: Conjured golden weapons (swords, daggers, spears, hammers), bloodflame blade strikes, wide-area rain of swords

Weaknesses and resistances: Slash damage; vulnerable to Bleed and Frostbite

Tactics: Use Margit's Shackle during Phase 1 to pin him to the floor; summon Melina at the fog gate to draw aggro; dodge sideways past his leaping spear throws

Rewards: 120,000 Runes, Morgott's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Omen King

Enemies
Oracle Envoy

Leyndell Knight
Weak to Lightning, PierceSteal / drop Leyndell Knight Armor set

Uses Greatbows with lightning arrows at range and wields electrified swords and spears up close.

High Perfumer
Weak to Physical, SlashSteal / drop Perfume Bottles

Throws explosive and buffing perfume powders; highly aggressive at mid-range.

Tips
  • Complete all item pickups and NPC quest interactions inside the Fortified Manor and lower streets before defeating Maliketh in Crumbling Farum Azula, as doing so permanently converts the capital into Leyndell, Ashen Capital and buries most of the city under ash.
  • Margit's Shackle, purchased from Patches earlier in the game, works twice during the first phase of the Morgott boss fight to bind him to the ground for free damage windows.
