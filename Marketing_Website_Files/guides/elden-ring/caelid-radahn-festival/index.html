Caelid (Radahn Festival)

ELDEN RING guide · Returning for the Radahn Festival

Redmane Castle transforms into the staging ground for the Radahn Festival once triggered by game progression, clearing out the standard enemies and opening the way to face Starscourge Radahn. Speak with Castellan Jerren in the courtyard to initiate the festivities, then descend via the elevator and portal to the Wailing Dunes to battle the demigod. Defeating Radahn halts the stars, causes a star to crash into the Lands Between, and unlocks access to Nokron, Eternal City.

Items

  • Flamberge: in the room behind the Pumpkin Head arena stairs, accessible before/during specific festival states
  • Armorer's Cookbook [5]: in a room off the central courtyard of Redmane Castle
  • Remembrance of the Starscourge: awarded upon defeating Starscourge Radahn in the Wailing Dunes
  • Radahn's Great Rune: awarded upon defeating Starscourge Radahn in the Wailing Dunes

Secrets

  • After defeating Radahn, ride north across the vast Wailing Dunes to the northernmost cliffside to find the War-Dead Catacombs entrance hidden in the rock face.

Enemies

Starscourge RadahnScarlet Rot, Pierce damage, FrostbiteDemigod boss fought in the expansive desert arena with multiple summonable NPC allies.

Shops and people

  • Castellan Jerren: initiates the Radahn Festival ceremony and permits access to the teleporter leading down to the dunes
  • Iron Fist Alexander: summonable ally inside the boss arena; talk to him in the arena after the fight to progress his questline
  • Blaidd the Half-Wolf: summonable ally inside the boss arena; talk to him near the Site of Grace after the fight to advance his questline

Tips

  • Summon the gold summon signs scattered across the dunes repeatedly; if the NPC allies fall in battle, their signs will reappear after a short cooldown elsewhere in the arena.
  • Applying Scarlet Rot to Radahn using Rotten Breath or rotting arrows deals massive percentage-based damage over time, allowing you to focus on dodging his wide-sweeping gravity attacks.
  • Mount Torrent during the first phase only after closing the distance, as dodging his initial long-range gravity arrows is far easier using invulnerability frames on foot.
