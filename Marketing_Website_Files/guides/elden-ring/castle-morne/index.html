Castle Morne

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the southern legacy dungeon

Located at the southern tip of the Weeping Peninsula, Castle Morne has been overrun by a bloody rebellion of the resident Misbegotten servants. Players must fight their way through the courtyards and ramparts to aid the castle commander, Edgar, and eventually reach the rear coast. Clearing the castle culminates in a boss fight against the Leonine Misbegotten to claim a legendary armament.

Items

  • Grafted Blade Greatsword: dropped by Leonine Misbegotten upon defeat at the coastal arena behind the castle
  • Claymore: inside a chest in a small room along the wall of the main courtyard where a group of Misbegotten is idling
  • Twinblades Talisman: inside a chest at the top of the tower on the far eastern ramparts
  • Whip: found on a corpse inside a room in the rear residential quarter near the rampart jail
  • Stonesword Key: on a hanging wooden platform suspended off the side of the rear walls

Secrets

  • Along the sea cliffs behind the castle, you can drop down across a series of wooden scaffolding and stone roofs to reach the Behind the Castle site of grace and bypass the main rampart drop.

Tips

  • A large group of Misbegotten in the main courtyard is distracted by a mound of burning corpses, allowing you to sneak around them or ambush them with area-of-effect attacks.
  • You can summon the NPC Edgar to assist you against the Leonine Misbegotten if you have already spoken to him in his tower and delivered Irina's Letter.
