Deeproot Depths is a submerged subterranean area accessed via a stone coffin after defeating the Valiant Gargoyles in the Siofra Aqueduct. Navigating the massive roots leads through ancient ruins infested with basilisks, mausoleum soldiers, and giant ants. Exploring fully reveals the Prince of Death's Throne, where Fia's questline reaches its conclusion.

Enemies
Basilisk
Weak to Holy, strike damage

Can rapidly inflict Death Blight, so keep safe distances or use Beast-Repellent Torch.

Giant Flying Ant
Weak to Fire damage

Attacks while airborne and shoots corrosive spray that damages armor durability.

Crucible Knight Siluria
Weak to Parry, lightning damage

Uses a Greatspear with high reach; found guarding a hollow tree trunk in the northwest.

Fia's Champions
Weak to Standard physical damage, status effects

Three consecutive waves of NPC spirits fought near the Prince of Death's Throne.

Lichdragon Fortissax
Weak to Pierce damage, holy damage

Boss fought inside the Deathbed Dream that casts red ancient lightning with Death Blight buildup.

Shops and people
  • Fia: Offers Deathbed Companion dialogue, holds the Cursemark of Death for her quest, and grants the Baldachin's Blessing
  • Finger Reader Enia (Roundtable Hold): Exchanges Remembrance of the Lichdragon for Fortissax's Lightning Spear or Death Lightning

Tips
  • Equip the Prince of Death's Pustule or carry Rejuvenating Boluses to avoid instant death from the numerous basilisks.
  • A Beast-Repellent Torch held in either hand prevents the basilisks from actively attacking you throughout the sunken ruins.
  • You must give Fia the Cursemark of Death (found on the Divine Tower of Liurnia) to unlock the Lichdragon Fortissax boss encounter.
