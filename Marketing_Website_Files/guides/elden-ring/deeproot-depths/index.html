Deeproot Depths

ELDEN RING guide · Reaching the roots after the gargoyle boss fight

Deeproot Depths is a submerged subterranean area accessed via a stone coffin after defeating the Valiant Gargoyles in the Siofra Aqueduct. Navigating the massive roots leads through ancient ruins infested with basilisks, mausoleum soldiers, and giant ants. Exploring fully reveals the Prince of Death's Throne, where Fia's questline reaches its conclusion.

Items

  • Prince of Death's Staff: Found on a corpse inside the tower ruin near the Deeproot Depths Site of Grace
  • Staff of the Avatar: Dropped by the Erdtree Avatar wandering near the broken waterfall ruins
  • Ash of War: Golden Land: Dropped by a Teardrop Scarab located on a thick root branch path
  • Crucible Tree Armor Set: Found in a chest behind Crucible Knight Siluria in the hollowed-out tree trunk
  • Siluria's Tree: Dropped upon defeating the boss Crucible Knight Siluria
  • Twinned Armor Set MISSABLE: Handed over by D's brother or retrieved from him after finishing Fia's questline
  • Inseparable Sword MISSABLE: Looted from D's brother at the Prince of Death's Throne after completing Fia's questline
  • Mending Rune of the Death-Prince MISSABLE: Obtained from Fia's body after entering the Deathbed Dream and defeating Lichdragon Fortissax
  • Remembrance of the Lichdragon MISSABLE: Acquired after defeating Lichdragon Fortissax inside the Deathbed Dream

Secrets

  • Warping Coffin | Ride inside a stone coffin near the edge of the waterfall northwest of the Nameless Eternal City site of grace to be transported to Ainsel River Main.
  • Walking Mausoleum | This area's Walking Mausoleum has its skull-like growths on its body rather than its legs, requiring you to jump onto it from nearby tree roots to bring it down.
  • Teleporter to Leyndell | A portal near Fia at the Prince of Death's Throne directly teleports you into the capital city of Leyndell, though it only activates after you claim two Great Runes.

Shops and people

  • Fia: Offers Deathbed Companion dialogue, holds the Cursemark of Death for her quest, and grants the Baldachin's Blessing
  • Finger Reader Enia (Roundtable Hold): Exchanges Remembrance of the Lichdragon for Fortissax's Lightning Spear or Death Lightning

Tips

  • Equip the Prince of Death's Pustule or carry Rejuvenating Boluses to avoid instant death from the numerous basilisks.
  • A Beast-Repellent Torch held in either hand prevents the basilisks from actively attacking you throughout the sunken ruins.
  • You must give Fia the Cursemark of Death (found on the Divine Tower of Liurnia) to unlock the Lichdragon Fortissax boss encounter.
