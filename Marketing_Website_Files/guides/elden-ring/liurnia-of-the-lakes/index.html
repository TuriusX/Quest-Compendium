Liurnia of the Lakes

ELDEN RING guide · The region north of Limgrave
← All areas in this guide

Liurnia of the Lakes is a vast, mist-shrouded wetland region directly north of Limgrave, dominated by shallow waters, sinking ruins, and sheer cliffs. Players explore this region to secure the Academy Glintstone Key, breach the barrier surrounding the Academy of Raya Lucaria, and locate routes leading upward to the Altus Plateau. Exploration here branches into Ranni the Witch's service at the Three Sisters in the far northwest, along with extensive side quests involving Sorcerer Thops, Diallos, and Rya.

The short version

What matters

  • •Secure the first Academy Glintstone Key from behind Smarag to enter Raya Lucaria and advance toward the Altus Plateau.
  • •Collect the Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right) from Albus at the Village of the Albinaurics to initiate key late-game access.
  • •Purchase Rya's Necklace from Blackguard Big Boggart to preserve his questline and gain access to Boiled Prawns.
  • •Loot the spare Academy Glintstone Key from the church chandelier to complete Sorcerer Thops's quest and gain Thops's Barrier.

Common mistakes

  • •Killing Blackguard Big Boggart at Boilprawn Shack instead of paying 1,000 runes locks you out of Boiled Crabs and his summon sign.
  • •Burning the Erdtree before obtaining the right half of the Haligtree Secret Medallion locks out the Ensha ambush and Royal Remains Armor.
  • •Attempting to damage Glintstone Dragon Smarag with magic spells rather than physical strikes will deal negligible damage due to his high magic resistance.
  • •Advancing past Altus Plateau into the Mountaintops before assisting Yura on the northern broken bridge permanently forfeits the Raptor of the Mists Ash of War.

What you can skip

  • •You can safely bypass the battle with Glintstone Dragon Smarag by quietly grabbing the Academy Glintstone Key from behind him.
  • •You can ignore the Three Sisters and Caria Manor completely if you are solely focused on reaching the Altus Plateau via the Ruin-Strewn Precipice.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Southern Shore to Irith

    When you exit Stormveil Castle into the Lake-Facing Cliffs, rest at the first Site of Grace, speak with Hyetta, and visit the Church of Irith to meet Sorcerer Thops. Then, descend past the Nomadic Merchant camp down into the flooded marsh basin at the southern lake shore. Before leaving, check beneath the throne room exit of Stormveil to loot the first Shabriri Grape.

    Tip: Thops will sell basic sorceries and explain that he needs a spare key to enter the Academy.

    Watch out: Missing the Shabriri Grape by the throne room exit will delay Hyetta's pilgrimage toward the Frenzied Flame.

  2. 2

    South-Central Pavilions and the Shacks

    Ride north across the marsh toward Laskyar Ruins and the Scenic Isle to meet Patches and find Rya waiting inside a stone bird pavilion. Head west into Boilprawn Shack to confront Blackguard Big Boggart and retrieve Rya's stolen necklace, then locate White-Mask Varré at Rose Church and find Adan, Thief of Fire inside the nearby Malefactor's Evergaol. Before moving deeper into the lake, journey southwest into the dark caverns beneath the plateau to explore the Village of the Albinaurics and speak with Latenna at Slumbering Wolf’s Shack.

    Tip: Pay Blackguard Big Boggart 1,000 runes for the necklace rather than slaying him to keep his shop and future summon sign available.

    Watch out: Slaying Blackguard Big Boggart locks out his vendor inventory, including Boiled Prawns and Boiled Crabs.

  3. 3

    Central Marsh to Academy Gate Town

    Push north into the sunken ruins of the Academy Gate Town to grab the local Map stele and speak to Diallos while he mourns his slain servant Lanya. Ride up to the South Raya Lucaria Gate to find the massive magical barrier sealed tight, then examine the corpse resting against it to claim the Meeting Place Map. Before leaving the area, swing east toward the Purified Ruins to uncover the hidden underground cellar.

    Tip: Smashed floorboards inside the Purified Ruins lead directly down to a hidden room containing a Shabriri Grape.

    Watch out: Answering White-Mask Varré incorrectly at Rose Church can disrupt progression for his Bloody Finger recruitment.

  4. 4

    Western Shallows and the Glintstone Key

    Ride west from the Academy toward the Temple Quarter and Crystalline Island, where the sleeping Glintstone Dragon Smarag slumbers beside towering blue crystals. Dash behind the dragon to grab the first Academy Glintstone Key, or fight the beast using physical and thrust attacks while remaining mobile on Torrent. Before heading inside the legacy dungeon, visit the Four Belfries on the western ridge and solve the Erudition puzzle at the Converted Tower.

    Key fight: Glintstone Dragon Smarag

    Tip: Smarag is heavily resistant to magic, so focus on heavy physical blows to his head to trigger posture breaks.

    Watch out: Fighting Smarag while dismounted leaves you extremely vulnerable to his wide-sweeping Glintstone breath attacks.

  5. 5

    Unlocking the Academy and the Northern Span

    Approach the blue barrier at the South Raya Lucaria Gate and use your new key to warp through to the Main Academy Gate. Head across the broken bridge to the north to locate a red summon sign and aid Bloody Finger Hunter Yura in vanquishing his quarry, earning the Raptor of the Mists Ash of War. After exploring the rooftops inside the Church of the Cuckoo to obtain the second Academy Glintstone Key from a chandelier, deliver it back to Sorcerer Thops at the Church of Irith.

    Tip: Giving Thops the spare key causes him to return to the Academy, where he leaves behind Thops's Barrier and his bell bearing outside the classroom.

    Watch out: Advancing past the Altus Plateau and Mountaintops before assisting Yura on the broken bridge will permanently lock you out of Raptor of the Mists.

  6. 6

    Western Ridge and Caria Manor

    Travel up the northern road of the western ridge past War Counselor Iji, who can upgrade your armaments and sell valuable crests. Infiltrate the estate of Caria Manor, fighting through the grounds until you reach the upper manor garden to face Royal Knight Loretta. Once Loretta is defeated, pass through the manor into the Three Sisters sub-region to pledge your service to Ranni the Witch.

    Key fight: Royal Knight Loretta

    Tip: War Counselor Iji sells Somber Smithing Stones, making him an invaluable stop before tackling high-level manor enemies.

    Watch out: Striking Iji or progressing Ranni's path without exhausting his dialogue can interrupt helpful advice and shop access.

  7. 7

    The Eastern Plateau, Frenzy Village, and the Ravine

    Ascend along the Bellum Highway toward the Grand Lift of Dectus, detouring through the madness-afflicted Frenzied Flame Village up to the Church of Inhibition. Defeat the invader Festering Fingerprint Vyke near the church to secure his war spear and the Fingerprint Grape, then drop down into the northern water basin of Liurnia. Follow this sheer chasm into the Ravine and scale the Ruin-Strewn Precipice to find an alternate route leading directly into the Altus Plateau.

    Tip: Stagger Vyke out of his weapon arts with heavy jumping attacks to avoid suffering massive madness buildup through your guard.

    Watch out: Lingering in the open line of sight near the Frenzied Flame Village causes continuous madness accumulation that rapidly burns through your health.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Rya's Necklace from Blackguard Big Boggart

Confronting Blackguard Big Boggart at Boilprawn Shack

  • Buy Rya's Necklace for 1,000 runes→ Befriends him, unlocks purchasing Boiled Prawns and later Boiled Crabs, permits summoning him against Magma Wyrm Makar, and relocates him to the Leyndell moat
  • Kill him→ Drops the necklace, Iron Ball, and Iron Mask, but permanently breaks his storyline and prevents buying Boiled Crabs

Most players pick: Buy Rya's Necklace for 1,000 runes

Telling Hyetta the Truth

Talking to Hyetta at the Gate Town Bridge

  • Tell her what the grapes actually are→ She gags and retches before deciding to persist with her Frenzied Flame pilgrimage
  • Withhold the truth→ She stays unaware of their nature and carries on with her Frenzied Flame pilgrimage

Key fights
Glintstone Dragon Smarag

Enemies: Glintstone Dragon Smarag

Threats: High-damage magic breath sweeps and aerial stomps

Weaknesses and resistances: Piercing damage, strike damage to the head; highly resistant to magic

Tactics: Mount Torrent to circle behind its left hind leg; dodge breath attacks by riding outward, then close in to strike the head when it lands

Rewards: Dragon Heart, 14,000 Runes

Royal Knight Loretta

Enemies: Royal Knight Loretta

Threats: Greatbow magic snipes, sweeping polearm glintstone attacks

Weaknesses and resistances: Lightning, Piercing; highly resistant to magic

Tactics: Roll under her long-range magic bow shots rather than sprinting; stay close to her horse's flanks to punish slow recovery frames

Rewards: Loretta's Greatbow, Ash of War: Loretta's Slash, 10,000 Runes

Enemies
Giant Crayfish
Weak to Sleep, LightningSteal / drop Lump of Flesh

Capable of sniping players from extreme distances with high-velocity water jets while mounted.

Royal Revenant
Weak to Healing Incantations, HolySteal / drop Ghost Glovewort

Attacks with rapid, erratic flailing; casting any multi-target area-of-effect heal spell nearby deals massive direct damage and immediately stuns it.

Marionette Soldier
Weak to LightningSteal / drop Marionette Armor pieces

Prone to berserk melee flurries when their health drops or when triggered by repeated hits.

Shops and people
  • Miriel, Pastor of Vows: Sells Sorceries and Incantations; accepts Magic and Prayerbook scrolls; provides absolution using Celestial Dew to reset NPC hostility.
  • Master Smith Iji: Sells Somber Smithing Stones [1] through [4] and the Carian Filigreed Crest; upgrades armaments up to max standard level.
  • Blackguard Big Boggart: Sells Boiled Prawn (boosts physical damage negation) after purchasing Rya's necklace from him.

Tips
  • The shallow swamp water slows dismounted running speed and lowers lightning resistance while increasing fire defense; fight mounted on Torrent to maintain mobility against the snipers and giant crustaceans.
