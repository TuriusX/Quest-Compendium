Weeping Peninsula

ELDEN RING guide · An optional southern region accessible early
← All areas in this guide

The Weeping Peninsula is a rainy, somber sub-region located directly south of Limgrave across the Bridge of Sacrifice. It serves as an ideal early-game training ground filled with upgrade materials, multiple Sacred Tears, and minor dungeons. The primary objective here is exploring Castle Morne at the southernmost tip to assist Edgar and Irina.

The short version

What matters

  • •Three Sacred Tears (Church of Pilgrimage, Fourth Church of Marika, Callu Baptismal Church) and a Golden Seed provide massive early flask upgrades.
  • •Radagon's Scarseal from the Weeping Evergaol gives substantial early attribute boosts, while Oridys's Rise grants a bonus Memory Stone.
  • •Clearing Castle Morne grants the legendary Grafted Blade Greatsword and advances Edgar's storyline for a future Shabriri Grape.

Common mistakes

  • •Killing Irina when you first meet her; doing so permanently locks you out of Hyetta's questline later in the playthrough.
  • •Attacking Castellan Edgar early for his +8 Banished Knight's Halberd, which ruins early multiplayer matchmaking brackets.
  • •Attempting the Minor Erdtree Avatar without Fire damage, which needlessly drags out a boss fight that is otherwise trivialized by fire.

What you can skip

  • •Exploring the high-risk roads and fields at night if you are unprepared for surprise encounters with the Night's Cavalry or Deathbird.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Crossing the Bridge to Irina

    When you cross the heavily fortified Bridge of Sacrifice past the Godrick Soldiers and ballista turret, follow the road south to find Irina resting on a rock. Speak with her to receive Irina's Letter and agree to deliver it to her father Edgar at Castle Morne.

    Tip: You can bypass the fortified bridge entirely by dropping down along the cliffside ruins southwest of the Forlorn Hound Evergaol in south Limgrave.

    Watch out: Attacking or killing Irina here permanently prevents Hyetta from appearing later in the game.

  2. 2

    Eastern Plateau and Demi-Human Forest

    Head east up the plateau to Oridys's Rise, solve the puzzle to seek three wise beasts, and climb to the top to claim your Memory Stone. Afterward, venture into the Demi-Human Forest Ruins to eliminate the swarms and defeat Demi-Human Queen Gilika for the Demi-Human Queen's Staff and Crystal Burst sorcery.

    Tip: Clear out the smaller Demi-Humans first so you can exploit Gilika's slow movement and break her poise uninterrupted.

  3. 3

    Central Plateau and the Minor Erdtree

    Ascend the central bluffs to Ailing Village to loot The Flame of Frenzy from a ruined house, then head directly south to Callu Baptismal Church to pick up a Sacred Tear. Push onto the high central ridge overlooking the region to challenge the Erdtree Avatar guarding the Minor Erdtree.

    Key fight: Erdtree Avatar (Weeping Peninsula)

    Tip: The Erdtree Avatar is extremely weak to fire, and sprinting laterally will safely avoid its homing holy projectiles.

  4. 4

    Northern Bluffs and Western Plains

    Ride north to the cliff edge to collect the Sacred Tear at the Church of Pilgrimage, then cut across to the western side of the peninsula to the Fourth Church of Marika for another Sacred Tear. Clear the subterranean crypt of Tombsward Ruins to grab the Winged Scythe behind an illusory wall, unlock the Weeping Evergaol using one Stonesword Key to defeat the Ancient Hero of Zamor for Radagon's Scarseal, and track down the walking Wandering Mausoleum pacing the western watery field.

    Tip: Use strike weapons or Crystal Darts on the Erdtree Burial Watchdog in nearby catacombs to trigger friendly-fire confusion, and roll forward through the Ancient Hero of Zamor's frost breaths.

  5. 5

    Southwestern Coast and Tower of Return

    Ride down to the Isolated Merchant’s Shack on the western coast to purchase a Lantern and the Zweihander, then investigate the underground cellar of Witchbane Ruins where Sorceress Sellen's real body is bound. Continue south along the coast to the elevated Tower of Return and climb to the summit to find a chest.

    Tip: Opening the chest at the top of the Tower of Return ensnares you in a teleportation trap that transports you straight to the Divine Bridge in Leyndell, Royal Capital.

  6. 6

    The Ramparts of Castle Morne

    Rest at the Castle Morne Rampart Grace where you can loot a Golden Seed beside a glowing sapling and leap up the nearby Spiritspring to obtain the Great Turtle Shell Shield from a watchtower. Speak with the Nomadic Merchant beside the rampart, then head down the southern road past the giant archer golem and the Map Stele to enter Castle Morne, looting the Claymore from a storehouse chest and climbing to an isolated tower to give Irina's Letter to Castellan Edgar.

    Tip: Note that Night's Cavalry and the Deathbird only spawn along the roads of the peninsula at night.

    Watch out: Do not kill Edgar on the tower; acquiring his +8 Banished Knight's Halberd early prematurely spikes your multiplayer matchmaking bracket and disrupts his questline.

  7. 7

    Reclaiming Castle Morne

    Drop down to the beach behind Castle Morne and pass through the mist to defeat Leonine Misbegotten, which rewards you with the Grafted Blade Greatsword and 3,800 Runes. Speak to Edgar atop his tower once more, then return to the road past the Bridge of Sacrifice where he weeps over Irina's slain body to complete the sequence and ensure he invades later as Edgar the Revenger in Liurnia.

    Key fight: Leonine Misbegotten

    Tip: Summon Castellan Edgar outside the fog gate and utilize heavy jumping attacks or guard counters to easily break Leonine Misbegotten's low poise.

    Watch out: Killing Edgar out of sequence forfeits the natural invasion later in Liurnia that yields a Shabriri Grape for Hyetta's questline.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Irina's Fate

Meeting Irina along the road past the Bridge of Sacrifice

  • Deliver her letter to Edgar at Castle Morne→ Progresses her quest, causing Edgar to discover her body after you clear the castle and enabling Edgar to invade later in Liurnia to drop a Shabriri Grape and a +8 Banished Knight's Halberd
  • Kill Irina immediately→ Yields 500 Runes, her letter, and a corpse you can use to soak the Lord of Blood's Favor for White Mask Varré, but permanently stops Hyetta from ever spawning

Most players pick: Deliver her letter to Edgar at Castle Morne

Killing her prematurely locks you out of Hyetta's questline.

Edgar's Confrontation

Meeting Castellan Edgar atop Castle Morne

  • Hand over Irina's Letter and kill the Leonine Misbegotten→ Edgar leaves Castle Morne to grieve at Irina's corpse, later invading at Liurnia as Edgar the Revenger to drop a Shabriri Grape and a +8 Banished Knight's Halberd
  • Slay Edgar in Castle Morne on the spot→ Instantly receive a Shabriri Grape and the +8 Banished Knight's Halberd, but prematurely pushes your character into a higher multiplayer matchmaking bracket

Most players pick: Hand over Irina's Letter and kill the Leonine Misbegotten

Picking up Edgar's +8 weapon immediately limits early-game online matchmaking.

Key fights
Leonine Misbegotten

Enemies: Leonine Misbegotten

Threats: Fast leaping strikes, multi-hit sword combos, and roaring poise buffs

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Fire and physical Slash; prone to stance breaks

Tactics: Use heavy jump attacks or guard counters to break poise, or summon spirits to interrupt its continuous attack strings

Rewards: Grafted Blade Greatsword, Runes

Erdtree Avatar (Weeping Peninsula)

Enemies: Erdtree Avatar

Threats: Staff slams, jumping butt-stomps, and tracking golden projectiles

Weaknesses and resistances: Extremely weak to Fire; resistant to Holy

Tactics: Dodge roll through the horizontal staff swings, circle behind when it leaps into the air to avoid the golden splash, and retaliate with fire-infused attacks

Rewards: Opaline Bubbletear, Crimsonburst Crystal Tear, Runes

Shops and people
  • Nomadic Merchant (Castle Morne Rampart): Sells Cracked Pot, Stonesword Key, Bastard Sword, Light Crossbow, Iron Helmet, and standard crafting materials
  • Isolated Merchant (Weeping Peninsula): Sells Lantern, Zweihander, Stonesword Key, Lost Ashes of War, and Arrow types

Tips
  • Visit the three churches across the peninsula early; gathering their Sacred Tears provides an immediate and massive boost to your flask healing potency.
  • Purchase the Lantern from the Isolated Merchant on the western coast so you can explore dark tombs and caves without sacrificing an offhand weapon or shield slot.
