Weeping Peninsula

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the southern region

The Weeping Peninsula is a rainy region directly south of Limgrave, accessed across the Bridge of Sacrifice. It is an ideal early-game area packed with three Sacred Tears, several dungeons, and Castle Morne at its southernmost point. Players should clear this zone to level up weapons, upgrade the Flask of Tears, and complete Irina's initial questline before tackling Stormveil Castle.

Items

  • Flask of Wondrous Physick Tear (Opaline Bubbletear): Dropped by the Erdtree Avatar at the Minor Erdtree
  • Flask of Wondrous Physick Tear (Crimsonburst Crystal Tear): Dropped by the Erdtree Avatar at the Minor Erdtree
  • Sacred Tear: Found at the altar inside the Callu Baptismal Church
  • Sacred Tear: Found at the altar inside the Church of Pilgrimage
  • Sacred Tear: Found at the altar inside the Fourth Church of Marika
  • Grafted Blade Greatsword: Rewarded upon defeating the Leonine Misbegotten boss at Castle Morne
  • Memory Stone: Found inside a chest at the top of Oridys's Rise
  • Irina's Letter MISSABLE: Given by Irina sitting near the road just south of the Bridge of Sacrifice

Secrets

  • Oridys's Rise seal | Unlocked by hitting three spectral turtles nearby: one directly behind the player on the path, one hidden in a bush to the left, and one invisible in the center of the shallow pond to the right.
  • Tower of Return chest trap | Opening the treasure chest atop the Tower of Return in the southwest teleports the player directly to the Divine Bridge in Leyndell, Royal Capital.

Shops and people

  • Irina: Initiates a delivery quest involving her father, Edgar, at Castle Morne
  • Edgar: Castle Morne castellan who accepts Irina's Letter and can be summoned for the area boss
  • Nomadic Merchant (Castle Morne Rampart): Sells the Cracked Pot, Bastard Sword, Light Crossbow, and a Stonesword Key
  • Isolated Merchant (Weeping Peninsula): Sells the Lantern, Zweihander, and Arteria Leaf from his shack on the far western coast

Tips

  • Acquire the Lantern from the Isolated Merchant on the western shore immediately to navigate dark caves without sacrificing an off-hand slot.
  • Look for the three Sacred Tears in the churches across this region early, as they significantly boost your flask restoration potency for the rest of the early game.
  • Aim for the legs of the Wandering Mausoleum found in the west to knock off the skull-like barnacles, causing it to lower so you can duplicate a remembrance.
