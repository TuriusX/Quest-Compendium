Ordina, Liturgical Town

ELDEN RING guide · Solving the puzzle to enter the Haligtree

Ordina, Liturgical Town is a deserted settlement in the Consecrated Snowfield serving as the gateway to Miquella's Haligtree. To break the seal on the grand staircase, you must enter an evergaol dimension and light four specific candle statues scattered throughout the town. Completing this puzzle opens a teleporter that sends you directly to the Haligtree canopy.

Items

  • Black Knife Set: Under the arches beneath the large stone staircase at the back of the town

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Albinauric Wolfback ArcherPhysical damageStationary snipers perched on rooftops firing high-damage tracking arrows
Black Knife AssassinStrike damageLurks completely invisible at ground level in the evergaol to backstab the player

Tips

  • Equip the Sentry's Torch in either hand to reveal the invisible Black Knife Assassins while exploring the evergaol.
  • Lighted candles remain lit even if you die, meaning you do not need to light all four statues in a single life.
  • Use a Greatbow with Knockback or the Jar Cannon to knock the Albinauric Archers off the high rooftops from a safe distance.
  • Three of the candle statues are located on high rooftops, while the fourth is placed in a small square at ground level.
