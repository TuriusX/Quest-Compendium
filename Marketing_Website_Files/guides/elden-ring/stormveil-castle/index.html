Stormveil Castle

ELDEN RING guide · The first major legacy dungeon encountered ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Stormveil Castle is the first legacy dungeon in Elden Ring, looming over northwestern Limgrave and serving as the domain of the demigod Godrick the Grafted. Players can infiltrate either through a breach in the outer cliffside guided by Gatekeeper Gostoc or by charging straight through the heavily fortified main gate. Navigating its ramparts, wine cellars, rooftops, and deep crypts culminates in confronting Margit, the Fell Omen at its threshold and Godrick the Grafted in the inner court.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating Margit, the Fell Omen and Godrick the Grafted earns you Godrick's Great Rune and the Remembrance of the Grafted.
  • •Grabbing the Iron Whetblade, Godslayer's Seal, Godskin Prayerbook, and two Golden Seeds provides massive early-game character upgrades.
  • •Talking to Nepheli Loux before Godrick allows you to summon her and advances her storyline toward receiving the Arsenal Charm.

Common mistakes

  • •Slaying Gatekeeper Gostoc prematurely; although it prevents his 30% death tax on lost runes, it permanently cuts off an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone later.
  • •Forgetting to collect the Chrysalids' Memento before Roderika leaves Stormhill Shack, which locks you out of delivering it directly to her.
  • •Rolling backwards away from Godrick's Phase 2 dragon head; always roll forward underneath the flamethrower cone to strike his flanks safely.

What you can skip

  • •Charging Gostoc's main gate gauntlet, as taking the cliffside route lets you pick off enemies with far less incoming artillery damage.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Stormveil Main Gate

    Follow the path through Stormhill past Stormgate, proceed through Castleward Tunnel, and defeat Margit, the Fell Omen to access the bridge leading to the Stormveil Main Gate Site of Grace. Speak to Gatekeeper Gostoc in the adjacent room to decide whether to charge through the main gate or take the cliffside breach. Choose the side opening in the wall to follow a safer approach around the castle's outer edge.

    Key fight: Margit, the Fell Omen

    Tip: Taking the cliffside path prevents being obliterated by the main gate's ballista batteries, warhorns, and barricaded Exile Soldiers.

    Watch out: Killing Gostoc stops his 30% rune theft on death, but locks you out of purchasing an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone from him late in the game.

  2. 2

    Stormveil Cliffside

    Climb along the windy cliffs past horn-blowing Exile Soldiers and Bladed Warhawks until you reach the Stormveil Cliffside Site of Grace. Ascend the outer wooden scaffoldings toward the castle's wine cellar, dealing with bird attacks along the sheer drops. Loot the ledge corpse on the scaffolding before stepping indoors to secure your traversal tools.

    Tip: Eliminate horn-blowing Exile Soldiers immediately to prevent them from alerting reinforcements.

  3. 3

    Wine Cellar & Dark Storeroom

    Enter the multi-level wooden wine cellar, descend to the bottom floor to grab the Hookclaws, and climb toward the upper locked doors. Gatekeeper Gostoc will lock you inside the pitch-black storeroom with a Banished Knight, where you can find the Rusty Key on a corpse and the Curved Sword Talisman inside a chest. Climb to the upper floor of this trapped room to loot the Mimic's Veil from another chest before unlocking the exit with your new key.

    Tip: Break poise with heavy jumping attacks or fish for backstabs when fighting the Banished Knight in the dark.

    Watch out: Gostoc stalks you here and locks the door behind you; be prepared for an immediate close-quarters fight.

  4. 4

    Rampart Tower & Exterior Roofs

    Ascend to the Rampart Tower Site of Grace and step outside onto the exterior walkways patrolled by Bladed Warhawks tossing explosive barrels. Leap across the parapets and roof gaps toward the highest watchtower to claim the Claw Talisman, or strike the wall near the elevator shaft to reveal hidden drops across the ramparts. Navigate down across the high wall walkways to reach the chapel where Sorcerer Rogier sits praying.

    Tip: Detonate the explosive barrels carried by the Bladed Warhawks from afar using ranged spells or arrows before they reach you.

  5. 5

    Larder & Scion Butchery Hall

    Drop down from the chapel into the cavernous dining hall draped with hanging limbs and patrolled by a Grafted Scion. Clear the side shed near the butchery hall to find the Chrysalids' Memento on a corpse pile, then enter the adjoining hall beneath the giant painting of Godfrey to pick up the Highland Axe. Use a Stonesword Key on the armory's Imp Statue fog door to claim the Iron Whetblade and the Misericorde.

    Tip: Rain ranged attacks down onto the Grafted Scion from the elevated wooden walkways to defeat it safely.

    Watch out: Roderika moving to Roundtable Hold on her own replaces the Chrysalids' Memento at the corpse pile with the Crimson Hood.

  6. 6

    Courtyard Barricades & Shortcuts

    Fight through the sunken courtyard filled with flamethrowers, Exile Soldiers, barricades, and guard dogs to reach the Lift Side Chamber Site of Grace. Open the Imp Statue white fog wall down a courtyard stairwell using a Stonesword Key to obtain the Godslayer's Seal and Godskin Prayerbook, then pick up the Prophecy Painting in a nearby side room. Ride the shortcut lift from the courtyard to reconnect this central avenue back up to the Rampart Tower.

    Tip: Head toward the high walkway near the Divine Tower gateway to find the Boltdrake Talisman on a hanging corpse, but beware the agile Lion Guardian guarding the gate path.

  7. 7

    Castle Crypt & Subterranean Depths

    Drop through the exterior courtyard ledges beneath the Lift Side Chamber Site of Grace to enter the castle's lowest subterranean crypt. Slay the Ulcerated Tree Spirit in the underground depths to earn a Golden Seed, and defeat the nearby Teardrop Scarab to acquire the Rancorcall sorcery. Run past the boss remains to find the Prince of Death's Pustule resting beside the gigantic mutated face of Godwyn.

    Tip: Stick tight to the flank and ribs of the Ulcerated Tree Spirit to evade its lunging jaw snaps, and use Fire attacks to exploit its severe elemental weakness.

  8. 8

    Secluded Cell & Godrick's Arena

    Rest at the Secluded Cell Site of Grace, take down the courtyard Troll, and collect the Golden Seed growing near the glowing ethereal sapling. Speak to Nepheli Loux in the nearby room so her golden summon sign appears outside the golden fog wall, then step through into the grand throne room arena to confront Godrick the Grafted. After defeating Godrick, head down into the lower throne room basement exit toward Liurnia of the Lakes to collect the Shabriri Grape from the floor.

    Key fight: Godrick the Grafted

    Tip: Jump over Godrick's ground shockwaves during Phase 1, and dodge directly underneath his grafted dragon arm when he spews fire in Phase 2.

    Watch out: Missing your conversation with Nepheli Loux beforehand prevents you from summoning her for the boss fight and halts her immediate quest progress.

Stormveil Castle Entrance

Talking to Gatekeeper Gostoc at the Stormveil Main Gate

  • Accept Gostoc's advice→ He opens the breach in the wall leading along the cliffs for a safer, methodical path through the ramparts
  • Insist on opening the gate→ Gostoc opens the main portcullis, forcing a direct rush through an intense gauntlet of ballistae, barricades, and heavy soldier groups

Most players pick: Accept Gostoc's advice

Gatekeeper Gostoc's Fate

Meeting Gatekeeper Gostoc in Stormveil Castle

  • Slay him→ Obtains his Bell Bearing and immediately halts his passive theft of 30% of your dropped Runes upon death, but permanently locks you out of purchasing an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone from him later
  • Leave him alive→ He continues stealing 30% of your Runes whenever you die in the castle and locks you in a dark room with a Banished Knight, but survives to sell an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone at the conclusion of Nepheli Loux's and Kenneth Haight's quests

Most players pick: Leave him alive

Killing him early cannot be undone and permanently forfeits an Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone vendor purchase.

Key fights
Margit, the Fell Omen

Enemies: Margit, the Fell Omen

Threats: Delayed overhead staff slams, conjured holy daggers, jumping hammer crush, sweeping tail strikes

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Slash and Bleed; resistant to Holy

Tactics: Dodge into his delayed swings rather than early; use Margit's Shackle to pin him twice during phase 1; summon Sorcerer Rogier outside the fog gate for distraction

Rewards: 12,000 Runes, Talisman Pouch

Godrick the Grafted

Enemies: Godrick the Grafted

Threats: Ground-pound shockwaves, whirlwind projectile rolls, grafted dragon fire breath in phase 2

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Standard, Slash, Strike, and Bleed; resistant to Holy

Tactics: Jump over his two-part earthquake shockwaves; roll towards his left side when he unleashes the flamethrower sweep in phase 2; summon Nepheli Loux from her gold sign outside the gate

Rewards: 20,000 Runes, Godrick's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Grafted

Tips

  • If you choose the side path, Gatekeeper Gostoc stalks you and steals 30% of your dropped Runes each time you die until Godrick is slain.
