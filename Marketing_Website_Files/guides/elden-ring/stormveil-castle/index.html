Stormveil Castle

ELDEN RING guide · Storming the first legacy dungeon

Stormveil Castle is the first major legacy dungeon of the Lands Between, guarded by Margit the Fell Omen and ruled by the shardbearer Godrick the Grafted. You can either charge straight through the trapped main gate or take a hidden cliffside path to infiltrate the castle's interiors, ramparts, and courtyards. Exploring thoroughly allows you to recruit allies, collect essential upgrade materials, and eventually defeat Godrick to claim his Great Rune.

Items

  • Stonesword Key: Found inside the armory room accessed near the Rampart Tower site of grace
  • Claw Talisman: Located at the top of a high watchtower accessed by jumping along the rampart rooftops
  • Godrick's Great Rune: Dropped directly upon defeating the area boss Godrick the Grafted
  • Remembrance of the Grafted: Rewarded directly upon defeating Godrick the Grafted
  • Shabariri Grape: Found down the stairs in the throne room directly past the Godrick boss arena
  • Godskin Prayerbook: Behind an imp statue fog door in the courtyard cellar near the ballistae
  • Godslayer's Seal: Found in a chest behind the imp statue fog door alongside the Godskin Prayerbook

Secrets

  • Below the castle lies an ulcerated tree root monster and a giant petrified face; reach it by jumping down the ledges from the Liftside Chamber courtyard.
  • You can jump over the broken rampart wall near the birds throwing explosive barrels to access rooftops leading to an isolated tower.

Shops and people

  • Gatekeeper Gostoc: Buckler, Caestus, Bandit armor pieces, and a consumable Silver-Pickled Fowl Foot
  • Sorcerer Rogier: Sells the Ashes of War: Glintstone Pebble, Carian Greatsword, and Spinning Weapon

Tips

  • Choosing the side cliff path recommended by Gostoc is significantly easier than rushing the heavily fortified front gate.
  • You can summon Nepheli Loux for the Godrick boss fight if you speak with her in the side room near the secluded cell beforehand.
  • Defeating Gatekeeper Gostoc before clearing the castle stops him from stealing a percentage of your runes every time you die in Stormveil.
