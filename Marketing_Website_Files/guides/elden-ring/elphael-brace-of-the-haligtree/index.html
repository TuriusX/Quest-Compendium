Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree

ELDEN RING guide · Reaching the city at the base of the Haligtree

Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree is a massive fortified city built into the trunk of Miquella's Haligtree, reached by descending past the Loretta boss encounter at the Haligtree Town. Players must navigate multi-layered parapets, rot-filled canals, and heavily guarded courtyards to reach the Roots. The path leads to the final boss of the area, Malenia, Blade of Miquella, and concludes Millicent's questline.

Items

  • Cleanrot Knight Finlay Ashes: In a chest guarded by a Cleanrot Knight inside a room accessed from the rooftops near the Prayer Room Site of Grace
  • Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman: Inside a chest on the high rafters of the indoor pest chapel before the drainage canal
  • Miquella's Needle MISSABLE: Rewarded after defeating Malenia and using the Unalloyed Gold Needle on the scarlet bloom she leaves behind
  • Marika's Soreseal: In a chest inside an altar room guarded by an Erdtree Avatar, requiring a Stonesword Key near the Elphael Inner Wall

Secrets

  • Millicent's quest climax can be found near the Drainage Canal Site of Grace, where you must defeat a Lesser Ulcerated Tree Spirit in a rot pool to reveal two summon signs to either assist or challenge her.

Tips

  • Equip the Flame, Cleanse Me incantation or stock up on Preserving Boluses before dropping into the lower canal areas due to extensive Scarlet Rot hazards.
  • Beware the lower ground level full of Royal Revenants; casting high-tier AoE Healing incantations near them will instantly stun them and deal massive damage.
