Mountaintops of the Giants

ELDEN RING guide · Reaching the snowy peaks after Leyndell

The Mountaintops of the Giants is a frozen endgame region unlocked after defeating Morgott the Omen King in Leyndell, accessed using the Rold Medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold. Your primary objective is to cross the frozen peaks to reach the Forge of the Giants, where the Erdtree can be committed to the flame. Along the way, you face harsh blizzards, giant trolls, ancient dragons, and the monumental Fire Giant guarding the forge.

Items

  • Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right): Found in the Village of the Albinaurics in Liurnia, but used here at the Grand Lift of Rold
  • Rivers of Blood MISSABLE: Dropped by the invading NPC Bloody Finger Okina near the Church of Repose
  • Roar Medallion: Dropped by the Stonedigger Troll boss in the Limgrave Tunnels, useful here
  • Flame, Cleanse Me: Found in Liurnia of the Lakes, though essential for Frostbite throughout this region

Secrets

  • The hidden path to the Consecrated Snowfield can be unlocked at the Grand Lift of Rold by switching action prompts to hoist the Secret Haligtree Medallion instead of the Rold Medallion.

Shops and people

  • Finger Reader Enia: Trades Remembrance of the Fire Giant for the Giant's Red Braid whip or Burn, O Flame! incantation

Tips

  • Equip armor and talismans that boost robust Frostbite resistance, as the freezing weather and many local enemies build up Frostbite rapidly.
  • Defeat Bloody Finger Okina at the Church of Repose before slaying the Fire Giant, or the invasion will no longer trigger and you will miss the Rivers of Blood katana.
