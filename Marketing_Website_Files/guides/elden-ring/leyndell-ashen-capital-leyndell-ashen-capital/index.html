Leyndell, Ashen Capital (Leyndell, Ashen Capital)

ELDEN RING guide · Returning after burning the Erdtree

After unleashing Destined Death in Crumbling Farum Azula, you awaken in Leyndell, now completely buried under deep drifts of ash. You must traverse the ruined city toward the Erdtree throne to face the final gauntlet of bosses and complete the game. Most of the original Royal Capital is inaccessible, though a sewer opening still allows access to the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds.

Items

  • Erdtree's Favor +2: Resting on a tree root surrounded by ash in the courtyard guarded by three Lesser Ulcerated Tree Spirits, accessed from the Forbidden Lands lift
  • Crimson Amber Medallion +2: Found dropped down an open manhole beam near the Ashen Capital site of grace

Secrets

  • The Subterranean Shunning-Grounds can still be entered by jumping into an open manhole in the sea of ash near the Leyndell, Capital of Ash site of grace.

Tips

  • Equip Holy damage negation talismans such as the Haligdrake Talisman +2 or cast Lord's Divine Aid to drastically reduce incoming damage during the final boss battles.
  • Attack Sir Gideon Ofnir immediately while he is mid-monologue to take off a large portion of his health before he begins casting spells.
