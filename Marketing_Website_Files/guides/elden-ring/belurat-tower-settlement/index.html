Belurat, Tower Settlement

ELDEN RING guide · The first legacy dungeon of the expansion ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Belurat, Tower Settlement is the first major legacy dungeon of the Realm of Shadow, sprawling across layered stone streets, sewer channels, and decayed rooftops. You must navigate through phantom shadow undead, horned warriors, and spider-scorpions to open shortcuts back to the main gates. Ascending to the top of the settlement leads through the theatrical stage to face the Divine Beast Dancing Lion and gain access to the Tower of Shadow's sealed stairway.

The short version

What matters

  • •Defeating the Divine Beast Dancing Lion to obtain its Remembrance and the Divine Beast Head helm.
  • •Collecting the Storeroom Key and Well Depths Key across the rooftops to open the locked Storeroom and the sewer swamp.
  • •Gathering Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ashes throughout the settlement to raise your Realm of Shadow blessings.
  • •Donning the Divine Beast Head when conversing with Hornsent Grandam to secure the Watchful Spirit incantation and Scorpion Stew.

Common mistakes

  • •Talking to Hornsent Grandam without equipping the Divine Beast Head; she will brand you a butcher and refuse to grant her incantation or stew.
  • •Progressing the campaign to defeat Messmer the Impaler before visiting Grandam, which places her into a permanent catatonic state.
  • •Rolling through the Divine Beast Dancing Lion's sweeping breath and frost storms; jump over these ground-based attacks instead.

What you can skip

  • •Slogging through the toxic Well Depths swamp if you do not need the Poisoned Hand, Immunizing Horn Charm +2, or Great Glovewort.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Gatehouse Entry and Lower Plaza

    Advance through the massive entryway stairs from Gravesite Plain to rest at the Belurat, Tower Settlement Site of Grace. Push forward past the gatehouse into the lower fountain plaza, clearing out hiding shadow spirits along the rancid water canal and dried fountain basin. Before moving to the side stairs, pick up the Revered Spirit Ash in the lower quarters before the worship altar room, and take note of the locked well ladder in the plaza.

    Tip: Use light weapon attacks to immediately stagger and interrupt the fragile shadow inhabitants before their casters fire tracking light darts.

  2. 2

    Canal Ascent to Small Private Altar

    Climb the side stairs from the canal, dodging spells from shadow casters as you cross the stone archways over the street. Follow the covered walkway to find a small chapel room containing the Small Private Altar Site of Grace. Rest here to establish your central checkpoint, noting the locked Storeroom door situated nearby.

    Tip: Strike damage rapidly shatters the poise of stone Gravebirds you encounter along elevated walkways.

  3. 3

    Patrolled Main Street and Queelign's Ambush

    Step north from the Small Private Altar onto the main street, where a deadly Horned Warrior armed with a curved sword patrols. Divert into the adjacent open courtyard guarded by small shades to trigger an invasion by Fire Knight Queelign. Defeat the invader to claim the Crusade Insignia, then collect the Ash of War: Shriek of Sorrow resting at the base of the twisted tree.

    Tip: Stagger Queelign with heavy colossal weapons or exploit his vulnerability to bleed and frost to break through his fiery thrust bursts.

  4. 4

    Rooftops, Hidden Keys, and the Tower

    Ascend onto the upper rooftops and water channel, avoiding Gravebirds and scorpions along the crumbling roof tiles. Follow the water flume toward the waterfall alcove ladder to find the Storeroom Key, then drop onto the sloped tiled roofs leading down to an isolated lower balcony room to secure the Well Depths Key. Move through the broken spiral staircase tower to grab the Bone Bow at the bottom while burning the spider-scorpions, and collect another Revered Spirit Ash in the shrine near the rooftop transition.

    Tip: Fire damage disrupts spider-scorpions and prevents their toxic stinger attacks.

  5. 5

    The Well Depths Sewers

    Return to the lower fountain plaza and descend the well ladder using your Well Depths Key to access the toxic sewer swamp. Slog through the sludge to locate the Poisoned Hand lying in the muck. Head toward the deepest corner of the swamp to engage the Ulcerated Tree Spirit on solid ground, using fire and stance breaks to overcome it and secure the Immunizing Horn Charm +2.

    Tip: Stick to firm terrain along the swamp's edges to prevent lethal poison buildup during the Tree Spirit battle.

  6. 6

    Grand Staircase and Residential Quarters

    Ascend toward the upper residential quarters to find Miquella's Cross alongside a Scadutree Fragment, as well as the Dried Bouquet inside the adjacent building. Continue toward the main settlement lift to grab a Revered Spirit Ash resting on an elevated pot pedestal platform, then trigger the elevator to create a shortcut down to the Belurat, Tower Settlement Site of Grace. Continue upward to the Stagefront Site of Grace located directly outside the performance arena.

    Tip: Horned Warriors can be parried or backstabbed during their combo recovery frames if you avoid their jumping slashes.

  7. 7

    The Theater Arena Boss Fight

    Check the floor outside the boss fog at Stagefront to summon Redmane Freyja, Hornsent, both, or neither to aid your battle. Step into the arena to confront the Divine Beast Dancing Lion, jumping over its spinning frost sweeps and exploiting its weaknesses to fire, pierce, and bleed. Slay the beast to earn the Remembrance of the Dancing Lion and the Divine Beast Head helmet.

    Key fight: Divine Beast Dancing Lion

    Tip: Jump over the boss's wide spinning breath and frost attacks instead of rolling through them to avoid lingering hitboxes.

  8. 8

    Unlocking the Storeroom

    Return to the Small Private Altar, unlock the Storeroom door using the Storeroom Key, and don the Divine Beast Head before speaking to Hornsent Grandam. Receive her blessing to obtain the Watchful Spirit incantation and a portion of Scorpion Stew. You can now choose whether to consume the stew yourself or deliver it to Hornsent at Three-Path Cross for three Furnace Visages.

    Tip: Wear the Divine Beast Head so Grandam mistake you for a divine emissary rather than a trespassing butcher.

    Watch out: Speaking to Grandam without the Divine Beast Head causes her to curse you and withhold rewards; defeating Messmer the Impaler beforehand will render her completely catatonic.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Hornsent Grandam's Storeroom interaction

When meeting Hornsent Grandam inside the locked Storeroom near the Small Private Altar

  • Wear the Divine Beast Head helm→ She mistakes you for an emissary, dialogues with you, and gifts the Watchful Spirit incantation and Scorpion Stew
  • Speak to her without the Divine Beast Head→ She denounces you as a butcher sent by Messmer and will not interact
  • Slay Hornsent Grandam→ You obtain Watchful Spirit on the spot, but permanently forfeit future dialogue and additional stew

Most players pick: Wear the Divine Beast Head helm

Defeating Messmer the Impaler causes Grandam to fall into a catatonic state, cutting off this interaction

Divine Beast Dancing Lion phantom summons

At the Stagefront boss fog gate before fighting the Divine Beast Dancing Lion

  • Summon Redmane Freyja→ She assists during the encounter and gains extra dialogue regarding Hornsent at Three-Path Cross
  • Summon Hornsent→ He aids you in combat
  • Summon both or fight alone→ Adjusts your NPC combat support accordingly

Most players pick: Summon Redmane Freyja

Key fights
Divine Beast Dancing Lion

Enemies: Divine Beast Dancing Lion

Threats: Alternates phases between Wind, Lightning, and Frost storms; executes erratic aerial lunges, spinning bites, and widespread ground lightning bolts

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire, Hemorrhage, Pierce; highly resistant to Frostbite and Lightning

Tactics: Roll toward its body rather than away during its lunging bites; when it lifts its head to summon lightning, sprint in a wide circle; punish recovery frames immediately following its spinning breath sweep

Rewards: Remembrance of the Dancing Lion; Divine Beast Head

Enemies
Horned Warrior
Weak to Pierce damage, FrostbiteSteal / drop Horned Warrior Curved Sword; Divine Beast Helm

Highly aggressive dual-curved-sword elite who chains relentless multi-hit combos and uses aerial stomps.

Spider-Scorpion
Weak to FireSteal / drop Scorpion Liver

Lurks on ceilings and walls in dark chambers, lunging down to inflict heavy physical damage and poison.

Shadow Spellcaster
Weak to PhysicalSteal / drop Spiral horn projectile items

Perches on high balconies casting tracking spiral light sorceries that stagger you from long range.

Shops and people
  • Hornsent Grandam: Offers Gourmet Scorpion Stew and unique dialogue rewards after being spoken to while wearing the Divine Beast Head

Tips
  • Equip the Talisman of all Crucibles or boluses to deal with the toxic pools in the lower well before attempting to retrieve the Poisoned Hand.
  • Equipping the Divine Beast Head obtained from the dungeon boss is mandatory if you want Hornsent Grandam to acknowledge you as an emissary and give you her rewards.
