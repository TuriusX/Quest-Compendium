Volcano Manor

ELDEN RING guide · Infiltrating the legacy dungeon on Mt. Gelmir

Volcano Manor is a stronghold on Mt. Gelmir serving as the base for Tanith and her recusants who oppose the Erdtree. Players can join the faction to carry out assassination requests across the Lands Between or secretly infiltrate the interior legacy dungeon hidden behind illusory walls. Exploring the dungeon leads through the Prison Town, across lava-filled ruins, and culminates in a battle against Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy.

Items

  • Serpent-Hunter: Found on a corpse inside the boss arena for Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy, immediately after entering the fog gate
  • Drawing-Room Key MISSABLE: Given by Tanith after agreeing to join Volcano Manor
  • Rya's Necklace MISSABLE: Can be reclaimed in Liurnia and returned to Rya to initiate her invitation path to the Manor
  • Missionary's Cookbook [6]: Found on a corpse inside a jail cell in the Prison Town Church area
  • Somber Smithing Stone [7]: Found on a corpse hanging over the ledge near the iron cogwheel lift in the lava district

Secrets

  • In the first drawing room on the right side of the hallway opened by the Drawing-Room Key, striking the painting on the back-right wall reveals an illusory wall that opens access to the hidden legacy dungeon.
  • Defeating the Abductor Virgin enemy at the bottom of the Raya Lucaria Academy waterwheel by letting it kill you with its capture attack will transport you directly to the Subterranean Inquisition Chamber beneath Volcano Manor.

Shops and people

  • Tanith: Offers assassination contracts targeting other Tarnished across the world in exchange for unique armor, talismans, and weapons.
  • Knight Bernahl: Sells various Ashes of War from inside the drawing room once you join the Manor.

Tips

  • Equip the Flamedrake Talisman and use fire-negating buffs or consume Fireproof Dried Liver before traversing the molten lava areas to reduce fire damage.
  • The Serpent-Hunter requires no stat investments to wield effectively, making it the optimal weapon for any build when fighting Rykard.
  • Complete all assassination requests for Tanith and Bernahl before killing Rykard, as defeating the shardbearer causes all manor inhabitants to permanently leave.
