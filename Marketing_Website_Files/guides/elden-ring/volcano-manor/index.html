Volcano Manor

ELDEN RING guide · The legacy dungeon located on Mt. Gelmir ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Volcano Manor is a sprawling legacy dungeon hidden behind a sinister faction headquarters on Mt. Gelmir. Players can choose to join Tanith's covenant to hunt fellow Tarnished across the Lands Between, or explore the secret passage behind an illusory wall leading to the molten Prison Town and temple. Navigating the rooftops, lava-filled streets, and subterranean passages ultimately leads to the Audience Pathway to challenge Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy.

The short version

What matters

  • •Pledging allegiance to Lady Tanith grants the Drawing-Room Key, unlocking the secret dungeon path behind the first drawing room's illusory wall.
  • •Grabbing the Serpent-Hunter right inside the Audience Pathway fog gate allows you to unleash long-range wind blade attacks that trivialize Rykard.
  • •Defeating Godskin Noble rewards the Godskin Stitcher and Noble Presence while opening the path toward the upper manor balcony and Audience Pathway waygate.

Common mistakes

  • •Defeating Rykard before turning in contracts, which permanently locks you out of the Magma Shot, Serpentbone Blade, and Taker's Cameo.
  • •Overlooking the back-right wall in the first drawing room, forcing players to assume the manor is only an assassination hub rather than a full dungeon.
  • •Ignoring Lady Tanith in the boss arena after Rykard's fall, missing out on the Consort’s Set and Aspects of the Crucible: Breath.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Manor Entrance and Recusant Contracts

    When you arrive at the Volcano Manor Site of Grace, speak with Lady Tanith by the fireplace and pledge your allegiance to receive the Drawing-Room Key. Head down the hallway to unlock the drawing rooms, where you will find assassination letters alongside Knight Bernahl and Rya. Defeating target Old Knight Istvan awards Magma Shot, eliminating Rileigh the Idle yields the Serpentbone Blade, and finishing Juno Hoslow earns the Taker's Cameo from Tanith.

    Tip: Explore the upper banquet floor to trigger an invasion by Inquisitor Ghiza, which yields Ghiza's Wheel upon his defeat.

    Watch out: Defeating Rykard before completing these assassinations terminates all pending Manor contracts, locking you out of their rewards permanently.

  4. 4

    The Temple of Eiglay

    Cross the raised bridge shortcut and enter the cathedral to confront the Godskin Noble. Defeat him to activate the Temple of Eiglay Site of Grace, then loot the Serpent’s Amnion from the altar behind him and activate the altar shortcut lever. Take the newly unlocked lift up to the rafters, where you can spend a Stonesword Key on an imp statue seal to retrieve the Dagger Talisman and a Seedbed Curse.

    Key fight: Godskin Noble

    Tip: Put the Godskin Noble to sleep with St. Trina arrows or Sleep Pots, and hide behind the stone pillar bases when he starts his rolling attack.

    Watch out: Handing the Serpent’s Amnion to Rya in her secluded room triggers critical quest branches where killing her or administering the Tonic of Forgetfulness affects the acquisition of Zorayas's Letter.

  5. 5

    Balcony Waygate to the Shardbearer

    Drop down from the rafters area to loot the Ash of War: Royal Knight’s Resolve, then pass the staff-wielding snake mage inside the hanging cage shrine to grab the Man-Serpent Ashes. Step out onto the exterior stone torture chamber balcony and enter the glowing waygate to reach the Audience Pathway Site of Grace. Cross the boss fog into the magma chamber and immediately loot the Serpent-Hunter Great Spear from the corpse on your left to battle the God-Devouring Serpent and Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy.

    Key fight: Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

    Tip: Equip the Serpent-Hunter Great Spear right away, and chain its Great-Serpent Hunt skill during Phase 2 to constantly stagger Rykard out of his skull storms.

    Watch out: Killing Rykard immediately empties Volcano Manor, failing all unfinished contracts and causing Tanith to relocate to the boss arena.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Pledging Allegiance to Volcano Manor

Speaking to Lady Tanith upon first arriving in the manor lobby

  • Agree to join→ Lady Tanith gives you the Drawing-Room Key, granting access to the drawing rooms, assassinations, and NPC questlines
  • Refuse to join→ She withholds the Drawing-Room Key and locks you out of the drawing rooms until you speak with her again to accept

Most players pick: Agree to join

You can change your mind and accept at any time with no negative consequences.

Route to Reach Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

After obtaining the Drawing-Room Key

  • Finish all three manor assassination targets→ Lady Tanith teleports you directly to Rykard's audience chamber while securing all contract rewards (Magma Shot, Serpentbone Blade, Taker's Cameo)
  • Travel through the hidden Prison Town dungeon route→ Reach the Audience Pathway portal on the balcony independently to fight Rykard early, but defeating him prematurely permanently cancels all unfinished assassination quests from Tanith, Bernahl, and Patches

Most players pick: Finish all three manor assassination targets

Defeating Rykard serves as an absolute point of no return for the manor's assassination contracts.

Fate of Rya (Zorayas)

Finding Rya hiding in the secluded lava room beneath the Temple of Eiglay elevator

  • Slay her as she requests→ She dies and immediately drops the Daedicar's Woe talisman
  • Administer the Tonic of Forgetfulness→ She falls asleep, returns to the drawing room, and eventually departs leaving Daedicar's Woe
  • Refuse both and leave her alive→ Slay Rykard, return to talk to her in her hiding spot, and reload the area to receive both Daedicar's Woe and Zorayas's Letter

Most players pick: Refuse both and leave her alive

Giving her the tonic or slaying her locks you out of receiving Zorayas's Letter.

Lady Tanith at Rykard's Corpse

Finding Lady Tanith devouring Rykard in his boss arena after his defeat

  • Slay Lady Tanith→ Trigger an invasion by her Crucible Knight bodyguard; defeat him to earn Aspects of the Crucible: Breath, and loot Lady Tanith's body for the Consort's Set
  • Leave her undisturbed→ She stays in the arena consuming Rykard indefinitely, and you forego her armor and the incantation drop

Most players pick: Slay Lady Tanith

Key fights
Godskin Noble

Enemies: Godskin Noble

Threats: Black Flame spells, rapid thrusts with heavy rapier, rolling body slam, area-of-effect shockwaves

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to Sleep, Bleed, and Slash; resistant to Fire and Holy

Tactics: Sleep Pots completely incapacitate him, allowing massive posture breaks; pillars around the Temple of Eiglay block his rolling attack

Rewards: Godskin Stitcher, Black Flame Scourge

Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

Enemies: God-Devouring Serpent (Phase 1), Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy (Phase 2)

Threats: Phase 1: venom spit, heavy sweeps, grab attack. Phase 2: flaming skull storm, massive sweeping blade, earthquake strikes

Weaknesses and resistances: Weak to the Serpent-Hunter Great Spear

Tactics: Equip and two-hand the Serpent-Hunter found at the arena entrance; light and heavy attacks unleash extended ranged wind blades that repeatedly stagger both phases

Rewards: Remembrance of the Blasphemous, Rykard's Great Rune

Tips
  • Completing all assassination contracts for Tanith, Bernahl, and Patches before defeating Rykard is crucial, as defeating Rykard causes all Manor members to depart immediately.
  • Equipping the Serpent-Hunter does not require meeting its attribute requirements to use its specialized wind blade attacks against Rykard.
