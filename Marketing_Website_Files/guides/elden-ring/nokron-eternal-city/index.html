Nokron, Eternal City

ELDEN RING guide · An underground city accessed after defeating Radahn ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Nokron, Eternal City is an underground realm unlocked after defeating Starscourge Radahn causes a falling star to blast open a crater in East Limgrave. Players navigate crumbling rooftops, face the Mimic Tear, traverse the Ancestral Woods, and venture into the Night's Sacred Ground to acquire a pivotal quest item for Ranni the Witch. The zone also extends north towards the Siofra Aqueduct, leading to a gateway to Deeproot Depths.

The short version

What matters

  • •Obtain the Fingerslayer Blade from the crypt beneath the colossal skeleton throne in Night's Sacred Ground to progress Ranni's storyline.
  • •Spend a Stonesword Key along the upper walkways of the chapel in Night's Sacred Ground to secure the Mimic Tear Ashes.
  • •Collect the Black Whetblade from the altar in the chapel before the main boulevard to expand your affinity infusions.
  • •Rest inside the stone coffin at the Great Waterfall Basin after defeating the dual Valiant Gargoyles to access Deeproot Depths.

Common mistakes

  • •Handing the Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni before acquiring the Magic Scorpion Charm from Preceptor Seluvis, which immediately kills him and locks his quest.
  • •Bringing slashing, piercing, or status-effect weapons against the Valiant Gargoyles; switch to strike damage and sprint away when they telegraph their poison breath vomit.
  • •Entering the Mimic Tear boss fog fully geared; strip off all equipment prior to walking in so the boss duplicates an unarmed target, then re-equip your weapons.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Rooftop Infiltration to the Town Square

    After dropping down the floating debris in the East Limgrave crater, leap across the arched masonry and rooftop edges to reach the interior of a church. Exit this structure and press east-southeast across the plaza filled with Fallen Hawk Soldiers until you reach the Nokron, Eternal City Site of Grace. Scour the nearby gazebo to pick up the Ghost-Glovewort Picker’s Bell Bearing , and check the adjoining ruined building courtyard populated by Silver Tears to gather a Larval Tear.

    Tip: Silver Tears are weak to lightning damage and can be burst down quickly before they morph into humanoids or electric spears.

  2. 2

    The Causeway and the Mimic Tear

    Proceed through the archway and descend the stairs to the fog gate leading to the Mimic Tear arena, making sure to search the graveyard just outside for the Greatshield Soldier Ashes. Once past the mist, defeat the boss to collect two Larval Tears and the Silver Tear Mask, then touch the Grace and cross the vast causeway heading northeast over Siofra River.

    Key fight: Mimic Tear

    Tip: Unequip all weapons and armor before stepping through the fog wall so the Mimic Tear spawns completely unequipped, then put your gear back on to easily defeat it.

  3. 3

    Ancestral Woods and the Regal Ancestor

    Branch left off the causeway into the overgrown forest of the Ancestral Woods, keeping clear of Ancestral Follower snipers while taking out singing Ancestral Shamans. Defeat the hidden Teardrop Scarab along the forest path to claim the Ash of War: Enchanted Shot, and loot the chest guarded by Followers behind a ruined pillar for the Ancestral Infant’s Head. Ignite the six flame obelisks scattered through the woods, then interact with the skeletal beast at Hallowhorn Grounds to challenge the Regal Ancestor Spirit.

    Tip: Deal fire, holy, or slash damage to the Regal Ancestor Spirit's head to quickly break its poise, and stay aggressive when it attempts its healing ritual.

  4. 4

    Descent to Night’s Sacred Ground

    From the Ancestral Woods Site of Grace, jump south-southwest across gaps onto the building ledges, roofs, and domes until you drop into a high chapel. Unlock the Imp Statue fog door on the chapel's upper walkway using a Stonesword Key to kill the Nox Swordstress and claim the Mimic Tear Ashes, then loot the corpse at the altar below for the Black Whetblade. Drop down to the main street, where you can jump from a ruined wall to a high roof to find the Nox Flowing Hammer, before entering the crypt beneath the giant skeleton throne to retrieve the Fingerslayer Blade from the chest.

    Tip: Take out the giant rolling spheres from range or with heavy blunt attacks, and avoid engaging them in tight corridors.

    Watch out: Delivering the Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni immediately locks out Preceptor Seluvis’s questline and kills him, so be sure to obtain the Magic Scorpion Charm from him beforehand.

  5. 5

    Siofra Aqueduct and the Twin Gargoyles

    Head north-northeast past the spirit jellyfish, drop along the cliffside, and enter the ruined Siofra Aqueduct while tackling the patrolling Lesser Crucible Knights. On the balcony right before the waterfall fog door, locate Devin and decide whether to hand over the Twinned Armor Set to receive the Inner Order gesture and make him summonable. Cross into the Great Waterfall Basin to defeat the Valiant Gargoyle and Valiant Gargoyle (Twinblade), then climb inside the stone coffin resting against the waterfall edge to ride it into Deeproot Depths.

    Key fight: Valiant Gargoyle & Valiant Gargoyle (Twinblade)

    Tip: Strike damage works best against the Valiant Gargoyles since they resist slash and pierce while remaining completely immune to all status effects; immediately back away whenever they spew poison breath.

    Watch out: Giving Devin the Twinned Armor Set will eventually cause him to assassinate Fia once her quest concludes in Deeproot Depths, whereas withholding the armor keeps him inert and preserves Fia.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

D's Twin Brother

Speaking to Devin on the Siofra Aqueduct balcony before the Valiant Gargoyles

  • Hand over the Twinned Armor Set→ Devin wakes up, awards the 'Inner Order' gesture, can be summoned for the Valiant Gargoyle battle, travels to Deeproot Depths to kill Fia after her questline ends, and leaves behind the Inseparable Sword alongside the Twinned Armor Set upon his death or departure
  • Keep the Twinned Armor Set→ You retain the armor right away, Devin stays unresponsive, his summon sign will not appear for the gargoyle fight, and Fia is left unharmed in Deeproot Depths

Most players pick: Hand over the Twinned Armor Set

Threats: Poison breath clouds covering wide ground areas, spinning twinblade slashes, sweeping sword combos

Weaknesses and resistances: Magic, Strike; immune to all status effects

Tactics: Focus down the first gargoyle aggressively before the second arrives at roughly 50% health, staying clear of poisonous mist pools

Rewards: Gargoyle's Greatsword, Gargoyle's Twinblade

Tips
  • Light the braziers scattered across the Ancestral Woods to unlock the fog gate leading to the Regal Ancestor Spirit boss fight at the Hallowhorn Grounds.
  • Stripping your weapons prior to passing the Mimic Tear's fog gate makes the encounter trivial, as the copy permanently mirrors whatever you wore upon entering.
