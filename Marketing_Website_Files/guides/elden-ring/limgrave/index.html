Limgrave

ELDEN RING guide · The starting region of the game ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Limgrave is the opening open-world region of the Lands Between, introducing the Tarnished to freeform exploration, horseback travel, and scattered catacombs. Players should acquire Torrent from Melina, unlock weapon upgrading at the Church of Elleh, and clear minor dungeons to build levels and gear before advancing toward Stormveil Castle. The region split into West and East Limgrave provides foundational crafting materials, flask upgrades, and early NPC side quests.

The short version

What matters

  • •Accept Melina's accord at Gatefront to receive Torrent and unlock attribute leveling immediately.
  • •Visit the Church of Elleh at night after gaining Torrent to collect the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes from Renna.
  • •Collect the Whetstone Knife from Gatefront Ruins and the Flask of Wondrous Physick with the Sacred Tear at the Third Church of Marika.
  • •Acquire both Map Stele fragments (West at Gatefront Ruins and East along the Mistwood road) to clarify navigation.

Common mistakes

  • •Fighting the Tree Sentinel immediately upon leaving the tutorial dungeon; bypass him until you gain levels and weapon upgrades.
  • •Opening the subterranean chest in Dragon-Burnt Ruins, which triggers a teleport trap sending you to Sellia Crystal Tunnel in Caelid.
  • •Killing Patches when he begs for mercy at half health, which permanently locks his shop and future quest interactions.
  • •Defeating Margit, the Fell Omen before exploring Stormhill, which despawns the invader Recusant Henricus and locks you out of the Hammer Talisman.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    The First Step to Church of Elleh

    When you exit the Stranded Graveyard through the large wooden doors, activate The First Step Site of Grace and speak with White Mask Varré nearby. Sneak past or bypass the heavily armored Tree Sentinel patrolling the road north toward the ruined chapel. Once inside the Church of Elleh, rest at the Grace, meet Merchant Kalé to buy essentials like the Crafting Kit, and use the nearby smithing anvil to upgrade your weapons.

    Key fight: Tree Sentinel

    Tip: You can circle clockwise away from the Tree Sentinel's halberd arm on Torrent later if you choose to engage him with strike or lightning attacks.

    Watch out: Do not attack Merchant Kalé or pick fights with the Tree Sentinel immediately upon walking out into the world underleveled.

  2. 2

    Coastal Cave and Dragon Communion Access

    Head down to the shoreline directly west of The First Step to discover the entrance of Coastal Cave. Traverse the cavern to confront the Demi-Human Chiefs and claim their drops before exiting through the rear tunnel. Emerging onto the island off the coast brings you to the Church of Dragon Communion, where you can later redeem Dragon Hearts.

    Tip: Bring a torch purchased from Merchant Kalé to illuminate the pitch-black tunnels in the cave.

  3. 3

    Gatefront Ruins and Meeting Melina

    Follow the road north from the Church of Elleh until you arrive at the military encampment at Gatefront Ruins, just before the Stormgate pass. Rest at the nearby Site of Grace to trigger Melina's appearance, where agreeing to her accord grants the Spectral Steed Whistle to summon Torrent alongside the ability to level up. Clear the encampment to collect the regional map from the stone stele and descend into the underground cellar for essential weapon gear.

    Tip: Wait until nightfall and return to the Church of Elleh to meet Renna sitting on the ruins for the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes.

    Watch out: If you refuse Melina's accord, you will be unable to level up your attributes or summon your mount until you speak with her again and accept.

  4. 4

    Stormhill Approach and Fringefolk Detour

    Venture north through Stormgate up the Stormhill path toward Stormveil Castle, picking up the golden sapling's bounty along the road before challenging Margit, the Fell Omen at the castle approach. You can also explore the northern rim past Warmaster's Shack to find Knight Bernahl for Ashes of War and Alexander the Iron Fist near Saintsbridge. If prepared for tough challenges, brave the Chariot hazards in Fringefolk Hero's Grave back at the start to conquer the Ulcerated Tree Spirit.

    Key fight: Margit, the Fell Omen

    Tip: Freeing Alexander by striking him from behind with heavy attacks earns Exalted Flesh and progresses his journey.

    Watch out: Beating Margit, the Fell Omen can permanently lock out the invasion of Recusant Henricus near the path to the Divine Bridge, costing you the Hammer Talisman.

  5. 5

    Agheel Lake and Limgrave Tunnels

    Head east into the massive dried basin of Agheel Lake, being mindful of the colossal Flying Dragon Agheel who drops from the sky to incinerate the waters. Delve into the Limgrave Tunnels at the northwest tip of the lake to harvest upgrade smithing stones and defeat the Stonedigger Troll. Further south in the basin, investigate the Dragon-Burnt Ruins, but exercise extreme caution around its underground rooms.

    Key fight: Flying Dragon Agheel

    Tip: Fight Flying Dragon Agheel mounted on Torrent, galloping toward his wings and legs during his fire breath wind-ups while aiming pierce damage at his head.

    Watch out: Opening the subterranean trap chest in the Dragon-Burnt Ruins engulfs you in smoke and teleports you straight to the dangerous Sellia Crystal Tunnel in Caelid.

  6. 6

    Murkwater Ravine and Hidden Seamster

    Ride along the canyon cutting north from Agheel Lake, watching out for an ambush by invader Bloody Finger Nerijus until Bloody Finger Hunter Yura arrives to assist you. Continue up the ravine into Murkwater Cave to confront Patches, sparing him when he yields to convert the cave into a shop. As you head up toward the Agheel Lake North Site of Grace, investigate the odd cries coming from the flora along the path to unveil a transformed creature.

    Tip: When Patches pleads for mercy around half health, cease all attacks immediately so he yields the Grovel for Mercy gesture and opens his merchant wares.

    Watch out: Slaying Patches locks out his future vendor stock and Volcano Manor interactions, while striking the transformed bush with lethal follow-ups will kill Boc the Seamster.

  7. 7

    Mistwood and the Third Church of Marika

    Journey across the eastern border into the dense forest of Mistwood to retrieve the regional map from the roadside stele while avoiding the colossal sleeping bears. Travel along the northern border of the woods to locate the Third Church of Marika to secure crucial upgrades for your flasks. Afterwards, ride southeast toward Kenneth Haight's seaside bastion to liberate Fort Haight from Godrick's Commander and earn the Erdsteel Dagger.

    Tip: After hearing wolf howls in Mistwood, speak with Merchant Kalé at the Church of Elleh to learn the Finger Snap gesture.

    Watch out: Progressing into Liurnia or reaching Roundtable Hold before meeting Renna at the Church of Elleh at night makes her permanently vanish from that location.

Choices here

What you decide here and what it leads to. Outcomes are hidden: tap one to reveal it.

Melina's Accord

Resting at the Gatefront Grace or a third surface Grace

  • Accept→ Receive the Spectral Steed Whistle to ride Torrent and gain the ability to level up
  • Refuse→ Cannot level up or call Torrent until you return to speak with her and accept

Most players pick: Accept

Boc's Disguise

Finding the speaking bush southeast of the Agheel Lake North Grace

  • Strike or roll into the bush→ Reverts Boc to demi-human form, grants 10 Mushrooms, and moves him to Coastal Cave
  • Ignore or kill him→ Permanently prevents him from offering free garment alterations across your journey

Most players pick: Strike or roll into the bush

Sparing Patches

Reducing Patches to roughly half health in Murkwater Cave

  • Cease attacking→ He ends the battle, grants the "Grovel for Mercy" gesture, sets up a merchant store, and unlocks his future trap chest
  • Kill him→ Forfeits his merchant shop and future Volcano Manor missions, but instantly yields a Spear +7, his Leather Armor set, and his Bell Bearing

Most players pick: Cease attacking

Patches' Treasure Chest

After forgiving Patches and returning to his Murkwater Cave chamber

  • Open the treasure chest→ Teleports you into Mistwood near sleeping Rune Bears via a trap, enabling the "Calm Down!" gesture upon returning to Patches
  • Leave it shut→ Avoid being teleported, but miss the interaction and gesture

Most players pick: Open the treasure chest

Kenneth Haight’s Service

After speaking with Kenneth Haight north of Mistwood and clearing Fort Haight

  • Clear Fort Haight and return→ Haight awards the Erdsteel Dagger and offers Tarnished knighthood. Pledge service to him
  • Kill him→ Drops a Golden Seed early, but permanently fails his and Nepheli's endgame resolution.

Most players pick: Pledge service to him

Key fights
Tree Sentinel

Enemies: Tree Sentinel

Threats: Heavy halberd swings, shield bash, leaping ground slam, golden shield deflection reflecting offensive spells

Weaknesses and resistances: Holy resistance, Pierce weakness, Lightning weakness

Tactics: Fight on horseback using hit-and-run tactics, bait his right-side swings, or bypass him entirely to return with upgraded weapons

Rewards: Golden Halberd, 3,200 Runes

Flying Dragon Agheel

Enemies: Flying Dragon Agheel

Threats: Wide sweeping fire breath, stomps, tail whips, leaping aerial divebomb

Weaknesses and resistances: Pierce, Head hitzone weakness, Fire resistance

Tactics: Fight mounted on Torrent, sprint horizontally away when he takes flight to breathe fire, and focus attacks on his feet and head during recoveries

Rewards: Dragon Heart, 5,000 Runes

Margit, the Fell Omen

Enemies: Margit, the Fell Omen

Threats: Delayed staff strikes, conjured golden daggers, jumping hammer crush, wide spinning sweep

Weaknesses and resistances: Slash, Bleed, Frostbite, Holy resistance

Tactics: Use Margit's Shackle to pin him twice during phase 1, dodge into his delayed swings rather than early, and summon Spirit Ashes or Rogier to distract him

Rewards: Talisman Pouch, 12,000 Runes

Shops and people
  • Merchant Kalé (Church of Elleh): Crafting Kit, Telescope, Furlcalling Finger Remedy, Torch, Large Leather Shield, Chainmail armor set, Cookbooks
  • Sorceress Sellen (Waypoint Ruins Cellar): Glintstone Pebble, Glintstone Stars, Glintstone Arc, Crystal Barrage, Scholar's Armament, Scholar's Shield

Tips
  • Rest at three outdoor Sites of Grace to trigger Melina's visit and receive the Spectral Steed Whistle, which grants access to your mount, Torrent.
  • Return to the Church of Elleh at night after acquiring Torrent to meet Ranni (introducing herself as Renna), who will gift you the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes.
