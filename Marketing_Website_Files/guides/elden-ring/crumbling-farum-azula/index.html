Guides / ELDEN RING

Crumbling Farum Azula

ELDEN RING guide · Arriving after burning the Erdtree

You awaken in this floating, storm-wracked ancient mausoleum immediately after igniting the Forge of the Giants. Navigating its fractured architecture and surviving the beastmen and draconic sentinels leads directly to confrontations with the Godskin Duo and Maliketh, the Black Blade. Clearing this legacy dungeon unlocks the final endgame sequence in Leyndell, Capital of Ash.

Items

  • Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone: Looted from a corpse near the large dragon on the platform past the Dragon Temple Rooftop Site of Grace
  • Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone: Dropped after defeating the target dragon past the Dragon Temple Rooftop Site of Grace
  • Old Lord's Talisman: In a chest inside the tower on the isolated balcony past the Beside the Great Bridge Site of Grace
  • Boltdrake Talisman +2: On a corpse perched on an archway reached from the Dragon Temple lift shortcut

Secrets

  • Dragonlord Placidusax's arena | Accessible from the Beside the Great Bridge Site of Grace by riding the elevator down, heading outside to the cliffside drop-down stones, and lying down inside an empty grave indentation before the boss storm trigger.

Shops and people

  • Iron Fist Alexander: Grants the Shard of Alexander and Alexander's Innards upon defeating him in an honorable duel on the upper platforms past the Dragon Temple Lift.
  • Bernahl (Recusant Bernahl): Invades near the Old Lord's Talisman chest and drops the Blasphemous Claw, Devourer's Scepter, and the Beast Champion set upon defeat.

Tips

  • Craft and bring Sleep Pots or St. Trina's Arrows to neutralize one boss during the Godskin Duo encounter to fight them one at a time.
  • Use the Blasphemous Claw tool to parry Maliketh's airborne, gold-glowing blade attacks during his second phase to create a large attack opening.
  • Equipping the Boltdrake Talisman and Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman significantly mitigates the red lightning and heavy physical damage dealt by the numerous dragons here.
