Castle Sol

ELDEN RING guide · Exploring the northern fortress in the Mountaintops

Castle Sol is a snow-covered, spirit-haunted fortress located in the northern sector of the Mountaintops of the Giants. You must fight through banished knights and spirit warriors to reach Commander Niall at the castle's peak. Defeating Niall grants access to the rooftop tower containing a crucial piece needed to reach the Consecrated Snowfield.

Items

  • Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left): Rooftop tower past Commander Niall's boss arena
  • Eclipse Shotel: Found on the altar inside the church in the lower courtyard
  • Cerulean Amber Medallion +1: On a wooden ledge hanging off the southern outer wall, accessed through a window

Secrets

  • The painting puzzle solution "Sorcerer" awards the Great Hood; the painting itself is found in a rooftop shack behind the main cathedral.
  • A wooden elevator just outside the Commander Niall fog gate can be activated from above to create a direct shortcut from the castle entrance.

Tips

  • Using Bewitching Branches on Commander Niall's summoned knights will turn them against him and heavily swing the battle in your favor.
  • Shoot the hanging screaming albinarics in the upper gallows from range to prevent them from summoning phantom spectral knights.
