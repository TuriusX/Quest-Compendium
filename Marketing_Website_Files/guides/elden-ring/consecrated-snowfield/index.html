Consecrated Snowfield

ELDEN RING guide · Accessing the hidden path via the Haligtree Secret Medallion

The Consecrated Snowfield is a late-game, blizzard-covered plains area accessed by using both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold. Players must navigate extremely low visibility in the southern half of the region while traveling north to solve the puzzle in Ordina, Liturgical Town. Completing this area unlocks the teleporter to Miquella's Haligtree and opens the way to Mohgwyn Palace via a waygate in the west.

Items

  • Haligtree Secret Medallion (Right): Found in the Village of the Albinaurics in Liurnia disguised as a pot, used to access this area
  • Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left): Found on the roof of Castle Sol in the Mountaintops of the Giants after defeating Commander Niall
  • Silver Scarab: Inside a treasure chest on the Hidden Path to the Haligtree behind an illusory wall
  • Albinauric Armor Set: Dropped by Albinauric Wolfback Archers roaming the snowy plains
  • Spelldrake Talisman +2: Found on a corpse on the Hidden Path to the Haligtree

Secrets

  • There are invisible walkways over the abyss inside the Hidden Path to the Haligtree dungeon leading to hidden rooms and an illusory wall.
  • A blood-soaked waygate along the western cliffs transports the player directly to Mohgwyn Palace.
  • Lighting four rooftop and ground-level candles inside the Evergaol puzzle at Ordina, Liturgical Town breaks the seal to the Haligtree teleporter.

Tips

  • Follow the glowing light orbs on the ground when navigating through the dense southern blizzard to reach the safe central ruins.
  • Equip the Sentry's Torch in either hand to reveal the invisible assassins inside Ordina, Liturgical Town.
  • Bring high-tier Holy damage or Sacred affinity weapons to eliminate the Death Rite Bird quickly.
