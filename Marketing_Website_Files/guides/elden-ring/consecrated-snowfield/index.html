Consecrated Snowfield

ELDEN RING guide · The hidden northern area of the Mountaintops
← All areas in this guide

The Consecrated Snowfield is a secret, blizzard-swept sub-region of the Mountaintops reached via the Grand Lift of Rold using the Haligtree Secret Medallion. The southern plains are blanketed in a blinding whiteout, while the northern frozen river leads to ruins, catacombs, and a gateway town. Traversing this harsh expanse is the essential path to reaching Miquella's Haligtree and Mohgwyn Palace.

The short version

What matters

  • •The waygate at Ordina, Liturgical Town unlocks access to Miquella's Haligtree once all four Evergaol statues are lit.
  • •Summoning Latenna at the Apostate Derelict and slaying the invading Anastasia along the frozen river grant Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones.
  • •The blood-soaked portal at the western cliffs provides an alternate entrance to Mohgwyn Palace.

Common mistakes

  • •Attempting the Ordina Evergaol without holding a Sentry's Torch, leaving you vulnerable to fatal backstabs from invisible Black Knife Assassins.
  • •Attacking or ignoring Phillia at the Apostate Derelict, locking you out of Latenna's max-tier weapon upgrade reward.
  • •Defeating the main area boss before crossing the frozen river bank, which in earlier patches permanently prevented the phantom Anastasia from invading.

What you can skip

  • •You can bypass the Consecrated Snowfield Catacombs and Yelough Anix Tunnel if you do not need Grave Gloveworts or the Meteorite of Astel.

Walkthrough

The area in the order you play it. Tick things off as you go: they tick off in the checklists below too.

  1. 1

    Emerging into the Whiteout

    After hoisting the secret medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold and traversing the Hidden Path to the Haligtree, step outside into a blinding blizzard with near-zero visibility. Follow the glowing stone light pillars directing you north-northeast through the deep snow. Along the blizzard borders, search a crevice guarded by Rock Fiends for the Stalwart Horn Charm +1, and loot a roaming carriage chest for St. Trina's Torch.

    Tip: Stick strictly to the glowing light markers so you do not wander off into the storm.

  2. 2

    Claiming the Map and Scouting the Catacombs

    Keep moving along the path markers until the dense whiteout clears and you arrive at the Consecrated Snowfield Site of Grace. Right beside the frozen riverbed, grab Map: Consecrated Snowfield from the stone stele near wolves chasing an invisible Teardrop Scarab. Head toward the cliffs beneath the Mountaintops border wall to find the Consecrated Snowfield Catacombs, where you can clear the dungeon to defeat the Putrid Grave Warden Duelist.

    Tip: Circle behind the Putrid Grave Warden Duelist during wide hammer sweeps to land easy backstabs.

  3. 3

    Yelough Anix and the Blood Portal

    Ride west to the Yelough Anix Ruins and Tunnel, an area swarming with Frenzied Trolls and madness-inflicting rats. Jump down into a walled basement cellar to loot Unendurable Frenzy, and venture through Yelough Anix Tunnel to slay Astel, Stars of Darkness for the Meteorite of Astel. Before leaving the western cliff edge northwest of the ruins, cut down an invading Sanguine Noble to access the blood-soaked teleporter portal leading directly to Mohgwyn Palace.

    Key fight: Astel, Stars of Darkness

    Tip: Astel's head takes double damage, so stay locked on and sprint away whenever he teleports directly overhead.

  4. 4

    Across the Frozen River

    Follow the river cutting through the region, separating the blinding southern storm from the clearer northern half. Journey east along the frozen waterway to take down the Putrid Avatar near the Minor Erdtree, delve into Cave of the Forlorn to win the Golden Order Greatsword from the Misbegotten Crusader, and defeat Great Wyrm Theodorix past the frozen waterfall. As you travel the riverbank, defeat the invading phantom Anastasia, Tarnished Eater near a lootable corpse to obtain a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone, and watch out for a Death Rite Bird roaming the icy expanse.

    Key fight: Death Rite Bird Key fight: Putrid Avatar Key fight: Great Wyrm Theodorix

    Tip: You can bait Great Wyrm Theodorix into fighting the nearby Land Octopuses to shred his health bar.

    Watch out: Be sure to trigger the Anastasia invasion along the river before defeating the area boss, as earlier patches could cause phantom invasions to stop spawning.

  5. 5

    Move onto the clear plains around the Inner Consecrated Snowfield Site of Grace.

    tip: Fighting the Night's Cavalry duo one by one on horseback makes dismounting them much simpler.

    Tip: Fighting the Night's Cavalry duo one by one on horseback makes dismounting them much simpler.

  6. 6

    Breaking the Seal of Ordina

    Head north to Ordina, Liturgical Town, a deserted settlement obstructing the grand northern stairs. Grab the Black Knife Armor Set from beneath the stone staircase archway, then enter the town's Evergaol realm. Equip a Sentry's Torch to reveal the invisible Black Knife Assassins stalking the courtyards while you pick off rooftop Albinauric archers and ignite the four candle statues (one on the ground, three on rooftops) to open the waygate to Miquella's Haligtree.

    Tip: Keep the Sentry's Torch in your off-hand so the invisible assassins cannot ambush you while navigating the alleys.

  7. 7

    Pilgrimage to the Apostate Derelict

    Ride to the far northwest corner of the region to reach the crumbling church of Apostate Derelict, staying mobile to evade the long-range artillery fire of the Walking Mausoleum. Inside the ruins behind the slumbering giant Albinauric woman Phillia, pick up the Silver Mirrorshield. Finally, stand directly before Phillia and summon Latenna from your inventory to finish her pilgrimage, which rewards you with a Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.

    Tip: Latenna's Spirit Ash remains in your inventory and can be summoned as normal even after completing her quest.

    Watch out: Do not attack or ignore Phillia, and ensure Latenna was not killed earlier in Liurnia, or you will permanently miss out on this Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.

Key fights
Death Rite Bird

Enemies: Death Rite Bird

Threats: Ghostflame explosion, frostbite-inflicting trail attacks, piercing beak thrusts

Weaknesses and resistances: Holy damage, Strike damage; immune to Bleed and Frostbite

Tactics: Fight on horseback during the night on the frozen river near the Apostate Derelict, hurling Sacred Blade attacks or Holy Water Pots at the head

Rewards: Explosive Ghostflame sorcery, 220,000 Runes

Putrid Avatar

Enemies: Putrid Avatar

Threats: Scarlet rot body slams, golden projectile bursts, wide sweeping staff strikes

Weaknesses and resistances: Fire damage, Strike damage; heavily resistant to Holy and immune to Rot

Tactics: Stay close to bait staff swings, roll forward through the swings, and circle behind during the rot jump to avoid the splash

Rewards: Thorny Cracked Tear, Ruptured Crystal Tear, 160,000 Runes

Astel, Stars of Darkness

Enemies: Astel, Stars of Darkness

Threats: Gravity meteor bombardment, cloning grab attack, tail strikes, teleports

Weaknesses and resistances: Strike damage, Magic damage to the head; highly resistant to Slash and Pierce

Tactics: Enter the Yelough Anix Tunnel, target only the head with jumping attacks or high-poise weapon arts, and sprint diagonally when meteors rain

Rewards: Meteorite of Astel sorcery, 120,000 Runes

Great Wyrm Theodorix

Enemies: Great Wyrm Theodorix

Threats: Sweeping magma breath, crushing body slams, giant curved greatsword swings

Weaknesses and resistances: Physical Strike to the head, Pierce damage; resistant to Fire and Slash

Tactics: Bait Theodorix into fighting the nearby Giant Land Octopuses to deal massive posture damage, then strike his head during stagger windows

Rewards: Dragon Heart (x3), Magma Breath incantation unlock, 180,000 Runes

Tips
  • Equip the Sentry's Torch in your off-hand when navigating Ordina, Liturgical Town's Evergaol to reveal the invisible Black Knife Assassins before they backstab you.
  • Follow the glowing light stones and small cairns scattered across the southern blizzard zone to keep your bearings and safely reach the inner river valley.
