USG Ishimura

Dead Space guide · Overview of the mining ship's layout and key decks
← All areas in this guide

The USG Ishimura is a massive Planet Cracker-class mining ship that serves as the interconnected setting for the game. Players navigate between its various functional decks—including Flight Deck, Medical, Hydroponics, Mining, Engineering, and the Bridge—primarily via the Tram network. As Isaac Clarke gains higher Security Clearance levels and the Master Override, previously locked doors and supply crates across the ship become accessible for backtracking.

Shops and people
  • Store Kiosk: weapons, ammunition, suit upgrades, med packs, and stasis recharges in exchange for Credits

Tips
  • Circuit Breakers found on multiple decks require rerouting power between essential functions like lights, oxygen, and door locks to proceed or reveal extra loot.
  • Look for doors and storage containers glowing with a purple light; these require the Master Override rather than standard numeric Security Clearances.
  • You can backtrack freely across the entire Tram system to scavenge previously locked rooms right up until boarding the shuttle in Chapter 11.
