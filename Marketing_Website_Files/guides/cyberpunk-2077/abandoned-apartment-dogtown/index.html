Abandoned Apartment (Dogtown)

Cyberpunk 2077 guide · Safehouse
← All areas in this guide

Located on the upper floors of an abandoned residential tenement in Dogtown, this hideout serves as V and Rosalind Myers' primary safehouse early in the Phantom Liberty storyline. After restoring power and defending the perimeter, players can rest, talk with Myers, and prepare for subsequent infiltration missions across the district.

Tips
  • Rest on the mattress in the safehouse to receive the temporary XP boost buff before venturing out into Dogtown missions.
  • Take time to explore all conversation branches with President Myers here, as your dialogue choices shape your relationship and early storyline context.
