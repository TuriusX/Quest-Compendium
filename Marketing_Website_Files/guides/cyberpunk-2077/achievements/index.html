Points of no return

  • The ambush in "Search and Destroy": You must ignore Johnny's prompt to flee through the corridor and instead double back through the hole in the wall to rescue Goro Takemura, or "The Devil" ending achievement becomes permanently locked out for the rest of the playthrough.
  • Entering the elevator at Embers during "Nocturne Op55N1": Reaching the rooftop meeting with Misty locks you into the endgame assault sequences, so you must create a manual save point here to reload and experience alternate ending pathways.
  • Dialogue choices in "Chippin' In" and Rogue/Kerry side jobs: Being hostile to Johnny at his unmarked grave or rejecting Rogue and Kerry's requests cuts off the remaining rockerboy questline, permanently locking out "Bushido and Chill" and Johnny's replica clothing items for "Breathtaking".
  • Deciding to betray Songbird or Reed during "Firestarter" (Phantom Liberty): Backing either Songbird or Reed branches the expansion toward incompatible final missions and achievements, requiring a hard save before making the matrix choice to complete all expansion endings in one run.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Scavenger Hideout - Rescue Sandra Dorsett during the prologue gig "The Rescue" to open early Watson progression and establish your main save.

  2. Step 2

    Viktor Vektor's Clinic - Receive your first Kiroshi optics and cyberware upgrades from Vik, then begin working towards fully outfitting every cyberware slot.

  3. Step 3

    Lizzie's Bar - Meet Judy Alvarez and Johnny Silverhand's braindance memories to progress the main line and initiate the Braindance storylines.

  4. Step 4

    Konpeki Plaza - Execute the heist during "The Heist," ensuring you grab Yorinobu's clothes and the iconic weapon on the penthouse roof if desired before escaping into the wider open world.

  5. Step 5

    Clouds - Infiltrate the club in Japantown during "Automatic Love" to gather information on Evelyn Parker's whereabouts.

  6. Step 6

    GIM - Infiltrate the Grand Imperial Mall in Pacifica during "I Walk the Line," resolving the conflict between the Voodoo Boys and NetWatch.

  7. Step 7

    Voodoo Boys Hideout - Enter the deep Net via the Pacifica chapel and the Voodoo Boys' subnet crypt to progress Johnny's Relic narrative.

  8. Step 8

    Japantown - Meet Goro Takemura at the diner, complete district gigs, and prepare tactical operations against Arasaka forces across Westbrook.

  9. Step 9

    Arasaka Industrial Park - Infiltrate the warehouse complex during "Gimme Danger" to hack the float for the upcoming parade.

  10. Step 10

    Arasaka Estate - Infiltrate Hanako Arasaka's guarded residence in North Oak during "Play It Safe" / "Search and Destroy" setup to orchestrate your meeting.

  11. Step 11

    EBM Petrochem Stadium - Enter the Dogtown black market via the stadium gates to unlock the district and commence Phantom Liberty operations.

  12. Step 12

    Abandoned Apartment (Dogtown) - Secure the hideout in Dogtown, establish your forward operating base with Solomon Reed, and complete district gigs.

  13. Step 13

    Terra Cognita - Clear out the abandoned spaceflight exhibition center to secure district activities, scanner hustles, and localized gigs across Dogtown.

  14. Step 14

    Cynosure Facility - Infiltrate the underground Militech bunker beneath Dogtown during the climactic decision of the expansion to conclude the Blackwall story arc.

  15. Step 15

    V's Apartment - Return to Megabuilding H10 to stash iconic gear, sleep for XP buffs, collect Johnny Silverhand's scattered items, and finalize all remaining city district gigs and NCPD scanner hustles across Night City.
