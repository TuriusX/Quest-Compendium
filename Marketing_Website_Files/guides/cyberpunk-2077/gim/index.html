GIM

Cyberpunk 2077 guide · I Walk the Line mission
← All areas in this guide

The Grand Imperial Mall (GIM) serves as the primary setting for the mission "I Walk the Line" in Pacifica. V infiltrates the Animals-occupied mall to locate a NetWatch agent stationed inside a surveillance van in the cinema. Progress requires sneaking or fighting through Animal gang members, dealing with their leader Matilda K. Rose (Sasquatch), and confronting the agent.

Don't miss 0/1

Enemies
Matilda K. Rose (Sasquatch)
Weak to Glowing weak point on her back (cyberware booster tank)Steal / drop Legendary clothing piece and weapon

Heavily armored boss equipped with a massive two-handed sledgehammer.

Animal Enforcers
Weak to Netrunning hacks, headshotsSteal / drop Assorted weapons and combat consumables

Hulking gang members who specialize in heavy melee attacks and close-quarters firearms.

Tips
  • Destroying the glowing cyberware component on Sasquatch's back cuts off her health regeneration and severely weakens her.
  • Sasquatch can be completely bypassed by sneaking through the mall's cinema area if you choose a stealthy approach.
  • Disabling or hacking security cameras in the atrium prevents the Animals from swarming your position early in the infiltration.
