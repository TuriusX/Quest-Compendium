Arasaka Tower

Cyberpunk 2077 guide · Final mission (The Devil ending)
← All areas in this guide

During the final infiltration of Arasaka Tower in "The Devil" ending path, you accompany Hanako Arasaka to confront Yorinobu Arasaka. You must fight your way through high-level Arasaka security forces across multiple executive floors, secure the boardroom, and navigate down toward the CEO's office. The operation culminates in an intense boss fight against Adam Smasher before finally reaching Yorinobu.

Don't miss 0/1

Enemies
Adam Smasher
Weak to Electrical / Cyberware MalfunctionSteal / drop Access Card

Heavily armored cyborg who employs a shoulder-mounted missile launcher and high-caliber firearms; maintaining distance is key during his missile barrages.

Arasaka Elite Guards
Weak to EMP / HeadshotsSteal / drop High-tier firearms and ammunition

Well-armored corporate security equipped with heavy automatic weapons, smart rifles, and mantis blades.

Tips
  • Hack or shoot down automated security turrets and cameras in the executive lobbies immediately to prevent reinforcements from overwhelming your position.
  • Equipping Cyberware Malfunction quickhacks significantly reduces Adam Smasher's ability to deploy heavy weapon systems and mobility bursts.
