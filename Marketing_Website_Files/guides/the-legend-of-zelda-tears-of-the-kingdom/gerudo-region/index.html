Gerudo Region

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Investigating the Gerudo phenomenon

The Gerudo Desert is suffering from a massive shroud of sand called the Sand Shroud that blocks your map and hides swarms of Gibdo monsters. Link must track down Chief Riju in the underground ruins, help defend Kara Kara Bazaar and Gerudo Town, and solve a light-reflection puzzle across the desert pillars. Unlocking the Lightning Temple at the center of the desert is the final objective to clear the phenomenon and defeat Queen Gibdo.

Items

  • Sand Boots: Awarded by Bozai in the Gerudo Shelter after starting his related side quest
  • Snow Boots: Awarded by Bozai in the Gerudo Shelter after returning his Sand Boots during the questline
  • Voe Headband: Sold at the Gerudo Secret Club in Gerudo Town

Secrets

  • The Gerudo Secret Club cannot be accessed from the front door at first; you must drop into the town's waterways underground and Ascend up through the floor.
  • In the Gerudo Underground Cemetery, clearing loose rock walls and following the cavern reveals hidden chests with valuable fuse materials and ancient weaponry.
  • Following the stone statues in the Valley of Silent Statues will guide you straight through the underground passages to the Gerudo Shelter.

Shops and people

  • Kara Kara Bazaar General Store: Arrows, Hydromelons, and Cold Darters
  • Gerudo Secret Club: Desert Voe Armor set and Sand Boots

Tips

  • Throw or shoot Splash Fruit or use Chuchu Jelly to clear away mounds of sand covering chests and switches throughout the desert.
  • Vehicles equipped with Big Wheels will not sink or lose traction when driving over deep desert sand.
  • Equip a weapon fused with a ruby or wear warm clothing at night, and switch to cold-resistance gear or sapphire fusions during the day to survive the extreme temperatures.
  • When lost inside the Sand Shroud, look for updrafts created by the sand tornadoes to gain altitude and regain a brief view above the dust cloud.
