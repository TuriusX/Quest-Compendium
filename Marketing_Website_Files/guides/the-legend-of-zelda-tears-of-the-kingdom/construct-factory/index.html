Construct Factory

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Assembling Mineru's construct

The Construct Factory is located in the Depths directly beneath the Dragonhead Island sanctuary. You must visit four surrounding depot facilities to retrieve limbs and attach them to a central frame to awaken Mineru, the Sage of Spirit. Once fully assembled, you can ride the Construct into the Spirit Temple.

Items

  • Right-Arm Depot Box: Right-Arm Depot
  • Left-Arm Depot Box: Left-Arm Depot
  • Right-Leg Depot Box: Right-Leg Depot
  • Left-Leg Depot Box: Left-Leg Depot
  • Large Zonaite: various locations around depot puzzle rooms

Secrets

  • The Muokuij Lightroot is situated on a high cliff right by the factory to illuminate the area, accessible via a nearby elevator.

Tips

  • Striking the terminal in each depot dispenses a component enclosed in a stone carrier block.
  • When a component block is delivered to the central mold, its outer stone casing breaks open automatically.
  • You must use the Ultrahand ability to properly align and snap each exposed limb into the torso skeleton.
  • Weapons, spiked balls, and Zonai devices found nearby can be fused directly onto the Construct's arms and back.
