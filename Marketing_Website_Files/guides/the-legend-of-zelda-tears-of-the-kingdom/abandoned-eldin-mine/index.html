Abandoned Eldin Mine

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Following the Master Kohga questline

The Abandoned Eldin Mine is an underground Depths counterpart to Eldin, reachable by dropping down the Death Mountain Chasm. As part of the "Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan" side adventure, Link confronts Kohga here for the third time in a battle centered around stone-armored machinery. Activating the local Steward Construct also rewards an Autobuild schematic for use across the Depths.

Items

  • Autobuild Schematic (Turboprop): Obtained by talking to the Steward Construct located in the mine
  • Huge Crystallized Charge: Found in a chest granted after defeating Master Kohga

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Forge Construct: Sells Crystallized Charges and Large Crystallized Charges in exchange for Zonaite and Large Zonaite

Tips

  • Bring Flamebreaker armor or drink Fireproof Elixirs before arriving, as the Eldin Depths carry intense extreme-heat conditions.
  • Use Dazzlefruit or Ice-fruit arrows during the Kohga fight to quickly stun him and open him up to ground attacks.
