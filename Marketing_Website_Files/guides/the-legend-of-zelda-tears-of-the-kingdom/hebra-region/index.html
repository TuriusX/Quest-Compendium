Hebra Region

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Investigating the Rito phenomenon

Link travels to the freezing northwest region of Hyrule to investigate a severe blizzard threatening Rito Village as part of the Regional Phenomena main quest. You must team up with Tulin, ascend through sky islands to reach the Wind Temple, and defeat the source of the storm.

Items

  • Snowquill Tunic: Brazen Beak armor shop in Rito Village
  • Snowquill Trousers: Brazen Beak armor shop in Rito Village
  • Snowquill Headdress: Brazen Beak armor shop in Rito Village
  • Feathered Edge: Inside a chest in the Hebra Trailhead Burrow
  • Swallow Bow: Found on a table at the Flight Range

Secrets

  • The Hebra Great Skeleton is hidden inside a massive ice cave on the north side of the Hebra Mountains, accessed by melting a large ice blockage.

Shops and people

  • Brazen Beak: Cold-resistant Snowquill armor set
  • Slippery Falcon: Warm Safflina, Sunshrooms, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, and arrows

Tips

  • Keep spicy food or elixirs ready before purchasing the Snowquill armor set, as the Hebra Region requires two levels of cold resistance in its highest peaks.
  • Shoot Fire Fruit or use a flame-emitting weapon on oversized blocks of ice to melt them quickly and reveal hidden chests or passageways.
  • Tulin's wind gust ability can be activated while paragliding to launch Link horizontally across wide gaps between the floating sky platforms.
