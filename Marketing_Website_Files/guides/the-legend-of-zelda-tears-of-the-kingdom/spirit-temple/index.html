The Spirit Temple is the culmination of the fifth Sage quest where Link brings Mineru's assembled construct body to reclaim her secret stone. Upon activating the temple's central pedestal, the platform descends into a Depths boss arena surrounded by barbed, electrified ropes. Link must ride Mineru's construct and defeat the corrupted Seized Construct in a mech boxing match to cleanse the temple and receive the Vow of Mineru.

Items

0/3

Enemies
Seized Construct
Weak to Being knocked back into the electrified barbed-wire ropes surrounding the ring

Must be fought while piloting Mineru's construct; it equips various Zonai devices and hovers in its second phase.

Shops and people
  • Mineru: Bestows the Vow of Mineru, allowing Link to summon her construct companion at any time

Tips
  • Guard with Mineru's construct when the Seized Construct attacks, then counter with a three-hit combo to push it directly into the electric ropes for massive damage.
  • Fuse a Cannon to one of your construct's arms to easily shoot the Seized Construct out of the air during its hovering phase.
