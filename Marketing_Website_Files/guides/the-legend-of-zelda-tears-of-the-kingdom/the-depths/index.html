The Depths

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · First descent into the chasm

Link first descends into the pitch-black underworld known as the Depths through a chasm on the surface, usually as part of Robbie and Josha's early questline from Lookout Landing. The goal here is to navigate through total darkness by finding ways to light the area, locate Robbie, and unlock the Camera ability. Players must avoid the dangerous Gloom covering the terrain, which restricts maximum hearts until a Lightroot or the surface is reached.

Items

  • Brightbloom Seed: found scattered on the ground around the Depths floor
  • Sundelion: sometimes found near chasm openings or brought down from sky islands
  • Poes: glowing blue flames floating across the ground and elevated terrain

Secrets

  • Activating a Lightroot permanently illuminates a section of the Depths map and mirrors the exact location of a Shrine of Light found directly above it on the surface.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Gloom-wreathed BokoblinHeadshots with arrowsAttacks deplete hearts with Gloom, preventing them from being healed by standard food.

Shops and people

  • Robbie: Offers to upgrade the Purah Pad with the Camera feature once located near the first Lightroot

Tips

  • Throw Brightbloom Seeds by hand instead of attaching them to arrows to conserve your arrow supply in the dark.
  • Walking over Gloom will crack your heart containers; stand directly under an activated Lightroot to restore them.
