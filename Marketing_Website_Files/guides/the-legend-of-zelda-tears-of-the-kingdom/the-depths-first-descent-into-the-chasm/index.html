The Depths: First descent into the chasm

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Exploring the underground

Entering the Hyrule Field Chasm marks Link's first venture into the pitch-black underworld known as the Depths during the quest to find Robbie. Navigating this dangerous subterranean space requires creating light sources, surviving gloom contamination, and locating initial landmarks. The primary goal is following campfire trails to locate Robbie and activate your first Lightroot to establish a permanent teleport point.

Items

  • Brightbloom Seeds: Growing along trees, walls, and ground near the drop zone
  • Sundelion: Occasionally found near debris fallen from the sky above
  • Muddle Bud: Found growing near trees and foliage across the floor of the Depths
  • Puffshroom: Growing around the roots of large subterranean trees
  • Poes: Floating blue wisps gathered on the ground and rocks throughout the area
  • Zonaite: Deposit nodes along rocky walls and dropped by local monsters

Secrets

  • Activating the Nisoq Lightroot directly beneath the Kamizun Shrine permanently illuminates the immediate region and cleanses gloom damage.

Shops and people

  • Robbie: Gives you the Camera ability for your Purah Pad after you locate his camp

Tips

  • Throw Brightbloom Seeds with your hands rather than attaching them to arrows to conserve your arrow supply in the dark.
  • Avoid stepping on red and black gloom patches on the ground, as standing in them will drain your maximum hearts.
  • Eat cooked meals containing Sundelions to restore hearts cracked by gloom while exploring far from a Lightroot.
  • Dropping down a chasm requires opening your paraglider before impact to safely cancel your terminal falling velocity.
