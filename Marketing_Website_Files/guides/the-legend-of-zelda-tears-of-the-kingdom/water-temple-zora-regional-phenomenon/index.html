Water Temple: Zora regional phenomenon

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Completing the Zora quest

The Water Temple floats high above Lanayru and is smothered in a purple, toxic sludge that blocks its plumbing. You must explore its low-gravity sky islands alongside Prince Sidon to activate four water faucets and restore the temple's flow. Doing so clears the central arena to challenge the boss Mucktorok and complete the regional phenomenon.

Items

  • Opal: Found inside floating water bubbles and metal chests around the temple islands
  • Zora Shield: In a treasure chest submerged beneath sludge on the lower floating platform

Tips

  • Keep a stash of Splash Fruit, Chuchu Jelly, or a fused hydrant shield handy to clean sludge off platforms and switches without waiting for Sidon's cooldown.
  • The low-gravity environment allows you to jump much higher, letting you easily enter bullet time with your bow from a standard jump.
  • Hit the water wheels with Sidon's water wave attack to spin the mechanisms and trigger each of the four locks.
