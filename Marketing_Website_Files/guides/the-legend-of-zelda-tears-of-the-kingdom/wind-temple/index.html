Wind Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Rito regional phenomenon

The Wind Temple is a massive flying airship trapped within a fierce blizzard high above the Hebra Mountains. Link explores the deck and interior levels alongside Tulin to activate five turbine gears using gust attacks. Unlocking all five locks opens the central deck hatch to trigger the boss fight and clear the region's storm.

Items

  • Captain I Reaper: Inside a frozen block of ice on the main deck that must be melted
  • Ruby: Inside a treasure chest submerged beneath icicles in the lower interior corridor
  • Sapphire: Inside a chest in the northern ship structure accessed by melting ice spikes

Secrets

  • Melting the patches of ice scattered across the temple deck reveals hidden chests and updrafts.
  • A hidden entrance to the temple's bottom level can be found by leaping off the side of the hull and gliding into openings beneath the oars.

Tips

  • Use Tulin's Power of Wind to spin the fan locks and bridge wide gliding gaps across the ship.
  • Equipping cold-resistant armor or eating spicy food is essential to survive the extreme cold on the ship.
  • Paraglide high above the central deck using the massive updraft to reach the tops of the ship's masts.
  • Falling through the ice shells on Colgera's back from above deals massive damage without spending arrows.
