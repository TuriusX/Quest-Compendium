Construct Factory: Assembling Mineru's construct

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Building the construct

After placing Mineru's mask onto the central pedestal in the Depths, the Construct Factory activates and requires you to retrieve four separate construct limbs from four surrounding storehouses. You must solve puzzles using Zonai devices in each depot to deliver the right leg, left leg, right arm, and left arm containers back to the central mold. Once all four parts are placed into the body frame using Ultrahand, Mineru's construct will be fully assembled and awakened.

Items

  • Mineru's Right Leg: inside the Right-Leg Depot puzzle room across the rail
  • Mineru's Left Leg: inside the Left-Leg Depot puzzle room at the top of the elevator
  • Mineru's Right Arm: inside the Right-Arm Depot puzzle room along the conveyor line
  • Mineru's Left Arm: inside the Left-Arm Depot puzzle room through the wheel-cranked door

Shops and people

  • Mineru: offers to inhabit the assembled construct and accompany you as a Sage vow

Tips

  • Transport containers are encased in rectangular boxes; the boxes will break open automatically once you deliver them onto the central assembly mold.
  • You must use the Ultrahand ability to align and attach each freed limb into its corresponding slot on the construct torso.
  • Zonai devices such as Rockets, Fans, and Steering Sticks are supplied in abundance inside each depot to help you transport the heavy limb boxes back to the factory.
