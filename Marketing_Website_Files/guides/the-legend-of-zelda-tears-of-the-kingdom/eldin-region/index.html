Eldin Region

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Investigating the Goron phenomenon
← All areas in this guide

The Eldin Region is troubled by strange, addictive Marbled Rock Roast that has altered the Gorons' behavior and caused their youth leader, Yunobo, to act hostiles. Link must investigate Goron City, confront Yunobo, and climb the fiery slopes of Death Mountain to enter the Depths. Inside the Fire Temple beneath Death Mountain, Link uses minecarts and Yunobo's rolling ability to defeat Marbled Gohma and clear the region's crisis.

Enemies
Marbled Gohma
Weak to Yunobo's charge attack into its rock legs

Knocking out two legs brings the boss's large glowing eye down to ground level for melee strikes.

Fire Breath Lizalfos
Weak to Ice attacks and headshotsSteal / drop Lizalfos Tail, Lizalfos Horn

Ice-infused arrows or weapons will defeat them instantly in a single strike.

Igneo Talus
Weak to Ice attacks applied to its body to quench the magmaSteal / drop Ore deposits and Igneo Talus Heart

You must cool its surface with ice fruit, water, or ice-infused arrows before you can climb onto its back.

Shops and people
  • Goron City Armor Shop: Flamebreaker Helm, Flamebreaker Armor, and Flamebreaker Boots that grant Flame Guard protection
  • Goron City General Store: Fire Fruit, Cane Sugar, Goron Spice, and Rock Salt

Tips
  • Eating fireproof elixirs or equipping pieces of the Flamebreaker set is required to prevent Link from catching fire in high-heat caves and the Eldin Depths.
  • Yunobo's avatar ability launches him as a flaming boulder, which can break cracked red rocks and ore deposits without consuming weapon durability.
  • Wooden weapons, bows, and shields will quickly catch fire and burn up in the high-heat sectors of Eldin unless they are unequipped.
