Abandoned Gerudo Mine

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Following the Master Kohga questline

The Abandoned Gerudo Mine is a Depths location situated directly beneath the Gerudo Desert where you track down Master Kohga. You engage in a vehicular boss battle against him here as part of the "Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan" side adventure. After driving him away, you can awaken the local Forge Construct to process materials and obtain a new Autobuild schematic.

Items

  • Schema Draft (Hovercraft): Given by a Steward Construct at the mine after defeating Master Kohga
  • Huge Crystallized Charge (100): Found inside the chest unlocked by defeating Master Kohga

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Master KohgaArrows shot directly at his headHe pilots a Zonai wing equipped with weapons, requiring you to use your own flying platform or ranged attacks to shoot him down so you can melee him on the ground.

Shops and people

  • Forge Construct: Sells Crystallized Charges and Large Crystallized Charges in exchange for Zonaite and Large Zonaite

Tips

  • Board one of the pre-assembled Zonai fan platforms stationed around the arena and use its steering stick to gain altitude on Master Kohga.
  • Equipping Keese Eyes on your arrows allows them to automatically home in on Kohga while both of you are maneuvering in the air.
