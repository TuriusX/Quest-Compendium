Hebra Region: Investigating the Rito phenomenon

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · First regional investigation

The Hebra region is trapped in a devastating blizzard, prompting an investigation into Rito Village and the storm above it. You meet with Tulin to track down the cause of the freezing winds, which leads through the rising sky ruins to the Wind Temple. Clearing the storm stops the regional freeze and grants you the permanent ability to call upon Tulin's wind gusts.

Items

  • Snowquill Tunic: Rito Village Armor Shop
  • Snowquill Headdress: Rito Village Armor Shop
  • Snowquill Trousers: Rito Village Armor Shop
  • Archaic Warm Greaves: Inside a hollowed tree trunk near Gutanbac Shrine on the Great Sky Island before arriving

Secrets

  • The Mayaumekis Shrine is found mid-ascent in the Rising Island Chain and serves as a vital fast-travel checkpoint while climbing toward the storm.

Enemies

Shops and people

  • Brazen Beak: Sells cold-resistant clothing: Snowquill Tunic (500 Rupees), Snowquill Headdress (650 Rupees), and Snowquill Trousers (1000 Rupees)
  • Slippery Falcon: Sells cooking ingredients including Tabantha Wheat, Cane Sugar, Goat Butter, and Wooden Arrows

Tips

  • You must have at least level two Cold Resistance to safely explore the upper reaches of Hebra and the sky above it.
  • Fuse Fire Fruit or Red Chuchu Jelly to your arrows to instantly shatter large ice blocks blocking paths and chests.
  • Tulin's gust ability can be activated while paragliding to cover massive horizontal distances between floating platforms.
  • Bouncing on the canvas tops of flying wooden ships launches you high into the air during the ascent to the storm.
