Great Sky Island

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Prologue

The Great Sky Island serves as the introductory tutorial zone where Link awakens and learns to navigate the sky islands. Guided by the spirit of Rauru, Link must explore the island to locate four Shrines of Light and unlock his core arm abilities. Completing these tasks allows him to open the Temple of Time's inner sanctum and take a leap of faith to begin his journey across Hyrule.

Items

  • Archaic Tunic: Inside Pondside Cave in a treasure chest
  • Archaic Warm Greaves: Inside the hollow of a large dead tree trunk near Gutanbac Shrine
  • Energy Cell: Given by a Steward Construct after exiting Nachoyah Shrine
  • Glider Wing (Zonai Device): Dispensed from the Device Dispenser near the island's southern lake area

Secrets

  • Bubbulfrog hiding inside the hidden depths of Pondside Cave, accessible by breaking cracked rock walls with a fused stone hammer.

Shops and people

  • Mining Construct Forge: Sells Crystallized Charges in exchange for Zonaite ore.
  • Device Dispenser: Dispenses portable Zonai devices like Fans, Flame Emitters, and Wings in exchange for Construct Horns or Zonai Charges.

Tips

  • Fuse rocks or boulders to decayed wooden sticks to easily break cracked stone walls and mine Zonaite deposits.
  • Cook Spicy Peppers found near the snowy ascent into meals to protect Link from hypothermia before acquiring warm clothes.
  • You can retrieve used arrows from the ground or walls if they do not hit an enemy directly.
