Lanayru Region: Investigating the Zora phenomenon

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Third regional investigation

Link travels to Zora's Domain to resolve a regional crisis caused by toxic sludge pouring from the sky. Working with Prince Sidon, Link investigates ancient ruins, deciphers a riddle regarding a "land of the sky fish," and creates a pathway into the sky. The questline ultimately leads to the Water Temple to purge the source of the sludge and awaken Sidon as the Sage of Water.

Items

  • Zora Armor: rewarded by Yona in Zora's Domain after completing the "Restoring the Zora Armor" quest by giving her an Ancient Arowana

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Sludge LikeWater-based attacks to wash away its sludge armorIts core is exposed to direct damage once the sludge covering its body is cleared away with water.

Shops and people

  • Marot Mart (General Store): sells Splash Fruit, Swift Violets, and Hylian Rice
  • Coral Reef (Armor Shop): sells the Zora Greaves after completing the main Water Temple questline

Tips

  • Attach Splash Fruit or Chuchu Jelly to your arrows to instantly clear away patches of sludge or dissolve the protective coating on sludge-covered enemies.
  • Use Sidon's vow ability to generate a temporary water shield that guards against a single strike and allows you to unleash a ranged water slash with your melee weapon.
  • Equipping the Zora Armor allows you to swim directly up waterfalls, providing a fast way to reach high floating islands in the Lanayru sky archipelago.
