Great Sky Island: Prologue

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Initial tutorial area

Link awakens on the Great Sky Island after the events beneath Hyrule Castle and must explore the floating landmass to restore power to his decayed right arm. Guided by the spirit of King Rauru, he must complete three introductory Shrines to gain fundamental abilities and unlock the Temple of Time. After visiting the Temple, a fourth shrine completes the sequence necessary to send the Master Sword to the future and dive down to the surface of Hyrule.

Items

  • Decayed Master Sword: In Link's inventory at the very start of the awakening chamber
  • Archaic Tunic: Inside a treasure chest in the Pondside Cave
  • Archaic Warm Greaves: Inside a hollowed-out tree trunk near the Gutanbac Shrine
  • Archaic Legwear: In a treasure chest directly along the path shortly after waking up in the Room of Awakening
  • Purah Pad: Given to Link by a Friendly Construct at the Garden of Time just outside the awakening chamber
  • Energy Cell: Given to Link by a Maker Construct after clearing the In-isa Shrine

Secrets

  • Inside the Room of Awakening, using the Ascend ability on the raised platform allows access to a hidden upper ledge with a chest holding the Archaic Warm Greaves' alternate access route and a Zonai device dispenser.
  • A Bubbulfrog resides inside the Pondside Cave, which drops a Bubbul Gem when shot down from the cave ceiling.

Tips

  • Fuse rocks or boulders to decayed wooden sticks to create blunt hammers capable of breaking cracked ore deposits and rocky cave walls.
  • Cook Spicy Peppers found growing near cold mountain passes to create cold-resistant meals before attempting the trek up to Gutanbac Shrine.
  • Ultrahand can be used to join multiple logs together to build functional rafts for crossing wide water bodies.
  • If a fan-powered boat or minecart stops moving, strike the fan with a melee weapon to turn the propulsion back on.
