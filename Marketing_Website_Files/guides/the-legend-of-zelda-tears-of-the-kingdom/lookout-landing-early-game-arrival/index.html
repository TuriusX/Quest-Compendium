Lookout Landing: Early game arrival

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Establishing the base of operations

Lookout Landing serves as the central hub of Hyrule where you regroup with Purah and the survivors after descending from the Great Sky Island. You will obtain the Paraglider here and activate the first Skyview Tower to reveal the surface and sky map. Checking in with the various characters sets up the main quest to investigate the four regional phenomena across the kingdom.

Items

  • Paraglider: Received directly from Purah after activating the Lookout Landing Skyview Tower

Secrets

  • An underground emergency shelter entrance sits in the center of the town square; talk to the guard Scorpis to open the hatch revealing beds, a cooking pot, and access to the Royal Hidden Passage.

Shops and people

  • Mubs: General store merchant selling apples, hylian rice, fresh milk, and arrows
  • Karson: Builder NPC who repairs structures around the base

Tips

  • Activate the Lookout Landing Skyview Tower as early as possible because doing so is required to unlock the Paraglider.
  • Rest at the beds inside the Emergency Shelter for free healing instead of spending rupees at an inn.
  • Search the rooftop and balcony of the research lab building to find loose equipment and materials left behind by researchers.
