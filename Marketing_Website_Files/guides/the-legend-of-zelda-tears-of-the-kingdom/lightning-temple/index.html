Lightning Temple

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Gerudo regional phenomenon

The Lightning Temple is the dungeon associated with the Gerudo desert regional phenomenon, requiring Link to explore an ancient pyramid alongside Riju. Link must solve puzzles involving light reflection and electrical conduction to charge four batteries and unlock the central elevator. Charging the batteries unlocks the roof of the temple, culminating in a boss fight against Queen Gibdo.

Items

  • Gerudo Bow: Found in a treasure chest buried beneath a sand pile on the 5th floor
  • Gerudo Scimitar: Inside a treasure chest on the 1st floor guarded by Gibdos
  • Mirror Shield: In a chest in the Light Room on the higher floors

Secrets

  • Sand piles covering floors and chests can be cleared away by using Korok Fronds, fan devices, or gust-generating weapons.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
GibdoFire and LightningHighly resistant to physical attacks until exposed to elemental damage, which turns their bodies white and brittle.
Moth GibdoFire and LightningFlying variant of the Gibdo that hovers out of reach until hit with elemental attacks.
Queen GibdoFire and LightningBoss of the temple that summons Gibdo swarms from hives and requires Riju's lightning to stun.

Tips

  • Use Riju's lightning ability to destroy the purple Gibdo hives hanging throughout the temple to stop enemies from spawning.
  • Equipping a Mirror Shield allows you to easily reflect beams of light into receptors without needing to place stationary Zonai mirrors.
