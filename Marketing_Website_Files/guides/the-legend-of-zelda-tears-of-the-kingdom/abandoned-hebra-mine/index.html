Abandoned Hebra Mine

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Following the Master Kohga questline

This location is found deep in the northwestern Depths directly beneath Rito Village and serves as the destination for the third encounter with Master Kohga. You must activate the nearby Lightroot, confront Kohga in a battle involving Zonai flying devices, and awaken the dormant Steward Construct. Defeating the boss unlocks a Schema Stone and advances the "Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan" side adventure toward its conclusion.

Items

  • Schema Stone (Rocket Platform): Given by the awakened Steward Construct after defeating Master Kohga
  • Huge Crystallized Charge: Rewarded from the chest unlocked by defeating Master Kohga

Secrets

  • Reaching the mine requires diving into the Rito Village Chasm, which is concealed inside a cave opening on the southern cliffside of Rito Village rather than out in the open on the surface.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Master KohgaArrow shots to the head, aerial attacksBattles Link using a hovering Zonai Wing equipped with flamethrowers and archer minions.

Shops and people

  • Forge Construct: Sells Crystallized Charges and Large Crystallized Charges in exchange for Zonaite and Large Zonaite

Tips

  • Use the launchpads, updrafts, or attached Rockets to gain height above Kohga's flying vehicle so you can easily shoot him with arrows and knock him onto the ground.
