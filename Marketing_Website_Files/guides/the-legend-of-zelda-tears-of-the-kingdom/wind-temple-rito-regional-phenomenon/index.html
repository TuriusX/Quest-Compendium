Wind Temple: Rito regional phenomenon

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Completing the Rito quest

Link and Tulin board the massive Stormwind Ark flying high in the blizzard above the Hebra region. To stop the unending snowstorm, Link must activate five turbine locks scattered across the multiple decks of the ship and then defeat the boss Colgera.

Items

  • Opal: Inside a treasure chest frozen in ice on the main deck
  • Sapphire: Inside a treasure chest on the lowest exterior deck reached by gliding off the side

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Soldier ConstructHeadshotsStationed across the exterior and interior decks guarding the turbines.
AerocudaBow attacksFlies around the exterior and will dive down to strike Link while gliding.
ColgeraThe glowing ice spikes on its segmented bodyThe massive boss of the temple that Link fights mid-air using updrafts and diving attacks.

Tips

  • Use Tulin's gust ability directly into the front of the ship's five turbine gear mechanisms to spin and activate them.
  • You can melt any ice blocking doors or chests by holding a fire-infused weapon near it, throwing a Fire Fruit, or shooting an arrow fused with Fire Fruit.
  • The massive wind updraft coming from the central grating allows Link to deploy his paraglider and easily reach the highest points of the ship at any time.
  • During the Colgera fight, dive-bomb directly through the weak points on its body using the skydiving pose if you run out of arrows.
