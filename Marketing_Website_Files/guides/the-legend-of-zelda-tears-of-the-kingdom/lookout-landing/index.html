Lookout Landing

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Early game arrival

Lookout Landing serves as the central hub in Central Hyrule where Link reunites with Purah after descending from the Great Sky Island. Players receive crucial main story quests, unlock the Skyview Tower network, and gain access to the Paraglider here. The settlement expands over time, providing essential amenities like a shelter, shops, and researchers.

Items

  • Paraglider: Received from Purah during the main quest "Crisis at Hyrule Castle"
  • Archaic Warm Greaves: Found in a treasure chest on the Great Sky Island before arriving, but can be worn here to prevent cold damage during early weather shifts

Secrets

  • Opening the grate in the center of Lookout Landing leads to the Emergency Shelter, which contains free beds, a cooking pot, and access to the Royal Hidden Passage beneath Hyrule Castle.

Shops and people

  • Mastro / General Store: Sells basic provisions including arrows, apples, and milk
  • Jerrin: Cleans up the debris to reveal the Horned Statue in the Royal Hidden Passage for exchanging Heart Containers and Stamina Vessels

Tips

  • Activate the Lookout Landing Skyview Tower as soon as Purah allows it, as it unlocks regional map data and the ability to launch Link into the sky.
  • Check the Emergency Shelter under the central courtyard frequently, as characters move in and out, offering new side quests and rumors.
