Thunderhead Isles: Investigating the storm clouds

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom guide · Accessing the secret island

The Thunderhead Isles are a series of sky islands shrouded in a massive, permanent thunderstorm above the Faron region. Exploring this area leads to Dragonhead Island, where you find a key Zonai relic required to awaken the fifth Sage. Clearing the thunderstorm via the main quest significantly improves visibility and removes the danger of constant lightning strikes.

Items

  • Rubber Armor: Inside a breakable dome on the southern portion of Thunderhead Isles
  • Large Zonai Charge: In treasure chests scattered across the ruined archways and floating platforms
  • Sage's Will: Inside a treasure chest on an isolated, lower platform on the isles
  • Relic Mask: On Dragonhead Island at the end of the chain, behind a locked door requiring ten hearts to open

Secrets

  • The blinding fog and constant lightning across the isles can be cleared completely by completing the "Secret of the Ring Ruins" main quest in Kakariko Village and the Zonai Ruins.
  • A hidden chamber beneath the large dragon head structure holds the Joku-usin Shrine, reachable by ascending through the ruins.

Tips

  • Unequip all metal weapons, shields, and bows before entering if the storm is still active to avoid being struck by lightning.
  • You need at least ten Heart Containers to pull open the relic chamber door at Dragonhead Island; temporary yellow hearts do not count.
  • Launching from the Popla Foothills Skyview Tower provides the easiest initial glide toward the northern edge of the storm cloud.
