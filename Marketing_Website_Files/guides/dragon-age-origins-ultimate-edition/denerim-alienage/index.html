Denerim Alienage

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After entering the alienage

The Denerim Alienage becomes fully accessible during the Landsmeet questline, suffering under an apparent plague and heavy tevinter presence. The Warden must investigate the locked-down quarantine, uncover a Tevinter slave trade ring operating under the guise of an apothecary hospital, and secure the support of the city elves. The area also connects to the City Elf origin prologue and hosts personal quests like Cyrion's fate and the blind templar Ser Otto's investigation.

Items

  • Blind Charmer MISSABLE: Looted from the weapon rack inside the Alienage Hospice
  • Dawn Ring MISSABLE: Reward from Ser Otto or looted from his corpse in the Orphanage
  • Gift of the Grey MISSABLE: Rewarded by Shianni after resolving the slavers questline

Secrets

  • You can find a blood-soaked doll, a rabid dog, and foul odors to aid Ser Otto's orphanage investigation before unlocking the deeper haunted wing.

Tips

  • Bringing a City Elf Warden unique dialogue and emotional reactions from local residents, including your father Cyrion.
  • You can enter the Tevinter warehouse either through the hospice's back door key or by entering the rear apartments and intimidating or bribing the guards.
