Fort Drakon

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · During the final battle

Fort Drakon serves as the final gauntlet of the game, where the Warden and their chosen party breach the interior and fight to the roof. The interior is heavily defended by darkspawn commanders, blood mages, and shades, requiring careful combat navigation floor by floor. Reaching the rooftop triggers the climactic showdown against the Archdemon to end the Fifth Blight.

Items

  • The Veshialle MISSABLE: On a darkspawn weapon stand or lootable container inside the fort
  • Masterwork Silverite Rune MISSABLE: In a chest on the second floor

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
ArchdemonSpirit, ColdThe final boss of the game, capable of flight, breath attacks, vortex abilities, and summoning darkspawn waves as its health drops.
Genlock EmissarySpellcaster darkspawn found throughout the interior corridors, dangerous due to crowd control and area-of-effect spells like Crushing Prison.
Hurlock GeneralElite melee darkspawn found leading defensive lines on the main floor.
ShadeSpiritEthereal enemies encountered in conjured ambushes on the upper levels.

Tips

  • Ballistas stationed on the perimeter of the roof can be fired at the Archdemon to deal significant physical damage and ground it when it perches out of melee reach.
  • A rogue with high Cunning and mechanical skill can repeatedly unjam broken ballistas on the roof, enabling continuous heavy ranged damage.
  • Do not forget to use the army summon radial mechanic during the rooftop battle to bring in summoned troops to tie up darkspawn adds while focusing on the Archdemon.
  • Stock up heavily on health poultices and lyrium potions before entering the fort, as there is no way to return to town merchants once the siege begins.
