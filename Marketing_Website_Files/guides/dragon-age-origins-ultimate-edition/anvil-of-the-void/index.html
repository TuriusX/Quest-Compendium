Anvil of the Void

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · During the Deep Roads expedition

This is the deepest part of the Deep Roads where the Warden confronts Branka and determines the fate of Caridin's creation. You must navigate deadly hazards, fight through waves of golems, and face either Branka or Caridin in a climactic showdown. The outcome directly decides who becomes the next ruler of Orzammar and whether the Dwarves retain the ability to forge golems.

Items

  • The Veshialle: Caridin or Branka's drop (depending on your choice)
  • Helm of the Legion MISSABLE: Legion of the Dead tomb at the edge of the area
  • Caridin's Shield MISSABLE: Rewarded by Caridin or taken after the battle

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
BrankaElectricitySummons rock golems, splits into mirror images, and consumes lyrium veins to restore health.
CaridinColdHits heavily with shield attacks and is accompanied by several defensive rock golems.
Ancient Rock GolemColdImmune to nature damage; possesses powerful area-of-effect stomps and hurls boulders.

Tips

  • Interact with the lyrium veins scattered around the boss arena before the boss uses them to restore their health.
  • Bringing Shale along triggers unique, plot-critical dialogue and reactions to the fate of the Anvil.
  • Equip the full Legion of the Dead armor set on a warrior before the final battle to gain an enormous bonus to attack and defense.
