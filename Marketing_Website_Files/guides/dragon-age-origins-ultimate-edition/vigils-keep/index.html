Vigil's Keep

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · Awakening expansion
← All areas in this guide

Vigil's Keep serves as the main stronghold and home base for the Grey Wardens in the Awakening expansion. After fighting off an initial darkspawn assault to liberate the fortress, you use the keep to recruit companions, manage upgrades, and decide the fate of Amaranthine. The area includes the exterior grounds, interior throne room, and extensive underground basements and crypts.

  • Herren: Armor, weapons, crafting supplies, and Masterwork upgrades
  • Ambassador Cauthrien: Runes and magical goods

Tips
  • Invest in all upgrades for the Keep's walls, soldiers, and granite supply with Seneschal Varel to ensure its survival in the endgame.
  • Complete all companion quests and talk to your party members inside the throne room before embarking on the final sequence, as access will be locked off.
