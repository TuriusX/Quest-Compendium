Korcari Wilds

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · During the visit to Ostagar

The Warden enters the Korcari Wilds alongside fellow recruits Jory and Daveth under orders from Duncan to retrieve three vials of darkspawn blood and a cache of ancient Grey Warden treaties. While exploring the swamp, the party encounters scattered darkspawn patrols, wildlife, and signs of the mysterious Chasind people before eventually meeting Morrigan and her mother, Flemeth. Completing these tasks allows the party to return to Ostagar and proceed with the Joining ritual.

Items

  • Rigby's Field Journal MISSABLE: Looted from the body of Rigby in an abandoned camp in the central area
  • Chasind Trail Signs map notes MISSABLE: Traced through various Chasind Trail Markers scattered throughout the Wilds
  • Chasind Robes MISSABLE: Found inside the Chasind Cache revealed after following all Chasind Trail Signs
  • Chasind Crusher MISSABLE: Found inside the Chasind Cache unlocked by completing the Chasind Trail Signs quest
  • Barbarian Mace MISSABLE: Found inside the Chasind Cache
  • Thane Helmet MISSABLE: Inside the Chasind Cache
  • Jory's Blood Vial MISSABLE: Looted from an emissary or genlock during combat encounters
  • Daveth's Blood Vial MISSABLE: Looted from darkspawn defeated in the Wilds
  • Warden's Blood Vial MISSABLE: Looted from defeated darkspawn
  • Grey Warden Treaties MISSABLE: Recovered during the confrontation with Flemeth at her hut
  • Wolf-Killer MISSABLE: Looted from a chest in the ruins near the Genlock Emissary encounter
  • A Poison of the Deep MISSABLE: Looted from the dead Missionary Jogby

Secrets

  • Chasind Cache: Inspecting the series of Chasind Trail Signs placed across the map eventually reveals the location of a hidden cache containing valuable weapons and armor near the ruins.
  • Missionary Jogby's Letter: Looting Jogby's body gives a letter pointing to a locked chest near a fallen bridge, which requires Jogby's key to open.
  • Rigby's Last Will and Testament: A sealed lockbox can be found in Rigby's cache; you can choose to take it unopened to his widow Jetta in Redcliffe Village later or pry it open on the spot for a small reward.

Tips

  • You permanently lose access to the Korcari Wilds once you return to Duncan and complete the Joining ritual, so thoroughly explore every path before returning to Ostagar.
  • Bring herbalism ingredients and utilize poultices frequently, as the mix of ranged archers and magic-casting emissaries can overwhelm an early-game party.
  • Set Daveth and Jory's tactics before engaging deep encounters so Daveth focuses down enemy spellcasters from a distance while Jory shields the frontline.
