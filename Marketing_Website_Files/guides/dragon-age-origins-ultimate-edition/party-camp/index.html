Party Camp

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After leaving Ostagar

The Party Camp serves as the main safe haven for the Grey Warden after escaping the disaster at Ostagar. Here, you can converse with your companions, increase their approval, manage their equipment, and prepare before traveling to major quest locations. It also houses key merchant and crafting services that remain available throughout the main campaign.

Items

  • Grimoire of the Frozen Wastes: Bodahn Feddic's store inventory
  • Tome of Physical Technique: Bodahn Feddic's store inventory
  • Tome of Arcane Technique: Bodahn Feddic's store inventory
  • The Rose's Thorn: Bodahn Feddic's store inventory
  • Golden Mirror: Bodahn Feddic's store inventory

Shops and people

  • Bodahn Feddic: Sells weapons, armor, crafting supplies, skill and attribute tomes, backpacks, and companion gifts
  • Sandal: Enchantment service to slot and remove runes from weapons with rune slots for free

Tips

  • Talk to companions whenever the camp is visited, as many dialogue trees, personal quests, and romance options advance based on approval thresholds reached here.
  • Buying the backpacks from Bodahn Feddic increases your total inventory capacity.
  • Giving specific gift items bought from Bodahn or found across Ferelden to companions at camp increases their approval and unlocks attribute bonuses.
