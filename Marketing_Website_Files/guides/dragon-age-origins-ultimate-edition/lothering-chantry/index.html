Lothering Chantry

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · During the visit to Lothering

The Lothering Chantry serves as a temporary refuge for displaced villagers and where the player can enlist Ser Bryant to receive supplies. Inside, you can recruit the companion Leliana if she was not already recruited at the local tavern, and accept several side quests from the chanters and notice board. This interior becomes permanently inaccessible once you finish your business in Lothering and travel into the wider world map.

Items

  • Ancient History MISSABLE: On a bookshelf inside the Chantry library room
  • Heavy Chainmail Boots MISSABLE: Inside a locked wooden chest in the back quarters
  • Potion Recipes MISSABLE: Found on bookshelves and tables in the side chambers

Secrets

  • A locked cabinet behind Revered Mother Francesca requires lockpicking or high Cunning to open without alerting the Chantry guards, containing lyrium dust and healing draughts.

Shops and people

  • Ser Bryant: Offers free health poultices and a small coin stipend if persuaded or spoken to about aid

Tips

  • Complete all interactions, looting, and companion recruitment inside the Chantry before leaving Lothering, because the Darkspawn overrun and lock off the entire town permanently as soon as you depart to another main quest area.
  • Bring a rogue with lockpicking talent into the Chantry to open all back-room chests, as you will not be able to return with higher skills later.
