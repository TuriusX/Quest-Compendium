Brecilian Forest

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After arriving at the forest

The Brecilian Forest is home to a clan of Dalish Elves afflicted by a werewolf curse that threatens their survival. The Warden ventures into the woods to find the source of the curse, navigating through aggressive wildlife, ancient undead, and sylvans to locate the Lady of the Forest. The quest ultimately requires choosing whether to broker peace, side with the werewolves, or wipe them out to secure an alliance for the Blight.

Items

  • Ironbark: Found on a fallen tree trunk in the West Brecilian Forest
  • Ancient Elven Boots MISSABLE: Found inside a locked sarcophagus in the hermits/ruins area within the forest ruins lower level
  • Juggernaut Armor piece: Found in a gravestone sarcophagus after disturbing the wisp in West Brecilian Forest

Secrets

  • Disturbing the ancient gravestones across the East and West Brecilian Forest summons Revenants that guard pieces of the Juggernaut Armor set.
  • Finding and speaking with the Mad Hermit in the East Brecilian Forest allows you to trade specific quest items or camp gifts for useful lore items and an acorn needed by the Grand Oak.

Shops and people

  • Mad Hermit: Trades the Grand Oak's acorn, a book of elven tales, and miscellaneous items in exchange for trinkets like scarves or statues

Tips

  • Bring mages with fire-based spells to easily dispatch Sylvans and Wild Sylvans, as they are exceptionally vulnerable to burning.
  • You must either help the Grand Oak retrieve its stolen acorn from the Mad Hermit or slay the tree to obtain a wooden token needed to bypass the impassable barrier of mist in the East Brecilian Forest.
  • Inspecting the small, glowing spirit-like wisps near gravestones immediately triggers difficult encounters with Revenants and their skeletal minions, so buff your party before interacting with them.
