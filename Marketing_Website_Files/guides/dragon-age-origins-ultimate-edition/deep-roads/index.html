Deep Roads

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After entering the roads

The Warden descends past the Orzammar gates into the vast subterranean ruins of the Deep Roads in search of Branka and the Anvil of the Void. Navigating through multiple distinct sectors—including the Aeducan Thaig, Caridin's Cross, Ortan Thaig, and the Dead Trenches—the party must fight through massive Darkspawn hordes and salvage ancient dwarven relics. The expedition culminates in deciding the fate of the Anvil alongside Paragon Caridin or Branka, determining the next King of Orzammar.

Items

  • Topsider's Blade MISSABLE: Rewarded after assembling the parts and placing them on the Warrior's Tomb in Ortan Thaig
  • Asunder Boots MISSABLE: Found in a rubble pile during the "Asunder" quest within the Deep Roads
  • Dead Trenches Boots MISSABLE: Looted from the sarcophagus near the entrance of the Dead Trenches
  • Dead Trenches Gauntlets MISSABLE: Found inside a sarcophagus in the central halls of the Dead Trenches
  • Dead Trenches Armor MISSABLE: Found on an altar near the tomb complex in the Dead Trenches
  • Dead Trenches Helm MISSABLE: Found on an altar surrounded by Legionnaire spirits in the Dead Trenches

Secrets

  • Placing the Hilt, Blade, and Pommel of the Topsider's Honor onto the Warrior's Tomb in Ortan Thaig summons an ancient spirit that rewards a powerful longsword.
  • Interacting with all bloody body bag parts scattered throughout the Deep Roads triggers the "Asunder" quest, which allows you to summon and either defeat or release a demon at an altar for a reward.

Tips

  • Carry abundant Lyrium Potions and Health Poultices before entering, as the Deep Roads spans several long areas with very limited vendor options.
  • Bring Wynne or a dedicated spirit healer to continually cleanse party members of lingering Darkspawn curse effects and sustained poison.
  • Target the Broodmother's tentacles first to stop them from grabbing and suffocating your frontline warriors.
  • During the battle at the Anvil of the Void, interact with the Lyrium nodes scattered around the arena to instantly replenish your resources and deny bosses the ability to absorb them.
