Elven Ruins

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After entering the ruins

The Elven Ruins lie beneath the Brecilian Forest and serve as the final dungeon of the Nature of the Beast questline. Deep inside, you navigate past traps, undead, and werewolves to reach the lair of the Lady of the Forest and Witherfang. Your choices here resolve the ancient curse and determine whether the Dalish elves, the werewolves, or both join your army against the Blight.

Items

  • Arcane Warrior Specialization MISSABLE: Upper Ruins, learned by communing with the glowing phylactery in a side chamber
  • Ancient Elven Armor MISSABLE: Lower Ruins, inside the sarcophagus opened by completing the Elven Ritual
  • Juggernaut Armor Pieces MISSABLE: Found on Revenants summoned by disturbing tombstones across the Brecilian Forest and Ruins

Secrets

  • An ancient fountain ritual puzzle in the Lower Ruins unlocks a sealed door leading to a burial chamber with a Revenant and the Ancient Elven Armor.
  • A glowing phylactery hidden in a ruined room of the Upper Ruins contains the trapped memories of an ancient elf who can teach you the Arcane Warrior specialization in exchange for setting them free.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes

RevenantFireSummoned by disturbing the sarcophagus after completing the elven fountain ritual.
WitherfangFought alongside werewolves if you choose to side with the Dalish elves or fail to negotiate peace.

Tips

  • For the fountain ritual, take the earthen jug, fill it with water, place it on the altar, kneel and pray, take a single sip, and pour the rest back into the fountain.
  • Disarm the floor glyph traps in the ruins carefully, as triggering them often roots your party while archers and undead ambush you.
  • If you want to achieve peace between the elves and werewolves, do not attack the Lady of the Forest and agree to bring Keeper Zathrian down into the ruins.
