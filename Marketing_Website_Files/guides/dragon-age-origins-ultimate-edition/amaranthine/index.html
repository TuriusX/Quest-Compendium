Amaranthine

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · Awakening expansion

Amaranthine is the primary human city and trading hub in the Awakening expansion, governed by the player as the Arl of Amaranthine. The Warden visits to investigate darkspawn sightings, resolve political conflicts, and recruit allies, notably encountering the rogue Anders early on and later finding the elven mage Velanna's lead or recruiting the dwarf Sigrun nearby. Later in the campaign, the city falls under direct darkspawn siege, forcing the player into a major decision to either burn Amaranthine to the ground to save resources or defend it street by street.

Items

  • Howe Family Bow MISSABLE: In a chest inside the Howe Estate/Crown and Lion inn area during the City Elves/Howe investigations
  • Key to Smuggler's Cove MISSABLE: Acquired during the Smuggler's Run or Law and Order questline from either the smugglers or Constable Aidan

Secrets

  • Smuggler's Cove entrance | Interacting with the suspicious trapdoor in the abandoned warehouse leads to an underground smuggling network beneath the city streets.

Shops and people

  • Glassric the Weaponsmith: High-tier weapons, armor, and runecrafting materials located in the market square.
  • Octham the Grocer: Crafting ingredients, gifts, and basic supplies found in the central market.
  • Henley: Various trade goods, crafting recipes, and potion reagents in the market district.

Tips

  • Siding with Constable Aidan or the smugglers locked in the tavern will permanently lock you out of the opposing faction's questline for the rest of the expansion.
  • Completing the defensive upgrades for Vigil's Keep before triggering the siege gives you the strategic freedom to save the city of Amaranthine without automatically dooming the Keep to total destruction.
