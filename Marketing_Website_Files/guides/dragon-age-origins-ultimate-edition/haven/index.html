Haven

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After arriving at the village

After arriving in Haven, the Warden seeks out Brother Genitivi to locate the Urn of Sacred Ashes. The secretive and hostile villagers attempt to hide their cult activities, culminating in an inevitable armed confrontation. Investigating the Chantry at the top of the hill reveals a hidden passage leading toward the ruined temple and the mountains.

Items

  • Small Carved Figurine MISSABLE: Found in a chest inside the first house on the left
  • Ancient Elven Boots MISSABLE: Found in the Chantry inside an iron chest

Secrets

  • Unlocking the hidden door inside the Chantry by examining the suspicious wall altar behind the pulpit reveals Brother Genitivi.

Enemies

EnemyNotes
Haven GuardHostile villagers armed with shields and blades that attack once your investigation is uncovered.
Cultist MageEnemy spellcasters stationed around the village and inside the Chantry who cast debilitating ice and spirit spells.

Tips

  • Search all villager homes for loot before entering the Chantry, as the entire village turns permanently hostile after you confront the reverend father inside.
  • Bring a rogue with decent Lockpicking skill to access the locked containers spread throughout the village huts.
