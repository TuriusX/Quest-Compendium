Denerim

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After arriving at the capital

Denerim is the capital city of Ferelden and serves as a major hub containing various sub-districts, side quests, and main story progression points. Players visit the city repeatedly to fulfill quests for companion storylines, faction tasks from the Blackstone Irregulars and Mage's Collective, and eventually to participate in the Landsmeet. As the story advances toward the climax, key areas like the Alienage and the Arl of Denerim's Estate become accessible to resolve the kingdom's political crisis.

Items

  • The Veshialle: Sold by Gorim in the Denerim Market District
  • Duncan's Shield MISSABLE: Found in the secret armory within the Arl of Eamon's Denerim Estate, accessed during "The Lost Courier" or through a hidden mechanism
  • Gift: White Runestone: Found in a chest inside the Wonders of Thedas shop

Secrets

  • Unlocking the hidden Back Alley locations requires accepting side assignments or simply traveling between Denerim districts to trigger ambushes in Dirty Back Alley, Dark Alley, and Rundown Back Alley.
  • Solving the "Friends of Red Jenny" quest requires delivering a painted box from the Circle Tower to a mysterious door found in the Denerim Market District.

Shops and people

  • Wonders of Thedas: High-end magical staves, robes, accessories, and enchanting ingredients
  • Gorim: Heavy weapons, armor, Masterwork crafting components, and various unique high-tier equipment
  • Alarith's Store: Elfroots, herbs, crafting recipes, and regional Alienage goods once the Alienage is open

Tips

  • Buy the Master Archer or Combat training tomes from the merchants in the Market District as early as you can afford them, as they offer permanent talent points.
  • Do not sell off miscellaneous quest items before checking the Blackstone Irregulars board and the Mage's Collective liaison located near the Wonders of Thedas.
  • When attempting to defeat Sir Cauthrien instead of surrendering, pull your party back into the entryway hallway to avoid archer fire from the upper balconies.
