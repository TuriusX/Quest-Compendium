Circle Tower

Dragon Age: Origins - Ultimate Edition guide · After recruiting the mages

After completing "Broken Circle" and siding with the Circle of Magi, you can revisit the tower across Lake Calenhad in peace. The survivors gather mainly on the first floor to regroup and help the Warden's war effort. You can turn in ongoing Mage's Collective side quests and access a merchant to stock up on crafting materials and spellcaster gear.

Items

  • The Spellweaver MISSABLE: Looted from the Arcane Horror during "Broken Circle" on the second floor, accessible if left behind
  • Black Greystone Collar: In a chest on the Apprentice Quarters floor

Secrets

  • Activating the four apprentice statues in the correct sequence on the Great Hall and Apprentice floors before the Fade sequence unlocks the Summons and the summoning portal; if you missed looting the resulting chest, it remains on the third floor.

Shops and people

  • Quartermaster: Sells lyrium dust in unlimited quantities, high-tier staves, potion recipes, and mage robes

Tips

  • The Circle Tower Quartermaster is the primary source of unlimited lyrium dust in the game, making this visit vital for stocking up on materials to brew potent lyrium potions.
  • You can turn in several completed Mage's Collective bag quests to the liaison stationed on the tower's ground floor.
