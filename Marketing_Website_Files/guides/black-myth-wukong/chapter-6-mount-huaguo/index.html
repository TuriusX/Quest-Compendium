Chapter 6: Mount Huaguo

Black Myth: Wukong guide · The final journey to the Destined One's ancestral home.
← All areas in this guide

The Destined One arrives at Mount Huaguo, Sun Wukong's birthplace, to retrieve the Great Sage's original relics and armor set. Traversing the vast misty valley requires using the Somersault Cloud to fly between cliffside arenas, defeating powerful mythical beasts guarding the legendary equipment. The chapter concludes with a trek up the peak to face the final tests and claim the mantle of the Great Sage.

Enemies
Gold-Armored Rhino
Weak to Heavy stance breaks

Its rocky horn deflects frontal attacks until broken with heavy charged strikes.

Cloudtreading Deer
Weak to Fire

Transitions between a frost phase and a blood phase with large area-of-effect hazards across the arena.

Feng-Tail General
Weak to High stamina and Fire resistance

Requires mounting and holding onto its feelers through repeated massive jumps to pacify it.

Supreme Inspector

A recurring celestial boss fought at the start of the chapter before gaining the Somersault Cloud.

Stone Monkey / The Great Sage's Broken Shell

The multi-phase final confrontation testing mastery over staff stances and counter-timing.

Tips
  • The Somersault Cloud is summoned by pressing the designated button in open outdoor spaces, allowing you to fly freely across the valley to scout boss locations marked by environmental anomalies like storms and lightning.
  • Equipping the full set of Wukong's original armor allows you to pull the Ruyi Jingu Bang from the stone pillar beneath the Water Curtain Cave.
