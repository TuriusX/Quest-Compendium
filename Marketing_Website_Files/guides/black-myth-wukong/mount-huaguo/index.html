Mount Huaguo

Black Myth: Wukong guide · The Destined One begins the journey
← All areas in this guide

Mount Huaguo is the setting of Chapter 6, serving as Sun Wukong's ancestral home where the Destined One gathers the legendary relics and original armor set. Players gain the Somersault Cloud early in the chapter, granting free aerial exploration across the expansive, open-world valley. The main objective is to defeat major elemental bosses across the region to reclaim the Monkey King's mythical equipment before confronting the final challenges.

Enemies
Gold-Armored Rhino

Possesses an armored horn on its snout that must be shattered with heavy strikes to expose its weak flesh.

Feng-Tail General
Weak to Fire resistance

A colossal flying insect that cannot be fought directly; you must land on its back and hold on while enduring massive bursts of fire damage.

Cloudtreader

A swift deer boss that creates freezing frost pools across the arena, requiring mobility spells to counter.

Supreme Inspector

A celestial boss utilizing a flaming wheel and aerial mobility; relies on precise evasion timings.

Giant Shigandang

A hidden colossal stone monster unlocked using all four Skandhas collected across previous chapters.

Tips
  • You can dismount the Somersault Cloud directly into a heavy aerial plunge attack to initiate battles on the ground with significant stagger damage.
  • Use the Fireproof Mantle vessel when landing on the Feng-Tail General to survive the intense burn phases while gripping its antennae.
