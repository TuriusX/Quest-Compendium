Chapter 5: Flaming Mountains

Black Myth: Wukong guide · The path leads to a volcanic landscape of fire and ash.
← All areas in this guide

The Destined One ventures through the blazing peaks of the Flaming Mountains to collect the next relic and resolve the strife within the Bull King's household. You must navigate scorching lava, bypass mechanical five-element carts, and extinguish dangerous magma rivers to reach the fiery summit. The chapter culminates in an emotional confrontation with Red Boy and the Yaksha King at the Rakshasa Palace.

Shops and people
  • Keeper's Shrine: Sells standard crafting materials, medicine ingredients, and gourd upgrades

Tips
  • Equipping the Fireproof Mantle vessel provides total immunity to environmental lava damage and greatly reduces fire damage in combat.
  • Using the Thrust Stance helps maintain safe distance when trading blows against enemies standing directly in lava pools.
