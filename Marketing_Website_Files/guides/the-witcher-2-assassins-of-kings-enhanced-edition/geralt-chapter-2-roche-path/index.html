Geralt, Chapter 2 (Roche Path)

The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition guide · Geralt infiltrates the Kaedweni camp and deals with the curse
← All areas in this guide

Geralt arrives at the Kaedweni military camp outside Vergen alongside Vernon Roche following King Foltest's assassination trail. To advance the main questline, Geralt must investigate conspiracies, lift King Henselt's blood curse, and acquire four symbolic artifacts to dispel the supernatural mist battlefield. The chapter culminates in an assault on Vergen and a decisive confrontation with Letho's allies and Henselt.

Enemies
Rotfiend
Weak to Silver Sword, Necrophage Oil, IgniSteal / drop Rotfiend Blood, Teeth

Explodes violently shortly after its health is reduced to critical levels.

Bullvore
Weak to Aard, Yrden, Silver SwordSteal / drop Bullvore Eyes

Bulky monstrosity capable of charging and dealing heavy knockback in narrow cave paths.

Kaedweni Conspirator
Weak to Steel Sword, Riposte, ParryingSteal / drop Orens, Common Steel Weapons

Armored human soldier that blocks frequently and attacks in coordinated groups.

Draug
Weak to Yrden, Quen, Silver SwordSteal / drop Draug Armor Fragment

Massive supernatural boss inside the mist that summons catapult bombardments and firestorms.

Shops and people
  • Mael: Armor diagrams, crafting materials, and weapon upgrades located inside the Kaedweni cantonment.
  • Sambor: Traps, common herbs, crafting supplies, and various camp camp follower oddities found outside the main palisade.
  • Relic Vendor: Sells specific plot-relevant talismans, silver items, and curiosities near the canteen tents.

Tips
  • Quen is critical during the mist crossing to shield against relentless wraiths and projectile fire from phantom archers.
  • In the crypt across the mist, you can bypass the Dun Banner ghost combat entirely by correctly recounting historical facts about the Battle of Brenna.
  • Roll away immediately whenever a Rotfiend stops moving and begins to spasm, as its corpse detonation can instantly deplete your vitality.
