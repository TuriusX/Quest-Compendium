Points of no return

  • Escaping the dungeons in Prologue: "Eagle Eye" and completing the ballista/stealth challenges in La Valette Castle.
  • Boarding the barge at the end of Chapter 1: All Flotsam side quests, fistfighting, poker, arm wrestling, and the choice between Roche's and Iorveth's paths.
  • Dispelling the blood mist in Chapter 2: All Vergen or Kaedweni Camp side quests, local contracts, and path-exclusive Chapter 2 achievements.
  • Entering the summit at the amphitheater in Chapter 3: All Loc Muinne side quests, the dragon-related choices, and remaining spell/rune puzzles.

Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Play the Arena tutorial mode to unlock basic combat achievements and practice parrying.

  2. Step 2

    Start Playthrough 1 on Hard or lower and side with Iorveth at the end of Chapter 1, ensuring you explore Vergen and complete all path-specific side quests, mini-games, and Chapter 3 choices.

  3. Step 3

    Farm or clean up general combat, alchemy, and crafting achievements before finishing your first playthrough.

  4. Step 4

    Start Playthrough 2 on Dark difficulty and side with Vernon Roche at the end of Chapter 1 to unlock the alternate narrative achievements and craft the exclusive Dark mode gear sets.

  5. Step 5

    Complete Insane difficulty only if you prefer it over Dark, though Dark difficulty is sufficient for the Enhanced Edition 100% completion.
