Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Establish your camp in Temple Woods, chop wood, mine ore, plant crops, and craft basic gear to clear tutorial and early survival tasks.

  2. Step 2

    Venture into Bramblemead Valley and Whispering Swamp, looting the local Kara vaults for cores, practicing combat parries, and completing local secondary quests.

  3. Step 3

    Progress into Fractured Plains and Stormtouched Highlands, unlocking utility spells and defeating regional bosses including the Black Knight Titan.

  4. Step 4

    Explore Bloodblight Swamp and Bleakfields Valley while upgrading your weapons with enchantments and whetstones.

  5. Step 5

    Traverse the Lake of Lost Souls and Hope's Fall, tackling high-tier vaults and continuing side quest completions.

  6. Step 6

    Journey through Alcarrid Oasis, Dunes of Uzzer, and The Burning Spire, defeating the Umbral Sands regional boss.

  7. Step 7

    Enter the Scorned Wilderness, summon General Velgar at the Dragon Altar, and slay Queen Kuldra.

  8. Step 8

    Finish any remaining gold agility course medals, complete 3 secondary quests per zone, and grind combat and artisan skills to Level 99.
