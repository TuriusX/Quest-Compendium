Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete the main story campaign on Normal or Hard difficulty while clearing Most Wanted side missions along the way.

  2. Step 2

    Tackle miscellaneous combat, Batmobile, and predator achievements across Bleake Island, Miagani Island, Founders' Island, and inside the Stagg Airships or Panessa Studios.

  3. Step 3

    Finish all Most Wanted missions, defeat all villains, complete all Batmobile trials, and lock everyone up in the Gotham City Police Department.

  4. Step 4

    Collect all 243 Riddler trophies, riddles, breakable objects, and bomb victims spanning Bleake Island, Miagani Island, Founders' Island, ACE Chemicals, Stagg Airships, Panessa Studios, Lady of Gotham Statue, and the Arkham Knight HQ.

  5. Step 5

    Apprehend the Riddler at his orphanage to complete the Gotham City Police Department lockup and activate the full Knightfall Protocol.

    Also in this step: Knightfall

  6. Step 6

    Complete all base game AR Challenges with 3 stars or maximum rival points to earn the required AR challenge achievements.

  7. Step 7

    Complete New Game Plus mode to finish the story at maximum difficulty with counter icons disabled.
