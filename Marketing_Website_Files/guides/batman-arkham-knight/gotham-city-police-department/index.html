Gotham City Police Department

Batman™: Arkham Knight guide · Batman drops off Poison Ivy
← All areas in this guide

Batman brings Poison Ivy into custody at the Gotham City Police Department headquarters and places her in an isolation cell inside the maximum security lockup. While inside, you speak with Commissioner Jim Gordon to coordinate the ongoing response to Scarecrow's chemical attack on Gotham. You can also explore the precinct's offices, talk to active staff, and observe holding cells before heading back out into the city.

Tips
  • Take time to interact with officers in the bullpen and lockup between story missions, as their dialogue updates to reflect current city events.
  • Look inside the evidence room to inspect equipment and memorabilia from Batman's past encounters with his rogue's gallery.
