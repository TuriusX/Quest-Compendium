Miagani Island

Batman™: Arkham Knight guide · Exploring the city after the airship mission
← All areas in this guide

After completing the Stagg Enterprises airships mission, Batman returns to Miagani Island to track the Cloudburst device and deploy defenses at Wayne Tower. During this phase, you must dismantle Arkham Knight drone checkpoints, watchtowers, and explosive devices scattered across the island while handling militia resistance. It also serves as a central hub for continuing Most Wanted side missions, such as tracking Firefly or rescuing firefighters.

Achievements here
Creature of the NightCapture Man-Bat after curing his condition through the "Creature of the Night" Most Wanted mission and lock him in GCPD.
The Cat and the BatFinish the third Riddler trial with Catwoman at the casino and claim your next key.
Riddler on the RampageComplete the fourth trial in Riddler's "Riddler's Revenge" side mission, located under Elliott Memorial Hospital. Navigate the Batmobile obstacle course successfully to retrieve Catwoman's collar key.
Jekyll & HydeStop Two-Face's heist at the Bank of Gotham on Miagani Island and defeat all robbers. Deliver Two-Face to lockup at GCPD to complete the "Two-Faced Bandit" Most Wanted mission.
Scar of the BatComplete the "Creature of the Night" Most Wanted mission by curing Kirk Langstrom. Track down Man-Bat across the city, administer both doses of the antidote, and deliver him to GCPD.
Death by DesignComplete the seventh trial in the "Riddler's Revenge" side quest line. Solve the spinning puzzle chamber with Catwoman and Batman to collect the next key.
The Cat Came BackRescue Catwoman from the Pinkney Orphanage by solving the final trial and defeating Riddler's initial mech phase.

Tips
  • Use the Disruptor to sabotage drone controllers and medic defibrillator packs before initiating rooftop predator encounters.
  • The central suspension bridge connecting Miagani to Bleake Island can be raised or lowered to cut off pursuing militia vehicles.
