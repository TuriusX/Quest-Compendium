The Stolen Cat Mummy

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle guide · Indy sneaks into Castel Sant'Angelo and Vatican City on the thief's trail ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Following a theft at Marshall College, Indy infiltrates Castel Sant'Angelo to reach Passetto di Borgo and connect with his Vatican contact, Antonio. After making his way inside Vatican City, Indy must secure a camera, document hidden inscriptions across sacred grounds, and unravel ancient clues pointing toward a giant's tomb.
