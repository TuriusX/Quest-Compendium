Points of no return

  • None: Fast travel and post-game map access allow you to return to all major regions to clean up missed collectibles, puzzles, and achievements.

Recommended order
  1. Complete the prologue and initial exploration in Marshall College, learning the basics of hand-to-hand combat, stealth, and whip mechanics.
  2. Travel to Vatican City and proceed through the story while completing local Fieldwork, solving puzzles, and collecting adventure books and journal entries.
  3. Head to Gizeh to explore The Great Sphinx region, tackling ancient tombs, open-world side quests, and unlocking upgrades.
  4. Explore the dense jungle temples of Sukhothai, utilizing stealth, camera photography, and complete all environmental puzzles and area-specific mysteries.
  5. Return to Marshall College (Night) and follow the narrative investigation, collecting any newly spawned notes and story items.
  6. Journey through the Himalayas, focusing on the linear story progression, stealth encounters, and immediate collectibles in the mountain passes.
  7. Complete the final story sequences during The Great Sphinx (Revisit) and The Himalayas (Revisit) to unlock the remaining story achievements.
  8. Access free-roam post-game or reload travel maps to clean up remaining Fieldwork, photos, journals, medicine bottles, skill upgrades, and miscellaneous combat achievements.
