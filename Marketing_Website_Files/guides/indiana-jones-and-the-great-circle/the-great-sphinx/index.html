The Great Sphinx

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle guide · Entering the secret chambers beneath the Sphinx
← All areas in this guide

Indiana Jones explores the Giza plateau and solves environmental puzzles around the Great Sphinx to unlock the sealed entrance leading into the subterranean chambers beneath it. Inside, players navigate dark corridors, avoid traps, and use light-based mechanics to access the deeper ruins of the Great Circle.

Tips
  • Light every wall brazier you encounter with your torch or lighter to permanently illuminate chambers and make navigation easier.
  • Equip a worker or dig-site disguise when traversing the exterior perimeter to avoid raising an alarm with guards before entering the Sphinx's subterranean entrance.
