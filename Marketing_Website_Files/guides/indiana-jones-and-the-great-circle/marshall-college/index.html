Marshall College

Indiana Jones and the Great Circle guide · Opening chapter, Indy's office and the museum
← All areas in this guide

Indiana Jones awakens late at night inside Marshall College to discover an intruder breaking into the museum archives. Indy must explore his office and the adjoining exhibits to investigate the disturbance, gather essential gear, and confront the giant thief who stole an ancient relic. This prologue serves as the tutorial area where the player learns primary movement, basic stealth, camera photography, and hand-to-hand combat mechanics.

Tips
  • Search around desks and display cases thoroughly to locate early Adventure Points and readable historical notes before confronting the intruder.
  • Use your camera to photograph notable artifacts around the museum displays to earn early Adventure Points for character upgrades.
  • Timing your block just as an enemy swings triggers a parry, staggering them and opening them up to heavy counter-punches.
