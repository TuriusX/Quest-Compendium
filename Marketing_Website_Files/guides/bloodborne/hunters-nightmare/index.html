Hunter's Nightmare

Bloodborne guide · After interacting with the Amygdala in the Cathedral Ward

The Hunter's Nightmare is the opening area of the The Old Hunters expansion, reached after being grabbed by the Amygdala outside Oedon Chapel while carrying the Eye of a Blood-drunk Hunter. You must navigate distorted, blood-drenched streets and twisted ruins filled with maddened hunters and beastly abominations to reach the grand cathedral. The main path culminates in unlocking shortcuts back to the first lamp and confronting the fallen holy blade, Ludwig the Accursed.

Items

  • Old Hunter Top Hat: Near the start of the area, found on a corpse on the curved staircase patrolled by Old Hunters
  • Old Hunter Garb: On a corpse in the ruined square guarded by several hounds and an Old Hunter
  • Old Hunter Gloves: On a corpse near the ruined steps leading up through the plaza
  • Old Hunter Trousers: On a corpse resting in the plaza near the base of the twisted architectural ruins
  • Beast Cutter: In the upper ruins near the grand staircase, looted from a corpse behind a collapsed stone pillar
  • Boom Hammer: Inside the dark house rigged with explosive delayed-fuse wheelchairs, on a corpse in the lower level
  • Firing Hammer Badge: Dropped by the Beast-possessed Old Hunter wielding a Boom Hammer inside the building leading down to the river
  • Beasthunter Saif: Down in the lower blood river, guarded by a red-eyed hunter near the ladder that leads back up
  • Constable's Gloves: Found on a corpse in the blood river patrolled by Bloodlickers
  • Constable's Trousers: On a corpse sitting in the blood marsh near the Bloodlickers
  • Constable's Garb: On a corpse submerged in the blood river near a group of Bloodlickers
  • Bloodletter MISSABLE: Given by Brador or dropped when slaying him inside his cell using the Underground Cell Inner Chamber Key
  • Holy Moonlight Sword: Dropped by Ludwig's severed head after the boss fight, or received by talking to him wearing Church attire

Secrets

  • The dark house on the path to the blood river mirrors the shortcut house in Central Yharn, but it is rigged with explosive delayed traps where a seated wheelchair mob waits.
  • You can speak to Ludwig's surviving severed head after defeating him; wearing Church chest armor triggers different dialogue allowing you to receive the Holy Moonlight Sword peacefully without striking it down.

Shops and people

  • Simon the Harrowed: Exhausting his dialogue throughout his locations progresses his questline and grants weapon and lore rewards

Tips

  • Old Hunters will actively fight the native beasts in the streets, so you can hang back and let them weaken or kill each other before engaging.
  • Equip armor with high Blunt and Arcane defense for the Ludwig encounter to survive his heavy charge attacks and arcane energy waves.
