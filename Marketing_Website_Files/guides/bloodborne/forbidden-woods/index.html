Forbidden Woods

Unlocked with the password obtained by examining Laurence's skull in the Grand Cathedral, the Forbidden Woods is a massive, winding wilderness filled with infected villagers and grotesque snake clusters. Navigating through the sprawling village and treacherous snake-infested swamps ultimately leads down to the forbidden tomb, where you must defeat the Shadows of Yharnam to gain access to Byrgenwerth.

Enemies
Shadow of Yharnam
Weak to Bolt, ArcaneSteal / drop Blood Rapture Rune

A trio boss consisting of a melee katana wielder, a hybrid candle-flame caster, and a long-range pyromancer

Snake Ball
Weak to Fire, Thrust

Groups of serpents that can spit poison and bite rapidly

Giant Snake Cluster
Weak to Fire

Massive tangled balls of serpents that possess high poise, deadly reach, and high poison buildup

Snake parasite villager
Weak to Fire, Thrust

Huntsmen whose heads burst into snakes when brought below half health, giving them extended range

Suspicious Beggar
Weak to Poison, FireSteal / drop Beast Rune

An NPC found atop a roof who is secretly an Abhorrent Beast; attacking him triggers a fierce miniboss fight

Shops and people
  • Valtr: Gives the Impurity rune to initiate the League, offering rewards and summon availability upon crushing Vermin

Tips
  • Equip armor with high slow poison resistance and bring plenty of Antidotes before entering the lower swamp areas.
  • Look out for tripwires on the forest floor, as stepping on them triggers swinging wooden spiked traps that deal massive damage.
  • During the Shadow of Yharnam fight, use the large tombstone in the arena to block fireballs while focusing down the aggressive melee katana user first.
