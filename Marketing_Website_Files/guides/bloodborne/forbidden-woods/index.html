Forbidden Woods

Bloodborne guide · After touching the skull in the Grand Cathedral

Unlocked with the password obtained by examining Laurence's skull in the Grand Cathedral, the Forbidden Woods is a massive, winding wilderness filled with infected villagers and grotesque snake clusters. Navigating through the sprawling village and treacherous snake-infested swamps ultimately leads down to the forbidden tomb, where you must defeat the Shadows of Yharnam to gain access to Byrgenwerth.

Items

  • Cainhurst Summons MISSABLE: Found on a desk inside the clinic sickroom, accessed via the hidden cave shortcut leading back to Iosefka's Clinic
  • Graveguard Set: Found on a corpse across a gap in the snake swamp near where giant snake parasite enemies patrol
  • Clockwise Metamorphosis: Found in the lower marsh section guarded by giant snake clusters
  • Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis: Found on a ledge accessible via roots near the windmill elevator shortcut
  • Cannon: Found on the upper rafters inside the large wooden windmill barn housing the elevator
  • Beast Roar: Found inside a locked house surrounded by hounds in the village section, requiring access through a window or nearby roof

Secrets

  • Shortcut to Iosefka's Clinic | Found by descending into a poisonous cave behind the dog cages in the village, leading to a long ladder that exits into the clinic's courtyard
  • League Oath | Speak to Valtr inside the shack next to the first lantern to acquire the Impurity rune and join the League

Tips

  • Equip armor with high slow poison resistance and bring plenty of Antidotes before entering the lower swamp areas.
  • Look out for tripwires on the forest floor, as stepping on them triggers swinging wooden spiked traps that deal massive damage.
  • During the Shadow of Yharnam fight, use the large tombstone in the arena to block fireballs while focusing down the aggressive melee katana user first.
