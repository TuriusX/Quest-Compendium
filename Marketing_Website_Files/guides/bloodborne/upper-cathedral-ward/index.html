Upper Cathedral Ward

Bloodborne guide · After obtaining the Upper Cathedral Key

Accessed by unlocking the locked door at the top of the Healing Church Workshop, this area contains the Orphanage and serves as the headquarters of the Choir. You will navigate dark corridors populated by Brainsuckers, restore power to the building, and defeat the Celestial Emissary to progress. Behind the main boss arena lies a hidden passage leading to Ebrietas, Daughter of the Cosmos.

Items

  • Cosmic Eye Watcher Badge: On a corpse in the darkened room patrolled by Brainsuckers, near the perimeter hallway
  • Make Contact Gesture: Obtained by interacting with the petrified Scholar corpse on the balcony overlooking the Lumenflower Gardens
  • Orphanage Key: Dropped by a Brainsucker on the upper floor of the main hall inside the building
  • Great Isz Chalice: Dropped by Ebrietas, Daughter of the Cosmos upon defeat

Secrets

  • The boss arena for Ebrietas, Daughter of the Cosmos is hidden behind the Lumenflower Gardens; break the large stained-glass window behind the Celestial Emissary lamp to reveal the elevator leading down to the Altar of Despair.

Tips

  • Equipping armor with high Arcane defense and stocking up on Sedatives will significantly reduce the danger posed by the Brainsuckers and Ebrietas's frenzy-inducing or arcane-heavy attacks.
  • Thrust weapons, such as the transformed Church Pick or Threaded Cane's whip thrusts, deal extra damage to the kin enemies in this area.
