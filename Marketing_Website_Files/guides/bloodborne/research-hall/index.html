Research Hall

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Ludwig, the Accursed

The Research Hall is a massive, multi-tiered facility within the Hunter's Nightmare populated by deformed, enlarged-head patients. Players must navigate a spiraling network of staircases and platforms to reach the top level and operate a mechanism that raises the central staircase. Altering the stairs grants access to the Lumenflower Gardens, the arena for the Living Failures boss fight.

Items

  • Brain Fluid MISSABLE: Obtained by interacting with and striking the helpless, swollen-head patient tied to a chair on the middle floor
  • Brain Fluid MISSABLE: Obtained by striking the pulsating head-mass creature on the highest platform of the raised staircase
  • Balcony Key MISSABLE: Given by Saint Adeline on the ground floor after providing her with two doses of Brain Fluid
  • Blacksky Eye: Found on a corpse sitting in an alcove behind a locked door in the balcony area, unlocked with the Balcony Key
  • Underground Cell Inner Chamber Key: Found on a corpse on the third-floor bed area accessible after raising the central staircase
  • Milkweed Rune MISSABLE: Given by Saint Adeline on the ground floor after giving her a third dose of Brain Fluid

Secrets

  • Loch Shield | Found by dropping onto a platform below the central staircase after the stairs have been shifted to their upper position

Tips

  • Beware of the pool of fluid on the lowest floor, as poisonous or toxic hazards and crawling patients lurk around the central pillar.
  • Look out for hanging bodies and patients hiding behind corners or doors, as they frequently ambush players in narrow corridors.
  • Rotating the central staircase alters access to several floors, cutting off old routes while revealing new shortcuts and items.
