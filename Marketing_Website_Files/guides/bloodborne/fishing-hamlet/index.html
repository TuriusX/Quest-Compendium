Fishing Hamlet

Bloodborne guide · After defeating the Living Failures

The Fishing Hamlet is the final area of the Bloodborne DLC, accessed after defeating Lady Maria of the Astral Clocktower. You must traverse a flooded, decaying fishing village, descend into the sunken underworld and cavern system, and ultimately slay the Orphan of Kos on the beach.

Items

  • Accursed Brewing MISSABLE: Given by the muttering fish priest at the entrance when spoken to while wearing the Milkweed rune
  • Blood Rock: Located in the cavern section shortly before the boss arena, guarded by two Winter Lanterns
  • Rakuyo: At the bottom of the central village well, dropped by defeating the two giant shark enemies

Secrets

  • The bottom of the central well contains two massive Shark Giants; defeating them rewards the Rakuyo, but using a Shaman Bone Blade makes them fight each other.

Shops and people

  • Brador: Invades in multiple forms throughout the village and drops pieces of the Bloodied outfit; his real body can be assassinated in his cell using the Underground Cell Inner Chamber Key to stop the invasions.

Tips

  • Equip the Deep Sea rune and carry plenty of Sedatives to survive the Frenzy buildup caused by the Winter Lanterns in the lower caverns.
  • A Shaman Bone Blade used on the first Shark Giant in the well makes it fight the second one when it drops from the ceiling, trivializing the hardest encounter in the area.
