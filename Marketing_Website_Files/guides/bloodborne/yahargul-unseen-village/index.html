Yahar'gul, Unseen Village

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Rom, the Vacuous Spider

Following the defeat of Rom, the blood moon rises and Yahar'gul is transformed into a nightmarish, ruined city overrun by Amygdala and chime maidens. The hunter must navigate past hordes of continuously respawning enemies to reach the Advent Plaza. At the deepest point of the village, you must defeat The One Reborn to gain access to the Lecture Building second floor and the Nightmare of Mensis.

Items

  • Upper Cathedral Key: On a corpse seated in a cage cell accessible by dropping through a hole in the railing near the chime maiden in the grand chapel
  • Tiny Tonitrus: Inside a treasure chest guarded by a brick troll inside a locked building near the street level
  • Tonitrus: Found on a corpse in the street guarded by bloodcurdling snatchers and hounds
  • Iron Yahar'gul Helm: On a corpse in the prison cells beneath the main street
  • Yahar'gul Black Garb: On a corpse in the prison cells beneath the main street
  • Yahar'gul Black Gloves: On a corpse in the prison cells beneath the main street
  • Yahar'gul Black Trousers: On a corpse in the prison cells beneath the main street

Secrets

  • Shortcut teleporter bath | Step into the glowing stone birdbath in the side building near the second lantern to instantly teleport to the other side of the street.

Enemies

EnemyWeaknessNotes
Bell Chime MaidenSummons and revives nearby enemies with boosted defense until she is killed.
The One RebornBolt, FireMassive boss that falls from the sky; chime maidens line the upper balconies and should be killed first.
Cramped CasketBolt, FireMobile mass of skeletons inside a carriage coffin that spits blood and lashes out with limbs.
Lesser AmygdalaClings to the exterior walls of buildings, shooting explosive laser beams across open courtyards.

Tips

  • Prioritize locating and eliminating Bell Chime Maidens immediately, as normal enemies will continually revive with a red aura until the maiden dies.
  • Rush the stairs on both sides of the arena during The One Reborn fight to take out the six chime maidens before focusing on the boss.
  • Sprint continuously in a zig-zag pattern across the grand staircase courtyard to avoid the sweeping laser fired by the Lesser Amygdala.
