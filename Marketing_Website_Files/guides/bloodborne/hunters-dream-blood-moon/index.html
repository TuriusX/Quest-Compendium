Hunter's Dream (Blood Moon)

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Rom, the Vacuous Spider

Upon returning to the Hunter's Dream after defeating Rom, the Vacuous Spider, the sky turns purple and the red Blood Moon hangs overhead. The workshop remains intact for now, but dialogue with the Plain Doll changes to reflect the shifting state of the night. You should use this visit to upgrade weapons and access newly opened path headstones before venturing into Yahar'gul, Unseen Village.

Shops and people

  • Plain Doll: offers level up services and provides new dialogue concerning the descent of the Blood Moon
  • Bath Messengers: sells standard equipment, consumable supplies, and newly unlocked inventory unlocked by mid-game hunter badges
  • Insight Bath Messengers: sells armor sets of defeated bosses and special consumable items in exchange for Insight

Tips

  • Spend accumulated Blood Echoes on leveling up or stocking on Blood Vials now, as the areas following Rom feature an immediate spike in difficulty.
