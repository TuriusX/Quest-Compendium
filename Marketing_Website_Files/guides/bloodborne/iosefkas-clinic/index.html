Iosefka's Clinic

Bloodborne guide · Beginning of the game

You awaken in Iosefka's Clinic after receiving a blood transfusion and must navigate the ground floor unarmed. After encountering a beast that will likely kill you, you awaken in the Hunter's Dream to collect your starter weapons before returning to finish exploring and unlock the path to Central Yharnam.

Items

  • Blood Vial (x1): On a corpse immediately to your right upon descending the stairs into the lower room
  • Blood Vial (x2): On a corpse in the hallway right past the Netherbeast
  • Blood Vial (x10): On the corpse slumped against the barred gate leading out to the Central Yharnam courtyard
  • Iosefka's Blood Vial MISSABLE: Received by talking to Iosefka through the closed clinic door on the upper floor

Tips

  • Do not worry about dying to the first Netherbeast; dying sends you directly to the Hunter's Dream to claim your starter weapons.
  • You can defeat the first Netherbeast bare-handed using charged backstabs and visceral attacks, but it is much easier after getting weapons.
  • Pull the lever located on the left side of the large gate outside to permanently unlock the path into the streets of Central Yharnam.
