Cathedral Ward

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Father Gascoigne
← All areas in this guide

The Cathedral Ward serves as the central hub area of Yharnam connecting multiple paths after the defeat of Father Gascoigne. Players must navigate past Church Servants and Church Giants to open large iron gates and reach the Grand Cathedral. Defeating the Vicar Amelia boss inside the Grand Cathedral reveals a password needed to access the Forbidden Woods.

Enemies
Church Servant
Weak to ArcaneSteal / drop Blood Stone Shard

Wields canes, crosses, or lanterns, and can inflict Frenzy with cross attacks.

Church Giant
Weak to ArcaneSteal / drop Blood Stone Shard

Massive enemy carrying an axe, cannon, or wrecking ball; slow but hits very hard.

Church Doctor
Weak to Physical attacks

Carries scythes, pistols, or crosses; aggressive humanoid enemies patrolling the upper plazas.

Shops and people
  • Oedon Chapel Dweller: Offers Oedon Chapel as a safe haven where you can send rescued survivors.

Tips
  • You can bypass the 10,000 Blood Echo cost of the Hunter Chief Emblem by defeating the Blood-starved Beast in Old Yharnam to open the right door inside Oedon Chapel.
  • Beware of the unseen Lesser Amygdala clinging to the wall outside Oedon Chapel; standing near the corpse there can cause it to grab and crush you.
