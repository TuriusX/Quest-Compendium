Central Yharnam

Bloodborne guide · After leaving the clinic

Central Yharnam is the first sprawling labyrinthine area of the game where you navigate plague-ridden streets to unlock vital shortcuts back to the central lantern. You must fight past hostile mobs, beast-men, and large brutes to reach the Great Bridge or the aqueducts. The area ultimately leads to the game's first two potential boss encounters: the optional Cleric Beast and the mandatory Father Gascoigne.

Items

  • Molotov Cocktail: Near the first large gate mob, found on corpses along the central street
  • Saw Hunter Badge: In the sewer waterway guarded by a giant boar, on a corpse near the beast
  • Hunter Garb Set: Found on a corpse on a ledge over the sewers, reachable by dropping down from the wooden scaffolding
  • Tiny Music Box MISSABLE: Given by the Young Yharnam Girl through her window after agreeing to find her mother
  • Red Jewel Brooch: Found on the rooftop of the tomb area where you fight Father Gascoigne

Secrets

  • Shortcut gate directly to the left of the Central Yharnam lantern, unlocked from behind after progressing past the dark house.
  • Hidden sewer entrance accessed by breaking wooden crates and coffins to the right of the dog cages near the old woman's house.
  • Eileen the Crow is located on the rafters above the sewers; drop down through the window near the dog cages and break the barrels in the corner of the dry balcony to access her platform.

Tips

  • Throw a pebble to lure enemies away one by one from large groups, especially around the burning beast effigy.
  • Play the Tiny Music Box against Father Gascoigne up to three times to stun him temporarily during the fight.
  • Rolling through barrels or coffins clears a path silently and can reveal hidden descents without consuming weapon durability.
  • The two Scourge Beasts on the Great Bridge cannot fit through the door of the dark house, making them easy to kill from the doorway with thrust attacks.
