Lecture Building

Bloodborne guide · After touching the altar in the Unseen Village

Entering via the altar past The One Reborn teleports you to the Lecture Building's second floor, an academic ruin suspended in the Nightmare. You need to navigate the upper balconies, unlock shortcuts, and descend to the first floor to clear out lingering scholars. Dropping through an open hatch in a lecture hall lets you access the path to the Nightmare of Mensis or double back to the Nightmare Frontier.

Items

  • Lecture Theatre Key: Found on a corpse draped over a desk in the main corridor of the second floor
  • Communion Rune: Found inside a chest in the small classroom on the second floor
  • Blood Stone Chunk MISSABLE: Looted from a Scurrying Beast in the upper side room
  • Sedatives: Found on a corpse sitting in the large auditorium seats

Secrets

  • Speaking through the door to the spider-like NPC Patches on the first floor is made possible by dropping through the hatch on the second floor behind a towering Church Giant.

Shops and people

  • Patches the Spider: Offers the "Beg for Life" gesture and the Anti-Clockwise Metamorphosis rune after speaking with him face-to-face via the ladder drop.

Tips

  • Thrusting attacks and elemental weapons, especially those dealing fire damage, make quick work of the large mob of scholars clustered inside the main lecture halls.
  • The ground-floor lecture hall filled with scholars is one of the most efficient mid-game Blood Echo and Quicksilver Bullet farming spots.
