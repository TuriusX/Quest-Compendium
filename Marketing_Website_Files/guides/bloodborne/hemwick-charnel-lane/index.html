Hemwick Charnel Lane

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Vicar Amelia

Hemwick Charnel Lane is a rural hillside graveyard and village populated by crazed women, executioners, and hunting dogs. You traverse the cemetery, cross rooftops and barns, and open shortcut gates to reach the Witch's Abode. Defeating the boss unlocks the Rune Workshop Tool, allowing you to equip Caryll Runes in the Hunter's Dream.

Items

  • Rune Workshop Tool: In a small room down the stairs directly behind the Witch of Hemwick boss arena
  • Bone Marrow Ash: On a corpse sitting on the outskirts of the cemetery near the first executioner
  • Thick Coldblood (6): Found on a corpse tucked behind gravestones in the lower graveyard area
  • Blood Stone Shard: Multiple scattered across corpses along the path and barn rafters
  • Twin Blood Stone Shards: Guarded by an executioner inside the barn shortcut area

Secrets

  • Shortcut elevator inside the small stone shed near the first lamp, activated by riding it down from the high path near the boss approach

Tips

  • If you enter the Witch of Hemwick boss fight with 0 Insight, no Mad Ones will spawn during the battle, making it extremely easy.
  • Watch out for grave women hidden behind foliage or around corners who throw Molotov cocktails from high perches.
  • Executioners have long recovery animations after heavy vertical swings, giving an ideal window for a charged backstab.
