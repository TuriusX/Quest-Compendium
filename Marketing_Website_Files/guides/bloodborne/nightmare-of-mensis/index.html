Nightmare of Mensis

Bloodborne guide · After passing through the Lecture Building

You arrive in a twisted dreamscape illuminated by an eerie glowing tower that inflicts Frenzy on anyone caught in its line of sight. You must advance up the rocky path, breach the grand citadel, and ascend past Micolash to face Mergo's Wet Nurse at the summit. Along the way, you can pull a lever to drop the source of the Frenzy into an abyss to safely explore the lower depths.

Items

  • Blood Rock: Dropped into a lower abyss after pulling a lever, accessible via a broken cage elevator hole
  • Eye Rune: Found on a corpse on the balcony near the Giant Brain before dropping it
  • Living String MISSABLE: Acquired by killing the Brain of Mensis after dropping it into the dark abyss below
  • Iron Key: Found on a corpse in the lower labyrinth area during the fight with Micolash
  • Mensis Cage: Dropped by Micolash, Host of the Nightmare upon defeat
  • One Third of Umbilical Cord: Dropped by Mergo's Wet Nurse upon defeat

Secrets

  • Stepping off the cage elevator mid-descent reveals a hidden hallway that leads to the switch that drops the Brain of Mensis into the pit.
  • Performing the "Make Contact" gesture in front of the dropped Brain of Mensis in the dark pit and holding it until your arms switch positions awards the Moon rune.

Tips

  • Stock up on Sedatives and wear the Ashen Hunter set to increase your Frenzy resistance before crossing the open bridges.
  • You can use Poison Knives to whittle down Micolash from the balcony above before dropping into the final room with him.
  • When Mergo's Wet Nurse shrouds the arena in purple fog, focus on running clockwise along the edge of the arena instead of attacking until the darkness fades.
