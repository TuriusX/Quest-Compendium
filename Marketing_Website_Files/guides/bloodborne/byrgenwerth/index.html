Byrgenwerth

Bloodborne guide · After navigating the Forbidden Woods

Byrgenwerth is a small, pivotal college area situated right after the Forbidden Woods where players learn about Master Willem and the truth of the Healing Church. You must explore the grounds and manor, defeat a hostile hunter, retrieve a key to unlock the balcony door, and jump into the Moonside Lake. Defeating the boss here triggers the Blood Moon phase, drastically shifting the state of the game world.

Items

  • Lunarium Key: On the second floor of the main manor, dropped by or found near the Scholar Master Willem / chest area
  • Empty Phantasm Shell: Found inside a chest on the top floor of the manor observatory reached via a ladder
  • Pearl Slug: Found in a chest inside the manor building
  • Student Uniform: Found in a chest inside the main manor building

Secrets

  • Shortcut gate | Open the iron gate near the lantern by interacting with the lever on the lake path to bypass the outdoor enemies.

Shops and people

  • Master Willem: Grants Insight when spoken to, and drops the Eye Caryll Rune if killed

Tips

  • Take shelter behind pillars or close the distance quickly when Yurie starts casting A Call Beyond, as its arcane projectiles can easily one-shot you.
  • Rom's head is heavily armored and takes very little damage; always aim your attacks at her unarmored soft body and flanks.
  • Make sure you have completed any time-sensitive NPC questlines before defeating Rom, as her death advances the world state to the Blood Moon phase.
