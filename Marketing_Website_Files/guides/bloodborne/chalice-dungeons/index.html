Chalice Dungeons

Bloodborne guide · Accessible after defeating the Blood-starved Beast

The Chalice Dungeons are an extensive, multi-layered underground network explored by conducting rituals at the gravestones in the Hunter's Dream. Players navigate labyrinthine corridors to unlock boss doors by pulling hidden levers, progressing through increasingly dangerous depths. They serve as the primary source for Bloodborne's highest-tier Blood Gems, alternate weapon variants, and the path to defeating the Pthumerian Queen.

Items

  • Pthumeru Root Chalice: Dropped by the Watchers boss on Layer 2 of the Pthumeru Chalice
  • Central Pthumeru Chalice: Dropped by the Watchdog of the Old Lords on Layer 3 of the Pthumeru Chalice
  • Defiled Chalice: Dropped by the Bloodletting Beast on Layer 4 of the Lower Pthumeru Chalice
  • Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice: Dropped by Amygdala in Layer 3 of the Defiled Chalice
  • Yharnam Stone: Dropped by Yharnam, Pthumerian Queen on Layer 3 of the Great Pthumeru Ihyll Chalice
  • Beast Claw: Found in a treasure chest in the pre-area bonus side-room of Layer 2 of the Ailing Loran Chalice
  • Red Jelly: Found in treasure chests within Layer 2 and Layer 3 of the Lower Loran and Great Isz Chalices

Secrets

  • Illusory walls conceal secret rooms containing rare materials and chests; attack or roll against suspicious, flat brick wall sections to break the illusion.

Shops and people

  • Bath Messenger: Sells Sinister Chalices as well as uncanny and lost weapon variants depending on the dungeon type

Tips

  • Progress through fixed "Story" chalices first to obtain the Root chalices and depth materials needed to create custom and glyph dungeons.
  • The Defiled Chalice permanently halves your maximum health for the entire duration of the dungeon, making high vitality and health-boosting Caryll Runes essential.
  • Side rooms located before the main area entrance or before the boss fog often contain essential ritual materials needed to perform the next chalice ritual.
