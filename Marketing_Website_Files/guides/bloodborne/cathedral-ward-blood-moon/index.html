Cathedral Ward (Blood Moon)

Bloodborne guide · After defeating Rom, the Vacuous Spider

Upon Rom's defeat, the Blood Moon descends, casting Cathedral Ward in a crimson light and triggering changes among the surviving NPCs in Oedon Chapel. The lesser Amygdala clinging to the architecture throughout the district are now fully visible to all players regardless of their Insight level. The primary objective here is to access the Grand Cathedral to conclude Eileen the Crow's storyline or proceed past the grand plaza toward the entrance to Yahar'gul, Unseen Village.

Items

  • Blood Rapture (Rune) MISSABLE: Dropped by the Bloody Crow of Cainhurst inside the Grand Cathedral
  • Crow Hunter Badge MISSABLE: Given by Eileen the Crow on the Grand Cathedral steps after defeating the Bloody Crow, or dropped by Eileen if you kill her
  • Hunter (Rune) MISSABLE: Given by Eileen the Crow outside the Grand Cathedral upon completing her quest

Secrets

  • Returning to Iosefka's Clinic via the Forbidden Woods shortcut during the Blood Moon reveals Iosefka defenseless on an examination table, allowing you to defeat her for One Third of Umbilical Cord without a difficult fight.

Tips

  • The Bloody Crow of Cainhurst heavily drains his own health when transforming his Chikage into its two-handed blood mode; keep your distance while he is in this state to let him weaken himself.
  • Check on Oedon Chapel's survivors, as characters like Arianna will enter advanced stages of their story arcs once the Blood Moon rises.
