Old Yharnam

Bloodborne guide · After opening the Cathedral Ward gate

Old Yharnam is a ruined district consumed by ashen blood and burned to contain the beast scourge. As the Hunter progresses downward through the ruins, a retired hunter named Djura fires upon them with a high-powered Gatling gun mounted on a tower. Navigating the dilapidated streets and churches leads to the Church of the Good Chalice, where the Blood-starved Beast must be defeated.

Items

  • Pungent Blood Cocktail: On a corpse hanging off a wooden walkway near the first building entrance
  • Blood Stone Shard: Multiple scattered on corpses throughout the streets and rooftops
  • Antidote: Found in a small alcove near the base of the rafters inside the first large chapel
  • Charred Hunter Set: Inside a burned building on the ground floor behind a broken wall, guarded by a hooded beast
  • Rifle Spear: In the same burned building as the Charred Hunter Set, hidden behind a wooden partition
  • Blood-tinged Gemstone: In a dead-end alleyway beneath the central bridge path
  • Pthumeru Chalice: Dropped upon defeating the Blood-starved Beast at the lowest point of the area
  • Powder Keg Hunter Badge MISSABLE: Dropped by Djura if killed, or received from him as a gift if befriended

Secrets

  • You can befriend Djura by avoiding his line of sight or ignoring his confrontation initially, entering the area from behind via the Graveyard of the Darkbeast after defeating Darkbeast Paarl, and climbing his tower without attacking any beasts in Old Yharnam.
  • A breakable wooden railing on the walkways behind the first chapel allows you to drop down onto intermediate rooftops, opening a hidden path to early shortcuts and the Charred Hunter equipment.

Shops and people

  • Djura: Offers the "Brush Off Dust" gesture and the Powder Keg Hunter Badge if you approach him peacefully from the rear route and agree to spare the beasts

Tips

  • Throwing a Pungent Blood Cocktail against a wall or pillar will distract the Blood-starved Beast and other beast-type enemies for several seconds, leaving them open to free attacks.
  • Equip armor with high slow poison resistance, such as the Gascoigne Set or Charred Hunter Set, and keep Antidotes equipped to your quick-item bar throughout this area.
  • You can quickly defeat Djura by climbing to the top of his ladder and immediately firing your firearm at him several times to knock him off the edge of the tower.
