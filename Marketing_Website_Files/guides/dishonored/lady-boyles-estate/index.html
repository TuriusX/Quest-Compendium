Lady Boyle's Estate

Dishonored guide · The mission to eliminate Lady Boyle
← All areas in this guide

Corvo infiltrates the Boyle estate during a lavish masquerade ball to identify and neutralize Lady Boyle, who is secretly funding the Lord Regent. Players can explore the ground-floor party freely if disguised with a mask, but the upper floors, basement, and exterior guard areas are strictly off-limits. The target can be eliminated directly or taken care of through a non-lethal arrangement involving Lord Pendleton's contact, Lord Brisby.

Enemies
Boyle Guard
Weak to Sleep darts, stealth takedownSteal / drop Mansion master keys, coin pouch

Patrols the grounds, doors, and upstairs restricted areas; hostile if you brandish weapons or enter restricted zones.

Overseer
Weak to Stealth takedown, sleep darts

Stationed at the entrance courtyard and inside with music boxes that neutralize Corvo's powers.

Tallboy
Weak to Plunge attack, rewire tool on Arc Pylon, Springrazor to the canister

Patrols the streets and courtyard outside the estate; extremely high vantage point and launches incendiary bolts.

Shops and people
  • Lord Brisby: Offers to permanently remove Lady Boyle from Dunwall alive if you knock her out and carry her down to his boat in the cellar.
  • Miss White: Offers to reveal the specific identity and costume color of the Regent's mistress in exchange for a drink of cider from the bar.

Tips
  • You can sign the guest book near the front foyer with Corvo Attano's real name for a unique reaction and later dialogue.
  • Possessing a rat allows you to move easily through the vents between the guarded second-floor bedrooms without being spotted by patrolling guards.
  • Do not draw weapons or enter upstairs rooms while inside the party hall, or the neutral masquerade guests will panic and summon guards.
