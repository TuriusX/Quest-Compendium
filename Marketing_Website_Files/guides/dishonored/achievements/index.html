Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Escaping Coldridge Prison through the sewers: Escaping the prison undetected (Vanished) if any guard raised an alarm.
  • Leaving The Hound Pits Pub to start a new mission: Interacting with allies or acquiring specific mission clues inside the pub for that stage of the game.
  • Leaving the Distillery District/High Overseer Campbell's territory to return to Samuel's boat: Poisoning the still for Granny Rags (Gentleman Caller) and completing Slackjaw's initial mission (Street Conspiracy).
  • Killing High Overseer Campbell directly in his office or meeting room: Branding him with the Heretic's Brand for the non-lethal achievement (Excommunication).
  • Leaving The Golden Cat via Samuel's boat: Completing Slackjaw's non-lethal resolution for the Pendletons and Granny Rags' assassination request.
  • Exiting Dunwall Sewers after recovering Emily: Any lingering side exploration or combat tasks inside the sewer section.
  • Leaving Lady Boyle's Estate during the party: Identifying the correct Lady Boyle, signing the guestbook (Well Mannered), or handing her over to Lord Pendleton's associate non-lethally.
  • Defeating Daud in the Flooded District: Spared Daud achievement (Just Dark Enough) and resolving the final confrontation between Granny Rags and Slackjaw (Street Conspiracy).
  • Infiltrating the final fortress on Kingsparrow Island: Completing the campaign on High Chaos (Dunwall in Turmoil) or Low Chaos (Resolution).

Recommended order
  1. First playthrough on Low Chaos: complete the game with zero kills (Clean Hands), zero alerts (Ghost / Shadow), no non-Blink supernatural powers (Mostly Flesh and Steel), while completing all Granny Rags and Slackjaw side quests (Street Conspiracy).
  2. Second playthrough on High Chaos: earn Dunwall in Turmoil by causing chaos across missions, trigger the dark ending, and farm combat/kill-specific achievements (such as Hornet's Nest, Tempest, Razor Rain, and Back Alley Duelist).
  3. Mission cleanup via Mission Select: unlock any remaining miscellaneous, stealth-specific, or situational achievements you did not earn naturally during the main runs.
