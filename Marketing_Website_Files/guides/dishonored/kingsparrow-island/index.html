Kingsparrow Island

Dishonored guide · The final mission to rescue Emily
← All areas in this guide

Corvo infiltrates Kingsparrow Island to eliminate the conspirators and rescue Emily Kaldwin from the fortress. Depending on the Chaos level, the weather and island layout change significantly, ranging from daylight and infighting on Low Chaos to a raging storm and heavy fortifications on High Chaos. Corvo must reach the top of the central lighthouse tower to complete his final objective.

Watchtower
Weak to Removing the Whale Oil tank or using a Rewire Tool

Shoots explosive rounds at Corvo when he is spotted

Treavor Pendleton
Weak to Combat, stealth takedownSteal / drop Pouch

On Low Chaos he is already poisoned in the courtyard; on High Chaos he waits in the exterior grounds

Teague Martin
Weak to Combat, stealth takedownSteal / drop Pouch

On Low Chaos he is already poisoned; on High Chaos he defends the lower courtyard with guards

Farley Havelock
Weak to Sleep darts, choking, assassinations, blade combatSteal / drop Key to Emily's room

The final conspirator waiting at the very top of the lighthouse

Tips
  • Removing the whale oil canisters powering the security stations disables nearby Wall of Lights barriers and Arc Pylons.
  • On Low Chaos, Pendleton and Martin are already poisoned by Havelock before you reach them, drastically reducing combat encounters.
  • Using Blink or Bend Time allows you to scale the structural exterior piping of the lighthouse, bypassing entire floors of heavily guarded corridors.
  • On High Chaos, Havelock holds Emily over the edge of the lighthouse platform; you must sprint, use Bend Time, or shoot him before he steps off.
