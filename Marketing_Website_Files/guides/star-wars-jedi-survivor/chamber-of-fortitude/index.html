Chamber of Fortitude

STAR WARS Jedi: Survivor™ guide · Optional High Republic Chamber
← All areas in this guide

The Chamber of Fortitude is an optional High Republic Jedi Chamber accessed via the Corroded Silo in Koboh's Southern Reach. Unlike puzzle-centric chambers, this area primarily consists of a single boss encounter arena followed by a reward room. Defeating the boss unlocks access to the chamber's core reward and associated collectibles.

Items

0/5

Enemies
Anubias

A Bedlam Raider boss wielding a dual-bladed lightsaber and a shield, accompanied by battle droids.

Tips
  • Focus on eliminating the battle droid minions first so you can deal with Anubias without interruptions from ranged fire.
  • Anubias uses aggressive lightsaber combo strings and unblockable attacks; maintain distance until he finishes a combo sequence to safely punish him.
