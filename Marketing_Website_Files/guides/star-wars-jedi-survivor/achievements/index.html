Recommended order
  1. Coruscant Undercity - Complete the opening tutorial section, defeat the first waves of stormtroopers, and progress through the lower levels.
  2. Rooftops - Navigate the industrial platforms, recruit Bode, and learn companion combat commands for the "You've Got a Friend" achievement.
  3. Senator Sejan's Yacht - Infiltrate the luxury vessel, defeat the Ninth Sister, and escape Coruscant aboard the Mantis.
  4. Rambler's Reach Outpost - Land on Koboh, fight off the Bedlam Raiders, and unlock the central hub region.
  5. Derelict Dam - Clear the tar pits, solve roller mine puzzles, and obtain early upgrade materials and health essences.
  6. Southern Reach - Explore the cliffs, unlock initial shortcuts, and practice diverse stance attacks.
  7. Chamber of Duality - Solve the High Republic orb puzzles to rescue Zee and unlock the perk system.
  8. Pyloon's Saloon - Meet Greez, unlock the cantina basement, interact with patrons, and check the rooftop garden plots.
  9. Fogged Expanse - Push through the misty terrain, fight wild Mogu beasts, and open the path toward the Forest Array.
  10. Forest Array - Realign the massive communication array, confront Dagan Gera, and unlock the Koboh Grinder tool for BD-1.
  11. Chamber of Reason - Locate and solve the High Republic bridge puzzle in the Basalt Forest to claim its perk.
  12. Chamber of Clarity - Complete the crate-pulling trial in Untamed Downs to secure another perk toward "Star Tours".
  13. Chamber of Fortitude - Infiltrate the Corroded Silo and defeat the Anvil brute boss to clear this High Republic trial.
  14. Chamber of Connection - Use the Koboh Grinder to create fire paths and unlock the trial's reward.
  15. Chamber of Detachment - Solve the dual-block magnetic puzzle in the Mountain Ascent to claim the perk.
