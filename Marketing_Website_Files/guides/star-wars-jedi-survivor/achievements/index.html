Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Coruscant Undercity - Complete the opening tutorial section, defeat the first waves of stormtroopers, and progress through the lower levels.

  2. Step 2

    Rooftops - Navigate the industrial platforms, recruit Bode, and learn companion combat commands for the "You've Got a Friend" achievement.

    Don't miss in this step
    • You've Got A Friend: Use the combat command prompt to have Bode assist you 10 times and Merrin assist you 10 times during story missions before Bode becomes unavailable.
  3. Step 3

    Senator Sejan's Yacht - Infiltrate the luxury vessel, defeat the Ninth Sister, and escape Coruscant aboard the Mantis.

  4. Step 4

    Rambler's Reach Outpost - Land on Koboh, fight off the Bedlam Raiders, and unlock the central hub region.

  5. Step 5

    Derelict Dam - Clear the tar pits, solve roller mine puzzles, and obtain early upgrade materials and health essences.

  6. Step 6

    Southern Reach - Explore the cliffs, unlock initial shortcuts, and practice diverse stance attacks.

  7. Step 7

    Chamber of Duality - Solve the High Republic orb puzzles to rescue Zee and unlock the perk system.

  8. Step 8

    Pyloon's Saloon - Meet Greez, unlock the cantina basement, interact with patrons, and check the rooftop garden plots.

  9. Step 9

    Fogged Expanse - Push through the misty terrain, fight wild Mogu beasts, and open the path toward the Forest Array.

  10. Step 10

    Forest Array - Realign the massive communication array, confront Dagan Gera, and unlock the Koboh Grinder tool for BD-1.

  11. Step 11

    Chamber of Reason - Locate and solve the High Republic bridge puzzle in the Basalt Forest to claim its perk.

  12. Step 12

    Chamber of Clarity - Complete the crate-pulling trial in Untamed Downs to secure another perk toward "Star Tours".

    Also in this step: Star Tours

  13. Step 13

    Chamber of Fortitude - Infiltrate the Corroded Silo and defeat the Anvil brute boss to clear this High Republic trial.

  14. Step 14

    Chamber of Connection - Use the Koboh Grinder to create fire paths and unlock the trial's reward.

  15. Step 15

    Chamber of Detachment - Solve the dual-block magnetic puzzle in the Mountain Ascent to claim the perk.
