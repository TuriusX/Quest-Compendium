Undercity Meats

STAR WARS Jedi: Survivor™ guide · Cal separates from the squad and flees through the underworld meat plant ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

After plummeting onto a conveyor belt in the meat processing facility, Cal must climb upward to escape toward the Freight Handling Depot while dodging Imperial patrols. You navigate through cold storage corridors, climb walls, avoid fiery incinerators, and slice locked security doors.

Key fights
Frenzied Jotaz

Enemies: Frenzied Jotaz

Threats: Heavy unblockable slams, sweeping arm strikes, and charging attacks

Weaknesses and resistances: Predictable recovery windows following charge attacks

Tactics: Maintain distance during red unblockable swings, dodge sideways rather than backward, and retaliate with fast saber strikes between slow recovery animations

Rewards: Health Essence upgrade

Enemies
Viper Probe Droid

Hovering Imperial surveillance units encountered along upper ledges that fire blasters and self-destruct upon taking fatal damage.

KX-series Security Droid

A heavy Imperial combat droid positioned on the upper ledge protecting the stim upgrade chest.

Purge Trooper
Weak to Parrying standard combo attacks to deplete stamina

Fast, aggressive Imperial staff-wielding melee specialist encountered blocking the path to the meat freezer.

Tips
  • If you want to claim every collectible here, hold off until later in the campaign when you possess both BD-1's Electro-Dart and Merrin's Charm to bypass the locked door and green energy barrier.
