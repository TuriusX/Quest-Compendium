Missable achievements

Points of no return

  • Escaping Castle Dimitrescu: Exploring Castle Dimitrescu rooms, collecting its Goats of Warding, Files, and defeating Lady Dimitrescu's daughters
  • Riding the boat to Moreau's Reservoir / Returning to the Village: Areas of the Reservoir, including specific Goats of Warding and Files in Moreau's domain
  • Leaving House Beneviento: Collecting any missed items or Goats of Warding within House Beneviento and its garden
  • Defeating Heisenberg (entering the Megamycete Roots sequence): Free-roam access to Heisenberg's Factory, the Village exploration, side bosses, and all Village treasures/collectibles
  • Completing Chris Redfield's segment in Megamycete Roots: Unique combat challenges and Goats of Warding exclusive to Chris's loadout and path

Recommended order
  1. First Playthrough (Standard or Casual): Focus on story completion while collecting all Files, Goats of Warding, Weapon Customizable Parts, and Crafting Recipes. Complete area-specific combat and puzzle achievements.
  2. Extra Content Shop & Preparation: Purchase the WCX assault rifle or S.T.A.K.E. magnum with infinite ammo using CP earned from challenges.
  3. Second Playthrough (Casual NG+ Speedrun): Complete the game in under 3 hours, spend less than 10,000 Lei, and use 4 or fewer recovery items.
  4. Third Playthrough (Casual NG+ Knife-Only): Complete the entire story using only close-combat weapons (Knives Out achievement).
  5. Fourth Playthrough (Village of Shadows NG+): Complete the highest difficulty mode using fully upgraded weapons with infinite ammo.
  6. The Mercenaries Mode: Achieve an S Rank or higher on all standard and hard stages to unlock the final combat achievements.
  7. Cleanup: Farm leftover miscellaneous combat challenges or crafting tasks via reloading saves or quick casual runs.
