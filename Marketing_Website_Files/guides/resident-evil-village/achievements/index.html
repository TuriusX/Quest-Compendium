Points of no return

  • Escaping Castle Dimitrescu: Exploring Castle Dimitrescu rooms, collecting its Goats of Warding, Files, and defeating Lady Dimitrescu's daughters
  • Riding the boat to Moreau's Reservoir / Returning to the Village: Areas of the Reservoir, including specific Goats of Warding and Files in Moreau's domain
  • Leaving House Beneviento: Collecting any missed items or Goats of Warding within House Beneviento and its garden
  • Defeating Heisenberg (entering the Megamycete Roots sequence): Free-roam access to Heisenberg's Factory, the Village exploration, side bosses, and all Village treasures/collectibles
  • Completing Chris Redfield's segment in Megamycete Roots: Unique combat challenges and Goats of Warding exclusive to Chris's loadout and path

Recommended order

  1. Step 1First Playthrough (Standard or Casual)

    Focus on story completion while collecting all Files, Goats of Warding, Weapon Customizable Parts, and Crafting Recipes. Complete area-specific combat and puzzle achievements.

  2. Step 2Extra Content Shop & Preparation

    Purchase the WCX assault rifle or S.T.A.K.E. magnum with infinite ammo using CP earned from challenges.

  3. Step 3Second Playthrough (Casual NG+ Speedrun)

    Complete the game in under 3 hours, spend less than 10,000 Lei, and use 4 or fewer recovery items.

  4. Step 4Third Playthrough (Casual NG+ Knife-Only)

    Complete the entire story using only close-combat weapons (Knives Out achievement).

    Don't miss in this step
    • Knives Out
  5. Step 5Fourth Playthrough (Village of Shadows NG+)

    Complete the highest difficulty mode using fully upgraded weapons with infinite ammo.

  6. Step 6The Mercenaries Mode

    Achieve an S Rank or higher on all standard and hard stages to unlock the final combat achievements.

  7. Step 7Cleanup

    Farm leftover miscellaneous combat challenges or crafting tasks via reloading saves or quick casual runs.
