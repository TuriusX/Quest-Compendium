Castle Dimitrescu

Resident Evil Village guide · Exploring the castle grounds
← All areas in this guide

Ethan Winters infiltrates Castle Dimitrescu to search for his kidnapped daughter, Rose, while evading Lady Dimitrescu and her three daughters. The primary objective is to collect four unique angel masks scattered across the estate to unlock the exit leading outside. Exploring the castle requires solving environmental puzzles, unlocking doors, and navigating both the grand upper floors and the dungeon basements.

Enemies
Lady Alcina Dimitrescu

Cannot be killed during castle exploration; she relentlessly stalks Ethan and must be evaded or slowed down.

Bela Dimitrescu
Weak to Cold temperatures / exposure to freezing airSteal / drop Crystal Torso

Dies permanently once defeated in the Kitchen after exposing her to the cold air from a broken window.

Cassandra Dimitrescu
Weak to Cold temperatures / exposure to freezing airSteal / drop Crystal Torso

Fought in the Armory; defeat her after blasting open the stone wall with a Pipe Bomb to let freezing air into the room.

Daniela Dimitrescu
Weak to Cold temperatures / exposure to freezing airSteal / drop Crystal Torso

Fought in the Library; pull the lever to open the ceiling skylight to make her vulnerable to normal weapons.

Moroaică
Weak to Headshots

Ghoul-like undead armed with melee weapons found mainly in the Dungeons and Cellar areas.

Samca
Weak to Headshots

Flying, winged creatures encountered on the Castle Dimitrescu rooftops.

Shops and people
  • The Duke: Sells firearms, ammunition, healing items, weapon attachments, recipe crafting supplies, and weapon upgrades

Tips
  • Running away is often better than shooting Lady Dimitrescu, as hitting her only staggers her briefly and wastes ammunition.
  • The Duke's room serves as a safe haven where Lady Dimitrescu cannot follow or attack you.
  • Rooms on your in-game map will turn blue once you have picked up every hidden item and resource located within them.
  • Examine the Animal Skull found on the wall above the fireplace in the Hall of Pleasure to unscrew the back piece, turning it into the substitute item needed to remove the Mask of Pleasure.
