Old Aperture

Portal 2 guide · The early, retro testing shafts
← All areas in this guide

After falling thousands of meters into the depths of the facility, you navigate the massive, decaying testing shafts built from the 1950s through the 1980s. Guided by prerecorded messages from Aperture founder Cave Johnson, you must master three distinct mobility gels to escape the abandoned structures. Your goal is to work your way back up toward modern Aperture while solving large-scale traversal chambers.

Tips
  • Blue Repulsion Gel bounces you higher depending on the height you fall from, so preserve your momentum by placing portals at extreme vertical distances.
  • Orange Propulsion Gel removes all friction, allowing you to build the horizontal speed required to cross massive chasms.
  • White Conversion Gel enables you to paint non-portalable surfaces, turning them into valid targets for your portal gun.
  • Water cleansing fields and broken sprinkler pipes will wash gel off surfaces, so avoid splashing water onto your intended gel paths.
