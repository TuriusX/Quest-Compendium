Wheatley's Testing Chambers

Portal 2 guide · The facility under Wheatley's control
← All areas in this guide

Chell navigates Aperture Laboratories after Wheatley takes over the central core and begins running the facility into total ruin. Guided and continuously mocked by Wheatley, the player must solve a series of poorly designed test chambers using Excursion Funnels and various gels alongside the portal gun. Progressing through these tests eventually leads behind the scenes to stop a catastrophic reactor meltdown and confront Wheatley.

Achievements here

Automated tripod weapons that fire on sight; can be redirected or knocked down using Excursion Funnels

Tips
  • Excursion Funnels can be extended through portals by placing an entry portal where the funnel hits a surface and an exit portal elsewhere.
  • Reverse the flow direction of an Excursion Funnel by using nearby switches to pull objects and yourself toward the emitter instead of pushing away.
  • Use blue Repulsion Gel and orange Propulsion Gel inside Excursion Funnels to create high-momentum trajectories for reaching distant exits.
  • Frankenturrets can be placed on Weighted Storage Buttons to activate door mechanisms, but they may hop off if not oriented carefully.
