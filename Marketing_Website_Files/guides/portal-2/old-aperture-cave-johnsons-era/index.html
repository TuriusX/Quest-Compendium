Old Aperture (Cave Johnson's Era)

Portal 2 guide · Falling into the depths of the facility
← All areas in this guide

Chell falls into the lowest sub-levels of the Aperture Science facility, navigating the abandoned 1950s through 1980s test spheres. Players must solve traversal puzzles using newly introduced mobility gels while listening to prerecorded audio messages from Cave Johnson and his assistant Caroline. The objective is to climb back up through the salt mine infrastructure, reactivate gel flow, and reach the upper modern facility.

Tips
  • Look for distant white panels or patches of Repulsion Gel across large chasms to place portals and maintain your momentum.
  • You can place portals on surfaces covered by Propulsion Gel or Repulsion Gel, as well as spray gels through portals to reach otherwise unpaintable surfaces.
  • Blue Repulsion Gel negates fall damage entirely, allowing you to jump safely into it from any height.
