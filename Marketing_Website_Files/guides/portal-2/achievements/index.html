Recommended order

  1. Step 1

    Complete the single-player campaign from the Aperture Science Facility through Old Aperture and Wheatley's Testing Chambers to Wheatley's Boss Arena while working on stage-specific miscellaneous achievements.

  2. Step 2

    Use chapter select to clean up any missed single-player achievements across all four main areas.

  3. Step 3

    Complete the cooperative campaign alongside a partner, solving all test courses without losing any portals where required.

  4. Step 4

    Complete remaining co-op miscellaneous tasks, gesture interactions, and cross-play/hug challenges to unlock the final achievements.
