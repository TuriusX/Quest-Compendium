Aperture Science Facility

Portal 2 guide · The facility after GLaDOS's destruction
← All areas in this guide

Chell wakes up in the overgrown ruins of the Aperture Science Computer-Aided Enrichment Center following the destruction of GLaDOS. Accompanied by the Personality Core Wheatley, she must navigate through decaying test chambers and utility areas to escape. The journey eventually leads to GLaDOS's ruined chamber, where an accidental activation brings the vengeful AI back online.

Sentry Turret
Weak to Knocking it over, dropping it into hazardous liquid, or blocking its line of fire with objects

Stationary automated defense tripod that fires a hail of bullets when its laser sights spot the player

Tips
  • In the ruined chambers, look for intact white portal-conductive panels, as overgrown, damaged, or debris-covered walls will not accept portals.
  • Use the environment and Weighted Storage Cubes as shields to safely approach and topple Sentry Turrets before they can fire.
