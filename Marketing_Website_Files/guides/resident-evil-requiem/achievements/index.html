Points of no return

  • Exiting the Rhodes Hill Care Center basement into the courtyards: All uncollected Files, safes, and Mr. Raccoon statues remaining within the main floors and basement of the care facility
  • Moving past the East Raccoon City barricades toward the R.P.D. precinct: Exploration and collectibles left behind in East Raccoon City, including the BSAA supply containers and area street notes
  • Entering the Bioweapon Repository lift to engage the final boss in ARK: All remaining lower-facility exploration, ARK laboratory safes, and unread archive logs

Recommended order

  1. Step 1First Playthrough on Standard difficulty

    Explore thoroughly to locate all 75 Files, 25 Mr. Raccoon figures, locked safes, and BSAA supply crates across Rhodes Hill, East Raccoon City, and the ARK, while completing specific area combat actions such as triggering the light-sensitive Girl encounter and defeating unique mutated threats.

  2. Step 2Second Playthrough on Casual difficulty

    Combine the under 4-hour speedrun objective with the restrictions to never heal using herbs or medical injectors and never use Grace's blood collector.

  3. Step 3Third Playthrough on Insanity difficulty

    Utilize unlocked bonus weapons and rewards to complete the campaign on the highest challenge setting and clean up any remaining kill-count achievements.
