Chapter 3: Rhodes Hill 2 (The Quartz pieces & Victor's Office)

Resident Evil Requiem guide · Key items and quartz fragments are gathered to unlock deeper areas while confronting Victor's influence ✓ Checked against sources
← All areas in this guide

Grace explores the Rhodes Hill Care Center wings to discover puzzle solutions and open locked puzzle boxes containing the key quartz items. Meanwhile, narrative confrontations with Victor Gideon unfold alongside Leon's search through the facility to locate Victor's office. The overarching task is collecting the quartz artifacts to bypass locked security doors and delve deeper into the complex.

Region
Rhodes Hill Chronic Care Center
Getting there
From the central hub of Rhodes Hill Chronic Care Center, through the West Wing and East Wing corridors
Connects to
Care Center Central Hall, West Wing, East Wing, Victor's Private Office (3F)
Related quests
Door with Indents puzzle · Collect 3 Quartz · Locate Victor's Office
Services and people

Zombies · Large Roaming Monster · The Girl
