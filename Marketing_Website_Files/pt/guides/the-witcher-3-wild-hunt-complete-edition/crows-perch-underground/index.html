Poleiro do Corvo (Subterrâneo)

Guia de The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition · Explorando o porão/cavernas do Barão
← Todas as áreas deste guia

Esta caverna subterrânea inundada oferece uma entrada secreta sob o Poleiro do Corvo caso os portões principais estejam trancados após um confronto na Estalagem da Encruzilhada. Geralt nada para dentro da caverna através da entrada de uma gruta subaquática, escala terraços de pedra naturais, lida com um monstro à espreita na escuridão e sobe por uma escada de mão dentro de um poço de pedra para emergir no pátio do jardim interno.

Região
Velen (No Man's Land)
Níveis
Level 5–6
Como chegar
Located northwest/west of Crow’s Perch down in the moat/riverbank; dive underwater into a submerged opening and swim southeast/south into the cavern.
Conecta-se a
Crow's Perch Upper Courtyard, Crow's Perch Castle Interior, Moat/Riverbank surrounding Crow's Perch
Missões relacionadas
Bloody Baron · Family Matters

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Infiltrating Crow's Perch through this underground route if you killed the Baron's men at the Inn at the Crossroads and found the main gate locked.
  • •Defeating the Level 6 Water Hag to collect standard necrophage ingredients, Water Hag Teeth, and a Water Hag Mutagen.
  • •Finding the secret cellar shrine and reading A Written Prayer to unlock unique dialogue exposing Tamara's religious fanaticism to the Baron during Family Matters.

Erros comuns

  • •Missing the underwater cave entrance by looking on land; you must drop down to the riverbank northwest of Crow's Perch and dive below the surface.
  • •Letting the Water Hag submerge and escape by relying solely on physical strikes; burn her with Igni immediately to keep her above ground.
  • •Forgetting to take the Rusty Key and track the incense scent from Tamara's wardrobe, which locks you out of the secret altar room for the rest of Family Matters.

O que dá pra pular

  • •Avoiding the underground cavern entirely during Bloody Baron if you avoided bloodshed at the Inn at the Crossroads, as the front gates will open freely.
  • •Ignoring the basement altar room during Family Matters if you only care about baseline quest completion, as finding the wine scent and talisman upstairs is enough to progress.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    Underwater Ingress

    Head to the moat and riverbank northwest of Crow's Perch, then dive into the submerged opening in the water. Swim southeast through the flooded fissure, checking the seabed for sunken containers as you advance through the dark tunnel.

    Dica: Loot the Underwater Cavern Chests resting on the floor of the flooded tunnel before you run out of breath.

  2. 2

    Submerged Cavern Basin & Rock Slopes

    Surface inside a dim, damp cavern filled with knee-deep water and rocky ledges. Wade through the pool, clamber onto dry ground, and follow the ascending natural rock steps upward.

  3. 3

    Monster Lair Section

    Enter the small clearing ahead where a lone monster ambushes you from the shallow water. Search the nearby dry rock ledges for supplies once the threat is eliminated.

    Luta importante: Water Hag (Level 6)

    Dica: Dodge sideways when the hag winds up her ranged sludge throw, then ignite her with Igni to stop her from submerging while you chain fast attacks.

    Atenção: Getting hit by the hag's thrown sludge will temporarily distort and blind your vision.

  4. 4

    The Old Well Shaft

    Advance past the natural stone into an area of constructed masonry. Ascend the stone stairs and climb the wooden ladder straight up the interior shaft to emerge inside the garden courtyard of Crow's Perch.

  5. 5

    The Keep's Basement & Secret Altar Room

    Head down into the keep's basement hallway until you reach a padlocked wooden door. Unlock it using the Rusty Key, step into the hidden stone chamber, and inspect the ritual setup inside.

    Dica: Coat your blade with Necrophage Oil beforehand to make quick work of underground threats.

    Atenção: You will permanently miss the Altar to the Eternal Fire and A Written Prayer if you complete Family Matters without looting the Rusty Key and tracking the incense scent from Tamara's wardrobe.

Batalhas principais
Water Hag (Level 6)

Inimigos: Water Hag

Ameaças: Throws mud/sludge at range to distort and temporarily blind the player’s camera view; rapid melee claw strikes; submerges into wet soil/puddles and teleports behind Geralt

Fraquezas e resistências: Igni, Quen, Northern Wind, Necrophage Oil

Táticas: Dodge sideways when she winds up to fling sludge; cast Igni when she surfaces to set her ablaze, preventing her from ducking under the mud and allowing continuous fast attacks

Recompensas: Standard Necrophage loot: Water Hag Teeth, Water Hag Mutagen, alchemy crafting fluids; experience points toward Bloody Baron quest progress
