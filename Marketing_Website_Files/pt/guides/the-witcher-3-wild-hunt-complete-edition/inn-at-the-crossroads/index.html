Pousada da Encruzilhada

Guia de The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Complete Edition · Encontrando os homens do Barão Sanguinário
← Todas as áreas deste guia

Geralt visita a Pousada da Encruzilhada durante a missão A Conexão Nilfgaardiana para localizar um espião imperial chamado Hendrik. Lá dentro, capangas locais que trabalham para o Barão Sanguinário entram de repente e confrontam o bruxo, gerando escolhas de diálogo com limite de tempo nas quais você pode apaziguar o encontro ou lutar contra eles. Assim que a situação se acalma, o estalajadeiro revela o paradeiro de Hendrik no vilarejo de Heatherton.

Região
Velen (No Man’s Land) / Mudplough
Níveis
Level 5 (main story visit), Level 33+ (Contract: Missing Brother)
Como chegar
From Hanged Man's Tree, ride southwest across the Pontar river crossing and past Mulbrydale along the road directly to the inn.
Conecta-se a
Heatherton, Mulbrydale / Hanged Man’s Tree, Border Post / Pontar delta, Crow’s Perch
Missões relacionadas
The Nilfgaardian Connection · Gwent: Playing Innkeeps · Collect 'Em All · Fists of Fury: Velen · Contract: Missing Brother · Wild at Heart · Bloody Baron · Family Matters
Serviços e NPCs
The Innkeep

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Buying the Commander's Horn, Siege Engineer, and Nilfgaardian deck cards from the Innkeep's merchant stock.
  • •Challenging the Innkeep to win the Menno Coehoorn card for your Gwent collection.
  • •Grabbing the notice for Contract: Missing Brother from the exterior notice board just outside the tavern door.

Erros comuns

  • •Picking hostile dialogue options with the Baron's men, which forces you to sneak into Crow's Perch via an underwater cavern later.
  • •Skipping Gwent or trading with the Innkeep early, since his potential disappearance will permanently lock you out of his cards.

O que dá pra pular

  • •Fighting the three Baron's henchmen, as keeping peace grants smoother entry to Crow's Perch.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    Arrive at the Crossroads

    Ride southwest from Hanged Man's Tree past Mulbrydale across the Pontar river crossing until you reach the dirt crossroad outside the compound. Discover the Inn at the Crossroads Signpost right outside the gate to unlock fast travel.

  2. 2

    Cross the Outer Stables

    As you walk through the outer village yard by the stables, observe a scripted scene of a villager urgently telling his daughter to hide from approaching riders. Before heading inside, check the exterior notice board right outside the tavern's front door to pick up local notices, including Contract: Missing Brother.

  3. 3

    Enter the Common Room

    Step into the ground floor common room, where you will see wary patrons, Bruno sitting in a corner booth, and the Innkeep behind the taproom counter. Head directly over to the Innkeep to trade and play cards before progressing your quest.

    Dica: Challenge the Innkeep to Gwent to win his card and browse his stock to purchase useful Nilfgaardian deck cards and Commander's Horn.

    Atenção: If conditions cause the Innkeep to vanish, you will permanently miss out on buying his cards or winning Menno Coehoorn.

  4. 4

    Confront the Baron's Men

    When you speak with the Innkeep regarding your contact Hendrik, three of the Bloody Baron's soldiers will barge inside to aggressively question you. Choose to buy them drinks ("Having a drink" / "Cheers") or intimidate them by stating you are a witcher to de-escalate the situation peacefully. If you pick the hostile path ("Back off or die" or draw steel), you must battle all three soldiers inside the tavern.

    Luta importante: Baron's Henchmen / Deserters

    Dica: In the tight common room, use Hanged Man's Venom, parry standard swings, stun with Axii, or knock foes into walls or flat on their backs with Aard for quick ground-finisher kills.

    Atenção: Killing the thugs turns the men outside hostile, causes Crow's Perch front gates to lock shut later, and makes the Baron's men at the Pellar's hut in Blackbough attack to the death.

  5. 5

    Question the Innkeep

    After the standoff ends peacefully or violently, speak to the Innkeep to learn that Hendrik is located in Heatherton. If you killed the thugs, the terrified Innkeep panics before divulging the information, whereas de-escalating keeps him relaxed.

  6. 6

    Search the Upper Floor

    Take the indoor staircase to the upper floor and rooms before heading to the rear grounds exit. Locate the player stash chest upstairs to store your excess gear.

Dealing with the Baron's Men

When thugs confront Geralt in the common room during "The Nilfgaardian Connection"

  • Hostile options ("Back off or die" / drawing steel)→ You slaughter the three thugs inside, making outside yard thugs hostile, panicking the innkeeper for Hendrik's info, locking Crow's Perch's main gates later (forcing an underwater cavern entry), and causing the Baron's men at the Pellar's hut to fight to the death
  • De-escalation ("I'm a witcher")→ The thugs back down peacefully, the innkeeper calmly shares info, and Crow's Perch's outer gates remain open later
  • Peaceful drinking ("Having a drink" / "Cheers")→ Tensions dissipate peacefully as the men leave Geralt alone, and Crow's Perch's outer gates remain open later

A maioria escolhe: De-escalation ("I'm a witcher") or Peaceful drinking ("Having a drink" / "Cheers")

Choosing violence may cause the innkeeper to vanish, permanently locking you out of his purchasable Gwent cards and his unique Menno Coehoorn card.

Batalhas principais
Baron's Henchmen / Deserters

Inimigos: Baron's Henchmen / Deserters (Level 3–5 Humans, 3 inside, optional small group outside near stables)

Ameaças: Close-quarters indoor combat with minimal room to maneuver around furniture; sweeping two-handed weapons and staggers in confined space

Fraquezas e resistências: Axii, Aard, Parry / Counter, Hanged Man's Venom

Táticas: Use Axii to stun or drop guard; use Aard to knock enemies into walls or flat on backs for instant ground-finisher kills; parry and counter standard sword and club swings; apply Hanged Man's Venom to steel sword

Recompensas: Common weapons (Rusty Velen Swords, Bludgeons), minor crowns, alcohol, basic crafting junk

Inimigos
Homens do Barão
Fraqueza: Espada de aço, Óleo de Enforcado

Humanos hostis encontrados dentro da taverna se você escolher diálogos agressivos ou não conseguir resolver o impasse pacificamente.

Lojas e NPCs
  • Estalajadeiro: Vende bebidas, comida e várias cartas de Gwent

Dicas
  • Apaziguar o confronto pacificamente dizendo que você é um bruxo ou pagando uma bebida aos capangas permite que você entre no Poleiro do Corvo mais tarde pelos portões principais, sem ser mandado embora ou forçado a entrar pela caverna subaquática.
