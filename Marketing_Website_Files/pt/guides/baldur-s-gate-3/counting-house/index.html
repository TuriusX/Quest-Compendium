Casa de Contabilidade

Guia de Baldur's Gate 3 · O banco de alta segurança na Cidade Baixa ✓ Conferido com fontes
← Todas as áreas deste guia

A Casa de Contabilidade é o imponente banco localizado ao longo das docas ao sul da Cidade Baixa, onde você rastreia Minsc durante as missões para ajudar a Guilda dos Ladrões e auxiliar Jaheira. Depois de conversar ou passar furtivamente pela recepção para ter acesso aos níveis inferiores, você deve resolver um código de segurança em ladrilhos no chão para abrir os cofres mais profundos. Uma vez lá dentro, você testemunha um assalto orquestrado pelo culto de Bhaal, enfrenta os emboscadores deixados para trás e arromba os cofres individuais dos clientes para reivindicar suas riquezas.

Região
Lower City
Níveis
10-12
Como chegar
south from the Lower City Central Wall waypoint, crossing the wide stone bridge at the southern waterfront between Flymm Cargo and Old Garlow's Place (X: -97 Y: -155)

Lower City, The Counting House - Vaults, The Counting House - High Security Vaults, The Docks
Missões relacionadas
Aid the Underduke · The High Harper · Return Rakath's Gold
Serviços e NPCs
Rakath Glitterbeard · Head Clerk Bloris Meadhoney · Cashguard Captain Faril · Minsc · 'Jaheira' (Doppelganger)
Tipos de inimigos
Bhaalist Invaders · Doppelgangers · Cashguards · Steel Watchers

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Defending Rakath Glitterbeard during the Bhaalist ambush so you can complete "Return Rakath's Gold" for thousands in profit and the Vault nº9 Key.
  • •Cracking High-Security Vaults 1, 3, 6, 7, and 9 for the Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen, Spell Savant Amulet, and Elegant Studded Leather Armour.
  • •Entering the code 1–3–5–6 on the floor grid (or electrifying a pool of water) to open the massive vault gate without needing a natural 20 lockpick.

Erros comuns

  • •Paying 10,000 gold for entry at the front desk when Meadhoney yields a pass via easy dialogue checks, pickpocketing, or office looting.
  • •Ignoring Rakath Glitterbeard during combat; Bhaalist assassins will quickly execute him if you do not shield or heal him immediately.
  • •Returning all 10,000 gold without attempting the Persuasion check, which lets you legitimately keep 5,000 gold while still collecting the full quest reward.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    Secure Vault Clearance

    When you enter the Counting House foyer through the main northern doors at X: -98, Y: -148, speak with Head Clerk Meadhoney behind the central counter. Inquire about the "Stone Lord" to learn that Head Banker Rakath Glitterbeard just escorted him down into the vaults, then pass an Insight or Detect Thoughts check followed by a Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation check to obtain a Bank Pass for free. Show your pass to Cashguard Captain Faril at the heavy iron gates leading down into the vault access corridor.

    Dica: If dialogue checks fail, you can pickpocket the pass directly from Meadhoney or steal one from the Head Banker's office upstairs rather than paying the exorbitant fee.

    Atenção: Do not pick locks or force the gates in front of Captain Faril without a pass, or the Cashguard sentries and Steel Watchers will become permanently hostile.

  2. 2

    Scout the Perimeter and Vault Level Passageway

    Descend the spiral staircases into the basement corridors where the basic client Safes (1–8) and regular Vaults (1–6) are situated. If you take the back exterior route behind the building facing Flymm Cargo, you can discover hidden exterior docks and storage stashes before entering the sub-levels. Head toward the fortified gates leading to the deepest vault, keeping your eyes on the stone walkway ahead.

  3. 3

    Disarm the Transposition Glyphs

    Approach the hallway before the main security gate, where two large Glyphs of Transposition are inscribed across the floor at X: -971, Y: 797 and X: -975, Y: 797. Spot the hidden wall buttons with a DC 15 Investigation check and interact with them to deactivate the runes, or leap clean over the runes using Misty Step or a high-Strength jump. Ensure every member of your party clears the threshold without touching the active glyphs, which fling you back to the entrance hall.

    Dica: You can shoot the wall buttons with ranged attacks or arrows from safe range to disable the runes without approaching them.

    Atenção: Stepping on either active glyph teleports your character back to the entrance and can displace companions into hostile vision cones.

  4. 4

    Unlock the High-Security Vault Door

    Stand before the massive vault gate blocked by a 3x3 grid of numbered floor plates laid out like a keypad from 1 at the top-left to 9 at the bottom-right. Step across the plates in the precise sequence 1 – 3 – 5 – 6 to disengage all four locking mechanisms, taking care not to step on any incorrect number in between. Once the four lights above illuminate and the gears grind open, gather your party before crossing the threshold.

    Dica: You can bypass the puzzle entirely by casting Create Water on the tiles and hitting the pool with a lightning spell, which short-circuits the door lock.

    Atenção: Stepping on an incorrect plate resets the entire code mechanism and triggers an electric jolt trap across the grid.

  5. 5

    Repel the Bhaalist Ambush

    Enter the innermost vault chamber to trigger a scene where the Stone Lord breaks out of a mimic chest alongside a doppelganger disguised as Jaheira. After Minsc and the false Jaheira teleport away, defeat the surviving ambushing Bhaalist cultists before they cut down Rakath Glitterbeard and his Cashguards. Target the invisible Reapers and Death's-Heads quickly, stripping their Unstoppable stacks using multi-hit abilities like Magic Missile or Scorching Ray.

    Dica: Cast Sanctuary or throw an Invisibility Potion onto Rakath Glitterbeard immediately on turn one to guarantee his survival against the assassins.

    Atenção: If Rakath Glitterbeard dies during this encounter, you permanently lose the ability to complete and negotiate the quest "Return Rakath's Gold."

  6. 6

    Plunder the High-Security Vaults

    Once the chamber is secure, explore High-Security Vaults 1 through 9 lining the perimeter wall, picking their DC 30 locks with Sleight of Hand tools or using keys collected across Faerûn. Loot Vault 1 for the Nymph Cloak, Vault 3 for The Long Arm of the Gur, Vault 6 for a Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen, and Vault 7 for the Knock Knuckle Gloves. Rakath Glitterbeard will merely complain verbally rather than calling guards if you pick these locks right in front of him.

    Dica: Having Astarion or a Rogue with Expertise in Sleight of Hand, the Guidance cantrip, and the Graceful Cloth makes hitting the DC 30 lockpicks reliable even without finding the scattered keys.

  7. 7

    Negotiate Rakath's Bounty

    Speak with the surviving Rakath Glitterbeard to accept the task of recovering the 10,000 stolen gold, noting the muddy footprints inside the open vault that lead toward the sewers. When you return here after retrieving the stolen pouch, hand over the gold or pass a DC 20 Persuasion check (reduced to DC 15 if Minsc is present) to pay only 5,000 gold while keeping the rest. Rakath rewards you with bank gold and the High Security Vault nº9 Key, which unlocks the final vault containing the Elegant Studded Leather Light Armour.

    Dica: If you bring the recruited Minsc in your active party when turning in the recovered gold, the Persuasion DC drops significantly.

    Atenção: Flatly refusing to return the gold sourly closes your business with the bank and deprives you of the High Security Vault nº9 Key.

Escolhas aqui

O que você decide aqui e as consequências. Os resultados estão ocultos: toque em um para revelar.

Gaining vault security clearance

Speaking to Head Clerk Meadhoney in the main lobby

  • Persuade/deceive/intimidate him, or steal/buy a pass→ Grants lawful passage past Captain Faril without angering the guards
  • Trespass/sneak past the guards→ Triggers immediate hostility from Cashguard sentries and city watch

A maioria escolhe: Persuade/deceive/intimidate him

A pass can be stolen from the first-floor office if checks fail.

Fate of Head Banker Rakath Glitterbeard

During the Bhaalist cultist ambush in the High-Security Vault

  • Prioritize protecting Rakath so he survives→ Unlocks the quest "Return Rakath's Gold" and permits peaceful presence in the vault
  • Let Rakath die in combat→ Permanently fails "Return Rakath's Gold," locking out his official dialogue, finder's fee, and legitimate key reward (must loot his corpse for key)

A maioria escolhe: Prioritize protecting Rakath so he survives

Bhaalists focus on him; cast Sanctuary, crowd-control, or healing on him immediately to prevent instant death.

Settling the stolen bank funds

Upon returning to Rakath after retrieving the 10,000 gold from the sewers

  • Return the full 10,000 gold→ Complete quest, receive ~2,600 gp reward + High Security Vault #9 Key (contains Elegant Studded Leather)
  • Pass History/Persuasion/Intimidation to make the Guild absorb half, returning only 5,000 gold→ Keep 5,000 gp outright, plus receive the ~2,600 gp reward + Vault #9 Key
  • Refuse to return any gold→ Keep the full 10,000 gp, but fail quest rewards and forfeit Vault #9 Key unless pickpocketed/lockpicked

A maioria escolhe: Pass dialogue check to return only 5,000 gold

Maximizes profit (yields ~7,600 gp total plus the armor key).
