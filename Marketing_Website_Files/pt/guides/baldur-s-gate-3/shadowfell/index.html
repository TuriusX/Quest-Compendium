Umbraevum

Guia de Baldur's Gate 3 · O plano de existência acessado por meio do Desafio ✓ Conferido com fontes
← Todas as áreas deste guia

Atravessado após passar pela piscina no Desafio de Shar, o grupo desce por rochas flutuantes no domínio de Shar para alcançar a Prisão da Canção da Noite. Aqui, você confronta Balthazar caso ele ainda não tenha sido derrotado e decide o destino final da Canção da Noite (Dama Aylin). Este momento crítico serve como um ponto sem retorno para o Ato Dois, bloqueando locais anteriores e moldando profundamente o destino de Umbrathriz.

Região
Shadow-Cursed Lands
Níveis
7-10
Como chegar
Through the pool / Shadowfell Entrance at The Verge of Shadows in the lower levels of the Gauntlet of Shar (X: -606, Y: -1435)
Conecta-se a
Gauntlet of Shar, Moonrise Towers
Missões relacionadas
Find Ketheric Thorm's Relic · Find the Nightsong · The Chosen of Shar · Daughter of Darkness

Dame Aylin · Balthazar

Undead (Necromancer, Apostate Destroyers, Apostate Craniums, Skeletal Invokers, · Justiciar Avengers · Justiciar Soulhunters

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Deciding whether to spare or execute Dame Aylin, which drastically shapes Shadowheart's character path, party gear, and the fate of the entire Shadow-Cursed Lands.
  • •Searching Balthazar's corpse for the Circle of Bones headpiece immediately after defeating him in the prison plateau.
  • •Grabbing the missable Dark Justiciar Helm from the skeleton on the side platform before leaping to the final encounter.

Erros comuns

  • •Entering the Shadowfell without Shadowheart in your active party; doing so causes her to permanently abandon your camp in fury once you return.
  • •Entering the pool before finishing open Act 1 and Act 2 quests, which permanently locks out the Wilderness, Underdark, Mountain Pass, and causes the prisoners in Moonrise Towers to perish.
  • •Trying to force Shadowheart to spare Aylin with the initial dialogue prompt instead of letting Aylin mention her wolf memories, which unnecessarily subjects you to a brutal DC 30 Persuasion check.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    Arrival and Descent

    When you arrive on the high rocky landing of the Shadowfell, note the reduced gravity that allows your party to leap vast distances across the chasm without sustaining fall damage. Follow the chain of floating stone islands downward toward the northwest. Before leaping across to the central platform, check the skeleton on an intermediate ledge to your northeast for missable gear.

    Dica: Use the altered gravity to leap effortlessly between islands without needing movement-enhancing spells.

  2. 2

    Confronting Balthazar

    When you leap onto the central plateau of Nightsong's Prison (X: -606, Y: -1435), Dame Aylin sits bound in Shar's circle, where Balthazar confronts you to claim her for Ketheric Thorm if you did not destroy him earlier in the Gauntlet. If Balthazar is present, refuse to let him take her to trigger an all-out battle across the platform while he summons legions of undead from bone piles. Once you destroy Balthazar, search his remains immediately to claim his unique headwear, alchemy supplies, and rare scrolls before speaking with the captive.

    Luta importante: Confrontation over the Nightsong

    Dica: Counterspell Balthazar's high-level Animate Dead or shove his heavy undead summons off the edges into the void.

    Atenção: Handing the Nightsong over to Balthazar causes Shadowheart to immediately turn hostile and permanently leave your party.

  3. 3

    Deciding Dame Aylin's Fate

    When you approach the trapped Dame Aylin with Shadowheart, speak with the captive aasimar to initiate the defining moment of Shadowheart's devotion. If you urge Shadowheart to fulfill Shar's decree and execute Dame Aylin with the Spear of Night, Shar immediately blesses her with legendary armaments. Alternatively, if you trust Shadowheart to choose for herself after Aylin reveals her childhood memories—or succeed on a difficult Persuasion check—she casts the spear into the void, liberating Dame Aylin and granting your party a radiant weapon upon her ascent.

    Dica: Let Aylin mention Shadowheart's childhood wolf memories first so you avoid an otherwise staggering DC 30 Persuasion check.

    Atenção: Killing the Nightsong causes the protective barrier at Last Light Inn to collapse, condemning Isobel, Jaheira, Dammon, and nearly all inn residents to death.

  4. 4

    Departure from the Shadowfell

    After the confrontation concludes, speak with Shadowheart to process the outcome of her devotion and her standing with her goddess. Once Dame Aylin ascends toward Moonrise Towers or her corpse lies cold, interact with the planar portal to leave the Shadowfell and return to the material plane. Ensure your entire party is thoroughly healed and rested before stepping through, as you exit back into the Gauntlet of Shar before marching on Moonrise Towers.

    Atenção: You cannot return to the Shadowfell once you step through the exit portal, permanently sealing off any unlooted items.

Escolhas aqui

O que você decide aqui e as consequências. Os resultados estão ocultos: toque em um para revelar.

Balthazar's Claim

Reaching Nightsong's Prison if Balthazar was not killed in the Gauntlet

  • Refuse and attack Balthazar→ Triggers a boss battle against Balthazar and his undead horde; keeps Shadowheart in the party
  • Let Balthazar take Aylin→ Balthazar teleports Aylin to Moonrise Towers; Shadowheart permanently leaves the party in outrage

A maioria escolhe: Refuse and attack Balthazar

Missable combat encounter if Balthazar was already killed in his Gauntlet laboratory.

Fate of the Nightsong

Speaking to Dame Aylin after Balthazar is dead

  • Spare Aylin (persuade Shadowheart or let her decide)→ Aylin is freed to assault Moonrise Towers, Shadowheart defies Shar, Last Light Inn stays protected, and you receive Selûne's Spear of Night later
  • Kill Aylin (let or urge Shadowheart to strike)→ Aylin is slain, Shadowheart becomes a Dark Justiciar and receives Shar's Spear of Evening, but the protective shield over Last Light Inn collapses, corrupting and killing almost everyone there

A maioria escolhe: Spare Aylin

Forcing Shadowheart directly requires a high Persuasion check (up to DC 30), whereas letting her think for herself allows her to throw the spear away naturally if you have sufficient approval/points.

Checklists

Imperdível 0/4

Batalhas principais
Confrontation over the Nightsong

Inimigos: Balthazar, Apostate Destroyers, Apostate Wraiths, and Reconstituted Duelists / Marksmen / Warriors

Ameaças: Animate Dead (raises huge waves of undead simultaneously), Cloudkill, Ray of Sickness, Misty Step, and bone-shoving undead that can push party members into the surrounding chasm

Fraquezas e resistências: Undead vulnerabilities to Radiant damage, Turn Undead, and Silence/Counterspell to neuter spellcasting; completely immune to Poison damage

Táticas: Leave ranged attackers and spellcasters back on the elevated rock platforms before jumping down to avoid the chasm edges, shut down Balthazar immediately with Silence or burst Radiant damage before his summons overwhelm you, and use area denial (Spirit Guardians, Wall of Fire) or throw bone piles off the ledge before the dialogue begins

Recompensas: Circle of Bones, rare scrolls/alchemy supplies, and determining the fate of the Nightsong (Dame Aylin)

Inimigos
Balthazar
Roubo / drop Círculo de Ossos

Necromante morto-vivo acompanhado por mortos-vivos invocados, como Destruidores Apóstatas, Defensores Apóstatas e Bibliotecários Apóstatas, se enfrentado no Umbraevum

Dicas
  • Cruze o limiar para o Umbraevum apenas após concluir todas as pendências nas Terras Amaldiçoadas pelas Sombras e nas Torres do Alvorecer, pois entrar avança o estado do mundo e bloqueia o acesso às regiões do Ato Um.
