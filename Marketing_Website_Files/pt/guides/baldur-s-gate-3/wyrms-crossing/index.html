Travessia do Wyrm

Guia de Baldur's Gate 3 · A ponte que leva à cidade ✓ Conferido com fontes
← Todas as áreas deste guia

A Travessia do Wyrm é uma ponte movimentada e de vários andares situada entre Rivington e a Fortaleza da Rocha do Wyrm, pela qual os jogadores precisam passar para entrar na cidade propriamente dita. A travessia abriga vários estabelecimentos, incluindo bordéis, tavernas e mercadores de armas, servindo também como um centro crucial para investigações de assassinatos em andamento e missões de companheiros. Os jogadores podem reunir pistas, negociar equipamentos de alto nível e obter autorização oficial ou encontrar rotas alternativas para passar pelos guardas do Punho Flamejante.

Região
Act Three (Baldur's Gate)
Níveis
9-12
Como chegar
North from Rivington across the South Span Checkpoint or by climbing around the eastern cliffs/balconies beneath the checkpoint ramparts (X: 15, Y: 74)
Conecta-se a
Rivington, Angleiron's Cellar, Wyrm's Rock Fortress
Missões relacionadas
Investigate the Murders · Aid the Underduke · The High Harper · Deal with the Gnomes · Confront the Elder Brain · Free Orpheus · Investigate Cazador's Palace
Serviços e NPCs
Danthelon's Dancing Axe (Entharl Danthelon - weapons/trader) · Sharess' Caress (Mamzell Amira - brothel mistress · Valeria · Raphael · Korrilla) · Carm's Garms (Carmen Pennygood - clothing/trader) · Potion Shop (Stylin' Horst - potions/alchemy trader) · Thodric Shedeveer (fishmonger)
Tipos de inimigos
Flaming Fist Guards · Steel Watchers

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Interrogate Pale Petras on the first floor of Fraygo's Flophouse to learn the truth about Cazador's Black Mass ritual before looting the hidden attic murder den.
  • •Show the Bloodstained Parchment to Investigator Valeria in Sharess' Caress to obtain the Lower City Pass for peaceful entry into Wyrm's Rock Fortress.
  • •Protect Harper Geraldus in the cellar ambush beneath Danthelon's Dancing Axe to preserve Jaheira's questline.

Erros comuns

  • •Letting Harper Geraldus die in the first round of the cellar ambush; place defensive buffs like Sanctuary on him immediately before attacking the doppelgangers.
  • •Attacking the guards at the northern drawbridge chokepoint instead of presenting the Lower City Pass from Valeria or an Archducal Coronation pass.

O que dá pra pular

  • •Carmen Pennygood's tailor shop in Carm's Garm, unless you want cosmetic camp clothing or wish to entertain Naaber's dialogue banter.
  • •The drow courtesans Sorn and Nym Orlith in Sharess' Caress, which provide roleplay cutscenes without critical story progression or powerful rewards.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    1. South Span Gate and Fraygo's Flophouse

    When you cross the South Span Checkpoint from Rivington, activate the South Span of Wyrm's Crossing Waypoint and enter Fraygo's Flophouse on the west side of the bridge. Take Astarion up to the second floor to confront his spawn siblings Pale Petras and Dalyria about Cazador's Black Mass ritual. Climb the attic ladder at X:-12, Y:87 and interact with the ornate red wardrobe at X:-17, Y:82 to unlock the false back using the Flowery Key, a DC 25 Sleight of Hand check, or the Knock spell. Inside the hidden murder den, pull the corpse of Ffion Goldgrind from under the bed to take Ffion's Key, and pick up the Bloodstained Parchment from the desk to advance the serial murder investigation.

    Dica: Bringing Astarion to Petras grants valuable approval and reveals that Cazador requires 7,000 souls for the ritual.

    Atenção: Killing Dalyria and Petras here prevents you from interrogating Petras for full details on Cazador's palace and removes them early from Cazador's ritual chamber.

  2. 2

    2. Angleiron's Cellar

    Before pushing further up the busy main street, climb down the knotted roots and rock ledges on the bridge's western abutment to find the entrance to Angleiron's Cellar beneath the bridge spans. Inside, rendezvous with Wulbren Bongle and Barcus Wroot at the hideout of the Ironhand Gnome resistance to advance their crusade against Gortash's Steel Watch. Trade with Bumpnagel to browse his unique inventory of anti-construct ordnance and specialized gloves before returning to the bridge surface.

    Dica: Stock up on Bumpnagel's Flashblinder Bombs, as their blinding and stun effects against Steel Watchers are invaluable later.

    Atenção: If Wulbren was killed or never rescued from Moonrise Towers in Act 2, the gnomes will not appear in this cellar and Bumpnagel's shop is permanently lost.

  3. 3

    3. Sharess' Caress Suites and Valeria

    Cross back to the east side of the bridge and enter the opulent brothel Sharess' Caress, where you can speak to Mamzell Amira at the front desk and Hoots Hooligan at the bar [S3]. Head upstairs to the second-floor alcove to present the Bloodstained Parchment to the drunken hollyphant Investigator Valeria, proving Father Lorgan's murder is part of a serial conspiracy to secure a Lower City Pass [S3]. Explore the remaining suites to hire Sorn and Nym Orlith for the Underdark Pleasures parlour, or investigate the Nymph's Grotto where Fist Jara undergoes ceremorphosis and turns into a Mind Flayer.

    Dica: Securing Valeria's Lower City Pass provides legitimate clearance through the northern drawbridge into Wyrm's Rock Fortress without risking hostility or arrest.

    Atenção: Speaking to Valeria without the Bloodstained Parchment from Fraygo's Flophouse causes her to dismiss you as an annoyance, refusing to grant you the entry pass.

  4. 4

    4. The Devil's Den in Sharess' Caress

    Ascend to the Devil's Den suite on the top floor at X:-14, Y:105 to interrupt a heated conference between Raphael and Kith'rak Voss. Raphael wards the room against the Emperor and offers a binding contract granting you the Orphic Hammer immediately in exchange for the Crown of Karsus upon the Netherbrain's defeat. If you sign the contract, head downstairs to show the hammer to Voss to claim the Silver Sword of the Astral Plane; if you refuse, speak with Voss in the taproom to agree to break into the House of Hope later to steal it yourself.

    Dica: Signing Raphael's deal gives you Voss' Silver Sword immediately, but you will have to invade the House of Hope in the Lower City later if you wish to void your contract.

    Atenção: Signing the pact severely upsets Karlach and Gale, enrages the Emperor, and binds your soul unless you steal back your contract later.

  5. 5

    5. Carm's Garm and Danthelon's Dancing Axe

    Return to the street level and visit Carm's Garm on the western side north of the flophouse to trade with Carmen Pennygood and endure the chatter of the indecisive Naaber. Continue north across the street into Danthelon's Dancing Axe, where you must trade with the Shield Dwarf merchant Entharl Danthelon to purchase his stock of high-tier martial armaments before proceeding with any quests. Ensure Jaheira is in your active party and speak with Danthelon using Harper passphrases to receive the safehouse key for the cellar.

    Dica: Buy Blightbringer, Horns of the Berserker, Gauntlets of the Warmaster, and the Cloak of Displacement before triggering the safehouse encounter.

    Atenção: Progressing the dialogue with Danthelon or initiating the safehouse events can disrupt or lock out his merchant inventory, so finish all trading first.

  6. 6

    6. The Harper Safehouse Ambush

    Unlock the cellar hatch behind Danthelon's counter and descend into the hidden Harper safehouse with Jaheira to investigate the local cell. Confront the figures gathered around Harper Geraldus, who reveal themselves through dialogue to be Bhaalist Doppelgangers sent to slaughter the cell. Slay the shapechangers while keeping Geraldus alive through focused crowd control and burst damage, then speak with the shaken survivor to confirm the infiltration of the Harpers.

    Luta importante: The Harper Safehouse Ambush (The High Harper)

    Dica: Cast Sanctuary, Banishment, or throw an Invisibility potion on Geraldus on turn one to prevent the doppelgangers from focusing him down.

    Atenção: Geraldus has low hit points and can be cut down in a single round if you fail to act immediately, permanently ending his role in the quest.

  7. 7

    7. Northern Bridge Chokepoint

    Head north to the end of the crossing where the raised drawbridge to Wyrm's Rock Fortress blocks the road, guarded by Blaze Elin, Fist Gai, Fist Shah, and stationed Steel Watchers. Present your Lower City Pass from Investigator Valeria, provide an Archducal Coronation Admission Pass, or pass dialogue deception and persuasion checks to have the guards lower the drawbridge. Cross over the lowered rampart to enter Wyrm's Rock Fortress, or use the wooden scaffolding and suspension balconies on the bridge's outer edges if you prefer a stealthy approach.

    Dica: You can pickpocket an Archducal Coronation Admission Pass from the toy-maker Arfur Gregorio inside Sharess' Caress as an alternative way across the bridge.

    Atenção: Attacking the guards or attempting to force the drawbridge without a pass or successful dialogue check triggers an alarm that turns all bridge Steel Watchers and Flaming Fists hostile.

Escolhas aqui

O que você decide aqui e as consequências. Os resultados estão ocultos: toque em um para revelar.

Confronting the Vampire Spawn

Fraygo's Flophouse first floor, when interrogating Dalyria and Pale Petras with Astarion

  • Interrogate and spare/release them→ learn about Cazador's Black Mass ritual under the palace, and triggers a camp ambush encounter later
  • Attack and kill them→ miss out on dialogue details and the subsequent camp kidnapping attempt

A maioria escolhe: Interrogate and spare/release them

Astarion must be present to trigger full dialogue and the camp ambush progression.

Investigator Valeria's Murder Case

Sharess' Caress second floor, when speaking to Investigator Valeria

  • Present the Bloodstained Parchment from Fraygo's Flophouse attic→ clears Brilgor's name, grants a Lower City Pass, and advances *Investigate the Murders*
  • Dismiss or withhold evidence→ keep the pass locked behind other progression methods and delay the murder questline

A maioria escolhe: Present the Bloodstained Parchment

Directly provides legitimate passage across the Wyrm's Rock drawbridge into the city.

Deal with the Devil

Sharess' Caress Devil's Den (third floor), when Raphael offers a contract for the Crown of Karsus

  • Sign Raphael's contract→ receive the Orphic Hammer immediately and unlock Kith'rak Voss' Silver Sword of the Astral Plane downstairs, but promise Raphael the Crown of Karsus
  • Refuse the contract→ keep your soul unpawned and must break into the House of Hope later to steal the hammer, leaving Voss waiting

A maioria escolhe: Refuse the contract

Signing locks you into a Faustian pact that triggers Raphael's epilogue unless you break into the House of Hope to destroy the contract anyway.

Saving Harper Geraldus

Danthelon's Dancing Axe cellar, during the doppelganger ambush

  • Eliminate the doppelgangers before they kill Geraldus→ Geraldus survives, points Jaheira toward the Guild and Minsc, advancing *The High Harper*
  • Let Geraldus die during the fight→ Geraldus perishes, leaving Jaheira without her informant though the questline continues via other leads

A maioria escolhe: Eliminate the doppelgangers before they kill Geraldus

Jaheira will be deeply shaken if Geraldus dies under her watch.

Batalhas principais
The Harper Safehouse Ambush (The High Harper)

Inimigos: Bhaalist Doppelgangers posing as Harpers (with Harper Geraldus as a hostage)

Ameaças: Doppelgangers possess multiattacks, Shapechanger traits, Surprise strikes, and high burst melee damage targeting Geraldus

Fraquezas e resistências: No innate damage vulnerabilities or elemental resistances; vulnerable to radiant damage and prone/crowd-control effects (e.g., Hold Person/Monster, Hypnotic Pattern)

Táticas: Position high-initiative martial or tank companions directly in front of Harper Geraldus to intercept initial attacks; use crowd control or AoE disabling spells (Hold Monster, Banishment, or Stinking Cloud) to shut down the grouped shapeshifters within the basement's narrow chokepoint before they execute Geraldus

Recompensas: Information on Minsc and the Cult of Bhaal's operations, progression for companion quest "The High Harper", approval from Jaheira, and assorted minor loot/weapons from the fallen impostors
