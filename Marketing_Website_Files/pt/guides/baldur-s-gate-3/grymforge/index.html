Forja de Grym

Guia de Baldur's Gate 3 · A antiga fortaleza sharista acessada por barco ✓ Conferido com fontes
← Todas as áreas deste guia

A Forja de Grym é uma antiga fortaleza subterrânea sharista alcançada ao pegar um barco pelas águas do Lago de Ébano, no Subterrâneo. Os jogadores navegam pelas tensões entre os escravistas duergar e os gnomos das profundezas escravizados, enquanto tentam libertar ou eliminar a Alma Verdadeira Nere, que está presa. Uma exploração mais profunda leva, por meio de enigmas de plataforma e armadilhas, até a lendária Forja de Adamantina.

Resumo

O que importa

  • •Strike a mutiny deal with Elder Brithvar beforehand so half the duergar fight on your side against True Soul Nere.
  • •Keep Nere's body on solid ground so you can loot the Disintegrating Night Walkers and sever his head for Sovereign Spaw.
  • •Mine both Mithral Ore nodes to forge two pieces of endgame Adamantine gear at the Ancient Forge.
  • •Lure the boss Grym into flowing lava to inflict the Superheated condition, then smash it with bludgeoning strikes or the central forge hammer.

Erros comuns

  • •Long Resting or fast-traveling outside after triggering Nere's telepathic dialogue suffocates him, wipes out all deep gnomes, and fails Barcus Wroot's questline.
  • •Shoving or thunderwaving Nere into the molten rock destroys his head and permanently loses his rare loot in the lava.
  • •Activating the Ancient Forge without bludgeoning weapons or failing to keep Grym standing in flowing lava, rendering the golem totally immune to damage.

O que dá pra pular

  • •You can safely skip paying Morghal's entry fee simply by refusing to pay ("I'm not giving you a single coin"), which causes her to back off without requiring an ability check.
  • •You can safely skip navigating the entirety of the upper fire-trap catwalks by casting Feather Fall or Misty Step directly down to the Ancient Forge.

Detonado

A área na ordem em que você joga. Marque o que fizer pelo caminho: os itens também serão marcados nas listas abaixo.

  1. 1

    Docks Confrontation

    When you steer the duergar skiff into the Underdark – Grymforge docks, pay Morghal 100 gold, intimidate her, or simply refuse to pay to avoid an immediate dockside brawl. Speak with Corsair Greymon if he survived the crossing to browse his wares, then watch Brathwen and Viss tossing dead gnomes into the water. Before they kick the next body in, pass a DC 10 Perception check followed by a DC 10 Deception or DC 20 Sleight of Hand check to loot it for pocketed valuables.

    Dica: Trading with Corsair Greymon now is wise because subsequent faction choices can make him permanently hostile or turn the entire dock area into a war zone.

    Atenção: Refusing to pay Morghal without passing an Intimidation check will turn the dock guards hostile before you explore the fortress.

  2. 2

    The Rebel Pact & Scavenging

    Head southwest into the main lower chamber to meet Stonemason Kith examining the masonry, passing three consecutive DC 10 checks (History, Investigation, Perception) to gain lore and free Infernal Alloy. Turn up the adjacent stairs to find Elder Brithvar and Stone Guard Kur plotting mutiny over unpaid wages; hear them out and agree to betray True Soul Nere for a cut of his gold. Before moving toward the dig site, climb down the cragged rock west of Kith to defeat three disguised Mimics and reveal a Harper stash.

    Dica: Getting the Infernal Alloy from Kith gives Dammon vital smithing material for Karlach's quest without needing to purchase or steal it.

    Atenção: Attacking Brithvar or declining the mutiny pact forces you to fight all duergar factions simultaneously during the later cave-in encounter.

  3. 3

    Inspecting the Cave-In

    Walk south into the excavation site where Sergeant Thrinn is driving enslaved deep gnomes—including Barcus Wroot, Lunkbug, and Laridda—to clear collapsed rubble. Approach the blocked passage to establish a telepathic link with True Soul Nere, learning that lethal poison gas is filling his sealed chamber. Speak with the gnomes and Thrinn to learn you need explosives to crack the rockfall, and ask Lunkbug to hear about Philomeen slipping away with runepowder.

    Dica: Do not Long Rest or fast-travel away once you initiate the telepathic contact with Nere, as a hidden timer will trigger his death and the massacre of all gnomes.

    Atenção: Taking two Long Rests after contacting Nere causes him to suffocate, failing Barcus Wroot's rescue and locking you out of all deep gnome quest rewards.

  4. 4

    Hunting for Runepowder

    Head north past the kitchen where Skickpit offers to poison the guards, and unlock the heavy iron doors into the flooded Sharran corridors. Fight through the Ochre Jelly ambush in the flooded hallway, climb the upper stairs, and circle around the Sharran doors into Philomeen's hideout. Talk her down from detonating her stash by mentioning Laridda or passing a DC 10/15 Persuasion check to secure a Runepowder Vial, or use turn-based stealth to steal the entire Runepowder Barrel behind her before she flees.

    Dica: Splitting a rogue off in Turn-Based Mode allows you to pickpocket or grab the massive Runepowder Barrel from the floor while another character occupies Philomeen in dialogue.

    Atenção: Aggacking Philomeen or failing dialogue checks without de-escalating will cause her to ignite the barrel, triggering a lethal blast that wipes out your party and destroys the powder.

  5. 5

    Freeing and Executing Nere

    Return to the dig site, toss your Runepowder Vial against the cave-in debris, wait for the working gnomes to clear away, and detonate it with a fire bolt or arrow. When True Soul Nere emerges and attempts to execute the slaves, side with Elder Brithvar's rebels and strike Nere down alongside Thrinn and her loyalist cultists. Once Nere falls, pass a DC 10 Intimidation or Persuasion check against Brithvar so he abandons the gnomes, invite Barcus Wroot to your camp, and sever Nere's head for Sovereign Spaw's bounty.

    Luta importante: True Soul Nere

    Dica: Position ranged characters up on the wooden scaffolding above the dig site before detonating the rubble to gain high ground and avoid the lethal lava pits below.

    Atenção: Pushing or blasting Nere into the surrounding lava destroys his head and submerges the Disintegrating Night Walkers, making both crucial quest items unlootable.

  6. 6

    The Upper Catwalks & Hellfire Ruin

    Climb the iron staircases behind the dig site to access the high catwalks running suspended above the molten fortress. Use the two hanging platform levers to ferry your party across the yawning central chasm toward the ancient Sharran dormitories. Leap or disarm your way across the fire-trap bridge, pick the far iron gate to hit the master disarm lever, and clear out the side dormitory containing a Merregon Legionnaire and his Hellboars.

    Dica: Collecting the six forge moulds (Longsword, Shield, Scale Mail, Splint, Scimitar, Mace) scattered across these catwalks and tables gives you the full suite of Adamantine crafting options.

    Atenção: Rushing across the fire-trap bridge without disarming the pressure plates or gargoyles will trigger repeating flame jets that can knock companions off the narrow ledge into the abyss.

  7. 7

    The Ancient Forge & Grym

    Descend to the Underdark – Ancient Forge waypoint by jumping or using the catwalk, smash the two glowing blue Mithral Ore deposits, and jump out to the lava-locked island to open the Adamantine Chest for the Sentient Amulet. Place a mould in the mould chamber and a Mithral Ore into the crucible, pull the forge lever to drop the central platform into the magma chamber, and turn the lava valve to summon the adamantine golem Grym. Keep Grym wading through the lava to maintain its Superheated debuff, lure it onto the central anvil, and pull the Forge Hammer lever to crush it with massive bludgeoning damage before claiming your forged gear.

    Dica: Using blunt weapons like warhammers or mauls deals double damage to Grym while it is Superheated, allowing you to bypass relying solely on the environmental forge hammer.

    Atenção: Stepping into the flowing lava without fire resistance or flight deals catastrophic damage, and letting the lava drain makes Grym completely immune to all attacks until you open the valve again.

Escolhas aqui

O que você decide aqui e as consequências. Os resultados estão ocultos: toque em um para revelar.

Confronting Corsair Greymon on the lake

Boat ride across the Ebonlake

  • Pass dialogue checks or claim Absolute allegiance→ Corsair Greymon survives to serve as a merchant at Grymforge docks
  • Shove him or attack→ Combat starts immediately on the boats, throwing enemies into the water and forfeiting Greymon's shop inventory

A maioria escolhe: Pass dialogue checks to keep him as a vendor

Plotting with Elder Brithvar

Meeting Brithvar on the balcony before clearing the cave-in

  • Conspire with Brithvar and destroy the Scrying Eye→ Brithvar and half the duergar fight alongside you against Nere
  • Refuse or ignore Brithvar→ Face all duergar and Nere at once, or fight only Brithvar's rebels if siding with Nere

A maioria escolhe: Conspire with Brithvar

Destroying the Scrying Eye unseen prevents camp-wide aggro

Confronting Philomeen in her hideout

Finding Philomeen in the northwest ruined corridors

  • Persuade/Intimidate her to share powder→ She hands over a Runepowder Vial to blow the cave-in and escapes alive
  • Attack her or fail conversation checks→ She detonates the entire barrel, killing herself and potentially wiping your party
  • Steal the barrel via stealth before/during dialogue→ Obtain the unique 50–120 damage Runepowder Barrel

A maioria escolhe: Persuade her for the vial (or stealth-steal the barrel)

Failing the check triggers an instant lethal explosion

Clearing the cave-in vs. resting

After getting the quest to free True Soul Nere

  • Clear the rubble with explosives in time→ Free Nere and the trapped gnomes alive to determine their fates
  • Take two Long Rests or leave Grymforge→ Nere suffocates in the poisonous gas, the trapped gnomes die, and the duergar abandon the dig site

A maioria escolhe: Clear the rubble before resting

Time-sensitive: taking two Long Rests or fast-traveling away locks in Nere's off-screen death

Siding with Nere vs. siding with the mutineers

Confronting Nere immediately after breaking open the cave-in

  • Side with Brithvar/kill Nere→ Slay Nere for his parasite, unique loot (Disintegrating Night Walkers), and his head for Sovereign Spaw
  • Side with Nere→ Slaughter the duergar rebels; Nere gives you a Moonlantern/Broken Lantern for Act 2, but the surviving gnomes are murdered

A maioria escolhe: Side with Brithvar to kill Nere

Good companions disapprove of helping Nere kill the gnomes; do not push Nere into lava or you lose his head and loot

Fate of the enslaved Deep Gnomes

Immediately after defeating Nere while allied with Elder Brithvar

  • Pass a Persuasion/Intimidation check or pay gold→ Brithvar frees Barcus Wroot and the clan gnomes, advancing their questlines to Acts 2 and 3
  • Let the duergar keep them or attack Brithvar→ Letting them take the gnomes fails Barcus's and the Ironhand gnomes' stories; attacking Brithvar turns remaining duergar hostile

A maioria escolhe: Pass the dialogue check to free the gnomes

Required to recruit Barcus Wroot to your camp and keep Ironhand allies alive for later acts

Segredos

0/1

Batalhas principais
True Soul Nere

Inimigos: True Soul Nere, Sergeant Thrinn, and cultist Duergar (Mind Masters, Brawlers, Hunters; Duergar rebels under Elder Brithvar can ally or oppose you)

Ameaças: Legendary Action: The Dying Light / Soul Retribution (extra psychic damage upon taking damage), Coercive Infusion / Dominate Person, Misty Step, Sanctuary, and Duergar allies using Enlarge, Invisibility, and pushing attacks near lava

Fraquezas e resistências: Vulnerable to high burst physical damage and Silence/Counterspell to shut down spellcasting; Drow Sleep/Charm resistance; Duergar have Dwarven Resilience (Poison resistance)

Táticas: Position ranged characters and spellcasters on the high-ground stone balconies/scaffolding overlooking the dig site before detonating the cave-in rubble; recruit Elder Brithvar beforehand to halve enemy numbers; bottleneck melee reinforcements around the stairs, keep characters away from lava edges, and focus down Nere with burst attacks before he can dominate allies

Recompensas: Disintegrating Night Walkers, Sword of Screams, Broken Moonlantern, Mind Flayer Parasite Specimen, and Nere's Head

Inimigos
Grym
Fraqueza: Vulnerável a dano de concussão sob a condição SuperaquecidoRoubo / drop Elmo Crânio de Grym

O colossal constructo guardião defende a Forja de Adamantina e permanece imune a dano a menos que seja amolecido pela lava corrente ou pelo enorme martelo central da forja.

Lojas e NPCs
  • Corsário Greymon: Armas como o Arco da Banshee e a Espada Grande de Jorgoral

Dicas
  • Atraia o guardião Grym diretamente para baixo do martelo da bigorna central usando ataques à distância e, em seguida, puxe a alavanca para esmagá-lo, causando dano massivo e economizando espaços de magia.
