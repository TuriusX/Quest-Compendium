# 0.5.8 proposals: guide-backed markers (item 7) and playthrough memory (item 8)

Both are *(propose first)*. Nothing here is built yet. Written Oct 14, 2026.

## Item 7: Guide-backed markers

### The honest limit first

Guide pages have **no map positions**. Each entry has a text `where` ("Top of the stairs in the Tower of Mzark…"), and
sometimes in-game coordinates as text. The app also **can't see where the player is or where the camera points without
an AI call**. The only local vision it has is the camera-motion tracking that keeps markers stuck in place.

So a marker drawn at a spot on screen, with no AI looking at the screen, isn't possible. What's possible, from free
to cheap:

| Option | What the player gets | Cost | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. "Near you" from the guide** (free) | The quest log and the answer box already list the area's missables and collectibles with their `where` text. Add a compact view of the 3 nearest, in walkthrough order, ticking hides them. | $0 | As good as the guide's text; no position on screen. |
| **B. Guide entries in screenshots you already send** | When the player asks with a screenshot, the prompt also gets the area's unticked guide entries (names + where, about 300 tokens). The AI marks any it can see, with an amber "From the guide" marker. No extra question. | about $0.0006 a question on Pro, $0.0002 on Fast; under $1/month at today's volume | Only things visible in that screenshot. The existing precision pass checks each marker. Big things (chests, doors, NPCs, statues) work; small pickups mostly won't be seen. |
| **C. "Spot it" on a guide entry** | A button on a guide or quest-log entry: takes a fresh screenshot, one Flash-Lite call ("is X visible, and where?"), a marker if yes, "Not in view" if not. Doesn't use a question; 20 a day (like Locate me). | about $0.0004 a call | Flash-Lite is weak on small objects, and most guide items sit inside containers. Expect "Not in view" often and some wrong marks. |

**Recommendation: build A and B for 0.5.8; test C first.** Run C on 30 real screenshots with known items (about
$0.02) and build it only if it finds the item at least 60% of the time with few false marks.

### Design (A and B)
- **A:**
  - A "Near you" line in the compact view and on the quest log head: the next unticked missable or collectible and its `where`.
  - Click opens the entry in the guide.
- **B:**
  - In `server.ts`, the markers prompt gets an `[AREA GUIDE ENTRIES]` block: unticked only, at most 12, missables first.
  - A marker the AI matches to an entry carries `guideId`, is drawn amber with a small book icon, and ticks with the entry.
  - At most 2 guide markers per answer, so they never crowd out the answer's own markers.
  - The marker's note says "From the guide · position may be off" the first time a player sees one.

### Files
- `server.ts`: the markers prompt and `extractScreenPoints` (`guideId`).
- `guidesApi.ts`: the area's entries for the prompt.
- `src/types.ts`: `ScreenPoint.guideId`.
- `src/App.tsx`: tick sync between marker and entry.
- `electron/pointers.html`: the amber guide style.
- `src/utils/trackerPayload.ts` and `ChatArea.tsx`: Near you.
- `src/i18n.tsx`.
- C would add `/api/spot` in `locateMe.ts` and a button in `electron/tracker.html` and `QcGuidesView.tsx`.

### Risks
- **Wrong marks cost trust.** Mitigations:
  - the amber style and "may be off" note,
  - the 2-marker cap,
  - the precision pass drops markers it can't confirm,
  - a 👎 reason "marker" already exists, so we can measure.
- **Prompt growth:** capped at 12 entries, and only for areas with a guide page.
- No map or minimap arrows: the guides would need real coordinates, and most games and sources don't publish them. That's out of scope.

## Item 8: Playthrough memory

### What exists today
- `settings.gameDone[game]`: up to 30 things done (fights, quests, steps, areas passed), taken from the answers' `<qc-done>` block, ended fights and ticks. It is already sent as `ALREADY DONE`. Players can't see or edit it.
- `settings.gameProgress[game]`: the place and story point.
- The Notes tab (`tab.notes`): free text the player writes. It is **not** sent to answers.
- Guide ticks (`settings.guideDone`), synced to the account.

### Design
- **The panel:** "Your playthrough" per game, opened from the ⋯ menu, the quest log head, and a line under the Notes tab. It shows:
  - **Where you are:** place and story point, editable.
  - **Choices you made:** "Sided with the druids", "Spared Astarion's master".
  - **Done:** quests, fights and areas, i.e. today's `gameDone`.
  - **Found:** key items, from the answers and from guide ticks.
  - **Your notes:** the existing Notes text, now with a "Use in answers" switch (on by default).
- **Editing:** each row can be edited or deleted. There is a "Clear this game", and a switch in Settings, "Remember my playthrough" (on).
- **Where entries come from**, with **no new AI call**:
  - The answer's existing `<qc-done>` block gains two kinds, `choice` and `found`, so the model notes them as it answers.
  - Guide ticks add Found and Done entries for free.
  - Each entry keeps its source and date ("from your answer on Oct 12"), so a wrong one is easy to spot.
- **In answers:**
  - A `[YOUR PLAYTHROUGH]` block, at most 1,200 characters: choices first, then recent done/found, then notes.
  - The rule given to the AI: fit the answer to these, but trust a newer screenshot over the memory.
- **Storage:**
  - `users/{uid}/playthrough/{gameKey}`, its own document per game, so the main user doc stays clear of the 1 MB limit. It needs one rule in `firestore.rules`: the owner can read and write.
  - Guests keep it on the computer.
  - Caps: 40 entries per game, 160 characters each.

### Cost
- About 400 more prompt tokens per question: $0.0008 on Pro and $0.0003 on Fast (Flash prices double Jan 1).
- About 30 more output tokens: $0.0004 on Pro.
- At today's volume that's under $1 a month. No new calls, so no budget change is needed.

### Files
- `src/utils/progressMemory.ts`: new kinds, sources, edit and clear.
- New `src/components/PlaythroughPanel.tsx`.
- `src/App.tsx`: sending it, Settings switch.
- `src/hooks/useCloudSync.ts`: the per-game doc.
- `firestore.rules`.
- `server.ts`:
  - `<qc-done>` kinds,
  - the context block,
  - the privacy policy text.
- `src/i18n.tsx`.

### Privacy
- The privacy policy gets one paragraph: what Quest Compendium notes about your playthrough, that you can see, edit and clear it, and that it's used only for your answers.
- Clearing deletes the cloud doc too.
- Account deletion is still by email. There is no self-serve deletion yet, which would be worth a separate item.

### Risks
- **Wrong memories:** the AI misreads a choice. They are visible, editable and dated, and a newer screenshot wins.
- **Stale memories after a new game or New Game+:** "Clear this game", plus a prompt to clear it when the story point jumps back.
- **Prompt bloat:** the 1,200-character cap.
- **Sync conflicts:** the per-game doc is merged by entry id, the same as guide ticks.

### Suggested 0.5.8 scope
Item 7 A+B and item 8 make two items, within the 2-3 rule. Item 9 ("Previously on…") builds on item 8 later.
