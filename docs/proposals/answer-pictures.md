# More pictures in answers, without clutter (proposal, Nov 17 2026)

A proposal only. Nothing is built until Noah OKs it.

**Noah's rules for this:**
- A picture only where it helps the player act:
  - an item or enemy to recognise;
  - a place or a route;
  - a puzzle or a hidden spot.
- None for general advice.
- Placed beside the step it explains.
- A small limit: about 1 per step, 2 or 3 per answer, any more behind "More pictures".
- Leave a picture out rather than show a weak one.

## 1. What the last answers got (free review)

**The sample:** every visual decision the server logged from Nov 14 (when the log line was added) to Nov 18 (UTC). That's 70 answers across the desktop app, web, Steam Deck and the site, mostly Elden Ring and Baldur's Gate 3.
- The log keeps the game, the first 80 characters of the question, and what was chosen and why.
- It does not keep the answer text, so this review reads questions and decisions, not the answers themselves.

| What happened | Answers | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A video or picture shown | 28 | Mostly right: "how do I get X" questions got a route or questline video (the Nagakiba, Regalia of Eochaid, Haligdrake Talisman, the Gold Scarab) |
| Nothing: "What's next?" / "What should I do next?" | 15 | **The biggest gap.** These answers name a concrete next place, character or item, and a picture of that thing would help the player find or recognise it. | Nothing: "best X" (best longbow, heavy armor, charm, gear for here) | 5 | **A gap.** The answer names specific items; a small picture of each helps recognise it in a shop or chest. |

**Pictures that missed the point:** "Show me where on screen" (4 times) got the character's or item's wiki portrait (Yura, the Whetstone Knife). That helps recognise the thing, but doesn't show where it is on the screen, which is what was asked.

**Placement:** today an answer gets at most one visual, under the whole answer. It's never beside the step it's about.

## 2. The proposal

**Pictures chosen from the answer's steps, not only from the question.**
- After an answer is written, one small check (the existing visual check, extended) reads its steps. It picks at most 3 that name something to recognise or a place to find: a character to talk to, an item to pick up, an enemy, a landmark, a hidden spot, a puzzle.
- **The pictures come from where visuals already come from:** wiki pictures, checked and credited. Never our own guides' pictures, and never AI images.
- Each picture goes **beside its step** as a small thumbnail (about 56 px). Tapping or clicking it enlarges it.
- **At most 1 per step and 3 per answer.** If more steps qualified, a "More pictures" line shows them only when opened.
- A step with no good picture gets nothing; the check drops any picture it's unsure about.
- **The question's own visual** (a route video, a boss fight video) stays as today, under the answer. It doesn't count towards the 3.
- **"What's next?" answers** are the main gainers: the next place or the character to find gets its picture beside that step.
- **"Best X" answers:** the top 2 or 3 named items get a thumbnail each.
- **General advice, builds, lore and clarifications** stay picture-free (the review shows the current rule is right there).

**"Show me where on screen" stops getting a portrait.** That question is about the player's own screen. It gets a close-up cut from the screenshot when the check confirmed the thing is there, else nothing. This is the same order as the pop-up card's picture slot.

**One rule for both places:** the pop-up card's one picture slot takes the best of these, in the order Noah set:
1. the answer's own visual;
2. else a confirmed close-up;
3. else nothing.

## 3. Cost

- **Today:** a visual costs about $0.0046 per answer on average in this sample (the most was $0.031, with YouTube searches), and saved results cost nothing.
- **Step pictures:** about 1–2 more Flash calls per answer that qualifies (choosing the steps, checking the pictures). That's about $0.002–0.004, only on answers with steps that name something. Wiki picture lookups are free, and no YouTube searches are added.
- **At this sample's volume** (about 18 answers a day), that's well under $0.10 a day.
- **Safety:** the existing visual counters in /stats and the daily search cap still apply, and a $1-a-day alert on visual cost could be added.

## 4. Steps

| Step | What | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The server: step pictures (choose up to 3 steps, find and check wiki pictures), saved with the answer like visuals today | Medium |
| 2 | The apps: thumbnails beside steps, "More pictures", tap to enlarge (desktop and web first, then the Deck's full answer page) | Medium |
| 3 | "Show me where on screen": a confirmed close-up instead of a portrait | Small |
| 4 | Check on the same 20 captured questions side by side, old vs new, before release (about $0.10) | Small |

**Recommendation:** do it, starting with "What's next?" answers, the biggest gap and the most common question in the sample. It costs fractions of a cent per answer, fits the rules above, and never changes the answer text.
